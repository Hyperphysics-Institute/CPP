## §2 — What clause 2 therefore requires, in physics

The A1 closure asserts a stationary measure generated by a gradient
(hence a well-defined θ_eff, hence the Boltzmann FORM at linear
order). S1's memory counterexample: **a dynamics whose response
depends on its history need not admit such a measure** — the
stationary state can carry non-Markovian structure that no
instantaneous gradient reproduces. PR7 clause 2 does not ask that
memory be ABSENT; it asks that it be **bounded subdominant at the
d_DP scale** — i.e. that whatever history-dependence exists is small
compared to the instantaneous response at the dipole spacing, so the
bridge holds to leading order there.

**Why PR3 does not close it (the panel's point, now mechanistic):**
PR3 measured the STATIC (zero-frequency) susceptibility and found the
fluctuation-response bridge holds. Memory is a FINITE-FREQUENCY
property: a system can have an exact zero-frequency response while
carrying large memory at ω > 0. The two tests are orthogonal, which
is exactly why the panel refused to let PR3 discharge clause 2.

## §3 — Measurability (scoping only; no prereg here)

Clause 2 appears tractable on machinery already committed, by either
of two standard routes:
- **(a) Memory-kernel route.** Extract the Mori–Zwanzig kernel K(t)
  from the force autocorrelation of a tagged CP on the existing Ewald
  chains; compare ∫K(t)dt against the instantaneous term at
  separation d_DP. Subdominance = a preregistered ratio bar.
- **(b) Frequency-response route.** Measure χ(k, ω) at small ω by
  driving the same external field used in PR3 with a slow
  oscillation, and compare its ω-dependence against the Markovian
  (frequency-independent) prediction over the band that matters at
  d_DP.

Route (b) reuses the PR3 apparatus almost unchanged and is therefore
the cheaper first attempt; route (a) is the more direct statement of
"memory" but needs a tagged-particle force decomposition the current
scripts do not emit.

**To S1's five-part specification (adopted 2829), each route must
supply:** a defined kernel or correlation function; evaluation at
physical d_DP; a preregistered subdominance threshold; uncertainty and
finite-size controls; and separation from the short-range transient
and regulator effects — the last being non-trivial, since the S4-X
campaign showed exactly that transient contaminating naive window
estimators.
