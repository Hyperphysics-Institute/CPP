> 0.15 bar needs only **v/c < 1.5 × 10⁻²**.

appears as a v/c bar, and it is wrong. It came from the handover's
compressed phrasing — *"the ambient DP-Sea centre-of-mass v/c, against
an operative bar of 0.15"* — carried without opening the sketch.

**This is the fourth handover-gloss failure in one session, and the

**~5000×** — not 4.74×. The error made the worker's own negative result
look *milder* than it is.
