## §1 — The error in 3189's framing

3189 declared the worker disqualified and wrote a question. **The
disqualification is real but ASYMMETRIC**, and 3189 treated it as
symmetric:
- **The permissive branch (RE-READ-PERMITTED)** would convert an
  INADMISSIBLE result into a favourable ABOVE-BAND reading. The worker
  may not take it alone. *That* is what 2b bars.
- **The strict branch (FUTURE-ONLY)** costs the worker the favourable
  reading and leaves 3188's refusals standing. **Nothing bars the
  worker from ruling against itself, and no authority needs to be
  spent to permit it.**

Escalating a choice where a self-denying option exists is not
impartiality — **it is asking permission**, and the founder named it as
such. Compounding cost: a panel asked *"is this permitted?"* commonly
answers with additional demands; three seats naming three alternatives
would generate work this programme would then owe, none of it
requested by the physics.
