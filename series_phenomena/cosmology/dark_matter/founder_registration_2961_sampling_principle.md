## §2 — PRINCIPLE-R1 (worker proposal, pending founder ratification): SAMPLING, NOT CONSUMPTION

**There is no double counting, provided one accounting rule is
frozen — and it is the rule Version B already implies:**

1. **Per-GP tally:** a GP's per-Moment SSV contribution from a
   given origin is the count of that origin's DI-bits TRANSITING
   (arriving at and being forwarded by) that GP during the Moment.
   Every DI-bit passes any given GP on its path exactly ONCE, so it
   contributes to that GP's tally exactly once.
2. **Non-destructive sampling:** transit is a READ — the DI-bit
   count is conserved through passage (verified exactly at 2959
   check 2). Interior GPs sample the flux; they do not consume it.
   Influence without deposition is precisely the conservative-field
   character.
3. **No summing across radii:** the field AT a radius is defined by
   the tallies AT that radius (which scale as the transiting flux
   density, ~1/r² by counting). One never adds interior tallies to
   PSR tallies — each shell samples the SAME conserved flux.
   Double counting would arise only from that forbidden sum, or
   from treating PSR arrival as a SECOND injection of the same
   DI-bits' influence. Under R1, arrival is not a separate source:
   **the PSR tally is the last sample of the same flux, under the
   same uniform rule as every interior GP.** No special arrival
   term exists to double-count.
4. **Where work happens:** sampling costs nothing; energy exchange
   occurs only when CPs DISPLACE in response to the sampled SSV —
   the DP polarization dynamic whose fore/aft ledger is exactly the
   K1 arc's business (2956). Conservation at the relay tier,
   dynamics at the response tier: the tiers do not double-book
   because only one of them moves energy.
