## §1 — WHAT SYMMETRY ALREADY DETERMINES (derived from registered facts)

**S1 (standard protection theorem, stated for the record):** for wave
propagation in a lossless medium possessing spatial inversion symmetry
OR time-reversal invariance, the dispersion satisfies ω(k) = ω(−k);
the orientation-averaged expansion of ω/ck in (k·d) then contains no
odd powers, and the leading mesh correction is quadratic. This is the
argument that makes "lattices give (kd)² corrections" the default
expectation.

**S2 (CPP's registered position — the protection is ABSENT):** the
corpus registers BOTH premises broken: spatial chirality is a
confirmed irreducible primitive (FI-C-9, closed at V3), and the
temporal arrow sign(δ) is closed at W3→W1 (conditional on Mechanism
A). Therefore **in CPP the linear term is not symmetry-forbidden.**
Its presence or absence in the PHOTON sector is a dynamical question:
the breakings could enter EM propagation at leading order, enter only
as a polarization-sector effect, or decouple from the EM relay
entirely. Assuming suppression "because lattices are quadratic" would
import a symmetry CPP explicitly does not have; assuming an O(1)
linear term would assume the couplings no one has computed. Hence the
relay computation of §4, with all three bands frozen now.

**S3 (the fine-grained classification, fixed in advance):**

- **P-broken, T-effectively-even in the EM relay** → the generic
  linear-order effect is CIRCULAR BIREFRINGENCE (reciprocal L/R
  speed split, optical-activity class): polarization rotation
  accumulates over distance, while polarization-averaged
  time-of-flight remains even in (kd), i.e. quadratic.
- **P- and T-breaking both active in the EM relay** → genuinely
  nonreciprocal LINEAR terms in group velocity (time-of-flight class)
  are allowed.
- **Both breakings decoupled from the EM relay at leading order** →
  quadratic leading correction (the naive lattice expectation,
  recovered as a result rather than an assumption).
