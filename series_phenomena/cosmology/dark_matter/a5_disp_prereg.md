entirely. Assuming suppression "because lattices are quadratic" would
import a symmetry CPP explicitly does not have; assuming an O(1)

**S3 (the fine-grained classification, fixed in advance):**

- **P-broken, T-effectively-even in the EM relay** → the generic
  linear-order effect is CIRCULAR BIREFRINGENCE (reciprocal L/R
  speed split, optical-activity class): polarization rotation
