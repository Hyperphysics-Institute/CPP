## §4 — THE DEGENERACY HAZARD (PRE-REGISTERED BEFORE ANY DERIVATION)

**A wavelength-type observable measures the medium's RESPONSE scale,
not automatically its GRANULARITY scale** — sound's wavelength is
metres while the lattice spacing is ångströms. λ_dB and packet
dispersion are response-scale observables unless a corpus-derived
dispersion relation ties response to spacing. Consequences, frozen
now:

- If an anchor's conversion establishes only "the Sea supports
  response at scale λ," the anchor yields an **inequality**
  (granularity ≲ λ), not a value. Inequalities count toward the
  triangle **only as bounds**, and a triangle of pure bounds does NOT
  close the item.
- The question "is λ_dB a response wavelength of a much finer Sea, or
  does it directly express the DP spacing?" is a **physical-picture
  question for the founder**, flagged under PD-006 contact rules. It
  is registered here as **FOUNDER-Q-SEA-1** (no response required
  yet). The anchors' conversion patches may proceed on both readings
  in parallel, but no numeric triangulation runs until FOUNDER-Q-SEA-1
  is answered or the panel rules the readings distinguishable by the
  anchors themselves.
