## §2 — Not reached, and why (the FEM boundary)

**Emergent inertia (P-A2-2), Gibbs statistics (P-A2-3),
fluctuation–dissipation balance (C24), and DP number-density
calibration are ALL out of reach of this arc, for one shared
reason:** C23 stores inertia in the Sea's arc configuration, and the
model's state vector (CP positions + scalar charge field; C21 bits
closed to charge/polarity/origin) contains no rotational or
orientational state for that storage to inhabit. A faithful
implementation requires — in the founder's own assessment, recorded
verbatim — an FEM-class treatment, "since every CP of every DP in
this system is also doing ZBW oscillation and also carrying the arc
momentum for other DPs in oscillation and carrying their own arc
momentum." Whether arc inertia is representable at any hostable
lattice size, or is irreducibly collective, is the open structural
question a successor must answer FIRST.
