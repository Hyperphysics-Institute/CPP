## §2 — Statistics (frozen)

- **Primary estimator:** per-pair difference D_i = S_step,i − S_ctrl,i on the frozen 2967 response statistic; ensemble estimate D̄ = mean(D_i), i = 1…512.
- **Variance model (heteroskedastic; DeepSeek + Copilot):** per-pair variances are NOT assumed equal; uncertainty of D̄ by **block bootstrap** over pairs with **frozen chunk length = 8 consecutive pairs** (64 chunks), 10,000 resamples, percentile intervals. The MEAS-1 measured floor **σ_ctrl = 3.93e-3 enters as the model's per-leg scale check**: the control-leg ensemble standard deviation must land within a factor of 2 of 3.93e-3, else an ENGINE-CONSISTENCY flag is raised before any branch is read (the instrument must agree with its own calibration).
- **α and power (GPT/Copilot):** two-sided **α = 0.01**. Conservative power computation, frozen: with per-leg σ = 3.93e-3 and NO pairing benefit assumed, σ_D ≤ √2·σ_ctrl = 5.56e-3, so SE(D̄) ≤ 5.56e-3/√512 = 2.46e-4; against the 2918-decomposition motion-response scale 2.6e-3 this gives z ≈ 10.6 — power ≈ 1 at α = 0.01. Any realized pairing benefit only improves this. **UNRESOLVED-BY-FLOOR is therefore preregistered as reachable only via an ENGINE-CONSISTENCY failure**, and if it fires, that is the reading — no retune.
- **Outlier handling (GPT):** a leg is excluded ONLY for engine-fault criteria, decided BLIND to branch and to D_i sign: NaN/Inf in state; checkpoint-resume mismatch; conservation violation beyond the 2968 tolerance. Exclusions void the pair (both legs). Counts and reasons disclosed verbatim. No statistical outlier removal of any kind.
- **PSD / chatter subtraction (Grok + DeepSeek):** control-ensemble mean PSD is computed and subtracted from the step-ensemble mean PSD over the frozen band (0, 0.6·ω_N] with ω_N the Moment Nyquist frequency (the L-3′ admissible band). The **tail statistic** is the residual PSD integrated over the late-time/low-frequency tail window defined EXACTLY as in the 2967 record; the **Markovian-plus-stiffness prediction** = tail statistic consistent with zero at α = 0.01 AND the domain-scaling discriminator consistent with ballistic scaling. The **passivity check** = in-band residual Re-part sign, reported as SECONDARY.
