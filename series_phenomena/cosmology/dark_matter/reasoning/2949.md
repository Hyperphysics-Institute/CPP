**Task provenance.** 2946 handover §4 queue position (2), second half; executes the 2936 CONV-001
M3 shortlist item A5-LENS (Grok P3, priority 3) under the triangulation charter's per-anchor
prereg rule and CONV-011.

**Design decisions and why.**

1. **The 2936 mandate quoted and operationalized.** "Must produce the susceptibility map without
   α1 inputs" is the item's entire constraint surface. §2 splits admissible (registered STRUCTURE
   of PR2/PR5/PR6 — ladder form, method-independence discipline, monotonic-screened class) from
   barred (α1-chain descendants, AUTOMATON occupancy values, Tier-2 constants, contaminated
   pre-2714 numerics). The PR2 verdict ambiguity flagged in the release-readiness audit is panel
   business; the prereg uses only the functional structure so the map cannot become a vehicle for
   pre-empting that ruling.

2. **PR6's open legs handled as HALT-class, not ignored.** Only the continuum + Ewald legs are
   confirmed monotonic; external-field and Moment-rule legs await PR3/PR4. If the map turns out
   to need the open legs, that is a roadblock (panel convenes), not something to route around —
   the same honesty shape as the FP-6 Stage A wall.

3. **Branch structure mirrors A5-DISP deliberately.** CASE-FLAT / CASE-SCALE / CASE-DEV is the
   lensing-sector analog of CASE-Q / CASE-CB / CASE-L: null-transparent, constraining, and
   falsifier-class respectively. CASE-DEV inherits the CASE-L frozen response verbatim (the
   comparison is performed, the tension filed, no quiet re-derivation, founder escalation) because
   the stakes are identical in kind: an O(1) predicted deviation against percent-level-consistent
   measurements indicts the structure, and the prereg must remove the motive to soften it before
   the motive can exist. Bound DIRECTION in CASE-SCALE is derived-with-sign, not chosen — closing
   a subtle degree of freedom the A5-DISP conversion handled via the weaker-sign rule.

4. **CASE-FLAT framed as corpus-valuable.** A lensing-transparent Sea discharges a falsifier
   class; freezing that framing now prevents a null result from being treated as a failure and
   quietly reworked.

5. **The A5-SHOT/A5-LENS independence question surfaced now.** Both are gravity-only n_DP legs.
   Whether they count as two triangle legs or one is exactly the A2/A3 ZBW-ancestry situation the
   charter's 2930 addendum adjudicated (pair counts as ONE). §6 registers the question and
   reserves the ruling to the panel — a worker self-serving "they're independent" assertion would
   be worthless at adjudication and corrosive before it.

6. **CONV-011 mapping.** FLAT/SCALE conservative → combined review permitted under
   PANEL-PENDING/VOID; DEV → pre-action review; PR6-open-leg dependence → HALT/roadblock;
   unmatched outcome → HALT per 2942 §5. Frozen at prereg time, discretion removed.

**What would have been wrong.** Citing any lensing measurement or amplitude numerically; using
PR2's successor κ value (a number, and panel-contested) rather than the ladder structure;
asserting the A5-SHOT independence ruling; deriving the map in this patch (the prereg-first
ordering is the entire point); minting a susceptibility symbol as a registered quantity before
the map exists.
