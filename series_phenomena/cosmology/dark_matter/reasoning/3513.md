# Reasoning — Patch 3513 (verbatim, at-patch)

The transcript answers the 3512 question in mechanism and not in rate, and I want the record to say exactly that split. Branch (i) is chosen: the founder names like-charge repulsion as what carries the expansion once superposition ends. That is a real answer — it tells the framework that the count law's stall is not the physical end of dilution. But 3512 showed that p is fixed by how fast that thinning runs in Moments per e-fold, and the transcript has no number and no picture of the rate, so D1 is no more readable than it was. I resisted supplying the rate from a plausible picture (an edge dispersing at one step per Moment thins the interior only as 3c/R, which would be catastrophically slow) — that is a D-7-class substitution and the question belongs to him.

The larger content is that the residue mechanism changed under my feet. 3510 had the residue frozen at the dilution cutoff; the founder now has the leftover inventory captured into trios and the naked qCPs produced by knock-off later. That flips the subsidiary question (a third does not spoil a pair — my K1 was wrong, and K2 is closer but still wrong because the third is bound) and it moves p from "fraction never paired" to "trio inventory × knock-off yield". I did the one cheap check D-4 asks for: landing statistics alone give trios ∝ n_q², super-linear. I have labelled it a pressure flag and not a reading, because it has no dynamics and the founder's own words put the residue's origin in the repulsive era's collisions, which the flag does not model. Its use is to say where the band would have to come from if it comes at all.

One thing I nearly missed: the like-charge push he names as the driver is the quantity the EU lane found cancelled pairwise by the DP sea's binding at 3805. That is not mine to resolve from the DM window; it is flagged to the EU lane, not answered.
