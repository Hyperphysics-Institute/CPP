*Context:* directed the record-either-way discipline and the pre-panel self-check that

I want the record to show the sequence honestly, because it is the best argument the
self-red-team protocol has for its own existence. At 2333 I wrote that the exemption was
inconsistent because "the split has no place to cut." Within an hour of starting this
patch I had found a place it cuts: duration. The Λ mode stands forever, the rod persists,
the floor events live for τ ~ ħ/E — a persistence threshold separates them cleanly, and
my seven-door table simply did not contain that door. If the panel had found it first,
the finding would have been correct and the embarrassment earned. What saves the kill is
not that the door doesn't exist but that it is locked by an axiom: I went to the A3′
text expecting to have to argue about what "perceive" means, and instead found the
answer sitting in the packet inventory — LSP′ carries position, absolute time, and nine
field components. There is no slot for an age. A sourcing rule that waits N Moments needs
somewhere to keep the count, and the founder had already said, this very session, why
nothing keeps counts on the substrate: carried state is a new theory. The repaid-borrow
door closed on the same fact from the other side — netting is memory too.

The other thing this patch did was take check 3 apart, which I flagged at 2333 as the
place to attack first, and I was right to flag it: as framed, the Λ anchor was Case 3
wearing a costume. The honest structure underneath is better — a lemma that forces any
exemption to name its axis, with five axes each excluded by a named registered item.
Better because it converts the kill's weakest rhetorical moment into its most precise
claim, and because it hands the panel a correctly-shaped question: is the enumeration
exhaustive? I do not know how to prove exhaustiveness of an axis list, and I am not
going to pretend to. That is what adversarial review is for. What I can say is that the
five axes are the five ways two physical objects can differ in this framework's
registered vocabulary — where they came from, what form their energy takes, how steep
they are, how long they last, how big they are — and a sixth would be news about the
framework, not just about the kill.

One process note. The founder's "it's just something that didn't work when you tried the
derivation the way you did" was, I think, an offer of the merciful reading — outcome
(iii) — and I declined it at the time because the record says (ii) fired. This patch is
the right way to honor the instinct behind that offer: not by regrading the result, but
by attacking it myself before anyone else does, and by putting real work into the
"something else we haven't looked at." The elastic channel is next, and it is a genuine
something-else: a window realization that never touches the gate.
