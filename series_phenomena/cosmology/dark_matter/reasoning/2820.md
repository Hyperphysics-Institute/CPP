# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2820 (PR3: an underpowered design, reported as such)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-26.**

## The defect was in my own arithmetic, and it was findable at freeze

The 5% linearity guard and the 3000-sweep chain length were both my
[W] choices, frozen in the same document, and they contradict each
other: a guard that caps the signal at 5% of the RMS fluctuation
cannot be discriminated by an estimator whose error is ~10% of that
same RMS. I could have computed that ratio before freezing — the
unperturbed variance was already known from the campaign — and did
not. This is the same class of error as the 2784 D1 gate defect
(jointly unsatisfiable frozen constraints), recurring in a new
costume, and the record says so.

## Why the result is UNRESOLVED and not a pass

Both legs are consistent with Lambda = 1, and the 2A0 leg even lands
at 1.31 +/- 0.91 with the right sign and rough magnitude. It would be
easy, and wrong, to write "consistent with the fluctuation-response
bridge." An error bar that admits Lambda = 1 also admits Lambda = 0.4
and Lambda = 2.2; a test that cannot exclude a factor-of-two failure
has not tested anything. The frozen class for exactly this case
existed before the data and is applied without softening.

## Not running the other three shells

At identical design power, shells 2-4 would produce three more
uninformative numbers and a longer table. The honest move is to stop
and say the design is underpowered, not to spend compute making an
UNRESOLVED look thorough. Reported unrun with the reason, as P-X2 and
P-X3 were.

## Declining to fix it

Three successor routes are named and none is chosen, because I have
now seen that the raw-mean estimator is noisy and would be choosing
the replacement that most flatters the next attempt. A fresh prereg
under founder or panel authority is the correct vehicle.
