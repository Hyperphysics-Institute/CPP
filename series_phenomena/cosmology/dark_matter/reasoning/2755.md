# Reasoning — Patch 2755 (B-CHECK-80B amended charter, frozen pre-run)

The amendment's design question was what the minimal clean act is
that adjudicates the defect's causal status without letting the
excitement of the find contaminate the readout. Three decisions.

First, the fork had to run entirely on clean quantities. The 2753
comparators — tilt −1.185, A-DRV −1.922 — were produced by the
defective sampler, so any fork branching on distance-to-legacy would
be conditioning a causal verdict on contaminated numbers. They stay
in the record as descriptive context (the size of the defect's bite
is itself a finding), but J1–J4 see only A-FIX-UND, A-FIX-DRV, and
B-DRV. This is why A-FIX-UND is in the act at all: the exact-tilt
identity needs an undriven ensemble sampled by a correct chain, and
at ~2 minutes per A-path chain the clean tilt is nearly free.

Second, the fix itself had to be minimal and auditable: `mn[i] =
False`, one line, nothing else touched. Any broader cleanup would
turn "does removing the self-pair remove the enhancement" into "does
this rewrite remove the enhancement" and forfeit the causal reading.
Gate v2 then certifies the fixed increment against B's from-scratch
totals — the same instrument that caught the defect, now required to
pass at 1e-8 before any of the three chains starts. The gate's
asymmetry is the design's core: B and A-FIX agree at the increment
level while sharing no state machinery, so a J1 agreement at the
ensemble level cannot be a common-mode artifact of shared code.

Third, the J-fork's branch structure encodes what each outcome is
allowed to mean. J1 (clean r_fix ≈ 1, clean samplers agree) is the
only branch permitted a mechanism claim, because only there does the
enhancement track the defect's presence exactly. J2 — both clean
samplers exceeding the exact tilt — would be strange (a correct,
converged chain cannot beat its own Gibbs identity), which is
precisely why its enacted reading is confined to "(b)-like,
automaton adjudicates" rather than any interpretation I might be
tempted to write in the moment: if J2 fires, the interesting
possibility is equilibration failure in correct code, and the
automaton owes nothing to Metropolis. J3 keeps the honest possibility
that my fix or my B still disagree — no physics from a disagreement.
The residual-strip lesson from 2753's I-fork carries over: branch
conditions are mutually exclusive by construction, with everything
else routed to J4.

Seeds: 20260796/97 are fresh sequential assignments; 20260795 is
finally consumed by the run it was returned for. Power arithmetic
says the legacy 3.46σ enhancement is detectable at ~3.5σ if it
survives and unity confirmable within ~0.2 if it does not — the act
is sharp in both directions, which is what an amendment written
after a surprise must be.
