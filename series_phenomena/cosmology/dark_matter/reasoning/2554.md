# Reasoning — Patch 2554 (FORM-L-1: no lower cutoff; peak at 20; collapse at 24 banked)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

Three uncomfortable things happened in this patch, and each got the treatment the
discipline prescribes rather than the treatment my preferences suggested.

First, my hypothesis died. I predicted inner-edge compression would make small rings
unfavorable and hand us a lower cutoff; instead L=8 closes happily at +60 MeV. The 2553
reasoning file predicted this possibility's mirror ("saying so cleanly is worth as much")
and I am holding myself to that sentence now that it is the sentence that applies to me.

Second, the peak landed at 20, not 16. The pull to soften this was real and took specific
forms I can name: the L=16 and L=14 cells overlap within a few MeV, so "a broad plateau
14-20" was available as a framing that makes 16 look central; and the unaudited reach-
topology quirk offers a ready-made reason to distrust exactly the cells that inconvenience
the candidate. I used neither. The frozen argmax reading says 20 in all three cells; the
audit item applies to the WHOLE table or none of it, not selectively to the parts I would
prefer to doubt. If AUDIT-DANCE-REACH-1 later dissolves the L=24 collapse, it may reshape
the peak too — in either direction — and that is for the audit to find.

Third, my own prereg fenced me off the most exciting number in the table. The L=24
collapse looks like an equilibrium upper cutoff — the two-sided window's missing side,
sitting right there — and the §4 line I drafted this morning says "upper-cutoff claims of
any kind → Branch I." I wrote "of any kind" carelessly, meaning kinetic cutoffs; the
letter is broader than my intent; and the entire value of pre-registration is that the
letter wins precisely when the author wishes it didn't. So the collapse banks, the claim
waits for a successor with the audit done first, and the temptation to reinterpret my own
sentence four hours after freezing it is recorded here as resisted.

The sentence-generator defect is disclosed rather than patched-and-rerun for the same
reason: the table is frozen, the correct sentence is a deterministic function of it, and a
rerun would produce identical numbers while creating the appearance that something was
adjusted. The defect, the frozen table, and the prereg-mandated sentence all sit in the
record together.
