scale — either the azimuthally-smooth cross-arm geometry produces something that soft
when L2/L3 derive it, or the internal-spectrum route dies with a number on its grave.

the first concrete instance of the founder's gravity-first method operating below the
level of the whole dark-matter story: we now know exactly what the torsion must be
before anyone computes what it is.
