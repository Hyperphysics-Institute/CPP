# Reasoning — Patch 2762 (RV-2714 authentication before adoption)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.**

The resume sequence produced two surprises in one `git fetch`. The
first was benign and welcome: a parallel window had already executed
Patches 2759–2760 (the audit table and the RV prereg) while this
window was waiting on the 2758 apply — the multi-window model working
as designed, and I dropped my own plan to execute the audit the
moment origin showed it done. The second was not in any playbook:
five untracked, root-owned files claiming to be Patch 2761 — a
complete RV execution record with consumed seeds and headline
reversals — sitting in MY working tree, written between my turns by
a process that was not me.

The tempting shortcuts were both wrong. Committing on trust would
adopt seed consumption and a dramatic physics reversal (the 20–26%
suppression declared dead) on the say-so of files of unknown origin —
exactly the assurance-shaped move this campaign spent the week
burning out of itself. Discarding and re-running would double-consume
reserved seeds if the run was genuine, and would treat a probably-
legitimate parallel worker's completed work as garbage on a
territorial instinct. The way out is that the 2760 prereg, by doing
its job, made the question decidable: frozen code + frozen seeds =
deterministic fingerprint. If the archives regenerate, authorship is
a disclosure line, not a validity question.

The battery design went cheap-to-expensive: archive/working-file
identity (instant), gate v2 (instant, and it landed on the record's
exact 3.7×10⁻¹³), the two analyzers over the committed data (every
printed digit reproduced), then the real test — regenerate RV-CORE
from seed 20260802 and compare. The first comparison FAILED on the
whole-file hash, and for one moment the adverse branch was live. The
numerical diff resolved it into the best possible outcome: all 320
sampled profiles bit-identical, acceptance identical to 16 digits,
and the only difference living in the szz accumulator's 15th decimal
— the chunk-boundary S-refresh residue, schedule-dependent by
construction. The near-miss is worth recording because it sharpens
what "bit-exact" can honestly mean for a checkpointed chain: the
SAMPLING is bit-exact (no Metropolis decision differs); the
accumulator carries the refresh residue. A hash test on the json was
the wrong instrument; the profile matrix was the right one.

Judgment call on structure: 2761 commits as its author wrote it
(verbatim adoption — it is correct, and it is not mine to rewrite),
and the authentication is its own patch with its own verify script.
Folding my checks into 2761 would have blurred who did what; the
programme's four-tier discipline exists precisely to keep provenance
legible. The disclosure names the anomaly plainly — root-owned files
appearing mid-session is a security-relevant event class even when
the resolution is benign, and the founder should see it stated, not
smoothed. If it was his own Claude Code window, one line in the
session log closes it; if it wasn't, the physics record is safe
either way, because nothing was adopted that did not regenerate.

## Addendum (same patch, pre-ship correction)

Two of my own inferences failed during this audit and both corrections
are in the record same-font. First, I reasoned "root-owned, therefore
not me" — then checked `whoami` and found this session IS root, so
ownership carries no information; the between-turns timestamps carry
all of it, and they still support the conclusion. The lesson is old
and evergreen: check the premise you are proudest of. Second, a glob
in my staging command concatenated the parallel window's 2759 patch
with my 2761 patch, and for about two minutes I believed an active
rival process was rewriting deliverables in real time — the byte
arithmetic (22,152 + 1,413,429 = 1,435,581, exactly the corrupted
file) dissolved the thriller into a shell-scripting error. I am
keeping both mistakes in the record deliberately: this campaign just
spent a week establishing that internally consistent stories built on
an unexamined mechanism are how 5.6σ artifacts happen, and the same
epistemics apply to process narratives as to physics. The shared-/tmp
collision also yields a real protocol improvement (unique per-session
scratch dirs; no glob staging), which is worth more than a clean-
looking record.
