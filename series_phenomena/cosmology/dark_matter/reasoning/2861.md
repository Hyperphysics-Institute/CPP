against "an operative bar of 0.15" for v/c, took the phrase from the
handover, and never opened the sketch that defines it. 0.15 is the
threshold on epsilon_mem. The v/c requirement is 1.5e-2 or 1.4e-4

5000x. I have been watching for the failure mode where I flatter a
result, and this was the failure mode where I flatter a result by
understating how badly it failed -- which does not feel like flattery
at all while you are doing it, because the headline is still negative.
