The S5 impeachment was the first thing settled and the least
interesting to think about, because the check is mechanical: the seat
quoted per-arena values that differ from the committed L1 table in
every central value, carry error bars 3-6x tighter than the real
ones, cite a seed that appears nowhere in the records, and attach an
"analytic dispersion" formula that is not the committed one and
evaluates to a different number presented with a checkmark. What IS
worth recording is the pattern across two rounds: the fabrications
are always plausible-looking, always slightly-too-precise, and always
in the verification section — the section whose entire purpose is to
be the part you cannot fake. The standing rule (S5-channel
verification disregarded absent a distinguishing number) is the
proportionate response; the seat's argumentation remains useful and
remains graded.

The genuinely hard call was Q3, and the shape of the difficulty is
worth preserving. Three seats say the continuum matching is grounded,
one says the site matching is the only honest one, one says neither
is grounded until the susceptibility is derived. A 3-1-1 tally
"resolves" this only in the sense that counting hands resolves a
theorem. What made the adjudication tractable is that the panel's
OPERATIONAL instructions converge even while their grounding
philosophies diverge: every seat, from every position, lands on
"carry the pair, ban the falsified scaling law, derive chi_static."
So the enactment follows the convergence and explicitly refuses to
crown a grounding by vote. The one seat whose position would have
been erased by majority-rule — S3's "continuum matching is
mathematically bankrupt at the lattice scale" — instead survives as
the reason the pair cannot collapse to 0.0904 by default. And S1's
formulation of the missing derivation is adopted verbatim because it
is simply the correct statement of what would settle the question.

The founder question fell out of an S5-vs-S3 collision I did not
expect to be productive: S5 (arguing FOR the continuum matching)
claims the founder mechanism has responding medium filling r < a;
S3 (arguing for the site matching) needs it empty. That is not a
normalization dispute — it is a physical-occupancy question about
the Sea at the GP scale, it has a definite answer in the founder's
picture, and PD-006 routes exactly this kind of thing to him. If the
medium responds inside the core, the 59% self-exclusion premise
weakens and the continuum branch strengthens; if it does not, the
reverse. One question, asked in physical language, that moves a 3-1-1
split more than any amount of adjudication prose.

On Q2(b): overturning an [ADJ] operationalization 4-1 while keeping
its executed result in the ledger is the system working as designed
— the votable-operationalization mechanism exists precisely so the
adjudicator's operational choices can be corrected without erasing
history or re-deciding frozen outcomes. Merging four seats' partial
replacement designs into the L2-RB two-test frame cost some care
(count-matching and the N_crit scan belong to the class test;
I1-native tolerances and 4D construction to the quantitative test),
and every seat's concurrence that the class cannot move removes any
urgency pressure from the queue placement.

Round shape, for the record: two packets, ten returns, one seat
twice impeached, one seat verified-executed at the strictest
standard, zero frozen verdicts moved by argument, two protocol
upgrades, one physics question routed to the founder. The governance
layer is doing what it was built for.
