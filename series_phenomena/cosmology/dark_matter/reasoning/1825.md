## What the stand-in missed

Two couplings the single-velocity curve omitted, both material:
1. **v_thr is N-dependent.** The backing mass is rod-length-limited, so **long rods penetrate at lower v_thr**
   (more backing). And long rods **dominate σ/m** (= 0.11·N). So the suppression preferentially removes the
   high-σ/m rods — it bites harder than a fixed-N estimate.
2. **The suppression is cumulative.** The per-collision penetration probability p_pen is modest (~0.07 at
   clusters), but it **compounds over the collision history**: f_fused = 1 − exp(−N_coll·p_pen). With N_coll ~
   hundreds at clusters, even a small p_pen drives f_fused → 1.

## The convolution

Monte Carlo over: relative speed v_rel ~ Maxwell(σ_rel = √2 σ_1d); isotropic impact angle (penetrating
component v_rel sin θ); uniform impact position x → rod-length-limited backing → v_thr(N, x); N-distribution
P(N) ~ exp(−N/N_char) (equilibrium polymerization, 0861), mass ~ N. Penetrate if v_rel sin θ ≥ v_thr(N, x);
f_fused(N) = 1 − exp(−N_coll·p_pen(N)); fused → σ/m × drop. Population σ/m mass-weighted. N_coll ~ v_rel
(similar core densities), normalized cluster → ~600; drop = 0.25 (flexible, bracket 1/8–1/2).

## The critical flag — (3) is now decisive

At clusters **N_coll·p_pen ~ 10–40**, so a rod is hit-and-penetrated **many times**, not once. The curve above
assumes **single-fusion** processing (floor = σ/m₀·drop ~ 0.8). But repeated fusion of X-crosses into larger
aggregates could drive σ/m **below** this floor — potentially **over-depleting** clusters (a marginal-fusion
steady state N_coll(σ/m)·p_pen ~ 1 would sit near σ/m ~ 0.07, far under the bound). The saving grace, if it
holds, is that **floppy aggregates saturate**: a large multiply-fused coil has σ/m → const (the coil regime,
0860), not → 0. So **(3) self-limiting now decides the cluster floor**: single-fusion (~0.8, viable) vs
coil-saturation (~const) vs runaway over-depletion. This is the decisive remaining calculation — the convolution
has turned it from a sanity check into the determinant of the cluster value.
