# Reasoning — Patch 2601 (retiring an assert, and proving the contraction)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The assert retires with honors. It spent three campaigns converting would-be artifacts
into honest non-verdicts, and its replacement is not laxity but construction: a law
whose speeds are subluminal by algebra needs a monitor, not a guard. The convexity
proof of the sink contraction is one sentence and I wrote it into the rider because
"obviously fine" is how the last three defects were born; the segment between two
interior points of a ball lies in the ball, and now no future auditor has to wonder.

The two disclosures in S2 are the rider's conscience. The eta band was derived with
half-m-v-squared and I am carrying it into a gamma world with a queued re-derivation
rather than a silent assumption; and the momentum ledger exists because a sink that
sheds energy also sheds momentum, and C-SR's promise (uniform motion sheds NOTHING)
deserves a ledger line that can falsify it, not just an argument that it holds.

G4-gamma's phrasing -- the frame subtlety is MEASURED, not assumed -- is where I expect
tonight's most interesting number. The drift/osc split is a Newtonian idea imported
into relativistic kinematics; at 0.3c the import may cost a percent-level frame
artifact, and if it does, the honest path is to see it, size it, and route it, not to
redefine the split until the number looks clean.
