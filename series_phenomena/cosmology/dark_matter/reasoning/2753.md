# Reasoning — Patch 2753 (B-CHECK-80 prereg + code, frozen before any run)

The handover's queue was unambiguous — B-CHECK at N = 80 under a
frozen prereg — so the substantive reasoning of this act was not
*whether* but *how to make the act self-certifying*, and that question
was forced by something I found while reconstructing the B design from
the committed 2746 file.

The 2747 record discloses that B's k-space prefactor initially carried
a spurious 0.5 relative to path A's convention, caught by inspection
before any comparative result was read, fixed, and B restarted clean.
But the committed `code/2746_s4x_x5fe.py` evaluates
`PREF*0.5*np.sum(...)*2.0/2.0` — arithmetically 0.5·PREF·Σ — with an
adjacent dead line multiplied by 0.0 and a comment claiming "prefactor
already full". Left-to-right evaluation makes `*2.0/2.0` the identity;
the committed file therefore still encodes the half-prefactor the
record says was fixed. Two readings are possible: the runtime fix was
applied in /tmp and the repo snapshot lagged (a capture defect), or
the `*2.0/2.0` is a mangled fix (an intended `*2.0` neutralized by a
stray `/2.0`). I tried to adjudicate empirically from the archives:
if the F3 run's B chain had sampled half the k-space energy, its
Hamiltonian differs from A's, and the driven variance should show it.
Archived Var(A): B-DRV 18.86 vs A-DRV 16.13 at N = 64 — a ~17%
excess, direction consistent with a softer driven mode under a halved
k-space stiffness, but within the range that driven-ensemble scatter
and the N-trend (Var grows 16.7 → 21.8 across 64 → 112) can produce.
Inconclusive. I therefore do not reopen F3 — its physics conclusion
was triple-anchored (A-DRV, B-DRV, exact tilt mutually consistent at
0.3–1.1σ), and the un-tilt proof of the large-N pathology is
independent of B entirely — but the discrepancy goes into the
corrections ledger as a capture defect, disclosed same-font in the
prereg (§5.1).

The design consequence is the Hamiltonian-identity gate (§3). The
weakness of "inspection before results" as practiced in 2746 is that
inspection certifies the code the inspector read, not the code that
ran. The gate mechanizes it: five random trial moves, ΔE from B's two
from-scratch totals against dE from A's incremental machinery, ≤1e-8
relative agreement, blocking. This certifies at runtime that B's
Hamiltonian is bit-identical to A's while B still shares none of A's
state machinery — which is the entire epistemic point of B. The gate
uses seed 1 and reads no observable, so it cannot contaminate the
frozen readout. Whatever ran in 2747, THIS act cannot inherit the
ambiguity.

The fork needed care at one point. With expected e_B ≈ 0.15–0.17, the
two exclusive 2σ windows (around A-DRV at −1.922 and around tilt at
−1.185, separation 0.737 ≈ 3.4σ) nearly tile the axis but overlap in
a narrow central strip near −1.5; a naive two-branch fork would be
ill-defined there. The frozen logic routes the strip to I3 (each
exclusive branch requires BOTH proximity to its hypothesis AND >2σ
exclusion of the other), so the fork is total and mutually exclusive
by construction. I3's remedy is deliberately conservative — extend
under a fresh prereg or hand to AUTOMATON-1 — because an intermediate
value at the onset would be exactly the regime where the Metropolis
family should stop adjudicating itself.

Seed accounting: 20260795 was assigned to the H3-replicate contingency
that never fired and was returned unconsumed at 2751 close; the
handover explicitly offers it, and consuming it here keeps the pool
gapless. Comparators are recomputed at analysis time from the archived
N = 80 series rather than copied from the record's rounded values —
I verified the recomputation reproduces −1.185 ± 0.144 and
−1.922 ± 0.157 exactly before freezing.
