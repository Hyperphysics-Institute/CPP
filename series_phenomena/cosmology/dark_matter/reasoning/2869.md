# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2869 (scope correction; Stage B/C discrepancy)

**STATUS: contemporaneous, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-29.**

He asked whether I was saying his mechanism self-accelerates. I was,
and I should not have been. What 2868 measured is a forward self-force
proportional to v on a bare point whose motion was PRESCRIBED by the
ramp-and-hold. Nothing in that run was free to run away. "Self-
accelerates" was inference dressed as measurement, and after a session
spent cataloguing exactly that move in myself I made it again in the
same breath as reporting a clean result.

His conservation argument is wrong but not in the way that matters most.
Conservation does not preclude acceleration -- conservative systems
convert potential to kinetic constantly, and Abraham-Lorentz is
conservative and still runs away because the bare point's self-energy
reservoir is unbounded. So charging = discharging is necessary and not
sufficient. But the important part is that for a closed finite structure
his balance argument is probably right, which is 2496's own reading, so
our disagreement is narrower than either of us was treating it. It is
about the bare point only.

His proposed derivation I think will produce a consistency condition
rather than a velocity, and I want that on record before he does it
rather than after. Transferred volume per Moment is proportional to v,
amplitude is not, so SSV_net = alpha v, and the primitive gives
v(1 - mu alpha) = 0. Scale-free. Every v works if mu alpha = 1 and none
otherwise. That is the knife-edge restated, and it is a reason to do
the derivation rather than skip it, because it turns "is there a
knife-edge" into "what is mu alpha", which is a number. 2868 already
measured alpha. So the open question has gone from a picture argument
to a single coefficient, which is the sharpest it has been in this
thread.

The thing I did not expect is that Stage B and Stage C of 2496 appear
to disagree. Hold gives a forward self-force proportional to v. Coast
gives exponential momentum decay. Those are opposite signs for what
ought to be one physics, and under the primitive a forward force gives
marginality, not decay. I do not know how to reconcile them. I listed
candidates and did not pick one, because the pattern of this session is
that I pick the reconciliation that keeps things moving and it is wrong
about half the time.

What strikes me is why nobody caught it: the Stage B residual was
labelled an integrator artifact, so there was nothing to reconcile
Stage C with. One dismissive parenthetical hid a sign contradiction
between two stages of the same computation for two weeks. That is the
same failure as the C23 gloss and the PR7 "unexamined" claim -- a
characterisation standing in for a check -- and this time it was in a
shipped result rather than in my own prose.
