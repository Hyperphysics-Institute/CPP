# Reasoning — Patch 3528 (verbatim, at-patch)

I went into the chirality arc expecting to find that a radially polarised shell around a lone charge is mirror-symmetric and therefore blind to handedness at first order — and that the sign would have to come from a twist in the shell, a second-order object I could not build in one patch. F.1's Theorem 5.1 changed that: at vertex-aligned Reading C the substrate direction is the host's own position, and all twelve neighbours sit at projection −1/(2φ). The shell is polar with respect to n̂ by construction, so the on-file polarity rule sums coherently over it and gives a first-order, sign-odd energy. The computation is then five lines, and it returns the founder's sign for the quark sector.

I distrusted that immediately, for two reasons. First, the whole result hangs on reading C-W46's "−qCP centre stabilised" as "the − member sits n̂-ward"; I could not extract the convention unambiguously from the sketch's doublet notation, so I have stated the reading once as an input and flagged that its mirror flips everything. That is the difference between a computed sign and a chosen one: the input is on file and checkable, and I did not pick it because it lands. Second, I ran the same rule on the lepton sector, because the founder's composition puts matter at opposite electric polarities in the two species, and a purely electric rule cannot serve both. It does not: the electron comes out destabilised. I recorded that as a fail rather than reaching for a species-dependent sign, because reaching for one would be 3907's error. It is now the charter's decisive check, and it is a good one — the picture either has a species-dependent coupling on file in the joint qDP/eDP theorem or it does not.

The satisfying part is that the 3524 dilemma dissolves: one sign serves both the down quark's extra and the dressing selectivity, and SM-2 §10's sentence is confirmed as a slip. The unsatisfying part is that I now have a mechanism that works for quarks and fails for leptons under its simplest form, and I have said so in the same patch.
