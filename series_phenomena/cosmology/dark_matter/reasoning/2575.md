# Reasoning — Patch 2575 (the founder was right, the statics said no, and both are true)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The tempting move after R2 fired was to keep exploring geometries until one produced a
well -- sheared stackings, lateral approaches, single-CP-onto-face, partial coats. I
had three more configurations sketched before the frozen reading stopped me: R2 says
route back with the curves, and the reason the fence is right is that geometry-shopping
for a minimum is exactly convention-shopping wearing a lab coat. If the core were
static, the REGISTERED stacking should carry it; it doesn't, in any union member.

What kept this from being a disappointing patch is that the failure has a name and the
name has a registered answer. Earnshaw is not an obstacle the programme discovered
today -- the corpus hit it at the flexure/coat work and answered it with the
ponderomotive lineage, four registered computations deep. The founder's cube sentence
reads as static geometry, but his own prior physics says the cube's stiffness is
DYNAMIC: the ZBW drive is what makes "repulsion right behind the well" true. So the
sharpened route is not a correction of his adjudication; it is his adjudication read
through his own corpus. I still put F-c-prime to him as a question rather than
executing, because "your static picture, dynamically carried" is an interpretation, and
interpretations of founder physics get confirmed, not assumed -- that line has held
since the reach-S arc and it holds here.

One consequence I made sure not to soften: the 2571 maps are species-superseded-
pending. It would have been comfortable to say the maps "still stand as bounds" -- but
their species is the same impoverished dipole, and a bound computed on the wrong
species bounds the wrong thing. Suspended-not-softened is the honest state: the DEAD
verdicts may well return under the corrected species, but they return by re-derivation,
not by inertia.

The impoverishment lesson generalizes and I want it findable: when a campaign
abstracts a species from the registered geometry, the abstraction is a physics choice
and belongs in the prereg's capability census explicitly -- "what does this
representation DISCARD" is now a question I will ask at every species definition.
