STATUS: verbatim, captured at Patch 2685, 21 July 2026.

The construction choice mattered more than the solving. The [ADJ] demanded
z=12-equivalence with a sanity gate BEFORE the operator lands, so I built
from the Barlow family: every close-packing of equal spheres is z=12 with
identical nearest-neighbour shells, and the family contains genuinely
distinct motifs — FCC (ABC), HCP (ABAB), and the uncountable random-
stacking sequences between them. That gives independence where it counts:
beyond the second shell the environments differ, so if the staggered
envelope were an FCC-proxy artifact, HCP or the seeded random stacking
would break it. They did not. Central values 0.0892, 0.0909, 0.0924 —
pairwise inside the frozen 2-sigma criterion, with the worst pair (HCP vs
random Barlow) at 0.0032 against a 0.0036 tolerance. Honest note: that
worst pair is at 90% of tolerance, not comfortably inside it; the panel
should see that number, not just "COMPAT."

Two things I flagged same-font rather than letting them flatter the
result. First, A3 (rotated FCC, cubic boundary) came out numerically
identical to A0 — of course it did: rotation preserves the distance
matrix and the boundary is fifteen screening lengths from the window. So
A3 demonstrates boundary-insensitivity (a real property) but is NOT a
fourth independent motif; I said so in the record so the realization
count reads honestly as three-plus-one. Second, R=7 vs R=9 identity to
four decimals is the screening doing its own convergence proof — the same
fact 2671 reported, now reproduced across motifs.

The J2 scan surprised me. I decoupled d_DP from the lattice edge expecting
either "l tracks d_DP" (supporting the level assignment as a scale choice)
or "l tracks a" (indicting it). Neither: the identity l = d_DP/4 exists AT
kappa*a = 2 and dissolves on both sides — at d_DP = phi*a the staggering
itself vanishes (neg-frac exactly zero; the response goes pure Yukawa).
So the committed assignment is not one point on a smooth family; it sits
at a structurally distinguished operating point. I resisted the pull to
call that support for INF-S1C-1 — the non-elevation clause exists for
precisely this temptation — and wrote it as what it is: the d_DP/4
pattern is a non-generic signature of kappa*a = 2, which the panel can
weigh in either direction.
