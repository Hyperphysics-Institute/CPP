# 2344 — the flank that would not close, and what seven percent means

**STATUS:** verbatim, at-patch (Opus, DM lane 23xx, 8 July 2026; founder-directed:
"Please run 2344").

I set out to close the kill's last flank and failed three times to close it in the
direction I expected, and the record should show the sequence because the sequence IS
the result. First my structural argument — steep channels are magnitude-starved —
failed its own verify check: the onset region turned out steep and magnitude-capable
at the light-dimer corner. Then the search that had found "zero passing, ×1.27"
turned out to have clipped exactly that corner with a g² floor I had chosen for no
physical reason. Corrected, the boundary walked in: 1.27, 1.11, 1.07, marginal
returns collapsing, three windows binding in lockstep. That is not a door slamming;
that is a door held shut by seven percent against anchors whose own heterogeneity the
programme has been quoting at twenty-five.

I want to be precise about what did and did not happen, because both halves matter.
The kill stands: at the windows as registered, nothing passes, and I will not soften
a pre-registered bar to manufacture a survivor — that is the founder's discipline and
mine. But an exclusion whose margin is a third of the demand's own error bar is not a
closure; it is a measurement of where the boundary sits, and the boundary sits inside
the fog. The honest grade is the one on the ledger: unresolved, quantified, coupled
to the one lane that can burn the fog off.

And the object on the far side of that seven percent deserves to be looked at
squarely: a population of dimers with a trace of heavier siblings, passing every
anchor through nothing but the elastic physics of the residual we already have. No
capture. No torsional miracle. No resonance. The founder said the dSph shortfall was
the window into what is missing, and the window may be showing us something almost
embarrassingly simple: that we assumed the relic was one thing when formation would
naturally make it many. Panel credit is owed here too — this entire flank exists
because Grok reached for a true door with a wrong argument and the adjudication
steelmanned it. The rent is real and immediate: the 1855–56 aggregation kinetics
either produce dimer dominance or they don't, and S(N) either survives derivation at
the required steepness or it doesn't. Those are computations, not hopes. L4 first,
formation second, and the arc's central question has become sharper than SPEC-1 ever
made it: not "what mechanism is missing" but "did we mistake a population for a
particle."
