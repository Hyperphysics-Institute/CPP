The first thing I checked was whether any clause of the pre-committed
handling fires in its severe branch, because those branches execute
without discretion and would reorder everything else: no seat returned
REJECT on Q1a (so no demotion), no seat found INSUFFICIENT on Q1b (so
Q2/Q3 are not frozen), and no seat alleged a blind-protocol breach (so
nothing voids). That leaves the benign path: tally, reconcile amendments
strictest-union, execute.

The Q1a split (3 ADOPT / 2 ADOPT-AMENDED) is not a disagreement about the
class — all five sustain FG-OTHER — it is a disagreement about how much
epistemic dressing the class carries. The pre-committed handling resolves
this without my judgment: strictest-union reconciliation means both
amendments attach. I note they are complementary rather than redundant:
GPT's A1 constrains what the RESULT may claim to be (a property of the
present operator construction, not the unique Sea scale), Copilot's A2
constrains what CONSUMERS must say when they cite it. A1 guards the
upstream claim, A2 the downstream propagation. Folding A2 into the
canonical rider text rather than leaving it free-floating is the only
editorial decision I made here, and it is forced by the 2664 precedent:
riders that are not given canonical text drift.

Q1b unanimous S-W-R-Q is the panel doing exactly what the packet's J1
invited. The interesting adjudication content is in the remedy spec,
where the seats did NOT converge on a single construction — GPT wants
independently chosen large realizations, DeepSeek wants the registered

Strictest-union applies to amendments; I read the remedy specs as

multi-motif arena" — an example, not a spec), so the union governs and
FA-SG-R1 carries all four legs plus the optional J6 branch. Grok's
grade cap (observation grade until the remedy executes) is the
strictest single constraint any seat placed on the numerical value and
therefore binds.

On Q2 I want the record to be honest about what "unanimous" is worth

were also handed a packet in which the readout already said neither

what discovers. So: replication route CLOSED, discovery route re-scoped
to the derived scale. The one thing I refused to do is schedule it —
FA-SG-R1 must run first, because a discovery route pointed at a number
that has not survived its own proxy-dependence remedy would be aimed at
a target that might move.

Q3 is where the strictest-union rule does real work. Four seats say
rider; Gemini says rider AND demotion of prior width-dependent results
to phenomenological-fit grade. Demotion is strictly stronger, so it
binds. But I bounded its blast radius deliberately, using the 2660

answered and the answer falls outside the instrument bracket {2, 4}.

on the WIDTH VALUE; existence and classification claims whose Morse-axis
dependence was on the form class survive in kind. Reading the demotion
wider than that would demote results the panel's own reasoning does not
implicate; reading it narrower would evade Gemini's clause. The seam
was already in the registry; I used it rather than inventing one.

queue: remedy before re-derivation campaign. The alternative (re-derive

did, under PD-006, and it is recorded here as my call.
