# REASONING FRAGMENT — PATCH 2945 (ceiling reissue)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch capture.**

Mechanically this patch changes nothing numerical, and that is its
point. The 2943 ceiling died by the letter of its own banner and is
reborn identical under the same frozen formula, one patch later, with
two more named conditions and a lemma behind it. The audit trail now
shows: a seat objected, the objection was adjudicated, the void
clause fired exactly as written, and the reissue used no discretion —
the 2942 freeze meant the resurrection was substitution, not
re-derivation. If the ceiling had instead been quietly kept alive by
reinterpreting "conditions," every future banner in this corpus would
be worth less. The one-patch gap where no d_DP constraint existed is
deliberate and recorded; cheapness of the void-and-reissue path is
what made honoring the letter affordable, which is itself an argument
for writing void clauses onto conversions rather than onto
derivations.
