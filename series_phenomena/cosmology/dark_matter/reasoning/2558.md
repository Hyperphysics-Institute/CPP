# Reasoning — Patch 2558 (FORM-L-2 prereg: the fence falls in daylight)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

Two decisions worth their ink. First, the fence-lift is its own numbered section, citing
the founder's go and the original fence's motivation, because a lifted fence is the most
dangerous kind of precedent — the record must show it fell by deliberate act with the
authorization named, before any number, so no future patch can cite this as "fences bend
when results are exciting." Second, consuming the frozen 20/24 cells instead of re-running
them: with a chaotic estimator, every re-roll is a lottery ticket, and re-rolling only the
cells adjacent to a hoped-for bracket is p-hacking wearing a reproducibility costume. New
information comes only from new grid points; old points keep their frozen values.

The 26-point matters as much as the 22-point: if the wall is real, 26 should be negative
too (confirming a wall, not a dip), and the prereg's non-monotonic-re-entry reading exists
precisely so that a +/-/+ pattern gets called under-resolved instead of being narrated
into "the wall with an island."
