# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2786 (X3/X4 joint prereg freeze)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-23.**

## Reading the target correctly before designing the instrument

The near-catch of this freeze was chronological, not mathematical: the
X6 record (2735) carries 5.8–6.6σ matched-functional discrepancies and
reads like the live target, but it predates the 2754 defect discovery —
the clean rv2714 re-run killed those numbers to 0.49σ (pole) and left
only the marginal F1 near-window residual (2.56σ / 2.82σ, ~10% below
DH). An X3/X4 prereg designed against X6's monster would have been an
instrument aimed at a corpse. The dispatch_2763 packet's phrase "not
the former monster" is the load-bearing orientation sentence for this
entire freeze, and the prereg's header quotes the clean numbers as
inputs-of-record precisely so no future session re-inherits the stale
σ's.

## Instantiating X3 at both tension points

The charter's X3 is "one longer chain at the tension point," written
when the tension point was singular (a_s = 0.04). The clean data moved
the ground: the residual now appears at BOTH a_s values, same sign,
comparable size. Replicating only 0.04 would leave the 0.02 residual —
which is actually the more significant of the two — resting on a
single chain forever. Two chains, 2× the RV lengths, fresh seeds from
the continuing reserved block. The replication classes are frozen with
a pooling rule (inverse-variance within a_s, Stouffer across) because
the alternative — eyeballing four numbers at adjudication time — is
exactly the post-hoc judgment the arc keeps paying for.

## X4: incorporate the archive, spend compute where PR2 demands it

PR2's bar is verbatim-frozen and expensive: four rungs, ≥2 sizes and
≥2 chains at every a_s ≤ 0.01. The economical and honest move is to
let the archived clean chains serve the rungs they already satisfy
(0.04 fully; 0.02 partially) and spend new compute only where PR2's
letter requires it — eight new chains at 0.01/0.005, two completions
at 0.02. The reserve-chain mechanism (activated by a mechanical ESS
threshold, not by taste) exists because the soft-core hardens as
a_s → 0 and sampling quality at 0.005 is genuinely unknown; without a
preregistered reserve rule, a poorly-mixed chain at execution time
forces a choice between accepting bad statistics and improvising new
runs — both defects.

## The seven requirements as operationalizations, not gestures

Each binding requirement is frozen as a mechanical procedure with
named constants (24 blocks × 2000 resamples; the window grid with the
r_min ≥ 2a_s exclusion; ΔAIC ≥ 10; the |ρ| > 0.9 monotone criterion;
the (a_s, 1/L) surface form with p ∈ {1,2} both reported) because the
adjudication history shows requirements stated as intentions get
re-litigated at returns. Requirement 7 is deliberately sequenced
FIRST: it runs on archived accumulations at zero simulation cost and
its output (the k-range of any persistent S_zz deviation) shapes
nothing in the frozen design but informs the panel's reading of
everything downstream.

## The 2785 lesson, mechanized

Yesterday's D1 defect was an asserted-but-unchecked freeze constraint.
This prereg's freeze declaration therefore ships with a machine check
(`code/2786_freeze_check.py`) that verifies every internal-consistency
claim the prereg makes — window-cell validity per rung, the PR2
size/chain minima, seed uniqueness and non-collision — before the
freeze was committed. The check is trivial; the discipline of running
it is the point. ALL CHECKS PASS is now a quoted line, not a hope.

## Governance decision under PD-006 (economy governance, founder's ruling this session)

The founder's direction — find what works, send the panel results to
approve — is enacted as a batching decision: the L2-RB record's four
questions gate nothing (the fences guarantee no class consequence),
so rather than one CONV-001 paste per artifact, ONE consolidated
packet will carry the L2-RB record + this prereg notice + the
requirement-7 archived-data results once that cheap leg executes.
Fewer pastes for the founder, richer context per seat, nothing
hidden — the same-font records are all committed regardless.
