The recorded private expectation went one-for-two, and the miss is the interesting
half. The floor did drop below 0.5 exactly as sub-quadratic KE predicts — but by two
percent at the binding cap, not the ten I half-imagined from the 1 − 2/π asymptote.
The asymptote is real and the closed form is pretty, but the pair's binding cap
(γ ≈ 1.47) sits so far from the ultra-relativistic regime that the bound barely
bends. The admitted point survives with the floor beneath it everywhere, which is
what the gates deserved after saying 0.5 twice.

The interior half taught the better lesson. I expected relativistic period-lengthening
to walk the lag curve; instead the three-column isolation shows the walk is almost
entirely Newtonian — Morse-outward anharmonicity does at γ_char = 1.2 what I had
mentally attributed to γ, and the γ column adds at most +0.016 on top. The tension
between the relaxation-face route and the gate-admissible set is therefore an
AMPLITUDE problem the Newtonian derivation always had and never evaluated (the 2596
points were small-oscillation); the γ-campaign's real contribution is localizing the
blame. A re-derivation that had only confirmed would have hidden this.

The saturation window is where discipline earned its keep twice in one hour. The
first run printed complex numbers — the lag formula cheerfully exponentiating a
negative base — and the temptation was to cap f at 1 and move on. Capping is a
formula change; a repaired candidate is a new candidate; and the 2600 adoption says
the evaluator consults its guard before speaking. So the guard went in, the rows say
SAT-DOM, and the route's valid domain is now a named, disclosed boundary instead of
a silent patch. The near-dissociation tail (η ≈ 0.04 for oscillations slower than
the Sea clock) is physically sensible and slightly haunting: the sink is
clock-quantized, and motion slower than the clock is nearly invisible to it.

point at the grid top, and the point-candidate routing clause was RIGHT THERE — but

fire. Writing the readings before the numbers is what makes that a fact instead of
a negotiation. The rider discharges either way, which is the quiet win: a disclosed
assumption carried for six patches is now a derived percent-level statement.
