# Reasoning — Patch 2593 (opening the fence's one door, and writing its hinges first)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The eleven-word fence has governed every dissipation temptation since the panel coined
it, and N2-A is the first time the programme deliberately walks toward the thing it
forbids. The charter therefore spends its care on the hinges: the door opens only to a
DERIVED law (form and coefficient from the ramp-hold-release lineage, zero freedom),
and any candidate walks a validation battery written before any candidate exists. The
battery's members were chosen so that each one guards against a distinct way a sink
can lie: eating true binding (strong-pair), being a disguised thermostat that fakes
convergence (the x2.0 cell must converge FOR THE RIGHT REASON, ledger-tracked),
breaking frame cleanliness (a velocity-dependent term is the classic Galilean sin --
naming it now costs nothing and forgetting it later costs everything), and deleting
energy the founder's own picture treats as an observable (the Sea-deposit ledger).

The Sea-deposit ledger is the addition I expect to matter most downstream. In the
founder's census the tertiary and quaternary layers feed the Sea SSV every cycle --
the absorbed energy is not exhaust, it is the Sea's input. A sink that merely removes
energy would be numerically fine and physically deaf; tracking the deposit keeps N2's
bookkeeping ready for the day a Sea-side campaign asks where its energy came from.

N2-C is written provisional on purpose, with its escape hatch visible: the registered
seeded direction may make de-novo nucleation unnecessary, and chartering a hard
question as OPTIONAL-IF-CONSUMED is more honest than either promising it or omitting
it.
