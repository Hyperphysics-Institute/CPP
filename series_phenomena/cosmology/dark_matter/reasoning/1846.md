## The calculation

The dwarf σ/m normalization (1845) needs a Cross-Rod formation population peaked at **N_form ~ 400–1000 elements**.
Cross-Rod aggregation is reversible linear (equilibrium) polymerization — elements attach/detach at the rod ends,
detachment gated by the E_qq element–element bond. Two standard results:

1. **Equilibrium size:** ⟨N⟩ = √φ · exp(E_bond/2kT_form), where φ is the monomer activity and E_bond the
   per-element detachment energy.
2. **Freeze-out:** the distribution freezes when the detach rate ν_PCD·exp(−E_bond/kT_form) drops below the Hubble
   rate H_form. Setting them equal gives E_bond/kT_form = ln(ν_PCD/H_form), so **⟨N⟩ = √(φ · ν_PCD/H_form)** — the
   formation size is the square root of the ratio of the PCD attempt frequency to the cosmic expansion rate at
   freeze-out.

**So the entire formation-size question collapses to one substrate quantity: kT_form (equivalently ν_PCD/H_form).**

## What it takes to hit the target

- N_form ~ 400–1000 requires **E_bond/kT_form ~ 12–14** (φ ~ 1; ~16–18 if φ ~ 10⁻²).
- For the element–element **E_qq** detachment bond (E_bond ~ 66–264 MeV, i.e. 1–4 core bonds), this is
  **kT_form ~ 4.7–19 MeV**.
- **Exponential sensitivity (the honest concern):** at E_bond = E_qq = 66 MeV, ⟨N⟩ runs 6×10⁴ (kT=3 MeV) → 740
  (5 MeV) → 27 (10 MeV) — a factor ~3 in kT_form spans ~4 orders in ⟨N⟩. The dwarf normalization therefore needs
  kT_form pinned to **~10–15%** of ~4.7 MeV.
