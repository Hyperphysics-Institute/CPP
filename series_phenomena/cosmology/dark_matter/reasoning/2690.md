Assigning the class was deliberately anticlimactic — the §5 machinery is
mechanical and I ran it as written: PASS blocked twice over (L2, J3),
FRAGILE and NEG contradicted by L1/L3, SHIFT's own definition names
J3-REVISE explicitly. The work of this patch was making the SHIFT
honest, because the frozen SHIFT text imagines "a revised value with its
band," and the arc did not produce a clean revised value: the site-level
route lands at 0.168 fm with the envelope quality visibly degraded and
the operator at 59% of its own stability threshold. Writing "l' = 0.168"
without that qualification would launder a degraded readout into a
number. So the readout states the revision as what it is — the value is
normalization-conditional — and hands the packet the genuine fork: which
matching does the S1b/S1c chain ground?

I want the strongest and weakest facts adjacent for the panel. Strongest:
four arenas, seventy-two extraction variants, one structure, one band —
and at assembly level, a theorem (the hop branch CANNOT stagger) that
tells us what the staggering is made of. Weakest: the committed L2 test
failed, and part of that failure is my own operationalization's defect,
which I disclosed rather than re-derived; and the entire numerical edifice
sits on a normalization choice the panel flagged sight-unseen as the
highest-risk joint — they were right, and the packet says so in those
words.

distinguished point, therefore the derivation is right" assembled itself
unbidden. That is the coincidence-as-support move the non-elevation
clause forbids, and the operating-point findings went into the packet as
OBS-class with the elevation burden assigned to the panel, not claimed.
The cap stays; the riders travelled untouched; FA-C3 stays closed unless
