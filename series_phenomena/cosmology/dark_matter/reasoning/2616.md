# Reasoning — Patch 2616 (the chain finishes what the aim started, and the fence
# holds against a very handsome temptation)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

Thirty seconds of compute closed a question that spent two days wearing the wrong
costume. The soft chain never had a growth problem; it had a targeting problem, and
the moment the incident was pointed at the cluster instead of at the cluster's
former address, stages 7 and 8 went through like the first six — bigger deposits,
higher transit gammas, same ending: bound, compact, books closed. The finished soft
core settling INSIDE the steep benchmarks is the detail I did not expect; the widths
differ by 3.5x in stiffness and the assembled objects are nearly the same size. The
core's geometry is set by the capture physics, not the spring constant — which is
the kind of width-independence the sink derivation showed once already, at the clock.

Width-universality is the sentence that matters for the arc. The steep width was
2433-motivated; a completion that existed only there would always carry a "did the
motivation pick the answer" asterisk. Both widths completing — one of them after its
stall was exposed as an artifact — removes the asterisk in the only way that counts:
by execution.

The fence did its work at the exact moment fences exist for. An 8-object with a
fresh face, a launcher one line away, and "stage 9" would have taken forty seconds
— and would have converted the charter's registered target into a treadmill. The
prereg spent that temptation before the run, which is the only time it is cheap.
