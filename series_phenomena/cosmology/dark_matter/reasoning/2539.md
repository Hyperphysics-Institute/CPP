high" as convergent; the founder's Q4 disclosure ("only repeating what we discovered earlier") shows
that component was echo. Two options: quietly narrow the claim in the successor documents, or
register the correction explicitly at the capture. Anti-erasure discipline picks the second — the

content and now the e–q–q–e assembly ordering, whose mechanism (edge reactivity + qDP-surface

The second-generation claims are the ones I deliberately did not adjudicate. The muon case looks like
a convergence hiding in plain sight — the glossary has carried "tetrahedral cage hosting an electron,

realization of the second shell or a competitor to it. Registering candidate-convergence and
candidate-tension separately, both open, keeps the SF-3/SM-9 lane's registered results untouched
until a reconciliation session walks them. The neutrino-oscillation mechanism is the most striking
single claim in the message (a Sea-collision-driven oscillation would carry a natural matter-effect
analog, which is testable-shaped), and the most important thing to do with it today is nothing — it
is outside the campaign, and grabbing it now would be exactly the scope creep the review-economy
ruling exists to prevent.

strategy wants: if the window mechanism is right, a subdominant tail at other N is natural, and its
absence would constrain the kinetics.
