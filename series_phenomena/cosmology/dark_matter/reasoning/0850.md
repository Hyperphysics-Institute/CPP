Charter item: confront one or two specific observed dwarfs quantitatively rather than the representative NFW. I picked two systems ~5x apart in velocity on purpose, because the candidate's distinctive claim is that σ_V/m ≈ 0.20 is velocity-INDEPENDENT — so the sharp test is whether the SAME σ/m fits both a low-σ classical dSph and a higher-v rotation-supported dwarf. Fornax (classical dSph, σ_1D ≈ 11 km/s) and IC 2574 (large-core dwarf/LSB, v_max ≈ 80 km/s).

Data are real, web-verified, and tagged [obs] with sources in the script, not recalled: Fornax central DM density 0.016–0.07 M⊙/pc³ (Mateo+ 1991 isotropic 0.07±0.03; Jardel & Gebhardt 2012 cored 0.016) and core ≲0.3–0.7 kpc (Goerdt+ 2006); IC 2574 v_max ≈ 80 km/s and pseudo-isothermal core ρ_0 ≈ 0.006 M⊙/pc³, R_C ≈ 8 kpc (de Blok+ 2008 / Martimbeau+ 1994). I did not invent any value.

The honesty correction that shaped the result. My first-pass verdict called Fornax "consistent" and IC 2574 "tension" — but the script's own numbers show pred/obs = 2.8x and 3.1x, essentially the SAME factor. Calling the same ratio two different things is exactly the kind of motivated reading to avoid. So I rewrote the verdict to what the numbers actually say: ρ₁(0.20) sits ~3x above the observed central density in BOTH, and the offset is FLAT across the 5x velocity span. That flatness is the genuinely positive, discriminating result — a velocity-dependent σ/m (rising toward dwarfs) would make the Fornax offset far exceed IC 2574's, which is not seen — and it is the paper's actual claim (velocity-independence vs light-mediator SIDM's v⁻⁴). The common ~3x is within the stated factor-few (f x halo-model) band; if real it nudges the normalization slightly below 0.20. I let the data revise the story rather than dressing a fixed conclusion.

Scope honesty. This is the DENSITY test, which ρ₁ delivers directly and cleanly. I deliberately did NOT manufacture a core-RADIUS confrontation here, because predicting r_core requires an NFW r₁ inversion (a concentration–mass relation and its assumptions) — that belongs to the dedicated core-radius-vs-σ/m panel, the next charter item. IC 2574's large ~8 kpc core is flagged as the case that test will have to face, rather than glossed. The velocity convention (⟨v_rel⟩ = 2.26 σ_1D, σ_1D = v_max/√2 for the rotation-supported system) is stated and tagged [conv] with its factor ~2 sensitivity, applied identically to both galaxies so the comparison is fair.
