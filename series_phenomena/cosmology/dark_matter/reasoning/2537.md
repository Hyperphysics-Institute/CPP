null-baseline trap); here, the audit already shows the honest expectation is *partial* — theorem-grade
leading order, three Branch-I-shaped magnitudes. So the prereg's job is to make partial outcomes
bankable without letting "near-threshold" leak into a composition claim. That is the §0 trap

The prereg therefore pre-commits the shape of the likely terminus (Branch I at R-C with NB-S3a-1 named)
so that arriving there is a clean record, not a disappointment to be argued around.

differ by 2–5 orders; whichever the compute patch picks determines whether the second-order terms are
MeV-scale or keV-scale objects. Fixing the adjudication *criteria* now (structural identity +
registration depth, never output) while leaving the *verdict* to the compute patch keeps the decision
auditable — the same move 2529 made with route ordering. My own read (recorded, not binding): the
tetra's e–e channel dissociates into a free eDP, which makes the DP-internal scale the structurally
natural one; but the aggregate-edge band exists precisely because screened residuals differ from
internal bonds, so the question is genuinely open until the compute patch walks the registrations.
