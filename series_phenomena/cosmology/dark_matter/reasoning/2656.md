# Reasoning — Patch 2656 (the model that converged everywhere, and why that is the most informative thing it could have done)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The frozen reading fell against my own mechanism section, and registering it
that way — FAIL, FAIL, Tier 2 UNMET — is the patch's entire integrity. The
temptation after seeing the flat face was to call the single-pair model
"insufficiently faithful" and quietly upgrade it until the numbers moved;
that is the exact iterate-until-match hazard the panel's Tier-2 amendment
named, and the only clean exit is the one taken: register the negative at
the frozen reading, then diagnose in the open.

The diagnosis is worth more than a pass would have been. A 1-dof system
converging at every width is a clean experimental statement about WHERE the
boundary's carrier lives: not in the sink map, not in the integrator, not in
the single Morse well — all three are present in the model and produce
nothing — but in what the reduction removed: the target's coupled internal
modes. And the corpus already contains the fingerprint of what coupled stiff
anharmonic modes do to convergence: Branch U's chaotic floor. I had walked
into this session carrying one mechanism (phase-sample decoherence) and the
model forced the honest admission that a second, older candidate fits at
least as well.

Splitting FB-MECH into A and B and making H2 their discriminator converts a
failed tier into a designed experiment. Under A the steep width must
re-converge as dt falls; under B it must not. I genuinely do not know which
way it goes — the 2626 record's "the discretized sink law itself, meeting a
stiff well at finite gamma" reads like A, while the mode-resolved SIG-NEG
coherence of 2635 reads like B — and writing both predictions before the run
is the only position from which either outcome teaches something.

The RM-A1 amendment deserves its sentence of self-scrutiny: it was made
before any run, sourced to the 2628 convention, and it is exactly the kind
of amendment that would look like tuning if the sequencing were not
documented. It is documented. The G-C guard's +9..17% electric-curvature
shift on the frequencies is reported for the same reason — a model whose
frequencies sit a tenth high is a model whose location-class readings must
carry that band, and hiding it would have been cheap tonight and expensive
at the next audit.
