*Context:* programme-direction contribution — the founding stance of OPEN-DM-DSPH-1.
The inverse method (determine the required theory, then derive it), the
anomaly-as-window reading of the dSph shortfall, and the enrollment of the adversarial
panel as a scout team are all his; the arc charter is the registration of this text
into programme machinery. His subsequent "Please proceed as recommended" gated the
registration, the two-part brief, and the 2340 first patch.

Two design decisions in the charter need their reasoning preserved.

First, Clause 1 is PROPOSED, not registered. The founder's anchor statement is the
strongest commitment language the programme has carried, and the discipline I attached
to it — rent and exit — only means anything if the exit condition is his, in his words,
ratified with the same deliberateness as the anchor itself. I drafted a candidate so
the arc is not stalled on a blank, but I built the machinery so the arc's first verdict
cannot move until the clause carries founder-attested text. Writing his falsification
condition for him would have been efficient and wrong: the entire epistemic force of a
pre-registered exit comes from who registered it.

Second, the charter contains a correction against my own patch from hours ago, and I
put it in the charter rather than a quiet errata because it changes a lane's shape.
2338's check 5 argued no narrow feature could hide between scan points because s-wave
threshold structure is generically broad. True, and insufficient: the argument does not
cover barrier-protected p-wave resonances, whose widths are penetration-suppressed by
(kR_s)³ ~ 10⁻² and which my σ-scanning could straddle. Finding that hole while writing
the specification's own exclusion clause is the inverse method already working — the
act of stating precisely what was excluded revealed what was not. L3-a exists because
the specification was honest.

What I feel about this arc, for the record: the six-flank kill was the most rigorous
thing this campaign has produced, and the founder's response to it was not to dispute a
single grade but to change what question the result answers. That is the correct
relationship between falsification machinery and research vision — the machinery says
what is true within the registered structure; the vision decides which registered
structure to build next. My job across the last two days was to keep those from
contaminating each other, and the charter is the wall between them: the kill stands in
one section, the hunt is chartered in the next, and the rent-and-exit rules are the
gate through which the hunt must eventually pass back into the machinery. Neptune or
Vulcan, we will know which this was, and we will know it by the arc's own pre-stated
lights.
