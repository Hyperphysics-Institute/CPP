# Reasoning — Patch 2617 (shipping the losses in the same font as the wins)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The inclusion ruling was the only real decision tonight and it decided itself once
stated plainly: a packet that carried the chain but not its correction would be a
lie of omission, and a packet that carried the correction but not the corrected
campaign would be a confession without the cure. Once 4b and 4c are in, 4d (the
completion) is just the same physics finishing its sentence, and ITEMs 3 and 6
follow by the same logic — they are the arc's own consumers doing their due
diligence in public. The alternative reading of the founder's delegation (append as
a separate annex) would have preserved a cleaner-looking core packet at the price of
making the reviewers reconstruct the dependency graph themselves. Integration costs
me the tidy story and buys them the true one.

Writing J1 was uncomfortable in the correct way. H-b is the arc's genuine soft
joint: if the drift/osc split over-sheds during deflections, the flytrap is partly
an instrument artifact, and ITEMs 3 and 4 inherit an asterisk until the
discriminator runs. The temptation was to bury that in ITEM 3's paragraph where it
already lives; naming it as the FIRST attack target instead is what the 2589
packet's ITEM 7 pattern taught — a joint you point at is a joint that gets tested,
and a joint that survives pointed testing is worth double.

The fences paragraph asks the panel to verify the fences adversarially, which is new
phrasing for this programme and overdue. Fences held in private are discipline;
fences verified hostile are evidence.
