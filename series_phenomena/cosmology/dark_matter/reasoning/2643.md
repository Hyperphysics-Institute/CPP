# Reasoning — Patch 2643 (a cell where both answers are wins for the exhibit, and the discipline of saying so beforehand)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

This is the rare cell where neither outcome is adverse and the prereg's main
job is to say so BEFORE the run, so that whichever way it lands, no one can
suspect the reading was chosen to flatter the result. Width-shared walls say
the stage-7 marginal-aim boundary belongs to the grown target's geometry —
the strongest form of the anisotropy exhibit. Width-dependent walls say
stiffness enters the boundary — a different, also-interesting fact that widens
the soft envelope. The only outcome that registers as a limitation is
RS-UNDRAWN, and even that routes cleanly to derivation via the ladder imported
verbatim from ROB-2.

The control design has one deliberate softening, stated openly: C-S1 gates on
completion + dt-stability, not on exact settle reproduction. The matched
launcher is a different instrument from the economy one; at the steep width the
two happened to agree to the decimal on the registered channel (2638 C-M1),
but demanding that coincidence at the soft width would confuse a happy accident
with a requirement. The settle numbers print beside the RS1 values so any
wholesale disagreement is visible, and C-S2 carries the real conservatism
burden: if the matched launcher flips a DRAWN soft survivor, everything stops.

The conditional φ=37.5 refinement earns its place because the steep bracket is
the most falsifiable thing ROB-2 produced: (37.5°, 45°] is a 7.5-degree claim.
If the soft width halts at 45 AND completes at 37.5, the bracket itself is
width-shared — a much sharper sentence than two isolated wall confirmations.
If the soft width halts at 45 but the refinement was never licensed to run
(Cell A completes), the bracket question dissolves on its own.
