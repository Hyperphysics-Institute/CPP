I came into S3 half-expecting to write "the staggering is an artifact"
in provisional ink, and the derivation would not let me. The same
moderate-coupling number that validates the local kernel puts the
operating point past the charge-layering crossover of continuous
charged fluids — kappa d = 2 against a crossover near 1 — which means
the CONTINUOUS medium, treated honestly at O(Gamma), is itself a
candidate oscillator. The two prior intuitions (mine at 2697 SS4:
artifact; the lattice result: physical) turn out to be the two sides of
one undecided computation, and the computation now has a name, frozen
inputs, and a place in the queue (S4, after the panel and after theta).

I checked the temptation ledger twice here. First: the period
comparison (layering ~ d_DP vs lattice staggering ~ 2 nn) is
suggestive-adjacent, and I kept it as a ballpark statement, not a lock.
Second: FG-UNDECIDED is not a dodge — the guard would have forced
provisionality anyway, but the honest content is that the derivation
genuinely does not yet decide, and writing UNDECIDED-with-a-named-
instrument is more useful to the panel than a provisional lean they
would have to discount. The packet asks them to ratify the instrument,
not to guess the answer.
