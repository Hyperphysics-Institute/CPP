# Reasoning — Patch 0725 (DM Arc Step 4: power spectrum)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (Opus, 1 June 2026). Single-window; synced to origin 84db3e5. This is the patch where I most needed to resist the pull toward a manufactured "pass." Falsification-first means reporting the kill/tension when the physics shows one.

I split the problem deliberately. Q1 (growth): given near-scale-invariant adiabatic seeds, does CPP reproduce P(k)? Yes, trivially — the transfer function (BBKS) + growth is standard and CPP inherits it via the Step-D background. CHECK 1 confirms turnover at k~0.02 h/Mpc, low-k slope ~n_s, high-k ~n_s-4. This part is NOT the test; it would be dishonest to present "CPP reproduces P(k)!" on the strength of Q1 alone.

Q2 (seed origin) is the test, and it is where CONJ-COSMO-1 is weakest. The swirl mechanism ("radial-expansion collisions") is a CAUSAL / active-source mechanism. The literature on active sources (cosmic strings, textures) is decisive: causal seeds cannot produce (a) correlated adiabatic power on super-horizon scales at recombination, nor (b) the coherent harmonic acoustic-peak series. I quantified (a): horizon at recombination subtends l~157, yet the observed Sachs-Wolfe plateau (l~2-50) and especially the TE anti-correlation at l~100-150 are super-horizon — the textbook smoking gun (Spergel-Zaldarriaga) for perturbations laid down acausally. Swirls as causal seeds fail this.

The fair counterweight, which I went looking for rather than assuming absent: CPP's atemporal Nexus enforces non-local coordination INDEPENDENT of light-cones. That is a genuine disanalogy from standard active sources — it could, in principle, seed acausal/super-horizon correlations and resolve the horizon problem without inflation. So this is a TENSION, not a clean kill. But I refused to upgrade it past "live escape" because: it is undeveloped (no derived Nexus-seed spectrum), CPP's own review flags the Nexus as "lacking physical grounding," and — the real bar — even granting acausality, producing a near-scale-invariant (n_s~0.96) adiabatic spectrum with A_s~2e-9 is a strong, non-generic requirement that nothing in CPP currently meets.

Verdict I stand behind: Step 4 is NOT passed; CPP does NOT reproduce the power spectrum; the structure-formation mechanism is CONJ-COSMO-1's weakest link and its dominant open problem. This is the genuine frontier — the origin of the primordial spectrum, the problem inflation exists to solve. Registered OPEN-COSMO-DM-2 (Nexus-seed / scale-invariance), likely shared with OPEN-SR-6 (Big Bang) and the horizon problem.

Why this matters for the relationship: Thomas asked me to go for the most discriminating step. The right service here is the honest negative, clearly stated, not a face-saving "conditional partial." The cheap kills survived and the rotation curve is consistent — real progress — but the conjecture is not confirmed, and the one test that could most distinguish it from generic DM is currently failed. Saying so plainly is the point.
