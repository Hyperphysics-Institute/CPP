# Reasoning — Patch 0835 (f as a number; coupling vertex resolved)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (Opus, Session 156, 10 June 2026). Derivation of f to factor-~3; coupling resolved. No verdict, no THEO/ID.

Thomas asked for f as a number, which 0834 had reduced to one question: which coupling (3*alpha DP-binding or alpha_s confinement) mediates the neutral qDP-qDP residual. The resolution I landed on is a physical observation rather than a new postulate, and I want to be clear it is an observation and not a fit: a color-SINGLET qDP has no net color charge (SS-1: no long-range color field) but it DOES have a nonzero color polarizability -- the two qCPs are pulled oppositely by a color-field gradient. So there are two van der Waals channels, electric (3*alpha) and color (alpha_s), and the color one wins by (alpha_s/3alpha)^2 ~ 500x. That settles the vertex in favor of alpha_s, and -- the part I like -- the dipole suppression that I had flagged in 0834 as a worry is carried automatically inside the polarizability (which is set by the Compton/oscillator scale, not the tiny internal r_min=0.016 fm that had given the spurious f~1e-5 permanent-dipole answer). So I did not have to separately model the suppression; the oscillator polarizability is the suppression.

The number: f ~ 0.2 central (alpha_s~0.5, contact a~1.15 fm), factor-~3 range 0.07-0.6. The honest shift from 0834 is that the residual uncertainty is now PHYSICAL (alpha_s at a nonperturbative scale; the contact distance entering as 1/a^6) rather than the structural 10^3 coupling ambiguity. I was careful not to present f~0.2 as more precise than it is: a~1 fm is comparable to the qDP size, so the multipole/van der Waals expansion is at its edge, and alpha_s below Lambda_QCD is nonperturbative. Factor-~3 is the honest precision. It lands in the original "realistic ~0.1" estimate, which is reassuring but I did not tune to make it do so -- the central value fell out of alpha_s~0.5 and a~1.15.

Two things I made sure to deliver because Thomas explicitly wanted them: (1) f as a NUMBER (0.2, factor-3) not just a bound; (2) the residual Yukawa depth now QUOTED -- V0 = f*E_qDP ~ 53 MeV, range 1.3 fm, behind a ~1 fm hard core -- so the 0831/0832 potential is a specific well, no longer a scan over f.

The caveat I refused to drop: the color vdW is the computable dominant channel, but if the hDP is a pion-like Goldstone, coherent one-hDP exchange could add to it (as OPE dominates two-gluon in nuclei), raising f toward the f<1 ceiling. I flagged this as a possible enhancement of a known mechanism, not a new parameter, and noted the arc absorbs it (collisionless and diffuse for all f<1). I considered whether to chase the Goldstone question now but it is a separate SS-sector determination (is the hDP a Goldstone?), out of scope for f-as-a-number and not needed for any Era-2 verdict.

Arc consistency checked at the derived f~0.2: sigma/m=0.12 (0831), de Boer Lambda=1.7 (0833) -- diffuse and collisionless confirmed, and I noted Lambda=0.747/sqrt(f)>0.75 for all f<1 so the diffuse conclusion is f-independent within the physical range. So the derived value confirms the arc rather than straining it.
