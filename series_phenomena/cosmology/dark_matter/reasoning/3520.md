# Reasoning — Patch 3520 (verbatim, at-patch)

The founder's ruling at 3519 made the residue thermal, and I had told him the band looked natural in that picture on the strength of the 3517 toy. The proper computation says otherwise, and the difference is instructive: the toy cooled and diluted on a single time τ and started in equilibrium at kT = E_b; the real system has H ∝ T² and the Saha population tracking through many e-folds, and once pairing is even a hundred times faster than expansion the residue is set by the rate and H — it forgets the local qCP density, exactly as the residual ionisation after recombination forgets the baryon density. So p sits at ½ only when nothing pairs, is in the band for one decade of pairing strength, and is ~0.02 at the colour strength the DM lane itself uses for qDPs. I had raised his expectations in the 3519 summary; this note corrects that plainly.

I checked whether I was reading the model rather than the physics: g* barely moves p; the Saha prefactor enters logarithmically; the implicit integrator reproduces the ½ limit and the standard freeze-out scaling; the freeze temperatures come out where they should relative to 2543 in the band and below it at colour strength. The reading is robust to everything I can vary that is on file. What I cannot vary from the record is the one thing it hinges on — the pairing rate of a lone qCP in the sea — and the founder's cocoon is the only mechanism that could move it by the seventeen orders required.

I applied the charter's D1 rule as written: p outside the band on the registered inputs kills C-5 by name and number. I did not carry it as a conditional, because carrying a candidate on an unregistered eighteen-order suppression aimed at a one-decade target is the pattern 3907 warned about. I registered the reopening route instead, with the only legitimate form it could take — a derived cocoon suppression, not a chosen one.
