# Reasoning — Patch 2609 (the model I built lost to the substrate I registered, and
# the loss is the finding)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

I wrote the transit-shed model expecting it to be roughly right and the grid to
decorate it with brackets. Instead the substrate captured everything, including a
0.95c head-on carrying more kinetic energy than any off-axis well is deep, and the
honest sentence is that my model asked the wrong question. The single-pass criterion
treats capture as a race the incident can win by being fast; the map says capture is
a ratchet — shed ANY margin on the first pass and the flytrap owns you, and the shed
GROWS with violence because the sink couples to acceleration bursts, not to dwell
time. Both of tonight's hypotheses were nameable in advance and I did not name them
at prereg; the model's failure mode is therefore mine, recorded as such.

The deposits growing monotonically with v is the number I keep returning to. A
dwell-time picture says fast transits shed less; the instrument says fast transits
shed MORE, and the mechanism (the drift/osc split reading deflection curvature as
oscillation) is either the 2601 frame-subtlety inflated to a defect, or it is
radiation-reaction physics arriving exactly where a capture-and-delay substrate
should produce it — bremsstrahlung with the Sea as the field. I genuinely do not
know which tonight, and the declared sink-ON/sink-OFF single-pass measurement is
the experiment that distinguishes them. Until it runs, neither reading is promoted;
the record holds both by name.

The dt-gap confession cost a corollary I wanted. Forty-eight captures through 0.95c
begs to extend B4's geometric band, and the prereg's bracket-conditional dt rule —
my drafting — left every cell unconfirmed at the finer step. Confirm-stingy exists
for exactly this moment: the extension waits for the follow-on's dt-union, and the
next prereg carries the rule unconditionally because this one's gap is now in the
record with my name on it.

FRG never firing is the quiet result I suspect the founder will care about most.
The 4-square ate forty-eight relativistic passages without fragmenting. Whatever
else the core-assembly story needs, fragility under bombardment is not on the list
at this layer — and that robustness is banked as reported physics for N3 without
tonight's patch claiming one millimeter more than the cells showed.
