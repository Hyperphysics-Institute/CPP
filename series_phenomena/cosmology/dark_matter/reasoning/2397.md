## 2. Contract-drafting decisions, owned

- **Triple duty decomposed into an ordered cheap-kill ladder (Q4a → Q4b → Q4c)
  rather than a single monolithic derivation.** Rationale: the three deliverables
  have strictly increasing cost and strictly decreasing kill-cheapness. Q4a's √χ
  kill (pre-registered since §34.4) moots everything for the price of a
  response-order argument. Q4b's Class-S kill lands the family fully dead with the
  §34.9/§34.11 over-determination already in the record — no residual work needed.
  Q4c is the expensive fork-resolver and is only reached if both cheaper kills
  miss. This is the same discipline that made Q1–Q3 clean (Q3 preceding Q4 for
  exactly this reason), applied inside Q4.
- **The Q4b discriminant named in the contract.** The 2393 V2 adjacency record
  (V-t ⇒ end-sourced O(N⁰) rod sourcing vs bulk ∝ N) is the one registered
  substrate-level discriminant between the classes; the contract binds the
  derivation to reproduce the rod's registered D5-A′ phenomenology under whichever
  class it lands, so the class cannot be chosen by preference and dressed as
  derivation.
- **The Q4c grading window imported as a CLOSED input, not re-derived.** The
  2395 residual-demand table (envelope/1.8e7–4.6e7 LZ in-coverage;
  envelope/5.3e2–1.1e3 DAMIC) is the quantified survival window; the contract
  cites it and pre-arms the 2395 generalized ladder (V2 pass-gate re-armed) as the
  mechanical re-confrontation instrument. No new confrontation contract is needed
  on a derived residual — deliberate, to keep the fork-resolution path short.
- **Branch (b) carries the theorem-registry unblock explicitly.** The Q2 exact
  identities were deliberately NOT registered at 2393/2394 (class-conditional on a
  held branch). A Q4b V-t landing discharges that condition; writing the unblock
  into the contract's own grading prevents the registration from being forgotten
  or, worse, done early.
- **The LZ 9-GeV edge pin stays founder-gated by amendment.** 2395 §6 recorded
  the deliberate non-pin; the contract makes any future pin a named CONV-004
  amendment action rather than something a derivation session does in passing.
- **Per-session battery structure for a weeks-scale arc.** Q1–Q3 batteries were
  per-contract because each was 1–2 sessions. Q4 is multi-session, so V1
  (scratch-copy chain green) and V5 (cache discipline, redirected output) bind
  per-session, and V2 becomes a per-stage known-limit pass-gate with the specific
  limits named per stage (1872 anchor for Q4a; D5-A′ rod phenomenology for Q4b;
  2393 zeros/envelopes + 2395 rod grid for Q4c). This keeps the pass-gate
  discipline that caught nothing-wrong-but-would-have at Q3 (V2 there passed
  first, and the grading is trusted because of it).
