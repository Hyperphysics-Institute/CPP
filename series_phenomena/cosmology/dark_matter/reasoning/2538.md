# Reasoning — Patch 2538 (§6g capture: the model shift)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The first thing I did on reading the founder's message was count CPs, because the claim "the eDP-qDP
is the elemental plane of DM and will stack 16 high" is checkable against a structure that was
registered from a completely different direction. Candidate B's geometry came out of the survival
campaign — stability analysis, bend stiffness, dance dynamics — with element = 4 eDP + 4 qDP = 1408
MeV, one element-plane = 4 qCP + 4 eCP, N_planes = 16. The founder's story is a formation narrative:
DP pairing → unit → plane → rod → ring. Plane = 2 units = 8 CPs ✓ matches the element-plane; element =
2 planes ✓ matches the 1408 MeV pricing; 8 elements × 2 planes = 16 ✓ matches "stack 16 high." Two
independent routes, seven days apart, one from stability and one from formation, landing on the same
stoichiometry — this is the convergence class the programme's swarm-validation strategy is built on,
and it is the strongest single reason to take the shift seriously rather than defending the 2537
target.

Second observation: the shift does not break the 2532–2534 arc; it re-purposes it, and arguably
IMPROVES its standing. The activation barrier was awkward as a Sea-side kinetic gate — it required a
thermal window argument with no registered rates (which is exactly where 2537 expected to die at
Branch I). As a fold-block it needs no rates at all: the barrier simply wins in the Sea (no folding
force present), and loses in baryogenesis (quark attraction supplies the force). The theorem's
"barrier is a path property" is doing cleaner work in the new picture than in the old one. Meanwhile
the near-threshold ΔE_b result, which was uncomfortable as a composition-setter (the trap extension
existed precisely because ≈ 0 licenses nothing), becomes unproblematic: the Sea composition no longer
routes through ΔE_b(hTetra) at all.

Third: the epoch anchor upgrade is real and material. T_form ≈ T_form(hTetra) had zero registered
quantitative inputs (the 2536 audit's central negative finding). The rod→ring bending anchor has the
corrected pure-bend stiffness (+291, 2450, independently validated) and the eCP-coat bending mechanism
already registered at DM-lane depth. The campaign's decisive number moves from an unregistered
territory to a partially-registered one. That said, I held the line on not computing anything: the
bending-energy-vs-kT comparison needs the stiffness's units and normalization walked carefully, plus
the Q5 question (does bending predict N=16 or consume it) resolved first — a successor prereg's job.

Fourth, the discipline decision: 2537 is HELD, not executed and not deleted. The prereg was committed
before the founder's message arrived, and no reading was ever taken under it — so there is no
anti-erasure tension, just a role supersession to annotate. Its apparatus (trap + extension,
bond-identity criteria, closed-list method) transfers to the successor intact; writing the successor
now, before Q1–Q5 resolve, would repeat the §6f mistake of anchoring on an epoch the founder was about
to move. The five clarification questions are scoped so that each answer changes something concrete:
Q1 gates a transfer channel, Q2 names the new bulk energetics target, Q3 closes the old anchor, Q4
re-shapes the NB-F-1 dial itself, Q5 decides whether the bending computation is a prediction or a
consumption.

One tension I looked for and did not find: the ZBW-dance bullets (5 qCP + 1 eCP oscillation partners
in the cubic core/coat configuration) are consistent with the registered dance-lane structure
(core/coat species, qCP pile contention) as far as the capture goes; the 90°-rotation claim is new
geometric content that the dance implementation can eventually check, but nothing registered
contradicts it. Flagged Q1 because "converts" is the one verb in the message that could smuggle a
pathway where the rest of the text describes a comparison.
