## What the charter left open, and how it was closed

The frozen charter specifies the Moment rule completely at the level
of physics commitments; what it deliberately left to this prereg —
by its own sentence — is sizes, Moments, observables, and pass
bands, plus the implementation stratum every automaton needs (what
exactly a relayed parcel carries, how flux divides over a discrete
shell, what a uniform-Sea PSR discretizes to). The three DR
resolutions follow the synchronous-semantics precedent: declared,
frozen, revisable only by fresh prereg — and DR-1, the load-bearing
one, is not trusted but TESTED, by a blocking gate (V-1) that
demands the emergent static field be inverse-square before any
deliverable is evaluated. If the minimal faithful reading of the
founder's Q4 does not produce Coulomb, the run stops and says so;
nothing downstream would mean anything.

## DR-3 is the 2714 lesson applied prospectively

The self-pair defect cost this programme its worst month. The
automaton's design excludes a CP's own immediately-preceding parcel
from its displacement sum at the specification level, so the class
of bug that manufactured the dead 6.6σ cannot exist here by
construction rather than by audit.

## The verdict structure respects what PR4 actually asks

PR4's six deliverables are questions about whether the REAL dynamics
(the founder's Moment rule) statistically justifies the Metropolis
machinery every screening result rests on. The design therefore
never assumes a temperature: θ_eff is MEASURED twice (energy route,
correlation route) and their ratio is itself deliverable (vi)'s
committed observable. A NOT-GIBBS outcome is written into the
classes as a first-class physics finding — the charter's "adverse
outcomes report in full" — and the robustness axis (three shell
radii) guards the verdict against being an artifact of one
discretization, in both directions.
