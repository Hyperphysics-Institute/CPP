# Reasoning — Patch 2631 (an envelope wider than expected, two honest holes in it, and stage 7 as the recurring character)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The result I braced for was a tight envelope -- the 2612 history made aim feel
fragile -- and instead the chain shrugged off 2.0 diameters of systematic offset
and 30 degrees of orientation error at both widths. In hindsight the funnel record
already said why: FUNNEL-1 measured a sink-mediated reach of at least 8D for a
single capture, and a chain whose every stage enjoys that reach has enormous slack
before systematic mis-aim matters. The envelope and the funnel are the same
physics seen at two levels, and the record lets that connection stay implicit
rather than promoting it -- one registered fact per patch.

Both failures landing at stage 7 is the structural note worth keeping. Stages 5
and 6 launch against the compact square; stage 7 is the first launch against the
grown, asymmetric 6-object, where the transverse direction rule and the object's
actual shape can conspire at large phi or on an unlucky face. That both undrawn
cells sit exactly there, with the identical miss signature, says the marginal-aim
physics is about the TARGET's grown geometry, not about the launcher arithmetic.

The dt-instability at the two halts forced the reading I least wanted to write
and most needed to: no wall drawn. The temptation was strong to call phi=45 "the
orientation wall" -- it halts, after all -- but the 1/100 leg misses while the
1/200 leg captures, and the launcher's own economy re-settle means the two legs
aim at slightly different objects. An instrument that disagrees with itself at
exactly the marginal cells does not get to draw walls there. Declaring the cells
UNDRAWN with the signature disclosed, and queueing the economy-matched re-run,
costs two successor items and buys the record its integrity.

The +x-face geometry (1.17/1.70 steep, 1.01/1.27 soft -- replicated across
widths) is the observation I most want FORM-1 to see: if the finished core has
face-dependent settled shapes, that is either an isomer spectrum or an
orientation degeneracy, and either answer matters to the Morse-form session. It
registers tonight as raw shape numbers and nothing more.
