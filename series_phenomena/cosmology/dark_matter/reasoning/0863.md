## What this changes
### (1) The 0862 dilution tax is defanged — as a rate competition, not by fiat
A bare ribbon faces two fates: chaperone-to-cross (rate R_cross ∝ [hTetra]·σ_accrete·v) or appose-and-collapse-to-glueball (rate R_appose ∝ [ribbon]·σ_appose·v). The glueball mass-fraction is f_glue = R_appose/(R_cross+R_appose) = 1/(1+ρ), ρ = R_cross/R_appose ≈ ([hTetra]/[ribbon])·(σ_accrete/σ_appose). Computed thresholds: **f_glue < 10% needs ρ > 9; < 1% needs ρ > 99.** With σ ratio ~ O(1), ρ ≈ [hTetra]/[ribbon]; Thomas's "[hTetra] ~ 50% of Sea, [ribbon] small/transient" makes ρ ≫ 9 plausible, so f_glue sits at the few-percent level and **σ_eff ≈ σ_ext** (e.g. ρ=30 → f_glue=3.2% → σ_eff=0.97 at σ_ext=1). The 0862 dilution tax is paid down to a small correction. **Honest framing:** this is a statement about concentrations and accretion cross-sections — checkable, not derived. It rides on [hTetra]/[ribbon] and the σ ratio; the suppression is real *if* those hold.
**Consequence:** the surviving present-day mixture is **two EXTENDED species (4-wide cross + hTetra loop), both σ/m ∝ N** — no compact glueball dilutant dominating the population average. Dilution is downgraded from the likely program-killer (its 0862 status) back to a sub-dominant correction.

### (2) The hinge "medium" stiffness becomes a concrete, in-window target — a near-cancellation the SSV calc must evaluate
Thomas's charge geometry *is* the form of the G1 hinge potential: a screened-repulsion restoring well, harmonic for modest bends with a ~90° anharmonic ceiling. The two config types bracket κ_θ (2rep-1att stiffer, 2att-1rep softer).
