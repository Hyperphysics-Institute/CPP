## What was computed

The edge-hinge bond is itself hinge-soft (rotating about the edge does not stretch it, 1831), so κ_θ is set by
the **perimeter eCP charges** near the hinge, whose separations change with the dihedral φ. Model: the nearest
perimeter charge pair sits ~d from the hinge edge on each rod; across the hinge their separation is r(φ) = 2d
sin(φ/2); the Coulomb energy U(φ) = kq²/r with kq² = E_ee·d (the eCP–eCP scale). κ_θ = d²U/dφ² at φ = 90°.
