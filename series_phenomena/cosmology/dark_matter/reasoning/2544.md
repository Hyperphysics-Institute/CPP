# Reasoning — Patch 2544 (OPEN-DM-ENDBOND-1 pre-registration)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The design problem here is unusual: the answer is surrounded. Three registered numbers already point
at the target from three directions — the map band [40,170], the 102 MeV contact lock, the ≈85 MeV
dance back-implication — and the whole value of a dedicated computation is that it arrives WITHOUT
consulting any of them. So the prereg's center of gravity is §2 input 4: all three are fenced as
post-freeze gates, with a fixed disclosure order, and the Branch-T trigger is written against the
specific sin available here (convention-shopping toward a gate). The union rule inherited from 2541
removes the shopping degree of freedom the same way it did for comparators.

The honesty clause I most wanted in early is §0's non-payoff statement: pinning E_endbond does NOT
narrow kT_form, because that band is comparator/convention-dominated. Without saying so now, a
successful pin would create pressure to advertise a sharpened epoch, and the arithmetic doesn't
support it. What the pin actually buys is the ΔE_close band and the survival margin — worth having,
honestly scoped.

The predictable failure mode is the functional walk. The 2450 electric functional is fully specified
in its artifact, but the strong-sector cross-plane content is registered more sparsely (contact
scales and saturation lengths at 2455; the pile-up/contention rules live partly in dance-lane
implementations). Whether those artifacts pin (i) which pairs couple, (ii) the radial form at and
beyond saturation, and (iii) the parity sign structure — without invention — is genuinely uncertain,
and I set Branch I as the honest terminus for any under-determined limb rather than allowing an
in-campaign model choice. If it goes Branch I, the named blocker is itself valuable: it tells the
founder exactly which physical registration (the strong-sector pairwise form) would unlock both this
pin and, eventually, the stiffness-free parts of the old distortion question.
