# Reasoning — Patch 2572 (the instrument gets its physics honestly; the clause I froze
# against my own future excitement)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The design center of this prereg is Section 5's emergent-transfer clause, and it exists
because I can already feel the temptation it fences. The conservative instrument has no
sink -- and yet the contention layer is non-Hamiltonian, so translational energy CAN
leak into the dance. If a dt-stable leak shows up during encounters, it will look
exactly like the founder's Sea-excitation mechanism emerging unbidden from the
registered dynamics, and the pull to declare "the sink was in the instrument all along,
capture is licensed" will be enormous. The clause freezes the honest handling now:
such a signal is an OBSERVATION of direct interest to K1b R-A -- possibly the single
most valuable thing K1a could produce -- and it still licenses nothing, because an
uncharacterized leak in a choreographed integrator is not a derived transfer law. The
opposite temptation (dismissing a real signal as integrator noise) is fenced by the
same clause's dt-stability criterion. Both doors closed before either opens.

Second design choice: rotation is in. A translation-only back-reaction was genuinely
tempting -- simpler, fewer failure modes -- but the charter hazard is right that
off-axis encounters transfer angular momentum, and an instrument that silently discards
it corrupts exactly the impact-parameter structure sigma(E,b) exists to measure. The
torque is evaluated at home points to keep the rigid layer self-consistent; the choice
is declared rather than buried.

Third: TRAPPED-AT-END. Renaming the classifier's CAP outcome was not cosmetic. A table
column headed "captured" would be quoted as capture within two sessions no matter what
footnote hedged it; a column headed "trapped-at-end (permanence unresolved)" carries
its own conditionality into every downstream quotation. Names are load-bearing in a
programme that runs on its own records.

The economy structure (probe-set union, primary-convention production) is the one place
I spent cheapness deliberately: the full convention product is ~30 minutes of compute,
affordable, but running it all would produce four parallel production tables and invite
post-hoc table-shopping. One production table with a disclosed systematic band from a
pre-declared probe set is less data and more evidence.
