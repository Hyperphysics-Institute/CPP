## Framing before computing
The scoping doc asks method (a) to fix the *sign* of the transverse charge-switched bending eigenvalue under
G1–G7. I set it up as the lowest transverse bending coordinate obeying x'' + ω²(τ)x = 0 with ω² charge-switched
between +A (same-charge, restoring) for fraction δ and −A (opposite-charge, Earnshaw anti-restoring) for 1−δ. Taken
as a square wave this is the Meissner equation, which is the right move here because its monodromy is closed-form:
I get the Floquet sign rigorously instead of trusting a perturbative Kapitza expansion whose validity is exactly
what's in question. The single dimensionless magnitude is ε = A/(mω_sw²) = (ω_A/ω_sw)². The static average
coefficient is ε(2δ−1), negative for δ<½, and δ=3/7<½ — so the mode is statically inverted and the only survival
route is dynamical stabilization of an inverted mode. That's the 2437 picture restated cleanly.

## The self-correction (this is the important part)
I had told Thomas, one turn earlier, that fast ZBW-scale switching (ω_sw ≫ ω_θ, small ε) would generically give a
positive Kapitza/ponderomotive stiffening — that the deep-fast regime was the favorable one. The computation says
that is wrong for a δ<½ inverted mode. At δ=3/7 the small-ε limit is UNSTABLE: tr(M)/2 = 1.003, 1.027, 1.131 for
ε = 1e-3, 1e-2, 1e-1. The reason is an order-counting fact I should have seen analytically before asserting the
heuristic: the negative static average enters at O(ε)·(2δ−1), the ponderomotive gain only at O(ε²). For small ε the
O(ε) negative term dominates the O(ε²) positive one, so fast switching does NOT stiffen — it just follows the
inverted static average and runs away. This is precisely the failure mode the guardrails target (an appealing story
asserted ahead of the arithmetic), and it is the actual computation, not review, that caught it. I flag it here
verbatim rather than quietly fixing it, because the lane's whole problem (2437) was motivated reasoning toward a
preferred answer, and the honest record has to show the correction.

## What the Meissner monodromy actually gives
Stabilization of the inverted δ=3/7 mode exists only inside a narrow intermediate-ε band, ε ∈ [0.179, 0.428]
(symmetric magnitude) — ω_sw/ω_A ∈ ~[1.5,2.4]. Below and above it, unstable. This is a stability tongue, not a
plateau. In-band the recovered stiffness is modest: k_eff/A ≈ 0.12 mid-band, up to ~0.57 only at the top edge where
the mode is at a period-doubling marginal boundary (fragile; I won't headline that number). The negative static
average to beat is |2δ−1| = 1/7 ≈ 0.143. So the parametric mechanism is real but it barely, and only in a fine-tuned
window, converts a −0.14·A static softness into a small positive stiffness.

## The two levers, and why I won't resolve them here
Branch asymmetry (R6): a weaker attractive phase helps a lot (k_eff_coeff 0.037→0.106 as ε_att/ε_rep 1.0→0.5) and a
stronger one kills it (unstable by ratio 1.5). Dynamical δ (R2): at fixed ε=0.3 the stiffness climbs monotonically
through δ=0.40→0.60, but since 3/7 is an upper bound the dynamical δ is likely below it, on the hard side. Both
levers are set by the driven equilibrium (R1) and the fragmentation-branch derivation (R6), neither of which I can
honestly fix at sign level. I resisted the temptation to pick a favorable ε (say 0.3) and a favorable branch
(ε_att/ε_rep=0.5) and quote k_eff/A≈0.1 as "survives" — that would be exactly the 2434 move (choose the favorable
sub-case, drop the rest, land on the founder's lean). G7 binds: the honest statement is conditional-and-narrow, not
survival.

## The netting point that can still flip everything
Even the in-window positive K_switch (~0.12·A) is only one term of the R5 sum. The 2430 ponderomotive tensor had a
−190 transverse eigenvalue on the far-out-coat geometry that geometry #3 supersedes; the geom-#3 version has to be
recomputed (R1 equilibrium first) and netted. If it is still strongly negative on the transverse mode, a 0.12·A
parametric term will not save it. So method (a) leaves the sign genuinely open, leaning unfavorable-to-conditional,
and hands R1 → R5 the decisive work. Method (b) (magnitude) is not worth running until the netted transverse sign is
known to be positive.
