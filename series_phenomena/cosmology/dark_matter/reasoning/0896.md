## Why this step, and what it is not

The panel (0895) converged — confirmers and dissenter alike — that the whole make-or-break is whether the
soft-eDP London enhancement survives at contact, and that Route B (the SSV charge-sum) or an equivalent
contact-scale treatment is the resolver. I did **not** attempt the full SSV charge-sum (that is the multi-
session OPEN-FP-SF-2-η arc). I took the one disciplined first step: **reduce the make-or-break to a single
computable quantity**, pin the endpoints, and use an external (noble-gas) anchor for the part standard physics
fixes — fabricating nothing.

## The reduction: one factor Q

Both constructions are the same object times one suppression factor. Write E_ee = Q · E_ee,free, where
**E_ee,free ≈ 41 MeV** is the free-cloud London depth at contact (construction 2, with the (E_qDP/E_eDP)⁶ = 729
polarizability enhancement already in it), and Q ∈ [0,1] is the **net contact suppression**. Then:
- in-window E_ee ∈ [0.8 keV, 2 MeV] ⇔ **Q ∈ [1.9×10⁻⁵, 4.9×10⁻²]**;
- construction 1 (170 keV) ⇔ **Q = 4.1×10⁻³**.
So the entire make-or-break is now the single question: **is Q ≲ 0.05?** (Q ~ 1 ⇒ ~40 MeV ⇒ falsification.)

Q factorizes into two physically distinct pieces:

  **Q = Q_damp · Q_stiff**

- **Q_damp** = (net well depth) / (bare dispersion at the hard-core radius) — the r-space damping of C₆/r⁶ at
  short range plus the exchange/Pauli repulsion that cancels most of the bare attraction at the minimum. This
  is *standard* damped-dispersion physics and is **anchorable externally**.
- **Q_stiff** = α_pol(contact) / α_pol(free-cloud) — the reduction of the eDP's polarizability at contact
  because the composite's internal modes are stiff/locked rather than freely responding. This is the
  substrate-specific quench Review II *asserted* but did not compute. **It is un-computed = OPEN-FP-SF-2-η.**

## What the external anchor delivers — and the uncomfortable part

Noble-gas benchmark (Argon, an external analogy, NOT a CPP derivation): ε / (C₆/σ⁶) = 12.4 meV / 24.8 meV ≈
**0.5**. The net well depth is ~half the bare dispersion at the hard-core radius — r-damping + repulsion buys
only a **factor ~2**. Applying Q_damp ~ 0.5 to E_ee,free gives **E_ee ~ 21 MeV — still OUT of window.**

This is the honest correction. In 0893/0895 the favorable reading leaned on "the London expansion breaks down
at f ≈ 0.5, so construction 2 is untrustworthy" — but the *breakdown of the expansion does not by itself
suppress the depth*. The dissent (Review IV) was right that this was an Appeal to Consequences. When you
actually estimate the net well depth with standard damping, you still land at tens of MeV. **r-space damping
alone does not rescue construction 1.**

## Where it bottoms out — the sharpened OPEN-FP-SF-2-η deliverable

In-window therefore requires the *second* factor to do real work:
- to reach the window **top** (2 MeV): **Q_stiff ≲ 0.10** (~10× beyond r-damping);
- to reach construction 1 (170 keV): **Q_stiff ≈ 0.008** (~120× beyond r-damping).

So the make-or-break is now a single, sharply-posed number: **does the eDP composite's contact polarizability
quench by ~10–120× relative to the free-cloud value?** Q_stiff ~ 0.01–0.1 ⇒ in-window, discriminating; Q_stiff
~ 1 ⇒ ~40 MeV ⇒ falsification. Q_stiff = α_pol(contact)/α_pol(free) is exactly the contact-scale internal-
response that OPEN-FP-SF-2-η must supply — the same undefined inter-CP-potential / substrate-stiffness
framework. This is a real advance: "needs the SSV charge-sum" (vague) → "needs Q_stiff, one factor with a
~0.1 in-window threshold" (specific and computable).

## The honest lean (corrected)

**At face value the candidate now leans OUT of window / toward falsification**, not in. Construction 1 / in-
window survives only if the composite-stiffness quench supplies a further ~10–120× beyond what standard
damping gives. That quench is **plausible** — composites *are* internally stiff, so a free-cloud 1/E³
polarizability is a genuine overestimate at contact (Review II's physical point is sound in direction) — but it
is **un-computed**, and standard molecular-physics damping does **not** deliver it on its own. So:
- 0893/0895's "leaning in-window (construction 1 favored)" was **over-optimistic**; the favorable case rests on
  an un-computed ~10–120× stiffness quench, and the no-quench face value is out-of-window.
- The dissent (Review IV) was **more nearly right** than the confirmers credited: at face value the physics
  leans falsification, and only the substrate-stiffness quench saves it.
- This **raises the stakes on Route B** (now well-motivated and sharply posed) and **argues against** registering
  a conditional in-window status now (0895 option 2) — that would bank a result whose favorable reading depends
  on the very quench that is still un-computed and that face-value physics does not support.
