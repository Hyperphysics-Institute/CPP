## The round's real content

I raised the polarisation-lag objection as a possibility worth
weighing. S1 turned it into an estimate: the fractional correction
from a causal response goes as omega*tau_P, and with omega ~ v/d and
tau_P ~ d/c that is v/c exactly. So a lag does not merely risk
reintroducing a first-order term -- it reintroduces one at precisely
the order the Darwin argument exists to remove, and the size is
generic rather than tuned. Worse for my case: CPP's relay is strictly
retarded by the founder's own ruling, so a lag is the DEFAULT, not an
exotic failure mode. My derivation assumed it away in one word,
"instantaneous," and I did not notice that the word was doing all the
work.

granted. S1's WITHDRAWN-PENDING-CONSTITUTIVE-CLOSURE is the honest
name for what all five described.
