The finding that matters most is the one whose author argued it wrong. Grok's J3 BREAK
rests on a mechanism the collapse voids — S(N) non-monotonicity does nothing, because
every S lives on the same curve per collision. I could have graded it down and moved
on, and the grading would have been correct and the adjudication would have failed.
Underneath the wrong mechanism is a right door: my family closure was monodisperse.
Dark matter formed by aggregation is almost certainly a spectrum over N, a mixture of
horizontally-shifted copies of F — and mixtures decouple the two ratio bars in exactly
the way single curves cannot, up to the onset ceiling of 5.11 that my own scan
computed. I found the door by steelmanning a broken argument, which is the adjudicator's
actual job: grade the argument as given, then ask what true thing the panelist might
have been reaching for. The mixture scan is queued as the wound's required repair, and
if it passes the suite, the corner reopens by panel action — which is what the brief
promised would be a welcome outcome, and I mean to keep the promise.

Gemini went off-brief and attacked the papers instead of the joints, and both of its
KILLs died on first arithmetic — by two and by forty orders of magnitude. I want to
record why they were still the round's second-best contribution: both checks were
legitimate and unregistered. "Cold by a wide margin" had never been costed against the
latent heat of the candidate's own formation; the icosahedral annihilation had never
been costed against spacetime curvature. The margins turned out enormous, but now they
are computed margins in the record instead of unexamined assumptions, and the
holonomy note even threw off a strong-field corollary for the Hawking lane. A panelist
who makes the programme compute what it had merely asserted has done the job, whatever
the severity label says.

DeepSeek's J4 BREAK I graded down on two factual errors — the scan was all-ℓ, and its
suppression numbers used the wrong k by an order of magnitude — and I did so while
executing, in the same session, the very computation it demanded. That combination is
the discipline in one gesture: the record is defended against wrong claims exactly as
vigorously as it is corrected by right ones, and the tiebreaker is always to run the
physics. Copilot returned honest emptiness — no repo access, no findings claimed it
could not verify — and honest emptiness is a valid return; the process fix is ours, not
its. The scout commission, on its first trial, produced two genuinely new registered
mechanism classes, an adopted L4 protocol with literature, and a unanimous priority
that is running as I write. The generative mode works. The founder's instinct to
enroll rather than merely audit is now a measured result.
