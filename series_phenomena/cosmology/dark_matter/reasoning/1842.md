## Definitions (pinned; carried into every deliverable)

- **CP** (Conscious Point): the substrate primitive. **eCP/qCP**: electric / color CP. **DP** = a bound CP pair;
  **eDP** (2 eCP) / **qDP** (2 qCP). Identities: **8 eCP ≡ 4 eDP**, **8 qCP ≡ 4 qDP** (same objects, grouped
  differently).
- **DM element**: the **8qCP + 8eCP moiety** (≡ 4qDP + 4eDP), ~**1408 MeV** — the elemental DM particle: a color
  core buffered by an eCP coat.
- **Cross Rod**: any assembly of N DM elements along a continuous axial **E_qq** color spine — the DM particle.
  Used interchangeably with "DM particle"; "DM element" is the unit. σ/m ∝ N counts **elements**.
- **E_qq**: the color bond — the axial core spine and the fusion-junction edge bond. The **edge bonds of concern
  are E_qq.** **E_ee**: the outer eCP-coat bonds — a **strippable coat + secondary bending-stiffness layer**, *not*
  an edge bond.
- **σ/m**: the **transport (momentum-transfer) self-interaction cross-section per unit mass** (cm²/g); for the
  extended rod it is geometry-driven, with the orientation-averaged projected/crossing area as its ceiling.
- **κ_θ**: the junction hinge's angular (scissor) stiffness. **g** = κ_scissor/κ_bend (both E_qq) — the
  scale-free flexibility ratio. **δ\***: the penetration depth where the collision-driven E_qq attraction balances
  the coat repulsion.

## Computation 1 — the σ/m coefficient (0.11 was a conflation)

**0.11 cm²/g is the dead point-scattering / compact value (0859)** — flat, velocity-independent, 5–20× too small.
It is **not** the coefficient of the extended σ/m ∝ N. "σ/m ≈ 0.11·N" conflated the compact floor with the
extended slope. Re-deriving the extended coefficient for the Cross Rod (two straight rods, self-interaction
cross-section ~ c_geo·L², L = N·ℓ_el, m = N·m_el; K = c_geo·ℓ_el²/m_el):

- Representative (ℓ_el = 2 fm, c_geo = π/4): **K ≈ 0.013 cm²/g per element**, so **σ/m ≈ 0.013·N** (not 0.11·N).
- Data band σ/m ∈ [0.6, 2] at **N ≈ 48–160 elements ≈ 380–1280 DPs** — consistent with 0860's hoop-derived
  ~10²–10³ DP band (the DP-count target survives).
- Dwarf σ/m ~ 3 needs **N ≈ 240 elements**, not ~28. **The 0.11·N shorthand made the required aggregate ~9× too
  small.** (Honest caveat: K carries ℓ_el² and c_geo — a factor-few band; ℓ_el ~ 2 fm and c_geo ~ O(1) are
  representative, not pinned. The *correction of the conflation* is robust; the exact K needs the element's
  effective crossing length.)

## Computation 2 — the two-coat δ*

The X-bond force balance was computed against a **single** E_ee coat; a real rod-rod approach drives through
**two** eCP coatings. δ* = the separation where the **two-coat** E_ee interaction (attraction *or* repulsion, per
coat charge presentation) balances the collision-driven E_qq force. In the simple repulsive limit the barrier
doubles → v_thr rises by ~√2 (e.g. N=28: ~2865 → ~4052 km/s; knee ~1000–1500 → ~1400–2100 km/s). But the coats
are **alternating-charge squares**, so the two-coat interaction is a **Madelung near-cancellation**, not a clean
2×: like-facing raises the barrier (up to √2× v_thr), opposite-facing can *aid* penetration (lower v_thr). Net
sign/magnitude = the two-coat Madelung residual — the same near-cancellation character as the E_qq edge-bond calc.
**Reported as:** two-coat balance replaces the single-coat δ*; bounded [~√2× up (repulsive) … <1× (attractive)];
exact = the coat-Madelung calc (mine to run next). This shifts the whole σ/m(v) gate and the catch/bounce boundary.
