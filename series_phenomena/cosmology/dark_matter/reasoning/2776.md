# Reasoning — Patch 2776 (C2R round-1 returns adjudication)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.**

The uncomfortable finding of this round is mine, and it needs to be
stated without softening: I broke the distinguishing-number test in
the act of specifying it. The 2775 packet named 0.3709 fm and
−9.0748e−01 as example distinguishing numbers, which converted the
test from "prove you executed" into "prove you read the packet."
Two seats then claimed VERIFIED-EXECUTED on exactly those numbers.
The tempting adjudication was to treat that as a third S5
fabrication instance and a first Gemini instance — the pattern
matches the impeachment history. I ruled otherwise: when the test
is broken by the author, echoing the disclosed values is weak
evidence of anything, and impeachment-grade findings should not
rest on a discrimination the packet made impossible. So the claims
are VOID rather than fabricated, the failure mode is registered
against the author, and the repaired challenge gives both seats a
clean way to authenticate next round. S5's ontology misstatement
("excluded core" — the exact premise 2767 rejects) is separate
evidence of non-engagement and is flagged, but it feeds the
already-queued hygiene entry rather than a new impeachment motion.

The second judgment was whether to adopt anything on a 3-2 split
where the three are unverified. CONV-006 made this easy in the
letter — authentication before adoption — but it is worth recording
that the spirit also cuts the same way: the two INSUFFICIENT seats
are the two behaving exactly as CONV-003 instructs, and adopting
over their heads on the strength of seats that did not execute
would invert the incentive structure the whole verification regime
exists to create. Nothing adopts; nothing is lost by the wait; the
conversion path is mechanical.

Third: executing S4's requested alternative-reading sweep same-arc,
rather than deferring it to a hypothetical future leg, was a
judgment call about where reviewer-requested computation sits
relative to prereg discipline. The precedent is the J2 rider:
labeled robustness scans, enumerated before results, OBS-class, not
fits. The sweep qualifies — four variants chosen by the reviewer's
own decision rule and by symmetry (isolate the diagonal; alternative
self-cell regularization; shape-scale probe), single configuration,
no tuning surface. And its result strengthens the round-1 record
materially: every variant fires the bound, including the control
that drops the self-cell entirely. The off-diagonal softening alone
is sufficient for non-convergence, which sharpens the L4 claim from
"this reading fails to converge" to "no reading in this family
converges" — exactly the robustness S4's decision rule demanded
before upholding the honesty-bound judgment.

Last, the tier distinction (VERIFIED-EXECUTED vs REVIEWED-OUTPUTS)
is new nomenclature and deliberately modest: S4 offered to convert
on pasted outputs, and refusing that offer would be process
maximalism — but a vote converted on pasted outputs is not the same
epistemic object as an independent execution, and the ledger should
say which happened. The label costs one word and preserves the
distinction the RV-2714 saga taught us to care about.
