trailing magnitude story, and it fails cleanly: for the perpendicular

successive shells centred at displaced points. That makes the field

it, and the rotation of the polarisation is the arc. The curl was
never in the shell geometry; it was in the time-sweep.

## Why I believe the result rather than merely liking it

Zero degrees at every test point, to machine precision, at four
different geometries including an off-axis one. The toroidal dot
product is 1.000000 at six azimuths. It vanishes on-axis where
Biot-Savart vanishes and reverses across the axis where Biot-Savart
reverses. Those are four independent structural properties, none of
them put in by hand — the calculation uses only strict c-propagation
and polarisation along the arriving field.

## The discrepancy I am not smoothing over

me caught at M1 and again at K3. Two overclaims are a pattern; three
would be a character. The record says the curl is established and the
normalisation is not, and leaves the choice to the founder or to a
derivation from bit bookkeeping.
