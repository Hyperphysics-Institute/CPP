## Why D5's split was not really a split

One position said the missing measurement blocks restoration; three
said it does not block but is mandatory before stronger claims. Those
are the same policy with different labels: under either, nothing may
be claimed unconditionally until a correctly-scoped transverse test
runs. Opening OPEN-C23-TRANSVERSE-VALIDATION as a named gate with an
explicit list of what it blocks gives the strict position everything
it wanted except the word "pending", and gives the permissive
positions the conditional theorem they granted. Recording it as a
gate rather than as a vote count is the honest synthesis.
