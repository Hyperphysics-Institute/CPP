## The three inputs, derived

**ν_PCD — the attempt frequency.** Two readings, and the distinction is load-bearing:
- The corpus substrate clock is 1/t_P (0774: every CP executes one PCD cycle per Planck tick), **ν ~ 1.85×10⁴³ Hz.**
- But the Arrhenius **detachment** prefactor is not the substrate's raw update rate — it is the frequency at which
  the bound element *assaults the bond barrier*, i.e. its vibrational frequency in the E_qq well,
  ν_vib = (1/2π)(1/d)√(2E_bond/m_el) ≈ **1.5×10²² Hz** (E_qq = 66 MeV, m_el = 1408 MeV, d = 1 fm). Using 1/t_P as
  the prefactor would be a category error (the substrate tick is the resolution, not a specific mode's escape-attempt
  rate). **ν_vib ~ 10²² Hz is the physically correct value**; I carry 1/t_P only as an (over-estimating) bound.

**H_form — the Hubble rate at freeze-out.** CPP cosmology rests on the standard FRW/Friedmann framework (early_universe
sector), so H(T) = 1.66 √g*(T) · T²/M_Pl. Freeze-out (thermal contact, kT_form = T_rad) is the self-consistent root
of ν·exp(−E_bond/kT_form) = H(kT_form).

**E_bond — the detachment energy.** The element–element **E_qq** spine bond, E_bond = n_qq·E_qq with E_qq ≈ 66 MeV
(n_qq = 1–4 ⇒ 66–264 MeV). Kept as a range.

## Why 1846's "kT_form ~ 5 MeV, ratio ~ 13" looked encouraging — the φ correction

The 1846 target E_bond/kT_form ~ 12–14 (kT_form ~ 5 MeV) used **φ ~ 1**. But φ is the monomer *volume fraction* of a
dilute relic gas, which 0881 already estimated at **φ ~ 10⁻⁹…10⁻¹⁴** — φ ~ 1 would mean the DM elements are
close-packed, which is unphysical. With the correct dilute-gas φ, the band-required ratio for N ~ 700 is **~34–45**
(kT_form ~ 1.5–2.0 MeV), not 13 (verify §2). And the forward freeze-out independently delivers ~49–51. So the two
agree to ~20% at ~40–50 — which, given the exponential sensitivity, puts ⟨N⟩ ~2–3 orders above the (φ ~ 1-based)
target. **The encouraging "kT_form ~ E_bond/13" was an artifact of the φ ~ 1 placeholder; the physical target is a
colder kT_form and a larger, over-cored ⟨N⟩.**

## The two escape routes — both unpinned, so neither rescues the prediction

1. **DM colder than radiation (verify §3).** If the DM sector decoupled and kT_DM < T_rad at freeze-out, H_form is
   set by the hotter T_rad → larger H → smaller freeze-out log → smaller ⟨N⟩. Landing ⟨N⟩ ~ 700 needs DM colder than
   radiation by ~10²–10³× at the QCD-scale epoch — a specific **unpinned decoupling** history, i.e. a tuning, not a
   prediction.
2. **Nucleation/depletion cap.** The equilibrium ⟨N⟩ = √φ·exp(E_bond/2kT_form) assumes an infinite monomer bath.
   At E_bond/kT_form ~ 50 the association is so strong that essentially all monomers polymerize, and the true size
   is set by the **monomers-per-nucleus ratio** (nucleation density), not the freeze-out temperature. An equilibrium
   ⟨N⟩ ~ 10⁵ almost certainly exceeds any plausible depletion cap → the real size is **nucleation-limited**. In that
   regime **kT_form is not the controlling variable at all** — which means the 1846 reduction ("size ↔ kT_form") is
   incomplete: pinning kT_form does not pin N_form.

## Verdict (the natural-vs-tuned-vs-kill fork, answered)

**Neither a clean zero-parameter prediction nor a clean kill — the natural case over-cores, and the reduction is
incomplete.** Precisely:
- The natural inputs (vibrational prefactor, thermal contact, dilute-gas φ) give ⟨N⟩ ~ 10⁴–10⁶ ⇒ dwarf σ/m ~ K·N ~
  10²–10⁴ cm²/g ≫ the observed few cm²/g ⇒ **over-cored** ⇒ the dwarf normalization is *missed*, in the "too large"
  direction the handover named.
- The window is reachable only via an unpinned DM-decoupling tuning (~10²×) **or** a nucleation cap that supersedes
  the whole kT_form reduction. Either way **N_form is not a zero-parameter prediction of kT_form.**
- **What actually controls N_form is now identified:** φ (the relic abundance / volume fraction, unpinned to orders),
  the DM–radiation thermal-contact history (unpinned), and the nucleation-site density (unpinned). kT_form is one of
  several inputs, not the sole pin.
- **The candidate is not killed:** the cluster σ/m ∝ 1/v² branch is parameter-free and independent (1845); only the
  dwarf normalization rides on this, and it is now the piece that lacks a zero-parameter route.
