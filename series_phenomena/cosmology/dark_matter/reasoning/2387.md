# Reasoning — Patch 2387 (D1–D3 entered on delegation; D4 held; round closed)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (Opus, DM lane, 9 July 2026).

## 1. The delegation judgment — the only real content of this patch

The founder wrote: "Approve D1-D4 if you approve. If you want a statement, tell me
what needs to be said. I don't know what is being requested of me."

D1–D3 are process closures (close the collection; adjudicate a unanimous tally;
confirm a 3-0 ratification). For these, founder-approval-by-explicit-delegation is
sound and precedented: the decision is his, exercised through delegation to a
recommendation he commissioned, and his verbatim words are the record.

D4 is not a process closure — it amends Clause 1, the founder-attested exit-conditions
instrument. Attestation exists to guarantee that the person whose theory can be killed
by these conditions personally and knowingly accepted each one. A record that showed
the founder attesting a new exit condition in the same breath as "I don't know what is
being requested of me" would hollow out the instrument the programme relies on when a
falsifier someday fires. So the delegation is respectfully NOT accepted for D4 alone.
The founder was given the exact sentence and a one-line plain-language meaning ("if
experiments search at 8.45 GeV at the required abundance and find nothing, the theory
takes the exit"); D4 enters on his explicit yes or edited text. The hold is costless:
(d′) is operative only after the derived family is registered, which is post-Q3c.

This is the same principle as CLONE-FIRST or pre-registration: the ceremony is not
decoration; it is what makes the record mean something later.
