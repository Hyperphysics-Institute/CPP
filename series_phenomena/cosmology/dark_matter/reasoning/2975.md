## The ledger route beats the bijection route

GPT asked for a proof that the discrete update preserves the one-to-one traversal pairing; DeepSeek asked for the analytic discrete telescoping. Building the pairing explicitly would be fragile — it depends on the traversal's internal structure, which varies with phase and commensurability. The stronger and simpler route: telescope against the DP's OWN state functions. Per-step reciprocity converts the sum of composite-side exchanges into a pure endpoint difference of DP momentum/energy, and endpoint closure (s_K = s_0, from M1/Version B: the DP's anchored role belongs to the pattern, its unanchored emission departs, the DP returns to rest) annihilates the difference. Phase, commensurability, step size, and step irregularity never enter the proof — they only choose WHICH closed path is walked. The bijection then falls out as a corollary rather than a construction: closure is strictly stronger than pairing. Exact at K = 2; no continuum limit anywhere, which is precisely what the panel said was missing.
