# Reasoning — Patch 2620 (the discriminator gets its spec before its chance to flatter)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The panel's five-part strengthening (Seats 1 and 5) is really one demand stated five
ways: show that the number the sink writes into the Sea ledger belongs to the physics
and not to the bookkeeping. The design choice that matters most is gating on S_1pass
rather than end-of-window Sea. The 2609 deposits were read at TC=120, which includes
the post-capture bound period — and bound motion in this system is chaos-exposed
(the 2513 lesson from another lane). A dt-convergence gate on a chaos-fed cumulative
would fail for reasons that have nothing to do with the partition, and the cell would
read adverse while measuring the wrong thing. The single-pass quantity is what
EDGE-2(ii) always meant, it is the quantity the relic campaign will eventually
consume, and it is the one a discriminator can honestly gate.

Method B had to be independent by construction, not by implementation. The sink's own
partition is per-particle and window-mean-based; the collective momentum decomposition
(invariant-style KE_trans from ΣP against Σm) shares no code path and no averaging
window with it. If both read the same oscillatory reservoir through a relativistic
encounter, the partition-artifact hypothesis has nowhere left to live at the 10% level.
If they disagree, the disagreement IS the finding — RD2 is written in the same font
as RD1, per the lane's standing rule.

The η=0 member is a gift of the registered law's structure: at η=0 the split is
algebraically the identity (Vn = Vbar + Vosc = V), so "split-removed" and "sink-off"
are the same run — Seat 5's spec item (e) costs nothing extra and C2's machine-zero
check falls out for free.

FUNNEL-1 folds in exactly as the adjudication said it would: the ON/OFF aim-offset
scan answers "sink artifact or not" numerically, while the zero-freedom well W(b)
bounds what the potentials alone could ever reach. The one temptation fenced in
advance: the 2609 transit-shed model is NOT resurrected for the analytic member —
it failed adverse and a failed model does not get to sneak back in as scaffolding.
Only the well, which is registered geometry.

The drift-compensation clause is the 2612 lesson paid forward at prereg time, as the
adoption demands: absolute-column launches, |Δcen| reported per cell, any boundary
reading coinciding with drift > 0.5D re-confirmed centroid-relative before its
verdict is read. The funnel cell of all cells does not get to be contaminated by the
artifact class it exists to classify.
