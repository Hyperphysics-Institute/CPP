**How the classification was reached, honestly.**

1. **The determinacy question was answered by construction, not by opinion.** The prereg's Q1
   asked whether registered data D determines ξ₂. The clean way to answer "no" is to exhibit two
   completions, both consistent with every registered fact, that disagree — and here they disagree
   in SIGN (continuous-time wave: subluminal 1/20-class; leapfrog at σ² > 3/5: superluminal).
   Opposite signs from the same D is as strong as underdetermination proofs get, and it
   retroactively justifies the 2950 sign-blind successor rule: had we frozen "weaker sign
   forever," a derived sign would have collided with the frozen text; had we left it open, the
   choice would now be discretionary post-revelation. The prereg closed that door one patch before
   this patch proved the door mattered.

2. **What made the geometric facts bankable.** The load-bearing observation is that I_h has no
   invariant spherical harmonics of degree 1–5, so EVERY I_h orbit is a spherical 5-design. That
   upgrades the prereg's S1 (scalar by invariant counting) to exact quadratic isotropy for ANY
   equivariant stencil — no single-shell assumption needed for the isotropy claim, though the
   moment VALUES (4, 12/5) are the uniform edge stencil's, which R4 + I_h transitivity force for
   the elementary step. G3 (exact polarization-degeneracy at quadratic order) combines the
   5-design fact with 2940's Fact 2 (inversion): linear birefringence needs a degree-4 anisotropic
   invariant (absent), circular terms need inversion-odd invariants (absent at all orders). The
   prereg's "report the birefringent piece separately" resolves to a derived zero.

3. **The CFL finding was unplanned and is the session's sharpest physical output.** Computing the
   leapfrog completion's stability limit (σ_max ≈ 0.61) was a due-diligence step that returned
   physics: the natural founder-picture reading "one edge per Absolute Moment" (σ = 1) is

   σ = 1 forces a transport/unitary-class relay (which can be edge-dispersion-free, moving all of
   ξ₂ into oblique reconstruction); wave-class forces σ < 0.61. FOUNDER-Q-XI2-1 was drafted to
   present exactly this fork in physical pictures (spring vs bucket brigade), per PD-006's
   contact rule for physics questions.

   distinction is the same one CONV-004 draws for the ceiling: a formula with its conditionality
