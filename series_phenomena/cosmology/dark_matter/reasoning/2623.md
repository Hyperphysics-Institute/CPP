# Reasoning — Patch 2623 (the corrected cell inherits everything except its mistake)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The whole design intent of this document is to change exactly two things and prove
nothing else moved: the 2613 precedent, applied deliberately. Carrying the 2620 text
by citation rather than re-typing it is itself a defect-avoidance choice — a
re-typed spec is an opportunity for silent drift, and this lane has a registered
method (the convention-pin) built on precisely that suspicion of re-typing.

The one genuinely new element is the UNGATEABLE-AT-THIS-INSTRUMENT reading under the
baseline guard. It exists because the honest alternative to a marginal reading is
not a generous reading — it is a declaration that the instrument cannot decide at
this resolution. The relic campaign taught the cost of letting near-threshold
numbers pick sides; if the funnel-edge deposits land inside 3x baseline, the record
will say the discriminator has a resolution floor there, and a finer-dt member can
be declared later as its own cell. Kill-generous, confirm-stingy, including against
the temptation to let the discriminator certify itself at the margin.

C2b re-executes rather than citing the 2622 numbers even though they already satisfy
it: a gate consumed from a diagnostic is a gate that never ran under its own prereg,
and the 68 seconds it costs to re-run is cheaper than the precedent it would set.
