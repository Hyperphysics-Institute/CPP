# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2843 (the charge-free tell, and the half the picture was missing)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-27.**

## The clue I walked past yesterday

omega has no q in it. I wrote it down at 2842, computed with it,
compared it to B — which is proportional to q — and did not notice
that no constant could ever reconcile a charge-free quantity with a
charge-proportional one. The 1/r mismatch was not a normalisation gap;
it was a category error announcing itself. Finding it required only
reading my own formula for units, which is the cheapest check there
is and the one I skipped.

## What the founder's picture had and what it lacked

He described the poles arcing. That is exactly right, and at closest
approach it is the ENTIRE response -- the stretch term vanishes
identically there, which is presumably why the picture felt complete
when he formulated it. What it lacked is the longitudinal half: away
from closest approach the poles also extend and relax as the charge
nears and recedes, and that stretch dominates near the axis. Both
together are dP/dt, and dP/dt is what sources B. So B is not the
response; B is what the response generates. The radial law then closes
without any fitted constant: 1/r^3 source, 1/r^2 field.

## Why I am not restoring the bound

The Darwin argument at 2838 needed the transverse sector normalised.
It now is. The temptation is obvious: declare 1B's conditional
discharged and put the second-order relaxation back, which would move
the last requirement from hard to nearly closed. But the withdrawal
was the frozen consequence of a frozen test executed yesterday, and
undoing it on the strength of my own derivation produced hours later
-- after two overclaims already required the panel to adjudicate to a
minority against me -- would be indistinguishable from motivated
reasoning even if it is correct. So the record states the conditional
is discharged in principle and leaves the restoration to a panel
motion. If they agree, they can move it in one line.

## What I would want checked

The relation curl B = (1/c^2) dE/dt is Maxwell, not new physics; what
is CPP-specific is the claim that the DP Sea's dP/dt is the physical
realisation of that source term. That identification rests on P being
along the arriving field, which is C23's own content. A seat could
reasonably ask whether CPP's polarisation response is instantaneous
and linear in E, which I have assumed throughout and which the
framework has not established.
