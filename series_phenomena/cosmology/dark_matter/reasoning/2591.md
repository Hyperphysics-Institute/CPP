# Reasoning — Patch 2591 (a battery that can only sharpen, never retract)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The design constraint I held throughout: no branch of this battery may touch the win's
registration. That is not protectiveness -- it is scope hygiene. The win registered "the
square is stable at its configuration under the completed interaction," and every
battery item measures a DIFFERENT property (robustness radius, endurance, minimality,
basin extent). Conflating "the basin is narrow" or "3 also holds" with "the win
weakens" would be the same scope error in reverse that the arc kept catching in the
adverse direction. The frozen readings say where each outcome lands, and none of the
landings is on the registration.

B-3's readings took the most drafting care because minimality is the founder's phrase
("merely 4") and a sub-4 hold would brush against it. The honest structure: the WIN
never claimed minimality, the founder's sentence reads naturally as a size statement
about the core, and if 3 holds we will say "3 holds" in the same font we said "4
holds." Pre-writing that sentence now, before knowing, is the entire value of the
freeze.

B-4 carries the 2590 flag ("the author's eagerness noted") and I wrote its fence
first, before its cells: basin-of-an-existing-equilibrium is measurable tonight;
nucleation-from-the-Sea is N2 and no annealing cell, however pretty its convergence,
may borrow that word. The declared-seeds discipline (fixed integers, named in the
prereg) is cheap insurance that no one -- including me -- reruns a seed until a
family converges.

The Galilean boost is the battery's quiet tooth: it tests the LAW, not the square,
and a boost-variant law would poison every result upstream. Putting an instrument
falsifier inside a confirmation battery is the arc's disciplines compounding --
confirmation and self-suspicion in the same document, which is what this programme
has been learning to make routine.
