# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2866 (SSV asymmetry as retardation)

**STATUS: contemporaneous, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-29.**

His acceleration half is not just right, it is the mechanism already
measured -- 2496 says the back-reaction tracks instantaneous
acceleration "retarded only by the cloud light-crossing time sigma/c",
which is his near-side-pushed-first in physical language. Nothing to
argue with.

The persistence half is where I think it strains, and the strain is
specific. Bare retardation gives an asymmetry proportional to the RATE
OF CHANGE of the drive. A uniformly moving source's retarded field is
just its boosted static field; in vacuum there is no fore/aft asymmetry
left once the push stops. To keep an asymmetry at constant v you need
the MEDIUM to have a finite response time -- a wake that lags because
the Sea cannot reorganise instantly. That is real physics, but it is
not the same mechanism he named, and it brings its own scale.

And that scale is the thing. The fractional asymmetry of a wake behind
a source crossing its own size is (response time)/(traversal time),
which is tau_Sea/(d_DP/v), which is eps_mem. His persistent asymmetry
IS eps_mem. The quantity his coasting account needs large enough to
sustain motion is the quantity clause 2 needs small enough to be
subdominant. The programme has been treating them as unrelated
quantities in different files.

Then k = 1 falls out again, from a completely different direction than
2864's "each Moment". Two independent routes to the same value is
normally corroboration and I notice that I want it to be. It is the
fifth consecutive turn that a route to closing the last gate has
appeared. The identity only holds if ALL of SSV_net is lag-induced,
which he asserts on the grounds that it is the only mechanism he can
think of, and that is not a derivation. So it is flagged and not banked,
and I have written the disqualifier down for the third time: anything
that closes clause 2 in one line does not get to close clause 2.

The useful move is the crux question. Everything turns on whether
SSV_net in the coasting case means the net external force or the total
local field asymmetry including the comoving cloud's own structure.
Under the first, he conflicts with 2496 and coasting drains. Under the
second, a lossless comoving cloud carries a permanent internal
asymmetry with no external force and no drain, Newton I survives, and
his "persistent cloud" and 2496's mu -> infinity limit are the same
object described twice. The second reading reconciles everything, which
is exactly why I am not the one to choose it.

I specified the F_hold refinement test and did not run it, and the
reason matters: under the reconciling reading the test measures an
external force and does not bear on an internal asymmetry at all. So
the number would arrive with its interpretation still open, and the
record of this session is unambiguous about what I do with numbers
whose interpretation is open. Crux first.
