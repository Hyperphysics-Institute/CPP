# reasoning/2672.md — the readout packet (verbatim, at-patch)

STATUS: verbatim, captured at Patch 2672, 20 July 2026.

Packet design followed the 2663 shape deliberately: adjudication items
narrow and votable, NOTICEs for what is recorded rather than asked,
named joints aimed at our own weakest points, and return handling
pre-committed before any return exists so no adjudicator discretion is
available when the returns land. The three adjudication items separate
what must not be conflated: the readout's own standing (Q1, split into
class and provenance because a seat can accept FG-OTHER while rejecting
the instrument extension), the fork's disposition (Q2 — the one routing
charter §5 left unnamed, so it is genuinely the panel's), and consumer
impact (Q3 — the 4x-shorter l against the composite's usage is the
highest-stakes downstream question and I refused to pre-judge it in the
worker seat).

The six joints are the six places I would attack this arc myself, in
descending order of leverage: provenance, INF-S1C-1, the homogenization
normalization used in a regime the arc itself proves is
post-homogenization (J3 is the joint I consider most likely to draw
blood), the envelope observable, the constitution argument, and the
operator form. Writing J3 honestly mattered to me: the alpha matching is
long-wavelength, the answer is short-wavelength, and a seat should be
invited to say whether that is self-undermining or self-consistent.

One discipline note: the OBS-class d_DP/4 coincidence went in as a
NOTICE with the non-elevation clause restated, because the last time
this sector had a pretty coincidence (beta_d = 2) the standing rule is
what kept it from steering an adjudication.
