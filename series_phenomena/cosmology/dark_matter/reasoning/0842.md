# Reasoning — Patch 0842 (dwarf core radius from σ_V/m ≈ 0.20)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (Opus, Session 156, 10 June 2026). First-pass; translates the cross-section into a core size. No verdict/THEO.

Priority-2 task: say quantitatively how mild "mild" is. The clean, robust quantity is the one-scatter density ρ_1 = 1/[(σ_V/m) v t] — the density at which a particle scatters ~once per halo age; cores form where the profile exceeds it. That part needs no halo model. At σ_V/m=0.2, t=10 Gyr: ρ_1 ~ 0.08 M_sun/pc^3 (dwarf), 0.0016 (cluster).

Translating ρ_1 to a core radius DOES need a halo model, and I was careful to flag that. For a representative dwarf NFW (ρ_s=0.02, r_s=1.5 kpc) the core comes out ~0.27 kpc vs ~0.81 kpc for strong SIDM (σ/m=1). So CPP's core is a factor ~3 smaller — a few hundred pc, at the small/detectable edge of the observed ~0.2-1 kpc dwarf-core range. That is the honest quantitative content of "mild": a real but small core, near the resolution floor, distinguishable from a CDM cusp but smaller than the strong-SIDM reading.

The cluster side is the consistency check and I did not soft-pedal it: ρ_1 sits below cluster central densities, so a modest cluster core forms, and σ_V/m=0.2 is at the EDGE of cluster bounds — fine vs the looser ~0.5-1 limits, mild tension vs the tightest ~0.1 cross-system inferences. Not excluded, but not comfortably central either. I said so.

The real result is the same one 0840/0841 set up: velocity-independence means CPP CANNOT produce the dwarf-large/cluster-small core pattern the cross-system SIDM fits prefer. So the falsifiable signature is the flatness, and the core-radius calc makes it quantitative: mild cores at EVERY scale, ~0.1-0.3 kpc in dwarfs, modest in clusters, no velocity scaling. Clear failure mode (data demand falling σ/m -> CPP wrong on both ends) and clear success mode (data consistent with flat ~0.1-0.3).

Caveat discipline: I separated the robust claims (mild, flat, detectable-edge — model-independent) from the magnitude (r_core factor-2 halo-model-dependent ON TOP of the factor-3 from f), and called the core size an order-of-magnitude first-pass (~0.1-0.5 kpc) rather than quoting 0.27 kpc as if it were sharp. The 0.27 is one representative halo, not a prediction to 2 figures.

Anti-priorities held: led with the model-independent ρ_1, flagged the halo-model dependence of r_core explicitly rather than presenting 0.27 kpc as precise; reported the cluster edge-of-bounds honestly (did not bury the mild cluster tension); kept the robust/uncertain split clean; first-pass, no verdict.
