after delegation would be false modesty dressed as discipline; rendering it as if

(a technical/epistemic judgment about whether the gate record satisfies F3's

be certification by omission. And the G3 condition needed its status stated
plainly: the collapse-strand miss is a demand-internal fact, so treating it as a
gate failure would hold the population to a bar no candidate can clear — that is
the one place where I judged, and I said so.
