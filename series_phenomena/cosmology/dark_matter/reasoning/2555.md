# Reasoning — Patch 2555 (AUDIT-DANCE-REACH-1: universal misclassification; favorable fidelity tension; structure robust)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The census number that stopped me was 48/64. I had registered the audit item expecting a
boundary quirk — a few planes near the ring's top — and found instead that three quarters
of the ring's qCPs carry cross-plane "in-plane" partners. This has been true of every ring
result the programme has banked, through the promotion. The immediate instinct was alarm;
the correct posture, which took a beat to reach, is that an implementation with a
spec-drift can still be a perfectly good registered functional — what matters is that the
drift is now measured, its direction known, and its adjudication routed to the only
authority that can settle what the words meant.

The direction of the shift is the part that demands the most care in reporting. The
ground-truth variant makes the ring MORE favored — by ~20 MeV on the promotion-class
observable — which means the discovery is that our registered numbers have been
conservative. It would be very easy to lead with that framing, and it would be subtly
corrupt: "the error helps us" is still "there is an error at the promotion-class
observable," and the panel deserves to hear it as a fidelity finding first and a
direction second. The pre-committed R1 threshold fired; the disclosure line is queued; the
verdict does not move on my say-so in either direction. That the favorable direction makes
this disclosure EASIER to make honestly is a luck I note without leaning on.

R2 was the audit's clean gift: the L=20 peak and the L=24 collapse survive the classifier
swap, and the collapse sharpens into an actual sign change — closure failing to pay at 24
under the spec-intent reading. The two-sided window is now one successor campaign away on
its upper side. I did not run that successor tonight, though it is cheap and the founder's
standing instruction would arguably cover it, because running it means choosing a
classifier, and the classifier is precisely what is now in dispute. Building on contested
foundations to keep momentum is how programmes accumulate silent debt; the founder's
adjudication costs one message and settles which functional the successor pre-registers.

One more debt recorded: the Dz<0.5D test was written in 2455 for the straight rod, where
it is exact, and every subsequent ring use inherited it without a ring-context re-check —
including by me, five patches running, until the FORM-L non-smoothness forced the look.
The audit habit that caught it was reactive, not proactive. A standing item for the next
CONV-001 packet: reviewers should be invited to hunt for other straight-rod-era
operationalizations that rings inherited untested.
