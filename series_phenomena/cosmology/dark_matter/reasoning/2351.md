## What I decided and why
1. **Sourced the demand exactly, then pre-registered the expected shortfall.** The
   collapse-strand demand (Correa 2021, read in full) was going to miss our curves
   by ×2–5 — visible arithmetic before any code ran. I put that arithmetic IN the
   pre-registration so that when the strand-split materialized it would be on record
   as anticipated-with-numbers, not narrated afterward. Three anchors only (the ones
   with in-text ⟨v⟩); the other six dwarfs stayed out of protocol rather than
   half-sourced.
2. **Computed her curve from her parameters, never digitized a figure.** Same
   discipline as 2345/2350: verbatim numbers or nothing.
3. **Let the re-search exhaust the trade before concluding.** The extended-variant
   optimum landed with Carina's floor and pin's ceiling tied at ×1.34 — that tie IS
   the measurement: the box has no remaining direction.
4. **Chased the tie to its source.** The tie looked like a candidate limitation
   until I evaluated Correa's own curve at the pin velocity: σ_C(50) = 28 vs pin
   ≤5–10. The collision is between two published demands, not between the demand
   and us. That reframe is the patch's real content, and it is checkable in one
   line of arithmetic.
5. **Did not self-certify the F3 trigger.** All three gates are traversed, but G3's
   grade is CLEARED-conditional-on-strand. F3 says adoption is auto-proposed when
   gates clear; whether conditional counts as clear is Thomas's reading. I assembled
   the proposal as an annex with the full record and left the decision block empty.
