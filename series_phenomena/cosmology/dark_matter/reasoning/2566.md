# Reasoning — Patch 2566 (adjudication #1 captured; the dispatch finally earns its trigger)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The founder answered a physics question with a process answer — "go for the possible one,
but ask the panel" — and the capture had to preserve that shape rather than flatten it
into "founder selected O3." He didn't. He expressed a feasibility posture and deferred
the selection to after advice, which is a different registry object than a decision, and
conflating them would let a future session cite "founder adjudication" for a route he
never actually picked. The interpretation paragraph exists to make that distinction
load-bearing.

He also asked what I think, directly, so the recommendation goes on the record as a
recommendation: O3 → O1, with O2 gated on postulate necessity. In 2565 I kept the same
lean out of the record because he hadn't asked and the options needed to reach him
unweighted; now he has asked, and withholding a view he requested would be a different
kind of thumb on the scale. The line between the two is whether the founder solicited it.

The governance piece I'm most deliberate about is sequencing item 2: no O3 drafting while
the panel deliberates. The temptation is real — O3 is cheap, I could have a criterion
prereg sketched tonight, and "just having it ready" feels like diligence. It's actually
contamination: a drafted route acquires gravity, the consolidation patch starts reading
panel feedback as amendments-to-my-draft instead of open advice, and the consultation
becomes theater. The dispatch is only worth its cost if the answers can still change the
route.

Finally, the packet edit honors the 2563 rider's exact letter: the queue clears on the
PASTE, not on the licensing. Two days ago the risk was a phantom dispatch clearing a real
queue; today the risk would be a licensing line doing the same thing one step earlier.
Same fence, same reason, applied to myself.
