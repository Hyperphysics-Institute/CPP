# Reasoning — Patches 2611 + 2612 (the control that refused to lie, and the shell
# that was never there)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (joint fragment; the scan and its defect diagnostic).

C-A failing was the best thing that happened tonight. The scan came back 46-for-46
captures and the pleasant reading was one keystroke away: "the soft cluster captures
on every face, RC1, register the map." But the control cell — the one whose only job
was to find the registered miss again — found a capture instead, and RC4 stopped the
whole reading before the pleasant version could exist. A scan that cannot reproduce
the phenomenon it explains has no standing to explain anything; I wrote that sentence
at prereg without knowing it was aimed at me.

The defect is a frame assumption so ordinary I never saw myself make it. The
registered chain aims every incident at a fixed laboratory column, faithful to how an
ambient flux actually arrives; my scan aimed at the cluster, faithful to how a
physicist thinks about faces. Both are legitimate instruments; they are different
experiments; and the prereg's control conflated them. The diagnostic then delivered
the number that ends the argument: the soft 6-cluster was thirteen femtometers —
eleven diameters — away from where stage 7's incident was sent. The "non-sticking
face" was a face nobody hit.

Retracting the 2604 classification stings precisely because that record praised
itself for classifying the failure mode "before feelings." The classification WAS
principled — miss-not-break was read correctly from the raw outputs — but the
interpretive layer on top (shell structure) outran what the cells could support, and
it took a control failure two patches later to expose it. The lesson banks next to
2597's: asserted physics scatters, and so does asserted GEOMETRY; only the executed
reproduction converges.

The steep chain surviving the same protocol is the part I want the founder to see.
Its captures happened at eight femtometers of aim error — the funnel reached seven
diameters sideways and took the incident anyway. The assembly story was never
rescued by good aim; it was carried the whole time by capture physics strong enough
to forgive bad aim. That is a better sentence for the packet than the shell ever was,
and it is now an honest one.
