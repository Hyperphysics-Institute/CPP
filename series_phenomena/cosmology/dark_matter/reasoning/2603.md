# Reasoning — Patch 2603 (closing a thread by running their code)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The kindest and most rigorous response to a suspect number is to execute its alleged
source, and thirty seconds of doing so settled two rounds of ambiguity: the reported
r_eq values are not outputs of the supplied minimizer, and the minimizer itself steps
too coarsely to find a 0.1-fm well. No presumption about the seat's process is needed
or made -- the record states what runs and what doesn't. Closing without a third round
is economy, not judgment: the contract is satisfied elsewhere, and a replication
programme that keeps polling until every seat agrees has forgotten what replication is
for. Two clean legs and one persistent discrepancy is itself a finding about the
ecosystem of instruments we work within, and it is banked as exactly that.
