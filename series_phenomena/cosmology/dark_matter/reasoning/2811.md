# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2811 (the examination sequence; momentum found to be the missing organ)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-25/26.**

## Taking the founder's objection seriously enough to test it

He argued that with enough real degrees of freedom the system cannot
lock in, and that my periodicity finding might be an artifact of a
fast ZBW cycle observed at low resolution. Both halves deserved a
test rather than a rebuttal. The resolution half was PARTLY RIGHT and
cost me a correction: at proper time resolution the attractor is
period-4, not period-2 — my earlier claim was itself aliased. The
degrees-of-freedom half was answered by the MSD measurement: centers
pinned at alpha = 0.14. Degrees of freedom cannot randomize a system
whose constituents cannot move; the collisions his mechanism relies
on were never occurring because nothing was travelling.

## Why the diagnosis is momentum rather than complexity

The chain is short and hard to escape: kinetic trajectories require
carried motion; carried motion in this doctrine lives in the Sea's arc
configuration (C23); the arc configuration has no representation in a
state vector of positions plus a scalar charge field, because bits
carry only charge, polarity, and origin (C21). So the model as built
CANNOT produce the mechanism, and the founder's physics was never on
trial. Naming that precisely mattered more than any further parameter
scan.

## The proxy result, and the discipline around it

Given momentum by hand, everything he predicted appeared at once:
transport (alpha 0.14 -> 0.76), collisions (797), the end of lock-in
(smooth autocorrelation decay, no alternation at any lag), and — the
result I did not expect to land this cleanly — Maxwell-Boltzmann speed
statistics, chi2 = 14.7 on 9 dof against a mean-matched MB
distribution, with the eta-scan bracketing the MB coefficient of
variation from both sides. That is a thermal bath appearing in a
deterministic automaton the moment its constituents can carry
momentum.

And the artifact he warned about is real: linear heating, because the
proxy takes momentum from a field that never pays for it. The honest
consequence is stated in the record rather than buried: this proxy can
demonstrate that momentum unlocks thermalization, and it CANNOT be
used to test fluctuation-dissipation balance or Gibbs statistics,
because a driven system would answer those questions falsely and
flatteringly. The founder predicted the misrepresentation before the
run; the run found it; the record refuses to spend it.

## What the next charter must decide

Whether arc inertia can be represented at hostable scale at all. The
founder's own read is that faithfulness would demand something
FEM-like, every CP carrying arc momentum for every other. If that is
right, AUTOMATON-3's honest scope may be narrower than the trio we
hoped for: Coulomb (banked), bonded ZBW Sea (banked), thermalization
mechanism (validated in proxy) — with emergent inertia and Gibbs
statistics deferred to an approach that is not a lattice automaton at
all. Stating that boundary early is worth more than discovering it
after another campaign.
