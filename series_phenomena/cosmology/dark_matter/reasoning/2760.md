# Reasoning — Patch 2760 (RV-2714 re-verification prereg freeze)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.**

The prereg's whole design principle is negative: add nothing. The
contaminated findings were produced under frozen criteria that were
themselves fine — 2713's C1–C5, 2735's functionals, the standing
spectrum estimator. What was broken was the data generator. So the
re-verification changes exactly two things (the code lineage and the
seeds) and freezes everything else by pointer to the original
preregs. Inventing new thresholds here would create a seam where a
future reader could ask whether the clean result was shaped by
criteria written after the contamination was known; re-applying the
originals verbatim forecloses that question structurally.

Two decisions worth recording. First, no fork. The B-CHECK acts
needed forks because they were discriminants — the question was
"which hypothesis." RV rows are replacement measurements — the
question is "what is the number." A fork on a replacement measurement
would smuggle in outcome classes ("suppression survives" vs
"suppression dies") and with them the temptation to root for one;
§3.1 instead names both possibilities explicitly and privileges
neither. Second, the C3 AIC criterion re-applies AS FROZEN even
though its defect (absorbing smooth curvature against underestimated
block-SEMs) is documented. Amending it now would be a post-hoc
criterion change on the very act meant to be maximally conservative;
the diagnosis travels alongside the readout instead, exactly as the
2714 record handled it.

Seeds …798–802 are RESERVED rather than consumed at freeze — the
2754 episode showed why that distinction earns its keep: a blocking
gate that fires before the seed is touched leaves the pool intact
and the prereg re-charterable. Sweep lengths mirror 2714's RUNS table
verbatim (686-chain 600+2400, etc.), read directly from the committed
script rather than reconstructed — the numbers in §1 are the code's
numbers.

Sequencing: the execution act is deliberately NOT this patch. Five
chains at N up to 1024 are hours of chunked container compute; the
freeze-then-execute boundary also puts the prereg on GitHub before
any datum exists, which is the discipline's whole point.
