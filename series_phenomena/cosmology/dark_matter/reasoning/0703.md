## The one conceptual move that mattered

The cross-section for σ/m is the **residual** interaction between two *separate* color-neutral qDP/hTetra structures — the nuclear-force analog — NOT the confinement string tension (σ ≈ 0.9 GeV/fm from c14) that binds constituents *within* a structure. Importing the string tension would have been a category error inflating the cross-section enormously. The residual range is ~ the structure size (~r₀), used conservatively.

## Inputs and their honesty tags

- α_s = 0.3, r₀ = 0.26 fm: read from c14 (Cornell potential). Solid.
- σ_geom = π r₀² = 0.212 fm²: geometric, conservative (subquantum ⇒ smaller).
- qDP mass ~0.3 GeV, hTetra ~1.5 GeV: **estimates.** c04 gives mass = Compton standing-wave energy (mc² = ℏν_C) but NO absolute qDP/hTetra value. Used the QCD/constituent scale; took the qDP as the *light* (worst-case, largest σ/m) bracket. This is the weakest input and is flagged everywhere.

## Result

σ/m(qDP) ≈ 4×10⁻³ cm²/g (252× below the ~1 cm²/g SIDM bound); σ/m(hTetra) ≈ 8×10⁻⁴ (1259× below). ~0.02 collisions/particle/Hubble time at halo density. Survives even ×100 (nucleon-like) resonant enhancement; fails only under ×1000 strong near-threshold resonance in the light qDP channel. **No kill.**

## Why this is not over-claimed

Two real gaps remain and are written into the result, not hidden: (1) the constituent mass is estimated, and σ/m ∝ 1/m, so a much lighter qDP would erode the margin; (2) a near-threshold resonance (large scattering length) is the only thing that could lift σ/m by the needed ~10³, and the residual color potential between neutral structures is not yet computed. The headline (robust survival to ×100) holds regardless; these two define the Gate-1 closure deliverable.
