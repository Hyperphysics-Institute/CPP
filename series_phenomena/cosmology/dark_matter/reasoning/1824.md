## The reframing (Thomas)

1823 left v_thr bracketed by the coat-thickness ratio δ/d, with my central guess δ/d ~ 0.5 giving v_thr ~ 2500
km/s — and a tension at typical clusters. Thomas corrected the meaning of δ: it is **not a fixed coat
thickness** (which is itself context-dependent on the applied force), but the **crossover distance at which the
two cores' E_qq color attraction equals the eDP-coat E_ee repulsion**. Past that distance, "the mutual 8qCP
color force attraction … will have exceeded the repulsive resistance … and E_qq binding will proceed
uninhibited." So δ* is set by a **force balance**, and it is computable rather than guessed.

## Computing δ*

Color is **confined**: E_qq(r) ~ α_s ℏc/r inside the color range R_color, screened to ~0 beyond. At contact
r = d, E_qq = α_s ℏc/d = **66 MeV** — which **overwhelms E_ee = 0.9 MeV by ~73×.** So inside the color range the
attraction dominates the repulsion overwhelmingly; outside it, color is off. The crossover δ* therefore sits at
the **color-range edge**: the distance where confined color switches on and instantly beats E_ee. And the color
range is set by the color-bond length in the rod itself (E_qq = α_s ℏc/d), so **R_color ~ d**.

Hence **δ*/d ~ R_color/d ~ 0.8–1.0** (R_color ~ 1 fm, d ~ 1.0–1.3 fm) — the natural fm/element scale, **not the
0.5 I had guessed.** This is a *pinning* by a physical argument (the confined-color range), not a tuning.

## The consequence — v_thr at the low end, tension relieved

With δ*/d ~ 0.8–1.0 in the 1822 threshold v_thr = 2 E_ee/(v_sound·(δ*/d)·m_element):

  **v_thr ≈ 1500–2200 km/s (central ~1770)** — the **low end** of the 1822 band.

So **typical clusters (~2000 km/s) now sit *above* threshold and are suppressed** (p_fuse(2000, 1770) ≈ 0.47),
where with v_thr ~ 2500 they were below it. The σ/m at typical-cluster velocity:
- **N ~ 10** (σ/m₀ ~ 1.1): cluster ~ 0.65 (flex) – 0.84 (rigid) — **comfortably within** the cluster bound (≲1);
- **N ~ 15** (σ/m₀ ~ 1.6): cluster ~ 0.98 – 1.27 — **OK (flex) to marginal (rigid)**;
- **N ~ 20** (σ/m₀ ~ 2.2): cluster ~ 1.30 – 1.69 — **still mildly over** the bound.

So the tension is **relieved for N ≲ 15**, and the residual N ~ 20 over-shoot is now small enough to be closed by
the two effects not yet in the curve: the **velocity-distribution convolution** (the high-v tail of a 2000 km/s
cluster reaches well past 1770, adding suppression) and **multiple-fusion compounding** (deepening the fall at
the high-collision-rate cluster end). With the force-balance v_thr and these, the candidate looks viable across
the plausible N range, with the threshold shape intact.

## Honest caveats

- The pinning rests on **R_color ~ d** — the confined-color range equalling the rod's own color-bond length.
  This is physically natural (both are the α_s ℏc/E_qq scale) but is the key O(1) input; if R_color were
  notably sub-d, v_thr would rise back toward the tension.
- The crossover uses the confined-color picture (E_qq overwhelming E_ee inside the range, off outside); a soft
  (rather than sharp) color turn-on would smear δ* but not move its scale.
- N ~ 20 remains mildly over the cluster bound pending the convolution and compounding — not yet closed.
- Everything downstream (the curve depth, the per-fusion drop 2–8) carries its prior bracket; this patch moves
  only v_thr (to the low end), which was the dominant lever on the typical-cluster tension.
