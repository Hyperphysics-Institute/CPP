# Reasoning fragment — Patch 3162 (CONV-024 returns adjudication)

**Registered Patch 3162 (K-MEM Route C / CONV-024).** Full reasoning:

Q1 and Q4 are unanimous and need no adjudication. Q2, Q3 and Q7 carry

UNDETERMINED-BY-SPLIT; the CONV-021 precedent (all five naming the same

## On the sustained worker error

GPT filed it, Grok concurred independently, and it is correct. I read a scope
condition into the minority clause that its operative text does not contain — I
took the rule's *label* for its content. That is a straightforward misreading of
a frozen rule and I sustain the finding without qualification.

I record one thing about it not in mitigation but because the record should be
accurate about direction: my erroneous reading was the one that kept the
falsifier alive. Had the clause been read correctly from the start, a2 would
have been non-interpretable from the first pass and the DISP-T counterfactual
would have been weaker, not stronger. So this was not motivated reasoning toward
a preferred outcome. It was, however, exactly the failure mode the programme
worries about most — an unstated exception imported into a frozen rule — and the
direction it happened to point is luck, not discipline.

## On why I checked Copilot's premise, and what it cost

Copilot and Grok voted opposite ways on Q7 while asserting contradictory facts
about the same artifact: whether the stored legs permit re-analysis. Two seats
cannot both be right, and the question is settleable by reading the code rather
than by weighing authority. I checked.

Copilot's premise is false — the legs store the full series `F`, and every
window is applied at analysis time. That was the whole of what I set out to
establish. But reading the window code to establish it put the S3-C statistic in
front of me, and the statistic uses a hard-coded `60:100` slice while the script
computes a per-arm `t_post` it never applies to S3-C. From there the AK
overlap — LATE = 92:104 against a sustained window of 60:100, sharing 8 of 40
points — is arithmetic, and AK is the arm with the campaign's most extreme
undershoot.

**This is post-hoc and I label it so.** I did not go looking for an
exculpatory defect; I went looking to settle a factual dispute between two
reviewers, and this was in the path. That is the honest provenance and it is
what the record says. A post-hoc finding is not thereby false — the code says
what it says, and anyone can read it — but its *provenance* is weaker than a
pre-registered one and the label has to travel with it permanently.

## On not overclaiming the finding

The temptation here is to declare the mystery solved. It is not. If the
hard-coded window explained everything, the undershoot ratios would order with
the misalignment, and they do not: a0p is the only arm whose window sits wholly
after its own `t_post` and it returns 0.35, while a0 sits 6 Moments early and
returns the best 0.84. So the defect is decisive for AK, plausible elsewhere,
and insufficient as a total account. I state that limit in the record rather
than letting the AK result carry the whole claim.

What the finding does do is reconcile the panel's 3–2 Q2 split into a single
object. WINDOW-DEFECT and BAND-TRANSPLANT-INVALID both turn out to name parts of
the same thing: the Route B S3-C recipe was carried across whole — its reference
value *and* its literal window indices — into Route C arms spanning T_END 104 to
504. The panel divided over which half of one transplant to blame.

## On the sting, and why it goes at the front

S1-C is length-adaptive where S3-C is not. So a corrected S3-C is more likely to
restore isolation arms to band than to sink them further — and if two or more
come back in band, item 1 stops firing, item 2 starts, and DISP-T fires against
Candidate (B). **My own diagnosis makes the falsifier more likely to fire.**

That belongs in the front of the record, not the footnotes, because a worker who
finds an instrument defect immediately after an instrument verdict has just
handed itself a rescue narrative, and the only defence against being read that
way — or against actually being that — is to state up front that the repair cuts
against the corpus. GPT independently made this the condition of legitimacy: the
repair must be able to kill Candidate (B) as readily as save it. Adopted as
binding.

## On not re-tallying Q7

One of the three REPAIR-AND-RERUN votes rests on a premise I have just refuted.
It is tempting to discount it and call Q7 3–2 the other way. I decline.

A worker who adjusts panel ballots on the basis of its own after-the-fact
findings has made the panel advisory. The whole value of a frozen round is that
the returns are what they are. So the vote stands as cast, the refutation is
recorded beside it, and the discrepancy goes to the founder and to any successor
round to resolve — not to me. This is the same principle as CONV-022's handling
of the frozen-rule-versus-panel-adjudication distinction: what a rule permitted
the worker to declare, and what the panel then decides, are different questions
and must stay separate.

## On what is not done here

No repair. No re-analysis. No successor prereg. The corrected statistic must be
frozen before the legs are re-read, or the re-read is a retune wearing a repair's
clothes — and the legs are already sitting on disk with their answer in them,
which is precisely the condition under which freezing first matters most.
