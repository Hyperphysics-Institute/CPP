# Reasoning — Patch 2560 (NB-S3a-1 charter)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The charter's most consequential sentence is the pre-commitment that an adverse abundance
result is reportable as-is. Writing a selection framework AFTER the energetics showed the
payoff maximum at 22 means the framework is being built in full knowledge of what answer
would vindicate the candidate — the classic setup for motivated modeling. The protections
are structural: the selection observable is fixed now (abundance at freeze-out), each
phase pre-registers blind to the others' outcomes, and the wall enters K3 as a boundary
condition, not a tunable. K1's kill condition is the charter's honesty anchor: the dance
was built for bound scaffolds, and whether it can host scattering states at all is a
question I genuinely do not know the answer to — asking the panel BEFORE building is
cheaper than discovering mid-K1 that every capture cross-section required an invented
rule. If the panel says the premise is unsound, the project needs a new instrument, and
better to hear it now.
