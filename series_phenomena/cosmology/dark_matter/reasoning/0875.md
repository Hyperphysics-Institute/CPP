# 0875 — ρ_spine/ρ_Sea: the ratio breaks, the corona question is mis-posed, the corona is retired

## What the computation showed

Computed literally, **ρ_spine/ρ_Sea is never ~1.** ρ_spine ~ m_unit/r_c³ ~ 4.7×10¹⁴ g/cm³ (nuclear,
matches the EoS). The vacuum DP-Sea lattice spacing is fixed at **ℓ_P (Planck)** [SF-2; EU-1], ~10²⁰× finer
than the spine's r_c ~ 1 fm spacing. So ρ_spine/ρ_Sea is **10⁴³** (vs the balanced vacuum's gravitating
ρ_Λ) or **10⁻⁷⁹** (vs the Planck-dense substrate) — never order-unity. The "≲ 1.15" bar implicitly assumed
a dilute, fm-scale, **accreting** eDP reservoir comparable to the spine. **No such reservoir exists.** That
is the tell: the corona question, as posed in 0873/0874, was mis-framed.

## The correct test (energy-scale), and the retraction it forces

A σ/m-diluting coat needs real eDP **mass** bound at the surface — which needs **(a)** a well deep enough to
bind a real eDP and **(b)** a reservoir to fill it. Both fail:

- **(a)** V₀_elec ~ 34–94 keV (0874) is **~1500× shallower** than the eDP creation energy E_eDP = 88 MeV.
  A well far below the particle's rest energy cannot create-and-bind a real quantum (creating the eDP costs
  88 MeV; the well returns ~0.05 MeV). It only **polarizes the balanced vacuum** → vacuum-polarization
  **self-energy**, already inside the dressed m_unit, scaling per-unit with N — **not** a surface coat.
- **(b)** the only dense reservoir is the vacuum Sea, which is **balanced** (ground state, no net mass). A
  hypothetical real-eDP halo gas is ultra-dilute (n·r_c³ ~ 10⁻³⁸ → coat/core ~ 10⁻³⁷).

**This overturns the 0873/0874 V_surf-vs-kT framing**, which computed the well depth and compared it to kT
without checking that a reservoir of *bindable real eDPs* exists. It does not. The Boltzmann excess
Δn = n_bulk(e^{V/kT} − 1) is negligible because the relevant n_bulk (real, bindable eDPs at the surface) is
either the balanced vacuum (self-energy, in m_unit) or the ultra-dilute halo gas (~10⁻³⁸).

## Result

**The corona does not preferentially dilute the extended aggregate.** The electric-vdW well is real but
**unfilled**. σ/m (the d_f physics) survives undiluted. **Corona risk RETIRED.**

**Consistency check:** the same argument means a **monomer** (the DM-1 baseline) also carries no real-eDP
coat — m_unit is the dressed mass DM-1 already uses, so there is **no correction to DM-1**. The reframing is
internally consistent with the shipped program (and explains why DM-1 never needed a corona term).

3. **Foundational claim.** Rests on the standard result that a sub-threshold well produces only virtual
   polarization (self-energy), not real bound quanta — solid physics, but should be checked against CPP's
   specific substrate dynamics rather than assumed.
