# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2950: ξ₂ derivation arc pre-registration

**Task provenance.** 2946 handover §4 queue position (3); the arc was registered in
`future_projects.md` at 2946 with two binding requirements (anti-α1 bar on any ξ₂ ≪ 1; CONV-011
timing freeze in the prereg), both discharged here.

**Design decisions and why.**

1. **Definition inherited, not invented.** ξ₂ already has a frozen operational meaning: it is the
   coefficient the 2942 conversion prereg parameterized when it froze
   d_DP ≤ (ℏc/E_lim)·ξ₂^(−1/2). §1 writes the dispersion form that reproduces that conversion and
   claims nothing more. Minting a fresh definition would create a seam between the arc and the
   ACTIVE ceiling it exists to sharpen.

2. **The sign-handling freeze is the document's most consequential clause.** 2942 §3's weaker-sign
   rule was premised on an underived sign. Once this arc derives sign(ξ₂), the natural successor
   binds at the derived-sign limit — which may be the STRONGER published limit. That choice must
   be frozen while the sign is unknown, or the successor patch makes a verdict-relevant selection
   after seeing the answer (exactly the discretion CONV-011 removed for review timing, applied
   here to a conversion rule). §4 CASE-O1 freezes it sign-blind, and §5 notes that contesting the
   rule later means contesting a prereg frozen in ignorance — the strongest position a frozen rule
   can occupy.

3. **CASE-ZERO treated as reopener-class, not as a curiosity.** If quadratic order is also
   protected, the 2945 ceiling constrains a vanishing coefficient — the exact no-information logic
   that made birefringence bounds inadmissible at 2942 §4, now aimed at the corpus's own ACTIVE
   constraint. The honest frozen response is pre-action review and expected voiding with trail
   preserved (the 2943→2945 mechanism proved the corpus can void-and-preserve without losing the
   number's history). The band also registers the positive framing: an exact quadratic protection
   would be a structural theorem candidate, not merely a lost bound.

4. **S2 blocks both overreach directions.** The 2940 CASE-Q was symmetry-only; its success invites
   the assumption that the same machinery yields the quadratic VALUE. It does not — even-rank
   invariants survive I_h, so ξ₂'s value is dynamics. Stating this in the prereg prevents a
   symmetry-dressed guess from being passed off as a derivation, and symmetrically bars the
   "lattices are O(1)" folk-normalization.

5. **CASE-DEP names its expected missing object now.** If the relay's registered structure
   underdetermines ξ₂, the missing piece is almost certainly the dynamical stiffness
   normalization — plausibly the same object FP-6 Stage A/B must derive. Registering that
   expectation pre-derivation (a) prevents it from being invented post-hoc as an excuse, and
   (b) makes the FP-6 cross-citation the natural unblock path, keeping the two arcs separate but
   mutually informative per the 2946/2947 no-motive separation.

6. **S1 is derived-with-condition, not asserted.** The scalar character of ξ₂ at quadratic order
   follows from I_h admitting no invariant rank-2 anisotropy — but only under the realized-state
   I_h premise, so the statement carries the R1-expanded condition explicitly, as every product of
   this arc carries the full 2945 stack (§6).

**What would have been wrong.** Computing or estimating ξ₂ in this patch (prereg-first ordering is
the arc's reason for existing separately); leaving the derived-sign handling to the successor
patch (post-revelation discretion); folding the derivation into the FP-6 arc (motive contamination
both ways); treating CASE-ZERO as a quiet footnote on an ACTIVE constraint; asserting the scalar
character unconditionally.
