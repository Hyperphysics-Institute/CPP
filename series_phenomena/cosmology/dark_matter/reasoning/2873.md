# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2873 (PR7 clause 2 evidentiary dispatch)

**STATUS: contemporaneous, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-29.**

The item had been named first priority four times and skipped four
times, and the handover was explicit about why: it needs Thomas to paste
a block, so it is the one thing on the list I cannot finish by myself.
Everything I would otherwise have picked, I could. That is the whole
mechanism and naming it does not defuse it, so I wrote this before
touching anything else in the queue, including the items that are more
interesting.

Two things came out of the reading that I did not expect.

The first is that the handover's own restatement of question (a) is
wrong, and wrong in the direction that would have wasted the round. It
asks whether clause 2 admits "an empirical bound on τ_Sea (e.g. via
measured inertia)." But 2864 §5 is precisely the patch where that
framing died. At 2863 the worker proposed the absence of observed
inertial lag as an upper bound; the founder's reframing inverted it —
the lag is not something inertia limits, the lag is what makes inertial
mass nonzero at all, so m_inertial = f(τ_Sea) and f is invertible. The
chartered question is whether m_inertial is admissible as a calibration
input with m_e's standing. That is a different question with a different
likely answer: K4's "independently of the measured ambient velocity"
language plausibly kills an empirical bound and plausibly permits a
calibration input. Had I put the handover's version to the panel I would
have got back a refusal that never reached the claim, and I would have
recorded it as a ruling on the claim. Fifth gloss failure of the arc,
first one caught before it left the building. The 2865 standing order is
the only reason — I went to the source to find out what the question was,
rather than to check a question I already had.

The second is a tension I did not go looking for. 2838 argues the first
order cancels by gauge structure: Coulomb-gauge scalar potential exactly
instantaneous, retardation confined to the vector potential, so the
field-eliminated force correction starts at Darwin order. The founder
ruled at 2863 that ε_mem is first order, and the transit-asymmetry
mechanism at 2865–2867 is an asymmetry — front arcs opposing, rear arcs
assisting — which is a surviving first-order effect. Read flat, those
contradict.

I think they probably do not contradict, and the reason is that they are
about different objects. ε_mem as the sketch defines it is τ_Sea/τ_slow,
a ratio of times, and it is first order in v/c by construction because
τ_slow ≃ d_DP/v — the v is in the denominator of the slow time, so
linearity is not a physical finding about the correction, it is
arithmetic on the definition. δ_mem as K4 defines it is |F_mem|/|F_inst|,
a ratio of forces. A kernel can be wide — finite ε_mem — while its first
moment vanishes by symmetry, so the force correction it induces starts at
second order. Width and first moment are independent. If that is the
resolution then both positions survive intact and the bar sits on δ_mem.

I did not put that in the dispatch as my answer, and the reason matters.
It is the fourth or fifth time in this arc that a clean reconciliation
has arrived within a turn of my noticing the problem, and three of the
earlier ones pointed at k = 1 and none is banked. The pattern-warning in
the handover says the correct response to a fifth favourable convergence
is the same as to the first, and this one is favourable twice over — it
rescues the founder's ruling AND keeps 2838's suppression. So it goes in
as question (iii) of Q3, phrased as a candidate reconciliation for the
panel to accept or reject, and I state plainly that 2838's own §3 says
the transverse leg is untested and that a different transverse weight
could restore an O(v/c) term.

The thing I nearly did and did not do: I nearly asserted in §3 that the
0.15 bar has been misapplied a third time — 2861 moved it from v/c to
ε_mem, and K4 attaches it to δ_mem, so on its face 2861's correction was
itself incomplete. But I cannot tell from the corpus whether the record
intends ε_mem and δ_mem as the same object under two names with k ≡
C_mem, in which case nothing is misapplied and I would be manufacturing
a third correction out of notation. Two patches ago the worker was wrong
twice in three tries about a single file by making exactly that kind of
confident call. So it is a question, not a finding, and Q3(ii) says only
that I read K4 one way and 2864 §3 says another.

Q4 is mine and is marked as mine. 2838 is conditional on a transverse
sector that no committed artifact tests, a preregistration for that test
already exists, and nobody has said whether the cancellation may enter
the ledger conditionally or must wait. That is a governance question with
a real fork in it and it was not in the 2864 charter, so it is labelled
as added rather than smuggled in among the three.

Withheld key is the 2868 dt/vf ladders and the sign. It is the right
choice because the script is committed, the path is nameable, the numbers
are unambiguous to four figures, and the sign is the one result in this
whole sub-thread that held up under four independent parameter axes and
that I would defend. Expected values stay out of the dispatch.
