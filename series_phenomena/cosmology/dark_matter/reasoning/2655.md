# Reasoning — Patch 2655 (the session the charter was for, and the derivation split into a theorem, a mechanism, and a model that is not allowed to be tuned)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The first decision of the session was where T1's reproduction actually lives.
It would have been easy to present the integral law as a result the continuum
derivation ACHIEVES; the honest statement is that it is a result the
transfer-channel form GUARANTEES — any sink written as a pure booking channel
has the ledger identity as its first integral, discrete or continuous, at any
gamma. Writing FB-T1 that way costs the derivation a trophy and buys it the
right shape: the charter already said T1 is the entry condition, and the
theorem section now says WHY it could never have been the test.

The mechanism section is where the session's real risk was taken. Two channels
compete for the boundary: a timing channel linear in omega and an anharmonic
channel quadratic in omega. My scratch estimates of their coefficients moved
by factors of four as I refined the force and velocity scales — which is
exactly why none of those estimates appear in the artifact as claims. The
disciplined move was to demote the hand arithmetic to mechanism structure
(which channels exist, their omega- and dt-orders, the window-sum cancellation
that protects the soft width) and delegate every coefficient to a reduced
model whose spec is frozen before it runs. The alternative — publishing a
hand-tuned Xi with coefficients chosen after seeing 0.41/7.2/11.6 — is
precisely what the panel's Tier-2 amendment was written to kill.

The window-sum cancellation deserves its own sentence because it was the one
piece of structure I did not expect to find. The soft width's 0.41% always
looked TOO clean for a phase-sampled schedule; the resolution is that the W-A
sum spans whole periods of the 2-omega oscillation, so a common phase offset
cancels at first order and the convergent regime is quadratically protected.
That is a derived cancellation, not a fit — and it makes a falsifiable
commitment: the convergent-side final-inc should fall FASTER than linearly in
dt (H3 probes this), while the saturated side should fall back toward
convergence as Xi ~ dt shrinks (H2 probes this). If the steep width stays
flat at {1/400, 1/800}, my account is wrong in a way the record will state.

The holdout set was chosen to be kill-generous in three independent
directions: H1 sits exactly on the boundary the derivation claims to locate;
H2 tests the saturation's dt-dependence against the fundamental-non-
convergence alternative; H3 tests the cancellation order on the clean side.
The w=2.5 width inherits the w=3 quarantine because a diagnostic width that
leaked into a physics claim would be the exact contamination class the
charter's FD-RC4 template exists for.

What I did not do tonight: run anything. The temptation to "just check" the
reduced model before freezing the spec was real and resisted — a checked
model is a tuned model the moment its spec can still move.
