# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2815 (PR4 analytic scoping)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-26.**

## Two failed hypotheses, reported first

I went into this pass expecting to prove non-injectivity: a
contracting field, a finite state space, attractors of measure zero,
therefore no full-support Gibbs measure. Both legs failed their own
tests — the relay kernel has non-decaying zone-boundary modes and the
field demonstrably does NOT forget (two runs with different initial
fields diverged rather than merged), and the state space is not
finite because the field is real-valued. Had I written the scoping
document from the plan rather than the data, it would have contained
two confident false theorems. They are recorded as failed because
that is the whole value of testing one's own argument.

## The result that survived is the stronger one anyway

The energy measurement needs no structural machinery: H swings 19
units along the trajectory, so nothing is conserved, so there is no
function with respect to which a marginal could be "Gibbsian." PR4's
question is not hard for the bare rule — it is ill-posed for it. That
converts AUTOMATON-1's empirical NOT-GIBBS from "we looked and did not
find" into "there was nothing to find," which is a materially
stronger and more useful statement for the panel.

## The consequence I must state and must not act on

If PR4 cannot be met by any route until C23/C24 are specified
quantitatively, then a frozen promotion requirement is currently
unmeetable, and the release chain runs through it. That is exactly the
kind of finding a worker must surface and exactly the kind of
disposition a worker must not choose: amending a frozen promotion
criterion after discovering it blocks the founder's prime goal is the
retargeting sin at maximum stakes. So §4 lays out three dispositions,
marks my preference as a recommendation, and hands the motion to the
panel. The DM-2 precedent (papers may carry an open condition
honestly; only a KILL blocks) is cited because it is the strongest
argument for option 2 and the panel should have it whether or not
they agree with me.

## What I did not do

I did not attempt the full analytic write-up. The scoping question
was tractability, the answer is yes, and the write-up belongs after
the panel rules on which disposition it is serving — otherwise I
would be building the artifact for my own preferred outcome before
anyone has approved it.
