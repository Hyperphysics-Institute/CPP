I went to compute evasion (a) — the two-temperature Sea, the equilibration time of the
cold gapless mode against a dilute hot component — and found before writing a line of
that computation that it cannot matter. The question "what carries T_amb" presupposes
the activation events are energetically free once the Boltzmann factor is paid; they are
not. Each event that relaxes the occupancy field is a real excitation with τ ≥ ħ/E, and
the standing energy density that sustains a knee ħΓ per cell is Γ·τ·E/V ≥ knee/R_s³ —
the event energy cancels out of its own floor. That cancellation is the whole patch: the
closure surface is not about temperature at all. It is about the knee. Any activated
mechanism that puts knee_tot in the PRW window today carries ×10³⁴ over closure as a
matter of arithmetic, and the R-III history carries ×10¹²–10²³ over the radiation density
of its own era. The T⁴ bound I registered yesterday at 2330 was the weak face; I am glad
it was registered rather than absorbed, because the strong face was underneath it.

So evasion (a) closes not by a hard equilibration computation but by irrelevance — the
floor rides the events, not the mode occupation — and the entire corner becomes binary on
(b): do activated Sea fluctuations fall on the quiescent (zeroed) or dynamical (sourcing)
side of the one-ledger split? I want to record why I did NOT try to answer that here,
because the temptation was real: the G1–G3 referential-zeroing machinery is in this
repository, the question is well-posed within it, and a plausible argument sketches itself
in either direction (floor-duration events resemble the virtual fluctuations the zeroing
annihilates; but they have observable consequences — the knee — which is the usual
criterion for "real"). A plausible argument in either direction is exactly the signature
of a question that needs a derivation, not a session-end paragraph. TAMB-1(b) is named,
scoped as Gate-1's own apparatus asked one more question, and left standing. The symmetric
consequence is on the record so the panel cannot read this patch as tilted: if (b) holds,
every closure face evaporates at once, including the F7 tension.

One structural observation for the record: the survive condition's three clauses now have
strikingly different characters — X4′ is a two-unknown corner (empirical, priors), R-III
genericity is derived, TAMB-1(b) is a pure internal-consistency derivation. The candidate
has been driven to the point where its fate rests on the theory's own gravity sector
answering a question it must answer anyway. That is the campaign working as designed.
