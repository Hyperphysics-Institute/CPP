# Reasoning fragment — Patch 3199 (Phase 1B verdict + hardware refutation)

Two places this patch had to work against its own interest.

1. THE CONFIRMED PREDICTION. The 1B prereg pre-declared BETA-LINEAR and
   BETA-LINEAR returned. The temptation is to bank that as a hit. It
   isn't one: the declaration was made AFTER seeing Phase 1's
   through-origin fit pass across all four rungs, so it was an informed
   update restating what the data already favoured, not an independent
   test. The honest ledger entry for the beta-ladder is one blind
   prediction made, one blind prediction FALSIFIED, one informed update
   confirmed. Written into the record's own section (§4) rather than a
   footnote, because a programme whose credibility rests on
   pre-registration must not let informed updates masquerade as hits.

2. THE WIDTH OF THE INTERVAL. Ratio CI [3.137, 18.883] excludes 1.0
   decisively but admits everything from 3.1 to 18.9. "Scales with drive"
   is established; "scales proportionally" is consistent but NOT pinned,
   even though the point estimate 5.839 and the through-origin fit both
   sit comfortably with proportionality. Stated in §3 so no downstream
   reader upgrades BETA-LINEAR into a proportionality claim.

Scope fence enforced as frozen: with SUST_REF void (3176), BETA-LINEAR is
an instrument property, not a physics verdict, and the 3175 coefficient
finding stays retracted rather than being revived by more data. The
hazard direction held — BETA-FLAT would have given AK's null a benign
account; BETA-LINEAR removes that escape, so this outcome does not
rescue Candidate (B).

HARDWARE. H-CORETEMP is refuted: the machine crashed with Core Temp
absent. My error, and the diagnosis of the error matters more than the
hypothesis — it explained the missing evidence too neatly and I let that
elegance substitute for support. What replaces it is better evidence than
the hypothesis ever had: file mtimes put the last leg at 10:11:24 and the
crash at 11:41:28, so the machine died AT IDLE, 90 minutes after ~75 h at
full load. The fault is load-independent, which eliminates the thermal
and current-draw families outright. The founder's USB-dongle observation
then supplies a second symptom on a different subsystem that also traces
to the +5V rails; one upstream power fault explaining both beats two
coincident faults, and that re-ranks PSU/rail health above the PWR_SW
header. The BMC log remains unread and is now the single highest-value
diagnostic in the queue.

DRIVER DEFECT FIXED: run_1b printed CAMPAIGN COMPLETE unconditionally, so
a fully-skipped post-crash relaunch announced success without evidence.
Completeness had to be verified by hand. Now counts files, as Phase 1's
run_all always did. My omission at 3180.
