# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2858 (OPEN-SEA-DENSITY-1; 2855 provisional)

**STATUS: contemporaneous, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-28.**

The founder ruled (b) and said plainly that he does not know the DP
density. My first instinct was that this weakens 2855, and it does, but
the more useful finding came from checking where the numbers I used
actually came from. They came from the alpha1 arc, where kappa_D =
kappa = 2/d_DP was IMPOSED, and that imposition DEFINES theta. The
alpha1 record says so in its own words and marks theta CHI-INCOMPLETE,
"question with the founder."

That referral was never made. The parameters then travelled through
KINETIC-1, S4-X, the Darwin restoration and into my 2855 verdict as
though the question had been closed. So the founder's "I don't know"
today is not new information overturning a settled value; it is the
answer to a question the programme asked itself in July and then
forgot it had asked.

This is the part I need to be hard about. 2855 section 7 said "every
input is a committed number with a cited patch," and offered that as
the guard against overclaiming. It was true. It was also the wrong
test. Committed is not ratified. A number can have a spotless
provenance chain leading back to a decision nobody ever made, and mine
did. The whole force of the 2855 verdict came from eta = 1.1225, and
eta is exactly the quantity the unasked question controls. I built a
careful self-check around the risk of choosing a flattering value and
never checked whether the values I inherited were load-bearing on an
open flag. One grep on the alpha1 record would have shown it.

So the guard gets extended: for a load-bearing input, check that its
own registration carries no open marker, not merely that it is
committed somewhere.

On what survives: the saturation mechanism, the 1/sqrt(2) ceiling and
the regulator-sensitivity of L_field are all functions of eta and are
untouched. I want that separation kept clean, because the temptation
now runs the other way -- having been corrected, to withdraw more than
was actually falsified. The physics is fine. Its application to a Sea
whose density nobody has measured is what is suspended.

One thing I am deliberately NOT doing: the founder named Schroedinger
dispersion and the de Broglie wavelength as pointing to larger DP
separation, which is the direction that would REVIVE route 2 and hand
me back a result. Both scales sit orders of magnitude above 0.364 fm.
It would be easy, and flattering, to run that conversion now and
announce that 1B may close after all. That is the control-parameter to
value conversion the register has caught three times, wearing new
clothes. The founder said triangulate, not guess, and he said it about
exactly this. So section 4 states the direction of the tension and
claims no number, and the bar including the RMS-versus-median choice
has to be frozen before anyone computes eta, not after.
