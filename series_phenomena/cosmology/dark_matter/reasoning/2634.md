# Reasoning — Patch 2634 (three invariance questions, priced honestly before the machines run)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The three items get three different verdict shapes because they carry three
different stakes. Item 2 guards a registry condition (C7), so its NOT-INVARIANT
branch does not quietly re-register anything -- it records and triggers founder
disclosure, because a condition that moved under a classifier correction is
exactly the kind of news the panel process exists to hear. Item 3 guards a null,
and the interesting design point is the structural-equality census: if the old
Delta-z rule coincides with plane membership on flat fragments by construction,
the cheapest honest discharge is a proof of bitwise identity, not a re-run
pretending to be independent. Item 4 guards nothing yet -- the statistics were
never characterized -- so its job is to create the registered baseline both
classifiers share, with the factor-2 band chosen wide because a first
characterization should not manufacture a crisis out of ordinary sensitivity.

The binding controls are non-negotiable in all three, and they differ by item on
purpose: item 2's is a MUST-DIFFER check (75 percent reclassification cannot leave
ring energies fixed), item 3's is a MAY-BE-IDENTICAL census (identity there is a
finding, not a failure), and item 4's is a convention-pin on the instrumented
wrapper. Same lesson from 2574(c), three correct local forms.
