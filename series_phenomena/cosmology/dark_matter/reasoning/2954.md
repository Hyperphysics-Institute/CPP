# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2954: SF-6 ZDC → ZBW-chain rewrite (v1.0.1, terminology only)

**Task provenance.** Executes the founder ruling registered at Patch 2953 §2: the ZDC acronym is
retired from SF-6 and is not entered in the glossary.

**Execution decisions.**

1. **Not a blind substitution.** The ruling was conditional on synonymy; the 2953 determination
   found ZDC names a chain of ZBW oscillators, not the oscillation. So the rewrite replaces the
   ACRONYM with ZBW-rooted "ZBW chain" language, preserving the chain concept everywhere the
   physics uses it (a "traveling ZBW-chain pattern" is still a chain, as the photon model
   requires). A literal ZDC→ZBW swap would have made SF-6 say the photon is a single dipole's
   oscillation — a physics corruption committed in the name of terminology hygiene.
2. **Mechanics kept auditable and minimal.** One macro rename (\ZDC → \ZBWC, 39 sites, rendering
   "ZBW-chain") carries the bulk; five spelled-out prose sites hand-edited (abstract, keywords,
   §2 intro, the Definition retitle, the §2 heading); two internal equation labels renamed with
   their \eqref partners (verified paired; no rendered change); one intentional explanatory
   comment retains the string "ZDC" to mark the retirement at the macro definition. No equation,
   tier label, open-problem registration, or claim was touched — this is v1.0.1, terminology
   only, and the changelog entry says exactly that.
3. **Version discipline.** Canonical filename unchanged (no suffix, per the OS rule); title-block
   version bumped 1.0 → 1.0.1; history recorded in documentation_suite/changelog-sf-6.md per the
   header pointer. The doc-suite glossary's four ZDC entries reworded with the retirement noted
   so a reader of either artifact learns the same history. Recompile is founder-side mechanical;
   flagged in the changelog as pending.
4. **Scope restraint.** Other corpus files mention ZDC historically (session records, shipped
   review packets); those are records of what was said at the time and are NOT rewritten —
   retroactive terminology edits to historical records would be anti-erasure violations. The
   retirement governs SF-6 (the shipped flagship the founder named) and forward usage.

**What would have been wrong.** Global corpus-wide ZDC erasure (history falsification); the
literal swap (physics corruption); leaving the doc-suite glossary stale (the drift would
regrow from it); bumping to v1.1 (reserved for content changes).
