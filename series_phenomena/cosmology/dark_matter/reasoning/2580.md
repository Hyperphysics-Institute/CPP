# Reasoning — Patch 2580 (the question dissolves, the expectation becomes an experiment)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The founder answered my either/or with neither -- the fork's premise was wrong, there
is no two-body relationship for phases to cohere or decohere ACROSS -- and the right
move was to recognize that as the adjudication rather than press for a token in my
taxonomy. His melee paragraph is the most mechanism-dense thing he has said in this
arc (the 6+6 relationship census, the tertiary/quaternary layers feeding the Sea SSV
per cycle), and the capture preserves it verbatim because future N-phase preregs will
mine it.

The design decision I care most about: NUCLEUS-1's equal-status readings, pre-committed
AT CHARTER LEVEL before any prereg exists. "There may be some resonance that ends up
producing a multi-body stability, which is what I expect" is a founder expectation --
and the single most valuable thing this programme does with founder expectations is
make them falsifiable fast. The banked O1a engine can test it within a patch or two:
free every CP's home, run the melee, see if the plane holds. If it holds, his picture
gets direct dynamical support and the arc's three negatives retroactively become the
clearing of the ground. If every rung disperses, his expectation takes a recorded hit
and we will say so in the same font. Writing both outcomes down TODAY, while everyone
expects the first, is what makes the eventual reading worth something.

I labeled the retrodiction carefully. Five convergent lines make agreement honest, and
"a collective substrate should show no pairwise core, and none was found" is a real
consistency -- but it was not pre-registered as a prediction, and inflating
retrodictions into predictions is precisely the epistemic sin the swarm methodology
exists to avoid. Earned strength, no higher.

Retiring the 2571 maps outright (not suspending further) took a moment of letting go
-- they were careful work. But their question ("can two bodies capture") is no longer
the campaign's question, and keeping superseded instruments half-alive is how a
registry grows barnacles.
