# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2870 (a failed test)

**STATUS: contemporaneous, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-29.**

I designed a test, ran it, and it did not work. Writing that down first
because everything else in this patch is downstream of it and because
the temptation was to lead with mu_crit = 1067.7 and bury the sweep.

The sweep is garbage. |v| reaches 10 in units where c = 1, so the
high-mu branches are unphysical before they get anywhere near the
transition I wanted to locate. And my diagnostic, v_final over v_init,
is meaningless for an oscillating signal -- ratios of -290 and +112 are
what a bad metric looks like, not what a located knife-edge looks like.
So mu_crit stays an arithmetic consequence with no numerical support,
and I am not allowed to quote the sweep in either direction.

Worse, the retraction I came in to make was itself wrong. I had reasoned
that v = mu*Fs forces Fs > 0 during coast, so 2869's claimed sign
contradiction could not exist. But v can go negative, so Fs can, and it
does -- at mu = 10 it runs from +6.7e-5 to -9.7e-5. So I was wrong at
2869 about the contradiction, and wrong again this turn about why. What
is actually there is an oscillatory coast that neither the pin's summary
nor either of my two accounts described.

His "no self-force in CPP" is the substantive item and it costs me an
argument. At 2869 section 2 I told him conservation does not preclude
runaway because a bare point's self-energy reservoir is unbounded. That
divergence is a continuum artifact. If there is no self-action, only a
declaration and other entities' responses re-entering as SSV_net, and if
the substrate is discrete, there is no integral to diverge and the
reservoir may be bounded by the lattice. So his conservation objection
recovers force I had denied it, and whether Fs in the scalar toy
corresponds to anything in CPP proper is now open rather than assumed.

The one thing I found and am stating narrowly: the coast fit filters to
v > 1e-4, which is exactly where the reversal happens, and at mu = 10
the coast starts at 6.7e-4, so tau = 7.87 is fitted across under one
decade. Filtering a noise floor is legitimate and the filter is visible
in the code. I have been wrong twice about this file in three patches
and I am not making a third confident claim about it.

What survives from my side is 2868 alone: F_hold flat under refinement,
linear in v, forward. That one held up under four independent parameter
axes and it is the only thing this sub-thread has produced that I would
defend.
