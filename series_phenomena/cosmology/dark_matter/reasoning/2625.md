# Reasoning — Patch 2625 (designing the repair so it can still lose)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The trap in writing a repair prereg is that you know what the disclosure bank showed
— S_cum stable, low-gamma methods in four-decimal agreement — and the temptation is
to build gates that can only confirm it. Two design choices push back. First, every
banked number re-runs fresh: the bank bought design (which windows to freeze, which
partial reading to pre-declare), not evidence. Second, RD2-prime exists and has
teeth: if the first-crossing window ALSO fails dt-convergence, the story "the window
was the problem" dies, the deposit itself stands convicted, and the escalation is
derivational work rather than a third instrument — with a founder flag, because at
that point the lane would be spending patches polishing a quantity the theory has
not yet earned.

The BANDED reading is the honest invention here, and I want its justification on
record. The 22-30 percent high-gamma method disagreement is almost certainly not a
bug: relativistic kinetic energy does not decompose additively, so "the oscillatory
share" is definition-dependent exactly where gamma is large. A discriminator that
can only say PASS or FAIL would be forced to call a structural property of
relativistic bookkeeping a failure forever. The banded outcome instead asks the one
question that matters for consumers: is the definitional spread itself STABLE under
refinement? If yes, it is not noise — it is a property, registerable, carryable as
a rider the way the aimed-convention firewall is carried. If the band wobbles, the
gate catches that too (0.05 stability tolerance) and RD2-prime takes over. Either
way the panel's ETA-1 clause stops being a vague caution and becomes a number.

G5's method-B exclusion under the band needed a sentence of defense: once a
decomposition spread is registered as a band, counting it again as "implementation
noise" in the spread ratio would double-charge the same physics. The exclusion is
disclosed in the gate itself, frozen now, so no one can accuse the execution of
inventing it when the ratio needed help.

R_in = 2D was chosen and frozen for one property: the approach crosses it
monotonically before any bound-phase chaos can touch it, so first-crossing time is
the most dt-robust event the trajectory offers. C4 exists because a window
definition that silently fails to trigger on a scattering event would poison the
ON/OFF comparisons downstream; better to gate its sanity than assume it.
