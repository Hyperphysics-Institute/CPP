# Reasoning — Patch 2548 (ENDBOND-2 pre-registration, dance route)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

Three decisions in this prereg were made specifically to protect the result from me.

First, the separated reference. Two planes at any finite gap still interact (Coulomb tails,
and worse, the dance rules would let the two planes' reach sets touch at moderate gaps); an
"almost separated" reference invites gap-shopping. Two INDEPENDENT single-plane dances are
exactly non-interacting by construction, so the reference has zero tunable content. The
20 fm finite-gap run stays as a disclosed robustness check because a mismatch between the
two would itself be diagnostic (long-range dance correlation), not because it feeds the
number.

Second, the FREF fork. The 2461 K1 principle ("one physical constant shared by the compared
scaffolds; fastest leg luminal") was written for the ring/rod comparison and genuinely
under-determines the fragment case: a local maximum honors the principle's letter at this
comparison's scope; importing the 16-plane value honors its "one physical sea" spirit.
Choosing either alone, after seeing numbers, would be exactly the convention-shopping the
2541 union rule exists to prevent — so both are in from the start and the spread is part of
the result.

Third, the normalization pre-commitment. The interface-total vs per-qq-pair ambiguity is
the single most dangerous degree of freedom in this campaign, because the two readings
differ by ×4 and the fenced band is wide enough that a motivated reader could park either
reading inside it depending on where the number lands. The 2542 downstream consumer
(ΔE_close) uses the interface total; that fixes the target, and the prereg says so before
any number exists. If the per-pair reading turns out to be the physically interesting one,
it can be a named follow-up — it cannot be a post-hoc rescue.

The G3 demotion was already made in 2547, before this document, deliberately: the prereg
merely inherits it.
