# Reasoning — Patch 2541 (OPEN-DM-RODCLOSE-1 pre-registration)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The design center of this prereg is the blindness protocol, and it needed to be stricter than 2537's
discipline because the temptation profile is worse. In 2537 the danger was a lazy pass (a baseline
that happened to land in-window); here the danger is a steered derivation — the target number is
KNOWN, it is famous within the campaign (16 planes, 11.26 GeV), and every choice in the window
computation (comparator form, band collapse, normalization convention) could be nudged toward it
without any single step looking dishonest. The protocol therefore forces the sequence: L symbolic
throughout, W(L) frozen, THEN one sentence comparing to 16. And §3's union rule (if multiple
defensible comparator forms exist, compute all, report the union with spread disclosed) removes the
one degree of freedom I judged most steerable.

The §4 entailment surprised me when it fell out: harmonic bending gives E_bend(L) = 2π²κ/L,
monotonically DECREASING in length — bending a longer rod into a ring is cheaper, not dearer, because
the per-junction angle shrinks faster than the junction count grows. That means the founder's
two-sided window (too short = too stiff, too long = too much inertia) is not merely plausible but
structurally forced under harmonic statics: the short cutoff comes from the 1/L bending cost, the
long cutoff must come from inertia because bending cannot supply it. The founder's Q5 intuition
matches the harmonic form's entailment without having been derived from it — a small convergence,
banked in the prereg where it belongs.

The 2450 normalization walk is the honest weak point and I registered it as such. The +291 stiffness
is validated lineage, but I do not currently hold its units and per-junction normalization in
context, and the difference between a per-junction and a whole-rod normalization is exactly a factor
that could silently rescale the window. Making the walk a frozen lineage task — with Branch I (not
re-derivation) as the failure mode — keeps the compute patch from quietly inventing a stiffness if
the artifacts prove ambiguous.

Scope limit: I froze "no composition reading under any outcome" because the adjacency is dangerous —
this campaign sits one step from the RELIC-1 reopening contract, and a good window result will create
pressure to read something about f_hTe's successor dial in the same breath. The plane-resident
fraction is a separate limb with its own missing inputs; the trap clause's whole lineage exists to
stop exactly that elision. The end-bond identity (2540) is what made this prereg writable at all —
before it, E_endbond was an unknown; after it, every quantity in the statics window has registered
lineage, and the campaign's honest uncertainty is confined to bands, normalizations, and the kinetic
limbs.
