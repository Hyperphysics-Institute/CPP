# Reasoning — Patch 2758 (REACH-AUDIT-2714 charter freeze)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.** Session opened on the 2757 handover;
single next act was the charter. What follows is the substantive
reasoning that shaped it, captured before the patch was cut.

Before writing anything I re-ran the genealogy grep in the fresh
clone rather than trusting the 2754 gate record's lineage list: the
`newp` increment machinery exists in exactly ten committed files, and
they are exactly the ten the gate record names (2709 clean by explicit
zeroing; 2714 origin; 2737/2740/2743/2746/2749 inherited; 2753/2754/
2755 audit instruments). That match matters for the charter's shape:
the seed set can be stated as a closed table at charter time, and the
audit's census obligation becomes an EXTENSION search (any per-move ΔE
over pair sums under other variable names) rather than a from-nothing
hunt. A charter whose seed set is already grep-complete is harder to
argue with later.

The design decision that took the most thought is §4's pre-registered
suspicion. Reading the 2756 clean-vs-legacy numbers next to the
X3-LONG/X6 headline finding, the coincidence is uncomfortable: the
defect suppresses the undriven chain's sampled fluctuations by
~20–25% at N = 80, and the standing "physics" finding is that the Sea
suppresses small-k charge fluctuations 20–26% below HNC — same sign,
same magnitude, and the finding's chains are all 2714-lineage. The
"septuply-consistent spectrum" is likewise consistency AMONG ensembles
sharing the defective path; internal agreement of contaminated
instruments is not cleanliness. The handover instructed the audit to
CONFIRM, not assume, that the headline items have no 2714-descendant
support — but an audit written after the auditor has noticed an
adverse pattern can drift toward saving the finding. The fix is the
programme's standard one: state the suspicion IN the frozen charter,
bind both directions (if the trace lands it contaminated, it reports
same-font; if the support traces clean, the suspicion dies on the
table), and let the table decide. This is the same move as freezing a
fork before the run — freezing the temptation before the trace.

Classification design: four classes, with CONTAMINATED split into
WITHDRAWN vs RERUN because the 2756 record already withdrew the S4-X
anomaly items — those owe no re-run — while any contaminated item
still load-bearing does. CLEAN requires POSITIVE genealogy (traced to
clean sources), not absence of known contamination; the SR-1 episode
(WORKFLOW sector) is the standing lesson on what "nobody checked"
costs. INDETERMINATE routes to the founder only for the load-bearing
question — whether an untraceable item may keep bearing weight is a
physics-weight call, which is a PD-006 founder boundary; everything
else in the audit is process and stays delegated.

The audit-level fork RA-1/RA-2/RA-3 exists so the audit act itself has
frozen consequences: RA-2 makes the re-verification prereg BLOCKING
before panel dispatch (otherwise the economy-rule bundle could ship an
exposure table with unresolved contaminated headline rows), and RA-3
forces same-font registry annotation rather than silent limbo. The
classes compose because a real table can contain both.

Gate v2 registration (CONV-005) does not wait for the table — the
handover ordered it registered as protocol, and there is no dependency:
the gate's value is independent of what the trace finds. It went into
`todolist.md` standing conventions alongside CONV-001..004, with the
origin story in two sentences (survived five acts; caught in five
trial moves) because that asymmetry IS the argument for making it
mandatory.

One economy note: per the Patch 2495 review-economy ruling, the audit
is verification of adverse findings — no panel round is spent on it.
Its outcome bundles into the single dispatch the 2756 record already
specified. The charter says so explicitly to prevent a later session
from "helpfully" convening a seat on the table itself.

No computation was performed at this patch (census greps are
repo-inspection, not physics); no verify script is owed. The census
script is chartered as `code/2759_reach_audit_2714.py` (or successor
number) for the execution act.
