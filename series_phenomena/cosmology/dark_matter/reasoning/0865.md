The 0860 fragmentation window is [0.8 keV, 2 MeV]. **The natural fm-scale Coulomb ceiling sits AT the window top.** This is the result that makes G2 an easy first win: it could have come out 10⁶× too big (atomic-Coulomb at sub-Planck would be ~10¹⁶ GeV) or absurdly small; instead the *effective fm-scale* ceiling lands right on the window's upper edge. At ℓ_rung = 1 fm, η ∈ [~6×10⁻⁴, 1] lands in-window — a wide, easily-satisfiable band. The window is **reachable and natural, not fine-tuned**. (Reaching the very top, 2 MeV, needs r<1 fm or near-zero screening; the bulk of the window is covered by modest screening.)
