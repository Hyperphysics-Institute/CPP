- **The stacked-block parser error (caught PRE-verdict by the battery).**
  Instrument v1 parsed the CSV naively (all numeric rows) — but HEPData
  CSV exports stack EACH dependent variable as its own `mass,<name>`
  block; the parse mixed observed-limit rows with sensitivity-band rows
  across blocks. **V-SHOT failed (9.0-GeV row ≠ screenshot) and V-ANCHOR
  failed (40-GeV ≠ the text anchor) exactly as designed** — the wrong
  parse produced σ(9.86) = 1.13×10⁻⁴⁶, which would have FAILED the N=7
  clear by only ×1.03, a hair's-width wrong-number verdict. The gates
  stopped it; the parser was fixed to read ONLY the `mass,limit` block;
  the battery then passed 11/11 with the screenshot and anchor matching.
  Lesson (standing): naive CSV parsing of HEPData exports is a named
  hazard; always block-scope to the observed column and always carry an
  independent-anchor check.
