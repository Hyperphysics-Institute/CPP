Priority-1 task: turn the two O(1) factors I flagged in 0840 (identical-boson ×2, viscosity ×2/3) into the actual SIDM cross-section. The right object is the viscosity cross-section σ_V = ∫(1-cos²θ)dσ — symmetric under θ→π-θ, which is the correct weighting for identical particles and matches what SIDM N-body work calibrates against.

The result is clean because the scattering is pure s-wave (kλ≪1): σ_V = (4/3)σ_0, the ×2 boson symmetrization times the ×2/3 viscosity weighting. They do NOT cancel — net ×4/3 — so σ_V/m ≈ 0.20 cm²/g central, slightly above the 0.15 distinguishable-total I quoted in 0840 and below the 0.30 boson-total. So "0.1-0.3 O(1) bracket" firms to "≈0.20."

So I used the correct s-wave δ_0 and the analytic factor, and did not let the buggy general-l routine into the result.
