**Task provenance.** 2946 handover §4 queue position (2), executing the 2936 CONV-001 M3 shortlist
(A5-SHOT, Muse Spark A5-2, priority 2) under the triangulation charter's per-anchor prereg rule
(charter §2/§6) and CONV-011's binding requirement that branch-dependent review timing be frozen
before any computation.

**Design decisions and why.**

1. **Structure-first ordering imported from A5-DISP.** The 2936 HIGH-STAKES lesson generalizes:
   for A5-SHOT everything turns on (Q1) whether ambient count fluctuations gravitate at all and
   (Q2) whether the substrate's statistics are Poissonian or hyperuniform. Assuming Poisson
   strengthens the floor illegitimately; assuming hyperuniform suppression weakens it
   illegitimately (the α1 pattern in mirror image). Both assumptions are barred symmetrically in
   §3 — the suppression, if claimed, must be a derived S(k → 0) with registered provenance.

2. **CASE-N is a real band, not a formality.** The corpus has no registered derivation tying
   ambient Sea counts to gravitational sourcing; in CPP the natural alternative is that only
   polarization/SSV gradients source response. A null outcome retires the anchor honestly and is
   itself a finding about what the Sea's uniform part is. Freezing it prevents the anchor from
   being kept alive by wishful reclassification.

3. **CONV-011 mapping.** All three primary bands are bound-type or null → conservative →
   combined completed-package review permitted, with the PANEL-PENDING/VOID-not-revise mechanism
   inherited from the 2941/2943→2945 precedent. The only pre-action trigger is the falsifier
   clause (contradiction with registered PR6-class screening structure or the 2940 I_h facts) —
   genuine corpus-self-contradiction stakes. This is the timing discipline A5-DISP lacked at 2937
   and had to retrofit at 2941; here it is frozen at prereg time as CONV-011 requires.

4. **η firewall.** A floor on n_DP plus the ACTIVE d_DP ceiling invites informal joint statements
   about η. The charter reserves all joint/triangulation statements to the numeric triangulation
   patch under its own frozen governance (§5: frozen statistic, no averaging, withheld key).
   The firewall in §4 makes the reservation explicit in this arc so no combination can happen as
   a side remark.

5. **L-1 restated.** The most likely contamination path for this anchor is importing AUTOMATON
   occupancy values as "the Sea's density statistics." The charter's L-1 bars occupancy values as
   anchors; §2 restates it as binding on inputs, while admitting registered response CLASSES
   (functional structure) — the same values/structure distinction the 2936 A5-LENS ruling drew.

**What would have been wrong.** Citing any smoothness observable numerically (conversion-stage
business, and doing it now creates the motive the ordering exists to remove); assuming Poisson or
hyperuniform statistics; letting the prereg quietly define η-relevant combinations; bundling the
conversion into this patch.
