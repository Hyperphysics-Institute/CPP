# Reasoning — Patch 2632 (the last live defect gets its union, with the exits pre-priced)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

This mini-prereg exists because the 2574 audit said it must: a frozen reading (the
window's lower endpoint) could genuinely move here, and readings that can move are
never re-derived under an unregistered convention choice. The union structure is
the same medicine the truncation defect got at 2574 -- when a cutoff rule is
arbitrary, you run the admitted alternatives and let agreement or disagreement be
the finding.

The reproduction control is doing real work, not ceremony: the override-binding
failure at 2574 (c) produced a silent old-classifier re-run that only a four-decimal
match exposed. Binding build_reach variants into the exec-loaded namespace is
exactly the operation that failed there, so the INDEX member's job is to prove the
binding took before DIST and FULL mean anything.

Superseding to L=10 rather than hunting a fractional endpoint is deliberate
economy: the grid is even-L by parity structure, 10 is the next admitted point and
it never carried the caveat. If the window narrows, it narrows to a clean
registered value, and anyone wanting the endpoint sharper can declare that cell
with its own stakes stated.
