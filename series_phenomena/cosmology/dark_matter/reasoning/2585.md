# Reasoning — Patch 2585 (the simplest law, last)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

There is a mild irony worth recording: after a relaxation law, a cycle-impulse law, and
a mean-field variant, the member that can actually carry the physics is plain Newton at
the pinned masses -- the first thing a physicist would have written. The arc took the
long way because each earlier law had a citation pulling toward it (the charter's
cycle-averaged clause, the SF-6 relaxation form), and citations are right to pull; the
resolution is that the SF-6 pins' deepest registered content IS the masses, and Newton
at those masses is the clause's intent once the motion is faster than the cycle. That
reading is now in the record for the founder to strike if he disagrees.

Two small design points defended: no velocity cap (a cap is dynamics; the relativity
assert converts approach-to-c into an honest UNRES rather than silently altering the
law), and no re-centering of initial conditions onto computed equilibria (starting the
rungs AT their equilibria would smuggle the answer into the setup; the registered
geometry is the question as posed, and bounded breathing around a nearby equilibrium is
exactly what HOLD's bands were built to recognize).

The displaced-pair sanity line exists because undamped oscillation will look strange to
a future reader trained by this arc to suspect everything: for a conservative law it is
the CORRECT behavior, and saying so in advance prevents a good instinct from
misfiring.
