# Reasoning — Patch 2768 (C2R charter freeze)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.**

The charter's central design problem was keeping three different
"continuum" objects from blurring into each other, because the
blur is exactly how a future session would go wrong. There is (1)
the continuum MATCHING — a normalization prescription for the proxy
operator's alpha, now licensed as leading order by the founder
ruling; (2) the continuum CANDIDATE — the separate ALPHA-1 physical-
Sea scale at d_DP/2 = 0.182 fm, KINETIC-1-conditional, living on the
other side of the v2.5 dual registration; and (3) the continuum
LIMIT the L1 derivation takes internally. Fence F1 exists because a
C2R result of, say, 0.09x fm sitting next to a registered
"continuum" 0.182 fm is a paraphrase accident waiting to happen; the
fence makes every C2R sentence carry its branch.

Freezing the class thresholds took the most deliberation. W = the
envelope half-width fraction is the honest materiality line for the
correction: a delta-ell smaller than the band cannot be
distinguished from the instrument spread the band already carries,
so CONFIRM collapses the pair without pretending new precision. The
5% tolerance on L1-reproduces-the-matching is looser than W
deliberately: the interesting binary at L1 is whether the derivation
lands NEAR the imposed prescription at the operating point or
somewhere else entirely, and a tight tolerance there would
manufacture CORRECTED outcomes out of derivation-error noise. Both
numbers are frozen now precisely so neither can be tuned after the
derivation starts producing values.

The OBSTRUCTED class is load-bearing in a way worth stating: rider
v2.5 already discloses that the ALPHA-1 side is conditional on
OPEN-DM-KINETIC-1, and there is a live possibility that a first-
principles chi_static on the proxy side hits the same wall — that
the static response cannot be closed without the kinetic term the
programme has not yet derived. The class converts that from a
campaign embarrassment into a named registration: the obstruction
BECOMES the KINETIC-1 question, concretely posed, and no partial
number ships. The alternative — a leg that "approximately closes"
with an unstated gap — is the kind of soft failure the frozen-class
discipline exists to make impossible.

L3's bounded role honors the enactment logic from 2767: the site
matching is premise-rejected, but the rider's consumer triage
requires a quotable bracket, and computing it once, fenced as
OBS-class with no physics claim, is cheaper and more honest than
letting each consumer improvise its own sensitivity statement.

One process note: no Metropolis anywhere in this campaign, so
CONV-005 does not fire — but I said so explicitly in the charter
rather than leaving it implicit, because the gate is now standing
law and a reviewer should see the applicability call made in the
open, not inferred from silence.
