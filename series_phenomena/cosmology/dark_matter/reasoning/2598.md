# Reasoning — Patch 2598 (a prereg for growth, and a substitution that isn't a loophole)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The B2 design decision I want on record: the chain feeds each stage the FAVORABLE
capture channel from the B1 map, and I wrote "a declared data-driven choice, not
tuning" knowing a hostile reader would test that sentence. The defense is the
question's shape: B2 asks whether growth is POSSIBLE (an existence claim), and
existence claims are honestly tested on the favorable channel; the RATE claims live in
B4, which integrates over the whole map including every miss. Conflating the two --
testing existence on the average channel or rates on the best -- would be the actual
sin in either direction.

UNRES-REL as a COUNT rather than a verdict is the prereg's quiet discipline. The
relativistic cells are real physics (fast captures ARE relativistic) that the
instrument honestly cannot certify yet; counting them preserves the information for
the gamma-member rider without letting uncertifiable cells leak into any verdict.

The seat-substitution ruling took more care than its four sentences suggest. The test
I applied: does the amendment change WHAT is required, or only WHO supplies it? Two
executed independent implementations remain the bar -- the substance Grok demanded and
the contract froze. Substituting a seat name when one seat's artifact failed does not
lower the bar; if anything a FRESH seat (no defect-notice anchoring, no visibility of
the other returns) is more independent than a corrected resubmission. The
recommendation of Grok specifically is partly poetry -- the condition's author as its
executor -- but mostly rigor: no seat has stronger standing to attack this claim, and
a hostile replicator who returns HOLD is worth two friendly ones.
