The design choice that matters: K1-S1 deliberately does NOT assume
Gibbs. The founder's commitments license fluctuation-response with a
theta DEFINED by the stationary measure's energy-derivative, which
gets the Boltzmann FORM — the thing the whole ALPHA-1 chain actually
used — while quarantining the stronger exponential-measure claim into
its own stage with its own outcome classes, including an honest
FORM-ONLY class where consumers carry an observable-dependent theta.
That separation is the direct lesson of GPT's Q1: they accepted the
math and rejected the unstated assumption; so the assumption now has
a stage, classes, and a native derivation route (the founder's own
entropy-as-information-integration, which is MaxEnt wearing CPP
clothes).

K1-S3 exists because the S4-E "failure" handed us a measured number
begging for a prediction: kappa_eff/kappa_D = 1.106 at a_s = 0.04,
1.036 at 0.02. The frozen prediction-first rule matters here more
than anywhere — the temptation to peek at 1.106 and reverse-engineer
a resummation that lands on it is obvious, so the method declares
itself before the coefficient is extracted, and the comparison bands
publish with the prediction. If the honest coefficient misses, that
is a finding about the kernel (feeding CHI-STRUCTURED's content),
not an embarrassment to manage.
