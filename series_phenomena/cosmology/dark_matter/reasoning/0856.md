# Reasoning — Patch 0856 (DM-1 .tex promotion)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (Opus, DM 08xx lane, 24 June 2026). The .tex promotion of DM-1 — the working markdown draft (Patches 0700–0855) to a shippable flagship .tex. Larger than a normal patch; the reasoning records the judgment calls.

House style, not invention. I did not design a LaTeX layout. I read the closest sibling flagship — EU-1 (cosmology, per-paper subfolder) — and matched it: article class + the same package set, natbib with plainnat, the CPP macro block, the title/author/date convention, and the per-paper subfolder with `documentation_suite/`. The one place EU-1 gave no precedent was figures (it has none); DM-1 has three, so I used `\graphicspath{{../figures/}}` to reference the already-committed PNGs one level up rather than duplicate ~300 KB of binaries into the paper folder. Discovered that EU-1 uses an EMBEDDED `thebibliography` with manual `\bibitem`, not a central `.bib` — so DM-1's references live in the .tex itself, and the "observational bib entries" task folded into this one patch rather than a separate central-bib edit.

Citation integrity — verified, not recalled. The embedded bibliography mixes CPP flagships (SF-3, SF-5, SR-1, QM-1 — keys taken from cpp_references.bib, matching the body \cite keys) with eight external refs. I web-verified the external citation details at promotion rather than trusting memory: Kaplinghat-Tulin-Yu (PRL 116, 041302, 2016 — and its abstract independently states the σ/m≈2→0.1 velocity trend this paper's §7 leans on), de Blok+ 2008 (AJ 136, 2648–2719), and confirmed Jardel & Gebhardt 2012 (ApJ 746, 89), Goerdt+ 2006 (MNRAS 368, 1073), Oh+ 2008 (AJ 136, 2761), Mateo+ 1991 (AJ 102, 914), Li 2004 (PLB 603, 1), McCrea & Milne 1934 (QJM os-5, 73). Project C / DM-2 are NOT given external keys — they are CPP companions in preparation, named in prose, consistent with the 0854 discipline.

Promotion = clean the scaffolding, keep the honesty. The publication form drops the internal dev markers ([FILLED]/[TIGHTEN]/[DONE]), the §11 checklist, and the provenance block — those are working-draft apparatus, not paper content. What it does NOT drop is the honesty: the IC 2574 high-velocity tension is in the abstract, §6, §7, and the grade; the calibrated mass scale and abundance are stated as calibrated (§8); the Sea-gravitation gate is reported as conditional-but-traversed, now one condition after the c08 discharge (§9). The grade reads exactly as the draft's: positive discriminant, not full identification. A promotion that quietly sanded off the tension to read better as a "paper" would have been the failure mode; I kept the markdown draft as the working source of truth and made the .tex its faithful publication form.

Validation. Compiled with pdflatex, two passes: 0 undefined citations or cross-references on the second pass, 9 pages. So every \citep resolves to a \bibitem and every \ref to a \label — the cross-reference graph is closed, not just syntactically parseable. Build artifacts (.aux/.log/.pdf) were removed and are not committed.

Bookkeeping: new subfolder `DM-1/` with the .tex + `documentation_suite/changelog-DM-1.md` (house-style version history, filename fixed per the no-version-suffix rule). Markdown draft §11 item 8 → [DONE]. Figures referenced, not duplicated.

Anti-priorities held: matched the shipped EU-1 template rather than inventing a layout; web-verified every external citation rather than fabricating details; refused external keys for the in-prep CPP companions; preserved the tension and the calibrated/conditional caveats in the publication form rather than letting "make it look like a paper" sand them off; compiled to a closed cross-reference graph before shipping; kept binaries un-duplicated via graphicspath. Writes confined to dark_matter/ (new DM-1/ subfolder + the draft's item-8 flip) — collision-safe vs 0900/1000/1100/2100 windows. No verify script (the figure-generating scripts 0849–0851 are the executable artifacts, already committed; the appendix points to them).
