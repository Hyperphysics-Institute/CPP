The founder's answer did something better than pick a side: it
dissolved the question's framing. I asked about a DP inching toward the
source, and the answer is that there is no DP — the pairing is a
Moment-by-Moment heuristic, and the response to a static bias is a
time-averaged statistical redistribution of ± CP occupation obeying
Gauss's law. Reading it, three things stood out as load-bearing and I
extracted them as commitments rather than paraphrase: the total-field
response (both matchings survive that), the superposition-scale
self-exclusion (this one CUTS — the r < a exclusion that made alpha'
physically attractive is not where the founder's mechanism puts the
exclusion; it puts it at the GP address, point-like), and the
continuity of the time-averaged medium (there IS responsive stuff
between lattice sites, which the 59% core argument denied).

The Grok rewrite the founder supplied is genuinely good — clearer than
the original on the statistical core — but I checked it clause by
clause against the original and it drops the superposition/nulling
detail entirely. That detail is exactly the physics that decides the
exclusion scale, so the record marks the original as normative and
names what the gloss lost. Faithfulness to the founder's text over its
smoothest summary is the same discipline as faithfulness to a frozen
charter over a friendlier reading.

Then the implication I could not honestly leave unstated: commitments 1
and 4 together put pressure on the discrete-site lattice itself, and
the staggered FG-OTHER structure is a lattice zone-boundary phenomenon.
If the physical susceptibility is continuous, the staggering could be
the proxy's artifact and the physical scale could be the smooth ~0.18
fm that BOTH the continuum limit and the hop branch independently give.
I wrote that in §4 with its equally live converse (the Barlow density
is real and may survive coarse-graining as kernel structure), and
routed the whole fork into the charter as frozen outcome classes with
a hard guard: FG-OTHER is panel property, and no worker derivation
reclassifies it without a panel. The temptation this time was not to
soften an adverse result but to chase an exciting one; the guard is for
that temptation too.
