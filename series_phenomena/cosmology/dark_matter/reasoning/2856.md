# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2856 (C23 refinement rejected and corrected)

**STATUS: contemporaneous, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-28.**

The founder rejected a 5-0 panel text and asked whether his original
conception was recorded. It was, in `founders_voice/`, since June, and
it says what he said today: poles move "nearer or farther" around the
dipole's centre. Longitudinal displacement, arc as swept path.

What makes this more than a wording fix is §5. I went to check whether
the correction would break the banked curl derivation, expecting to
find damage. Instead `magnetic_curl_derivation.md` §2 says the DP
"polarises along r-hat" and that the sweep of r-hat IS the arc. That is
the longitudinal reading, stated in the derivation, four patches before
the panel glossed it as transverse. So the mathematics was never wrong.
Only the sentence written about it was.

This inverts my expectation about the consequence. I had prepared for
"founder overturns panel, downstream results need re-examination." The
actual finding is "founder and derivation agree; the panel's sentence
disagrees with both." That is a much narrower failure and I should not
inflate it into a physics crisis to make the correction feel weightier.
Nothing inverts. One open item gets renamed because its name encodes
the rejected reading.

The integrity finding is the real content. Five seats ratified a gloss
without opening either the founder-voice file or the derivation file,
and the derivation file was in the same folder and had been cited by
the same panel four patches earlier. Unanimity did nothing. The seats
converged on each other. I want this named precisely, because the
existing two failure modes in the register are both about *producing*
bad content under motivation, and this one is about *characterising*
existing good content without looking at it. Different mechanism,
different mitigation: require a citation to the file and line being
characterised, and ship that file with the dispatch.

My own share is worse than the panel's and I want it recorded in the
same font. I carried the text into the handover, then into the founder
items, without checking `founders_voice/`. At 2854 I had just built the
founder-voice capture discipline and written that the founder's
magnetic message existed nowhere in the repository. Then I handled a
motion about the founder's magnetic mechanism without reading the
directory I had just established for exactly that purpose. The founder
caught it. That is the wrong distribution of labour and it is the
second time in two sessions that a minority position, in this case a
minority of one who happens to own the picture, corrected a majority.
