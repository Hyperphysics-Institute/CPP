## §1 Why open at charter level rather than compute

The forward queue names the route-B relic campaign as the only large item. The 0843 survey's verdict — "not
derivable as a first-pass" — rested on TWO unknowns in the ADM identity Ω_DM/Ω_b = (m_DM/m_p)(n_DM/n_b): the DM
unit's mass and the number-density ratio. Candidate (B)'s promotion removed the first: the ring is 11.26 GeV with
a fully registered composition (8 elements × [8 qCP + 8 eCP], element mass 1408 MeV = 8×(132+44) exactly at the
2496-pinned κ — I verified the arithmetic, not the identity; the identity is 2452's). That is a genuine change of
state: the campaign now has ONE unknown, and the observational target collapses to a pure number, n_ring/n_b =
0.4468 ± 0.0054. Fixing that target, the branch windows, and the kill conditions BEFORE touching the mechanism is
exactly the discipline the last two patches (2513/2514) demonstrated the value of: both fired adverse/unresolved
branches honestly because the readings were committed first. A relic campaign has more motivated-reasoning surface
than either (the 5.36 is famous; the temptation to numerological matching is real), so the pre-registration burden
is correspondingly heavier — hence a charter patch with zero derivation content.

## §2 The adverse fact stated at open

Both naive ADM readings fail: one-ring-per-baryon overshoots ×2.24; one-element-per-baryon undershoots ×3.57.
I put this in the charter's §1 rather than letting it emerge later, because it defines the campaign honestly: route
B for candidate (B) is NOT the canonical "n_DM ~ n_b, m_DM ~ 5 GeV" ADM story (the ring is ×2.4 too heavy for
that). It survives only if the formation physics supplies a derived counting factor. If the eventual mechanism
needs a knob to bridge ×2.24, Branch T fires and the derivation claim dies — pre-registered now, while no one is
attached to any mechanism.

## §3 What I deliberately did not do

I did not attempt S1 (baryon net-CP bookkeeping) in this patch, though it is tempting as "cheap" — because I do
not yet know whether the SS/SF-3 corpus registers a net-CP content for the nucleon, and improvising one under a
fresh charter would be exactly the failure the CLONE-FIRST GATE exists to prevent. S1 is its own patch with its
own grep-first discipline. I did not compute any candidate branching fraction. I did not touch the chirality-arc
asymmetry beyond restating its registered status (FI-C-9 primitive, not-yet-derived) — 0843 flagged it as a
direction and the charter preserves precisely that strength. I did not move, or discuss moving, the 79.5%.

## §4 Verdict-semantics pre-registration (the part I consider load-bearing)

The charter fixes that no campaign outcome except a panel-adjudicated D-strong can move candidate (B)'s verdict,
and that K1/K2/T kill the DERIVATION CLAIM, not the candidate (the ratio returns to calibrated status, which is
its current registered status — condition 5). Without this clause, an adverse relic result would invite a
motivated "the candidate is wounded" or, worse, a favorable-directional result would invite silent verdict creep.
Both doors are now closed in writing before any computation exists.
