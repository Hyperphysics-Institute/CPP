## Why this goes to the panel rather than into a decision

The chain is uncomfortable and worth stating flatly: I discovered
that a frozen promotion criterion is unmeetable; that criterion
blocks the founder's prime goal; and the disposition I prefer is the
one that unblocks it. Any of those three alone would be fine.
Together they are the exact configuration in which a worker's
judgment cannot be trusted by anyone, including the worker. So the
motion states the finding, lays out three dispositions including one
I argue against, names my preference AND my conflict in the same
paragraph, and hands the vote away. If the panel holds PR4 frozen,
the prime goal waits — and that is a legitimate outcome I must be
willing to accept for the process to mean anything.

## The challenge key, finally implemented

2813 recorded that publishing complete ranges lets a seat sound
verified without executing. This dispatch is the first to withhold a
key, and the key is chosen so that honesty is cheap: a seat that
declares REASONED-UNVERIFIED loses nothing, while a seat that claims
execution now has to produce a number that exists only downstream of
actually running the code. I deliberately did NOT compute the value
before dispatch — committing it first would defeat the purpose, since
the repo is readable.

## M4 folded in deliberately

The PR2 band ambiguity could have waited for its own motion, but it
has the same structure as PR4 (a worker operationalization stricter
than the frozen text it implements, discovered after seeing data) and
the same disqualification applies. Sending both together lets the
panel see the pattern — that this worker's gates have twice been
stricter than the requirements they served — and judge whether that
is diligence or drift.
