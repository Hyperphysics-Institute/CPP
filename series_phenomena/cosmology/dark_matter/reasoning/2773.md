# Reasoning — Patch 2773 (C2R-L2 execution and record)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.**

The result is adverse and large, and the first thing I did after
seeing it was look for an execution defect rather than believe it.
Three checks convinced me it is physics of the corrected operator,
not a bug. First, the analytic continuum limit of the SAME corrected
closure — computed independently of any lattice — has complex poles
with decay 0.210 fm and oscillation wavelength 2.29 fm, and the
lattice fits land in exactly the pattern that combination produces:
window-dependent ℓ between 0.19 and 0.37 fm with degraded log-linear
R², because a 2.3 fm oscillation superposed on a 0.2 fm decay is not
a single exponential over a 1-fm window. Second, the staggering
collapse is quantitatively predicted by machinery from a different
leg: the diagonal self-medium term raises the effective stiffness to
1.45, the effective coupling drops to 0.69α, and the 2688 three-
regime map already established a staggering threshold above the
resulting value. Third, R=7 and R=9 agree to four decimals in the
corrected operator too, so the finding is boundary-blind. When three
independent structures corroborate an unwanted number, the number is
the finding.

The harder judgment was what the +220% MEANS, and here the prereg's
committed honesty bound did the work it was frozen to do. The
correction being O(1) is not merely "large"; it is diagnostic. The
leading-order cloud shape was derived from the uniform-medium
closure, and distributing it while re-solving the site system
re-screens response that the shape already incorporated — a double
counting that is second-order when the correction is small and
disqualifying when it is not. At ακ = 0.45 there is no small
parameter, so "leading order plus correction" is the wrong
structure for this operating point altogether. That is why the
record refuses to promote 0.29 fm to ℓ_derived even though the
class arithmetic D3 > W would nominally route to CORRECTED: the
CORRECTED class presupposes the derivation CLOSES, and a first term
of a visibly non-convergent expansion is not a closed derivation.
My present judgment, recorded for L4 but not fired here, is that
this is the OBSTRUCTED class's anticipated dependency wearing a
slightly different face: the charter anticipated χ_static proving
inseparable from the kinetic closure, and what the leg found is
that the SHAPE of the per-site response — the short-distance
structure of χ_static — cannot be fixed by statics alone at κ·a = 2.
The point idealization and the cloud are two guesses at that shape;
they give 0.09 and 0.29 fm; the physics that picks the true profile
is the per-site stiffness structure that L1 already named as the
underived KINETIC-1-adjacent remainder. The obstruction and the
named open item are the same object seen twice.

One restraint note, mirroring 2770's: it was tempting to run the
shape iteration anyway — the fixed-point functional is computable —
and see whether it converges somewhere interesting. Refused: the
prereg explicitly de-chartered it, and an un-preregistered
exploratory iteration landing near some attractive number would be
precisely the kind of result that cannot be trusted afterward. If
the panel or the founder wants the fixed-point computed, it gets
its own frozen prereg with convergence criteria committed in
advance.

A subtlety worth preserving: the founder's oppositely-signed
superposed curves ARE present in the model — in the baseline
staggered mode, where adjacent induced charges alternate. The
corrected operator destroys that mode. So the leg did not fail to
realize the founder's picture; it found that making the picture's
near-field medium explicit in the operator removes the staggered
regime that the picture's superposition clause describes. That
tension — the medium the ruling says responds, versus the mode
structure the readout was built on — is, I think, the physically
interesting content of the adverse result, and it belongs in front
of the founder in physical-picture terms, which the session summary
does.
