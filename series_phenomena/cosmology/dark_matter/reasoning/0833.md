# Reasoning — Patch 0833 (deriving the three Era-2 inputs from the corpus)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (Opus, Session 156, 10 June 2026). Grounding/derivation pass; no verdict, no THEO/ID. Flags a numerical inconsistency in the DP-Sea appendix.

Thomas asked me to derive the three inputs the 0830-0832 arc kept pointing back to: m_qDP, the residual fraction f, and the qDP statistics. The discipline I held throughout: ground every claim in the actual corpus and report honestly what is derived vs estimated vs broken, rather than confabulating CPP microphysics to manufacture clean numbers.

INPUT 1 (mass). c04 gives the mass-generation mechanism (Compton standing-wave / ZBW, mc^2=hbar nu_C) -- the form, exactly as Step-1 said. The scale comes from the DP-Sea binding energies E_eDP=88, E_qDP=3*88=264 MeV. The color-factor-3 RATIO is the clean, load-bearing part, and it gives m_qDP~264 MeV, consistent with the 0.3 GeV estimate. But when I actually evaluated the appendix formula E=alpha*hbar*c/(phi*l_p) with its stated r_min=phi*l_p=2.61e-35 m (the Planck-scaled length), I got 5.5e19 MeV, not 88 -- off by ~6e17. The quoted 88 MeV requires r_min~0.016 fm, eighteen orders larger than the Planck length. So the ABSOLUTE scale is not Planck-derived as written; the appendix mislabels its own r_min. I chose to flag this plainly rather than quietly use 264 MeV as if derived, because (a) it is a real error in a flagship paper that Thomas will want to reconcile, and (b) honesty about it is the whole point of a "derive the inputs" pass. m_qDP~264 stands as scale-calibrated (to the constituent/QCD scale), not Planck-derived. I checked the sigma/m impact (264 vs 300): +14%, still safe.

INPUT 3 (statistics) was the most interesting and the place I had to resist a wrong turn twice. The corpus (c04, DP-Sea spin table: "fermion ZBW (1/2)") makes the qCP a spin-1/2 fermion, so the qDP (a pair) is a boson. My first instinct was that this RESOLVES toward diffuse; my second was that it REOPENS the nugget question (bosons are more bindable than the Fermi bracket I used in 0832, so f_crit could drop below realistic f). Both instincts were too fast. The correct diagnostic is the de Boer quantum parameter Lambda=hbar/(r_c sqrt(m eps)): He-4 self-binds at Lambda~0.18, and only Lambda<~0.5 self-binds at all. The light qDP gives Lambda~0.75-2.4 across the whole physical f range -- far too quantum to condense into a self-bound liquid. So the medium stays diffuse, and 0832's conclusion is restored on the CORRECT (bosonic) basis, not the Fermi accident. The bonus is a clean falsifier: a nugget phase needs Lambda<~0.2, i.e. a much heavier/deeper system (hTetra+), not the qDP. This is firmer than 0832, where I had only bracketed and worried about the f>~0.5 edge; the de Boer parameter closes that edge for the light qDP.

INPUT 2 (f) I did NOT manufacture. It is bounded f<1 (residue<source, the hierarchy from 0831/0832) and estimated f~0.05-0.2 by analogy (nuclear ~5-20%, molecular vdW <1%). Deriving it needs the qDP color polarizability -- the induced-dipole/London C6 between two neutral color dipoles, from the qCP separation and color coupling. The corpus fixes the ingredients but does not assemble the neutral-neutral residual. So I scoped it as the one genuinely open derivation rather than inventing a number. The arc is robust to it anyway: collisionless for all f<=1 (0831), diffuse for all f by de Boer (Input 3).

Net I was honest that the pass is mixed: two firmed (mass ratio; statistics->diffuse), one scoped (f), one corpus inconsistency surfaced (appendix mass scale). That is a more useful and more truthful outcome than a tidy "all three derived."

Anti-priorities held: refused to treat m_qDP~264 as Planck-derived once the arithmetic broke -- flagged the 6e17 discrepancy explicitly; resisted both fast instincts on the statistics and used the right (de Boer) diagnostic; did not invent a value for f, scoped its real derivation; grounded mechanism in c04 and the DP-Sea spin table by reading them, not from memory; no verdict/THEO.
