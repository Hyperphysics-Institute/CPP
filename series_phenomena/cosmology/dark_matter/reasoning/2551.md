# Reasoning — Patch 2551 (ENDBOND-3 R-A: barrier-free curve; E_close banked pin [128.9, 137.5])

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The 2550 expectation note said the interesting question was whether the 15/16 point would
reveal the seam engaging. It did — a clean 14–20 MeV drop at closure — but the curve's real
story was the one I did not predict: the ~115 MeV cooperative drop between 3/5 and 4/5,
where the 270°-wrapped arc brings distant planes into range. That is the fragment null of
2549 explained in one picture: the interface bond is small in isolation and the cohesion is
collective, arriving when the geometry lets many planes feel each other at once. I wrote in
2549 that this reading "makes the fragment null a datum rather than a failure" while
refusing to claim it; the curve has now earned it the status of a banked observation.

The local rise between 4/5 and 15/16 was the patch's honesty test. Under a looser prereg I
could have called it "the approach barrier" and extracted E_barrier ≈ 10 and E_close ≈ 25 —
numbers that would have landed the seam story in a completely different place. The knob-free
definition (grid argmax, committed before any run) says κ* = 0, full stop; the rise is
disclosed as shape, not promoted to structure. If a genuine approach barrier matters
physically, a successor with a finer pre-registered grid around [0.75, 1] can establish it;
it cannot be conjured here.

The reproduction check deserves its paragraph. When the ring endpoint came back 1.9 MeV off
the 2510 published value while the rod matched to the decimal, the options were: shrug
(it's 0.07%), or chase it. Chasing it cost twenty minutes and bought the right answer: the
scaffolds are bit-identical to 3e-15, the rules are verbatim, and the residual is the
chaotic floor the corpus already diagnosed in 2513 — this dynamical system amplifies
float-ordering differences across numpy builds. That converts an unexplained discrepancy
into a quantified rider (±2 MeV on the band edges) and, more importantly, into a standing
fact for every future dance patch: cross-build reproduction of v8 is floor-limited at ~2
MeV, and any future "discrepancy" inside that floor is expected, not alarming.

G2 failing while G1 passes is the result telling us the interface total and the single-pair
contact depth are different animals — as they plausibly should be, with four qq pairs plus
coat participation at the seam. The ×4-ish per-pair arithmetic is sitting right there and I
have not done it, because the prereg forbids the post-result per-pair reframe for exactly
this configuration of temptations. It is named as a future question. The pin banks; the
79.5% does not move; the collapse of the 2542 band is the pre-registered, licensed
consequence and nothing more.
