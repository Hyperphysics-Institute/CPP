# Reasoning — Patch 2577 (the assert that was secretly a theorem, and the discipline of
# a second negative)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The bracket assertion (rigid == independent jitter) went into the script as a
bookkeeping check and came out as the turn's most important line of physics. When two
supposedly different coherence conventions collapsed to identical integrals EXACTLY, the
right response was not satisfaction at a passed assert but suspicion at a distinction
without a difference -- and pulling that thread exposed that my entire frozen averaging
was smearing, not ponderomotive response. The registered identity was sitting one file
away with no free parameters. Two lessons banked: (1) when a convention union collapses
analytically, the formalism is probably missing the physics the union was meant to
bracket; (2) this is the second time in three patches that a prereg of mine quietly
replaced registered content with an invented operationalization -- 2572's cycle-average
transcription, now 2576's jitter statistics. The pattern has a shape: under derivation
pressure I reach for the formalism I can implement rather than the one the record
licenses. The capability census gets a new standing question: "does every formal step
cite its registered source, and does the implementation match the citation?"

The double negative is stronger than a single one and I resisted the pull to soften it.
The founder's core now fails at statics (Earnshaw), at smearing (disclosed-wrong but
recorded), and at the faithful registered ponderomotive layer with a quantified 15x
shortfall at maximum generosity. What keeps this from being a defeat is the same thing
as at 2573: the negative is INFORMATIVE. The pitch is not pairwise-held, not
smear-held, not field-magnitude-ponderomotive-held -- the space of what holds it is
collapsing toward exactly two candidates: the parametric switching channel, or the
collective/registration picture the corpus has been whispering since ENDBOND. My
recommendation to run F-c-double-prime first is partly economy (one bounded derivation)
and partly epistemics: an F-b built on "we exhausted every registered channel" is a
different and better object than an F-b built on "we got tired of looking."

I also note what I did NOT do: the non-monotone xi-dependence in the smearing table
(depth shrinking then re-deepening past xi = 1.5) is a curiosity begging for a side
investigation, and it belongs to a formalism already ruled wrong. Curiosities in
condemned buildings stay uninvestigated.
