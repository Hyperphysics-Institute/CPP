# Reasoning — Patch 2600 (the guard I built and then didn't read, again)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The 2596-G2 correction stings in a particular way: the relativity assert was MY guard,
built at 2585, doing its job faithfully -- and my gate code computed the flag, stored
it, and read the band verdict without looking at it. This is the third variation of the
same defect (the 2574 override that didn't bind, the 2579 verdict print, now a guard
computed-but-unconsulted), and the pattern's root is constant: the checking code and
the verdict code were written as separate thoughts. The structural fix -- classifiers
consult hb_ok FIRST, as a type-level habit, not a remembered step -- is the only kind
of fix that survives me.

What steadies the correction is that nothing certified was lost: every genuinely
sub-relativistic gate stands untouched, and the sink's registration re-scopes rather
than retracts. The scope line was always physically real; tonight it just got drawn
where it belonged.

The 96/96 count is the kind of negative that functions as a discovery. I expected a
capture EDGE somewhere in the grid -- slow grazing approaches captured, fast central
ones through. Instead the well depth dominates every kinematic variable we scanned:
the approach energy is a rounding error against 200 MeV of binding. In hindsight the
2592 five-cell was whispering this (60 MeV was the SHALLOWEST possible entry) and I
heard it as a special case. The substrate is telling us that DM assembly at this layer
is intrinsically a relativistic process -- which, for a theory whose founder built it
on ZBW motion at c, is less an obstacle than a homecoming.

I recommended the gamma member with a specific form in the record because vagueness at
a decision point is a cost passed to the founder; he can strike the form and keep the
door. One word opens it, and every blocked campaign is waiting on the same hinge.
