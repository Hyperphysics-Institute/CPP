## Why the 2443 core depth (4.7 MeV) was wrong
2443 floated the core depth by forcing K_ZBW from d*=1.15 fm with the charge-switched
(1/7) coefficient. That inverts the physics: the qCP spacing is NOT a Coulomb+ZBW
balance — it is STRONG-SSV-set (2433: "the strong force (qCP, steep SSV) sets d").
So the 4.7 MeV was a forced-K artifact, not the actual bond depth.

## The actual depth, from CPP anchors (m_qCP=132 MeV, alpha_s=0.386, d=1.15 fm)
A qCP confined to Δx ~ d=1.15 fm has zero-point energy (ℏc)²/(2μ d²) = 223 MeV (non-rel)
or ℏc/d = 172 MeV (rel), both ≫ μc²=66 MeV → RELATIVISTIC confinement. A soft few-MeV
Coulomb/ZBW bond cannot hold it; the confining well must be ~170–220 MeV deep. This
confirms 2433 (strong-SSV spacing) and kills the 4.7 MeV.

E_qq depth estimates (energy to separate one qCP to infinity):
 - charge-switched net at d, (1−2δ)α_s ℏc/d = 9.5 MeV   (fast-switch time avg; robust lower bound)
 - raw Coulomb at d, α_s ℏc/d = 66.3 MeV                 (static opposite-charge; Coulomb component)
 - confinement scale ~ ZP escape = 172–223 MeV           (must hold the qCP at 1.15 fm)
Robust claim: core depth ≥ ~9 MeV; physical value ~66–172 MeV. NOT ~0.49 MeV, NOT 4.7 MeV.

## Peel-stall test (the founder's "core out-resists the spring")
Released bend energy per hinge (2443 U) = 1.06 MeV. Coulomb attraction is monotonic (no
inflection/barrier) → rupturing a bond costs the FULL binding, not a fraction. So the
peel propagates through a bond only if 1.06 MeV ≥ its depth. Coat (~0.49 MeV) < 1.06 →
a peel WOULD rip the coat; but core depth (9.5 / 66 / 172 MeV) ≫ 1.06 MeV → margin ×9 to
×162 → the peel front reaches the core and STALLS. The loop cannot unzip through the core.

CORRECTION to my earlier hand-wave: I had worried the peel only needs the rupture
barrier (inflection), which for a bond with a barrier can be « depth. But the qCP–qCP
axial bond is Coulomb-like/monotonic — no inflection — so rupture DOES cost the full
binding. The founder's full-depth comparison was right for this bond; my "barrier «
depth" caveat does not apply here. Margin is ≥9× even on the most pessimistic (switched-
net) core estimate.
