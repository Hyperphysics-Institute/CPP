## Compare to actual regimes — the key result
 - present galactic DM dispersion (~250 km/s, β=8×10⁻⁴): E_cm ≈ 2 keV — far BELOW the
   0.49 MeV elastic scale. Even galaxy-cluster velocities (1500 km/s): 0.07 MeV, still
   sub-elastic. → present DM-DM collisions are COLLISIONALLY INERT.
 - primordial β~0.08 (23k km/s): E_cm ≈ 18 MeV > E_form → forms a ring. β~0.2: 113 MeV.
So ring FORMATION requires β~0.08 (early-universe/primordial turbulence; illustratively
kT~20 MeV, t~ms), NOT present galaxies. BREAK requires β~0.035. Hierarchy: galactic (keV,
inert) ≪ break (0.035) ≪ form (0.078). Consistent and clean.

before the inertial ends respond → efficient symmetric bending, exactly the founder's
mechanism). So galactic collisions are DOUBLY inert (sub-elastic AND adiabatic); formation
collisions are both energetic and sudden.
