**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-26.**

## The vote I had to lose

Four seats ratified my finding as I wrote it. One seat said I had
overclaimed. The overclaiming seat is correct: I tested one candidate
energy functional and wrote "the bare rule conserves no energy
functional," which is a theorem I did not prove and cannot prove from
one trajectory. S1 also caught a second sloppiness — Gibbs measures
do not require the subsystem to conserve energy when a bath enforces
detailed balance — and repaired the argument to its actual load-
bearing form: neither a conserved candidate NOR a bath is specified.
Adopting a 1-seat position over a 4-seat ratification is the correct
adjudication precisely because the four were ratifying MY error, and
a panel that agrees with the worker is not thereby right. The
withdrawn phrasing is named so it cannot be cited from the patches
that contain it.

## What the amendment nearly became, and what stopped it

My motion proposed "PR4 met by analytic demonstration + open
condition." S1's split makes explicit what mine left ambiguous:
PR4-BARE MET-NEGATIVE is a statement that the physics is ABSENT, and
without the (b) clause it could be read downstream as the physics
having been DEMONSTRATED. The distinction is the difference between
honest and self-serving, and it came from the panel, not from me —
which is the strongest argument I have for why this motion had to go
to them.

## The consequence I must surface even though it costs us

OPEN-PR4-C23C24 blocks thermodynamic-population claims, and
OPEN-DM-RELIC-1 is a relic-abundance arc. So the amendment that
unblocks PR4 simultaneously gates the relic strand of Candidate (B).
I did not anticipate this when drafting the motion and I could
plausibly have left it for someone else to notice later. Surfacing it
now, in the adjudication that benefits from the amendment, is the
only version of this that survives scrutiny.

## The protocol worked on its first outing

Five seats, five honest REASONED-UNVERIFIED declarations, zero
fabricated challenge values — after four fabrication events from S5
and one from S1 across prior rounds. The variable that changed was
mine: I stopped publishing everything needed to fake a verification
and made honest non-execution explicitly costless. The lesson
generalises beyond this panel, and it is now standing procedure.
