first paragraph: "derivation-covered" could tempt a W-strength world-call about
what the substrate does, when what I can actually prove tonight is a theorem about
the registered algorithm. T1 is exact -- three lines of the engine's own ledger

nothing yet about nature. Labeling it engine-theorem strength and handing FORM-1
the continuum question (reproduce T1 as your integral law, predict the
phase-robustness boundary) is the honest division of labor between what a night of
analysis can register and what the dedicated session exists for.

consumer so the diagnostic width can never leak into physics claims. P3 is the leg
I am least sure of, and the readings are built so its death is a partial result
rather than a face-saving footnote.

rent in its own currency instead of gating on a number I chose to be passable.
