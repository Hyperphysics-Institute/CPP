# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2791 (X4 rung 0.01)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-24.**

## Holding the line when the data gets loud

This rung is where frozen-charter discipline earns its keep. A ~25%
apparent below-DH deficit at a_s = 0.01 — after ~10% at 0.02 — is
the kind of number that invites an immediate story, and two
plausible stories point in opposite directions: "the deficit is
physical and the soft core was masking it" versus "the F1 window's
2a_s lower edge is sweeping progressively more short-range pair
structure into a one-mode fit." The record states the competing
mechanisms same-font and enacts neither, because the prereg already
contains the instruments that distinguish them — fixed-physical-
window maps across rungs, the ΔAIC two-component test, the
moving-feature scan — and they run only on the complete ladder.
Choosing a story now would contaminate the battery's authority to
choose it later.

## Why state mechanism (i) at all, if no conclusion is drawn

Because the rung table without it is misleading in the OTHER
direction: a bare "0.72 at a_s = 0.01" invites the anomaly headline.
The same-font rule cuts both ways — suppressing a known
window-mechanics fact would be spin toward excitement, exactly as
suppressing the number would be spin toward quiet. Both mechanisms
are on the record; the battery adjudicates.
