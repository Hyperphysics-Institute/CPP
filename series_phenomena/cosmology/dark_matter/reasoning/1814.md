**(2) Small-d / Compton-scale validity (Gemini sharp; Grok soft) — real concern, overstated basis; corrected
and registered.** Gemini: eDP Compton wavelength λ_C = ℏc/m ≈ 2.24 fm exceeds d ≈ 1.0–1.3 fm, so the
wavefunctions "must massively overlap" and fixed-point-charge electrostatics is invalid. **The concern (quantum
extent vs separation) is legitimate; the quantitative basis overstates it.** The operative scale for
point-treatment / pair-production breakdown is the *reduced* Compton wavelength ƛ = ℏ/(mc) = λ_C/2π ≈ **0.357
fm**, not the full λ_C. On that scale **d/ƛ ≈ 2.8–3.6** — the separation sits ~3× above the breakdown scale,
where a point-charge treatment is defensible. Moreover the ZBW oscillation amplitude is itself a ≈ ƛ (textbook
ZBW), so at the model's a/d ~ 0.3 the charges sit ~3 amplitudes apart (partial, not "massive", overlap), and
the spread factor s = 1/√(1−(a/d)²) ≈ **1.05** already in f_ZBW = ½·s carries the leading radial-extent
correction (~5%). **What survives, honored:** the residual field-theoretic overlap (exchange / pair effects) at
d ~ 3ƛ is a real, un-estimated O(1) check. It **reinforces the Layer-C cap; it does not falsify** (the model
is not internally contradictory, contra the hostile read — its own s factor and ZBW amplitude are consistent
with d ~ 3ƛ). Diagnostic: `code/1814`. Registered as a residual: estimate the overlap/exchange correction
explicitly.
