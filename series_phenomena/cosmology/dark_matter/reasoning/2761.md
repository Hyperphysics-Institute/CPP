# Reasoning — Patch 2761 (RV-2714 execution: the clean replacement measurements)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.**

The execution itself was deliberately boring — gate first, five
chains at the frozen lengths, checkpointed chunks sized to the
container's per-call limit (one early over-sized call was killed by
the environment mid-second-chain; the checkpoint discipline meant it
cost nothing: MAIN-A had banked its sweeps, MAIN-B restarted from
zero as if the call had never happened). The S-drift cross-check I
added at chunk boundaries — incremental S against a fresh full
summation — held at ~10⁻¹² throughout, which retires the one residual
worry about reusing the incremental k-space machinery at all.

The numbers are not boring. Three observations, in the order their
significance registered:

**First, the acceptance rate told the story before any analyzer ran.**
Clean chains accept at 0.91–0.93. The legacy chains ran lower —
every proposed move was carrying a spurious 2–28 MeV penalty against
Θ ≈ 35 MeV. I noticed the 0.92 on the very first benchmark chunk and
flagged it privately as the first visible signature of the fix;
it belongs in the record because it is the kind of macroscopic
diagnostic a reviewer can check without rerunning anything.

**Second, the suppression finding did not survive — at all.** I had
half-expected a partial outcome: some real below-HNC suppression with
the defect inflating it. Instead κ_S/κ_D came back 0.982–1.015 across
all five chains and the matched-k pole agreed with HNC at 0.49σ. The
entire 20–26% effect was the bug. The mechanism is exactly 2756 §3's:
the penalty stiffened the chains, under-sampled the fluctuations,
suppressed S_zz, inflated κ_S. What impresses me in retrospect is how
coherent the contaminated story was — suppression consistent across
sizes, "septuply consistent," surviving matched functionals —
because every instrument shared the same broken stationary law.
Internal consistency is not cleanliness; the audit charter said it as
a suspicion, and the clean data said it as a fact.

**Third, the tension inverted rather than vanished, and honesty cuts
both ways here.** The clean real-space κ_fit is ~10% BELOW κ_D
(legacy was 9–17% above). C1 still fails; RV S4-E still reads FAIL
under the frozen criteria; the frozen 2735 rule still prints
CLOSURE-ERROR CANDIDATE / UNRESOLVED because F1 = 2.56σ exceeds its
2σ bar. It would be easy to write this up as "everything is fine
now" — k-space agrees, far window agrees, C2/C4 flipped to PASS —
but the near-window slope sits below DH at both a_s values with the
same sign, and the frozen rule fired the verdict it fired. The
legacy interpretation ("moderate-coupling screening ENHANCEMENT, the
expected O(Γ) direction") is void with its number — the clean
deviation has the OPPOSITE sign, so whatever physics or extraction
systematics underlies it has not been diagnosed, only bounded. That
is X3/X4's job, exactly as the frozen rule routes it, and the honest
summary is: the monster is dead, a marginal residual remains, and
the instruments to interrogate it were already chartered.

One process note: C3 failing on clean data (AIC preferring the
oscillatory model on 2 of 5 runs, with zero significant alternations
anywhere) is actually informative — it reproduces the 2714-era
diagnosis that the criterion defect is a property of the CRITERION
(smooth-curvature absorption against block-SEMs), not of the defect.
The prereg's decision to re-apply C3 as frozen rather than amend it
paid off: had I "fixed" C3, the clean run couldn't have demonstrated
that its failure mode is contamination-independent.

The RV-3 driver was generated by mechanical path-substitution from
the committed 2735 file rather than hand-copied — the transformation
is three lines of Python in the session transcript and the diff
against 2735 is exactly the input path. This is the cheapest possible
way to make "the functionals are byte-identical" a checkable claim
instead of an assurance, and after F3's impeached assurance ("no
line-level defect exists") this campaign does not issue assurances
where it can issue diffs.
