cleverness inside the cycle quantum fixes it. H-C (plain per-step Newton at the pinned
masses) is the only member class that can resolve the motion, and I proposed it rather
than ran it, because the arc's whole disciplinary residue is exactly this: new rules
enter by rider, gated by the control that would have caught the last defect.

the only true word available tonight.
