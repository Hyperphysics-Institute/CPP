## The 2430 blocker and the way through it
2430 found the ponderomotive Hessian H = 2[(∇E)ᵀ(∇E) + E·∇²E] sign-indefinite
([−190,+173,+292]) — but evaluated at a hand-set structural site with |E|≠0, so the
sign-indefinite E·∇²E term contaminated it (2430's own caveat). KEY: at a MINIMUM of
|E|² (the ponderomotive equilibrium), H = 2(∇E)ᵀ(∇E) + 2·[E·∇²E]; the second term is
small/zero and the Hessian is positive semi-definite by construction of a minimum. So
the −190 is an OFF-NULL artifact, not a real transverse instability.

## Netting (G4) — and the honest, slightly-UNfavorable news for the transverse mode
Net = K_switch(ε) + K_pond(drive) + K_structural. K_structural (transverse Earnshaw) =
−1.83 (negative — the reason switching is needed at all). K_pond at the min = +0.84.
K_switch ~ ε²·S (S≈7.9, ponderomotive scale). Net turns positive only between ε=0.30
(−0.3) and ε=0.43 (+0.5), i.e. ε ≳ 0.40. So R5 does NOT make the transverse mode
unconditionally stable — it converts "−190 kills it unconditionally" into "stable only
near the TOP of method (a)'s window." Combined with method (a)'s upper edge (0.43,
too-fast instability), the transverse mode survives only in a NARROW sliver ε∈[~0.40,
0.43]. Netting SHRANK method (a)'s [0.18,0.43] window to its top edge — more marginal,
not less. (K_switch~ε²S is a crude parametrization; the robust claims are the sign
structure and the narrowing, not the exact 0.40 threshold.)
