# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2795 (erratum + PR2-PHYS successor draft)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-24.**

## The erratum matters more than its size

One stale sentence — "PR4 awaits the founder" — survived three
patches and two founder-facing summaries because it was inherited
from dispatch prose instead of re-derived from the charter files,
and the founder had to flag it twice. The failure mode is precisely
the one CLONE-FIRST exists to kill at the registry level: citing
state from memory of a summary rather than from the artifact of
record. The erratum names the origin, the propagation path, and the
process rule (PR-status lines re-derived from charters at each
citation). The founder's time is the programme's scarcest resource;
asking him twice for work he delivered at Patches 2729/2732 spent it
badly, and the fix is structural, not apologetic.

## Designing a gate whose inputs are half-known

The successor prereg's hardest question was §0: the battery already
published the per-rung joint poles, so a naive "fresh prereg" would
be preregistration theater of exactly the kind refused at the RB-1
void. The honest structure: disclose what is known, identify what is
still blind (the surface extrapolation, the GOF, the total
uncertainty — the actual verdict-deciders), attest that no surface
fit has been run, and put the known/blind split itself before the
panel (R2 offers them fresh chains if they judge the disclosure
insufficient). A gate ratified with eyes open on known inputs is
sound; a gate pretending its inputs are unknown is not.

## The GOF gate is the successor's soul

The retired PR2 failed adequacy (χ²/dof = 35.6) and had no clause
that cared. The successor hard-codes the lesson: a surface that
does not describe its data returns UNRESOLVED, not a verdict. That
single clause is the difference between an instrument and a ritual.
