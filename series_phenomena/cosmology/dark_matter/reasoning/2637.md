# Reasoning — Patch 2637 (the matched launcher, and why the control that can fail is the one worth having)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The design question was which matched convention to adopt. Three candidates: (a)
remove the economy re-settle so both legs launch at the identical propagated
state; (b) give BOTH legs the TC=20 re-settle at their own dt — but that
reproduces the original defect exactly, two legs at two states; (c) settle the
base object fully at each dt — expensive and it re-introduces the mismatch one
level down, since the two settles land on different microstates. Only (a) makes
the dt-union mean what the gate has always claimed to test: same initial data,
different step. The economy leg was a convenience that quietly changed the
experiment's initial conditions per leg; deleting it is not a new instrument so
much as the instrument the prereg language always described.

The control I most wanted is C-M2, the drawn-cell conservatism check at φ=30.
The nightmare outcome is not that the undrawn cells stay undrawn — RM2 is an
honest reading with a declared derivational successor — it is that deleting the
economy leg silently flips a cell the envelope already registered as drawn. If
that happens, the ROB-1 envelope itself acquires an instrument-dependence the
record must disclose, and no re-run of the marginal cells means anything. So the
control failure mode is wired to stop everything AND to flag the drawn entry for
adjudication, in the same font as any other adverse fact.

The escalation ladder is frozen at exactly one extra rung ({1/200, 1/400})
because the 2631 lesson cuts both ways: an instrument that disagrees with itself
at marginal cells doesn't draw walls there, and a session that keeps halving dt
hoping for agreement is fitting the instrument to the wish. Two unions, then the
cell reads RM2 and the question goes to derivation where it belongs.

The conditional φ=37.5 refinement runs only on a dt-stable wall. Refining an
undrawn boundary would manufacture precision about a number the instrument
cannot certify — the 2612 artifact taught what asserted geometry costs.
