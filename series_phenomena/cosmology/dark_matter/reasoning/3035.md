# Tier-4 reasoning fragment — Patch 3035 (founder ruling + T-1 v1.4)

**Captured at-patch, producing window.**

**The founder's answer did more than answer.** The 3034 §8 question
asked point-or-class; the ruling gave class PLUS mechanism (partner
switching), and the mechanism is what made T-1 v1.4 easy to write
honestly. The panel's demand — "state the closure as STATISTICAL" —
could have read as a concession extracted under review pressure. With
partner switching registered, the statistical grade has a positive
physical reason: if the identity of the unpaired CP hops chaotically
through the population, "this DP returns to its rest state" has no
stable referent, and population-level closure is not the weaker claim
but the RIGHT one. The v1.4 text says so explicitly and cites the
ruling; the demotion is principled.

**A boundary was respected in the worker reading.** The 3034 engine's
micro-chaos comes from the unit(net) primitive; the founder's
mechanism is binding exchange. These are consonant — both shuffle
micro-identity beneath a conserved pattern — but they are not the same
claim, and the registration says so rather than welding them. Whether
partner switching enters the committed engine as an explicit rule is
left as a watch-level note with the 3032-style anchor discipline (no
ID minted; a future proposal cites the registration). Welding them now
would have manufactured agreement the measurements don't own.

**Lemma PA's design: make the contested thing a parameter.** The
panel's partial-anchoring demand was, at bottom, "what if the
dichotomy isn't exhaustive?" The lemma answers by making
non-exhaustiveness a measurable number: ᾱ, the mean anchoring
fraction. Every reading then lives on one axis — ᾱ = 0 is H-PW
(pointwise), E[a]=0 with mixing is statistical closure (1/√N),
ᾱ > 0 is the falsifier — and the instrument that measures ᾱ is the
ensemble mean tail the campaigns already run. The correlated-residual
negative control mattered most: the 1/√N suppression is NOT free; it
needs independence or mixing, and the mixing is exactly what partner
switching supplies. That closes the loop between the founder's
mechanism and the lemma's load-bearing assumption — noted in PA-3's
statement, not just here.

**The "or" branch was answered without pretending.** The obligation
allowed a lemma OR an exhaustive partition construction. The
partition (imprinted / departing / handoff-window) is honest but its
contested step — the handoff window empties and nothing re-anchors —
IS H-PW. Presenting the partition as an independent proof would have
re-committed the v1.3 sin at higher volume. v1.4 supplies the lemma
as the deliverable and files the partition as the structure OF the
hypothesis. If a seat wants the construction upgraded to a proof,
the missing step is now named with one symbol.

**Grade notes at the theorem statement, not just the appendix.** The
CL section is where the argument lives, but the panel reads §2. The
v1.4 grade note sits directly under clause (iii) so that
"exactly"/"zero" cannot be quoted out of grade; §6's
does-not-establish list gains the pointwise line for the same reason.
The lesson from the CONV-013 Q2 tally is that provenance in an
appendix does not immunize a headline claim.
