## What the route genuinely delivers (two real results)

**(A) κ in units of kT_form.** With ℓ_rung = 1.0 fm (0861) and g ≈ 0.02:
- κ_bend/kT_form = ℓ_p/ℓ_rung ~ **105–702**
- κ_scissor/kT_form = g·ℓ_p/ℓ_rung ~ **2.1–14**

So the X-junction hinge is a few-to-~14× the formation thermal scale: **bound and thermally stable, but soft.**
Absolute MeV blocked (needs kT_form).

**(B) κ_scissor/E_bond — kT_form cancels (a kT_form-FREE ratio).** Since 0861 sets E_bond/kT_form ~ 13.8 for the
loop population ⟨N⟩ ~ 10³, kT_form divides out:
- κ_scissor/E_bond ~ **0.15–1.02** (0861's own loop population).

The scissor angular stiffness is a **sizable fraction of, but does not exceed, the bond depth** — physically
required (an angular stiffness cannot exceed the bond that provides it) and here satisfied. This is the
quantitative form of SF 2202's "softer of two stiff modes, not a fragile joint," now stated against the very
bond that holds the junction, and **without needing kT_form.** Cross-check: the bend-break angle θ_max =
√(2E_bond/κ_bend) ~ 11–29°, comfortably above the thermal θ_rms ~ 3–8°/hinge (0861) — the ribbon is thermally
stable, consistent with 0861.
