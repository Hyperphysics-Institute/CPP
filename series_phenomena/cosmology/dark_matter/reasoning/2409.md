## 3. The discipline question, answered before firing

Is reading the values from this mirror a CONV-004 violation? No — and the
distinction is load-bearing. CONV-004 bars MANUFACTURING a number:
assigning a value from curve-shape expectation, interpolating from
un-anchored assumptions, or eyeballing a figure. What happened here is a
READ of the published official data product — the same bytes HEPData
serves — through a mirror whose provenance is three-factor authenticated,
under the founder's explicit delegation verbatim. The route is recorded so
completely that any auditor can re-walk it. The residual risk mode
(a tampered or corrupted mirror) is bounded by the two text-anchor matches
and is orders below the verdict margins: the three exclusions hold at
×8.8×10⁶, ×7.1×10⁴, and ×361 — no transcription-error mode reaches them.
The founder's direct HEPData read stays invited as the gold-standard
backstop, non-blocking; a mismatch on direct read reopens without ceremony
(the 2352 grammar).

What was NOT done: the (1a) value was not inferred from the adjacent fact
(WS2025's 1.112×10⁻⁴⁶ at 9.0 GeV — wrong mass, wrong dataset, recorded and
not consumed); no curve-shape extension past 9.0 GeV; no pre-adjudication
of what the exclusions mean for member weights or the attestation record.
