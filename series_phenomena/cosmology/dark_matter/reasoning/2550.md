# Reasoning — Patch 2550 (ENDBOND-3 pre-registration: closure energy via the dance on the bend family)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The founder said "proceed," which under PD-006 makes the charter mine to write — and the
first draft I discarded is worth recording. It defined the cut ring topologically (sever
the dance reach across one seam, keep the closed geometry) because it looked maximally
clean: identical positions, one interaction removed. It is actually maximally dirty: the
sampled energy is all-pairs, so the seam planes would still interact at contact distance
while being forbidden from dancing with each other — a configuration that exists in no
physical formation path and whose energy difference measures the choreography rules, not
the bond. The geometric fact that killed the naive version — a rigidly cut 16-ring's ends
sit one pitch apart — is the same fact that makes the bend family the honest instrument:
in this system you cannot open the seam without unbending, so the observable IS the curve,
and the barrier/closure decomposition must come from the curve's own shape, knob-free.

Two protections written in before any number exists. First, the barrier-free reading
equals −(ring−straight) by construction, which is the echo family's own arithmetic — so if
the curve turns out monotone, "agreement with ~85" is not evidence of anything and the
prereg says so now, while I still don't know whether the curve is monotone. Second, the
win-class bar requires κ* dt-stability, because a barrier that migrates across the grid as
dt changes is an integrator artifact wearing a physics costume, and after ENDBOND-2's
dt-dominated null I have direct evidence this functional's differences are dt-sensitive at
the few-MeV scale.

What I expect, honestly: the ring end will sit well below the straight end (the registered
r−s), and the interesting question is whether the 15/16 point reveals the seam engaging —
a drop between 15/16 and 1 much larger than between adjacent interior points would be the
closure bond showing itself in-context. I am writing that expectation down here precisely
so that if the curve does something else, the record shows I did not quietly redefine
"interesting" around whatever appeared.
