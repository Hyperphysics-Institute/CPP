## The criterion (this is the contribution: a sharp test, not an assertion)

A glueball junction acts as a **hinge** (angularly flexible — the case the cluster floor needs) **iff pivoting
the junction is cheaper than bending the arm it joins**:

  **κ_θ < 3B/L_arm**

where κ_θ is the junction's angular stiffness, B the arm's flexural rigidity, and L_arm = (N/2)·d the arm
length. This replaces "is the X floppy?" with a computable inequality.

## The numbers — and they lean the wrong way

Arm flexural rigidity from the E_ee shell (the corpus's designated bending layer), B = (E_ee/d³)·I with I ≈
(π/4)(w/2)⁴ ≈ 0.79 fm⁴ → **B ≈ 0.71 MeV·fm**. The flexibility threshold:

| N (arm) | L_arm | 3B/L_arm |
|---|---|---|
| 28 | 14 fm | 0.15 MeV |
| 14 | 7 fm | 0.30 MeV |
| 8 | 4 fm | 0.53 MeV |

Even for the **shortest** relevant arm (N≈8), flexibility requires **κ_θ ≲ 0.5 MeV — below E_ee.** But the
**natural junction scale is ≥ E_ee ≈ 0.9 MeV** (shell/knot reorganization), up to E_qq ≈ 66 MeV if the junction
is a color-continuous spine. So the natural κ_θ **exceeds** the threshold by ~2× (E_ee) to ~100× (E_qq): **the
junction leans RIGID-to-MARGINAL, not clean-flexible.**

## Consequence — the comfortable floor is not supported

Per-fusion drop → cluster floor (σ/m₀ ≈ 3.1): clean-flexible (1/8) → ~0.39 (note's ~0.8 after convolution, OK);
**marginal κ_θ~E_ee (≈1/3) → ~1.0; rigid (1/2) → ~1.6 — both in mild-to-moderate tension** with the cluster
bound (~0.7–1.0). So the computed lean puts the **cluster floor at ~1.0–1.6, not 0.8.** The round-1 note's clean
0.8 assumed a flexibility the natural junction scale does not deliver. **This makes the cluster end a live
failure point, not a comfortable margin** — and it is consistent with what worried all four reviewers.

## What partly mitigates (but does not rescue)

1. **Geometry caps κ_θ below E_qq.** Two perpendicular spines cannot both be color-continuous through one
   junction, so the fully-rigid (E_qq, color-continuous-cross) limit is geometrically excluded — the junction is
   necessarily a reorganized color knot. That removes the ~1.6 worst case but lands near the E_ee-scale
   marginal (~1.0) case, still tense.
2. **N-dependence + self-limiting.** 3B/L_arm ∝ 1/N, so **shorter arms are more flexible**, and the
   self-limiting stops fusion at short arms (N~7–14) — the late, floor-setting junctions are the most flexible
   ones (threshold ~0.3–0.5 MeV). Helpful, but still needs κ_θ ≲ 0.3–0.5 MeV, i.e. **sub-E_ee** — not
   guaranteed.

- **Penetrating-tail rate (leg 3):** a lower tail fraction → less fusion → higher σ/m. Same direction.

So the three coupled knobs are not independent risks that might cancel; **pushed within their plausible ranges
they all raise the cluster floor.** That is the honest combined picture: the candidate is *more* exposed at the
cluster end than the staged note read, not less.

## Honest verdict

cluster floor required clean-flexible junctions, and the hinge criterion shows the natural junction scale (≥

**cluster floor: ~1.0–1.5 cm²/g — mild-to-moderate tension** with Bullet/cluster bounds. The candidate is **not
