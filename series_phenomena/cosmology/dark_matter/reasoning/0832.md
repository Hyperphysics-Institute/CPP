The honest result: at a REALISTIC residual depth (f~0.05-0.2, the van-der-Waals residue) the medium does not self-bind in EITHER bracket -- both E/N>0. Self-binding (Fermi bracket) needs f_crit~0.38, i.e. a residual nearing the qDP's own color binding. So the SAME residual-force hierarchy that killed the scattering resonance in 0831 (residual < E_qDP) also holds the medium below the self-binding threshold. That is the cleanest thing in the patch: the residue being weak makes the DM both collisionless and diffuse, one fact serving two requirements. There is therefore NO self-bound saturation density; 0830's geometric rho_sat is a compression ceiling (hard core + quantum pressure), not an equilibrium -- and quantum pressure reinforces the glueball-avoidance.

Correction I owe explicitly: in the 0831 summary I suggested the energetic EoS would let "the core density fall out as a number ... the core-vs-cusp signature." That conflated two different cores. The saturation/EoS density is nuclear-scale (~1e14 g/cm3); an observed DM halo core is ~1e-24 g/cm3 -- forty orders of magnitude apart. The halo core-vs-cusp is the SIDM core (sigma/m, 0830/0831), a separate mechanism at halo density. The energetic EoS does not produce the halo core. I should not have implied it would, and I am flagging that rather than quietly dropping it.
