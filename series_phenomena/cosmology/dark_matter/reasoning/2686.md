# reasoning/2686.md — R1-L3 robustness battery (verbatim, at-patch)

STATUS: verbatim, captured at Patch 2686, 21 July 2026.

The battery's job was to try to break the envelope readout and fail
honestly. Multi-window and observable axes behaved: spreads of a few
thousandths of a fm, filtered and unfiltered extractions agreeing, size
axis exactly zero (screening-enforced convergence). The number that
matters for the packet is the grand band, 0.0901 +/- 0.0031 over 72
variants — same order as 2671's +/- 0.002, so the L3 clause of R1-PASS
("no axis blowing the band open") is satisfied on this leg's own terms.

The instructive failure was mine. I added a Hilbert demodulation as a
bonus fourth extraction and it returned garbage — 0.22 to 0.65 fm. My
first impulse was to quietly drop it; the breach discipline exists for
that impulse. Diagnosis instead: the analytic-signal envelope assumes a
quasi-uniform carrier and negligible edge leakage, and the signed shell
profile violates both — the staggering is a zone-boundary, DIRECTIONAL
structure, so its radial projection has irregular sign runs, and seven
decades of decay makes the FFT window edges dominate. The leakage-free
alternative (fit through local |f*r| maxima) gives 0.0902, concordant.
So the record carries the attempt, the diagnosis, and the exclusion
rationale in the same font as the successes: the Hilbert channel was not
in the pre-enumerated axis set, it fails its own preconditions, and it
does not touch the band. The sign-run observation is itself a small new
structural fact — the staggered component is not a radial alternation but
a directional lattice mode — which feeds directly into how I built the
J3 supplementary sums at L4.
