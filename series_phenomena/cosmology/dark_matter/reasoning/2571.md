The coherence assert firing on the first run is worth dwelling on because of what it
prevented. A 7e-5 MeV violation is invisible at every physical scale in this campaign --
the temptation was to widen the tolerance and move on, and a tolerance of 1e-3 would
have been defensible-looking. But the violation's EXISTENCE meant the W-2 estimator was
not actually computing the definition (min over a superset can never lose to min over
its subset), and an estimator that undershoots the generous well makes DEATH easier to
declare -- the dishonest direction, microscopically today, unboundedly tomorrow. The
union fix implements the definition exactly, moves W-2 only deeper, and the assert now
guards the relation structurally. Small lesson banked: when an assert fires by epsilon,
the epsilon is the symptom; the question is always which direction the defect leans.

The result I did not expect: the map is not just triage, it is the campaign's first
piece of assembly phenomenology. The electric channel having ZERO accessible cells at
the epoch rows -- dead at 15 everywhere, dead-or-marginal at 10 -- while the strong
channel stays open at moderate impact is a clean, convention-union-robust statement
about HOW assembly can start at the formation epoch: through qq contact, not through
free electric pairing. I wrote it as an evidence-shaped note for K2 and stopped there,
because the step from "free qe capture is closed" to "eCP coats attach to existing
structure instead" is exactly the kind of mechanism reading the trap clause exists to
delay until a campaign owns it. The observation will still be true when K2's prereg
consumes it; it does not need me to narrate its implications today.

Also noted with some satisfaction: 39% of the O1a production grid just died for the
price of 83 seconds of statics. The cheap-map-first ordering paid for itself before the
expensive instrument exists.
