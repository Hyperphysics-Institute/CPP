# Reasoning — Patch 2552 (the licensed 2542 revision rider)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

A rider, not a rewrite: the 2542 document remains the statics record it always was, with
the revision appended, dated, and attributed to its licensing pin — so a future reader sees
both the wide statics band and what collapsed it, in order. The one line I was most careful
with is the kT_form non-collapse. After a 132→9 MeV collapse lands on ΔE_close, the
reflex is to expect everything downstream to sharpen; the formation-temperature band does
not, because its width was never dominated by ΔE_close in the first place, and saying so
for the fourth time in four documents is cheaper than one reader ever mistaking the pin
for an epoch refinement.
