# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2953: founder registration, ZDC ruling execution path, dependency map, root-node registration

**Task provenance.** The founder's second message in the FOUNDER-Q-XI2-1 exchange: a terminology
ruling, new founder physics (the DI-bit sub-Moment relay, flagged by the founder himself as never
concluded), and a direct request for orientation ("I'm not sure where we are now… What do you
think?"). PD-006 makes the orientation answer and all sequencing decisions the worker's to give.

**Design decisions and why.**

1. **The synonymy determination gated the rewrite.** The founder's instruction was conditional —
   "change ZDC to ZBW if it is a synonym." SF-6's own definition settles it: ZDC is a chain of
   DPs each stabilized by its ZBW oscillation; the founder separately defines ZBW as the
   oscillation between oppositely charged CPs. A chain of oscillators is not the oscillation, so
   a blind ZDC→ZBW substitution would flatten a real structural distinction in a shipped paper.
   The faithful execution of BOTH halves of the ruling (no ZDC acronym; ZBW as the vocabulary
   root) is the "ZBW chain" rewrite — the acronym dies, the concept survives, the founder's word
   is the root. Recorded before executing so the determination itself is auditable.

2. **The founder's "accepted and moved on" flag became a registry object, not a footnote.** An
   unratified assumption that four open items load-bear on is exactly what the frontier registry
   exists to hold. OPEN-FP-RELAY-MECH-1 registers the two candidate mechanisms, the corpus-
   internal tension they straddle (glossary DI-bit neighbour-hopping vs glossary PSR per-Moment
   reach — both registered, never adjudicated), and CRITICAL priority with the dependency list
   explicit. FP not SD (SD is Superdeterminism); placed ahead of OPEN-FP-6-CONSTANTS, which now
   names it as the Stage A wall's true identity.

3. **The dependency map's central claim — a tree, not a cycle — was checked before it was
   written.** Apparent mutual dependence dissolves on inspection: K1-MEMORY, KINETIC-1, FP-6
   Stage A, and the ξ₂ re-entry each need the SAME object (what the relay is and what it
   stores); none of them feeds back into determining it. The only genuine two-way coupling is
   informational (FP-6 and ξ₂ cross-check each other's eventual output), which is a
   cross-citation, not a dependency loop. Saying this plainly answers the founder's actual worry:
   the programme is not circular, it is bottlenecked — and a bottleneck has a work order.

4. **Re-sequencing under PD-006.** CAND-QM-DB-1 was next by standing queue; the root node
   displaces it. The decision is recorded with its rationale (one root unblocks four dependents
   including the promotion blocker; founder attention is here now) rather than silently applied —
   the queue's audit trail matters more as the queue gets re-ordered.

5. **What was deliberately NOT done.** No adjudication of M-A vs M-B (that is the next arc's
   business, with discrimination criteria and CONV-011 timing frozen in its own prereg); no
   glossary edit (the DI-bit entry's neighbour-hopping language is part of the tension the
   adjudication must resolve — editing it now would prejudge M-A); no upgrade of the
   photon-emission picture beyond the founder's own "not validated" status; no evaluation of any
   open quantity.

**What would have been wrong.** Blind ZDC→ZBW substitution (conditional violated); registering
the relay question inside the ξ₂ arc (it is bigger than the arc that surfaced it); presenting
the map as if the density campaign blocks promotion (it does not — conflating the two paths is
exactly the disorientation the founder reported); making the re-sequencing silently.
