(per-source bookkeeping) until translation invariance collapses it:
the directed relay from any origin is the same front kernel W_R
translated, so the whole field update is one convolution per Moment

why G1 is a genuine question rather than a formality: the A1 shell
and the A2 directed front are DIFFERENT kernels, and emergent
Coulomb must be re-earned, not inherited.
