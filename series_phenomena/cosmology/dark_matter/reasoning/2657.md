# Reasoning — Patch 2657 (a prereg whose predictions were already frozen, and the one gate that can stop it)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The only substantive decision here was what NOT to include. No escalation
rungs: the arc's job is to discriminate two mechanism accounts, and a
dt-unstable holdout cell is itself discriminating data (instability at
{1/400,1/800} is what FB-MECH-B looks like), so an escalation ladder would
blur the very signal the arc exists to read. And no third mechanism class:
the temptation to add an "FB-MECH-C mixed" reading that catches everything
was resisted because a reading class that cannot fail is not a reading.

The pin gate is deliberately brutal — nine cells reproduced to the printed
digit before anything counts. After the 2631/2638 lesson (an instrument that
disagrees with itself draws no walls) and the J4 sextet's first clean
campaign, the cheapest insurance is making the registered row the key to the
lock. The sequencing disclosure follows 2588's example: the prereg is
motivated by a seen negative, the predictions predate the instrument runs,
and both facts are stated in the same paragraph so no auditor has to
reconstruct which came first.
