# Reasoning — Patch 2546 (ENDBOND-1 R-A: walk finds the strong-sector pairwise form unregistered — Branch I; electric partial banks anti-bound)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The temptation this patch was built to resist was obvious from the first read of the
artifacts: the 2455 registration hands you a smeared-Coulomb form whose r=0 value IS the
102 MeV contact lock, and the geometry hands you exactly four same-corner axial qq pairs
sitting at r = D inside the dance-reach radius. Multiply four by the saturated form at
r = 1.15 and you get a number in the low 200s — inside nothing, near nothing registered,
but a NUMBER, and the urge to "just see" it was real. Computing it would have been the
precise invention the prereg forbids: the form is in-lineage, but the pair set, the duty,
and the static applicability are not, and any choice among them is mine, not the corpus's.
A labeled "illustrative conditional" would still have anchored every future reader —
including the founder whose adjudication the blocker now awaits — toward whichever gate it
happened to fall near. So it does not exist, anywhere, including in this file beyond this
sentence's refusal to write it.

The walk itself was cleaner than expected. The three questions all resolved from artifact
text: (i) resolved AGAINST computability — 2450 says in its own scope note that the binding
is the strong-sector registry and that it is unmodeled, and 2455's color-attraction language
lives entirely in the targeting rules, never in an energy sum. That the two artifacts agree
on where the gap is made the Branch-I call easy to make BEFORE running anything, which is
why the code prints the determination before the lattice sum: the blindness protection here
is not against the gates (no depth exists to compare) but against the electric partial's
sign contaminating the walk verdict. It didn't get the chance.

The electric partial coming out ANTI-bound is, on reflection, the most physically
informative thing in the patch. Under the switched-pair average, alternating parity puts
opposite pattern-charges on the axial qq shell, and opposite charges are repulsive in the
like-attract convention — so the very arrangement the founder identifies as the stacking
configuration is the one the electric statics mildly disfavors. Either the stack is bound by
the color channel against a small electric penalty (the 2450 scope note's claim, now with a
number: the penalty is only 2–4 MeV), or the sign convention's applicability to the
strong-superposition channel is itself part of what the missing registration must specify.
Both readings route to the same place: the founder's physical specification of the static
strong bond. The 2545 handover asked for exactly this input before this patch existed; the
campaign has now converted that invitation into the single blocking item with a
zero-decision drop-in contract.

One small satisfaction: the 2540 "90° offset = alternating parity" identity, which entered
the corpus as a founder statement, is now numerically exact (10⁻⁹ MeV agreement between the
rotated-pattern and charge-flip stacks). A capture became a check.
