# Reasoning — Patch 2553 (FORM-L-1 pre-registration: the L-scan)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The founder asked for the kinetic limb, and the honest first finding is that the kinetic
limb as chartered inside RODCLOSE-1 is already a closed book: 2542 walked R-B to emptiness
and R-C was Branch I by expectation. Re-executing a closed route to appear responsive would
be motion without progress. What HAS changed since 2542 is the instrument inventory —
ENDBOND-3 demonstrated that the dance resolves closure energetics at the ~10 MeV level on
full-size scaffolds — and the genuinely new question that instrument can reach is the
L-dependence. So the recommendation the founder approved resolves into this campaign.

The physics bet, stated before the run: the 2551 curve showed the ring's cohesion is
collective and geometry-mediated, and the known cost term that worsens as rings shrink is
inner-edge compression (0.83 vs 1.15 fm already at L=16). If that cost overtakes the
collective gain somewhere below 16, the dance will show closure going unfavorable at small
L — a lower cutoff derived from equilibrium energetics alone, no rates. If instead
E_close(L) stays positive and monotone all the way down to L=8, then the formation side
simply does not select at dance strength, and saying so cleanly is worth as much as the
cutoff would be: it would localize the entire selection burden onto the stability side
(where OPEN-SS-43 already carries it) plus NB-S3a-1, and stop the programme from hunting a
formation-side selection that is not there.

The circularity fence matters most here. The kT_form band is conditional on L=16; the kT·L
relation ties temperature to length. Let either into this campaign and the L-question eats
its own tail. Both are named as not-consumed, which keeps this a pure energetics scan whose
only contact with 16 is the single frozen sentence at the end.

Odd L being structurally excluded (parity seam clash + element integrity) went into the
prereg as a recorded fact rather than a silent grid choice, because it is itself a small
piece of the "why sixteen" answer: whatever the window turns out to be, the substrate only
offers even rungs.
