Even if the qDP mass estimate is off by 1-2 orders (it is an [est] from the confinement scale, not a derived value), it stays deeply cold -- a 3 MeV qDP would still be ~1000x above the keV bound. So unlike R1/R2, the conclusion is not fragile to the mass uncertainty.
