## FOUNDER CONTRIBUTION (verbatim — TLA, 2026-07-08)
> Please begin the frame adoption and the 20-July question.
*Context:* gated the two founder-level processes open. "Begin," not "decide" — this
patch is the structuring of both decisions into documents with options, consequences,
recommendations, and empty decision blocks. The blocks stay empty until they carry his
words; that is not ceremony, it is the mechanism by which the programme's claims
remain his.

Two design choices worth preserving. First, both documents state the honest floor
before the options — the set of things the papers must carry under ANY decision,
because the repository already contains them. I wrote that section first deliberately:
options are only meaningful above the floor, and a founder choosing between R0 and R2
should never be under the impression that some option lets the correction chain or the
dual-frame status quietly disappear. Second, the frame document forces the Clause 1
re-draft into the same decision block. The 2339 proposal was written for a
mechanism-hunting arc; the arc has become a population program, and an exit condition
aimed at the wrong hypothesis is worse than none — it would let the programme claim
falsifiability while testing nothing. If the founder adopts F3, his exit clause should
say what would convince him the population picture is wrong: formation refusing to
make dimers, the satellite counter-check biting, S(N) failing derivation. His words,
his hypothesis, his exit.

On the recommendations themselves: F3 and R2 are the same instinct applied twice —
release what is finished, gate what is moving, and never let the two borrow each
other's status. DM-2 earned its date; the population section has not yet earned one,
and the fastest way for it to earn one is the formation check, which is why it appears
in both documents' first weeks. I notice, reading back across thirteen patches in two
days, that the arc's every turning point came from refusing a premature closure in
one direction or the other — the kill was not softened, and then the kill's frame was
not spared. The two documents on the founder's desk are that same refusal, written as
governance.
