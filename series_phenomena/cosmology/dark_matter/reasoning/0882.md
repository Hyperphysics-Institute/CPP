## The strategy

The panel's RESTATE granted the single-eDP energy argument (V₀ ≪ E_eDP) but required three field-theory
alternatives be *excluded*: metastable surface states, collective surface modes, induced condensates. The
derivation closes all three using two grounded substrate facts: (1) the matter sector is gapped at
E_eDP = 88 MeV (the pair/creation threshold = the supercritical-Z scale); (2) the only gapless Sea mode is
the photon, sourced by net charge (SR.md). Plus the object: the Cross-Rod surface is charge- and
color-neutral (genesis 0880).

## The three closures (code/0882)

- **Metastable single-particle states:** sub-critical (V₀/E_eDP ~ 6e-4 ≪ 1) ⇒ in-gap level stays virtual ⇒
  reversible (in m_unit), no real population. Standard Schwinger/supercritical-Z.
- **Collective mode softening:** matter modes gapped at E_eDP; weak well shifts them ~V₀²/E_eDP ~ 4e-7 of the
  gap ⇒ cannot reach zero. The gapless photon is charge-sourced ⇒ a neutral surface decouples at k→0, and
  the finite-k photon is propagating (ℏck>0), not a condensation channel. No soft channel.
- **Induced condensate:** even granting an attractive channel, BCS gap Δ ~ E_eDP·exp(−E_eDP/V₀) with inverse
  coupling ~900–2600 ⇒ Δ/E_eDP ~ 10⁻⁴⁰⁰…10⁻¹¹⁰⁰ ⇒ non-extensive, negligible vs m_unit. Weak coupling is not
  an extra assumption — V₀ ≪ E_eDP *is* weak coupling.

## Why this is more than the assumption it replaces

The key move is that the **neutrality of the surface** (from the genesis) removes the only gapless channel:
the photon needs net charge to be sourced, and the Cross-Rod has none at long range. So the response lives
entirely in the *gapped* matter sector, where a sub-threshold well is perturbative and every alternative is
either forbidden (sub-critical) or exponentially suppressed (BCS). The supercritical-Z analogy the reviewer
invoked is not just an analogy here — E_eDP is literally the substrate's pair threshold.

## Honest residual
