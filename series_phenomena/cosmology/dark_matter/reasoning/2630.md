# Reasoning — Patch 2630 (drawing the convention's walls with the corrected launcher only)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The design decision that took the longest was the launcher. The registered chain
lineage is mixed: stages 5-6 historically ran through the fixed-column
stage_capture -- the exact protocol whose absolute aim produced the 2612 artifact
-- while the corrected work runs through the centroid-relative scan_cell. A
sensitivity study that perturbs aim cannot be built on a launcher that mis-aims by
construction, so scan_cell carries every stage, stage_capture is excluded by
citation, and the price is honest: C-R-prime must show the uniform aimed
instrument completes the registered chain before any perturbed chain means
anything. If it does not complete, that is itself a finding about the lineage's
mixed protocols, and it will be read under RC4, not smoothed over.

RR2 is written as the expected class and explicitly NOT adverse, because the
epistemics here differ from the DISC arc: DISC asked whether a registered quantity
was trustworthy (a pass/fail question about the instrument), while ROB-1 asks
where a convention-scoped claim's walls sit (a measurement question with no bad
answer except an undrawable one). Pre-declaring that a finite wall is the honest
shape prevents tomorrow's reader -- or reviewer -- from mistaking a boundary for a
failure, and prevents tonight's author from nudging the grid to avoid finding one.

Perturbations apply to every stage uniformly because that is the realistic error
class: a mis-calibrated protocol mis-aims every launch the same way. Per-stage
independent perturbation would be a different (combinatorially larger) cell and a
different question -- resilience to noise rather than to systematic error -- and
it can be declared later if the envelope turns out interesting at its walls.
