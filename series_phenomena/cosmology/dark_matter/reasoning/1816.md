## The question v_pen answers

The five collisional mechanisms (1815 §4) split on one gate: **does a transverse collision drive the qCP cores
through their mutual eCP coats into contact?** If yes, the qq-contact channels open (chain-switching / glueball
/ X-bond, mech 3/4/5). If no, the coat holds and the physics is the **bending fork** (bend-fracture vs
crack-arrest, mech 1/2). v_pen is the relative velocity that just reaches qq contact; comparing it to DM
velocities **computes** the open "does the coat hold?" fork rather than guessing it — the point of doing this
calc first.

## The calculation (no new knobs — every input pinned)

Local per-contact energy balance: ½ μ v_pen² = E_barrier ⇒ **v_pen = √(2 E_barrier / μ)**.
- **E_barrier = κ_b · E_ee**, the coat barrier traced to the pinned eCP-shell bond **E_ee ≈ 0.9 MeV** (1813) —
  the energy scale holding the coat in place. κ_b ~ O(1), band [0.5, 4] (½ a coat bond to a few coat bonds).
- **μ = local colliding mass / 2.** Penetration is a fast, *local* event — only the locally impacted material
  participates (the rest of the rod is causally disconnected on the contact timescale). Central: one element
  pair, μ = m_element/2. Band: qCP-core-only (lighter → higher v_pen) … a few elements (heavier → lower v_pen).
- **m_element = 8 m_qCP + 8 m_eCP = 4 qDP + 4 eDP = 4·264 + 4·88 = 1408 MeV** (0886; corpus 1–2 GeV).

**The eCP coat holds across dwarf → typical-cluster velocities** by a comfortable margin (central ~8× at

at the most pessimistic O(1) assumptions, penetration could become marginally active; everywhere else it is
strongly suppressed.

## What this decides (the phase-space partition)

**The qq-contact channels — chain-switching, glueball, X-bond (mech 3/4/5) — are velocity-suppressed across the

crack-arrest.** The v_pen gate has therefore *selected the next calculation*: calc #2 is the bending-fracture

## Consistency with the existing 0860 ledger (and why it is coherent, not contradictory)

0860 found whole-rod collision energies reaching the bond scale at cluster velocities (the velocity-dependent
fragmentation DM-1 v1.0 reports qualitatively). That is the **collective, lever-arm-amplified beam energy** of
the whole rod — a different quantity from the **local per-contact** penetration energy computed here. The two
are consistent and in fact complementary: the whole rod can store enough bending energy to fracture at the
outer-fiber E_ee, while *local* per-contact energy cannot drive cores through the coat. So **fragmentation, if
it happens at cluster velocities, proceeds by bending (outer-fiber E_ee), not by local qq penetration** — which
is exactly the bending picture, and it is why v_pen holding does *not* contradict DM-1's velocity-dependent
signature. The signature survives; its *mechanism* is bending, now grounded.

## Honest caveats (the O(1) content)

- **κ_b** (coat barrier in units of E_ee) and the **local participating mass** are both O(1)-uncertain; the
  *qualitative* partition (coat holds dwarf→typical-cluster) is robust to them, the *absolute* v_pen is not.
- The local-mass assumption (only the impacted region participates on the contact timescale) is physically
  standard for impact but not derived here; a dynamic impact treatment is the refinement.
- The barrier is modeled as the static coat-bond scale; the true penetration barrier is a dynamic
  push-through, which the static E_ee approximates. These are the same O(1) refinements that the bending-fork
  calc (#2) will need to make quantitative.
