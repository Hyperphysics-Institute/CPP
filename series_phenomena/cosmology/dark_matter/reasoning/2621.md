# Reasoning — Patch 2621 (the control that refused to flatter the discriminator)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The temptation here was strong and specific: C2's Sea leg passed at machine zero,
C1 was bitwise exact, C3 was clean, and the Edrift number "obviously" looks like
integrator baseline rather than physics — so why not read M1–M5 and note the Edrift
matter in passing? Because the prereg says controls gate, and because the last time
this lane let a plausible story stand in for a discriminating measurement it spent
three patches un-registering a shell that never existed. The 25 MeV is PROBABLY
truncation error accumulating in the absence of the sink's constant drain — but
"probably" is an hypothesis, the dt-scaling is its test, and the corrected gate must
be designed from the measured scaling, not from the story that makes tonight
shortest.

What the failure actually teaches: FLOOR = 2.0 was born in the G-SP admission
battery — small oscillation about equilibrium, sink ON, TC = 60. I transferred it to
a 5-body transit at TC = 120 with the sink OFF, which is close to the worst case for
bare-integrator drift on stiff Morse wells: large excursions, no dissipation, twice
the window. The gate wasn't wrong to exist — an OFF-null needs an energy-integrity
leg, otherwise the ON/OFF comparison is contaminated by an instrument that leaks —
but its threshold was borrowed from a different regime without checking that the
regime transfer was licensed. That is precisely the class of unexamined assumption
DISC-1 exists to hunt in the deposit itself, so there is something fitting about the
discriminator's own prereg being the first thing it caught.

One bank for the corrected prereg: the C2 cell classified SCA at v = 0.95 with
η = 0 — the OFF instrument at high γ scatters where the ON instrument captures.
That is raw disclosure only (nothing reads from a stopped cell), but it flags that
M4's ON/OFF table is likely to be the most physically loaded member when it finally
runs, and the corrected prereg should keep its frozen readings exactly as
non-committal as 2620 wrote them.
