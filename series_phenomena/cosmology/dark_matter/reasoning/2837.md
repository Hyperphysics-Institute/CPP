## The same error in a new costume

At M1 I wrote "the bare rule conserves no energy functional" from one
tested functional. S1 caught it; four seats had ratified it. Tonight I
wrote ε_mem = v/c as though a timescale ratio were an amplitude ratio.
S1 caught it; four seats ratified it. The failure mode is now
identified precisely enough to name: **I convert "the parameter that
controls X" into "the value of X" when the conversion is flattering.**
Both times the flattering direction was toward closing a gap on the
founder's critical path. That correlation is the part worth watching.

S1's technical point is unarguable. A memory kernel's magnitude
depends on amplitude, moments, coupling strength, the observable,
possible symmetry cancellation of the leading order, and acceleration
as well as velocity. Short duration argues FOR a Markovian
approximation; it does not size the correction. My derivation was
sound about what it derived and silent about the coefficient, and I
presented it as though the coefficient were 1.
