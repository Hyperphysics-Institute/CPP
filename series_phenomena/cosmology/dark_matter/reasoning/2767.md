# Reasoning — Patch 2767 (core-occupancy founder ruling: record + enactment)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.**

The founder's answer does the thing the adjudication hoped it would:
it moves the 3-1-1 split not by adding a fourth opinion but by
supplying the ontological fact the three opinions were implicitly
disagreeing about. The α′ argument's 59% was never arithmetic in
dispute — GPT independently reproduced 1/7.5761 — it was the premise
that r < a contains nothing that responds. The founder says the
opposite, in his own physical language, twice: DPs were there before
the ring transited, nothing swept them out, and polarization
gradients form around every charge including inside the core. The
enactment therefore rejects the premise, not the calculation: the
site branch survives as a sensitivity bound, which is the honest
residual role of a correctly-computed answer to a wrongly-posed
matching.

Two judgment calls in how I consumed the ruling. First, I resisted
the temptation to let the ruling collapse the pair outright. The
S1 position (neither matching is grounded until chi is DERIVED) is
structurally correct even after the ruling: "occupied and responding"
licenses the continuum matching as leading order, but the founder
himself immediately supplies the complication — the near-core
gradient profile is a superposition of oppositely-signed curves, so
chi(r) is not the bulk value near the sites. That complication is
not a nuisance footnote; it IS the correction term, and the founder
stating it in physical language converts the FA-C2 deliverable from
an open-ended derivation into a specified calculation. The pair
collapses when that lands, with its leading order now fixed. Second,
the Grok-assisted material needed status care. The founder circulated
my question through a seat and brought back polished formulations
plus his own correction of them. The correction is the valuable part
— "opposite-charge CPs" was a subtle distortion (the medium is the
both-charge Sea; the gradient is an asymmetry, not a one-species
cloud), and the founder catching it is exactly the arrangement
working: the seat sharpens language, the founder owns the physics.
The record keeps his words canonical and marks the rewrites
secondary-as-corrected, so no future reader mistakes a Grok phrasing
for the ontology.

One meta-note: the seat that argued the founder's mechanism implies
core occupancy (S5, at the adjudication) was RIGHT about the physics
question even while its verification claims in the same return were
fabricated. Both facts go in the record with equal weight. Grading
arguments on merit and evidence on provenance, separately, is the
whole discipline; this patch is a clean instance of the first half
paying off for the seat that failed the second.
