## The setup

A long Cross-Rod struck transversely at mid-length (Thomas's "two rods collide mid-length, opposite directions,
90°") is a **three-point bending impact**. Bending strain is largest at the outer fiber, where the **E_ee
shell** bonds sit, and near-zero at the neutral axis, where the **E_qq core** sits — which is *why E_ee governs
the bending-fracture threshold even though E_qq is ~73× stronger* (Thomas's bending-stiffness reassignment,
1815). Two velocity thresholds, both from pinned quantities + one O(1) energy-focusing factor g.

## (A) Initiation — v_init(N): the outer shell cracks

The whole-rod transverse collision energy ½(N m_element/2)v² focuses into bending at the impact section; the
outer-fiber crack initiates when it reaches the breaking energy ~ g·E_ee:

  **v_init(N) = 2√(g·E_ee/(N·m_element)) ∝ 1/√N** — **longer rods initiate at lower velocity** (Thomas's "long
  rods break easier," quantified).

With E_ee = 0.9 MeV, m_element = 1408 MeV (0886), g ~ 1: v_init ≈ **6800 km/s (N=5) → 1500 km/s (N=100) → 875
km/s (N=300)**. So for the DM-relevant range (N ~ 5–60 from N_dwarf, up to ~100s for ℓ_p), **v_init sits in the
cluster band and ~30–100× above dwarf velocities (~60 km/s)**. Dwarfs are below v_init for all N (→ cores);
clusters reach v_init for the longer rods (N ≳ 30–60 at 2000–4000 km/s). This **reproduces the dwarf-cores /
cluster-active split mechanistically** — the qualitative DM-1 signature, now from bending rather than assumed.
(g is the one O(1) knob; v_init ∝ √g, so the *precise* cluster threshold shifts ~3× over g ∈ [0.1,10], but the
qualitative split — dwarf cores, cluster active for long rods — is robust to it.)

## (B) Propagation vs arrest — v_through(N): does the crack cut the core?

Once the outer crack initiates, running it **through** the E_qq core (clean bisection, mode 1) must supply the
core cut energy ~ n_w²·E_qq:

  **v_through(N) = 2 n_w√(E_qq/(N·m_element))**,  **v_through/v_init = n_w√(E_qq/(g·E_ee)) ≈ 17–34×**.

With E_qq = 66 MeV, n_w ~ 3 (w ≈ 2 fm, 0860): v_through ≈ **22,000 km/s (N=300) → 71,000 km/s (N=30) → 174,000
km/s (N=5)** — **far above any DM velocity, at every N.** The ~73× E_ee→E_qq toughness jump means the crack
**arrests at the core**. Even allowing a large dynamic-fracture-efficiency correction (a dynamic crack runs with
less than the static cut energy), v_through ≫ cluster is robust.

## The result — the scission-mode-vs-velocity curve (first piece)

| velocity regime | mode | where |
|---|---|---|
| v < v_init(N) (~1500–6800 km/s) | elastic / **cores** | **dwarfs** (~60), all N |
| v_init < v < v_through | **shell-stripping / crack-arrest (mode 2)**, number-conserving damage | **clusters** (~2000–4000), N ≳ 30 |
| v > v_through (~10⁵ km/s) | clean **fragmentation (mode 1)** | **never** in DM halos |

**The operative cluster mode is SHELL-STRIPPING, not clean fragmentation.** A cluster collision cracks the outer
E_ee shell on the tension side but the crack arrests at the 73×-tougher E_qq core — the rod is damaged
(loses shell), not bisected.

## The honest consequence — a DM-1 mechanism revision (flag, do NOT fold)

DM-1 v1.0's shipped signature is "fragments at cluster velocities (→ collisionless), intact at dwarf (→ cores),
σ/m falls with v." This calc **confirms the dwarf-cores end and the velocity-dependent threshold**, but
**revises the cluster mechanism from clean fragmentation to shell-stripping**:
- The *qualitative* signature (cores at dwarf; velocity-dependent activity onset in the cluster band) **survives
  and is now mechanistic**.
- The *cluster mechanism* changes: rods shed their eCP shell rather than bisecting. Whether this still yields
  **σ/m falling with v** is **plausible but not shown** — stripping reduces the interaction range (lower σ) but
  also sheds ~25% of the mass (the 8-eCP shell = 4 eDP = 352 MeV of 1408), so the net σ/m direction needs the
  *stripped-rod* cross-section. And true rod **shortening** (which the σ/m ∝ N story used) now requires
  **accumulated** shell damage / glueball weak-points (mode 4) over many collisions, not a single hit.

This is a **flag for DM-1, not a fold**: it touches the v1.0 mechanism description and is founder-gated. I do
not edit DM-1 or claim the σ/m(v) signature survives quantitatively — that is the next calc.

## Honest caveats (the O(1) content)

- **g** (energy-focusing fraction + bending geometry + the bond breaking-strain absorbed into it) is the one
  un-pinned O(1) input; v_init ∝ √g. The *qualitative* mode curve is robust to it; the precise cluster onset N
  is not.
- **Dynamic-fracture efficiency** on v_through is an O(1)–O(10) uncertainty, but v_through ≫ cluster is robust
  to it (the 73× toughness jump dominates).
- **Continuum beam theory** at the substrate scale is marginal for short rods (N ~ 5–10); the long-rod regime
  (the relevant one) is cleaner.
- **Single-collision** only — cumulative damage (mode 4) is the route to eventual true fragmentation and is not
  modeled here.
