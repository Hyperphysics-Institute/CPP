## Thomas's read (sense, verbatim points)

He confirmed the scissor mode is **considerably softer**, for concrete structural reasons:
- The longitudinal stiffness is the **E_qq compression between face-to-face 8qCP cubes** — close, face-to-face,
  steep SSV gradient.
- The hinge/scissor bonds sit **farther apart**: two eDP coat pairs on each side connected *across the hinge*
  (not through the face-to-face core), and qDP pairs nearer the hinge but still **farther than the face-to-face
  8qCPs** and at **variable distance because of the hinge**.
- Therefore "**the SSV gradients will be much higher between the E_qq of the face-bound 8qCPs than the qDPs out
  on the hinge, and even less gradient between the eDPs at the farthest end of the lever**" — i.e. **considerably
  softer gradients, and variable along the lever arm.**

## Why this is the lever — and the number

The ponderomotive stiffness goes as the **SSV/field gradient squared**, and the gradient falls steeply with
charge separation r. So "farther apart" is not a small correction — it is a strong suppression. Writing f =
(d/r_hinge)^p for gradient-squared falloff laws p (field 1/r² → grad² up to 1/r⁶ → p up to 6; softer SSV laws
give smaller p), even a **modest separation ratio r_hinge ~ 2d** (vs the face-to-face core's ~d) gives **f ~
0.05–0.25**. With the hinge torque dominated by the coat eDP pairs (E_ee intrinsic, softened by distance):

  **κ_θ ~ f · E_ee ~ 0.05–0.23 MeV** — **sub-E_ee, and below the flexibility threshold** (3B/L_arm ≈ 0.30 MeV at
  N = 14) across the whole range.

A further reinforcement from Thomas's geometry: the near-hinge qDP pairs, at 90°, are **beyond color range
(R_color ~ d)**, so they interact *electrically* (weak), not via color — so even the "stronger" near-hinge
contribution is sub-E_qq, consistent with a soft hinge. So κ_θ < threshold → the hinge is flexible → per-fusion
drop ~1/8–1/4 → **cluster floor ~0.4–0.8 → VIABLE.**
