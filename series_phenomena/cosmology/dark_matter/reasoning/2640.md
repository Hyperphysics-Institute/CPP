# Reasoning — Patch 2640 (eight for eight on the sign of nothing, and a funnel that obeys its well)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The F2b table is the most satisfying kind of diagnosis: not "consistent with"
the preregistered hypothesis but isomorphic to it. Every OFF cell from b=1 to
b=7 sits at d_inc ≈ 1.0–1.3 — bound beyond any argument — and the registered
classifier's verdict flips CAP/UNR in perfect lockstep with the sign of a
residue eleven orders of magnitude below the physics. Eight cells, eight
correlations, including the honest scatter at b=8. The 2624 "patchwork" was
never a physics texture; it was the classifier reading tea leaves in
floating-point dust. What makes this registerable rather than embarrassing is
the order of operations: the hypothesis went into the prereg before any cell
ran, the fix got its own conservatism control, and the control caught a bonus —
the REGISTERED w=4 bracket's b=5 CAP itself rests on a +5.7e-13 residue. The
number survives (the coin landed right), but the exposure is now written down
where a future auditor will find it.

The steep-width upper bound refusing to draw is the ROB-2 discipline paying
rent the same night it was restated: b=10 flips SCA→CAP across the halving, so
no wall, floor raised to 9D, successor declared. I felt the pull to run the
{1/200,1/400} union on the spot — sixty more seconds of compute — and did not,
because no escalation rung was frozen for F2a and an off-prereg run is an
off-prereg run regardless of how cheap it is. The rung goes in the successor's
prereg where it belongs.

The result I did not expect and like best: the soft width out-reaches the steep
one ([10,12) vs ≥9-with-a-marginal-10), in exactly the ordering the analytic
wells demanded all along (4.5D vs 2.5D). When an emergent numeric boundary
tracks a zero-freedom analytic ordering without being asked, that is the
substrate being coherent, and it is worth a registered observation even with no
claim promoted on it.

RF1 extending to the soft width closes a small asymmetry that has irritated me
since 2624: the funnel classification was the record's cleanest survivor on the
DEP-1 map, but only at one width. Now the classification and the width-universal
existence claim have matching scope, each with its own dt-stable legs.
