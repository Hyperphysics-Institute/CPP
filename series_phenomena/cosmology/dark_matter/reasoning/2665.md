## The substantive reasoning, preserved

**The nearest-neighbor trap.** The scoping's most important finding was
negative-space: the obvious way to build "the discrete Sea response operator"
— gap times identity plus a nearest-neighbor graph Laplacian — would quietly
assume the answer to the question the operator exists to decide. A
nearest-neighbor Laplacian encodes edge-locality of the response; its Green
function decay is naturally expressed in hop units; the fork would be tilted
toward the edge reading before a single physical input entered. The
registered mechanism says otherwise: the Perceive summation runs over local
AND distant GPs with 1/r² shell superposition (founder verbatim at 2662;
SSV_net glossary definition). So the kernel's range is not a modeling
convenience — it is physics the derivation must produce. I registered this as
P1's controlling clause ("NO locality assumption imported") because the
majority's J3 finding at 2664 was precisely that the phase-localization
argument lacked quantitative form; the way to honor that finding is to make
the operator construction itself carry the burden of proof, under the same
blind-session protocol class that protected the SF-6 inertia derivation.

**Why the instrument demonstration computes so little.** The temptation was
to demonstrate the full machinery — build the graph, pick a symbolic gap,
show decay-length-versus-gap curves for a nearest-neighbor kernel and a 1/r²
kernel. I cut everything after the Laplacian spectrum sanity check.
Decay-versus-gap curves for two kernel families would let the worker (me,
next session) pre-see which kernel family lands which ℓ-scale for which gap
scale — and then P2's "identify the gap from the mechanism, not from which
one lands a preferred ℓ" clause would be compromised before the charter
freezes. The demonstration proves exactly what scoping needs proven — the
graph is constructible from the icosian registration, the coordination
matches SF-4's z = 12, the spectrum is computable — and nothing that
narrows the blind.

**The gap contest (P2).** Three registered candidates surfaced, and I listed
them without ranking: the DP internal bindings (E_qDP = 264 / E_eDP = 88, the
0880/0886 constituents that already earned a zero-parameter cross-check at
2452), the bound-CP oscillator static limit (the (ħω_A)² form, E_qq-carrying),
and the 2443 bond curvature (also E_qq-carrying). The mechanism question that
separates them: what does a static polarization pattern in the FREE Sea
actually stretch — the internal structure of Sea DPs (candidate a), or a
bound-CP configuration that only exists inside elements (candidates b/c)?
Stated that way the answer looks like (a), and I deliberately did not
register that leaning: the FA-C3 lesson two patches old is that a plausible
mechanism reading registered at strength invites a 3–2 demotion, while a
question registered cleanly invites a derivation. The contest goes to the
chartered session, where the identification will be argued from the PCD cycle
with the founder's picture on the record.

kernel and gap share one energy scale, window-banded if not — and that which

would deliver a zero-parameter βd — exactly the class of claim the programme's
swarm methodology values most and exactly the class that must be computed
under a frozen charter, not discovered in a scoping patch.
