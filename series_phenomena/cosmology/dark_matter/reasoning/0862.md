## What this resolves and what it newly threatens
1. **The hTetra risk flips sign.** It was "possibly fatally floppy." The pinned hinge makes it stiff; the new risk is *over*-stiff (ℓ_p overshoots the window). The same single number decides it — the per-hinge angular tolerance — now from the other side.
2. **The leading extended-relic candidate is the 4-wide CROSS,** not the bare ribbon. The cross is simultaneously the stiffest (top of the stiffness ladder) and the most collapse-resistant (central strip shielded). The bare ribbon is mostly a transient that either collapses to a glueball or, in a minority of cases, loops before contact. This sharpens the geometry target.
3. **A new, quantitative threat: the dilution tax.** If glueball population is high (Thomas's lean), a large mass fraction sits in compact, cocoon-arrested blobs with σ/m ~ 0.11 (the dead 0859 value). The present-day cross-section is then a *population-weighted average*, σ_eff = f·σ_ext + (1−f)·σ_glue. This was not in 0860/0861 and it can fail the program even if a stiff extended species exists.

## What the script computes (numbers)
- **(G1) Angular tolerance.** Wormlike link ℓ_p = ℓ_rung/⟨θ²⟩ ⇒ θ_rms = √(2ℓ_rung/ℓ_p). For ℓ_p ∈ [100, 700] fm the pinned hinge must hold its dihedral to **θ_rms ≈ 3–8° per hinge**. Tighter than ~3° overshoots 700 fm (loops too large/rare, population stays open, σ/m slides to the floppy constant).
- **Stiffness ladder → geometry-selection rule.** ℓ_p = g_geom·ℓ_p0, with g_geom the geometric second-moment multiplier (illustrative {hTetra 1, ribbon 10, cross 40}; real values = a second-moment-of-area calc, to be done) and ℓ_p0 the base persistence set by the *common* edge-bond SSV strength. The base-ℓ_p0 windows that put each candidate in [100,700] fm are **hTetra [100,700], ribbon [10,70], cross [2.5,17.5]** — they barely overlap. So **one substrate edge-bond strength sets ℓ_p AND selects which geometry is the viable relic**; the candidates do *not* co-occupy the window → adding geometries added **no** free parameter. If the bond is so weak the cross is still floppy, all fail; so strong the hTetra overshoots, all fail.
- **(G3-tax) Dilution.** Required extended mass-fraction f to reach the data band (σ_glue = 0.11): to reach **0.6** needs f ≈ 1.0 at σ_ext=0.6, 0.55 at σ_ext=1, 0.26 at σ_ext=2, 0.10 at σ_ext=5. To reach **2.0** is impossible unless σ_ext > 2 (then f = 1.0 at σ_ext=2, 0.39 at σ_ext=5, 0.19 at σ_ext=10). **The tax:** extended species must *both* exceed the target individually *and* hold a large mass fraction. A new constraint on the formation branching ratios.
