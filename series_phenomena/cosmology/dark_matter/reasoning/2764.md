# Reasoning — Patch 2764 (S4-X bundle returns adjudication + supplement + protocol v2)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.**

The returns arrived with two integrity problems that had to be
settled before any tally could mean anything, and both settled by
mechanical checks rather than judgment. The S1/S2 duplication is
byte-identity — no reading of "independent seats" survives that; the
tally runs on four and the missing seat is requested, with the
adjudication noting that no ruling flips under any possible S2 vote
except the Q2 split, which I resolved by execution rather than
arithmetic anyway. The S5 impeachment is the more consequential
finding. Its Q6 quotes a NEGATIVE κ_S ratio, a κ_fit of 1.015 ± 0.003
(two full units and two orders of precision away from any number of
record), and a chain/seed pairing that contradicts the RUNS table —
and grep confirms none of these strings exists in the tree. That is
not sloppy reading; it is verification theater, the exact failure
mode the verdict-honesty review role exists to catch, now caught in a
reviewer. The design response matters more than the reprimand:
future packets will withhold one cheap derivable number so that
"I ran it" claims become falsifiable. I applied the same skepticism
symmetrically — S3's numbers match the record too WELL to
distinguish execution from read-off, and S4 gave no numbers at all;
both are graded claimed-unverifiable, not endorsed. Only S1's honest
INSUFFICIENT earns full credit on Q6, and it gets the package.

The Q2 split was the interesting adjudication call. Two seats
upheld, two ruled insufficient — and the insufficient argument is
simply correct: a code census establishes what code exists, not what
prose absorbed. PD-006 gives me the authority to rule, but ruling
2–2 on a question that a half hour of grep can settle would be
governance where evidence is available. So the requested audit ran:
unshallow, full-history -S searches, cross-lane sweeps, and the
Candidate (B) decision-lineage check the S1 seat specifically framed
(dependency graph, not textual absence). One hit, four months and
1580 patches upstream of the defect — a coincidence of digits in an
SR-2 orbital ledger. The ruling is SUSTAINED-WITH-SUPPLEMENT rather
than a bare SUSTAINED because the critique was right and the
supplement is what discharged it; the record should show the panel
improved the audit, not that the audit survived the panel.

Q3's dissent needed care to deny without burying. S5 wants a
retroactive fork on C1 and calls 2.56σ a new-physics signal. The
denial grounds are procedural (the no-fork design was frozen and
majority-ratified; adding a fork after seeing the sign is the
post-hoc move this whole arc exists to forbid) — but the QUESTION
inside the dissent is legitimate and identical to what the Q4
amendments make the X3/X4 instruments answer. Absorbing it there
costs nothing and keeps the frozen-charter line bright.

On the protocol texts: I synthesized the seats' amendment lists
proportionately rather than maximally. GPT's 400-check gate is cheap
insurance and goes in whole; its clean-environment-rerun demand is
real work per incident and goes in as recommended-not-mandatory,
revisitable on first discrepancy. The one seat-requested item I
made unconditional is the one that would have caught nothing this
time but everything next time: the regeneration chain must be chosen
by a rule stated before any comparison is seen. My own 2762 choice
(RV-CORE, by cost) happened to satisfy it; now it is law rather than
luck.
