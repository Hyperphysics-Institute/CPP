# Reasoning — Patches 2594 + 2595 (the constraint that writes the law, and the spec that
# confesses its holes)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (joint fragment; both patches this session).

C-SR is the prereg's spine and it was nearly a casualty of enthusiasm. My first sketch
of N2-A had a -gamma*V drag with the coefficient to be derived -- clean, familiar,
wrong. The SR track settled two months ago that capture-and-delay is REACTIVE for
uniform motion; a velocity drag would un-ground SR-1 and delete momentum conservation
in one line. Writing C-SR first means every candidate law inherits the SR grounding as
a birth constraint, and the charter's Galilean worry inverts into a PREDICTION: boosted
clusters coast, oscillations damp. If the derivation lands, that asymmetry is itself
CPP phenomenology -- inertia and dissipation as two faces of one Sea coupling,
partitioned exactly where special relativity requires.

The cross-derivation design (R-A1 vs R-A2 arriving at the coefficient from the
relaxation face and the microscopic face independently) is the strongest structure the
lineage affords: agreement would be a small swarm-node in its own right; disagreement
is a finding with a band. Either way the founder's "deepest job" phrase gets an
answer with grounds.

The replication spec cost more drafting care than any prereg this week, because its
failure mode is inverted: a prereg fails by permitting too much; a replication spec
fails by SPECIFYING too little, invisibly. Hence item (v) -- every decision a seat had
to make is a confessed hole in my specification, and I want those holes listed by the
people who fell into them. The consolidation contract deliberately does NOT require
their radius ratios to match ours: their instruments will lack the choreography layer,
and demanding our exact 0.78-0.86 would be requiring them to replicate our
IMPLEMENTATION rather than the claim. Bounded-motion bands are the claim; the decimals
are ours.

One quiet satisfaction: the gate travels. The strong-pair control, born three patches
ago from a slingshot artifact, is now the first thing two rival models will compute
about this physics. Good controls propagate; that is how you know they were controls.
