## The gate fired; then the diagnosis kept moving

V-1 failed at p = 2.683 and the first diagnosis looked tidy: the
frozen window included its own 2R edge point, and dropping it gave
1.988. Had the session stopped there, a re-prereg tonight would
have widened the window and "passed." Three further diagnostics
dismantled that tidiness in sequence: the wide-window fit steepened
again (the pinned dipole cancels its own far field); the clean
monopole-jellium redesign STILL steepened, with local slope rising
toward the midplane (the torus forces every field to zero at M/2 —
exact Coulomb included); and the quick Fourier comparator built to
control for that produced impossible slopes for exact Coulomb
itself, indicting the comparator. Each fix revealed the next
confound. The lesson is structural: a validation gate must compare
the automaton against a VERIFIED reference computed under identical
distortions, not against a free-space exponent the geometry cannot
express.

## Why no re-prereg tonight

The 2784 D1 defect was born exactly here — a constraint frozen
under momentum, one diagnostic short of understanding. The
comparator is demonstrably not yet trustworthy; freezing a
comparative band against an unverified reference would launder the
same sin through fancier machinery. The frozen clause says STOP and
re-prereg; it does not say re-prereg within the hour. The FAIL
record commits tonight with every measurement same-font; the fresh
gate freezes when the reference is validated against the free-space
law in the regime where free space is actually expressible
(r ≪ M/2, real-space Ewald ground truth).

## What is genuinely still unknown

Nothing tonight showed DR-1's relay is NOT Coulombic — and nothing
showed it is. The automaton's monopole slopes (2.14–2.64 across
windows) and exact torus-Coulomb's (unknown, comparator suspect)
have not yet been compared under a common trusted procedure. PR4's
deliverables remain untouched behind the blocked gate, which is the
system working: no Gibbs comparison will be run through a field law
nobody has verified.
