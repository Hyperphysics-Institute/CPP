## What was computed

ΔB = ½ ∫∫ n(r₁)n(r₂) V(|r₁−r₂|) d³r₁d³r₂ with the exact shell–shell angular average of the screened Yukawa
kernel (Coulomb limit reproduces the analytic uniform-sphere ⟨1/r⟩ = 6/5R to 5 decimals — sanity anchor (0)),
for both a uniform sphere (R = 1.2·A^{1/3}) and Woods–Saxon (R = 1.1·A^{1/3}, a = 0.55 fm), at every 1865

(A−2Z)²/A, 1} — the residual after refit is the detectable part.

## Three verdicts

**1. The crude estimate was ~1.6–1.8× too high.** Refined/crude ≈ 0.55–0.65 across the whole band; uniform vs
Woods–Saxon agree within 2% (profile-insensitive). At the χ point the raw refined ΔB is **0.29–0.80 MeV for
N = 12–20 — everything at or under the ~1 MeV anchor even with NO absorption credit.** (1864's crude values
were 0.45–1.25 there.) N = 5 stays in raw tension (1.5–3.2 MeV); N = 8 drops to 0.65–0.84 (safe-to-edge).

**2. Absorption voids the heavy-nucleus channel almost completely.** The smooth ΔB(A) is absorbed by the SEMF
basis to a residual of ~2×10⁻⁵ of raw (RMS ~4×10⁻⁶ MeV at physical coupling) — functionally perfect — and the
implied coefficient shifts are milli-MeV-scale, orders of magnitude inside each coefficient's independent
determination (e.g. δa_A ~ 0.02 MeV vs a_A ≈ 23.7 MeV known to ~few %; δa_C ~ 2×10⁻⁴ MeV vs mirror-nuclei
tolerance ~7×10⁻³ MeV). Collinearity caveat tagged: individual shifts are fit-unstable, but the ~zero residual
is robust. **A smooth, valley-wide binding shift of this shape is not detectable in mass fits.**

**3. The constraint MIGRATES to the np scattering length — a two-body observable with no fit freedom to hide
in — and it still enforces the χ corner.** At the χ point: δa_np = 3.7–4.6×10⁻³ fm at N = 12 (**TENSION**,
above the ~3×10⁻³ anchor; a_t = 5.4194(20) fm confirms 2–4×10⁻³ is the right scale), 2.9×10⁻³ at N = 15
(edge), 1.6–2.2×10⁻³ at N = 20 (safe). **The surviving χ corner is N ≈ 15–20 — unchanged, but now carried by
a cleaner channel, with N = 15 marginal and N ≈ 18–20 comfortable.**

## Honest accounting — the low-N leg weakens

The 1864/1865 statement "the baryon window pushes N to ≳ 12" rested on the crude ΔB WITHOUT absorption credit.
Under this refinement: (a) conservative reading (raw refined ΔB, no absorption) still disfavors N = 5 and is
marginal at N = 8-flat; (b) absorption-credited reading voids channel A entirely, and channel B (δa_np) only
excludes low-N corners at HIGH dwarf targets (N = 5 at σ_target ≥ 5; N = 8 at ≥ 10). So the generic low-N
low-target corner (N = 5–8, σ_dwarf 1–2) is **no longer robustly baryon-excluded**. The four-way convergence
statement must be restated honestly: for the **χ point specifically**, np scattering enforces N ≳ 15 and the
corner N ≈ 15–20 stands; for the **generic calibration band**, the baryon leg now bounds (N, target) jointly
rather than N alone. What survives untouched: floor ceiling (N ≲ 20), tail pruning, cluster safety, and the
χ-point convergence.

Other caveats carried: pairwise additivity (J4) still conditions everything here — a collective residual voids
both channels; the δa_np Born formula is first-order (adequate at these couplings, tagged J5); the ~3×10⁻³ fm
anchor is now pinned to the actual triplet-length uncertainty rather than order-of-magnitude.
