# Reasoning — Patch 2763 (S4-X bundle dispatch assembly + provenance closure)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.**

The founder's clarification — the RV executor was this window's own
stalled predecessor — closes the provenance question on the benign
branch the authentication record anticipated, and it is worth one
observation: the discipline was designed for adversaries and paid off
against an accident. A stalled-and-restarted window is the most
innocent possible source of orphaned artifacts, and the battery still
earned its keep, because "it was probably me" is exactly the kind of
plausible story the campaign has just spent a week learning not to
accept without a fingerprint.

The bundle packet itself is an exercise in honest compression. The
arc's shape — tension sharpens, arbiter fails, forensics narrow,
gate kills, audit cleans, re-run replaces — is genuinely flattering
to the process, and the temptation in assembling a panel packet is to
let the narrative's arc do persuasive work the numbers should do
alone. Three choices guard against that: the corrections ledger sits
in the packet at full strength (including F3's impeached assurance,
which is the least flattering line in the arc); the PR status is
stated with its failures first (PR1 unmet, PR3's instrument verdict
withdrawn, PR4 still gated on the founder); and Q1's commission asks
the seats directly for any post-hoc choice in the J1 chain — inviting
the attack rather than pre-empting it. The v3 enactment adjudication
is explicitly NOT convened; a packet that reports a cleaned ledger
must not smuggle in a promotion round.

One scoping decision: the packet quotes the clean-vs-legacy
mechanism (two-sided bias manufacturing r ≈ 1.6) rather than
re-deriving it, and routes reviewers to per-sample archives for
everything — after this arc, the programme's credibility instrument
of choice is the reviewer's own rerun, and the packet's job is to
make that rerun one command away.
