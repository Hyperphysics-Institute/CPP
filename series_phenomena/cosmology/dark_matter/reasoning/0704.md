## The expectation going in, and how it changed

I forecast Step 2 would land as a tension around the ~5:1 ratio (free qDP/hTetra having no antiparticle to annihilate, so naively *over*-producing DM relative to the asymmetry-suppressed baryon relic). The computation reframed the problem and made it sharper.

## The reframe

DM in CONJ-COSMO-1 is not the *ambient* Sea (uniform → no ΔSSV gradient → no gravity) but the *swirl-seeded overdensity* of qDP/hTetra. So the bookkeeping is overdensity-vs-baryon, not total-Sea-vs-baryon. Once framed that way:

- **Abundance is not the constraint.** ρ_Sea (QCD-scale, conservative) ~3e19 kg/m^3, ~100x nuclear. The swirl overdensity to make all cosmic DM is delta ~ 7e-47. The reservoir is vast; ~5:1 is trivially reachable.
- **Double-counting is ~19%, not orders.** Baryon-internal qDP/hTetra ~ rho_b; counting it wrongly inflates 5.36 -> 6.36. Cleanly avoidable; the gate's stated worry is misplaced.
- **The real constraint is whether the uniform Sea gravitates.** If it did, Omega_Sea ~ 1e45 (QCD) to 1e120 (Planck) — vacuum catastrophe. The DM picture requires the uniform Sea to be gravitationally inert cosmologically while its inhomogeneities gravitate. That is consistent with "gravity = gradient of dSSV" in spirit, but reproducing Friedmann is unbuilt. Ties Step 2 to OPEN-SR-5.

## Why "no kill, no clean pass" rather than a verdict

The gate is satisfiable (vast reservoir) but empty: passing it costs one tuned input (swirl amplitude) the framework does not predict. The ~5:1 is relocated from LCDM, not solved — and §6c already concedes "relative abundances are empirical questions," so this is the framework's own stated position, not an external imposition. Calling it a "pass" would overclaim; calling it a "kill" would be wrong (no scarcity/double-counting failure).

## Honest provenance

rho_Sea rests on the [est] m_DP ~0.3 GeV and [c14] r0 = 0.26 fm; it is a conservative lower bound (subquantum DPs or Planck-scale Sea make it larger, deepening the catastrophe-if-it-gravitated and shrinking the required delta further). The qualitative conclusions (reservoir vast; ratio undetermined; Sea-gravitation is the live question) are robust to these.
