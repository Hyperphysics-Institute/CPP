# Reasoning — Patch 0840 (σ/m(v) from the residual potential; the SIDM discriminant)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (Opus, Session 156, 10 June 2026). First-pass derivation + observational overlay; no verdict, no THEO/ID. Opens the publication-track DM-1 discriminant.

This is the concrete first move on the "publication-worthy microphysics + discriminant" paper. The pitch I gave Thomas last turn was that the Era-2 σ/m (~0.1-0.5, derived) puts CPP DM "squarely in the live SIDM window," and that deriving σ/m(v) would be the discriminant. The computation partly confirmed and partly corrected that, and I led the writeup with the correction because the honest result is both milder and sharper than the pitch.

The computation: finite-energy s-wave scattering of the 0831 residual potential (V0=f*264, lam=1.3, hard core 1.0, m=264) across v=30-3000 km/s. The result is that σ/m is FLAT to all digits over the whole range. I checked why before trusting it: kλ ~ 9e-5 (dwarf) to 9e-3 (cluster-merger), so kλ<<1 everywhere and scattering never leaves the scattering-length limit until v~c. That is robust and kinematic — heavy constituent (264 MeV) + short range (1.3 fm) — and it is the OPPOSITE of light-mediator SIDM (1/v^4). So the real, distinctive prediction is not "in the SIDM window" but "VELOCITY-INDEPENDENT σ/m," which is a sharper and more falsifiable claim than I had pitched.

The honest correction I made explicit in the doc: "squarely in the window" was optimistic. Central σ/m~0.15 (distinguishable s-wave), ~0.3 with the identical-boson ×2 (0833), f-band 0.02-0.25 with a Ramsauer dip near f=0.5. Overlaid on the constraints: consistent with clusters, but at/just above the dwarf coring threshold (~0.1), BELOW the strong-core preference (~0.5-few). So it is a MILD, flat self-interaction at the low edge of the window — not the strong-coring story. I did not want the doc to read as if CPP fits the dwarf diversity problem; it predicts mild cores, and the headline discriminant is the flatness, not the magnitude.

Why it is still a genuine discriminant and publication-worthy: (1) vs collisionless CDM, σ/m~0.1-0.3 != 0 is clean and derived; (2) vs velocity-dependent SIDM, flat-vs-1/v^4 is a clean qualitative test over the velocity range. And it is falsifiable with a clear failure mode: if the data demand velocity-dependent SIDM (strong dwarf cores + tight clusters), CPP's flat ~0.15 fails the dwarf side because it physically cannot rise at low v. That is exactly the structure a real prediction should have.

Robustness bookkeeping I was careful about: the FLATNESS is robust to f and to the boson/viscosity O(1) factors (it is kinematic); only the MAGNITUDE is factor-3 (f), and pinning it is partly Project C's territory. So I separated the robust qualitative claim from the uncertain magnitude rather than quoting a single confident number. Independent of DM-2 and Project C, so it is safe to develop in this lane now.
