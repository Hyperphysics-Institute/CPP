Headline: **3 not-falsified (Layer-C discriminating) + 1 falsification dissent.** But the discipline is honor-

equivalent contact-scale derivation) is what settles it.**

## FIX 1 (folded) — demote argument (b) to a consistency consequence

Both III (required fix) and IV (hostile) flagged it, and I flagged the hazard myself in the CONV-001 ask. The
argument "construction 2 contradicts the ratified corona/width-cap/σ-floor, therefore construction 2 is false"
is **circular as an independent physical argument**, because those ratifications themselves assumed the
coupling-only reading (construction 1). **Corrected status:** construction 2 forcing a re-examination of those
results is a *consequence* of construction 2, recorded as an internal-consistency cost — **not** evidence that
construction 2 is physically wrong. (Review II's claim that the coupling hierarchy is cleanly separable from the
London E-scaling is *partly* true — the coupling ratio is a distinct factor — but the corona's V₀_elec is itself
built on the 0835 London machinery, so the separation is not clean enough to rescue (b) as independent evidence.
The conservative, panel-majority position is the demotion.)

## FIX 2 (folded) — construction 1 is the minimal-transfer hypothesis (new Layer-C residual)

Per III ("minimal-transfer hypothesis") and IV (mass/spatial scaling): construction 1 transfers the q–q result
to the e–e edge holding **geometry fixed** (same contact distance, same overlap), changing only the coupling.
But the eDP (88 MeV) is one-third the qDP mass (264 MeV); a lighter composite is spatially larger, so the e–e
contact distance r_c and overlap are **not identical** to the q–q bond's, and V ∝ 1/r⁶ is steep. This is a
**real un-modeled factor**, distinct from the polarizability-scaling residual, and it cuts both ways (a larger
r_c would *reduce* the depth, partially opposing construction 2's enhancement). It is **not** resolved by Route
A. Recorded as a second Layer-C residual. So E_ee ≈ 100–280 keV is the minimal-transfer estimate, with two named
residuals: (i) the polarizability-scaling question, (ii) the contact-geometry transfer.

## What survives and gets stronger — argument (a)

Review II supplies the positive physics that converts (a) from "the math gets hard" (correctly called an Appeal
to Consequences by IV) into a physical statement: the London 1/E⁶ enhancement is a *far-field* property; at
contact the relevant physics is exchange/overlap/internal-stiffness, which **quenches** the soft-cloud
polarizability — so the 729× factor is not realized at contact. This is the standard reason London dispersion
coefficients do not extrapolate to contact in molecular physics. It is a **physical argument, not a contact-
scale calculation**, so it favors construction 1 (and answers the dissent's "must survive") without proving it.
