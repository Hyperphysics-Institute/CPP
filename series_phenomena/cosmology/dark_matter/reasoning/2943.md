# REASONING FRAGMENT — PATCHES 2942 + 2943 (A5-DISP conversion)

**STATUS: verbatim, at-patch capture.**

The ordering discipline was the substance of this pair of patches, so
I record how it actually ran: the conversion prereg (2942) was written
and committed with zero numbers in it — formula, ξ₂ handling,
weaker-sign rule, source admissibility — and only after that commit
did I run the literature search. This matters more than usual because
the search produced a surprise in the tension-generating direction,
and the frozen rules absorbed it without any discretion on my part.

The surprise: the ξ₂ = 1 ceiling is ~3.5 × 10⁻²⁸ m — thirteen orders
of magnitude below a femtometer, about 2 × 10⁷ Planck lengths. The
2936 HIGH-STAKES framing had worried that a LINEAR term would indict
a femtometer-scale mesh; what the conversion quietly reveals is that
even the QUADRATIC limits are strong enough that a femtometer d_DP
with O(1) relay coefficient would be excluded by ~13 orders. I want
to be precise about why this is an anchor and not a filed tension:
the ledger is explicit that no value of d_DP exists anywhere in the
corpus — the "femtometer-scale" language in the prereg's CASE-L band
was a hypothetical scale for stakes-illustration, not a registered
corpus expectation. So there is nothing to file a tension against.
But the ceiling has teeth going forward: any sector arc that implies
d_DP above ~10⁻²⁸ m must either derive ξ₂ ≪ 1 with registered
provenance (the anti-α1 bar applies) or collide with this anchor.
That is exactly what an anchor is for, and it is the honest content
of the density campaign's first landed number-class result.

Source selection ran into the expected mess: three analyses of the
same burst (GRB 221009A/LHAASO) with quadratic limits spanning
5.6–13.7 × 10¹¹ GeV. The frozen peer-review rule did the work — only
the Piran–Ofengeim PRD letter was verifiable as published from the
session's search surface, so it binds, and the stronger 2025 claims
enter as secondary corroboration with the substitution path already
frozen for their eventual admission (≤ ~1.6× tightening; immaterial
to the anchor's order of magnitude). The weaker-sign rule then chose
superluminal (4.6 × 10⁻⁸ E_Pl) over subluminal (5.8) — the
conservative direction, chosen by rule, not by me.

One deliberate scope restraint: I did not attempt to derive ξ₂. The
temptation is real — the quadratic relay coefficient is a
well-posed O(kd)² computation and the 2940 machinery is sitting
right there — but it is its own arc with its own prereg, and bundling
it here would have meant computing a coefficient in the same patch
that cites the bound it rescales, which is the exact motive-structure
the ordering discipline exists to prevent. The ξ₂ = 1 reference
normalization with explicit scaling is the CONV-004-compliant way to
land the anchor now and let the coefficient arrive clean later.
