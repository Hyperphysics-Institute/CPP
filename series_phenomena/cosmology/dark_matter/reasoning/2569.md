# Reasoning — Patch 2569 (the charter absorbs its review; the near-miss on my own convention)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

Small confession first: I drafted this as a suffixed file (charter_v1_1.md) and caught the
standing no-version-suffix convention only at commit prep. The convention exists so the
repo never forks into parallel versions of one governing document; the CHANGELOG carries
history and git carries the reviewed v1.0 verbatim. Fixed before it entered the record,
recorded here because the convention almost lost to muscle memory, and the next instance
should feel the same tug and resist it.

The re-registration itself is mostly transcription — the panel and founder did the
design work — but two choices were mine. First, K1a carries BOTH instruments (O3-prime
and O1a) as mandatory rather than alternatives: the bound without the dynamics invites
threshold-as-capture creep; the dynamics without the bound wastes runs on dead cells;
together each disciplines the other. Second, the K1b routes are deliberately independent
(R-A Sea/SF-6, R-B three-body) with no ordering between them, because the founder's
direction makes R-A the beautiful route and beauty is precisely why it must not be
allowed to crowd out the boring alternative — if R-A's derivation stalls at Branch I,
R-B proceeds on K1a outputs without waiting.

The hazard inventory is the longest section on purpose. Every clause in it is a place a
future session under deadline pressure would otherwise improvise, and the panel handed
us the complete map of those places for the price of one dispatch. Freezing it at
charter level means no phase prereg can quietly omit the clause it finds inconvenient.
