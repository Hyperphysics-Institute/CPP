## The substrate sequence

qCP / eCP (first moments) → bond into qDP, eDP, and hDP (qCP–eCP hybrid) → hDPs bond into hTetra
(hybrid tetrahedron; the eight gluons are hDP structures, SF-5). Constituent scales (SF-3): E_eDP = 88,
E_hDP = 152, E_qDP = 264 MeV (E_qDP = 3·E_eDP, color factor 3; E_hDP = √(E_eDP·E_qDP)).

## Two assembly routes, one attractor

**Route 1 (hTetra).** Four e:q:q:e hTetras bond through their central q:q edges into one Cross-Rod
element — an 8-qCP cubic core wrapped by an 8-eCP shell (≈ 4·m_hTetra ~ 1–2 GeV). Elements then stack
axially, 4qCP-face to 4qCP-face, lengthening the rod.

**Route 2 (chain).** qDPs bond end-to-end into a qDP chain; eDPs are drawn to its exposed (unshielded)
qCP surface, giving an eDP–qDP chain; four such chains bundle four-fold around a shared qDP core; the
outer eDP coats shield the lateral surface.

Both routes converge on the same object — a robustness signal. (The W-boson "two-ring" image was Thomas's
mental starting point only — break the hexagonal W bracelet, extend it — and is **omitted from the
record**: SF-2's W is a single hexagonal hDP ring, D₆ bracelet, which the Cross-Rod does not descend from.)

## The glueball is the failure branch, not a rival

The campaign had taxed the glueball away as a competing morphology (0862/0863). The genesis recasts it:
a floppy two-chain ribbon has an open, strongly-attractive qDP edge; before its core is saturated it
grabs another chain and **folds back on itself — that fold is the glueball**. Saturating the central qDP
core from four sides *outruns* the fold and yields the cross. So glueball and Cross-Rod are the two
outcomes of one unstable intermediate, and the same instability is the one that kills the single strand
(0867/0869). One mechanism now covers three results (strand kill, glueball, cross selection).

## The shielding hierarchy does triple duty

The fifth lateral chain is **not forbidden — it is out-competed**. A shielded lateral surface (eDP-coated)
is a far weaker attractor than the unshielded qCP end-faces and than the free single/ribbon chains still
in solution, which keep an unshielded qDP surface. Free chains/hTetras go to the strongest SSV gradient
(the q:q edges, the unshielded ends), so a fifth chain bonds only rarely, when free chains/hTetras are
already depleted. The governing ratio is the **electric-vs-color hierarchy from the corona calc (0874):
the shielded electric channel is ~190–520× weaker than the color channel.** That single ratio explains
three things at once:
1. the corona cannot bind real eDPs (retirement, 0875–0877),
2. the shielded lateral surface cannot out-attract a fifth chain (width cap, this patch),
3. the same unshielded color residual *is* the σ/m floor of 0.11 (0860).

## Why the rod stays thin (the d_f = 1 mechanism) — code 0880

A rod of N elements presents **2** reactive (unshielded) axial end-faces against **~N** shielded lateral
faces. Lateral bundling overtakes axial growth only when N·(weak/strong) ~ 2, i.e. at a crossover
**N\* ~ 2·(strong/weak) ~ 380–1040 elements** (code/0880). The dwarf-core band wants N_dwarf ~ 5–60
(0878), so the Cross-Rod is **robustly thin (d_f = 1) with a ~6–200× margin** across the entire
band-relevant range. The d_f = 1 assumption behind σ/m = 0.11·N now has a *mechanism* under it
(shielding competition), not merely an input.

## The eDP coat → surface neutrality → the seed of OPEN-COSMO-DM-3

The coat that caps the width also **neutralizes the surface**: a color-singlet cube under a charge-balanced
eCP shell presents a neutral face to the Sea, which can then only *polarize reversibly* around it — the
physical content of ASM-DM-CORONA-LOCALITY (OPEN-COSMO-DM-3). The timing closes the loop: the structural
coat is laid down **early**, when free eDPs were abundant (baked into the dressed mass m_unit), whereas the
diluting corona would have to accrete **now**, from a Sea that is already balanced and off a surface that
is already neutral. This is the most promising lead toward *deriving* OPEN-COSMO-DM-3 (follow-on #1) and
lifting LEMMA-DM-CROSS-ROUTE-1 to unconditional — a mechanism, not yet a derivation.

## The one open quantitative piece (honest)

The genesis is population-dependent: the thin rod survives only if assembly **freezes out early, at
N ~ tens**, before depletion starves the cross into the weak-lateral regime. Late freeze-out either
overshoots (still thin, too big) or thickens (d_f → 2) — both miss the band. Which route dominates
(Route 1's four-body hTetra rendezvous vs Route 2's fold-vs-saturate race) sets the relic abundance and
the glueball contamination. This is the **same N_dwarf(v) kinetics** named in 0878, now with the width
side mechanistically closed and the freeze-out condition sharpened to a falsifiable target: early, thin,
N ~ tens.
