## §1 Why the rival had to be D2 and why the incumbent had to be named

A pre-registration whose "rival" is a strawman is worse than none. D2 (homogeneous condensation) is the honest
rival because it is the DEFAULT physics — bound states favored at kT ≪ E_bond, vast feedstock, no registered
gate — and because it is computable enough to kill or to win: if its ungated bound lands anywhere near

With D3 named, a D1 failure has a dignified landing — the ratio stays calibrated, exactly where DM-1 put it —
and the readings can be severe.

misses, and candidate B is quietly tried. §2 forecloses it: the criterion (closure-forced; strong attractor

## §3 OBS-RELIC-1 — why I recorded the coincidence instead of suppressing it

T1 sits 0.077σ from 1/√5, and √5 = 2φ−1 is as structural as numbers get in this substrate. Suppressing the
observation would poison any future honest derivation that lands there ("did the worker know and steer?");
celebrating it would be numerology. The fence does the third thing: the observation is on the record as
NON-EVIDENTIAL with the look-elsewhere caveat attached (2χ was checked and missed at 4.7σ; φ-expressions are
dense; no exhaustive scan), and any derivation reverse-engineered toward it is Branch T by the charter's own
anti-numerology clause. If a structural derivation of m ever produces 1/√5 with every step independent of this
observation, the record will show the coincidence was known, fenced, and not steered toward — which is the only
way such a result could ever be believed.
