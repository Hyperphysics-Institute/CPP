# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2818 (the PR4-BARE artifact)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-26.**

## Widening the class was the panel's best correction

My scoping tested one functional and I drew a conclusion about all of
them. S1 forced the distinction, and item 2 answers it in the only
way available short of a theorem: test a CLASS — two-body kernels of
three different ranges, field energy in two norms, and the natural
total — across four independent geometries. Six functionals, twenty-
four measurements, every one non-conserved by at least 54% of its own
mean. That is not a proof of nonexistence and item 6 says so twice,
but it is a great deal more than one trajectory, and the honest
conclusion (no IDENTIFIED energy, no bath) is now supported at the
strength it is stated.

## Item 7 is the one that matters for the programme

Every screening number this programme owns comes from Metropolis
sampling of e^{-beta H}, which imposes Gibbs by construction. The
artifact states plainly that those results remain valid CONDITIONAL
on the Gibbs assumption and that the assumption is unsupported by the
bare dynamics. That is uncomfortable and it is exactly what PR4 was
created to force into the open. Writing it as vindication of PR4's
anti-importation sentence rather than as a workaround is the
difference between meeting the criterion and evading it.

## What I did not claim

No theorem. No "impossibility." No extension of the conclusion beyond
the rule as specified. Falsifier 5 names the live route by which this
whole finding could turn out to be an artifact of incompleteness --
and if the founder's C23/C24 completion conserves energy and reduces
to the bare rule in a limit, this artifact will have been the map that
showed where to look.
