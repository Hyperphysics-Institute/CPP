## The sequencing decision — I changed my recommendation

Last turn I (and the geometry lane) recommended running panel round 2 now on the honest bracket, with the f calc
in parallel. The founder proposed instead: **focus on the f calc first, present the panel the full package, refine
on objections.** I considered it and **updated my recommendation to match** — I now think it is the better call,
for a reason I had under-weighted:

- My "parallel" argument was "don't make the ready cluster branch wait." But the cluster branch is a finished,
  parameter-free result — it does not decay, and there is no time pressure on it. That cost is weak.
- Against it: a **complete package** (cluster prediction + a *settled* dwarf branch, whether a pinned prediction or
  a clean kill) is a materially stronger panel submission than a headline plus an unresolved bracket. And each
  adversarial panel round is an independent check worth husbanding — spending one on a known-incomplete result,
  whose predictable dwarf-branch feedback is "go pin f," half-wastes it. "Full package, then refine on objections"
  is the right way to use the panel.

**Caveat recorded:** this holds while the f calc stays *bounded* (a finite element-geometry derivation on a defined
substrate, as the geometry lane posed it). If f spawns its own sub-arc, or if priority pressure appears, revisit —
present the cluster branch alone rather than stall. Absent those, focus-on-f is right.
