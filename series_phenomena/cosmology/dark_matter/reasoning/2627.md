# Reasoning — Patch 2627 (the ledger as a load-bearing map, drawn the night the load shifted)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

Seat 1 asked for five axes; I added a sixth because the DISC arc just spent three
patches proving it exists. A dependency ledger written before 2624/2626 would have
had no split-clock column, and every deposit-consuming row would have looked one
grade safer than it is. Writing DEP-1 immediately after the discriminator arc, with
the convictions fresh, is the cheapest moment this document will ever be accurate.

The grading forced one honest distinction I had been carrying loosely: RE2's
CLASSIFICATION map (dt-union-stable, 96 cells) and RE2's deposit VALUES are
different objects with different exposures, and the ledger splits them into
separate rows. The classification survives the DISC verdicts; the values are
blocked. Collapsing them into one row graded MED would have been the comfortable
smear; the panel's Seat 3 would have caught it in review, and deserved to.

Section 2's reading guide stops exactly at the line between map and claim. "REPL-1
and FORM-1 both discharge risk at the WIN-times-sink cell; nothing else does" is a
statement about the ledger's own geometry, checkable by looking at the table -- not
a new argument for either condition. The temptation in bookkeeping cells is to
editorialize under cover of summary; the fence I held is that every sentence in
section 2 must be re-derivable from the table above it.
