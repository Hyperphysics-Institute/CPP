# reasoning/2675.md — executing the 2674 rulings into the consumer files

**Patch 2675, 20 July 2026. Verbatim at-patch reasoning per the sector
capture convention.**

Four files needed same-font notes, and the discipline question for each
was the same: what is the minimal additive text that makes the file
readable-correct in isolation, without editing anything above the line?
The sector's additive-note pattern (2664's PANEL NOTE on the composite
adjudication is the template) exists precisely so closed records never
get rewritten; I followed it in all four places.

The S2 readout note is the simplest: that file explicitly deferred two
items to the panel ("routing is the panel's call"; "flagged, not
resolved, here"), so the note answers exactly those two deferrals plus
the Q1a/Q1b dispositions that qualify how the file's own §4 numbers may
be cited. A reader landing on the S2 record now cannot cite the readout
without encountering the A1 construction-conditionality and the
observation-grade cap.

The FA-C3 founder-answer note required the most care, because it closes
a founder registration. Two things had to coexist without tension:
Copilot's adopted formulation (historical record, removed from active
interpretive use) and honesty about what the founder got right. The
founder's 2662 statement contained a specific identification (per-unit-
cell) that failed, sitting inside a structural insight (screening is a
response property; the elementary step is neither lattice quantity) that
the derivation vindicated — indeed the founder's own 2666 charter
consequence predicted that both candidates might fail to emerge. The
note says both, in that order of specificity. Recording only the failure
would misrepresent the arc; recording only the vindication would evade
the closure. founders_voice itself untouched, per the pre-commit.

The DEP-1 §5 note is where the demotion clause becomes operational, and
I anchored it to the ledger's own §4 structure rather than writing a
free-standing rule: §4 already split the Morse axis into form-class
(off the table) and width (live via FA-C3). The new note completes that
sentence — FA-C3 answered, the answer is outside the bracket — and
gives the two-branch reading rule. One deliberate choice: I did NOT
re-grade any row. The ledger's charter says it is a map, consumers cite
it as a map, and rows are added at their own patches; a reading
instruction preserves that while a re-grade would constitute editing
rows the panel never examined individually. The row-by-row re-grade is
properly the re-derivation campaign's output, not this note's.

The composite note closes the loop on the FD-PARTIAL verdict's
conditionality: A-CLASS was conditional on FA-C1 + FA-C2, and the
adjudication moves FA-C2 in both directions at once — the FORM leg
strengthens (Sea-derived now, so the conditionality is partially
discharged), the SCALE leg supersedes. The note states the asymmetry
plainly because a hurried reader could take "FA-C2 tension" to threaten
the A-CLASS verdict itself; it does not — A-CLASS's content was the
form class, and the form class came out of the blind derivation
stronger than it went in.

Contamination scan for this patch: no row edited, no verdict re-worded,
no founders_voice file touched, no number recomputed, no fence moved.
The four notes cite the 2674 record as the single canonical source
rather than restating the full rider in four diverging local copies —
one canonical text, four pointers, which is the only maintainable shape
for a rider that the re-derivation campaign will eventually retire.
