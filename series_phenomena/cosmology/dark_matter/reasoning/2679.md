## The substantive reasoning, preserved

**The amendment event, first, because it is the patch's most instructive
content.** A charter draft was composed and presented in this session
against the 2674 §3 spec directly, at a moment when the container clone's
origin/main stood at 2676. The founder's local (and, by apply time,
pushed) Patches 2677–2678 — the frozen inputs file with the F1–F4 scope
fence, and the handover directing the charter to consume it — were
invisible to that draft. The apply failed on `future_projects.md` context
(the 2677 queue-head extension), and the failure surfaced the real
problem: the draft was not merely mis-numbered, it was **missing its
frozen input**. The fix chosen was the one the draft's own §6 prescribed
for exactly this situation: withdraw, re-charter from the frozen input,
disclose same-font — never resolve the merge and keep a charter that
silently lacks a founder-directed fence. The charter header carries the
amendment note; nothing from the withdrawn draft's presentation landed in
the repo, so the record is clean by construction. Process lesson,
registered here for the handover: a charter session must `git fetch` and
re-verify head IMMEDIATELY before composing a freeze document, not only
at bootup — the bootup-time head is stale the moment the founder pushes.

**The charter's dual-authorship structure.** FA-SEA-GREEN's charter froze
the adjudicator's design of an arc the founder's picture had motivated.
FA-SG-R1's charter transcribes TWO upstream authorities, neither of which
it may edit: the panel's five-leg remedy spec (2674 §3, 5–0,
strictest-union) and the founder's scope fence (2677, "include these
concepts in the charter to focus and avoid distraction"). What the freeze
act CAN and MUST do is supply the operational content both left open: how
many realizations count as "independently chosen," what "the same ℓ
emerges" on an arena already ruled READOUT-INCONCLUSIVE can operationally
mean, what concordance numerically is, and in what order the legs run.
Every such supplement is marked [ADJ] in the charter text and declared
votable at the returns. The fence, by contrast, is carried BY REFERENCE
with one-line handles — restating F1–F4 in full would create a second
authoritative text that could drift from the 2677 original; the inputs
file stays the authority and the charter points at it.

**The L2 problem, and why the restriction test is the honest reading.**
DeepSeek's leg — "registered-arena confirmation that the same ℓ emerges
(not only the extended instrument)" — collides with the 2671 §2 finding
that the registered I1 arena has no clean decay regime (largest pair
distance 3.2× smallest; sign-oscillating under both metrics; disclosed
pre-spectrum). Read literally as "extract ℓ from I1," the leg demands
something the committed record says the arena cannot yield, and an
execution session trying to satisfy the literal reading would be pushed
toward exactly the kind of creative extraction the blind protocol exists
to prevent. The faithful operationalization is the restriction/prediction
test: the extended solution governed by ℓ, restricted to I1's geometry,
must reproduce the registered-arena field. That is a genuine confirmation
(the I1 field becomes a prediction target rather than a readout source)
and it is falsifiable (a wrong ℓ produces a wrong restricted field). The
operationalization is flagged for the panel explicitly, with a
supersession clause: if the execution session finds a legitimate direct
readout, the stronger form wins. The risk accepted: DeepSeek may have
meant something else; the returns can say so, and the charter's §7
unworkable-as-frozen clause routes any mid-execution discovery of a
better reading back through amendment rather than silent patching.

**Why the concordance thresholds are where they are.** Pairwise
compatibility within 2× combined 1σ bands is deliberately loose relative
to the 2671 band (±0.002 fm, ~2%): the arc's question is
representation-independence, not precision replication, and independent
constructions carry independent discretization effects that a 1σ
criterion would spuriously fail. But the threshold cannot be looser than
"same scale" or the arc proves nothing — 2σ pairwise with a reported
joint band keeps R1-SHIFT reachable: a stable value 20% away fails
CONCORD cleanly and lands in the shift class rather than being absorbed.
The three-realization minimum (with the 2671-D2 FCC proxy counting as
one) forces at least two genuinely new constructions while acknowledging
the proxy already ran; requiring the proxy to be re-run as if new would
be make-work, but letting it satisfy the leg alone would let the remedy
confirm itself with its own instrument.

**J3's propagation clause, and where the fence touches it.** The panel
graded J3 the unanimous highest residual risk, and the danger is
specifically that the continuum matching κ² = 4παn normalizes the site
response in a regime the arc itself showed is post-homogenization
(κ·a = 2). The charter therefore demands the attack be executed as a
derivation with explicit propagation (ℓ ∝ α^(−1/2) at fixed gap
identification) rather than a sensitivity scan — a scan would report how
bad a revision COULD be; the derivation reports what the discrete Green
function actually gives. F1 intersects here materially: the density in
any normalization is the LOCAL DP density n and nothing else — the fence
forecloses in advance any construction that would smuggle a cosmic count
into α. J3-REVISE routes into R1-SHIFT rather than into a failure class
because a revised normalization with a stable staggered-exponential
structure is a better value, not a broken arc — the panel's own framing
("any α revision propagates directly into ℓ") treats it as a
propagation, not a refutation.

**Why the fence got its own charter section rather than a guards
bullet.** The inputs file's §3 makes the fence load-bearing physics: the
IR-decoupling is forced by the screening the arc confirms, and the
screened/unscreened split R1 lives on is the same split the CC
reconciliation runs on. Burying that in a guards list would present it as
hygiene. As a section it does two jobs: it gives the execution session a
decision rule ("a sentence needing a fenced quantity is out of scope —
the sentence is cut, not the fence") and it pre-commits the arc's framing
so that no result, however striking, gets narrated as bearing on the
holographic principle or the CC magnitude story.

**Why FA-C3 stays closed under every class.** Considered whether R1-NEG
should reopen the fork — if the readout were an artifact, was the fork
really killed? The adjudication record answers this: all five seats held
the proxy "adequate to kill the fork" (J1) with the provenance risk
explicitly on the table, and closed FA-C3 5–0 in that knowledge. The
closure's evidentiary basis was the operator's quantitative divorce from
both candidates, which survives even a demoted numerical value. Reopening
by charter would be the adjudicator overriding a unanimous panel ruling —
exactly what the pre-committed handling forbids. So the charter fences
it: every class routes to the packet, and a seat that reads R1-NEG as
grounds to revisit says so in its return.

**What the charter deliberately does not contain.** No new physics, no
new IDs beyond the frozen outcome-class labels, no restatement of the
fence's authoritative text, no touch on the d_DP/4 coincidence beyond
continuing its non-elevation, no schedule for the re-derivation campaign
(it stays sequenced behind the returns per 2674 §5 / inputs §4), and no
L5 execution decision — the optional leg's classes are frozen so that IF
it runs nothing is named after results, but whether it runs is left to
the legs' outcome and the founder's word, as Grok's new-branch ruling
implies it must be.
