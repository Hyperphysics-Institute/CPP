## What I set out to do
Scope R1 (the geometry-#3 driven equilibrium) while Thomas applies 2440, and in particular put a real number on ε =
(ω_A/ω_sw)² so we know whether the substrate even lands in the method-(a) stability window [0.18,0.43]. Method (a)
had reduced the sign to "does ε fall in the window," so the value of R1 scoping is to size that before committing to
the full self-consistent solve.

## The input that unlocked the estimate
I found in reasoning/2435 that the charge-switching is "the SU(3)-type ZBW hop" — i.e. ω_sw is not a free parameter,
it's a ZBW/Compton-scale frequency, ℏω_sw ~ m c² of the hopping constituent. That plus the pinned d = 1.15 fm,
r_q = d/√2, k_rep = 2E_bond/s², and the α_s/α ≈ 53 branch factor is enough for an order-level ε.

## The honest update I have to flag
Last turn I called the R1 prior "leaning unfavorable," on a hand-estimate that gave ε ~ 10⁻³. That estimate
implicitly used the shallow (E_ee ≈ 490 keV) branch with the light constituent. When I did it properly across the
branches, the picture is not uniformly unfavorable — it's branch-dependent, and there's a genuinely favorable corner:
deep E_qq core fragmentation + qDP ZBW hop gives ε ≈ 0.21, in the window. The deep branch works because its bond
oscillation is ~120 MeV, comparable to the ~264 MeV qDP hop, so ω_sw/ω_A ≈ 2.2 lands in the required [1.5,2.4]. I'm
flagging this as a correction to my own earlier framing rather than quietly restating it, same discipline as the
2440 self-correction — the lane's failure mode is sliding the story toward a preferred answer, and that cuts both
ways: I over-pessimised last turn on a sloppy estimate, and I have to own that as much as I'd own over-optimism.

## Why I am NOT calling this favorable-corner survival
ε = 0.211 is right at the lower edge of the window, and E_qq is a scale estimate. The sensitivity is brutal: E_qq
40→100 MeV moves ε 0.13→0.32, i.e. unstable to comfortably-in-window. So the favorable corner is plausible, not
established, and seizing it as "survives" would be exactly the 2434 move (pick the favorable sub-case, drop the
caveats, land on the lean). What the estimate legitimately does is tell R1 what to pin: the fragmentation branch
(R6 — deep vs shallow, and only deep is even in-window) and the effective ω_sw (qDP hop vs eDP hop vs
residence-suppressed rate). Both must be derived, not assumed. R6/G6 is explicit that the branch comes from the
fragmentation coordinate, so I'm not going to ask Thomas to hand me a lean on it — R1 derives it.

## The thing behind both that can still kill it
Even the favorable corner only gets K_switch to ~0.12·A and in-window stability. R5/G4 — netting against the
recomputed geom-#3 ponderomotive tensor (the 2430 analog had a −190 transverse eigenvalue) — sits behind it and can
flip the net sign negative regardless. So R1's favorable corner, if it survives the branch and ω_sw derivations, is
necessary but not sufficient; R5 is still the gate. I want that on the record so the next run doesn't read
"ε in-window" as "candidate survives."
