## Inputs (all established)

## Calculation

Fractal self-interaction scaling (memo §2): (σ/m)_agg/(σ/m)_mono = N^((2−d_f)/d_f), normalized to 1 at
N = 1. For a **rigid rod** (L < ℓ_p, d_f = 1): **σ/m = 0.11·N·g**, g = O(1) geometric/orientation prefactor
(R_g normalization, ~1/√12 … 1). For a flexible coil (L ≫ ℓ_p, d_f → 2) the enhancement → 1 → σ/m → floor.
So the enhancement lives in the rigid regime, and the band-sized aggregates are robustly rigid (N ≪ ℓ_p).

**Result:** the band 0.6–2 is reached at **N_dwarf ~ 5–60 rungs** (5–18 at g = 1, up to ~60 at g = 1/√12) —
a modest rigid cross-rod, far inside the rigid regime, so coiling does not spoil it. Large rods overshoot
hard (N = ℓ_p = 200–500 → σ/m ~ 22–55, ~10–28× over band), so the dwarf-scale size must self-limit to tens.

**Velocity dependence (the discriminating signature).** A collision depositing E_dep breaks ~E_dep/E_bond
bonds → fragments a rod by ~factor E_bond/E_dep. Dwarf (0.78 keV) is below the E_bond window → no
fragmentation → N_dwarf retained → cores. Cluster (1.95 MeV) → fragments ~4–40× → σ/m_cluster ~ 0.1–1 →
collisionless. **The split is automatic and in the data-preferred direction** (cores in dwarfs, collisionless
in clusters), and is a genuine discriminator vs the monomer's velocity-**independent** 0.11–0.20 (DM-1 §2).

## Verdict (Layer C, bracketed — band REACHABLE)

The cross-rod reaches the dwarf band at N_dwarf ~ 5–60 rigid rungs; the velocity split falls out of the

testable core-size-vs-halo-mass relation, distinguishable from both CDM (σ/m = 0) and the monomer
