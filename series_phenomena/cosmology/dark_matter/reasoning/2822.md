## The temptation, stated plainly

Λ(n²=1) = 2.135 ± 0.312 is 3.64σ from unity. If true it would be the
most consequential single number this campaign has produced: a
factor-of-two failure of the fluctuation-response bridge, which every
screening result in the programme assumes. The founder granted
authority to re-run; a headline was available; and the run's own
control had failed at 2.08σ. Reporting the headline anyway would have
been indefensible, and the fact that it was tempting is exactly why
the cross-talk control was frozen into the design before any data
existed.

## What the control actually taught

The undriven shell responded at the same magnitude as two of the
three driven shells. That is not a small contamination — it says the
measured "responses" are substantially not mode-specific. The most
economical explanation is under-equilibration: 200 sweeps, chosen
because the compute budget forced it, leaving a common transient that
the slope fit reads as signal. The prereg warned in its own power
statement that the budget rather than the physics was setting the
design; that warning turned out to be the leg's epitaph.

## What PR3 costs now

Two legs spent, no verdict, and the requirement is exactly where it
was. That is the honest price of controls that work. A third attempt
needs equilibration sized to the control rather than to the budget,
which likely means a longer session or a smaller system — and that
choice belongs in a fresh prereg, not in tonight's disappointment.
