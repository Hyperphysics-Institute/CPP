# Finding — Patch 0859 (DM-1 self-interaction recompute: the 0.20 is an artifact; pivot to extended-aggregate geometry)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (Opus, DM 08xx lane, 25 June 2026). A FINDING patch, not a paper edit. Captures a load-bearing negative result that changes DM-1's status, plus the physics pivot it motivates. Triggered by Thomas's refusal to paper over the IC 2574 magnitude tension with wording ("theoretical calculations that don't match empirical evidence are not convincing").

What I did, and why it is trustworthy. Built a radial-Schrodinger phase-shift solver and VALIDATED it to machine precision against the analytic square-well scattering length a = Rw[1 - tan(KRw)/(KRw)], including across the first bound-state resonance (V0=100, KRw=1.647 > pi/2: a flips to +17.945, matching analytic exactly). Only then trusted it on the qDP problem. The viscosity integral reproduces the (4/3)sigma_0 identical-boson factor in the s-wave limit analytically and numerically. So the negative result below is not a solver artifact.

The result. Three potentials, all at f=0.2, v=50:
  - screened-LJ (Fig.1, the PHYSICAL potential with the real eDP-coat hard core): sigma_V/m = 0.115 cm^2/g, and FLAT ~0.08-0.12 across f in [0.07,1.0] and v in [30,3000]. The hard core caps the scattering length at ~rc regardless of well depth, so there is no resonance to climb and no f reaches the data band.
  - Yukawa + wall@1.0 (what code/0841 ACTUALLY computed, by starting its integration at r=rc=1.0 with u=0): 0.197 -> this is the shipped "0.20". It is an artifact of an accidental hard wall, not a derived value.
  - pure Yukawa, no core (what 0841 intended): 0.474 at f=0.2, resonating to ~95 by f~0.5 -- wildly f-dependent.

So the data want sigma/m ~ 0.6 (density) to ~1-2 (core sizes), and the physical point-scattering cross-section is ~0.11, flat, ~5-20x too small, with NO closure anywhere in the allowed f-band. The coring discriminant, computed honestly, is closer to a NULL than to the shipped 0.20. The velocity-INDEPENDENCE is real and robust (flat in v at every f) -- that part survives.

Honesty content: the shipped DM-1 central number (0.20) does not survive a correct calculation. The panel's convergent "soften the wording" verdict was, in hindsight, polite about a deeper problem the package's own s-wave-limit framing hid: the magnitude was never robustly 0.20. I did not let the review's "advance with calibration" outcome stand once the recompute showed the number itself was wrong. Better found now at v0.1 than after a referee runs the same solver.

The pivot (Thomas's proposal, which I think is the real fix). If DM is not compact eDP-cocooned spheres but EXTENDED 2qDP:2eDP charge-offset ribbons/loops (the W-boson substrate in neutral looped form) and 2eDP:1hTetra ribbons, the cross-section is GEOMETRIC, not scattering-length. For a 1D object sigma/m ~ N (grows with loop size), reaching 0.6-2 at loops of ~10^2-10^3 DPs (R ~ 40-500 fm), velocity-INDEPENDENT by construction -- and possibly velocity-DEPENDENT in the data-preferred direction (rising toward dwarfs) via collisional fragmentation of large loops at high cluster velocities, a mechanism the point-particle picture fundamentally lacked. This REPLACES the point-scattering section; the new linchpin is deriving the loop-size distribution from PCD formation dynamics (else the size is just a relocated free knob). The geometric cross-section here is an order-of-magnitude estimate (loops can pass through / link / deform); the real transport cross-section needs the DP-contact dynamics.

What I could and could not evaluate: the cross-section scaling I can estimate (done, favorable); the structural/stability claims -- 2qDP:2eDP ribbon stability, glueball avoidance via a color-singlet qDP ladder, the 3-eDP-cocoon charge-asymmetry argument, the W-boson (SF-2) substrate link -- are strong-sector (SF-2/SF-5) questions I flagged as plausible-but-unverified.
