# Reasoning — Patch 2638 (fourteen agreements, two walls, and an artifact convicted in both directions)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The cleanest fact of the night is a count: fourteen stage-verdict pairs, fourteen
dt-agreements. The instrument that disagreed with itself at exactly the marginal
cells, once handed identical initial data per leg, never disagreed again — not at
the registered channel, not at the drawn φ=30 cell, not at either marginal cell,
not at the refinement. That is what convicting an instrument defect should look
like when the conviction is correct: the repair doesn't merely move the answer,
it removes the symptom everywhere at once.

The direction of the resolution surprised me. Walking in, the natural prior was
that the 1/200 leg — finer integration — was the truthful one and the cells would
draw as completions, widening the envelope to ≥45° and 4-of-4 faces. The opposite
happened: the coarse leg's clean miss was the physics, and the fine leg's capture
was the artifact, because the economy re-settle had nudged the 6-object into a
marginally different presentation that happened to catch. There is a small lesson
about priors in that: "finer dt" earns trust about integration error, not about
initial-condition error, and the 2631 defect was entirely the latter.

Both walls landing at stage 7, again, completes a pattern the record has been
accumulating since 2631: the marginal-aim physics belongs to the grown 6-object's
geometry. The face map is now genuinely anisotropic — +x completes into its
distinct shape, +y misses outright, ±z complete canonically — and the orientation
wall sits in a 7.5-degree bracket. I kept the anisotropy as an exhibit rather
than a claim because the right consumer is FORM-1: whether this is an isomer
spectrum, an orientation degeneracy, or a capture-cross-section anisotropy is a
derivational question, and tonight's numbers are its data.

One row is owed to DEP-1 rather than written into it silently: the walls are
sink-independent facts (the halts are misses, Sea ≈ 0 — the sink never engages),
so they sit on the aimed-convention and γ-member axes only. That makes them among
the most exposure-robust registrations in the N2-B family, which is worth a line
precisely because so much else in the family is sink-HIGH.
