Re-running forty-four deterministic cells I had already run felt, for about a minute,
like ceremony. It is not. The 2611 runs existed under a prereg whose control was
defective; verdicts drawn from them would inherit the defect's shadow no matter how
clean the numbers looked. Three minutes of compute bought a read with no asterisk:
controls that reproduce BOTH registered phenomena on demand, then the scan, then the
verdict. The discipline's price has never been lower relative to what it purchases.

The face map coming back uniform — deposits within five percent across six axes,
three offsets, three azimuths, and a charge flip — is the simplest possible answer,
and after the week's plot twists, simplicity is itself information. The soft
6-cluster has no faces worth the name at this incident class; it is a ball of glue.
The entire apparent structure lived in the aim.

What I carry forward: the question "why did it stall at 6" had consumed a queue slot,
a classification, and a paragraph of the win-candidate packet, and the answer was a
frame convention. The N3 interface work ahead is FULL of frame conventions — ambient
flux is defined in the lab, clusters drift by construction. Tonight's artifact class
goes on that campaign's hazard list before it opens.
