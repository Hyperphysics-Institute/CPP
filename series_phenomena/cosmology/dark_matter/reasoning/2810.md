# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2810 (the diagnosis: we were simulating the wrong regime)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-25.**

## The founder's instinct was right and cheap to vindicate

He held the packet and asked whether the crystals were physics or
model. Two runs at identical density settled it: 13 CPs per Planck
sphere gives a dead 1447-CP condensate; 325 gives no clusters at all
and every CP moving. Everything the last four legs measured about
droplets, sheets, and parked singletons was a property of
PSR/spacing ≈ 1.5 — a regime that is not the Sea. The right response
to "is it my physics?" was a scan, not an argument.

## Two premises of my own that the data killed

First, the "static crystallite" of leg 4: in the G3RR state all 28
sheet members move within 200 Moments; the frozen population was the
parked singletons, and leg 4's framing generalized from the 2803
seed. Second, P-X1's 80% band missed at 75% — but the substantive
finding is stronger than the band: NOT ONE static CP is force-free.
They are all being pushed, and the lattice is refusing to let them
move. Corrections recorded same-font, including a fill-arithmetic
error in my own scan script.

## The finding I did not predict

Period-2 lockstep. At R = 6 the velocity autocorrelation is exactly
-1.000 at lag 2 and +1.000 at lag 20, with zero displacement spread:
the entire Sea blinks in unison. That is the classic synchronous-
update attractor, and it means the thermal bath the founder's
dissociation equilibrium needs has never existed in any run we have
done. Liveness (mover fraction 1.000) had been my proxy for
thermalization, and it was a bad proxy — a metronome is not a heat
bath. Any future gate must test the velocity DISTRIBUTION, not the
count of movers.

## Why the desynchronizer is the founder's physics, not a hack

The obvious fixes for lockstep in the CA literature are
asynchronous update or injected noise, and both would violate the
charter (synchrony is committed; noise would answer PR4's question
about the wrong dynamics). But the doctrine already contains a
symmetry-breaking mechanism that we simplified away: SSV_abs sets
PSR locally. The proxy test shows it working -- lag-1 correlation
collapses to 0.012, lag-3 to -0.075, displacement spread appears,
lockstep degrades from -1.000 to -0.902. Partial, honest, and
pointing at exactly one founder question: the exact SSV_abs -> PSR
law, which he has stated qualitatively and which A3 needs
quantitatively.

## Reporting unrun predictions as unrun

P-X2 and P-X3 were frozen against structures now known to be
artifacts. Running them would have produced tidy-looking data about
nothing. They are recorded NOT RUN with the reason, because a frozen
prediction that quietly disappears is how prereg discipline rots.
