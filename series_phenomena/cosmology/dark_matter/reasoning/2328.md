Grok's check looked like bookkeeping: re-run the 1855 kinetics with capture and the floor switched
on, confirm the N distribution still lands where the floor MC assumed. The static layer is indeed
bookkeeping (the 1855–1860 arc had already closed it), and the formation epoch is clean — at 337
km/s the reach criterion is infeasible even at ceiling weight, so capture cannot touch the
monomer-addition endpoint. The surprise is what "capture active" means after formation. Nothing in
the registered corpus had ever run the capture channel through the radiation era, and the
free-streaming descent walks the population's relative velocities down through the entire anchor
ladder — 337 km/s at formation, the LSB regime by z ~ 4×10⁷, the pin by 10⁷, the dwarfs by 2×10⁶,
meters per second by equality. Θ_crit falls as v² the whole way while the encounter response at any
fixed knee falls slower, so capture activates for every spectrum; the only questions are when and
how hard. The answers are violent: ~10⁵ captures per rod per Hubble time at activation in the
survive window, ~10⁴ even at the light-speed transport cap, above unity for the whole radiation
era. I caught one arithmetic slip while checking the kill-branch numbers (a 10³ error in ħω that
had briefly made the light-speed case look dark-age-delayed); the corrected scan puts even that
case at z ~ 1.3×10⁷. Only the bare-Ohmic floor grades as borderline — and 2327 just argued
conservation forces spectra above it.

So the reconciliation Grok asked for cannot be closed as asked: the floor-N check is conditional
on an activation history. What rescues the session from being a bad-news delivery is the shape of
the escape. The knee required to hold capture off early is 0.6-MeV-class at the pin epoch,
escalating down the descent — which is to say, a hot early Sea whose D cools into today's PRW-D
window. That is the same quantity, as a function of Sea state, that decides G4. Stage-3's target
was already "derive D"; it is now "derive D(T_amb)" — one derivation deciding two gates, and the
survive branch's required history (early-high, late-in-window) is a monotone-cooling statement
that is arguably more natural than the constant D whose consequences it must avoid. I registered
OPEN-DM-AGG-1 with both routes named, graded the branches honestly (moot in the kill branch, a
new conjunct on the survive branch), and moved no verdict. The caveats that matter are the
perturbation velocity floor under the O(1) dark-age tail and the D = const assumption itself —
which the finding exists to overthrow.
