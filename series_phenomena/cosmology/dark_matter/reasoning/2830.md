# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2830 (what "R1" turned out to mean)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-27.**

## Three senses, and the one I guessed was not among them

I had guessed PR7's R1 was a Mori-Zwanzig memory kernel. The physics
content of that guess survives; the provenance does not. The label
traces to S1's counterexample list at the K1 adjudication — current,
anisotropy, memory — produced to show that A1's gradient-generated
stationary measure does NOT follow from the dynamics. PR7 carried the
memory item forward as a residual obligation and inherited "R1" from
the panel round it came from. Meanwhile a completely unrelated R1 —
the first Required Element of OPEN-DM-FLOQUET-1, a driven-equilibrium
geometry problem — has been sitting in the same directory the whole
time. Had I written a prereg on my guess, I would have measured
something real and possibly answered a different question than PR7
asks.

## Why the panel's refusal was mechanically right

They said PR3 does not discharge clause 2 and I recorded that without
being able to say why. Now it is sayable: PR3 measured the static
susceptibility, memory is a finite-frequency property, and a system
can have an exact zero-frequency response while carrying large memory
at nonzero frequency. The two tests are orthogonal. That is a better
reason than "the panel said so," and it belongs in the record.

## The naming motion, and why I am not making it myself

The clean fix is to rename PR7's referent OPEN-K1-MEMORY-1 and leave
FLOQUET-1's R1 alone. But PR7's text is frozen and amendable only by
panel motion, and the worker who discovers a label collision on the
last remaining promotion gap should not also be the one who rewrites
the label. Recommended, not enacted.

## What the founder's answer cost and saved

He said plainly that he did not define the term and does not carry it
in memory. That is the second time this week his correction has
redirected work that would otherwise have gone confidently in a wrong
direction (the first being the AUTOMATON-1 status line). The pattern
worth keeping: when a term's provenance is a prior worker session
rather than founder physics, excavate before scoping.
