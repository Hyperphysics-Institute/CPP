## 2. Design choices worth recording
Part I carries V1–V3 verbatim and merges V4+V5 into one grade — panelist
verification effort is compressed, not diluted, to make room for R. Part II
anchors generation three ways: (i) the seven-row computed-facts table makes
"evade one, quantitatively" a concrete demand rather than a vibe; (ii) the
three-part rent (named evasion / substrate-derivable, no smuggled
parameters / self-supplied one-session cheap kill) prices proposals so the
record only accepts what can be tested; (iii) the one-proposal cap with
honorable abstention selects for quality — five panelists producing zero
rent-paid proposals is itself a finding about the impasse. The ordering rule
(refuting V1–V3 moots one's own R) prevents a panelist from simultaneously
attacking the wall and selling a door in it. Seed directions are listed but
explicitly unpriced, so no proposal inherits unearned standing.
