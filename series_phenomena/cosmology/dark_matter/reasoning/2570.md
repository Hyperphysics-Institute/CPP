# Reasoning — Patch 2570 (the map's honesty lives in three asymmetries)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch.

The prereg's design work was deciding where to be conservative and where generous, and
making each choice cut AGAINST the temptation it faces. Three asymmetries carry that:

First, DEATH requires the generous convention. A dead cell is a falsifier-grade negative
-- the strongest thing this map can say -- so it must survive W-2, the reading most
charitable to capture. ACCESSIBILITY, the weak claim, uses the conservative W-1. Anyone
tempted to inflate the dead count has to beat the generous well; anyone tempted to
inflate accessibility has to clear the stingy one. The MARGINAL band between them is
reported as marginal forever; resolving it by convention choice is the Branch T this
document exists to foreclose.

Second, orientation classes are enumerated, never averaged. The moment a "typical" or
"effective" well depth appears, a phase-space measure has been smuggled in, and measures
are kinetics -- K1a's property. Per-class reporting is clumsier and honest.

Third, the grids are inherited rather than chosen. The 2564 grids were declared before
any of this campaign's results existed and were never consumed; carrying them forward
means nobody -- including me -- picked grid points knowing where the wells are. That the
epoch band sits interior to the E-grid was 2564's decision, made blind; re-deciding it
now would un-blind it.

Rigidity is the map's one load-bearing approximation and it is disclosed rather than
defended: internal deformation could deepen access, so a DEAD verdict is conditional on
rigid DPs. The clause that matters is that overturning a dead cell requires K1a/K1b
EVIDENCE of the deformation channel, not a relaxation of this prereg -- the map cannot
be softened retroactively by the campaigns it was built to triage.
