# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2784 (L2-RB prereg freeze)

**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-23 (post-2783).**

## On freezing fresh rather than inheriting

The 2783 handover §4 barred inheriting the reconnaissance as settled —
the round-1 packet defect happened as a late-session commitment under
momentum, and the L2-RB prereg was deliberately left unfrozen for that
reason. So the freeze began by re-deriving all three flagged items
computationally rather than transcribing §2 of the handover.

## Check 1 — the "likely yes" was false, and the failure is structural

The reconnaissance guessed that chord and geodesic metrics induce the
same vertex-shell partition on I1 because both are monotone functions
of the 4D angle. Monotonicity is true but irrelevant: graph-geodesic
distance is quantized to integer edge count, and the 600-cell's eight
chord shells from a vertex map onto only five graph distances. The
computed partition: chord multiplicities {12, 20, 12, 30, 12, 20, 12,
1} coarsen to geodesic {12, 32, 42, 32, 1} via {c1}→g1, {c2,c3}→g2,
{c4,c5}→g3, {c6,c7}→g4, {c8}→g5, single-valued in the chord→geodesic
direction only. This does not kill candidate (a); it relocates it. The
prediction must live at the geodesic partition level, with the chord
solution aggregated through the explicit coarsening map. Had the prereg
been frozen from the sketch, the executed test would have hit an
ill-posed comparison mid-run — precisely the class of defect the
fresh-freeze rule exists to catch.

## Check 2 — the torus family is honest but needs a mechanical gate

FCC periodic supercells behave exactly as the reconnaissance hoped:
every site z = 12 at every ladder size, minimum-image exact over 27
images, and the (5,3,2) = 120 / (3,3,3) = 108 count-matches land. But
two plausible close-packed constructions fail silently: 2-layer HCP
stacks give z = 9 (a site sees its own image through the short
c-period), and dhcp(5,2)×12 gives z = 11 (in-plane period too thin).
These are exactly the constructions a hurried execution would have
reached for. The remedy is not "use the ones that worked" as a private
fact — it is a frozen, mechanical, pre-solve construction audit gate
(all-z = 12 AND shortest lattice vector ≥ 3 nn) so that construction
validity is checked in the open and cannot function as a post-hoc
result filter. The committed six realizations all pass the gate at
freeze time.

## The disclosure question

Verifying that the minimum-image kernel actually feeds the frozen
solver required one solve, and one line beyond necessity read out
neg-frac = 0.429 on FCC(5,3,2)/src 0. That number is result-adjacent to
committed prediction P-RB1. Options considered: (i) stay silent —
rejected outright; the entire FA-SG-R1 arc's credibility rests on
prereg hygiene, and an undisclosed pre-observation is the same genus of
defect as the S5 fabrications the panel just spent two rounds policing,
differing only in direction. (ii) Exclude FCC(5,3,2) from the committed
set — rejected; it is the exact-count match to I1 and excluding it
would weaken the test to protect the tester. (iii) Declare it the
disclosed pilot: the ALL-realizations prediction still binds it, but
the blind content of the test is explicitly re-inventoried (five other
realizations, the pilot's nn-flip, the full ladder). Option (iii) is
what an adversarial reviewer would demand and is what the prereg
records. The honest cost is stated: the pilot's neg-frac datapoint is
not blind.

## RB-2 candidate selection

(b) held-out-shell fails well-posedness on the reconnaissance's own
caution: the held-out sites' response is physics, not error, and every
scoring convention I could construct either penalizes it silently or
introduces an estimator that is itself untested — an execution-time
judgment call waiting to happen, which is what a prereg exists to
eliminate. (c) spectral fails for a simpler reason once RB-2 is
recognized as single-object: with only I1 in scope there is no
committed comparison target, so the observable is a measurement
wearing a test's clothes. (a) is the only candidate that is
simultaneously I1-native, fit-free, and equipped with a genuine failure
mode — the geodesic operator is a different operator, and the
metric-robustness of the staggered structure at quantitative (not just
class) resolution is exactly the thing the panel said the original L2
never honestly tested.

## Tolerance construction

S1's requirement puts tolerances on preregistered perturbations ON I1.
Source-orbit resampling contributes nothing here (I1 is
vertex-transitive; source choice is an exact symmetry), so the envelope
axes are the frozen α × (1 ± 0.031) and single-vertex-deletion
resampling (119 runs), both predictor-side only — the target side must
not participate in its own tolerance, or the test grades itself. The
INDETERMINATE-PREDICTOR clause on the sign sequence handles the case
where a perturbation run flips a predicted sign: a shell whose
predicted sign is not stable under the committed perturbations carries
no honest sign prediction, and pretending otherwise would let
predictor noise be scored as target failure or success at execution
time. The ≥3-of-4 ratio threshold is set at freeze, with the outer
shells (g4 ratio involves the 32-vertex shell against the antipodal
singleton g5) expected to be the widest-envelope entries — the
threshold is chosen so that a single antipodal-dominated excursion
cannot decide the leg alone, while two misses fail it.

## What was deliberately not decided

No execution numbers beyond the disclosed pilot readout exist at
freeze. The onset ladder carries no prediction — the 2694 P-A failure
was the 3D→4D transfer, not the threshold's reality, and the ladder's
value is characterization for the K1-S1 fluctuation-response arc (which
now carries the founder's ZBW-as-agitation-bath route to deriving
κ·a = 2). Whether the torus staggering thresholds bear any relation to
the I1 values is exactly what P-RB1 refuses to assume and what the
execution will report same-font.
