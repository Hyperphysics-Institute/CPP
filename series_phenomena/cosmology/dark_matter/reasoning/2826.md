**STATUS: verbatim, captured at patch. Session of 2026-07-27.**

## The factor of two

Taken at face value, the founder's data said Λ = 1.26, 1.82, 2.04 —
rising with wavenumber, with the largest shell 3.9σ above unity. That
is a factor-of-two violation of the fluctuation-response bridge: a
headline result that would have undermined every screening number in
the programme and made this the most consequential leg of the campaign.
It was entirely an artifact of my own print label. The engine records
Re ρ; I printed it as |ρ|²; the frozen formula's ½ belongs to |ρ|² and
not to what was measured. Catching it required reading my own code
rather than my own output — which is precisely the check that the
2822 leg's tempting number should have taught me to run first, and
did.

## Why the verdict is trustworthy this time

Every guard that failed before now passes on its own terms: the
control is quiet at 1.10σ (against 2822's 2.08σ, now understood as the
slow mode), linearity passes at all six checks with margins of 3-25x,
and the power bar frozen before data is cleared at every shell. The
τ ≈ 16 estimate I derived from the founder's aborted run predicted
Λ errors near 0.12; the achieved errors are 0.13-0.19. The design
behaved as analysed.

## The marginal shell, stated rather than buried

n²=1 lands at 1.99σ — inside the agreement condition by a hundredth of
a sigma. It would be easy to present three clean agreements. The
record instead states the margin exactly, notes that row 5 is
satisfied by the other two shells alone so the verdict is independent
of it, and names the low-side pull as unexplained and the first target
for follow-up. A verdict that depends on a coin-flip boundary is not
one I would want to defend later.

## What this cost and bought

Three attempts (2820 underpowered, 2822 voided, 2826 passed), three
disclosed defects of my own, and about an hour of the founder's
compute. It bought a genuinely tested bridge -- the assumption every
screening result silently leans on is now measured at three wave
numbers -- and it discharges PR6's external-field leg as a side
effect. PR3 was the last promotion requirement needing real
computation.
