## The founder's reframe and why it retires the transverse mode
The transverse plane-slip mode (2445) assumed a delta-in-space impulse: all momentum on
one plane, sharply localized, unstructured. But a colliding DM ring/baryon is an extended
+/− object; its field couples over several planes with a rising-peaking-falling axial
envelope. Such a blow is a BENDING load distributed over a footprint ℓ, not a shear on one
junction. So the physical collision channel is DISTRIBUTED FLEXURE, and the transverse
plane-slip is not the deformation a collision excites — non-physical, not merely marginal.
The question becomes: how distributed is the bend, and where do form/break thresholds sit.

## What was computed (2443 bond model; N_planes=16, θ_ring=22.5°/hinge)
Fragmentation angle: the most-stretched outer coat bond reaches its inflection (r=1.5d,
V″=0 → dissociating) at θ_frag = 36.4°. Ring closure (22.5°) sits BELOW it, margin ×1.62.
U(θ_ring)=1.06 MeV/hinge, U(θ_frag)=3.54 MeV/hinge.

(A) BENDING TO FORM: E_form = 16·U(θ_ring) = 16.9 MeV bends all 16 hinges to 22.5° — every
hinge below θ_frag → the rod curls into a loop ELASTICALLY, no fragmentation, then the ends
bond. Requires the bend delivered COHERENTLY over ~the whole rod (galactic collision /
primordial turbulence spanning the rod length) — not just total energy.

(B) BENDING TO BREAK (distributed): a raised-cosine hinge-angle bump over ℓ hinges; the
loop fragments only where a hinge reaches θ_frag. E_break(ℓ): 3.5 MeV (ℓ=1, focused) → ~7
(ℓ=2–4) → 10.6 (ℓ=8) → 19.7 (ℓ=16). E_break RISES with ℓ → distribution is PROTECTIVE, as
the founder argued. Focused-blow worst case: a 0.49 MeV blow bends one hinge to 15.9°, 1 MeV
to 22.0°, 2 MeV to 29.4° — ALL elastic (< θ_frag=36.4°). Even a FULLY FOCUSED 1 MeV blow
cannot fragment a hinge.

## The hierarchy (the clean, consistent result)
form (16.9 MeV, coherent) ≫ break (≥3.5 MeV focused, ×ℓ more if distributed) ≫ collision
quantum (0.49–1 MeV). A 0.49–1 MeV blow — the σ/m collisional-fragmentation scale (0860) —
bends a hinge to ≤22° even fully focused, so the loop RIDES IT OUT as elastic flexure and
springs back. Fragmenting a hinge needs ~3.5 MeV focused; forming a loop needs ~17 MeV
coherent. The three regimes are cleanly separated and self-consistent (a blow too weak to
break is far below the coherent energy that forms — the same flexure channel handles both).
