## Why you are being convened

In the prior round, this panel reviewed the "ZBW rebound dance" treatment of dark matter candidate (B) — an N_planes=16 closed ring of 8 DM elements at 11.26 GeV, the sole LZ-surviving candidate of the OPEN-SS-43 campaign. The panel landed two accepted hits: (H1) the founder's specification mandates per-CP speeds v ∝ SSV, which the implementation had simplified to constant speed; (H2) the registry (lattice-registration) channel was priced asymmetrically — invoked as tilt stiffness while the ring's inner-edge compression was never charged. The adjudication pre-registered, before either computation existed: *if the ring survives both computations, the verdict returns to ~70–75% and the promotion decision goes to the founder with the panel record attached; if either flips the ring, ~30% leaning-FALSIFIED.*

Both computations have now been executed (Patches 2461, 2462). At the pre-registered criteria, the kill branch did not fire and the survive branch fired at its letter; the working verdict was set to ~70% — the low end of the band, discounted for named contingencies below. **The founder has elected NOT to exercise the promotion decision unilaterally. His words: the promotion would not mean anything if he cannot weigh the criteria himself; the panel should judge whether the story told is worthy of promotion.** That judgment is this round's commission.
