## §1 — The frozen Moment rule (charter, restated operationally)

Synchronous over a periodic M³ simple-cubic GP lattice (torus),
lattice unit δ = 1. All Moment-t quantities computed from Moment
t−1 state. Per Moment:

1. Every GP g transmits its outgoing payload to every GP on its
   PSR shell S_R(g) (Euclidean band: g′ with R − ½ < |g′ − g| ≤
   R + ½, minimum-image).
2. Every GP sums arriving parcels: SSV_net(g) = Σ q_p · û(src → g);
   SSV_abs(g) = Σ |q_p| (û = minimum-image unit vector from the
   transmitting GP; q_p = parcel's signed charge content).
3. Every CP c at GP g(c) displaces
   d = (|SSV_net(g)| / SSV_abs(g)) · R along SSV_net(g)/|SSV_net(g)|
   (its own polarity σ_c multiplying the direction: displacement
   along σ_c · SSV_net); SSV_net = 0 → no motion (commitment 15).
   The CP relocates to the GP nearest the continuum endpoint
   (minimum-image); multiple CPs may occupy one GP.
4. Carriers recycled (commitments 17–18): received bits are erased
   after aggregation.

## §2 — Implementation conventions (worker design resolutions, declared per the synchronous-semantics precedent; revisable only by fresh prereg)

- **DR-1 (relay payload):** each GP's outgoing payload at Moment t is
  its NET signed charge content of Moment t−1 — the aggregation
  "imprints the summation of ± data" as the net sum — divided
  equally among its |S_R| shell GPs (surface-integrated flux
  conservation). Source injection: each CP injects σ_c · 1 charge
  unit into its GP's payload every Moment. This yields an emergent
  retarded ~1/r² flux geometry with propagation speed R per Moment
  and a bounded field for a neutral Sea; it is the minimal faithful
  reading of Q4's "surface-collection … summation of type and ±
  data," and its adequacy is TESTED, not assumed, by gate V-1 below.
- **DR-2 (uniform-Sea PSR):** PSR_eff = R, one frozen shell radius
  per run (the uniform Sea's SSV_abs variations discretize to a
  constant lattice shell); the charter's resolution-robustness axis
  is R ∈ {2, 3, 4} (three full runs).
- **DR-3 (CP–field coupling):** a CP's own injected charge
  contributes to the fields other GPs receive but its self-parcel
  (the parcel whose source path is its own GP at the immediately
  preceding Moment) is excluded from its displacement sum — the
  2714 self-pair lesson applied at design time.

## §4 — Production runs and committed observables

**Runs (all frozen):** M = 24, N = 432 CPs (216 +, 216 −; matches
the screening-campaign count), R ∈ {2, 3, 4}; warm-up 2×10⁴ Moments;
production 8×10⁴ Moments, configuration sampled every 20 Moments
(4000 samples); analysis rng seed 2796. A paired GIBBS REFERENCE
ensemble is generated by the committed 2761 Metropolis machinery
ADAPTED to the automaton's lattice Hamiltonian proxy: H(config) =
Σ_{i<j} σ_i σ_j / d_min(i, j) (minimum-image lattice distance,
self-pairs excluded), at temperature θ_eff MATCHED to the automaton
(below). H is likewise the energy functional for deliverables
(i)/(vi) on automaton samples.

**Committed evaluations (per R; 24-block × 2000-resample bootstrap
for every verdict quantity):**

- **D-(vi) first, θ_eff calibration:** θ_H = the Gibbs temperature
  at which ⟨H⟩_Gibbs = ⟨H⟩_automaton (bisection, frozen tolerance
  0.5%); θ_κ = the Gibbs temperature at which the like/unlike
  pair-correlation contrast C = ⟨g_unlike/g_like⟩ over lattice
  r ∈ [2, 6] matches. **Committed observable: θ_κ/θ_H; band
  [0.9, 1.1]** (outside → observable-dependent temperature,
  reported as an adverse finding for the Gibbs reading).
- **D-(ii) Gibbsian screening accuracy:** at θ_H, compare the
  charge-correlation envelope decay constant κ_auto vs κ_Gibbs
  (identical extraction: log-envelope slope of |g_zz| over lattice
  r ∈ [2, 6], fixed window, both ensembles). **Band: agreement
  within 2σ combined AND |Δκ|/κ_Gibbs ≤ 10%.**
- **D-(i) energy-only marginal:** split each ensemble's samples at
  its own median H; committed statistic Δ_split = the L2 difference
  of g_zz between splits. **Band: |Δ_split^auto − Δ_split^Gibbs| ≤
  3× pooled bootstrap error.**
- **D-(iii) stationary currents:** time-asymmetry statistic
  A = ⟨H(t) · D(t+τ)⟩ − ⟨D(t) · H(t+τ)⟩ (D = |net dipole|), τ = 5
  samples, normalized by the geometric mean of autocovariances.
  **Band: |A| ≤ 2σ_boot** (detailed-balance-consistent).
- **D-(iv) slow variables:** integrated autocorrelation times of H,
  D, and N_close (pairs at d_min ≤ 2): reported; any τ > 10 × τ_H
  is flagged SLOW-VARIABLE (report-only, no band).
- **D-(v) directional susceptibility:** three biased runs per R
  (ε = 0.02 uniform vector added to every SSV_net, one axis each,
  10⁴ Moments): dipole response per axis; **band: anisotropy of the
  three responses ≤ 2σ, response sign positive.**
