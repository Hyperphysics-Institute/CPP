\begin{abstract}
\noindent DM-1 identified a dark-matter candidate --- the Cross-Rod, an $N \approx 15$--$20$ element
substrate aggregate at $25.3$~GeV whose screening length is the zero-parameter value
$\Rs = r_c/\chi = 25.4$~fm --- and DM-2 certified its gravitational footing. DM-3 is the harvest: the
candidate's complete testable surface, assembled as nine observational protocols, each with an
instrument class, an observable, and explicit decision bands. Three results organize the suite.
\textbf{(1) The plateau.} The candidate's high-velocity self-interaction is a \emph{measured} geometric
floor ($\som = 0.05 \to 0.02$ across $1150$--$3500$~km/s), while the standard velocity-dependent SIDM
form fitted to the same dwarf and LSB anchors has collapsed to $10^{-3}$--$10^{-5}$ there: a shape
divergence of $\times 56$ (groups), $\times 121$ (clusters), $\times 2000$ (Bullet) with zero remaining
normalization freedom. Group-scale analyses at current sensitivity are therefore a \emph{three-way}
channel (F1$'$): a detection at $0.03$--$0.05$ selects the candidate over single-mediator SIDM; a firm
$0.5$ kills it; a firm null below $0.02$ kills its floor and favors SIDM. \textbf{(2) The running-slope
law.} The dissipative-reach capture mechanism yields a closed-form local velocity exponent,
$p(v) = 4\Rs/(b_{\max}(v) + \Rs)$, running $0.60 \to 0.98 \to 1.80 \to 3.0$ across $10 \to 600$~km/s ---
a shape neither a power law nor an SIDM knee reproduces --- and the law \emph{inverts}: two slope
measurements determine $\Rs$ from halo data alone. Since $\Rs = r_c/\chi$, this is, to our knowledge,
the first proposal to measure a lattice-geometry constant from galactic dynamics (F-DM3-2).
\textbf{(3) The bound-state threshold.} The rod--nucleus attractive channel supports bound states only
above a sharp critical mass number: at the ruling coupling \emph{no terrestrial element binds}
($A_{\rm thresh} = 257$; Pb sits at $s = 1.32$ against the critical $1.68$), so the anomalous-isotope
prediction is a clean null over most of the survival island --- itself discriminating against generic
composite dark matter --- switching to a heavy-element-only population ($A \gtrsim 186$) at the island's
top edge, four orders of magnitude below the pinned AMS limits (Javorsek et al., anomalous Au at
$200$--$350$~amu; a rod-bound Au nucleus is $224$~amu). The suite further carries the XQC-class reflight
prediction ($46$ events at the ruling point; $8$--$50$ region-weighted, a factor $11$--$30$ below
existing data), the deep-Earth population, the np consistency bound, the multipole kill-branches, the
dSph saturation band $\sigma(10\,{\rm km/s}) \approx 15$, and the live DESI-era $w(z)$ watch inherited
from DM-2. Everything is conditional on DM-1's explicitly provisional coupling ruling and DM-2's named
open gate, and every kill propagates: this paper states where the candidate dies, where it is merely
constrained, and --- unusually --- where it can be positively selected.
\end{abstract}

\textbf{S2$'$ --- logarithmic saturation.} As $v \to 0$ the reach grows as $b \sim \Rs\ln(1/v^2)$, so

\section{The bound-state channel (P2)}
\label{sec:bound}
The rod--nucleus channel at the DM-1 ruling ($S_c = R_N/\Rs = 0.035$, island $[0.012, 0.05]$) is
attractive, $V = -A\,E_{rN}S_c\,(r_c/r)e^{-r/\Rs}$, and an attractive Yukawa binds only above the
critical screening coupling $s = 2\mu g^2 \Rs/(\hbar c)^2 \geq 1.680$. Both $g^2$ and $\mu$ grow with
$A$, so binding has a sharp mass-number threshold: $A_{\rm thresh} = 257$ at the ruling point ---
\emph{no terrestrial element binds}; even Pb sits at $s = 1.32$ --- falling to $186$ only at the
island's top edge. Consequences: the stringent light-element anomalous-isotope limits
($\lesssim 10^{-28}$) are satisfied by across-the-board sub-criticality, not threshold-dodging; over
most of the island the prediction is a clean null (generic strongly-interacting composite dark matter
binds --- the null discriminates); and at the top edge a heavy-element-only population
(W, Pt, Au, Pb, Th, U) switches on at a crustal ceiling $\sim 10^{-15}$/nucleus. \textbf{The pin
(J-DM3-1, closed):} Javorsek et al.\ (PRD \textbf{64}, 012005) searched anomalous Au and Fe over
$200$--$350$~amu --- a rod-bound Au nucleus at $\approx 224$~amu is in-window --- with limits
$X/{\rm Au} \sim 10^{-11}$--$10^{-8}$: the top-edge ceiling undershoots by $\geq 4$ orders. Safe across
the island; an AMS sensitivity gain of $\sim 10^4$ would probe the top edge, and a heavy-only
anomalous-isotope detection with the predicted $A$-threshold is a signature no generic WIMP shares
(F-DM3-1). The opening heuristic for this channel predicted Fe binding at $\sim$12 keV; the computation
overturned it before any claim --- recorded per discipline.
