## 1. What this registration establishes

This document fixes the date and content of a specific scientific conjecture: that the dark matter of cosmology is composed of **charge-neutral aggregates of qDPs and hybrid tetrahedra (hTetras)** — substrate structures already defined within Conscious Point Physics — rather than any new, undiscovered particle. It records the mechanism by which the conjecture is meant to work, the quantitative falsification gates it has already passed, the one role that has been *conditionally falsified and is therefore explicitly disclaimed*, and the open dependencies that remain.

It does **not** claim the conjecture is proven. It claims priority on the *idea, the mechanism, and the evidentiary state* as of the date above. The standing of each component is labelled throughout so that no reader mistakes a cleared admission-gate for a completed derivation.

## 2. The conjecture (precisely scoped)

**Primary claim (the dark-matter microphysics).** Dark matter is identified with gravitationally-bound, electromagnetically-neutral aggregates ("clouds") of qDPs (quark-type dipoles, +qCP/−qCP) and hybrid tetrahedra (hTetras), frozen out of the early universe. These aggregates are the gravitating, electromagnetically dark, collisionless, cold component responsible for the dark-matter phenomenology — galactic rotation curves, cluster dynamics, lensing mass, and the matter budget — and they require no new particle, only a known configuration of the existing CPP substrate.

**Explicitly disclaimed (the structure-formation role).** This conjecture does **not** claim that the dark-matter aggregates *generate* the primordial spectrum of density perturbations from which cosmic structure grows. The early "swirl-seed" generation mechanism has been examined and found to be a causal/active-source mechanism that fails the standard super-horizon adiabatic-correlation tests (the same wall that excluded cosmic-string and topological-defect models as the primary structure source); within the CPP early-universe dynamics it is a clean conditional false. The generation of the primordial spectrum is instead supplied separately by **substrate inflation**, derived in the companion paper EU-1 (§8 below); the dark-matter aggregates **inherit** that spectrum passively and amplify it gravitationally, exactly as cold dark matter does in the standard cosmological model. The claim registered here is the *microphysical identity of dark matter*, not the origin of structure.

## 3. Mechanism

The conjecture rests on the following physical chain, each link of which is internal to CPP and stated as the framework's proposal.

**Composition and abundance.** In the hot early universe, oppositely charged Conscious Points bind into dipoles. The qCP carries a color-type affinity to other qCPs in addition to the ordinary electric attraction, making the qDP a doubly-bound species, favored over the singly-bound hybrid dipoles; charge-conservation bookkeeping then ties the surviving populations together and drains the hybrids, many of them into the more deeply bound hТetras. The early universe therefore freezes out a population rich in qDPs and hTetras.

**Electromagnetic darkness.** A qDP is a bound pair of opposite charges and an hTetra is charge-balanced; each is net neutral, so at any distance large compared to its sub-quantum size the fields of its poles cancel and only its stored-energy (gravitational) monopole survives. A cloud of such structures is electromagnetically quiet by construction — it neither emits, absorbs, nor scatters light.

**Gravitation.** In CPP, gravity arises from the Space Stress Velocity (SSV) field sourced by stored energy density; the companion derivations c05 (Newtonian limit, with G = ℏc/m_P² fixed, no free parameter) and c07 (weak-field general relativity) supply the force law. A qDP and, more strongly, an hTetra each carry organized binding energy and therefore a real SSV excess and gravitational mass; the energy that sources gravity is unsigned, so the interaction is universally attractive.

**Collisionlessness and coldness.** The aggregates are sub-quantum in size, so their geometric cross-section is minuscule; the only long-range force they project is gravity (the electromagnetic field cancels at range; the bonding interaction is short-range and near-contact). At galactic-halo densities the collision rate is negligible. At the relevant masses (GeV-scale) they are cold, non-relativistic from the QCD era onward. A collisionless population cannot dissipate energy, so it builds extended, dispersion-supported halos rather than collapsing into disks, and collisionless self-gravitating dynamics generically produces the extended density profiles associated with flat rotation curves.

## 4. Evidence status — the falsification gates

The conjecture has been pursued under a hard-ordered, falsification-first sequence (cheapest potential kills first). The status of each gate, as of this registration, is as follows; "pass" denotes survival of an admission gate, not a discriminating proof.

- **Step 1 — self-interaction cross-section (σ/m vs the SIDM bound).** PASS. The short-range bonding momentum-transfer cross-section integrated over halo encounter rates gives σ/m ≈ 4×10⁻³ cm²/g (light/worst-case qDP) to 8×10⁻⁴ cm²/g (hTetra) — roughly 250–1250× below the ~1 cm²/g self-interacting-dark-matter bound, surviving even a hundredfold resonant enhancement. Residual: pin the constituent mass and bound a possible near-threshold resonance in the light channel.

- **Step 2 — abundance bookkeeping.** NO KILL; not a clean closure. Double-counting the hybrid-tetrahedral content already bound into nucleons is a ~19% effect and cleanly avoidable; abundance is not the binding constraint (the Sea reservoir is ample). The ~5:1 dark-to-baryonic ratio is **not derived** — it enters as the free primordial perturbation amplitude, which relocates rather than resolves the standard cosmological coincidence.

- **Step 3 — coldness.** PASS, by a wide margin. At GeV-scale constituent mass the aggregates are non-relativistic from the QCD era, some 10⁵–10⁶× above the warm-dark-matter velocity bound by matter–radiation equality.

- **Step 5 — galactic rotation curve.** PASS (representative galaxy). The c05 zero-parameter force law applied to a collisionless qDP/hTetra halo yields an approximately flat rotation curve (~220 km/s) with halo-to-baryon mass ratio ≈ 4.5, against a Keplerian decline for baryons alone. Stated honestly: flat curves are generic to *any* collisionless halo and are therefore not, by themselves, discriminating between this candidate and standard cold dark matter; the CPP-specific content is the derived G, the no-new-sector composition, and consistency with Steps 1–3.

- **Step 4 — origin of the primordial spectrum.** CONDITIONALLY FALSE for the dark-matter aggregates as the *generating* mechanism (see §2). This role is disclaimed; the generation is supplied by EU-1 (§8).

## 5. Open dependencies and falsifiers

The conjecture remains open and is falsifiable along the following named lines.

- **R2 — Sea gravitation (the principal open dependency).** The uniform Dipole Sea must *not* gravitate cosmologically (or it would produce a vacuum-energy catastrophe), while its inhomogeneities *must* gravitate (or they could not be dark matter). A single mechanism delivering suppressed uniform-mode gravitation together with unsuppressed inhomogeneity gravitation and a consistent Friedmann background is required. This is gated on the cosmological-constant problem (registry item OPEN-SR-5) and is the hard prerequisite the candidate most depends on.

- **Constituent-mass and resonance closure (Step 1 residual).** The σ/m result must be sealed by pinning the constituent mass and excluding a large near-threshold scattering resonance in the light channel.

- **Discriminating halo structure.** A derivation of the halo density profile ρ(r) from the substrate dynamics, predicting Tully–Fisher and core/cusp behavior, would convert the (non-discriminating) rotation-curve consistency into a genuine test. This is not done.

**Clean falsifiers.** The conjecture would be refuted by: a self-interaction cross-section measured above the SIDM bound; a demonstration that net-neutral qDP/hTetra aggregates cannot be both gravitationally active in their inhomogeneities and cosmologically inert in their uniform mode (failure of R2); or a constituent-mass determination forcing warm/hot rather than cold behavior.
