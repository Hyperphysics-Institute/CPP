## §2 — Worker extraction (commitments 10–13, joining the 2697/2700 set)

10. **Micro time-reversal symmetry.** The update rule carries no
    intrinsic arrow: DI-bit reversal universe-wide steps the state
    back exactly; two adjacent frames are locally order-indistinguishable.
11. **No isolation, ever.** DI-bits penetrate all space; every CP's
    Moment integrates the whole universe. Apparent isolation is a
    resolution artifact.
12. **Global irreversibility by information integration.** The
    universal evolution has a clear arrow: entropy grows through the
    continuous integration of arriving information; reversal would
    require universe-wide coordination.
13. (Carried from 2697:) **Stationarity under a static bias** — the
    time-averaged local pattern around a stable source is stationary
    and Gauss-law-obeying.

## §3 — The Grok gloss: preserved, with one conflation corrected

The founder-supplied gloss sharpens 10–12 usefully (local rule
T-symmetric; global dynamics effectively irreversible; entropy =
information accumulation). **One overreach is corrected for the
record:** the gloss asserts "detailed balance is almost certainly
violated at the fundamental level." That conflates two objects.
GLOBAL detailed balance (the universe in equilibrium) indeed fails —
commitment 12. But the object KINETIC-1 needs is LOCAL: the stationary
coarse-grained occupation statistics of a Sea region under a static
bias (commitment 13). For THAT object, micro T-symmetry (10) plus
stationarity (13) is precisely the classical license for an effective
detailed-balance / fluctuation-response structure — and commitment 11,
inverted, is not an obstacle but the MECHANISM: if no region is ever
isolated, then every region is permanently coupled to an enormous
environment, i.e., **the universal DI-bit field IS the bath**. The
impossibility of system–bath separation is the guarantee that a bath
exists everywhere. The founder's answer therefore supplies exactly the
three properties the fluctuation-response route (the R1 panel's
option 4) requires: reversible microdynamics, universal bath coupling,
stationary local states. What it correctly withholds — and what
KINETIC-1 must still derive rather than assume — is that the
stationary local measure is GIBBSIAN (exponential in the energy
functional); commitments 10–13 license the response FORM via
fluctuation-response with an effective θ, and the founder's own
entropy-by-information-integration framing (12) points at the native
derivation of the Gibbs question: maximum entropy subject to the slow
local constraints.
