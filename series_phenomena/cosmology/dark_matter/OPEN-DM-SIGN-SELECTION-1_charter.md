## §3 The mechanism chain (each step named; each is a computation, not a posit)
(a) **Freeze** (done, 3520): lone ±qCP, ±eCP populations R₊ = R₋ at T_f(λ).
(b) **Cocoon** (owed): a lone centre of sign s polarises the surrounding sea DPs radially (inward for s = −, outward for s = +); stabilisation energy E_coc(s) — the same quantity whose *suppression of pairing* was C-5's reopening route (3520 §3), now computed for its own sake.
(c) **Chiral split** (owed): in the 600-cell of stated handedness the inward and outward shells are not mirror-equivalent: ΔF_dress = E_coc(+) − E_coc(−) with |ΔF_dress| = (χ/6)·E_coc to leading order (THEO-SD-CHIR-2's matrix element); **sign from the geometry with the handedness as input.**
(d) **Undoing** (owed): the less stable centre is dissociated by partner switching at rate ∝ e^{−E_coc(s)/kT}; the more stable survives. Selectivity a = tanh(ΔF_dress/2kT_dress) in the equilibrium limit; kinetic corrections by the same Boltzmann treatment as 3520.
(e) **Return** (founder's picture): the undone −qCP re-forms hDP-B and is absorbed into hTetra planes and DP entities.
(f) **Closure**: n_B = a·R read against η_B; the DM sector's B-content = the dressing deficit.
