   retraction + pivot to extended aggregates — kept in the record. The reviewers later singled this out as the
   paper's credibility hinge.

   physics (strand buckles; ball dilutes to d_f ≈ 2.5). The cube-core element is the founder's structural
   contribution.
3. **Honor the corona dissent, don't outvote it (0883–0885).** A 3-1 panel could have lifted the lemma; instead
   the lone RESTATE was honored, the residual computed (surface-mode spectrum), and the lift taken only on the
   dissenter's own concession (4/4).
4. **Refuse to fabricate E_bond; scope it instead (0865/0887).** Alternative: manufacture a coupling for a
   single σ/m number. Chosen: bracket what's robust, route the absolute number to the external make-or-break.
5. **v0.2 review-candidate, v1.0 on convergence (Thomas, 0888).** Don't flip the v1.0 label without the
   paper-level panel; promote only on 4/4.
