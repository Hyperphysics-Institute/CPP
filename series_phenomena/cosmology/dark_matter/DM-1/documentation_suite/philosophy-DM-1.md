# Philosophy — DM-1

## Certainty level
**Empirically-motivated candidate / consistency result — Layer C** (with the corona retirement at Layer B).
NOT a theorem and NOT a confirmed identification. The Cross-Rod is a *viable, corona-safe, velocity-dependent
SIDM candidate*; σ/m-viability is established, a discriminating σ/m *value* is not. CONJ-COSMO-1 stays
NOT-confirmed.

## Relationship to the Standard Model
**Extends without replacing.** Adds no new field: dark matter is identified with charge/color-neutral
aggregates the CPP substrate already contains. Reproduces the standard SIDM phenomenology (cores + cluster
collisionlessness) and the cold, collisionless admission bar; *disagrees* with light-mediator SIDM on the sign
of the velocity dependence (CPP: σ/m falls with v).

## Falsifiability inventory (thresholds)
1. **σ/m must fall with v.** If cross-system data require σ/m flat or rising with velocity → the fragmentation
   mechanism fails.
2. **N_dwarf ~ tens.** If the kinetics force N_dwarf ~ hundreds → overshoots the band by 10–30× → fails.
3. **E_bond ∈ [0.8 keV, 2 MeV], E_bond/kT_form ~ 24–41.** Pre-registered: if the SF-2/SF-5 edge-bond SSV
   returns a depth outside this window → the candidate is falsified (too fragile to survive 14 Gyr, or too
   stiff to fragment in clusters).

## Paper-type declaration
**Candidate / consistency paper** (per the OS §4 taxonomy) — not a zero-parameter-prediction paper. It earns
v1.0 by being a fully-specified, internally-consistent, falsifiable candidate with its one open number honestly
routed to a named external calculation, not by supplying a confirmed prediction.

## Limits of scope
Does **not** establish: the absolute σ/m value (external SF make-or-break); the abundance / Ω_DM (calibrated
swirl amplitude); the full cosmological identification (DM-2 conditional-but-traversed); halo-by-halo scatter.
The conclusions are **framework-internal** — conditional on the correctness of the CPP inputs inherited from
SF-3/SF-5/SR-1/QM-1.
