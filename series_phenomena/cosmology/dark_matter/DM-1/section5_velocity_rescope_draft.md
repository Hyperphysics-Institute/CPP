## 0. Definitions (for any reader; every term defined once)

- **CP (Conscious Point)** — the CPP substrate primitive. **eCP / qCP** — the electric and color varieties.
- **DP** — a bound pair of CPs. **eDP** = two eCPs; **qDP** = two qCPs. (Identities: 8 eCP = 4 eDP, 8 qCP = 4 qDP
  — the same objects counted in different groupings.)
- **hTetra** — a tetrahedral bound cluster of DPs; ~50% of the ambient "DP-Sea" (the substrate vacuum population).
- **DM element** — the elemental dark-matter particle: an **8qCP + 8eCP moiety** (~1408 MeV), a color core
  buffered by an electric coat.
- **Cross Rod** — the dark-matter particle: an assembly of **N DM elements** joined along a continuous axial
  **E_qq** color spine. "Cross Rod" and "DM particle" are used interchangeably; "DM element" is the repeating unit.
- **E_qq** — the strong (color) bond: the axial core spine and the fusion-junction edge bond. **E_ee** — the
  weaker electric bond of the outer eCP coat; a *strippable coat and secondary bending-stiffness layer*, not a
  structural edge bond.
- **σ/m** — the **transport (momentum-transfer) self-interaction cross-section per unit mass**, in cm²/g. For an
  extended body it is set by geometry (the area the object presents to a collision, weighted by how much momentum
  an encounter transfers), with the orientation-averaged projected/crossing area as its upper bound.
- **δ\*** — the penetration depth at which the collision-driven E_qq core attraction balances the eCP-coat
  repulsion; the threshold that separates capture from bounce.

## 1. What this re-scope changes (for the reviewer)

DM-1 v1.0 shipped with a **fragmentation** velocity mechanism (the rod *breaks* at cluster speed → collisionless).
The 29–30 June geometry correction retired it: the rod's core is a **continuous axial E_qq color spine**, so a
transverse cut costs ~600 MeV — a ~1000× gap above any cluster collision. The rod does not fragment. The velocity
dependence is carried instead by a channel that never cuts the core — **strip-then-fuse** — which runs in the same
data-preferred direction (σ/m falling with v) by a sounder mechanism. Only the *origin of the velocity dependence*
changes; the microphysics and the σ/m ∝ N logic are unaffected.

## 2. The Cross Rod, and why σ/m ∝ N

The relic is the **coat-inclusive Cross Rod**: a stack of DM elements (each an 8qCP core buffered by an 8eCP
coat) along the E_qq spine. The bare core is reactive; the coat is what makes the element durable. Because the
particle is extended, its self-interaction cross-section is **geometric** — two rods of length L = N·ℓ_el present
a rod–rod crossing area that scales as L², over a mass that scales as N, so **σ/m ∝ N**. *(Correction of record:
the coefficient is not 0.11 — that number is the dead compact/point-scattering value. The extended coefficient is
K = c_geo·ℓ_el²/m_el ≈ 0.013 cm²/g per element, so the data band σ/m ∈ [0.6, 2] sits at N ≈ 48–160 elements
(~10²–10³ DPs, consistent with the independent hoop estimate). Reaching the dwarf σ/m ~ 3 needs N ~ 240 elements.)*

**Formation** proceeds by three tracks, all *precursor chemistry* upstream of the E_qq structure: (1) four hTetras
apposed and qq-edge-bonded directly into the 8qCP core; (2) a 4-wide ribbon plus two hTetras; (3) a 4-wide ribbon
plus two eCP-qCP chains. The 4-wide ribbon is an *intermediate*, not obligatory. The competing hTetra-loop species
is ruled out (amorphous polymerization) — the Cross Rod is the sole relic.

## 3. Strip-then-fuse (the operative velocity channel)

Three collisional pictures are walled off — transverse fracture (~600 MeV E_qq spine), direct penetration
(~10⁴ km/s against the coat), and instant bare-core repair — and stating them motivates the channel that clears
all three: a collision **strips the cheap E_ee coat** (not the core), exposing bare E_qq, and two exposed cores
**fuse into an X-cross**. Color beats recombination (the strong E_qq bond outcompetes a Sea eDP re-coat, subject
to a velocity threshold because the attraction must also haul the rod mass), and the collision KE is **stored in
the Sea and released as sustained relative motion** (SF-6) so the cores stay in contact; rod-tail inertia backs
the contact, and penetration is set by a **force balance** — the inertial drive (∝ velocity × perpendicularity ×
centrality) against the coat repulsion. That balance depth is **δ\***, and — a founder correction folded in here —
it is set against **two** eCP coats (one per rod). Computed (1844): because the coats are alternating-charge
squares, the two-coat interaction is a **Madelung near-cancellation** — registry-averaged it is a small residual
(~0.36× the single-coat scale) and the coats can relax to the attractive registry, so **δ\* penetration is easier,
not harder** (v_thr ~0.6× or lower). The earlier √2-barrier estimate is retracted. The X arms are shorter than the reactants, so fusion
carries the σ/m-lowering effect fragmentation was meant to provide, and the formation rate rises with velocity.

## 4. The three encounter channels and the σ/m(v) curve

Every rod–rod encounter falls in one of three channels, all now pinned:
- **Slice-through — ruled out.** Halo velocities are far below the energy to cleave the E_qq spine (the same
  ~600 MeV wall, from the collision side). No transparent channel bleeds the cross-section.
- **Catch — the interaction we detect.** Penetration past δ* to E_qq contact → perpendicular bonding → the
  variable-arm X-hinge. Maximal momentum transfer, at matched mass/L/N.
- **Bounce — the elastic channel.** Sub-threshold encounters that don't reach δ*; momentum transfer graded by
  penetration depth and **capped at the bonding threshold** (past it, the encounter catches instead). Oblique and
  near-tail encounters are glancing; near-perpendicular, near-central sub-threshold ones bounce hardest.

So catch and bounce are one continuous transfer scale seamed at δ*, and the same velocity/perpendicularity/
centrality knobs that gate strip-then-fuse also weight the transport. σ/m sits **near the geometric ceiling**
(no transparent channel), pulled down only by glancing bounces — the earlier "lower-bound-only" hedge is removed.
Convolving with halo kinematics gives σ/m **falling from the dwarf value to a cluster floor**, with a knee whose
location tracks δ* (shifted by the two-coat correction).

## 5. The junction and the cluster floor (velocity sector — CLEARED)

The strip-then-fuse junction is a **single qq (E_qq) edge bond acting as a hinge**: stretch-stiff (E_qq holds it,
forbids fragmentation) but **angularly soft** (rotating about the edge does not stretch the bond). So the fused X
hinges freely by construction — the arms inertially decouple, the self-limiting works, and the floor stays viable.
This is confirmed quantitatively by a **scale-free ratio** g = κ_scissor/κ_bend, with **both stiffnesses the same
E_qq bond** (scissored at the junction vs bent along the spine): g ≈ 0.02 ≪ g_crit ≈ 0.43 — the hinge is ~50×
softer than the arm is stiff. It is pure geometry (E_qq/E_qq, absolute scale cancels; no ℓ_p, no formation
temperature, no substrate-thermodynamics pin — the Earnshaw/dynamic-operator subtlety cancels in the ratio). The
E_ee outer layer only mildly stiffens the denominator, making the hinge *more* clearly free. **The stiff-ribbon /
soft-scissor tension dissolves:** a stiff arm is the very denominator that makes g small — the junction is the
softer of two *stiff* modes, not a fragile joint. **Cluster floor: VIABLE, direction robust.** (The exact floor
value is a collision-dynamics quantity — the σ/m(v) convolution with the two-coat δ* — not a substrate pin.)

## 6. The present-day population (dilution — robustly defanged)

The relic population is dominated by the extended Cross Rod. Compact glueballs are a **strongly suppressed
minority**: a 4-wide ribbon that could bend-and-appose into a glueball is instead chaperoned into a Cross Rod by
**two abundant channels** — hTetra precipitation *and* eCP-qCP-chain apposition — so the chaperone concentration is
([hTetra] + [eCP-qCP chain]) / [ribbon] ≫ 1, over-determined by the Sea-vs-relic hierarchy. Glueball formation is
therefore *extremely rare*, the population-weighted σ_eff ≈ σ_ext, and the Cross Rod's eDP cocooning is mere
polarization (not a chained blanket), so it stays a clean extended relic. The remaining glueball-arrest question is
a de-risked residual (gated by the small glueball fraction).

The candidate reduces to the **E_qq structure plus collision dynamics**. The **velocity-sector floor is CLEARED**
(structural free-hinge + g ≈ 0.02, direction corpus-pinned); the exact floor pends the collision-dynamics

bond depth / fragmentation window and the aggregate-size formation dynamics (does PCD produce N ~ 10²–10³?).
