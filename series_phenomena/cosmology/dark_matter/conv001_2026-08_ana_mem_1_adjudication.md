## Q2 — CORRECTED. The charter's identification was a category error.

The charter (3504 §2) identified K̄ = ∫K dτ with the pinned SF-6
coefficients. **S2's ruling against this is adopted:**

1. **Units.** κ_q = 132, κ_e = 44 MeV/c² are MASSES. K̄ has units of
   force per velocity — a DRAG coefficient. They cannot be the same
   object. This is not a judgment call.
2. **P4 forces K̄ = 0 at first order.** A nonzero K̄ means a net force
   on a uniformly moving charge — exactly what the fore/aft
   cancellation forbids. Deeper: a drag on uniform motion is the old
   aether-drag killer; the Sea must resist CHANGES of motion (that is
   inertia, the founder's KE mechanism) and must not resist motion
   itself.
3. **The correct home of κ is the kernel's FIRST MOMENT.** Expanding
   the convolution: ∫K(τ)v(t−τ)dτ = K̄·v(t) − M₁·a(t) + …, with
   M₁ = ∫τK(τ)dτ carrying mass units — a mass renormalization /
   added-Sea-inertia term. S3's "effective mass shift" framing and
   S5's first-moment analysis converge here independently.

**Amended decomposition (supersedes 3504 §2; charter banner-amended
this patch):** organize by kernel moments, not by δ-function split —
- zeroth moment K̄ = 0 (fore/aft cancellation; testable, see T1);
- first moment M₁ = the pinned SF-6 inertia (modeled in 2510);
- **F_res ≡ kernel content beyond the first moment** (curvature/
  frequency-dependence; Darwin class and above). δ_mem bounds THAT.

**Verification tests adopted (synthesized from S2/S3/S4 specs):**
- **T1 — uniform-motion null (the no-aether-drag test).** Drive a
  charge at constant v through the built Sea; the net Sea force must
  be consistent with zero at first order. S4's proposed constant-v
  measurement of "K̄" becomes this null test under the correction.
- **T2 — first-moment match.** Extract K(τ) by linear-response probe;
  compute M₁ per species in the pinning convention; require agreement
  with κ_q and κ_e within 5% relative. Failure of T1 falsifies the
  cancellation regardless of T2 (S2's precedence rule adopted).
