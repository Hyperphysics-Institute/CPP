## §1 — The founder's ruling (verbatim, 2026-07-27)

> *"The CPs of the DPs that arc due to momentum will be influenced by
> the field/DI-Bits, not particles… the CP arcs will be affected at c
> each Moment by informing their neighbors of the positions of their
> moving charge. Thus, the number of PSR (l_P) that the CPs of the DP
> are displaced from each other is the length of time/number of
> Moments the displacement of one CP of the DP will take to influence
> the CP of the other DP… The arc will persist as long as the moving
> charge is moving, and then decay when the charge stops moving, and
> respond to the charge's acceleration or deceleration at local
> lightspeed."*

**Reading:** the arc configuration is not an independently relaxing
object with its own internal clock. It is **field-mediated**, and its
response time over a separation is the **light-crossing time of that
separation** — in the framework's native units, the number of PSR
hops, one per Moment (C22: c = one PSR per Moment).

**This supersedes both 2834 readings.** Not (A) Compton-scale ZBW; not
(B) Sea-lattice ZBW period. **Reading (C): τ_Sea(d) = d/c.** The
scale-transfer worry named as the sketch's weakest joint is thereby
**dissolved** — τ_Sea carries no imported lattice number at all; it is
fixed by the separation itself at whatever scale the question is
asked.

## §2 — The derivation

At the scale d_DP:

- **τ_Sea = d_DP / c** (founder's ruling: field-mediated response at
  local lightspeed across the separation).
- **τ_slow = d_DP / v**, v = DP-centre translational speed (the
  retained slow variable; §2 of the sketch, unchanged).

Therefore

> **ε_mem = τ_Sea / τ_slow = (d_DP/c)/(d_DP/v) = v / c.**

**d_DP cancels identically.** The induced-memory parameter is
scale-independent and equals the velocity ratio.

## §3 — Three consequences worth stating

**(i) ε_mem IS the framework's own primitive.** CPP's displacement law
is d = (|SSV_net|/SSV_abs)·PSR with **v/c ≡ |SSV_net|/SSV_abs**
(founder-confirmed, Patch 2732; C20). So

> **ε_mem = |SSV_net| / SSV_abs** — the quantity every CP already
> computes each Moment. The memory-subdominance parameter is not a new
> construct requiring new machinery; it is the ratio the Moment rule is
> written in, and it is **bounded ≤ 1 by construction**.

**(ii) This is retardation, recovered.** In any field-mediated theory,
projecting out the field leaves the retained charges with a memory
that *is* the retardation, and the expansion parameter for a source
moving at v is v/c. The derivation reproduces standard electrodynamic
structure from the Moment rule — consistent with the arc's identity as
the magnetic field (C23) and with AUTOMATON's emergent inverse-square
result.

**(iii) It explains the founder's qualitative claim exactly.** His ZBW
draft's "rapidity of the ZBW cycle compared to the translational
velocity of the DP center" is precisely the statement v ≪ c. The
qualitative assertion and the quantitative criterion are the same
statement.
