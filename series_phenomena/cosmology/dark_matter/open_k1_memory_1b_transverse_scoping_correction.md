## §1 — The founder's correction accepted, with one subtlety that must be kept straight

**Accepted:** propagation is strictly at c; the A2 relay implements
this correctly (Q_{t+1} = W_R ∗ (Q_t + inj_t) reaches distance nR only
after n Moments). No instantaneous action exists anywhere in the
engine or in the doctrine.

**The subtlety, stated because the test's logic depends on it:** the
electrodynamic result invoked at 2839 — *field points at the
instantaneous source position for uniform motion* — is **NOT a
statement that communication is instantaneous.** It is a theorem
ABOUT a strictly retarded theory: for constant velocity, the signal
delay is exactly compensated by the source's displacement during
transit, so the retarded field *appears* to point at where the source
is now. A fully retarded theory can, and electrodynamics does, produce
apparent instantaneous pointing. **The test was therefore not
premised on instantaneous communication and remains correctly posed.**

## §2 — What the test actually measured, and the scoping error it exposes

In electrodynamics that compensation requires **two** sectors: the
retarded scalar potential AND the vector (current) potential. The
cancellation is an interplay between them.

**The 2839 configuration contained only one.** The source sat in a
**static uniform neutralising background** — the committed jellium
construction. There were **no dynamical Sea CPs, no displaced DP
poles, and therefore no arc configuration**. But C23 places the
magnetic field precisely THERE: *inertia stored in the arc
displacement of DP poles; the arcing motion of the DPs around a moving
charge IS the magnetic field.*

**So the test measured the bare relay's scalar sector in isolation and
found — correctly — that a purely scalar retarded field points at the
retarded position.** That is what a scalar retarded field does. The
sector whose interplay produces the cancellation was structurally
absent from the configuration.

**Consequence for interpretation:** the 2840 verdict is a valid
statement about **the bare relay without a responding Sea**. It is
**NOT** a refutation of C23, because C23's mechanism had no
representation in the run. The record is corrected on that point.

only (C21, closed), and that content cannot generate a current sector
by itself. Whether the *Sea-mediated* arc response supplies the
missing sector is **untested and, on the record, untestable at
present**: it requires a dynamically responding Sea, which is the
AUTOMATON arc's closed FEM boundary (2812 §2, L-2).

**Therefore:**

**This is a narrowing, not a reprieve.** A worker who used a scoping
