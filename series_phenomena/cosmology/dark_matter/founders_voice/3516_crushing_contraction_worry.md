## Worker's reply (from the corpus, with the file names; nothing minted)

1. **There is no 10⁷⁹-CP stack at a GP.** The founder's own ruling R-IGNITION-BALL-IN-ONE-PSR (3816, `eu1_derivation/occupancy_regrounding.md`) put the pre-ignition ball at one Planck sphere across with **twelve CPs per address** over ~10⁸³ sub-Planck addresses; the "crowd at one address" was disowned at FORK-EU-OCCUPANCY-1 (3814). n̄ is the count within one rest-frame Planck sphere, not a stack depth.

2. **The pull does not scale as every CP on every CP.** AP-4 has every GP emit a **fixed number of DI-bits per Moment regardless of occupancy** (the premise of exact shell dilution, verified at 2959; confirmed in the 3862 positive return). SSV_abs is a census that saturates: from Moment 2 every register is at capacity and the PSR sits at its floor (`occupancy_regrounding.md` §1). Twelve CPs at an address, or 10⁷⁹, pull as one emitter. And SSV_net over a homogeneous ball cancels by symmetry except the shell-theorem inward residual.

3. **The inward residual has nowhere to act.** A maximally packed ball cannot contract: there is no empty address inward to move into. The corpus computed exactly this for the icosahedral ignition at 3805 (`ignition_handoff_bonding_time.md` §2–3): *the push does not expand and does not collapse* — the stacks pair within ≤ 15 Moments, and the like-charge self-energy is **stored** (the reheating budget, 3710 iii; OPEN-EU-REHEAT-BUDGET-1) and released as the lattice de-saturates outward. The strong channel, polarity-blind and attractive, can only add binding between neighbours already at the floor spacing: pressure, not motion.

4. **Expansion in the registered picture never was a field push.** The H-engine's driver is the held-stack entropy, μ = kT ln n̄ (S-HENGINE-HELD, ratified 3709–3711; `hengine_driver_statement.md`): every CP hops every Moment, every empty address is outward, and the crowd's free energy is what thins the lattice. The strong affinity enters as a **landing bias of 2.7** (3902) — it moves *where* a hop lands, not *whether* it leaves. That is why the C-5 arc could be built on it without SSV_net rescuing anything. The Big Bang idea does not die when SSV_net fails to point outward, because it was never carrying the expansion.

So like-charge repulsion is unlikely to be the post-count-law expansion driver. What the corpus has in its place is the **release of the stored self-energy at de-saturation** — reheating — which is the EU lane's open item and is not computed.
