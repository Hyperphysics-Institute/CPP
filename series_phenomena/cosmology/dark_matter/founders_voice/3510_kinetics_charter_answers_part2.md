## Q2 — When
> This pairing and qSCP-qSCP attraction and SCP formation is inherently Planck-scale. The reason being that there is a high probability (reducing as the epoch proceeds) during the Planck epoch of landing on another SCP. As dilution proceeds, a plus and minus qCP landing on a qCP of opposite polarity forms a qDP, which oscillates as a ZBW cycle, the seed of the DP-entities.

## Q3 — Who pairs with whom
> The qCP that lands on another SCP will simply be drawn to the SCPs around it, but the qSCPs will have a greater affinity, and their released qCPs will migrate toward each other, concentrating their qCPs due to the external biasing toward the other qSCP on their next Moment's hop.

## Q4 — Why a quark stays bare
> I don't think the quarks form strongly/reliably in the high SCP concentration environment. The up quark, the simplest unpaired qCP particle, is complex in the sense of being surrounded by polarized eDPs and qDPs. If the SCPs are incorporated into that environment in large numbers, they are likely to turn the central unpaired qCP of the up quark into a DP, which would dissipate its charge influence and eliminate the central organizing force of its central unpaired polar charge.
