# Founder correction, 12 Sep 2026 — "the −qCP centre is not up/down; it is anti-up/anti-down" (Patch 3524, DM block, from this window under PD-006)

**Founder text verbatim:**

> the -qCP center is not correct for a up/down, it is correct for an anti-up and anti-down.

## What this does to the record

**SM-2 §10 as written** (`series_standard_model/papers/SM-2_mass_generation_geometric_hierarchies.tex` §"Quark Charge Asymmetry and Capotauro"): *"Down-type quarks carry a linear ZBW extra DP (qDP/hDP) that up-type quarks do not. The CPP mechanism is the Capotauro chiral-polarity bias: the 600-cell's intrinsic chirality … preferentially stabilises linear ZBW extras on negative (−qCP) centres."* The chirality-continuum Sector B sketch (§15, THEO-CHIR-CONT-3) inherits the same sentence: −qCP centres preferentially stabilised, "the substrate-level account of the up/down quark charge asymmetry."

With the composition as the founder states it — matter up and down quarks are **+qCP centres** (down = up + a captured eCP / linear extra), −qCP centres are **anti-up and anti-down** — §10's mechanism sentence stabilises the extra on the *antiquark*, and so explains the anti-down, not the down. **Either §10 (and the sketch that inherits it) carries a sign slip and should read +qCP centres, or the sentence is right and the down/anti-down assignment in §10 was made with the opposite composition.** This is a corrigendum candidate for the SM lane (SM-2 is shipped: CONV-038 corrigendum path) and for the substrate-chirality arc; it is flagged here, not edited from the DM window.

## What it does to 3523's keyhole — the two readings, stated so the charter can test them rather than assume one

Let S be the centre polarity the chirality stabilises for a linear-ZBW extra.

- **Reading I — §10 corrected, S = +qCP.** Fixes the up/down story (the matter down quark, a +qCP centre, keeps its extra). Transferred naively to the hybrids, it stabilises hDP-A (the +qCP-centred hybrid): **A stays bound, B breaks, the freed lone charges are +eCP and −qCP — antimatter. The wrong sign for sequestration.**
- **Reading II — §10 as written, S = −qCP.** Gives 3523's sequestration sign (B stabilised, A breaks, matter = −eCP, +qCP) but then §10's own up/down explanation fails, exactly as the founder says.

**So one value of S cannot serve both stories under the naive transfer.** 3523's claim that "the registered operator gives the required sign without a choice" is therefore **withdrawn as stated**: the operator gives *a* sign, and which story it serves depends on a sign slip that has to be settled first. The keyhole is not closed; it is narrower and better defined:

1. **Settle S** — is §10's sentence right or slipped? (SM lane; the founder's composition is the arbiter; the up/down derivation in SM-1/SM-2 must be re-read with +qCP matter centres.)
2. **The transfer is not naive.** The operator was built for a qCP *centre* with a linear extra; in a hybrid the question is *which CP is the centre* — the qCP or the eCP — and THEO-SD-CHIR-2 is a qDP/eDP-sector theorem, so an eDP-sector statement exists to be read. If the hybrid is eCP-centred (the qCP oscillating about the eCP), then hDP-A is −eCP-centred and hDP-B +eCP-centred, and the sign comes from the eDP-sector operator, not the qDP one. If the chirality prefers a definite *electric* sign of centre regardless of species, both hybrids own one negative centre and this route gives **no** A/B split — the keyhole would have to be elsewhere (the ZBW sense d·L coupling, 3523 §1's symmetry argument, which survives this correction untouched).
3. **The symmetry statement of 3523 §1 stands:** whatever the mechanism, only hybrids can carry a CP-odd A/B split. That is the part of 3523 that did not depend on §10.

**Charter item 2 is now item 1**, and it is a cross-lane item: OPEN-DM-SIGN-SELECTION-1 cannot compute a sign until the SM lane settles S. The DM-side work that can proceed without it: item (2) — which CP is the hybrid's centre, from the ZBW dynamics of a mixed-species pair (the qCP's emission is 3× the eCP's, E_qDP = 3E_eDP; the centre is plausibly the qCP, but that is to be derived, not assumed).
