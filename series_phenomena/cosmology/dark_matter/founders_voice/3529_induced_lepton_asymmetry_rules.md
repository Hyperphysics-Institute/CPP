# Founder rules, 12 Sep 2026 — the lepton asymmetry is INDUCED by the quark asymmetry through hDP-B formation (Patch 3529, DM block, from this window under PD-006)

**Founder text verbatim:**

> rules:
> * The +qCP is dressed more stably than the -qCP (and this differential is more important than the stability of the eCP because of its greater binding affinity to its polarized cloud). Therefore, the +qCP does not escape from its dressing momentarily to react with the -eCP as often as the -qCP escapes to react with the antielectron.
> * The -qCP is dressed more weakly and escapes more frequently than the +qCPs, during which time it can react with the +eCP (anti-electron). Note that although the +eCP does not have the dressing weakness that the -qCP does, it can react with the bare -qCP. The bare -qCP can react with the dressed +eCP if it collides with enough collision force to get through the polarized DPs and release the orbital DP bound to the +eCP (which is released as a neutrino).
> The dressed +eCP need not be less stable inside its dressing (and escape its dressing to react with the -qCP). The +eCP forms as antimatter leptons (the dressed +eCPs with polarized DPs and orbital DP) can be reacted with by a bare -qCP and form an hDP-B. If extra bare -qCPs are present in the Sea. The -qCPs can bind with dressed +eCPs. Sufficient KE must be available in the collision to release the orbital DP. Note: when the bare -qCP binds with the dressed +eCP (anti-electron) it will generate a single photon (as opposed to the two photons generated by pair annihilation) and a neutrino (from the release of the eDP orbiting the +eCP).
> What do you think?

## Worker's reply

**I think it is the better structure, and it survives the check it invites.** The lepton sector needs no chiral sign of its own: the quark-sector split ΔF_q makes bare −qCPs common and bare +qCPs rare, and a bare −qCP that breaks into a dressed positron's cloud forms hDP-B — the +eCP/−qCP hybrid that is *already* the sequestration vessel of 3522 — releasing one photon and, from the positron's orbital eDP, one neutrino. Antimatter of both species leaves the free sector through one reaction. That is Sakharov's three conditions with one CP-odd input: the chiral split in the quark dressing; the out-of-equilibrium step in the escape-and-collision kinetics at 2543's window; and "number violation" replaced by sequestration, since every CP keeps its identity.

**The mirror channel must be in the ledger, and it loses.** The same kinetics permit bare −eCP + dressed +qCP → hDP-A + γ + ν, which removes *matter*. Both channels are the product of one escape and one penetration, so their Boltzmann factors cancel in the ratio and only the chiral splits remain (`code/3529_induced_lepton_asymmetry_check.py`, 3/3): B/A = exp[ΔF_q/kT] if the lepton dressing is sign-symmetric (the founder's statement), and B/A = exp[(ΔF_q − ΔF_e)/kT] if 3528's electric-polarity rule also destabilises the electron. **Either way B wins**, because the strong-sector split is three times the electric one (E_qDP = 3E_eDP): net exponent 2ΔF_e > 0 in the worst case. So 3528's lepton-sector "not passed" is not fatal — the composition (+qCP, −eCP) is consistent with a single chiral rule provided the asymmetry is generated by the cross-species channels rather than by lepton dressing itself. Per reaction, (B−A)/(B+A) = tanh(ΔF/2kT) ≈ 0.4–0.7 on the 3528 per-DP splits at kT = 10–17 MeV (pointer, not E2).

**Two things the picture now owes, pre-registered:**
1. **Counts (into E3).** Each hDP-B removes one −qCP and one +eCP; each hDP-A one −eCP and one +qCP. The surviving matter must obey charge neutrality, n_e⁻ = n_p, with three +qCP centres per baryon; the lone-population ratio N_q/N_e at the freeze (different bonds, 264 vs 88 MeV; different λ) and the channel rates must reproduce it. A mismatch is a fail of the picture, not a tuning target.
2. **Signature (into E4).** One γ + one ν per removed antilepton, against 2γ for pair annihilation: a relic neutrino count and a relic photon count each tied to the removed antimatter, and the same single-photon-plus-neutrino reaction wherever a bare −qCP meets a dressed +eCP.

**Charter amendment enacted:** E1's lepton clause is replaced — the lepton sector is not required to carry a chiral sign; E1 is the quark-sector sign (3528, conditional on the C-W46 convention). E2 becomes the two-channel net rate. E3 gains the neutrality count. E4 gains the γ+ν signature.
