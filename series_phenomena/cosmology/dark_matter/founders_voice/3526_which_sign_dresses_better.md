# Founder, 12 Sep 2026 — "your story would work if you can make the −qCP dress better than the +qCP" (Patch 3526, DM block, from this window under PD-006)

**Founder text verbatim:**

> your story would work if you can figure out a way to make the -qCP dress with polarized DPs better than the+qCP dresses.

## Worker's reply

**One question first, because the sentence can be read two ways and the two readings point in opposite directions.**
- Reading (i): "dresses better" = *forms the more stable centre and stays lone as a quark*. Then the −qCP dressing better makes the −qCP the matter quark — which contradicts 3524 (matter up/down are +qCP centres). Under this reading the founder is testing the story, and the story needs the +qCP to dress better; that is the same requirement as the SM-2 §10 corrigendum (S = +qCP).
- Reading (ii): "dresses with polarized DPs better" = *is wrapped by the sea more completely* — so completely that it is taken back into DP structure and sequestered (the founder's B population), rather than surviving as a bare centre. Then the −qCP dressing "better" is exactly the sequestration of the antimatter sign, and the story is consistent as told.

**The physics answer is the same under both readings: the sign of the dressing asymmetry is a geometry computation with the lattice's handedness as its input, not something to be argued to.** A lone centre of either sign is surrounded by sea DPs polarised *radially* — dipoles pointing toward a − centre and away from a + centre. In a chiral lattice (the 600-cell with the handedness selected at Capotauro, χ = φ⁻³), a radially-inward and a radially-outward dipole shell are not mirror-equivalent: their energy differs by a term odd under the combined flip (centre sign × lattice screw sense), and that term *is* ΔF_dress. Its magnitude is |M| = χ/6 of the shell's stabilisation energy (THEO-CAP-1 / THEO-SD-CHIR-2 give the matrix element); its **sign** is fixed by which screw sense the substrate has — a fact about the universe (Q7.1's framings (b)/(c): random or initial-condition), not derivable from symmetry alone. So "figuring out a way to make one sign dress better" means: compute ΔF_dress for a radially polarised shell on a ± centre in the 600-cell of stated handedness, and read the sign off. The DM lane's contact-polarizability scoping (qstiff) is the nearest instrument; the computation is doable and is the first item of the charter.

**What cannot be done:** choose the sign so that the story works. If the geometry returns S = −qCP for the stated handedness, then either the handedness assignment is the other one (a legitimate initial-condition choice — Q7.1's framing (b) — but it must then be checked against every other chirality-sensitive prediction on file, PRED-O-25 among them), or the dressing-selectivity story is wrong. That check is the falsifier the charter should pre-register.

**Please say which reading you meant; the charter's first computation is the same either way, but its pass/fail reading is not.**
