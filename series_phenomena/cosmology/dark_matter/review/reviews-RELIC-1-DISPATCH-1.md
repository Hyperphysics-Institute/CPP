## Q1 — Charter pre-registration review

**VERDICT: AMEND, THEN FREEZE.**

The overall branch structure is sound: D-strong requires a zero-knob result inside the observational window; Branch T kills only the derivation claim; Branch I parks on a named blocker; and K1/K2 do not improperly convert failure to derive (\Omega_{\rm DM}/\Omega_b) into falsification of candidate (B). That separation is appropriately conservative.

### (a) D-weak window

The proposed interval [0.30,0.67] is mathematically consistent with "within a factor 1.5" of the target (0.4468): 0.4468/1.5=0.2979, 1.5(0.4468)=0.6702. So it is not arbitrary. I would retain it, but rename the label:

> **D-directional**, not D-weak.

"Favorable-directional" should mean only that the registered mechanism naturally lands in the correct order-of-magnitude corridor without tuning. It must not be described later as partial derivation.

### (b) Tunable escape routes

Branch T should be broadened explicitly. At present it catches a continuous unpinned parameter, but an apparent zero-knob match could still be manufactured through:

* selecting among several discrete unpinned mechanisms;
* selecting a favorable conserved charge after seeing the outputs;
* choosing a formation cutoff, freeze-out epoch, or termination rule not independently registered;
* combining several order-one factors whose individual choices are unconstrained;
* conditioning on only the surviving population while omitting failed formation channels.

Add this wording:

> **Branch T also fires for discrete model selection, post-result mechanism selection, unregistered cutoffs or stopping rules, and products of individually unpinned order-one factors.**

That closes the principal escape route.

### (c) Missing S4 gates

The charter currently includes N≥8 cascade dominance, N_eff/BBN, and double-counting. I would add four gates before S1:

1. **Symmetric-component depletion:** show that the symmetric ring/anti-ring or precursor population is sufficiently annihilated or otherwise removed.
2. **Conserved-charge closure:** baryons plus DM plus every residual carrier must reproduce the assumed total shared asymmetry.
3. **Entropy/dilution history:** the predicted number ratio must survive reheating, annihilation, phase transitions, and any late entropy injection.
4. **Perturbation compatibility:** exclude an unacceptable baryon–DM isocurvature mode or a formation history that disrupts the observed matter power spectrum.

Also require uncertainty propagation. D-strong should mean the **predicted distribution including registered uncertainties** overlaps the observational ±2σ window—not merely that a central value falls inside it.

With those amendments, the charter is strong enough to freeze.

## Q2 — Registry condition 2 duty convention

**VERDICT: OPTION (c), ENERGY-WEIGHTED DUTY; NEW PRE-REGISTERED RUN REQUIRED.**

The registry should not retroactively switch its primary from contact scale to bond scale merely because contact produced the adverse result. The contact-scale outcome must remain recorded exactly as the pre-registered test found it: δ_qq^contact ≈ 0.001–0.010, which is decisively inconsistent with a scale-unspecified expectation of 3/7. The bond-shell result, δ_qq^bond ≈ 0.368–0.381, is a useful secondary observation but is still below 3/7.

For future physical interpretation, however, neither raw contact counting nor unweighted shell counting is the best object. The relevant question is how much same-charge apposition contributes to the actual interaction energy, force, or stiffness. A brief, weak interaction near the singular/contact region can matter more than a long dwell at larger separation; conversely, soft-core saturation may suppress that intuition. Only an energy-weighted measurement answers it.

I recommend defining, before running, something like

δ_E = ⟨ Σ_{qq, same} |V_ij^qq| ⟩ / ⟨ Σ_{qq, all} |V_ij^qq| ⟩,

with separate force-weighted and curvature-weighted diagnostics if condition 2 is supposed to bear specifically on stiffness.

Registry wording should become:

> **Condition 2 — same-charge duty mechanism:** the original unqualified 3/7 contact expectation is adverse as measured; bond-shell count duty is near but below 3/7; the physically relevant energy/force-weighted duty remains open pending a pre-registered measurement.

This preserves the adverse primary result and avoids a post-hoc scale substitution.
