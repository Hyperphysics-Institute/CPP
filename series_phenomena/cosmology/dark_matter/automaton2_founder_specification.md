## §1 — Founder commitments (C19–C30, continuing the A1 series C14–C18; each anchored to the founder's session wording)

direction of SSV_net. The "gradient-survey branch" at neutrality is

direction of the greatest gradient is merely a restatement that the
CP moves in the direction of the [SSV]_net summation… The CP always
moves in the direction of the SSV_net." Steepest-gradient language
survives only as an interpretive correlate, never as a second rule.

**C20 (Displacement magnitude law retained).** v/c =
|SSV_net|/SSV_abs remains the computation and the normalization

|SSV_net|/SSV_abs to be the normalization"), with displacement

**C21 (DI-bit content is closed).** DI-bits carry: source type
(strong/charge), polarity (±), and origin address. NOTHING ELSE — in
particular, no velocity or displacement metadata. All knowledge of a
source's motion at a distance is emergent from the time sequence of
its bits and the Sea's configurational response (see C23).

**C22 (Transit clock and relay).** DI-bits travel strictly on
lattice edges by re-radiation to the 12 icosahedral nearest
neighbors. The number of hops executed within one Moment is set by
the local SSV_abs by the same law that sets the PSR — i.e., **the
speed of light is one PSR per Moment**, with the sub-Moment
structure being nothing but the hop sequence. Stress-dependence of
PSR thereby yields stress-dependent local light speed (the SR/
gravity sector's refraction mechanism) natively. The relay is
DIRECTED by the origin address (a relaying GP forwards outward
relative to the bit's origin), which the worker's diagnostic

undirected all-12 relay diffuses and produces no front — measured,
Patch 2800 appendix). The founder's two stated design problems —
uniform shell population, and superposition bookkeeping — are
resolved respectively by the directed relay and by C25.

**C23 (Inertia lives in the Sea; the magnetic sector is emergent).**
There is NO velocity memory on any CP or GP — "there is only the
current state." The current state includes the Sea: the arc
displacement of DP poles circulating around a moving charge stores
the motion ("a storage of inertia in the configuration of the DP
Sea that produces Lenz's law, which is… actually the underlying
phenomenon behind it"). The founder's fore/aft resolution of the
drag question is committed: the leading edge of the arc field

transition lies in the plane orthogonal to the velocity — "at the
trailing edge, attraction decreases with distance, and repulsion
increases, so the process reverses and pushes as much as the
original one retards." Consequence, registered as PREDICTION rather
than input (P-A2-2): constant-velocity motion through the

DM-campaign inertia derivation (DM-ring stiffness / ZBW moderation),

one complete fluctuation–dissipation loop; steady-state detailed
