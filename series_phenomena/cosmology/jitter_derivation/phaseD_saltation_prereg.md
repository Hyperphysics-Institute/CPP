sampled in TIME, is heavy-tailed** — most Moments quiet, rare Moments

## §2 — The statistic (frozen)

For each CP, the time series of experienced force magnitude over the
final third (1002 Moments), at the calibrated spacing (d_s = 4.636,
n = 5, seed 5; 250 CPs). **Two series, BOTH reported (B-5 discipline):**
- **|F|_total** — everything the CP feels, partner arc included.
- **|F|_nonpartner** — the partner's Coulomb term removed; this is
  where encounters live and is the picture's direct target.
Statistics per CP, then the across-CP mean with its SE:
- **γ₂** — excess kurtosis in TIME.
- **BURST** — fraction of Moments with |F| > 5× that CP's median |F|
  (a tail-occupancy measure that does not depend on fourth moments;
  a Gaussian gives ≈ 0.0002).

## §3 — THE POWER GATE (runs FIRST; blocks everything)

**This is the clause Phase B lacked.** Before any engine number is
computed, both statistics are applied to two synthetic series matched
in length, CP count, and variance:
- **NULL**: Gaussian white noise (no saltation, by construction).
- **SALT**: a synthetic saltatory series — quiet baseline with rare
  bursts (2% of Moments at 8× amplitude).
**GATE PASSES** iff each statistic separates SALT from NULL by
**≥ 5 SE**. If a statistic fails the gate it is declared **BLIND** and
may carry no reading in §5 — its engine value is still printed, marked
BLIND, so the record shows what a powerless statistic would have said.
If BOTH fail, D-SALT-1 is VOID and no engine measurement is reported.
