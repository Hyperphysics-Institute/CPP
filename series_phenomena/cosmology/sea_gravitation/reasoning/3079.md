# Reasoning — Patch 3079 (Stage 2). Verbatim capture.

The design intent was to make the environment pay for itself: no
dial survives if the field a member feels is built from the same
<delta^2> the loop is solving for, scaled by an exactly computed
lattice sum and the committed dipole factor. The fixed point then
told a three-act story I consider the session's finest result. At
close spacing the loop runs away — jitter feeds field feeds jitter —
and the switch fraction saturates at one: the founder's plasma, not
as a postulate but as an instability of the equations built from his
own rulings. Above six-to-seven spacings the branch stabilises, and
two of his qualitative claims become measured quantities: fidelity
dominates switching (0.14 falling to 0.07 with dilution), and the
cycle-average shape settles near eta_z ~ 0.2 almost independently of
density — which quietly de-risks phi_3 against whatever the d_s
determination turns out to be. The third act is the strangest: under
a hard 1-GP step quantum the self-consistent Sea freezes at every
spacing scanned, because its own fields are sub-quantum. The
founder's offhand caveat that 1 GP is "probably not correct" is
thereby promoted to a requirement — the substrate needs sub-GP
effective steps or an external seed, and that is his call, not mine.
The temptation ledger for this patch: the runaway boundary at 6-7 GP
is a number with obvious downstream consequence through the 1/d^2
anchoring, and I have deliberately not carried it into the assembly;
it sits in the record as geometry until the founder rules where the
Sea sits. Stage 3 exists because every caveat I listed (1D, mirror
approximation, FCC C6 for a disordered sea, the poach bookkeeping)
is exactly the kind of thing a mean-field loop gets subtly wrong,
and the explicit array is cheap enough to owe ourselves.
