# Reasoning — Patch 3435 (comoving build, error budget)

STATUS: verbatim (captured at patch)

---

The handover fixed the order of operations and the order is the whole
point: freeze the error budget before sizing. I want to be explicit
about why that ordering is not bureaucratic. If you size first — pick
N because it fits in 32 GB, pick the step count because it finishes
over a weekend — then the achieved precision is whatever falls out,
and the reading rule gets written against it. That is a rule tuned to
the instrument's convenience, which is the same defect as tuning a
threshold to the answer. The 3419 threshold-artefact verdict is the
lane's own demonstration that a guard written beforehand will refuse a
reading, and a guard written afterward would not have.

On the estimator. I considered whether to keep the p-based chain
(measure n_eq under expansion, convert via w = −1 + p/3) so that the
comoving result would be directly commensurable with 3415/3419. I
decided against it, and the reason is that commensurability is exactly
what we do not want here. The p-chain carries the n_eq identification,
the entity-threshold choice, and the T ∝ 1/a substitution — three
assumptions, one of which (the threshold) already produced an
artefact verdict, and one of which (the T substitution) is the very
weak link the build exists to test. Reading 1 + w = −∂ln ρ/∂ln V
directly off the substrate shares none of them. The two instruments
should be independent so that agreement means something; F-C3, the
frozen-expansion null mode, is what re-establishes the bridge between
them under controlled conditions rather than by construction.

On the paired design. The unpaired alternative — independent
ensembles at each volume — is simpler to implement and much worse
statistically, because the seed-to-seed offset in ρ_Sea enters every
point independently instead of cancelling in the within-seed slope.
3172's re-attribution of the 4× seed spread at the 11:2.0 cell is the
relevant precedent: seed variation there was benign and geometric, not
a sampling defect, but it was large. A design that lets a large benign
offset into the slope error is throwing away precision it does not
have to.

On what I refused to supply. I could have produced a full sizing
table in this patch — N, box, steps, ensemble — by taking var(f_b)
from 3172 and converting it into an s_within estimate. I want it on
record that I considered this and rejected it, because the conversion
would have required assuming a relation between a partition variance
measured in a fixed box and a density scatter measured under
expansion, and CONV-003 exists precisely because DM-1 v1.0 shipped
with numbers carried across a pivot on exactly that kind of unstated
assumption. It would also have looked like more progress than it was.
The honest deliverable is a budget with one named hole and a pilot
assigned to fill it.

The GRADIENT-1 lesson from last session pushed on this too, in the
other direction: that route's premise was already refuted in the
corpus and cost a session slot because the corpus was not read first.
So I checked the fixed-box estimator chain (3413's p definition,
3419's execution, 3172's variance record, 3401's relaxation time)
before writing a budget that depends on all four. The relaxation time
is the one I am least confident transfers — ~15 Moments is a fixed-box
equilibration figure, and whether the Sea relaxes that fast *while the
box is growing* is a genuinely open question, not a parameter. That is
why it is pilot deliverable 2 rather than a budget input.

On the lever arm. Worth stating because it is the non-obvious part of
the design surface: L enters σ_w linearly while M·S enters as a square
root, so extending the expansion range is roughly quadratically more
efficient than adding runs. My instinct was to reach for more seeds
when the tolerance looked tight; the algebra says reach for lever arm
first. The constraint on L is physical — the correlation length must
stay inside the box at V_final — which is why that is pilot
deliverable 3 rather than something I can set here.

Finally, on the pre-declaration. §5 of the 3434 handover reads as a
standing instruction against them, and I agree with it on the
evidence: two correct in ten, with both of last session's authors
overturned by their own executions. I am not recording what I expect
this instrument to say. I will note only that "confirm-or-refute
instrument for a standing conclusion" is the correct framing and that
the temptation, if it returns something surprising, will be to promote
the magnitude into a reading — which is why §6's REFUTE-PROXY clause
withholds that promotion in advance.
