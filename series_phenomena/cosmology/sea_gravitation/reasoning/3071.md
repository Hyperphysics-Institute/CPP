## Addendum — Patch 3072 (founder correction pass)

The founder's two corrections landed in instructively different
places, and saying so precisely is the value of this addendum. The
superposition correction attacks the geometry, not the census: I had
read 3059's adjacent-GP statement statically and put dipoles at edge
midpoints; his cycle — superimpose at a GP, excurse in exactly
opposite directions, recall — pins the pair centre to the
superposition GP for the whole cycle, because opposite displacements
leave the centroid fixed. But the factor-of-two in SCR-1 never came
from where the centres sit; it came from how many there are, and
transient double occupancy by a pair's own members does not change
N/2. Recomputed, the two pictures differ by 3% and the corrected one
lands φ₁ in 0.48–0.50: the correction tightened the answer around
one-half rather than moving it. The fidelity correction is cleaner
still: a static 1/r^4 ensemble sum sees only the marginal position
distribution, so the reshuffle RATE is invisible to SCR-1 — which is
fortunate, because the founder explicitly registers the swap
fraction as an unknown. It goes to SCR-3 as a flagged input, with my
testable preliminary claim on the record: a completed cycle's <d^2>
should not care whether the close approach ends in return or swap,
so the unknown may drop out of eta_z; if it survives, it is named,
not guessed. The pleasing consonance: his insistence on
superposition dwell is exactly why eta_z < 1 — the picture he
corrected me toward is the one the frozen parameterization already
assumed.
