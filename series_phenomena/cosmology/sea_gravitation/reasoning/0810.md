# Reasoning — Patch 0810 (DM-side: does the O(δ³) NESS current accumulate into the skew?)

STATUS: verbatim, at-patch (Opus, Session 156, 8 June 2026).

This is the escape I deliberately left unclaimed in 0809 — that the O(δ³) object is a steady current, and a current may not accumulate into the scalar <d d'^2>. Thomas asked me to attack it from the DM side, so I tried to make the distinction precise rather than just assert it.

The clean statement is that current and skew are different kinds of object. <F>_bulk = -4k^2 <d d'^2> sources from an equal-time SKEW (a third moment of the stationary measure pi). A NESS current is a FLOW (broken detailed balance, net circulation). I demonstrated on a controlled 2-D Fokker-Planck system that these genuinely decouple: drift = -gradV + b with V symmetric and b = omega(-y,x) solenoidal gives a real NESS — curl(drift) = 2 omega != 0, steady current |J| != 0, div J ~ 0 — yet because b.gradV = 0 the stationary pi ~ exp(-V) is UNCHANGED, so every odd/third moment is zero to machine precision. A circulating current with a perfectly symmetric equal-time distribution. So "detailed balance broken at O(delta^3)" does not by itself force the skew that would source a bulk Lambda residual.

That sharpens condition (b) to a single scalar: does the Mechanism-A stationary pi acquire an O(delta^3) THIRD MOMENT, or only the O(delta^3) current (plus the O(delta^1) tilt)? I checked the chirality lane's own characterization (read-only): they report a tilt at O(delta^1) — a first moment, a mean shift, gradient-controlled away if uniform — and a steady current at O(delta^3). They do NOT report an O(delta^3) skew of pi. On their characterization the natural reading is the current-without-skew (pi-solenoidal) case, which leaves Lambda clean. So clean horizon-only Lambda is now the FAVORED branch.

I was careful to keep this de-risking honest and not promote it to closure, because this is exactly the seam the handover warned about. The 2-D demo shows a current CAN coexist with symmetric pi; it does not prove the CPP current IS pi-solenoidal. A general NESS drift can skew pi. So I named the decisive remaining check — the O(delta^3) third moment of the lifted measure — as the sharp sub-target, and stated plainly that closure still awaits the (H-NESS) lift.

The part I am most pleased with as a contribution rather than a hope: this decomposes the shared gate usefully for both lanes. The lift produces the field/eta measure; DM-2 needs only its third moment (skew), the chirality sign needs the current. If the lift's O(delta^3) content is purely current, BOTH land cleanly and they are not in tension — they read complementary parts of one lifted measure. That is a concrete way the DM side feeds the chirality work, which is the right kind of cross-sector result to leave on the record.
