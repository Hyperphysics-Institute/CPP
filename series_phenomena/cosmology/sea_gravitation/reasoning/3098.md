the kinematics produced what looked like a disaster: CP permanence
plus dilution-as-expansion forces d ∝ a, and naively running the
dilution from a Planck-density start through the full thermal history

considered. The resolution was hiding in the corpus's own unit
structure: the EU-1 exit lands at one CP per FINE GP, and the fine

units kept honest and the post-exit expansion factor divided by
N_fine, and the mapping lands d_s(today) at tens of l_P for
Planck-adjacent exit — the campaign's working decade falls out of
{exit at n̄ = 1} + {10³⁰ nesting} + {standard thermal expansion} with
nothing else. I checked this twice because consonances that good are
where self-deception lives; the arithmetic is three lines and it is
in the script. It is recorded as a consonance and not an adoption,
because the missing input — the exit temperature — is EU-1's named
reheating debt, and it is now load-bearing here, which is worth
saying out loud: FQ-7.3 hands it to the founder.

The second finding forced itself. The relic reading forks on whether
inter-unit dilution continues after the weave binds, and the two
branches are not observationally equivalent: ρ ∝ 1/(a²R_h²) versus
1/R_h² differ by exactly a⁻², which displaces the equation of state
by exactly +2/3, on any background, with no free anything. The frozen

self-consistent ODE rather than the fixed-background shortcut after
the shortcut failed its own regression gate (w came out −0.91, not
−1.02; the discrepancy taught me that 0723's number lives in the

±0.003). The comoving branch is that curve plus 2/3: w_now ≈ −0.36,
at the zero-acceleration class. This is the same adjudication move
that killed the Hubble radius at D3 — shape, not magnitude — so I
used it and said so, and the anti-extraction line held: no ρ_Λ
appears anywhere in the note.

What survives is pleasingly tight. The relic history needs a freeze;
the corpus has exactly one natural freeze scale — the fidelity
boundary, which is the founder's own transition-zone language in the
FQ-5.2 verbatim and the quantity the Stage-2/3 machinery already
brackets. Under that reading the old option (a) is not the rival of
the relic reading but its endpoint: the Sea dilutes TO the boundary
and binds there, and d_s becomes a zero-parameter output. I did not
adopt it. Two things stopped me. First, R-CC-DIST-CONT's present
tense reads most naturally as the branch the dynamics just excluded —
that is a consonance tension with a ruled statement, and the T-flag
protocol exists precisely so that I raise it and the founder rules,
rather than my choosing the reading that flatters the derivation.
Second, a frozen Sea on a picture where dilution IS expansion leaves
"what expands afterward" genuinely open — the transient exchange
population is a candidate carrier, but that is the founder's physics
to rule, not mine to assume. Hence FQ-7, with no CC consequence
attached to any branch, per the 3089 discipline.
