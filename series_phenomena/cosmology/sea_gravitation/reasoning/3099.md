# Reasoning fragment — Patch 3099 (verbatim, per the reasoning-capture rider)

The founder answered FQ-7.1 with a mechanism rather than a selection,
which is the best kind of answer: expansion-opened volume shifts the
exchange equilibrium toward new pair formation, and the new species
fill the space. My first reaction was the physicist's reflex — a gas
pressure story cannot accelerate a Friedmann universe; positive
pressure decelerates, and there is no wall for the balloon to push
on. I checked whether that reflex was importable before using it, per
the G-2 discipline, and found it is not an import at all: the
1161-closed field equation gives CPP its own D1 acceleration
criterion, ρ + 3p, derived in-corpus, radiation decelerating twice as
hard as dust. So the sign check has teeth here, natively.

Then I looked at what the mechanism actually does rather than the
analogy it wears, and the sign flips. A medium that answers opened
volume by creating structure at constant density has dE = ρdV, and
dE = −pdV makes that p = −ρ: tension, w = −1, exactly the frozen
branch's phenomenology. The founder's balloon and the formal
thermodynamics are describing the same observable — the medium does
not thin — from opposite ends; the "outward push" language is what
not-thinning feels like classically. I wrote both readings into the
record with the failure mode stated plainly, because a future
ruling-grade version that keeps the kinetic-push reading would
contradict D1 and someone must be able to see that seam.

The check that genuinely bites is inventory. CP permanence makes
"fill growing volume at constant density forever" arithmetically
impossible — entity count cannot grow ∝ a³ against a fixed stock. The
resolution the corpus itself suggests is a partition: the bound weave
holds its local spacing (the attractor — and the attractor reading is
a real upgrade on the rigid freeze of 3098, because pairing kinetics
RESTORE the spacing instead of merely resisting), while the transient
gas is the component that honestly dilutes. Whether that gives w ≈ −1
turns on a fact we already own: the 1/r⁴ arrival sum is nearest-
neighbour-dominated, so the imprint tracks the pinned local spacing,
not the falling mean. That is a derivable proposition with a clean
falsifier — if the sum tracks the mean, the mechanism inherits the
excluded comoving w(z) and dies on arithmetic already committed — so
I minted D-VOLFILL-LEDGER rather than asserting it. The pleasing
part, which I flagged but did not lean on: the mechanism dissolves
T-CC-CONT-1 by making the founder's present tense literally true —
the distance increase continues to operate, as the driver of the
filling — while the secular thinning the dynamics excluded never
occurs. And the exchange population picks up its fourth job under the
founder's own triangulation standard. None of that was computed
toward the band; nothing here touches ρ_Λ.
