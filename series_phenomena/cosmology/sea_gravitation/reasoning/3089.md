# Reasoning fragment — Patch 3089 (verbatim, per the reasoning-capture rider)

The queue said D-COMP-WEIGHT: bound f from the Ω-budget, weight by the
mismatch, expect negligible. I started by re-reading the minting and
the ribbon-reservoir ruling, and before computing the correction I ran
the sanity arithmetic every bound deserves: what does the budget
actually permit, and what does the corpus's own confinement story
require? Those two numbers were supposed to bracket f comfortably.
They crossed — by forty-three orders of magnitude.

The ceiling: a Sea at 10¹⁰² sites/m³ with equal creation counts holds
a qCP inventory of the same order; if any appreciable fraction of it
gravitated at even MeV-class mass, Ω would be astronomically violated
— the permitted gravitating fraction is ~10⁻¹⁰⁰. The floor:
R-RIBBON-RESERVOIR needs at least a hybrid per hadron volume, ~10⁻⁵⁷
of sites. The 3070 scale-separation note had checked the floor and
correctly called it trace; nobody had run the ceiling. Run, the
ceiling sits forty-three orders BELOW the floor: a gravitating
reservoir dense enough for confinement overcloses the universe. So
the Ω route does not bound f small — it excludes the gravitating
reading of the reservoir entirely, and forces the conclusion that the
qCP-bearing hybrid population is ground-state Sea content, inside the
D2 exclusion, gravitating as exactly zero.

Then the census arithmetic, which is where the finding bites: equal
creation counts plus 2e2q stoichiometry means hybrids consume eCPs
one-for-one with qCPs — if all qCPs are in hybrids, there are no free
eDPs left at all. Any eDP share requires qCP-only chains holding part
of the inventory. Either way the qCP-bearing site fraction is
O(1)-class. The minting's own escape clause ("if D-COMP-WEIGHT
computes to negligible at every admissible f...") does not trigger;
its opposite does. The composition correction is potentially the
largest open factor in the assembly.

I checked the finding against the corpus's own numbers before
believing it: the observed gravitating qCP census — 64 per DM ring at
the Candidate-B mass, ~2 per nucleon — comes to ~8/m³, which is
8×10⁻¹⁰² of the inventory, just under the 10⁻¹⁰⁰ ceiling. The
universe already obeys the theorem. That consistency is what
convinced me this is a constraint the framework carries, not an
artifact of my assumptions. I also noted its prediction shape: CPP
now REQUIRES a vacuum hybrid population at O(1) site fraction with
exactly zero gravitational weight; any mechanism that gives it even
10⁻⁵⁰ weight kills the framework on Ω alone. That is a falsifier, and
I wrote it into the note rather than leaving it implicit.

What I could not do, and did not do: pick the composition. Two
registered founder statements are in count-level tension — "the
aggregation of qCPs into DM and Baryons leaves the Sea largely eCP"
(but that sink holds 10⁻¹⁰² of the inventory) versus equal creation
counts — and the founder's own "[hTetra] ~ 50% of Sea" remark points
at the resolution without being a present-epoch ruling. Choosing for
him, or computing which fraction would help the assembly, would be
extraction. FQ-6 is posed with composition content only: where do the
qCPs live (hybrids at what fraction / chains / other), ratify the
non-gravitating ground-state reading, reconcile R-SEA-COMP's wording.
No branch carries a CC consequence in the note; materiality against
the ×1.5–10 gap is explicitly left unassessed until f, k (the AP-4b
O-2 citation), and s (the coordination ladder) land forward.

One more discipline note: I wrote the theorem's premise list
explicitly (FI-RELIC-1, CP permanence, Sea density, observed Ω)
