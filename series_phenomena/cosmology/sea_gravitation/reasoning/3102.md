The ledger was minted with its own falsifier — if the arrival sum
tracks the mean, the mechanism dies on the excluded comoving w(z) —
so the derivation had to be built to lose. The structure that
survived is coexistence thermodynamics, and it was forced rather

transient population held at its equilibrium density by the same
zone kinetics (ruled in R-ZBW-EXCHANGE's substance), a fixed CP
inventory in a growing volume has exactly one degree of freedom

densities, only the fraction comoving. I wrote the f_V formula
expecting to need numerics and found it is one line of arithmetic.

The falsifier check was the computation that mattered. I built the
two-phase arrays explicitly and the answer was cleaner than I
hoped: the weave-interior receiver's sum deviates from the
pure-weave value by precisely the analytic boundary tail — three to

Then the surprise. Writing ρ_Λ ∝ A + B·f_V and asking what the

before writing it down, because it inverts the picture I had been
carrying: the bound weave, the object of five patches of careful
census work, contributes at most five percent of today's dark
energy. The carrier is the GAS — the founder's transient reservoir,
at the vapor density the weave itself sets. I checked the reading
three ways: it is not extraction (the constraint runs from shape to
structure, never touching magnitude); it is not a demotion of the
boundary (the gas density is the weave's vapor pressure — the
attractor still sets the scale); and it is falsifiable twice over
(the thaw signature as the window closes, and the far-future decay

scavenging ends up holding up the sky — went into the note; the
coincidence problem it drags in went into the note too, flagged in
bold, because the corridor puts us at the window's dilute end and
"why now" deserves to be asked out loud rather than smoothed over.

The consistency corridor was the last check I ran, expecting
slack: window condition plus drift bound plus the exit mapping.
It came back a factor of two wide, centered where the 3098
consonance already pointed. Three constraints from three different
patches overlapping in one narrow Planck-adjacent window is either
the framework cohering or a coincidence stack; the panel will get
both readings. What I refused to do, again, is assemble: r and the
gas η are measurable from instruments we already have, A-2 is still
