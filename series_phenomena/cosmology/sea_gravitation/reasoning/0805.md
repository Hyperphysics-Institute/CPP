The Step-0 audit produced a favorable-looking finding (D2's c08 dependency is narrower than the trail states), and the falsification-first discipline says to push that straight into a kill-test rather than bank it as progress. Step 1 is that test: can ground-state exclusion be gotten from c08's *established* results without the strong-field nonlinear closure (OP1)?

Two routes give the mean uniform Sea for free, and I checked both symbolically rather than asserting them. Source route: 𝓕 = [2ku²/(1+ku)²]·box(ln(1+ku)); for uniform u the box(ln) factor is the d'Alembertian of a constant = 0, so 𝓕=0 exactly, and the linear box(u) term is likewise 0. Geometry route: constant g_tt ⇒ all Christoffels 0 ⇒ Riemann 0 ⇒ G=0. Both are weak-field/trivial-field statements; OP1 is a strong-field statement, so neither touches it. That settles the *mean*.

The real content is the fluctuation channel, because that is where the CC catastrophe actually lives (in QFT the zero-point ⟨T⟩ gravitates). My first draft reasoning called 𝓕 "quadratic-leading"; the symbolic expansion corrected me — it is CUBIC-leading: prefactor O(u²) times box(ln(1+ku)) O(u) gives 2k²u²u'' + O(u⁴). F(0)=F'(0)=F''(0)=0. This is a STRONGER suppression than I expected and it changes the analysis: for a zero-mean fluctuation, the linear term averages to zero exactly, and the cubic term averages to zero BY PARITY for any statistically symmetric fluctuation (the integrand 2k²δ²δ'' is odd-leading; ⟨for δ=sin x⟩ = 0). A skewed δ (sin x + ½cos 2x) gives ⟨𝓕⟩ = 3k²/2 ≠ 0. So the surviving source is exactly the parity-breaking part.

This is the verdict: CONDITIONAL PASS. D2 is separable from OP1; what is actually load-bearing is a much sharper, local condition I named the net-broadcast lemma — (a) weak-field k·Δ|SSV| ≪ 1 at the ZBW scale, and (b) statistical symmetry of the ZBW zero-point. The reframe is the value: "rests on c08's central conjecture" becomes "rests on the symmetry and weak-field character of the ZBW zero-point."

I deliberately did NOT let this become a PASS. Condition (a) is a genuine kill-risk and I flagged it as the load-bearing Step-2 check: the ZBW is Planck/Compton-scale, and if k·Δ|SSV| ~ O(1) there, the weak-field truncation fails and ⟨𝓕⟩ needs the full nonlinear term — which IS OP1. In that case separability fails and the gate is back on c08's hardest problem. That is the honest wall-scenario, and it is cheap to check, which is the whole point of doing Step 1 before any writeup.
