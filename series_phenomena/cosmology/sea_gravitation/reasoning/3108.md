# Reasoning fragment — Patch 3108 (verbatim, per the reasoning-capture rider)

The founder asked the question every falsified framework's author
asks, and the discipline of the answer mattered more than its
content. Three things had to be true at once: his question deserved
a real mechanism survey, not a stonewall; the survey could not
compute a single candidate toward the band, because post-fire every
such computation is contaminated by knowing the target; and the one
arithmetic fact that cuts against his intuition had to be said
plainly — the constant-velocity dynamics he pointed at are
approximately what the fired assembly already measured, since the
saturated instrument's members ride the cap. The cap is inside the
number that fired. The unmeasured branch is his own morning ruling.

That inversion is the heart of the patch. The strongest bridge
candidate is not new physics and not my regularization — it is
R-SSV-NOEXCEPT actually calculated: what a strongly-bound pair does
when displacement is the freshly computed SSV_net with no cap has
never been derived, it is the least-constrained input in the
assembly, and un-capping points toward larger excursions. Setting
that up exposed the question underneath, which I posed as FQ-10: is
the bare CP memoryless — jump to the computed address, carry
nothing — or does velocity persist at the CP level? The corpus leans
memoryless (SF-6 makes inertia emergent, which only makes sense if
the bare rule carries none), the instruments have modeled
persistence throughout, and the two pictures give qualitatively
different stiff-pair dynamics. A memoryless CP cannot run away.
Every integration catastrophe of the last three patches may have
been an artifact of simulating inertia the automaton does not have.

The successor protocol went in before anyone asks for it: verdict
permanent, fresh prereg, blind re-measurement, same frozen band,
F-CLI-2 in the same words, panel adjudicates the opening. I also
kept one sentence for the record that felt necessary: the method did
not fail. A falsifier that fires is the method succeeding. What
failed the band is one assembly of one mechanism at one state of
knowledge, and the door back is made of rulings and derivations,
which is the only material doors in this programme are allowed to be
made of.
