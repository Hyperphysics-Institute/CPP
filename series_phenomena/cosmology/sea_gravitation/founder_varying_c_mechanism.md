## §1 — The founder's mechanism, recorded (so it is not lost again)

**Expansion kinematics.** The universe's expansion is the Big Bang's
radial kinetics — CPs moving outward via SSV_net each Moment at
constant radial velocity. There is NO metric expansion and no absolute
acceleration. **No new Grid Points appear in the bulk of space**; new
GPs may be built each Moment at the edge of the universe as it expands
into new volume (alternatively the GP lattice was pre-made to its
ultimate extent — the founder finds this difficult to fathom and does
not commit). Inflation is only the START of dispersal; the dispersal
relevant to DE is **evaporation**, which proceeds faster the further
it has already gone.

**The composition clock.** DP entities have a hierarchy of
evaporability set by their symmetry: **DM rings most resistant**
(symmetric); **glueballs intermediate** (approximately spherical but
discretely stacked, with angularity susceptible to asymmetric forces
and collision displacement); **the light entities (2DPs, 4DPs, chains,
ribbons, rods, amorphous aggregates) most evaporable** (open,
angular). Glueballs are a common species formed in the hot dense early
Sea by strong-force attraction (and are candidate nucleation seeds for
early black holes); qDPs are what DP entities congeal around — eDPs
lack the mutual attraction to hold structure against Sea thermal
collisions, and an eDP coat surrounds entities, lowering formation
rates. Evaporation proceeds by qDP escape; escaped free qDPs rebond.

**The late-time speedup.** In the colder expanded universe glueballs
no longer split and only merge or evaporate; with more space they
collide and merge less, so their concentration falls monotonically —
they convert one-way into lighter entities. As all entities shrink,
**surface-to-volume ratio rises, raising evaporation rates**; and with
entities farther apart, escaped DPs are less likely to be reabsorbed
by their parent (proximity recapture falls), so **retention of new
light entities rises**. Light-entity and free-DP concentration stays
roughly constant by evaporation/condensation balance — this constant
filling is the source of the Hubble-law effect — while the heavy
species (glueballs; DM rings by dilution) decline in concentration and
the **mean CP count per entity falls everywhere**.

**The optical consequence.** SSV_abs of a volume tracks its CP content
per volume. Falling glueball concentration plus falling mean entity
mass ⇒ **SSV_abs decreases from early epochs to now** ⇒ macroscopically
the μ and ε of space decrease ⇒ **PSR displacement per Moment — the
speed of light — increases over cosmic time**. Light from the early
universe traveled slowly through a dense, heavy-entity Sea; recent
light travels faster. **The apparent acceleration of expansion is
light traveling faster as the universe ages, not the universe
expanding faster.** DE is an effect, an appearance — no absolute
velocity increase exists.

**Founder's own cap, recorded:** all of the above are tentative
mechanisms underneath the Hubble expansion and DE effects; it may
require calculation of the evaporation rates of glueballs, DM rings,
and each entity type, and of whether the resulting SSV_abs decline is
sufficient to produce the DE-sized effect.

glueballs and DM rings are a special type of DP entity because **no

for glueballs, by conversion (evaporation into lighter species).
By contrast the light entities (2DPs, 4DPs, chains, ribbons, rods,

  mechanism inherits DM-sector consistency for free.

  falls) or merge (mean CP count rises but absolute number falls) —

- The founder's second statement adds that the other DP entity types

**Consequence for the c(t) form:** with the heavy class defined by
