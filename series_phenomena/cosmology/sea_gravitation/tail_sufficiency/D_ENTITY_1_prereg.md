# PREREGISTRATION — **D-ENTITY-1: MEASURE ENTITY CONCENTRATION, NOT PAIR PARTITION** — the founder's n_eq, obtained as a NEW OBSERVABLE on the CERTIFIED dynamics rather than from a new model with assumed rate laws

**Patch 3416, 23 August 2026. Frozen before any cluster datum exists.**

## §1 — Why not a rate model

The obvious build is a reaction-kinetics model: entities, free DPs,
free CPs, with evaporation/aggregation rates. **The worker declines
that**, because its n_eq(T) would be fixed by binding energies the
worker chose — **the answer would be whatever was put in**, and the
corpus has spent this session eliminating exactly that kind of result.

**Instead: entities are DETECTED in the existing certified dynamics.**
No new physics, no new rate law, no new parameter in the equations of
motion — a new **observable** computed on runs the instrument already
produces. Aggregation and evaporation are then whatever the Coulomb
dynamics actually do.

## §2 — The observable

At sampled Moments in the final third: build a graph on DP centres
with an edge wherever centre-centre separation < **r_clus**; take
connected components. Record **N_clus** (number of components with
≥ 2 DPs, i.e. genuine aggregates), **⟨k⟩** (mean component size in
DPs), and the singleton count.

**n_eq ≡ N_clus per unit volume** — the founder's entity
concentration, at last measured as a COUNT rather than a partition.
**p ≡ d ln N_clus / d ln T = 2 · d ln N_clus / d ln σ.**

## §3 — The arbitrary-threshold guard (frozen)

r_clus is a free choice and could manufacture any answer, so:
**r_clus ∈ {1.2, 1.5, 2.0} × d_s is swept, and the frozen reading
requires the SIGN of p to be identical at all three.** Magnitude may
vary; **sign-dependence on r_clus ⇒ THRESHOLD-ARTEFACT and no
reading is issued.**

## §4 — FROZEN READINGS

**w = −1 + p/3**, with p from the all-points log-log fit at each
r_clus.
- **QUINTESSENCE-SIDE** iff p > 0 at all three thresholds ⇒ w > −1,
  the DESI-preferred direction — **and the founder's stated sign
  vindicated at entity level despite its failure at pair level.**
- **PHANTOM-SIDE** iff p < 0 at all three ⇒ w < −1, the same side the
  pair proxy gave and the retired overlay occupied.
- **THRESHOLD-ARTEFACT** iff the sign differs across thresholds ⇒ no
  reading.
Reported always: N_clus, ⟨k⟩ and singleton counts at every (σ, r_clus).

Heating breaks large aggregates into many small ones: **entity SIZE
falls while entity NUMBER rises** — the opposite behaviour to the
bound FRACTION, which simply falls (3415). **If that is what happens,

pair level, and the two results are consistent rather than
contradictory.** **Worker's pre-declared expectation:
QUINTESSENCE-SIDE.** This IS blind — no cluster observable has ever
been computed in this corpus, and no partial data exist. Eight
pre-declarations, two correct.

## §6 — Limits, stated before the result

The box is fixed-N and fixed-V, so N_clus is bounded above by the DP
count: **this measures how a FIXED CP inventory redistributes among
aggregates at temperature T — which is precisely the founder's
reservoir picture in miniature, but NOT an expanding volume.** The
transfer from "redistribution at fixed V" to "concentration held
constant as V grows" remains untested. **No cosmological claim may be
issued from this patch alone**; what it can do is decide the SIGN of
the founder's p, which is what fixes which side of −1 the mechanism
predicts.
