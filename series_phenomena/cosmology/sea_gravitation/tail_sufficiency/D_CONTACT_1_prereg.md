# PREREGISTRATION — **D-CONTACT-1: IS FREE-DP FORMATION CONTACT-DRIVEN?** The founder's mechanism — intermittent CP-CP contact between DIFFERENT DP entities liberating members into the free population — has never been an observable in this corpus, and this test makes it one

**Patch 3197, 22 August 2026. Container. Frozen before computation.**

## §1 — The gap, stated plainly

Every instrument here defines "free" by a SEPARATION THRESHOLD
(W > d_s/2). **No instrument has ever counted CONTACT EVENTS between
CPs belonging to different DPs.** The founder's mechanism — ZBW-driven
CPs of one DP making intermittent contact with those of another, and
that RATE being the controlling quantity — is therefore untested, not
disfavoured. **Also acknowledged: the ZBW cycle itself is unresolved
at Moment cadence, so individual contacts are invisible; what follows
measures the AGGREGATE contact rate, which is the mechanism's
observable consequence, not the mechanism's microstructure.**

## §2 — The observable (new to the corpus)

**R_c** — inter-DP contact rate: per Moment, the count of CP pairs
belonging to DIFFERENT DPs whose separation falls below the contact
radius r_c = 1.0 (the engine's own softening radius, the only
intrinsic short-distance scale in the dynamics), normalized per CP.
Measured over the final third, alongside the existing f_b.
**CV** — coefficient of variation of the per-Moment contact counts:
CV ≈ 1 is Poisson (independent contacts), CV > 1.5 is clustered
(bursty), CV < 0.7 is regular.

## §3 — Design (the geometry confound controlled)

R_c and f_b both vary with d_s for trivial threshold reasons, so
spacing is HELD FIXED at the calibrated d_s = 4.636, n = 5, seeds
{5, 11}. The three D-TAIL-1 drives — **G, S05, S2, matched in
variance** — are used as the lever, because they are already KNOWN to
move f_b (7.8 SE) without changing geometry at all. **The question is
whether R_c moves WITH f_b under that lever.**

## §4 — FROZEN READINGS

Let Δf_b and ΔR_c be each arm's shift from G.
- **CONTACT-DRIVEN** iff every arm's ΔR_c has the sign predicted by
  the mechanism (more contacts ⇒ fewer bound pairs ⇒ ΔR_c and Δf_b
  OPPOSITE in sign) **and** the largest ΔR_c exceeds 3 SE.
- **NOT-CONTACT-DRIVEN** iff the ΔR_c values are within 3 SE of zero
  while Δf_b is significant ⇒ f_b moves without contacts moving, so
  the threshold population is not being fed by contact events.
- **CONTACT-DECOUPLED** iff ΔR_c is significant but its sign does NOT
  track Δf_b ⇒ contacts respond to the drive, but free-DP formation
  is not what they produce.
- **UNRESOLVED** otherwise.
Reported in every branch: R_c's absolute value and CV per arm — the
first measurement of either in this programme.

## §5 — Hazard direction (declared)

**CONTACT-DRIVEN supports the founder's mechanism and would make R_c
a first-class observable the corpus currently lacks.**
NOT-CONTACT-DRIVEN says the threshold definition of "free" is not
capturing a bonding process at all, which would be a real limitation
of every f_b-based result including this week's calibration work.

recorded before the numbers: heavy-tailed drives were measured to

lowering f_b — fewer exchange events but more free pairs — which
already hints that the free population and the event population move
independently. Six pre-declarations, one correct.
