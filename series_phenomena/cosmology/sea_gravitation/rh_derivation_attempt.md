## §1 — The dilemma in the A4 route

1162 proposes: the vacuum residual's coherence is Nexus-enforced (A4),
so the residual sits at the boundary where global coherence is
severed — the future event horizon.

**The load-bearing step is "a comoving region beyond `R_h` can never
again be Nexus-coordinated."** That is a statement about causal
reachability. But A4 is defined as *"a global consistency constraint
enforcing lattice-wide coherence at each Absolute Moment
(instantaneous, non-local; the Bell-violation mechanism)."*

**The dilemma:**
- **If A4 is genuinely instantaneous and non-local**, it coordinates
  the whole lattice at each Moment regardless of light cones. Then
  **no causal horizon limits it**, and the natural cutoff is the
  lattice itself — not `R_h`, not any horizon.
- **If A4's coordination requires eventual causal contact**, then it
  is not a-causal. **That is the very property A4 exists to provide**
  (Bell violation), and weakening it here would ripple into QM/SD,
  where A4 is currently used.

**The corpus already exposed this without naming it as a dilemma:**
1164 left *"coordinable = ever-reachable"* standing as **a physical
principle**, and 3058 still calls it *"open as a possible
sharpening."* **That principle is exactly the extra premise doing the
work, and it is asserted rather than derived.** Until it is
established, the A4 route cannot close 1162's condition — and if it
IS established, its cost must be paid in the QM/SD sectors.

## §2 — A different route, using only A3′

**Claim (candidate):** the IR cutoff is set not by what can be
*coordinated* but by what can be *cancelled*.

1. Bulk vacuum cancellation in this corpus is **local shell-sum
   monopole annihilation** (1107–1108) — a mutual, two-way process:
   a mode cancels when its opposing parts actually annihilate.
2. Annihilation requires the parts to exchange DI-bits — **retarded,
   light-cone causal (A3′)**. Nothing a-causal is invoked.
3. A comoving mode of wavelength λ therefore cancels only if its
   parts retain **future mutual causal contact**.
4. Regions separated by more than the **future event horizon `R_h`**
   never again exchange signals — by definition of `R_h`.
5. **Therefore modes with λ ≳ `R_h` can NEVER complete cancellation,
   and the surviving uncancelled residual is set by `R_h`.**

**And this supersedes Step C exactly as 1162 demands.** Step C used
`R_H = c/H`, the scale over which the Sea equilibrates *in a Hubble
time* — a **RATE** scale, answering "how fast does cancellation
proceed now?" The residual is fixed instead by a **COMPLETION** scale,
answering "which modes can ever finish cancelling?" **Step C's error
was category, not arithmetic: it used the rate scale where the
completion scale governs.** That is a specific, checkable diagnosis of
why CPP's own substrate argument pointed at the wrong horizon.
