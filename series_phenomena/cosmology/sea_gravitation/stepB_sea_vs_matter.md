## The question (OPEN-SR-5 requirement iii)

What *principled CPP criterion* makes the uniform Sea contribute only a suppressed Λ while uniform matter/radiation drives normal expansion? A naive reading needs three separate assumptions (Sea suppressed; matter gravitates; the two distinguished). The goal is one mechanism.

## The criterion — gravity couples to dSSV-excess above the local Sea ground state

c05 is explicit: gravity is sourced by **∇(ΔSSV)** — the gradient of net SSV relative to the local background, **not** the absolute SSV value. Read the Sea ground state as the reference level (ΔSSV ≡ 0 for the unperturbed uniform Sea). Then the entire distinction follows from this **one** mechanism, applied consistently:

- **Uniform Sea (vacuum ground state):** It defines ΔSSV = 0. However large its *absolute* (Planck-scale) energy density ρ_P ~ E_P/l_P³ ~ 10¹¹³ J/m³, it is the reference, so it has **zero excess and sources zero gravity** (CHECK 1). The naive CC catastrophe — ρ_vac ~ ρ_P, ~120 orders too big — **does not arise**, because CPP gravity does not couple to absolute energy density at all. This is the *same* fact the DM picture already needs ("uniform Sea locally inert"), now applied cosmologically.
- **Matter / radiation:** localized **excesses** above the ground state — eDP/ZBW mass cages; photon DP-chains carrying energy density. They have ΔSSV > 0, source gravity (CHECK 2), and drive the Friedmann expansion via the Step-A shell-theorem aggregation. A *smooth* uniform matter density is still a uniform density of discrete excess concentrations: M_enc(<r) = (4π/3)r³ρ_excess > 0 (CHECK 3) — so uniform matter gravitates cosmologically while the ground state does not (the Step-A consistency requirement is met).
- **Sea inhomogeneities (swirls):** also ΔSSV gradients → unsuppressed local-gradient gravity = **dark matter** (requirement ii; the DM arc's CONJ-COSMO-1).
- **The cosmological constant:** the **only** gravitating part of the Sea is its tiny **residual** departure from the perfect uniform reference. So Λ is **not** the Sea's full energy density (the QFT naive value) but this residual — which is *why* Λ is enormously suppressed below ρ_P.

## Why this is principled, not three assumptions

It is the single statement "gravity is sourced by ΔSSV-excess above the Sea ground state" (c05's gradient-sourcing) applied to four cases. Matter, dark matter (Sea swirls), and Λ (Sea residual) all gravitate by the **same** mechanism, distinguished only by *what the gradient is*: localized mass concentrations (matter), Sea inhomogeneities (DM), large-scale residual non-uniformity of the ground state (Λ). The Sea's bulk uniform energy gravitates zero. This is a genuine restructuring of the CC problem: from "why is Λ 120 orders below ρ_P?" to "what is the small residual non-uniformity of the Sea, and why is it (l_P/R_H)²-scaled?" — which is Step C.

A natural Step-C hook (not claimed here): the Sea is a mixed medium (eDP < qDP < hTetra SSV hierarchy; bonding–debonding viscous equilibrium — founders_vision §6c). Its ground-state SSV level is set by the equilibrium composition, which changes slowly as the universe expands and the Sea dilutes — plausibly the origin of a horizon-tracking, dynamical residual (the (l_P/R_H)² "why now" behavior). Step C must derive this or replace it.

## The load-bearing forward consistency check (the real risk)

This criterion is a **departure from naive GR**, where *all* energy (including vacuum energy) gravitates. CPP says gravity couples to gradients/excess, not absolute energy — and c07/c08 derived GR only in *gradient* (matter) situations (Schwarzschild around a mass, light bending); the uniform-energy sector was explicitly listed as future work. So the claim "the Sea ground state is gravitationally inert" is a **prediction** of CPP's gradient-sourcing that must be checked for internal consistency with the full GR derivation:

> **Forward check (gates Step D):** does the GR limit of CPP correctly *exclude* the ground-state vacuum energy from the gravitating part of T_μν, while keeping the equivalence principle (all *excess* energy gravitates equally) intact? If a consistency requirement from c07/c08 forces the ground state to gravitate at full density, the picture breaks (falsifier A1/B1).
