# **WHAT IS MISSING IS NOT A RATE — IT IS AN IMBALANCE PRINCIPLE.** The measured gross contact rate exceeds the space-filling rate the universe requires by **10^59.3**, so evaporation, collision-binding, and free-DP fusion are all ~59 orders of magnitude *more than sufficient*; what the framework lacks is any reason why formation and dissolution should cancel to fifty-nine decimal places and leave a residue of **exactly 3H** — **which is the cosmological-constant problem restated in rate language, not dissolved by it**

## §1 — The confrontation

| quantity | value |
|---|---|
| DP number density at the calibrated spacing | n = 1.004e−02 per l_P³ |
| H₀ in inverse Moments (Moment = t_P assumed) | 1.186e−61 |
| **REQUIRED net formation** to hold density constant, 3H | **3.558e−61 new DP per DP per Moment** |
| **MEASURED gross inter-DP contacts** (3198) | **0.0663 per DP per Moment** |
| **ratio** | **1.86e+59** |

**The mechanism is not short of throughput. It is over-supplied by
fifty-nine orders of magnitude.** Whatever the branching ratio from
contact to binding, from binding to free DP, from free DP to bound
entity — each is a number ≤ 1, and their product would have to be
1.9e−59 for the net to come out at 3H.

## §2 — Therefore: none of the three candidates is what is missing

The founder's three candidates — evaporation rate, collision-binding
rate, free-DP fusion rate — are all **gross** rates, and gross rates
are abundant. Adding a measurement of any of them changes nothing,
because the answer is not set by any of them individually. **It is
set by their DIFFERENCE**, and the difference is 1 part in 2e59 of
the individual terms. **A quantity that small cannot be obtained by
measuring its large constituents to any achievable precision** —
which is also why our instrument, whose f_b agrees across seeds at
1e−3, is roughly fifty-six orders of magnitude short of resolving it
directly.

## §3 — What IS missing, in order

1. **AN IMBALANCE PRINCIPLE.** Something must FORCE formation and
   dissolution to cancel almost exactly, and something must set the
   residue to 3H. In equilibrium statistical mechanics detailed
   balance forces the cancellation to be EXACT — residue zero, no
   expansion. **A universe that fills itself at rate 3H is a system
   held permanently just off detailed balance by a fixed, tiny
   amount.** Nothing in the nine axioms currently does that. **This
   is the whole problem, and it is a structural question, not a
   measurement.**
2. **A CONSERVATION RULING (founder's, not the worker's).** If the CP
   inventory is FIXED, constant DP density during expansion is
   impossible — the Sea dilutes, d_s grows with cosmic time, and the
   calibrated 4.636 is a snapshot rather than a constant. **That
   branch is not a defeat: a slowly growing d_s is a time-varying
   vacuum energy, and the corpus already carries a live prediction of
   exactly that character (F-W-1, w_now = −1.023 ≠ −1).** If instead
   CP number GROWS, the creation rate is a new fundamental input
   requiring its own calibration — a second anchor, not a derivation.
   **These two branches make different predictions and the framework
   must choose.**
3. **AN INSTRUMENT THAT CAN EXHIBIT A NET RATE AT ALL.** Every engine
   in this corpus is a closed periodic box at fixed CP count and
   fixed volume. In such a box formation and dissolution balance **by
   construction** — the net rate is identically zero, not
   approximately zero. **No measurement made in it can ever be a
   filling rate.** Representing the founder's picture requires an
   open or comoving-expanding instrument that does not exist. That is
   a build, not a run.

## §4 — Why this is worth knowing (and what it does NOT claim)

**The favourable reading:** the corpus already ruled Λ a calibration
anchor rather than a derived quantity (R-CC-CALIBRATE, 3125). This
computation explains WHY that ruling was correct in mechanism terms —
the free parameter is the imbalance, and nothing in the framework
fixes it. The ruling was right for a reason nobody had stated.

**The unfavourable reading, stated plainly:** the DP-formation
picture, as posed, **does not dissolve the cosmological-constant
problem; it relocates it** — from "why is the vacuum energy 10^122
times smaller than its natural scale" to "why do these rates cancel
to 59 decimal places." **Note the arithmetic: 10^122 in energy
density corresponds to ~10^61 in amplitude, and we find 10^59.3. The
same problem, in the same place, wearing rate clothing.** Recorded as
an observation about orders of magnitude, **not as a claimed
identity** — establishing that would require the energy-to-rate map
written down and checked, which this patch does not do.

**A relocation is not nothing.** A rate imbalance held off detailed
balance by a symmetry-breaking parameter is a more tractable object
than a divergent vacuum sum: it invites the question *what small
parameter?* rather than *what cancels 122 orders?* **But the corpus
should not describe this as progress on Λ's magnitude until an
imbalance principle exists.**
