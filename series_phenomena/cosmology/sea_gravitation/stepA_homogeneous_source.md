## The worry

c05 sources gravity from the GRADIENT of net SSV: F = m'c²·k·∇(ΔSSV). A uniform field has zero gradient → zero force ("the uniform Sea is locally inert by construction"). But the Friedmann expansion is driven by *uniform* matter/radiation density (the decelerating matter era; BBN and CMB acoustic physics depend on the standard H(z)). If "only gradients gravitate" cannot reproduce homogeneous-density-driven expansion, the gradient-sourced picture conflicts with BBN/CMB — a hard kill (outcome (b) of the handover's Step A). This was flagged as the cheapest potential kill, to confront first.

## Resolution — outcome (a), via standard Newtonian cosmology

The "uniform density → zero gradient → zero force" feature is **not a new CPP problem**. It is **Seeliger's paradox**, identical to ordinary Newtonian gravity — which is exactly what c05/c07 establish CPP reduces to in the weak field. Newtonian cosmology has resolved it since **Milne–McCrea (1934)**:

- The **shell theorem** holds in CPP: shell-broadcast gives a clean 1/r² law (c05) with linear SSV superposition, so for a spherically-symmetric exterior the net interior gradient vanishes (CHECK 2). A comoving particle at radius r about any chosen center therefore feels only the mass **interior** to it.
- Interior mass M(<r) = (4π/3)r³ρ gives r̈ = −GM/r² = −(4π/3)Gρ·r, i.e. **ä/a = −(4π/3)Gρ** — the matter-era Friedmann acceleration equation (CHECK 3), independent of the chosen center (→ the cosmological principle).
- Because CPP gravity = Newtonian / weak-field GR in this regime (c05/c07), it **inherits this resolution for free**; the standard H(z) follows, so there is **no BBN/CMB conflict**.

## The conceptual point that dissolves the paradox

**"Uniform Sea is locally inert" and "uniform matter drives expansion" are the same fact seen two ways, not a contradiction.** "Zero force at a point" is the *absolute-force* statement (and every point is a symmetry center — which *is* the cosmological principle). The expansion deceleration is a *relative* acceleration between comoving points, which is well-defined and nonzero. Cosmological gravitation is tidal/relative, not absolute-force-at-a-point.
