## Part (a) — The residual is a zero-point coherence mode, not a transient retarded gradient (PRECONDITION CLEARED)

2-i reduced the arc's survival to: is the Sea vacuum residual a **retarded gradient** (transported, c-bounded → IR scale = retarded-equilibration scale R_H → arc breaks) or a **global coherence mode** (zero-point mode structure → IR scale = coherence scale → A4 route open)?

**The residual is a coherence mode.** The argument turns on what the residual *is*:

1. A ground state is, by definition, the lowest-energy *stationary* configuration — not a system still relaxing toward equilibrium.
2. A ground state's residual energy is therefore its **zero-point mode structure**, not a transient relaxation. In a finite, causally-bounded universe the vacuum's mode spectrum runs from a UV cutoff (l_P, the lattice spacing) to an IR cutoff L (the longest mode that fits the coherent region); the energy in the longest mode is ρ ~ c⁴/(8πG L²) — the holographic form Step C derived. 3. **Step C's "the Sea equilibrates over a Hubble time, leaving an un-smoothed gradient" was a heuristic that imported the transient-relaxation picture.** The same formula ρ ~ g²/8πG with g ~ c²/L is dimensionally identical either way; what the heuristic mis-set was the *interpretation of L*. The physical object — the ground-state vacuum energy with an IR cutoff — is the zero-point coherence mode, so L is a **coherence scale**, not a retarded-equilibration scale.

**Consequence:** the PSR "energy is c-bounded transport → L = R_H" objection is **defused** — zero-point energy is not transported; it is the inherent energy of the mode structure. ## Part (b) — Does A4 set the coherence scale to the future event horizon? (ADVANCED, NOT CLOSED)

The residual's IR scale L = the **vacuum coherence scale** = the size of the region over which the Sea is one coherent ground state. The question is which causal scale that is. A4 (the Nexus) is the governing primitive (2-i: it is CPP's only non-retarded global-coherence constraint). The argument that L = R_h (future event horizon):

**Why finite (not the whole lattice).** A4 enforces *consistency*. Two regions can be held mutually consistent only if there exists — now or ever — a channel by which their consistency can be enforced (the consistency is consistency of *local, c-bounded rules*; A4 coordinates their outcomes, it does not transmit energy/signal — cf. Bell: correlation without signalling). Two regions permanently beyond each other's reach (no signal ever, in either time direction) have **no operational consistency to enforce** — they are causally disconnected forever. So A4's coherence region is **bounded**, hence a finite IR cutoff, hence a nonzero Λ. (An unbounded coherence region would give zero residual — no dark energy.)

**Why the event horizon (not the particle horizon).** A4 is **spatially a-causal**: the Bell mechanism coordinates spacelike-separated regions ("entangled particles share a common determination at the Nexus level… the Nexus's global computation"). So A4 is **not** limited to the past light cone — the particle horizon (what *has* reached us) does *not* bound it. A4 coordinates everything that *can* be coordinated, which is the region of **persistent mutual reachability** — bounded by the **future event horizon R_h** (beyond which a comoving region recedes permanently and can never again exchange a signal with our patch). A region inside our particle horizon but outside R_h (seen, but now permanently receding) is *decohering out of* our vacuum: its future evolution can no longer be kept consistent with ours. So the *present* coherent vacuum is bounded by R_h, giving ρ_Λ ~ c⁴/(8πG R_h²) — the Li-2004 form that reproduces the observed history (w_now ≈ −1.02; `0723_horizon_wz.py`).

**F-COST-1 (the future-dependence), addressed in principle.** R_h depends on future a(t'); A4 "computes the next state" (forward in time). The resolution: A4 does not *predict* R_h. The event horizon is a **geometric property of the spacetime** (the boundary of the causally-coordinable region), determined by the actual evolution — which itself unfolds via A4's forward, self-consistent computation. A4's role is the standing one — "enforce consistency over what is coordinable" — and *which* events are coordinable is a fact about the spacetime causal structure (the event horizon), not a foreknowledge requirement. The future-dependence sits in the *definition of the coordinable set*, exactly as it does in standard HDE; A4's pre-existing **spatial a-causality** (Bell) is what makes a global, light-cone-transcending coherence boundary natural rather than pathological. **This dissolves the "foreknowledge paradox" reading of F-COST-1**; it does **not** prove the boundary is R_h rather than another global scale.
