## §2 The hinge, and it is a corpus fact (verify T3, T4, T5)
The founder's reservoir picture turns on something AP-4 already says:

> **"Every GP emits the same fixed number of DI-bits every Moment."** — **per GP, not per CP.**

**So CPs superimposed on a single GP are perceptually one.** A GP hosting twelve superimposed CPs emits exactly what a GP hosting one emits. It follows that:

> **Evaporation from superposition raises the *perceived* count without changing the *conserved* CP count.** What grows is the number of **occupied GPs**. The source is a **rate**, not a density.

**And that breaks the 3835 no-go's premise.** The no-go runs: only the **conserved** count enters δN; conserved densities obey integral constraints forcing P(k) → k²; hence blue; hence excluded. **If the perceived count is the occupied-GP count, a non-conserved rate enters δN and the first step fails.**

**This is not a refutation of the no-go — it is an escape from its scope**, which is the only kind of escape the no-go ever admitted, and 3837 said so: *"the surviving escape is an independent light field, not the count, modulating the end condition."* C-5 is that, with the reservoir playing the field's role.
