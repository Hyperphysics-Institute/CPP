## §1 It had not been assessed (verify T1)
The seven candidates closed at 3847 were the register spring, the conserved count, δkT, compositional disorder, C-3 (frustration), C-2 (edge), and C-4 (orientational Goldstone). **DM clumping was not among them.** This is a genuine eighth proposal, founder-raised rather than worker-manufactured, and the standing instruction not to invent an eighth candidate does not apply to assessing one that is asked about.

## §2 And it is the best-aimed proposal of the arc (verify T2)
**In ΛCDM the CMB anisotropy genuinely is dominated by dark-matter potential wells** — the Sachs–Wolfe effect. So DM clumping is not a speculative connection to the CMB: **it is exactly what the CMB displays.**

The question is the one this arc keeps returning to: does it **make** the pattern, or **carry** it?

## §3 Amplitude — and here it does something no previous candidate did (verify T3, T4)
CPP's DM is **charge-neutral qDP/hTetra aggregates** (CONJ-COSMO-1). Aggregation from a random start is **Poisson-seeded**, and Poisson noise is large when the objects are massive and therefore few:

| aggregate mass | N in (100 Mpc)³ | Poisson δ |
|---|---|---|
| 10⁶ M⊙ | 3.3×10¹⁰ | **5.6×10⁻⁶** |
| 10³ M⊙ | 3.3×10¹³ | 1.8×10⁻⁷ |
| 1 M⊙ | 3.3×10¹⁶ | 5.6×10⁻⁹ |

Required: ~10⁻⁵. **At ~10⁶ M⊙ per aggregate the Poisson contrast lands within a factor of two.**

**That is a first for this arc.** Every other candidate missed the amplitude by forty orders or more — C-2's incoherent floor was 41 short (3831 §3). The reason DM does better is structural: **aggregates are massive, hence few, hence noisy.**

**But it is a fit, not a derivation.** The aggregate mass is a free parameter and was chosen to land on the observed contrast; three decades of mass span three decades of contrast. Under PD-007 that makes the amplitude **reachable, not predicted** — and reachable-by-choice is exactly what the bar exists to refuse.

## §4 Three independent exclusions (verify T5, T6, T7)
**(1) The shape.** Poisson seeding is **white**. A white primordial spectrum in the CDM component **is** the isocurvature-CDM model, long excluded, and Planck bounds the isocurvature fraction to a few percent. *(The same bound that excluded compositional disorder at 3822 and the qCP chains at 3833.)*

**(2) The timing.** Clumping that develops **after** inflation is an **active, incoherent source**. Active sources do not reproduce the observed acoustic-peak series — this is precisely why cosmic-defect models were ruled out as the primary perturbation source. *(The same exclusion that demoted C-3 at 3829 §4.)*

**(3) The ordering.** The CMB requires δ ~ 10⁻⁵ already present and **coherent on super-horizon scales** at recombination. **Aggregation is a growth process**: it amplifies a seed and cannot supply the seed it starts from.

Any one of these is decisive; the three are independent.

## §5 The verdict, and what is genuinely new (verify T8, T9, T10)
> **It relocates the question rather than answering it** — the same verdict as the fractal cascade, but reached from a better starting point and failing later in the argument. DM clumping is how the primordial spectrum **reaches** the CMB; it is not how the spectrum **arises**.

**What is worth keeping:** this is the **first candidate in the arc to come close on amplitude**, and the reason is instructive — massiveness buys noise. Any future proposal that needs a large primordial contrast should look at **few, heavy objects** rather than many light ones. That is a real lesson from a failed candidate and it is recorded as such.

**And it does not disturb the no-go.** 3835 stands untouched: while inflation ends at a fixed geometric threshold, only the conserved count enters δN. **DM clumping is a post-inflation phenomenon and never enters the e-fold count at all** — it was never a candidate for the no-go's subject matter, which is why it fails on CMB grounds rather than on the no-go's.
