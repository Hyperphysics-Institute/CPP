## §1 The knob turns, and it is a real second knob (verify T1, T2)
Generalising 3823's identity from the founder's twelve to an arbitrary ignition occupancy k (CPs per GP):

> **N = ln(l_P/s) + ⅓ ln k + ⅓ ln(4π/3)**

At k = 12 this reduces exactly to 3823's N = ln(l_P/s) + 1.306. The k-dependence is real and **independent of the lattice resolution**: at the baseline spacing s₀ = 3.7×10⁻²⁸ l_P, the budget closes at

> **k ≈ 5.8×10¹⁴ CPs per GP** — a factor 4.8×10¹³ over the founder's twelve.

So the founder is right that this is a second knob and not a restatement of the first. My 3823 formulation (resolution alone) held k fixed at twelve without saying so, and the generalisation was owed.

## §2 But both knobs converge on one number (verify T3, T4)
The master relation is N = ⅓ ln N_CP − ln(R_init/l_P). Both k and s enter *only* through the total CP count N_CP = k·(4π/3)(R_init/s)³. So:
- via stacking (k → 5.8×10¹⁴ at fixed lattice): N_CP → 4.8×10⁹⁷
- via resolution (s → 10⁻³² l_P at k = 12): N_CP → 5.0×10⁹⁷

**The budget does not care how the CPs are arranged. It cares how many there are.** The two "knobs" are two parameterisations of one quantity. Independent cross-check: at N = 75 the end condition (one CP per rest-frame Planck sphere) applied to a ball of radius l_P·e⁷⁵ ≈ **6.0 mm** gives N_CP = 5.2×10⁹⁷ — the same number arrived at without reference to either k or s.

That is the clean statement the corpus should carry: **the e-fold budget is a constraint on the total CP count, N_CP ≈ 5×10⁹⁷ at R_init = l_P**, against the 10⁸⁴ on record.

## §3 The two costs

**(a) It resurrects the stack the founder disowned (verify T6).** At 3814 the founder wrote that "the crowd is at 'one address' is not my statement; or it was an error," and gave twelve CPs per GP, one to each icosahedral vertex. An occupancy of 5.8×10¹⁴ per address is a crowd at one address, 10¹⁴ deep — precisely the reading he retracted, and the retraction is what opened FORK-EU-OCCUPANCY-1 and led to the small-ball ruling. This is his picture and his to revise; but the corpus should not quietly re-adopt by arithmetic what he explicitly withdrew. **Flagged, not decided.**

*Two things survive a deep stack, and are worth stating so the cost is priced correctly.* T-1's isotropy is unaffected provided the stack is distributed k/12 per icosahedral vertex: the twelve directions still cancel, and the ℓ ≤ 5 result is about directions, not occupancies. And AP-5 permits saturation by depth — a 10¹⁴-deep register is the black-hole-like reading, not a protocol violation. So the objection is to the founder's own retraction, not to consistency.

**(b) Calibration is the move the charter forbids.** Charter bar (1) and PD-007: *a constant is an output or it is nothing*. Fixing k (or s, or N_CP) by requiring the universe to come out the observed size is fitting one parameter to one observable. It would convert the e-fold total from a failed zero-parameter output into a calibration input. The corpus would then have no remaining zero-parameter content in the count law except the tilt — and the tilt survives only because the pivot N_rem = 57 is adopted separately, not derived from N_total.

**What it would NOT cost:** PRED-C-96. n_s = 1 − 2/N_rem reads the pivot's remaining e-folds. Calibrating N_CP does not touch it. So the honest accounting is: calibration buys back consistency with the observed universe at the price of the e-fold total's predictive status, and costs the tilt nothing.

## §4 The disciplined form of the founder's proposal
His instinct is sound and there is a version of it that clears PD-007. **N_CP was never independently pinned.** EU-1's eq. Nstar used 10⁸⁰, described as "the observable-universe CP count"; the founder's 3814 message gave 10⁸⁴. Both are *matter* counts. But CPP holds that most CPs sit in the DP sea, not in matter — so a matter-derived count is a lower bound on the total, and the gap between 10⁸⁴ and 5×10⁹⁷ is a statement about how much of the substrate is sea. **The number may be an output of the sea's density rather than a free parameter at all.**

That converts a fit into a check, because the required total implies a present-day CP number density (verify T5):

> **n_CP(today) ≈ 1.4×10¹⁷ m⁻³** — mean CP separation ≈ 1.9 μm.

Confirmed two independent ways (direct volume count over the observable universe; dilution of one-CP-per-Planck-sphere by a₀/a_end). This is a falsifiable handle. It is ~3.6×10⁸ times the CMB photon density. Any independent CPP determination of the DP-sea density — from the sea's contribution to Λ (DE lane), from the polarization constants ε₀/μ₀ (SF-6), or from the DP-sea gravitation work — either lands on 10¹⁷ m⁻³ or it does not, and that is a real test rather than a tuning.

**Recommendation (not enacted; the maintainer's call):** register N_CP ≈ 5×10⁹⁷ and n_CP ≈ 1.4×10¹⁷ m⁻³ as **targets** that an independent derivation must hit, not as adopted values. Keep the e-fold budget open until either (i) VSL removes the requirement, or (ii) a sea-density derivation supplies the count. Do not adopt k by calibration.
