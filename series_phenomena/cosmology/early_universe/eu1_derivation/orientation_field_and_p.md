## §1 Before computing p, a question that should have been asked earlier
C-4 rests on an orientational order parameter. Three patches have developed its coupling, its mass scale and its anisotropy without anyone asking whether that degree of freedom exists in the corpus. It does.

> **FI-C-RC-1 — n̂ as a substrate-foundational primitive**, registered at Capotauro v2.0 (Patch 0454, Session 134), grounding what the glossary calls the **Picture B substrate-orientation field**.

**C-4's field is ratified, not invented.** That is a real clearing and it should have been established at 3839 rather than at 3845.

## §2 It is continuous, and that settles the fork p depended on (verify T2, T3, T4)
FI-C-RC-1 registers n̂ as a **primitive direction**, not a vertex-quantised one. That decides a fork the p-computation turns on, and which 3843 did not name:

- **Vertex-quantised reading** (the one implicitly assumed): the mean director is built by mixing the twelve axes, intermediate directions require local mixing, local SSV_net is O(1), and λ ≈ λ_hard = 0.052 ⇒ m/H = 7×10⁴ ⇒ **C-4 dead**.
- **Continuum reading** (what FI-C-RC-1 actually says): a **uniform** n̂ configuration is translationally homogeneous **for every n̂**. In a homogeneous state SSV_net = 0 exactly (S-HENGINE-HELD §2.2, via T-1's dipole cancellation). So the displacement anisotropy vanishes identically over **all** uniform configurations.

> **V(n̂) = const across uniform configurations ⇒ m = 0 from this mechanism, at every order in x.**

**There is no p.** 3843's λ ≈ λ_hard·x^p is withdrawn as the route — not because the arithmetic was wrong but because it was the right question under the wrong branch. The mechanism generates no uniform-configuration potential at all, so there is no order at which it reappears.

I should record that 3843 §4 flagged the parameterisation as an ansatz rather than a derivation, and that flag is what makes this withdrawal cheap rather than costly.

## §3 The mass is not zero — it runs through FI-C-RC-2 (verify T5)
n̂ is not decoupled from the lattice. The corpus registers the coupling:

> **FI-C-RC-2 — n̂-induced edge perturbations**, with magnitude fixed by a **perturbative distance ratio**, already used to ground the chirality magnitude at the sharper epistemic position of a *derived* magnitude rather than a posited one.

That is the actual mass mechanism: n̂ perturbs lattice edges, the perturbed lattice is no longer exactly icosahedral, the dipole cancellation is no longer exact, and the displacement anisotropy reappears at the order set by that distance ratio. It is the same structure as before — the anisotropy is proportional to the departure from exact cancellation — but the departure is now sourced by **n̂'s own coupling to the geometry**, not by the cosmological perturbation.
