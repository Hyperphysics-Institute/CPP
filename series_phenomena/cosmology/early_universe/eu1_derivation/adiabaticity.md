> **A Q-dominant patch produces more qDPs. And a qDP is *two qCPs of opposite charge* in ZBW oscillation.**

**Net-neutral.** A Q-rich patch has **more pairs, not more net charge.**

> **So the variation is in vacuum structure, not in any conserved quantum number.**

**That is the whole discriminator.** Isocurvature in a **conserved** charge — baryon number — survives thermalisation and is tightly bounded. **Isocurvature in vacuum composition does not survive:** at reheating the species equilibrate and the mode is erased, leaving only the adiabatic component at recombination.

> **C-5 passes the test that killed composition and DM clumping — and passes it for a reason internal to the founder's own mechanism rather than by a rescue bolted on afterwards.** Pair-neutrality is not an extra assumption; **it is what a qDP is.**
