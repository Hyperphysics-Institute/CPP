## §1 Claim (1), the compositional seed — the physics is right and it amends T-1

The founder's point stands on its own terms: with three interaction classes (eCP–eCP electric; eCP–qCP electric; qCP–qCP electric *and* strong, the S slot being the only species identifier per AP-4), no assignment of species and sign across addresses makes every local environment equivalent. A perfectly homogeneous, equidistant, simultaneously strong- and electric-neutral mix is not constructible. That is a correct statement about the lattice and the corpus should carry it.

**It amends T-1 (3820), and that amendment is owed.** S-IGNITION-ISOTROPIC has two parts. Part (b) — isotropy at each grid point, exact through ℓ = 5 — is *geometric*: it depends only on the twelve icosahedral directions, is untouched by what species sit on them, and survives intact. Part (a) — homogeneity *across* grid points — was derived from the initial condition assigning "the same twelve-vertex configuration to every address," and I did not state that this presumed species-uniformity as well as geometric uniformity. Under the founder's picture the geometry is uniform and the species content is not. **Part (a) is therefore conditional on a species-uniformity assumption that the founder has now denied.** The correction is recorded here and carried to the T-1 document; the ℓ ≤ 5 result is unaffected.

**But as a ζ seed it fails, and it fails the same way the count failed at 3812.** Three independent walls, none of which the compositional picture clears:
- **Frozen, not light.** The species assignment is fixed at ignition and does not evolve. 3812's requirement is a collective variable with ω ≲ H that *wavers* Moment to Moment. A fixed pattern has ω = 0 in the wrong sense — it does not oscillate, it simply sits. It is in the same class as the pairing pattern (3812: complete within 15 Moments, frozen before one e-fold).
- **White, therefore n_s = 1.** A pattern laid down once with no correlation across scales is scale-independent. EU-1's tilt derivation rests on P_ζ ∝ H_eff²; a scale-independent source gives n_s = 1 exactly — the 0741 cliff, 8.4σ from Planck. This is the same exclusion that removed the count at 3812 §2.
- **Species-blind end condition, therefore δN = 0.** This is the 3818 wall, and it is the decisive one. Under the re-grounded count (3816 §3) inflation ends when the density reaches one CP per rest-frame Planck sphere. That threshold is a *count*; it carries no species index. A patch with a different eCP/qCP ratio reaches it at the same expansion factor. δN = 0, ζ = 0, exactly as for δkT.
- **Amplitude, for completeness.** Even setting the above aside, the Poisson amplitude of a compositional pattern is ~8×10⁻⁸ per Hubble patch at the tensor line (verify T1) against the ~4.6×10⁻⁵ required — three orders short, and this is the *most* generous reading.

**Verdict on (1): correct physics, real amendment to T-1's homogeneity clause, not a ζ source.** Registered, not adopted.

## §1b CORRECTION (Patch 3831) — "three-for-three" was wrong about the count

§1 records the end-condition wall as having taken δkT, the count and composition. **The count does not belong on that list.** The count *is* n̄, so it enters δN directly (ζ = ⅓ δρ/ρ, 3831 §1); it was rejected at 3812 on **amplitude** (Poisson, astronomically short) and **whiteness** (n_s = 1), not on the end condition. The wall is **two-for-three** — δkT and composition. This matters because it places C-2 in the count's own class (initial-slice density perturbations), so C-2 must beat it on amplitude and spectrum. ## §2 Claim (2), the edge — the strongest candidate yet, and honestly so

The founder is right that this is the most obvious asymmetry, and it is qualitatively different from everything the corpus has tested. The interior of the ball cancels: twelve neighbours, momenta summing to zero (3820, exact through ℓ = 5). The boundary layer does not — a CP at the edge has neighbours on the inward side and vacancy outward, so the icosahedral cancellation that makes the interior isotropic *fails precisely and only there*. This is not a perturbation of the cancellation; it is its absence. Inward-propagating disturbance follows, through exactly the chain the founder names (CP → DP → mass entities).

Two properties make it more interesting than the failed candidates:
- **It is coherent, not stochastic.** Unlike a Poisson composition pattern, the edge is a single large-scale structure. Coherence over a large region is what ζ needs and what shot noise can never supply.
- **It is dynamical, not configurational.** Reflections propagate; they are not laid down once and frozen. This clears the objection that killed claim (1) and the count.

Two properties are problems, stated plainly:
- **One feature is not a spectrum.** The edge supplies a characteristic scale — the ball's radius, and the propagation time inward. A scale-invariant spectrum over ~57 e-folds of dynamic range is a different object. Whether inward reflections in an expanding medium can produce approximate scale invariance is a real question and not one the corpus can answer by inspection; it is not a fatal objection, but it is not a small one either.
- **The volume fraction is nil, but that is the wrong measure.** A boundary layer a few GP spacings thick is ~10⁻²⁷ of the ball by volume (verify T4). That would matter if the edge acted by admixture; it does not, because a coherent wave propagating inward carries its influence far beyond its source layer. The volume figure is recorded so that no one later dismisses the edge on it, and so that no one later *claims* it without addressing the propagation.

**Verdict on (2): the first ζ candidate that clears 3812's light-mode wall and 3818's δN wall by structure rather than by hope — registered as candidate C-2 (edge-reflection mode), not adopted.** What it owes before any computation: a statement of how an inward-propagating boundary disturbance imprints on the *end condition* (the δN bridge's missing end), and whether its spectrum can be near scale-invariant. Neither is guessed here (PD-007).
