## §2 Definitions, plainly
- **GP (Grid Point).** A fixed lattice site. **The lattice never grows** — EU-1: the universe "expands by dilution on a fixed lattice scaffold." GPs are not created.
- **CP (Conscious Point).** The occupant. **CP number is conserved** — CPs are neither created nor destroyed.
- **Planck sphere.** A fixed *physical* volume, (4π/3)l_P³, containing a fixed number of GPs.
- **n̄.** The count of CPs within one rest-frame Planck sphere (3816's re-grounding).

**The question:** when EU-1 writes n̄, which population is being counted — and does it dilute?

## §3 Why it matters — everything in the paper runs through it

- **The engine:** H_eff = κ₀ kT **ln n̄**. The expansion rate *is* the log of this count.

So if n̄ names the wrong population, the engine has the wrong rate, the tilt has the wrong shape, and inflation has the wrong ending — or no ending. **This is not a bookkeeping question; it is the paper's central mechanism.**
