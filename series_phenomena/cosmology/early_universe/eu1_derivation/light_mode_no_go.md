## §1 What "light" costs here (verify T1)
H at the tensor-line ceiling is 1.93×10⁻⁵ M_Pl, so a mode relaxing at the Planck rate has **ω/H ≈ 5×10⁴**. "Light" therefore means *five orders of magnitude slower than one Moment* on a lattice whose every microscopic process ticks at t_P. **No local mechanism can qualify by accident.** The register spring failed by exactly this margin (3812), and it would have been surprising if any other local relaxation had done better. Slowness must be **parametric** — protected by something — not a matter of finding a sluggish process.

Physics supplies exactly three ways to protect it, and each is checked below.

## §2 Mechanism 1 — conservation (verify T2)
A locally conserved density cannot relax locally: to smooth a long-wavelength excess you must transport the conserved stuff across that wavelength. Hence the hydrodynamic law ω ≈ D k², and with the only available diffusivity D ~ l_P²/t_P,

> **ω = H at λ ≈ 230 l_P — 0.44% of the Hubble radius.** Every mode at or beyond horizon scale is light, by a wide margin.

This is a genuine hit. The conserved held count (S-HENGINE-HELD's n̄, D4-conserved) is collective, is slow at all relevant scales for a reason rather than by accident, and — uniquely among everything tested — **it is the very quantity the end condition reads**, so it clears the 3818 wall automatically. It is the best-fitting candidate the search produces. §5 is why it nonetheless fails.

## §3 Mechanism 2 — symmetry, i.e. a Goldstone mode (verify T3)
A broken continuous symmetry leaves a mode with ω = c k, massless by symmetry rather than by tuning: light for **k < H**, which is exactly the condition to freeze at horizon exit with δ ≈ H/2π per mode. Scale-invariant, Gaussian (free field), no tuning.

CPP does have a candidate order parameter: the DP sea's **orientation** — the paired sea's director, and the antiferromagnetic-type order whose frustration was computed at 3827/3829. An orientational Goldstone is the one structurally clean light field in the corpus's ingredients.

Two problems, both stated rather than waved at. A Goldstone is a *phase-like* variable: it modulates orientation, not total energy density, so its perturbations are generically **isocurvature**, which Planck bounds to a few percent. And it does not obviously change the **count**, so on present structure it hits the 3818 wall exactly as δkT did. **Registered as candidate C-4 (orientational Goldstone), conditional and not adopted** — it becomes interesting only if §6's escape opens, and it then owes a coupling to the driver.

## §4 Mechanism 3 — a near-flat potential
The standard route (a light scalar) needs a field with a flat potential. CPP's driver is H_eff = κ₀ kT ln n̄, and the only factors are κ₀, kT and the count. **kT was killed at 3818** — it changes the rate, not the e-fold total. **κ₀ fails identically**, and for the same reason: it multiplies H, so it changes how fast inflation runs and not how long. Neither reaches the end condition. There is no fourth factor.

## §5 The no-go (verify T5, T6)
Assembling what the last several patches established:

1. **ζ = δN** (3831 bridge).
2. **N = ⅓ ln n̄_init**, because n̄_end = 1 is a *fixed geometric threshold* — one CP per rest-frame Planck sphere (3816 §3).
3. Therefore **only the count contributes to δN.** Anything else — kT, κ₀, species composition, orientation — cancels out of N entirely. This is not three separate failures; it is one theorem applied three times.
4. **The count is a locally conserved density.** CPs are neither created nor destroyed, and D4 conserves the held count.
5. A locally conserved density with causal correlations obeys integral constraints forcing **P(k) → k² (conserved number) or k⁴ (causal, conserved stress)** as k → 0.
6. Hence **n_s = 3 or 5**, against the observed **0.9649**.

> **NO-GO. While inflation ends at a fixed geometric threshold, the only variable that can source ζ is a conserved density, and conserved densities are blue. No light mode — however slow, however collective, however cleverly found — can produce a near-scale-invariant ζ in this structure.**

This is the same integral constraint that excluded causal-defect models as the primary CMB source, arriving here from CPP's own internals rather than from the defect literature. It also explains the shape of the whole search: the count was slow and reached the end condition but is blue; everything non-conserved could in principle be red but cannot reach the end condition. **The two requirements have been mutually exclusive all along**, and no amount of candidate-hunting could have resolved that.

Under the perceived reading, inflation ends when the *perceived* density reaches one per Planck sphere — and the PSR is set by SSV_abs, i.e. by local state, which is **not conserved**. That makes n̄_end dynamical, exactly as required. It is also 3818 §5's route 1, named there as an amplitude escape and left uncomputed.
