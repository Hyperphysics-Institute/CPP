## §1 My error, first (verify T1, T2)
At 3876 §5 I wrote that the founder's phrase *"the entirety of the universe that will ever be populated"* **is** the end condition, and let it stand unqualified. **That was wrong in a way that matters**, because the phrase invites a boundary reading and the physics has none.

**The end condition is n̄ = 1 CP per rest-frame Planck sphere.** That is a **density**: intensive, local, and satisfied everywhere at once. It says nothing whatever about where anything ends. Inflation stops when the crowd thins to one per Planck sphere — not when the crowd reaches a wall.

**And the phrase conflates two lattice scales.** "One CP per Planck sphere" is **not** "one CP per GP":

> a Planck sphere holds **~4.3×10⁹⁶ GPs**, so at the end of inflation the CPs occupy about **one GP in 10⁹⁷**.

I wrote "all GPs that will ever be populated" while the arithmetic said "one GP in 10⁹⁷." That is the error, and it is mine rather than the founder's — his phrase was offered tentatively and I adopted it as though it were exact.
