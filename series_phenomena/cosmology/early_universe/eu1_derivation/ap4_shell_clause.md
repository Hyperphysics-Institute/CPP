## §1 Half one: "deposit exactly once" is forced (verify T1, T2)
This half derives cleanly, and it had never been argued.

Every GP emits a **fixed number** of DI-bits every Moment (AP-4). Suppose instead that each intervening GP tallied a bit passing through. Then the total deposits per Moment would scale with the **path length in GP spacings** — and a PSR-length path traverses

> **~10³² GP spacings** (at the sub-Planck spacing the e-fold budget requires).

The emission budget would be inflated by that factor and would not close: a fixed emission would produce a deposit total 10³² times larger, with no conserved quantity to account for it. **Exactly-once delivery is therefore required, not chosen.** AP-4c's "pass-through tally excluded" is an entailment of the fixed-emission rule, and can be recorded as such rather than as a stipulation.

**And the shell as the deposit locus gives inverse-square for free.** A fixed emission spread over a sphere of radius PSR arrives at areal density ∝ 1/PSR². No force law is assumed anywhere; the inverse square is the sphere's area. That is a satisfying amount of physics from pure counting.

## §2 Half two: the near field, and where it fails (verify T3–T6)
For a source–receiver separation r < PSR there is **no direct deposit** — the bit passes through untallied and lands at the shell. AP-4c assigns this case to "**the relay recursion**": successive re-emission by intervening GPs, each carrying influence onward through its own emissions.

That works in a running universe. It does not work at the first Moment, for a reason internal to the protocol:

1. **The relay requires prior-Moment content.** AP-3 has the GP compute its registers from the *previous* Moment's arrivals and imprint its outgoing bits from those registers. **A GP can only relay what it has already received.**
2. **At Moment 1 every register is empty.** This is not an extra assumption — it is 3816 §1's own argument, the very premise that puts SSV_abs = 0 and the PSR at its l_P ceiling. **The premise that opens the reach also empties the relay.**
3. **The shell lies outside the ball.** Under the small-ball ruling R_init ≤ l_P ≈ PSR, a bit emitted from any interior GP deposits at radius ~l_P — beyond the pre-ignition ball entirely, on GPs that host no CPs.

> **At Moment 1, an interior GP of a sub-PSR ball receives 0 direct + 0 relayed = 0.**

**The check flagged at 3816 §6 — "the near-field relay clause must deliver the full count to interior GPs; this is load-bearing and should not be assumed silently" — returns negative.**

## §3 The escape is closed (verify T8)
The one way out would be a **sub-Moment relay**, letting intervening GPs re-emit *within* Moment 1 so that influence propagates inward before the Moment closes. The corpus carries a **founder ruling that ZBW is NOT sub-Moment**, and no other sub-Moment tier is available to carry it. **The escape is closed on the founder's own ruling.**
