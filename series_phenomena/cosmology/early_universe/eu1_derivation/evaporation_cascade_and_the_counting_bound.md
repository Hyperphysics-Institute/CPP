## §1 A correction to my own 3856 (verify T1)
The founder states the DE lane **is not complete**, and that its working hypothesis is a **mass cascade**: high-mass DP entities evaporate into lower-mass ones and fill space at the same density/occupancy.

**Two things follow, and the first is a correction I owe.** At 3856 I treated the d_s = 4.636 l_P calibration as a settled corpus determination and built an 86-order confrontation on it. ## §2 But the clash does not dissolve (verify T2, T3)
Here is the part the cascade does not reach.

> **Evaporation redistributes CPs among entities. It does not create CPs.**

So a bound survives that is independent of every dilution law, every cascade, and every epoch:

> **N_CP ≥ 2 × N_entities**, always. Entities cannot outnumber their constituents.

The DE calibration puts **8.5×10¹⁸² DP entities** in the observable volume. Hence

> **N_CP ≥ 1.7×10¹⁸³.**

The cascade actually *sharpens* this rather than relieving it: a mechanism that keeps entity density constant while the volume grows must keep *making* entities, and every one of them must be paid for out of a fixed CP budget. The constant-density hypothesis is therefore the most CP-expensive reading available, not the cheapest.

## §4 If the calibration were taken literally (verify T5, T6)
The consequence is not a worse budget but a wildly better one:

> **N_total = ⅓ ln(1.7×10¹⁸³) = 140.6 e-folds** against the 75.0 required — a **~65 e-fold surplus**, where 3823 had a 10.5 e-fold shortfall.

And the pivot still fits: N_rem = 57 sits comfortably inside 140.6, so **the tilt is unaffected either way** — as it has been through this entire arc.

That is worth stating carefully, because it cuts both ways. The DE calibration would *solve* the e-fold budget outright. It would also make EU-1's own N_CP wrong by 103 orders, which is not a small price for a solved budget.

## §5 Three exits, none adjudicable in this lane (verify T8)
1. **d_s is not a CP-composed entity spacing** — an effective or coarse-grained scale rather than a count of constituent-bearing objects. Then the counting bound does not apply and nothing follows. *This is the exit I would look at first: 4.636 l_P is suspiciously close to the lattice scale for a structure meant to be built from lattice constituents.*
2. **N_CP really is ~10¹⁸³.** The budget closes with a large surplus; EU-1's 10⁸⁰ and the founder's 10⁸⁴ are both wrong by ~100 orders; the tilt survives on the adopted pivot.
3. **The DE calibration is provisional.** The founder states the lane **is not complete**, so its numbers are working values. The confrontation then waits on the DE lane rather than constraining the EU lane. **This is the founder's own framing and the cheapest hold.**
