## §1 Claim (1): KE conserved in the bulk — consistent, and it fills a hole
Under AP-5, energy that arrives as a flux and cannot be acted on is held (D4; 3706 §2). A CP that moves one GP under its local charge differential and polarizes the surrounding DP arcs has done exactly that: its "KE/inertia" is the bulk's polarization. 3808 §3 identified the D1-clipped flux of the pairing motion as the only D4-eligible reheating quantity and left it blocked on a demand-magnitude instrument. The founder's picture is that same quantity named from the CP's side, with one addition the corpus lacked: **it is the source of the bath.** LEMMA-NS-BATH (0767) posits kT ~ E_Pl by timescale separation and never says where the bath's energy comes from. Here it comes from the first move of every CP. That is a unification, not a new constant, and it makes REHEAT-BUDGET-1 and the bath clause one problem.

## §2 Claim (2): KE as the driver — no; as the light mode — the first live candidate (verify T1, T2)
*Driver.* A kinetic push has a direction and a finite supply; 3805 measured pushes at ≤ 0.45 e-folds, and any push driver is power-law, excluded by the tilt (0745/0746). In FRW language kinetic energy is stiff (w = +1) and decelerates. S-HENGINE-HELD stays the driver; nothing here disturbs CONV-045.
*Light mode.* 3812's requirement: a collective variable, ω ≲ H, whose fluctuation ∝ H_eff. The bath's energy density in a Hubble patch is collective; it dilutes over many e-folds (slow); and because H_eff = κ₀ kT (ln n̄ − 0), a fractional waver δkT/kT is a fractional waver δH/H of the same size — the ∝ H_eff property is built in, not assumed. **C-1 (bath-energy mode)** is registered on that basis. What is *not* shown: that δkT produces ζ. In δN, ζ is the difference in e-folds between patches to a common final surface; under the count law N_rem = ⅓ ln n̄ the e-fold total is fixed by the count alone and δkT changes the rate but not the total — ζ = 0. ζ ≠ 0 needs the end of the window (or the surface ζ is measured on) to depend on kT — e.g. inflation ending when H_eff falls to the bath's own rate, or the last-scattering surface being one of fixed kT. That is a real computation with a real answer, and it is T-3b's first job. Pre-expectation, on record: I expect ζ ≠ 0 through the end condition, with S set by d(ln N_end)/d(ln kT), and no reason for it to equal 57.

## §3 Claim (3): the packed sphere — homogeneous, but literal packing removes the crowd (verify T3, T4)
Two like charges per GP, neighbours alternating, over a hypericosahedral sphere: every CP's first move is to a nearest neighbour, pairing is one Moment, the start is homogeneous up to the lattice, and the KE injection is uniform. That would discharge T-1 and make 3805's fifteen Moments one. It also supersedes 3710's twelve-vertex picture, which would need a founder ruling to retire (not done here; both are on record).
The cost: EU-1's e-fold count is N = ⅓ ln(N_CP/N_GP) with N_CP/N_GP ≈ 10⁷⁹ at thirteen seed GPs. Two per GP is n̄ = 2 → N = 0.23 e-folds, and the log-law driver μ = kT ln n̄ is ~0.7 kT instead of ~180 kT. A sphere packed at two per GP holds ~5×10⁷⁹ GPs, radius ~2×10²⁶ l_P ≈ 4 nm — a nanometre-scale ball, not a Planck point. So either (a) each address on the sphere holds a *stack* of many CPs arranged as like pairs (the crowd survives; the sphere is the twelve-vertex picture spread over a shell), or (b) the occupancy in the log law is not per-GP (a redefinition the paper would have to carry). The corpus cannot choose; the founder's picture can.
