## §1 What the tilt already assumes (T1)
EU-1 eq. tilt: n_s − 1 = d ln P_ζ/d ln k = 2 d ln H_eff/dN, from P_ζ ∝ H_eff². That is the spectator prescription: the fluctuation that seeds ζ has amplitude ∝ H_eff. It is asserted, not derived, and it is load-bearing — a source with scale-independent patch-to-patch variance gives n_s = 1 exactly, the 0741 cliff, 8.4σ from Planck. So T-3a is not a new demand: **the paper's own tilt presupposes a crowd variable whose waver grows with the crowd's density.** Naming it is the whole of the amplitude problem and half of the tilt's grounding.

## §2 The three Q1 candidates against the requirement (T2–T4)
- **The register spring** (charter default, worker's E-1). R-CAP-SPRING: a saturated register yields and settles; at the cap a hop takes two Moments, so the settling frequency is ω ≳ 1/(2t_P). At the tensor pass line H ≤ 3.9×10⁻⁶ E_Pl that is ω/H ≥ 1.3×10⁵. A mode that heavy does not freeze at horizon crossing; its super-horizon power is suppressed by at least (H/ω)² ≈ 6×10⁻¹¹ relative to a light spectator. **Withdrawn.** (It may still be the *reheating* degree of freedom of 3808 §3 — a heavy mode is exactly what stores a clipped flux — but it cannot seed ζ.)
- **The count per address.** Conserved under dilution: whatever patch-to-patch variance it has is fixed at ignition and does not track H_eff (T1 kills it), and its Poisson part is 67 orders short (AS-NORMALIZATION §2) — worse still per Hubble patch, which holds ~2×10¹⁶ GPs at the pass line.
- **The pairing pattern.** Complete within 15 Moments (3805), before one e-fold: frozen, scale-independent, white. T1 kills it.

## §3 The re-cut
**T-3a.** Identify a *collective* crowd variable with ω ≲ H — something that changes slower than the expansion, is coherent over a Hubble patch, and wavers more strongly when ln n̄ is larger. **T-3b.** Compute its δN sensitivity S; pass line S ≥ 220 (charter §2) unchanged.
What survives as the place to look (not claimed): the unstacking *rate* itself — H_eff = κ₀(μ(n̄) − μ(1)) is the one quantity in the engine that is both collective and ∝ ln n̄; a patch-to-patch waver of the rate at which a patch's crowd spreads is ζ by definition. Whether the substrate has a mechanism that makes the rate waver at horizon scale with amplitude ∝ H — rather than being fixed by the local count — is the physical question, and it is a picture question.
