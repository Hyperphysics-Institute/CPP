## §1 What the distinction actually means (verify T1, T2)
**n̄ is an occupancy** — how crowded a perceptual sphere is. It has to be, because μ = kT ln n̄ is a **configurational entropy per particle** (0749): the log of how many ways the crowd can be arranged.

The question is what "crowded" counts:

- **Reading A — CPs.** Twelve CPs sharing one GP count as twelve.
- **Reading B — occupied GPs.** Twelve CPs sharing one GP count as **one**.

> **And the two differ only where CPs share a GP.** With no superposition they are identical. This is not a global ambiguity in the theory; it is a local one, and it opens only if something forces CPs onto the same site.

That is narrower than my 3888 framing suggested, and it is the key to resolving it.
