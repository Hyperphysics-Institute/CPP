EU-1's tilt runs on μ = kT ln n̄ + const, where n̄ is the superposed-stack occupation per GP within the causal patch (ln n̄ = 3 N_rem; n̄ ≈ 10⁷⁴ at the observable epoch) and kT is the ZBW/substrate bath temperature (LEMMA-NS-BATH). ## §2 Why it is the unique consistent identification (a bridge statement, v1.0 — not entailment)
*v1.0 relabel (CONV-045).* 0746 selects "count rather than stress"; AP-5 later splits the GP register into held and acted-on sectors; S-HENGINE-HELD specifies what "count" means once that split exists. The three points below are why it is the only consistent completion.
1. **0746 chose count over stress.** The PCD boost-law fork (0746) found two readings of the H-engine: (i) COUNT-driven / configurational — the boost acts on PSR_base, which is SSV-independent by construction, and grows with the occupation count → μ ∝ ln n̄ → n_s = 0.9649; (ii) SSV-STRESS-driven — the boost follows the displacement field (SSV_abs ~ n or SSV_net ~ √n) → n_s = −5 or −2, excluded, with neutrality unable to rescue it. Data required (i). **The acted-on displacement is reading (ii)'s quantity; the held count is reading (i)'s.** AP-5's D1 clips (ii). S-HENGINE-HELD names 0746's fork (i) in AP-5's vocabulary — the one identification under which fork (i) survives the split. (AP-5 also supplies an *independent* reason (ii) could not carry the tilt: a clipped displacement is depth-independent, and a depth-independent driver gives n_s = 1 — 0741/0744. Reading (ii) is now excluded twice.)
2. **(Supporting, not load-bearing — CONV-045 4–1.) The acted-on displacement is zero in the homogeneous state before any clip.** In an FRW/VSL-homogeneous saturated era (0776) every GP's twelve neighbours host identical stacks; the census is icosahedrally isotropic and its vector sum vanishes (the twelve icosahedral vertex vectors sum to zero exactly — verify T2). The expansion cannot be a net displacement of CPs in that state; it is, as the corpus has said since 0731, **occupancy dilution on a fixed lattice** — the causal reach grows, n̄ per GP falls (ln n̄ = 3 N_rem), and no GP is created or moved. That is a register (held-sector) process.
3. **LEMMA-NS-BATH already places μ in the held sector.** The lemma states μ is evaluated against the bath that thermalises the CP occupations — the ZBW/substrate bath at kT ~ E_Pl. Under AP-5 the stack's thermalised state is exactly D4's stored pattern (the CPs' bounded lockstep motion within their cells, R-CORE-STORES-AS-CP-VIBRATION), and the D2 copy at every depth is a PCD cycle — so the ZBW mixing the bath clause needs is the layer hierarchy's own dynamics, not something the cap starves.
