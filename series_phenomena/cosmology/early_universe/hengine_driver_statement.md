## §0 What is being stated, in one paragraph
EU-1's tilt runs on μ = kT ln n̄ + const, where n̄ is the superposed-stack occupation per GP within the causal patch (ln n̄ = 3 N_rem; n̄ ≈ 10⁷⁴ at the observable epoch) and kT is the ZBW/substrate bath temperature (LEMMA-NS-BATH). Under the ratified AP-5 and the founder's ruling R-STACK-SENDS-EACH, every GP is saturated by ~74 orders throughout inflation, and D1 clips what a GP *acts on* each Moment to the floor. The corpus had never said which sector of the GP the H-engine reads. **This statement fixes it: the H-engine reads the held sector — the stack's count n̄, which D4 conserves and D3 relays whole — and not the acted-on sector, which D1 clips.** Because the two sectors are distinct by AP-5's own definitions, the clip cannot reach the driver; H_eff ∝ μ is untouched, and n_s = 1 − 2/N_* stands under Reading S exactly as it stood before AP-5.

## §1 The statement (S-HENGINE-HELD)
**S-HENGINE-HELD.** In the CPP early universe the boost of the baseline causal reach (the H-engine, PSR_base → PSR_base(n̄), 0746 fork (i)) is a function of the occupation count n̄ a GP **holds** — the Gibbs entropy k ln n̄ of the superposed stack, evaluated against the substrate bath (LEMMA-NS-BATH) — and of nothing the GP **acts on** per Moment. Under AP-5: n̄ is D4's conserved content and D3's relayed content; the D1 cap binds only the acted-on displacement and is silent on n̄. Consequently H_eff ∝ μ = kT ln n̄ holds at every depth of saturation, and the D1 clip has no cosmological signature during the observable window.

## §2 Why it is the corpus's own statement and not a new choice
1. **0746 already chose the sector.** The PCD boost-law fork (0746) found two readings of the H-engine: (i) COUNT-driven / configurational — the boost acts on PSR_base, which is SSV-independent by construction, and grows with the occupation count → μ ∝ ln n̄ → n_s = 0.9649; (ii) SSV-STRESS-driven — the boost follows the displacement field (SSV_abs ~ n or SSV_net ~ √n) → n_s = −5 or −2, excluded, with neutrality unable to rescue it. Data required (i). **The acted-on displacement is reading (ii)'s quantity; the held count is reading (i)'s.** AP-5's D1 clips (ii). S-HENGINE-HELD therefore says nothing new about the H-engine; it names 0746's fork (i) in AP-5's vocabulary. (AP-5 also supplies an *independent* reason (ii) could not carry the tilt: a clipped displacement is depth-independent, and a depth-independent driver gives n_s = 1 — 0741/0744. Reading (ii) is now excluded twice.)
2. **The acted-on displacement is zero in the homogeneous state before any clip.** In an FRW/VSL-homogeneous saturated era (0776) every GP's twelve neighbours host identical stacks; the census is icosahedrally isotropic and its vector sum vanishes (the twelve icosahedral vertex vectors sum to zero exactly — verify T2). SSV_net = 0, so the acted-on displacement is zero by symmetry, and the floor cap on its magnitude is idle. The expansion cannot be a net displacement of CPs in that state; it is, as the corpus has said since 0731, **occupancy dilution on a fixed lattice** — the causal reach grows, n̄ per GP falls (ln n̄ = 3 N_rem), and no GP is created or moved. That is a register (held-sector) process.
3. **LEMMA-NS-BATH already places μ in the held sector.** The lemma states μ is evaluated against the bath that thermalises the CP occupations — the ZBW/substrate bath at kT ~ E_Pl. Under AP-5 the stack's thermalised state is exactly D4's stored pattern (the CPs' bounded lockstep motion within their cells, R-CORE-STORES-AS-CP-VIBRATION), and the D2 copy at every depth is a PCD cycle — so the ZBW mixing the bath clause needs is the layer hierarchy's own dynamics, not something the cap starves.
