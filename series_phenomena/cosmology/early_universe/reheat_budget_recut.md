## §1 The corpus already answers the question the item asked (T1, T4)
The item's premise was 3805 §3.2: the intra-stack like-charge repulsion is "lockstepped (D2) and stored (D4)." But 3706 §2/§4, enacted one day earlier in the GR lane, clarified D4 for exactly this case: **a GP above the cap holding the overflow of a *static* field holds a count re-supplied every Moment, not an energy; D4 conserves energy that arrived as a flux and was held.** A stack of like charges at rest at one PSR is a static field. Its overflow is a count. Under the corpus's own reading, the "stored repulsive energy" of a stationary stack is zero — the same conclusion 3706 reached for a stationary R-core, and for the same reason.

The cancellation makes this more than a bookkeeping point. In the ignition every +stack pairs with a −stack and the two co-occupy one floor cell (THEO-1; 3805: 100 % of assignments, ≤ 15 Moments). The pairwise sum for N charges of each sign at one point is

U = u [ C(N,2) + C(N,2) − N² ] = −uN,

exactly (T1, brute-force N ≤ 60 and closed form): the self-energy of *both* stacks is cancelled by their mutual attraction, and what remains is N pair bindings — one per DP. That residual is the DP sea's ground-state binding, the vacuum EU-1 and every other paper already sit in; the corpus does not count vacuum formation as heat. So the object the item named — a stored surplus of like-charge repulsion — does not exist after pairing, and before pairing it is a count, not an energy.

A demand-magnitude instrument would have to choose a force law for 10⁷⁴ co-located CPs inside a floor cell — the PCD-level detail PD-007 says not to settle by picture, and the place a constant would get minted to make the number come out. ## §4 The release law's form, for the record [PCD-EXT] (T5)
The item also asked for the D4 release law of a de-saturating homogeneous lattice. The form follows from what is ratified, without a new constant:
- Expansion under S-HENGINE-HELD is dilution on a fixed lattice; the per-PSR count v (the cap sees D ~ 12 n̄, 3711) falls as a⁻³.
- Depth is demand-activated, d = ⌈1.5 v⌉ (AP-5, derived at 3699). It falls monotonically as v falls (T5).

This is the same law 3706 used for a collapsed object (release needs a 67 % fall in demand), applied to a homogeneous field with a⁻³ dilution as the driver. It is labelled [PCD-EXT] because the timing of the up-transfer between layers (one layer per Moment? all at once?) is a working extension, not a ratified clause; the *form* — no layer-1 release until v < ⅔ — does not depend on it. No GR-lane derivation is needed.
