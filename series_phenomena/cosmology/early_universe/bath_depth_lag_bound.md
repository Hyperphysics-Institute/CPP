## §1 What could go wrong, and what the structure says
The bath clause's leg (1), "fast enough," was grounded at 0769 as R = τ_eq H_inf = N_mix (H_inf/E_Pl) ≪ 1 for an *unsaturated* register, with τ_eq = N_mix t_P from the ZRP spectral gap (0772: gap O(1) in ZBW units, finite and size-independent). Under R-STACK-SENDS-EACH the n_s epoch is saturated to AP-5 depth ~10⁷⁴. Two ways that could slow the bath:
1. **Serial depth.** If the layers had to be traversed in sequence, τ_eq would carry a factor ~10⁷⁴ and R ~ 10⁷⁰: the tilt would fail by seventy orders (T1). But AP-5 D1 activates every needed layer *for that Moment* and D2 makes each layer a copy of the PCD cycle *repositioned each Moment*: the hierarchy is parallel. Every layer completes its cycle every Moment; depth costs no time.
2. **The clip.** The between-GP occupation dynamics — the ZRP of 0772/0774 whose product-Poisson stationary state is the Gibbs ln n̄ — is the layer-1 ZBW hop of individual CPs. At the cap the local lapse is ½ (3634, R-CLOCK-RATE-IS-DISPLACEMENT), so a hop takes two Moments: the per-CP rate is ≥ ½. On the exact L = 3, N = 6 generator of 0772, halving the rate halves the spectral gap exactly (1.500 → 0.750, T2): τ_eq at most doubles, N_mix → ≤ 2 N_mix. Deeper layers reposition CPs *within* the cell (D2) and move nothing between GPs; they neither help nor hinder the count mixing.

## §2 Lag → tilt (T3)
Let μ relax to μ_eq = 3kT N_rem(t) with time constant τ while N_rem falls at rate H. The steady lag is δμ = 3kT Hτ = 3kT R, so H_eff ∝ μ ∝ (N_rem + R) and n_s − 1 = −2/(N_* + R): **Δn_s = 2R/N_*²**. Integrating the ODE and differentiating numerically reproduces the formula (R = 0.1: 6.12×10⁻⁵ vs 6.16×10⁻⁵). A lag makes the tilt *slightly less red*, never bluer than the budget allows.

## §3 The bound (T4)
R ≤ 2 N_mix H/E_Pl with N_mix ≤ 30 (the 0753 toy's upper value) and H ≤ 4.7×10¹³ GeV (r < 0.036): **R ≤ 2.3×10⁻⁴, Δn_s ≤ 1.4×10⁻⁷.** PRED-C-96's stated theory error 5×10⁻⁴ (0774, from the SSV coupling ~α) is exhausted only at R = 0.81, i.e. H ≥ 1.7×10¹⁷ GeV — Planckian inflation, doubly excluded at 0769 (tensor bound; the H-axiom growth ceiling). The withdrawn v0.1 claim ("O(α)-level corrections inside the theory error") is replaced by a bound three orders smaller and of a different origin (a lag, not a coupling).

## §4 Premises (for attack)
- **ZBW hops are per-CP and independent** (0772/0774: "each CP hops independently at the ZBW rate"; "PCD = per-CP cycle, elementary moves single-CP"). D2 lockstep (derived at 3701 for V_i = 0) governs the *SSV-driven* repositioning of co-located CPs that see one census; it is not a statement about the intrinsic ZBW cycle, whose phases are random (0738, the CLT source of Gaussianity). If saturation lockstepped the ZBW as well, the ZRP would freeze: the occupation distribution would be carried unchanged by D4 and would remain product-Poisson only if it started so. That alternative is not the corpus's reading; it is named here as the one premise the bound rests on.
- N_mix from the 0753 toy (10–30); the H-theorem fixes a finite gap, not the prefactor. A prefactor 10× worse still leaves Δn_s ~ 10⁻⁶.
- H at the n_s epoch is an observational bound, not a CPP derivation (the A_s/κ layer of OPEN-EU-1). A CPP H would only tighten this.
- The lag model is first-order relaxation; multi-mode relaxation with the same gap gives the same steady lag.
