## The derivation chain reproduced in the paper (the spine)

1. **The log = A1.** CPs are identityless (A1) ⇒ same-type CPs on a GP are occupation-number objects
   ⇒ Gibbs 1/n! ⇒ partition Z = z₁ⁿ/n! ⇒ μ(n) = kT ln n + const. (0749)
2. **Leg 1 — bath, derived to leading order.** Minimal-PCD/ZBW occupation dynamics = a symmetric
   constant-rate **zero-range process** (LEMMA-NS-ZRP-DERIVE, 0774): independence (per-CP PCD, only
   inter-CP coupling = SSV, O(α)), g(n)=n (A1 + Absolute-Moment universal clock), symmetric kernel
   p(i,j)=1/12 (vertex-transitive 600-cell + homogeneous inflation). ZRP independence+symmetry ⇒
   product-Poisson stationary measure for any g(n); g(n)=n ⇒ Poisson ⇒ μ ∝ ln ρ ⇒ **p=2**.
   **LEMMA-NS-HTHEOREM** (0772): detailed balance + KL-divergence Lyapunov ⇒ provable relaxation to
   that indistinguishable Gibbs state; the distinguishable/labelled cliff state (n_s=1) is NOT a
   stationary measure within the A1 occupation space.
3. **Dilution → e-folds.** n̄(N)=n̄_init e^{−3N} ⇒ ln n̄ = 3 N_rem (N_rem = e-folds remaining).
4. **Boost coupling.** H_eff = κ(μ(n̄)−μ(1)) ∝ ln n̄ ∝ N_rem (count-driven branch, 0746).
5. **δN spectrum.** spectator P ∝ H_eff² ⇒ n_s − 1 = 2 d ln H_eff/dN = −2/N_rem.
6. **N_\*** = (1/3) ln(N_CP/N_GP) ≈ 60.5 total; observable pivot N_\* ≈ 57 ⇒
   **n_s = 1 − 2/57 = 0.9649**, α_s = −2/57² ≈ −0.0006. (verified `scripts/0781_eu1_numerics.py`)
7. **Leg 2 — neutrality, grounded.** DP-Sea = bound ± pairs ⇒ n₊=n₋ exactly ⇒ Q=0 identically ⇒
   leading mean-field (∝Q²) cancels at all n ⇒ μ_excess flat ⇒ no tilt contamination. (0770)
8. **Long-range √n̄ Debye residual — CLOSED PASS** (LEMMA-NS-BATH, 0764–0768): SSV kernel Coulomb ⇒
   |μ_excess|/kT = c·Γ^{3/2}, Γ=α/κ; μ evaluated w.r.t. the ZBW/substrate bath (κ~1) ⇒ Γ~α ⇒
   residual ~ c·α^{3/2} ≈ 3.6×10⁻⁴ ≪ ln n̄ ≈ 170; fails only at strong coupling Γ~tens (cold plasma,
