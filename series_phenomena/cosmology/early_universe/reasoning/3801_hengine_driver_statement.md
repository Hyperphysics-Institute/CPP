I wanted a reason that does not lean on 0746's fork being accepted. So SSV_net = 0 and the acted-on displacement is zero before the clip touches it. I put this in as §2.2 and asked the panel whether it is VALID-BUT-UNNEEDED, because I can see the objection: fluctuations (the CLT-over-ZBW-phases seed, 0738) are precisely non-zero SSV_net at O(√n̄) and those ARE what the clip could act on.
