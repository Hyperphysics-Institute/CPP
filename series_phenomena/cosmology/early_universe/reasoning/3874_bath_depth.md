# Reasoning capture — Patch 3874: OPEN-EU-BATH-DEPTH-1 resolved

*Session 196, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verbatim at-patch capture. Finding: `eu1_derivation/bath_depth_resolved.md`. Verify 8/8.*

This was the last item the handovers had been carrying as a genuine candidate, and I took it rather than declaring the lane closed for a fourth time. D-1 first, and it half-paid: the mechanism is at 0769 §(b), which sets the test as the ratio of the equilibration time to the e-fold time and finds that with a mixing count of order ten to thirty the bath re-thermalizes a few thousand times per e-fold.

But 0769 established that for the generic case, and the item asks about depth ten to the seventy-four. That distinction is the whole content of the question, and I nearly missed it — my first instinct was that 0769 had already answered it and I could close the item the way I closed κ two sessions ago. It has not, because mixing times generally grow with system size and a stack of that depth is not a generic system. So the item was correctly scoped when it was registered, and my handovers had been right to keep it even while mis-describing what it owned.

The reduction is clean and the threshold is sharp: the ratio hits one at a mixing count of about fifty thousand, so the bath clause survives depth if and only if the count at that depth stays below that. Four candidate scalings split with no middle ground. Depth-independent and logarithmic both pass with margins of thousands and hundreds. Diffusive fails by thirty-two orders and serial by sixty-nine. There is no scaling that leaves the answer uncomfortable — either the margin is large or the bath clause is annihilated, and with it the Gibbs logarithm the tilt rests on.

The resolution comes from the protocol rather than from any estimate, which is why I am comfortable with it. AP-3's per-Moment synchrony has every grid point executing perceive, compute and displace every Moment, and every Conscious Point displacing every Moment. That makes the substrate massively parallel with one actor per Conscious Point. It cannot be serial — there is no queue and no single agent stepping through occupants — and it is not single-agent diffusive either, because every point acts simultaneously rather than one walker wandering the state space. Those are exactly the two failing scalings, and the protocol excludes both by construction rather than by assumption. Parallel mixing of n items by n actors is logarithmic, so the count goes as the logarithm of the depth, about a hundred and seventy, and the ratio comes out at three parts in a thousand — roughly three hundred re-thermalizations per e-fold.

A detail I liked enough to record: the depth penalty is exactly the logarithm of the occupancy, which is three times the remaining e-folds — the same logarithm that carries the count law. Going from the generic case to depth ten to the seventy-four costs a factor of about eight and a half in mixing time and nothing else.

On scope I was careful to claim no more for the sub-question than 0769 claimed for the clause it serves. The mixing count itself is still toy-measured rather than derived; what this patch establishes is its scaling, not its value. The scaling is argued from parallelism rather than simulated at depth, so what is excluded is serial and diffusive behaviour and what is asserted is that the protocol cannot exhibit either. A direct measurement at large occupancy would harden it and is the obvious follow-on, though at a margin of three hundred it is not urgent. Grounded, not proven — deliberately the same phrase 0769 used, because it would be inconsistent to grade a sub-question above its parent.

With this the lane's queue is empty in fact rather than merely in description. Every item the handovers have been carrying has now been either delivered, closed with a reason, or resolved.
