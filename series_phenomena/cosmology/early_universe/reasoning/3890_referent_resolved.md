# Reasoning capture — Patch 3890: the referent resolved

*Session 204, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Finding: `eu1_derivation/referent_resolved.md`. Verify 10/10.*

He asked what the question means and which way it comes out, and both halves turned out answerable from the corpus without needing a ruling from him, which is a better outcome than the one I set up last session.

Explaining it forced me to be precise about what the occupancy is. It has to be a configurational entropy per particle, because that is where the chemical potential's logarithm comes from — the log of how many ways the crowd can be arranged. So the only question is what counts as a member of the crowd: a Conscious Point, or an occupied site. And stating it that way makes the thing obvious that I had missed: the two readings differ only where points share a site. With no superposition they are literally the same number. That is a much narrower question than I described at 3888, where I called it a tension between two settled results.

The dynamical half is settled by his own words from August, which I had read three sessions ago in the ring registration and not connected: points respond only to the messengers that arrive at each site each Moment. Put beside the emission clause — same fixed number of messengers per site, regardless of occupants — it follows immediately that twelve points sharing a site deliver one site's worth of signal. A point cannot respond to what it does not perceive. So whatever drives the engine must be the perceived count, which is the occupancy. That is a consequence rather than a choice, and I said so, because the temptation with a result that helps a live candidate is to present a preference as a deduction.

Then the obvious worry, which is whether this wrecks the e-fold law. It does not, and the reason is a number I should have computed before calling anything a tension. Ten to the eighty-four points in a Planck sphere holding four times ten to the ninety-six sites is an occupancy of two times ten to the minus thirteen — the lattice is some thirteen orders emptier than one point per site, and the Poisson chance of any site holding two is around ten to the minus twenty-six. Points do not share sites by accident at that density. So occupied sites equal the point count generically, the two readings coincide, and the count law is untouched.

Which means there was never a contradiction in the corpus. There is one referent — occupancy — whose generic value happens to equal the point count because the lattice is nearly empty. My 3888 framing, that two settled results were in tension and one had to give, was wrong, and one sparsity calculation would have prevented it. That is the same failure as the invented dark-matter mass two sessions ago in a different costume: I reasoned about a quantity before computing it.

For the candidate the reading is favourable and I said so plainly, but the gate moves rather than opens, and that is the part worth carrying. Superposition is not generic. At that sparsity, points sharing sites needs a mechanism that forces them together against a lattice with overwhelming room. His picture posits such a reservoir and the corpus does not contain one. So the debt I had ranked third — a corpus home for the reservoir, with a capacity, an evaporation law and a depletion behaviour — is now the gating debt, ahead of the amplitude.

I finished by restating the amplitude requirement sharply, because that is where this will be decided and where the temptation to calibrate will arrive: the reservoir must hold enough points and release them at a rate tracking the engine to move the logarithm by the required amount. Nothing in this patch computes that, and PD-007 still forbids fitting it.
