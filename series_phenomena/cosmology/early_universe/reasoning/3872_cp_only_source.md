# Reasoning capture — Patch 3872: R-CP-ONLY-SOURCE enacted

*Session 195, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verbatim at-patch capture. Founder text: `founders_voice/founder_clarification_cp_is_the_only_source_2026-09-09.md`. Verify 8/8.*

The founder confirmed the reading and directed that it be stated in the axiom, which gives me the sanction I declined to assume last session. I had put the one-liner to him rather than editing a ratified text on my own authority, and that was the right call: he is the ratifying authority, and the difference between a worker rewording an axiom and a founder directing the rewording is the whole of the corpus's amendment discipline.

His statement is stronger than what I proposed, and the difference is worth having in the record. I argued that resident CP charges must enter the E sum alongside the arrivals, which is the minimum the reductio forces. He gives the principle underneath it: the CP is the only source for all signal magnitude and direction. On that reading the arrivals are not a second, independent source at all — they are prior CP contributions relayed onward. That is a cleaner statement and it explains why the omission was invisible for so long: if CPs are the only source of anything, saying that a grid point's own CPs source its emission feels like saying water is wet. I registered it as R-CP-ONLY-SOURCE.

The scoping took some care. This is a clarification and not an amendment, and the corpus has a precedent for exactly that in AP-4c, which was registered at 3032 as "clarification, not correction." The founder states the content was intended from the start and merely implied, so no axiom count moves, no result moves, and no re-ratification is needed. I checked that the registry's own count line still reads nine and left it alone.

I made the change in both places the payload clause appears — the glossary's AP-4 entry and the axiom registry's — because a clarification that lands in one and not the other recreates the ambiguity in the file a future worker happens to open. Both now carry the resident-source phrase, the R-CP-ONLY-SOURCE tag, the patch number, and a short statement of why: that the arrivals-only reading is excluded by reductio, since it makes the recursion homogeneous from a zero start and leaves the theory with no physics. Naming the excluded reading inside the text is deliberate. A clarification that only asserts the right reading can be re-misread; one that says which reading is wrong and why cannot.

The founder's stated reason for wanting it explicit is the operative one and I quoted it: the text might be mistaken or not recognised by another turn. That is not a hypothetical — it is what happened at 3870, to me, with this text. And it is the second time in this arc that something true-but-implicit had to be made explicit because a worker could not be relied on to reconstruct it: the first was the standing discipline going into the bootup file three patches ago, after I discovered my own governance notes were filed where nobody would read them. Both changes rest on the same principle, that what is implicit is not what gets read, and I noted the parallel in the enactment record.

What I did not do is touch anything else in AP-4. The static-snapshot clause, the three slots, the absence of an oscillator or per-messenger phase, the receiver-side reconstruction of SSV — all untouched. The temptation with a sanctioned edit to a ratified text is to tidy neighbouring wording while you are in there, and that is exactly how a clarification turns into an unratified amendment.
