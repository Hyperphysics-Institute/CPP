# Reasoning capture — Patch 3892: the reservoir mechanism

*Session 205, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Founder text: `founders_voice/founder_mechanism_superposition_reservoir_2026-09-09.md`. Finding: `eu1_derivation/reservoir_mechanism.md`. Verify 10/10.*

I asked what forces Conscious Points to stack, expecting a new mechanism, and the answer is that nothing does — they stack because the summation of their arriving messengers directs them to the same address, which is the ordinary displacement rule. Two points whose summations point the same way land the same place. For a candidate that needed a reservoir, getting one out of machinery already in the axioms is the cheapest outcome available, and I said so, because the alternative would have been a posited structure with no home.

The second half is what I had missed entirely. He says they start out with a large number of points on every grid point. The reservoir is not something to be introduced; it is the initial condition. That discharges the debt I had ranked as gating one session earlier.

And it overturns my own sparsity argument, which I should record plainly rather than fold into a footnote. At 3890 I computed the ignition occupancy as two parts in ten trillion per site and concluded superposition needed a forcing mechanism. That calculation assumed the points were spread across a Planck sphere's four times ten to the ninety-six sites. His initial condition has them stacked on far fewer. The number I computed is correct; what was wrong was treating it as a description of the starting state when it describes a hypothetical spread one that I supplied. That is the same error class as the invented dark-matter mass three sessions ago — assuming an unstated quantity instead of asking — and it is the second occurrence in four sessions, which I noted.

The substantive gain is that the candidate's perturbation is now derived rather than hoped for. Writing f for the fraction of points that have left superposition, the occupied-site count goes as f times the total, so the logarithm of the occupancy picks up a term in log f on top of the existing count law. Since the curvature perturbation is a third of the variation in that logarithm, the perturbation is a third of the variation in log f — the fluctuation in the local unstacking fraction. That is a rate fluctuation rather than a conserved density, which is exactly the sort of thing the no-go's premise does not cover, and it is now a definite quantity one can go and compute.

Two checks then came out favourably and I ran both before writing anything else, because a new term in the engine's logarithm is exactly the kind of thing that quietly breaks results already banked. The e-fold count survives exactly: inflation ends with everything unstacked, so the end volume is still the point count in Planck spheres and the total is still sixty-four and a half. The growth in occupied sites compensates the reduced starting occupancy precisely because f reaches one at the end. And the tilt survives exactly, for a reason I found satisfying: if the unstacking fraction's logarithm is linear in the remaining e-folds, the occupancy's logarithm stays linear too, and the slow-roll parameter depends only on that linearity and not at all on the coefficient. So the spectral index is unchanged and, more importantly, is independent of the stack number.

Which matters, because he explicitly says he does not remember the stack number. I carried it as a parameter and showed the three results independent of it rather than picking a value. That is the discipline from the dark-matter retraction actually operating instead of being written about, and I would rather the record show it working once than describe it again.

The last thing I did was write the warning before anyone gets attached to this. The structure that makes the candidate different — a rate rather than a density — does not by itself guarantee a red spectrum. If the unstacking is Poisson, the fluctuation in log f goes as the inverse root of the number of events per coherence volume, which is white, and the candidate meets exactly the wall that stopped the conserved count, composition, the edge and dark-matter clumping. I stated that as the next test and explicitly did not pre-judge it, and I pointed at the founder's own charge rule as the first place to look for the correlation that would save it, since a rule distinguishing outcomes is more promising than one treating all landings alike.
