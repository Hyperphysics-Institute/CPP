## The grounding move (decisive)
Before opining I grounded the one load-bearing premise: PSR at zero SSV. SR-1: PSR_eff = l_P/(1+k·ΔSSV),
ΔSSV≥0 ⇒ max PSR = l_P at zero SSV — FINITE (= one lattice step/Moment = c), NOT infinite. So Axiom H's
"infinite PSR" motivation is wrong, and more importantly its multiplicative boost asks PSR to exceed l_P, which
the SR-1 ceiling forbids (that ceiling IS the speed-of-light limit, lattice saturation).

## The deep point that organized the toy
de Sitter = constant H = distant comoving points receding faster than c (the horizon). On a FIXED lattice
(founders L33; 0731), all dynamics is CP motion through the scaffold, capped at c. So CP motion cannot produce
super-luminal comoving recession ⇒ cannot produce de Sitter. Inflation needs the metric to stretch; the lattice
doesn't. This is the SAME obstruction as 0729 (no constant-H source) and 0731 (no lattice-growth DOF).

## The toy (10/10)
Two variants, identical but for the ceiling. CAPPED (v_edge≤c): H clamps to c/(a q_max) and falls → linear,
~O(1) e-folds, no de Sitter. UNCAPPED (Axiom H literal): H=ln(1+ε) const → de Sitter, but recession ~12c
(super-luminal, violates SR-1). BOTH: e-folds = ln(1/s0) = ln(occupancy); 60 e-folds needs occupancy ~e^60
~1e26 CPs/GP — unphysical. So inflation appears only by overriding the c-ceiling, and even then the
saturation-dilution mechanism can't supply 60 e-folds.

## Why this is fair, not a dismissal
Axiom H is a proposed new primitive; an axiom CAN override SR-1. The finding is that the override required is
LARGE — it changes the speed-of-light law and the fixed-lattice foundation that the SR/SM zero-parameter
predictions rest on — and it adds a free parameter ε. So Axiom H is not a free CPP-native inflation; it is a
big structural modification with named costs. That's a real, constructive evaluation: it tells Thomas exactly
what an inflation primitive must overcome. I credited the proposal's correct shape and Copilot's correct catches
(esp. Gaussian-field vs Gaussian-over-pattern-types).

## The Gaussianity note (for the record, not yet a patch)
Thomas's "Gaussian over pattern-type frequencies" is a different object from cosmological Gaussianity (the field
amplitude PDF / Fourier-mode amplitudes being multivariate Gaussian). A multiplicative cascade — and Axiom H's
boost IS multiplicative — gives a heavy-tailed, high-kurtosis field (0730 toy: kurtosis ~1e2–1e3), non-Gaussian
regardless of how pattern-types are distributed. So the 0730 non-Gaussianity wall stands; the reframing does not
dissolve it. This is the prediction for any future spectrum toy built on Axiom-H + qCP bias.
