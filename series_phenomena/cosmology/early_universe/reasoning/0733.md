⇒ `l_P` = baseline PSR = a radius enclosing ~10^30 sub-Planck grid points. "One PSR/Moment" ≈ 10^30 GPs/Moment,
NOT one grid step. PSR is the max REACH per Moment, not the grid step.

1. The "super-c ceiling" objection is WITHDRAWN. Fast early expansion at one PSR/Moment with a large PSR is rapid

   baseline PSR — a Variable-Speed-of-Light (VSL) cosmology — which REOPENS the inflation question.
3. The 0732 toy survives only as a conditional: a fixed-baseline-PSR engine gives linear expansion; whether the
   baseline is fixed or epoch-dependent is the open question. Its "uncapped" run = the VSL regime, not "super-c."
4. The primordial SPECTRUM (Gaussianity + scale-invariance + adiabaticity) remains the deepest owed piece,
   UNCHANGED: VSL/large-early-c addresses the horizon problem, not the spectrum; the 0730 non-Gaussianity stands.
