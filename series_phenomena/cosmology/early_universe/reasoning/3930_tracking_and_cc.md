# Reasoning capture — Patch 3930: three questions, two of them corrections

*Session 224, 11 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verify `scripts/3930_tracking_and_cc.py` (10/10).*

The founder asked where the owed items are tracked, whether we had made progress on a particular numerical discrepancy, and whether we are still working the cosmological constant. Two of the three answers are corrections and the third is a repair.

On tracking: there is a to-do file at the repository root, it has a stated discipline attached to it, and this arc put nothing in it across fifty-five sessions. I only located it because he asked. Everything owed has been living in handovers, and handovers are superseded every session — an item survives there only by being hand-copied forward, which is not a register but a relay race. Nothing has visibly been lost, which is exactly why the failure went unnoticed: the mode is silent. I registered the whole owed list this patch, including the items owed to other lanes, the two recompiles, and the four things owed into the next paper version.

I also registered what is *not* owed, in the same entry, because an unfiled negative is how work gets re-opened. That matters for his third question.

On the numerical discrepancy: there isn't one. The figure came from a patch that assumed the horizon radius was the inverse expansion rate, derived a degeneracy from that, and concluded a coefficient had to equal three against a derived three-hundredths. That patch was retracted in full two sessions later, when I found the corpus specifies a future event horizon instead. There is no such coefficient and no such requirement.

The part worth recording is not that I was wrong — that was already on file — but that the number reached him anyway. I wrote the retraction, filed it, put a full-width banner on the retracted document, and summarised it. And the figure still travelled. Looking at what I actually wrote, I said the patch was retracted and explained why; I did not say the sentence that would have worked, which is that the figure three does not exist. A retraction that replaces a claim is weaker than one that names the specific number being withdrawn, because a reader carries numbers forward more readily than they carry the status of the patch that produced them.

The real residual, for the record, is the holographic coefficient: derived between roughly a quarter and a half from the fine-structure constant and lattice geometry, against an observational band of six to nine tenths. A factor between one and a fifth and three.

On whether we are still working the problem: no, and this lane should not be. Its two contributions to the cosmological-constant question were an error and a retracted patch. The net contribution is zero, less the cost of the corrections. What survives is that this lane now understands correctly what the other lane has, which is not the same as having contributed to it, and I was careful not to dress it up as a clarification of the framing. That lane owns the residual and has already fired its own falsifier on it. Saying we are working on it would be false, and saying we had made progress would be worse.
