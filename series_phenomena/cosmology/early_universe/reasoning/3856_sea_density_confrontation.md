# Reasoning capture — Patch 3856: the DE/EM excursion

*Session 187, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane with a founder-sanctioned read-only excursion into the DE and SF-6 material. Finding: `eu1_derivation/sea_density_confrontation.md`. Verify 8/8.*

Half the check evaporated before I started. The SF-6 side was supposed to supply an independent density through its electromagnetic constants, and the open problem attached to those constants is titled "First-principles electromagnetic constants without parameter tuning." Obtaining them untuned is the open work; the shipped values are tuned. So they cannot pin anything, and I should have known that before asking for the excursion, since it is recorded in the FP sector file and was noted when SF-6 shipped. That is a small instance of the pattern I wrote up two sessions ago about premises not being re-examined, and it applies to items I propose as much as to items I inherit.

The DE side does have a number, and a well-grounded one: the sea is calibrated at a spacing of 4.636 Planck lengths against a Λ-free Lithium observable, giving about one hundredth of a DP per cubic Planck length. Converting, that is around 2×10¹⁰² per cubic metre, against the EU requirement of 1.4×10¹⁷. Eighty-six orders apart.

A gap that large is usually an arithmetic error, so I checked it a second way that shares none of the first way's steps. EU-1's dilution law puts today's CP spacing at roughly a Planck length times the post-inflation expansion factor, about 7×10²⁸ Planck lengths, against the DE lane's 4.6. Twenty-eight orders in length, which cubes to eighty-five in density. The two routes agree, so the discrepancy is real rather than a slip.

Then the interesting part, which is that I cannot tell whether it is a contradiction. If the two lanes are counting the same population, this is an eighty-six-order cross-sector inconsistency, far larger than the ten-e-fold budget question that sent me looking. If they are counting different populations — the DE sea being a non-diluting paired vacuum sitting near the lattice scale, EU-1's occupancy being the diluting excess riding on top of it — then there is no contradiction at all, and also no pin, because the DE number then says nothing about the EU count.

The textual evidence favours the second reading. The DE lane holds its spacing fixed by construction and calibrates to a present-epoch nucleosynthesis observable rather than to a cosmological history, and a population that dilutes does not have a spacing held fixed. But favouring is not settling, and I stopped short of settling because the ambiguity is not mine to resolve: EU-1's own sentence says the universe expands by "dilution of DP-Sea occupancy," and the word occupancy is exactly ambiguous between the sea and the excess above it. That means the referent question is load-bearing for the paper's central mechanism and not merely for this check, which is a bigger finding than the check I was running.

So route 2 is inconclusive and the blocker is definitional rather than numerical. I registered the referent question and escalated the first reading to the maintainer rather than adjudicating it, because an eighty-six-order inconsistency between two lanes is above this lane's authority to settle and equally above its competence to wave away. I recommended it go to the panel with the amplitude package, since a cross-sector density contradiction and a cross-sector amplitude negative are the same species of question — whether the sectors are describing one substrate.

What that leaves for the budget is uncomfortable but clean. Route one closed three sessions ago; route two is now blocked on a definition. The ten and a half e-folds are not resolvable inside the EU lane as it is currently scoped, and I wrote that in those words rather than leaving the item open with an implied next step that does not exist.
