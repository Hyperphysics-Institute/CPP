# Reasoning capture — Patch 3850: T-2 delivered

*Session 184, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verbatim at-patch capture. Finding: `eu1_derivation/T2_result.md`. Verify 8/8.*

T-2 turned out to be a matter of putting two existing results next to each other, which nobody had done in the year they have both been on file.

0774 deforms the ideal zero-range rate to g(n) = n(1 + λ(n−1)) and says λ ~ Γ, the plasma coupling. 0766 derives Γ = α/κ with κ the bath temperature in Planck units — and derives it exactly rather than approximately, because the corpus fixes the grid spacing at the Planck length, so at kT = E_Pl the coupling is q²/(a kT) = q²/(ℏc), which is the fine-structure constant on the nose. Put together, λ = α/κ. The coefficient was never a free order-one multiple of α, and the 0.1α–10α bracket was never a bracket on two things: it is κ between 0.1 and 10, one parameter, and that parameter is the bath temperature.

That also means I got something wrong in the specification I wrote two patches ago. I noted there that α in this context is the SSV occupation-dependence parameter and explicitly not the fine-structure constant, and warned that the notation invites confusion. I was the one who was confused. It is the fine-structure constant, by the identity above. I corrected it in the finding rather than quietly fixing the spec, because a warning that points the wrong way is worse than no warning.

The bound came from asking what the bath actually is. LEMMA-NS-BATH's bath is the ZBW/substrate bath and its clock is the substrate clock, so its temperature cannot exceed the substrate scale: κ ≤ 1, hence λ ≥ α. That excludes the entire lower half of the bracket, and it is one-sided. The charter's expectation E-3 predicted a two-sided collapse to roughly [0.5α, 2α]; it was right about the magnitude and wrong about the shape, and I recorded that rather than letting a half-right prior pass as confirmed.

The part I did not expect, and which I think is the real content, is that the correction has a sign. η is positive, so the shift moves n_s up, and at the bath clause's own reading the shift is +5×10⁻⁴. EU-1 quotes that same number as a symmetric theory uncertainty. But a derived shift and an error bar are different objects: the paper says 0.9649 ± 0.0005 and the derivation says 0.9654. The agreement with Planck survives easily at 0.12σ, so nothing is in trouble — but the headline number changes, and this is the first result in this whole arc that touches the paper's quoted value rather than its scaffolding. I flagged it as owed to EU-1's next version and said explicitly that it should not be folded in silently.

Then the relation runs backwards, which I did not anticipate when I started. Since λ = α/κ, the observed tilt constrains κ: keeping the shift inside Planck's one-sigma needs λ below about ten α, so κ above about a tenth, so the substrate bath temperature above about a tenth of the Planck energy. That is not a fit and does not offend PD-007 — nothing is being tuned to match an observation; an observation is being used to bound a substrate parameter the theory independently claims is of order E_Pl. The corpus did not have that bound before, and the bath clause can now be checked against it.

The structural point is the one I would want a reader to take away. T-2's uncertainty is not independent of anything. It is κ, and κ is already a named conditionality leg of PRED-C-96 through the bath clause and its sharpening at 3806. So T-2 does not add a theory error to EU-1; it re-expresses one that was already being counted. The prediction's three framework legs stay three, and what changes is that an order-of-magnitude bracket becomes a function of a quantity the framework already tracks.

Worth noting where this leaves the charter. T-1 was delivered at 3820, T-3 closed negative at 3847, and T-2 is delivered here. All three of OPEN-EU-1's frozen targets are now discharged — two positively and one as a characterised gap — which is a natural stopping point for the arc rather than a place where more candidates should be sought.
