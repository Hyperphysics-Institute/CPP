# Reasoning capture — Patch 3835: the light-mode search and the no-go

*Session 177, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verbatim at-patch capture. Finding: `eu1_derivation/light_mode_no_go.md`. Verify 8/8.*

The founder said he had no ideas and asked me to think of something. That is a different kind of request from the picture questions, and it deserved a systematic answer rather than a guess dressed up as a proposal.

I started by pricing the requirement, because "light" had been used loosely for several patches. H is about 2×10⁻⁵ in reduced-Planck units, so a mode relaxing at the Planck rate sits at ω/H ≈ 5×10⁴. Light means five orders slower than one Moment, on a lattice where every microscopic process ticks at t_P. That immediately explains why the register spring failed by exactly that margin and why any other local relaxation would have too. Slowness at that ratio is never accidental; it has to be protected. And physics protects it in exactly three ways, which is what made a systematic search possible rather than an open-ended hunt.

Conservation was the first and it looked like a hit. A conserved density cannot relax locally — smoothing a long-wavelength excess means transporting the stuff across that wavelength — so ω ~ Dk², and with the only available diffusivity the crossover sits at about 230 Planck lengths, under half a percent of the horizon. Every horizon-scale mode of a conserved density is light, and light for a reason. Better still, the conserved held count is the one quantity the end condition actually reads, so it clears the wall that stopped δkT and composition. For a while this looked like the answer.

Goldstone modes were the second, and CPP does have an orientational order parameter to hang one on — the sea's director, and the antiferromagnetic order whose frustration I computed two sessions ago. A Goldstone is massless by symmetry, light exactly for k < H, freezing at horizon exit with the right amplitude and Gaussian for free. But it is a phase-like variable, so it modulates orientation rather than total density, which makes it generically isocurvature; and it does not change the count, so it hits the same wall δkT did. I registered it as conditional rather than either adopting it or dropping it.

The third route, a near-flat potential, has nowhere to live: the driver is κ₀ kT ln n̄, kT was already killed, and κ₀ fails for exactly the same reason kT did — it multiplies H, so it changes how fast inflation runs and not how long it runs. There is no fourth factor.

Then I went back to the conserved count, because it was the only survivor, and found the obstruction. A locally conserved density with causal correlations obeys integral constraints that force its power spectrum to vanish as k² or k⁴ at small k. That means n_s of 3 or 5 against an observed 0.965. The count is slow, collective, and reaches the end condition — and it is blue by a mile.

Putting that beside everything else produced the result I did not expect to be writing. The end condition is a fixed geometric threshold, so only the count enters δN; the count is conserved; conserved densities are blue. So no light mode of any kind can source a near-scale-invariant ζ in this structure. The three months of failures were not three months of bad luck in candidate selection. The two requirements — reaching the end condition, and having a red spectrum — have been mutually exclusive from the start, because the only variable that reaches the end condition is the one variable that cannot be red.

I want to note that this is the same integral constraint that excluded causal-defect models as the primary CMB source, and that it arrived here from CPP's own internals rather than by importing the defect literature. That independence is worth something.

The escape is narrow and specific, which is what makes it useful. The no-go depends on step 2 and only on step 2: that n̄_end is a fixed number. If the end condition instead depends on something non-conserved, that something enters δN and can be both light and red. And CPP already has exactly one such mechanism registered — PSR-EARLY-1's question of whether the engine reads the held count or the perceived count under a contracted PSR, where the PSR is set by local state and is not conserved. That was registered as a budget question and named at 3818 as an amplitude escape route, and left uncomputed both times. It is now the only structural escape from a no-go, so I promoted it and said plainly that it is the most consequential open item in the lane.

The honest form of the answer to the founder is that the thing fitting his criteria does not exist under the current end condition, and that I can show why rather than merely report another failure. That is a better answer than a fourth candidate would have been.
