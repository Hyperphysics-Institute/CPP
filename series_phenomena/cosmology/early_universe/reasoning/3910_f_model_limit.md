# Reasoning capture — Patch 3910: which limit holds

*Session 214, 11 Sep 2026, EU lane. Finding: `eu1_derivation/f_model_limit.md`. Verify 10/10.*

This was the last thing on the candidate's list that I could do without leaving the lane, and it closed faster than I expected because the quantity that decides it had already been derived for another purpose.

The caveat was real and I had flagged it at 3902 rather than burying it: the relation between the unstacking fraction and the affinity holds only in the limit where re-stacking dominates. In the opposite limit the fraction saturates at one regardless of affinity, the ratio between the two patch types goes to one, and the whole amplitude chain collapses. So which limit obtains is not a technicality; it decides the candidate.

What settles it is the fraction itself, and 3892 already fixed that. At the start one grid point is occupied per stack, so the fraction is the reciprocal of the stack number; and its logarithm runs linearly in the remaining e-folds. The pivot sits at fifty-seven out of sixty-four and a half, so the logarithm there is a little under nine tenths of the starting value, which puts the fraction at something between a millionth and ten to the minus twenty-two depending on the stack number. Either way, vanishingly small — so the system is deeply in the re-stacking-dominated regime at the pivot and the relation used at 3902 is the right one. And the conclusion holds for any large stack number, which means the validation does not depend on the quantity the founder does not remember. That is the fifth session in which that parameter has been carried rather than chosen.

I then checked where the crossover into the failing regime actually falls, because "the limit holds at the pivot" would be a thin result if the boundary were nearby. Setting the fraction to a half puts the transition at somewhere between one and three remaining e-folds. The observable modes leave the horizon at fifty to sixty. So the failing regime is confined to the very end of inflation, more than an order of magnitude away from anything we can see.

The thing I nearly mis-stated is that the system passes through both limits. My first reaction was to treat that as a problem, since one of the limits is fatal. It is not a problem, it is required: the count law only closes if the fraction reaches one at the end, which means the transition must happen. And the transition is what ends the unstacking. So the fatal regime is not a flaw lurking in the model but the mechanism's own terminus, arriving after the observable window has already been written.

What I was careful about in the finding is the difference between closing a caveat and closing a debt. This closes a caveat. The headline does not move: there is a source with the right spectrum, and the normalisation is not derivable as the framework stands. The two items still outstanding are both outside this lane — the particle-sector check that gates the adiabaticity pass, and the assignment that makes the normalisation axiom-gated. So the lane's own work on this candidate is finished, and I said that plainly rather than looking for an eighth thing to do, which is the habit the handovers have been warning about since the sector first emptied.
