# Reasoning capture — Patch 3829: the founder's PD-007 challenge computed

*Session 174, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verbatim at-patch capture. Founder text: `founders_voice/founder_picture_frustrated_patches_2026-09-09.md`. Finding: `eu1_derivation/frustration_patch_dynamics.md`. Verify 7/7.*

He handed this over correctly — a qualitative picture with a specific force rule, and the computation explicitly delegated. That is the division PD-007 describes, and it is the first time in this arc the delegation has been named by him rather than inferred by me.

The first decision was what lattice to compute on. The 600-cell has 120 vertices, which is enough to prove non-bipartiteness (3827) and far too few for bulk domain statistics. FCC is the three-dimensional lattice in the same frustration class — coordination twelve, triangulated, the textbook geometrically frustrated Ising antiferromagnet — so I used it and said in the finding that it is a proxy for local frustration physics and not the 600-cell itself. Bulk domain behaviour transfers; global topology does not; I claimed only the former.

Two of his three claims came out confirmed and the third came out confirmed-but-relocated, which is the interesting part.

Irregular patches: yes. The annealed state sits at 0.359 frustrated, just above the forced third, and all three stacking orientations appear in comparable proportion rather than one winning globally. His stated reason — that some edges must join like charges and some unlike, so no single arrangement is available — is the right reason. I also got an independent confirmation of 3827's triangle bound from a different direction: the ordered type-I state saturates it at exactly 0.33333, which the triangle counting had predicted without ever constructing a state.

His force rule is where the computation earned its keep. Implementing it literally, a perfect domain interior has net force identically zero — not small, exactly zero, and for the same symmetry reason that gave T-1 its result. The four in-plane repulsive bonds sum to zero and the eight out-of-plane attractive bonds sum to zero. So both effects he describes are real and both cancel where the pattern is locally regular. In the annealed state every unit of net force sits on wall sites: interiors at exactly zero, walls at 1.05, wall share 100.0%. His mechanism is right and its support is the wall set rather than the bond set. That also answers my own standing question in the affirmative and more sharply than I asked it — the walls are not merely what is still moving, they are the only thing that moves at all.

Then the part I did not want to find. C-3 owes a spectrum argument, and I tested it rather than deferring it, because a candidate registered with enthusiasm one patch earlier deserves its first real test immediately rather than after months of scaffolding. The wall field's structure factor is peaked at finite k by a factor of about twenty over the small-scale value. That is one characteristic length, which is what a domain pattern has, and it is not the near-power-law the observed spectrum requires. I was careful about what I did not establish: L = 24 is small and the wall fraction moves with annealing depth, so I claimed the peak's existence and explicitly declined to claim any coarsening exponent.

The harder thought came from asking what class C-3 has joined. Frustration walls are topological defects, and defect seeds carry two well-known problems: walls overclose unless they annihilate, and active incoherent seeds fail the CMB acoustic-peak structure, which is why defect models were excluded as the primary perturbation source. Setting that against CPP's own 3805 result — every start pairs into a neutral sea within fifteen Moments, before one e-fold — produces a clean dilemma. Either the walls persist long enough to seed, and inherit the defect exclusion, or they pair away almost immediately and seed nothing. I do not know which, and the honest thing is that C-3's prospects are materially worse now than when I registered it one patch ago, not better.

I said so in the finding in those words. Registering a candidate with enthusiasm and then quietly letting it decay is how a programme accumulates dead weight; saying "worse than it looked one patch ago" costs nothing now and saves a great deal later. And the cheapest next step is not a spectrum computation but the survival question, which decides whether C-3 is alive at all.
