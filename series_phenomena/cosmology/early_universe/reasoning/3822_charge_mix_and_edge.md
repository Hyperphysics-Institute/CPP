# Reasoning capture — Patch 3822: the founder's charge-mix and edge picture assessed

*Session 171, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verbatim at-patch capture. Founder text: `founders_voice/founder_picture_charge_mix_and_edge_2026-09-09.md`. Finding: `eu1_derivation/charge_mix_and_edge_assessment.md`. Verify 6/6.*

The founder answered Q1 with something other than what Q1 asked for. The question presumed a single wavering variable — name the thing that oscillates slowly and more strongly when the crowd is denser. He gave two symmetry-breaking *sources* instead. That is a legitimate answer to a badly-framed question, and I want to record that the framing was mine and was narrower than the physics.

Claim (1) first, because it touches work I did two patches ago. He is right that the species mixture cannot be made perfectly uniform — three interaction classes with different responses, and no assignment makes every environment equivalent. Checking that against T-1, I found I had been sloppy in a way that matters. S-IGNITION-ISOTROPIC part (b), the ℓ ≤ 5 isotropy, is purely geometric: it depends on the twelve icosahedral directions and not at all on what sits on them, so it survives his picture untouched. But part (a), homogeneity across grid points, I derived from "the initial condition assigns the same twelve-vertex configuration to every address" — and I never said that this presumes uniformity of *species*, not merely of geometry. The founder has now denied exactly that premise. So part (a) is conditional in a way I did not flag. That amendment is owed and I have written it into the assessment rather than leaving it for someone to find.

As a ζ source, though, claim (1) dies on the same three walls that killed the count at 3812, and I checked each rather than assuming the analogy. It is frozen at ignition, so it is not the light mode 3812 requires. It is white, so it gives n_s = 1 — the 0741 cliff. And decisively, it cannot move the end condition: under the re-grounding, inflation ends at one CP per Planck sphere, which is a count with no species index in it, so a patch with a different eCP/qCP ratio still ends at the same expansion factor. δN = 0. That is the 3818 wall again, and I note that the wall is now three-for-three — δkT, the count, and composition all die on it. The end condition being purely geometric is starting to look like the structural fact of this problem rather than an incidental feature.

Claim (2) is different and I think he is right to call it the most obvious seed. The interior cancels because every GP has twelve neighbours; the boundary layer has vacancy on one side, so the cancellation that 3820 proved exact through ℓ = 5 fails *precisely and only there*. That is not a perturbation of the cancellation, it is its absence, and the distinction matters. Two things make it the best candidate yet: it is coherent rather than stochastic, which is what ζ needs and what shot noise structurally cannot supply; and it is dynamical rather than configurational, so it clears the freeze objection that killed everything else.

I registered it as C-2 but did not adopt it, and I want the two problems stated rather than buried. One feature is not a spectrum — the edge supplies a characteristic scale, and ζ needs approximate scale invariance across fifty-seven e-folds. Whether inward reflections in an expanding medium can do that is a genuine question I cannot answer by inspection, and I am not going to guess it (PD-007). And the volume fraction of the boundary layer is ~10⁻²⁷, which I computed and then had to think about how to present, because it is the wrong measure — a coherent wave carries influence far past its source layer — but if I omit it someone will later either dismiss the edge on it or claim the edge without addressing propagation. So I recorded it with the reason it is the wrong measure attached.

Then the thing that actually mattered. An edge has a location, and I realised the corpus had never asked where it is today. That is a one-line question with a five-order-of-magnitude answer, and it belongs in its own patch (3823) because it turns out to be about the re-grounded count rather than about the founder's picture at all.
