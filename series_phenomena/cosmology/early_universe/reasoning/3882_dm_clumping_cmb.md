# Reasoning capture — Patch 3882: DM clumping as a CMB source

*Session 200, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Founder text: `founders_voice/founder_proposal_dm_clumping_cmb_2026-09-09.md`. Finding: `eu1_derivation/dm_clumping_cmb.md`. Verify 10/10.*

First I checked whether this was a re-run, because the standing instruction is not to open an eighth candidate and I did not want to violate it by accident. It is not a re-run: the seven closed at 3847 were the spring, the count, the bath temperature, composition, frustration, the edge and the Goldstone, and dark-matter clumping is none of them. The instruction is against manufacturing candidates, not against assessing one the founder raises, and this one he raised.

It is also the best-aimed proposal of the whole arc, which I wanted to say before saying it fails. In the standard cosmology the microwave-background anisotropy genuinely is dominated by dark-matter potential wells through the Sachs-Wolfe effect. So dark-matter clumping is not a speculative link to the background; it is precisely what the background displays. The question is only whether it makes the pattern or carries it, and starting from something that demonstrably carries it is a better position than any previous candidate occupied.

The amplitude then did something no other candidate has managed, and I checked it twice because I did not expect it. The framework's dark matter is charge-neutral aggregates, and aggregation from a random start is Poisson-seeded. Poisson noise is large when the objects are massive and therefore few, and at around a million solar masses per aggregate the contrast at a hundred megaparsecs comes out at five parts in a million against a required ten. Within a factor of two. Every other candidate in this arc missed the amplitude by forty orders or more.

But it is a fit and I said so plainly, because an amplitude that lands only when you choose the free parameter that makes it land is exactly what PD-007 exists to refuse. The aggregate mass is unconstrained, and three decades of mass give three decades of contrast; I tabulated that rather than quoting only the flattering row.

The exclusions are three and they are independent, which is what makes the verdict comfortable despite the amplitude near-miss. Poisson seeding is white, and a white primordial spectrum in the cold component is the isocurvature model, long excluded and bounded by Planck to a few percent — the same bound that has already killed compositional disorder and the chain picture. Clumping that develops after inflation is an active, incoherent source, and active sources do not reproduce the acoustic-peak series, which is why defect models were ruled out and why frustration was demoted. And most simply, aggregation is a growth process: it amplifies a seed and cannot supply the seed it starts from, while the background requires the contrast already present and coherent on super-horizon scales at recombination.

So the verdict is the arc's familiar one — it relocates the question rather than answering it — but reached from a stronger start and failing later in the argument than the fractal cascade did.

What I think is genuinely worth carrying forward is the amplitude lesson, and I recorded it as a lesson from a failed candidate rather than burying it in the verdict. Massiveness buys noise. Every candidate that failed on amplitude in this arc failed because it was made of enormously many enormously light things, and the Poisson floor scales as the inverse root of the count. If anyone ever proposes another primordial-contrast mechanism here, the shape to look for is few and heavy rather than many and light. That is a real piece of information extracted from a negative result.

One scoping point I made sure of: this does not touch the 3835 no-go. That result concerns what can enter the e-fold difference while inflation ends at a fixed geometric threshold, and dark-matter clumping is a post-inflation phenomenon which never enters the e-fold count at all. It fails on microwave-background grounds rather than on the no-go's grounds, and the two should not be conflated.
