# Reasoning capture — Patch 3837: PSR-EARLY-1 worked, and the escape closes

*Session 178, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verbatim at-patch capture. Finding: `eu1_derivation/psr_early_dichotomy.md`. Verify 8/8.*

Two things needed clearing before spending a session on this, and I did them first because either could have made the work pointless.

The first was whether the perceived reading contradicts CONV-045, which was ratified five to nothing. It does not, and the check took one grep. S-HENGINE-HELD settles held versus *acted-on* — the engine reads the D4-conserved stack rather than the D1-clipped displacement — and its own text says the cap is "silent on n̄." Held versus perceived was never adjudicated. Nobody had checked that in the two prior patches where this route was named, and it would have been embarrassing to build on a route that a ratified statement had already closed.

The second was the PSR law's functional form. Its series 1 − ε + ε²/2 is the expansion of e^{−ε}, which I verified numerically rather than asserting from the pattern. So the ratified law is the exponential, as its name says, and the feedback below has a fixed form rather than a guessed one.

Then the physics. SSV_abs is count-like and the count is what arrives within the reach, so the perceived count and the reach determine each other, and the self-consistency n = n_ref e^{−3λn} has a solution that saturates logarithmically. At the founder's 10⁸⁴ with λ of order one, a point perceives a crowd of about sixty-three. That number stopped me, because ln n_ref is 3N_rem, so the perceived crowd is N_rem/λ — it tracks the remaining e-folds. That relation is the hinge of everything that follows and I think it is worth keeping regardless of the verdict.

Branch B1 does what 3835 asked. The perceived count depends on ε, hence on local state, hence is not conserved, so the end condition becomes dynamical and the no-go breaks. For about ten minutes this looked like the answer.

Then the tilt. Putting ln n̄_perc ≈ ln(N_rem/λ) into the driver adds a logarithm, so the tilt becomes −2/(N_rem ln(N_rem/λ)) instead of −2/N_rem. Matching the observed value needs λ ≈ 21, and at λ = 21 the total is 43.5 e-folds while the pivot is 57 — the pivot falls outside its own window, which is incoherent. Keeping the pivot inside caps λ at 7.5, and across that whole range the tilt is off by four sigma or more. There is no value that buys both. B1 is excluded, and it is excluded by the corpus's own confirmed prediction rather than by anything external.

Branch B2 is the other reading of the same physics: ε saturates at the D1 cap, so the PSR sits at a floor. Then the volume factor is constant again, the perceived count is proportional to density, and it is conserved after all. The tilt survives and the no-go stands verbatim. B2 preserves the prediction by supplying no escape.

At that point I went looking for what was common to both failures, because a clean dichotomy usually has something underneath it. It does, and it is the result of the session. The tilt −2/N_rem follows from H ∝ ln n̄ only if ln n̄ is exactly linear in N_rem, and linearity requires n̄ ∝ ρ with a constant volume factor — a conserved count in a fixed volume. 3835 shows a conserved count is blue. So PRED-C-96's tilt formula itself requires the conservation that forbids a viable amplitude. Any responsiveness that makes n̄ non-conserved, of any functional form and not just my λ model, bends ln n̄ away from linearity and moves the tilt. The tilt and the amplitude are not two open problems; the first causes the second.

That is uncomfortable and I wrote it plainly rather than softening it, because it is the kind of statement a programme needs early rather than after another six months of candidates. I was careful to state its conditions — it assumes the engine's form and assumes ζ is δN sourced by the count — and to mark it as the load-bearing result while §3 and §4 carry my ε model.

The escape falls out of naming the assumption. The conflict exists because the same quantity drives expansion and carries ζ. Standard inflation does not require that, and EU-1's own text already invokes the spectator prescription without supplying a spectator. So the surviving route is an independent light field, not the count, that modulates when inflation ends: the count keeps ln n̄ linear so the tilt is untouched, and the spectator supplies a non-conserved δN through the end condition. That is structurally modulated reheating, and I said so, because naming the standard mechanism it corresponds to is more useful than inventing a CPP-specific name for it.

C-4, the orientational Goldstone I registered two patches ago as conditional, is exactly the right shape for this and I promoted it accordingly. It is light by symmetry, freezes at horizon exit, is Gaussian, and is not the count — so it cannot disturb the tilt, which was the objection that killed everything else. Its isocurvature problem is also softened in this role rather than by hand-waving: a modulated-end mechanism shifts when everything ends, not one species' abundance, so it produces adiabatic perturbations. What it owes is now a single specific thing rather than a list: a coupling by which the sea's orientation shifts the end condition. I computed nothing on it.
