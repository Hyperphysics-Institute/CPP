# Reasoning capture — Patch 3866: κ's status, and §0.5's first use

*Session 192, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verbatim at-patch capture. Finding: `eu1_derivation/kappa_status.md`. Verify 8/8.*

This was the first session opened with the standing discipline section in place, and I used it deliberately rather than proceeding from the handover's description of the work. Both rules I applied fired, which is worth recording because the six entries in that section are all failure stories and none of them is yet a success story.

D-3 says locate the object before building on it. The handover I wrote last session said OPEN-EU-BATH-DEPTH-1 was the last substantial local item and that it owned κ. One grep on the item's name shows it is registered as "bath speed at depth ~10⁷⁴," a rate question, Priority 3, explicitly non-blocking, from the CONV-045 ratification. It has nothing to do with the bath temperature. My handover had propagated a description I invented rather than one the registry carries, which is exactly the failure mode §0.5 exists to prevent, and I had committed it one session after writing the rule.

D-1 says search for the mechanism before reasoning forward. Grepping for the bath temperature turned up a file in this very lane — the bath-temperature lemma, Patch 0767 — which states that the chemical potential is evaluated with respect to the ZBW substrate bath at the substrate scale, hence κ ~ 1, and which grounds that as a corollary of the bath clause rather than an independent postulate. So κ was settled a hundred patches ago and I had been about to derive it.

The lemma also states its own limit, and that limit is what makes this patch short. It carries no theorem status because it rests on the bath clause, which is a working postulate. Deriving κ exactly therefore means deriving the bath clause — establishing that the substrate dynamics thermalize the occupations, and at what precise scale. That is foundational work, not EU-lane work, and it is the same postulate the whole spectral-index result already rests on. There is no local κ derivation to do, and the handover's last substantial item does not exist as described.

Having established that, I looked for whether anything was actually new, because a session that only corrects its predecessor is thin. One thing is. Two lower bounds on κ exist in the corpus for two different requirements, and nobody had set them next to each other. The lemma's bound, from requiring the long-range residual to be negligible, is a tenth of a thousandth. T-2's bound, from requiring the order-α tilt shift to stay inside Planck's one-sigma band, is a tenth — three orders tighter. Combined with the upper bound of unity from the substrate clock, the corpus's tightest window on κ is between a tenth and one, and it comes from the tilt rather than from the residual. I derived that bound myself at 3850 and did not notice at the time that it was the binding constraint on a framework parameter; it is recorded now, and it gives any future work on the bath clause a target window an order of magnitude wide, supplied by an observation.

What I then had to decide was whether to manufacture a next item. The κ item has dissolved, the charter is discharged, the amplitude is closed as a characterised negative, the budget is closed in-lane with its remaining route belonging to another lane, and the occupancy basis was restored four patches ago. What is left is maintainer decisions, a founder picture question, a small paper clarification and two recompiles owed to Isak. None of that is worker work. I wrote that the lane's substantive work is complete rather than inventing an eighth thing, because after seven closed candidates and two closed budget routes the temptation to keep producing is exactly the thing that would waste the next session. Saying a lane is finished is a result too, and it is more useful than a manufactured item.
