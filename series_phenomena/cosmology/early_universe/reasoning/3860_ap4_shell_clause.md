# Reasoning capture — Patch 3860: AP-4c's shell clause

*Session 189, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verbatim at-patch capture. Finding: `eu1_derivation/ap4_shell_clause.md`. Verify 10/10.*

The clause has two halves and I worked them separately, which turned out to matter because they come out opposite ways.

The first half derives, and pleasingly. Every GP emits a fixed number of DI-bits per Moment. If a bit were tallied by each GP it passed through, the total deposits would scale with the path length measured in grid spacings, and a path of order the Planck sphere crosses about 10³² of them at the spacing the e-fold budget demands. A fixed emission would then produce a deposit total inflated by that factor with nothing conserved to account for it. So exactly-once delivery is forced by the emission budget rather than stipulated, and "pass-through tally excluded" is an entailment of the fixed-emission rule. I had expected to find this a convention and it is not. The shell as the deposit locus then gives inverse-square for nothing: a fixed emission spread over a sphere of radius PSR arrives at areal density proportional to one over PSR squared, with no force law assumed anywhere. That is a lot of physics out of counting.

The second half is where it goes wrong, and the reason is internal to the protocol rather than imported. For separations shorter than the reach there is no direct deposit at all — the bit passes through and lands on the shell — and AP-4c assigns that case to the relay recursion, meaning successive re-emission by intervening points. That is fine in a running universe. It fails at the first Moment because a point can only relay what it has already received: AP-3 has it compute its registers from the previous Moment's arrivals and imprint its outgoing bits from those registers. At the first Moment there are no previous arrivals.

What makes this sharp rather than arguable is that the empty registers are not an extra assumption I brought in. They are 3816's own premise — the very thing that puts SSV_abs at zero and the reach at its ceiling, which is how Branch P got full contact in the first place. The premise that opens the reach also empties the relay. And the shell lies outside the ball under the small-ball ruling, so the direct channel deposits beyond the pre-ignition region entirely, on points that hold no CPs. Nothing direct, nothing relayed, zero.

I looked for the escape before writing the conclusion, because a negative that overturns the base of an arc deserves that. A sub-Moment relay would do it, letting intervening points re-emit within the first Moment so that influence works inward before the Moment closes. The corpus carries a founder ruling that ZBW is not sub-Moment, and there is no other sub-Moment tier to carry it. The escape is closed on the founder's own ruling rather than on my judgement, which is the right way for it to be closed.

So the consequence chain runs: no Moment-1 full contact, so n̄_init is not N_CP, so Branch P's re-grounding loses its basis, so the count law's absolute normalisation is unsupported as a derivation. That is my own 3816 and the arc has rested on it since. I wrote the chain out rather than softening it, and I also wrote the ordering out, because the ordering is the lesson: 3816 §6 flagged this exact check as load-bearing and did not perform it; 3820 §5 re-pointed it and did not perform it; 3841 §3 used the same clause to fix the amplitude's interaction range and did not perform it. Three patches leaned on a clause that does not carry the weight at the one Moment that mattered, and it took a founder instruction to get it worked.

What I was careful about is scope, because a result like this invites over-reading. The tilt reads the adopted pivot, not the count law's normalisation, and has never depended on n̄_init — so PRED-C-96 does not move, and I said so twice. T-1's isotropy is a fact about twelve directions and is indifferent to what anyone received. T-2 is a rate coefficient. The amplitude closure turns on the form of the end condition rather than the value of the initial count. What is hit is precisely the absolute normalisation of the e-fold total, and precisely the patches that used it.

The honest repair is one of two things and I did not choose between them: either some mechanism the corpus has not stated delivers the Moment-1 count, or Branch P's n̄_init = N_CP is a posit rather than a derivation and should be relabelled, with 3816's "derived, not assumed" language withdrawn. That is a founder question and I framed it as narrowly as I could — whether anything arrives at a point in the first Moment, or whether the first Moment only sends.
