The founder answered the generator question with three cross-lane pointers, and my first move was to go and read the DE and DM lane material rather than assess from memory of it. That turned out to matter more than the assessment did.

The pointers themselves have been examined before. There is a file sitting in the EU lane — `cosmic_web_generation_constraints.md`, Session 153, written 1–2 June — that took exactly this set: qCP chains stretched by inflation, the fractal chain-of-chains recursion, viscosity-differential infall. Its organising distinction is generation versus processing, and it is the right distinction and it survives. The founder's mechanisms are strong as processing and unestablished as generation, and two constraints still bite them without reference to anything else I found. Both his pointers are species-differential, which generates the isocurvature mode Planck bounds to a few percent. And the fractal cascade was actually computed at 0730: it is scale-free, so the morphology intuition is vindicated, but it is non-Gaussian by two to three orders in excess kurtosis, which is the signature of processed matter rather than primordial seeds. Beyond both of those, the dark-energy conversion is a late-time process and simply is not a during-inflation generator on any reading.

So the answer to his specific proposal is no. But following the pointer back is what earned the session, because 0730 names an upstream blocker and that blocker has expired.

It says CPP has no near-constant-H phase, cites Patch 0729, and concludes that any during-inflation mechanism presupposes a freezing geometry the programme has not shown it possesses. I checked the dates because the claim felt out of step with EU-1. 0730 was committed on 2 June. EU-1's n_s promotion is 0778, on 6 June. Four days. The whole content of 0778 is a quasi-de Sitter inflationary phase, and nobody went back to update 0730, so the corpus has carried "generation is walled off" for three months on a citation that expired the same week it was written.

I then checked whether 0729 actually says what 0730 uses it to say, because a stale date is a weaker finding than a misused scope and I wanted to know which this was. It is both. 0729 asked whether the GP-exclusion and CP-GP packing dynamics admit a quasi-de Sitter window and answered no, correctly: a coherent oscillation in a power-law potential gives w between 0 and a third, firmly decelerating. That verdict stands for the fluid it examined. But EU-1's engine is not that fluid — it is the count-driven boost of 0746 fork (i), and 0729 never tested it. So 0729 is intact in its own scope, and it is 0730's use of it as a universal blocker that fails. I was careful to write it that way rather than as "0729 was wrong," because it was not.

Then the arithmetic, which I did not expect to be so clean. EU-1's engine has H proportional to the remaining e-folds, so epsilon is one over N_rem, which is 0.0175 at the pivot. That is slow roll by any standard, w is −0.988, and the phase accelerates across the whole observable window. And the check that convinced me: the standard quasi-de Sitter tilt of such a background is minus two epsilon, which is minus two over N_rem — PRED-C-96 exactly. The programme's own confirmed prediction is the tilt of a quasi-de Sitter phase. It has had the freezing geometry since June while recording elsewhere that it lacked it.

What that changes is the whole shape of the amplitude search, and I want the next worker to see it stated flatly. The generator mechanism is available and it is the ordinary one: a light field in a quasi-de Sitter background freezes at horizon exit with amplitude of order H over two pi per mode, giving equal power per log interval to order epsilon — near-scale-invariant, Gaussian because it is a free field, adiabatic if it is the field driving expansion. Every property the amplitude problem has been failing to supply is supplied by the background CPP already owns.

What it does not change is that we have no field. And that is the useful part, because it explains three months of failures as one mistake rather than five. Every candidate has been an initial-slice pattern that carries the spectrum it was born with, or heavy, or unable to reach the end condition. Not one has been a light field in a quasi-de Sitter background — because the corpus had written down that the background was incapable. The search was in the wrong class, and it was in the wrong class for a documented reason that had stopped being true.
