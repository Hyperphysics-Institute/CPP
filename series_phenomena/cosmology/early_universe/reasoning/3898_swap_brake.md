# Reasoning capture — Patch 3898: the swap brake, and the order I found it in

*Session 208, 11 Sep 2026, EU lane. Finding: `eu1_derivation/swap_brake.md`. Verify 10/10.*

I have to write the uncomfortable part first, because leaving it to the end would be a way of hiding it. **I noticed that 138 is close to 137 before I had any reason for it.** The target number from last session sat next to the inverse fine-structure constant and I saw the resemblance, and only then went looking for a mechanism that would produce α. That is the wrong order, it is the order that produces numerology, and I have put it in the finding as well as here.

What makes me willing to report the result anyway is that the derivation stands on its own once written down, and I checked that by asking whether I would have reached α without the target in view. The answer is yes, and for a dull reason: pair-swapping between electric-type points is an electromagnetic process, and electromagnetic transition rates carry the electromagnetic coupling. Anyone asked for the per-Moment probability of an electromagnetic transition in Planck units would answer α without hesitation. The inference is not clever and it is not tailored.

The rest is protocol. The build rate is not an estimate: the hop cascade reaches the perceptual shell within one Moment, established two weeks ago for an entirely different purpose, so correlation extends one perceptual radius per Moment. Ballistic growth against a constant decay rate saturates where the two balance — correlation reaches a distance in that many Moments and survives with exponentially decaying probability — so the correlation length is the inverse of the decay rate. One over α. A hundred and thirty-seven perceptual radii.

Against last session's requirement of a hundred and thirty-eight, that is one percent. And I spent most of the session making sure the finding does not let that one percent stand unqualified, because it is not real precision. The requirement carries the per-patch fluctuation as an order-unity quantity rather than exactly one, and the required length goes as that to the minus two-thirds, so for a per-patch value anywhere between a half and two the requirement runs from about two hundred and twenty down to about eighty-seven. The derived value sits inside that band. Consistent. Not confirmed. Those are different words and the difference is the whole of what this patch can honestly claim.

I wrote the table out rather than describing it, because a band stated in prose gets remembered as its central value, and in two sessions someone — probably me — will be tempted to quote the one percent.

What would convert this is a single quantity: compute the per-patch fluctuation from the dynamics rather than calling it order unity. If it comes out one for a reason — from the affinity strength and the spread of compositions across sites — the band collapses and the amplitude is derived rather than accommodated. If it comes out a third or three, the brake is merely compatible, which is worth something but is not a result. So the candidate's gate is now one number again, and a smaller one than it has had at any previous point.

The thing I keep returning to is that two of the three inputs are protocol facts I did not choose — the reach per Moment and the Moment cadence — and only the decay rate is an inference. That is a better ratio than any previous candidate has managed, and it is the reason I think this is worth carrying forward despite the order in which I stumbled onto it. But I would rather the record overstate my carelessness than overstate the result.
