# Reasoning capture — Patch 3922: the fourth substituted definition

*Session 220, 11 Sep 2026, EU lane. Finding: `eu1_derivation/degeneracy_retracted.md`. Verify 10/10.*

I was about to take last session's tracking result forward into an observational confrontation when the label stopped me. The coefficient in the cosmological-constant lane's assembly is written c_Li. That is not a neutral symbol — it is the standard name for the parameter in Li's holographic dark energy, where the density goes as the inverse square of the future event horizon. If that is what the lane means, then the horizon radius in their formula is the event horizon and not the inverse Hubble rate, and my entire previous patch collapses.

So I looked, which I should have done a session earlier, and the corpus says it plainly: the scale is a Li-analog, explicitly not a retarded scale, with the particle-horizon reading ruled out by a check that passed back at 0723. The event horizon is not the inverse Hubble rate. There is no cancellation. Everything I built last session — the degeneracy, the requirement that a coefficient equal three, the claim that the vacuum problem reduces to one pure number on that basis, the tracking behaviour, and the re-posed exit resting on all of it — falls with the premise. It is not a partial correction; the patch does not survive.

What made me willing to write the retraction quickly rather than looking for something salvageable is that the correct picture is better than the one I was giving up. Holographic dark energy with the event horizon accelerates, and its equation of state evolves, which is why that lane computed a present-day value at all — something I had treated as incidental. And the coefficient is derived: a little under a half times the root of a bounded cycle average, which over the admissible range gives roughly a quarter to a half, against an observational band of six tenths to nine tenths. That is a miss of between one and a fifth and three in the coefficient, or one and a half to ten in the density — and the density figure reproduces the lane's own published miss exactly, which is how I know the reading is right this time rather than merely different.

So the framework has a first-principles prediction of a dark-energy observable, from the fine-structure constant and lattice coordination alone, landing within about a factor of two of the measured band. That is a considerably stronger position than the degenerate identity I described last session, and the retraction improves the standing rather than damaging it.

The part I want on the record most is the pattern, because it is now unmistakable and it is mine. Four times in this arc I have substituted a plausible definition for a term the corpus defines: an invented aggregate mass where a registered ring mass existed; an assumed spatial arrangement where the founder had stated a stacked one; the bare inventory where a derived gravitating density existed; and now the inverse Hubble rate where an event horizon was specified. Three of the four are in the last twenty patches.

And the discipline rule I already have does not catch this. D-1 says search before declaring a protocol failure, and I have been searching topics. The failure here is narrower and more mechanical: a formula was borrowed across a lane boundary and its symbols were not resolved against that lane's definitions. Definitions do not travel with a formula by default. So the rule I want recorded is to resolve every symbol in a borrowed formula against the lane that wrote it, before using the formula for anything — which is a cheaper and more specific check than searching a topic, and would have caught all four.

The containment is worth stating too. The retraction of the eighty-three orders two sessions ago stands on its own evidence and is unaffected. Nothing in the boost-coupling ruling, the panel's amendment, the tilt, or the candidate's ledger depended on last session's patch. The damage is one patch wide.
