# Reasoning capture — Patch 3870: the E slot must be resident-sourced

*Session 194, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane. Verbatim at-patch capture. Finding: `eu1_derivation/e_slot_source.md`. Verify 10/10.*

The lane had no queued item, so I took the residual I had left open two patches earlier rather than inventing something. It turned out to be worth more than a residual.

The observation at 3862 was that AP-4 defines the payload's directional slot as the vector sum of polar-charge contributions integrated by the origin grid point in the previous Moment, and that at the first Moment nothing has been integrated, so the first bits would carry a count but no direction. I had recorded that as an observation bearing on ignition dynamics and moved on.

I applied D-1 before reasoning, and the search came back empty. Nowhere in the glossary, the axiom registry or the founders' voice does anything say that a grid point's own resident Conscious Points source the field it imprints. Every appearance of the E slot words it as previous-Moment integration. I reported that as an absence rather than filling it in, which is the rule's whole point.

Then the reductio, which is short and I think airtight. If E is built only from arrivals, the recursion is linear and homogeneous — each point's E is a sum of its neighbours' previous E. The initial condition is the one the occupancy work established and the retraction confirmed: every register empty. A linear homogeneous recursion started at zero stays at zero, whatever the weights, however many neighbours. So under the strict reading E vanishes at every point for all time.

What makes that decisive rather than merely awkward is the consequence. It is not that ignition fails to start; it is that SSV_net is identically zero forever, so no Conscious Point ever displaces, and nothing in the theory happens at all. The recursion never leaves its fixed point. A reading that yields no physics is excluded, and since the two readings exhaust the possibilities, the other one is forced: the resident charges are a source term, the recursion is inhomogeneous, and E is nonzero from the first Moment.

I want to be clear with myself that this is a derivation of the reading rather than a preference between glosses. It has the same shape as the surviving half of the shell-clause work, where exactly-once deposit turned out to be forced by emission-budget conservation rather than stipulated — a consistency requirement doing the work a postulate would otherwise have to do. Those are the satisfying results in this programme, and there have now been two.

It also discharges the residual completely and in the better direction. First-Moment bits carry count and direction both: the count from the unconditional fixed emission, the direction from the resident points. My earlier note that they carry count without direction was true only under the reading now excluded, and I withdrew it.

A confirming detail I noticed afterwards: the founder's ignition picture needs this. Twelve Conscious Points per grid point each stepping to an icosahedral vertex requires a directional SSV_net at the first Moment, which under the strict reading could never exist. So the picture presupposed the resident-source reading all along, which is further evidence about what the programme has always meant even though nothing states it.

What is owed is small and is the founder's to decide: one line in the E-slot definition saying that the origin point's own resident charges enter the sum alongside the previous Moment's arrivals. Not an amendment, no axiom count change, no result moved — but a ratified text should not admit a reading under which the theory has no physics, and this one does.

On scope I made two admissions. The reductio depends on the recursion being linear in the arrivals, which AP-4's "vector sum" gives; a threshold or non-linear element would require redoing it, though nothing suggests one exists. And I did not find Reading R stated anywhere, so I am claiming that the text admits an excluded reading, not that anyone believed it.
