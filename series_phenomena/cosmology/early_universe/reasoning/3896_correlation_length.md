# Reasoning capture — Patch 3896: C-5's correlation length

*Session 207, 11 Sep 2026, EU lane. Finding: `eu1_derivation/correlation_length.md`. Verify 10/10.*

The bare range turned out not to need estimating, which I had expected to be the hard part. The affinity between stacks is mediated by arriving messengers — an SCP's pull on another is its net steering vector, built from what reaches it — and the messenger protocol gives that a hard reach: deposit at the perceptual shell, near field by the hop cascade inside it. So the bare correlation length is one perceptual radius, as a protocol fact rather than a modelling choice. That is the kind of input this programme is good at supplying and I should have looked for it before reaching for an estimate.

Gaussianity then cleared by a margin I did not anticipate. The excess kurtosis of a sum of independent patches falls as the patch value over their number, and at the bare range a Hubble volume holds ten to the fourteen of them, so even a cascade-like patch kurtosis of a thousand lands at seven parts in ten to the twelve at mode scales. Planck's bound is order five. Eleven orders of margin.

I want the significance of that stated properly rather than as a passing pass. This is the first time in the whole arc that a candidate has cleared a test which killed a predecessor. Every previous result has been a failure, or a failure by a smaller margin than the last. The fractal cascade died on non-Gaussianity; C-5 does not, and the reason is structural rather than lucky — a cascade at observable scales is one correlated object, while this affinity is microscopic and gets averaged over enormously many independent patches before anything observable is formed.

The amplitude at the bare range then fails, and I made myself say so plainly before saying by how little. Reaching the observed curvature amplitude would need the log-fraction to fluctuate by sixteen hundred within a single correlation volume, and such a fluctuation cannot plausibly exceed order one. So the candidate is short by about three orders. That is still a failure. It is also the first three-order failure in a sequence of forty-order ones, and the difference is large enough to be worth recording rather than filing under the same heading.

Inverting the relation gave the session's real content. With a per-patch fluctuation of order one, the curvature amplitude goes as the three-halves power of the correlation length over the Hubble radius, and the observed value picks out about a hundred and thirty-eight perceptual radii. It is a sharp number rather than a window, since a factor of two in the length moves the amplitude by nearly three. And the two requirements do not conflict: at that same length the mode kurtosis is still two parts in a hundred thousand, comfortably inside any bound. They could have been mutually exclusive and they are not, which was not guaranteed and which I noted for that reason.

Then the uncomfortable inversion, which I nearly wrote the wrong way round. My first instinct was to ask whether a hundred and thirty-eight radii is reachable, and it obviously is — local dynamics extend correlations about one radius per Moment, there are fifty-two thousand Moments per e-fold, and the system has sixty-four e-folds. A hundred and thirty-eight Moments is under three thousandths of an e-fold. But that is the problem rather than the solution: if nothing stops the growth, correlations run all the way to the horizon, the patch count collapses to one, and the amplitude overshoots by four orders. So the question is not reach but saturation.

I listed three places a saturation mechanism might come from and evaluated none of them, which I flagged explicitly because the temptation with a candidate this close is to pick the most convenient and call it found. The perceptual radius contracts under load, which would shorten the reach as occupancy rises. The founder's own pair-swapping channel destroys correlation as fast as the other channel builds it, which is a natural competing rate and the one I would look at first, since it comes from his mechanism rather than from outside it. And horizon exit itself would cap the build-up if the affinity propagates slower than one radius per Moment.

What I am wary of is that the candidate now has a number to hit, and a number to hit is exactly the condition under which PD-007 gets quietly violated. The hundred and thirty-eight came from the observed amplitude, so it is a requirement rather than a prediction, and any saturation mechanism proposed from here must be derived from the dynamics and then compared against it — never tuned to land on it.
