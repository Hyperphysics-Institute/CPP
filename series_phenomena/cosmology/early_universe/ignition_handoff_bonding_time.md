## §1 The instrument

Under D1 every stack's acted-on displacement is the floor, l_P/2 per Moment, so the *magnitude* of the net field is irrelevant and a stack moves along the *direction* of its net SSV (signed sum of r̂ over the other charged stacks; weightings 1/r², 1/r and count-only all run). The handoff is the Moment of the last pairing. The founder did not say which six vertices are +, so all C(12,6) = 924 assignments are run. A positive control (twelve like-sign stacks) checks that the instrument sees a push when there is one.

**The frustrated family.** The 20 antipodes-same assignments (and relatives) are inversion-symmetric: each stack's net field nearly balances, a fixed floor step overshoots, and the twelve stacks jitter in a period-2 cycle at ~0.8 l_P separation, never entering one cell — a spring at the floor (R-CAP-SPRING), not a breakout. It is measure-zero: any l_P/20 displacement of the start breaks it and the stacks pair within 15 Moments. The founder's "after the first Moment, the mixing" is that asymmetry.

**The push does not expand.** For every 6/6 assignment the first-Moment mean radial displacement is between −0.25 and +0.07 l_P (floor step 0.5): the stacks move *toward opposite neighbours*, not outward; the mean radius falls to ~0.8 l_P; the largest radius any stack reaches before pairing is 1.5 l_P (one floor step). The RMS separation changes by at most ln-ratio 0.45 over the whole push. A charge-neutral 6/6 configuration on the icosahedron has no outward acted-on component: each vertex has five neighbours, 10–20 of the 30 edges are opposite-sign, and attraction to opposite neighbours governs every direction.
