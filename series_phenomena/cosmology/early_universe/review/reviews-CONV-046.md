## §2 The κ\* split, and why the minority is adopted against a 3–2 majority

**Two returns rejected the package's strongest sentence**, and they did so on grounds the three affirming returns did not address:

> **"we have not derived X" is not "X is underivable."** Calling the relation an assignment establishes its **current epistemic status**, not its ultimate mathematical status.

> **"permanently foreclosed, not pending" contradicts the package's own Exit 1** — *"the engine's form may be incomplete."* **Both cannot be true.**

**That second point is decisive and it is not a matter of preference.** §7.1 of the package lists Exit 1 as live. §5 says foreclosure is permanent. **The package is internally inconsistent, and the worker wrote it.**

> **A 3–2 majority cannot make a contradiction consistent. The minority position is adopted on logic, not on count.**

**The three affirming returns are not thereby dismissed.** Their substantive finding — that κ\* cannot be derived without first deriving H ∝ μ, and that A_s is therefore not predicted **as the framework stands** — is accepted in full and was never in dispute. **What is struck is the modal claim about the future**, which no return established and which one return showed contradicts the package's own text.

## §3 The six amendments enacted

1. **3847** — restated as **class-scoped**: *"No viable amplitude from conserved-count, initial-slice, or post-inflation active sources in the substrate-orientation sector. Obstruction structural for that class."* **Not "the amplitude problem is closed."** C-5 is a different class (a rate); its liveness is **compatible with 3847, not a silent reopening of it.**
2. **κ\*** — **"permanently foreclosed" STRUCK.** Replaced by: *"A_s is foreclosed **given the present assignment H ∝ μ**. It becomes pending if that assignment is derived or replaced. **κ\* is an asserted, calibration-level coupling — not minted as an axiom in this round.**"* **`axiom-registry.md` untouched**, as all five returns either required or permitted.
3. **C-5** — **adiabaticity removed from the cleared-test table** and placed in its own **unmet-condition block**. *"The table should not read as eleven greens and one footnote."* Accepted.
4. **T-1** — **one result with a reuse log, counted ONCE in any evidence ledger.** Each dynamical application must **separately establish that the relevant observable depends only on the cancelled moments**. The angular identity proves the multipole cancellation; it does not by itself prove mass suppression, idle-breaking or non-collapse.
5. **Near-misses** — the **1.0% and 2.2% figures must not be quoted separately** in EU-1, the registry, or any founder-facing summary. **One correlated chain; hypothesis-generating, not confirmatory.**
6. **Retractions stay visible** — including the withdrawn 4.5×10⁻⁵ computation, with its circularity note, **to prevent accidental re-claiming.** All five returns endorsed this; one called it "part of what makes the arc reviewable."
