## §1 TO THE FP LANE — does the Q:E composition touch the leptogenesis sector?

### §1.1 What is already on file, so this is not asked blind
**D-7 applied before drafting, and it removed half the question.** The corpus states the sea is **net-neutral and colour-neutral** — *"there is no charge obstruction to a muon neutrino being a qDP."*

> **The electric-charge and colour halves are answered. Do not re-derive them.**

**And the FP lane already owns the relevant machinery:** **η_B ≈ 6×10⁻¹⁰ via leptogenesis**, with a CP-asymmetry parameter ε_CP, at **zero free parameters** under FI-C-1…FI-C-10 plus four CPP axioms.

### §1.2 The question
> **Does a local enrichment in Q-dominant SCPs shift η_B or the lepton asymmetry — or is the Q:E composition orthogonal to the leptogenesis sector?**

**What "Q-dominant" means here:** an SCP is the whole stack of CPs resident on one GP; its **composition** is the proportion of qCPs to eCPs; **Q-dominant** means qCP-rich. The EU lane's C-5 requires patches to differ in this composition.

**Why it is not already answered by §1.1:** net-neutrality is stated for **charge and colour**. **It is not stated for baryon or lepton number.** A qDP is two qCPs of *opposite charge*, which is particle–antiparticle-like and suggests B = 0 per pair — **but that is an inference drawn in the EU lane, not a corpus statement, and the EU lane has no standing to make it.**

### §1.3 Why FP should care — the consequence, so the request is not an unexplained errand
- **If a Q-enriched patch carries net B or L:** the compositional perturbation is a **baryon-isocurvature mode**. Those **survive thermalisation** and are tightly bounded by Planck. **C-5 then fails by ~30× and dies.**
- **If the composition is orthogonal to B and L:** the perturbation is **vacuum-structure only**, thermalises away at reheating, and **C-5's adiabaticity holds** (3908, conditional pass).

> **This is the single existential gate on the EU lane's only live amplitude candidate.**

## §2 ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA FOR EXIT 1 — what would count as deriving H ∝ μ

### §2.1 Criterion 1 — κ₀ must come out, not be fitted

**PD-007 bars obtaining it from A_s.** And **3902 showed the end-to-end route is circular**, because H is itself A_s-normalised — an amplitude computed through such an H reads as a derivation and is an artifact. **That computation was withdrawn; do not reinstate it.**

### §2.3 Criterion 3 — it must preserve linearity
> **ln n̄ must remain linear in N_rem.**

**That linearity — not its coefficient — is what gives ε = 1/N_rem and hence n_s = 1 − 2/N_rem.**

> **A derivation that fixes κ₀ but breaks linearity loses the tilt, which is this lane's one confirmed prediction. That is not a trade worth making, and a proposal that makes it should be rejected rather than negotiated.**

### §2.4 Criterion 4 — consistency with ρ_Λ, not reproduction of it
**Per 3922:** the CC lane's ρ_Λ is **Li-analog holographic dark energy on the future event horizon**. It answers *what is the vacuum's gravitating density relative to the horizon?*; the engine answers *what is H?*

> **A derivation of H ∝ μ owes CONSISTENCY with ρ_Λ — not reproduction of it.**

**This criterion exists because the EU lane got it wrong once:** 3920 read R_h as 1/H, concluded the two were the same equation, and was retracted in full. **The criterion is written so the error is not re-committed inside the acceptance test itself.**

### §2.5 The negative criterion — named in advance because it is the likeliest near-miss
