=== gpt ===

**Q1: STIPULATED.**
0746 excludes an SSV-stress/displacement-driven H-engine and favors a count/configurational driver, but that does **not logically entail** the stronger AP-5-era statement that the driver reads specifically D4-held content and is completely insensitive to every acted-on quantity. The package itself concedes that "the corpus had never said which sector of the GP the H-engine reads." S-HENGINE-HELD is therefore a reasonable completion of 0746 in AP-5 vocabulary, but it is still an additional bridge statement, not a theorem of {0746, LEMMA-NS-BATH, D1–D4}.

**Q2: VALID-BUT-UNNEEDED.**
For an exactly homogeneous icosahedral census, the vector sum vanishes, so SSV_net = 0; and the package's fixed-lattice occupancy-dilution picture is internally consistent with expansion not being literal bulk CP displacement. But this does not prove S-HENGINE-HELD: a count-driven H-engine could still, in principle, depend on some pre-/post-cap register quantity other than D4-held Gibbs entropy. It is supportive geometry, not the deciding bridge.

**Q3: YES.**
Under R-STACK-SENDS-EACH, the observable inflationary epoch is saturated by construction, so the prior requirement κ < κ_cap as a condition for an **unsaturated** n_s epoch is no longer applicable. If PRED-C-96 is to survive Reading S, the burden moves from "show the epoch is unsaturated" to "show the H-engine remains count/entropy-driven while saturated."

**Q4: ADDS A LEG — S-HENGINE-HELD.**
Because Q1 is STIPULATED rather than ENTAILED, PRED-C-96 now has an additional conditional leg: **during AP-5 saturation, H_eff must read the held/count/entropy sector rather than any clipped acted-on sector.** The prior "count-driven coupling" strongly motivates this leg, but does not already identify it uniquely with D4-held content.

**Q5: NO.**
OPEN-EU-BATH-DEPTH-1 is not obviously non-blocking. PRED-C-96 uses μ = kT ln n̄ as a thermodynamic/Gibbs quantity evaluated against the substrate bath; if a stack at depth ~10⁷⁴ does **not** thermalise or track the bath rapidly enough, then H_eff ∝ μ(n̄) need not hold with the required time dependence. The assertion that any failure can only produce O(α)-level corrections within ~5×10⁻⁴ is not derived in this package. Until a rate/separation calculation bounds the lag, this residual bears directly on the tilt mechanism.

**Q6: NONE.**
I find no direct contradiction with R-STACK-SENDS-EACH, R-IGNITION-HANDOFF, or R-LAYER-REPOSITIONING-IN-CELL. The issue is not contradiction with the founder's rulings; it is whether the corpus already forces the proposed held-sector interpretation. It does not.

**Verdict: ADMISSIBLE-WITH-AMENDMENTS.**

I would require two amendments. First, relabel S-HENGINE-HELD as an **explicit EU-lane bridge/conditional completion** of 0746 under AP-5, rather than claiming it is entailed by the prior corpus. That preserves its economy and makes the governance honest: 0746 selects "count rather than stress," while S-HENGINE-HELD specifies what "count" means once AP-5 splits held from acted-on sectors.

Second, change OPEN-EU-BATH-DEPTH-1 from **non-blocking** to **blocking for full PRED-C-96 closure under Reading S**, unless a calculation shows that bath equilibration at enormous saturation depth is parametrically fast enough that deviations from μ = kT ln n̄ remain below the stated theory error.

The central hostile finding is that §2.1 makes an identification that is plausible but not logically compulsory:

**0746 COUNT-driven ≠ automatically AP-5 D4-held-sector-driven.**

0746 tells you what the H-engine is **not**—the SSV-stress/displacement branch—and what observable dependence worked—ln n̄. AP-5 later introduces a new held/acted-on register distinction. Mapping the old count variable specifically onto D4-held entropy is exactly the new statement CONV-045 is proposing. That is admissible physics bookkeeping, but it should be recorded as such rather than retrospectively called entailed.

The good news for the lane is that this does **not** kill S-HENGINE-HELD. It changes its status. With that status correction, the statement is coherent, compatible with the founder's rulings, and supplies the bridge Reading S now requires.
