## The clean structural statement (the real content)

did NOT fix the MAGNITUDE κ. Shape vs magnitude are the orthogonal pieces of one boost law — which is the
κ-orthogonality of 2003, now traced to its source: the magnitude is the H-axiom constant. n_s lives on
the shape (clean, derived); A_s lives on the magnitude (posited). That's why one is a prediction and the
other is adopted, structurally.
