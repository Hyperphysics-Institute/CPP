## §2 Why the value does not transfer (T2)
r = 16ε with ε = −Ḣ/H² holds when P_ζ = H²/(8π²εM_Pl²), i.e. when ζ is sourced by a canonical scalar's fluctuation. The crowding engine sources ζ in the δN formalism from the held-stack boost H_eff = κ₀(μ(n̄) − μ(1)), with an amplitude prefactor (κ kT)² that EU-1 explicitly leaves underived (A_s adopted, not counted; predictions.md PRED-C-96 note). So the tilt fixes the *shape* of H(N) and nothing about P_ζ's normalization; r = 16ε is not available. What is available, if the tensor sector is GR's — 3706 found the AP-5 gravitational-wave sector is GR's to observable precision for mergers, and the same D3 whole-relay is what carries a tensor pattern through a saturated lattice — is P_t = 2H²/(π²M_Pl²): tensors depend on H alone. Then

r = P_t / A_s = 2H² / (π² M_Pl² A_s),

a function of H with no engine constant in it.
