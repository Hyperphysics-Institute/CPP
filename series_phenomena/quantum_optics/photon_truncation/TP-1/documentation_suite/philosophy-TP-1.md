# Philosophy — TP-1: The Truncated Photon and the Lattice Regularization of Shutter-Induced Photon Creation

The honest-scoping framing: what TP-1 establishes, where its weakest link is, and why it is deliberately modest.

## Certainty level
**Empirically-supported hypothesis at the framework-conditional level — NOT a theorem.** The compatibility mappings are structural embeddings entailed by QM-4/QM-5; the logarithmic divergence class is *derived* from the RGS kernel; the regularization is a proposition (PROP-TP-1-1) proved at framework-conditional level, with no global theorem claimed. The one quantitative residual (the coefficient $C$) is explicitly open (OPEN-TP-1).

## Relationship to the Standard Model / standard QFT
**Reproduces and embeds — does not replace or disagree.** The RGS effect is a correct result of standard quantized-EM optics; CPP *accommodates* it, supplying a concrete substrate (DP-Sea medium, 600-cell modes, Nexus partial trace, driven-boundary dynamical Casimir) that realizes the structures RGS assume. CPP is at parity with standard QFT on the phenomenon itself; its only departure is the fate of the instantaneous-shutter divergence, which the finite lattice spectrum regularizes.

## The weakest link (stated plainly)
Two reviewers (ChatGPT, Copilot) named it directly: until the 600-cell Hilbert–Schmidt mode sum pins $C$ *from the lattice*, the framework-specific content is "RGS log divergence + a CPP UV cutoff." TP-1 owns this rather than overclaiming. What v1.0 *does* establish beyond a generic Planck-cutoff move is that the cutoff is **intrinsic** (the lattice has a hard band top, no modes above it) and **$z$-specific** ($\omega_{\max} = \sqrt{12}/t_P$ carries the coordination $z = 12$). The remaining step — does the band-top density of states give a definite, distinctive $C$? — is OPEN-TP-1, the substantive post-v1.0 arc.

## Falsifiability inventory
- **Compatibility falsifier:** a CPP treatment requiring global-unitarity violation (threshold: any failure of the QM-4 partial-trace account at the full-Sea level).
- **Cutoff falsifier:** the lattice mode ceiling failing to act as a sharp cutoff — e.g. band-top DOS reshaping the $1/\omega$ tail before $\omega_{\max} = \sqrt{12}/t_P$ (this is OPEN-TP-1's open edge).
- **Coefficient falsifier:** a computed $C$ far from $O(1)$ would shift the ceiling away from $\approx 64.5\,C$ (not the class).

## Paper-type declaration
*Compatibility plus foundational regularization* (per `operating_system.md` §4 taxonomy: a structural/foundational paper, not a predictive flagship). NO THEO, NO PRED. Framework-conditional, EU-1 precedent (a framework-conditional paper that ships v1.0 with an honest open residual).

## Limits of scope
TP-1 reads one external, contested continuum result against the CPP substrate. It does **not**: re-derive RGS; predict a measurable number; introduce an axiom or theorem; or claim the regularization is experimentally consequential (it caps only the unphysical instantaneous limit). It is the foundational bridge for the quantum-optics domain — the correspondence on which later optical phenomena (beam splitters, entanglement swapping, homodyne measurement) can build.
