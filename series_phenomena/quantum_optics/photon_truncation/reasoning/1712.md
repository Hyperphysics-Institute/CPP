## The question
OPEN-TP-1 asks for the $O(1)$ coefficient $C$ in $\langle N\rangle_{\max}=C\ln(\omega_{\max}/\omega_\gamma)$ via the
600-cell Hilbert–Schmidt mode sum with the band-top density of states. The pivotal sub-question: does the
600-cell DOS **enhance** $C$ (making the coefficient itself lattice-distinctive), or is the lattice's whole
distinctive role the cutoff $\omega_{\max}$?

## Result (honest, partial close of structure)
Write $\langle N\rangle=\int|\xi(\omega)|^2 g(\omega)\,d\omega$, RGS tail $|\xi|^2\sim A/\omega$, $g$ = photon DOS.
1. **$C$ is the CONTINUUM coefficient $A g_0$.** The log is range-dominated: it accumulates over the many
   decades $[\omega_\gamma,\omega_{\max}]$ where the lattice dispersion is effectively continuum ($g\approx g_0$).
   The band-top van Hove pile-up ($g\sim(\omega_{\max}-\omega)^{-1/2}$) is integrable and contributes only a
   finite tail. So the lattice does **NOT** enhance $C$. The framework-specific content is confirmed to be
   **entirely the cutoff** $\omega_{\max}=\sqrt{12}/t_P$, not the coefficient — this CONFIRMS (and quantifies)
   the reviewers' (ChatGPT/Copilot) "content is modest until $C$ is pinned" caveat rather than overturning it.
2. **Lattice contribution = van Hove $\Delta$.** $\langle N\rangle=C[\ln(\omega_{\max}/\omega_\gamma)+\Delta]$,
   $\Delta\sim O(1)$, model-dependent. For $\omega(k)=\omega_{\max}\sin(\pi k/2k_{\max})$: $\Delta=\ln2$
   **exactly** (analytic: $\int_{u_\gamma}^1 du/(u\sqrt{1-u^2})=\ln2+\ln(1/u_\gamma)$; numerically confirmed
   0.693). A parabolic-top model gives $\Delta\approx1.39$ — same $O(1)$ scale, $\sim$2× spread. Effective
   ceiling $64.5\,C\to\approx65.2\,C$ ($+1.1\%$ for the sin model).
3. **Residual (still open):** the ABSOLUTE $C=A g_0$ needs the RGS field-normalization constant for a chosen
   photon profile (convention-dependent); the exact $\Delta$ needs the true 600-cell extended-lattice
   dispersion (vs these 1D models). So OPEN-TP-1 is not fully closed — but its STRUCTURE is resolved.
