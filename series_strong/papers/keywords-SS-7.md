## Elevator pitch

CPP predicts the binding energies of eight alpha-chain nuclei — ${}^{12}$C, ${}^{16}$O, ${}^{20}$Ne, ${}^{24}$Mg, ${}^{28}$Si, ${}^{32}$S, ${}^{36}$Ar, ${}^{40}$Ca — from one zero-parameter formula $B(N_\alpha) = N_\alpha\Balpha + (3N_\alpha-6)B_{\text{pair}}$ using only constants inherited from SS-5, with RMS error 0.88% against AME 2020. The $3N_\alpha-6$ factor is Euler's formula for the edge count of any simplicial convex polytope on $N_\alpha$ vertices; alpha-chain nuclei realize such polytopes (assumption C4, empirically supported by Table 1 plus five hostile-geometry stress tests in §6.5). ${}^8$Be's 92 keV unboundness is re-derived in-formula from $N_\alpha = 2$ ($E = 0$) plus Coulomb at $R_{\alpha\alpha} = 2.37$ fm. Systematic $-2\%$ underbinding at $N_\alpha \geq 12$ (${}^{48}$Ti onward) is registered as OPEN-SS-22 and targeted for SS-8 (icosahedral closure). First-principles derivation of the simplicial contact structure is OPEN-SS-24, targeted for SS-9 candidate. Both round-2 external reviews returned "Accept with minor revisions."
