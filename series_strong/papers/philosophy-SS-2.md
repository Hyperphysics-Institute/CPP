# Philosophy — SS-2: Lattice-Scale Grounding and Nucleon Structure

**Paper:** SS-2 v1.0 (10 April 2026)
**Series:** Standard Model

## The Grounding Achievement

SS-2 solves the most fundamental epistemological problem in CPP: the theory predicted ratios but not magnitudes. A framework that cannot state what "one lattice unit" equals in metres is mathematically interesting but physically untestable. The conversion l_unit = 0.589 fm transforms CPP from a ratio-prediction framework into a complete physical theory.

## Derivation vs Construction

Sonnet's review correctly distinguishes between derivation (α_s running from α_geom) and construction (the string tension formula σ = M₀zπ/(φ l_edge)). The paper is honest about this: the α_s running is derived (1-loop QFT is standard), while the string tension is a motivated ansatz. Both produce correct predictions, but their epistemic status differs.

## The Self-Consistency Argument

The most philosophically significant result may be r_ZBW/l_unit = 1.07. If this ratio were 100 or 0.01, the tetrahedral model would be physically absurd — quarks would be vastly larger or smaller than the lattice cells they occupy. The ratio being ~1 means the model is self-consistent: the quark fits inside its cell. This is not a prediction in the usual sense, but it is a necessary condition for the entire framework.

## The Neutron as a One-Parameter Success

The neutron charge radius requires one fitted parameter (δ), but this parameter has a clear physical interpretation (eCP displacement) and a small value (7% of one edge length). The sign and order of magnitude of r²_n emerge from geometry alone. This is the difference between "fitting a number" and "calibrating a physical mechanism."

## Falsifiability

SS-2 is falsifiable through: (1) improved proton radius measurements, (2) α_s measurements at new energy scales, (3) neutron form factor measurements at higher Q², (4) discovery of a fourth quark generation, (5) deuteron predictions from the open-vertex model (SM-12).
