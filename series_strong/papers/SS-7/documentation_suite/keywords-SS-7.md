## Elevator pitch

CPP predicts the binding energies of twelve strict $N{=}Z$ alpha-chain nuclei from ${}^{12}$C through ${}^{56}$Ni (including ${}^{12}$C, ${}^{16}$O, ${}^{20}$Ne, ${}^{24}$Mg, ${}^{28}$Si, ${}^{32}$S, ${}^{36}$Ar, ${}^{40}$Ca, ${}^{44}$Ti, ${}^{48}$Cr, ${}^{52}$Fe, ${}^{56}$Ni) from one zero-parameter formula $B(N_\alpha) = N_\alpha\Balpha + (3N_\alpha-6)B_{\text{pair}}$ using only constants inherited from SS-5. All twelve residuals are within $\pm 1.5\%$ against AME 2020; RMS error $0.80\%$ across all twelve, $0.91\%$ across the primary set $N_\alpha \in [3,10]$. The $3N_\alpha-6$ factor is Euler's formula for the edge count of any simplicial convex polytope on $N_\alpha$ vertices; alpha-chain nuclei realize such polytopes (assumption C4, empirically supported by Table 1 plus five hostile-geometry stress tests in §6.5). ${}^8$Be's 92 keV unboundness is re-derived in-formula from $N_\alpha = 2$ ($E = 0$) plus Coulomb at $R_{\alpha\alpha} = 2.37$ fm. Extension to non-$N{=}Z$ and odd-$A$ nuclei is OPEN-SS-23 and the primary target for SS-8. First-principles derivation of the simplicial contact structure is OPEN-SS-24, targeted for SS-9 candidate. DP-sea screening of alpha-alpha Coulomb in bound polytopes is OPEN-SS-25 (new in v1.2). v1.2 retired OPEN-SS-22 (heavy-nuclei icosahedral closure) after three-reviewer verification established that its empirical anchor was an isotope-selection artifact; first retired open problem in the CPP programme record. Both round-2 external reviews on v1.1 returned "Accept with minor revisions"; v1.2 verification from three reviewers converged independently on interpretation (a).

### Programme-level status transition introduced in v1.2
- **RETIRED** as a new open-problem status distinct from RESOLVED (solution found), PARTIALLY RESOLVED (sub-scope solved), FALSIFIED (claim disproved). Retirement applies when a problem's registered empirical anchor is subsequently found to be an artifact (isotope selection, measurement, framework contamination) such that no well-defined replacement anchor exists for the hypothesis that motivated registration. OPEN-SS-22 is the precedent.

### Theorems invoked but not registered (pure mathematics)
- **Theorem 2.1** (SS-7 internal): Any simplicial convex polyhedron on $N_\alpha \geq 4$ vertices has exactly $3N_\alpha - 6$ edges. Proof by Euler's formula + triangular-face constraint. Classical result; presented for completeness.

### Findings
- **Finding 4.1** (SS-7 internal): $R_{\alpha\alpha} = 2.37$ fm extracted as consistency parameter from ${}^8$Be unboundness. Not a forward prediction; inversion of the binding condition.
