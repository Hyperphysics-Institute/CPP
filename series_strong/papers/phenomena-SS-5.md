## Falsification routes

**Quantitative:** Any of the four A=2,3,4 bindings predicted outside the CPP residual band ($\sim \pm 5\%$) would falsify. Currently all four are within the band.

**$(A-1)$ multiplicity:** Alternative factors $(A-1)^2$ or $(A-1)/2$ give $^3$H/$^3$He predictions outside the band. The data select $(A-1)$.

**Pauli coefficient:** Alternative coefficients $M_0/\varphi^2$ (1.45 MeV) or $M_0/\varphi^4$ (0.55 MeV) give $^3$H, $^3$He, $^4$He predictions outside the band. The data select $M_0/\varphi^3 = 0.895$ MeV.

**Closure bonus:** Removing the A=4 bonus would push $^4$He to 25.55 MeV (−9.7% error, outside band). The data confirm the closure bonus.

**Unboundness predictions:** Discovery of bound $^5$He, $^5$Li, or $^8$Be would falsify the closed-polytope-gap argument. No such observation in 70+ years of nuclear physics.
