## Elevator Pitch

A neutron added to an alpha-cluster nucleus gains an extra binding energy that depends only on the polytope geometry of the alpha cluster — specifically, on the average number of edges meeting at each vertex of the polytope. Across twelve nuclei from ${}^{12}\mathrm{C}$ to ${}^{56}\mathrm{Ni}$, this geometric prediction matches observed binding to better than 15% with no adjustable parameters, and to better than 1% at the two most symmetric polytopes (${}^{26}\mathrm{Mg}$ octahedron and ${}^{42}\mathrm{Ca}$ gyroelongated square bipyramid).
