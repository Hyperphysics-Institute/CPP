## Q: What does "grounding the lattice" mean?

**A:** CPP's 600-cell lattice predicted mass RATIOS beautifully but never said what one lattice unit equals in metres. Grounding means establishing the conversion: 1 lattice unit = 0.589 fm. This transforms CPP from a ratio-prediction framework into a theory that predicts lengths, radii, and cross-sections in physical units. (§1)

## Q: How do you know l_unit = 0.589 fm is correct?

**A:** Two completely independent routes converge on the same value. Route 2 uses the pion decay constant f_π via the Pagels-Stokar relation to get Λ_QCD = 335 MeV. Route 4 runs the geometric coupling α_geom = 1/√5 from the lattice scale to the Z mass. Both give l_unit = ℏc/Λ = 0.589 fm. Independent convergence is the strongest evidence for a scale identification. (§2)

## Q: Is α_geom = 1/√5 fitted or derived?

**A:** Derived from the 600-cell's icosahedral symmetry — it appears throughout CPP (EW-series, SS-2) as the geometric coupling. It is NOT fitted to α_s(m_Z). Rather, α_s(m_Z) determines the SCALE Λ at which α_geom applies. Predicting α_s(m_H) = 0.1132 (measured 0.1130) is then genuinely parameter-free because m_H ≠ m_Z. (§2.3)

## Q: How can a geometric constant predict QCD observables?

**A:** In CPP, QCD IS the effective theory of the lattice's colour sector. The lattice geometry determines the bare coupling (α_geom), the confinement scale (Λ), and the string tension (σ). QCD parameters are not fundamental — they're consequences of the 600-cell structure. (§2.3, §4)

## Q: Why is the proton a tetrahedron?

**A:** The 600-cell has exactly 600 tetrahedral cells — they are the fundamental building blocks of the lattice. Each cell has 4 vertices with alternating ± polarity. Three quarks occupy three vertices (u,u,d for the proton), leaving one open for nuclear binding. The charge assignment is forced by polarity rules and charge conservation. (§5.1)

## Q: How accurate is the proton radius prediction?

**A:** r_proton = 0.883 fm vs measured 0.841 fm — 5% error with zero fitted parameters. This is comparable to the best bag model and constituent quark model predictions, achieved from pure geometry + force balance. The 5% discrepancy likely comes from the simplified two-body force balance and neglect of the eCP trading effect. (§5.4)

## Q: What about the neutron?

**A:** The neutron charge radius (r²_n = −0.1161 fm²) is reproduced exactly with one fitted parameter (δ = −0.067, the down quark eCP displacement). Remarkably, the correct SIGN (negative) emerges from the tetrahedral distortion alone, before any fitting. The linear oscillator mechanism (Thomas's insight) provides the physical explanation: the two down quarks' −eCPs repel and oscillate 180° out of phase. (§6)

## Q: Is the string tension formula σ = M₀zπ/(φ l_edge) derived?

**A:** Not rigorously — it is a motivated ansatz (CONJ-SS-2-1). Each factor has an identified physical origin (z = coordination bonds, π = ZBW orbit, 1/φ = propagation efficiency), but the specific combination has not been derived from the lattice mode spectrum. It produces the correct proton radius to 5%, which supports it, but a first-principles derivation from the SS-series would strengthen the result. (§4, §7.2)

## Q: What's the strongest criticism from the hostile review?

**A:** Sonnet correctly identified that the string tension formula and the α_geom identification need stronger theoretical foundations. The σ formula is acknowledged as a conjecture. The α_geom identification is derived from icosahedral geometry but the full derivation chain should be made more explicit in v1.1. Sonnet's "circular reasoning" objection was incorrect — the geometry fixes the coupling, not the QCD measurement. (Reviews file)

## Q: How does SS-2 connect to SM-8/9/10?

**A:** SM-8 identified the quark cages. SM-9 derived the scaling exponent. SM-10 proposed the FEM mechanism. SS-2 grounds all of these in physical units. The same K₄ tetrahedral cell that produces the strange quark mass in SM-8 now determines the proton structure. The FEM simulation proposed in SM-10 will eventually refine σ. (§7)

## Q: What comes next?

**A:** SM-12 should address: (1) the deuteron — predict binding energy from open-vertex model, (2) the Y-junction three-body colour geometry, (3) other hadron predictions (Δ baryon, mesons), and (4) nuclear saturation from the single-open-vertex constraint. The SS-series is needed to derive σ rigorously. (§7.3)

## Q: Could this all be coincidence?

**A:** Seven zero-parameter predictions spanning three sectors of physics (nuclear structure, QCD, electroweak) all within 5% — from one geometric framework — is difficult to attribute to coincidence. The theory is falsifiable: a wrong proton radius at higher precision, a fourth quark generation, or failure for other hadrons would refute it. (§7)
