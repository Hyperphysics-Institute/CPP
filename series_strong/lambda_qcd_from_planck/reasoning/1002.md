## The realization that reshaped Route B

I went in expecting Route B to be "find a discreteness form factor `g(Q)` that enhances the running
enough to bridge 20 orders." Working it, the controlling fact turned out to be sensitivity, not
mechanism. Because `Λ = E_P e^{−N}` with `N = 2π/(β₀ α_UV)`, the map from `α_UV` to `Λ` has
amplification `N/α_UV ≈ 2300`. So whatever Route B (or A) eventually supplies for `α_UV`, it has to
be right to sub-percent — an *exact* relation, not a numerical near-match. That single observation
reorganizes the whole project: the hard part is not finding *a* mechanism that gives ~0.2 GeV
(order-of-magnitude is easy and already roughly there), it is finding an *exact* one. I promoted
that to the patch's headline theorem.

## Why I built the 2π² futility demo

I noticed the "needed" enhancement ratio `R₀ = N_target/N(0.386) ≈ 19.57` sits ~1% from `2π² = 19.74`
(the S³ volume — and the 600-cell tiles S³, so it is exactly the kind of "natural" number one would
be tempted to declare a hit). This is a trap, and I wanted the patch to *inoculate the arc against
it* rather than fall for it. So I computed what `R = 2π²` actually predicts: `Λ = 0.147 GeV`, −33%.
A 1% match in the factor is a 33% miss in `Λ`. Putting this in the verify script as a hard assertion
(`|err| > 0.20`) means the arc now has a standing guard: any future "natural constant matches at ~1%"
claim is automatically flagged as insufficient. This is the discipline the chirality arc's framing
guards enforced; the same hazard exists here and I wanted it nailed down at the door.

## Why I did not attempt the g(Q) derivation in this patch

The actual derivation of the discreteness profile `g(Q)` (equivalently the `PSR_eff → l_P/2`
approach rate) requires the 600-cell mode structure — a real calculation that deserves its own patch
and its own verify. Attempting it here, under time pressure to "show progress," is exactly how one
ends up reverse-engineering `g` to hit `0.0197`. Given the sensitivity theorem I had just proved,
that temptation is acute and dangerous. The disciplined move is to stop at the sharpened target and
let 1003 do the mode-structure calculation cleanly, falsification-first.

## The honest tension I chose to state plainly

Dimensional transmutation deriving a dimensionful IR scale to sub-percent accuracy from a UV coupling

Standard Model either. CPP's claim to do better rests entirely on the 600-cell giving an exact
`α_s(E_P)`. I wrote §6 to say this out loud: the project may well terminate in the falsifier
("permanently calibrated"), and that is a legitimate, non-failure outcome (it is TODO-016 Track 1's
honest stance). Naming that now keeps the arc from drifting into motivated closure later.
