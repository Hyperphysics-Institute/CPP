# Reasoning fragment — Patch 1004 (framing correction: PSR currency)

**Session 156-parallel (1000-series window), 10 June 2026. Per `templates/reasoning_capture_protocol.md`.**

## What prompted this

Thomas pushed back on the framing: l_P is not the Grid-Point spacing, and it is environment-dependent
(small near a nucleus, large in flat space). He was right, and checking SR-1 / the c0x companions
confirmed it is the corpus's own canonical position, not a new idea. This patch records the
correction and — more importantly — reinterprets the 1003 negative result in its light.

## The naming slip and why I'm owning it explicitly

I wrote "PSR (Phase-Space-Restriction)" in the 1002/README text. The glossary is unambiguous:
PSR = Planck Sphere Radius, the per-Moment CP displacement, = l_P in the rest frame. The math didn't
depend on the gloss, but in a discussion whose whole crux is "what is l_P," using the wrong expansion
for the very quantity under debate is the kind of error that breeds downstream confusion. Better to
correct it loudly than bury it.

## The substantive realization

Once PSR is correctly the Planck Sphere Radius and l_P is the rest-frame PSR, the 1001–1003 premise
(E_P = ℏc/l_P as a fundamental UV cutoff) is exposed as the wrong currency. l_P is emergent and
SSV-dependent; the GP spacing is finer (nested hierarchy, 0736); and — the clincher — c05/TODO-014
already say the absolute Planck scale is one shared calibration, not derived. So the negative result
wasn't a failure of the strong sector; it was asking for an exact derivation of an absolute scale
that CPP treats as calibrated everywhere. The confinement scale is PSR_eff → l_P/2, a fraction of
baseline PSR in the same currency — tied to l_P by a ratio, not separated from it by a derivable
20-order run. That's why I reinterpret rather than retract: the computation is fine, its meaning was
mis-set.

## Why I did not let this become a rescue

It would be easy to spin "l_P is emergent" into "so the hierarchy dissolves and we can derive Λ after
all." I checked the two ways it could cut and reported both: relocating the cutoff to the GP scale
makes the lever arm *worse*, and the GP-count route is foreclosed by 0736 (resolution enters no
prediction). The only honest beneficiary is the *reinterpretation* (calibration-consistency) plus the
*redirect* to op:sigma at the IR end. I wrote §4's forward expectation deliberately downbeat — op:sigma
may also bottom out at the calibration — so the next run is a test, not a foregone win.

## On the 10^30 number

Thomas flagged that "~10³⁰ GPs per l_P" is a Grok-origin estimate never fully processed. I therefore
refused to harden it into the glossary correction; the patch relies only on the qualitative
sub-Planck-nesting fact (canonical per 0736) and explicitly marks 10³⁰ as unverified. This matters
because the DP-Sea paper's N⁴ ≈ 10⁻¹²⁰ vacuum-energy claim appears to rest on N ≈ 10³⁰ — i.e. the
same shaky number — which is exactly the CC reconciliation thread Thomas raised separately.

## Housekeeping decision

I kept the glossary fix OUT of this in-lane patch and flagged it for a separate STOP-and-warn patch,
because master_glossary.md is a shared, cross-lane, SR-sector-owned foundational file. Editing a
foundational definition from the strong-sector window — even with authorization — should be visible
and sequenced against the DM window, not folded silently into an in-lane correction.
