## What this patch is

The first real attempt at Route B's sharpened target, and a negative result. I computed the actual
600-cell graph Laplacian spectrum, tested whether any natural invariant fixes `α_s(E_P)` to the bar

## Why I ran it as a falsification scan and not a search for a hit

Having proved the sensitivity theorem in 1002 myself, I knew going in that the failure mode was
motivated matching: with ~2300× amplification I can *always* find a number that "works" by tuning a
denominator (I showed `gap/116.1` does), and it means nothing. So I pre-declared the candidate list
(spectral invariants, φ-powers, sea_strength, the PSR-motivated 1/2), interpreted them uniformly as
the bare coupling, and reported every miss. The honest object of the exercise is the *distribution*
of misses, not the best one. The distribution is damning: −83% to +224%, nothing within 20%.

## The g₀ = 1/2 residue — why I flagged it but refused to promote it

`g₀ = 1/2` is the one candidate that is both principled-looking (PSR_eff → l_P/2 carries the same
1/2) and close-ish (Λ ≈ 0.31 GeV, +42%). The temptation is to write "the QCD scale is Planck-derived
to 40% from g₀ = 1/2 = PSR saturation fraction" and call it a win. I deliberately did not, for two
reasons. First, +42% on Λ is not a derivation by the bar I set last patch — it is two orders of
magnitude short of sub-percent. Second, and worse, I have *not* derived that the bare coupling
equals the PSR saturation fraction; the shared "1/2" is a numerical echo until someone shows the
running boundary condition literally is PSR_eff/l_P. Writing it up as a result would be exactly the
coincidence-grade reasoning I built the 1002 guard to forbid. So it goes in as "strongest positive
lead," not as a finding.

## Why I am willing to call this a negative lean toward the falsifier

The programme's whole epistemics (zero-parameter predictions, √N implausibility) depend on
predictions being real, not fitted. An honest negative here is worth more to that ledger than a
fabricated closure that a reviewer (or Thomas) later has to unwind. The handover names "permanent
calibration" as a legitimate terminal outcome, and the evidence now points that way: the mechanism
gives the right sign (PSR → small UV coupling) and the right order of magnitude (g₀ = 1/2 → 0.31
GeV), but not the precision that "derived" requires. I phrased the verdict to stop short of the
*final* falsifier, because only one mechanism-family (invariant matching) has been tested; a
genuinely derived mode-sum→running calculation (spectral-zeta of the Laplacian into the vacuum
polarization) is still untried and is the one live path. That is the honest boundary: this attempt
fails, the claim leans false, one route remains.
