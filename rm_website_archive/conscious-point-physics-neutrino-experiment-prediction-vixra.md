\section{Derivation of the Cage Scaling Factor $\lambda = \phi^{3/2}$}

The CPP graph is built by iterative golden-ratio inflation of icosahedral–dodecahedral cages. At each self-similarity level $n \to n+1$, the linear size of the new cage is multiplied by the cubic root of the volume growth factor.

\begin{equation}
\lambda = (V_{n+1}/V_n)^{1/3} = \phi^{3/2} = \left(\frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}\right)^{3/2} \approx 4.236\,067\,977.

\begin{equation}
m_{n+1}/m_n = 1/\lambda = \phi^{-3/2} \approx 0.236\,067\,977.

This is an exact geometric prediction of CPP with no free parameters.
