But what if this notion isn&#8217;t mere fancy? <div class="css-175oi2r"><span class="css-1jxf684 r-bcqeeo r-1ttztb7 r-qvutc0 r-poiln3 r-1adg3ll r-1g7jtus r-1x3r274"><span class="css-1jxf684 r-bcqeeo r-1ttztb7 r-qvutc0 r-poiln3"><span class="css-1jxf684 r-bcqeeo r-1ttztb7 r-qvutc0 r-poiln3 r-1x3r274">The idea that hair serves as an extension of human perception dates back millennia. In the Bible&#8217;s Book of Judges 16, Samson&#8217;s legendary strength is tied inexorably to his uncut locks, which are severed by Delilah, stripping him of his power. This narrative implies hair as a reservoir of vitality and sensory acuity, perhaps symbolizing an intuitive connection to the divine or the unseen.</span></span></span></div>

<div><span class="css-1jxf684 r-bcqeeo r-1ttztb7 r-qvutc0 r-poiln3 r-1x3r274">A compelling modern anecdote emerges from World War II-era U.S. military experiments, as recounted in Palo Da Floresta&#8217;s chapter &#8220;Trackers, WWII Special Forces&#8221; in </span><span class="css-1jxf684 r-bcqeeo r-1ttztb7 r-qvutc0 r-poiln3 r-1x3r274 r-36ujnk"><span class="css-1jxf684 r-bcqeeo r-1ttztb7 r-qvutc0 r-poiln3 r-1x3r274">Science to Sage: Bioelectromagnetic—Secrets in the Field</span></span><span class="css-1jxf684 r-bcqeeo r-1ttztb7 r-qvutc0 r-poiln3 r-1x3r274"> (2021).
