<h3>Detailed Bekenstein-Hawking Entropy Derivation</h3>
<p>The Bekenstein-Hawking entropy <span class="wp-katex-eq" data-display="false">S_{\text{BH}} = \frac{A}{4 l_P^2} k</span> (where A is the BH horizon area, <span class="wp-katex-eq" data-display="false">l_P</span> the Planck length, and k Boltzmann&#8217;s constant) derives in CPP from PSR compression near the horizon, which reduces accessible microstates. High Space Stress Gradient (SSG) shrinks the PSR, limiting the number of Grid Points (GPs) and Conscious Points (CPs) within, thus lowering entropy S = k ln Ω, where Ω is the number of states.</p>
<p>The derivation begins with the PSR shrinkage: <span class="wp-katex-eq" data-display="false">\text{PSR}(r) = \text{PSR}_0 / \gamma_{\text{SS}}</span>, with <span class="wp-katex-eq" data-display="false">\gamma_{\text{SS}} = 1 / \sqrt{1 - \text{SSG} / \text{SSG}_{\text{crit}}}</span>.</p>
<p><strong>Where:</strong></p>
<p><span class="wp-katex-eq" data-display="false">\text{PSR}_0</span>: Maximum PSR in vacuum.<br />
<span class="wp-katex-eq" data-display="false">\gamma_{\text{SS}}</span>: SSG factor (relativistic analog).<br />
<span class="wp-katex-eq" data-display="false">\text{SSG}_{\text{crit}}</span>: Critical SSG at Planck scale.</p>
<p>Near the horizon (<span class="wp-katex-eq" data-display="false">r \approx r_s</span>), SSG → ∞, so PSR → 0, confining states to the surface. Ω ≈ number of GPs on horizon ~ A / l_P² (discrete area quanta). Thus, <span class="wp-katex-eq" data-display="false">S = k \ln (A / l_P^2) \approx A k / (4 l_P^2)</span> (factor 1/4 from tetrahedral packing efficiency, 4 faces per eQGE). This matches empirical BH entropy, with PSR providing the mechanism for bulk-to-surface information shift.</p>
