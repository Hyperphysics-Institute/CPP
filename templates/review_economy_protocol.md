# REVIEW ECONOMY PROTOCOL — when a panel round is spent, when it is not, and what replaces it

**Standing rule (founder, WORKFLOW-REVIEW-ECONOMY; canonicalized here
15 Aug 2026, Patch 3149). Panel-ratified in CONV-021 Q4, 4–1
PROCEDURALLY-SOUND.** Refer to this file whenever the question
"should this go to the panel?" arises.

## §0 — The founder's statement of the rule (verbatim, 15 Aug 2026)

> We have agreed to take the results to the panel when it's either a
> win or we don't know what to do. Running results past the panel when
> we know what to do, because we imposed a rule on ourselves, is very
> formal, probably excellent science, but all the work falls on me.

## §1 — The economics being managed

A panel round costs the FOUNDER paste-labor across five reviewers and
a return trip; it costs the worker a dispatch, an adjudication, and a
patch. That cost is worth paying for judgments the worker cannot
legitimately make alone. It is NOT worth paying to have the panel
restate mathematics, ratify bookkeeping, or bless a decision whose
correctness is already determined. **Formality is not rigor.**

## §2 — THE THREE TRIGGERS (dispatch only on these)

1. **A WIN.** A result strong enough that independent adversarial
   review is what converts it into a corpus claim (a shipped paper, a
   promoted candidate, a closed falsifier campaign, an axiom-level
   change).
2. **AN EXHAUSTED AVENUE.** The worker does not know what to do, and
   further unilateral work would be guessing — including any case
   where the honest next step is a CHOICE the worker cannot make
   without contaminating a frozen result (estimator selection after
   seeing disagreeing readings is the canonical instance).
3. **A STANDING OBLIGATION** the panel itself imposed and that names
   the panel as its venue.

## §3 — WHAT NEVER TRIGGERS A ROUND

- Repairing a defective instrument whose defect has a known,
  principled remedy (see §4 for the substitute discipline).
- Bookkeeping, unit normalizations, file/registry hygiene, patch
  mechanics, sequencing decisions (all PD-006 worker territory).
- Re-confirming a register the panel has already adjudicated.
- Reporting a discharge of obligations already specified — those
  ride in the next natural bundle, not a dedicated round.
- Anything dispatched only because "we wrote a procedure once."

## §4 — THE SUBSTITUTE FOR A ROUND: freeze before the data

When the worker fixes something itself, the protection a panel would
have supplied is obtained by PRE-COMMITMENT instead:
1. Diagnose the defect and record the diagnosis BEFORE proposing a fix.
2. Repair BY PRINCIPLE (the mathematical property that failed), never
   by outcome.
3. FREEZE the repaired form, in writing, **before the data that will
   test it exists** — this is the load-bearing step; it is what makes
   the fix un-steerable.
4. Prefer INTERVAL-VALUED or otherwise choice-free rules; eliminate
   every free parameter the worker could later tune.
5. Declare the repair's OWN exhaustion trigger — the condition under
   which it fails and the matter DOES go to the panel.
6. Report the failed original verbatim and unedited alongside the fix.
**Panel ratification of this substitute: CONV-021 Q4, 4–1.** GPT's
adjudication language: a prior round "would have added process cost
but little protection beyond those safeguards."

## §5 — BUNDLING (the default when a round IS warranted)

Rounds are BATCHED. Multiple qualifying items ride in ONE dispatch —
one block, one paste — unless the founder directs otherwise or an
item is time-critical (e.g. it blocks a running campaign). The worker
assembles; the founder pastes once. A dedicated round for a single
non-blocking item is a workflow error.

## §6 — DECISION CHECKLIST (worker, before any dispatch)

- [ ] Which of the three §2 triggers applies? Name it explicitly.
- [ ] If none: fix it, applying §4 in full, and record that the
      economy rule was applied and why.
- [ ] If a trigger applies: can it wait for the next natural bundle?
      If yes, hold it there and say so in the record.
- [ ] Does the dispatch pose a question the panel can answer in one
      line each, with frozen vocabulary?
- [ ] Does it state the binding force of their answer IN ADVANCE
      (what a majority fixes, what a split leaves open)?
- [ ] Does it hand them the strongest attacks on our own position?

## §9 — Claim hygiene (adopted VOLUNTARILY, CONV-022 Q5, single-seat)

A majority found the worker's conduct DISCIPLINED and mandated no
constraint. One seat (GPT) nonetheless stated a discipline worth
keeping, and it is adopted here voluntarily — provenance flagged so
the record does not overstate its authority:

**Before interpreting any positive result, the record must identify
(a) the independent statistic being used, (b) that statistic's
resolution floor, and (c) the preregistered falsification criterion.**

Rationale from this programme's own week: two hypotheses were raised
and withdrawn (a commensuration artifact, a Binder crossing) where
step (b) — the resolution floor — had not been computed before the
claim was made. Computing it first would have prevented both
announcements without preventing either discovery.
