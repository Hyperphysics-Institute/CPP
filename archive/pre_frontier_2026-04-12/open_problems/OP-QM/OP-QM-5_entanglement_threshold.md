# OP-QM-5: Entanglement Decoherence Threshold at $\sim 10^{15}$~eV

**Priority:** MEDIUM — falsifiable prediction requiring quantitative backing  
**Status:** OPEN — threshold estimated, not derived  
**Series:** QM#4 (Entanglement and Bell's Inequalities)  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

---

## Statement

Derive the energy threshold $E_\text{thresh}$ above which CPP
predicts entanglement fragility — a departure from the standard QM
prediction that entanglement is energy-independent.

QM#4 estimates $E_\text{thresh} \sim 10^{15}$~eV from Nexus lattice
path limits.  Derive this from the Nexus geometry.

---

## What QM#4 Establishes

Entanglement in CPP arises from shared DI bits conserved via the
Nexus.  The Nexus enforces correlations through atemporal conservation
of a shared $t_\text{abs}$ stamp.  The correlation range is limited by
the maximum lattice path length over which the Nexus can maintain
coherence — i.e., by the number of 600-cell edges in the path.

**The mechanism:** At high enough energy, the de Broglie wavelength of
the entangled particles becomes smaller than the Nexus correlation
length.  The Nexus can no longer maintain the shared $t_\text{abs}$
stamp, and entanglement degrades.

**Predicted observable:** Bell inequality violation parameter $S$
(CHSH) decreases from the QM maximum $2\sqrt{2} \approx 2.83$ toward
the classical limit $S = 2$ for particle energies above
$E_\text{thresh}$.

---

## What Remains

- Express the Nexus correlation length $\xi_\text{Nexus}$ in physical
  units from the 600-cell geometry.
- Derive $E_\text{thresh} = \hbar c / \xi_\text{Nexus}$.
- Verify $E_\text{thresh} \sim 10^{15}$~eV or determine the correct value.
- Compute the functional form of $S(E)$ for $E > E_\text{thresh}$.

## Experimental test
High-energy entangled photon pairs or electron-positron pairs near
$E_\text{thresh}$ would show reduced Bell violation.  Current collider
energies are $\sim 10^4$~GeV $= 10^{13}$~eV — still below the
estimated threshold.  Future multi-PeV accelerators would be needed.
