# OP-EW-5: W$^0$ Virtual Particle — Quantitative Properties

**Priority:** MEDIUM  
**Status:** OPEN  
**Series:** EW#4  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

## Statement

Derive the mass, width, and coupling of the CPP virtual W$^0$ particle
(the neutral weak current mediator before mixing with the B boson).
The Weinberg mixing angle $\theta_W$ emerges from the W$^0$–B mixing
matrix; the CPP derivation of $\sin^2\theta_W \approx 0.231$ requires
a quantitative model of this mixing.

## Feeds Into
- $\sin^2\theta_W$ from CPP geometry
- OP-G-2 (electroweak unification angle)
