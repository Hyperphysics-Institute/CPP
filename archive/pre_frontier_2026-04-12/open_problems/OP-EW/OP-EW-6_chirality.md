# OP-EW-6: Chirality from Eigenvalue-Weighted Phase Bias

**Priority:** MEDIUM  
**Status:** OPEN  
**Series:** EW#5; unified EW v2.1  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

## Statement

Prove that the chirality of the weak interaction (coupling only to
left-handed fermions) arises in CPP from a phase bias in the
eigenvalue-weighted loop traversal of the icosahedral subgraph.
The icosahedral adjacency matrix has eigenvalues $\{+\phi, -1/\phi, ...\}$;
show that only one helicity of CP eigenstates couples to the W
loop geometry.

- Parity violation in CPP (foundational)
- OP-G-2 (full SM gauge structure)
