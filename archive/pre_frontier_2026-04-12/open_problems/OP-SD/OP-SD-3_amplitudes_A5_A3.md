# OP-SD-3: Amplitudes $A_5 = \phi^{-3}/(2\pi)$ and $A_3/A_5$

**Priority:** HIGH  
**Status:** OPEN  
**Series:** SD#2–3  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

## Statement

Derive from first principles:
1. $A_5 = \phi^{-3}/(2\pi)$ — the five-fold symmetric amplitude
2. The ratio $A_3/A_5$ — three-fold to five-fold amplitude ratio

These amplitudes determine the relative probability of icosahedral
vs.\ tetrahedral cage excitations and are the CPP replacements for
QFT coupling constants.

- OP-SD-1, OP-SD-2
