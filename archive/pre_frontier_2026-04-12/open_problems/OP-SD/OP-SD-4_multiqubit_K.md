# OP-SD-4: Many-Body $K$ for Entangled Multi-Qubit States

**Priority:** MEDIUM  
**Status:** OPEN  
**Series:** SD#4–5  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

## Statement

Extend the single-qubit CPP non-locality parameter $K$ to entangled
multi-qubit systems.  Show that $K$ for an $n$-qubit entangled state
is a tensor product of single-qubit $K$ values modified by the
entanglement geometry, and that Bell inequality violations arise
from the CPP DP Sea correlation structure.

## Prerequisite
- OP-SD-1, OP-SD-2

## Feeds Into
- CPP interpretation of quantum non-locality
- Experimental tests with photon pairs
