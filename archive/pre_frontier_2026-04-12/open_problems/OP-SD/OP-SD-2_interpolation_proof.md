# OP-SD-2: Non-Perturbative Proof of the Interpolation Conjecture

**Priority:** HIGH  
**Status:** OPEN  
**Series:** SD#3  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

## Statement

Prove rigorously (or find a counterexample to) the conjecture that
the CPP DP Sea update rule interpolates smoothly between the
classical and quantum limits as the SSV field strength $S \to 0$
and $S \to \infty$ respectively.

Perturbative evidence: the weak-field expansion reproduces standard
QM Born rule; strong-field limit gives classical determinism.
A non-perturbative proof would establish that CPP is a valid
completion of QM for all field strengths.

- OP-SD-1 (explicit $K_0$)
