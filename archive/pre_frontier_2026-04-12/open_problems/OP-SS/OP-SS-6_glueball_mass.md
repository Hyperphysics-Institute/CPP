## Statement

Compute the mass of the lightest scalar glueball using the CPP
$f_\text{geom}$ formula (EW companion C2 / EW\#2), applied to
closed hDP loops on the tetrahedral cage without a central qCP.

Lattice QCD prediction: lightest scalar glueball at $\sim 1.5$–$2$~GeV.

---

## What is Known

In CPP, boson masses arise from the SSV compression energy:
$$M_X = \frac{\text{sea\_strength} \cdot \hbar c}{l_P} \cdot f_\text{geom}(X)$$

For the EW bosons:
- W (bracelet): $f_\text{geom} = 0.219$
- Z (icosahedron): $f_\text{geom} = 0.263$
- H (dodecahedron): $f_\text{geom} = 0.1415$
- photon/gluon (open path): $f_\text{geom} = 0$

A glueball is a bound state of pure gluonic matter — a closed hDP
loop without a central qCP.  Its $f_\text{geom}$ is set by the
geometry of that loop.

**Key connection:** the glueball mass arises from the same formula
that gives the EW boson masses.  This would be the first instance of
the EW and strong sectors sharing a single formula.

## What Remains

- Compute $f_\text{geom}$ for this subgraph.
- Verify that the resulting mass falls in the 1.5–2~GeV range.
