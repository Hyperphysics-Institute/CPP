# OP-SS-2: Three SM Generations from Cage Geometry

**Priority:** HIGH  
**Status:** OPEN — coincidence noted, not proved  
**Series:** SS\#1  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

---

## Statement

Prove that the three SM quark generations arise from a single geometric
principle of the 600-cell, and that the same principle produces exactly
three — not two, not four.

---

## What is Known

Two independent observations point to the same answer but neither is
yet a proof:

**Observation 1 (cage depth, SS\#1):** Thomas's quark architecture
groups the six quarks into three generation pairs by cage depth:

| Generation | Cage depths | Quarks |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 0 | (up, down) |
| 2nd | 1–2 | (strange, charm) |
| 3rd | 3–4 | (bottom, top) |

**Observation 2 (eigenvalue pairs, SS\#1 Remark):** The six 600-cell
adjacency eigenvalues $\{12,\ 1+\phi,\ \phi-1,\ 1-\phi,\ -\phi,\
-(1+\phi)\}$ also naturally group into three conjugate pairs:
$\{12, -(1+\phi)\}$, $\{1+\phi, -(1+\phi)\}$... (pairing to be
made precise).

Whether these two observations share a common geometric origin is the
question.

---

## What Remains

- Identify the precise eigenvalue-pairing that matches the cage-depth
  pairing.
- Show that the 600-cell has exactly three such pairs (and not more).
- This would prove that the 600-cell geometry forces three generations.
