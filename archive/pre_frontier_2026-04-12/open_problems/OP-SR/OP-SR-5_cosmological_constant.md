# OP-SR-5: Cosmological Constant from Vacuum DP Sea ZBW Energy Density

**Priority:** MEDIUM — mentioned in GR paper conclusion; deferred to cosmology companion  
**Status:** OPEN — mechanism proposed; quantitative derivation missing  
**Series:** GR companion §7 (cosmology)  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

## Statement

Derive the observed cosmological constant:

$$\Lambda_\text{obs} \approx 1.1 \times 10^{-52}\ \text{m}^{-2}$$

from the background ZBW energy density of the equilibrium DP Sea,
showing why CPP gives the observed value rather than the QFT estimate
(which is 120 orders of magnitude too large).

## The GR Paper Proposal

$$\Lambda \sim |\text{SSV}|_\text{abs,vacuum} / c^2$$

where $|\text{SSV}|_\text{abs,vacuum}$ is the background ZBW energy
density of the DP Sea in the absence of matter.

## Why This Is Hard

The QFT "cosmological constant problem" is the worst fine-tuning
problem in physics:

| Source | Λ estimate |
|---|---|
| Observed (dark energy) | 1.1 × 10⁻⁵² m⁻² |
| QFT vacuum energy | ~10⁶⁸ m⁻² |
| Ratio | 10¹²⁰ |

CPP must explain why the DP Sea ZBW energy density — which is
certainly non-zero (all CPs oscillate at ν_ZBW = 1/(2t_P)) —
gives a vacuum energy contribution that is 120 orders of magnitude
smaller than the naive QFT estimate.

**Possible CPP resolution:** The ZBW oscillations of paired eCPs in
the DP Sea cancel in pairs. The residual vacuum energy is from
*unpaired* CPs at the boundary of DP regions. This boundary energy
scales as the surface area of DP domains, not their volume — a
possible suppression mechanism. Needs quantitative development.
