## Statement

Derive the initial cosmological expansion rate (Hubble parameter
$H_0$ and its time evolution) from the CP/GP density ratio at the
initial Moment, without invoking an inflaton field.

## The CPP Mechanism (from GR paper)

At $t = 0$, the total CP count exceeded the available GP destinations,
forcing pairwise repulsion under the CP Exclusion Rule (one CP per
GP per tick). This repulsion drives the initial expansion — inflation
is kinetically necessary, not an independent field.

The Hubble parameter:
$$H_0 = \dot{a}/a$$

should emerge from the ratio (total CPs) / (available GPs at t=0).
