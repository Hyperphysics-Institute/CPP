## Overview

SS-1 derives the SU(3) colour gauge algebra exactly from the tetrahedral cage geometry of the 600-cell lattice. The derivation includes 9 theorems: SU(3) generators from cage face permutations, gluon masslessness from topological face closure, the one-loop beta function coefficient β₀ = 7, asymptotic freedom, and 33 Gell-Mann–Okubo mass relations verified by Monte Carlo. The sea_strength parameter (≈ 0.178) is derived from the 600-cell Voronoi geometry with a 3.8% residual.
