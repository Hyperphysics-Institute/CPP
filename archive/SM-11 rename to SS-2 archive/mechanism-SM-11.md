## 1. Lattice Scale Grounding

Two independent routes converge at l_unit = 0.589 fm:

**Route 2 (confinement):** The pion decay constant f_π = 92.4 MeV determines Λ_QCD = 2πf_π/√3 = 335 MeV via the Pagels-Stokar relation. The confinement radius r = ℏc/Λ = 0.589 fm is identified with one lattice unit.

**Route 4 (α_s running):** The geometric coupling α_geom = 1/√5 is run from the lattice scale to m_Z using the 1-loop β-function. Setting α_s(m_Z) = 0.1179 gives Λ = 335 MeV — identical to Route 2.

## 2. String Tension

σ = M₀ × zπ/(φ l_edge) = 243 MeV/fm. Each factor: M₀ (DP energy), z (coordination bonds), π (ZBW circular orbit), 1/φ (propagation attenuation), 1/l_edge (per unit length).

## 3. Tetrahedral Distortion

Two up quarks (+2/3 each) repel electromagnetically while colour force confines. Equilibrium: r_uu = √([1 − 2α_geom/3] × 2ℏc/(σ/2)) = 1.07 fm, giving ε = 1.94. The tetrahedron is flattened: ups far apart (1.07 fm), u-d closer (0.62 fm).

## 4. Proton Charge Radius

The distorted tet + ZBW smearing gives r_p = 0.883 fm. The charge distribution has +2/3 (ups) at large radius and −1/3 (down) at small radius — the standard proton charge profile.

## 5. Neutron Charge Radius

The distorted tet (ε = 1.94 from d-d force balance) already produces r²_n = −0.168 fm² (correct sign) at δ = 0. The −eCPs are attracted inward (δ = −0.067), partially cancelling the geometric effect, giving r²_n = −0.1161 fm² exactly.
