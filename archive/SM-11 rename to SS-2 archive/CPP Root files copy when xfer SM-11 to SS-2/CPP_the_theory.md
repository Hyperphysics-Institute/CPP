From these two postulates, everything else follows.

### Chapter 2: The Vacuum — A Sea of Paired Consciousness

The vacuum is not empty. It is filled with Dipole Pairs (DPs) — bound pairs of opposite-polarity CPs sitting on adjacent lattice vertices. The DP Sea is the medium through which all forces propagate and all particles exist.

There are five species of paired structures in the vacuum:

- **eDP** (electric Dipole Pair): +eCP bound to −eCP. Carries the electric sector.
- **qDP** (quark Dipole Pair): +qCP bound to −qCP. Carries the colour sector.
- **hDP type A**: +eCP bound to −qCP. A hybrid pair crossing the electric/quark boundary.
- **hDP type B**: −eCP bound to +qCP. The complementary hybrid.
- **hTetra** (hybrid Tetrahedron): 2 eCPs + 2 qCPs in a tetrahedral arrangement with 4 attractive bonds and 2 repulsive bonds. More stable than simple DPs but requiring activation energy to assemble.

The vacuum at standard conditions is a dense mix of all five species. The relative abundances were set during a cosmological phase transition (the CPP analogue of electroweak symmetry breaking) when the universe cooled enough for hTetras to precipitate from the simpler DP medium.

The DP Sea is not the lattice. The lattice is the mathematical scaffolding — every Grid Point exists whether or not a CP occupies it. The DP Sea is the physical medium — the set of all CPs actually present on the lattice. Not all Grid Points are occupied. The occupancy fraction at standard conditions is close to 1 but not exactly 1; the small deviations may account for fine corrections in the predictions. [Founders Vision, Sections 2-3]

### Chapter 3: Forces from Geometry

Every force in CPP arises from the lattice geometry. There are no force-carrying particles in the conventional sense — no photons, gluons, or W/Z bosons as fundamental entities. Instead, forces are displacement patterns propagating through the DP Sea.

**The Space Stress Vector (SSV):** At each Grid Point, at each Moment, the total force is decomposed into two components:

- **SSV_abs** (absolute): The scalar sum of all signal magnitudes arriving at that point. Always positive. Determines the local speed of light, the effective lattice spacing (via subgraph selection in the nested 600-cell hierarchy), and all gravitational/relativistic effects.

- **SSV_net** (net): The vector sum of all signals. Determines the actual displacement of a CP — which direction it moves and how far.

SSV_abs sets the speed limit. SSV_net determines how much of that limit is used. A CP in a high-SSV_abs region (near a massive object) has a shorter maximum displacement per Moment — this IS time dilation. The same CP receiving a large SSV_net moves as far as its limit allows in that direction. This decomposition is the complete mechanism for special and general relativity. [SR-1; Founders Vision, Section 12]

The 600-cell lattice supports two fundamentally different types of displacement propagation, distinguished by their dimensionality:
