\subsection{Shell broadcast and the scalar source}

In CPP, every mass-energy concentration broadcasts its presence through
the Dipole Sea by the same shell-broadcast geometry that carries the
Coulomb interaction: Displacement Increment bits (DI-bits) fan outward
at every hop to all outward-component neighbours, so that the integrated
influence crossing any concentric shell is conserved and the per-site
influence falls as the inverse square of the shell radius.  The
gravitational case differs from the electromagnetic case in exactly one
respect, established in c05: \emph{the source is mass-energy, a positive
scalar, rather than charge polarity, a signed quantity}.  Two
consequences follow immediately and with no adjustable input:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=2em]
\item \textbf{Universal attraction.}  Mass-energy SSV has no polarity;
every ZBW Dipole Particle unit contributes compressive SSV in all
directions with no cancellation.
\item \textbf{The coupling.}  $G = \hbar c/\mP^2$, fixed entirely by the
Planck mass already determined by the 600-cell lattice.  The weakness of
gravity relative to electromagnetism is the pure Planck-unit ratio
$F_{\rm grav}/F_{\rm EM} = (m/\mP)^2/(e/e_P)^2$.
\end{itemize}
This is the Coulomb--Newton half of the unification: one broadcast
geometry, two source types.

\subsection{The nonlinear response}

The substrate's response to accumulated stress is the Planck Sphere
Radius formula
\begin{equation}
\PSR_{\rm eff}(r) \;=\; \frac{\lP}{1 + k\,\Delta|\SSV|(r)},
\label{eq:psr}
\end{equation}
which is \emph{already nonlinear}: it is not a linearisation awaiting
higher-order corrections, but the full constitutive statement.  Combined
with the shell-broadcast source relation $k\,\Delta|\SSV| = GM/rc^2$
(exact, from c05), Eq.~\eqref{eq:psr} is the single input from which the
strong-field results of c08 follow.  This is the reason one mechanism
spans four regimes: expanding Eq.~\eqref{eq:psr} to zeroth order in
$k\,\Delta|\SSV|$ gives flat space; to first order, Newtonian gravity
and the weak-field metric; keeping it unexpanded gives the exact
Schwarzschild solution.  The regimes are not separate theories patched
together --- they are Taylor truncations of one response, and the paper
series is organised accordingly.  Every ``generates'' and ``exact'' in
this thesis carries the conditionality stated in the abstract: given the
PSR constitutive form at its W2-strength grounding, not derived
unconditionally from the axioms.

\section{The scalar--vector transition, argued at series level}
\label{sec:scalarvector}

The step from c05 to c07 is where gravity stops resembling
electrostatics, and it deserves an argument rather than an assertion;
this section supplies it at series level.

c05 works entirely with the \emph{absolute} SSV scalar
$|\SSV|_{\rm abs}$: a positive stress density sourced by the compressive
polarization energy $E_{\rm pol} = mc^2$ of the source's eDP cloud.  A
scalar stress field can slow clocks --- it sources $g_{tt}$, time
curvature --- but it cannot by itself curve space: there is no direction
in it.  Newtonian gravity, which is blind to spatial curvature, is
exactly the theory of the scalar alone.

The observational fact that forces the extension is the factor of two in
light deflection: the Newtonian (time-curvature-only) calculation gives
$0.875''$ at the solar limb, and the measured value is $1.75''$.  c07
resolves this by extending the DI-bit broadcast to carry, alongside the
scalar, the \emph{net} SSV vector $\mathbf{\SSV}_{\rm net}$, whose
directional information sources the spatial components $g_{ij}$.  The
Lattice State Packet (LSP) is the two-component broadcast object:
$|\SSV|_{\rm abs} = E_{\rm pol}/V_0$ sources $g_{tt}$;
$\mathbf{\SSV}_{\rm net}$ sources $g_{ij}$.  In the weak field the two
contributions to null deflection are equal, which is precisely the
GR factor of two --- a retroactive prediction of the two-component LSP
structure rather than a fitted correction.

At series level the transition is therefore not an ad hoc enrichment but
a forced move: a scalar broadcast cannot produce equal temporal and
spatial curvature contributions, the data demand them, and the vector
component is the minimal directional content the broadcast can carry.
Everything downstream of c07 --- the exact static metric of c08, the
rotational (azimuthal $\SSV_{\rm net}$) extension to Kerr in c11, the
charged extension to Kerr--Newman in c12 --- is built on the
two-component LSP, with rotation and charge entering as new
\emph{configurations} of the same broadcast content rather than new
mechanisms.

\label{sec:exact}

\begin{claim}[Exact static solution --- established in c08]

the PSR response Eq.~\eqref{eq:psr} yield the line element
\begin{equation}
ds^2 \;=\; -\left(\frac{1-\vrho}{1+\vrho}\right)^{\!2} c^2\,dt^2
\;+\; (1+\vrho)^4 \left( d\riso^2 + \riso^2\, d\Omega^2 \right),
\label{eq:iso}
\end{equation}
which is \emph{exactly} the Schwarzschild metric in isotropic
coordinates, with no approximation and no free parameters.  Standard
Schwarzschild coordinates follow by the transformation
$r = \riso(1+\vrho)^2$.
\end{claim}

``exact Schwarzschild \emph{in isotropic coordinates}'' is checkable,
and the identity of Eq.~\eqref{eq:iso} with the standard form under

verify script (\texttt{code/3228\_classical\_tests\_verify.py}, check~0).
