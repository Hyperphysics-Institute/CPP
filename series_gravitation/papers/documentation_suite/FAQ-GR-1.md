# FAQ — GR-1

**Q1. If the metric is exactly Schwarzschild, does this theory predict
anything general relativity doesn't?**
Not in the four classical tests, and the paper says so in the same
breath as it reports them: those tests discriminate CPP from Newton, not
from GR. Equivalence at those orders *is* the claim. Where the theories
can differ is in the substrate regime — the Planck core at r_S/2 instead
of a singularity, the echo and evaporation signatures of the companions,
and the strong-field census-speed note. A theory that reproduced GR
everywhere and differed nowhere would be a rewriting; this one is
honest that most of the tested regime is a rewriting.

**Q2. Isn't "no free parameters" doing a lot of work when the whole
thing rests on the PSR formula?**
Yes, and CONV-026 sustained exactly that objection 3–2. The abstract now
carries the conditionality in its first sentence: everything is
conditional on the PSR constitutive form, whose SR-1 grounding stands at
W2 *viability* strength — viable, not derived. Within that form nothing
is fitted; the coupling G = ħc/m_P² comes from the lattice. The
conditional is one premise, stated, not a family of tuned dials.

**Q3. What's the difference between recovering solutions and deriving
equations, and why did the paper make such a point of it?**
One can reproduce specific metrics without possessing the general field
equation those metrics solve — and a reader trained in GR will make that
distinction whether or not the author does. V0 made it first, registered
the gap as OPEN-GR-FE-1, and declined to claim the equations. The gap
was closed in Session 150 (companion GR-1j), which is why the ledger's
V0 rows now carry Status-at-V1.0 notes rather than deletions.

**Q4. So does CPP now derive the Einstein field equations?**
No, and this is the boundary most worth being precise about. What is
derived is T-1: a variable-speed wave equation for the census potential,
whose static sector is exactly the corrected GR-1c Proposition. T-2 gives
uniqueness (exact) and a *conditional* Birkhoff-type theorem — conditional
because the bare equation admits monopole radiation, and staticity is
enforced by census conservation rather than by the equation. T-3 gives a
conserved current, not a rank-2 energy-momentum tensor. Full rank-2
equivalence is bounded at op:einstein, which remains open.

**Q5. Where does the factor of two in light deflection come from?**
From the two-component LSP broadcast. The scalar channel |SSV|_abs
sources clock rate (g_tt) and gives 0.875″ at the solar limb — the
Newtonian answer. The vector channel **SSV**_net sources the spatial
metric (g_ij) and contributes equally in the weak field, giving 1.75″.
This is the arc's cleanest discriminant against scalar-only substrate
theories, which cannot reach the measured value without adding
machinery.

**Q6. If space is a rigid flat lattice, what is "curved spacetime"?**
A continuum shadow. Rulers and clocks shrink according to the PSR
response; the lattice never bends. GR-1j turns this from a picture into
a derivation: because the absolute lattice is flat, lattice coordinates
*are* isotropic coordinates — which explains why the arc's exact
solution was always most naturally written that way.

**Q7. Why is cosmology missing from a gravitation series?**
By declaration, not by oversight (§8). FRW solutions belong to
OPEN-EU-1 and the dark-energy programme; a local-gravitation arc that
quietly extended itself to cosmology would be claiming ground it hadn't
worked. The scope boundary is stated so a reader doesn't have to
discover it.

**Q8. The paper inherits from SR-1 — didn't parts of SR-1 get
withdrawn?**
Yes, and §7 states it in full rather than leaving it to be found: SR-1's
prediction set was withdrawn at Patch 2474 and its class-coverage
theorem at Patch 2475. What this arc inherits is the PSR machinery at
W2 strength. A consolidation silent about partially withdrawn ground
would hand a hostile reader their finding.

**Q9. What would falsify the arc?**
A measurement isolating spatial from temporal curvature contributions
that returned anything other than equal weak-field shares would break
the two-component LSP. A demonstration that the PSR response requires a
second mechanism somewhere in the four regimes would break the arc's
central claim of unity. Below those: any of the companions' own
falsifiers, and — for the equation layer — a census-conserving isolated
source shown to radiate a monopole.

**Q10. What's still open?**
op:einstein (the dynamic/rank-2 sector) is the arc's frontier — a future
charter, not unfinished business from this paper. The W2 grounding of
the PSR form is the standing conditional, owned by the relativity
programme. Cosmology is elsewhere by design.
