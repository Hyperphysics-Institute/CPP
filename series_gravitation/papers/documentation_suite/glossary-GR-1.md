# Glossary — GR-1

- **SSV (Space Stress Vector)** — the substrate's local stress state.
  Two channels matter for gravitation: |SSV|_abs, a positive scalar
  sourced by compressive polarization energy, and **SSV**_net, its
  directional part.
- **Δ|SSV|** — the local excess of absolute SSV over the ambient Sea
  value; the gravitational potential variable of the arc. The shell
  relation k·Δ|SSV| = GM/rc² is exact (GR-1a).
- **PSR (Planck Sphere Radius)** — the reach of a Grid Point's service
  volume in one Moment. The constitutive response
  PSR_eff = l_P/(1 + k·Δ|SSV|) is the arc's single nonlinearity and its
  single conditional premise (W2 strength, inherited from SR-1).
- **Shell broadcast** — DI-bits fan outward at every hop to all
  outward-component neighbours, conserving integrated influence across
  concentric shells; this geometry supplies the 1/r² falloff shared by
  Coulomb and Newton.
- **DI-bit** — Displacement Increment bit: the conserved messenger
  conscious-point type, emitted at fixed per-GP count each Moment and
  delivered at the PSR shell.
- **LSP (Lattice State Packet)** — the two-component broadcast object:
  |SSV|_abs sources g_tt (clock rate); **SSV**_net sources g_ij (spatial
  metric). The two-component structure is what produces the factor of
  two in light deflection.
- **Scalar–vector transition** — the step (GR-1a → GR-1b) at which a
  clock-only scalar theory, which reproduces Newton and half the
  deflection, is extended by the vector channel to reproduce GR's full
  deflection.
- **Isotropic coordinates** — the coordinate system in which the arc's
  exact solution is naturally written. GR-1j later derives *why*: the
  absolute lattice is flat, so lattice coordinates ARE isotropic
  coordinates; only rulers and clocks shrink.
- **Planck core** — the regularization PSR_eff ≥ l_P/2, which excludes
  the point singularity and fixes r_core = r_S/2 (GR-1c Theorem 2).
- **Epistemic ledger** — §6 of this paper: the arc's aggregated
  statement of what is exact, what was correspondence-level, and what is
  conditional or inherited. Its correspondence rows were superseded at
  V1.0 and retained verbatim.
- **W2 viability strength** — the grading of the PSR form's SR-1
  grounding: viable, not derived. Every "exact" in the arc sits under it.
- **op:einstein** — the arc's registered open frontier: the dynamic,
  rank-2 sector. Equivalence to the full Einstein equations is not
  claimed anywhere in this series.
