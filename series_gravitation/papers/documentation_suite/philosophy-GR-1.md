# Philosophy — GR-1

**A parent paper is an act of aggregation, and aggregation is where
theories get caught.** The eight companions each hedged locally — GR-1b
carries six limitation-flagged passages, GR-1c seven — but nothing had
ever summed them, so the arc's true standing was invisible even to its
own author. §6's epistemic ledger exists to make the sum visible, and
its first act was unflattering: it recorded that reproducing the
Schwarzschild, Kerr, and Kerr–Newman *solutions* is not deriving the
*equations* those solutions solve, and that a reader in general
relativity would make exactly that distinction. The paper set the
standard it would later be held to, in writing, before anyone else could
set it.

**The standard was then met — and the ledger row was kept anyway.**
When OPEN-GR-FE-1 closed, the honest move was not to delete the row that
had named the gap but to mark it superseded and say what was delivered
at what strength. A programme that erases its own open problems once
they close leaves no evidence it ever took them seriously. The V0 rows
sit in the shipped paper verbatim, each followed by what became of it.

**Curvature as shadow.** The deepest philosophical content of the arc is
not a formula but a reframing: nothing bends. The lattice is rigid and
absolutely flat; what conventional physics writes as curved spacetime is
the continuum shadow of a conserved messenger census, expressed as
shrunken rulers and slowed clocks. That the exact solution comes out in
*isotropic* coordinates was a curiosity for as long as it was an
observation; it became an explanation the moment GR-1j derived it from
the lattice's flatness. Explaining a coordinate preference is a modest
thing to be proud of, and it is exactly the kind of thing an emergent
theory should be able to do that its continuum parent cannot.

**One mechanism, honestly one.** The claim that the arc has no second
mechanism is falsifiable by inspection: anywhere a companion needed
machinery not derivable from the PSR response and the shell broadcast,
the claim would fail. It has not, across eleven papers — but the reason
this is stated as a CP/GP Signature line rather than a triumph is that
the entire structure rests on one unearned premise, and the paper's own
abstract says so in its first sentence rather than its last.

**Conditionality in the first sentence, not the footnote.** CONV-026
returned 3–2 OVERCLAIMS and 3–2 RESTATE-REQUIRED against V0 — not
because the ledger was wrong (it was right; the panel said so 4–1) but
because the abstract had outrun it. The restate promoted the W2
conditionality into the thesis. The lesson generalizes past this paper:
a correct hedge in §6 does not cure an unhedged claim in §0, because
readers quote abstracts.
