# Development log — GR-1 (Local Gravitation from SSV Shell Broadcast)

## Session 149, 19 Aug 2026 — the parent the arc never had

**Vignette 1 — eight papers, no thesis.** The gravitational work existed
as eight companions (c05, c07–c13) with no series parent. Nothing stated
the unifying claim once, and nothing aggregated the hedges: GR-1b
carried six limitation-flagged passages and GR-1c seven, each honest
locally, none summed. GR-1 V0 (Patch 3228) was assembled to do both
jobs. Its most consequential decision was a self-imposed restriction:
**solutions, not equations.** Reproducing Schwarzschild, Kerr, and
Kerr–Newman exactly is not the same as deriving the field equation those
metrics solve, and rather than blur the line the paper drew it, declined
the stronger claim, and registered OPEN-GR-FE-1. The classical tests
went in as a results-only table with all four derivations deferred to a
dedicated companion (OPEN-GR-TESTS-1) — the corrected form of a founder
instruction that had first placed them inside GR-1. Verify: 8/8, with
two numerical traps documented for the eventual companion implementer.

**Vignette 2 — the panel takes the abstract, not the ledger.** CONV-026
bundled GR-1 V0 with the spin arc. The tests table passed 5–0 with
Grok's execution fingerprint-credited, and the ledger passed 4–1. What
failed was the front matter: Q1 3–2 OVERCLAIMS and Q4 3–2
RESTATE-REQUIRED, on the abstract's "exact … with no free parameters"
and the §1 thesis sentence. The substance was unanimous in direction —
even the two SHIP seats specified the same amendment — so V0.2 (Patch
3243) promoted the W2 conditionality into the thesis and left the ledger
alone, because the ledger had been right all along. A targeted
confirmation pass returned 3/3 (Patch 3247) and the cycle closed.

## Session 150, 19–20 Aug 2026 — both open problems discharged, and the ship

**Vignette 3 — the promises kept.** GR-1's two registered open problems
were closed by dedicated companions in a single arc. GR-1i derived all
four classical tests on the isotropic metric and cleared CONV-029
unanimously (OPEN-GR-TESTS-1 finally discharged). OPEN-GR-FE-1 ran
charter → founder physical-picture session → T-1 from the messenger
census → CONV-027 → T-2/T-3 → CONV-028 → founder confirmation, and
produced GR-1j. Along the way the derivation's own HALT check caught a
defective compensator formula in the *already-shipped* GR-1c, which was
diagnosed, corrected, approved 5–0, ratified, and enacted with the
defective formula preserved. GR-1 V1.0 shipped at Patch 3270 with the
abstract and Open Problems moved to the closed state.

**Vignette 4 — the founder asks whether the protocol was actually
followed.** It had not been. The PPP audit (Patch 3271) found GR-1
shipped V1.0 *missing* Keywords, Plain Language Summary, and the CP/GP
Signature subsection; the arc's other ten papers were missing pieces
too, and every documentation suite was changelog-only. The honest answer
was given, V1.0.1 fixed this paper, and OPEN-GR-PPP-1 was registered as
the arc-wide completion program (W-A formatting, W-B suites ×11, W-C
anthology chapters).

## Session 151, 20 Aug 2026 — the suite, and what writing it found

**Vignette 5 — the ledger contradicted the abstract.** Building this
suite meant reading GR-1 as a claim structure. The abstract said
OPEN-GR-FE-1 was CLOSED; the Open Problems section said CLOSED; the
epistemic ledger's correspondence rows still said the field-equation
derivation "is not attempted in V0" and that Birkhoff-type uniqueness
and the energy-momentum object "are likewise open ... the corpus
currently contains neither." All three sentences were in the shipped
paper. The V1.0 prep had checked the ledger's *conditionality* — which
was correct — and not its *status rows*. V1.0.2 (Patch 3276) corrected
them anti-erasure: the V0 rows retained verbatim under a superseded
heading, each followed by what was delivered and at what strength,
including what is still not claimed (rank-2 equivalence, op:einstein).
A second, smaller defect surfaced beside it: the title block's version
line had lagged at "Version 0 (assembly draft)" — the same defect class
caught in SPIN-3 six patches earlier, now flagged as a corpus-wide grep
for the PPP program. The suite itself followed at Patch 3277.
