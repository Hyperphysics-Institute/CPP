# Mechanism — GR-1

The arc's physical engine, in causal order. Every step is conditional on
step 3's constitutive form.

1. **Matter loads the substrate.** A mass-energy concentration polarizes
   its Dipole Sea; the compressive polarization energy E_pol = mc² is a
   positive scalar stress density — mass-energy has no polarity, so
   nothing cancels. This is the whole reason gravity is universally
   attractive while electromagnetism is not: same broadcast, different
   source type.
2. **The load is broadcast by shell geometry.** DI-bits fan outward to
   all outward-component neighbours at every hop. Integrated influence
   across any concentric shell is conserved, so per-site influence falls
   as 1/r² — the identical geometry that carries Coulomb's law. The
   source relation k·Δ|SSV| = GM/rc² is exact, and the coupling is not
   fitted: G = ħc/m_P², with m_P already fixed by the 600-cell lattice.
3. **The substrate responds nonlinearly.** PSR_eff = l_P/(1 + k·Δ|SSV|).
   This is the full constitutive statement, not a linearization awaiting
   corrections — and it is the arc's single premise beyond the axioms
   (W2 strength).
4. **The regimes are truncations, not theories.** Expand step 3 to
   zeroth order in k·Δ|SSV| → flat space. First order → Newtonian
   gravity and the weak-field metric. Unexpanded → the exact
   Schwarzschild solution in isotropic coordinates. No patching occurs
   between regimes because there is nothing to patch: one expression is
   being read at different strengths.
5. **The vector channel supplies the factor of two.** A scalar stress
   field slows clocks (sources g_tt) but has no direction in it, so it
   cannot curve space; scalar-only gravity deflects starlight by 0.875″,
   half the measured value. The LSP's second component, **SSV**_net,
   sources g_ij; in the weak field the two contributions to null
   deflection are equal, giving 1.75″. This is the arc's sharpest
   internal discriminant — it separates CPP from any scalar-only
   substrate theory.
6. **The core is regular.** PSR_eff ≥ l_P/2 caps the response, excluding
   the point singularity and fixing r_core = r_S/2.
7. **The equation underneath (V1.0, companion GR-1j).** The same
   conserved census that produced steps 1–2 yields the general field
   equation directly: statics are exactly flat-lattice Laplace, and
   time-reversal invariance forces a two-level relay whose continuum
   limit is a variable-speed wave equation (T-1). The static sector is
   provably the same equation as the corrected GR-1c Proposition,
   written in two variables.

**The load-bearing novelty is step 4.** Most emergent-gravity proposals
recover Newton and then add machinery to reach GR. Here the strong-field
result is not an addition; it is what happens when you stop expanding.
