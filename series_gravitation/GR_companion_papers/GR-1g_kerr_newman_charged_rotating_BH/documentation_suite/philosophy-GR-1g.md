# Philosophy — GR-1g

**A closing paper, and the modesty that suits one.** This is the paper
that finishes the black-hole family, and its content is largely
addition: three broadcast components already derived elsewhere, brought
together. It makes no attempt to dress that up. The interesting claim is
structural — that the three superpose *without interference terms* and
reproduce Kerr–Newman's exact form — and the evidence for it is that
every limit checks out and the extremality bound comes out matching.

**Why the no-hair count is not a coincidence here.** General relativity
tells us a stationary black hole is fixed by mass, charge, and angular
momentum, and offers no reason there should be exactly three. In this
substrate there is a reason of sorts: three source types produce three
broadcast components, and there is nothing else to broadcast. That is a
satisfying reframing rather than a proof — the theory has not shown a
fourth channel is impossible — and the suite states it at that strength.

**The same rule kills a second kind of singularity.** GR-1c's CP
Exclusion bound removed a *point* singularity; here the same bound
removes a *ring*. A cutoff introduced to tame one divergence is
unremarkable; the same untouched rule handling a structurally different
divergence in a different geometry is the sort of thing that is hard to
arrange after the fact. It is the strongest indirect support the
Exclusion argument has, and worth noticing precisely because the paper
mentions it almost in passing.

**Honesty about relevance.** The paper notes that charge neutralises
rapidly for most astrophysical holes, so the charged branch is a
limiting case rather than the generic history. A paper less secure in
its purpose would have left that out. Saying it costs nothing and tells
the reader what the result is for: completeness of the family, not a
prediction about objects in the sky.

**An open-problem list that aged well.** Three of four items are still
genuinely open and the fourth only moved partway. After four consecutive
suites turning up overtaken sections, this one is close to clean — which
sharpens the earlier finding rather than softening it. Status sections
rot where the programme actually advanced past them, and the programme
has not advanced into charged evaporation, charged echoes, or the
all-orders rotating-charged structure.
