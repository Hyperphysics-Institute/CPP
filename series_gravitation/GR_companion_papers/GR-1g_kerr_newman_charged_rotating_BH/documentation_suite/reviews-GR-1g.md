# Reviews — GR-1g

**Review basis: NONE.** No dedicated five-seat CONV round exists for
GR-1g, and no adjudication record.

**Inherited support, and how thin it is.** This paper is a composition
of three earlier results, so its standing is no better than theirs:

- The **scalar** component (GR-1c's exact Schwarzschild) is the only
  piece with strong external confirmation — verified to machine
  precision within CONV-029's clearance of GR-1i. But GR-1c's *own*
  theorems (exact Schwarzschild, Planck core) were never reviewed;
  CONV-027 covered only its field-equation Proposition.
- The **azimuthal** component and the subluminality argument come from
  GR-1f, which has had no review at all — including the Kerr bound
  theorem that this paper generalizes to M² ≥ a² + Q².
- The **radial charge** component is inherited from the Stiffness C
  companion, outside this arc.

The composition therefore rests on three unreviewed foundations, and the
bound derived here inherits whatever exposure GR-1f's pattern-velocity
argument carries.

**What has NOT been reviewed:** the additivity claim (that three SSV
components superpose without interference terms); the derivation of
Δ_KN by adding the Coulomb contribution to the Kerr Δ; the Kerr–Newman
bound; and the Planck ring-core argument.

**Where a future round should start: additivity.** The paper's whole
method is that the three components add. In general relativity the
Einstein–Maxwell system is nonlinear and charge does *not* simply
superpose onto vacuum solutions in general — the Kerr–Newman form works
out, but for reasons internal to that structure. Whether the CPP
broadcast components genuinely superpose, or whether the agreement with
Δ_KN is being obtained by construction, is the question a panel should
press first.

speed c_* → ~0.29c near the exclusion radius) bears on any bound derived
from a near-horizon velocity reaching c. Unworked here too.
