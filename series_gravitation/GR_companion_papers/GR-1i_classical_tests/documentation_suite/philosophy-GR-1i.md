# Philosophy — GR-1i

**Passing is a requirement, not a triumph.** The paper says this in its
own Plain Language Summary, and the discipline holds for twenty pages
afterward. A theory that recovers Einstein's metric exactly cannot then
present Einstein's test results as its own achievements; the honest
accounting is that these four numbers separate the theory from Newton
and from nothing else. Writing a paper whose central result is "we
scored zero new predictions" takes a kind of restraint that is easy to
praise and hard to practise — the Swarm-Validation section could have
counted four confirmations and nobody outside would have objected.

**Reproduces is not shares.** The DeepSeek seat's contribution at
CONV-029 was to insist that "identical to GR by construction" invites a
misreading — that CPP is GR in other notation. It is not: the mechanism
beneath is a rigid lattice of Grid Points, compressed-DP aggregates, and
a Planck-sphere response, and the agreement is a *derived consequence*
of a wholly different substrate. The distinction matters because it
locates where the theories could ever come apart — not in the
solar-system tests, but in the regimes the companions probe.

**One test is not like the others.** Three of the four read the
first-order gradient; perihelion precession reads the ∝u² term, which is
present only because the PSR response was never expanded. That makes
Mercury's orbit the first observable in history to have seen — without
anyone knowing it — that the substrate's constitutive law is not a
linearization. Whether one finds that a satisfying reframing or an
after-the-fact story is a fair question, and the paper's claim
discipline is what keeps it answerable.

**Falsifiability found by looking, not by inventing.** The achromatic
bending note is the paper's only registered predictive content, and it
was not manufactured: it was noticed that n(r) is frequency-independent
and therefore that chromatic dispersion at the limb would kill the
mechanism as formulated. It is registered *unminted* — a falsifier the
programme declines to count as a prediction because it follows
immediately from what is already claimed. The prediction ledger stays
honest at the cost of looking less impressive.

**Documenting the traps.** Two numerical traps — φ-accumulation drift
and crossing overshoot — were found on the verify script's first run,
either capable of silently corrupting the answer at percent or ten
percent level against signals of 10⁻⁷ and 10⁻⁶ radians. They are
promoted into the paper body for reimplementers rather than left in a
reasoning fragment. A result nobody can reproduce is a rumour; a result
whose traps are published is a method.
