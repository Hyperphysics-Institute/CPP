# Reviews — GR-1e

**Review basis: NONE.** GR-1e has never had a dedicated five-seat CONV
round, and no adjudication record exists for it.

**Inherited support, and its limits:**

- The paper's entire foundation is GR-1c's Planck core — and **that
  theorem has not been reviewed either.** CONV-027 examined GR-1c's
  field-equation Proposition only. So the CP Exclusion floor
  PSR_eff ≥ l_P/2, on which both the termination argument and the
  stability fixed point rest, is unexamined by anyone outside the
  programme. Stated plainly because the alternative is to imply coverage
  that does not exist.
- The **exterior** physics is standard and independently confirmed: the
  exact Schwarzschild exterior was verified to machine precision
  (3.3×10⁻¹⁶) by two seats within CONV-029. That matters here, because
  it is what licenses the claim that ordinary Hawking evaporation runs
  unchanged for M ≫ m_P.
- **G = ħc/m_P²** from GR-1a supplies N = 2M/m_P, the quantity the
  termination argument turns on. GR-1a is likewise unreviewed, though
  its central relation was independently re-derived from the messenger
  census (GR-1j, T-1).

**What has NOT been reviewed:** the geometric termination proposition;
the claim that geometric breakdown precedes thermal (the T_P/8π
computation); the mechanical-stability fixed point; the information
capacity estimate; and the treatment of the Page-time argument.

**Where a future round should start:** the stability fixed point. The
termination argument is a breakdown-of-validity claim and is relatively
robust — if the semiclassical treatment is undefined at N = 2, that is
hard to dispute. The *stability* claim is stronger and more exposed: it
asserts an exact balance between gravitational compression and exclusion
repulsion at a specific radius, which a reviewer could attack on
dimensional grounds, on the form of the repulsive response, or on
whether "exactly balanced" survives perturbation.
