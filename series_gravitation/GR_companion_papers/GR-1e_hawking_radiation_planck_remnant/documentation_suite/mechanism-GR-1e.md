# Mechanism — GR-1e

In causal order. Nothing is postulated beyond GR-1c.

1. **Standard evaporation runs unchanged.** For M ≫ m_P the Hawking
   calculation is untouched: CPP's exterior is exactly Schwarzschild, so
   the near-horizon physics the semiclassical derivation uses is GR's.
   Total evaporation time is essentially the standard result
   (~2.1×10⁶⁷ yr for a solar mass), since the correction enters as
   (m_P/M₀)³.
2. **The hole shrinks toward the lattice scale.** The
   semiclassical treatment requires the Schwarzschild radius to span
   many lattice cells: N = r_S/l_P = 2M/m_P ≫ 1. As M → m_P, N → 2.
3. **The horizon runs out of room.** At M = m_P, r_S = 2l_P — the
   "horizon" is a boundary of width l_P separating two lattice cells
   from three. Hawking's mechanism needs a smooth thermal gradient
   across a near-horizon *region*, and at this size no such region
   exists. The approximation is not inaccurate; it is **geometrically
   undefined**.
4. **The binding constraint is geometric, not thermal.** One expects
   termination when T_Haw → T_P. It doesn't happen first: at M = m_P,
   T_Haw = T_P/8π ≈ 0.040 T_P — 4% of Planck, well below the lattice
   maximum. Evaporation stops because the geometry gives out, with
   thermal room to spare.
5. **What remains is held up, not merely stranded.** The remnant is
   mechanically stable at a self-sustaining fixed point: gravitational
   SSV compression exactly balanced by CP Exclusion repulsion at
   PSR_eff = l_P/2. No external pressure is invoked, and the balance is
   between two effects already in the theory.
6. **Consequences.** The remnant cannot evaporate further, so it is a
   stable relic; it interacts gravitationally through G = ħc/m_P²; and
   because there is no singularity, nothing destroys the infalling
   information — though whether the remnant plus the radiation train
   preserves unitarity is deferred, not claimed.

**The load-bearing move is step 3→4.** The termination condition is
derived from the *substrate's* discreteness rather than from a
divergence in the effective theory — and the paper then shows that the
obvious alternative mechanism (thermal runaway) demonstrably does not
fire first. That check is what turns a plausible cutoff into a result.
