**Elevator pitch:** Black holes in this theory stop evaporating — but
not for the reason you'd guess. It isn't that the temperature runs away;
at the endpoint the Hawking temperature is only 4% of Planck. It's that
the horizon runs out of *room*: when the Schwarzschild radius spans two
lattice cells, the smooth near-horizon region Hawking's mechanism needs
doesn't exist. What's left is a Planck-mass remnant held up by an
exclusion rule.
