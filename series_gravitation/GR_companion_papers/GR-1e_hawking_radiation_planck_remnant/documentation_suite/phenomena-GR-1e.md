# Phenomena — GR-1e

**Explained (within the CPP ontology):**
- **Why evaporation terminates**, and specifically why it terminates
  *geometrically*: the semiclassical approximation requires
  N = r_S/l_P ≫ 1, and at M ~ m_P the Schwarzschild radius spans two
  lattice cells, so the smooth near-horizon region the Hawking mechanism
  needs does not exist.
- **Why the thermal explanation is not the right one** — a discriminating
  result rather than a hedge. At termination T_Haw = T_P/8π ≈ 0.040 T_P;
  the temperature is nowhere near the lattice maximum. Geometry binds
  first, with a factor of ~25 to spare.
- **Why the remnant is stable rather than merely stranded**: a
  self-sustaining fixed point in which gravitational SSV compression is
  exactly balanced by CP Exclusion repulsion at PSR_eff = l_P/2, with no
  external pressure required.
- **Why the information paradox looks different here**: there is no
  singularity, so nothing destroys the infalling information; the
  question becomes where it resides rather than where it went.

**Predicted:**
- **A stable Planck-mass remnant**, M_rem ~ m_P ≈ 2.18×10⁻⁸ kg, as the
  universal endpoint of black-hole evaporation.
- **Unchanged evaporation time** to observational precision, since the
  CPP modification is confined to the final Planck epoch:
  (m_P/M₀)³ ≪ 1 for any astrophysical hole.
- **Unchanged early-time Hawking radiation**, hence an untouched
  Page-time analysis.
- Implicitly, a **cosmological relic population** if primordial black
  holes formed and have since evaporated — though the paper defers
  deriving the bound.

**Not claimed (the paper's own open problems, all still open):**
- **A full quantum derivation.** The termination argument is geometric
  and thermodynamic; the Bogoliubov-coefficient calculation with the
  inner boundary at r₀ = r_S + l_P — which would determine whether the
  late-time spectrum departs from thermal *before* termination — has not
  been done.
- **Information recovery.** Stated against the theory rather than for
  it: the remnant's capacity is of order k_B, **far below** the original
  Bekenstein–Hawking entropy S_BH = k_B A/4l_P². The paper concludes
  that the resolution must involve the entire radiation train, not the
  remnant — i.e. the remnant alone does not solve the paradox.
- **The remnant's charge state**, hence its electromagnetic
  interactions, is an open question.
- **A cosmological remnant-density bound.**

**Status note (Session 152 suite pass):** no staleness finding. All four
open problems require quantum-field or strong-field-interior treatment
the programme has not attempted, so none has been overtaken.
