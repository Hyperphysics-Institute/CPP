# Keywords — GR-1f

**Primary:** Kerr metric; frame dragging; Lense–Thirring effect;
rotational SSV; azimuthal broadcast; Kerr bound; ergosphere; cosmic
censorship; Conscious Point Physics.

**Secondary:** Boyer–Lindquist coordinates; 12-edge selection rule; LSP
off-diagonal metric mapping; Gravity Probe B; Penrose process; outer
horizon; subluminality; extremal black hole.

**PACS/MSC (nearest):** 04.70.Bw (classical black holes), 04.20.Jb
(exact solutions), 04.80.Cc, 97.60.Lf; MSC 83C57, 83C25, 83C15.

**Elevator pitch:** Spin the source and the broadcast acquires an
azimuthal component — that is frame dragging, and it reproduces
Lense–Thirring exactly in the weak field. The striking result is the
Kerr bound: a hole cannot spin faster than J = GM²/c because the
azimuthal broadcast would have to outrun light. Cosmic censorship stops
being a conjecture about singularities and becomes a causality theorem
about a lattice.

**SEO:** Kerr metric derived from substrate; frame dragging emergent
mechanism; Kerr bound from causality; cosmic censorship theorem;
ergosphere from broadcast drag velocity.
