# Phenomena — GR-1f

**Explained (within the CPP ontology):**
- **Frame dragging**, as the metric expression of an azimuthal SSV
  broadcast from a rotating source.
- **Why the Kerr bound exists**, and why it is a *theorem* rather than a
  conjecture: the azimuthal broadcast cannot exceed c on a lattice with
  a minimum tick, and subluminality reduces algebraically to a ≤ M.
  **Cosmic censorship becomes a causality theorem** — the paper's
  sharpest claim.
- **The ergosphere**, as the region where the azimuthal drag velocity
  reaches c and static observers cease to exist.
- **The Schwarzschild limit**, recovered exactly at J = 0.

**Reproduced:**
- **g_tφ = −2GJ sin²θ/(c²r)** exactly in the weak field — the
  Lense–Thirring term, matched against Gravity Probe B at 0.3%.
- **The full Boyer–Lindquist Kerr metric**, shown *consistent* with the
  CPP mechanism at all orders. Consistency is the honest word: the
  all-orders derivation from the CPP field equation is open.

**Not claimed:**
- **Σ and Δ from first principles.** `op:allorders` — deriving the
  Boyer–Lindquist structure functions from the CPP field equation
  remains undone, and belongs to the same family as `op:einstein`
  (the dynamic/rank-2 sector).
