# Glossary — GR-1f

- **Azimuthal SSV** — the rotational component of the net broadcast from
  a spinning source: k(SSV_net)_φ = GJ sin²θ/(c²r³), generated by the
  same 12-edge selection rule that produces the Schwarzschild scalar.
- **Frame dragging / Lense–Thirring** — the off-diagonal metric term
  g_tφ = −2GJ sin²θ/(c²r), obtained from the azimuthal SSV via the LSP
  metric mapping. Exact in the weak field; confirmed by Gravity Probe B
  to 0.3%.
- **a ≡ J/Mc** — the spin parameter, with dimensions of length.
- **Pattern velocity v_φ = ac/r₊** — the azimuthal broadcast's
  propagation speed at the outer horizon. The quantity the Kerr bound
  constrains.
- **Subluminality condition** — v_φ ≤ c, required because every LSP
  broadcast propagates at exactly c and the Absolute Moment tick is the
  minimum time interval. Faster-than-c propagation would violate
  causality *on the lattice*.
- **Kerr bound** — J ≤ GM²/c, equivalently a ≤ GM/c². Derived here as a
  **theorem** from subluminality rather than assumed as cosmic
  censorship: a hole with a > GM/c² cannot form because its azimuthal
  broadcast would have to exceed c.
- **Ergosphere** — the region where the azimuthal SSV drag velocity
  reaches c, so that no observer can remain static.
- **r₊ = M + √(M² − a²)** — the outer horizon (units G = c = 1).
- **Σ, Δ** — the Boyer–Lindquist structure functions
  (Σ = r² + a²cos²θ, Δ = r² − 2Mr + a²). The paper shows the full Kerr
  metric is *consistent* with the CPP mechanism at all orders; deriving
  Σ and Δ from the CPP field equation is `op:allorders`.
- **Penrose process** — energy extraction from the ergosphere, treated
  here classically; the wave version is GR-1h's superradiance.
