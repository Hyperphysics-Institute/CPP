# Reviews — GR-1h

**Review basis: NONE.** No dedicated five-seat CONV round exists for
GR-1h, and no adjudication record.

**Inherited support, and its limits:**

- The paper rests almost entirely on **GR-1f's azimuthal SSV** and the
  horizon angular velocity derived from it. GR-1f has had **no review at
  all**, including the Kerr bound theorem and the pattern-velocity
  argument behind it.
- The **standard results** recovered here — the threshold ω < mΩ₊, the
  irreducible mass, the 29.3% extraction figure — are textbook Kerr
  physics and are not in dispute. Their agreement is not evidence about
  CPP; it is evidence that the mechanism reading reproduces known
  results, which is a weaker and different thing.
- The **Planck core** that three open problems depend on comes from
  GR-1c's Theorem 2, which CONV-027 did not examine.

**What has NOT been reviewed:** the identification of Ω₊ with the SSV
broadcast's angular frequency; the phase-velocity-lag derivation of the
threshold; the reading of the irreducible mass as the azimuthal-SSV
energy content; and the Planck-core-bomb speculation.

**Where a future round should start: the identification in step 1.**
Everything here follows from treating Ω₊ as the literal angular
frequency of a physical broadcast rather than as a metric coefficient.
That is the same class of move as GR-1f's pattern-velocity
identification — and GR-1f's version carries the Kerr bound theorem
while this one carries the superradiance mechanism. A single panel round
could sensibly examine both papers together, since one objection would
land on both.
