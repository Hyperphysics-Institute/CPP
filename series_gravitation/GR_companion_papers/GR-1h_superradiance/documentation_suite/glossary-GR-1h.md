# Glossary — GR-1h

- **Horizon angular velocity Ω₊ = ac/(r₊² + a²)** — in CPP, the angular
  frequency of the SSV broadcast at the outer horizon. Not an abstract
  metric quantity but the rotation rate of a physical field.
- **Superradiance threshold ω < mΩ₊** — a bosonic wave of frequency ω
  and azimuthal quantum number m is amplified when its phase velocity
  lags the rotating SSV field. The mechanism reading is the paper's
  contribution; the condition itself is standard.
- **Kerr–Newman threshold ω < mΩ₊ + qΦ₊** — the charged generalization,
  with Φ₊ the electrostatic potential at the horizon (corollary; this is
  the form GR-1g's open problem 4 names).
- **Irreducible mass M_irr** — the minimum mass reachable by reversible
  processes, √((r₊² + a²)c⁴/4G²). For extremal Kerr, M/√2.
- **Maximum extraction ΔE_max = (1 − 1/√2)Mc² ≈ 0.293 Mc²** — the energy
  available in going from extremal (a = M) to non-rotating (a = 0). In
  CPP this is **the fraction of rest-mass energy stored in the azimuthal
  SSV at extremal spin** — the mechanism reading of a standard number.
- **α ≡ GMμ/ħc** — the "gravitational fine-structure constant" of the
  boson cloud; equivalently (μc²/E_P)·(M/m_P). Cloud growth is efficient
  for α ~ 0.1–0.5.
- **Boson cloud / gravitational atom** — the bound state ultralight
  bosons form around a spinning hole, growing by extracting angular
  momentum until superradiance shuts off.
- **f_GW ≈ 2μc²/h** — the continuous gravitational-wave frequency from
  cloud annihilation. With μc² ≈ α × 1.3×10⁻¹² eV for stellar-mass
  holes, this lands in the LIGO O4 band for μ ~ 10⁻¹³–10⁻¹² eV.
- **Regge gap** — the depleted region in the (M, a/M) plane where
  superradiance has spun holes down. A direct, existing observational
  test of the whole mechanism.
- **Planck-core bomb** — the paper's own registered speculation (open
  problem 2): if the Planck core reflects bosonic as well as
  gravitational waves, the cavity between the potential barrier and the
  core would *enhance* the superradiant instability.
