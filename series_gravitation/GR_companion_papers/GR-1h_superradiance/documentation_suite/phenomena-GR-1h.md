**Explained (within the CPP ontology):**
- **Superradiance itself**, as a rate comparison: a wave whose phase
  velocity lags the rotating SSV broadcast (ω < mΩ₊) is dragged and
  amplified. Ω₊ is the broadcast's literal angular frequency at the
  horizon.
- **What the 29.3% actually is.** The standard maximum extraction
  (1 − 1/√2)Mc² for extremal Kerr becomes, in CPP, *the fraction of
  rest-mass energy stored in the azimuthal SSV at extremal spin*. The
  number is standard; the reading is the contribution.
- **The Kerr–Newman threshold** ω < mΩ₊ + qΦ₊, from the same argument
  with the electrostatic channel added.

  observably from standard superradiance, and it is unresolved.
