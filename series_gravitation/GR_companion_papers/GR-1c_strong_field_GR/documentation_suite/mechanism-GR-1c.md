# Mechanism — GR-1c

In causal order. The method is a single refusal: stop expanding.

1. **The inputs are already established.** The nonlinear PSR response
   PSR_eff = l_P/(1 + kΔ|SSV|) (the arc's one constitutive form) and the
   exact shell-broadcast source kΔ|SSV| = GM/rc² (GR-1a). Nothing new is
   postulated in this paper.
2. **Don't truncate.** GR-1a expanded the response to zeroth order and
   got flat space; GR-1b to first order and got the weak field. Kept
   whole, the same expression integrates over shells to the **exact
   Schwarzschild metric in isotropic coordinates** — no approximation,
   no free parameters. Standard Schwarzschild follows by coordinate
   transformation.
3. **The horizon is where GR puts it.** g_tt(r_S) = 0 exactly, and the
   CPP exterior is identical to GR at every r > r_S. There is no
   observational daylight between the theories outside the hole.
4. **The contraction runs into a floor.** As the metric tries to
   contract toward zero, the CP Exclusion Rule — no two Conscious Points
   on one Grid Point — bounds the effective lattice spacing at
   PSR_eff ≥ l_P/2. The lattice cannot compress further, so the metric
   cannot reach zero.
5. **The singularity is replaced, not renormalized.** What sits at the
   centre is a Planck-density core: r_core = GM/c² = r_S/2, with
   ρ_core ~ m_P/l_P³ ≈ 5.16×10⁹⁶ kg m⁻³. The resolution is structural —
   it follows from an occupancy rule already in the axioms, not from a
   cutoff introduced to tame a divergence.
6. **Consequences of the floor.** Hawking evaporation cannot proceed to
   zero mass, since r_S ≥ 2r_core ≥ l_P: a Planck-mass remnant is left
   (qualitative here; quantitative work deferred). The core acts as a
   reflective inner boundary for perturbations, implying post-ringdown
   echoes (GR-1d).
7. **The field equation, as corrected (V2.2).** The self-consistency
   condition — reconstructed metric equals propagating metric — is
   exactly the statement that the **logarithmic lapse**
   N = ln√(−g_tt/c²) is harmonic with respect to the effective metric,
   with vacuum □_g N = 0. In Δ|SSV| variables the same content carries
   the true compensator F_true. The generic-v equivalence identity shows
   this is algebraically the flat-lattice census equation of T-1: one
   law, two variables. Weak-field reduction to ∇²Φ = 4πGρ and linearised
   Einstein is unchanged.
8. **Rotation, sketched.** An azimuthal SSV_net component supplies
   frame-dragging, with the Lense–Thirring weak-field limit confirmed;
   the full Kerr structure is completed in GR-1f.

**The load-bearing novelty is step 4→5.** Most singularity resolutions
add machinery at the Planck scale. Here nothing is added: an exclusion
rule that was already in the theory for entirely unrelated reasons turns
out to forbid the divergence.
