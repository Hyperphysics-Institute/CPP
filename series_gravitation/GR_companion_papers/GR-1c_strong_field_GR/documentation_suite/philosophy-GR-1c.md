# Philosophy — GR-1c

**The result is a refusal.** GR-1a expanded the response formula to
zeroth order, GR-1b to first. This paper's method is to stop expanding —
and the exact Schwarzschild metric appears. That is an unusual kind of
theoretical achievement: not new machinery but the removal of an
approximation nobody had noticed was optional. It is also the arc's
strongest structural claim, because it means the strong field is not a
separate theory bolted onto the weak one.

**A singularity resolved by an unrelated rule.** The CP Exclusion Rule
was in the theory long before anyone asked it about black holes; it is
an occupancy constraint on Conscious Points and Grid Points. That it
forbids the metric from reaching zero — replacing the singularity with a
Planck-density core at r_S/2 — is the sort of consequence that is
epistemically cheap to obtain and expensive to fake. Nothing was
introduced at the Planck scale to tame a divergence; the divergence
simply cannot happen in a substrate with a minimum spacing.

**The corrigendum is the paper's most important episode, and it is not
about this paper.** The field-equation Proposition shipped defective:
its compensator failed against **this paper's own exact solution** at
leading nonlinear order. What matters is how that was found and handled.
It was found by a *later* derivation's HALT check — machinery built to
compare a new result against published ones, which fired against the
published one. It was then registered rather than quietly patched,
diagnosed to its root (the Proposition had been written for the wrong
potential — the exact statement lives in the logarithmic lapse), sent to
five external seats, approved unanimously, ratified by the founder, and
enacted with the defective formula preserved verbatim beside its
replacement. A programme is characterised less by whether its shipped
work contains errors — all of it does — than by whether its machinery
can convict its own published papers.

**Two languages, discovered by repair.** The diagnosis produced
something better than a fix: the equivalence identity showing the
measured-frame equation and the lattice-frame census equation are one
equation in two variables. Clock rates compose multiplicatively, so the
observer's natural potential is a logarithm; messenger counts add, so
the substrate's is not. Much of what looked like nonlinearity in static
gravity turned out to be the dictionary between the two.

**An open problem kept open under pressure.** The corrigendum restated
the Proposition, proved its equivalence to the ratified T-1, and could
easily have been narrated as closing `op:einstein`. It was not. The
enactment patch records explicitly that no Einstein-equivalence claim
was smuggled in, and the problem still stands. Resisting an upgrade
during a repair — when everyone's attention is on the repair — is harder
than resisting one in the abstract.

**Staleness, a third time.** This suite pass found three of the paper's
five open problems delivered or superseded, including one that states a
geometry the founder has since retired. That finding was recorded and
not acted on, for the same reason as at Patch 3283: correcting a paper
that contradicts itself is bookkeeping; deciding how a legacy document
should read once the arc has overtaken it belongs to the founder.
