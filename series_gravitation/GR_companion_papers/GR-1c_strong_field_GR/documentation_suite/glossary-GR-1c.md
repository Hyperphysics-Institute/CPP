# Glossary — GR-1c

- **The unexpanded PSR formula** — PSR_eff = l_P/(1 + kΔ|SSV|), used at
  full strength rather than truncated. This paper's entire method: what
  GR-1a and GR-1b obtained by expanding, this paper obtains by not.
- **Theorem 1 (exact Schwarzschild)** — the nonlinear PSR formula plus
  the exact shell-broadcast source kΔ|SSV| = GM/rc² yield the
  Schwarzschild metric in isotropic coordinates without approximation;
  standard Schwarzschild follows by coordinate transformation.
- **ϱ (varrho)** — the compactness variable kΔ|SSV|/2 = GM/2rc², in
  which the isotropic metric takes its simplest form.
- **CP Exclusion Rule** — no two Conscious Points may occupy one Grid
  Point. In the strong field this is what stops the contraction.
- **PSR_min = l_P/2** — the floor the Exclusion Rule imposes. The metric
  cannot reach zero at r = 0 because the lattice spacing cannot.
- **Planck core** — the replacement for the GR singularity: radius
  r_core = GM/c² = r_S/2, density ρ_core ~ m_P/l_P³ ≈ 5.16×10⁹⁶ kg m⁻³.
  For a solar-mass hole, 1.48 km — well inside r_S ≈ 2.95 km, hence
  unobservable from outside.
- **Self-consistency condition** — the fixed-point requirement inherited
  from GR-1b: the metric reconstructed from the LSP distribution must
  equal the metric governing LSP propagation.
- **Logarithmic lapse N** — N = ln√(−g_tt/c²) = −2·artanh(kΔ|SSV|/2).
  The **corrected** field-equation variable (V2.2): the Proposition is
  the statement that N is harmonic with respect to the effective metric,
  with vacuum □_g N = 0.
- **Form A / Form B** — the two statements of the corrected Proposition:
  A in the log-lapse (boxed, harmonic); B in Δ|SSV| with the true
  compensator F_true = (k²Δ|SSV|/2)/(1−(kΔ|SSV|/2)²)·|∇Δ|SSV||²_g.
- **The equivalence identity** — □_g artanh(kv/2) = [32k/((2−kv)(2+kv)⁵)]·∇²_flat v
  for generic v, with no derivative terms surviving. This is why the
  measured-frame equation and the lattice-frame census equation (T-1) are
  one equation in two variables.
- **The defective F** — the originally published compensator,
  [2k(Δ|SSV|)²/(1+kΔ|SSV|)²]·□ln(1+kΔ|SSV|). Preserved verbatim in the
  paper's Corrigendum Remark (anti-erasure); it fails against this
  paper's own Theorem-1 solution at leading nonlinear order.
- **op:einstein** — the arc's central open problem, registered here:
  whether the corrected quasilinear structure produces the exact Einstein
  tensor. **Still open** after the field-equation programme.
