rather than a singularity, the core reflects — and a merger's ringdown
should be followed by echoes. The delay is fixed by the mass and the
fundamental constants alone: no free parameter, unlike every competing
echo model. For the GW150914 remnant that is 112 ms, sitting just below
LIGO's band and squarely inside the Einstein Telescope's.
