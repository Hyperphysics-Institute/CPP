# Mechanism — GR-1d

In causal order. Nothing is postulated here beyond GR-1c.

1. **The centre is not a singularity.** GR-1c's CP Exclusion Rule bounds
   PSR_eff ≥ l_P/2, replacing the classical singularity with a
   Planck-density core at r_core = r_S/2.
2. **A core is something to bounce off.** GR's horizon is a perfect
   one-way membrane: nothing returns, so a ringdown decays and that is
   the end of the signal. A structure at finite density is a boundary
   condition — perturbations reaching it are partially reflected.
3. **The exterior is untouched.** Because GR-1c's exterior solution is
   *exactly* Schwarzschild, the potential-barrier structure outside the
   horizon is GR's, unmodified. The tortoise-coordinate calculation
   therefore proceeds on standard GR machinery; CPP contributes only the
   inner boundary.
4. **A cavity forms between two surfaces.** Outer: the photon-sphere
   potential barrier at r ≈ 3r_S/2, which reflects inward-scattered
   perturbations back down. Inner: the effective reflective surface at
   r₀ = r_S + l_P. A perturbation trapped between them leaks out on each
   bounce.
5. **The delay is the round-trip time, and it is parameter-free.**
   Δt = (2r_S/c)[ln(r_S/l_P) − 0.193 + …] ≈ (2r_S/c)·ln(r_S/l_P). Since
   G = ħc/m_P² gives r_S/l_P = 2M/m_P, the delay is a function of M/m_P
   alone — fixed by the remnant mass and G, ħ, c.
6. **The signal morphology.** A train of pulses at spacing Δt, each
   carrying the QNM frequency content, with amplitudes decreasing by the
   reflectivity factor per bounce.
7. **Where the discreteness speaks.** Everywhere outside the
   near-horizon band the waveform is GR's. The lattice is visible at
   exactly one place — the region where it stops being smoothable — and
   echoes are that visibility.

**The load-bearing feature is step 5's parameter-freedom.** Echo models
are common; what distinguishes this one is that its reflective surface
is not adjustable. Gravastars place it at r_S(1 + 10^−X) for a
model-dependent X; fuzzballs at the string scale. Here it is r_S + l_P,
and l_P cannot be tuned to fit a non-detection.
