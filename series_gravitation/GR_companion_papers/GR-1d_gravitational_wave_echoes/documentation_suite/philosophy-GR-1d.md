# Philosophy — GR-1d

**The one place the lattice can speak.** Across the whole gravitational
arc, discreteness is invisible: (l_P/r)² is 10⁻⁹⁰ at Mercury's orbit and
10⁻⁷⁶ in the LIGO band. The papers keep saying that what survives
smoothing is *structure*, not grain. This paper is the exception that
proves the rule — the near-horizon region is where smoothing fails, and
echoes are the substrate becoming audible at exactly the boundary where
it stops being smoothable. If CPP is ever confirmed by direct
observation of its discreteness rather than by its correspondences, this
is the most likely channel.

**Parameter-freedom as a liability, deliberately embraced.** The paper's
comparison table is unusual in that it advertises the theory's own
vulnerability. Gravastars and fuzzballs can absorb a non-detection by
adjusting X or ℓ_s. CPP cannot: the surface sits at r_S + l_P and the
Planck length is not a dial. That makes the delay prediction the arc's
easiest result to falsify, and the paper presents this as the point
rather than hedging it. A theory that puts a number where its
competitors put a parameter is making a bet, and this is the bet.

**A prediction just out of reach is the honest kind.** The flagship
number — 112 ms for GW150914 — corresponds to 8.9 Hz, just *below* the
LIGO O4 cutoff. It would have been easy to lead with the 30 M_☉ case at
19 Hz, comfortably inside current sensitivity. The paper leads with the
famous event and states plainly that its prediction sits at the edge of
detectability now and inside the Einstein Telescope's band later. That
is the epistemically uncomfortable position — a prediction that cannot
yet be tested is neither confirmed nor safe — and it is the correct one
to report.

**Half a prediction, labelled as half.** There is a delay formula and no
amplitude formula, because |R_core| = 1 is an assumption, not a result:
the real reflectivity needs strong-field interior dynamics the programme
does not have. Without amplitude there is no matched-filter template,
and without a template there is no search. The paper says all three
things in its open problems rather than presenting a delay prediction as
though it were a detection strategy.

**Consequences of a consequence.** Everything here descends from the CP
Exclusion Rule — an occupancy constraint introduced for reasons having
nothing to do with black holes, which produced a Planck core in GR-1c,
which produces reflectivity here, which produces an observable. That
chain is the kind of thing a theory cannot easily arrange after the
fact, and it is worth more than any single number in the table.
