# Glossary — GR-1d

- **Echo** — a repeat pulse following the main ringdown, produced when a
  perturbation trapped between the photon-sphere potential barrier and
  an inner reflective surface leaks back out.
- **Tortoise coordinate r\*** — the radial coordinate in which wave
  propagation near a horizon is uniform; the echo delay is the
  round-trip tortoise-coordinate travel time between the two boundaries.
- **The two boundaries** — outer: the photon-sphere potential barrier at
  r ≈ 3r_S/2. Inner: the effective reflective surface at r₀ = r_S + l_P,
  the quantum Planck boundary just outside the horizon.
- **The delay formula** — Δt = (2r_S/c)[ln(r_S/l_P) − 0.193 + O(l_P/r_S)]
  ≈ (2r_S/c)·ln(r_S/l_P). The −0.193 correction is negligible against
  ln(r_S/l_P) ≈ 88–93 for all astrophysical holes.
- **Δt(M) = (4GM/c³)·ln(2M/m_P)** — the same formula in mass form, which
  is the searchable template: given a remnant mass from the inspiral, the
  delay is fully determined.
- **f_echo = 1/Δt** — the echo repetition frequency, not to be confused
  with **f_QNM**, the frequency *content* of each pulse
  (0.3737·c/2πr_S for the ℓ = 2 fundamental).
- **|R_core|** — the core's reflectivity, **assumed = 1** in this paper.
  The paper's largest declared idealization; the real value requires the
  strong-field interior and is the key input for any amplitude
  prediction.
- **Planck core** — inherited from GR-1c: the CP Exclusion Rule bounds
  PSR_eff ≥ l_P/2, replacing the singularity with a Planck-density core
  at r_core = r_S/2. Everything here follows from that structure.
- **Gravastar / fuzzball** — competing echo models, each placing the
  reflective surface at a **model-dependent free parameter** (10^−X, or
  the string length ℓ_s). The CPP surface sits at r_S + l_P, and l_P is
  not adjustable — which is the paper's comparative claim.
