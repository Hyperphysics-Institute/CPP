# Phenomena — GR-1d

**Predicted (this is a genuinely predictive paper — the arc's most
directly falsifiable):**
- **Post-merger echoes** following black-hole ringdown, with delay
  Δt = (2r_S/c)·ln(r_S/l_P), equivalently Δt(M) = (4GM/c³)·ln(2M/m_P).
- **Specific numbers**, per remnant mass: 1 M_☉ → 1.73 ms (578 Hz
  repetition); 10 M_☉ → 17.8 ms; 30 M_☉ → 54.0 ms (19 Hz, comfortably
  inside current LIGO sensitivity); **GW150914 at 62 M_☉ → 112 ms
  (8.9 Hz)**; GW190521 at 150 M_☉ → 275 ms; 10⁴ M_☉ → 19.1 s (LISA band).
- **An observational window statement**: 8.9 Hz sits just below LIGO
  O4's ~10 Hz cutoff, but within the Einstein Telescope's ~5 Hz design
  sensitivity — so the flagship prediction is *currently* out of reach
  and shortly will not be.
- **Persistence**: because evaporation terminates at a Planck-mass
  remnant (GR-1c), the echo structure persists, with increasing delay as
  M decreases, until M ~ m_P.

**The comparative claim (the paper's sharpest point):**
- The CPP delay has **no free parameter**. Gravastars place the
  reflective surface at r_S(1 + 10^−X) with X model-dependent; fuzzballs
  at the string length ℓ_s. Both can be tuned; l_P cannot. Given a
  remnant mass, CPP's prediction is fully determined by G, ħ, c — which
  makes it the most tightly constrained echo model and the easiest to
  kill.

**Not predicted here (honest boundaries, all in the paper's open
problems):**
- **Amplitude.** The calculation assumes |R_core| = 1. The real
  reflectivity requires the strong-field interior dynamics at
  PSR_eff = l_P/2 — which needs the full field equation in the interior,
  still open. Without it there is a delay prediction but no strain
  prediction, and therefore no complete matched-filter template.
- **Kerr echoes.** The barrier is computed on the Schwarzschild
  potential; spin introduces an a-dependent correction at order (a/r_S)²
  and requires the Kerr QNM spectrum.
- **A search.** The template Δt(M) is specific enough to run against
  O1–O4 data, and that analysis is deferred to a dedicated effort, not
  performed here.

**Status note (Session 152 suite pass):** unlike GR-1b and GR-1c, **this
paper's open problems are all still open.** Item 1 depends on the
strong-field interior, which the Session-150 field-equation programme
explicitly did not reach — T-1/T-2/T-3 are exterior and census-level
results, and the interior stays at `op:einstein`. No staleness finding.
