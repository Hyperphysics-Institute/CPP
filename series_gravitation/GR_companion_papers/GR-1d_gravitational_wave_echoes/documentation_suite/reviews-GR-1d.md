# Reviews — GR-1d

**Review basis: NONE.** GR-1d has never had a dedicated five-seat CONV
round, and no adjudication record exists for it. It predates the
protocol's application to the gravitational companions.

**What indirect support exists — and its limits:**

- **The inherited structure has been reviewed; this paper's use of it
  has not.** GR-1c's Planck core is the input, and CONV-027 reviewed
  GR-1c's *field-equation Proposition* only — not Theorem 2, the
  Exclusion-Rule argument that produces the core. So the foundation of
  this paper rests on an unreviewed theorem in another unreviewed paper.
  That is worth stating plainly rather than routing around.
- **The exterior machinery is standard and independently exercised.**
  The tortoise-coordinate calculation runs on the exact Schwarzschild
  exterior, and CONV-029 (**VERIFIED 5–0** on GR-1i's numerics)
  independently confirmed that exterior to machine precision. The
  barrier structure this paper uses is GR's, not a CPP construction.
- **GR-1j's T-2** establishes that the isotropic solution is the unique
  static spherical vacuum exterior — which tightens the setting in which
  the echo cavity is defined, though it says nothing about the inner
  boundary.

**What has NOT been reviewed:** the partial-reflectivity assumption at
the core; the identification of the effective reflecting surface with
r₀ = r_S + l_P (as opposed to r_core = r_S/2, which is where the core
actually sits — the paper's choice of the near-horizon Planck boundary
deserves external scrutiny); the −0.193 correction term; the amplitude
and morphology discussion; and the comparative claims against gravastar
and fuzzball models.

**Where a future round should start:** the r₀ = r_S + l_P identification.
The entire delay formula is logarithmically sensitive to that choice,
and it is the step where a reviewer could most plausibly move the number.
