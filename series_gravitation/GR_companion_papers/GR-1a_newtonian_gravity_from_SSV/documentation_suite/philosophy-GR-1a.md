# Philosophy — GR-1a

**One difference, many consequences.** The paper's whole content is that
gravity's source is a scalar where electromagnetism's is signed. From
that single asymmetry follow universal attraction, unshieldability, the
identity of inertial and gravitational mass, and the equivalence
principle at Newtonian order — none of them separately postulated. There
is an aesthetic claim buried in the structure: a good foundation should
be *narrow*, and should pay out broadly. Whether the payout is earned
depends entirely on the no-polarity proposition, which is where a
skeptical reader should push.

**Explaining a constant rather than measuring one.** G is not fitted
here; it is identified as ħc/m_P², with m_P already fixed by the
lattice. The honest framing — and the one the suite adopts — is that
this is *derivability*, not prediction: G was known, and the achievement
is that the theory has nowhere to put a free parameter, not that it
foretold an unknown number. The same care applies to the hierarchy
result, which reframes a famous puzzle as a consequence of where the
electron sits in the assembly hierarchy. Reframing a puzzle is real
progress and is also less than solving it, and the difference is worth
keeping visible.

**Equality by construction is a stronger claim than equality by
observation.** General relativity takes the equivalence of inertial and
gravitational mass as a principle, elevated from an extraordinarily
well-tested coincidence. Here the two are the same object — E_pol = mc²
— so the equality is not something the theory must arrange but something
it cannot avoid. This is the most philosophically pointed thing the
paper does: it converts an input into an identity.

**A ground floor that knows it is a ground floor.** The final section
names what this paper cannot reach — strong field, dynamic spacetime,
and by implication the factor of two in light deflection that a
scalar-only treatment must miss. Written in March 2026, that section
functioned as a promissory note; the arc has since paid it (GR-1b's
vector channel, GR-1c's exact metric, GR-1j's field equation). A
foundation paper that maps its own boundary is what made the later
papers checkable against it.

**On reading an early paper late.** This suite is written eleven papers
downstream of its subject, and there is a temptation to tidy the early
work in light of the later — to describe GR-1a as if it had known where
the arc was going. It did not. Its language is the language of March
2026, and its claims are the claims that could be made then. The
documentation records it that way, including a version history that is
honestly marked reconstructed, with the content deltas of V1→V2→V3 left
blank because nobody wrote them down.
