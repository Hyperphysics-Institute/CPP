# Reviews — GR-1a

**Review basis: NONE at paper level.** This is the honest statement and
should not be dressed up. GR-1a was written in March 2026, before the
CONV multi-seat review protocol was applied to the gravitational
companions. It has never had a dedicated five-seat round of its own, and
no adjudication record exists for it.

**What review coverage it does have, indirectly:**

- **CONV-026** reviewed the series parent GR-1, whose thesis rests on
  this paper's shell-broadcast source relation and no-polarity argument.
  Q3 (the classical-tests table and verify) returned **SOUND 5–0**, and
  the ledger question **COMPLETE 4–1**; neither is a review of GR-1a's
  own derivations, but the results this paper feeds were exercised.
  Records: `../../../review/reviews-CONV-026.md`.
- **CONV-027/028** ratified the field-equation programme, which
  **independently re-derived this paper's central relation**:
  k·Δ|SSV| = GM/rc² emerges from the messenger census as the unique
  decaying spherical vacuum solution (GR-1j; T-1). That is the strongest
  indirect check GR-1a has — a result obtained here by shell-broadcast
  analogy in March was obtained again in August from a different
  starting point. Records: `../../../review/conv027_adjudication.md`,
  `../../../review/conv028_adjudication.md`.
- **CONV-029** cleared GR-1i, whose four test derivations sit on the
  metric this paper's source relation ultimately produces. Records:
  `../../../review/conv029_adjudication.md`.

**What has NOT been externally reviewed:** the no-polarity proposition
itself; the Q_grav normalization through Q_Planck by which G is
identified; the hierarchy-ratio argument; and the equivalence-principle
section. A future round on this paper would have real work to do, and
the derivation-by-analogy step in §3 — where Q_grav is written "by
analogy" with Q_EM — is where a panel would properly start.
