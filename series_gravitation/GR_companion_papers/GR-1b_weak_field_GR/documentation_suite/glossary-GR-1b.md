# Glossary — GR-1b

*(The paper carries its own in-body Glossary of CPP Terms; this file is
the suite-level companion, focused on what is specific to GR-1b.)*

- **LSP (Lattice State Packet)** — this paper's central construction:
  the two-component broadcast object built explicitly from the eDP
  cloud's compressive polarization energy. |SSV|_abs = E_pol/V₀ sources
  g_tt (time curvature); **SSV**_net sources g_ij (spatial curvature).
- **The two channels** — the temporal and spatial halves of the
  broadcast. Everything that distinguishes this arc from a scalar-only
  substrate theory lives in the second one.
- **Holographic LSP summation** — the metric-reconstruction procedure:
  the weak-field g_tt and g_ij are assembled by summing LSP content over
  the broadcast, reproducing the weak-field isotropic Schwarzschild form
  with no free parameters.
- **The factor of two** — α = 4GM/c²b rather than the Newtonian
  2GM/c²b: equal contributions from temporal and spatial curvature.
  1.75″ versus 0.875″ at the solar limb.
- **Self-consistency condition** — the paper's definition of the CPP
  field equations: the metric reconstructed from the LSP summation must
  equal the metric governing LSP propagation. In the weak field this is
  equivalent to the linearised Einstein equations.
- **The strong conjecture** — the paper's own label for the *full
  nonlinear* Einstein equations: deriving G_μν = 8πG/c⁴·T_μν from the
  self-consistency condition would require showing the discrete 600-cell
  lattice sum converges to smooth Riemannian geometry. Explicitly
  deferred, not claimed.
- **Maximum-gradient selection rule** — the 12-edge rule from the
  Absolute Moment companion, whose continuum limit gives geodesic
  motion.
- **GP Exclusion rule** — the occupancy constraint that produces the
  effective horizon, and (in the paper's open-problem list) a proposed
  driver of initial expansion without an inflaton.
- **Z₀ = √(μ₀/ε₀)** — the impedance of free space, derived here from
  lattice parameters, closing an open problem inherited from the ZDC
  Chaining companion.
- **OBL-CAL-LABEL** — the standing calibration-labelling obligation
  applied to this paper at V3.3: the cosmological-constant item names a
  candidate *mechanism* only; Λ's magnitude is ruled not derivable at
  current knowledge, and d_s^emp = 4.636 is cosmology-calibrated, never
  predicted.
