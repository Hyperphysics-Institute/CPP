# Reviews — GR-1b

**Review basis: NONE at paper level.** GR-1b has never had a dedicated
five-seat CONV round. It predates the protocol's application to the
gravitational companions, and no adjudication record exists for it.

**Indirect coverage:**

- **CONV-026** (GR-1 V0): the parent's thesis rests on this paper's
  two-component LSP, and Q3 returned **SOUND 5–0** on the classical-tests
  table whose deflection value this paper's mechanism produces. Not a
  review of GR-1b's own derivations. Records:
  `../../../review/reviews-CONV-026.md`.
- **CONV-027/028** (the field-equation programme): bears directly on
  this paper's §6, which defined the CPP field equations as a
  self-consistency condition and labelled the full nonlinear case a
  strong conjecture. GR-1j's T-1 derives the general equation from the
  messenger census by a different route; the conjecture GR-1b named is
  *advanced but not closed* — full rank-2 Einstein equivalence stays at
  `op:einstein`. Records: `../../../review/conv027_adjudication.md`,
  `../../../review/conv028_adjudication.md`.
- **CONV-029** (GR-1i): the classical-tests companion derives the
  deflection in full and confirms the factor-of-two split structurally
  (**VERIFIED 5–0** on numerics; **DISCIPLINED 5–0** on claims). The
  closest thing to external validation this paper's central result has.
  Records: `../../../review/conv029_adjudication.md`.
- **CONV-020** (cosmological constant): the round whose ruling produced
  the V3.3 calibration label and the withdrawal of the "brackets the
  observed Λ" language from item (5).

**What has NOT been externally reviewed:** the LSP construction from
E_pol; the holographic summation by which the metric is reconstructed;
the claim that the temporal and spatial shares are *equal* in the weak
field (the paper's central quantitative result); the geodesic derivation
from the 12-edge selection rule; and the Z₀ derivation. A future round
would properly start with the equal-shares claim, since the entire
factor-of-two argument rests on it.
