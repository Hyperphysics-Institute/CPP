# Philosophy — GR-1b

**A prediction that arrived a century early.** The paper describes its
own factor-of-two result as a retroactive prediction: the two-component
LSP was introduced because a scalar-only broadcast *cannot* reach
Eddington's 1919 measurement, and once introduced, the temporal and
spatial shares come out equal, giving exactly 1.75″. The honest reading
sits between two framings. It is not foresight — the number was known
for a century. Neither is it a fit — the split is not adjustable, and
the extension is constrained everywhere else in the arc. What the
observation did was *force a structural choice*, and the structure then
had to survive the rest of the programme. It has.

**Naming a conjecture a conjecture.** The self-consistency condition
yields the linearised Einstein equations, and the paper could have
gestured at the full nonlinear case as "expected in the continuum
limit." Instead it says: strong conjecture, requiring proof that the
discrete lattice sum converges to smooth Riemannian geometry, deferred.
That sentence, written in early 2026, is what made the later
field-equation programme honest work rather than a victory lap — GR-1j
could be measured against a standard the arc had already set for itself,
and the result was a *conditional* Birkhoff theorem and a conserved
current rather than the tensor a looser paper would have claimed.

**Withdrawal is part of the record.** The cosmological-constant item was
downgraded at V3.3, not quietly: the calibration label states that the
mechanism is a candidate only, that Λ's magnitude is ruled not derivable
at current knowledge, that d_s^emp is cosmology-calibrated and never
predicted, and that the earlier bracketing language was withdrawn at
CONV-020. A programme that lets its own retractions ride along inside
the papers that made the claims is harder to accuse of drift.

**Documentation catches what shipping misses.** This suite pass found
the paper's Open Problems list stale — two of six items delivered by
companions written since. That is the second such finding in two
sessions (GR-1's ledger was the first), and the pattern is worth naming:
*status sections rot silently*, because nothing in a compile gate or a
review round checks whether last March's open problem is still open. The
suite is where a paper is read as a whole, which is why the suite is
where staleness surfaces.

**Restraint at the edit boundary.** The finding was recorded and scoped
to the founder rather than executed. The rule being followed: correcting
a paper that contradicts *itself* is bookkeeping (GR-1's ledger, Patch
3276); correcting a paper that has been overtaken by *later work* is a
content decision about how a legacy document should read, and that
belongs to the founder — not to a worker mid-way through a
documentation pass.
