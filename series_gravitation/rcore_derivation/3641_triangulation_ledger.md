## §1 What the policy says, and why it is the right one
The founder cannot adjudicate PCD-level detail by picture, and says so. The corpus's method everywhere else has been: derive from the nine Axioms and the PCD cycle; compare with empirics; validate by convergence across independent reviewers and across independent empirics. PD-007 says: do the same in the saturation regime, treating each empirical case as a calibration of *how the PCD cycle extends* to over-supply, and treating agreement across cases as the inductive proof of the extension. This is the method that produced the corpus; it is not a retreat.

## §2 The discipline (so that calibration cannot absorb failure)
1. **Pin once, never refit.** Any free element of the extension is fixed by *one* named empiric and then frozen; every other empiric it touches is a test. The working law (§3) has *no* free element; if a calibration is ever needed, it is named here first.
2. **Label everything.** Results in the regime carry `[PCD-EXT]` until the ledger closes; a `[PCD-EXT]` result never enters a paper's abstract as a theorem.
3. **Quantifier discipline** (3347): "the theory predicts X" is written only for results that survive every ledger row that constrains them.
4. **Swarm/inductive proof** = the existing five-seat convergence protocol (CONV rounds), applied to the *extension*, not to each derivation separately: one round per ledger closure, not per patch (review economy).
6. **The founder's method (6 Sep, enacted 3646):** derive from the nine axioms; on failure, state a statistics-level hypothesis that satisfies the phenomenon and plausibly connects to the axioms — labeled `HYPOTHESIS`, never adopted; apply it *unchanged* to a group of related phenomena; only after several show it descriptive, attempt to connect it to the axioms. A hypothesis never enters an abstract.
5. **A failed row is a failed extension**, not a datum to refit. *(Amended 3645, founder 6 Sep: the former clause "unless the founder pins it explicitly under rule 1" is struck. A constant may enter the ledger only as a labeled `UNEXPLAINED` derivation target with a stated reason the elemental computation is beyond reach and a swarm test defined before its value is used; the worker never routes adoption of a computed constant to the founder.)*
