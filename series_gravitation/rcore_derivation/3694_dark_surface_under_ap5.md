## §2 Three structural facts (T2–T4)
1. **No release channel (D1).** A layer deactivates only when the demand at that GP falls below its cap and its held content returns to the layer above. At an R-core the surface demand is v = cap by definition and the interior demand exceeds it for as long as the mass is present; storage capacity is unbounded (3693 T2, equal capacities). So absorbed energy — infalling matter's kinetic energy, absorbed waves, accretion power — enters layer 2 and is *never returned to layer 1*. To the exterior it appears only as mass (BH.5, the area theorem). The Broderick–Narayan premise (a surface in steady state must re-radiate what it absorbs) presupposes a thermal reservoir that fills; here the reservoir is mass and does not fill. **f = 0 by D1.**
2. **Charge-blind and lockstep (D2 + 3374).** The channel that saturates is the *count* (SSV); the layer-2 copy repositions every CP in a cell identically, so the two CPs of a DP move together and the DP's EM degrees of freedom — arcs and separation, which is where EM energy lives (founder, 4 Sep) — are not driven at linear order. No dipole, no radiation. The first coupling that survives is tidal across the DP: relative displacement ~ (l_DP/λ) × amplitude for a stored pattern of wavelength λ, power suppressed by ~(l_P/λ)² — **3×10⁻⁹³ for Sgr A*, 10⁻⁸³ for a 62 M☉ remnant** (T3) — and emitted at the *pattern's* frequency (mHz to ~100 Hz), not in the infrared.
3. **No spectrum to cascade through (3693 T3/T4).** Translational modes are frozen at the floor (rigid under compression); shear modes are zero-frequency (an ideal fluid's flow); the layer-2 motion lives at the Moment cadence, 29 decades above the NIR (T4). Thermal emission at 10¹⁴ Hz would require down-conversion through a phonon spectrum the saturated lattice does not possess. The shell's temperature in the ordinary sense is zero.
