## §1 What the empirics decide — the exterior

Two candidate exterior dynamics for the CPP perturbation near the R-core: (a) GR's tensor equations on the curved background (Zerilli/RW/Teukolsky — what the arc has used; A3′'s isomorphism in vacuum), or (b) a component-wise scalar-type relay of the packet's nine components with the position-dependent census speed (the naive "C3 shell-sum on the curved lattice"). Decision (a). This is what the arc has used since 3297, now on empirical rather than borrowed grounds.

## §2 What the empirics do not decide — the wall's residual gauge

The register content at the wall depends on the residual harmonic pattern at O(1) (3611). The quantities that would fix it observationally are the *echo* lines — position and amplitude — which depend on the wall condition. **No echo has been detected.** The LIGO–Virgo–KAGRA searches (2017 onward) report no significant echo signal and set upper limits on echo amplitude relative to the ringdown (the early tentative claims did not survive the collaboration's analyses). So today the residual is bounded, not fixed: any CPP wall model must predict echoes *below* the current limits at the detected events' sensitivities.

Under the founder's steer: **the residual is designated the arc's one calibration parameter — `ρ_w`** (the homogeneous harmonic pattern at the wall; three complex numbers per ℓ at fixed ω, reducible by regularity and parity). "Avoid if you can": the way to avoid it is a *derivation* of the near-wall relay in the packet's own variables (the nine components with C3's operator on the curved lattice plus the C4/C5 constraints, whose gauge-invariant content §1 has just shown must be GR's) — that derivation would produce the register at the wall with no residual to choose. It is OPEN-GR-LATTICE-FRAME-1, and it is the honest route. Until it is done, the line set is a **family over `ρ_w`**, and the record will say so.
