4. The Moment is universal; the far observer's clock counts Moments (R-SIMULTANEITY-UNIVERSAL).

Then the number of lattice steps a DI-bit covers per Moment is the proper PSR divided by the proper step:

    hop per Moment (lattice units) = PSR_eff / (ψ² l_P) = 1 / [(1 + u) ψ²].

The founder's precedent is honoured — the transit is edge-by-edge and GP-counted — with the count per Moment set by a proper-length budget.

## §4 The consequence that matters more than the number

The ratified **log-lapse dictionary** `N = (1 − u/2)/(1 + u/2)` was chosen (T-1 CHARTER) to make the exterior *exactly* Schwarzschild. The no-axiom census map says local light speed is `c` by local clocks only if `N = 1/(1 + u)`. The two agree to first order (all weak-field tests) and differ at second: `1 − u + u²/2` vs `1 − u + u²`. At the wall: 1/3 vs 1/2. So **CPP's mechanism, taken literally, does not reproduce Schwarzschild's lapse at strong field** — it reproduces Schwarzschild's *spatial* metric (from the register) and a *different* lapse.
