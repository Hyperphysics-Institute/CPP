## §1 The error, located
3676 T3 wrote: first-echo amplitude ≈ R_ret × |T_barrier|² = 0.29 *of the ringdown amplitude*, and then called that "at the level the O3 injections (30% of merger SNR) detect." Those are different denominators. The ringdown is a fraction of the merger: GW250114 gives 26/80 = 0.325 (from 6 t_Mf; 21/80 = 0.26 from 10.5 t_Mf). So the alternative's echo train is 0.29 × 0.325 ≈ **0.094 of the merger SNR** (T1). Uchikata et al.'s injections were at 30% of the merger (SNR_inj 4.7–8.0 across events); Nielsen et al.'s GW150914 bound is 15% of the merger amplitude. Neither reaches 10%. **The O1–O3 population null does not test the alternative.** 3676's "disfavored" was an overstatement by exactly this factor and is withdrawn.
