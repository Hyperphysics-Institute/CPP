## §1 The picture, read exactly
Wave energy (EM: arcs + separation of DPs; by 3600–3609 the same for the tensor register) enters the surface, excites CP vibration in the register-saturated medium, and is held there as a **dynamic organizational pattern** — CPs at the PSR floor repositioning each Moment, transmitting their state through the core at c/2. Energy stored; information kept.

That is **mode conversion**: energy leaves the wave and enters internal CP motion. It is not dissipation into nothing, and it is not (necessarily) heat.

## §2 What the ringdown adds (3621)
The stored pattern must **not re-couple to the exterior tensor channel within about one crossing** — otherwise the core is a lossless resonator at the ringdown frequency and the observed mode splits. So the conversion is one-way on the ringdown timescale: tensor wave → internal CP pattern, with the return channel closed or slow. This is a constraint on the core's dynamics the founder's picture allows but does not yet specify (OPEN-GR-CORE-DISSIPATION-1, reframed: *the rate and mechanism of mode conversion, and the closure of the return channel*).

## §3 What the infrared adds — the dark surface
A surface with a *lapse of ½* (z = 1) is not hidden the way a horizon is. If it thermalised what it absorbs, it would radiate. For Sgr A* (4 × 10⁶ M_⊙, surface at 1.33 r_S, accretion power ~10³⁶ erg/s — recollection): a thermal surface would sit at ~4900 K locally, ~2400 K as observed, radiating ~2.5 × 10³⁵ erg/s peaking at 1.2 μm — **10–100× the observed quiescent near-infrared** (~10³⁴–10³⁵ erg/s at 2 μm; recollection, to be checked). This is the Broderick–Narayan argument, and at z = 1 it is not marginal: **a thermalised R-core surface is excluded by existing observations.**

So the founder's picture is required in its *strong* form: the absorbed energy is stored **coherently**, as an organizational pattern that populates no photon-emitting thermal states. The R-core is a **dark absorber**: it swallows EM and GW alike, converts them into internal CP organization, and re-emits nothing thermal. (Kirchhoff's law — emissivity equals absorptivity — holds for thermal states; a coherent, non-thermal store evades it. That is exactly the difference between "vibration" and "heat," and the observations put the R-core on the vibration side.)

**Prediction, zero parameters:** the R-core surface has no thermal signature at any accretion rate. Any detection of thermal emission from a black-hole surface falsifies CPP's black hole; none has been seen.
