# Patch 3621 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder asked for the point instead of the bracket. I built the
theory's wall as the arc has built it all along — the count reflects
at the level set, all the energy goes into the core through the
tensor register, the core is a flat lossless cavity at half light
speed — and it failed the ringdown, decisively. The core's first
standing wave sits at the ringdown frequency itself; the wall's
impedance swings through infinity across the band; the observed mode
splits into two lines neither of which is anywhere near it. A lossless
core is excluded by GW150914.

So the point, as the theory can currently model it, is not on the
path. What the data force instead is that the core absorbs: whatever
the surface admits must be dissipated within about one crossing, or
it would resonate and be seen. And a surface that absorbs everything
at the horizon's real impedance is, to the ringdown, a black hole. The
theory's point is therefore near the black-hole end of the bracket —
not derived, but forced by an observation, which is what the founder
told me to let decide.

I have said plainly what the corpus lacks: the dynamics of the tensor
register inside a saturated medium, and where the absorbed energy
goes. That replaces the junction as the item that fixes the point.
And I have said what this does to the arc's ambitions: if the core
absorbs as the ringdown demands, an R-core rings like a Kerr black
hole to present precision, the retrograde-lifetime signature survives
only as an upper bound, and the theory's black holes may differ from
Einstein's observably somewhere other than in their ringing. That is
a smaller claim than the arc began with in July, and it is the one
the data allow.

The paper carries the bracket, with one sentence added: the observed
ringdown requires the core to absorb what the surface admits. That
sentence is an inference about the inside of a black hole from its
ringing, and it is new.
