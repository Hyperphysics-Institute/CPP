# Patch 3695 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

I had frightened myself with the wrong number. Reading the tilt
derivation I thought the initial lattice held every CP in thirteen
points, so that each point carried something like ten to the
seventy-nine and the whole of inflation would be saturated. Then I
read the founder's own description of expansion: the lattice never
grows; the sea thins out on a fixed scaffold; the Big Bang is the
nearly full lattice and a black hole is the nearly full lattice with
nowhere to thin into. That reframed the question as timing, and the
timing is not close. From full occupancy the cap is crossed in a
seventh of an e-fold. The modes we see left fifty-seven e-folds later
at an occupancy ten trillion times below it.

The useful thing came from asking what would have happened otherwise.
A saturated era clips the driver: every CP moves at the floor rate no
matter how crowded it is, so the expansion forgets the crowding and
the tilt goes to zero. Planck excludes that at eight sigma. So the
amendment imposes a condition on the cosmology, and the cosmology
already meets it by a margin so wide it is not a coincidence but a
consequence of the same thinning. The list is empty. The panel is
next.
