# Patch 3352 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The upgrade CONV-035 asked for, and it came out cleaner than the thing
it replaces.

I expected to have to prove the scan's two-branch mechanism: trapped
implies extreme-retrograde implies no superradiant window, and
window-capable implies corotating implies buried. That is what the
3339 record says the physics is. Instead the algebra collapsed the
second branch entirely.

The move is one line: evaluate R at the WALL rather than hunting for
turning points. In the superradiant window omega*S - a*m is strictly
negative (Step 1), so the positive term is bounded by its value at
omega = 0, while the negative term is bounded below using
(m - a*omega) > m(1 - a*Omega_w) (Step 2) and Q > 0 (Step 3). What
survives is a condition with NO ell, NO m and NO omega in it:
a < sqrt(Delta_w)(1 - a*Omega_w). The mode indices divide out. That is
why the theorem is unbounded in ell where the scan needed a cutoff.

BURIAL TURNS OUT TO BE IRRELEVANT to stability, which I did not
expect. The instability recipe needs trapped AND superradiant; those
two sets are disjoint on their own, so whether a mode is exposed never
enters. This is worth more than tidiness: the stability conclusion no
longer inherits the (2,+2) thin margin or the mu-correspondence
grading that GPT attacked at CONV-034. A result that used to lean on
the corpus's shakiest numbers now does not touch them.

WHAT I CHECKED BECAUSE I DID NOT TRUST THE ALGEBRA. Each of the four
steps is asserted separately rather than being asserted as a block,
and then the theorem is checked DIRECTLY against the definition of R
at 1736 sample points out to ell = 500 — far past anything the physics
needs — because an algebraic derivation I did alone at speed is
exactly the kind of thing that has been wrong twice this session. The
least negative sampled R is -3.56; nothing is near zero, so the result
is not sitting on a knife edge.

THE MARGIN NARROWS WITH SPIN and I put the table in the record rather
than just the verdict: 0.750 at chi = 0, 0.283 at the benchmark, 0.104
at chi = 0.99. It never closes in the tested range, but the trend is
monotone and a reader should be able to see where it is heading rather
than take "holds at every spin" on faith. If someone later pushes the
surface construction to extremality this is the number to watch.

ONE HONEST ASYMMETRY, flagged for the same reason I flagged it at
3349: the eikonal Q correspondence is WEAKEST at low ell, which is
where GPT's CONV-034 objection bit — and STRONGEST at large ell, which
is exactly where this theorem does its new work. So the upgrade lands
in the regime where the method is most trustworthy. That cuts in my
favour and I would rather say so than let it pass unremarked.
