# Patch 3704 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

I expected this to hurt more. The flat core was built on the register
acting on nothing, and once the ringdown said the register acts on
its sample, the core got three-quarters of its gravity back and the
star became an ordinary star with a slightly weaker centre. The
threshold at 1.78 is untouched because it is reached before the cap
does anything. What is gone is the knee: radii do not rise with mass,
they fall a little more slowly, and no telescope will see a slope of
minus two against a slope of minus five behind an equation of state
nobody knows to that precision. What is left is the maximum mass, a
third higher than Einstein's for the same nuclear physics — real,
possibly the reason the heaviest pulsars exist on soft tables, and
for now indistinguishable from a stiffer table. So the neutron-star
test I had been promising as "available now" is honestly "available
once the equation of state is pinned independently." The paper says
otherwise and needs its ninth version. I wrote the loss into the
sheet before the gain.
