# Patch 3355 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

A corollary, cashed. 3352 proved superradiant and trapped modes are
disjoint; the Zel'dovich instability needs a mode that is both; so
the growth rate is zero and item (d) closes without computing a rate.
I wrote the script expecting exactly that and nothing more.

What I did not expect was check 3. Writing the "residual channel"
bound — a non-trapped superradiant wave still gets one reflection off
the surface, so what is the single-pass gain? — I went to bound the
gain and realised the wave never gets there. R(r_w) < 0 in the window
means the wall is in the forbidden region FOR THAT WAVE: it turns
around at the barrier's outer face and never touches the rotating
surface. There is nothing to amplify it. The inequality I proved for
one purpose (no cavity) closes a second one (no contact) for free.

I checked that this is not me over-reading a sign. The residual
channel would need a superradiant wave to reach r_w; reaching r_w
means propagating there; propagating there means R(r_w) > 0; 3352
says R(r_w) < 0. It is the same inequality read once more.

Still a corollary at eikonal-WKB grade, and section 3 says what would
reopen it: an s = -2 correction big enough to flip R(r_w)'s sign in
the window, which at a margin of 0.283 would need a ~40% effect
against a measured angular correction of +4.6%. Or a breakdown of the
A1-A3 surface itself, which is RCORE-4's question, not this one.

Item (d) is the last of RCORE-3's cheap items. What remains is
exactly the heavy build, and nothing else.
