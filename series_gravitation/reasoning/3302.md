## Design of the amendment

The panel's instruction was "replace the 112 ms formula with the
ms-scale closed forms" — but replacement-by-deletion would violate the
anti-erasure discipline that made the FTERM corrigendum trustworthy. So
the structure is: every V2 statement preserved verbatim; dated
supersession notes at the five load-bearing spots (PLS, Theorem, the
predictions section, the amplitude ratios, Open Problems 1 and 4); and
ONE new section (§Amendment) carrying the corrected physics as a unit —
surface, reflectivity, delays, table. A reader encountering the paper
cold reads the V2 story, hits the supersession note, and is routed to
the amendment; a reader auditing the correction history has both layers
with dates and the panel record.
