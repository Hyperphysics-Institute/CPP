## Route B failed, and I should have seen it before proposing it

On 31 Aug I proposed a census-reach fixed point: shrink the PSR, reach fewer sources, lower the register, which raises the PSR — negative feedback, hence a floor. Setting it up today, the premise collapsed at once: AP-4's relay recursion carries DI-bits beyond one PSR. The PSR is the per-Moment *hop*, so it sets the census *speed* (`c_* = PSR/(√3 t_P)`), not the census *reach*. The interior register of a uniform body under a 1/r kernel is just the interior potential, PSR-independent, maximal at the centre. There is no fixed-point map because there is no reach truncation to feed it. I put this in the verify script as a check rather than in prose, so it cannot later be described as "still under investigation."

The error was the same one Patch 0733 corrected in another lane: treating the PSR as if it bounded what a GP can see rather than how far a CP moves per tick.

## Route A closed, cleaner than expected

The 3297 finding — surface at exactly Buchdahl — was recorded as a striking match and then treated as a consequence of `u = 1`. Run backwards, with the founder's "register limit" as the premise instead of Exclusion: an incompressible static body must satisfy Buchdahl; Buchdahl in the saturation variable is `u² − 5u + 4 ≥ 0`; roots 1 and 4; 4 is behind the horizon; so `u_max ≤ 1`. The half is the smaller root of a quadratic. I did not expect the bound to land on a clean quadratic with the second root censored — that is the part that reads as a derivation rather than a coincidence.

Two things I am careful not to overstate. First, P3: Buchdahl needs Einstein's equations to hold in the saturated interior, and GR-1c itself says the exterior formula "ceases to apply" inside. FE-1 was closed for the exterior; at saturation it is RCORE-4. The record carries DERIVED-CONDITIONAL and names the condition. Second, P4: Buchdahl gives an *upper bound* `u_max ≤ 1`; that the register saturates exactly there is an extremality principle I have stated as a premise. It is natural — the exterior register climbs inward and can only stop where it must — but natural is not derived.

## Why I did not call it a triangulation

The founder asked for one. A triangulation is two independent derivations meeting. This is one derivation that replaces a void one and recovers the old number. The second leg — why the register saturates at all, from inside the substrate, without borrowing FE-1 in the interior — is still owed, and if it arrives it turns Buchdahl from an input into a prediction. I would rather hand him one honest leg than two with one of them leaning on the other.
