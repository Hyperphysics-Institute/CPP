## The distinction that governs the form

At 3276 I corrected GR-1's epistemic ledger unilaterally because the
paper contradicted **itself** — abstract said CLOSED, ledger said open.
That is bookkeeping.

These four papers do not contradict themselves. They were accurate when
written and were overtaken by *later* work. Editing them is a decision
about how a legacy document should read, which is why I escalated rather
than acted at the time. What makes execution appropriate now is not that
the distinction dissolved but that the decision was delegated back.

The distinction still governs the **form**: because these papers were
never wrong, nothing is rewritten. Original item text stays verbatim; a
dated, bracketed note is appended beside it. The precedent is the paper's
own V3.3 calibration label (Patch 3204), which sits inline in exactly
this shape — so the notes will read as native to the document rather than
as a later hand.

## Three rules I held while writing the notes

**1. Name the delivering companion *and its limits*.** A status note
that says only "DELIVERED by GR-1c" converts an honest open problem into
an unqualified claim, which is the failure W-D exists to prevent, not to
commit. Item (2)'s note gives Theorem 1 and Theorem 2 — and then records
that **neither has been externally reviewed**, because CONV-027 examined
GR-1c's field-equation Proposition only. Item (3)'s note gives GR-1f and
GR-1g and carries forward that GR-1f shows *consistency* at all orders
rather than deriving Σ and Δ.

**2. Do not upgrade item (1).** The temptation is real: the
field-equation programme was the arc's biggest result of the month, and
a note could easily have read as though it closed this item. It does
not. T-1 was derived and ratified; producing the Ricci tensor — what the
item actually names — is untouched. The note says STILL OPEN first and
"substantially advanced" second, in that order.

**3. Leave alone what does not need touching.** Items (4) and (6) are
genuinely open and outside this arc. Item (5) already carries its V3.3
calibration label, which is more informative and more carefully hedged
than any status note I would write; adding one would dilute it. Three of
six items touched, not six.
