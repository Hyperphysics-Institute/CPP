the package to break it rather than to sell it. The claim in it is

flagship numbers; decide whether that is structure or coincidence and
say what would tell.

wrong if the surface has dynamics of its own. I have put the
spin-insensitivity conjecture in as a question, because it is the
thing that would make the agreement structural if it is real and a
warning sign if it is not.

The key asks for a number that requires running 3359's surface
criterion, not reading the package, so that the three seats who
plugged rounded coefficients last time cannot do it again.

goes: "reproduces" if structure, "within 1%" if undetermined, and
nothing if coincidence. I would rather the paper's verb be chosen by
five seats than by me, this week of all weeks.
