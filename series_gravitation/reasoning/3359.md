# Patch 3359 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The last rung, and the one I was most afraid of. Sasaki-Nakamura from
memory is a dozen coefficients any one of which, wrong, gives QNMs
that look fine and are not. So nothing Kerr was reported until three
tests passed: SN collapses to Regge-Wheeler at a = 0 pointwise (1.6e-9),
it goes short-range in Kerr, and — decisively — the a = 0 wall mode
matches 3356's independent RW code to 2.7e-6. The recall was right.
That is not a claim I would have made before the tests, and I do not
think I should have been trusted to make it on the lower tier.

THE PHYSICS MOVED. The gravitational (2,-2) line at GW150914 is
191 Hz. Every prior number was higher: 211 (eikonal top), 247 (the
transport CONV-034 withdrew), 251 (exact scalar, 3358). The scalar
sector was NOT a faithful proxy for line positions — 24% off at
(2,-2) — and the a = 0 case had already told me so (RW/scalar = 0.794)
had I compared. 3358's framing is corrected: right for structure,
wrong for positions. And the withdrawn "+17% above the top" was
wrong-SIGNED for gravitational waves: the line sits 7% BELOW the
geodesic top. The panel's withdrawal was more right than anyone knew.

TWO THINGS NEARLY WENT WRONG AND THE ASSERTS CAUGHT THEM.

First: (2,+1) came back as 0.62000 - 0.25000i — my guess, to the last
digit. I nearly reported it. The reason it did not ship is that the
sharpness test, which I had run only on the first mode at 3356 and
3358, now runs on EVERY reported mode individually. Contrast 0.75 means
the root-finder never moved. It is NOT LOCATED, and the record says so
in the table where a number would otherwise sit.

Second, and worse: that same failure had ALREADY shipped, in 3358. The
scalar (2,+1) = 0.63877 - 0.30216i passed no test but "fsolve
returned." Q ~ 1 means |Im omega| ~ 0.3, and the inward-integration
instability grows like e^27 over the range — the method cannot see
such modes at all. 3358's value is withdrawn, its record corrected in
place with strikethrough, and the "ordering survives at exact grade"
claim that leaned on it is withdrawn with it. The retrograde-keyed
ordering is back at eikonal-WKB grade until a different instrument
exists for very broad modes. That is a real downgrade of something I
said yesterday and it is stated as one.

I want the general lesson on the record because it is the fourth
instance this session: a validation applied to the FIRST result and
then assumed for the rest is not a validation of the rest. Every
number gets its own test or it is not a number.

WHAT IS OWED. RCORE-3 is discharged at the grades stated, every item.
But the flagship line moved from 211-251 Hz to 191 Hz, and that goes
through the panel (CONV-036), not through me. The GR-2 amendment is
owed, not enacted.
