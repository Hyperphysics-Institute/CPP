# Patch 3611 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

I did the first step of yesterday's order of work — the gauge map —
instead of asserting it, and it took the wall out from under three
weeks of numbers.

The map is derived, not recalled: the divergence of the Regge-Wheeler
mode and the vector wave operator on Schwarzschild, three coupled
equations, theta-independent once the harmonic identity is applied,
solved as a two-point problem with the coordinate displacement set to
zero at the wall — grid points do not move; time is universal there —
and outgoing at infinity. It converged. And at the wall the same
mode's register content in the lattice frame differs from its
Regge-Wheeler content by a factor of several: the trace part flips
sign and triples; a component that Regge-Wheeler gauge sets to zero
appears at full size. Every wall law since 3378 pinned the
Regge-Wheeler trace on the assumption that Regge-Wheeler gauge is the
lattice frame. It is not. Two panels said so in their caveats and I
wrote "SOUND-WITH-CAVEATS" and carried on.

Then clause C5, which was in the registry in June along with A3-prime
and which I read yesterday for the coupling and not for the map: the
count is h-bar-zero-zero, the lapse channel; the spatial trace is a
conservation completion, not register content; the isotropic
conformal-factor dictionary I have used for the wall is the static
special case. So the level set at the wall is on the lapse content,
one dictionary, not on the spatial trace through two. The 3391
elimination collapses to one condition in a different frame.

What survives is what was gauge-invariant: the parity theorem, the
shift reconstruction, the pole machinery, the surface at 8M/3, the
transmit picture for the odd sector. What does not survive as lattice-
frame physics is the line set — 195, 292, 208, and the indicative Kerr
numbers. They are solutions of a boundary-value problem, and the paper
already calls them a model; the record now says which model, in words
the paper's next version must carry.

I have not enacted anything and not hidden anything. The next
computation is the wall under C5, and one choice in it — what "the
grid points do not move" means for the residual harmonic pattern at
the wall — is the founder's picture, asked as F-16. The lesson of the
week, a third time: the registry had the answer; the panels had the
caveat; the computation is what made me read them.
