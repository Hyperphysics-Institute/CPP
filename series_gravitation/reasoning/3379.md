instinct, wrong accounting. Today I did the l = 3 line and the peak

subtleties. I could write down something that looks like it. It would
be a guess. The scaled estimate — 166 Hz and 250 Hz — is in the record
labelled as a guess so the magnitude is on file, not as a number.
