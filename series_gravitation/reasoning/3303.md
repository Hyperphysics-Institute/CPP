## Why GR-1e's amendment is interpretive, not substitutive

GR-1d had a superseded formula and ratified replacements ready — the
amendment could substitute. GR-1e is different in kind: its central
mechanism (Hawking pair production straddling a horizon) presupposes the
horizon that F-R1 removes, and NO replacement mechanism has been derived
— surface emission from a z = 2 hard body is new physics nobody has
computed. Writing a replacement into the paper would be exactly the
silent-rederivation move the HALT discipline forbids. So the amendment
does three honest things: relabels the geometry (the ratified part),
RE-OPENS the emission question explicitly (widening Open Problem 1
rather than closing it), and promotes the one result that survives
untouched — the fixed-point stability Proposition, which never used the
horizon and is now a pillar of the |R| = 1 derivation. The paper exits
the amendment MORE honest about what it knows: geometry ratified,
stability promoted, emission mechanism openly unknown.

## The version-number choice

GR-1d took V2 → V3 (a prediction changed). GR-1e takes V1 → V1.1: no
equation or number in the paper changed; the reading did. Keeping the
minor bump signals to a reader that the delta is interpretive scaffolding
plus an honest widening of the open-problem set.
