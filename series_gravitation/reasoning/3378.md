# Patch 3378 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The missing step from 3377 was: what does "register pinned" mean for
the Zerilli function. I assumed on the way in that it meant Dirichlet
on K, and half-expected to find Dirichlet on Z+. It means neither. It
took a wrong turn to see why.

The wrong turn: I wrote the CPP metric as conformally flat in isotropic
coordinates and tried to treat that as a gauge. It is not a gauge. Once
G and h1 are fixed to zero, the residual freedom is a time
reparametrization that does not touch g_rr or g_thth, so H2 - K is
invariant — and for a Zerilli mode it is not zero; it is c1 Z + c2 Z'
with both coefficients nonzero. I checked that with two reconstruction
formulas I recalled separately, which agree identically, and I trust
the agreement more than either memory. So a spatial slice that is
purely conformal cannot carry an even-parity gravitational wave. The
register is not the wave. The wave's traceless part is something CPP's
scalar dictionary does not contain. The CONV-028 seat that flagged
"scalar vs rank-2 charter language" was pointing at this.

What the register does pin is the trace, and to first order the trace
is the conformal factor. So the wall law is H2 + 2K = 0, one condition,
which is all a second-order equation needs. Through the reconstruction
it is Robin on Z+, with a coefficient that depends on frequency because
H2 contains Z'' and Z'' brings in omega squared. The coefficient
crosses zero — Neumann — at M omega = 0.415, twelve percent above the
flagship frequency. That is not a coincidence I understand yet, and I
have not claimed it is one.

I ran the same Wigner scan 3297 ran, on the even sector, with the old
Dirichlet wall and the derived one. The resonance moves down and
narrows. A Neumann wall by itself supports a near-trapped mode at the
barrier top with four times the delay of the Dirichlet hump, and the
derived wall passes through Neumann at the resonance. The delay spike
under the dispersive wall is large, but part of it is the boundary's
own d(phase)/d(omega), which is not a cavity time. I have written the
shift and the softening as the result and the width as not claimed.

The odd sector: the register does not govern it, and CPP has nothing
else that does yet. That means the ladder GR-2 shipped was on the
sector the theory does not constrain, with a wall nobody derived. I
have said that plainly in the record because it is true and because
saying it is what makes the even-sector result the prediction rather
than an alternative.

Not the win. The number that moves is at a = 0; the flagship is Kerr.
The Kerr recompute is the next patch, not a panel.
