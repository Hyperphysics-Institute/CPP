# Patch 3633 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

I set out to test the census against the exterior and found the test
was empty as I had posed it. A gauge vector can make any one scalar
harmonic. So "is δv harmonic in lattice coordinates" is not a question
about the exterior; it is a question about which frame is the lattice.
Once I saw that, the job became: build the two frames the corpus has
actually named, and read the Love number in each.

The harmonic-pattern frame first, because 3611 said it is the lattice
frame. I derived the harmonic condition on Schwarzschild for the static
even sector — two radial ODEs, the t and φ components dying
identically — and found the RW tide is not harmonic beyond first order:
the divergence is 4(1 − M/r). The homogeneous exponents are 3, 1, −2,
−4, and the particular solution for the tide carries the logarithm
harmonic coordinates on Schwarzschild always carry. That log means the
frame at infinity is a convention; it does not reach the wall if the
GPs do not move there, which is F-16 option 1, which I imposed. With
that, the lapse level set is untouched and the +0.033 of 3632 stands.

Then two things I did not expect. The spatial trace of h̄ at the wall
is not zero — 0.69 against an h̄₀₀ of −2.67 — so C5's "statics: τ = 0"
is a weak-field sentence: the c07 dictionary linearised gives a lock
of −3(1 − v/2), which is C5's −3 only at v = 0 and is −2 at the wall.
The corpus's nonlinear static dictionary and its linear dynamical one
disagree at O(v), and nobody had linearised c07 at the wall before.
And the register read from the lapse in this frame is nowhere near
harmonic in lattice coordinates near the wall (residual −1.2 at 2.7M),
though it becomes so slowly outward. So 3389's p = 0 census and 3611's
harmonic frame are one frame at first order and two frames at
O(M·tide). That is the finding I promised, in a sharper form than I
had guessed.

The census frame I then built directly: the gauge in which δv is
harmonic with the exterior's own far-field coefficients and the c07
lock holds. Both gauge functions come out algebraic, the frame always
exists, it has no residual, and in it the whole problem is Newtonian
in lattice radius: λ = (3/2)R̄⁵, k₂ = (3/4)(R̄/R)⁵ = +0.042. I checked
that its level set reproduces the Newtonian one to a part in a
million, so the algebra is closed.

Three readings — lapse in the harmonic frame, h̄₀₀ in the harmonic
frame, the census frame — give +0.033, +0.088, +0.042. Different by a
factor of three; the same sign. The negative bracket of 3632 belongs to
no frame the corpus has. So under Thomas's working postulate the sign
is positive, and the published falsifier — "a positive k₂ falsifies" —
is inverted. That is a corpus claim changing sign, which is exactly
the win-check trigger, and I will dispatch the round rather than
enact V2.3 on my own reading of three frames I built in one session.
