# Reasoning fragment — Patch 3313 (20 Aug 2026)

**Scope:** W-C chapter three, "Four Ways the Sky Said Yes" (GR-1i).

## The frame-first decision

The chapter's biggest risk was triumphalism: four passed tests invite
a victory lap, and a victory lap would betray the paper it serves —
GR-1i's whole character is its frozen claim discipline. So the honest
frame LEADS: before any story, the reader is told what the evidence
does not prove (no CPP-vs-GR discrimination; values inherited by
construction; zero predictions minted). Trust the reader with the
limitation first and the four yeses land as earned rather than
oversold. The closing then answers the question the frame provokes
("what is a guaranteed exam worth?") with the paper's own two
answers: execution is where errors live, and the certification that
the machinery terminates in the observed sky is what chapter 4's
dangerous predictions stand on.

## Story-level choices

- Mercury gets the Vulcan hook (a phantom planet hunted for decades —
  historically true, pedagogically irresistible) and the "whisper"
  reading; the constants sensitivity is planted mid-story ("hold that
  thought") and paid off in the ethics section — same banked-thought
  device as chapter 2, now a house style.
- Starlight is deliberately SHORT (chapter 1 owns the factor of two;
  repeating it would cheapen both chapters) and instead carries the
  achromatic-lens beat — the CONV-029 adoption turned into the
  chapter's delight moment: a lens with no rainbow, because the ruler
  changes, not a medium. Restraint quoted (logged unminted).
- Shapiro's mechanism is given the arc's cleanest formulation yet:
  "locally always c, globally late" — one reach per Moment, fewer
  meters per reach. Cassini honesty per the paper: compliance is
  structural (β = γ = 1 identically), not numerical.
- Clocks end on GPS-as-infrastructure with the homeschool-ready
  answer to the doubting student: a road trip and a navigation app.

## The ethical core

The constants-provenance section is the panel's CONV-029 §3 adoption
told as a story ("The decimal that taught a lesson"), with the
counterfactual examined honestly: the unstated-constants habit is
SYMMETRIC — it hides worsenings as easily as improvements — which is
why the rule matters even when, as here, the correction improved the
agreement. The closing line of that section ("discipline is cheapest
to install where the stakes feel low; it is load-bearing later") is
the bridge to the whole programme's operating character.
