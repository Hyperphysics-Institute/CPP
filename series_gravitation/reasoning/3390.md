# Patch 3390 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder ratified the half and said nothing in the lattice prefers
the old guess. I recorded the ruling and went to enact the consequence
— the surface at 1.33 Schwarzschild radii, the poles redone there, the
papers. The poles stopped me.

At the new radius the odd sector is fine: a sharp line at 208 Hz, Q 8.
The even sector is not. Its derived wall supports modes that GROW, with
a growth time of nine milliseconds for a GW150914 remnant. The roots
are r0-independent to fifteen figures; this is not numerics. The
reason is one I had already recorded on Wednesday and not understood:
the wall coefficient's two terms flip sign beyond about 2.4 M. In the
time domain the frequency-squared term is a boundary mass, and beyond
2.4 M that mass is negative. A negative mass on a spring grows. The
stability boundary of the trace-pinned even wall is 2.38 M, and the
ratified floor sits at 2.67.

So three ratified things collide at second order: the PSR law's floor,
the even wall's stability, and the window that permits both. I have
not enacted the surface move. I have not written the paragraph. I have
minted the collision as an open item with three branches: the
compliance term the arc has been deferring may regularize a negative
boundary mass; the PSR law's third-order coefficient, still open, may
move the floor inside 2.38 M; or the R-core really is unstable at its
surface, which would be a prediction and a strange one.

There is a fourth reading I want to put to the founder as a picture:
maybe stability is what locates the surface. The register saturates
where it saturates; but a surface at which the even sector grows is
not a surface that persists. If the substrate cannot hold a surface
beyond 2.38 M, then the PSR law's third order is fixed by that, the
way its second order was fixed by Mercury. That would be the theory
completing itself at the next order by the next constraint, which is
the pattern of this whole week.

The half is ratified and stands. Everything downstream of it is held
until the wall is stable somewhere the floor can be.
