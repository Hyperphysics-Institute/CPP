# Patch 3605 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder built the source. Two bodies orbit; each is a source of an
inverse-square register gradient; the gradients superimpose; the
superposition rotates; the pattern repeats every half orbit, so the
wave is at twice the orbital frequency; the energy comes out of the
orbit. Every piece of that is right, and the last piece is what the
binary pulsar has been measuring for forty years.

What I had to test is whether the picture carries the wave to the
detector, and it does not by itself. Near the pair, the second
derivatives of the superposed scalar field are the tidal tensor, and
it does stretch one way and squeeze the other, turning at twice the
orbital rate. That is a real quadrupolar deformation of the Sea and it
is exactly the near-zone limit of general relativity's wave. But
compute the tidal tensor of a propagating scalar wave and the
stretch/squeeze is gone at one over r: the leading term is purely
along the direction of propagation, and the transverse part dies as
one over r cubed. I did this symbolically rather than by argument. A
scalar field can hold the tide near the source and cannot carry it
away. The tensor field does both.

So the founder's picture and GR's wave are the same object where the
founder built it and different objects where the detector sits, and
the difference is the rank of what propagates. The completion in his
own terms is the one he gave for light three days ago: the near-zone
deformation of the Sea propagates as a transverse polarization, one
over r cubed near, one over r far, crossover at a wavelength over two
pi. The DI-bits carry the deformation; the count carries only the
breathing part, which the binary pulsar says must be small. What is
to be derived is the relay law for a quadrupolar deformation — the
spin-2 version of the scalar relay he already has — with four
conditions on it, the last being GR's luminosity to two parts in a
thousand.

The question back is the mechanism at the single-Moment level: when
the DI-bits from a deformed region arrive one PSR further out carrying
a quadrupolar vector pattern, does the receiving DP take on the same
deformation, or only the count. If the former, the gravitational wave
exists in CPP and the derivation is the template of T-1. If the
latter, it dies at the source.
