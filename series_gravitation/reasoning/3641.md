# Patch 3641 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

Thomas has said he cannot guide the PCD-level detail by picture and
that we should treat the saturation regime as a calibration exercise:
derive from the nine axioms and the cycle, compare with empirics, and
let agreement across cases and across reviewers be the proof. I think
that is right, and I think it is what the corpus has been doing all
along without saying so; the danger is only that "calibration" can
become a place to hide failures, so I wrote the discipline down before
adopting anything: pin once and never refit, label everything until the
ledger closes, and treat a failed row as a failed extension.

Under that policy the choice of working law is mine to make, and I
made it: the budget extension of 3640, because it assumes the least
and because it removes a structure — the shell and its load — rather
than adding one. It has no free element, which is the best kind of
thing to calibrate: there is nothing to tune, so every row of the
ledger is a test. The decisive row is the echo delay, 0.95 ms at 62
solar masses with nothing fitted; the heavy pulsars pass as Einstein's
stars with a slower clock; the PPN sector is untouched by
construction. Three rows are owed — the surface stability with a
smooth interior, the reflectivity from the returned bits, and the Love
number as a spring deflection — and I have put them in order.

What I told Thomas is that the swarm proof he wants already exists in
the corpus as the convergence protocol, and that under review economy
it should be run once, on the extension as a whole, not once per
patch. The held CONV-042 becomes that round when the owed rows are in.
