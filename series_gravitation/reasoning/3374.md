The first thing the slab model said was that there is no wall. I had
been carrying the picture of a surface that reflects — the whole
"clamped register" thread was about WHAT KIND of reflector the surface
is — and the model's first line is that under the ratified dictionary
the census speed is continuous across the surface, so a linear medium
reflects nothing there. 3297's mirror was the Dirichlet condition and
only the Dirichlet condition. That reframes CONV-038 Q4: |R| = 1
survived because the core is lossless and the wave comes back from the
CENTRE; the phase pi died because nothing at the surface flips it.

Three boundary laws, one wavelet. The old mirror: all prompt, phase
pi. The linear slab: nothing prompt, everything back after the round
trip. The one-sided slab: a third prompt (the compression lobe, refused
at the cap, phase pi), two thirds delayed, with harmonics. Which of the
last two is CPP's is decided by attainment — whether the interior
register sits AT the cap or below it. So FLOOR-1(a), which the panel
reduced to "an asserted extremality step," turns out to choose the
echo morphology. That is the argument for computing it.
