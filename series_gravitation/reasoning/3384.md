# Patch 3384 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder's rule for the twisting half of the wave — it is registered
in SSV_net, which has no cap, so nothing refuses it — became a boundary
condition in two lines: regular solution inside a flat core, matched at
the surface. Everything about it hangs on one number, the ratio of
tortoise to lattice distance at the wall, which is the strong-field
c_* map that has been parked since August. So I computed it at both
values the corpus offers and let the map's cost show as a number.

Under the CPP value the shipped axial line does not move. Not "moves a
little": 0.1%. A Robin coefficient of 1.5/M at the barrier top behaves
like a wall. The X = 0 the arc carried on the axial sector for two
months was, for this bracket, an accident that landed on the derived
answer. Under the Schwarzschild value the line drops five percent,
sharpens, and a second family of echoes appears in the band, spaced by
the core's round trip. So the parked question decides three things at
once and is now the single most valuable open input in the arc. It is
also a scalar dictionary question, not a reconstruction, which means
it is answerable. I have said it should be minted.

The two sectors now sit side by side at a = 0 and they are not alike.
The even sector has the sharp line — Q 25 — because the scalar
register is over-demanded and the surface is Neumann-like at the
barrier top. The odd sector has a broad line because the vector
register is uncapped and the surface transmits. The wave's two halves
see two surfaces because they are recorded in two places. That is the
sentence the flagship should eventually carry.

paragraph will carry both, and it should go in with the c_* map or
with the Kerr work, not alone.
