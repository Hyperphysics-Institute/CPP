# Patch 3375 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

I went in to answer "is the interior at the cap?" and found the panel
had been asking two questions under one word. Whether the cap's VALUE
is exactly the Buchdahl number is still open and the founder has no
picture for it. Whether the interior register SITS at the cap is a
theorem, and a short one: the register records min(demand, cap);
demand is a 1/r-kernel census of non-negative sources, which is
monotone inward for any such source (d|phi|/dr = -M(r)/r^2); the
surface is where demand equals cap; so demand exceeds cap everywhere
inside. The interior is pinned, with an excess that grows to half the
cap at the centre for a uniform core. That kills 3374's law (A) for
the R-core (headroom needs an unsaturated body) and its law (B) (zero
excess needs no interior source). The R-core obeys a fourth law.

The fourth law is the one that rescues 3297 honestly. Compression can't
move a register with no headroom: exact Dirichlet at the surface, phase
pi. Rarefaction can pull the register off the cap only where the pull
beats the local excess — a skin whose depth is the amplitude over the
excess gradient, d = R delta/u_max. As the amplitude goes to zero the
skin vanishes and both signs reflect promptly at pi. So X = 0, |R| = 1,
phase pi is the LINEAR LIMIT of the over-demanded core. 3297 had the
right limit for the wrong reason. GR-2's line is the linear-response
prediction, and it now has a derivation instead of an assumption.

The nonlinear correction is small and one-sided: at delta/u ~ 0.1 the
rarefaction half-cycles of the first echo lag by 0.1-0.4 ms (the clock
map still unminted) and carry harmonics; by the third echo it is gone.
3374's two-timescale echo train is not the generic prediction; it was
the (A)/(B) prediction, and the R-core is neither.

I got the skin dynamics wrong once in code before getting the brackets

SIGN of the field inside the skin. That leaks at the surface node

positive and positive is what the cap forbids. The register in the
skin is a string pulled off a ceiling it is pressed against; when the
pull ends it goes back to the ceiling and cannot overshoot. That is a
peeling contact line, not a Dirichlet floor, and its reflection is
somewhere between Dirichlet at the surface and Dirichlet at the skin
floor. I computed both brackets and left the peel for rung 3 rather
than ship a wrong model of it.
