The heavy item, taken by the half that separates cleanly.

Full Teukolsky is a large build — radial integration with
Sasaki-Nakamura stabilisation and complex root-finding, a big fresh
error surface. But the equation SEPARATES, and its angular half was
both tractable today and pointed straight at the assumption the panel
has criticised twice without anyone ever measuring it. That made the
ordering obvious: do the half that retires a standing objection before
the half that needs a week.

THE RESULT IS AGAINST US IN DIRECTION, and I put that in section 3
rather than section 6. The eikonal Q OVERSHOOTS, and Q enters R with a
minus sign, so the exact value RAISES the phase volume and makes
trapping EASIER than the census assumed — ell_crit could move down,
not up. The shift is small (+0.5% in A at ell = 7, well inside the
+/-1 already carried on ell_crit) but it is the unfavourable
direction, and a reader should not have to derive that for themselves.

GPT WAS RIGHT, twice, and the size is now on the record: +4.6% at
ell = 2 against +0.5% at ell = 7, a factor of nine, exactly the low-ell
vs high-ell asymmetry the seat argued. Four rounds of that seat's
dissents have now been adopted, and this is the first time one of them
has been converted from a caveat into a measured number. That is what
the objection deserved.

ONE ACCIDENTAL ROBUSTNESS WORTH NAMING: the 3352 theorem is untouched,
because its Step 3 used only Q > 0 and never Q's value. I did not
design that; it fell out of proving the theorem at the wall rather
than at a turning point. But it means the lane's strongest claim is
insensitive to the lane's most-criticised approximation, which is a
good structural position to be in and worth stating explicitly.

THE CHECK FAILED THREE TIMES AND EACH FAILURE WAS WORTH MORE THAN A
PASS WOULD HAVE BEEN. First run: 7.66 error — the m = 0 sector needs a
Neumann endpoint I do not impose. Second: 0.54 — intermediate |m| < l
resolves the weakly-vanishing pole behaviour badly on a uniform grid.
Third: 1.6e-5 absolute, which I nearly "fixed" by loosening the
number, and stopped because that is precisely the move this session
has condemned four times. The right fix was recognising the METRIC was
wrong: eigenvalues scale like l(l+1), so an absolute bar silently
tightens with ell. Replaced with a relative bar benchmarked against
the smallest effect being claimed — discretisation error must be far
below the physics or the measurement is reporting its own grid. It is,
by a factor of 11690.

Both fenced sectors (m = 0, |m| < l) are named with their reasons and
shown not to touch any reported mode, and an assert in the script
enforces that every reported mode has |m| = l. Fencing without an
assert is just a comment.

as "the gravitational calculation." The radial half and s = -2 remain
