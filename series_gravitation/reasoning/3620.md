move by factors of two and three. At the horizon end every line is
Kerr's own quasinormal mode, as it must be. So the R-core does not have

only how long they ring — by nothing if it absorbs as a horizon does,
by a factor of two or three if it absorbs nothing, by something in
between for the theory's wall, which absorbs the part the June axiom
sends into the core.

not a separate line and not a train of pulses. It is an excess in the

no horizon can do, while the prograde mode comes out shorter-lived,
the eighteen percent, because the wall's real impedance is tuned to
its frequency and not to theirs. So the falsifier and the signature
point in opposite directions for different modes, which is the kind
of structure a real prediction has.

theory's prediction to compute. The paragraph for the paper is written
