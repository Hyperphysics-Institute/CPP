# Patch 3389 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

I went to compute the second-order coefficient of the census and found
it is zero, exactly, and that I had been wrong about the mechanism
twice in two days. The ratified census is a shell mean, and a mean of
a harmonic function is the function, for any radius: the register is
the bare potential to all orders. My 3388 story — shorter hops deposit
more densely — assumed a flux streaming from a source; the relay is
not that; every grid point re-emits its fixed count, so the arrival
count is that count everywhere, up to the square of the hop-length
gradient, which is Planck-small. Withdrawn, and the founder's "no
special rule" holds with nothing carved out of it.

So where does Mercury come from, if not the census? From the one place
the bare theory had explicitly left a number blank. SR-1 writes the
PSR law as a series and says the linear form is exact to first order;
the Padé 1/(1+eps) it uses is a working choice with second-order
coefficient 1. With the founder's clock — the lapse IS the PSR — the
PPN beta is one half plus that coefficient. Mercury says beta is one.
So the coefficient is one half. And the law with coefficient one half
is (1 - eps/2)/(1 + eps/2): the log-lapse, exactly, the very
dictionary the charter imposed to get Schwarzschild. The theory was
not changed. It was completed at the order it had left open, by the
oldest test there is.

The consequence is the same one I priced on Tuesday under a wrong
mechanism: the floor l_P/2 is reached where the lapse is one half, and
that is at 1.33 Schwarzschild radii, not at Buchdahl. The surface
moves, the cavity shortens to 0.7 ms, the strong-field departure
closes, and every wall pole is to be redone at the new radius. I have
enacted none of it. Two things go to the founder: ratify the
coefficient, and say whether anything in the substrate prefers the
Padé's second order over the log's. I do not think anything does — the
Padé was chosen as the lowest-order rational form, not for a reason in
the lattice — but the law is his.

The lesson I am writing down for myself: three times this week I
reached for a mechanism when the record already held the answer as an
unfixed constant. Look for the blank before inventing the rule.
