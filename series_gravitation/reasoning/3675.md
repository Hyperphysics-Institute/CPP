# Patch 3675 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The handover sent me at the re-cut question — does the cycle above
the cap propagate the tensor wave as Einstein's equations do — and
told me it was not to be another surface law. Before touching it I
read what the lane had already scored, and the table in 3644 settled
something I had not expected: every way of reading the K-of-D census
as a wave law is already out. If the untaken bits are discarded each
Moment the surface is an absorber and damps too fast. If the wave
rides the register it is the lossless graded transmit and the
frequency is off by a fifth. If the untaken bits come back coherently
it is a mirror. Only GR's own admittance from 8M/3 fits, and 3670 had
shown it is a function, not a number. And 3670 §5(iv) had already
named the one structure that meets both data with nothing to tune:
GR's wave equation continued inward with the horizon behind it.

So the question was never what law the cap obeys. It was which
quantities the cap acts on. THEO-PCD-BUDGET took K of D and applied
that fraction to everything the GP records, and because a GP re-emits
its state, the wave saw the cap at every hop. But the cycle has two
outputs, not one: what the GP does with the bits and what it passes
on. A finite Moment bounds the first — a CP can only act on so much.
Does it bound the second? The shell-sum in A3′ is a transport step.
If the GP passes on what it cannot process, then the wave propagates
on the demand field, which is just GR's exterior solution carried
inward until the assembled lapse reaches zero at v = 2, and nothing
comes back from there. That is the excised region, and it comes with
a reason instead of a fit.

I did not want to believe it too quickly, so I priced it. The row
that killed the budget law, the Love number, passes: the static tide
is Q_ij content, it rides the sea, GR's black hole has none. The row
the lane has been fighting since 3644, the reflectivity, passes at
every spin because the wall is the horizon-equivalent wall by
construction; my own implementation of that wall gives Leaver's poles
to three parts in a hundred thousand and gives back the pinned s to
under a percent as a by-product — the calibration is explained and
no longer needed. The echo prediction becomes a null, which is where
the record had already been pushed. And the falsifier is sharp: if
the modulation were split with the returned excess and came back
coherently from just outside v = 2, it would arrive a quarter of a
second after the ringdown, which is precisely the Planckian echo the
searches have looked for. The extension forbids that. The published
bound is its first cold test, and I have not pulled it yet; that is
the next patch, not this one.

What this is not: a derivation from the nine axioms. The axioms do
not say whether the relay is truncated with the register. A3′'s
wording leans one way, AP-4d's leans the other, and the gap between
them is the extension. It is a partition with zero constants, it is
bimetric inside the cap, and I am aware that is a strong thing to
say. But it is what every wave observable has been asking for since
3612, and PD-007 says I choose and the ledger decides. I have chosen,
labelled it, and not asked Thomas to pin it.
