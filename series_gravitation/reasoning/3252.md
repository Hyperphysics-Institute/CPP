## Route decisions

1. **Derive in standard coordinates, not isotropic.** The four tests are
   coordinate-invariant observables; the exact transformation
   r = r_iso(1+ϱ)² is machine-verified (3228 check 0), so the standard
   Schwarzschild form gives the familiar Binet machinery with no loss of
   claim to the isotropic metric. The isotropic form returns in the
   mechanism section, where it is the physically transparent frame
   (conformally flat spatial part ⇒ graded-index reading;
   n(r) ≈ 1 + 2GM/c²r to first order). Ruled out: redoing all four
   derivations natively in isotropic coordinates — correct but longer,
   and it obscures rather than illuminates, since every textbook reader
   will check against the standard-coordinate forms.

2. **Perturbative + numeric, not numeric-only.** Each dynamical test
   gets the closed-form perturbative derivation (the paper's content)
   plus the statement of the 3228 numeric cross-check (the machine
   check). The perturbation calcs re-verified by hand this session:
   - Perihelion: resonant-term extraction from u₀ = A(1+e cosφ) in
     u″+u = A+Bu², δ = AB = 3GM/(c²a(1−e²)); 6π×1.3275e20 /
     (5.7909e10 × 0.957716 × c²) = 5.0197e-7 rad/orbit × 415.20
     orbits/cy = 42.99″/cy. Matches 3228 closed form and numeric
     (42.9917 both).
   - Deflection: u₁ particular solution of u″+u = (3GM/c²b²)sin²φ is
     (3GM/2c²b²)(1+⅓cos2φ) ≡ (GM/c²b²)(1+cos²φ) — the identity is
     EXACT (3/2 + ½cos2φ = 1+cos²φ), which caught a draft error: an
     "+…" originally appended to that expression was removed (nothing
     is omitted). Asymptote value 2GM/c²b² ⇒ α = 4GM/c²b = 1.7516″.
   - Shapiro: leading log only, matching 3228; the sub-leading
     percent-level terms explicitly deferred to the γ-parametrized
     framing (Cassini bound quoted as the modern observable).
   - Redshift/GPS: gh/c² = 2.455e-15; GPS grav +45.7, SR −7.2, net
