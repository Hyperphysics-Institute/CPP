# Patch 3351 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

GR-2 V1.5. The content edit was routine: 3349 and 3350 made V1.4's
"inherited, not computed" sentence false, and it had to be replaced by
what is now actually true. I ordered the three arguments BY MARGIN
rather than by how they were discovered, and put band separation
first, because it is the one with room. A reader deciding how much to
trust this should meet the strongest argument first and the thinnest
last, with the thinness labelled.

The real content of this patch is what happened to the checker.

V1.4's provenance checker validated LEDGER -> PAPER: every script it
knew about exists, and its count matches. It never checked
PAPER -> LEDGER. So when I added numbers from 3349 and 3350, the
checker had NO OBJECTION. That is exactly the mechanism that grew the
original defect across V1.0-V1.3 — numbers from new scripts arriving
while the verification sentence stayed put. I built a guard against
the disease and left its main transmission route open.

Found it by asking, before editing, "will the checker catch this?" —
and the answer was no. Worth keeping as a habit: the question to ask
of a new guard is not "does it pass?" but "what would it fail to
notice?"

Then the fix failed twice on its way in, and both are in the code:

(1) The scope-creep detector PASSED VACUOUSLY on first run. It matched
raw LaTeX, the filenames contain \allowbreak, so it found exactly ONE
citation and cheerfully reported "all in the ledger." A detector that
passes because it saw almost nothing is the same fail-open mode the
3347 audit condemned in the audit script itself — second occurrence,
same session. It now matches normalized text AND fails closed on its
own reach: it must find at least as many citations as the ledger has
entries.

(2) It then flagged 3348 itself as unledgered, correctly. I excluded
it — but with the reason written next to the exclusion (this script
produces no physics number for the paper; it verifies the table), not
as a silent exemption. Silent exemptions are how ledgers rot.

Adversarial test as at 3348: inserted a bogus 9999_bogus_script.py
citation, confirmed the detector catches it, removed it, confirmed
7/7. An assertion nobody has watched fail is a decoration.

NET: the paper now says something true about its own verification, and
the mechanism that made the old sentence false is itself under test in
both directions. That is a better outcome than the sentence fix alone,
and it cost one extra hour.
