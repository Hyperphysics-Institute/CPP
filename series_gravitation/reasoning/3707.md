# Patch 3707 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

Yesterday I called the albedo the first genuinely new question the
lane had produced. Today it is answered by the thing I found in
between: there is no surface. The matter drained through; what is
left at eight-thirds is a line in the field where the registers fill,
and light does not know a full register from an empty one because the
relay is whole either way. So a photon falls through the contour, on
Einstein's metric, to Einstein's horizon, and is absorbed the way
Einstein says. The reflector I imagined has a mass fraction of ten to
the minus eighteen.

I am aware that three of the last four patches have closed questions
by discovering that the object is emptier than I had pictured. That
is not a trick; it is the same fact — the drain — reaching its
consequences one at a time. The black hole in this theory has become
very close to Einstein's from the outside, dark for a stated reason
where his is dark by fiat, and different only where nobody can look.
The neutron stars are where the difference is left. One physics item
remains, in the early universe.
