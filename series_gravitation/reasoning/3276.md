# Reasoning — Patch 3276 (GR-1 V1.0.2, ledger-consistency correction)

**Session 151, 20 Aug 2026. Verbatim at-patch capture.**

## How this was found

The session opened on the Session-150 queue: W-B, documentation suites
×11, GR-1 and GR-1j first. Writing a suite means reading the paper as a
claim structure rather than as prose — the `phenomena` file has to say
what is explained, the `philosophy` file what "proved" means here, the
`FAQ` file what a hostile reader will ask. Reading GR-1 that way, the
epistemic ledger and the abstract disagreed.

The abstract (V1.0): OPEN-GR-FE-1 "is now CLOSED ... the general CPP
field equation is derived from the messenger census, panel-ratified
across two review rounds, and consolidated in companion GR-1j."

The ledger, §"Correspondence claims (not derivations)", unchanged since
V0: "The general field-equation derivation ... is registered as
OPEN-GR-FE-1 and is not attempted in V0," and "With the general
equations underived, Birkhoff-type uniqueness statements and the CPP
form of the energy-momentum tensor are likewise open; the corpus
currently contains neither."

Both sentences are in the shipped V1.0.1 paper. The Open Problems
section had been updated correctly. The ledger had not.

## Why it was missed at V1.0 prep

Patch 3270's own reasoning fragment says the ledger was deliberately
left alone because its "W2/PSR conditionality" was already correct.
That judgment was right about the thing it was about. The ledger has
three subsections; the conditionality lives in the third
("Conditional and inherited"), and that one needed nothing. The
field-equations rows live in the second, and they were a different
claim entirely — a status claim about what the programme had and had
not derived. The audit asked "is the conditionality right?" and got
the right answer to that question. Recorded as a process finding: a
paper's ledger has as many independent staleness surfaces as it has
rows, and a status change closes some rows while leaving others
correct; "the ledger is fine" is not a checkable statement.

This is the same failure shape as the founder's PPP audit finding at
3271 — the V1.0 ship was checked for review basis and claim state, not
for internal consistency across sections — and it is the reason W-B is
worth doing before the deposit wave rather than after. Zenodo DOIs are
permanent.

## Discipline observed in the edit

**Anti-erasure.** The V0 rows are retained verbatim under a superseded
heading rather than rewritten. The reason is stated in the paper
itself: the distinction those rows drew — reproducing solutions is not
deriving the equation — is precisely the standard the arc was
subsequently held to, and deleting the standard once it is met reads
as though it was never set. The corrigendum precedent (GR-1c V2.2,
defective formula preserved) governs the form.

**No upgrade smuggled.** The status notes say what was delivered and
at what strength, and each names its own limit:

- T-1: derived, but equivalence to the full rank-2 Einstein equations
  is explicitly NOT claimed — bounded at op:einstein.
- T-2: uniqueness exact; Birkhoff-type CONDITIONAL, because the bare
  equation admits monopole radiation and staticity is enforced by the
  census, not the equation; asymptotically-flat local class only (the
  CONV-028 §3 adoptions).
- T-3: a conserved current, NOT a full energy-momentum tensor. The V0
  row asked for the "CPP form of the energy-momentum tensor," and the
  honest answer is that the programme has the trace-sector current and
  not the tensor. Saying so in the row that asked the question is the
  whole point of retaining the row.

Writing these, the temptation was to let "delivered" do more work than
it earned — the arc did have a very good session and the notes could
have read as a victory lap. The rows are the place where that would
show, so they carry the conditionality first.

**Assert before write.** Three str_replace edits, each anchored on
exact text verified by view immediately prior (the 3271 lesson:
assert every anchor, including version lines). Post-edit grep
confirmed the stale title string survives only inside the changelog
comment that documents its removal.

**Compile gate.** Baseline compile run BEFORE editing (0 errors, 10
pages) so that any post-edit failure would be attributable to the edit
rather than to the container's toolchain — the stash-verify discipline
from W-A2 applied to a fresh environment. Post-edit: pdflatex ×2, 0
errors, no undefined references, 10 pages.

## Second defect, found in passing

The title block's version line read "Version 0 (assembly draft)"
against a `\date` line reading Version 1.0.1. Identical defect class to
SPIN-3 at Patch 3253, where the title version line had lagged at
"Version 0 (draft)" after the v1.0 ship. Two instances in two papers
in two days is a pattern, not a coincidence: the ship protocol updates
`\date` and the header changelog, and the `\title` block's subtitle is
not on the checklist. Flagged for the PPP program — a one-line grep
across the corpus (`\title` blocks whose version string disagrees with
`\date`) would find the rest, and belongs in W-A's successor rather
than in this patch, which is scoped to GR-1.

Also closed here: V1.0.1 was recorded only in the documentation-suite
changelog and never entered in the .tex header changelog. Entered now.
