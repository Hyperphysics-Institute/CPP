# Patch 3684 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

Writing down what the partition leaves to matter, I drew the two
radii and saw the thing I had skated over: the sea's horizon is at
2M and the R-core's surface is at 8M/3, so a third of a mass-radius
of register matter sits outside the horizon in plain view. The dark
interior cannot answer a tide. That shell can. I had said the Love
number was zero by construction; it is zero for the part nobody can
see and conditional for the part they can. The condition is a
number — the shell's k₂ must be below 0.39 — and the corpus has
never computed it, because the only register law it ever ran gave a
k₂ of eight, which is not a stiffness any material has.

So the cleanest pass in my proposal is now a conditional pass, and
the register law, which I had been happy to leave open, is
load-bearing for exactly one row. That is the correct shape for the
panel to see: three seats scored, one of them by a real measurement
that came in one detector short of the word I wanted, and one seat
that turns on a computation still owed. I wrote the attacks on my
own position into the bundle myself. The panel's job is to tell us
whether the partition is a reading of the axioms or a new one; ours
is to compute the shell.
