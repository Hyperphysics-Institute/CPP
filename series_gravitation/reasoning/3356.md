# Patch 3356 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The heavy build, started the only way it should be: on the case where
the answer is already known.

Rung 1 was a test of ME as much as of the code. I wrote Leaver's
recurrence from memory, and this session's record says memory-algebra
has been wrong twice. So the recurrence went straight against
published QNMs — if it were wrong, 0.37367 - 0.08896i would not
appear, and nothing would be built on it. It appeared, to 7.5e-6, at
ell = 2, 3 and 4. That is a validation, not a decoration.

Rung 2 is where the lane gets its first exact complex number. And the
lesson is the same one as 3354, wearing different clothes: Leg A's
POSITION was right (0.4488 from the TD peak, against 0.44859 exact —
half a permille) and its WIDTH was wrong by 2.5x. The Wigner delay at
a resonance sitting on the barrier top does not map cleanly to a
lifetime; I took tau = 21.5 as if it did, called it Q ~ 5, and that
number rode through 3334 and 3349 into GR-2 as a "directional note"
after CONV-034 demoted it. It was directionally right (above the top)
and quantitatively wrong (too narrow). The panel's instinct to demote
it was correct; it should have been demoted further. Now it is an
exact number, and the line is broader than the paper implies. Queued
for GR-2, and stated here as a correction to my own earlier work.

WHAT I CHECKED BECAUSE THE METHOD IS KNOWN TO BE DANGEROUS. Direct
inward integration for Im(omega) < 0 is unstable — the unwanted
ingoing solution grows as you go in. For Q ~ 2 the growth over the
integration range is around e^8. So two things are asserted, not
hoped: the root does not move when r0 changes (40, 60, 80 agree to
2.5e-8), and the zero is SHARP (|psi| at the root is 6e-8 of its value
a small step away — a genuine node, not a shallow minimum). Beyond
r0 ~ 120 the root/off-root contrast degrades to order unity and the
method should not be trusted; that boundary is in the record.

I also fitted the outgoing asymptotic series numerically to the ODE
residual rather than writing the recursion from memory. After rung 1
I could have trusted myself. I chose not to, because the cost of the
fit is nothing and the cost of a wrong recursion is a wrong QNM that
looks plausible.

SCOPE, said plainly: Schwarzschild, RW and Zerilli. Not Kerr, not
s = -2, not Sasaki-Nakamura. Rung 3 is the heavy remainder and it
starts from an instrument that has now passed on a known case — which
was the entire point of doing rungs 1 and 2 on this tier.
