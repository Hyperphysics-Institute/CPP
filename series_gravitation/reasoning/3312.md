## The structural bet: two acts on the real chronology

The chapter's material handed me a gift no fiction editor gets: the
five-month gap between the exact solution (March) and the coordinate
correction (August) is REAL, and the mechanism of the surprise — the
lattice ≡ isotropic identification — is genuinely planted in the
March paper itself. So the chapter is built as a fair-play mystery:

- The gun is loaded in act one, in plain sight, at the end of the
  isotropic-coordinates section: "the paper's radial coordinate is a
  lattice address, not a distance any ruler would report" — with an
  explicit "hold this thought."
- The gloss is laid down with the false calm it actually had:
  "That gloss sat in print for five months. Nobody checked which KIND
  of radius it was."
- Act two fires the gun with the paper's own Theorem-1 dictionary,
  and the student exercise makes the point structural: the whole
  discovery is one line of algebra a reader can own. Nothing in the
  reveal uses information the chapter didn't teach.

## Claim discipline decisions

- The "exact agreement is suspicious" objection is raised BY the
  chapter itself and answered honestly by deferral to chapter 5's
  independent re-derivation — better pedagogy than pretending no
  reader will think it, and it stitches the anthology together.
- Buchdahl is presented as consilience with the programme's own
  restraint quoted (logged, declined as prediction); the panel's
  numerology-attack framing is kept ("primed to attack... came back
  unanimous") because it is true and it models adversarial review.
- The 1/3 lapse "computed twice by two different routes" states the
  double derivation without importing the artanh dictionary the
  entry-level reader lacks.
- The founder's ratification sentence is quoted with light editorial
  smoothing of punctuation only; "the math was corrected TO the
  intuition" is my framing, clearly narratorial.
- Numbers restricted to shipped ones: 3.3e-16, 9/8 r_S, 1/3, 112 ms,
  2 ms, 5%.

## CHS ethical core

Chapter 1's ethic was "label your scaffolding." Chapter 2's is its
darker sibling: "welcome the correction that exposes you" — the
112 ms → 2 ms trade as a profit in the falsificationist ledger, with
the executioner line as the memorable formulation. The two lessons
sequence deliberately: honesty in construction, then courage in
correction.
