# Patch 3349 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The last borrowed assumption, and it did not fall the way I expected.

The hypothesis was clean: a trapped mode lives below its barrier, so
exciting it and then seeing it both cost a tunnelling factor, and if
that factor were tiny at ell_crit the inherited claim would be retired
outright by our own machinery. I wrote the script expecting a
discharge.

It failed at ell = 7. Gamma = 0.404, e^(-4Gamma) = 0.20 — a factor of
five. And the reason is obvious in hindsight and worth stating,
because it generalizes: ell_crit is by definition where trapping just
barely begins, so the first trapped mode sits just barely below its
barrier top, so the barrier it must tunnel is thin. The place where a
ladder starts is the place where it is least hidden. I should have
predicted that before running.

WHAT I DID ABOUT THE FAILURE. The threshold (e^-4Gamma < 1e-3) was
written into the script BEFORE the numbers came back, which is the
only reason I can now say honestly that ell = 9 is where it is met
rather than where I decided to put it. Checks 2 and 6-7 were rewritten
to encode the true result — a check that asserts "the hypothesis is
false, here is by how much" — rather than lowering the bar until the
original check passed. That temptation was concrete: moving the bar to
0.25 would have "discharged" it at ell = 7 and closed the item today.

SO THE ITEM STAYS OPEN, and that is the right outcome. What changed is
its size: "excitation is negligible for all ell >= 7", inherited and
uncomputed, becomes "derived for ell >= 9; inherited for ell = 7 and 8
only". Two named multipoles instead of an open-ended tail. A narrowing
is a real result and I would rather report one than manufacture a
closure.

THE UNSOUGHT FINDING is the better half of the patch. While tabulating
the trapped frequencies I noticed they run 602-986 Hz, while the
predicted low-ell line set is 211-294 Hz. The high-ell ladder is
SPECTRALLY SEPARATED from the band anyone would search — a factor two
clear of its top. So even at ell = 7, where the barrier hides it only
fivefold, it cannot contaminate the prediction, because it is not in
the same part of the spectrum. That protection is derived here and
holds at ALL ell, independent of the gap that remains.

Net: three shields now stand between the high-ell ladder and the
observable prediction — barrier suppression (ell >= 9), band
separation (all ell), and the inherited source-side hierarchy. The
borrowed one is now the weakest and the least load-bearing, which is
where a borrowed assumption belongs.

ONE HONESTY NOTE ON THE METHOD. First-order WKB has no Miller-Good
correction near the barrier top, and ell = 7 sits exactly there — so
its Gamma = 0.404 is the least trustworthy number in the table. It is
also the number the conclusion leans on LEAST, since it is reported as
a failure to discharge rather than as a discharge. If it is wrong, it
is wrong in the direction that would only strengthen the result, and
the record says so rather than leaving a reader to work it out.
