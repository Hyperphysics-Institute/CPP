# Patch 3692 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

Thomas said yes to the picture, and in the same breath credited it
with the spring. The spring was my sentence, not his, and I had
written it without doing the sum. So before I let the ruling and the
draft go out I did the sum: twelve neighbours on an icosahedron, a
displaced CP, the count law we use everywhere. The linear force is
zero — the shell theorem, sharpened by icosahedral symmetry to fifth
order — and the first term that survives points back toward the
centre. He was right that it restores. I was wrong that it gives a
stiffness for free. A quintic well is a spring with no spring
constant at small amplitude, and the two numbers we need are both
small-amplitude numbers.

So the amendment draft says exactly that: four definitions from his
four anchors, sixteen lines of the sheet discharged by the definitions
alone, four things that become computable, and four things owed
before anyone outside sees it — the second of which is the stiffness,
which now has three candidate sources and no result. That is the
honest shape. The picture is thrifty and it is his; the number is
mine to find.
