# Reasoning — 3661

Item 5 asked for the closure interior's stability "properly." I read that as two things 3654 had not
done: a zero count in the upper half-plane on a contour that actually reaches the static corner,
and a resolved sign on the two barely-damped modes. I used the pole-free form of the junction so
that a zero of the Z′-coefficient could not manufacture a winding, checked that coefficient never
vanishes on the region anyway, and ran the same contour on the horizon-equivalent wall as a control
that must give zero.

All three windings came out zero on the first run, but my own phase-step threshold failed, and I
did not relax the threshold. I located the large steps instead: not at the trapped poles, where I
had expected them and had already refined, but on the vertical edges next to ω ≈ 0, where the
phase of the junction turns fastest. Refining there brought every step under 0.7 rad and the count
stands. The lesson — refine the ω → 0 corner, not just the poles — is worth more than the result,
and I wrote it into the note as a method item.

The refinement of the trapped modes changed one number in the record: c07's mode is damped at the
1e−8 level, not the 1e−3 level; it is marginal, which is what sitting 0.8% from the static zero
mode should give. I said so and superseded 3654's value. Neither lock is live, so this changes no
prediction; it changes what the record says the closure does, which is the point of a record.
