# Patch 3609 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder asked whether the rank-2 register is thrifty, elegant,
important and minimalist, and said that if so he approves. Before
answering I finally did what I should have done six patches ago: I
read the axiom registry. Row A3-prime. The broadcast carries Phi, V_i
and Q_ij — the complete set of rotationally protected irreps of the
lattice point group, one plus three plus five — with a completion
theorem that there is no fourth rung, zero new parameters, the
Einstein quadrupole formula derived, the luminosity inherited, and a
cancellation theorem that the breathing mode vanishes exactly. Ratified
on the twelfth of June with the founder's own sign-off. The SR lane
did it. I did not look.

So the answer to his four criteria is yes, and for a reason better
than any I had: it is not one more term, it is the last term. The
moment expansion of the census ends at rank two because the point
group's irreps end there. That is his "not a single-use fix" criterion
met by a theorem. He ratified this refinement three months ago and
re-ratified it today on his own standards without knowing he had.
There is no stronger evidence that it was the right refinement.

What the week did, then, was derive A3-prime from pictures — the
detector, the binary pulsar, the wave zone, the vector-relay theorem,
and finally the founder's own construction of Q_ij from the DI-bit
addresses — and land on the same axiom the group theory had produced.
Two lanes, three months, one object. That is triangulation, and it is
worth having. But it was done in ignorance of the registry, and five
questions were put to the founder to rebuild a sector he had already
signed. The record says so. The lesson of last week was "look for the
blank before inventing the rule." Its corollary is written today:
look in the registry before declaring a blank.

Two things follow. The exterior equations the R-core arc has been
using are the theory's own — the tensor sector is Einstein's in vacuum
by isomorphism — and the pause I imposed at 3603 lifts. And the surface
changes: the register at the R-core has a rank-2 part with no cap, so
the even mode's traceless content is Q_ij content and relays into the
core the way the odd sector's vector content does. The two channels of
3396 were right after all, and 3601's "one channel" was right only for
a register without rank two. The difference now is that the coupling
is not free: A3-prime's metric map fixes it. The junction is
determined and computable, and it is the next thing.
