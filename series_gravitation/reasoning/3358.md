# Patch 3358 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

First patch on Fable, and the ladder paid for itself immediately.

I wrote both Leaver Kerr recurrences — angular and radial, s = -2 —
from memory. Both went straight against tabulated Kerr QNMs at four
spins before anything else was touched. All four reproduced to
<= 1.2e-4. If either recurrence had a wrong sign, no tabulated value
would have appeared; that is the whole point of running the known
case first, and it is why I did not attempt this on the lower tier.

The angular validation quietly closed the ell = 2 gap 3357 fenced:
Leaver's series carries the pole behaviour in its prefactor, so the
zero exponent that broke the finite-difference method is simply not
a problem here. A(2,-2) for s = -2 at the census frequency is 4.665,
and its residual beyond the trivial -2 offset (+0.68) matches 3357's
first-order prediction (+0.72). Two instruments, one number.

THE JUDGMENT CALL was to do the SCALAR wall first. For a scalar,
R = 0 is the natural Dirichlet condition and there is no
transformation to get wrong. For gravitational perturbations the wall
condition must be posed on the Sasaki-Nakamura variable, whose
defining functions I do not trust myself to recall — and a wrong SN
transform would produce plausible-looking wrong QNMs, the worst
failure mode. So: scalar now, validated three ways (a = 0 limit
against an independent code to five decimals, r0-independence,
sharpness), and s = -2 as the last rung with its own reduction test.

WHAT THE SCALAR SPECTRUM SAYS. The (2,-2) line is at 251 Hz, +21.6%
above the eikonal top, Q = 2.06 — broad, no comb, retrograde keying
preserved against the prograde (2,+1) at 333 Hz. The withdrawn
"~247-344 Hz" band from CONV-034 turns out to have been roughly right
in the scalar sector. It was still correctly withdrawn: it was an
unearned transport of a one-point calibration, and "roughly right by
luck" is not a grade the corpus recognises. Now it is computed.

NOT ENACTED into GR-2 or PRED-O-39. These are scalar-sector numbers
for a gravitational-wave prediction. The flagship gets its exact
frequencies when s = -2 lands, through a panel round, not by fiat.
