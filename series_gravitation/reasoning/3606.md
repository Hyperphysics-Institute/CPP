# Patch 3606 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder corrected my language and he was right: the DI-bits do
not travel. They are transient, one hop, fixed in number, carrying
the origin's registers; the receiving GP integrates and re-emits its
own state. What propagates is the register, hop by hop, and the medium
sets the speed. That is AP-4 and T-1 and I had written "the DI-bits
carry the deformation one PSR further" the way one says "the wire
carries the current" — loosely, and wrongly for a theory that is
precise about it.

But the correction sharpens the problem instead of dissolving it. If
the register is what relays, then the wave can carry only what the
register holds. The ratified register holds the count — rank zero, the
breathing wave — and the vector sum — rank one, the founder's
electromagnetic sector. A gravitational wave is rank two: stretch one
way, squeeze the other, in the plane perpendicular to the motion. I
proved, for any transverse plane vector wave, that its strain in the
transverse plane is identically zero — no plus, no cross — and that
the plus pattern is only ever a displacement growing with position, a
field acting at each point and not a bounded thing passed along. A
vector relay makes light and S-waves. It cannot make what LIGO sees.
So under the register as ratified, CPP has no gravitational wave, and
that is not a failure of the relay; it is the rank of what it carries.

The way out is in the theory's own vocabulary, and it is one moment
higher. The census is directional — DI-bits arrive from directions —
and the register already keeps its zeroth moment, the count, and its
first, the vector sum. Keep the second moment and the perception
sphere is no longer a sphere: it shrinks more along the axis the
census came from. A perception ellipsoid is a rank-2 register, and a
wave in its anisotropy IS the plus and cross polarizations — length
along x set by PSR_xx, along y by PSR_yy. In GR's words, PSR_ij is the
spatial metric, of which the isotropic part is what the arc has been
calling the register all week.

Whether the perception sphere can be an ellipsoid is a picture, and
the founder has already said the real question is what the CPs do in
response to what they receive. So that is what I have asked him.
