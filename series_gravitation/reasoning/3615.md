# Patch 3615 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder brought back the GWTC-2 echo section and it does not say
what I hoped. No amplitude limit — the posteriors recover the priors —
and a template built for walls a Planck length from the horizon, with
echoes a tenth of a second apart. The CPP cavity's round trip is seven
tenths of a millisecond. Its echoes are one cycle apart; they are not
pulses, they are a modified ringdown. The searches were not looking
there, and my 3614 sentence about lossless walls being disfavoured
does not follow from this paper. Withdrawn, and replaced by something
better.

Because a modified ringdown is what LIGO measures best. The prograde
(2,2) mode of GW150914 matches Kerr's to a few percent in frequency
and tens of percent in damping, and a wall at the ratified surface
moves that mode by an amount set by its impedance. I computed it: a
Neumann-like wall leaves the frequency within two percent of Kerr; a
slightly softer one, minus point-oh-five, matches both frequency and
damping within the tests' precision; soft walls push the frequency
down by fifteen percent and more, hard walls push it up by forty. The
shipped hard wall would have been visible in GW150914's ringdown. So
the ringdown, not the echo search, is the arbiter, and it calibrates
the wall to a narrow band around Neumann at the mode frequency.

That is the founder's instruction fulfilled by an observation already
in hand. And it closes a loop I could not close by thought: the
theory's derived laws kept putting their Neumann crossing at the
barrier top, and I recorded it three times as unexplained. The
ringdown says that is exactly the property a surface at 1.33 r_S must
have to look like a black hole. Whether the lattice-frame law has it
is the computation that remains; the band it has to land in is now a
number.
