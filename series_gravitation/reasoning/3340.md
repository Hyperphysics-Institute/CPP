# Patch 3340 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

GR-2 V1.1 → V1.2, the Leg-C narrowing plus two additions.

The narrowing is written into the SAME remark rather than a new one,
deliberately. A second remark saying "the previous remark was too
broad" would be theatre; the remark's job is to tell a reader what
the finite-ell calculation found, and what it found is a ladder whose
bottom rungs are empty and whose top rungs are full. The scope now
sits INSIDE the claim sentence ("for ell = 2, 3 ... that census was
computed over ell <= 3 and does not generalise in multipole"),
because the whole lesson of 3339 is that a quantifier belongs in the
claim, not in an honest-limits appendix where it can be read as
boilerplate.

Two things I refused to soften:

(a) The inherited-excitation admission. The observable prediction
now leans on "ell >= 7 excitation is negligible," which this
programme has not computed. The paper says so in the remark, names
the open item, and calls it load-bearing. A reader who wants to
attack GR-2 should be able to find its weakest joint in the paper
itself, not by re-deriving the census.

(b) The consistency win goes in the same breath as the correction,
not in a separate triumphant sentence. The honest structure is: the
census does not generalise, AND the reason it does not is the reason
the eikonal picture is right at large ell. Those are one fact.

The two additions are genuinely new content and both strengthen the
paper. Co-rotation being count-neutral discharges an item the V1.1
text had to flag as untested one patch after shipping — that flag
lasted about four hours, which is the system working. The stability
result is the stronger one: exposed + trapped + superradiant is the
ergoregion-instability recipe, and the (l,m) grid forbids it
structurally, so the censorship theorem now protects the finite-ell
sector too. GR-1h's "Planck-core bomb" question, which the arc has
carried since March as a load-bearing worry, is closed one level
deeper than the eikonal argument could close it.
