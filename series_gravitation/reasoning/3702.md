# Patch 3702 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

I sat down to compute a tidal number and found that I could not,
because the number depends on where the matter is, and then found
that the ringdown had already decided where the matter is. The whole
wave-sector success rests on the exterior solution continuing
unchanged down to the wave horizon, and that is only true if almost
nothing sits between the horizon and the surface. Put a tenth of the
mass there and the fundamental mode climbs five percent, which is the
edge of what GW150914 allows and well past what GW250114 allows. So
the black hole in this theory is not a flat core filling its own
surface; it is a deep, concentrated core inside a nearly empty shell,
with the surface at eight-thirds marking where the field saturates,
not where the matter stops.

That is a constraint the register law now has to meet, and it is not
the same as the neutron-star case, where there is no wave horizon and
the flat core stands. I think that is right: the two objects are the
same medium at very different depths. And the tidal signature I had
advertised at 1.7 comes down to a fifth of that or less — still not
zero, which is the point, but honestly further from any detector.
The panel would have found this eventually. Better that I did.
