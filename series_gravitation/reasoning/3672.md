# Reasoning — 3672

The queue's next act was the complex ray for the Kerr (2,+1) comparator, with the note from 3668
that the CD Z⁺ potential is the better carrier than SN. The reason is mechanical: 3659's ray needs
the potential evaluated at complex r along a path, and SN's U contains a numerical derivative of
A⁺(r) by central differences — fine on the real axis, not something I trust on a complex ray. The
CD potential is closed-form and analytic in r away from its denominators, so the ray can carry it
with no new numerics. That is the whole design choice.

The validation order was the same as 3668's: the ray must close on the real wall from both sides
(the complex r state returning to r_w is the check that the path was integrated consistently), then
the fundamental against Leaver, then the thing the instrument exists for — a broad mode. Leaver's
radial continued fraction gives the n = 1 overtone at χ = 0.68 from a guess with Im ≈ −0.25, and I
checked it is the overtone by its imaginary part being three times the fundamental's before using
it as a target. The ray reproduced it to 5e−5. Direct integration cannot get near that at Kerr;
3359 §4 recorded exactly this limit and withdrew a scalar result because of it.

For the wall I did not want to invent a Kerr law; the lane has none, and 3670 says why. So I used
the two ends of the compliance family the corpus already carries — Dirichlet and Neumann on the
even variable — and said plainly that these are model walls. The one subtlety I flagged is that
Z⁺ = 0 is not X = 0: 3359's Dirichlet was on the SN variable, and a node in one master function is
not a node in another. The (2,−2) numbers differ accordingly (164.6 Hz, Q 1.0 here vs 191 Hz,
Q 2.1 there), and neither supersedes the other; both are model walls.

The result is cleaner than I expected. On either wall Q rises monotonically from m = +2 to m = −2:
the prograde modes are the broad ones, the retrograde the sharpest. That is the ordering
PRED-O-39 (e) had at eikonal grade, now exact for the model walls. I noted that the Kerr QNMs
themselves order the opposite way, which is what makes the wall's ordering a discriminator rather
than an echo of the exterior. I did not promote this to a value prediction: the two walls differ
by a factor of several in Q, and no amplitudes are computed, so which line dominates remains (d)'s
open item. What is closed is the instrument gap.
