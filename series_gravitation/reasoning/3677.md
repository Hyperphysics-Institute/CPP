# Patch 3677 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

I went to get the per-event number and it turned my own score over.
The LVK paper gives GW250114's ringdown at SNR 26 out of 80, and
the moment I wrote those two numbers next to each other I saw what I
had done in 3676: I had compared a fraction of the ringdown to a
fraction of the merger and called them the same. They differ by a
third. The alternative I said the searches would have seen sits at
ten percent of the merger; they injected thirty and bounded fifteen.
They could not have seen it. So "disfavored" was mine, not the
data's, and it comes out.

What replaces it is better. GW250114 is loud enough that the same
alternative predicts a first echo at SNR about seven and a half, a
quarter of a second after the peak, and the injection study shows
that is recoverable. No O3 event gets near that. So the falsifier now
has a name, a time, a template, and a number on each side — eight if
the untaken bits carry the wave back, zero if they do not. That is a
test someone can run on public data, and possibly one I can run.

Thomas asked earlier which door 3676 went through, and I read the
protocol and said neither. This patch is what that answer looks like
when it is honoured: the branch against my interest, taken without
being asked, and the panel held until there is a closed seat to bring
it.
