# Patch 3706 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

I wrote the test to confirm that nothing de-saturates during a merger
and the test told me I was wrong: eighty of two hundred points saw
their demand fall, because the body nearest them was moving away.
That is obvious in hindsight — a moving crush leaves an uncrushed
wake — and it is a general fact about every black hole in motion in
this theory. So I could not answer the release question by saying
release never happens. I had to ask what is released.

And the answer is: a count. The deeper registers of a lattice point
under a static field hold last Moment's excess of a demand that
arrives again next Moment regardless; there is no energy in that, only
bookkeeping. Energy is held where a flux came and stayed, and both
such places — the horizon region and the core — are so deep in the
cap that a merger's four percent of mass loss does not bring them
within sight of releasing. So the burst I had advertised on Monday as
the amendment's only new gravitational-wave signal is withdrawn on
Tuesday, and the amendment's gravitational-wave sector is now Einstein's
with no reservation at all. What it still says that Einstein does not
is that the surface is dark for a reason and that soft nuclear matter
can hold up heavier stars. That is the whole of it, and I would rather
have the whole of it true.
