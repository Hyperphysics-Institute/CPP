Thomas asked whether a neutron star meets the criteria, and the
corpus had already answered it three days ago: the cap is a
central-lapse condition, and every realistic sequence crosses it at
1.78 solar masses, well below the heaviest pulsars we can see. So the
second level, if it is real, is loaded first in matter that shines,
and weakly. That is a gift: it means the story can be tested where
the matter is visible before it is trusted where it is not.

The rest of the sheet is nothing but the record read back in one
place: one cap constant; nothing changes below it; storage is not in
the messenger; the floor. Then each domain with its numbers and its
patch. I let the founder's self-similar idea in as a section, because
it is the most economical story and because it carries two
consequences he may not have weighed yet — a duplicated framework has
a duplicated cap, and a hierarchy must end. I did not write the story.
The two lines without numbers are exactly where it would go.
