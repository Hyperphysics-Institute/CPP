# Patch 3391 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder answered my stability question with three sources of
asphericity and the honest closing line "you might be talking about
something else." I was. His three are seeds; my problem was the
amplifier: with the 3390 wall any seed grows. So no substrate
principle was going to save it, and none did. What saved it was
finding my own counting error.

At 3378 I pinned the register at a fixed radius and read that as
pinning the conformal factor. But the register now sets two
dictionaries — the conformal factor and, since the founder's clock
mechanism and the ratified PSR law, the lapse — and a register
perturbation shows up in the even sector twice. Pinning it at a fixed
radius pins both, which is two conditions on a second-order equation:
the even sector is not reflected, it is forbidden. Imposing only one
of the two, as I did, gave a wall whose boundary mass goes negative
beyond 2.38 M. That was the instability.

A saturated surface is not fixed. It is where the register reaches
its cap, and that place can move. Pin the register on the moving
surface and the surface displacement enters both dictionaries with the
same coefficient; eliminate it and you get one law: (4 - 3v/2) H2 + 2K
= 0. Its boundary mass is positive everywhere. At the ratified surface
the even sector is stable and it rings: 195 Hz with Q near a hundred
for l = 2, and a trapped mode at 292 Hz for l = 3.

Those two numbers are within two percent of the 191 and 288 Hz GR-2
shipped in August — computed for a spinning black hole, on the other
parity, with an assumed wall, at a different radius. I have recorded
that as a coincidence to be tested and not as a confirmation, because
it is not one. But I have looked at it for a while.

Two things I got wrong this week are now both closed by the same
move, and I want that on the record plainly: the 3378 even-sector wall
that CONV-039 audited was the fixed-surface limit, and 3390's
instability was that limit failing where it should. The panel audited
what I gave them; the correction is mine to disclose, and it is
disclosed. The surface at 1.33 Schwarzschild radii is no longer held.
