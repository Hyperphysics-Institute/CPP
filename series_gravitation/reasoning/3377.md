# Patch 3377 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

I went to build the spherical l = 2 version of the mirror and the first
thing I had to write down was which master function the mirror is a
condition on. The register is a scalar. It sets the conformal factor.
A conformal-factor perturbation is even parity. GR-2's ladder is the
Regge-Wheeler axial equation — odd parity. The mirror and the ladder
are on different parities, and nobody, including me at 3297, had said
so. That finding needs no assumption and would have been true with the
Exclusion Rule intact.

What a scalar mirror does to the odd sector is a real computation, and
I did it the paranoid way: I did not trust my memory of Chandrasekhar's
transformation. I integrated a Zerilli solution, applied the transform
under all four sign conventions, and kept the one under which the
transformed function satisfies the Regge-Wheeler equation to 6e-8. Then
I found the inverse the same way. Only then did I derive the wall law:
Dirichlet on the even function forces Robin on the odd one, with slope
W/12M = 2.02/M at the Buchdahl wall, and I checked it constructively —
an RW solution launched with that slope transforms to zero on the even
side to machine precision.

The number: at the flagship's 191 Hz the odd-sector reflection phase is
twenty degrees off pi. That is not a small correction to X = 0. It is
conditional on one map — that pinning the register at the surface is
Dirichlet on the gauge-invariant Zerilli function — which nobody has
written down, and which I am not going to guess at, because the Zerilli
function is a combination of K and H2 and their derivatives and
Dirichlet on K alone is generally Robin on Z+. What is unconditional is
narrower and sharper: Dirichlet on both parities at the same wall
forces the trivial solution, so the shipped X = 0 cannot be the odd
image of any scalar mirror.

Three ways this can go and I have listed them for the panel rather than
choosing: the map is Dirichlet-to-Dirichlet and the ladder gets a Robin
wall; the map is Robin-to-something and the odd law follows from it; or
the odd sector is not governed by the register at all but by the vector
sector, which has no wall ruling, in which case X = 0 was always a
separate assumption — or the echo is even-parity only. The founder's
statement that the average SSV_net is zero at cap is about the mean,
not the perturbation, so it does not settle this.

The 3376 skin term and this parity term are orthogonal: one is an
amplitude correction on the even sector, the other a linear-order
correction on the odd sector. CONV-039 gets both, plus a scoped
deliverable — the ladder recomputed under the Robin wall — so the round
returns a number and not only a ruling.
