# Patch 3613 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The plan said: build the C5-frame wall with the residual explicit and
compute the family. I started, and stopped, for a reason worth
recording: the residual is a coordinate object, and building a family
over it would have meant carrying the same gauge trouble that sank the
last three weeks into the calibration parameter itself. What the
exterior sees is one gauge-invariant number per frequency — the log-
derivative of the master function at the wall, the wall's impedance.
Every law the week produced is a point on that map, and every residual
choice in the C5 frame will be another. So the family is the map from
impedance to echo line, and it needs no gauge choice to state.

The map, at zero spin and the ratified surface: from the shipped hard
wall at 201 hertz, broad, through the Neumann wall at 204 with Q three,
down to soft walls that trap the mode at 117 hertz with Q above thirty.
The line moves by tens of percent up to a factor two — not orders of
magnitude — and it moves in one direction: softer is lower and sharper.
The odd sector tracks the even one almost row for row, which says the
wall dominates and the two potentials barely differ where the mode
lives.

The week's laws sit on it where they should: the free-surface law near
the minus-point-one row but far sharper, because its positive boundary
mass is a frequency dependence that a constant wall cannot mimic; the
odd transmit law near the plus rows. The C5-frame law is not yet a
point, and the residual moves it along the map. That is the honest
shape of the calibration now: one function of frequency, bounded by
the echo null results — which, I expect, bite hardest at the trapped,
narrow, low corner, where a lossless wall would have produced a line
the searches should have found.

Next is the reflectivity across the map, to turn the null results into
an excluded region; then the theory's own point; then the same map at
the flagship spin, which the 3392 machinery can do with any impedance,
so that the Kerr numbers become a family rather than an ansatz. The
paper's next version should be the map.
