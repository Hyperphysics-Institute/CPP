# Patch 3373 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

Three things in one patch because they arrived in one message.

THE RULING. Same-polarity CPs can co-occupy under drive; THEO-1's
same-polarity clause was a description of ZBW cycling, not a
prohibition. That amends the theorem's statement and leaves its proof
(isolated pair) alone. It also means the surface is generally
compliant — any strong enough impact displaces surface CPs regardless
of polarity — which simplifies the impedance problem rather than
complicating it: one channel, not two.

THE LEDGER. The founder asked a question I should have anticipated:
who is keeping the list of what Isak has to regenerate? "Logged" was
true and useless — a note in a sector frontier is not a list a person
can work from. So the list now exists as a file at the root with one
row per paper, a status vocabulary, and the rule that every patch
touching a paper updates it in the same commit. Eleven papers owe an
edit that nobody has made; four were changed and need regenerating.
Assembling it caught a version collision of my own: GR-1b had already
used 3.3, 3.4, 3.5, 3.6 and I had labelled my corrigendum 3.3. Now
3.7. The queue generator is the check.

ON ZENODO. I gave facts and one recommendation, not a decision. The
pipeline is fail-closed with zero eligible; the four modified papers
are honest but carry banners that say "not final"; GR-2 will move to
V2.0 when the wall and the floor are settled. Depositing V1.7 now would
be publishing a paper we already know we are about to supersede.
Waiting for the DE and DM lanes is a separate question and his.

THE TRACK. Next is the wall impedance under the corrected boundary law.
The physics shape is now clear enough to state: the core is an
incompressible medium at c_* = c/2 (if the register is at cap) or a
linear medium with headroom (if it is not), and the surface is a
deformable interface for l >= 2. The wave does not see a wall; it sees
a slab. |R| = 1 is the slab being lossless; the phase is the slab's
round trip. First rung is the 1D lossless slab with the interface
mismatch, which reproduces the CPP numbers as a delay: proper core
radius 2.25 mu at c/2 is a 9 mu/c round trip, 2.75 ms at 62 Msun. That
is a second echo spacing, not a phase correction. I would rather state
that shape now and compute it next than compute it in the same patch
as a registry amendment.
