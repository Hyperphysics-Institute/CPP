and to say plainly whether the data can tell. It cannot: three cap constants between 3.16 and 4 all
sit in the GW150914 box. So I named the closest one, with its mechanism, as a candidate and wrote down
what would discriminate — the damping spread, against a tighter ringdown, which means the Kerr wall
and GW250114. I resisted the temptation to call ψ⁴ "the" identification on the strength of a 1.9%
match to a number that was itself fitted.

I also closed the loop on the static coincidence from 3654 by recomputing 3633's reading in the same
convention: 3%, not zero. Coincidences at 3% get recorded and left alone.
