# Patch 3693 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The depth sum was meant to be a warm-up and it produced a theorem
instead. If each deeper layer had a quarter of the capacity of the
one above — the natural guess for a copy at half the scale — then all
of them together could hold four thirds of one layer, which is not
enough for a black hole. The founder's fourth definition says nothing
is ever discarded, so the guess is wrong: the copies must keep their
capacity. I did not expect the amendment to constrain itself on the
first arithmetic, and I like it better for doing so.

The stiffness I had been dreading turned out to be two facts I already
had. The count law has no linear response to strain — the Hessian sum
over the twelve vertices is exactly zero, the same shell theorem that
killed the single-cell spring yesterday, now for gradients. And the
floor is rigid. Free to shear, rigid to compress: that is an
incompressible fluid, with an equals sign. The class I had to assume
on Monday is the class the amendment derives on Tuesday, and the
reviewers who called the class too narrow were right to, because now
it is not a class at all.

Which leaves the amendment holding a number of its own: about 1.7 for
the tidal deformability of a black hole, where Einstein says zero.
Small, real, and someday measurable. That is what a working
extension is supposed to earn.
