# Patch 3643 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

Row 8 is the one ledger row that can kill the extension from inside the
corpus, with no observation needed, so it goes first. The instability
3390 found was specific: the trace-pinned wall's Robin law has a negative
boundary mass at 8M/3, and a negative boundary mass is an active wall —
it feeds the mode. The question is not whether some other wall is
stable; it is whether the wall the extension actually implies is.

What does the budget law imply at the surface? Nothing is pinned. The
count keeps recording above the cap, scaled; the metric is C¹; the
founder's own words in 3639 were that collision ringing transmits
internally. So the even sector is no longer a boundary-condition problem
at all — it is a transmission problem into a regular centre, exactly the
problem 3384 already solved for the odd sector with a flat core. The only
new thing is that the core is graded: the coordinate speed N/ψ² falls
from 0.28 at the surface to 0.18 at the centre. I kept 3384's rules
exactly (master function = r̄ × a regular scalar, value and r̄-derivative
continuous, J = dr*/dr̄ at the surface) and only replaced the flat medium
by the budget metric's scalar wave operator. The check that the frozen
profile reproduces the Riccati–Bessel wall to 10⁻¹¹ is what makes the
graded computation the same computation and not a new model.

Before any pole I expected the answer on general grounds: a lossless,
regular interior with an outgoing exterior is a passive system; its wall
admittance is real on the real axis and its resonances are in the lower
half-plane. The negative boundary mass could only come from a clamp that
does work on the field. So the thing to establish is not one damped root
but the emptiness of the upper half-plane, and the honest way is the
argument principle on the same contour for both walls: the trace wall
must count 1 (3390's growing mode), the budget wall must count 0. It did,
once I removed the interior-resonance poles from the wall function by
multiplying through by u — a detail, but a coarse contour near a pole
gives phase jumps that look like a fractional winding, and I did not want
the row's verdict resting on a threshold.

Two things I did not expect. First, J. The corpus said 6.75 "at any wall"
(3389) and 3390 used it at 8M/3; the dictionary gives 32/9 there, and the
budget interior's own ψ²/N at the surface is 32/9 — the speed is
continuous, as it must be if the join is C¹. So 3390's odd-sector line at
208 Hz was computed with the wrong J; it is owed a re-run, and I have
said so rather than fixing it inside a row that is about the even sector.
Second, the even line itself: the clamp's sharp near-trapped lines (Q 25,
Q 92 at 9M/4; Q 8 growing at 8M/3) are gone, and the budget line is broad
and Dirichlet-like — 239 Hz, Q 1.7. That is a real change to what GR-2
can say: the even-sector echo lines of 3383 were the clamp's, and V2.3
must withdraw them. The map statement of the 3622 banner comes out
stronger — the R-core rings like a black hole because its interior is a
transmitting medium, not a mirror.
