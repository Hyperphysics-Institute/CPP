# Reasoning — 3651 (cold test)

3650 said the near-pole side was a live constraint and that I would check it cold. I searched instead of
trusting 3624's recollection, and the current bound is an order of magnitude tighter than the recollection:
Λ̃ < 34.8 from GW250114. The convention matches the corpus's, the masses are near-equal so the effective and
component deformabilities coincide, and only positive values were sampled — all three facts matter for the
score and are recorded.

The result is the kind PD-007 was written for. The register closure's static junction, computed in one gauge
from the corpus's own dictionary, is excluded by a factor of twenty. I did not soften it. What I did compute
is the survival window, because a falsification without a window is a verdict and with one is a
requirement: the wall ratio must rise by 14% (or cross to the negative side). That is inside the known
dictionary discrepancy, so the extension is on notice rather than dead — and the dictionary question, which
3650 made a sign question, is now a survival question with a direction.

I also recorded that every earlier corpus value passes this bound comfortably. The data test the junction I
derived, not the object. That distinction is the honest one and the useful one.
