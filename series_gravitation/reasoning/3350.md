# Patch 3350 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The last borrowed assumption, closed — and it was closer than I
would have liked, which is the part worth recording.

THE BUDGET was three factors: multipole scaling from the moment
expansion, the counter-rotation mismatch, and 3349's barrier factor.
I deliberately entered the second as a BOUND of 1.0 rather than
modelling it. The physics says it is well below 1 — the trapped modes
are extreme retrograde while the remnant forms from a prograde
inspiral — but I cannot compute the counter-rotating amplitude ratio
without a real waveform, and a number I cannot compute should not
carry weight in a closure. Bounding it at unity makes the whole budget
an UNDER-estimate of the suppression, so the conclusion survives the
factor being unknown. That is the right way to handle an ingredient
you believe in but cannot calculate.

THE EDGE. First run failed: at v = 0.60 the ell=7 budget came out
1.20e-3, just over the 1e-3 bar declared in 3349. Two bad options were
available — trim the scan to 0.55 and pass, or move the bar. Both
would have been invisible in the record. What I did instead was
compute the physical range rather than assert it: the source velocity
at merger is bounded by the remnant's ISCO, which for chi = 0.68 gives
r_ISCO = 3.484 M and v_ISCO = 0.536. The scan then runs PAST that on
purpose, so the break point (v = 0.589) is found and reported rather
than avoided. Closed inside the physical range; marginal just outside
it; margin +0.053 in v, which is thin and is stated as thin.

I want the thinness on the record because a reader deserves to know
how much room this closure has. It is not comfortable. It closes on a
leading-order scaling that is itself marginal at v ~ 0.5, with a
conservatively bounded factor doing no work. If someone later computes
factor (B) properly, the margin becomes large; if someone later
sharpens factor (A) at merger velocities, it could move either way.
The item is closed, not settled.

WHAT ACTUALLY CARRIES THE PREDICTION, and this is the reassuring part:
of the four shields, the one with real margin is BAND SEPARATION —
602-986 Hz versus a 211-294 Hz search band, a factor two, independent
of excitation entirely and holding at every ell. Even if this patch's
budget were wrong by an order of magnitude, a search in the predicted
band still cannot be contaminated. The closure I just made is the
third-best of the three derived arguments, and the prediction does not
lean on it alone.

THE ARC: 3(e) began at 3339 as a sentence nobody had computed, holding
up a flagship prediction. 3349 derived two thirds of it and honestly
reported failing the rest. 3350 closes the rest with the margin
stated. Three patches to convert one borrowed sentence into three
derived arguments, one of which is comfortable. That is roughly what
an inherited assumption should cost.
