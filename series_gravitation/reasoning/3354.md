# Patch 3354 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The cheap owed item, and it corrected me.

3353 measured the eikonal Q's error and I wrote, in section 3 of that
record, that the direction was unfavourable: smaller Q, larger R,
larger Phi, trapping easier, ell_crit could move down. I put it in
section 3 rather than section 6 precisely so a reader would not have
to derive it. It was a one-sided argument and it was wrong. The exact
Q also lowers the barrier top, so Phi_max is taken at a lower omega,
and the two effects cancel to better than a part in a thousand — with
the residue pointing the OTHER way.

The check I built on that prediction failed on its first run. I
rewrote it to say what actually happened rather than to pass, which
by now is reflex, but the lesson underneath is new to this session:
I had been careful to state the direction of a correction, and I
stated it from half the physics. Being conscientious about a
prediction is not the same as being right about it. The record now
carries both the prediction and its correction, one patch apart, so
a reader can see the one-sidedness rather than a tidied conclusion.

THE PHYSICS OUTCOME is the good kind of anticlimax. GPT was right
that the Q correspondence is 4% off at ell = 2 and right that it
should be computed. It turns out not to move a single census
conclusion, because the census depends on Phi_max, and Phi_max is
insensitive to Q by near-cancellation. That is a better answer than
"the error is small": it is "the error is 4%, and here is the
mechanism by which 4% becomes 0.03%." ell_crit = 7 at every spin
tested; the ell = 6 margin of +0.0164 is untouched; N = 0 at ell = 2,3
stands; the reflection-phase envelope is unchanged to four figures.
The queued GR-2 amendment now CONFIRMS the ell_crit line rather than
revising it.
