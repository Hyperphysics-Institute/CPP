# Patch 3703 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The clock was easy: cap the register and the clock it is stops at
one half, while the traffic keeps its own time down to zero at the
horizon. Two clocks, one for matter and one for waves, derived from
two definitions. The force was the thing. A capped register can
either act on nothing more or act on the sample it took, and the
corpus had used both readings in different places without saying so
— "static core" in one patch, a scaled net in another. Yesterday's
ringdown bound settles it: if the matter stopped at the surface the
fundamental mode would sit twenty percent off, so it does not stop; it
drains at half the speed of light into a Planck-density speck a
thousand trillion trillion times smaller than the horizon. The shell
is empty. The tidal signature I had been carrying since 3685 goes to
effectively zero with it, and I would rather lose a prediction than
keep one the theory does not make.

What the same reading costs is the neutron-star picture as computed:
inside a saturated core gravity is reduced by the sample fraction,
not removed, and the flat-core stars were built on "removed." The
threshold survives; the branch above it has to be rebuilt. That is
now the most important thing on the list, because the neutron stars
were where this amendment could be tested with instruments that
already exist.
