# Patch 3697 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

Four to nothing on the question that decides, four to nothing on
fidelity, and both seats that ran the code returned the sealed key.
That is the amendment through. What the same two seats then did was
take back three things I had written as derivations — the equality
to an incompressible fluid, the darkness as a proof, the tilt as a
check — and hand them back as bounds, conditions, and consequences
still to be embedded. They were right each time, and I had half-known
it: the Hessian is a fact about one cell, the floor is a fact about
one CP, and a constitutive law is a fact about a body. I wrote the
equality because it was beautiful. It is a bound.

The one clause they added is the one I had used without stating: the
copy repositions every CP in a cell together. That is what the
founder's own words say, and once it is in the definition the
no-dipole argument is honest. Its derivation is now the first debt.

Two housekeeping things for the steer sheet. One paste signed itself
with a different seat's name and claimed a hash match it did not
have; the correct key was returned by the two seats that computed
it, and the false claim is on the record. And Copilot asked what we
wanted instead of reviewing; its seat was vacant.

The clause goes to Thomas with the dissent's last paragraph as its
rider. He ratifies four definitions, not the things I hoped they
implied.
