own: read the budget metric as a star, perturb it as a star. Before perturbing I read its
stress-energy, and that alone settled 3640 §4 — the interior is held by tangential stress, the

validation caught a sign in the surface-jump term, which is why I insist on it.

The failure is the right kind: not a number off the box, but a statement that the medium is not
matter. That pushes the one interior law back onto the theory's own closure — the count. Attempt 3b
is that closure done in one gauge, and its static run doubles as the arbiter for the 3648 caveat on
row 6. I named it and stopped; executing it is its own patch.
