# Patch 3632 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

Thomas gave me the postulate rather than the picture, so the premise is

First a correction of my own number. I wrote "k₂ = 3/2, Kelvin" in 3631
and compared it to −0.080. Those are different conventions. Hinderer's
formula, which is what the whole corpus uses, is exactly λ/(2R⁵) for
the decaying amplitude — I checked it at four values to a part in a
billion — and its Newtonian limit is (2 − y)/(2(y + 3)), which for the
incompressible body gives 3/4, not 3/2. Love's k₂ is twice Hinderer's.
So Kelvin's body is 3/4 in our convention. The conclusion survives —
positive, an order of magnitude above the family — but the number in
3631 §5 was in the wrong convention and I say so.

The closure itself is one line. At far field the register is minus the
Newtonian potential; the census of a uniform-count ball whose boundary
moves by δ̄ gives an induced coefficient (3/5) M R̄ δ̄; the exterior's
gives −λ/2; so λ = −(6/5) M R̄ δ̄. Feed it the Newtonian level set and
3/4 comes back, which tells me the line is right.

Then it stops, and it stops in the right place. δ̄ is the level set's
displacement in lattice coordinates, and the lattice frame is not RW
gauge — 3611 spent a patch establishing that for the wave sector, with
the residual at the wall (do the GPs move or not, F-16) changing the
register content by a factor of several. The static case has the same
residual. If the GPs do not move, δ̄ is the RW displacement mapped by
the background and k₂ = +0.033. If instead the level set's own areal
radius is what the lattice sees, k₂ = −0.22. Opposite signs, and k₂ is
linear in the residual with its zero exactly where the lattice-frame
level set does not move — the black hole. So the sign of the R-core's
Love number is now a question about one gauge vector at the wall, and
it is the same question 3611 left open for the ringdown. That is a
better place for the datum to sit than "the shell's constitutive law":
it is one number, it is the lattice frame's, and the census gives me a
way to test the frame — if δv is harmonic in lattice coordinates and
the exterior is Einstein's, they had better agree at second order, and
whether they do is a computation, not a picture.
