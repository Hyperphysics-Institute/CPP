never considered that the GW was anything but a pressure wave; it

The scalar wave also has to be dealt with, and the numbers moved on
me while I did it. The scalar quadrupole from a binary scales exactly
as GR's tensor strain, with nothing in T-1 to suppress it: naively a
quarter of the amplitude. Then the surprise: an isotropic PSR

less than about five percent of GR's amplitude, five times below the
naive estimate, or the theory fails Hulse-Taylor. Whether the census
dynamics with its conserved source supply that suppression — the way
they already kill the monopole — is a computation, not a charter, and
nobody has done it.

So the lane has two items in front of it and both have numbers
attached: derive the tensor channel and hit GR's luminosity to 0.2
percent; compute the scalar luminosity and come in under 0.2 percent.
The question to the founder is the mechanism as a picture: does the
Sea respond to a rotating incoming vector pattern by deforming
quadrupolarly, the way it responds to a rotating field by polarizing
dipolarly. If yes, that is the gravitational wave.
