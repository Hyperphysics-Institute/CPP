# Patch 3646 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

Thomas asked whether what I had been doing was his method, and the
honest answer is: the first half. I derived and tested; when the
derivation failed I went straight to adoption. His middle step — carry
the hypothesis, unchanged, across a group of related phenomena before
asking whether it connects to the axioms — is the step that makes a
fitted number into evidence or into nothing, and I had skipped it. So
this patch does two things in his order: another derivation attempt,
and the group.

The derivation attempt was the obvious one from A3′: the even mode as
a mixture of a reflecting count channel and an absorbing tensor
channel, with the wall's admittance the weighted sum. It fails for a
reason that is itself informative. The trace-pinned element has a real
part of about −1.3 at the ringdown frequency, and the horizon-
equivalent law has essentially none. Whatever the count channel does at
the surface, it does not do what the clamp did; the reflection the
ringdown wants is almost purely reactive-free absorption at a third of
the admittance, and adding a lossless Robin element cannot produce
that. The two channels, if they are the answer, have to be coupled
through the metric map, not summed. That is JUNCTION-1 with a number to
hit, and it is the next derivation act.

The group: I added the ℓ = 4 fundamental, checked first that the
horizon-equivalent wall reproduces GR's value there (it does, to
0.01%), then carried s = 3.22 to it unchanged. It lands at −1.0% in
frequency and −5% in damping. That is three lines from one number with
no refit, and the frequency residual shrinks with ℓ. I tried the first
overtone too and the solver stalled — direct integration cannot follow
a mode with Im ω of −0.27 — so I have written it down as not computed
rather than let a returned guess pass as a result, which is exactly
what the first run of this script would have done.

What I think this is worth: the hypothesis is descriptive on the
non-spinning ringdown spectrum. That is real, and under Thomas's method
it earns the hypothesis a place on the ledger as a hypothesis. It does
not earn it a place in the theory. The number is still a stand-in for
mechanics we have not written, and the next member that could break it
is the one that matters most — Kerr — which I cannot score until the
wall dictionary exists.
