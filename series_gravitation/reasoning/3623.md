# Patch 3623 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder's answer to "what becomes of the energy" is that it is
stored: the wave excites the CPs in the core, the pattern repositions
each Moment and moves through the core at half light speed, and the
structure holds it. Information is kept.

I checked that against the two things the data say. The ringdown says
the pattern must not come back out through the tensor channel within
a crossing — otherwise the core rings at the black hole's own frequency
and splits the observed mode. His picture allows that; it does not yet
say why the return channel is closed, and I have written that down as
the reframed open item: conversion, not dissipation. Then the
infrared. A surface with a lapse of one half is not hidden the way a
horizon is; if it turned what it absorbed into heat it would glow, and
for Sagittarius A* the glow would be a few-thousand-kelvin blackbody
at ten to a hundred times the observed quiescent infrared. The
Broderick-Narayan argument, and at this redshift it is not close. So
the data require the founder's picture in its strong form: the energy
is stored coherently, as organization, and populates no states that
emit photons. Vibration, not heat. Kirchhoff's law binds thermal
states and not coherent ones, and that is precisely the difference.

That gives the theory a prediction with no parameters: the surface is
dark. It swallows light and gravitational waves alike and re-emits
nothing thermal, at any accretion rate. It is also the only way an
R-core at this radius survives the observations, which I take as the
founder's picture and the data agreeing on a fairly narrow thing.

The tidal Love number is next, and it is the first place where the
dark, absorbing, non-singular object should differ from a horizon in a
number that a detector can read.
