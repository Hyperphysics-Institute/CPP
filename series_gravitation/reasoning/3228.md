## The one piece of genuinely new work: the classical-tests numbers

The scoping assessment called the tests "bounded and low-risk — standard
computations on a metric the corpus already claims," and that held, with two
numerical lessons worth keeping:

- **First run failed its own cross-checks** (perihelion numeric 42.87 vs
  closed form 42.99; deflection numeric 1.93" vs 1.75"). Both failures were
  instructive and neither was physics:
  - *Perihelion:* accumulating phi by `phi += dphi` over ~10^7 steps injects
    rounding drift of order 1e-6 rad — percent-level against a per-orbit
    advance of 5e-7 rad. Fix: phi = i·dphi from an integer step counter.
  - *Deflection:* stopping at the first grid point with u ≤ 0 overshoots the
    asymptote by up to dphi = 1e-6 rad — 10%-level against a deflection of
    8.5e-6 rad. Fix: linear interpolation of the zero crossing. The same
    interpolation applied to perihelion crossings, with a telescoping
    estimator (last crossing − first crossing)/N so interior interpolation
    errors cancel.
  After the fixes: perihelion numeric = closed form to 4 decimal places
  (42.9917"/cy); deflection to 0.006% (1.7517" vs 1.7516"). 8/8 checks pass.
  This is the same class of lesson as the corpus's standing "the instrument
  can be the finding" pattern — recorded here because any future worker
  writing geodesic integrators for the tests companion will hit both traps.
- **Check 0 is the load-bearing one:** the isotropic form in c08's
  Eq. (isotropic_schw) transforms to standard Schwarzschild g_tt at machine
  precision (3.3e-16 max relative mismatch over five radii spanning 1e9 m to
  10 AU). GR-1's claim is "exact Schwarzschild in isotropic coordinates,"
  and someone will check it; now the repo checks it first.

## Wording decisions a reviewer may probe

- **"The tests do not discriminate CPP from GR"** is stated affirmatively in
  §5. Since the metric is claimed exactly Schwarzschild, the predicted test
  values are identical to GR's by construction; claiming them as
  discriminating confirmations would be the kind of overclaim the Patch 2474
  retraction exists to prevent. Equivalence at these orders IS the claim.
- **No new predictions registered.** The tests are retroactive/equivalence
  results, not zero-parameter novelties; adding them to predictions.md would
  inflate the swarm count with GR-identical values. Deliberately not done.
- **Companion references use current c-numbers** with "pending
  re-identification" language, because the file moves (c05, c07–c13 into the
  GR series, dissolving the c8/c9 spin collisions) are a separate
  organizational patch that touches the deposit generator's inputs — bundling
  a 500-line new paper with an 8-paper restructuring in one `git am` would
  make failure recovery miserable on Windows. Registered as OPEN-ORG-023
  (Patch 3229) instead, together with the wave-1 deposit hold.
