# Patch 3627 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder said proceed, and the two items on the table were the
spin couplings and the paper. The paper is done: GR-2 V2.1 carries the
tidal signature — the numbers, the sign argument, the axial number
with its stated uncertainty, the phasing, the thin-shell reading —
and the prediction register has a new row with a falsifier and a
reach. Thirteen pages, compiles clean.

The spin couplings I have scoped and not computed, and I want the
reason on record. At the flagship spin the square of chi is nearly a
half, and the frame-dragging couplings between the l = 2 electric
perturbation and its neighbours enter the diagonal Love number at that
order. Doing it properly is a second-order-in-spin expansion of the
static problem on a Kerr surface that is itself an ansatz. That is
several patches of careful work and I would rather write "order-unity
uncertainty on the spinning value" in the paper than a number I got
by cutting the expansion short. The zero-spin numbers are exact
within the model, and they are the clean claim: the R-core's Love
numbers are not zero, and a black hole's are.

The round on the tidal signature stays recommended. The next
zero-parameter observable is the one I keep passing over: every
R-core has compactness 0.375, whatever its mass, which puts it against
the neutron-star mass-radius data — and against the objects in the
mass gap.
