a = 0 — where the old X = 0 line moved eighteen. The surface move by
itself would have dropped the line to 182; the derived law brought it
back.

yesterday survives spin, at the level the corpus can test. I have also
written the reason to distrust it: one-percent agreement resting on
two ansätze that could each be off by several. And a conjecture for
why the spin-insensitivity might be real — the mode is a trapped
cavity mode pinned where the wall's Neumann crossing meets the barrier
top, and both move together — as a conjecture.
