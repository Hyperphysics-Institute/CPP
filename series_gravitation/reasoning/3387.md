# Patch 3387 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

The founder gave the clock mechanism: clocks advance by the displacement
per Moment, which is the PSR, which shrinks toward saturation. Taken
literally that is the lapse equals the PSR shrink, and it makes local
light speed c by local clocks with no assumption at all — his picture
and my no-axiom census map are the same sentence.

Then I did what the last three patches taught me to do: test it at the
next order. With the register the corpus actually ratified — linear,
u = mu/rbar — the mechanism gives a lapse of 1/(1+u), whose square has
a second-order coefficient of 3 where Schwarzschild has 2. That is PPN
beta = 3/2, and Mercury's perihelion comes out at five sixths of the
observed value. Excluded, cleanly, by an 1859 measurement. So the
founder's mechanism and the corpus's register cannot both be exact.

The rescue costs no axiom and changes one identification. If the
register self-sources — if a grid point with a large register
broadcasts a larger census — then the field it sets is
v/(1 - v/2), and the mechanism's lapse 1/(1 + u_reg) is EXACTLY the
log-lapse the T-1 charter imposed to get Schwarzschild. The log-lapse
becomes a derivation: the founder's clock plus Mercury. The self-
sourcing coefficient comes out one half, which is a check on the
census, not a knob.

What it costs the arc is large and I have not hidden it. If that is
the register, then it saturates where the lapse is one half, not where
Buchdahl is extremal: the surface sits at 1.33 Schwarzschild radii, the
redshift there is 1 not 2, the echo cavity is 0.70 ms not 2.15, the
strong-field departure I priced yesterday closes to zero, and every
wall computation since July would be redone at a radius where the even
sector's wall has the opposite sign. The floor l_P/2 is untouched. The
window survives, re-labelled, and the extremality problem the panel
stripped from me dissolves because saturation happens inside the
window rather than at its edge.

I have enacted nothing. The choice is a picture: does gravity
gravitate in CPP — do heavier registers broadcast heavier messages? If
he says yes, the arc moves its surface and gains a derived lapse. If he
says no, his clock mechanism needs a second-order companion he has
already said he does not have. Either answer is progress; only he can
give it.
