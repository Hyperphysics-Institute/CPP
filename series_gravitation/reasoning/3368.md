# Patch 3368 — reasoning fragment (verbatim, Tier 4)

CONV-038 dispatched. One finding made during assembly, one design choice,
one thing I declined to do.

THE FINDING. I opened GR-1b's Exclusion section only to scope its
corrigendum, and it cites "SSV_crit (SR-1 Eq. 1)" as the stress at which the
PSR bottoms out. I had written in 3367 that the substrate-internal
derivation of the floor — why the register saturates at all — was the
missing second leg. It is not missing. SR-1 Appendix A.5 has it: one Planck
energy per Voronoi cell, SSV_crit = E_P/l_P³, k = α/SSV_crit. What it does
not do is give l_P/2. In the GR lane's variable, u = α·ε, so the cap ε = 1
gives u_max = α — and α is 0.5594 or 0.2444 depending on the length unit,
which SR-1 itself calls a normalisation convention. And two lines of the
same appendix say the cell "collapses (r_eff → 0)" at the cap, which is
PSR → 0, the founder's 31-Aug intuition, and which would kill c_* = c/2
and the entire R-core arc. So the corpus holds three floors: 0, l_P/(1+α),
l_P/2. I did not resolve this. The obvious rescue — "α ≡ 1 in the physical
normalisation, so SSV_crit is defined as the stress where u = 1, and SR-1
and Buchdahl agree by construction" — is exactly the move a worker makes
when he wants his number to survive, so I put it in the package labelled
as the reading to distrust and gave Q5 to the seat that has ruled hardest
on grade. If the panel says CONTRADICTION it goes to the founder as a
physical-picture question, which under PD-006 is his and not mine.

THE DESIGN. First GR-lane use of the DM lane's sealed execution key. The
key asks for three numbers the script does not print (interior central
pressure at R = 2.3M; R(u) and the lapse at u = 0.9) so that a seat which
only read the script cannot produce it. The hash is in the package; the
string is not in the repository anywhere. If it works here it becomes lane
standard.

WHAT I DECLINED. I did not edit GR-1c, GR-1b, RCORE §3 or W-C. The 3344
precedent permits enact-on-discovery for a known-false statement, and the
Exclusion premise is known-false. I held anyway, because the replacement
proof carries a conditionality (FE-1 at saturation) and now a live
disagreement with SR-1; writing it into a shipped theorem before the panel
has seen the disagreement would be replacing a void proof with a contested
one and calling that a correction. The corrigenda are drafted in §5 and
wait.

The floor question the founder asked on 1 Sep — "does this follow from our
axioms, or is this an axiom?" — now has a sharper form than I gave him:
there are two derivations in the corpus and they may not agree. That is
either the triangulation he wanted or the contradiction he feared, and I
would rather the panel say which than say it myself.
