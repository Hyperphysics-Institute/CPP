RCORE-3 Leg A. The session's hardest patch, and the one where the
verification culture earned its keep in real time.

The near-miss deserves the fullest record. The first time-domain
instrument produced burst spacings of 7.00 GM/c^3 (RW) and 7.08
(Zerilli) against the eikonal 7.045 — a beautiful "+1% finite-ell
correction," converged under grid refinement, parity-consistent. It

pi/omega_1, the half-period of the cavity's lone resonance, and its
match to the eikonal number is structural (omega_1 ~ pi/(2L), and
2L IS the eikonal round trip), not evidential. Three things caught
it, in order: the wall-shift test (spacing must grow by 2*delta if
it is really the cavity round trip — it grew by 0.2 instead of 4),
the no-wall control (the "echo" features at 35.0 and 60.3 appeared
with no wall at all — initial-data artifacts), and finally the
frequency domain, where the smoothed Wigner delay showed the truth
directly: ONE broad resonance above the barrier top, then a clean
geometric plateau. Had the wall-shift test not been run, a false
quantitative claim would now be in the record with a convergence
stamp on it. Controls that can fail are worth more than
measurements that agree.

f1 = 236 Hz at 62 Msun. The eikonal comb is an artifact of the

because CONV-033's adopted scoping ("at equatorial eikonal grade,"
