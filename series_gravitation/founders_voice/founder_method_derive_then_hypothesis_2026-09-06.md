# Founder method statement (Thomas, 6 Sep 2026), verbatim. Filed Patch 3646. Refines PD-007.

> In general, derive every phenomenon from the axioms. If you can't, then look for a statistics level hypothesis that satisfies the phenomenon and plausibly connects to the axioms, and apply that to a group of related phenomena, and then see if we can connect that hypothesis with the axioms after several phenomena have shown the hypothesis is descriptive.

Enacted as 3641 §2 rule 6 (3646): (1) derive from the nine axioms; (2) on failure, state a statistics-level hypothesis that satisfies the phenomenon and plausibly connects to the axioms — labeled `HYPOTHESIS`, never adopted; (3) apply it unchanged to a group of related phenomena; (4) only after several phenomena show it descriptive, attempt to connect it to the axioms. A hypothesis is never a result and never enters an abstract.
