# Glossary — SF-5 Strong-Sector Unification (v1.0)

**Paper:** SF-5 v1.0. Terms organized by category; each entry gives definition + first-use § + related terms.

## Constants
- **M_0 (DP energy quantum)** — M_0 = m_e z/φ = 3.79 MeV; anchors the string tension and nuclear cascade. §2. (Related: B_pair.)
- **B_pair (binding quantum)** — B_pair = M_0/φ = m_e z/φ² = 2.342 MeV; the zero-parameter np-pair binding. §7. (Related: M_0, alpha cascade.)
- **C_F (colour Casimir)** — (N²−1)/(2N)|_{N=3} = 4/3 (SS-2). §2. (Related: SU(3).)
- **φ (golden ratio)** — (1+√5)/2; propagation-efficiency factor η = 1/φ. throughout.

## Structural terms
- **Tetrahedral cage** — qCP base triangle {V1,V2,V3} + apex, embedded in the 600-cell; carrier of colour. §2.
- **Three base edges** — the colour-changing channels (six real/imag hopping operators). §3, §4.
- **Diagonal modes** — the two colour-neutral phase-difference channels. §4.
- **Closed alpha-polytope** — the deltahedral cluster of N_α alpha units; has 3N_α−6 edges. §7.

## Mechanism terms
- **hDP (hybrid displacement pair)** — transient configuration propagating along a cage edge; the gluon. §4.
- **Colour-changing gluon** — hDP on a base edge that permutes colour labels (6 of 8). §4.
- **Colour-neutral diagonal gluon** — phase-difference mode (2 of 8). §4.
- **Confinement** — absence of any stable isolated-colour open-path hDP configuration. §5.
- **Face-mode fraction** — the share of the adjacency spectral trace carried by face (A³) walks → α_s. §6.
- **Edge-mode fraction** — the share carried by edge (A²) walks → sin²θ_W. §6.
- **Mode complementarity** — sin²θ_W + α_s = 1/φ exactly. §6.

## Methodology terms
- **Picture 1 / Picture 2** — the two gluon-counting pictures: shipped theorem-level SS-1c octet (headlined) vs the CONJ-SS-Gluon-4Vertex conjecture (flagged). §4, §8.
- **Single-m_e calibration spine** — all SF-line mass scales from M_0 = m_e z/φ. §9, §12.
- **Inherited-open** — an open problem carried forward from a source paper without closure (OPEN-FP-5-GLUEBALL, OPEN-SS-19, SS-9 conditionality). §8, §9.
