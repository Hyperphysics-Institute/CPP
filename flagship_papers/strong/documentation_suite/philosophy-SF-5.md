# Philosophy — SF-5 Strong-Sector Unification (v1.0)

**Paper:** SF-5 v1.0. Paper type: Flagship synthesis paper (reframing of shipped results; no new derivation).

## Certainty level (graded by result)
- **Theorem-level:** SU(3) algebra exact (SS-1b); SU(3) uniqueness within the operator representation (SS-3); the eight gluons with masslessness + spin-1 (SS-1c). These are the headline.
- **Derivation-level:** confinement + positive β (SS-1d, β-correspondence qualitative); string tension σ = 926.5 MeV/fm (SS-4).
- **Structural correspondence:** α_s = 5/(8φ) and sin²θ_W = 3/(8φ) as adjacency-spectral mode fractions — numerically matching the measured couplings, not RG-derived.
- **Zero-parameter empirical:** the twelve-nucleus alpha cascade (RMS < 1%); the binding quantum 2.342 MeV.
- **Conjecture / open:** CONJ-SS-Gluon-4Vertex (flagged); OPEN-FP-5-GLUEBALL; the deuteron NLO residual (OPEN-SS-19); SS-9 conditionality.

## Relationship to the Standard Model
- **Reproduces / derives** what the SM postulates or measures: the colour group, the gluon count, masslessness, spin, confinement, asymptotic freedom, the strong coupling.
- **Reframes** the SM's "postulated SU(3) + eight measured gluons + two unrelated couplings + a table of nuclear binding energies" as one cage-hopping algebra + one spectral partition + one binding quantum.
- **Does not replace** QCD's perturbative apparatus; CPP operates at finer (sub-quantum) granularity, and the mapping (§11) is at the level of degrees of freedom and observables.

## Falsifiability inventory (threshold values)
1. A ninth gluon, a massive gluon, or a non-spin-1 gluon → falsifies SS-1c.
2. A substrate-scale determination forcing sin²θ_W + α_s ≠ 1/φ → falsifies the complementarity.
3. A string tension materially outside the band around 926.5 MeV/fm → falsifies the z² face-mode derivation.
4. A strict-N=Z alpha-chain nucleus (N_α ∈ [3,14]) violating the 3N_α−6 law beyond the residual band → falsifies the cascade.

## Paper-type declaration
Flagship synthesis. The contribution is the assembly + one presentation decision (lead with the shipped theorem-level gluon octet; demote the four-vertex conjecture) + the honest carry of the deuteron residual and the SS-9 conditionality.

## Limits of scope
SF-5 derives masses/couplings/binding at the strong-sector scale on one calibration; it does NOT close the glueball mass, the deuteron NLO residual, the SS-9 sub-conditions, or the four-vertex gluon-counting conjecture. The §1 honesty box collects these limits in one place.
