## Part 2 — FAQ

**Q (methodology): Is any of this new derivation?**
No. Every load-bearing result is inherited from the cited SS/SM papers; SF-5's contribution is synthesis + one presentation decision + honest carry of the open items.

**Q (scope): Why headline the eight-gluon octet rather than the four-vertex gluon-counting claim?**
Because the octet (SS-1c) is theorem-level and shipped, while the four-vertex picture is an open conjecture whose own falsification route collapses it to "the SM octet restated" if enumeration yields eight — and SS-1c is arguably that enumeration. Headlining the conjecture would weaken the flagship.

**Q (falsifiability): What kills SF-5?**
A ninth/massive/non-spin-1 gluon; a substrate-scale sin²θ_W + α_s ≠ 1/φ; a string tension far outside ~926.5 MeV/fm; an alpha-chain nucleus violating 3N_α−6 beyond the residual band.

**Q (SM relationship): Does the deuteron match to 0.1%?**
No. The zero-parameter content is the 2.342 MeV binding quantum; the physical deuteron carries an honest +5.3% leading-order residual (OPEN-SS-19). Earlier staging material's "0.1%" was an over-optimistic figure, corrected here.

**Q (future work): What's still open in the strong sector?**
The lightest-scalar-glueball mass (OPEN-FP-5-GLUEBALL), the deuteron NLO residual (OPEN-SS-19), the SS-9 sub-conditions (C5–C8), and the four-vertex gluon-counting conjecture (OPEN-FP-5-GLUON).
