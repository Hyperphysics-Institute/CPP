## Source-grounding before transfer — and two honest refinements over the staging docs

The handover's load-bearing discipline is "the verdict comes from the text, never from a reviewer's authority — grep the actual source." I applied that to the staging docs themselves rather than transcribing them on trust, and it caught two places where the staging numbers were not the best source-grounded figures:

1. **The nuclear cascade is tighter than the staging docs said.** The outline §2 table and the core both cite the twelve-nucleus alpha-cascade at "<1.5% RMS." Grepping SS-7's own version history shows the v1.2 figure is **RMS below 1% across the twelve strict-N=Z nuclei (N_alpha in [3,14])**, with the primary eight-nucleus set (N_alpha in [3,10]) at **0.91%**. I used the sub-1% / 0.91% figures, which are both more accurate and stronger. The "<1.5%" was conservative but left accuracy on the table.

2. **The deuteron figure — the staging docs already flagged this, and I honored the correction.** The outline §2 table still carries the *stale* "deuteron 2.222 MeV, ~0.1%" line, but the structural core (1309, later) explicitly corrects it: the zero-parameter content is the binding quantum **B_pair = M_0/phi = m_e z/phi^2 = 2.342 MeV**, and the physical 2.2246 MeV carries a **+5.3% leading-order residual that is OPEN (OPEN-SS-19)**. SS-5 confirms this verbatim, including that the candidate prolate-cage NLO mechanism was explored in Appendix B and **rejected as not validated** (opposite sign under honest second-order PT). I presented the deuteron with its 5.3% residual openly and did NOT let the stale 0.1% line into the `.tex`. Flagship credibility depends on this — presenting a 5.3%-open residual as a sub-percent hit is exactly the kind of over-claim the SF-line strict posture exists to prevent.

Both of these are the structural-core's own corrections; my contribution was to verify them against SS-5/SS-7 source and carry the corrected (and in the cascade case, strengthened) figures rather than the stale outline table.

## The gluon-counting presentation decision (inherited from 1304, restated)

The one load-bearing presentation call is the gluon-counting question. Two pictures live in the corpus: Picture 1 (SS-1c, theorem-level: 8 gluons = 6 edge + 2 diagonal, 1-to-1 with the SM octet, massless + spin-1) and Picture 2 (CONJ-SS-Gluon-4Vertex, open: the octet as a dressing of a 4-baryon-vertex taxonomy). The SF-5 README originally proposed headlining Picture 2's forced-choice claim. The outline (1304) reversed that: lead with Picture 1, flag Picture 2 as forward-looking. I carried that decision into the `.tex` and stated the reason in §4 and §8 — Picture 2's own falsification route (a) collapses it to "the SM octet restated" exactly when substrate enumeration yields eight types, and Picture 1 arguably IS that enumeration. Headlining an unclosed conjecture that the corpus's own shipped, stronger result appears to absorb would weaken the flagship. This is the third predecessor (after SF-1's SM-4→SM-6 phase arc and SF-3's m_c route) whose honest move is restraint.
