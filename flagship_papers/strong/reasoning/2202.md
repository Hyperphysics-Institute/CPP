## Why this patch exists

2201 delivered a *conditional* verdict: the ponderomotive scissor is soft (g_pond < g_crit) for steep/screened
gradient laws (p ≳ 3), but goes tense for a shallow/long "softer SSV law" (p ≲ 2). I flagged that p ≲ 2 branch

## The close — electrostatics forbids the escape hatch (not an assumption)

1835 stated the falloff options exactly: *"field 1/r² → grad² up to 1/r⁶ → p up to 6; softer SSV laws give
smaller p."* The escape hatch is the "softer SSV laws" clause. The question is whether the real edge-bond SSV
field can be *softer than Coulomb*. It cannot, for a reason that is structural, not fitted:

- The edge bond is a **localized electrostatic** near-cancellation residual (0865: closer like-charge repulsion
  − farther opposite-charge screening, with the hinge torque *"dominated by the coat eDP pairs"* at r_c ≈ 1.0 fm,
  1835). A localized electrostatic source has the **monopole 1/r² as its shallowest possible field** — nothing
  localized falls slower. So the SSV field is 1/r² **or steeper**, i.e. s ≥ 1 in the ponderomotive |E|² = grad²,
  i.e. **p = s+2 ≥ 3** — the viable branch, guaranteed by electrostatics.
- It is moreover a **near-cancellation residual**: the monopole largely cancels, so the leading surviving term
  is **dipole-like, 1/r³ or steeper** → s ≥ 2 → p ≥ 4, deeper still in the viable branch.
- **Screening only steepens it further**: the eDP coat gives a hard core / cutoff at r_c ≈ 1.0 fm, and the
  color-vdW leg carries λ ≈ 1.3 fm (ℏc/E_hDP, E_hDP ≈ 152 MeV, the confinement scale). Both are *derived*
  (0834–0836), not assumed.

Evaluated at every one of these corpus-physical legs — Coulomb floor (s=1), coat-screened Coulomb, the
dipole-residual (s=2), and the color-vdW leg (s=6) — g_pond lands **0.000–0.025, all well below g_crit = 0.43**
(verify §1). The verify also walks the boundary (§2): viability is lost only as **s → 0**, an essentially flat,
screening-length-limited field — a *sub*-Coulomb pathology a localized charge residual cannot produce. The
escape hatch is closed by physics the corpus already owns.

**Net:** the 2201 contingency "conditional on p ≳ 3" is discharged. p ≳ 3 is not a hope about the bond; it is a
theorem about localized electrostatic residuals, reinforced by the derived fm-scale screening.

## The consistency close — stiff ribbon and soft scissor are the same requirement, not a tension

This looked like it could be a contradiction, and it is worth recording that it is not. The DM cross-section
result σ/m ∝ N **requires a stiff ribbon** — 0860 is explicit (*"stiffness is not optional; it is precisely the
property the structure calculation must deliver"*), 0861 pins ℓ_p ~ 100–700 fm, and 0862 **derives** the bend
hinge stiff (*"the hinge is stiff, not floppy"*). The DM floor **requires a soft scissor**, g < 6/N. Same edge
bond, two geometries — so how can the bond be both stiff and soft?

Because **g is a ratio**, and the stiff-ribbon requirement is the *denominator*. A large κ_bend (stiff ribbon,
large ℓ_p) is exactly what drives g = κ_scissor/κ_bend *down*. The two DM requirements do not compete; they
**reinforce**. My ponderomotive geometry realizes this directly: the dense line-contact bend is ponderomotively
very stiff, the sparse point-crossing scissor ~50× softer *per core*, so g ≈ 0.02 with a large κ_bend. Verify §3
confirms the physical g_pond band (0.000–0.025) clears g_crit with a **~17× margin**, for *any* absolute κ_bend.

One further point this makes, worth carrying to the panel: g ≈ 0.02 with κ_bend *large* means κ_scissor is
**sizable in absolute terms** (~1/50 of a stiff bend), not near zero. The junction is the **softer of two stiff
modes**, not a fragile/floppy joint. The floor's flexibility does not require — and does not imply — a weak
bond. That answers the natural panel worry that "flexible X-junction" smuggles in "fragile candidate."

## What this does NOT close (unchanged residual, honestly restated)

The *direction* (viable) is now corpus-pinned. The *magnitude* is not. Within the physical band g_pond still
spans a factor ~5 (0.005–0.025 here; wider, to ~0.30, under the density/normalization spread of 2201), so the
exact floor (comfortably-viable ~0.4 vs marginal ~0.8) is still unset — it needs the **ZBW amplitude**. And the
absolute κ_θ (G1b) still needs the absolute edge-bond potential. Both root on **OPEN-FP-SF-2-η**. This patch
moves the *verdict direction* from conditional to pinned; it does not touch the blocked magnitude.
