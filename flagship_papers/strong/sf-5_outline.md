## 1. The gluon-counting recommendation (the centerpiece)

Two pictures of gluon counting live in the strong-sector material:

**Picture 1 — SS-1b / SS-1c (shipped, theorem-level).** SU(3)$_c$ emerges *exactly* from the qCP tetrahedral cage: eight hopping operators $T^a = \lambda^a/2$ satisfy $[T^a,T^b]=if^{abc}T^c$ with the standard structure constants (SS-1b, Main Theorem). The eight gluons are then derived (SS-1c) as transient hDP configurations on the **3 base edges** of the tetrahedral cage: **6 color-changing** gluons (hDP along the three edges) + **2 color-neutral diagonal** gluons (phase-difference modes) = 8, with masslessness (transient open-path, no confining closed subgraph → zero SSV compression energy) and spin-1 derived. **This is 1-to-1 with the SM SU(3) octet, at theorem level.**

**Picture 2 — CONJ-SS-Gluon-4Vertex (open conjecture).** Proposes the 8-fold octet is a phenomenological *dressing* of a smaller-but-richer bonding taxonomy among the **4 baryon vertices**, with experimentally divergent predictions vs the SM octet. This is the SF-5 README's proposed headline forced-choice claim.

**The tension.** Picture 1 already supplies a theorem-level 8-gluon derivation from a 3-edge structure that matches SM SU(3) exactly. CONJ-SS-Gluon-4Vertex's **own falsification route (a)** (per `frontier_sectors/SS.md`) states: *if substrate enumeration yields exactly 8 distinct types, the conjecture collapses to "the SM picture, restated."* Picture 1 is arguably already that enumeration (8, from 3 edges + 2 diagonals). So leading a flagship with Picture 2 risks (i) headlining an **unclosed** conjecture, (ii) one the corpus's own **shipped, stronger** result appears to undercut, and (iii) inviting reviewers to note the internal tension.

**Recommendation for SF-5 (and for the SF-7 strong-sector synthesis):**
> **Lead with Picture 1** — SU(3) exact + unique (SS-1b + SS-3) and the theorem-level 8-gluon derivation (SS-1c) — as the flagship headline. **Treat CONJ-SS-Gluon-4Vertex as an explicitly-flagged forward-looking conjecture** in the discussion/open-work section, with its falsification route (a) stated honestly (it may reduce to a re-description of the SM octet). Do **not** make the unclosed 4-vertex forced-choice the flagship's primary claim. Register any SF-5 attempt to close it as `OPEN-FP-5-GLUON` and inherit.

This is the third predecessor whose pre-survey surfaced a load-bearing reconciliation (after SF-1's SM-4→SM-6 phase arc and SF-3's m_c route adjudication). Here the honest move is restraint: the shipped strong-sector results are strong enough to carry the flagship without the speculative claim, and headlining the speculative claim would weaken, not strengthen, the paper.
