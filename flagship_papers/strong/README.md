## Scope

SF-5 covers the **strong sector** of the Standard Model: gluons, glueballs, mesons, baryons, and the confinement mechanism. It is the apex synthesis of the SS series (SS-1 through SS-9+) plus the open glueball derivation (OPEN-SS-6) and the SS-9 conditional-theorem closure routes (OPEN-SS-37).

**Headline as shipped (the gluon-counting decision reversed the original proposal).** During the pre-survey (Patch 1304) the SF-5 headline strategy was adjudicated. The original proposal — headline the forced-choice claim that the SU(3) octet is a phenomenological dressing of a 4-tetrahedral-vertex bonding structure (CONJ-SS-Gluon-4Vertex) — was found to be **in tension with the shipped SS-1b/SS-1c result**, which already derives the eight gluons at theorem level (6 base-edge + 2 diagonal modes), one-to-one with the SM SU(3) octet. The conjecture's own falsification route (a) collapses it to "the SM octet restated" if enumeration yields exactly 8 — and SS-1c is arguably that enumeration. **SF-5 therefore leads with the shipped theorem-level result** (SU(3) exact + unique-in-operator-representation; the eight gluons; masslessness + spin-1) and demotes CONJ-SS-Gluon-4Vertex to a flagged forward-looking conjecture in the open-work section. The headline result:

> The strong sector — the SU(3) colour algebra, the eight gluons, confinement, the string tension, the strong coupling α_s = 5/(8φ), and the light-nucleus binding cascade — derives from the tetrahedral cage geometry of the 600-cell, at theorem-level rigor where marked, on a single calibration (m_e), with the strong coupling unified with the Weinberg angle as the complementary mode fraction (sin²θ_W + α_s = 1/φ).

Specifically, SF-5 covers:

- **Gluon-counting:** the 4-tetrahedral-vertex bonding structure from which the 8-gluon SU(3) octet phenomenology emerges. The relationship between the SM's 8 gluons and CPP's 4-vertex bonding relationships — what the 8-fold count phenomenologically captures, what's distinct, where the predictions diverge.
- **Glueball:** OPEN-SS-6 territory; the glueball as a neutral spherical mass of qDPs (per CPP, distinct from the cage-bound mesons). Mass derivation, decay channels, why the glueball has been hard to observe experimentally.
- **Mesons:** neutral chains of qDPs extending between unpaired qCPs of opposite polarity. Meson mass spectrum from chain length and 600-cell coordination.
- **Baryons:** the 4-tetrahedral-vertex structure as the substrate-stable proton/neutron geometry. Baryon mass scale from SS-1 through SS-7.
- **Confinement:** why color-charged objects cannot exist in isolation — the CPP-internal mechanism that produces the SM-observed confinement phenomenology.
- **The hadron spectrum:** SS-7's twelve-nucleus binding-energy predictions extended to the broader hadron mass spectrum where corpus exists.
