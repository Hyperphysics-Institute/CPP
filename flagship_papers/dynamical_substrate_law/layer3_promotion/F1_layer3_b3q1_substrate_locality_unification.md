## §4 Proof of the corollary

### §4.1 Proof of part (I) — substrate-locality inheritance

Let $\mathcal{O}$ be any temporal-sector substrate object built from first-shell content at $v_{\text{host}}$. By assumption, $\mathcal{O}$ aggregates contributions from a subset of the host-to-first-shell + first-shell-to-first-shell edges at $\mathcal{O}(\delta)$. Apply Patch 0544 Theorem 5.1.1 edge-by-edge:

- For each first-shell-to-first-shell edge $(v_i, v_j)$ that $\mathcal{O}$ aggregates: by Patch 0544 §4 Identity G3, $\hat{e}_{ij}\cdot\hat{n} = 0$, so the Mechanism A rate perturbation is zero at $\mathcal{O}(\delta)$. No first-order contribution from this edge.

- For each host-to-first-shell edge $(v_{\text{host}}, v_j)$ that $\mathcal{O}$ aggregates: by Patch 0541 §5 Identity I1, $\hat{u}_j\cdot\hat{n} = -1/(2\phi)$, so the Mechanism A rate perturbation is uniform $-r_0\delta/(2\phi)$. Contribution from this edge is $\propto \sigma_{cycle}\cdot\delta$ with universal coefficient.

- By Patch 0544 §6 Theorem 6.1 (Perturbative-Locality Propagation Rule), $\mathcal{O}$'s contribution at $\mathcal{O}(\delta^1)$ is bounded to graph-distance-1-from-$v_{\text{host}}$ edges. Second-shell and beyond contributions enter only at $\mathcal{O}(\delta^2)$ and higher.

Therefore $\mathcal{O}$ inherits all three parts (a)/(b)/(c) of Patch 0544 Theorem 5.1.1 at $\mathcal{O}(\delta)$. $\square$ Part (I)

### §4.2 Proof of part (II) — universal Schur factor structure

Let $\mathcal{O}$ have a Wigner-Eckart-datum doublet $\{|+_{\mathcal{O}}\rangle, |-_{\mathcal{O}}\rangle\}$ of dimension $d_\Gamma$ with stabilizer subgroup $G_{\mathcal{O}} \subseteq I_h$ acting on a cage of $V_{\text{cage}}$ first-shell vertices.

By Patch 0543 §3.3's $I_h$-isotypic decomposition of $\mathcal{V}_{12}$ (or its restriction to $G_{\mathcal{O}}$ acting on the $V_{\text{cage}}$ subset), the matter-doublet states in the trivial-irrep isotypic component aggregate the $V_{\text{cage}}$ vertex contributions with uniform weight.

By Patch 0543 §8.3's Schur-orthogonality $d/N$ formula applied to the trivial $A_g$ irrep ($d_{A_g} = 1$) on the $V_{\text{cage}}$-dim carrier with $d_\Gamma$ matter-doublet projection:

$$\text{Schur factor} = \frac{d_\Gamma}{V_{\text{cage}}}$$

For the thermodynamic-arrow matter doublet at $v_{\text{host}}$: $d_\Gamma = 2$, $V_{\text{cage}} = 12$, factor $1/6$ — recovering Patch 0543 §8.3 explicitly.

For any other temporal-sector substrate object built from first-shell vertices, with appropriate $d_\Gamma$ and $V_{\text{cage}}$ values, the Schur factor takes the analogous $d_\Gamma/V_{\text{cage}}$ form. The structural universality is established. $\square$ Part (II)

### §4.3 Proof of part (III) — universal substrate-primitive identification

The Mechanism A primitive $r(\hat{e}) = r_0(1 + \delta\,\hat{e}\cdot\hat{n})$ is a UNIVERSAL substrate-physics primitive — the same A11 axiom applies framework-wide. Combined with the universal $\sigma_{cycle}$ from A5 (TI-odd cycle phase), the temporal-sector substrate-primitive content is $\sigma_{cycle}\cdot\delta$ across all first-shell-built substrate objects.

Substituting Patch 0541 Identity I1 + Patch 0544 Identity G3 into Mechanism A confirms: only host-to-first-shell edges contribute at $\mathcal{O}(\delta^1)$, all with uniform perturbation $-r_0\delta/(2\phi)$. The $\sigma_{cycle}\cdot\delta$ aggregation is therefore identical for all temporal-sector substrate objects built from first-shell vertices, modulo the object's specific geometric coefficient. $\square$ Part (III)

### §4.4 Combined proof of the corollary

Parts (I) + (II) + (III) combined give the Substrate-Locality Unification corollary at Layer 3: substrate-locality structure inherited from Patch 0544 Theorem 5.1.1; Schur factor of universal $d_\Gamma/V_{\text{cage}}$ form; substrate-primitive $\sigma_{cycle}\cdot\delta$ identification universal. $\square$
