## Scope

The electroweak sector unified: $\sin^2\theta_W = 3/(8\phi)$ derivation, W/Z masses from substrate structure, electroweak symmetry breaking mechanism, the eCP linear-oscillator phenomenology. The paper presents the EW sector as a coherent first-principles derivation from CPP primitives plus 600-cell geometry, deliberately framed against the Standard Model's three free parameters (Weinberg angle + Higgs VEV + Higgs mass) being reduced to zero or one.
