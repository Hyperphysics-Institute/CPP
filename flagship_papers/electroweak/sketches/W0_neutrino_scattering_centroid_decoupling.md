## Mechanism in CPP primitives

The insight proposes a substrate-level mechanism for neutrino-W⁰ scattering angle distributions:

1. **Free propagation baseline.** A neutrino is propagating through the DP Sea as a spinning DP or h-tet (hybrid tetrahedron). Under normal propagation, ongoing KE-enrollment exchange with the surrounding DP Sea provides directional buffering — the neutrino's trajectory is smoothed because at each Nexus tick, the surrounding DP environment biases the next-moment direction continuously.

2. **Encounter with W⁰ centroid.** The W⁰ bracelet has a centroid (the $D_6$-symmetric center of the 6-vertex regular hexagonal ring; geometrically the SSV-gradient minimum referenced in SF-2 §5 propositions). When the spinning DP/h-tet passes through this centroid, the local DP Sea environment is structurally altered by the bracelet's presence — specifically, the DP Sea KE-enrollment pattern that normally buffers the propagation is disrupted.

3. **Momentary decoupling.** At the instant of centroid passage, the spinning DP/h-tet loses its coupling with the DP Sea. This is the substrate-level analog of a "free" propagation moment with no directional buffering.

4. **Abrupt emergence.** As the neutrino emerges from the W⁰ centroid region, it re-couples with the DP Sea — but now its direction is set by whichever DP Sea directions are "allowed" at the post-centroid spatial location. Because the re-coupling is discrete (Nexus-tick by Nexus-tick) and the prior buffering has been disrupted, the emergence direction is discrete rather than smoothly continuous: a discrete scattering angle.

## Connection to known phenomenology

Neutrino scattering through weak interactions has specific angular distributions in the Standard Model:

- **Charged-current (CC) neutrino-nucleon scattering** has the V-A angular distribution: $d\sigma/d\Omega \propto (1 \pm \cos\theta)^2$ depending on neutrino vs antineutrino. The angular dependence reflects the V-A coupling structure.

- **Neutral-current (NC) neutrino-electron scattering** has $g_V$ and $g_A$ contributions with characteristic angular dependencies.

If the centroid-decoupling mechanism produces specific angular distributions for the post-emergence direction, this would be a **CPP-specific structural derivation** of the V-A angular content rather than a free parameter input.

## Physical assessment

**Is this scientifically substantive?**

Yes — three reasons:

1. **CPP-specific mechanism with empirical content.** The insight proposes a substrate-level mechanism (centroid-decoupling) that maps onto an observable (scattering angle distribution). If the mechanism predicts specific angular distributions, those are testable.

2. **Connects SF-2 to SF-4 via shared substrate primitives.** The W⁰ centroid is an SF-2 structural feature; the DP/h-tet propagation is an SF-4 structural feature. The centroid-decoupling mechanism is a shared substrate-level concept linking the two flagships.

3. **Falsifiable claim available.** The mechanism predicts specific behaviors: discrete (rather than smooth) scattering angles, angular distributions determined by DP Sea symmetry constraints at the post-centroid location, possible characteristic timescales (Nexus-tick discretization). Any of these could be tested against precision neutrino-scattering data.

**Is it ready for paper integration?**

No, not at SF-2 v0.7 → v0.8. Three layers of substantive development are required first:

- **Layer A: Operational definition of "centroid passage" and "decoupling."** What does it mean substrate-mechanically for a spinning DP/h-tet to "pass through" a W⁰ centroid? Is this a wave-function overlap with the centroid region? A literal spatial coincidence? A geometric-resonance condition? The mechanism's predictive content depends on this.

- **Layer B: Quantification of "abrupt re-coupling."** How does the post-emergence direction get selected? Is it uniform over allowed DP Sea directions (giving isotropic scattering), or biased by the W⁰ bracelet's $D_6$ stabilizer structure (giving anisotropic scattering with hexagonal symmetry imprint)? Or biased by the incoming neutrino's spin orientation (giving V-A-like angular dependence)?

- **Layer C: Bridge to V-A phenomenology.** Standard Model V-A is a specific angular structure derived from the chiral structure of the weak interactions. The centroid-decoupling mechanism would need to either reproduce V-A as a derived consequence, or modify it (predicting deviations from V-A at high precision).
