\begin{lemma}[$Z$/$\nu_2$ derivational independence at the $V=12$ shell; supports C2]\label{lem:c2indep}
The SF-2 derivation of $m_Z$ and the SF-4 derivation of $m_{\nu_2}$ --- the two co-occupants of the 12-vertex first shell --- share \emph{exactly one} input, the single calibration $m_e$ (entering both only through $\Mzero = m_e(z/\phig)$ and the electroweak base scale). They share no other parameter, fitted or geometric-beyond-the-substrate. Consequently every parameter exclusive to one derivation leaves the other's mass strictly invariant, and the two masses are computed without double-counting any degree of freedom beyond the declared single calibration.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
We discharge the three independence conditions in turn.

\emph{(i) Disjoint state variables.} The $Z$ is fixed by the icosahedral cage-\emph{shape} occupying the shell --- the unique $I_h$-stabilised 12-vertex first-shell cage of \citet{abshier2026sf2} --- so its state variable is a discrete shape-class label within the SM-1 four-cage taxonomy together with the boson's stability data. The $\nu_2$ is an unbound three-dimensional orbital ZBW mode \emph{occupying} the shell \citep{abshier2026sf4}, with no rigid cage; its state variable is a shell-occupation excitation. The shape-class space and the occupation space are disjoint. The common index $V=12$ is a fixed \emph{geometric locus} of the 600-cell distance-shell structure, not a free variable that either derivation tunes.

\emph{(ii) Disjoint parameter sets.} The $Z$ absolute mass is a function of the icosahedral geometry together with the per-boson holographic dilution $\eta_Z\sim10^{-17}$ (OPEN-FP-SF-2-$\eta$) and the loop factor $\ell_Z$ (OPEN-FP-SF-2-loopfactor), both \emph{exclusive to SF-2}. The neutrino mass is $m_{\nu_2} = \Mzero\,(12)^2\,\sigma_\nu$ with $\sigma_\nu = z^{-10}$ \emph{theorem-derived and parameter-free} (SF-4 Picture-A closure, v2.0), exclusive to SF-4. The two suppression factors differ in both scale and mechanism ($\eta_Z\sim10^{-17}$ from cosmic-horizon holographic embedding; $\sigma_\nu\sim10^{-11}$ from walk-dimension primitives). Hence the fitted/calibrated parameter sets are disjoint: $\{\eta_Z,\ell_Z\}\cap\varnothing=\varnothing$.

\emph{(iii) Perturbation invariance.} From (ii), $\partial m_{\nu_2}/\partial\eta_Z = \partial m_{\nu_2}/\partial\ell_Z = 0$ (the neutrino mass depends only on $m_e$, $V^2$, $\sigma_\nu$), and $\partial m_Z/\partial\sigma_\nu = 0$ (the $Z$ mass depends only on the icosahedral geometry, $\eta_Z$, $\ell_Z$, and the base scale). The sole nonzero cross-coupling is through $m_e$, $\partial m_Z/\partial m_e\neq0$ and $\partial m_{\nu_2}/\partial m_e\neq0$ --- the \emph{declared} single calibration of the entire SF-line. The content of the no-double-counting claim is precisely that there is no \emph{additional} shared degree of freedom beyond it. Therefore the two co-occupants of the $V=12$ shell are derived independently up to the single $m_e$ calibration, and clause C2 holds at theorem level. \qed
\end{proof}

\noindent Two corollaries. \textbf{(a)} C2's theorem status is \emph{independent of whether $\eta_Z$ is closed}: OPEN-FP-SF-2-$\eta$ is a separate, carried \S9 caveat on the \emph{absolute} boson mass, not a precondition for no-double-counting. \textbf{(b)} Naming the single $m_e$ coupling explicitly is exactly what converts the v0.1 ``consistent by construction'' assertion into a checkable theorem --- the lemma is stronger than the assertion it replaces, because it isolates the one permitted dependency rather than gesturing at ``the same framework.''
