\begin{lemma}[$Z$/$\nu_2$ derivational independence at the $V=12$ shell; supports C2]\label{lem:c2indep}

\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
We discharge the three independence conditions in turn.

\emph{(i) Disjoint state variables.} The $Z$ is fixed by the icosahedral cage-\emph{shape} occupying the shell --- the unique $I_h$-stabilised 12-vertex first-shell cage of \citet{abshier2026sf2} --- so its state variable is a discrete shape-class label within the SM-1 four-cage taxonomy together with the boson's stability data. The shape-class space and the occupation space are disjoint. The common index $V=12$ is a fixed \emph{geometric locus} of the 600-cell distance-shell structure, not a free variable that either derivation tunes.

\emph{(ii) Disjoint parameter sets.} The $Z$ absolute mass is a function of the icosahedral geometry together with the per-boson holographic dilution $\eta_Z\sim10^{-17}$ (OPEN-FP-SF-2-$\eta$) and the loop factor $\ell_Z$ (OPEN-FP-SF-2-loopfactor), both \emph{exclusive to SF-2}. The two suppression factors differ in both scale and mechanism ($\eta_Z\sim10^{-17}$ from cosmic-horizon holographic embedding; $\sigma_\nu\sim10^{-11}$ from walk-dimension primitives). Hence the fitted/calibrated parameter sets are disjoint: $\{\eta_Z,\ell_Z\}\cap\varnothing=\varnothing$.

\emph{(iii) Perturbation invariance.} From (ii), $\partial m_{\nu_2}/\partial\eta_Z = \partial m_{\nu_2}/\partial\ell_Z = 0$ (the neutrino mass depends only on $m_e$, $V^2$, $\sigma_\nu$), and $\partial m_Z/\partial\sigma_\nu = 0$ (the $Z$ mass depends only on the icosahedral geometry, $\eta_Z$, $\ell_Z$, and the base scale). The content of the no-double-counting claim is precisely that there is no \emph{additional} shared degree of freedom beyond it. \addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Appendix: Programme identifiers}

\noindent This programme tracks its unresolved questions under short identifiers so that a claim and its open dependencies can be cited precisely. The identifiers appearing in this paper are glossed below; no external document is needed to read them.

\begin{description}
  \item[\texttt{OPEN-FP-1-P3}] \hfill \\ An open problem of the SF-1 charged-lepton flagship, inherited openly by later flagship papers rather than resolved.
  \item[\texttt{OPEN-FP-3-CKM}] \hfill \\ Derive from CPP primitives the Cabibbo–Kobayashi–Maskawa (CKM) mixing angles and the quark CP-violating phase, which have no derivation anywhere in the CPP corpus.
  \item[\texttt{OPEN-FP-5-GLUEBALL}] \hfill \\ Derive the lightest scalar glueball mass from a closed tetrahedral hDP loop in the 600-cell substrate.
  \item[\texttt{OPEN-FP-6-CONSTANTS}] \hfill \\ Derive the electromagnetic constants μ0, ε0, c (and the relativistic γ(v)) from the 600-cell dipole stiffness without parameter tuning, replacing the DP-Sea-Polarization toy-model values.
  \item[\texttt{OPEN-FP-6-EMHAND}] \hfill \\ Build the electromagnetic-phenomenology *expression* of the already-closed substrate chirality — a physical-EM narrative connecting the group-theoretic substrate handedness to observable electromagnetic phenomena.
  \item[\texttt{OPEN-FP-SF-2}] \hfill \\ (family) Open problems of the SF-2 electroweak flagship, chiefly the first-principles derivation of the per-boson holographic dilution factor on absolute electroweak boson masses.
  \item[\texttt{OPEN-SS-19}] \hfill \\ Derive the two working conjectures at the heart of CONJ-SS-11 — the \$(A-1)\$ cascade reinforcement factor and the Pauli penalty coefficient \$M\_0/\textbackslash\{\}varphi\textasciicircum{}3\$ — from CPP primitives. Also derive the NLO correction \$\textbackslash\{\}varepsilon\_d \textbackslash\{\}approx 0.050\$ closing the 5.3\% LO residual.
  \item[\texttt{OPEN-SS-6}] \hfill \\ Compute lightest scalar glueball mass using f\_geom formula applied to closed hDP loop.
\end{description}
