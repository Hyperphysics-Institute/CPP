## Scope

The grand-unification synthesis of the six predecessor flagship papers (SF-1 through SF-6) into a single apex artifact. SF-7 is the **summit of the SF-line**: the paper that demonstrates, by reference to the six predecessors plus its own synthesis content, that all 17 Standard Model particles plus the W⁰ CPP-novel prediction derive from a single substrate calibration ($m_e$) plus 600-cell geometry.

The "hierarchy problem" — the headline named known-unknown of the SM-mass program — is solved demonstrably by the cumulative content of SF-1 through SF-7. But SF-7 is more than the hierarchy paper alone: it is the cross-particle-class synthesis that includes the strong sector (SF-5) and the electromagnetism unification (SF-6), bringing the entire SM under one substrate-derivation umbrella.

This is the Track-1 ambition originally framed at Session 36 close+ (the "Hierarchy Without Hierarchy" paper), promoted at Session 38 to its proper role as synthesis-on-top-of-four-family-papers, and promoted again at Session 41 to grand-unification-on-top-of-six-flagship-papers. The Session 41 promotion reflects honest scope: with strong sector and electromagnetism each having distinct enough mechanisms to warrant their own flagships, the grand unification is *more substantial* than originally framed, not just renumbered.
