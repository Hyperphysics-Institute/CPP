## Scope

The "hierarchy problem" — the headline named known-unknown of the entire SM-mass program — is solved demonstrably by the cumulative content of SF-1 through SF-5.

This is the Track-1 ambition originally framed at Session 36 close+ (the "Hierarchy Without Hierarchy" paper), promoted to its proper role in the Option-3 architecture: not a single all-encompassing paper but the synthesis on top of four self-contained family flagships.
