## What kind of result is this?

A **conditional theorem closure at zero fitted parameters** delivering one primary empirical prediction validated within 2% of its empirical anchor. The Composite Capotauro Wigner-Eckart Theorem (THEO-CAP-1) establishes $\|M\| = \chi/6 = \phi^{-3}/6 \approx 0.0394$ as the chirality matrix element on the TBM-aligned K3-doublet of charged-lepton substrate states, under foundational inputs FI-C-1 through FI-C-10 + 4 CPP axioms (A1, A3, A4, A7). The primary empirical prediction $\Delta p_{LR} \approx 0.0394$ agrees with the leptogenesis-back-derivation empirical anchor $\sim 0.04$ within 2%.

**Paper-type declaration (per `operating_system.md` §4 taxonomy):** This is a **conditional-theorem closure paper** with partial-closure status on a multi-sub-claim open problem. Success criterion: rigorous theorem-level derivation of the primary mechanism on stated foundational inputs + zero-parameter empirical prediction within stated falsifier threshold. The paper satisfies this criterion for sub-claim (c) of OPEN-SM-4; sub-claims (a) and (b) remain OPEN and are scope-deferred to v2.0+ work.

**Main claim:**

$$\|M\| = \left|\langle\Phi_-^{(1)}|\hat{C}_\chi|\Phi_-^{(2)}\rangle\right| = \chi/6 = \phi^{-3}/6 \approx 0.0394 \qquad \text{(THEO-CAP-1)}$$

on the TBM-aligned K3-doublet extended basis $\{\|\Phi_-^{(1)}\rangle, \|\Phi_-^{(2)}\rangle\}$, conditional on FI-C-1 through FI-C-10 + CPP axioms A1, A3, A4, A7.

## Relationship to Standard Model

The Capotauro mechanism is primarily **extends**: provides a CPP-substrate-level account underlying SM electroweak parity violation, with one direct empirical prediction that has no SM counterpart at this precision (Δp_LR on K3-doublet eigenstates is not a conventional SM observable).

**Categorized relationships:**

- **Extends SM** in the following respects:
  - Provides substrate-level mechanism for the existence of parity violation in weak interactions (PHEN-E-CAP-1). The SM accommodates parity violation phenomenologically via the V-A structure of W± couplings; CPP/Capotauro derives the underlying chirality magnitude $\|\chi\| = \phi^{-3}$ from the 600-cell substrate geometry.
  - Provides substrate-level mechanism for the right-hand-rule structure of the ExB law at all scales (PHEN-E-CAP-2). The SM treats the right-hand rule as a convention; CPP/Capotauro derives it from substrate-vacuum primitive chirality magnitude.
  - Predicts cross-sector consistency: the K3-doublet's $D_6$ stabilizer + cage-shell averaging matches the SF-2 W bracelet's $D_6$ stabilizer with structural identity $V_\text{cage} = \|D_6\| = 12$ (PHEN-X-CAP-4). This is a CPP-specific cross-sector structural prediction not present in SM.

- **Reproduces SM** in the following respects:
  - PMNS TBM mixing structure (inherited from SM-5; the K3-doublet basis is TBM-aligned by construction).
  - W± V-A coupling structure (cross-sector closure pair with OPEN-FP-SF-2-CHIR; the Capotauro $D_6$ structure and W bracelet $D_6$ structure share substrate-symmetry origin).
  - Charged-lepton mass hierarchy (inherited from SF-4 v4.0 via FI-C-6 precedent; FI-C-10 extends this to chirality observable class).

- **Disagrees with SM** in no current respects — the Capotauro v1.0 prediction $\Delta p_{LR} \approx 0.0394$ has no SM counterpart at this level of structure-vs-fitting distinction. If a future SM analysis derives a different value for the same observable, this would constitute a disagreement; at v1.0 SHIP the prediction is in the "new prediction at zero parameters" category, not the "disagreement" category.

- **SM accommodations** (where SM is preserved but Capotauro provides additional structure):
  - $\eta_B$ matter-antimatter asymmetry — SM cannot derive $\eta_B$ from first principles (this is one of the open problems of SM cosmology); Capotauro provides the primary parity-violation asymmetry $\Delta p_{LR}$ that feeds the leptogenesis chain. Full first-principles derivation of $\eta_B$ from Capotauro chain remains beyond v1.0 scope (PHEN-O-CAP-6).
  - $\delta_{CP}$ — SM treats $\delta_{CP}$ as a free PMNS parameter; Capotauro provides the substrate-level chirality observable on which $\delta_{CP}$ depends via PMNS perturbation machinery (PHEN-O-CAP-5). Full derivation of $\delta_{CP}$ beyond v1.0 scope.

## Limits of scope

The Capotauro v1.0 paper's scope is bounded by the following explicit limits:

**1. Sub-claim scope**: The paper closes sub-claim (c) of OPEN-SM-4 (substrate-to-K3-doublet matrix element derivation $\|M\| = \chi/6$ on TBM-aligned K3-doublet). It does NOT close sub-claim (a) (Capotauro nucleation event derivation) or sub-claim (b) (substrate chirality mechanism candidate derivation). These remain OPEN; PARTIAL CLOSURE status registered at `Research_Frontier.md` OPEN-SM-4.

**2. Foundational input scope**: The conditional theorem closure of THEO-CAP-1 is established under FI-C-1 through FI-C-10. First-principles derivation of these foundational inputs from CPP axioms A1–A11 is OUT OF SCOPE at v1.0. FI-C-9 first-principles derivation tracked at OPEN-FI-C-9-FP-MECHANISM with Reading C candidate; FI-C-10 first-principles derivation tracked as separate open work entry.

**3. Mathematical framework scope**: The Wigner-Eckart factorization on $D_6 = S_3 \times Z_2$ uses standard finite-group representation theory. Continuum-EFT bridge to the Capotauro mechanism (Layer 4 of PD-004 publication-pathway taxonomy) is OUT OF SCOPE at v1.0; Layer 4 closure for cross-sector integration with SM EFT registered as future work.

**4. Empirical-anchor framework scope**: The leptogenesis back-derivation of the $\Delta p_{LR} \sim 0.04$ empirical anchor uses standard cosmological-leptogenesis assumptions (sphaleron-equilibration efficiency, washout factor, lepton-flavor-mixing) with ~10% framework-uncertainty. Refinement of the empirical anchor via direct laboratory measurement of $\Delta p_{LR}$ on K3-doublet eigenstates is OUT OF SCOPE at v1.0 (presently inaccessible; technical obstacle is K3-doublet structure averaged out in conventional flavor-basis observables).

**5. PMNS perturbation framework scope**: Downstream observables from $\|M\| = \chi/6$ via PMNS perturbation machinery — specifically $\sin^2\theta_{13}$ corrections ($\delta_{CP}$, $\eta_B$ derivation chain) — are OUT OF SCOPE at v1.0. Q11 $\sin^2\theta_{13}$ derivation re-scoped to SF-2 v2.0+. $\delta_{CP}$ and $\eta_B$ are downstream observables requiring future sub-claim work.

**6. Cosmological-timing framework scope**: The Capotauro nucleation event's cosmological timing (sub-claim (a)) inherits the Abshier & Grok December 2025 Capotauro nucleation paper framing ($z \simeq 32$ lattice nucleation epoch) but does not derive the timing from first principles. Cosmological-timing first-principles derivation is OUT OF SCOPE at v1.0 (deferred until sub-claim (b) substrate-vacuum dynamics framework is developed).

## Methodological observations

Three methodological observations from the Capotauro v1.0 SHIP arc are worth Tier 4 capture and registered for programme-level codification opportunities:

**1. Primitive-feature framing per CPP-core-principle methodological commitment**: The Session 120 Patch 0413 reframe of §2 from SSB to primitive-feature framing is the canonical reference case for distinguishing CPP's discrete-substrate ontology from continuum-EFT methodological traditions. The reframe was forced by SHIP-readiness convergence at v0.8 Session 119 — at that point the SSB framing had been carried through v0.x development on the premise that the mathematical content was unchanged, but v1.0 SHIP commits the paper to a primary framing for external readers, and the primitive-feature framing was the only one consistent with CPP's no-prior-substrate methodological commitment. **Generalizable pattern**: methodologically critical framings forced by SHIP-readiness convergence cannot be deferred to v2.0+; they must be resolved at the polish-and-ship trajectory before v1.0 commits the paper to a primary framing.

**2. Theorem-registry registration ahead of paper publication**: THEO-CAP-1 was registered in `theorem-registry.md` at Session 103 Patch 0397 (first such pattern in CPP corpus) under four-condition methodological pattern: rigorous proof + end-to-end numerical verification + primary empirical prediction validated + honest scope-limitation framing for re-scoped sub-problems. **Generalizable pattern**: theorem-registry registration from flagship-paper-pending working sketches is appropriate when those four conditions are met; the convention establishes a path for swarm-validation theorems to enter the programme registry before their flagship-paper venue is shipped.

Discipline-tightening-after-precedent principle**: The Session 123 OPEN-WORKFLOW-DOCS-CATCHUP deferral decision (Patch 0416 forward queue) captures the methodological principle that doing one full example end-to-end before codifying programme-wide discipline makes the discipline credible. **Generalizable pattern**: paper-specific innovation that becomes programme-wide rule via codification patch — first observed at Sessions 115–116 (Patch 0408 per-paper changelog file reference implementation → Session 116 Patch 0409 programme-wide codification); applied to Session 123 Capotauro doc-suite catch-up (Patches 0416A–M reference implementation → Patch 0416M OPEN-WORKFLOW-DOCS-CATCHUP programme-wide codification).
