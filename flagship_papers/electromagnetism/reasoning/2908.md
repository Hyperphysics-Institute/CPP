# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2908 (the gate aborted the round; chaos measured; the power wall named)

**STATUS: contemporaneous. Analysis at
`sketches/mobile_sea_chaos_and_power_analysis.md`.**

The gate I froze three hours ago killed my own round before it started,
and it was right to. The frozen legs proved the construction exactly
symmetric to 1e-16; the mobile leg broke to 8e-4 anyway; and the twin
diagnostic showed why: the ZBW dynamics amplify machine rounding with
an e-folding time under two Moments. I predicted a symmetry-protected
zero and the system is chaotic. The prereg said abort, so nothing
beyond beta zero was run, and the moving-leg bands died unread.

Diagnostic code, for the record (run at 2908, class A seed 1, T=70):
evolve the symmetric config and a twin with one CP shifted by 1e-12;
log the source drive and the max position divergence. Divergence fit
over Moments 5-40 gives lambda = 0.562 per Moment. The symmetric run's
drive sits at 2e-16 for ten Moments, then climbs six orders in thirty.

What the chaos gives with one hand - fully legitimate ensembles, and a
clean retrodiction of every standard error rounds 1 and 2 produced - it
takes with the other, and I did the arithmetic I should have done at
Patch 2902 before promising curvature bands at all: the curvature is a
sub-percent modulation of a signal that is itself six times smaller
than the per-Moment chatter. Ten-million-Moment territory. Three rounds
of increasingly clever noise engineering were never within three orders
of magnitude of the question they carried bands for. Writing that
sentence is uncomfortable and overdue.

The restructure follows the arc's own precedent rather than novelty.
The sign of the response is reachable - it is a first-order effect, the
tally already leans 13 of 15, and the required data is a small multiple
of what exists - so it becomes its own narrowly-scoped round. The
curvature goes to a hybrid: measure the Sea's response function as an
integrated observable (the chatter of ten thousand pairs averages; the
response adds), then push it through the 2900-class integral where
there is no chatter at all. That is the toy-and-FEM division of labour
from Patch 2901, no longer as taste but as necessity. Under PD-006 the
sequencing decision is mine and I have made it; the founder's physics
eye is invited on one point only: whether the entrainment response
function - the Sea's per-pair displacement field around the moving
charge - is, in his picture, the right carrier of the fore/aft
disparity, since that is the object the hybrid promotes to centre
stage.
