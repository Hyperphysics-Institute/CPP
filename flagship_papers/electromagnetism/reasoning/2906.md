**STATUS: contemporaneous. Record at

Three rounds, three inconclusives, and this one finally taught the
lesson the first two only hinted at. I assumed the enemy was statistical
noise and planned power accordingly; the frozen legs — which have zero
chatter by construction — read six-times-ten-to-the-minus-eighteen at
the symmetric phase and minus-two-point-four-e-minus-three at the
quarter phase. That is not noise. That is the truncated cylinder being
asymmetric about every source position except one, at exactly the
amplitude where the physics lives. My quarter-phase ensemble, chosen to
kill the washboard, planted three of its four legs on positions with a
built-in thousandth-scale bias. The design fought itself.

Two honest retractions follow. First, the 2904 clause promising that a
third inconclusive would bound the response below 1e-3 assumed a
standard error we never reached; the bound is not claimed, and I wrote
that in the record rather than quietly letting the clause expire.
Second, my repeated expectation of cancellation has now gone three
rounds without support; it stands declared for round 3, but I note for
the next worker that its epistemic status is unchanged since Patch 2900
— it rests entirely on the steady-state argument, and nothing measured
since has strengthened it.

What HAS accumulated, and what I am carefully not banking: thirteen of
fifteen differential values across both rounds are positive — the
forward, repulsive, Condition-A direction — pooling this round to 2.6
sigma. If that were a banked number the arc would celebrate; it is not,
because every constituent sits under its floor criterion. The round-3
design exists to make this either bankable or dead: source positions
restricted to the two provably symmetric classes (static asymmetry
exactly zero, shown by symmetry, checkable by frozen legs), a genuine
Monte Carlo ensemble via seeded jitter of initial pair separations
(the engine is deterministic; repetition without jitter is a no-op, a
fact easy to forget), and per-leg detrending of the slow accretion that
makes a charged source in a mobile dipole Sea non-stationary. That
accretion, incidentally, is not a nuisance in the physics — fore-aft
asymmetric accretion may BE the founder's volume-transfer mechanism in
steady state — but for now it is a nuisance in the estimator.

The gate story deserves its sentence: the compiled kernel's first build
had the bisection branch inverted — no retardation at all — and passed
V1 cleanly because static emitters cannot expose a retardation bug. The
frozen 10-Moment dynamic gate caught it at once. Guards that seem
pedantic keep catching real bugs; that is now a datum of this arc, not
an opinion.
