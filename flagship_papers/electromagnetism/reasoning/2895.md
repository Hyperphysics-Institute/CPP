# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2895 (Branch 1 verified)

**STATUS: contemporaneous.**

I registered at 2894 that Branch 1's 1/r^2 followed "by construction" and
that I would not claim it, because that phrase had failed on measurement
three times already this session. Running it justified the caution
immediately, though not in the way I expected.

The first test passed. All three emission schemes gave slope -2.0,
including the twelve-ray case that I had myself flagged at 2889 as
FAILING to produce a continuous field. Two of my own results
contradicting each other again -- and this time the contradiction was the
signal. Shell-averaged density is 1/r^2 by conservation alone: bits per
shell constant, shell volume growing as r^2, so ANY conserved ballistic
flux returns -2 whether the field is smooth or is twelve pencil beams. The
observable cannot discriminate. Had I stopped there and reported it, it
would have been the fourth false confirmation of the session and of
exactly the established shape.

The discriminating question is whether a test charge sitting OFF a ray
feels anything, which is angular isotropy at fixed radius. I built that
measurement carefully because I broke the equivalent one at 2891 by
binning on lattice shells whose members are symmetry-equivalent; equal-area
solid-angle patches avoid that. Continuum and lattice-path emission both
land at the Poisson floor. Twelve-direction emission sits two orders of
magnitude above it and, tellingly, is CONSTANT in radius -- radial rays are
scale-invariant, so they never fill in. No amount of distance rescues them.

The finding that matters is that the discriminator is not lattice versus
continuum. Lattice paths aimed at arbitrary shell GPs are isotropic. The
lattice was never the problem. The problem was twelve directions, and
twelve directions came from the 2802 kernel, which I inherited at the
start of this arc and carried unexamined through five successive engines.
At 2889 section 7 I recorded the ray structure as a DEFECT OF BRANCH 1 and
listed it as the reason a third engine design was needed. It was a defect
of my implementation. The founder's text says bits transit all the edges
between the origin and all GPs on the PSR shell -- the whole shell, not
twelve neighbours -- and I had read that sentence and implemented
something else.

That is the same failure as 2893, one layer down. There I inherited the
engine from the arc rather than the spec; here I inherited a parameter of
the engine from the arc rather than the spec, after having explicitly
diagnosed the first instance. Diagnosing a failure mode does not
inoculate against it.

Condition B closes under Branch 1 and I want to be exact about how much
that buys. The relay is retarded and not Lienard-Wiechert, which was the
question. It does not touch Condition A, the marginality condition, or
B1. And it does not lift the statics suspension: this was a particle-count
measurement with no background field at all, which is why it was clean,
and that makes it not a substitute for the field steady-state measurement
that 2892 suspended.
