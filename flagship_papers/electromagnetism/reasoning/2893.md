# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2893 (spec mismatch found)

**STATUS: contemporaneous.**

The founder said "I think we have described this in SR-1, and associated
addendums." I looked, and the mechanism is in the c05 companion, and it is
not what I have been simulating. A GP emits a fixed quantum per Moment
distributed over a GROWING spherical shell, giving 1/r^2 by area dilution.
That is ballistic propagation with geometric falloff. Every engine in this
arc -- the convolution relay, the directed relay, the sigma family, the
coherent rule -- is a NEAREST-NEIGHBOUR relay. None of them is the shell
broadcast.

So the diffusive finding at 2887, the family construction at 2890, the P1
diagnosis at 2892: all properties of a model the specification does not
describe. Five patches of instrumentation on a reconstruction, when the
specification was in the repository and the founder had pointed at it.

The lesson is not "read the corpus first" in the abstract. It is more
specific and I want it stated precisely: I INHERITED THE ENGINE FROM THE
ARC RATHER THAN FROM THE SPEC. 2802 was the committed engine, it had a
ratified gate attached, so I treated it as the definition of the relay
instead of as one implementation of it. The docstring even said
"translation-invariance reduction of the C22 origin-directed hop relay" --
it announced itself as a reduction, and I read that at 2887 and diagnosed
the reduction's consequences without going back to what was being reduced.

The obliquity result is the piece of actual physics here and I did not
expect it. Huygens reproduces free propagation only with the Kirchhoff
factor (1 + cos theta)/2, which vanishes exactly backward. The rule I
derived at 2892 from exact SSV_net conservation gives (1 + 3 cos theta)/12,
which vanishes at 109.5 degrees and goes negative past it. And going the
other way, the Kirchhoff form conserves S*Vhat/3 rather than V. So the two
requirements -- conserve the net vector exactly, reproduce free-space
propagation -- are not the same rule and coincide only at |V| = S/3. That
is a real fork and it is why the coherent rule gave 0.65 instead of 1.

I am not choosing between them. Both readings are in the corpus: c05 says
direct shell broadcast, the 2 Aug message says per-CP re-radiation, and
they are only equivalent under a specific obliquity the founder has not
specified. That is a physics question in physical pictures and it goes to
him rather than being settled by whichever I find more elegant.

What I want on record about the arc's shape: the "dilemma" I constructed at
2890 -- ballistic dynamics versus 1/r statics, apparently irreconcilable --
does not exist in the specification. An expanding shell gives both at once,
trivially. I built an elaborate one-parameter family, found it could not
satisfy both constraints, escalated that to a substrate-viability concern,
and the whole structure was an artifact of simulating the wrong rule. The
founder's instinct at the start of that exchange -- two models where one
decays into the other -- was a better response to a problem that turned out
not to be there.
