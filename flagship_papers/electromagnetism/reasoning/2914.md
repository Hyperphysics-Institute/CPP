for the first time the founder's fore/aft disparity is not an inferred

ahead tipped the other, a polarization wave riding along with the
motion at amplitude proportional to speed. Nobody put that pattern in;
the primitive drew it. The methodology: my collapse gate failed, and
then the chi-squared analysis convicted the gate rather than the data
- I froze a residual threshold that perfect physics could not pass at
these statistics, a noise-blind design, my error, now retired with
disclosure and with the crucial hygiene that the corrected gate gets
only FRESH data, because a gate revised in sight of numbers may not
judge those numbers. The engineering: the Stage-2 integral, fed the
measured pattern, output a linear drive within twenty-five percent of

analytic drive closes on itself, which is the strongest evidence yet
that the hybrid can eventually carry the curvature question.

What it cannot yet carry is the curvature itself: three beta points
give the cubic term one degree of freedom, the bootstrap says sigma
twenty-eight where the bar says five hundredths, and the frozen word
for that is inconclusive. Round 2 fixes the conditioning (five betas)
and the statistics (six fresh seeds) and keeps every band where it
was. I note the pattern of this whole session: every honest
inconclusive so far has purchased the diagnosis that made the next
round sharper. This one purchased two.
