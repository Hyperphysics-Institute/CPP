# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2896 (addressed routing; 2895 corrected)

**STATUS: contemporaneous.**

The founder asked directly whether his described mechanism was what I had
tested, and added that since it appeared to work it was probably right. I
checked instead of accepting, and it was not what I tested. The
lattice_paths branch of the 2895 code has a comment describing hop-by-hop
FCC routing and a body that assigns d=tgt, straight-line continuum travel.
So that row was the continuum row computed twice with different random
draws. The two columns agreeing to three decimals looked like a
cross-check and was a duplicate.

That is worth stating plainly because it is the second time in two patches
that a comment and its code disagreed in my own work, and because the
founder was about to build on it. Had he not asked, the false row would
have stood.

Then I nearly repeated 2891 one level deeper. My first run of the real
mechanism used solid-angle patches and returned CV 2.6 at r=4, which looks
like catastrophic near-field anisotropy. But at small radius the lattice
has almost no sites -- 12 GPs at sqrt2, 6 at r=2, 24 at 2.449 -- so
patches containing no lattice sites at all register as empty and inflate
the CV. I was measuring lattice discreteness again, exactly as at 2891
where symmetry-equivalent shells gave spurious zeros. The right observable
follows from the physics rather than from convenience: a CP can only sit
at a GP, so what matters is the variation of bit count ACROSS GPs at the
same radius.

Under that observable the nearest-neighbour shell is essentially perfectly
isotropic, CV 0.014, in both routing modes. That is not a lucky result, it
is symmetry: uniformly distributed addresses give uniformly distributed
first hops across the 12 edges. And it is the scale that matters, because
the DP arcs carrying the inertia mechanism are nearest-neighbour
structures. So the mechanism has the smooth background it needs exactly
where it operates.

The intermediate radii are genuinely imperfect and I have not resolved
them. Softmax beats greedy by 5-8x there, which incidentally supports
reading the founder's 're-radiation to the 12 nearest neighbors' as a
distribution rather than a deterministic pick -- plural, and the plural
performs better. But CV 0.2-0.4 against a 0.03 floor is not clean, and I
have registered it as open rather than describing it as adequate.

The trade-off in section 5 is the part I would flag for the founder's
attention. Diffuse routing buys isotropy and costs speed: beta=1.5 gives
53 hops where 21 is the minimum, so the effective propagation speed drops
well under c_lat. There is no free parameter to escape this with, since
beta is whatever physically sets the re-radiation distribution. That is
his question, not mine.

Condition B is untouched by any of this -- retardation follows from
ballistic travel and does not care about the routing rule.
