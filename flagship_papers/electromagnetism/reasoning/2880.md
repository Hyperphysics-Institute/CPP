# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2880 (CONJ-FP-1 registered)

**STATUS: contemporaneous, captured at patch. Session of 2026-08-01.**

The founder proposed declaring his own inertia mechanism a conjecture
rather than pressing it to a theorem, on the grounds that it bottoms out
on substrate specifics we do not have. I agreed, and I want the reasoning
recorded because agreeing was not the obvious move: the mechanism is the
best account of inertia the programme has, and there was a version of
this exchange where I helped him find a way to call it derived.

He was right about the bottom. He was wrong about one leg, and I said so.
The arc-to-spacing ratio is not unmeasurable — AUTOMATON-2 recorded the
ZBW turning radii at sqrt2 and 2sqrt2 in a run of definite density, both
in the same units, so the ratio is computable from data already banked,
and L-4 exempts those results from the arc-closure limitations. The
density sweep that would test its invariance is already a registered item.
So the conjecture goes on the books with an executable route rather than
as a dead end, and that is a materially different object.

I also found an indication pointing his way — the pair correlation shows
no like-charge contact structure at all, which reads as arcs comparable to
their spacing rather than small against it, which is the regime where my
momentum objection fails. That is the ninth favourable turn in this arc.
It went in as an indication explicitly not banked, in the same paragraph
that names it as the ninth. Six of the previous eight died, one of them to
my own computation four days ago.

The hardest thing in this patch is section 7, and it costs me something.
I read the 2496 code and it is a scalar wave field: no arcs, no charge
separation, no charging, no discharge, and no relaxation time. Two turns
earlier I had handed the founder the forward F_hold measurement as the
strongest in-model support for his sign, and it is not evidence for his
mechanism at all -- it measures a wave toy containing none of the
structure his account requires. I withdrew it in the conjecture text
rather than quietly dropping it, because he made an argument on the
strength of a card I dealt him.

The same inspection produced something worse for the wider programme, and
I nearly did not follow it through because it was not what I was looking
for. eps_mem is a relaxation ratio. 2496 has no relaxation time. So the
bridge the inertia arc has been assuming between the SF-6 pin and dark
matter clause 2 runs through a model that contains neither of the two
quantities it is supposed to connect. That is registered as a gap in both
places. It does not change the 1B ledger but it removes a support the arc
had been leaning on without anyone checking whether it was load-bearing.

On the over-determination protocol: the founder's triangulation plan is
legitimate and it is how Avogadro's number was settled, but the condition
that makes it legitimate has to be written down BEFORE the cross-checks
are run, or it decays into fitting one parameter per phenomenon and
calling the agreement a convergence. So xi_arc is pinned once by Route 1
and must predict the rest. If I register that afterwards it is worthless.

I updated frontier_sectors/FP.md because it was stale in the direction of
overclaiming -- it still read "Newton I emergent with finite-mobility
drain" a week after the panel demoted the result and five days after 2875
qualified the coast. Nobody would have caught that from the CONJ entry
alone. The original text is retained beneath the correction rather than
overwritten, because a frontier entry that quietly changes its own history
is worse than one that is wrong.

What I am least sure of: whether CONJ-FP-1 belongs in FP at all, given the
mechanism is about the Sea and DPS exists. I put it in FP because the
inertia item already lives there and traceability beats taxonomy, and
cross-referenced DPS. If a later session wants it moved, the reasoning is
here.
