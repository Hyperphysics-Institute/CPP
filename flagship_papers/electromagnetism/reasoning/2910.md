Four rounds in, the first banded verdict — and it is worth recording
what changed, because it was not the physics. The physics has been
whispering the same thing since round 1: forward. What changed is that
the question was finally cut to the size of the instrument. Three
rounds of curvature bands produced three inconclusives because the
bands asked for a precision four orders beyond reach; one round of
sign-only bands produced 5.7 sigma in an afternoon of legs. Scope
discipline is not a virtue on top of the science; here it WAS the
science.

The result itself: seventeen of eighteen differentials positive, every
speed's mean positive, the pooled mean five and a half standard errors
from zero, and the floor - six mobile legs at rest - sitting quietly at
half a sigma, certifying that the chaos does not fake a signal. And the
gift I did not bandage into the bands: the response climbs linearly
with speed across a factor of four, which is what a drive should do,
and which retroactively indicts the systematics of rounds 1-2 for every
non-monotone shape they showed. I am keeping that in the unbanded
section on purpose; linear-looking at twenty percent errors is not a
curvature statement, and the renunciation stays renounced.

What this means where the arc lives: the founder's mechanism needed two
things from the substrate - a relay that is not Lienard-Wiechert, and a
Sea whose net answer to motion is a push, not a drag. It now has both,
one provisionally. The chaotic Sea, which two patches ago looked like
the enemy of every measurement, coheres into a forward response the
moment you ask it a first-order question. The temptation to write more
than that sentence is large; the promotion variation is frozen in the
record, including the clause under which HOLDS is revoked, and until it
runs, provisional means provisional.

Expectation bookkeeping, sixth declaration: confirmed this time, which
per my own rule makes it weaker evidence than a surprise would have
been. The declared caveat - that the expectation rested solely on the
2900 steady-state argument - is now partially discharged for the SIGN;
it remains fully in force for cancellation of curvature, where nothing
has yet been measured at all.
