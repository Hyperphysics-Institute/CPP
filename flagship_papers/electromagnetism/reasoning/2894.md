# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2894 (obliquity tested; branch identified)

**STATUS: contemporaneous.**

Two things resolved and one prediction of mine went the wrong way.

First, the fork I raised at 2893 was my own doing. I imposed SSV_net
VECTOR conservation at 2892 and then discovered it conflicted with clean
forward propagation. The founder specifies DI-BIT conservation, which is
scalar, and scalar conservation holds for ANY dipole coefficient because
the FCC direction sum vanishes. Measured: bit total 1.00000 for all three
obliquities. So scalar conservation constrains the obliquity not at all
and there was never a fork in the specification -- only in my version of
it. Withdrawn.

Second, I predicted Kirchhoff would beat the 2892 rule for shell
formation, because (1+cos)/2 is the textbook Huygens obliquity and
vanishes exactly backward. It loses badly: p = 0.43 against 0.67, shell
fraction 0.002 against 0.145. The reason is that vanishing backward is
weaker than going NEGATIVE backward. The 2892 rule actively cancels; the
Kirchhoff rule merely declines to add. The founder's phrase was
"subtraction and addition" and the subtraction is what matters. I had the
textbook and he had the physics.

Third and decisive: even the best re-radiation rule leaves 86% of the
amplitude off the shell, while the directed relay -- which re-radiates
nothing and simply lets each channel continue -- gave p = 1.0000 exactly
at 2889. The pattern across four rules is monotone in how much angular
information the update discards. That is not a tuning problem. ANY closure
to a low-order moment loses ballisticity, because the full angular
distribution IS the thing that makes propagation ballistic.

So the sub-Moment re-radiation proposal cannot build the shell broadcast,
and I am saying so under the licence the founder gave in the same message:
if the phenomena do not fall out, the rule should change. It would have
been easy to report 0.674 as encouraging progress toward 1.0 and propose
more obliquity tuning. The monotone pattern says tuning will not close a
gap of that shape.

The good part is that the founder had already written the answer in a
parenthesis he flagged as an ontology problem: the DI-bit is a conscious
point MOVING between GPs, communicating at each hop. That is a travelling
conserved entity, not a re-computed amplitude, and it gives ballistic
propagation, a real shell, 1/r^2 from conservation plus spherical
spreading -- which is exactly c05's geometric dilution -- and retardation
by construction. His aside was the resolution and the body of his message
was the branch that fails.

His sub-Moment counting question also answers itself under that branch: a
ballistic bit reaches the PSR shell in PSR/l_P hops, which is his own
"maximum possible steps" suggestion, with no free parameter. Under the
re-radiation branch the count stays ambiguous because a sqrt(N) front
never cleanly arrives.

I did not verify Branch 1's 1/r^2 numerically here. It follows from
conservation and area by construction, but "follows by construction" is
what I said about several things this session that then failed on
measurement. Registered as owed rather than claimed.
