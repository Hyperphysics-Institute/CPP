# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2926 (c = 1/5 derived; 7/240 dead; one band failed as pre-registered)

**STATUS: contemporaneous. Record at
`sketches/c_one_fifth_analytic_derivation.md`; script at
`code/2926_c_one_fifth_derivation.py`.**

First patch of a new context window, inheriting the 2917–2924 handover
whose §1 pointer was unambiguous. The checks were pre-registered in chat
before any algebra ran, including the failure modes — that ordering
mattered twice within the hour (below).

The derivation itself took one symbolic step to crack open: I fed the
outgoing-leg discriminant to sympy expecting a mess to expand in β, and
it factored to r²(1+βμ)². Everything downstream is bookkeeping — d_out
closes, the radial dependence factors out exactly, and the angular
integral is a first-year substitution. Two things about that are worth
saying honestly. First, the perfect square is not luck; it is the
statement that the retarded-position construction with a common signal
speed has an exact conformal-like structure, and someone should ask
whether it survives the entrained geometry (I did not pursue this — it
is a fresh question, not this patch's). Second, the factorization
*derives* the m- and r-range-invariance that 2900 measured. I find that
more convincing than the 1/5 itself: an invariance observed across six
variations is now a two-line consequence of the kernel's form. The
"⟨cos⁴θ⟩ = 1/5" story queued at 2900 turned out true but incomplete —
the real structure is that every coefficient is a second difference of
even sphere moments, with 1/5 the n=1 instance.

The surprise was c₄. The series says 1/35 = 0.02857, and the measured
invariant was 0.02916 ≈ 7/240 = 0.02917. For a few minutes those looked
like a contradiction that would kill the closed form. The resolution —
the [1, β², β⁴] truncation of a series whose β⁶ term is 1/105 biases the
fitted c₄ upward by exactly the observed amount — was checkable, and
Check 2's c₄ band confirmed it to 3e-5. The 2900 worker's refusal to
claim 7/240 ("a number landing near an expected value gets accepted" —
the failure mode named four times in that handover) is what made this
clean: nothing had to be retracted, only a candidate declined.

Then my own band failed. I pre-registered |c_fit − 0.200008| < 2e-6 and
got a 1.5e-5 gap. The temptation at that moment is to call 1.5e-5
"obviously quadrature" and move on — it is small, the main result is
pretty, and no reviewer would blink. That is precisely the move the arc
keeps catching in others. So the follow-up was pre-registered with a
kill condition before running: if the numeric fit under refinement
converges anywhere but the closed-form fit value, the closed form is
missing something real. It converged to 0.199993 monotonically and hit
it exactly at 3840×5760, and the coarsest grid reproduced 2900's
0.200008 to 1e-6. The honest lesson recorded: I set a 2e-6 band on
numbers that carried ~1e-5 of angular quadrature noise — the band was
wrong, not the physics, but the way to know which is to let a
pre-registered follow-up decide, not to eyeball it. The Check-1
diagnostic that the discretization error is β-independent and equals
the radial-sum error to four digits is the closing of that loop: it
explains *why* 2900 could measure c to 4e-5 through 1% absolute error,
which had quietly bothered me from the start.

obstruction is now exact, which makes it *harder* to wave away, not
easier — the entrainment mechanism must now cancel a known function,
and the one-shot result (one dial kills β² only, flips β⁴'s sign) can
be re-derived against exact targets. That re-examination is cheap and
is my suggested next item, but I am not queueing it over the standing
secondary queue (ANTISCREEN-1, LINK 2, K1-MEMORY-1) without the next
