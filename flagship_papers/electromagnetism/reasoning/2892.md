# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2892 (coherent re-radiation)

**STATUS: contemporaneous. Derivation and tables in
`founders_voice/founder_ruling_reradiation_coherent_2026-08-02.md`.**

The founder answered the scattering question and the answer inverted my
model. I had built a family parameterised by how OFTEN bits scatter. He
says scattering is not particulate at all -- every CP re-radiates every
Moment, unconditionally -- and the thing that matters is that the
superposition is signed and conserves SSV_net. So the free parameter I
introduced was never the physical one. The physical distinction is
coherent versus incoherent re-emission, not frequent versus rare.

That reframing is Huygens, and I should have reached it myself: every
point of a wavefront re-radiates and the front stays sharp anyway. I had
been treating "re-radiates at every point" as synonymous with "randomises
at every point", which is only true if you throw away the phase. The
founder's "subtraction and addition" is the phase.

The rule falls out rather than being chosen, which is the part I trust
most. Demanding that the outgoing distribution conserve both the scalar
total and the vector net fixes the coefficient uniquely at 1/4 given the
FCC sums, and the backward channel then goes negative for a directed
pulse. That negative weight IS the founder's subtraction, and it is the
mechanism by which a wave differs from a random walk. Nothing was tuned.

Measured, coherence roughly doubles the transport range and lifts the bulk
exponent from 0.37 to 0.65. Real work, but not ballistic. The diagnosis is
that keeping only the 0th and 1st angular moments is the P1 closure of
radiative transfer, whose continuum limit is the telegraph equation --
wave at short times, diffusion at long. So residual diffusion is intrinsic
to a net-vector-only rule. Whether that is right depends on a physics
question I have put back to him in physical terms: does a CP's emission
pattern depend on the full angular pattern of what arrived, or only on the
net.

The statics work is the failure of this patch and I want it recorded as
such rather than softened. I ran a convergence study and both rules drift
toward a FLAT profile. The neutralising background accumulates as a
uniform component, |Q| becomes dominated by it, and the 1/r structure
becomes a ripple on a constant -- so the fitted slope tends to zero and
longer runs make it worse. That is the SAME contamination I diagnosed at
2889 for the LW observable, three patches ago, and I did not carry it
across. At 2891 I fixed the estimator and reported the result as though
the measurement were then sound; it was not, because a second independent
defect was sitting underneath. Fixing one and declaring victory is exactly
the error.

So every statics number in this arc is suspended, including the ones I
produced in this patch. What I can defend is the dynamics: the light cone
is invariant across every rule tested (third confirmation, no fitting
anywhere), and the coherent rule transports further than the incoherent
one under identical conditions.

The stability result was not anticipated and I nearly did not look at it.
max|F| decays under the coherent rule and grows under the incoherent one,
still rising at T=1600. A rule that does not reach a bounded steady state
is a worse candidate for a substrate than one that does, and that is a
point in favour of the founder's specification arrived at from a direction
I was not aiming.
