# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2886 (moving-source LW test)

**STATUS: contemporaneous.**

I wrote the prereg expecting non-LW, said so in the prereg, ran the test,
and got twelve negative numbers with none within 2x of the LW band. That
is the answer I predicted and the one the conjecture needs. The frozen
linearity clause says the spread must be under 30% of the mean and it came
in at 67.5%, so the verdict is inconclusive.

I want to record that I considered arguing around it, because the
temptation was concrete rather than abstract. The specific argument
available was: the LW band is a separate declared criterion, it fails
decisively on its own terms, so I could report "LW excluded, retardation
unquantified" and keep most of what I wanted. That is re-banding with
extra steps. The linearity clause says INCONCLUSIVE REGARDLESS OF THE
VALUE OF A, and I wrote those words two commits ago specifically so that
this move would be unavailable. It would be worthless to have written them
and then find a reading that lets me past.

The diagnosis is the useful part and it took looking at the columns rather
than the summary statistic. The r=4 column is stable to 6% across a 4x
range in beta -- that is a clean measurement. The scatter is entirely in
r=6 and r=8. So the instability is radius-driven, not beta-driven, which
points at geometry rather than at physics. And the worst cell is beta=0.40
at r=8, which is exactly where periodic wrap would bite hardest: at that
speed the source covers about 65 units in 40 Moments on a 48-box, so it
laps itself, and an r=8 test point is then sampling images of the source
and of its own wake. That is a setup defect I built, not a property of the
relay.

The honest read is that the test is probably measuring the right thing at
small radius and garbage at large radius, and that the fix is boring:
bigger box, no wrap, interpolate the field instead of rounding to a site,
stop pooling fore and aft placements which have different systematics
under retardation. None of that requires new physics or a band change.

One thing I did right and want to keep doing: measuring c_lat from the
kernel rather than assuming it equals R. I had guessed R*sqrt2 = 5.66 from
the FCC neighbour geometry; the actual mean front radius is 4.038. Had I
assumed, every beta would have been wrong by 40% and the whole sweep would
have been mislabelled, which would have looked like a beta-dependence and
might have been diagnosed as physics.

Condition B stays open. The 2884 escalation -- that an LW-like relay would
make coasting impossible outright -- stays live. I did not get to reduce
the open-item count today and the record should say so plainly.
