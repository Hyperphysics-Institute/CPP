# REASONING FRAGMENT — PATCH 2922 (verbatim worker reasoning, Tier 4)

1. The clean instrument vindicated itself immediately: k_tot moved
   from +0.0176 (defective) to +0.0120 (clean), landing inside the
   register band measured three independent ways. That single number
   is the strongest evidence the parity fix was a repair and not a
   steer -- it moved the answer TOWARD the independent measurements.
2. c_tot = 16 +/- 3.4 with a passing gate and a parity-exact
   evaluator is no longer an artifact story. But sigma <= 0.05 at
   c ~ 16 demands 0.3% relative precision -- the bar was written for
   a c near 0.2 and cannot be met at 80x that value by any ensemble
   size. Declaring INCONCLUSIVE while pretending seeds {10-15} could
   fix it would be procedure worship (the 2917 s5 precedent); the
   2921 s4 commitment exists precisely so this outcome converts into
   a shape question, not a rhetorical one.
3. The turnover at beta ~ 0.15 is the arc's most consequential curve
   to date IF real: past it, the Sea's forward answer to motion dies
   and reverses -- the B1 obstruction materializing as drag exactly
   where the founder's constant-SSV_net requirement needs sustained
   drive. Before believing it I went looking for the one dataset
   that could contradict it for free: the direct sign-round drives.
   They agree below beta = 0.1 and disagree at 0.2 -- 2 sigma, not
   decisive, and the honest reading is that the two instruments
   disagree about the high-beta regime specifically.
4. The discriminator design followed the 2916 lesson before anything
   else: feasibility arithmetic first. beta = 0.25 needs T <= 50 and
   beta = 0.30 needs T <= 33 to keep the transit inside the Sea with
   a >= 3.75 buffer -- literals, multiplied out, in the prereg.
5. What I did NOT do: extend the hybrid pipeline to beta > 0.2 by
   extrapolating patterns beyond the measured windows. The
   discriminator uses the DIRECT differential method (2907-class
   legs) -- the instrument whose high-beta claim is being tested is
   not allowed to referee the test.
