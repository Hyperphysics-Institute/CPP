# REASONING FRAGMENT — PATCH 2919 (verbatim worker reasoning, Tier 4)

Design choices and why, in the order made:

1. p0 free in the joint fit rather than imposed from the control:
   imposing it would launder the control's noise into every channel
   invisibly; fitting it jointly and CHECKING it against the control
   (s5.ii) keeps the two measurements independent and the agreement
   falsifiable. The control's job in the fit is design leverage: two
   beta=0 points at opposite window extremes.
2. tau and p_tr are degenerate as a product, so the regressor is
   p_tr/T with tau absorbed -- the model stays linear, the bootstrap
   stays honest.
3. Conditioning computed DATA-BLIND before commit (14.3, smallest
   singular value 0.13): the round-1/round-2 failure sequence taught
   that conditioning discovered after the fit becomes an excuse; here
   it is a design fact frozen beforehand.
4. The transient is excluded from the drives because it is the one
   component with a known non-substrate origin (radial-dipole
   initialization). Including it would let an artifact vote on
   Newton I.
5. Bands: the old c_hyb bands are retired, not reinterpreted -- they
   were defined over a basis that measured a different quantity.
   The new bands separate the two physical questions the arc actually
   has: does the static-Sea mechanism carry its exact c0 = 1/5
   (2900), and does the TOTAL drive carry curvature that obstructs
   Newton I (B1). A REFUTED band is included so the static-Sea
   target is genuinely falsifiable, with a deliberate no-mans-land
   (0.05..0.15) where only INTERMEDIATE can be claimed.
6. D0(0) = 0 by mirror symmetry is asserted numerically rather than
   trusted: the failure-mode ledger says expected values get
   interrogated, even the exact ones.
