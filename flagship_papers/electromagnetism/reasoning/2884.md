# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2884 (round-trip mechanism)

**STATUS: contemporaneous. Derivation lives at
`sketches/tier4_derivation_record_inertia_arc.md` §7 per CONV-010 — this
fragment carries judgment, not the algebra, and points at it.**

The founder proposed that the round-trip timing asymmetry replaces every
other mechanism rather than joining them, and I think he is right, for a
reason worth stating plainly: it is the only one of the three that is pure
kinematics. It follows from the CP moving during a finite-speed
out-and-back. It assumes nothing about what an arc IS -- no collapse
asymmetry, no store-and-release lifecycle, no arc-to-spacing ratio, no Sea
density. So it eliminates xi_arc outright, and with it the dependence on
OPEN-SEA-DENSITY-1 that was the whole reason CONJ-FP-1 was filed as a
conjecture rather than pressed further.

That is the second time in this arc the founder has resisted a move that
would have made his own conjecture harder to falsify. He declared it a
conjecture unprompted when it bottomed out, and here he refused to let
three mechanisms accumulate when I flagged proliferation as a risk. Worth
recording because the pattern runs against the usual direction.

The computation is the deliverable and it returned a mixed answer that I
want to be careful not to round off in either direction.

The kinematics is confirmed exactly -- drive linear in beta to four
significant figures. That is the founder's claim and it holds.

The sign is not settled by the geometry. It is the inverse of the response
sign, and nothing in the integration can change that: forward DPs were
further away at emission so their amplitude is smaller, rear DPs were
closer so theirs is larger, and whether "larger from the rear" pushes or
pulls is entirely down to whether the Sea's response is repulsive or
attractive. The founder has said repulsive since the beginning. I have
not verified it and I am not treating his consistency as evidence.

The Lienard-Wiechert null is the part I did not expect and it is the most
important thing in the patch. Under LW propagation the drive is exactly
zero at every beta -- not small, zero to 1e-17. And that is not a defect
of the model, it is the model reproducing a known result: a uniformly
moving charge in a wave-equation field has no self-drag, because the LW
field points at the instantaneous position and the retardation is
compensated by the field direction. Recovering that null is what makes me
trust the case-A number. A machine that manufactured a spurious drive
there would be worthless everywhere else.

Then the consequence that I nearly filed as a footnote and should not
have. If the relay IS LW-like, the Sea exerts no net drive on a coasting
CP, so SSV_net = 0, so the primitive gives d = 0, so NOTHING CAN COAST.
That is not a constraint on the inertia mechanism. It is a constraint on
the substrate. The programme needs a non-LW relay for uniform motion to
exist at all, whether or not the founder's account of inertia is right. I
escalated it in the conjecture, the registry and the derivation record
because a reader who meets it only as "condition B of CONJ-FP-1" will
underrate it.

The uncomfortable adjacent fact: AUTOMATON measured the STATIC Coulomb
profile to 0.4% under two independent relays, and everyone including me
has been treating that as though the relay's dynamics were thereby
established. It is not. Static Coulomb does not imply LW. Whether a moving
source produces a field pointing at its instantaneous or its retarded
position has never been run, and it is the same shape of gap as the
untested magnetic sector -- a derived-not-measured leg that the programme
has been leaning on. That is now the new Route 1 and it is cheap: an
existing committed engine, no new physics.

On filenames, which is not a trivial point after this session. I made the
verbatim capture a NEW file rather than appending to the 1 August one,
because the mechanism it records supersedes that file's contents and
appending would have left its name misdescribing it -- the exact defect I
criticised in the retardation file two patches ago. But I did NOT rename
the CONJ-FP-1 sketch, even though its name now misdescribes it too,
because that path is the registered pointer in CONJ.md and renaming breaks
the registry. Banner instead. Both calls are stated in the files so a
later reader does not have to guess which principle won and why.
