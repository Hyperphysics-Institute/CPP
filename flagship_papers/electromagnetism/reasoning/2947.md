**Design decisions and why.**

1. **Scoping, not prereg.** The 2936 language calls for a scoping patch; the qstiff scoping
   (`qstiff_contact_polarizability_scoping.md`) is the format precedent: pose the calculation
   precisely, separate pinnable from framework-blocked, lay out the multi-session attack, mint
   nothing. This document follows that shape one lane over.

2. **CONV-011 handling.** A pure scoping document freezes no verdict bands and runs no
   computation, so a timing freeze here would be vacuous — but the handover flagged "including
   this one if it freezes any verdict bands." Resolution: the scoping freezes the arc's DEFAULT
   branch classifications (§7) — which outcomes are falsifier-class (pre-action review),
   roadblock-class, and conservative-class — while requiring each stage prereg to confirm or
   re-freeze before its own computation. This gives CONV-011 maximal prospective bite without
   pretending the scoping itself is a computation prereg.

3. **The falsifier-class list.** Three entries: (a) Stage C contradicting CASE-Q — the corpus
   would be refuting itself, highest-stakes outcome; (b) Stage C reproducing the toy-model
   absolute-frame c_eff magnitude — re-arms the Michelson–Morley falsifier that this arc exists
   to defuse; (c) Stage B showing Z₀ density-dependence does NOT cancel — kills the conjecture
   and the confinement route, structurally the arc's CASE-L analog. The Stage A
   substrate-thermodynamic HALT is classified roadblock-class, not falsifier-class, because it
   blocks rather than refutes (same status the wall has at OPEN-FP-SF-2-η).

4. **Ledger discipline on n_DP.** The DM ledger's "no VALUE of η, d_DP, or n_DP anywhere" is a
   live corpus invariant. Stage B necessarily introduces n_DP as a variable; the scoping makes
   formula-only status binding and routes any future n_DP determination to its own DM-lane prereg
   (no-motive rule, same split applied to ξ₂ at 2946). This keeps the FP-6 arc from becoming a
   backdoor mint of a scarce campaign quantity.

5. **ξ₂ interface.** The finite-k expansion of Stage B's lattice polarization law is the obvious
   producer of ξ₂, but the 2946 registration deliberately made ξ₂ its own arc. The scoping
   registers cross-citation (each arc cites the other's frozen structure) rather than merging —
   preserving the no-motive separation while preventing duplicate derivation.

6. **Registry touch.** One UPDATED block appended to OPEN-FP-6-CONSTANTS in
   `frontier_sectors/FP.md` recording the 2936 fold + work-plan adoption and pointing to the
   scoping doc. Status stays OPEN; no other registry files touched (no theorem, no prediction,
   no axiom change; the SSV-independent-Z₀ theorem registration is explicitly a Stage C decision).

7. **No verify script.** No computation was performed; per the reasoning-capture protocol the
   script requirement attaches only to computation patches.
