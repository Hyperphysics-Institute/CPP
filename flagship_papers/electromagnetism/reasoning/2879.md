# Reasoning fragment — Patch 2879 (SF-6 pin adjudication addendum)

**STATUS: BACKFILLED at Patch 2881. NOT contemporaneous — written after

2879 recorded S4's late return and re-scored the SF-6 pin round on a
complete roster. The judgment worth recording is the A2 dissent.

S4 argued that F = kappa a stands as a substrate-mechanism result because
kappa is statics-pinned and therefore uncontaminated by the correspondence
question. I ruled the dissent fails as stated, and the reason is that the
inference does not follow: F = kappa a is a statement about F, and a clean
coefficient on the right does not supply a CPP referent for the object on
the left. S4 was running A2 and A3 together -- that kappa is uncontaminated
was already ruled, as A3(i), and is not additionally an answer to A2.

But I adopted S4's narrowing, and I think that was the right call rather
than a consolation prize. S4 correctly located WHERE the doubt lives: in
the identification of F, not in the coefficient and not in the mechanism's
general shape. My own 2876 phrasing -- "demoted to a scalar-toy analogue"
-- is accurate but reads as though the whole construction were provisional,
when in fact one specific bridge is missing and everything on the statics
side of it is sound. So OPEN-FSELF-CORRESPONDENCE-1 was scoped to one
question and explicitly relieved of any obligation to re-derive kappa.
That made the item materially smaller, and the credit is S4's.

The other thing 2879 settled is that CONV-008 worked on first use. The
duplicated return did not get scored as consensus, and when the true fifth
return arrived the reduced-roster adjudication needed NO reversal. That is
the outcome a procedural gate is supposed to produce and it is worth
noting, because the previous three occurrences were handled by per-round
flagging and the failure recurred every time.

Why this fragment was missing: 2879 was an addendum patch, and I treated
addenda as bookkeeping rather than as substantive. It contained an adopted
dissent and a re-scoping of an open item -- neither is bookkeeping. The
same misclassification is what caused me to count duplicate returns without
checking: work that FEELS like clerical work gets clerical attention, and
the reasoning-capture rider does not exempt addenda anywhere.
