# Philosophy — SF-6 (v1.0)

**Type classification:** cross-domain synthesis / ontological-reduction paper. NOT a derivation paper.

## The conceptual content
SF-6's deepest claim is ontological before it is algebraic: there is no mass/radiation dichotomy at the substrate level, only a boundary condition. Mass is a standing ZDC pattern, the photon a traveling one; E=mc² and E=hν are the same relation E=ℏν_C. What conventional physics records as three separately-discovered pillars (Maxwell EM, special relativity, QED) is, in CPP, one polarizable dipole sea seen in three limits.

## The honest assessment (what the review cycle sharpened)
The headline E=ℏν_C unification is a **substrate-level identification**, not a new mathematical derivation. The ν_C assignments (mc²/ℏ standing, ν traveling) match the standard Compton/Planck–Einstein relations and the relativistic dispersion is imported from SR-1. The *value* is the ontological reduction itself — collapsing mass and light to one substrate object — not novel predictive math. The v0.2 draft's "derived… most rigorous result" language overclaimed this; the 4-reviewer panel (3/4) flagged it and v0.3 relabeled it. This is the paper's defining honesty move.

## The weakest link (reviewer-surfaced)
Two: (1) the quantitative EM constants μ0,ε0,c,γ(v) rest on a parameter-tuned toy model (OPEN-FP-6-CONSTANTS) — a lower rigor floor than SF-1/3/5; (2) the toy model predicts an absolute-frame c_eff(v) in genuine tension with the Michelson–Morley null — carried as a named flagship-level open falsifier, not glossed and not resolved. The breadth of SF-6 is bought honestly precisely by labeling both gaps loudly rather than hiding them.

## Why ship a paper with two opens + a foundational tension
Because the conceptual unification (Tier 1) is clean and broad-reaching, and the two-tier accounting keeps every claim's rigor floor explicit. The synthesis is informationally complementary to its sources, not redundant: no single source paper carries the cross-domain narrative that mass, light, and the three pillars are one substrate mechanism.
