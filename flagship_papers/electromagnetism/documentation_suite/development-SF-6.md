# Development — SF-6 (v1.0)

How the electromagnetism flagship came to be.

## Timeline
- **Pre-survey (Patches 1305, 1310):** the SF-6 outline + structural core were staged from inside the SF-7 window. The core flagged the load-bearing honesty finding — two-tier rigor (Tier-1 unification clean; Tier-2 EM constants toy-model) — and the Michelson–Morley tension.
- **1600 (v0.1):** first .tex assembly to the 16-section apex standard, mirroring SF-3. Synthesis only; the spine is c06 + the DP-Sea-Polarization model, NOT the EW-3/EW-5 the README originally (wrongly) cited.
- **1601:** README source-map correction (EW-3/EW-5 → c06 + DP-Sea).
- **1602 (v0.2):** OPEN-FP-6-EMHAND re-scope — a registry grep revealed substrate manifestation (iii) is already closed (THEO-SD-CHIR-2 / THEO-CHIR-CONT-3), so SF-6 owes only the EM-phenomenology *expression*. TP-1 consistency thread added (TP-1 had shipped after the 1310 core).
- **1603–1607:** review cycle. 4-reviewer panel (single-document dispatch). 

## The key decision
At v0.2 the unification was framed as "derived… no new postulates… the most rigorous result." Three of four reviewers (Gemini, Copilot, ChatGPT) said this overclaims — E=ℏν_C is a definitional/ontological *identification*, not a derivation. Rather than defend on the 3-SHIP majority, the call (1606, v0.3) was to fix the text: relabel as a substrate-level identification / ontological reduction. ChatGPT's v0.3 confirm pass cleared it; v1.0 shipped (1607) on unanimous advance.

## Process learning
The review protocol earned its keep: cycle 1 caught a real overclaim, the restate fixed it, cycle 2 confirmed. The "verdict comes from the text" discipline decided the disposition over the raw vote tally. Also surfaced and fixed mid-cycle: the per-reviewer dispatch wrappers were retired in favor of a single shared document (review_dispatch_protocol Patch 1604).
