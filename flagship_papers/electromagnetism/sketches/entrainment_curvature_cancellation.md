## §3 — WHAT THIS MEANS, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

**It means the three-way conflict is no longer rigid.** The 2884 static
Sea is not merely a simplification — the sign and size of the curvature
depend on the Sea's response, and a physically modest entrainment
(displacements ~6% of the response amplitude) is enough to reach exact
linearity at β²-order. The previous worker's c = 0.201 is a property of
the *undressed* model, exactly as the 2898 caution anticipated — but this
was established by pre-registered test, not by re-description.

**It does NOT mean Newton I is recovered.** Three gaps, in order:

1. **ε is a dial here.** The physical entrainment is not free — the DP's
   displacement per Moment is set by the same primitive
   d = (|SSV|/SSV_abs)·PSR that everything else obeys. The next
   computation must *derive* the substrate's actual ε from the interaction
   law and check whether it lands on ε\*. If the substrate's own ε ≠ ε\*,
   the curvature survives at reduced size. (Note the structural rhyme with
   LINK 2: C·PSR = SSV_abs may be forced by PSR's definition; ε = ε\* may
   likewise be forced by full self-consistency. Neither is established.)
2. **One-shot is not self-consistent.** The displaced DPs alter the field
   that displaces them. The correct object is the *fixed point* of the
   coupled CP+Sea dynamics — the travelling steady state, time-independent
   in the co-moving frame. The founder's constant-SSV_net picture is
   precisely the claim that this steady state exists at every v and
   regenerates itself each Moment (aft discharge re-supplying fore
   charge). **In the steady-state formulation, Newton I is the statement
   that a one-parameter family of travelling solutions exists — not that
   the bare kernel is linear.** B1's analysis computed D on the wrong
   background: an eternally-undisturbed Sea is not a state the substrate
   can ever be in around a moving CP.
3. **The β⁴ residual.** If full self-consistency cancels curvature order
   by order (as the steady-state argument suggests it should — transients
   carry the curvature, and the steady state has none), c₄ → 0 too. If it
   cancels β² only, the coasting family decays on a β⁻⁴ timescale and the
   conflict survives in weakened form. This is the discriminating
   observable for the fixed-point computation, and it is registered now,
   before that computation is written.
