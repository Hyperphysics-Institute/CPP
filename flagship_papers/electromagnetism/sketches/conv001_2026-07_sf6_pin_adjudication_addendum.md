### Why the dissent does not carry as stated

**It asserts precisely what S5's argument denies, and does not engage it.**
S5, adopted at 2876 §4: κ is statics-pinned and survives, **but the
dynamical reading F = κa depends on what F represents**, so if F_self maps
to nothing in CPP the law does not travel even though the number does.

The inference S4 makes —

> κ is uncontaminated ⟹ F = κa stands as a CPP substrate-mechanism result

— **does not follow.** F = κa is a statement about **F**. A clean
coefficient on the right does not supply a CPP referent for the object on
the left. That κ is uncontaminated was already ruled and is A3(i); it is
not additionally an answer to A2. **The two halves of A3 and A2 are being
run together, which is the same conflation 2876 §5 had to separate.**

And the founder's ruling is stronger than S4's reading allows: **there is
no self-force in CPP at all.** A mechanism whose engine is a self-force
measured on the source is therefore not self-evidently the CPP mechanism;
SSV_net is others' responses re-entering at the CP's location, and whether
that route yields the same κ is exactly what is unestablished.

### What S4 is nonetheless right about, and it is worth having

**S4 correctly locates the doubt.** What is open is the **identification
of F**, not the coefficient, and not the mechanism's general shape.
2876's phrase *"demoted to a scalar-toy analogue"* is accurate but
under-informative: read carelessly it suggests the whole construction is
provisional, when in fact one specific bridge is missing and everything on
the statics side of it is sound.

**The correspondence item does NOT require re-deriving κ, and does not
