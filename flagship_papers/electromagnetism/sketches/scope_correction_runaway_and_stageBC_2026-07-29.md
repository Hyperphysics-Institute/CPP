# SCOPE CORRECTION — WHAT 2868 MEASURED, AND WHAT IT DID NOT

**Patch 2869, 29 July 2026. The founder asks whether the worker is
claiming his mechanism self-accelerates. It is not. The claim is
narrowed here, one of the founder's arguments is answered, his proposed
derivation is evaluated, and a discrepancy INTERNAL to 2496 is
surfaced.**

---

## §1 — Direct answer: no

**2868 measured a forward self-force ∝ v on a bare point under
PRESCRIBED motion.** 2496's Stage B ramp-and-hold *imposes* v(t); the
source does not move under its own primitive. So the measurement shows
a forward self-force **exists** at constant v. **It does not show that
anything runs away**, because nothing in that run was free to.

**"Self-accelerates" was the worker's inference, not the worker's
measurement, and it was stated more strongly than the evidence
supports.** Corrected here.

## §2 — But the conservation argument does not settle it either

> *"Acceleration would not be possible in this mechanism because it is
> conservative. The amount of DP-arc charging is equal to the amount of
> discharging."*

**Energy conservation does not preclude acceleration.** A conservative
system accelerates all the time — it converts stored potential into
kinetic. What conservation forbids is *creating* energy, not
*redistributing* it.

**The Abraham–Lorentz runaway is itself conservative** and still runs
away: it draws on the source's own field self-energy, which for a bare
point is formally divergent. That is precisely why 2496 §7(a) reads the
runaway as evidence that *"a bare, undressed CP is not a stable
asymptotic object of the substrate."*

So charging = discharging is necessary and not sufficient. **The
question is not whether energy is conserved but whether the reservoir
it is drawn from is bounded** — and for a bare point it is not.

**For a closed, finite, self-bound structure the founder's balance
argument is very likely correct**, and that is 2496's own reading:
physical particles are dressed standing patterns. The disagreement
between us is narrower than it looked — it is about the bare point
only.

## §3 — The proposed derivation, evaluated

> *"the derivation could be done by showing that the energy/SSV
> increment of the DP-arc-charged volume, in the increment of distance
> advanced each moment, equals the SSV_net."*

**This derivation is worth doing, and the worker's expectation is that
it will yield a consistency condition rather than a velocity.**

Per Moment the CP advances δ = v·Δt. The transferred front→rear volume
is ∝ δ ∝ v; the arc amplitude in it is set by the polarization, not by
v. So the transferred organization per Moment is **∝ v**, giving

> **SSV_net = α·v**

Feed that back through the primitive v = μ·SSV_net:

> **v = μαv ⇒ v(1 − μα) = 0**

**Every v is a solution if μα = 1; no v is a solution otherwise.** The
mechanism is **scale-free in v** — it cannot determine a velocity, only
sustain whichever one it is given, and only on the marginal line.

**That is the knife-edge, restated exactly.** And this is a reason to
do the derivation, not a reason to skip it: **it converts "is there a
knife-edge?" into "what is μα?", which is a computable number.** The
2868 measurement already supplies α in model units (F_hold/v ≈
9.4 × 10⁻⁴, positive, constant to 0.6% over 4× in v). **The linearity
the founder's mechanism predicts is confirmed. What remains is the
coefficient.**

## §4 — NEW: 2496's Stage B and Stage C appear to disagree

Surfaced by 2868 and **not previously noticed, because the Stage B
residual was written off as an integrator artifact:**

| stage | condition | result |
|---|---|---|
| **B (hold)** | prescribed constant v | self-force **FORWARD**, ∝ v (F_hold/v ≈ +9.4e-4) |
| **C (coast)** | free evolution at mobility μ | momentum **DECAYS**, v ≈ v₀e^(−t/κμ); τ = 7.87 at μ=10, 16.25 at μ=25 |

**A forward self-force and an exponential momentum decay are opposite
signs for what should be the same physics.** Under free evolution the
primitive v = μF with a forward F ∝ αv gives marginality, not decay.

**The worker does not know how to reconcile these and is not
guessing.** Candidate resolutions — the hold-phase force being an
incomplete-equilibration transient despite the t > Tr+10 window; the
Stage C drain being a field-momentum bookkeeping effect distinct from
the self-force; a sign or frame convention differing between the two
measurements — are listed as candidates only.

**Specified test, not run:** during the Stage C coast, record the
instantaneous self-force alongside v and check whether its sign matches
Stage B's. If forward, Stage C's decay has a source other than the
self-force and the drain interpretation needs revisiting. If rearward,
the sign flips between prescribed and free motion and **that flip is
the physics**.

**This is the second load-bearing claim in the shipped inertia pin to
come apart under three parameter values.** It goes to the panel with
2868.

## §5 — Where this leaves the founder's mechanism

**Confirmed in-model:** the sign (forward), and the linearity in v —
both predictions of the transit-asymmetry account, both measured.

**Not established:** that μα = 1, i.e. that the mechanism sits on the
marginal line rather than above or below it. **The founder postulates
this via exact recovery; it has not been derived, and §3 shows the
proposed derivation will produce the condition rather than satisfy it.**

**Not in dispute:** that a closed self-bound structure could force the
condition. Laue's theorem gives coefficient exactly 1 for the SF-1
cage, and the cage simulation under the 2496 protocol remains the
registered next step.

**Are we finished with this?** Not quite, and the remaining piece is
small and concrete: **compute μα.** The founder's proposed derivation
gives α analytically; 2496 gives μ. **That is a well-posed calculation
with a number at the end, and it decides between marginal, runaway, and
drag.** It is the first time in this thread that the open question has
been that sharp.
