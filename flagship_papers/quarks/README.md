## Scope

The complete quark mass spectrum ($m_u, m_d, m_s, m_c, m_b, m_t$) and the three-generation structure, derived from a single calibration ($m_e$) plus 600-cell distance-shell geometry plus SU(3) colour algebra. The paper presents the existing shell-distance derivation as the apex-paper answer to: Why three generations? Why $m_t/m_e \sim 10^5$? Why the specific mass values across four orders of magnitude?

## Inheritance status

Strong. SM-9 derives both the $V^{7/3}$ exponent and the $M_0$ prefactor from first principles. SM-10 closes the cage-assignment story via FEM. The remaining inheritances are: SM-3's K3 framework (charged-lepton-related, inherited via the Koide-phase/quark-Koide structure of SM-7) and the post-gap multiplier $z \cdot C_F$ for the top quark (corrected from $z = 12$ to $z \cdot C_F = 16$ in SM-8 v4.0 based on angular-weighted pair model).

## Strategic role

SF-3 ships after SF-1 (charged leptons) and possibly in parallel with SF-2 (EW). Quarks are the mass-hierarchy headline because they span the largest mass range; the apex paper presents the full 600-cell distance-shell derivation in one place rather than scattered across SM-7/8/9/10.
