# Philosophy — SF-3: The Quark Sector from 600-Cell Geometry

## Type Classification

**SF-3 is a synthesis / reframing paper.** It introduces *no new derivation*. Its
content is the assembly of shipped CPP results (SM-7, SM-8, SM-9, SM-10, SS-1,
SS-2, SM-6) into a single quark-sector account, plus two original *organizational*
contributions:

1. **The calibration-route adjudication** — a decision (Route A canonical, `m_c`
   demoted to derived), not a derivation.
2. **Proposition 5.1** — a bookkeeping separation of inherited SM-7 structure,
   not a theorem.

This classification is load-bearing for honesty: the paper's value is *coherence
and consolidation*, not new physics. It is deliberately *not* registered as
adding theorems or swarm predictions.

## Key Philosophical Points

### One cage-scaling law plus one spectral partition
The deepest claim is reductive: what conventional physics records as *four
unrelated quark masses plus two unrelated couplings* is, in CPP, *one cage-scaling
law plus one spectral partition*. The macroscopic regularity (masses on a single
geometric progression; couplings summing to a fixed golden-ratio constant) is the
"macroscopic shadow" — within the CPP ontology — of the sub-quantum claim that
all four heavy quarks are cages on one lattice and both couplings are mode
fractions of one adjacency spectrum.

### Single-calibration discipline as the unifying spine
The decision to demote `m_c` and keep a single `m_e` calibration is not an
accuracy optimization (it slightly *worsens* `b/t` residuals). It is a
*structural* commitment: a uniform single-calibration claim across leptons (SF-1),
neutrinos (SF-4), and quarks (SF-3) is the spine of SF-7's "hierarchy without
hierarchy". A sub-percent gain is not worth a second calibration that contradicts
that spine.

### Honest parallelism: masses derived, mixing open
SF-3 and SF-4 share a posture — both derive masses while deferring a mixing-sector
CP observable (CKM in SF-3 ↔ δ_CP in SF-4). This is stated as a *parallel of
posture, not an equivalence of difficulty or status*. Naming the parallel
strengthens the SF-line's uniform "masses derived, mixing-sector open" stance
without overclaiming that the two gaps are equally hard.

## Honest Assessment

### Weakest links (surfaced by reviewer pressure)
- **`α_s` and `sin²θ_W` are structural correspondences, not RGE-derived gauge
  couplings.** The mode-fraction values numerically match the measured couplings;
  they are not derived from renormalization-group running. The v0.4 review
  pressed this, and the paper now says so explicitly.
- **Proposition 5.1's independence is conditional.** It holds *because* `α_s` is
  the structural value `5/(8φ)`; a charm-scale fit would re-introduce `m_c`. This
  conditional was the cycle's most valuable catch — the difference between a
  proposition that is true and one that is *defensibly* true.
- **The generation count is model-dependent.** SM-8 gives a *selection* within an
  antipodal-identification model, not a uniqueness theorem. The verb was walked
  back across four rounds to "selected within the SM-8 ... model".
- **CKM is undelivered.** Honestly registered, not finessed.

### What would change the assessment
A first-principles derivation of `α_s`/`sin²θ_W` from substrate dynamics (rather
than as mode-fraction correspondences), or a derivation of CKM via a quark-sector
analog of SM-5's K₃ → tri-bimaximal mechanism, would upgrade SF-3 from a
consolidation flagship toward a genuinely predictive one. Neither exists yet.

### The Sonnet gate
The designed review cycle ends with a Sonnet hostile pass. It was not run for the
repo-internal v1.0 SHIP (which rests on the same four-round multi-reviewer
convergence as SF-2/SF-4). A formal Sonnet pass remains available — and advisable
— before public OSF/arXiv deposit.
