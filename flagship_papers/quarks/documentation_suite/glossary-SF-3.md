# Glossary — SF-3: The Quark Sector from 600-Cell Geometry

## Core Mathematical Objects

- **`M_q = m_e (z/φ) V^{7/3}`** — the cage-mass formula. The mass of a
  colour-charged cage with vertex count `V`, anchored at `M_0 = m_e z/φ`. Top
  quark carries an extra relay factor `z·C_F = 16`. [DERIVED, SM-8/SM-9]
- **`M_0 = m_e z/φ ≈ 3.79 MeV`** — the mass anchor (the per-link cage-energy
  scale). [DERIVED, SM-9]
- **`V ∈ {4, 12, 20, 30}`** — the four bonded distance-shells of the 600-cell,
  mapping to (s, c, b, t). [DERIVED topology, SM-1/SM-8]
- **`7/3`** — the cage-mass exponent: displacement-increment pair interactions ×
  the linear cage dimension. [DERIVED, SM-9]
- **`z = 12`** — the 600-cell nearest-neighbour coordination number. [AXIOM-anchored]
- **`φ`** — the golden ratio, `(1+√5)/2 ≈ 1.6180`. Appears throughout via the
  propagation-efficiency factor `η = 1/φ`.
- **`C_F = 4/3`** — the colour Casimir, derived from the 600-cell. [DERIVED, SS-2]
- **`α_s = 5/(8φ) ≈ 0.386`** — the strong coupling, as the *face-mode* fraction
  of the 600-cell adjacency spectral trace. [STRUCTURAL VALUE, SM-7]
- **`sin²θ_W = 3/(8φ) ≈ 0.232`** — the Weinberg angle, as the *edge-mode*
  fraction of the same spectral trace. [STRUCTURAL VALUE, SM-6]
- **`sin²θ_W + α_s = 1/φ`** — the exact electroweak–strong complementarity
  (their sum is fixed at `1/φ`; ratio `α_s/sin²θ_W = F/E = 1200/720 = 5/3`).
- **`θ_quark = 124.04°`** — the quark Koide phase. [DERIVED structural observable, SM-7]
- **`ε = ε_S + ε_EW = −27/(52φ)`** — the net isotropic self-energy shift; strong
  `ε_S = −z α_s/(z+1)`, electroweak `ε_EW = +3/(52φ)`.
- **`A_q`** — the mass amplitude in the Koide parametrisation
  `m_i = A_q(1 + √2 cos(θ + 2πi/3))`. An overall scale; cancels from the phase
  ratio (the heart of Proposition 5.1).

## Physical Concepts

- **Cage** — a colour-charged configuration of Conscious Points occupying one of
  the four bonded distance-shells of the 600-cell. A quark, in CPP ontology.
- **Face-mode / edge-mode fraction** — the share of the adjacency spectral trace
  carried by oriented face-adjacency walks (`A³`, colour-flux relays across cage
  faces → `α_s`) versus edge round-trips (`A²` → `sin²θ_W`).
- **Top relay (`z·C_F = 16`)** — the colour-Casimir-weighted coordination factor
  by which the top quark's icosidodecahedral shell relays its self-energy across
  all `z = 12` neighbours.
- **Isotropic self-energy shift** — the colour + electroweak shift that rotates
  the K₃ eigenphase away from its bare `−2/3` value to fix the Koide phase.
- **Antipodal identification** — the tessellated-lattice identification (SM-8)
  that limits the Standard Model to exactly three generations and predicts no
  fourth quark.

## Standard Physics Terms (used non-standardly)

- **Strong coupling `α_s`** — in CPP a *mode fraction* of one substrate spectrum,
  not a running gauge coupling. The structural value `5/(8φ)` is matched to a
  renormalization-scheme-dependent effective value at the charm scale; it is a
  structural correspondence, not an RGE-derived coupling.
- **Weinberg angle `sin²θ_W`** — likewise a mode fraction of the *same* spectrum,
  complementary to `α_s`.
- **Koide phase** — used here for the quark sector (Koide's relation is usually
  invoked for charged leptons); SF-3 inherits the SM-7 quark-sector application.
- **Calibration** — SF-3 uses exactly one dimensionful calibration, `m_e`. The
  MeV scale enters only through identifying the electron cage with the physical
  electron mass (shared across SF-1/3/4), not as a second scale-setting step.

## Status Labels

- **DERIVED** — follows from `m_e` + geometry at zero shape parameters (the four
  masses, `M_0`, `7/3`, `C_F`).
- **STRUCTURAL VALUE** — a geometric mode-fraction that numerically matches a
  measured coupling (`α_s`, `sin²θ_W`); zero-parameter but a *correspondence*,
  not an RGE derivation.
- **SELECTED (model-dependent)** — the three-generation count, selected *within*
  the SM-8 antipodal-identification model (not a uniqueness theorem).
- **BOOKKEEPING SEPARATION** — Proposition 5.1; reorganizes inherited structure,
  derives nothing new; NOT a registered theorem.
- **INHERITED-OPEN** — OPEN-FP-3-CKM (quark mixing + quark CP phase); the
  structural analog of the open neutrino δ_CP.
