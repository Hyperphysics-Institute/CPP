## Phenomena Explained

### The heavy-quark mass hierarchy
The masses `m_s, m_c, m_b, m_t` span four orders of magnitude yet sit on a single
geometric progression `M_q = m_e (z/φ) V^{7/3}` with `V ∈ {4, 12, 20, 30}`.
RMS residual 2.1% from `m_e` + geometry alone. Charm is a *prediction*
(1249 MeV, −1.6%), not an input.

### The strong coupling value
`α_s = 5/(8φ) ≈ 0.386`, the face-mode fraction of the 600-cell adjacency
spectral trace. Matched to the renormalization-scheme-dependent effective value
at the charm scale.

### The electroweak–strong complementarity
`sin²θ_W + α_s = 1/φ` exactly, with the two couplings as edge-mode and face-mode
fractions of the *same* spectrum (ratio `5/3`). Conventional physics records two
unrelated couplings; CPP records one spectral partition.

### The quark Koide phase
`θ_quark = 124.04°` vs the PDG-quark-mass-extracted `124.09°` — 0.05% agreement,
a zero-parameter prediction from the net isotropic self-energy shift.

### The generation count
Exactly three generations, selected within the SM-8 antipodal-identification
model.

## Phenomena Predicted

### No fourth-generation quark
A clean falsifier. Discovery of a fourth-generation quark would directly
contradict the antipodal-identification three-generation prediction.
**Test:** direct collider search; current bounds already exclude a sequential
fourth generation at accessible masses, consistent with the prediction.

### Charm mass as a derived quantity
`m_c = 1249 MeV` (−1.6% vs PDG) — not fitted. **Test:** any future tightening of
the PDG charm mass that moved it materially outside the stated residual band
would pressure the `V^{7/3}` cage scaling.

### Quark Koide phase stability
`θ_quark = 124.04°`. **Test:** a measured quark Koide phase departing materially
from this (well outside the present 0.05% agreement) falsifies the isotropic-shift
derivation.

### Mode-complementarity at the substrate scale
`sin²θ_W + α_s = 1/φ`. **Test:** an independent measurement forcing the substrate-
scale sum away from `1/φ` — i.e. a demonstration that `sin²θ_W` and `α_s` do not
share the single 600-cell spectral-trace origin — falsifies the complementarity.

### Mass-formula structure
The `V^{7/3}` cage scaling. **Test:** a heavy-quark mass found to violate
`V^{7/3}` beyond the stated residual band falsifies the mass formula.
