**Patch:** 4184. **Date:** 20 September 2026. **Lane:** EW.
**Corrects:** Patch 4183 §2–§3.

---

>
> * Only GPs with a CP will mix their axial content with a CP's axial content.
> * Every GP in the universe will receive LSP info via DI-bits, integrate it, and broadcast the next
>   Moment with its new LSP, which contains the newly computed axial content in the LSP/DI-bit

## Reading

**He is right, and 4183's conclusion does not follow from its arithmetic.** The occupancy figures
(no matter CP within the PSR shell, by seventy-six orders) are **correct as facts about sources**,
but I read them as meaning the shell is silent. It is not: **every GP relays**, so the axial field
is present everywhere and the shell is full of carriers even where it holds no sources.

**Worked out at Patch 4184, the correction settles the ratio he asked for — in his favour, by fifty
to sixty-five orders, and without calibration.**
