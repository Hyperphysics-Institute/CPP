# Founder — No Bound Quarks and No Loose Quarks, Only Oscillating Ones; Fractional Charges Are Effective

**Patch:** 4263. **Date:** 23 September 2026. **Lane:** EW → strong (TODO-4234-DELTA).
**Answers:** Patch 4262 §4 (does the −qCP grip the second up quark by the strong force when its charge is cancelled?).
**Answer:** the question dissolves. A quark falls into superposition with its opposite vertex (no SSV gradient there),
reverses, and moves toward the strongest gradient at that moment: the DP Sea, the sideways vertex, or the diagonal
vertex when the other up has left it open. The strong force attracts every quark. CP charges are integer; ±2/3 and
±1/3 are effective charges of the quark's arrangement, not compositional. Asked *"What do you think?"* — answered at
4263 §3.
