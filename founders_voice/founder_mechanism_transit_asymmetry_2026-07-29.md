> **A(v) = v/μ exactly.**

- **A(v) > v/μ** ⇒ the CP accelerates itself. **Runaway.**

**Nothing in the mechanism as stated forces the equality.** A mechanism
that yields Newton I only when a coefficient lands exactly on a
