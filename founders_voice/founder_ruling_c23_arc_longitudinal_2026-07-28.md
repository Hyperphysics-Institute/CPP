This is the part that settles it beyond the founder's authority over
his own picture. **The banked magnetic-curl derivation at Patch 2842
(`magnetic_curl_derivation.md` §2) is built on the longitudinal

> *"The DP at the observer **polarises along r̂**, so the DP poles sweep

appears because **r̂ rotates as the source moves**, which is precisely
the founder's "longitudinal displacements that make arcs around a DP

is the angular velocity of the *line of sight*, not of any transverse
CP motion. **The mathematics was right. Only the later verbal gloss was
wrong.**
