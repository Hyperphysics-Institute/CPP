# Founder ruling — the pre-ignition ball sits inside one ordinary Planck Sphere Radius; the "large early PSR" retracted as a description; the reach at the first Moment is the ordinary l_P reach every Moment has (9 September 2026, Session 169)

**Status:** verbatim founder text, registered Patch 3816 (Session 169, 9 Sep 2026, EU lane). Answers the picture question of `eu1_derivation/occupancy_fork.md` §4 as narrowed at Session 169 (reach literally larger than l_P, or the small-ball reading). Ruling minted: **R-IGNITION-BALL-IN-ONE-PSR** — the pre-ignition ball of ~10⁸⁴ CPs at twelve per GP fits within one rest-frame Planck Sphere Radius; the CP's reach at the first Moment is the ordinary l_P ceiling, not an enlarged one; "large early PSR" is withdrawn as a description. Resolves **FORK-EU-OCCUPANCY-1 → Branch P (small-ball reading)**. Derivation and consequences: `series_phenomena/cosmology/early_universe/eu1_derivation/occupancy_regrounding.md`.

**Worker's note on reading.** The founder adopts the small-ball reading: the *ball* is Planck-sized and the *reach* is the ordinary one; nothing enlarges the PSR beyond its rest-frame ceiling. "The PSR in this case is very small in the first Moment" is read as the ordinary l_P reach relative to the later universe, consistent with his second sentence; it is not read as a reach below l_P at Moment 1 (the registers are empty then — see the re-grounding §1). The three questions of Message 1 are answered in the re-grounding: (Q1) no new axiom — one initial-condition clause (the ball's radius) and one definitional ruling on the count law's referent; (Q2) the ordinary PSR rule from Moment 2, immediately, no hand-off; (Q3) one Moment of full contact, which is all the rules permit and all the held-sector engine needs.
