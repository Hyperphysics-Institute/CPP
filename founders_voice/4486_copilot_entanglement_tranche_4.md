## Copilot's answer (summarized; a third party's text, not the founder's, not filed verbatim)

- **Overall:** a plausible route to all eight, not a demonstration. Copilot states that the existing CPP description already has GPs discretizing spacetime
  and CP moves as stepwise displacement increments coordinated by Quantum Group Entities while conserving energy and momentum, so the proposal fits the
  ontology. (Unlike tranche 3, this answer draws on a CPP document: the founder identifies it as a Microsoft 365 Word document of 29 January 2026, written with
  Grok, too large to copy and not filed here. The repository's website text says the same: `rm_website_archive/conscious-point-physics-version-1-1-part-4.md`,
  "QGEs orchestrate saltatory Displacement Increments … under conservation constraints".) The Group Entity is the non-factorable relational structure
  among its CPs, not an object located somewhere.
- **Per constraint:** (1) each CP carries a local tensor with bond indices of finite dimension χ, which CPP must derive (from CP types, GP occupancy, DP
  phases, conserved quantities or PSR capacity), not choose; (2) the GP update is a reversible, conservation-respecting local operator on the records in its
  PSR, of the quantum-cellular-automaton kind; (3) a shared index is one mathematical object with several local ends, a shared constraint rather than a
  transmitted instruction; (4) the Born rule needs a further principle (e.g. a count of GP-compatible histories) proved to equal |Ψ|², as a later theorem,
  not a postulate; (5) no-signalling becomes a test that rejects any GP rule under which one side's choice changes the other side's local statistics;
  (6) Bell correlations (−cos θ; CHSH up to 2√2) must come from the displacement rules, which needs amplitude and phase in the bond, not a label; (7) field
  theory recovered in stages, starting with a free wave equation in a continuum limit; (8) deviations (bond or capacity saturation, phase granularity,
  lattice residuals) only when forced by the same rule that reproduces known physics.
- **Build order:** state space; one reversible two-CP interaction; entanglement formation; interference, which-path loss and erasure; no-signalling;
  probabilities; Bell correlations; continuum limit and deviations.
- **Assessment:** conceptually coherent and representable; suited to local GP computation "potentially"; not yet a derivation of quantum mechanics; not
  expected to meet all eight automatically; strong enough to justify a minimal formal model. Proposed foundational sentence: a shared index carries no force
  or message; it shapes the probabilities of locally allowed, conservation-compatible GP transitions.
