# Founder — Each Up Quark Is Bound to Both Minus Vertices; the Two Ups Approach Each Other at Random Times

**Patch:** 4262. **Date:** 23 September 2026. **Lane:** EW → strong (TODO-4234-DELTA).
**Answers:** Patch 4261 §5 (along its third direction, how firmly is an up quark held on its vertex?).
**Answer:** the u is held along that direction. It ZBW-oscillates with its own minus vertex and with the diagonal one,
sideways with the d. **Clarified (second message, supersedes the first):** the two minus vertices differ. They are the
hTetra's −qCP and −eCP (`founders_vision.md` §3: hTetra = 2 eCPs + 2 qCPs). One up quark's own vertex is the −qCP
(charge + strong); the other's is the −eCP (charge only). The two ups repel each other, and they approach each other
at random times when both move toward the down quark. Asked *"What do you think?"* — answered at 4262 §3.

## What it settled

Each u's mode set gains a third mode, anchored on a frame vertex: toward the diagonal minus vertex, along the u–u line.
The two ups sit on inequivalent seats: u_q on the −qCP, u_e on the −eCP. With the radial mode and the u–d mode, this holds the u in all three
directions and removes the unlocalised axis found at 4261 (B). "At random times" means the modes are independent,
which is the product state the computation already uses.
