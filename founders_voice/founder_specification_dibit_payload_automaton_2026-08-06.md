## §1 — The name and role (founder, first post, cleaned verbatim)

- The DI-bit has that name because DI is the acronym for Displacement
  Increment. It is a bit because it is a point of consciousness and
  carries one message among many that will be integrated by the GPs. It
  is called a displacement increment because it tells the CP the
  displacement increment to take according to the SSV_net; it tells the
  DI-bits how many edges to take to go to the PSR; it carries two types
  of displacement information.
- The DI-bit is a conscious point.
- Every GP releases the same number of DI-bits every Moment.

## §2 — The payload (founder, consolidated across the first post and the Q1 answer)

Every DI-bit carries exactly three pieces of information, all imprinted
by its originating GP:

1. **Origin address** — the identity/address of the GP that emitted it.
2. **Electric vector E** — the vector sum of every polar-charge
   contribution (both eCP and qCP) that the originating GP integrated in
   the previous Moment. Magnitude and polarity are folded into the
   vector itself; direction is computed from the relative addresses of
   the contributing charges. No explicit e-versus-q flag is carried:
   electric effects from both species are treated identically and summed
   together.
3. **Strong vector S** — a separate vector containing the vector sum of
   every strong-charge contribution (qCPs only), magnitude and direction
   summed at the emitting GP. Occupying its own vector slot is the only
   species identifier needed.

**Species-by-slot ruling (founder, Q1 answer, cleaned verbatim):** "The
species/type (eCP or qCP) of the plus and minus, encoded onto the DI-bit,
will not carry e or q as binary identifier information as to the
source/species of the electric-type vector elements. Rather, the
electric-vector and strong-vector are by their vector entry locations the
de facto identifiers of their species type." "We can only look at the
system from a black-box perspective. The information about whether
quark-type or electron-type is compressed into a count of strong
magnitude and location of origin, and a count of plus and origin, and a
count of minus and origin. The addition is vectorial, so each CP's
polarity and its origin address are used to compute the electric vector
for the SSV_net."

**Receiver extraction:** SSV_net = E + S (the net directed signal);
SSV_abs per §4.

## §3 — The PCD processing sequence (Grok-drafted, founder-adopted, as amended by §6.3)

- **Perceive:** all DI-bits that arrived during the previous Moment's
  Displace phase deposit their payloads (origin address + E + S). Any CP
  sitting on the GP announces its presence and its own charge content
  (polar ±, and strong if qCP). The GP's data sources are: the local
  CP(s) and the DI-bits that landed on it. *(The third source in the
  original draft — a tally of DI-bits merely passing through en route to
  a more distant PSR shell — is STRUCK by founder ruling §6.3.)*
- **Compute:** pure vector summation of every electric contribution →
  new E; pure vector summation of every strong contribution → new S;
  the scalar SSV_abs per §4. The resulting (SSV_net, SSV_abs) pair is
  the complete local environmental summary for the next Moment.
- **Displace/Emit:** the GP imprints exactly the same newly computed
  (origin address, E, S) onto every DI-bit it releases. The number of
  DI-bits released is fixed and identical for every GP every Moment. The
  DI-bits then move; movement is governed by the local equalization rule
  (§6.5: Version B, the even split over outward neighbors of the 12).
  Simultaneously, any resident CP is displaced according to the
  just-computed SSV_net.
- **Recursion (founder, first post):** the imprinted values "reflect the
  total number and direction of the polar charges and strong charges in
  the environment of the previous volley of DI-bits (which reflects the
  previous moment, which reflects the previous moment …)". The entire
  history of the environment is recursively compressed into the current
  SSV values; each Moment only needs the previous Moment's summary.

## §4 — SSV_abs: the weighted form (founder ruling, in dialogue with Grok)

> **SSV_abs = Σ|polar| + k × Σ|strong|, with k > 1.**

- The founder questioned the polar-only draft definition: qCPs carry
  both polar and strong charge, and the strong charge has an even
  greater effect on absolute stress; a polar-only SSV_abs would
  systematically under-represent environmental intensity wherever qCPs
  are present. The weighted form was adopted.
- **k is a fixed universal constant** (founder ruling): "the polar vs.
  strong force is a constant, created to have the force that it has, and
  is unchanged by external conditions, which merely modify the perceived
  force" — environmental effects act through arrival counts, directions,
  and geometric dilution, not through k.
- **k's value (founder recollection, 6 Aug):** the strong-to-electric
  ratio "may have been 67"; the founder believes the derivation may
  already exist in the corpus. Worker lead registered for the citation
  hunt: the factor 3(11+5√5) ≈ 66.54 inside α_geom's closed form
  (c02); plus the shipped coupling ratio route (α_s = 5/(8φ) from SM-7
  against the SF-6/c02 electric coupling). k enters AP-4 as an
  IDENTIFICATION OBLIGATION, not a free parameter.

## §5 — Energy is emergent; the payload has no energy slot (founder, 6 Aug, cleaned verbatim)

"Energy does not live in the DI-bit information payload. Energy in all
its forms is an emergent configuration of concentrated and/or ordered
CPs/DPs/unpaired CPs that is low entropy compared to the native Sea."

"The gravitational effect and the KE effect both are emergent effects
due to different mechanisms. The result is a differential in SSV_abs.
The SSV_abs is going to be larger as one goes toward the gravitational
body. The KE is stored in the Dipole Sea as per the Newton 1 and
Newton 2 mechanisms of the movement of the CP-current charge moving
through space. These changes in the configuration of the DPs have the
effect of producing gravity in one configuration and storing
KE/inertia/momentum in the other. The automaton is primitive, with
gravity and KE emerging. Gravity arises with the configuration of
concentrated unpaired CPs, resulting in a gradient of SSV_abs with
distance from the mass, the region of unpaired DPs. In the case of the
KE, the differential production of an SSV_net gradient between fore and
aft of the CP-current results in a perpetual conservative regeneration
of the bolus of energy/work done in accelerating the charged particle
to constant speed, and then maintenance of it by the perpetual SSV_net
that displaces the mass at delta displacement per Moment, to produce a
constant velocity."

**Worker synthesis, founder-confirmed in-session:** the charge census
serves as an energy meter because in CPP energy is never anything but
configured charge — moving or bound matter keeps the surrounding Sea
polarized, and polarization is exactly what the census counts, unlike
the isotropic native Sea, which sums to background. The quantitative
claim (census perturbation ∝ stored energy, as the SR-1 PSR formula and
c05's mc²-proportional sourcing require) is AP-4's central derivation
obligation.
