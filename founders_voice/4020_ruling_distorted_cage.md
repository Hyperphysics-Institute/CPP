# Founder ruling — the 600-cell space may be slightly distorted (14 September 2026)

**Filed verbatim per CONV-009.**
**Context:** Patch 4019 established that the 600-cell carries a **7.356° angular deficit per edge** (five
regular tetrahedra about an edge give 352.644°, not 360°), that this positive deficit *is* positive
curvature, and that all five previously closed construction classes are symptoms of that one frustration.
It then restated the choice the founder was actually facing — which was **not** the strict-vs-weak reading
this lane had been asking about for six patches.

---

## The question as put (Patch 4019, this lane)

> Is the substrate's 600-cell exactly regular, or near-icosahedral with the frustration carried as strain?
>
> **(A) Flat space, distorted cage.** Keep z = 12 and near-icosahedral local order; give up the icosahedron
> being *exactly* regular; the 7.356° absorbs as strain, which is what real icosahedral matter does.
> **(B) Exact cage, curved space.** Keep the regular 600-cell and pay the curvature — priced and closed at
> 4017, since the only regular honeycomb of 600-cells is hyperbolic and costs z = ∞.

## The founder's reply, verbatim

> I think the 600-cell space is allowed to be slightly distorted, just like materials with icosahedral
> packing.

---

## Standing — **(A) is ruled.**

**What it costs: nothing yet.** The distortion does not change **who** a vertex's neighbours are at any
amplitude tested, so **z = 12 survives**, which is what SS-1, SM-1, SM-7, SM-8, SM-9 and SF-4 require.

**What it does NOT supply: a chirality mechanism.** Tested immediately at Patch 4020, because the whole
V3 defence rests on `R = diag(1,1,1,−1)` being an *exact* symmetry and a distorted cage does not have it
exactly. Twelve independent strain realisations at each of ε = 0.01, 0.03, 0.08: the 3-hop chirality sum
becomes nonzero in **each** realisation, but its **sign wanders with the seed** and the mean sits well
inside one standard deviation of zero (|mean|/sd = 0.16, 0.01, 0.15). **Each strained cage is chiral; the
ensemble is not** — which is the difference between a chiral *object* and a chiral *law*. **FI-C-9 = V3
survives a fourth independent test.**

**What it DOES supply: a named place where a mechanism could live.** Random strain is not the physical
strain. The physical distortion is the **relaxation** of the 7.356°-per-edge frustration, which is
structured rather than random, and in 3D icosahedral matter that relaxation organises into **disclination
networks**. Whether such a network carries a handedness is a genuine open question — and it is now the
arc's, registered rather than assumed. Random perturbation cannot settle it either way and Patch 4020 does
not pretend otherwise.

**Two statuses corrected.** SF-4's z = 12 inheritance (4016) is now **physically motivated** — uniform
z = 12 with distorted icosahedra is exactly what real icosahedral matter does — but **still not shown**.
And the lateral-construction **target** is now well-defined for the first time: **flat 4D, uniform z = 12,
near-icosahedral local order with 7.356°/edge carried as strain.** The cut-and-project class remains the
wrong one (4015: z ∈ {12, 13, 14, 18, 19, 26}); what it failed to hit is now stated.
