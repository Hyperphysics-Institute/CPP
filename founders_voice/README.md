# founders_voice/

First-person mechanism records and conceptual notes in Thomas Lee Abshier's own voice — the billiard-ball-and-cue-stick accounts that sit *behind* the formal papers. Parallel to `opus_voice/`.

These are **pictures, not proofs**: physical mechanisms stated mechanically and consistent with the corpus and the experiments, each carrying an honest status note on what remains to be derived. They record the founder's mechanical intent so the formal derivations have a target to aim at.

---

## The `phenomenon_*.md` convention

`founders_voice/` is the home of the **phenomenon stories** — the fully articulated, expanded, examined version of each paper's Plain Language Summary, in the founder's voice. The intent (TLA): *every* paper and *every* glossary item should have one of these. The paper's PLS is the seed; the `phenomenon_*.md` file is the full mechanical story behind it — "this IS my story; the mathematics is what makes my story credible."

**Standard shape of a phenomenon story:**
1. **The claim** — the one-paragraph billiard-ball statement of what the thing actually is.
2. **The mechanism, step by step** — the mechanical-sequential account, in CP-level objects (qCP/eCP/qDP/eDP/hDP/hTetra, ZBW, SSV, DP Sea).
3. **Where [the phenomenon] comes from** — the origin of the feature the paper explains.
4. **Links** — to the neighbouring phenomena it touches.
5. **Honest status** — what is *proven* (the cited mathematics that makes the story credible), what is *picture* (mechanism, not yet derived), and what is *open*.
6. **For [window / paper]** — how it serves the formal work.

**Discipline:** a phenomenon story is a *picture*. It must name, in its Honest-status section, the mathematics that grounds it and the derivation still owed. Newer or less-examined conjectures inside a story are flagged as such, never presented at the confidence of the established core.
