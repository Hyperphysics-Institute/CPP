**Date:** 17 September 2026. **Context:** EW lane; answer to the 4081 picture question, filed at Patch 4082. **Capture convention:** CONV-009 (founder verbatim is the primary source

## Verbatim

> If the captured charge is retained for a few Moments, then the momentum it was carrying is absorbed by the bracelet. As the bracelet dissolves, reconstituting the momentum on the captured charge is exceedingly unlikely and is more likely to be dispersed randomly among the various CP/DP constituents into which the bracelet dissipates.

**Answer:** the captured charge's direction is not held as a free vector at the decision point; its momentum is
absorbed by the bracelet and dispersed on dissolution. **Consequence (Patch 4082):** sound physics, but
conservation puts the absorbed momentum into the bracelet's **bulk motion**, which CPP's absolute frame makes
substrate-visible at order v/c. The residual is ε ≈ p/M_W — the founder's own mass-ratio suppression, quantified —
and it **refuted** the tie-break route K3, because V−A is maximal from 1 MeV to 40 GeV.
