# Founder ruling — the linear −eCP in the down-type cages carries mass

**Date:** 16 September 2026. **Context:** SM lane; asked at Patch 0994 (SM-2 corrigendum thread). **Capture convention:** CONV-009 (founder verbatim is the primary source
from which conjecture entries, frontier updates and dispatches derive); filed late at Patch 4094 — see the
capture-audit note below.

## Verbatim

> the linear -eCP does contribute mass, but I don't know how to answer the question about it being absorbed by N_k = 2.5.

## What it settled

**Ruling:** the linear −eCP **does** carry a rest-mass contribution of its own. **Not ruled:** whether that mass
is already folded into the N_k = 2.5 assignment. Under PD-007 that second half is a *calibration* question and is
therefore Claude's, not the founder's: refit the three down-type cages with an explicit −eCP mass term and test
whether the fit demands N_k ≠ 2.5. Registered in `todolist.md` (TODO-0937-CHIR); the SM-2 corrigendum edit (g)
stays held on OPEN-EW-5 meanwhile.
