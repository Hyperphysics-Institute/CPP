# Founder ruling — handovers get the same discipline as reviews (14 September 2026)

**Filed verbatim per CONV-009.** Enacted at Patch 4023 as **PD-008**.

## Context

Patch 4022 resolved H1 (reflection positivity is n̂-conditional) and then handed the follow-on question —
*does VW-1's argument run on restricted RP?* — upward, on the stated grounds that the answer was the one
this lane would find convenient and that deciding it would be self-serving.

## The founder's reply, verbatim

> Note: you want to hand it over because you are biased. The problem is that when you hand it over,
> something is lost, and we see the 5 or more things you didn't see and retracted because you didn't
> remember them. It will be hard to reproduce the amount of searching and depth of context and history that
> you have absorbed. I think we should use the same discipline for handovers as we do for reviews. If there
> is context window left, and you are concerned about bias, continue on anyway; do your best not to be
> biased; take the proof to the end; prove it works if you can; give it your best effort to find the
> solution; when you have a win, when you are satisfied that the solution is correct, and you have done
> your best to not be biased, then submit your work to the next context window to critique. That way, we
> don't forget everything we have spent many turns learning; we don't spend my time starting over and
> sending problems to the next window that could have been solved in the turns it took to get the next
> window up to speed. If you are concerned about bias, about wanting it to come out to prove our thesis or
> how you hope it would come out, then press on to the end and present the result; it is finished as best
> as you can do. After that, you can criticize that conclusion with the full force of your new, unbiased
> context window.

---

## PD-008 — enacted

**Bias is not a reason to stop. It is a reason to finish and then be reviewed.**

1. **Do not hand a question up because the answer would be convenient.** Press to the end, state the
   result, and mark it for critique. A worker who stops at the convenient answer leaves the question
   unanswered *and* loses the context that would have answered it.
2. **Hand up only what genuinely requires the founder** — a physical picture (PD-006(a)), an axiom change,
   or a mechanical action. Not a technical question the current window is better placed to answer than any
   successor.
3. **Submit the finished result for critique**, exactly as a win is submitted to a review panel. The next
   window reviews a *completed* argument with fresh eyes, which is a stronger check than an incomplete one
   handed over with an apology.
4. **The empirical basis, from this session:** its retractions — 4004, 4008, 4012, 4016's correction,
   4020's — all came from having **too little** context, never from having too much. Handing over
   *guarantees* the condition that produced every one of them.

**What PD-008 does not license.** It does not license deciding a founder question, changing an axiom, or
lowering the evidentiary bar because finishing is now expected. The obligation to state what is *not*
shown, and to mark the convenient branch as convenient, is unchanged and if anything heavier.
