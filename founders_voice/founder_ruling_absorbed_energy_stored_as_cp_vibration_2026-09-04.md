# Founder ruling — absorbed wave energy is stored in the BH medium as a dynamic organizational pattern of CP vibration, repositioned each Moment and transmitted through the BH at c/2; information is not lost (4 Sep 2026, Session 161)

**Status:** verbatim founder text, registered Patch 3623. Ruling minted: **R-CORE-STORES-AS-CP-VIBRATION** (answers 3622 §4).

> "EM Wave energy is carried in the DP displacement of both DP arcs and DP
> separation. When an EM wave hits the surface of the BH, it excites the
> vibration of the CPs in the BH structure and thus stores its energy (dynamic
> organizational pattern) in the medium of the BH, whose locations are at 1
> PSR and which reposition themselves each Moment and transmit their position
> to the rest of the BH at the c/2 rate and thus hold that energetic
> organizational pattern in the structure of the BH. This answers whether
> information is lost that is incorporated into the BH."

*Opus (Patch 3623):* this is **mode conversion with storage** — the wave's energy leaves the transverse channel and enters CP motion inside the core, coherently, information preserved. It is consistent with the ringdown's requirement (3621) that the core not return the tensor wave within a crossing, *provided* the stored pattern does not re-couple to the exterior tensor channel on that timescale. It also settles the EHT question in the only way that passes: the storage must be **non-thermal**. A thermalised surface at z = 1 re-radiating Sgr A*'s accretion power would appear as a ~2400 K blackbody at ~10³⁵ erg/s — 10–100× the observed quiescent infrared — and is excluded (the Broderick–Narayan argument). The founder's "dynamic organizational pattern held in the structure" is the non-thermal option: a coherent absorber with no photon-emitting thermal states populated.
