**Patch:** 4185. **Date:** 20 September 2026. **Lane:** EW.

---

## Verbatim

> I think you probably have this right, but I wanted to make sure that the words you and I were
> using were the same.
>
> * You said the grid point wasn't making any signal if it wasn't occupied. More precisely, I mean
>   that GPs without resident CPs simply integrate their input DI-bits and add nothing more to that
>   integration; and that the GP with a resident CP does the same integration but adds axial vector
>   information from the resident CP to that integration.
> * Again, I think that is what you were saying; this was just my way of saying it.

## Reading

**Yes — same thing, and his wording is better than mine.** "Isn't making any signal" was loose; it
could be read as an empty GP being inert or silent, which 4183 is the record of me actually
believing for one patch. **"Integrates its inputs and adds nothing more to that integration"** is
exact: it names the operation (integrate) and the difference (add nothing / add the resident CP's
axial information).
