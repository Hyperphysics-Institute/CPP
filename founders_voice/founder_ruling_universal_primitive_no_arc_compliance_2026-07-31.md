# FOUNDER RULING — ONE PRIMITIVE FOR ALL CPs; COMPLIANCE, STRETCH, AND ε ARE EMERGENT, NOT PRIMITIVE

**31 July 2026, Patch 2901. Captured in the same patch that acts on it,
per CONV-009. Given in answer to the worker's question of whether DP arcs
have a distinct compliance rule that would set the substrate's
entrainment strength ε.**

## §1 — THE RULING, VERBATIM

> I think every CP affects every other CP in its environment. There will
> be differences in its effect due to distance, polarity, and type (e or
> q). The difference in how CPs affect other CPs will change with their
> relative velocity, because there will be an associated cloud of KE
> organization associated with its velocity, but that is all taken into
> account by the summation of the DI-bits and resultant SSV_net and
> SSV_abs at each GP. There is no change associated with any CP in its
> emission of DI-bits regardless of whether it is bound (temporarily) as
> a DP, or whether the CP is the unbound central CP of an electron or a
> quark. The same rules apply to all CPs at all velocities, at all
> densities, at all accelerations - the specifics of the environment are
> the only modifiers, and the environment CPs follow the same rule. The
> ultimate simplicity is that the only factor that matters is the sum of
> the DI-bits that have landed on the GP that the CP is on. The
> environment is a black box that each CP responds to based on what
> arrives on its GP.

## §2 — ON THE WORKER'S SPECIFIC QUESTION, VERBATIM

> Specifically, you asked, "Do arcs (as bound DP pairs) have a different
> compliance — for instance, limited by the arc's own restoring stretch?
> That determines the substrate's ε, and whether it's forced to ε* or has
> to be measured." All of these things like compliance and restoring
> stretch, and substrate epsilon ... are all emergent phenomena that we
> see going on in the space. We can calculate those parameters
> analytically from the big picture of charges in various configurations
> and velocities, bondings, and collisions, and densities, but all those
> phenomena are ultimately the result of an underlying simple DI-bit
> addition that produces SSV_net and SSV_abs. The problem is, at that
> level, the summation is not visualizable other than by FEM-type
> calculation, and all we get is a net number rather than a principle.
> The analytical, higher-level computation based on emergent complexity
> of patterns that you are asking about may very well be present and
> variable, and appear to be the causative reason for an effect, but as I
> said, they are emergent; they are not the primitives that drive action.
> That said, noting that those emergent forces or conditions are present
> is a much more intuitive and satisfying way of understanding/
> visualizing the forces that are driving movement and changes in
> configuration in a system. Manipulating these macroscopic emergent
> properties and patterns is essentially the domain and method of
> physics. E.g., force and energy are useful macroscopic, emergent
> objects and processes that we can work with intuitively and
> analytically. What the CPP program is is the machine language
> underneath the objects and processes that we identify as the patterns
> acting in the physical world.

## §3 — WORKER NOTES ON CONSEQUENCE

1. **There is no arc-compliance input.** Bound and unbound CPs obey the
   identical rule; a bound DP's members each respond to the DI-bit sum on
   their own GP. In any substrate-level engine, pairs are therefore moved
   **per-CP**, and stretch/restoring behaviour must *emerge*.
2. **ε is not a parameter of CPP.** It is a coefficient of an
   emergent-level analytic model, computable in principle from the
   primitive, never an input. Any promotion that treats it as a measured
   input would misstate the theory (relevant to the OPEN-CALIB-COUNT-1
   accounting: this ruling *removes* a candidate second input rather than
   adding one).
3. **The FEM remark is methodological guidance:** at the primitive level
   the summation yields "a net number rather than a principle." The
   division of labour is therefore: emergent-level toys identify *which*
   observable to extract (here, the β²/β⁴ curvature of the dressed
   drive); the primitive-level AUTOMATON computes it with nothing
   adjustable.
