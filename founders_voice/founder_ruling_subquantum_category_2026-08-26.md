# Founder ruling — the subquantum/quantum category distinction for PSR and SSV_abs (26 Aug 2026)

**Captured verbatim per CONV-009. Registered at Patch 3442. Context:
UNIV-1 (Patch 3441) proposes to test whether SSV_abs couples to
physics through the PSR alone; the founder cautions on the epistemic
level at which PSR and SSV_abs exist.**

## The founder's statement, verbatim

> The problem with the test is that the SSV_abs is measured at the
> Moment level, and we have no tool for its observation other than
> indirectly at the average level. As you will note in the corpus,
> the SSV_net it relate to the SSV_abs by the lorentz factor, but the
> SSV_net can be wildly anomalous in the Moment, far beyond the
> SSV_abs in phenomena such as tunneling, and the Schroedingers
> distribution of the electron. So, yes, the SSV_abs is the
> determinant for the speed of light as evidenced by the envelope of
> photon energy transfer, and the passage of time for the atom in its
> macroscopic effects, and metrics we could use to measure on the
> quantum level, but at the subquantum level the anomalies are
> tremendous, as evidenced by the GP computing the PSR and the
> DI-bits landing on GPs covering 10% of the radius of the PSR, and
> nothing in between or beyond until re-radiation swaths the entire
> volume of the region in between the GP_origin and GP_PSR. So, in
> your examination of the PSR and SSV_abs we must recognize that we
> are looking at subquantum phenomena vs. quantum measurements which
> will be necessarily averaged. The category distinction is important
> to maintain. On our level, the PSR and SSV_abs are concepts that we
> triangulate on their existence, rather than use as primary metrics
> that we measure observationally.

## Mechanism details newly on record in this statement

1. **SSV_net ↔ SSV_abs via the Lorentz factor**, with SSV_net free to
   be wildly anomalous within a Moment — the stated substrate face of
   tunneling and of the Schrödinger distribution of the electron.
2. **DI-bit landing discreteness:** DI-bits land on GPs covering
   ~10% of the PSR radius, with nothing in between or beyond, until
   re-radiation swaths the full volume between GP_origin and GP_PSR.
3. **Epistemic status:** at our level, PSR and SSV_abs are
   *triangulated constructs*, not primary observables; all
   quantum-level measurements of them are necessarily averages.
