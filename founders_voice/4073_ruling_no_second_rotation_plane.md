# Founder answers — a CP has no second plane or second rotating parameter; antiparticle = opposite polarity

**Date:** 16 September 2026. **Context:** EW lane; answers to the three picture questions posed at Patch 4072. **Capture convention:** CONV-009 (founder verbatim is the primary source
from which conjecture entries, frontier updates and dispatches derive); filed late at Patch 4094 — see the
capture-audit note below.

## Verbatim

> I don't know the answer to 1 and 2. That would be an axiomatic declaration.
> 
> 3) the CP antiparticles have been defined exactly as you describe. If there is an additional reversal, such as a spin, that would be axiomatic, and is not defined by CPP.
> 1) there is no second parameter to be turning other than if it had a spin direction/pole, which would be an axiomatic addition.
> 2) There is no known second plane of rotation, and no second parameter to be rotating in. This is an axiomatic addition to add an additional variable, such as spin.

## What it settled

**Rulings:** (i) a CPP antiparticle is the same CP with **opposite polarity**, and nothing else is reversed —
this resolved filter F9. (ii) A CP has **no second plane of rotation and no second rotating parameter**; adding a
spin pole or second plane is an **axiomatic addition**. **Consequence (Patch 4073):** candidate χ as drafted at
4072 was corrected — it *adds a variable* and is not native.
