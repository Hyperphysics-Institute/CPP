## The ruling

The ZBW cycle is a conservative oscillation **centred on superposition**. The oscillating CP passes through its
opposite-charge partner at **maximum speed**, turns at the extremes, and returns. The kinetic energy carried by the
DP arcs survives superposition. The one Moment of co-location stays (R-DWELL-1's dwell is now co-location in
transit). The stop-and-reverse at superposition is retired.

**The name question** is answered at `series_standard_model/axiom_maturation/4265_zbw_name_and_sweep_start.md` §2:
keep "ZBW". The ruling brings CPP's cycle closer to Schrödinger's.
