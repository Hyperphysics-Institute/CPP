# Founder proposal — reincorporate (r, θ, φ) through the DI-bits: every GP_PSR records the PSR of every DI-bit it receives; SSV_net's computed direction and magnitude embed θ, φ implicitly; SSV_net is compared to SSV_abs in the direction of its vector (4 Sep 2026, Session 161)

**Status:** verbatim founder text, registered Patch 3608 as **P-DIRECTION-VIA-ADDRESSES**. Assessed in `rcore_derivation/3608_will_this_work.md`: as stated it is rank 1 and will not carry a gravitational wave; with one addition — keep the *second moment* of the same addresses — it will, in principle, and the charter can then be checked in order.

> "The theory has been considerably refined since the original anisotropic
> sphere was dropped. The DI-bits did not exist in that model. So, let's see if
> it is possible to reincorporate the r, theta, phi, and DI-bits into the
> theory.
> * Each GP_PSR receives a DI bit from every GP_origin at a distance from the PSR.
> * So, the PSR is recorded in every GP_PSR for every DI-bit it receives.
> * When SSV_net is computed, it embeds theta and phi implicitly in the computed
>   SSV_net vector's direction and magnitude.
> * The SSV_net will thus be compared to the SSV_abs in the direction of its vector."

> "There is probably a lot more detail that could be added to this story to do
> an exact accounting for the DI-bits in the GP register, and then addition of
> the addresses of the incoming DI-bits to produce the E_e and E_q vectors, but
> that has been largely documented in other patches."

> "What do you think? Will this work?"
