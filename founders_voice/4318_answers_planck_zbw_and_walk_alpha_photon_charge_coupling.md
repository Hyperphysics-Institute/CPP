## 2. Walk-and-talk: α, the coupling of light and charge (edited rendering)

**The question.** Today's topic is α, the fine-structure constant. It is the coupling between electromagnetism and
charge, with the value 1/137, and that coupling produces nearly all electromagnetic phenomena: Compton scattering,
chemical bonds, and the rest. Since every Conscious Point carries charge, electromagnetism will interact with charge in
a particular way. The question is: by what method do electromagnetism and charge interact at the level of the Grid
Point, the Conscious Point and the DI-bit?

**The magnetic effect is DP-arcs.** We have been examining how CPs, when they move (as electrons do in a current),
create DP-arcs, and the DP-arc is where the magnetic effect arises. So one coupling effect may be this: when an
electromagnetic wave passes by a charge, it interacts with the charge. The wave has an E field and a B field. The E
field is simply the stretching effect of a field on a plus and a minus charge; on a dipole it stretches the dipole
apart. That is essentially what an electromagnetic wave is: a region where the electric field travels forward at the
speed of light, pulling plus and minus dipoles apart. So there is coupling at the level of the electric field, on
either a dipole or a single bare charge.

**There is no separate B field.** The B field is the effect of a moving charge on a dipole. In effect it is a kind of
information storage of movement — although nothing actually stores the movement. The B-field effect propagates through
space, causing dipole particles to form DP-arcs. The advance of an E-field region automatically carries a B field with
it, which is really just a differential movement of the plus and minus ends of the dipole particle. So there is no B
field: there is only the advance of the electric field, and that advance acts on the dipole particles. We are therefore
not looking at a magnetic field coupling to electric charge; we are looking only at the electric field coupling to
charge. Because the electric field travels forward at the speed of light, through its volume of charge orientation, it
aligns the dipole particles along the axis of the E field as it passes.

**Emission: a moving charge orients the sea.** The source of an electromagnetic wave is an orientation of charges caused
by the movement of charge. Suppose a charge moves up to down in a transition from a higher-energy to a lower-energy
electron orbital. That velocity orients the dipole particles. The photon released by the orbital decay is then, say,
an up-to-down arrow. The DP-arcs formed by that velocity repel the negative end of each DP and attract its positive end,
causing a rotation. Suppose the electron had been activated by a collision with a mass at a certain angle, transferring
some energy to the orbital; that sets the velocity and the direction of the photon later released. The electron's drop
between levels creates a circular, toroidal structure that sets the dipole particles spinning. The forward motion of
the electric field impressed on the Dipole Sea has the same effect as the disturbance advances. All that is needed is a
triggering motion: the E field is in transit. It does not matter where in the electron the transit happens — only that
there is movement, an absolute movement of the activated electron between top and bottom.

**The energy of the quantum.** Take the direction from the higher activation to the lower energy level. The activation
energy is the difference between the two levels, and that energy is lost in going from the higher orbital to the lower.
The electron was moving; it fell, and it had a direction — the direction that carried it from the activated orbital to
the ground-state orbital — and there was an E-field difference between them. It was moving toward the stronger
electrostatic attraction, which shows up in the field as a gradient, an SSV gradient, felt at each point along the
Conscious Point's GP track.

**The coupling.** So the coupling between the electromagnetic field and the charged Conscious Point is the amount of
movement — basically current — associated with a particular strength, which is essentially intensity: how many units of
ħ are transmitted to each Conscious Point as it moves because an electromagnetic wave passes the dipole particle. At the
start (emission) it is how much electromagnetic action is produced by the movement. There is a potential-energy
difference, related to h because it is an amount of action — the total action of that quantum — and the ħ/2 quantum is
the amount of energy applied over a Moment that becomes available to the photon when it is released. It is a packet, an
amount of displacement available to the charge when it is coupled. The total energy available to the coupled charge is
what the released quantum carries. A very large quantum is released if there is a large gradient of attraction to the
nucleus.

**Absorption and the cycle.** When that quantum couples with a charge — for example an orbital electron — it is absorbed
because the structure of the photonic volume collapses, and the energy is transferred to the electron orbital, which
goes into an activated state. So there is a cycle: from the high-energy orbital to the low-energy orbital, releasing a
photon; the photon meets a ground-state orbital and carries that amount of energy, which accelerates the electron up to
the higher orbital. That energy is then bent around the nucleus by the difference between the higher and lower SSV_abs
inside and outside, which shapes the orbital. This might be simply a charge-gradient bending of space; in some way the
space is bent. The electron follows an isopotential and creates its own orbital shape. I suspect all of this connects
with the geometry of space and the rate at which space communicates, so that there is a resonance that lets the
electron concentrate the kinetic energy of the photon's rotating dipoles. Those rotating dipoles were the rotating
dipoles of the original moving electron as it dropped from the activated to the ground state. The DP-arcs are conserved,
and they become the kinetic energy of the electron in its new activated orbital: striking the ground-state orbital,
the photon gives it more rotating-dipole DP-arc propulsion, which drives the electron and lets it carry the energetic
quantum the photon held — a quantum whose origin was the drop of the electron between shells.
