## Copilot's answers (summarized; a third party's text, not the founder's, not filed verbatim)

**First answer (all interactions; back-reaction):**
- "Interactions generically entangle" is defensible; "always" has exceptions that are genuinely separable (one alternative only; alternatives acting alike on
  the other system; product-to-product maps; entanglement transferred rather than increased).
- A mirror or crystal spreads the tie over many internal and environmental modes (distributed entanglement); when those become distinguishable, the coherence
  is inaccessible (decoherence), not necessarily gone.
- A later photon measurement gives the mirror no further kick (the momentum was exchanged at the interaction); it selects which correlated outcome applies.
  The mirror's own local statistics do not change, so nothing usable is sent. An Aspect-type claim would further need two measurement choices per side,
  spacelike separation and a Bell violation. The main obstacle is less the small recoil than whether the recoil states can be told apart and handled
  coherently.

**Second answer (an accounting system for the Group Entity):**
- One variable is being asked to do three jobs: group membership, the joint-state bookkeeping, and the correlated update. Separate them.
- Do not carry a map of every CP within the PSR. Each CP carries its own state, a group address, a phase/action variable, and shared "bond" records holding
  amplitude, relative phase, correlation channel and coherence; partners share a common index, so the connection is relational rather than addressed.
  A group label alone cannot distinguish entangled from unentangled states.
- The GP is a local, reversible transition rule acting on the records that arrive within its PSR; a Group Entity forms when a local interaction leaves a
  joint state that no longer factors. Copilot cites quantum cellular automata and tensor networks as an established mathematical class of this kind.
- Strongest caution: do not make entanglement a new force. The record should change the weights among displacements already allowed locally, not add an
  energy-bearing push, to keep no-signalling and avoid double-counting momentum.
- Four layers of accounting (CP record; GP neighbourhood; Group Entity network; what a local observer can reach) and a draft "Group Entity Postulate".
- Still unresolved: the bond's type and capacity, the exact GP rule, keeping shared records consistent without a faster-than-light signal, the Born rule,
  no-signalling, quantitative Bell correlations, recovery of field theory, and one falsifiable deviation.
