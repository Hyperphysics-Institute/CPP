# FORK-DISS-1 ruling — bath availability (founder, verbatim; 7 July 2026, Patch 2320)

**Context:** the Gate-1/B1 campaign's G4 (OPEN-DM-CAPTURE-1) reached FORK-DISS-1 at Patch 2318;
the fork was restructured at 2319 (D-B withdrawn on THEO-QM-8 grounds; question narrowed to: is
the Sea's sub-resolution DP configurational disorder available as the tracing bath for
encounter-scale near-zone energy — D-C yes / D-A no). The founder's ruling, **verbatim and
complete**:

> The answer, in short, a strong Yes. You are asking whether the processes at the CP and Grid
> Point/lattice levels exhibit local configurations that change in a nonlinear or chaotic way,
> which in turn affect the GP state, which affects the CP. The answer is yes. The SSV_abs at every
> point is unique and time-varying. The ZBW oscillation is one example of a process that produces
> an eternally time-varying state at a GP. Each different state at a GP produces a different effect
> on the CPs residing at that Moment on that GP. All CPs and GPs within its PSR are influenced by
> the local state, which changes dynamically depending on both the near and far fields. The state
> of the cycle will change the interaction with the local environment. The configuration of the CPs
> changes SSV_abs and SSV_net at each GP, which in turn changes the PSR at each GP each Moment and
> changes the trajectory of the CP on each GP. The instructions for action from the GPs will differ
> each Moment depending on the local signals from the near and far fields. Thus, any
> configurational change in the system, whether near or far, will affect the instructions that
> direct each CP by its GP of current location. The result is a local displacement (within the
> PSR), but a different vector magnitude and distance depending on the local and far fields. In
> short, the local system is extremely complex and locally modified every Moment, both because of
> transiting CP interactions and because of local ZBW oscillation. In your language, the Sea's
> sub-resolution DP configurational disorder IS available as the tracing bath for encounter-scale
> near-zone energy.

**Substance registered from the ruling (campaign's reading, kept distinct from the verbatim):**
(1) the bath's microstate is per-GP unique and time-varying every Moment (ZBW named as one
generator; transiting CP interactions as another); (2) the coupling loop is closed in both
directions — configuration → SSV_abs/SSV_net → PSR → CP trajectory → configuration — i.e., the
bath is *dynamically engaged*, not spectator; (3) sensitivity is to near AND far fields each
Moment; (4) the displacement response varies in vector magnitude and distance with the local state
— the microstate visibly modulates the dynamics at the per-Moment level.
