## §1 — The founder's statement (verbatim)

> this is not an issue in my framing. Every GP in the universe has a
> summation of the PSR shells that land on that GP. Everything
> arriving at that GP arrived at the speed of light (one PSR/Moment)
> from its surrounding GPs. There is no tension due to observers at
> the universe horizon distance away from each other. The local
> superimposition of DI-bits is simply what arrives at each GP each
> Moment. The disappearance of the universe volume behind the
> observability horizon is an emergent phenomenon arising because of
> the relative position between two points in the volume of the
> physical universe, as described before - it is the summation of
> the distance between points on the horizon, and the increment of
> expansion happening along each increment that sums to produce the
> disappearance of the universe from observability beyond its
> horizon, not a signal from far away influencing a point between
> that is locally confused/ambiguous/indeterminant because of the
> fact that there are observers at the observability distance
> horizon of a point between them. Such conflation mixes two
> categories: the local effect of PSR/Moment displacement and local
> summation, with the fact of an emergent phenomenon arising because
> of the separation of two objects at observability horizon
> distances. The two are not incompatible; the conflict arises when
> examining two phenomena which appear at different scales and then
> comparing them as though they were on the same scale.

## §2 — Rulings extracted

- **R-LOCAL-SUM:** the vacuum state at a GP is the LOCAL
  SUPERIMPOSITION of what arrives each Moment (PSR shells at one
  PSR/Moment). Nothing is negotiated, reconciled, or agreed between
  distant patches — there is no distant-agreement structure to
  ground a "class," a clique condition, or a mutuality requirement.
- **R-CATEGORY:** the worker's fork conflated two categories at
  different scales — the LOCAL phenomenon (per-Moment displacement
  and summation) and the EMERGENT phenomenon (horizon disappearance
  from summed expansion increments along the separation). The two
  are compatible; comparing them as same-scale objects manufactured
  a false conflict. The AGREEMENT-OBJECT FORK is DISSOLVED, not
  answered — neither horn exists.
- **R-HORIZON-EMERGENT (reaffirming 3059 R-DISCONNECT):** the
  horizon is the emergent sum of per-increment expansion along the
  path; not an agent, not a boundary of negotiation.
