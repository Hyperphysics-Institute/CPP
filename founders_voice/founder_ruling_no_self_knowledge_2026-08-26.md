# Founder ruling — no self-knowledge: the total-denominator commitment (26 Aug 2026)

**Captured verbatim per CONV-009. Registered at Patch 3446. Context:
UNIV-1's Q1b asked whether the SSV_abs in matter's displacement ratio
is the system's own (boost-attributed) stress or the total at the GP,
ambient included.**

## The founder's statement, verbatim

> In my conception of the CPP principles, I have not used any kind of
> self-knowledge as a force to isolate a system. The atom is as
> connected to the environment as the sun is to the earth. The
> intermediating connectors between environment and locale are the
> totality of DI-bits arising from all intermediate DPs, DP-entities,
> and DP-arcs carrying KE, DI-bits carrying from all sources, and CPs
> on GPs informing them of their presence to be broadcast as DI-bit
> information regarding the direction and magnitude of displacement
> from the GP_origin toward GP_PSR for the extent of the light
> influence and toward GP_SSV_net for the CP displacement.
> Measurements such as Sun-Earth effects will fall out of the
> proportional local effects of local and environmental magnitudes
> and direction.

## What this rules

1. **Attribution-free computation.** No GP distinguishes "the
   system's own" DI-bits from ambient ones; there is no self-knowledge
   in the substrate. The SSV_abs in every computation is the **total**
   at the GP. Q1b's first branch (a boost-attributed denominator) is
   **architecturally unavailable** to CPP — not wrong, impossible.
2. **New mechanism details on record:** DP-arcs as KE carriers among
   the DI-bit sources; displacement is *directed* — toward GP_PSR for
   light influence, toward **GP_SSV_net** for CP displacement (the
   SSV_net computation selects a target GP, giving direction and
   magnitude).
3. **The founder's conjecture, explicitly a conjecture:** observable
   consistency (e.g., Sun–Earth effects) "will fall out of the
   proportional local effects of local and environmental magnitudes
   and direction" — i.e., re-equilibration preserves the observable
   ratios. This is the claim S3a-α must now prove or refute; it is
   not assumed.
