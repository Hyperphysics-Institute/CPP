# FOUNDER RULING — FQ-1/FQ-2: THE ZBW OSCILLATION AS THE PAIR SEPARATION (dynamic, not static) AND THE eDP VACUUM (unit charges; qCPs sequestered into DM/baryons)

**Patch 3068 (11 Aug 2026). The two Stage-3 questions answered.
Registered verbatim.**

## §1 — FQ-1 answer (verbatim)

> the CPs are in constant ZBW oscillation. This is the foundation of
> the Dark Energy concept. They superimpose due to attraction, and
> then when in superposition, they move toward the environment toward
> the position of greatest attraction and repulsion, in exactly
> opposite directions. They go as far as they can until the
> attraction of the opposite charge recalls them, or they are
> attracted locally at their perigee and do a partner switch and
> oscillate with another CP of opposite charge, creating a new DP for
> as long as that pair lasts before partner switching separates them,
> etc.

## §2 — FQ-2 answer (verbatim)

> * The eCP and qCP have a unit electric charge. The qCPs have a unit
>   of strong charge, which is attractive to all qCPs.
> * The DP Sea has different compositions at different times during
>   the evolution of the universe.
> * In the beginning, shortly after the Big Bang, there were an equal
>   number of plus/minus eCPs and plus/minus qCPs (axiomatic; may not
>   be true, but this is symmetric).
> * The qCPs attract each other and form chains, end to end, as
>   discussed in the Dark Matter, early universe Founders Vision.
>   This takes out most of the qCPs, leaving largely eCPs with islands
>   of Dark Matter, baryonic Matter, and remnants of qCPs and eCPs
>   aggregated in different configurations. The DM is much heavier
>   than baryonic hydrogen, and hence more gravitationally attractive
>   than lighter baryonic mass below approximately carbon 5 qCP vs.
>   64 qCPs in DM rings.
> * In short, the aggregation of qCPs into DM and Baryons leaves the
>   DP Sea largely filled with eCPs, with the occasional DM and
>   baryonic island.
