# Founder ruling — the mass/number split of the DP-entity population (26 Aug 2026)

**Captured verbatim per CONV-009. Registered at Patch 3439. Context:
asked whether "higher concentration of heavier DP-entities in the
past" meant higher number concentration (which would contradict the
standing w = −1.00 ± 0.02 through the 3438 estimator) or higher mass
per entity at constant number concentration (which is consistent with
it).**

## The founder's answer, verbatim

> Yes, the average mass of the DP entities decreases with time, and
> the number of DP entities per unit volume stays constant. Thus, time
> slows more and more in the environment as average DP-entity mass
> rises, which corresponds to an increasingly dense space/DP Sea the
> farther back in time we go.

## The structure extracted

One population, two properties, two observables:

| property of the entity population | behaviour under expansion | observable it carries |
|---|---|---|
| **number concentration** n_eq = N_ent/V | **constant** (N_ent ∝ V) | **Λ** — via 3438's `w = −∂ln N_ent/∂ln V`, giving w = −1 |
| **average mass per entity** m̄ | **decreases with time** | **SSV_abs** — Sea mass density ρ_m = m̄·n_eq ∝ m̄, so higher in the past → slower local time |

This parallels the founder's 26 Aug thermal ruling exactly: a
conserved-or-reservoir-fed total redistributed over an entity
population growing ∝ V, so the *intensive* quantity (temperature
there, mass per entity here) falls while the *number concentration*
holds. Λ and SSV_abs are carried by different moments of the same
population, which is why constant dark energy and epoch-dependent
time rate can coexist without contradiction.

**Left open, deliberately:** the exponent of m̄(a). The founder's KE
argument used explicit conservation (fixed total redistributed →
T ∝ 1/N); for mass, the stated source is the reservoir, and whether
the total entity mass is conserved, grows, or shrinks fixes m̄(a)'s
slope. Not needed by the comoving pilot; needed by any quantitative
SSV_abs(a) claim. To be put to the founder when that lane opens.

**Status note.** The number-concentration constancy asserted here is
the theory's claim, and it is exactly the quantity the comoving
instrument measures. The ruling therefore does not pre-empt the run:
`w = −∂ln N_ent/∂ln V` remains the thing being tested, and this
ruling is what the theory predicts the test will return.
