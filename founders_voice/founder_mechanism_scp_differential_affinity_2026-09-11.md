## The mechanism, set out coherently

### 1. The SCP is the whole stack, and it has a composition
A **superimposed CP (SCP)** is the founder's term for **the entire aggregate of CPs resident on one GP** — *"whether they're eDPs or qCPs, I'm calling that a superimposed CP."*

Crucially, **an SCP is not a generic quantity of stuff.** Each one carries a **composition**: a proportion of eCPs and a proportion of qCPs. Since that proportion varies from site to site, **each SCP is effectively a distinct entity** — *"each superimposed CP will be essentially a different entity."*

### 2. Two classes follow: Q-dominant and E-dominant
Sorting SCPs by composition gives **Q-dominant SCPs** and **E-dominant SCPs**. The classes are not labels of convenience; they behave differently, and the difference is the mechanism.

### 3. The affinity is asymmetric — and this is the core
> **Q-dominant SCP ↔ Q-dominant SCP: strong affinity.** *"When Q-dominant SCPs are next to other Q-dominant SCPs, the signals present inside them tend to go toward each other, and we'll have a greater affinity."*
>
> **Q-dominant SCP ↔ E-dominant SCP: weaker.** *"You won't get the same level of Q-SCP-to-Q-SCP attraction as you'll get between Q and E."*
>
> **E-dominant SCPs are indifferent** — attracted equally to both classes.

> **"So the Q-dominant SCP is the asymmetric attractor, and produces a skew toward the formation of qCPs."**

**One class discriminates and the other does not.** That is the whole of the asymmetry, and it is what makes the aggregation non-random.

### 4. The bonding outcomes, and why the qCP channel persists
- **Two qCPs of opposite charge landing together** enter **ZBW oscillation** and form a **qDP**.
- **Two eCPs landing together** form an **eDP** — but *"those eDPs are not faithful."* They **pair-swap** readily with other eCPs bound as eDPs, so the eDP channel does not accumulate.
- **When pair-swapping happens with qDPs, the strong-force interaction binds all three together**, giving *"two minus qCPs on either side of a centre plus qCP."* **The qDP channel therefore builds persistent structure where the eDP channel churns.**

**So the skew has two reinforcing sources**: Q-dominant SCPs preferentially attract each other *(§3)*, and only the q-channel's products persist *(§4)*.

### 5. The consequence the founder draws
> **The unpiling is not purely random.** Aggregation is driven by composition, composition varies between sites, and one composition class is a discriminating attractor. **The evaporation is weighted, not Poisson.**
