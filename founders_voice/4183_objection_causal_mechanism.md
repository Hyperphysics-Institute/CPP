# Founder Objection — Spin Must Have a Causal Mechanism, and the Ratio Is Miscounted

**Patch:** 4183. **Date:** 20 September 2026. **Lane:** EW.
**Objects to:** Patch 4182's 10⁻⁴⁷ weighting.

---

## Verbatim

> I offered/conjectured the mechanism only because it seems there must be some way for the spin of
> CPs to affect other CPs. If there is no mechanism for one CP's spin to affect another CP's spin,
> then the CP has no causal mechanism to alter its spin, which, to me, doesn't sound physical.
>
> * Obviously, if the local CP contributes only 10⁻⁴⁷ of the total spin, then postulating the mixing
>   of the received GP spin with the local CP's spin has no significant effect. But I don't think
>   that is the correct proportion. Not every CP in the sphere surrounding a GP is contributing a
>   DI-bit to the GP_origin.
> * Only GPs at PSR will contribute.
> * And given that the environment's effect is at a distance versus the local effect, it seems like
>   the local CP weighting versus the GP summation may have a ratio greater than 1 of the total. If
>   this logic makes sense, then the CP vs. GP mixing ratio would probably have to be calibrated.

---

## Reading

**Two objections, and the first is the strongest argument for keeping A3′ that anyone has made.**

**(1) The causal argument.** If no CP's spin can affect another's, a CP's spin can never change by
any mechanism — it is conserved by having no interaction at all, which is not a physical property
but an absence of physics. **That is a principled reason to keep the channel, and it is better than
any of the four I constructed at 4181 §1.**

**(2) The counting objection.** He is right that I miscounted, and Patch 4183 shows the miscount is
worse than he says — by seventy-six orders, in his favour.
