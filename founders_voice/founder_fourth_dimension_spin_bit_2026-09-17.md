## 1. The written note (founder, verbatim intent, lightly formatted)

We propagate the CP's information between GPs by DI-bits, which carry the address of their origin GP.

- The GPs have a 3D address set locating them in 3D space, plus a fourth dimension carrying a
  plus-or-minus-unity value associated with every address.
- We shall call this value the **spin bit** until we discover a more appropriate use. The spin bit's value is
  the direction of the spin.
- The ± value of the spin bit is carried with the DI-bit messengers.
- The sum of the spin bits is tipped either + or − by the majority number of DI-bits arriving at each GP.
- A symmetric system, such as an electron orbital, is seeded by the single central unpaired CP, and this biases
  the spin-bit register of the local GP to + or − (a direction, not a charge, so *up* or *down* is the better
  generic name). The value propagates to the GPs on which the locally polarized DP cloud and orbital DP are
  imprinted. Each charged quark or lepton therefore has its entire spin-bit dimension locally enrolled as up or
  down, making it spin-polarized and reactive — because there is room for another orbital DP with the opposite
  spin bit in the same 3D space but a separate 4D space.
- Possibly only two spin orbitals can exist within the spherical charged polar volume, because the space can
  accommodate two spin bits of opposite polarity in the same place.
- For the spin bit to have any meaning, it must elicit a response. **What is that response?** What does having
  an up or down spin bit dictate? How does the CP move as a result of the 4D result?
- Is this where the exclusion principle happens? Perhaps when a CP lands on a GP carrying the same up or down
  spin bit as another CP, the same thing happens as in every ZBW cycle — perhaps a displacement. But that would
  be a special rule, so probably not; the ZBW oscillation should be enough to produce the effect.

## 2. The walk-and-talk (edited for coherence)

The issue is what happens to the 4D bit associated with the lattice, with the way the GPs are arranged, and with
the way the CPs move on them. The 4D lattice address is a four-element vector: three elements spatial, the fourth
a further spatial dimension. All four have an integer address, or a decimal address to a given number of
significant digits, which amounts to the same thing. The three spatial elements have the obvious meaning of
spatial distance. The fourth dimension is commonly taken to be time, but here it is chosen as spatial, so each
point has an address in the fourth dimension that allows tetrahedra along its edges between adjacent points.

The question is how that spatial lattice could be used as a metric for producing chirality, parity and
mirror-symmetry compliance with reality — and apparently it can. The further question is how that stands in a
causal relationship to the physical expression of Conscious Point aggregations and structures.

Perhaps the method of creating up and down is to use the address as even and odd. That is difficult, because
there is movement, so a fixed even-or-odd location seems unworkable. But perhaps only the evens are located, with
the odds being only the position in an orbital that is plus or minus, up or down, within a space that allows only
one entity in that space. So the ups and downs occupy the evens and odds, and a third spin type in that space
would not be allowed: the double-occupancy superimposition would put it into a ZBW-type oscillation, so it would
lose its attraction to the local environment and be pulled out of that fourth dimension. If both left, another
local DP would fill the space, and when it was occupied another would take its place. The extreme variability of
the orbital position follows, because of the superimposition on the fourth dimension.

The other half of the ZBW pair, once neutralized, becomes the new active orbital placeholder CP — the new orbital
entity. Each time there is a cancellation there is the ZBW excursion, and the member of the pair not involved in
the superimposition becomes the new orbital CP: the new electron going around the nucleus, for example.

This raises the question of whether there is really an orbital DP around the electron. It is possible that the
orbital DP is an artifact of the electron's orbiting: a DP arc set created around the motion of each −eCP acting
as the electron. An isolated electron might have an orbital DP pair, but in orbital capture that DP may be a
summation over the volume. So there is an effective orbital DP, and each would have its own four-dimensional
odd/even spin. Around the nucleus we have a spin and an orbit associated with the −eCP, and around the −eCP we
have the DP arcs — their own spin, created by the motion of the −eCP through the dipole. The −eCP with a DP
surrounding it is the picture of a static electron, but the electron is always moving through absolute space, so
there is always movement past the DP. Hence the DP arcs: pairs of DPs, each with its own plus and minus nature
and probably its own odd/even location once they begin spinning.

Unpolarized DPs probably have only a 3D character and can take any value of the 4D odd/even spectrum. Once they
begin spinning they would not share the same address, but they could share the same 4D address, which would lie
along the plane. One could be on the odd integers and the other on the even integers. The rule would be that
plus and minus eCPs have a 3D address which, if the motion is linear, can be the same address — the same name,
simply a line in 4D space. But if it is orbiting, there must be movement on that plane.

This relates to the W⁰ boson. There is a bracelet configuration of the plus and minus eCPs and qCPs forming the
W⁰, and each is in a ZBW relationship with its neighbours. *(Correction, founder's own:* the W⁰ is twelve hDPs,
half hDP-A and half hDP-B.*)* Each has a ZBW relationship with two of its own kind, so the plus and minus qCPs
have an oscillating relationship and the eCPs have an oscillating relationship with each other, while each qCP
and eCP also has an oscillating relationship across the two kinds. The relationship would probably be even–even
between the eCPs and between the qCPs, but perhaps odd between the eCPs and qCPs — giving a cyclic kind of
handedness associated with the qCP and the eCP. So we have an eCP–eCP linear relationship on the fourth
dimension, a qCP–qCP oscillatory relationship on the fourth dimension at a single value, and the eCP–qCP pair,
if both are at an odd fourth-dimensional integer, able to move along the fourth dimension in a way that does not
interfere and creates an orbital relationship between the two — somewhat analogous to the orbital relationship
between the −eCP and the nucleus of an atom.
