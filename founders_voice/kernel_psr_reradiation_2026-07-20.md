## The mechanism, restated (Opus paraphrase, for the charter)

1. **One-Moment kernel:** each CP's DI-bit signal reaches the shell of GPs
   at PSR distance in one Absolute Moment (rest-frame PSR = ℓ_P, glossary;
   PSR shrinks with SSV_abs per the registered SR-1 formula).
2. **Iteration:** influenced GPs re-radiate identically at the next Moment;
   the process repeats Moment after Moment — influence propagates as an
   iterated shell process, one PSR step per t_P.
3. **Summation:** the SSV (net and abs) at any GP is the holographic
   superposition of all successively re-radiated, environment-added DI-bit
   signals — the totality summed and converted per the Compute phase.
4. **Fading:** geometric — each individual signal dilutes as the inverse
   square over the surface of its expanding sphere of influence. The fading
   the founder names is 1/r² spreading, NOT an absorption per hop.
