# Founder working postulate — under a static tide the count inside the R-core stays uniform at cap out to the moved level set (5 Sep 2026, Session 162)

**Status:** verbatim founder text, registered Patch 3632. Minted as a **working postulate, not a ruling**: **P-COUNT-UNIFORM-TO-LEVEL-SET** (answers 3631 §6's picture question). The founder declined to rule on the picture; the census closure of 3631 §5 proceeds on this postulate and every result that depends on it carries the label.

Opus asked (3631): *under a static tide, is the count inside the R-core still uniform at cap all the way out to the moved level set — the bulge just contains more at-cap GPs, the waist fewer — or does count pile up on the bulge (a surface excess)?*

> "I don't have an informed opinion on this question. Please use this
> assumption as a working postulate."

*Opus (Patch 3632):* the assumption used is the first alternative — uniform at cap to the level set, no surface excess — which is what 3375 says of the static interior (register = min(demand, cap), demand > cap everywhere inside). It is the census closure's premise. If it fails (a surface excess), the datum δσ of 3631 §3 reopens as a free number. See `rcore_derivation/3632_relativistic_census_closure.md`.
