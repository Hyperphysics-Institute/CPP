## Elements, as stated (no content added)

1. The centroid has zero SSV gradient **from the W⁰ itself** (geometric-charge symmetry); it is not shielded from
   outside DI-bits, so a gradient from other sources is present there.
2. A −eCP in the centroid is in a *somewhat neutral* state, likened to the ZBW opposite-charge superposition.
3. The W⁰ configuration holds the kinetic energy carried by the DP-arcs **for one Moment**, as any medium carrying a
   speed-of-light correlation wave does; it vibrates, relaxes, and propels the −eCPs forward.
4. Passage is carried by inertia: the SF-6 DP-arc build-up in front and collapse behind.
5. The trajectory's direction is influenced by the CP's own A_I and by the A_i on each GP along the path, at each Moment.
6. The two −eCPs leave in a mixed state that resolves in each interaction.
