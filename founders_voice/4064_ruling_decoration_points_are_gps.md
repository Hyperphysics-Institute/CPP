# Founder ruling — decoration points are additional GPs; no interpolation

**Date:** 16 September 2026. **Context:** EW lane; asked at Patch 4064 after the 4063 chiral-cluster construction. **Capture convention:** CONV-009 (founder verbatim is the primary source
from which conjecture entries, frontier updates and dispatches derive); filed late at Patch 4094 — see the
capture-audit note below.

## Verbatim

> There are no conditions in the CPP program that restrict or require any number of GPs in a cell. I don't know what a decoration point is. The CPs are DPs - only in multiples; the CPs move between GPs; Any configuration of GPs would be acceptable; the decoration points sound like additional GPs. Adding more GPs to the lattice is acceptable. If decoration points are interpolations between GPs, that would require a significant reworking of the theory.

## What it settled

**Ruling:** a "decoration point" is an additional GP, and adding GPs to the lattice is acceptable; **interpolation
between GPs is not** — that would require reworking the theory. **Consequence (Patch 4064):** the 7,200-point
chiral structure U is *admissible* but was **not** promoted to a corpus object, because its handedness would be a
global geometric initial condition rather than a derived mechanism.
