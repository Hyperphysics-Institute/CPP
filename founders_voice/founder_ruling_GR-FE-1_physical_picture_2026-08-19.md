## THE FOUNDER'S ANSWERS (verbatim)

**Q1 — What is conserved, per Moment, at a lattice cell? When a DI-bit
broadcast crosses a Voronoi cell, what exactly does the cell's CP
perceive, compute, and displace — and what quantity is neither created

> PSR. The CPs do not PCP in the large sense; the GPs do the PCD cycle.
> (See Dark Energy, Dark Matter explanations of their detailed
> function.) The CPs perceive what the GPs have received, integrated
> from the local PSR of the local GPs, and present the results of that
> perception and computation to the CP on each GP, and the CP then
> displaces the distance associated with the SSV_net the GP has
> computed. The GPs, the CPs, and the DI-bits are all conscious points,
> and all are conserved.
>
> The GPs are stationary position-keeper Conscious Points whose
> positions locate the vertices that define the metric of space and
> form the 600-cell composing the background lattice of the universe,
> with approximately 10^30 GPs per PSR at STP at absolute rest.
>
> The CPs are the moving Conscious Points of 4 types, plus/minus eCP
> and plus/minus qCP, and compose the DP Sea as pairs of DPs in various
> aggregate forms (DM rings, DP entities, DP ribbons and aggregates,
> and momentarily free DPs) and fermionic mass, which is composed of an
> unpaired CP, polarized DPs, and DP aggregates. Photonic energy is a
> correlation wave/volume of DPs organized in electrical (separation)
> and magnetic (DP-arc rotation around the DP center) polarization.
>
> DI-bits are conserved messenger Conscious Points (carrying the
> SSV_net and SSV_abs, both EE_q and EE_e, and GP_origin address) and
> are reused each Moment. All GPs have the same number of DI-bits at
> the beginning of each Displace Cycle (having redistributed after

> dispersing the DI-bits radially toward the PSR in a fan-wise
> distribution, with all DI-bits ending up on GPs within 10% of the PSR
> (and then redistributing equally to all GPs prior to their next
> imprinting with data from GP_origin and next Displacement
> deployment).

**Q2 — What is the source, physically? Is the gravitating source the
polarization energy of the bound eDP cloud alone, or does kinetic/flux

> Thomas: There are only the above primitive processes. All
> gravitational effects emerge from those primitives.

> primary effect producing gravity. The reduced PSR reduces
> displacement per Moment, reducing the distance/Moment displaced for

> cause the center of mass to be bent toward an orbital tangent, to
> orbital, to falling depending on the gradient between the inner and
> outer radius of each CP composing the non-relativistic

**Q3 — How does the Sea "know" to be self-consistent? Is the analogous
statement local — each cell balances its own books each Moment — or
does it require the completed-broadcast (A3′) structure?**

> Thomas: There are no "knowing" to be self-consistent rules or
> processes governing gravity or geometry. The PCD cycle among the 3
> types of conscious points (GPs, CPs, DI-bits) is the primitive,
> engaging in what we named the "automata".
>
> Any effect that appears to "know" how to do a process is a high-level
> emergence of effect arising from the local configuration of CPs
> (i.e., masses) that produce the emergent gravitational effect from
> these primitive processes.
>
> Geometry matters because CPs (mass) move on GPs (space), progressing
> over Moments (time). CPs (mass) couple with other CPs on different
> GPs (space) by the communication of DI-Bits emitted by each of the
> GPs. The CPs inform the GPs of their presence, and then the GPs
> inform their local space of that presence, and other CPs respond to
> the presence of other CPs due to the messages relayed by the DI-bits.

> not instantaneous; the evolution of DI-bit messages over time and
> distance prevents everything from happening at once; a speed-of-light

> coupled with mass because mass influences the speed of light (PSR) in

> every GP (point in space), every GP's DI-bit total influence on its

> PCD cycle/Moment of time.

> configuration.
