**Founder's verbatim:**

> "These are just some thoughts. I don't have a clear picture of where handedness comes from. The
> players that are involved are the 4D Helicity bit and the 3D vector elements. All the CPs in a
> structure, like a neutron or W^0 boson, will have CPs bonded in a way that interacts to produce
> a handed effect. The bonding mechanism is mediated by GPs emitting DI-bits. You can read all
> about that in the axioms and founder's voice and reasoning that we have developed in the last
> round. I think handedness is emergent, not carried or imprinted; I think it derives from a
> combination of the 3D bits and the 4D Helicity bits from all the CPs composing the composite
> particle (e.g., W^0) and how the superimposition of the DI-bits from other CPs in this complex
> aggregate of CPs is bonded with the summation of the 3D bits and 4D Helicity bits. Somehow, the
> handedness arises from DI-bits adding together each Moment and producing the complex of bonded
> CPs. The particles (CPs) receive messages from the GP they land on, telling them how far to
> travel as an SSV_net, but they also carry a 4D-Helicity bit that interacts with messages the GP
> receives. The CP, upon landing on the GP, responds to the local GP's Helicity bit and to the
> GP's summation of the 3D messages it has gotten from its environment. Thus, the CP and the GP
> interact and tell the CP where to move. The entire structure of aggregated CPs is interrelated,
> and in some way the GP's helicity bit, the CP's helicity bit, and the Helicity bit carried by
> the DI-bit end up producing handedness, I think. The corpus provides more detail.
>
> What do you think?"
