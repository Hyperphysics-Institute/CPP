# Founder — Seat Rules: The Up Quark Bonds to 2 of 4 Vertices, the Down Quark to 4 of 4

**Patch:** 4270. **Date:** 23 September 2026. **Lane:** EW → strong (TODO-4234-DELTA).
**Answers:** Patch 4269 §4 (does the down quark ever sit on a minus vertex?).
**Answer:** the down quark may take turns at every vertex, including the empty +eCP vertex when the neutron is free.
This depends on its linear −eCP localising on the side of its +qCP core away from a minus vertex. The up quark never
bonds to the plus vertices (+qCP, +eCP), so it uses 2 of 4 seats; the down uses 4 of 4. Asked *"What do you
think?"* — answered at 4270 §2.
