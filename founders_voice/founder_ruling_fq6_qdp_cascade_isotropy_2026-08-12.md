## §2 — FQ-6.2 answered (does the reservoir gravitate): verbatim

> The ground-state reservoir of eDPs does not gravitate. It is
> mutually attractive as it creates a zero SSV_abs gradient with
> distance. The isotropic nature of the ground state reservoir
> prevents mutual gravitation. The qDP structures of all types will
> gravitate, to a lesser extent for the small light qDP assemblies,
> and to a greater extent for the larger assemblies such as the DM
> rings. The force opposing migration and concentration will be the
> brownian/Thermal collision forces for the lighter qDP species, and
> with those eDP thermal forces being negligible for the heavier qDP
> assemblies, allowing them to migrate and concentrate in the
> galactic volumes and very little in the intergalactic volumes.
