## Copilot's answers (summarized; a third party's text, not the founder's, not filed verbatim)

- **The crystal:** β-barium borate (BBO), which makes photon pairs (signal and idler) by spontaneous parametric down-conversion.
- **Bell tests:** Aspect (1982) and the loophole-free tests of 2015 rule out local hidden variables. They show correlations no local account can give, but
  no faster-than-light signal: each side's own results look random, and the pattern appears only when the records are compared. What the correlation means
  physically depends on the interpretation.
- **Mach-Zehnder:** one photon in two paths at once (one quantum, no half-photon in the other arm). This can be written as entanglement between the two path
  modes, but it is not a photon split into two. BBO gives two real photons. Interference recombined in one place is not a Bell test.
- **Suggestion to CPP:** explain single-particle coherence and two-particle correlations together, without allowing usable faster-than-light signals.
  Proposed test: change one arm of a large interferometer at an event spacelike-separated from the photon's arrival in the other arm.
