## Reading

A realist criterion: an axiom's job is to say what *is*, not only what can be measured. Coherent, and
consistent with the programme — the substrate itself is unobservable.

**But the ruling is conditional, and the condition is now the whole test.** *"If the argument
describes reality"* — the argument in question is the structural one, *spin is a source and had no
channel*. With observability set aside, **that argument is the only thing holding the channel up**,
and Patch 4178 tests it.
