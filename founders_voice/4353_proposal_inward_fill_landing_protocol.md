> The empirics point toward a solid band of one DI-bit per GP in a band inward from the PSR. The DI-bit advancement
> from GP_origin to GP_PSR protocol is as follows:
>
> - At each submoment of the displace phase, 12 DI-bits leave the GP_origin and travel toward the GP_PSR.
> - Upon reaching the GP_PSR, each DI-bit stops and occupies that GP_PSR.
> - If they have not reached the GP_PSR and land on an occupied GP_PSR from that GP_origin family, the DI-bit moves
>   inward and occupies the most radial position available in that set of GPs.
