# 4109 — Founder picture: CPs declare to GPs; GPs sum over the PSR shell

**Date:** 18 Sep 2026. **Session:** 233. **Patch:** 4109.
**Source:** Thomas's answer to the sharpened R-F3 (per-ARC vs per-VECTOR-SUM).

**Founder's verbatim:**

> "The CPs inform the GPs of their presence and their spin/(Helicity bit?). The GPs emit
> DI-bits to the GPs at the PSR radius in a 10% width. The GPs sum the contributions from all
> the GPs (including the Helicity bit - a term I have not used, but which I assume is the 4D
> vector element) which are at the 1 PSR radius. The CP carrying the 4D element/Helicity bit
> is a new concept that has never been used. Let's make sure this concept fits and calibrate
> it to what works rather than using my concept as the correct picture. I think this is right,
> but let's compare it with the corpus."

**What this answers.** The per-ARC vs per-VECTOR-SUM dichotomy posed at Patch 4108 is
**neither**. The bit is declared **per CP, per Moment, to its own GP**; the **summation happens
at the RECEIVING GP** over its PSR shell. That is A3′ + AP-4 as already ratified — the founder's
picture is the existing broadcast mechanism, not a new one.

**What the founder flagged, and the calibration he ordered.** He names "the CP carrying the 4D
element/Helicity bit" as a concept never used, declines to assert it, and directs that it be
calibrated against the corpus rather than taken as correct. Patch 4109 executes that.
