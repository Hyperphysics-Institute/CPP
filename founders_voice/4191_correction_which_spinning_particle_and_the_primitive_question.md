> the magnetic field.
>
> We knew this spin-magnetic-field relationship when we started trying to solve the chirality
> problem.  And it didn't work because the magnetic field lacked the handedness relationship needed

> produce handedness at the CP, GP, and DI-bit level, which is why we developed A3' and that family

> of theorems in gravity or other series.  So the real question is whether there is any fundamental
> axial vector like A_i at the DI-bit/LSP level that carries spin in a handed way, or whether the
> handed axial vector is emergent from some other effect.  My bet is that the A_i axial vector we
> worked out is primitive and that other effects emerge from it, but I might be wrong.  I think we
> determined that handedness/chirality was a primitive in the Chirality lane (you can check that out
> in the corpus).
