## The claim — what the magnetic field actually is

The magnetic field is not a fundamental field. It is the name for the **rotational response of the Dipole Sea to a moving charge** — and the handedness of the world, the thing the right-hand rule encodes, is not a twist stamped into the universe at its birth. It is generated at the Moment of movement, by every charge acting on every dipole — both locally (within a Planck distance $\ell_P$) and in the far field (on the DPs at each distance, and at every Moment after the initiating motion).

## The mechanism, step by step

1. **Space is full of dipoles.** The DP Sea — eDPs and qDPs — fills all of space. It is not a backdrop; it is a responsive medium with structure. (Note: the qDPs exist as chains in the background of space / DP Sea. Note 2: hDPs — hybrid DPs, **type A: plus qCP and minus eCP; type B: minus qCP and plus eCP** — exist as free entities only in the early, high-temperature universe. Below the freezing temperature, all hDPs freeze into hTetras — the combination of a type-A and a type-B hDP into a tetrahedral structure. The hDP is the foundational backbone of baryons.)

2. **A bare charge moves through it.** Take a single moving charge — say a −eCP travelling along a line. As it passes a dipole in the Sea, it acts on that dipole's two poles unequally: it draws the *opposite* pole toward itself and pushes the *like* pole away.

3. **The dipole turns.** Each Moment, the two poles are pushed and pulled from a different distance and with a different force magnitude, so each pole moves in its own polar plane (angle $\theta$ and radius $r$) — nearer or farther — around the dipole's centre. The two poles move in different, opposite senses each Moment about that centre, so the CPs *rotate about the dipole's centre*. The dipoles in the surrounding Sea undergo no net translation; each one's poles rotate around its own centre.

4. **The sense of the turn is fixed by direction and polarity.** Which way the dipole pivots — clockwise or counter, in the moving charge's frame — is determined entirely by (a) the direction the charge is moving and (b) the sign of the charge. Point your thumb along the charge's motion; your fingers curl in the direction of the rotation vector (the vector perpendicular to the plane in which the CPs rotate about the dipole axis). There is no freedom in this push–pull of the CPs in the DP. The CPs rotate around the DP axis between them, and **the direction of the angular-momentum vector of that CP rotation (perpendicular to the plane of rotation) is the direction of the magnetic-field vector.**

5. **All the dipoles around the path turn together.** Every dipole in the spherical volume around the charge's line of travel pivots in the same coordinated way, wrapping the line of motion in a toroidal spin of the Sea at every radial distance.

6. **That curl *is* the magnetic field.** What we have always called the magnetic field — and assigned a direction to by the right-hand rule — is exactly the result of this coordinated rotation of the DP Sea around a moving charge. The right-hand rule is not a postulate of nature; it is the *consequence* of "opposite pole drawn in, like pole pushed out, around a centre."

7. **Reverse the charge, reverse the field.** Flip the sign of the moving charge and the attractions and repulsions invert, so the dipoles turn the other way, and the field reverses. The field's direction is downstream of the charge's polarity, not an independent fact of the world.

8. **It acts, and it induces.** A region of coherently spinning dipoles pushes and pulls on free charges — which is why a magnet moves iron (iron has free electrons for the spinning Sea to act on). And the effect runs both ways: a *moving* DP Sea passing a *stationary* free charge induces motion in that charge, exactly as a moving charge induced rotation in the Sea. That reciprocity is induction.

## Where handedness comes from

This is the part that matters for the chirality arc. The usual account of why the world has a handedness is that the universe was born with a slight twist — a primordial, inflationary asymmetry — and that every chiral phenomenon downstream is an echo of it. The motion induced in the CPs of each DP in the Sea may be the mechanism underlying the empirically observed chiral preference across many phenomena.

This mechanism removes the need for the primordial twist. **Chirality is what charges-moving-through-dipoles produce.** The centre of each dipole is the rotation axis; the rotational sense relative to the charge's motion is one way or the other *purely by polarity*; and that is the whole of it. You do not need a handedness installed at the beginning of time that then influences all electromagnetic interactions throughout all subsequent time. You need only charge, dipoles, and motion, and the handedness follows necessarily — and reverses cleanly when the charge does.

The same idea reaches into the weak sector. In the beta-decay mechanism worked out alongside this, the neutrino's near-total left-handedness is generated when a moving −eCP spins the eDPs of a W⁰ bracelet as it glances past. The passage of a charge carrying sufficient action (½ ℏ of angular momentum) can impart a quantum of angular momentum to one DP pair and exert a directional force on the now-spinning pair, freeing it from the W boson; that freed, spinning pair becomes the electron neutrino. The helicity of the neutrino is that of a spinning coin with its face pointing forward along the direction of the neutrino's motion — matching **Goldhaber's** observed (purely left-handed) neutrino helicity.

In beta decay there is enough linear momentum to liberate a *second* spinning DP from the W boson. This second eDP is captured by the −eCP that was internal to the down quark (in ZBW oscillation) and becomes the *spin* of the electron. The electron's orbital eDP has two components: an inner +eCP in ZBW oscillation, and an outer −eCP in a roughly circular orbit. The handedness observed in beta-decay neutrinos is a locked-in *sign*, set by exactly the polarity rule above. If the curl-wraps-velocity geometry here can be shown to force not just the *direction* of the dipole rotation but its full *alignment* along the charge's motion, then the same mechanism that makes the magnetic field would also force the *maximal* longitudinal handedness of the neutrino — i.e., it would derive parity violation. One mechanism, two payoffs: the magnetic field, and the handedness of the weak force.
