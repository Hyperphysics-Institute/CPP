## §2 — Worker reading of the statement (for the record)

1. **Affirmation of exhaustiveness as ratified.** The founder affirms
   the Q-B clause's exclusive-and-exhaustive division for physical
   phenomena: {origin address, E, S} is the complete messenger content,
   and anything further "would have to be added later" — i.e., by
   future amendment through the same proposal→endorsement→ratification
   process, exactly the discipline the clause encodes. No softening of
   the clause was requested; the text enacts as written.
2. **A named future-amendment trigger (registered here, NOT minted as
   an open item).** The founder foresees that consciousness-associated
   parameters — specifically the linking of Conscious Points into
   larger conscious point groups — may exist, while stating plainly
   that he does not presently know how to identify, quantify, describe,
   or justify such a parameter within the memory, identification, or
   processing space of the CPs as described. Per the founder's own
   framing this is a foreseen possibility without a describable
   mechanism, so no registry ID is minted and no obligation attaches;
   this paragraph is the canonical anchor for any future proposal that
   would extend the payload or the CP state space on
   consciousness-grouping grounds. Any such proposal cites this
   registration and proceeds as an AP-5-or-later amendment; it does not
   reopen AP-4.
3. **Scope of the ratified claim.** "Adequate to describe physical
   phenomena" is the ratified scope. The exhaustiveness clause binds
   the physics; it does not assert metaphysical completeness of the CP
   description, and the founder's statement records that distinction.
