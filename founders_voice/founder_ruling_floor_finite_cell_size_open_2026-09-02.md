# Founder rulings — the floor is FINITE; the cell's size is OPEN (2 Sep 2026, Session 161, GR lane)

**Status:** verbatim founder text, registered at Patch 3370, in answer to the two
physical-picture questions escalated by the CONV-038 adjudication (Patch 3369 §4).
Rulings minted: **R-FLOOR-FINITE**, **R-CELL-SIZE-OPEN**.

**The questions as put (Patch 3369 §4):** (1) at the cap, does the perception
sphere go to zero (SR-1 App. A.5/D.4 "collapses (r_eff → 0)") or to a finite floor
(SR-1 Padé; GR-1c)? (2) is the Voronoi cell an invariant physical object whose
volume is a fact about the lattice, or is `SSV_crit` a unit-setting convention?

---

## 1. R-FLOOR-FINITE

> "When the cell is full, it shrinks to a minimum size, as this is required to
> meet the criteria of reality."

*Opus note:* SR-1's collapse statement (`r_eff → 0` at `SSV_crit`, App. A.5 Step 2
and App. D.4) is superseded as physics by this ruling; the Padé working form (finite
floor) is the live SR-1 claim. **SR-1 corrigendum owed (SR lane; registered in
`frontier_sectors/SR.md`, not enacted by the GR lane).** The zero-floor branch of
CONV-038 Q5 is closed. `c_* = c/2` survives *if* the floor is `l_P/2`; the value
remains open (below).

## 2. R-CELL-SIZE-OPEN

> "I do not have a conception of why the cell is a particular physical size. I do
> not know of a logical, natural reason for the cell being of the size that it is.
> It seems logically/empirically/axiomatically necessary, but I don't have a
> geometric boundary or argument to justify why the cell's boundary is the size it
> is."

*Opus note:* the founder declares no physical picture for the cap's magnitude. That
makes OPEN-GR-FLOOR-1(c) — the map `SSV_crit → u` — a genuinely open problem with
no founder-side input to draw on, and it means **neither** `u_max = 1` (Buchdahl
attainment) **nor** `u_max = α_geom` (SR-1 at ε = 1) has priority by authority.
What the record CAN say (Patch 3370 verify): Buchdahl bounds the cap from above
(`u ≤ 1`); the requirement that a GW150914-class remnant have a light ring bounds it
from below (`u > 4 − √12 = 0.536`); SR-1's insphere value 0.2444 is therefore
**empirically excluded** as a physical cap, and its circumradius value 0.5594
clears the bound by 4% while shrinking the echo cavity to ~0.14 ms. The founder's
"criteria of reality" phrase is, quantitatively, that window.
