> * Thomas: The issue is one of probability. The chance of one or the other in the DI-bit shell increases with SSV_abs,

> [3. Which is the speed of light? …]
>
> * Thomas: The speed of light is the progression of one PSR per Moment. The GP_PSR and all the GPs in the DI-bit Shell
>   will receive the signal. Each GP in the DI-bit shell will receive the message and will pass it on as their signal in
>   the next Moment. Thus, the PSR per moment is the rate at which the influence is traveling to the next set of GPs at
>   the next PSR distance.
> * The presence and movement of the DP's CPs due to the DI bit thickness will have an effect on the PSR, but it will be
>   minimal in ordinary space, and will get increasingly significant as the space gets more densely filled with mass:
>   compressed mass (white dwarfs), subatomic mass (neutron stars), eCP-qCP mass (black holes).
> * The effect of the movement and presence of DPs in space and the back-reaction of it on the PSR is probably the
>   source (or at least a contributing source) of the mu-epsilon of space.
