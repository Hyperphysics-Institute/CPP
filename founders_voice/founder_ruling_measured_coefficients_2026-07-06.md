# Founder methodological ruling — the measured-coefficient (Galilean-layer) discipline (6 July 2026)

**Context:** DM campaign, D5/F3′ juncture — two "derivation debts" (the m_s = χ·ħc/r_c gap; the multipole
order of the colour-neutral coupling) stood as promotion gates after DM-1 v1.3 shipped 5/5.

**Founder (Thomas Lee Abshier, ND), verbatim:**

> "The fact is, none of us knows exactly what is going on inside the DM rods. I am betting that the candidate
> is correct, but beyond that I am much less certain about the DP density, the lattice occupancy, the ZBW
> oscillation, the cancellation, and superposition. I look at this more as a Galileo-type computation of the
> Gravitational law than as a strictly vision-based prediction. I would like the data to dictate the physics
> at the deepest level of complexity and density. The high-level structure will dictate the first- and
> second-order effects, but at some level the predictions will break down. My claim is not on prediction at
> that level. I'm willing to let the data inform us of the physics, the complex summation of effects at a
> dimension that conventional physics does not even believe exists. Use the data to determine what is
> present, leaving the candidate as the general pattern and the mathematical detail as the fine structure."

> "I would of course like to derive the coefficients from first principles, and I think that will be possible
> from the framework of structure and substance that we have already built, but I think using the structure
> and substance of the CP paradigm at this point is premature. ... This is the first time we have retreated
> from the derivation-level method, but it may be the right time to relax the vision enough to develop a deep
> theory consistent with the CPP program and the complete theory. That would then leave us with an
> empirically defensible theory that is complete on every level except the deeper organizing principle that
> yielded the coefficients by axiomatic argument to theorem. I think that coefficient-free, derivation-only
> layer will fall faster, with the coefficients in place and the unified theory confirming them by
> overdetermination."

**Adopted as CONV-004** (todolist.md): measured/derived/conjectured tagging; overdetermination requirement;
scope limited to derivation-premature sectors; derivation layer retained as the standing goal with the
tag-firewall against circular confirmation. DM sector adopts immediately (the two debts re-read as the
campaign's first two substrate MEASUREMENTS: m_s ≈ 7.76 MeV; S_c ∈ [0.005, 0.05] ∼ R_N/R_s); other sectors
case-by-case at founder discretion. Recorded by Opus, Patch 1886.
