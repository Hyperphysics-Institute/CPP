# Founder idea — the fourth dimension as an axis of oscillation, possibly driven by polarity

**Date:** 17 September 2026. **Context:** EW lane; assessed at Patch 4089. **Capture convention:** CONV-009 (founder verbatim is the primary source
from which conjecture entries, frontier updates and dispatches derive); filed late at Patch 4094 — see the
capture-audit note below.

## Verbatim

> My walk-and-talk transcript explored the concept of the 4th dimension as an axis of oscillation. The problem with that idea is: what is causing the motion along that axis? Maybe the plus/minus polarity of the CP would produce the movement along that axis. I'm not clear on this path, but it's an idea. Is this avenue worth pursuing?

## What it settled

**Assessment (Patch 4089):** the **driver fails** — displacement along w is P-even and polarity is P-even. The
**oscillation succeeds**: a w-oscillation phase-locked to 3D motion is the second rotation plane that carries the
pseudoscalar, with no new variable. Geometry forced to (w, direction-of-travel) × transverse spin = **helicity**.
**Sharp constraint:** exactly zero at a 1:1 frequency ratio, so the oscillation must be **detuned** from the ZBW —
satisfied by SPIN-1's derived 2√2 ratio (Patch 4090).
