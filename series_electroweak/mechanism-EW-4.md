## Part 1: Maximum Frustration → Maximum Mass

**Step 1 — λ = −(1+φ) is the most frustrated state of the 600-cell spectrum.**
The most negative eigenvalue of the 600-cell adjacency matrix corresponds to the maximum anti-correlation state: adjacent Grid Points have opposite-sign DI-bit amplitudes. Every hDP on the subgraph is in anti-phase with every nearest neighbour. This is maximum frustration — the highest-energy configuration of the lattice. The Higgs-like resonance corresponds to this maximally frustrated state, which is why it is the heaviest of the three electroweak bosons.

**Step 2 — Maximum frustration maps to the dodecahedral 20-vertex shell.**
The stable closed subgraph for λ = −(1+φ) is the dodecahedral 20-vertex shell: 12 pentagonal faces, 20 vertices, fully closed with A₅ symmetry. CP placement: 5 each of +eCP, −eCP, +qCP, −qCP, net Q = 0. The icosahedron-dodecahedron duality connects the Z (12-vertex icosahedron) to the Higgs (20-vertex dodecahedron): every face of the icosahedron becomes a vertex of the dodecahedron.

**Step 3 — A₅ symmetry forces spin 0 (scalar).**
The dodecahedron's full symmetry group is A₅ (alternating group of order 60, equivalently the icosahedral rotation group). A₅ has no preferred axis — it contains no subgroup isomorphic to Z₂ that would select a polarisation direction. Without a preferred axis, the Higgs-like resonance cannot have a non-zero spin projection in any direction, forcing it to be a scalar (J = 0).

## Part 2: No Stable Boson Between Z and Higgs
