## Open Problems Registered

**OPEN-P-EW-3 (Loop density factor from 4D projection):** The loop density factor ℓ_Z reduces from the ideal geometric estimate 1.437 to the effective Monte Carlo value 1.2. This reduction is attributed to stereographic projection losses but has not been computed analytically. Resolving this would tighten the m_Z/m_W prediction.

**OPEN-P-EW-4 (m_Z/m_W ratio from geometry):** The loop density factor alone gives m_Z/m_W ≈ 1.20; the observed ratio is 1.134. The 5% discrepancy between loop-density-predicted and observed mass ratio is the central EW-3 open problem.
