## CPP vs SM Higgs Mechanism

The most philosophically significant EW-4 result is what it does not contain: no Higgs field, no vacuum expectation value, no spontaneous symmetry breaking. The SM Higgs mechanism is an elaborate structure; CPP replaces it with the dodecahedral shell confinement energy. This is not merely a different description of the same physics — it is a different physical picture. In CPP there is no sense in which SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y is a symmetry that gets broken; instead the three bosons arise with their observed masses from the eigenvalue topology and have always had those masses.

The current limitation: because η is calibrated, the individual mass predictions are reproduced rather than derived. Until OPEN-P-EW-1 is solved, the CPP account of why m_H = 125 GeV rather than some other value is "because we calibrated η to match it" — which is honest but incomplete.
