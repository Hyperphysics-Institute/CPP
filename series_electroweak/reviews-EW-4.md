## Review 1: Internal Review (March 2026)

**Overall verdict:** Higgs topology is cleanly assigned; scalar nature from A₅ is the paper's strongest result; mass is reproduced not derived; "no boson between Z and H" is the most directly testable prediction; CPP account of electroweak symmetry breaking is philosophically coherent but structurally different from the SM.


### S1 — Strength: J^PC = 0^++ from A₅ Symmetry Is a Genuine Derivation

The spin J = 0 of the Higgs follows rigorously from the A₅ symmetry of the 20-vertex dodecahedral shell. A₅ has no preferred axis → no polarisation direction → J = 0. The C = +1 parity follows from the equal distribution of opposite-charge CPs across the shell. The P = +1 follows from the dodecahedron's inversion symmetry. J^PC = 0^++ is therefore derived, not postulated. This is a genuine CPP result: the SM must postulate the Higgs spin as part of the field content; CPP derives it from topology.


### C1 — OPEN: Shell Density Factor s_H Not Analytically Derived

**The concern:** s_H ≈ 1.4 (effective Monte Carlo) vs s_H^ideal ≈ 1.014 (geometric estimate from icosahedron-dodecahedron duality). This is a 38% discrepancy attributed to 4D projection effects but not derived analytically.

**Assessment:** Same category as the Z loop density factor (OPEN-P-EW-3). The dodecahedral shell is the more complex geometry (20 vertices vs 12) and the 4D projection correction is larger. Until s_H is derived analytically, the Higgs mass remains reproduced rather than derived even in the structural sense.

**Status: OPEN** — registered under OPEN-P-EW-3.


### C2 — OPEN: CPP Account of Electroweak Symmetry Breaking Is Incomplete

**The concern:** The SM explains why m_W and m_Z are non-zero (Higgs mechanism: W and Z eat Goldstone bosons from the VEV) while the photon stays massless (unbroken U(1)_EM). CPP's account is topological (closed structures are massive, open paths are massless) but does not explain why the symmetry breaking pattern is SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y → U(1)_EM rather than some other pattern.

**Assessment: VALID — structural gap**

CPP explains the individual masses and spin/charge quantum numbers of the three bosons, but it does not derive the symmetry breaking pattern from geometry. The pattern SU(2)_L × U(1)_Y → U(1)_EM means the photon remains massless and acquires the form γ = cos θ_W W₃ + sin θ_W B. In CPP, the photon is an open-path eDP (massless by topology), but the specific linear combination of W₃ and B that corresponds to the photon is not derived — it is taken from the Weinberg angle.

**Status: OPEN** — connected to OPEN-P-EW-4 (mass ratios from eigenvalues).
