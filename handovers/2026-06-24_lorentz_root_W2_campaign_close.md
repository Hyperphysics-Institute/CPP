## 3. Candid appraisal — significance, and the caveats (recorded for the record, not a triumphal note)

**What it means.** The campaign ran CPP into one of physics' hardest walls — Lorentz invariance, tested to
parts in 10¹⁷ and beyond — and CPP survived: W2 means consistency with the entire body of Lorentz tests,
reached through the substrate's own icosahedral/golden-ratio geometry rather than by assumption. Had the
answer been W3, CPP would be in immediate empirical tension. So this is a genuine viability result.

**Where to temper it.** The strongest pieces are *correct applications of already-known results*, not new
physics: regular lattices give Lorentz only in the continuum limit (textbook lattice field theory); exact
Lorentz on a discrete set wants randomness (the causal-set lesson); icosahedral quasicrystals have isotropic
elasticity (established quasicrystal theory). What the campaign did well was *place* CPP's substrate in a
recognized structure class (icosahedral quasicrystal; the H₄/E₈ family) and show the known theorems land it
in the survivable world. That is a careful placement/consistency exercise, not a discovery.

**The three caveats that must travel with the result.** (1) **Framework-conditional** — "W2" means *if the
substrate is as the corpus specifies (A3′, C3, φ-self-similar/aperiodic reading), then W2*, not a
frame-independent fact. (2) **Proxy dynamics** — the load-bearing Round-5 inference (that the actual
broadcast dispersion inherits the substrate's icosahedral symmetry) is reasonable but is an *inference*; the
verify scripts model the broadcast as a simple hopping sum, a proxy. The full PCD dynamics with the Φ/V/Q
multiplet and the real kernel were **never computed** — a genuine from-dynamics derivation of the dispersion
is still owed. (3) **Unfalsifiable by construction** — the residual sits at ~10⁻³⁰ Planck, so this is a
consistency win, not a discriminating test; it does not distinguish CPP from standard physics, and it still
leans on C3 and the open OPEN-SR-9 (from-substrate c_photon).

**Net:** bank this as a foundational **viability** result — CPP clears the Lorentz bar from substrate
structure, and we understand *why* (icosahedral/golden-ratio geometry) — with the from-PCD-dynamics
dispersion derivation and OPEN-SR-9 flagged as the real remaining work, not papered over.
