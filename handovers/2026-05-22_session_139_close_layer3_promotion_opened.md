## §5 Condensed B.1.q4 Layer 3 algebraic derivation summary

*This §5 preserves the substantive Layer 3 derivation work authored pre-conversation-compaction in Session 139. The full 538-line document was preserved at `/home/claude/F1_layer3_b1q4_algebraic_derivation.md` in the Session 139 window but is ephemeral to that window. §5 is the condensed form sufficient for re-authoring the full document if Patch 0541 = G.b is chosen.*

### §5.1 Setup and primitives

**Mechanism A primitive** (CPP axiom):
$$r(\hat{e}_{ab}) = r_0(1 + \delta \cdot \hat{e}_{ab} \cdot \hat{n})$$

**At first order in $\delta$**, the net DI-bit current at vertex $v$ is:
$$\vec{j}_{DI}^{net}(v) = 2 r_0 \delta \sum_{j=1}^{12} (\hat{u}_j^v \cdot \hat{n})\hat{u}_j^v + \mathcal{O}(\delta^2)$$

where $\hat{u}_j^v$ are the 12 unit edge vectors from $v$ to its first-shell neighbors.

**600-cell first-shell geometric primitives:**
- **G1**: $\hat{v} \cdot \hat{v}_j = \cos 36° = \phi/2$ uniform across all 12 first-shell neighbors.
- **G2**: The 12 first-shell neighbors form a regular icosahedron in the 3D hyperplane perpendicular to $\hat{v}$ (the link of $v$ in the 600-cell).

### §5.2 Identity I1 — 600-cell first-shell vertex-projection

**Statement**: $\hat{u}_j^v \cdot \hat{v} = -1/(2\phi)$ uniform across all 12 first-shell neighbors.

**Derivation**:
From G1 + chord length $|v_j - v|^2 = 2 - 2 \cdot \phi/2 = 2 - \phi = 1/\phi^2$ (using $\phi^2(2-\phi) = (\phi+1)(2-\phi) = 2\phi + 2 - \phi^2 - \phi = \phi + 2 - (\phi+1) = 1$):

$$\hat{u}_j^v \cdot \hat{v} = \frac{(v_j - v) \cdot v}{|v_j - v|} = \frac{(v_j \cdot v) - 1}{1/\phi} = \phi(\phi/2 - 1) = \phi^2/2 - \phi = (1 - \phi)/2 = -1/(2\phi)$$

(using $\phi^2 = \phi + 1$ and $1/\phi = \phi - 1$).

### §5.3 Identity I2 — perpendicular-magnitude

**Decomposition**: $\hat{u}_j^v = \alpha\hat{v} + \beta\hat{w}_j^v$ with $\alpha = -1/(2\phi)$, $\beta = |\perp_j^v| = \sqrt{2+\phi}/2$.

**Statement**: $|\perp_j^v|^2 = (2+\phi)/4$ uniform.

**Derivation**:
$$|\perp_j^v|^2 = 1 - \alpha^2 = 1 - \frac{1}{4\phi^2} = \frac{4\phi + 3}{4(\phi+1)}$$

Verify $(2+\phi)(\phi+1) = 4\phi + 3$:
$$(2+\phi)(\phi+1) = 2\phi + 2 + \phi^2 + \phi = 3\phi + 2 + (\phi+1) = 4\phi + 3 \quad \checkmark$$

Therefore $|\perp_j^v|^2 = (2+\phi)/4$, and $\beta = \sqrt{2+\phi}/2$.

The $\{\hat{w}_j^v\}$ are 12 unit vectors of a regular icosahedron in the 3D hyperplane perpendicular to $\hat{v}$ (per G2).

### §5.4 Identity I3 — icosahedron central symmetry (elementary)

**Statement**: $\sum_{j=1}^{12} \hat{w}_j^v = 0$.

**Derivation**: Regular icosahedron has 12 vertices in 6 antipodal pairs $(\hat{w}_j^v, -\hat{w}_j^v)$ via the central inversion $-\mathbb{1} \in I_h$ acting on the icosahedron. Pairwise sums vanish; total sum vanishes. □

**Does NOT require Schur's lemma** — elementary from central symmetry.

### §5.5 Identity I4 — icosahedron tensor sum isotropy via Schur's lemma

**Statement**: $\sum_{j=1}^{12} \hat{w}_j^v \otimes \hat{w}_j^v = 4 P_{\perp v}$ where $P_{\perp v}$ is the projection onto the 3D hyperplane $H_\perp^v$ perpendicular to $\hat{v}$.

**Derivation**:
Let $T \equiv \sum_j \hat{w}_j^v \otimes \hat{w}_j^v$ on $H_\perp^v$.

**Step 1**: $T$ is $I_h$-invariant. The icosahedral group $I_h$ permutes the 12 vertices $\{\hat{w}_j^v\}$ among themselves; for $g \in I_h$:
$$g T g^{-1} = \sum_j (g\hat{w}_j^v) \otimes (g\hat{w}_j^v) = \sum_{j'} \hat{w}_{j'}^v \otimes \hat{w}_{j'}^v = T$$

**Step 2**: Schur's lemma. The 3D action of $I_h$ on $H_\perp^v$ is irreducible (standard 3D irrep $T_{1u}$ or $T_1$). Schur ⇒ $T = c \cdot P_{\perp v}$.

**Step 3**: Trace. $\text{tr}(T) = \sum_j |\hat{w}_j^v|^2 = 12$. $\text{tr}(c P_{\perp v}) = 3c$. Equating: $12 = 3c \Rightarrow c = 4$.

Therefore $T = 4 P_{\perp v}$. □

### §5.6 Main derivation — expansion of $\sum_j (\hat{u}_j^v \cdot \hat{n})\hat{u}_j^v$

Let $a \equiv \hat{v} \cdot \hat{n}$. Then $\hat{u}_j^v \cdot \hat{n} = \alpha a + \beta(\hat{w}_j^v \cdot \hat{n})$, and:

$$(\hat{u}_j^v \cdot \hat{n})\hat{u}_j^v = \underbrace{\alpha^2 a \hat{v}}_{\text{A}} + \underbrace{\alpha\beta(\hat{w}_j^v \cdot \hat{n})\hat{v}}_{\text{B}} + \underbrace{\alpha\beta a \hat{w}_j^v}_{\text{C}} + \underbrace{\beta^2 (\hat{w}_j^v \cdot \hat{n})\hat{w}_j^v}_{\text{D}}$$

**Term A**: $\sum_j \text{A}_j = 12 \alpha^2 a \hat{v} = 12 \cdot (1/(4\phi^2)) \cdot a \hat{v} = (3a/\phi^2)\hat{v}$

**Term B**: $\sum_j \text{B}_j = \alpha\beta \hat{v} \cdot (\hat{n} \cdot \sum_j \hat{w}_j^v) = 0$ via I3.

**Term C**: $\sum_j \text{C}_j = \alpha\beta a \sum_j \hat{w}_j^v = 0$ via I3.

**Term D**: $\sum_j \text{D}_j = \beta^2 (\sum_j \hat{w}_j^v \otimes \hat{w}_j^v) \hat{n} = 4\beta^2 P_{\perp v} \hat{n}$ via I4. Using $P_{\perp v} \hat{n} = \hat{n} - a\hat{v}$ and $\beta^2 = (2+\phi)/4$:

$$\sum_j \text{D}_j = (2+\phi)(\hat{n} - a\hat{v})$$

**Total**:
$$\sum_j (\hat{u}_j^v \cdot \hat{n})\hat{u}_j^v = (3a/\phi^2)\hat{v} + (2+\phi)(\hat{n} - a\hat{v}) = (2+\phi)\hat{n} + a\left[\frac{3}{\phi^2} - (2+\phi)\right]\hat{v}$$

### §5.7 Identity I5 — golden-ratio reduction

**Statement**: $\frac{3}{\phi^2} - (2+\phi) = -\frac{4}{\phi}$.

**Derivation**:
Using $\phi^2 = \phi + 1$:
$$\frac{3}{\phi^2} - (2+\phi) = \frac{3 - (2+\phi)(\phi+1)}{\phi+1}$$

Numerator: $(2+\phi)(\phi+1) = 4\phi + 3$ (computed in §5.3). So $3 - (4\phi + 3) = -4\phi$.

$$\frac{3}{\phi^2} - (2+\phi) = \frac{-4\phi}{\phi+1} = \frac{-4\phi}{\phi^2} = -\frac{4}{\phi}$$

### §5.8 Final boxed result

$$\boxed{\vec{j}_{DI}^{net}(v) = 2 r_0 \delta\left[(2+\phi)\hat{n} - \frac{4a}{\phi}\hat{v}\right] + \mathcal{O}(\delta^2)}$$

where $a = \hat{v} \cdot \hat{n}$.

### §5.9 Sanity check at $v = v_{\text{host}}$

At host, $\hat{v} = \hat{n}$ and $a = 1$:
$$\vec{j}_{DI}^{net}(v_{\text{host}}) = 2r_0\delta[(2+\phi) - 4/\phi]\hat{n} = 2r_0\delta \cdot \frac{3}{\phi^2}\hat{n} = \frac{6r_0\delta}{\phi^2}\hat{n}$$

(via I5). This matches **Phase 1 Finding DSL-1** (Patch 0524 boxed result) exactly. Internal consistency check between Phase 1 and B.1.q4 at Layer 3 rigor.
