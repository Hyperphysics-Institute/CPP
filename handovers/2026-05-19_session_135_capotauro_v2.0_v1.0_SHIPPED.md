**The diagnostic-framing → user-mediated re-submission with attached visual → revised-letter pattern**: when an LLM reviewer letter appears to miss specific v0.N content additions (line-cited evidence that the content exists in the submitted version), the productive next move is:

1. Claude surfaces the unacknowledged content with line-cited evidence in a diagnostic-framing note that respects the reviewer's epistemic autonomy (no claim of inattention; only evidence that the content exists);
2. User submits the diagnostic flag + attached visual artifact (e.g., rendered `.png` of TikZ figure source) to the reviewer for re-engagement;
3. The reviewer either confirms acknowledgment or revises the verdict-state.
