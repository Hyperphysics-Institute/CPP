## What was assessed, and why it was legitimate
The founder asked whether DM clumping of the DP Sea had been considered as the CMB source. **It had not** — the seven closed at 3847 were the register spring, the conserved count, δkT, composition, C-3, C-2 and C-4. **The standing bar is against *manufacturing* an eighth candidate, not against assessing one the founder raises.**

**It is the best-aimed proposal of the arc.** In ΛCDM the CMB anisotropy genuinely **is** dominated by dark-matter potential wells (Sachs–Wolfe), so DM clumping is **exactly what the CMB displays** — the question is only whether it *makes* the pattern or *carries* it.

## The result — and one number worth remembering
**Amplitude within a factor of two. A first for this arc.** CPP's DM is charge-neutral qDP/hTetra aggregates (CONJ-COSMO-1); aggregation from a random start is **Poisson-seeded**, and Poisson noise is large when objects are **massive and therefore few**:

| aggregate mass | δ at 100 Mpc |
|---|---|
| **10⁶ M⊙** | **5.6×10⁻⁶** (required ~10⁻⁵) |
| 10³ M⊙ | 1.8×10⁻⁷ |
| 1 M⊙ | 5.6×10⁻⁹ |

**Every other candidate missed the amplitude by 40+ orders** (C-2's incoherent floor was 41 short).

**But it is a fit.** The aggregate mass is free and was chosen to land on the observed contrast — three decades of mass span three of contrast. **PD-007: reachable, not predicted**, and reachable-by-choice is precisely what the bar refuses.

## Three independent exclusions
1. **Shape** — Poisson is **white**; a white primordial CDM spectrum **is** the isocurvature model, long excluded and Planck-bounded to a few percent. *(The bound that killed composition at 3822 and the qCP chains at 3833.)*
2. **Timing** — post-inflation clumping is an **active, incoherent source**, and active sources do not reproduce the **acoustic-peak series**. *(The exclusion that demoted C-3 at 3829 §4.)*
3. **Ordering** — the CMB needs δ ~ 10⁻⁵ already present and **coherent on super-horizon scales** at recombination; **aggregation grows a seed and cannot supply the one it starts from.**

**Verdict: it relocates the question rather than answering it** — the arc's familiar result, from a stronger start and failing later in the argument than the fractal cascade.

## The lesson worth carrying
> **Massiveness buys noise.** Every amplitude failure in this arc came from enormously many enormously light things, the Poisson floor going as the inverse root of the count. **Any future primordial-contrast proposal should look for few and heavy, not many and light.**

Real information extracted from a negative result, and the first such lesson the arc has produced.
