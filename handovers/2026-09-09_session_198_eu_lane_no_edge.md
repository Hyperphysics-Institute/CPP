## Correction to carry — 3876 §5 was misleading
**3876 §5 said the founder's phrase *"the entirety of the universe that will ever be populated"* IS the end condition, unqualified. It is not, and the phrasing invited a boundary reading the physics does not have.**

- **The end condition is a DENSITY:** n̄ = 1 CP per rest-frame Planck sphere — intensive, local, satisfied everywhere at once. **It says nothing about where anything ends.**
- **And the phrase conflates two lattice scales by ~10⁹⁷.** One CP per **Planck sphere** ≠ one CP per **GP**: a Planck sphere holds **4.3×10⁹⁶ GPs**, so at inflation's end the CPs occupy **one GP in 10⁹⁷**, and **one in 10¹⁸³ today** (today's density is **87 orders below** the end-of-inflation value).
- **So nothing fills up and nothing is squashed.** Inflation ending is not expansion ending; the threshold was crossed 13.8 Gyr ago and density falls monotonically.

3876's *arithmetic* is correct and survives; its finding is **qualified in place**, not rewritten.

## Governance note — how this error propagated
The founder offered a phrase **tentatively** ("no idea if this concept is relevant or even possible"). It turned out to reproduce the count law exactly, so I praised it — correctly — and in the same breath let its looser implication ride unqualified into a finding, a frontier entry and a handover.

**Praise and precision came apart.** The arithmetic I checked; the phrase I did not. **When adopting a founder's wording as a reading of the corpus, check the wording as carefully as the result it reproduces** — a tentative phrase that happens to be right about one thing is not thereby exact about everything it suggests.
