## Eigenmode Expansion

## CP Non-Co-Occupation (THEO-QM-8)

The CPP principle that two identical CPs cannot occupy the same lattice site at the same Absolute Moment. This is the microscopic origin of the Pauli exclusion principle and fermion statistics. For DI bits (bosons), co-occupation is allowed, giving Bose-Einstein statistics.

## Finite Renormalization

In CPP, the Planck-scale lattice cutoff makes all loop integrals finite — no infinities arise and no renormalization procedure is needed. The bare and renormalized parameters are the same. This eliminates the hierarchy problem: the Higgs mass is naturally at the electroweak scale because the lattice cutoff is physical.

## Three Generations

The six golden-ratio eigenvalues of the 600-cell ({6φ, 4φ, −4φ⁻¹, −6φ⁻¹} plus {12, −2}) naturally group into three pairs, identified with three generations of fermions. The mass ratios between generations arise from the eigenvalue ratios.
