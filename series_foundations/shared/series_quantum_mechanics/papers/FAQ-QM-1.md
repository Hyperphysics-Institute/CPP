### Q1. "The Schrödinger equation already works perfectly. Why rederive it?"

CPP adds two things: (a) a mechanical explanation (DI-bit hopping on the 600-cell, not an abstract postulate) and (b) Planck-scale corrections — E = hν[1 − (ν/ν_P)²] — not predicted by standard QM. The equation is rederived to show it emerges from the same lattice geometry that produces SU(3), the Koide formula, and the Weinberg angle.

### Q2. "How do you extract a continuous wavefunction from a discrete lattice?"

By the continuum limit Δs → 0 with T × Δs² fixed. The 600-cell graph Laplacian converges to ∇² with corrections at order (l_P/λ)² — unobservably small for all current experiments.
