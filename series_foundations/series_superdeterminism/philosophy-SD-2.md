## Group Theory as Physical Content

SD-2's most important philosophical point is that the angular structure of the CPP Bell correction is not a free function — it is determined by the group theory of the 600-cell. Once CPP postulates the 600-cell as the lattice, the D₅ projected symmetry and the cos(5θ) dominant mode are forced by mathematics, not fitted to data. This is the same philosophical stance as SS-1 (SU(3) from the tetrahedral cage) and EW-5 (SU(2)_L from phase interference): the group theory of the 600-cell is not a model-building choice but a mathematical consequence of the postulate.

The CHSH blind spot theorem (THEO-SD-4) is philosophically interesting because it shows that 60 years of Bell experiments have been using the worst possible angle choices for testing CPP. This is not a criticism of those experiments — they were designed to test quantum mechanics against local hidden variables, not against superdeterminism. But it means that no existing Bell data constitutes a precision test of CPP's angular prediction.
