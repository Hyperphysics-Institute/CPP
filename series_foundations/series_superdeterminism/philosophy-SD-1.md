## The CPP Instantiation of Superdeterminism

SD-1 makes the strongest claim in the foundations series: quantum mechanics is not the deepest level of physical description. It is the statistical average of a deterministic hidden-variable theory whose hidden variables are the DP Sea configurations and whose correlation mechanism is the Nexus.

This claim is philosophically significant in two respects. First, it responds to Bell's theorem not by abandoning determinism but by challenging the assumption of statistical independence between hidden variables and measurement settings. The Nexus atemporally correlates both — not by sending a signal between particle and detector (which would violate relativity) but by enforcing a global conservation law that spans both events simultaneously. Second, it identifies the Hossenfelder superdeterminism programme with a specific physical mechanism, making the programme falsifiable rather than merely metaphysical.

The suppression factor ε ~ 10⁻²⁶ is the honest statement of how far CPP currently is from being testable at this level. The philosophical position is that the suppression explains the success of quantum mechanics (QM is correct to 10⁻²⁵ relative precision for all current experiments), not that CPP is unfalsifiable.
