### Q1. "The cos(5θ) prediction has never been tested. Is it a real prediction?"

Yes. The cos(5θ) dominant mode follows from THEO-SD-1 (D₅ projected symmetry), which is a proved theorem from the H₄ group structure. Any precision Bell experiment that scans θ continuously with ~1° resolution and enough statistics to detect ~10⁻²⁸ deviations could test this prediction. The ratio δE(36°)/δE(120°) ≈ −1.065 is amplitude-independent and falsifiable at better precision than the absolute signal.

### Q2. "Why is the CHSH test blind to CPP?"

Because 45° ∉ ℚ(φ) — the CHSH optimal angle cannot be expressed as a rational function of the golden ratio, which is the algebraic closure of the 600-cell vertex coordinates. The 600-cell structure (and hence f_{H₄}) has extrema only at angles in ℚ(φ), so 45° is specifically not an extremum.
