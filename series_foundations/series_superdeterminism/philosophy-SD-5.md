## Honest Science at the Frontier

SD-5 is philosophically the most honest document in the SD series. It acknowledges that the K₀ derivation is not complete and records exactly what would be needed to complete it. This is in the spirit of the CPP programme's commitment to distinguishing proved results from conjectures from research agendas.

The A₅ = φ⁻³/(2π) conjecture, if true, would be one of the deepest results in CPP: the same number that sets the electroweak scale (φ⁻³ from EW-1) also sets the amplitude of the quantum foundations correction. This would mean that the scale of the W/Z boson masses and the scale of the deviation from quantum mechanics in Bell experiments are connected by the same 600-cell geometry. The philosophical implication is that electroweak physics and quantum foundations — usually considered completely separate — share a geometric root.

SD-5 version 0 is honest about being a programme, not a result. The CPP commitment to never deleting entries and always recording the status of open problems is embodied in this paper's existence.
