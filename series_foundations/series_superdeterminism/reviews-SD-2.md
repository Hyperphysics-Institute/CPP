## Review 1: Internal Review (March 2026)

### S1 — STRENGTH: Theorems Derived from Group Theory Alone

THEO-SD-1 through THEO-SD-4 require only the group theory of the 600-cell — no new CPP calculations. They are mathematical consequences of the H₄ Coxeter group applied to the measurement-angle problem. This means they are the most secure results in the SD series: even if other CPP details are wrong, the angular structure of f_{H₄} follows from the 600-cell symmetry alone.

### C1 — OPEN: Amplitudes A₅ and A₃ Not Derived

The Fourier decomposition gives the functional form but not the amplitudes. A₅ ~ φ⁻³/(2π) is a conjecture, not a theorem. Until A₅ is derived from the Nexus path integral (SD-5), the precise magnitude of the angular correction is unknown. **Status: OPEN — SD-5 programme**
