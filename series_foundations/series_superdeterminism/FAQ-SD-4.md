### Q1. "Does CPP predict a deviation from QM at any currently accessible energy?"

No, with one possible exception: the Planck-energy limit (THEO-SD-10) implies that particles with wavelengths approaching l_P would show deviations from QM. Current particle physics reaches ~10⁻¹⁹ m (LHC), which is 16 orders of magnitude above l_P = 1.6 × 10⁻³⁵ m. The deviation scales as (l_P/λ_dB)^n, which is immeasurably small at LHC energies.

### Q2. "The interpolation conjecture is the 'primary remaining open problem.' What has held it up?"

The conjecture requires evaluating the Nexus path integral non-perturbatively — beyond the saddle-point approximation. This is the same class of mathematical difficulty as computing lattice QCD non-perturbatively. The specific obstacles are: (1) the Nexus is an atemporal constraint, not a standard Euclidean action; (2) the lattice is the 600-cell with H₄ symmetry rather than a hypercubic lattice. New mathematical tools are needed.
