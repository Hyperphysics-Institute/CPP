## 3. The founder's mechanism has this shape, on one condition (script part 2)

A neutral pair in a field: the pulls on its + and − members cancel at first order. If the pair is polarisable, so that
the opposite charge is drawn in and the like charge pushed out, the surviving push is (αq²/2)·d(E²)/dx, second order in
the field. That is the founder's two-sided response, and it gives exactly what Einstein's factor needs: **first-order
cancellation and a square remainder.** If the polarising influence is proportional to the stress excess ϱ, the extra
slowing goes as ϱ².

**The condition.** The founder ruled (4359) that DPs already orient toward an unpaired CP in ordinary space. That
standing alignment pushes at first order. So the extra slowing from a deeper shell must come from the **additional,
induced** polarisation, on top of the ordinary baseline, which is already calibrated into the clock. If instead the
standing alignment itself strengthened in proportion to the field, there would be a first-order term, and Cassini
forbids that to 1 part in 10⁵.

**The size is not yet derived.** Einstein needs X = 1 − ϱ² exactly: 7.2% slower at the photon sphere and 11% at the
AP-5 cap. The ringdown box needs at least about half of that (4372, λ ≳ 0.47). A PCD-cycle model of the induced
polarisation and the collision slowing would supply the coefficient.
