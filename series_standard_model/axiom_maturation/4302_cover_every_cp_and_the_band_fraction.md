# Covering Every CP in the Shell Takes Order One DI-Bit; and if a GP Emits One DI-Bit per GP Layer of the Landing Band, α = f/4π, With the Founder's "About 10%" Giving 1/126

**Patch:** 4302. **Lane:** EW → foundations. **Session:** 240.
**Founder:** `founders_voice/4302_clarification_cover_every_cp_on_psr_sphere_not_every_gp.md`: the covering condition was one
DI-bit per **CP** on the PSR sphere's surface (spread over the 10% band), not per GP. 4299 and 4301 read it as GPs.
**Verify:** `series_standard_model/code/4302_cover_every_cp_band_fraction.py`.

## 1. Does the conception work? Yes, and it is easily met (rows 1)

CPs are vastly sparser than GPs, so the count becomes a statement about the density of the Sea. With the densities on
file:

```
  today's Sea (2 x 1.004e-2 per l_P^3)    : 0.025 CPs in the shell
  end of inflation (1 per Planck sphere)  : 0.300 CPs in the shell
  black-hole packing (~1 per PSR^3)       : 1.257 CPs in the shell
```

At every density on file, the landing shell (radius one PSR, thickness 10% of a PSR) holds of order one CP or fewer.
Giving every CP in it a DI-bit needs N of order 1. **So the conception is consistent: it does not over-emit.** 4301's
10³²-fold conflict was against my GP reading and is withdrawn. But the condition does not *set* N. α needs N of about
10²⁹–10³¹ per GP per Moment (4301), which is far more than there are CPs to cover. Today, with DPs about 4.6 PSR apart,
no CP even sits inside the band: all delivery from one CP to another goes by re-radiation, as the founder said.

## 2. What α's N is, counted in GP layers (rows 2)

4301's relation, α = N s/(4π PSR), rearranged:

  **N = 4π α · (PSR/s) = 0.0917 × (number of GP layers in a PSR).**

The 10% landing band is 0.1 PSR deep, i.e. 0.1 × (PSR/s) GP layers. So the emission α requires is **one DI-bit for each
GP layer across a band 9.2% of a PSR deep.** If that were the rule, if a GP emits one DI-bit per GP layer of its landing
band of fractional depth f, then N = f · PSR/s and

  **α = f / 4π**, independent of the GP spacing.

```
    f = 0.1000: alpha = 0.00796 = 1/125.7
    f = 0.0917: alpha = 0.00730 = 1/137.0
```

The founder's "about 10%" gives 1/126, within 8% of the measured 1/137.0. Exactly 1/137.036 needs f = 0.0917.

## 3. What I think

- The CP-covering conception is fine and is met with room to spare at any density on file.
- α is not set by covering; it is set by how many DI-bits a GP emits. §2 shows that number is, to within 8%, one DI-bit
  per GP layer of the founder's landing band. That could be a coincidence. It could also be the rule: the band is where
  the DI-bits land, one per layer, and its depth f then fixes the strength of charge as f/4π.
- If it is the rule, the derivation of α reduces to deriving the landing band's depth, f = 0.0917. That is a lattice
  question (why do DI-bits land 90–100% of the way out?), and it is the kind of question the corpus can attack with counts.
