## 2. The founder's question, answered

**I think he has said exactly what 4246 needs.** "Indistinguishable" is the operative word: when two histories reach
the same final state they add as amplitudes, and for two identical −eCPs the exchanged history carries the Fermi sign.
That superposition *is* the mixing he expects, and its sign is what makes the down quark −1 against the up quark's +1.
Where he says "unless the second −eCP releases both": that is a different event — the down quark would lose its linear
−eCP and stop being a down quark — an inelastic process, not the elastic neutral-current scattering Q_W measures. The
elastic case is the one where one −eCP stays, and there his answer is the mixing. Registered G-EW-NCMIX-4253,
provisional; 4246's occupancy premise (4248) and exchange premise (4253) are now both founder-supported.
