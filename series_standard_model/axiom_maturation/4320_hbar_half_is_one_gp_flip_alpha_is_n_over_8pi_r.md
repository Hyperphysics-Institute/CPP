## 1. The ruling

minimum energy increment stored in the DP sea, and is probably the action of ħ/2. It is an elemental motion of a
single GP over a single Moment. Something has to be the physical embodiment of the smallest increment of action."*

This gives ħ/2 — identified but not derived since 4300 — a physical embodiment. It fits his 4290 ruling (ħ/2 is the
fundamental unit) and his 4299/4301 pictures (a DI-bit moves a CP one GP; action is one GP of displacement against the
least stress over one Moment). His 4318 smooth profile (rest at apogee, fastest at the centre) is no longer the Planck
level. It remains available for the Compton-level ZBW and for the transient case he named.

## 4. 4301 re-run with ħ/2 as the one-GP push: α = N/(8πR) (rows)

4301 set ħ = f₁ s t_M. The ruling makes that ħ/2, so ħc = 2f₁ s·PSR and, with σ = s² (4301's stated assumption),
**α = N σ/(8π s PSR) = N/(8π R).** The unarticulated conversion f₁ still cancels.

```
    GR-FE-1  R = 1e+30:  N = 8 pi alpha R = 1.834e+29 DI-bits per GP per Moment  (4310 had 9.170e+28; x2)
    EU       R = 1e+32:  N = 8 pi alpha R = 1.834e+31 DI-bits per GP per Moment  (4310 had 9.170e+30; x2)
```

The calibration of N doubles; nothing else in 4301–4313 changes form. With the action and the charge now both
single-GP objects (ħ/2 = one GP flipped in one Moment; a unit charge = one GP's volley, 4312), **α is fixed by one
ratio: how many DI-bits a GP emits per Moment, against how many GPs lie along one PSR.**

## 5. What N/R says about the volley (rows)

```
    alpha = N/(8 pi R)  ->  N/R = 8 pi alpha = 0.18340 = 1/5.452
    R = 1e+30:  sphere cover (one DI-bit per GP of the PSR sphere, 4303) N = 4 pi R^2 = 1.3e+61 -> alpha = R/2 = 5e+29 (x7e+31 too strong);   line cover N = R -> alpha = 1/8pi = 1/25.13
    measured alpha needs N = R/5.452: about one DI-bit per 5.45 GPs along ONE radius --
    the volley scales with the PSR's LENGTH in GPs, not its area; within a factor 5.45 of a single radial line.
```

This is the first scale-free statement of what α asks of the lattice. **A GP's volley per Moment must be of the order of
the number of GPs along one PSR radius (a length), not along its sphere (an area).** Covering the sphere one DI-bit per
GP (the 4303 condition) makes charge R/(2α) ≈ 10³²–10³⁴ times too strong at the two R on file. A volley equal to one radial line gives
α = 1/(8π) = 1/25, which is 5.45 times too strong. The measured value needs about one DI-bit per 5.45 GPs of radius.

## 6. What I think

The ruling is the right kind of move: ħ/2 now has a lattice embodiment, and it is the same object as his action quantum
from 4301. Two things follow that are solid. **c04's Level-1 action is off by a factor 2 from the ruling** and must be

the most useful thing in this patch, because it turns α into a physical question he can picture: why a GP's volley is
of the order of one radius's worth of GPs rather than a sphere's. It is an observation, not a derivation. The 5.45 is
not claimed to be anything, and σ = s² is still an assumption that rescales it.
