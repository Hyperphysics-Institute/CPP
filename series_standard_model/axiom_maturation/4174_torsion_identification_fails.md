## 1. The scrutiny 4173 asked for, applied

4173 §6 flagged its own result as conditional and said the next window should test whether the
Einstein–Cartan identification is *earned* or merely *attractive*. Applying that test.

**Einstein–Cartan torsion is non-propagating.** This is not a detail of the theory; it is its
defining structural feature. Torsion satisfies an **algebraic** field equation — it is tied
point-by-point to the local spin density, vanishes identically outside matter, and carries no
independent degrees of freedom. That is precisely why EC modifies gravity only inside matter, and
precisely why its effects are confined to the contact term 4173 computed.

**A_i propagates.** A3′, verbatim:

> *"At every Absolute Moment each GP **broadcasts to its PSR shell** the Lattice State Packet …
> **propagating at c = l_P/t_P** in the absolute (Nexus) frame with flat per-hop transport, **all
> channels** obeying the same icosahedral shell-sum…"*

The amendment adds A_i as **a fourth channel of that packet**, so it propagates with the rest.

> **A propagating axial field is not Einstein–Cartan torsion. The identification fails on the
> central structural feature of the thing it identifies A_i with.**

## 3. What it opens — and this is better than what it closed

A non-propagating channel is unobservable by construction. **A propagating one is not.** If A_i is a
genuinely propagating field in the broadcast packet, then:

- it **carries energy** and can **radiate**;
- being a **T₁ (l = 1) axial** channel, its radiation is a **vector mode**, not a tensor mode;
- and **extra gravitational-wave polarisations are directly bounded.** The standard framework admits
  six polarisations — two tensor (GR's + and ×), two **vector** (x and y), two scalar — and
  LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA constrain the non-tensor ones from the observed signals' antenna-pattern
  response.

> **For the first time in this session, the A_i channel has a real empirical handle: it should
> contribute vector-mode gravitational-wave polarisation, and that is measured.**

This is the opposite of the torsion reading. Torsion made the channel safely invisible; propagation
makes it **exposed**. Filed as **TODO-4174-VECTORGW**: compute A_i's radiated vector-mode amplitude
from its source c(∇ × s) and confront the LIGO/Virgo polarisation bounds.

## 4. And a second exposure worth naming now

**Propagating-torsion theories are known to be delicate.** Adding dynamics to an axial channel
generically introduces ghosts or gradient instabilities unless the kinetic term is built with care;
the literature on dynamical-torsion gravity is largely a catalogue of which terms are safe. **A3′
gives A_i flat per-hop transport and no kinetic term at all** — so the question of whether A_i's
propagation is even well-posed has not been asked. Filed with the above.
