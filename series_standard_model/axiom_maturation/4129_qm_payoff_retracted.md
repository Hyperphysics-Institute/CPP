## 1. The circularity

Patch 4128 concluded the amendment needs A_i **quantized as a two-state spin-½ object**, and
filed TODO-4128-QUANTIZE to add that clause.

**That clause cannot be added.** OPEN-QM-3's statement is:

> *"Derive **s = 1/2** from ZBW orbital topology; derive Pauli exclusion from hDP chain
> antisymmetry."*

s = ½ *is* the two-dimensionality. So:

- At 4111 I listed spin-½/Pauli doubling as the amendment's **largest payoff**, on the grounds
  that it addresses OPEN-QM-3.
- At 4128 that payoff turned out to require A_i be quantized as spin-½.
- Quantizing A_i as spin-½ **is assuming OPEN-QM-3's answer.**

**The payoff was circular.** The amendment gives spin a *referent*; the hard part of OPEN-QM-3
is the *two-dimensionality*, and a broadcast channel does not supply it. Adding a channel was
never going to.

CPP's programme derives QM rather than postulating it (QM-1…QM-5). Postulating spin-½ to get
Pauli doubling would abandon that on precisely the question the programme cares most about.
