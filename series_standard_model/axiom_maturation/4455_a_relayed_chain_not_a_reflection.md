## 2. What can be rationalized (script parts 1–2)

**A reflected standing wave cannot be light.** A standing wave between reflecting walls stays in place: its crests do not move, while a photon's pattern
travels at light speed. Reflection would also need walls inside the photon at half-wavelength spacing. The only "walls" a photon has are the ends of its
packet, about half a metre apart for hydrogen's lines (4454), and a pattern moving at c cannot reflect between them.

**A relayed chain is a travelling wave.** Suppose the DPs along the photon all oscillate at one common rate f, and each starts its oscillation when the
relay reaches it, the relay moving at the local light speed c (4415, 4443) at the same speed for every rate. Then each DP is a little behind the one
ahead, and the pattern of crests moves at c, spaced c/f apart (c/(Kf) in a region of index K). **This is kinematics, not a derivation:** any travelling
wave without dispersion has wavelength = speed ÷ rate. What the chain does show is a CPP picture of how a travelling pattern could be carried: by the relay,
handing each DP's phase to the next. Claude reads his "synchronize" this way.

**The relay sets the phase between DPs, not their rate.** The chain assumes all the DPs swing at one shared rate, the photon's 10¹⁵ Hz. But each DP's own
ZBW-scale swing is about 10²⁰ Hz (4454). **What makes all the DPs share the photon's rate is exactly his "synchronize/resonate", and it is still unsolved:
it is 4454's collective mode.** The chain moves the question back one step; it does not answer it.

**The moving frame: a consistency check.** An observer moving toward the source meets the travelling crests more often, by the Doppler factor (1.106 at a
tenth of light speed, 1.732 at half). The photon's energy rises by the same factor, by relativity. So energy ÷ frequency stays h in every frame. The
chain gives the frequency shift; the energy shift is standard relativity; their staying in ratio is consistent with the chain, not derived from it. A
"ringing cohort as a clock" (4454's hypothetical) would instead slow (0.995 and 0.866). His own answer, that the photon always moves at c and is unchanged,
fits this; that the moving observer meets the crests more often is Claude's extension of it.

**For matter.** A particle at rest can carry a uniform rhythm in place, the same phase everywhere (4443's rest beat). Seen from a moving frame it appears as
a travelling phase wave with crests h/p apart: de Broglie's construction (4454). That is not a reflected standing wave with nodes: boosting one of those
gives two travelling waves with different Doppler shifts, not a single de Broglie wave. So a uniform rhythm fits matter at rest, and a reflected standing
wave fits neither light nor matter.

## 3. The rate and the energy (script part 3)

**His mechanism points the right way.** More energy, greater separation and SSV_net, faster arc rotation: the rate rises with the energy. What is needed is
a reason it rises in exact proportion, doubling when the energy doubles.

**A simple model shows the need.** In a kinetic model Claude chose (a fixed volume, a fixed number of movers, rate in proportion to their speed, energy as
their speed squared), the rate would rise only as the square root of the energy: 1.41 times for twice the energy. His picture is not that model. Its
energy sits partly in separation, its CP steps are capped at the local light speed (4415), which undercuts the "faster movers" model itself, and its
internal wavelength is not fixed (4454). But it shows that "more energy, faster" does not by itself give "twice the energy, twice the rate".

**"A fixed action per cycle" is Planck's relation rewritten.** Energy × the duration of one cycle = h is the same statement as E = hf. So it does not explain
the proportion; it restates it. The only CPP content would be a reason each cycle of a cohort's oscillation carries a fixed amount of DP arc-separation
action. There is a classical precedent for such a fixed quantity: for an oscillator changed slowly, its energy ÷ frequency, the action per cycle, stays
constant (Ehrenfest's adiabatic invariant). That is why the old quantum theory (Bohr and Sommerfeld) quantized action per cycle. That gives the idea a
footing, but it is not new.
