# The EW "Reset" Mechanism Restated as Transit: What Survives, What Must Be Recomputed, One Question

**Patch:** 4279. **Lane:** EW (TODO-4264-PASSTHROUGH step 5). **Session:** 239.
**Ruling applied:** R-ZBW-PASS-THROUGH (4265). **Restates:** 4231 (G-EW-ZBWRESET-4231), and 4232–4233 where they
lean on it. Banner appended to 4231; original unaltered.

## 1. Restatement

**Binding.** The orbital eDP's −eCP pole falls to the +qCP core and **passes through** superposition at top speed,
with one Moment of co-location in which the core's gradient on it is zero. It continues to the far side, turns, and
returns. The "decision Moment" of 4231 survives as that co-location Moment. For that Moment the core exerts no pull,
so whatever other gradient is present *steers the transiting pole*. It is no longer "reset and pulled from rest".

**Release (β⁻).** The W⁰ centroid is a second zero-gradient pocket. The pole **transits it by inertia**, and the
strongest gradient present (the stabilised linear −eCP, repulsive) steers it outward. This is the founder's own 4240
ruling (*"passage through the W⁰ centroid by inertia, steered by A"*), so the release was already a transit in his
picture. The word "reset" was the only part carrying the retired stop.

- 4230's charge branch: the release is the W⁰'s act, and it needs no screening.

## 3. What must be recomputed (filed)

**The pole-in-pocket time fraction (4231 §2(b); 4233's "pocket geometry fixed").** 1.24×10⁻³ was computed for a pole
moving at uniform speed on the orbit circle. In a pass-through swing, time spent at a place goes as 1/speed: the pole
is fastest at the crossing and lingers near its turning points. If the W⁰ pocket sits near a turning point, the
fraction rises, perhaps by an order of magnitude or more. If it sits near the crossing, the fraction falls. Which
applies depends on where the pocket sits relative to the swing. 4233's claim that the pocket factor is fixed at
m_const/m_W therefore does not survive unexamined. The lifetime budget's other factors are untouched.

## 4. A tension the ruling exposes, and a question (a physical picture)

A pole swinging *exactly* through the core's centre moves along a line through it, and so carries **no angular
momentum** about the core. But the orbital eDP is the quark's spin carrier, with L = ħ/2 (4223, 4228). Both cannot
hold if the path is a straight line through the core.

**Question.** When the orbital eDP's −eCP pole swings through the up quark's +qCP core, **does it pass exactly
through the core, straight in and straight out? Or does it pass just beside it, so that its path loops around the core
like the petals of a flower, turning a little each pass and so carrying the quark's spin?**
