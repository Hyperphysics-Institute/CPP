## 2. What the table says in the founder's objects

1. **The antineutrino carries the neutron's spin direction out in 99.4% of decays.** Not the electron. The
   Sea's counter-spinning partner — the object the founder called *the receipt for supplying the orbital* —
   is the one that leaves aligned with the parent, almost always.
2. **The electron keeps the quark's sense in 56% of decays and leaves reversed in 44%.** Its spin is only
   12% polarised along the neutron's. "The released charge inherits the quark's orbital DP *and its spin*"
   (SF-2 §5.7.1 step 3) is true as a statement about which DP it takes; it is **not** true that the DP
   leaves with its sense unchanged.
3. **The event the inheritance picture draws — proton unchanged, electron keeps s, antineutrino counter to
   the refill — happens in 0.64% of decays.** Its amplitude is (1 + λ)/√2: the same-sense and opposite-sense
   contributions to that configuration nearly cancel. **That cancellation is what λ < 0 means physically.**
   Flip the sign (in-phase) and the naive event becomes the 44% one; the measured sign says it is the
   *exchanged* configuration that dominates the non-flip sector.
4. **The 44% configuration cannot be produced by the naive bookkeeping at all.** Proton unchanged means the
   refill has the original sense s; the antineutrino leaves *along* s, i.e. **parallel to the refill, not
   counter to it**; and the electron leaves reversed. Angular momentum is still conserved (+½ −½ +½ = +½):
   the created pair (refill + antineutrino) carries one net unit, and the electron's reversal supplies it.
   So in nearly half of all decays **the electron's inherited orbital reverses its sense during the decay,
   and the Sea pair is created co-spinning rather than counter-spinning.**

## 3. The requirement on the mechanism, stated once

In the sector where the proton's spin is unchanged, the two leptons leave in the state
[(1 + λ)|e+ ν̄−⟩ + (λ − 1)|e− ν̄+⟩]/√2. At every λ the two configurations have comparable amplitude: at
λ → 0 they are equal and opposite (the singlet, Fermi); at |λ| → ∞ equal and alike (the m = 0 triplet, GT);
in between, both. **So the electron's final sense is never fixed by its inherited orbital; what the refill
sense fixes is the *exchange symmetry* of the two-lepton spin state — antisymmetric under swapping which
lepton carries which sense for the same-sense refill, symmetric for the opposite-sense refill.** Two DPs
leaving through the same Sea region, whose individual senses are not determined but whose relative
symmetry is: that is what "one coherent two-GP event" (4207 §3) has to mean, and it is the thing the
founder has not seen because nothing in the corpus describes two departing DPs exchanging sense.

The corpus does have the raw material. 4199 (founder): the captured orbital's motion *"creates the DP-Arcs
associated with that acceleration, which organize on a DP, and the spin induced on the nearby DP is the
neutrino."* That is a DP inducing a sense on a neighbouring DP through arcs — a mutual-induction process.
If the induction runs both ways while the two DPs are still within arc reach of each other, the pair's
individual senses are undetermined during the transit and only the symmetry survives — which is the
requirement. **Not shown; named.** The arc reach is CONJ-FP-1, the same object λ's magnitude is blocked on.

## 4. What the founder is asked (PD-006(a): a physics question in a physical picture)

When the electron — the −eCP with its inherited orbital DP — and the antineutrino — the Sea DP with its
internal counter-spin — leave the decay site through the same region of Sea, **can the two DPs induce
sense on each other through their DP-arcs, so that which of them ends up spinning which way is not settled
until they are out of each other's reach?** If yes, the pair state of 4223 is the natural outcome and the
measured numbers say the induction is strong (the "wrong-way" outcome is 44%, the naive one 0.6%). If no —
if each DP's sense is fixed at the Moment it forms and cannot be changed by a neighbour — then the
inheritance model cannot reach the 44% configuration, the pair-state result is a description without a
mechanism, and something else in the picture has to give.
