## 6. Question to the founder (physical picture)

Near the Sun, clocks run slow by about two parts in a million at its surface. Starlight grazing it bends by 1.75
arc-seconds, and radar signals passing it are delayed. Both of those are twice what the clock slowing alone would give.

**If a crystal keeps the same absolute size near the Sun,** then one of three things must happen:
- **(a)** Clocks slow exactly as much as light does. Then light bends only half as much as measured (0.875
  arc-seconds), which the measurements exclude.

  of its hop rate. Then the bending and delay come out right, but light measured with a local crystal and a local clock
  would travel slightly slower near the Sun. Experiments comparing a light cavity with an atomic clock as the Earth moves
  nearer to and farther from the Sun look for exactly that.
