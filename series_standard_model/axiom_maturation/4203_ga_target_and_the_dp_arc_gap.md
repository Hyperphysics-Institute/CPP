## 1. The target — rows verbatim (D-11)


So the working picture (P2, 4201–4202) needs the Sea at the core's ZBW radius, at the Moment of

## 2. Why the number cannot be computed from the corpus as it stands

The mechanism the founder invokes is **DP-arcs**. Located (D-10): they are defined only in
**CONJ-FP-1** (`flagship_papers/electromagnetism/sketches/conj_fp_1_volume_transfer_inertia.md`) —
a conjecture sketch of *inertia*: nearest-neighbour arcs **charging ahead** of a moving charge and
**discharging behind**. SF-6 §"Emission as ZBW discharge" uses them for photon emission. **No
document assigns DP-arcs a circulation, a decay length, or a lifetime,** and `master_glossary.md`
has no entry. So "the net circulation the departing DP's arcs leave at the core radius" has no
defined value to compute. **The g_A calculation is blocked on a missing object, not on effort.**

What would unblock it, in order of cost: (a) a rule for how long an induced partner sense persists
in a Sea DP after the inducing orbital leaves (one Moment? the ZBW period? indefinitely, until
re-paired?) — f is then the fraction of the core's captureable neighbours still carrying it;
(b) the arc geometry of CONJ-FP-1 promoted from sketch to rule. **(a) is the smaller ask and is the
founder's to picture, not mine (PD-006(a)).**

## 3. What the two values of f would mean

f ≈ 0.24: the refill draws from a shell mostly relaxed, a quarter still counter-turning — a *short*
persistence, of order the capture time. f ≈ 0.66: two-thirds still counter-turning — a *long*
persistence. **Once (a) is answered, this is a one-line check either way.**
