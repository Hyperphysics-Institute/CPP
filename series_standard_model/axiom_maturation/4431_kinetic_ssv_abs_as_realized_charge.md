## 4. The equivalence with gravity: energy and momentum, not geometry

**Energy and momentum.** The realized charge carries the electron's kinetic energy (S) and momentum (V). In general relativity the source of
gravity is the full energy-momentum: a fast mass pulls a slow test body roughly as γ(1 + v²)m, not γm, because momentum gravitates too. In
that sense kinetic and gravitational SSV_abs are the same kind of thing, realized charge, as he proposes, with the realized S and V both
counting.

**Not geometry.** It cannot be equivalent to a static mass of γm sitting at the electron:
- **Its PSR shortens along the motion only** (4428–4430: front and rear by the Lorentz factor, unchanged sideways), where a static mass, in the
  isotropic convention, shortens it all round (WC-DP-SEA-PV).
- **Nothing measurable changes for co-moving rulers and clocks** (the relativity principle, 4412). In CPP the realized charge is real in the
  absolute frame; what does not change is anything a co-moving observer can measure.
- **No horizon from speed.** Horizon formation is set by invariant mass (for a collision, the centre-of-momentum energy √s), so boosting a
  particle cannot make a black hole. The v → c field of a point mass is the Aichelburg–Sexl impulsive wave: a flattened shock with no
  horizon. If the realized SSV_abs acted like a static γm, an electron near γ ≈ 2.4 × 10²² (γm_ec² at the Planck energy, as an order of
  magnitude) would approach horizon-like conditions. The absolute-frame S at such γ is real in CPP, so CPP must explain why it does not act
  gravitationally as a static γm. (Accelerator electrons reach γ ~ 2 × 10⁵ and cosmic-ray protons γ ~ 3 × 10¹¹.)

So **the equivalence is of energy and momentum content, not of geometry**: the realized charge of motion is arranged as the flattened,
along-the-motion pattern of 4427–4430, where a mass at rest is round.

## 7. Answer to his "What do you think?"

I think it's the right mechanism, with two limits. Making DPs near the fast electron partly real is exactly what was missing. The step rule
needs the electron's SSV_abs to rise with speed, and this says where the rise comes from. It has to rise by exactly relativity's kinetic

The first limit is on counting. If only real charge counts toward SSV_abs, the resting sea's paired DPs stop watering the step down. But the
real charges in nearby matter would still water it down, and empty space would lose the background that gravity is measured against. So what
must count is the electron's own share, which is why I've asked about all three kinds of source.

The second limit is on how far the equivalence with gravity goes. In energy and momentum it holds: the realized charge carries the motion's
energy and momentum, and both pull like mass. In shape it doesn't: a moving electron's PSR shortens only along its motion, nothing changes
for rulers moving with it, and its gravity is flattened like its electric field. Speed alone never makes a black hole. So kinetic and
gravitational SSV_abs are the same stuff, realized charge, arranged differently.
