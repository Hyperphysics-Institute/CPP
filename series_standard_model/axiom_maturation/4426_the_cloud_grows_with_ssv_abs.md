## 3. A computed case: a moving charge's cloud (script)

**Assumed:**
- The census around a moving CP is the Liénard–Wiechert field (4411; conditional on the 1/R weight per arrival and a single-hop
  relay).
- The cloud's SSV_abs is its stored field energy (4417's square law, stated there as a requirement).
- The charge's core is contracted like the PSR, 1/γ along the motion and unchanged across (4410). This is an input.

With those conditions the classical field is a fair model of the organized cloud.

**The geometry grows (part 2).** Around the moving charge the potential is qγ/√(γ²x² + ρ²) (4411). A surface of fixed potential keeps
its extent along the motion and grows across it by γ, so the volume it encloses grows by γ²: ×5.26 at v = 0.9. That is a literal
counterpart of "the geometry grows". The contracted core, by contrast, was put in.

**The content grows (part 1).** The field's energy and momentum outside the contracted core, in Gaussian units with rest field
energy U₀ = 0.5:

| v | field energy | γU₀(1 + v²/3) | field momentum | (4/3)γU₀v | rest value √(E² − p²) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.499 | 0.500 | 0 | 0 | 0.499 |
| 0.3 | 0.539 | 0.540 | 0.209 | 0.210 | 0.496 |
| 0.6 | 0.698 | 0.700 | 0.499 | 0.500 | 0.489 |
| 0.9 | 1.453 | 1.457 | 1.373 | 1.377 | 0.476 |

(The 0.25% shortfall in every row is the integration cutoff.) The energy rises with speed (×2.9 at v = 0.9), and its rest value
drifts down: the field alone, defined this way, leaves the mass shell. That is the classical 4/3 problem (Abraham, Lorentz).

**The two classical cures** (as in 4417 §5):
- **(a) A covariant definition** of the field's energy and momentum (Fermi 1922, Rohrlich 1960) gives γU₀ and γU₀v for the field
  alone, on the mass shell. Stresses are then needed only to keep the charge stable (Poincaré).
- **(b) Binding stresses.** Adding classical Poincaré-stress contributions (−γU₀v²/3 to the energy, −γU₀v/3 to the momentum) also
  gives γU₀ and γU₀v. The script prints this column; it is imported from classical physics and is an identity by construction,
  not a finding. It also assumes the stresses carry no rest energy; counting their energy instead (Schwinger 1983) gives a rest mass
  of (4/3)U₀. So "binding makes up the difference" depends on the model.

So **the cloud's SSV_abs rises as exactly γ only under one of these cures, and which one CPP has is open.** The other comparisons in
play measure different things: CPP's open-cloud scalar toy gave 2/3 of the needed inertia (2496), and the vector field gives the
4/3 form.
