## 3. 4405's toy is withdrawn; what remains (script part 1)

mechanism**: the CPs carry no momentum, and the body's drift is stored in the sea. The toy is withdrawn (4405 is
annotated).

Two things remain, now stated in sea form:
1. **The drift under the cap.** R-CP-NO-REST-MASS still says each CP advances at most one PSR per Moment. If a drift were

   and binding energy included, with κᵢ = Eᵢ/c². That is 4405's energy-weighting condition, restated as a requirement on
   how the GP computation deposits DP-arcs and separation, not removed.

## 5. What lunar laser ranging forces (script part 3)

Bartlett and van Buren (1986) used the Moon's offset between its centre of mass and centre of figure (an aluminium-rich
crust against an iron-rich core) to bound any difference in the **active-to-passive** mass ratio between aluminium and
iron at about 4×10⁻¹². With passive = inertial (equal fall, §3), that bounds active-to-inertial. GR-1a has active mass =
E₀ (the SSV_abs a pattern sources). Then:
- if electrons stored only the open-cloud ⅔ of their energy while nucleons stored all of theirs, aluminium and iron would
  differ by about 3×10⁻⁶, excluded by about a million;
- in general, **the electron's stored κ must equal its E₀/c² to about 4×10⁻⁷** (the electron mass fraction differs by
  9.3×10⁻⁶ between aluminium and iron), whatever the open-cloud number turns out to be;
- **nuclear binding** differs by about 5×10⁻⁴ of the mass (8.33 against 8.79 MeV per nucleon), so binding-field energy
  must carry κ = E/c² to about 10⁻⁸.

The closed-pattern (Laue) route is the corpus's candidate for delivering this; the alternative, that active mass also
scales with κ, contradicts GR-1a's source rule. This is tested for electrons, nucleons and nuclear binding; other
particles are not covered by this test. **SF-6 debt (d) becomes an empirical requirement.**

**A tension inside GR-1a:** its "Inertial and Gravitational Mass Are Identical by Construction" section sets inertial
mass = E_pol for the eDP cloud, while SF-6's toy gives an open cloud ⅔ U/c². GR-1a's "by construction" holds only if the
electron's cloud is closed in Laue's sense. An annotation to GR-1a is owed.
