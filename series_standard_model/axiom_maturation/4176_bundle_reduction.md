Einstein–Cartan identification): the source with energy-density dimensions is **c(∇ × s)**, the
construction magnetisation already uses.

The coefficient is then forced by the programme's own commitments, with no analogy needed:

> **So κ_A = 16πG/c⁴, or the amendment introduces a new parameter. There is no third option.**

That is not a derivation of the value from first principles. It is a **forced choice**, and it is
the honest resolution of SOURCEEQ: the corpus's own zero-parameter commitment picks the branch.

## 4. What I am and am not claiming

**I am not proposing to withdraw A3′'s amendment.** There are reasons I have not evaluated:
the completeness argument of 4172 §§1–3 is structural and stands on its own (spin is a source and
had no channel); a channel may be required for consistency of the broadcast even if nothing measures
it; and 4120's "all three required together" may rest on a coupling argument I have not found.

**I am claiming the bundle's justification should be re-examined**, because 4120 presented the three
as inseparable and the evidence assembled today says **one of them is doing all the observable
work.** If A3′ and AP-4 are kept, it should be on the structural argument, stated as such — not on
the implied promise that they earn their place empirically. Filed as **TODO-4176-BUNDLE**.
