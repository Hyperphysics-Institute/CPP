## 2. How his statement relates to GR-1b

He says V_i is squeezed as SSV_abs squeezes the PSR, that is, the direction channel shrinks in step with the scalar
channel. That is **consistent with**, and supplies a candidate picture for, GR-1b's equal-magnitude rule: space
compresses by the same fraction clocks slow, which is the factor 2.

They are not the same statement. GR-1b's g_ij is a ruler metric built from the **gradient** of V. Its weak-field
proposition simply sets |∇V|_rr = GM/(k r c²). A static V falls as 1/r², so its gradient falls as 1/r³, not as 1/r.
So the equal magnitudes come from a calibration of the source values, not from the single k alone. "V_i squeezed" is
about a CP's step rate. The mismatch in GR-1b is filed.
