## 1. Where the picture puts the antineutrino

At the **electron's** capture: the bare −eCP draws a DP into orbit; the accelerating +eCP end lays
DP-arcs; the arcs organise Sea DPs into a spinning DP, the antineutrino, which carries off most of
the liberated kinetic energy. **One neutral lepton** — the count (4202) is satisfied, provided the
core does not *also* capture.

## 2. The fork — stated so the founder can pick

The picture says "a DP" is drawn in. Two candidates are nearest:

- **(a) a Sea DP.** Then the +qCP core keeps its own orbital DP and is already the up quark; nothing
  refills. This is P1 (4199). **Bookkeeping: the nucleon's spin projection can never change** — the
  quark orbital is untouched and the electron–antineutrino pair is created spin-balanced. **P1 is
  Fermi-only. The free neutron decays by Gamow–Teller 83% of the time (4200).** Under (a) the working
  picture has no GT decay at all.
- **(b) the down quark's own orbital DP** — the one DP that is already *there*, at 0.631 fm, when the
  −eCP is released inside the quark. Then the electron's spin is inherited, the core refills, the
  refill's partner is the antineutrino, and the refill sense is the channel. This is P2 (4201–4202),
  and everything in the founder's arcs-and-congealing description carries over to the *core's*
  capture unchanged.

**I lean (b), and say why:** it is the nearest DP; it is the only version with a GT channel; and it
is the only version in which the 4203 persistence question has any meaning. **But the founder's
sentence as written reads as (a), and this is his to decide (PD-006(a)).**

## 3. Energy — the picture against the spectrum (rows verbatim, D-11)

```
energy released (m_n - m_p)      = 1.2933 MeV, of which electron rest mass 0.511, shared KE Q = 0.7823
mean electron KE                 = 0.303 MeV   (39% of Q)
mean antineutrino energy         = 0.480 MeV   (61% of Q)
fraction of decays where nubar takes MORE than half of Q = 70%
nubar share ranges continuously from 0% to 100% of Q; its rest mass is < 1e-6 of what it carries.
```

- *"The large portion of the KE … is converted into the KE … of the anti-neutrino"*: **true on
  average (61%), and in 70% of decays — but not fixed.** The split is continuous from 0 to 1. A
  mechanism must allow the antineutrino to take almost nothing or almost everything.
- *"KE of mass formed by the anti-neutrino"*: the antineutrino's rest mass is < 10⁻⁶ of what it
  carries. **Whatever the spinning DP is, it must be nearly massless** — its energy is motion, not
  structure. SF-4's "unbound orbital ZBW with no anchor" is compatible; a DP with a bound ZBW
  frequency would not be.
- The electron's **rest mass** (0.511 of the 1.293 MeV) is created in the same event — the orbital
  DP acquired *is* that mass (4178). Under (b) it is transferred, not created, and the core must
  re-create it in the refill; under (a) it is created at the electron. **Either way the energy
  book balances; it does not choose between (a) and (b).**
