laser ranging, binary pulsars and the alignment of the Sun's spin). Any drift contribution must therefore cancel out of
what is observable, as the clock and ruler effects do (owed). In Einstein's theory a moving body's field is set by its
full energy and momentum; the momentum part is the frame-dragging channel (carried with the 4397 item).
