- **The Sea's lapse → 0.** The relayed traffic (DI-bits handed GP to GP through the Sea) slows to a stop as the enclosed

- **The matter's lapse is flat at ½.** A CP's own register (its clock, R-CLOCK-RATE-IS-DISPLACEMENT) is capped by D1:
