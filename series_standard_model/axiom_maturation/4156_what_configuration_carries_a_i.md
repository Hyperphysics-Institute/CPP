## 1. The founder's framing is the right one, and the corpus already committed to it

> *"In CPP, no forces act on a CP to move it… movement is purely rule-based."*

Exactly — and that is why Patch 4155's answer ("there is no chiral force") is not evasion. **There
is no charge force either.** The V_i rule is: the GP sums arrivals, imprints the CP, the CP displaces

is asking the wrong question about A_i **in the same way it would be about charge**. The right
question is the one the founder then asks: **what is the rule, and what configuration does it act on?**

**An axial vector is not an arrow that points; it is a circulation with an axis.** That answers "what

This is the founder's fourth-dimension intuition made concrete. The relative sense of two orthogonal

3D *process*.

## 3. What the detector actually sees — and it is never a configuration

An LHC detector never resolves a CP configuration. It measures **angular distributions of decay
products**: for W → eν, the charged lepton emitted preferentially against the W's spin. That is a
correlation between an **axis** (axial) and a **momentum** (polar) — a pseudoscalar, i.e. **b**.

So the chain from A_i to the detector is:

1. the W is SF-2's **bracelet**: a 6-cycle of hDPs with D₆ symmetry;
2. a 6-cycle has **two traversal senses**, mirror images — the bracelet's own chirality, a
   configurational degree of freedom with exactly two values;
3. when the bracelet decays it **opens at a vertex**, and which vertex is reached next depends on the
   traversal sense;
4. the opening vertex sets the emission direction relative to the ring axis → **the V−A angular
   asymmetry**.

**Nothing in that chain is a force.** It is the successor rule in a cyclic order, which is the same
kind of object as the 12-edge rule — a rule-based selection, exactly as the founder describes for
charge.

## 4. His sub-question, and a consilience worth noting

> *"Do the other CPs in the particle only take certain configurations within a particle?"*

**Yes, and they are enumerable.** SS-2's proton is a specific tetrahedral cell of the 600-cell, three
vertices occupied and one open; SF-2's W is the 6-cycle bracelet. These are discrete named objects,
not continuous shapes.

**And each carries a traversal sense.** The bracelet's 6-cycle has two. The proton's three occupied
vertices, ordered around the axis through the open vertex, also have two. So the chirality degree of
freedom the founder is looking for **exists in both structures.**

**The consilience:** Patch 4134 found the proton's pseudoscalar vanishes on orientation average — and
this picture says why. Nothing in the nucleon's ground state prefers one traversal sense over the
other, so both are equally populated and the average is zero. **The W is different** because its
traversal sense is set by the incoming particle that formed it, which is the free/confined
distinction of Patches 4135 and the filter table, arriving here from a third direction.
