## 1. Range — the charged-current half is already in SF-2

**Prop 5.2 (differential-gradient capture):** the centroid attracts only CPs *"in non-zero-gradient
host states"* — the linear-oscillator eCP of a quark is named — and *"does not attract a CP that is
already in an SSV-gradient-zero configuration."* **Prop 5.4:** the activated W disintegrates

## 2. The conflict

SF-2's β⁻ walkthrough, verbatim in substance: the −eCP transits to the centroid; the down quark,
*"having lost its linear-oscillator −eCP, structurally becomes an up-quark"* (**no refill**); the
freed −eCP *"induces a Zitterbewegung orbital from a nearby eDP"* and the reactive opposite-spin
eDP *"propagates away as the electron antineutrino."* **That is P1 (4199/4202): a fresh Sea
capture at the electron, core untouched.** 4202 §2 showed P1 conserves the nucleon's spin
projection in every decay and is therefore **Fermi-only**; the free neutron decays by Gamow–Teller
83% of the time (4200). **The shipped mechanism cannot produce the dominant decay channel.** The
founder ratified the inheritance picture (b) at 4205 (G-EW-INHERIT-4205), which can.

Two things in SF-2 survive unchanged under inheritance:
- **Prop 5.5's** *"spin is conserved de facto … no fundamental spin-conservation rule needs to be
  imposed"* — inheritance conserves it by construction (4201).
- **The EC-walkthrough criterion** for ν versus ν̄ — *freshly induced* partner = anti-type,
  *pre-existing orbital detaching* = matter-type. Under inheritance the β⁻ antineutrino is the
  freshly induced partner of the **core's** refill: still anti-type. **This is the ν/ν̄ label the
  arc has owed since 4199 — SF-2 already had it, attached to the wrong capture.**
