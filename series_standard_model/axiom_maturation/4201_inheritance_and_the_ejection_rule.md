## 1. The picture, read as one process

The founder's first bullet repeats 4199 (electron captures from the Sea, partner DP = antineutrino).
His bullets 2–5 give a different process, and **I take bullets 2–5 as the picture**, because the two
together would make **two** neutral spinning Sea DPs per decay and beta decay emits **one**:

1. the −eCP escapes **taking the down quark's own orbital DP** — the electron's spin is inherited;
2. the bare +qCP core **refills** from the Sea by electrostatic capture — it is now an up quark;
3. the Sea DP adjacent to that refill is left counter-spinning — **that** is the antineutrino.

## 2. What it gets right, by bookkeeping alone — rows verbatim (D-11)

```
down-quark orbital sense s = +1;  electron takes it -> A_e = +1
 refill sense  up quark  antineutrino  total after  lepton pair  channel   v_e   v_nubar
           +1        +1            -1           +1      singlet    Fermi   -1     -1
           -1        -1            +1           +1      triplet       GT   -1     +1

Fermi row: electron and antineutrino leave the same way (a = +1, measured).
GT row: opposite ways for this spin axis; the measured GT correlation is a = -1/3 (three orientations averaged).
Angular momentum: total after = total before in both rows. One neutral lepton per decay, as measured.
```

- **Angular momentum conserved** in both rows, with no transfer step.
- **Exactly one neutral lepton** per decay.
- **Both channels from one event.** The switch is nothing but **the sense of the refill**: the up
  quark takes the *same* sense as the departed DP → Fermi (nucleon projection unchanged, lepton
  singlet); the *opposite* sense → Gamow–Teller (projection flipped, lepton triplet). **4200's
  question "what decides the transfer" becomes "which way round does the core's new DP orbit?"** —
  a question about a capture from the Sea, not about excitation.
- **g_A restated:** the measured 3 g_A² : 1 says the refill takes the *opposite* sense to the departed
  DP about 4.9 times as often as the same sense. **Not derived.** A natural CPP place to look: the
  departing electron leaves along an axis set by its spin (§3) and drags DP-arcs with it, so the Sea
  the core refills from is not isotropic.

## 3. The handed rule moves — a correction to 4199

4199 put the primitive in **which sense the electron's captured DP orbits relative to its motion.**
Under inheritance **the electron does not choose a sense: it takes the quark's, whose orientation
has nothing to do with the electron's velocity.** So 4199's mechanism cannot give −v/c here. The only
remaining handed freedom is **the direction in which the −eCP + DP is ejected relative to the spin it
carries.** The rule keeps its form and changes its object:

> **Ejection rule: a lepton of label q leaves along q × (its own spin)** — electron (q = −1)
> against its spin, antineutrino (q = +1) along its spin.

With it: the Fermi row sends both leptons the same way (**a = +1, measured**); the GT row sends them
opposite ways along the spin axis (the measured a = −1/3 is that, averaged over the three triplet
orientations — **stated, not computed**). **The neutral-current form of the same term** (4193/4196,
"displace along qA") is untouched.
