## 3. Mixing — the route the founder names — computed with a polarised source

**Mechanism computed on, stated so it can be corrected** (4184, from the founder's relay ruling;
linearity from 4186/GR-1j): the register at a GP is the resident CP's self-term, cut off at one grid
spacing a, plus every other spin's contribution falling as a/r; the CP is stamped with the normalised
sum. **4186 proved the sum linear. Linear means N aligned spins add as N.**

4184 §3 compared **one** nearest spin (a/r) with 4170's cap and found three to eighteen orders of
margin. **But 4170's caps were set in polarised media** — a ferromagnet, the g−2 ring — where the
environment is not one spin. And the cap there is on **ε·P**, not ε. Rows pasted verbatim (D-11):


**A gram of magnetised iron a metre away would drag every spin into line with it in a seventh of a
second — no precession, just alignment.** NMR, neutron spin echo and the g−2 measurement itself would
be impossible. **The derived ε = a/r is excluded by six to twelve orders** once the source is a
magnet. GR-1j's homogeneous-cancellation lemma does not rescue it: it removes the *uniform infinite
Sea*, and a magnet is a departure from that.

**What this does to the FOR argument (p4187 §3).** Its second half — *"automatically consistent …
costs no parameter"* — **fails.** To survive, mixing needs a suppression of ~10⁻¹² or smaller put in
by hand: the calibration 4172 §5 and 4175 §4 priced and 4184 believed it had avoided. **The causal
argument itself (4183 §1) is untouched** — it asks that a mechanism exist, and 4191 found two.

**And it bears on the founder's idea directly.** Mixing at a strength that respects the caps changes
a spin by under 10⁻⁴⁶ per Moment. **It cannot organise an electron.** Mixing strong enough to organise
anything would already have been seen in every magnet.

needs no field, no mixing and no broadcast channel. **It is a worker proposal, unrun**
