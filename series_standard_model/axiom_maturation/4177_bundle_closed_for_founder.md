positively.** *Mass Generation: Geometric Hierarchies*, verbatim:

> *"**Orbital ZBW DP**: All fermions; produces **spin/magnetic moment**."*

**The corpus derives spin *and* magnetic moment from the orbital ZBW circulation of charge** — in
the EM/mass sector, without any axial broadcast channel, and long before Patch 4120. So A_i's
magnetism job is not merely redundant with SF-6; it is redundant with the mechanism that produces

## 2. And that clarifies what A1′ actually claims — modestly

If spin is already emergent from ZBW circulation, A1′'s *"CPs carry the attribute"* cannot mean a
new substance. Read against the χ draft — *"the ZBW is a double rotation whose relative sense
(self-dual or anti-self-dual) is fixed by the CP's polarity"* — it means:

> **The ZBW circulation's sense is a definite, carried property of the CP.**

That is a modest claim, it is compatible with spin being emergent, and **it is exactly what b needs**
(4165: b contracts the CP's own A). **A1′ is load-bearing and cheap.**
