# Every GP Relays — and That Settles the Mixing Ratio Without Calibration

**Patch:** 4184. **Lane:** EW. **Session:** 234.
**Corrects:** Patch 4183 §§2–3 (conclusion, not arithmetic) and Patch 4182 §2.
**Resolves:** the founder's ratio question; largely moots TODO-4183-SEASPIN.

---

## 1. The correction

I treated the occupancy result — no matter CP within the PSR shell, by seventy-six orders — as
meaning the shell is **silent**. **It is not.** Every GP receives, integrates and rebroadcasts, so
the shell is full of **carriers** even where it holds no **sources**. The axial content is a
**field**, not a particle-to-particle wire.

**The occupancy arithmetic stands. Its conclusion does not.** Both of my domination figures — 4182's
10⁴⁵ and 4183's implied "none" — are withdrawn.

## 2. The structure the relay picture forces

If every GP relays and only CP-bearing GPs source, the register at a GP is

  **A_register ≈ A_env(x) + (self-term from the resident CP)**

— a propagated field plus a local source term. And on a lattice **the self-term is cut off at one
grid spacing**, a = l_P/10³⁰ = 1.62×10⁻⁶⁵ m, rather than diverging.

So the ratio the founder asked for is **r/a**, where r is the distance to the nearest other spin:

| nearest other spin at | own : environment | ⇒ ε |
|---|---|---|
| 1 fermi (nucleon) | 6.2×10⁴⁹ | 1.6×10⁻⁵⁰ |
| 1 ångström (atom) | 6.2×10⁵⁴ | 1.6×10⁻⁵⁵ |
| 1 metre (lab) | 6.2×10⁶⁴ | 1.6×10⁻⁶⁵ |

> **The local CP dominates its own GP's register by fifty to sixty-five orders. The founder's
> suspicion — that the ratio is greater than 1 in the local CP's favour — is right, and by a margin
> nobody guessed at.**

## 3. And it is consistent, with no calibration and no parameter

The observational cap is **ε ≤ 5.4×10⁻⁴⁷** (4170, muon g−2). The relay structure gives:

| nearest spin | ε | margin below the cap |
|---|---|---|
| 1 fermi | 1.6×10⁻⁵⁰ | 3.3×10³ |
| 1 ångström | 1.6×10⁻⁵⁵ | 3.3×10⁸ |
| 1 metre | 1.6×10⁻⁶⁵ | 3.3×10¹⁸ |

**The mixing rule is automatically consistent with spin conservation.** It is not tuned to be: the
ratio falls out of the lattice spacing and the distance to the nearest neighbour.

> **So the ratio does not need calibrating. It is r/a — derived, not fitted. No new parameter.**

That answers the founder's *"would probably have to be calibrated"* with a no, and it removes the
cost that 4172 §5 and 4175 §4 both flagged.

**And it largely moots TODO-4183-SEASPIN.** Whether Sea DPs are spin-paired or independent changes
A_env by at most a √N factor; it cannot close a gap of 10⁵⁰. The Sea's structure is still worth
knowing, but the ratio no longer depends on it.

## 4. What this does to the retirement question

**It strengthens the case for keeping A3′, on the founder's own causal argument (4183 §1).**

- Spin **does** have a causal mechanism — the relay field reaches every GP;
- the mechanism is **consistent with observation automatically**, not by tuning;
- and it introduces **no parameter**.

**Against that: 4179 still stands.** The axial field A_i is not independent of ∇ × V_i in any part
matter can excite. So the relay carries axial information the polar channel's curl already
determines — the mechanism is real, and it may still be **redundant**. Those are different
objections and only the first has been answered.
