## 2. The numbers (rows)

```
GR-FE-1: R = 1e30   : alpha needs N = 9.17e+28 = one DI-bit per GP LAYER of a 9.2%-deep band
                      one DI-bit per GP on the sphere's surface: N = 1.3e+61  (1e+32 x alpha's N)
```

With R = GP layers per PSR (10³⁰ or 10³²):
- **α allows N ≈ 0.092 R per GP per Moment:** one DI-bit for each GP *layer* across a band 9.2% of a PSR deep (4302 §2).
- **One DI-bit for every GP on the sphere's surface** needs R² times more, about 10³²–10³⁴ times α's N.
- **One DI-bit for every GP in the 10% band** needs about 10⁶¹–10⁶⁵ times α's N, and would make α of order 10⁵⁹–10⁶³.

## 3. What has to give

Three statements cannot all hold: (a) every GP in the band receives a DI-bit each Moment, (b) the quantum of action is
one least-work over one Moment, (c) α = 1/137. Under (b), α fixes the emission at one DI-bit per GP *layer*, i.e. one GP
in R² ≈ 10⁶⁰ of the band's GPs per Moment. If (a) is instead the rule, then the action quantum must be about R² ≈ 10⁶⁰
least-works, and ħ_CPP would no longer be one DI-bit's work over one Moment.

The founder's own descriptions point both ways. "Every GP gets a DI-bit" favours (a). "The displacement against force in
the least stressed environment over one Moment corresponds to the action" is (b). And his account of delivery as diffuse
and re-radiated fits a sparse direct reach, which is what (b) with α implies.

## 4. What I think

The coincidence of 4302 §2 survives the correction and is now the crux: α's emission count is exactly "one per layer of
the landing band", within 8% for a 10% band. That is a small, regular number for CPP to explain. "One per GP" is not
consistent with α under the founder's action quantum. So the physical question is whether a GP's per-Moment emission
resolves the band's *depth* (one DI-bit per layer, about 10³⁰ per Moment) or its *whole volume* (10⁹⁰). I cannot settle
that by computation; it is a statement about what the emission is for.

## 5. Founder question (a physical picture)

**When a GP sends out its DI-bits for one Moment, is it painting the band's depth, one DI-bit for each layer from 90% to
100% of a PSR, or filling the band, one DI-bit on every GP in it?** Under your definition of the action quantum, α says
the first. If the second is right, what a single DI-bit's work over one Moment corresponds to must be about 10⁶⁰ times
smaller than the quantum of action.
