## 3. The condition the measurements add

His view is about which comes first: the energy sets the colour, and the frequency is read from it. For matter that is right. **An absolute de Broglie
frequency E/h is not observable.** It shifts with the choice of where energy is counted from, and only differences of it, and the wavelength h/p, are
seen (crystals diffract electrons and neutrons by h/p). So his de Broglie comparison favours him for matter.

**For light there is more: its field's oscillation is observed directly**, so the reading has to be a physical oscillation at E/h:
- **Beat notes.** Two lasers shone together on a fast photodiode produce an electrical signal at the difference of their frequencies: the light's
  oscillation, mixed down to where electronics can see it. This is how frequency combs work.
- **Field sampling.** The oscillating electric field of a light pulse has been traced cycle by cycle (Goulielmakis and others, 2004, by attosecond
  streaking; electro-optic sampling of terahertz and mid-infrared fields). Radio antennas record the field's oscillation directly, while the light
  still arrives in quanta of hf.
- **Combs count optical cycles** (script part 4). For hydrogen's 1S–2S line (Parthey and others, 2011), the comb counts the drive laser's cycles: a
  486 nm laser doubled to 243 nm, the two-photon transition at twice that light's frequency, about 2,466,061,413,187,035 Hz. This shows that an optical
  oscillation can be counted and that the atom answers that exact frequency. It is not an independent check of E/h, since the level energy is inferred
  from this frequency.
- **Gratings sort by crest spacing** (script part 1). On 600 lines per millimetre, hydrogen's red line (656 nm) leaves at 23.2° and its blue-green line
  (486 nm) at 17.0°, by sin θ = λ/d, without measuring energy.

**And the energy light delivers is proportional to that oscillation's frequency.** Millikan (1916) measured photoelectrons' energies electrically,
by a stopping voltage, against frequencies taken from spectral lines, and found them proportional with slope h (script part 3 is illustrative, made
from h, not from his data). Compton scattering tells the same story. This fits with 4446: the Bohr condition, used to assign level energies from
spectra, is an identity. E = hf for the energy light delivers is a finding.

**So the condition is:** whatever his "interpretation" means, the packet must hold a physical oscillation at E/h, with crests c/f apart, for beat notes,
field sampling, combs, gratings and the double slit (4444) to respond to. §5 asks whether that is what he means.
