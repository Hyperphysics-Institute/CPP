## 3. The secondary effect: an analogue, and a requirement (script parts 1–2)

**The standard analogue.** In a polarizable medium sitting in a bare vacuum, each polarized unit polarizes its neighbours: each
generation adds the Lorentz local field (P/3ε₀ for a cubic arrangement) that polarizes the next, and the generations sum to the
Clausius–Mossotti response to the macroscopic field. The script sums them and matches the closed form. Doubling the
macroscopic field doubles every generation, so the stored energy grows fourfold: **the cascade changes the coefficient, not the
power**, consistent with the square law relativity requires (4417 §4). In that analogue it converges only if each generation is
smaller than the last (else the polarization catastrophe), which is what "secondary" has to mean.

**Its limits.** The analogue needs a bare vacuum between the dipoles. If ε_bare = 0 (the condition 4417 left open), there is no bare background, and
in standard physics the vacuum is not treated as a discrete polarizable medium (ε₀ is a measured constant of the vacuum), so the generation series
is heuristic for the DP sea. What survives is this: whatever the sea's internal cascade, its macroscopic permittivity is the
measured ε₀ (and, for arcs, μ₀).

**The requirement.** If ε_bare = 0 (open, 4417), if realized mass is energy over c² (E = mc², assumed), and if the field energy is ½ε₀E² with
the measured ε₀, then the realized mass, primary and secondary together, totals the stored energy over c², and the secondary
effect is part of that mass, not an addition. That is a consistency condition CPP must meet, not a derived result. His wording
("not just the fermion's unpaired CP", "must be overcome in acceleration") can be read as something more; a total above E/c²
would be seen in the direct E = mc² test (Rainville et al. 2005) and the nuclear masses. Put to him (§6, question 3).

His secondary **arcing** (the magnetic analogue, μ₀) is not modelled here.

proportional to the gradient of E², so any crowding of DPs between the plates would be second order. But DPs pulled in from
the surroundings thin the surroundings by the same number: **the integrated count change is zero, so a redistribution adds no
net gravitating mass** unless DPs are created, the same objection 4417 §3 raised against 4416's count redistribution. Making
