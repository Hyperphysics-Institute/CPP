# α Fixes the Planck ZBW's Swing, Not Its Duration: at Full Covering the DP Swings 1/(2α) ≈ 68.5 PSR Apogee to Apogee, at Whatever V_i the Sea Allows

## 1. The ruling

At the Planck level the CP obeys the same axiom as at every scale: **it moves the distance of its SSV_net (V_i) each
Moment, not light speed.** DI-bits travel at light speed and build the 2–10% PSR shell each Moment. The CP's ordinary
speed is what lets the sequence of overlapping shells act on the surrounding DPs, building DP-arcs during acceleration
and exhausting them during deceleration. This settles the cycle-length fork of 4324 in favour of branch (B): the
ZBW₁ cycle is many Moments long, not the two-Moment flip of 4320.

**A correction to the question as he read it:** 4324's 215 Moments was not "at light speed". It was the slowing-apogee
profile with a *peak* speed of light at the centre; constant light speed gave 137. The ruling makes both moot: the CP's
speed is its V_i, which is below light speed.

## 2. What the ruling does to α: the Moments cancel (rows)

4324's general relation, α = c·PSR/(2L), rests on the half-cycle action being the sum of per-Moment actions against the
least force, ħ/2 = f₁·L·t_M. **It contains no speed and no count of Moments.** Any V_i history with the same swing L gives
the same α:

```
   full covering fixes L = PSR/(2 alpha) = 68.518 PSR apogee to apogee (amplitude 34.259 PSR)
      profile peak V_i (c)  Moments/half  Moments/cycle   L (PSR)      alpha
     constant        0.993            69            138    68.518   0.007297
     constant        0.100           686           1372    68.518   0.007297
         sine        0.996           108            216    68.518   0.007297
         sine        0.100          1077           2154    68.518   0.007297
    slow-ends        0.993           138            276    68.518   0.007297
    slow-ends        0.100          1371           2742    68.518   0.007297
   measured alpha = 0.007297: every row, whatever the speed or the number of Moments.
```

The table illustrates an identity rather than testing it. **Under his ruling, α does not constrain how long the ZBW₁
cycle takes; it constrains how far it swings.** With his full-covering picture (c = 1):

  **L = PSR/(2α) ≈ 68.5 PSR apogee to apogee — amplitude ≈ 34.3 PSR — so the Planck-level DP's two CPs are about 68.5
  Planck lengths apart at the ends of each swing** (in a quiet sea; an upper bound once the swing's own DP-arcs raise
  the local stress, §7).

At partial occupancy the swing shrinks in proportion (L = c·PSR/2α). Nothing on file gives a Planck-level DP's swing in
Planck lengths (searched), so this is a prediction of the picture, not a match to anything.

## 3. What is left free, and who sets it

The number of Moments per cycle is left **exactly where he put it**: in the V_i history, that is, in how fast DP-arcs
build up during the acceleration toward the centre and are exhausted during the deceleration toward apogee. It is at
least 137 (the light-speed bound) and could be far larger. It no longer enters α.
