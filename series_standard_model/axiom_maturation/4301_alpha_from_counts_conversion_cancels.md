# α From Lattice Counts: the Conversion Layer Cancels, α = N·s/(4π·PSR), and the "Cover Every GP" Picture Over-Emits by 10³²

**Patch:** 4301. **Lane:** EW → foundations. **Session:** 240.
**Founder:** `founders_voice/4301_picture_di_bit_transport_push_one_gp_action_as_least_work_per_moment.md`.
**Verify:** `series_standard_model/code/4301_alpha_from_counts.py`.

## 1. The founder's picture, taken literally

- Every GP emits the same number N of DI-bits each Moment (AP-4). They travel one GP per Moment out to the PSR and land in
  a band about 10% of a PSR wide.
- One intercepted DI-bit pushes a CP by one GP, the finest gradation.
- **The quantum of action is one GP of displacement against the least stress, over one Moment:** ħ_CPP = f₁ · s · t_M,
  where f₁ is the least force (one DI-bit's push), s the GP spacing, t_M the Moment.
- He cannot yet give the conversion between GP displacement and energy or angular momentum.

## 2. The conversion cancels out of α

Let a target CP of cross-section σ sit at distance r from a source. It intercepts N σ/(4π r²) DI-bits per Moment, each
pushing with f₁, so the force is F(r) = N σ f₁/(4π r²) and the potential energy is U(r) = N σ f₁/(4π r). **Coulomb's 1/r
law emerges from the counting**, as GR-1a's shell broadcast already argued for gravity. Then e²/(4πε₀) = U·r = N σ f₁/4π,
and with the founder's action quantum:

  **α = e²/(4πε₀ ħc) = N σ / (4π s · PSR).**

f₁, the conversion layer the founder cannot articulate, **cancels**. With σ = s² (the target intercepts what lands on its
own GP):

  **α = N · s / (4π · PSR).**

α depends only on two lattice counts: how many DI-bits a GP emits per Moment, and how many GPs fit in a PSR. This is his
expectation, that ħ should follow from the parameters under examination, in its only derivable form (4300 §3). U·r is
constant, so α does not depend on distance.

## 3. What α = 1/137 then requires (rows)

```
  GR-FE-1: PSR/s = 1e30       : alpha = 1/137 needs N = 9.17e+28 DI-bits per GP per Moment
      versus 'one DI-bit on every GP of the PSR sphere' N = 1.3e+61, or every GP of the 10% landing band N = 1.3e+90
  EU budget: PSR/s = 1/9.9e-33: alpha = 1/137 needs N = 9.26e+30 DI-bits per GP per Moment
      versus 'one DI-bit on every GP of the PSR sphere' N = 1.3e+65, or every GP of the 10% landing band N = 1.3e+96
With N = the covering count itself, alpha would be PSR/s = 1e30 -- charge 1e32 times too strong.
```

- **A prediction of N.** The measured α requires about 10²⁹ (GR-FE-1 spacing) or 10³¹ (EU spacing) DI-bits per GP per
  Moment. No value of N is on file (searched: AP-4 fixes that N is constant, not what it is).
- **The covering picture conflicts.** "At least one DI-bit on every GP of the PSR sphere" (founder 4299) needs 10⁶¹ to
  10⁶⁵ per Moment, and the 10% landing band 10⁹⁰ to 10⁹⁶. With that many, and one GP of push each, charge would be about
  10³² times stronger than measured. So **at most one GP in 10³² on the PSR sphere receives a DI-bit directly per Moment**,
  or each DI-bit's push is 10⁻³² of a GP, or the action quantum is 10³² of the founder's unit. Not all three of "cover
  every GP", "one GP per push" and "action = one push over one Moment" can hold.

## 4. What the founder's re-radiation does and does not change

Re-radiation through intermediate GPs makes the delivery from one CP to another diffuse and distance-dependent, as he
says. §2's counting assumes only that the total push delivered through a shell at r is conserved (nothing is lost, and the
shell's 1/r² spread is the whole distance dependence). If re-radiation *loses* push, the effective N is smaller still and
the conflict in §3 grows. If it merely redistributes, §2 stands unchanged.

## 5. What I think

The picture is now quantitative enough to be tested, and it passes one test and fails another. It passes: the counting
gives Coulomb's law and a distance-independent α, with the unarticulated conversion cancelling. It fails: the covering
requirement and the one-GP push cannot both hold at α = 1/137. The cleanest reading is that **N is about 10²⁹–10³¹ per GP
per Moment** and that a DI-bit's direct reach is sparse, one GP in 10³² on the PSR sphere, with re-radiation carrying the
information everywhere else. That is consistent with his own description of a diffuse, re-radiated delivery.

## 6. Founder question (a physical picture)

**Is "every GP on the sphere gets a DI-bit" a requirement of the model, or a hope?** If N is about 10²⁹–10³¹ per Moment,
each GP's DI-bits land on one GP in 10³² of the sphere, and the rest of the sphere learns of the source only by
re-radiation. Does that match your picture of how a CP's influence spreads?
