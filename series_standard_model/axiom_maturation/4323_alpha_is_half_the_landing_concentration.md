## 1. The proposal

Two ideas: (i) *"The PSR shell has a certain concentration of DI-bits on each GP. That concentration could be related to
the 1/137 coupling."* (ii) With a one-PSR flip the CP at each apogee *"would respond to its own message from the
previous apogee position"*; *"the timing must be adjusted to respond to the opposite charge's PSR-Shell"* (2–10% wide;
2% from the last computation, 4309).

## 2. (i) is exact: α = c/2 (rows)

Let **c be the number of DI-bits landing on one GP per Moment in the landing zone at r = PSR.** With a target taking what
lands on its own GP (σ = s², 4301) at a full push each (p = 1, 4299), the force at the PSR is c f₁. Conservation and
spreading (4309 §2: beyond the crowding radius a GP receives a DI-bit "only occasionally", thinning as 1/r²) make F r²
constant: Coulomb, with e²/(4πε₀) = c f₁ PSR². With the founder's action quantum (ħ/2 = f₁·PSR·t_M, 4320/4322) and
c = PSR/t_M, ħc = 2 f₁ PSR², so:

  **α = c/2.**

```
    measured alpha needs c = 2 alpha = 0.014595: one DI-bit per 68.52 GPs of the landing zone per Moment
    full covering (c = 1, founder 4303) gives alpha = 1/2 (68.5x too strong)
    chain check (arbitrary f1, PSR, t_M): e^2/(4 pi eps0 hbar c) = 0.007297  vs  c/2 = 0.007297
```

**His conjecture is not just "related": the concentration per GP is α's whole content.** No N, no R and no band
thickness appear. The measured α means that, each Moment, **one GP in 68.5 of the landing zone receives a DI-bit.**
"One landing in 68.5 acting on it" (4322 §7a) and "a partial push of 1/68.5" are the same statement as c = 2α when the
push per landing is whole; his picture supplies the first form, which needs no fractional push.

## 3. The band's width decides N, not α (rows)

```
    R = 1e+30: one-GP-deep surface (1.3e+61 GPs): N = 1.83e+59   (4322's 8 pi alpha R^2)
               2% band volume   (2.4e+89 GPs): N = 3.48e+87   (R returns through the band's depth)
```

N = c × (GPs in the landing zone). Whether the landing zone is a one-GP-deep surface (4322) or the 2% band's volume (4309)
changes N by 10²⁸ and brings R back into N, but it does not touch α = c/2. **The 2% width matters for N, not for α.**
This supersedes 4322's statement that "R drops out" of the relation for N: it drops out of α, which is what matters.

## 4. (ii) is right, and the table shows it (rows)

```
    Moment  +CP at  own last emission  dist  -CP last emission  dist
         1   -0.50               0.50  1.00              -0.50  0.00
         2    0.50              -0.50  1.00               0.50  0.00
    Each CP lands exactly one PSR from its OWN last emission point (on its own landing shell, within the 1.9% band)
    and at distance 0 from its partner's (at the centre of the partner's shell, never on it). A symmetric flip
    couples each CP to its own previous message; reaching the partner's shell needs a different timing.
```

This follows the PCD ordering as registered (`master_glossary.md` PCD: the GP perceives arrivals, computes and imprints
outgoing DI-bits, then the CP displaces, all within one Moment), with PSR taken as the landing radius (4309 §3's second
option). **His point stands exactly: a symmetric one-PSR flip hears only its own last message.** For the record, and not
proposed: if a GP acted on arrivals one Moment later than it does, the same flip would sit on the partner's shell and not
its own. That would change the PCD ordering, an axiom-level matter, so it is noted, not pursued.

The self-landing is itself a finding. **At every apogee, the flipping CP lands on the shell of its own previous volley.**
The Planck ZBW is therefore self-referential on the lattice. Whether that sustains the flip (a driven oscillation, as c04
describes for the Compton level) or damps it is a question for the ZBW lane (filed).

## 5. What I think

The founder has found the right variable. **α = c/2 says the fine-structure constant is half the probability that a GP
in the landing zone receives a DI-bit in a given Moment.** That is scale-free and needs no fractional push, no gravity and
no lattice count. Full covering (every GP, every Moment) gives α = ½; the world has about one GP in 68.5. The derivation
of α is now: **why is the landing zone's per-GP occupancy 2α per Moment?** The timing observation (§4) shows that the
partner coupling he is after is not delivered by the symmetric flip, so whatever sets c must be read on a charge's shell
at rest (the static case), not through the flip.

## 6. Founder question (a physical picture)

**In one Moment, does every GP on a charge's landing shell receive a DI-bit from it, or only some?** The measured α
needs about one GP in 68.5 to receive one each Moment (α = half that occupancy). If every GP receives one, α would be ½.
What in your picture keeps most of the shell's GPs empty in any one Moment — are the DI-bits fewer than the GPs, or do
they bunch?
