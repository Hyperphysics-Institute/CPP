## 1. What the answer changes

4244 and 4262 modelled each quark as several independent ZBW modes running *at once*, with their momenta adding.
The founder's picture is sequential. The quark falls into superposition with its vertex, where the SSV gradient
vanishes, reverses, and heads toward whichever gradient is strongest at that moment: the DP Sea, the sideways
vertex, or the diagonal vertex if the other up has left it open. At any instant it makes one excursion along one line.

The 4262 question dissolves on this picture. Nothing is "loose". The diagonal −qCP pulls when it is open, and it opens
whenever the other up swings away.

## 3. What I think

**(i) The sequential picture works only if the quark keeps its energy through the bounce, and the founder's own
words say it does.** Suppose each excursion were sized by its partner alone, as a Compton ground state for that one
line. Then g_A goes to 1.49–1.56, far worse than before, because one line's motion is much gentler than three added
together. Suppose instead the quark carries its whole breath energy through superposition. The founder says it
"reverses direction", which is a bounce, not a stop and restart. Then every excursion is as energetic as 4262's three
modes combined, and g_A = 1.316–1.383, bracketing 4262's 1.333. A classical harmonic bounce, fastest as it passes
through superposition, gives **1.322 (+3.6%)**.

**(ii) On that reading, which partner wins each time does not matter for g_A.** R depends only on how fast the quark
moves, not which way, once the frame is averaged. So the open-or-occupied timing of the diagonal vertex, and the Sea
versus the sideways pull, drop out. What matters is **how much breath energy each quark carries**. That is part of
the quark's mass, which ties this directly to the m_q tension (≈ 300 MeV needed to restore μ_p).

**(iii) Integer CP charges do not disturb anything computed.** The ±1/3 and ±2/3 arithmetic in 4262 §3 was a net-charge
check, now superseded by the founder's picture of superposition. No g_A row used fractional charges. They enter
only 4243 route (C), whose Coulomb amplitude used SS-2's colour coefficient, and that route was already rejected.

**(iv) Standing result.** Sequential bounce 1.32 before exchange; simultaneous 1.333 → 1.310 with exchange. Two open
items. First, the exchange term for sequential excursions: two ups both swinging toward the d is exactly the
founder's "move toward each other at random times", and it has not been recomputed for the sequential picture.
Second, the per-quark breath energy, which is the m_q derivation.
