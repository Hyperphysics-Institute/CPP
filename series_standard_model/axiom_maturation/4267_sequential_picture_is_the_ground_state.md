## 1. Why the owed "sequential exchange" dissolves

4263 left open whether the founder's picture — one line at a time, direction chosen at each passage through the
vertex, energy carried through (now R-ZBW-PASS-THROUGH) — is a different state from 4262's simultaneous modes. If it
were, it would need its own exchange calculation.

It is not a different state. In a harmonic well, a straight line through the centre is an exact classical orbit with
zero angular momentum. A quark that passes through the centre and leaves along a new line keeps L = 0. An ensemble of
such orbits in random directions is the classical L = 0 ensemble, and the quantum ground state is the L = 0 state of
the same well. The sequential picture is how that ground state looks as a trajectory. Sequential and simultaneous are
two descriptions of one state.

A pure one-line quantum state, by contrast, would carry zero momentum across its line. It would be unlocalised
there, which is 4261 (B)'s problem again. So the sequential picture cannot be taken as a quantum state in its own
right. Read as trajectories of the ground state, it is consistent.
