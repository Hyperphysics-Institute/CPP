1. **The core's weak charge is exactly 1/φ².** With sin²θ_W = 3/(8φ): 1 − (8/3)·3/(8φ) = 1 − 1/φ =

   antineutrino.** The founder pointed at the linear oscillation, not at the −eCP, and the numbers
   draw the same distinction. **An observation, not a derivation** — what a neutrino is in CPP has not
   been looked up here.
