## 5. Answer to his "What do you think?"

The split is right, and it passes the hardest test of its kind. The DP-arcs and separation carry the speed a body already
has, and the limb difference in SSV_abs changes it. Because his step adds the same increase in speed to every CP,
whatever it is, every body falls at the same rate, as the MICROSCOPE satellite measured to one part in 10¹⁵. "ΔSSV_abs is
the F" then means the force on a body is its whole mass times that common acceleration.

The one way to read it that would fail is a fixed push on each CP that the body's DP-arcs then resist. A body with more
CPs per unit of mass would fall faster: titanium and platinum would differ by about 2%. So it matters that the limb
difference raises the speed directly, as he described.
