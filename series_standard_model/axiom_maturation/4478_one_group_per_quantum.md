## 2. The at-once part is not new

Whatever gathers the photon must act at once, not merely faster than light, but the ground for that is not a theorem about one photon. For three or more
particles, any hidden influence travelling at a finite speed above c in a preferred frame, and reproducing the quantum correlations, would let people signal
faster than light (four parties: Bancal, Pironio, Acín, Liang, Scarani and Gisin, Nature Physics 8, 867, 2012; three: Barnea, Bancal, Liang and Gisin, Phys.
Rev. A 88, 022123, 2013). Bancal et al. assume the influence's speed is defined in a privileged frame, which is CPP's case. For two particles there is no such
theorem: a finite-speed model can reproduce the pair's correlations without signalling, and the speed is only bounded by experiment, at more than about 10⁴ c
for any preferred frame in which Earth moves at under 10⁻³ c (Salart, Baas, Branciard, Gisin and Zbinden, Nature 454, 861, 2008). If CPP's absolute frame is
the CMB rest frame, Earth moves at about 1.2 × 10⁻³ c in it (TODO-4364-PREFFRAME), just outside that stated condition. For one photon neither result applies:
its anticorrelation at two detectors is matched by a local model in which the energy rides one arm (the pilot-wave branch, 4477 g), and the spacelike
antibunching test (Guerreiro et al. 2012; 4477 §3) rules out a pull at light speed, not every finite speed above it. "At once" reaches the one photon only
because he proposes one mechanism for both ("the problem of entanglement/split photons and photon collaps timing resolves with the QGE concept"), with the
energy spread until a detector fires. So "faster than light", as he wrote the QGE, has to mean "at once" for the QGE as one mechanism for pairs, triples and
single photons. These are standard results, facts to match.

CPP already has an at-once element: A4, the Nexus, "a global consistency constraint [that] enforces lattice-wide coherence at each Absolute Moment"
(`axiom-registry.md`), used in QM-3 for entangled pairs. So the QGE does not need a channel of its own; his own suggestion, that it "may use the Nexus as a
connection point", is the economical one. (CPP's Absolute Moments give it a preferred frame; no-signalling is what keeps that frame hidden in any experiment.)

One registered text says otherwise. The Born-rule companion c03 (`series_relativity/SR_companion_papers/c03_born_rule/c03_born_rule.tex`, §"Collapse as
Progressive Positive-Feedback Cascade", v2.3) has collapse "not instantaneous", a cascade that "propagates outward at speed c" and locks within r_irrev, "not
because the Nexus forbids it". Read as the local locking of an outcome at the detector that fires, it is compatible with gathering at once. Read as the whole
of collapse, it is the light-speed pull that 4477 §3 ruled out for the split photon. Its wording is owed a reconciliation (§7 (g)), as SD-1's is (4477 e′).
(Claude's reading.)

## 3. What is new: bookkeeping per quantum (script)

Global conservation alone does not give one lump: half the photon's energy at each detector conserves energy exactly as well as all of it at one (script,
rule G). The absorber's gap does part of the work (4477 §4). Half of a 0.80 eV photon cannot cross a 0.75 eV InGaAs gap, so with gaps the halves would not be
absorbed at all and neither detector would fire, against the measured full rate (script, rule G with a gap). The gap forbids half-absorption; it cannot bring
the far half to the detector that fires. Two items below are already registered for densities and pairs: the Born weights (THEO-QM-2, via c03) and
no-signalling (THEO-QM-5). What the measured result needs (coincidences about a thousandth of independent, near detector noise; 4477 §3), and the registry
lacks, is a rule per quantum:
- **membership:** which CPs and DP arcs belong to this quantum;
- **gathered at once:** at the Moment of absorption, the quantum's whole energy is made available at the absorbing place, from wherever its members are;
- **made and unmade whole:** a quantum is absorbed or emitted only whole and at one place. Where energy is given up in part (Compton or Raman scattering, an
  electron in a collision), either the quantum is unmade and a new one made, or it keeps its identity and its group. ("Whole or not at all" for energy, as first
  written, fails for scattering; Claude's restatement.)
- **which one:** chosen with the Born weights (THEO-QM-2, extended from a density to the choice of one site; the script's rule Q gives zero coincidences and
  each detector its weight);
- **no-signalling for one quantum:** what one detector does cannot change the other's rate (THEO-QM-5 extended; script);
- **inheritance and merging:** when a quantum splits or is transformed (a photon into an electron–positron pair, as his archive has it), its products carry the
  group on, which is where entanglement begins; when groups meet (a joint measurement in entanglement swapping, two identical photons at a beam splitter), they
  merge.

That list is the physics core of his QGE. Its new content is membership, gathering at once, making and unmaking whole, and merging: nothing in the registry now
says that the Nexus keeps a membership per quantum or gathers a quantum's energy. In QM-3 the Nexus enforces DI-bit conservation and the pair's total spin, not
energy, and AP-4d gives energy no payload slot (it is configuration the census sees). So an energy rule per quantum also needs a lattice definition of a
quantum's energy (his archive: the total stretch of its DPs; §7 (i)). (The toy is bookkeeping, not CPP dynamics. It has no distance, no timing and no gathering, so it does not test "at once". Its zero coincidences and B's
unchanged rate hold by construction, since the site is drawn with fixed weights before A's setting is applied: it shows the rule, it does not test it. It does
not say whether the energy was spread before the choice, so it does not separate his picture from the pilot-wave branch, 4477 g.)

## 4. New axiom or a sharper A4? (recommendation, marked convenient)

His concern is the right one: an added mechanism is a cost. Two ways to record the core:
- **(A) A separate axiom, the QGE,** with its own membership and its own connection, using the Nexus as he suggests.
- **(B) The per-quantum scope of A4.** In QM-3 "the Nexus maintains this joint state globally" (CONV-031 §1) for a pair whose joint pattern state is non-separable: a state with members. Stating that A4 keeps each
  quantum's joint state (its members, inherited on splitting) and enforces conservation per quantum, whole or not at all, puts the QGE's core inside the axiom that is already at once. That is an amendment to A4, not a second mechanism, though it does add content.

**How much (B) saves.** As he proposed it, (A) also uses the Nexus as its connection, so (A) and (B) carry the same physics: membership, gathering at once,
making and unmaking whole, inheritance and merging. What differs is the label and what comes attached: (B) attaches only the bookkeeping, while his QGE comes
with the consciousness and character of groups. So (B) adds less than (A) as a whole, but not less physics. The saving is that no second at-once element and no
consciousness attribute enter the physics. The addition is not small either: A4 now enforces DI-bit conservation and a pair's spin, and (B) gives it energy per
quantum and a membership that changes in time. Either version must meet (§7 (h)):
- what a quantum is where the number of quanta is not definite (laser light, a coherent state: one group per mode rather than per photon?);
- identical particles (two photons meeting at a beam splitter leave together, so their groups act as one);
- entanglement swapping (a joint measurement on two pairs merges groups that never met);
- decoherence (an entangled set grows into its surroundings, so unless membership ends at an irreversible detection, c03's r_irrev, every group grows toward
  the whole lattice and the per-quantum scope falls back into the global A4; when membership ends is the measurement problem in another place).

**The road.** Either way this is an amendment by the registered process. His 8 August 2026 AP-4 ratification already names "linking conscious points into larger

§2, item 2). A per-quantum membership extends the state the substrate keeps, so it goes as a proposal (AP-6, citing that anchor), then to the panel, then to
his ratification. His ruling now sets the direction; it does not register anything.

Claude recommends (B), under PD-006: it adds no second mechanism and no consciousness attribute to the physics, uses the at-once element already registered, and gives A4 the reach the registry already says it lacks

## 5. His wider group entity

His QGE came from a larger idea: CPs bonding into groups whose character follows their shape and substance, from molecules to stars. The physics core above is narrower, but not because groups of many parts can be broken. An entangled pair is broken too, when one photon is absorbed and the
other goes on. A molecule is itself one quantum system: its levels take a photon whole, and whole molecules interfere (C60: Arndt, Nairz, Vos-Andreae, Keller,
van der Zouw and Zeilinger, Nature 401, 680, 1999). The difference is in what is claimed. The physics rule is about bookkeeping: quanta made and unmade whole,
joint states gathered at once. His wider idea is about character: what a bound group is like because of its conformation and substance. The first can be
tested against the experiments above. The second is not yet stated in a form physics can test, so it belongs in the vision lane. There the 2026-06-09 note
already frames it as a proposal on the combination problem, and its registry anchor is the AP-4 consciousness-grouping trigger (§4).
(Claude's reading; his "configuration of a substance creates the character" is recorded, not adopted into the physics.)

## 8. Reply to his "What do you think?"

I think you're right that something like your QGE is needed, and I don't think you made a mistake dropping and re-raising it; the record itself said "local" in
places, and we've now sorted that out before publishing. On the history: nothing in the record ever retired the QGE. It dropped out of use. The 'not necessary' from
June was about a chirality discriminator. And in August you yourself held group parameters back because you couldn't yet describe them. One of our own papers,
the Born-rule companion, still says collapse spreads at light speed; that needs fixing.

On your worry about adding a mechanism: the part that acts "at once" doesn't need adding. It has to be at once rather than just very fast (for three or more particles it's been shown that any finite speed would let people send signals faster than light; for two, experiments put it above ten
thousand times light speed; for one photon alone there's no such proof, so it rests on your QGE doing both jobs), and CPP already has an at-once element in the Nexus. Your idea that the QGE uses the Nexus
as its connection is the economical one.

What is genuinely new is the bookkeeping. Conserving energy overall isn't enough: half the photon at each detector conserves energy just as well. A detector's gap stops either half being absorbed,
but it can't bring the far half over. What's needed
is that each quantum is one group, with its members tracked, and it is absorbed or emitted whole at one place, its energy gathered there at once, at the usual odds, in a way no one can use
to send a message. That's the physics heart of your QGE. My recommendation is to write it into the Nexus, as the Nexus's reach for each quantum, rather than as a second mechanism; it adds no second mechanism and no consciousness attribute to the physics, though the
physics is the same as your QGE using the Nexus. Either way it would go to the panel as a proposal before you ratify it. That's convenient for me, and it changes an axiom, so it's your call.

One distinction: the physics rule is bookkeeping (quanta made and unmade whole, gathered at once), which experiments can test. Your larger picture is about
character, what a bound group is like because of its shape and substance, from molecules to stars. That isn't yet in a form physics can test, so I'd keep it in
the vision notes, anchored where you yourself flagged it at the AP-4 ratification.
