what is primitive and one step off about where it lives.** The primitive is the CP's handed
circulation (A1′). From it emerge: DP-arcs and hence the spin magnetic field (SF-6); and the twist in

learn about the axis. **The sense itself never needs to travel**, because the maturation record's
