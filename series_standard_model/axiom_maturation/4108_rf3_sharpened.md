## 1. What 4107 quietly committed the corpus to

Patch 4107 established that a polarized sea DP has its two CPs' arc cohorts **opposed**.
Notice what that requires.

A polarized sea DP at rest has **net momentum zero**. So:

- Under a **net-momentum** reading of the arc cohort — one arc encoding the particle's total
  momentum — v_arcs = 0, b is **undefined**, and the sea has no χ₄ response at all.
- Under a **per-interaction** reading — each CP carrying its own arc from its own displacement
  — the two arcs exist and are opposed.

SF-6 derives EM from *eDP-Sea Polarization*, which is precisely the statement that the two
constituents are displaced individually and oppositely. **So the corpus already uses the
per-interaction reading in the EM sector.** 4107's result depends on it.

## 2. The same reading decides R-F3

| reading | confined quark's arcs | ⟨b⟩ | F3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **per-interaction** (one arc per SSV partner) | along the 12 cage bond directions | **0, exactly** (20,000 random ω, max \|Σ bits\| = 0) | **HOLDS** |
| **net-momentum** (one arc along net displacement) | a single generic direction | \|b\| = 1 per quark, no cancellation | **FAILS** by ~10⁷ |

Under the per-interaction reading the arcs land on the antipodally paired cage shell and
cancel pairwise, exactly as Patch 4101 derived. Under the net-momentum reading each confined
quark carries b = ±1 outright, against a hadronic parity-violation bound of ~10⁻⁷.

**One reading yields both F2 and F3. The other breaks F3 outright and would have left b
undefined for the very sea DPs 4107 relies on.**

## 3. How the two readings reconcile with the 4097 ruling

The 4097 ruling says the arcs carry the KE/momentum/velocity — and momentum is a single
vector, which sounds like the net-momentum reading. The tension dissolves if the arc cohort is
a **set** whose **vector sum** is the momentum:

- polarized sea DP at rest: two opposed arcs, vector sum **0** ✓ (no net momentum, but each
  constituent still carries an arc — which is what 4107 needs)
- free particle: arcs summing to **p** ✓ (persistent direction, |⟨b⟩| = 1 — the weak sector)
- confined quark: arcs along cage bonds, vector sum ≈ **0** ✓ (bound, no net momentum)

All three cases work under one picture. The bit b is then written **per arc**, not once for
the sum.

## 4. The single question that settles both

R-F3 as filed at 4101 asked whether a confined quark's arcs lie on cage bond directions. That
was the right worry but the wrong level. The underlying question is:

> **Is the helicity bit b written once per ARC in the cohort, or once for the cohort's VECTOR
> SUM?**

- **Per arc** ⇒ arcs resolve per SSV partner ⇒ cage bonds ⇒ **F3 derived**, and 4107's F2
  result keeps its basis. Both founder questions close on one answer.
- **Per vector sum** ⇒ b is undefined for anything at rest (including the sea, undermining
  4107) and generic-direction for a confined quark ⇒ **F3 fails by ~10⁷**.

## 5. Honest status

This is an **argument from precedent, not a derivation.** That the EM sector uses the
per-interaction reading does not logically compel the strong sector to. But it does mean the
per-arc answer is the one already load-bearing elsewhere in the corpus, and that the
alternative would cost 4107 its footing.
