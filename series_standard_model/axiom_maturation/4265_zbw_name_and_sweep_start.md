## 2. The name: keep "ZBW" — it fits better now

**The ruled cycle is closer to Schrödinger's than the retired one was.** In the Dirac theory, the electron's position
carries a term that oscillates **sinusoidally**, at angular frequency 2mc²/ħ, with an amplitude of order ħ/2mc. That is
a smooth, energy-conserving oscillation about a centre. Nothing in it resembles a collision, a stop and a rebound, so
the retired stop-and-reverse was the part of CPP's ZBW that departed from the original. The pass-through cycle has
Compton-scale amplitude, Compton-order frequency, and speed peaking at the centre. That is the same kind of motion
Schrödinger named.

**Renaming would cost much more than it gains.** "ZBW" appears in 1,158 files across the corpus, glossary and papers.
A new name would break every cross-reference and would read to outside physicists as a claim of a *different*
phenomenon.

**Three honest differences** belong in the papers' first-use definitions. None argues for a new name.
1. **Mechanism.** Standard theory gets ZBW from the interference of positive- and negative-energy parts of a single
   Dirac wavefunction, and many modern treatments regard it as not directly observable for a free electron. CPP
   claims a literal mechanism: a CP oscillating through its opposite-charge partner. That is a CPP claim about what
   the formalism describes, and should be stated as such.
2. **Speed profile.** Dirac's velocity operator has eigenvalues ±c: the instantaneous speed is always c. CPP's cycle
   has speed peaking at the crossing and zero at the extremes. The two agree in kind only at the level of expectation
   values.
3. **Frequency factor, to be checked (filed).** Schrödinger's position oscillation runs at 2mc²/ħ. c04 sets the cycle
   at ν_C = mc²/ħ. In the pass-through cycle the CP crosses superposition twice per cycle, so speed and kinetic energy
   run at 2ν_C while position runs at ν_C. Whether that reconciles the factor, or whether c04's convention and
   Schrödinger's differ by 2 in what they call the cycle, is not settled here. It needs checking before any paper
   claims agreement with Schrödinger's frequency.

**Recommendation.** Keep "ZBW". On first use in each paper during the transition, say *pass-through ZBW cycle*, with
one sentence recording that it supersedes the stop-and-reverse reading.
