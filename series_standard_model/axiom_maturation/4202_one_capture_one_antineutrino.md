## 1. Yes — that is exactly the concern

Every capture of a fresh DP from the Sea creates new orbital angular momentum, and the Sea must
answer with a counter-spinning partner. **Two fresh captures give two partners; beta decay emits one
antineutrino.** Rows verbatim (D-11):

```
neutral spinning Sea DPs created per decay (measured: one antineutrino)
  1   P1 (4199): electron captures a Sea DP; core keeps its DP
  1   P2 (4201): electron takes the quark DP; core refills from the Sea
  2   P3 (founder 4202 as asked): electron captures a Sea DP AND core refills

SU(6): per down quark in a neutron, n_up - n_down = 0.667  ->  P(d spin parallel to neutron spin) = 0.833
P2 predicts the beta electron's spin is that d-quark orbital sense: parallel to the neutron spin 5/6 of the time before ejection.
```

## 2. The two pictures that give one

- **P1 (4199):** the electron captures from the Sea; the core keeps its DP. One partner. But then the
  core never changes, and the Gamow–Teller channel has no mechanism (4200's dead end).
- **P2 (4201):** the electron takes the quark's already-orbiting DP — **no new angular momentum is
  created, so no partner is owed** — and the core refills from the Sea. One partner, and the refill
  sense is the channel.

**P3 (both captures) is the founder's bullets as asked, and it is over by one.** The only way to keep
both bullets is if the DP the electron "captures" *is* the quark's departing DP — which is P2.

## 3. P2 makes a prediction P1 does not

In P2 the beta electron's spin **is the departed down quark's orbital sense.** In the standard quark
model a neutron's down quarks are spin-parallel to the neutron 5/6 of the time (§1 rows). So P2
says: **before the ejection rule acts, the electron's spin is parallel to the neutron's spin with
probability 5/6.** The measured correlation between electron spin and neutron spin in polarised
neutron decay (the parameter usually called N, with the ejection rule and the −v/c factor folded in)
is the number to compare against. **Not done here**; it is the first discriminating test between P1
and P2 and is filed.

answer is "opposite to the departed DP, about 5:1", that is g_A.
