## 1. LSP′ is not the Grid Point — it is what the Grid Point broadcasts

No renaming happened, and I checked my own usage across patches 4109–4119 for conflation and
found none. **LSP′ = Lattice State Packet.** From A3′:

> *"At every Absolute Moment each GP **broadcasts** to its PSR shell the Lattice State Packet
> LSP′ = (x_GP, t_abs; Φ, V_i, Q_ij)."*

**GP** = the grid point (the thing). **LSP′** = the packet it sends (the message). It is defined
in `master_glossary.md`:

> *"The GP→GP broadcast after the A3′ amendment (registered Patch 1129) — nine dynamical
> components: scalar Φ = |SSV|_abs, vector V_i = SSV_net, and symmetric-traceless Q_ij …
> Supersedes: DI-bit (scalar) → LSP (scalar+vector) → LSP′."*

**Why it was unfamiliar:** it is **GR-lane vocabulary**, introduced via
`general_relativity_from_LSP.tex` and ratified at Patch 1129. It now appears in **183 corpus
files**. A term that central being unfamiliar to the founder is worth noting as a
communication fact: when I say "LSP′" I mean **the nine-component packet**, and I will say
"the GP broadcast packet (LSP′)" on first use from here.

So "the vector string for CP, GP, and DI-bit" in your words = **the LSP′ content plus the CP
attribute and the DI-bit payload** — the same three carriers Option B names (A1′, A3′, AP-4).
We were describing the same thing in different vocabularies.
