## 2. Assessment

**Claim 3 is the adopted amendment, in his vocabulary.** GP / CP / DI-bit are exactly the three
matched carriers of Patch 4120: A3′ (the GP broadcast gains A_i), A1′ (the CP carries the
attribute), AP-4 (the DI-bit transports it). Consistent with the registry; nothing to change.

**Claim 1 cannot be true of the *seed*, and the corpus has already shown it.** If every
ingredient and every rule is mirror-symmetric, every sum of them is mirror-symmetric: the mirror
image of any composite is an equally good solution. Three results on file:

- maturation §1 — the nine axioms contain no parity-odd element; four routes closed (4046, 4068,
  4069/70, 4071);
- maturation §2d S1 (4073) — *any sum of rotations* confined to 3-space has B∧B ≡ 0, verified to
  five superposed rotations. Summation does not manufacture handedness;
- 4110 — the unique pseudoscalar buildable from (Φ, V, Q) vanishes on every named CPP structure,
  the W bracelet included.

And the weak interaction's handedness is **universal** — the same hand in every W, everywhere.
Handedness that emerged from assembly alone would come out either hand, varying by region.
So something parity-odd **is carried**. The founder supplied it himself: the 4D element he
flagged as new at 4109 and approved at 4120. Claim 2 lists it among the ingredients.

**Claim 1 is true of the *handedness*, and this is the part worth keeping.** The registry's
derived bit is **b ~ A·V** (axiom-registry A3′ row, Patch 4120). Neither factor is handed:

- **A** (the axial channel, the CP's ZBW spin) is a spin axis — by itself it has no hand;
- **V** (the polar channel, SSV_net — the GP's summed 3D message) is a direction — no hand either.

Handedness is the **relation** between them, and that relation exists at one place only: the GP
where the summed message and the landing CP's attribute meet, each Moment. That is the founder's
sentence — *"The CP, upon landing on the GP, responds to the local GP's Helicity bit and to the
GP's summation of the 3D messages."*

> **The seed is carried. The handedness is emergent.** A CP does not carry "left" or "right"; it
> carries a spin axis. "Left" or "right" is what the GP's summation makes of that axis against
> the summed push, Moment by Moment, and it depends on the whole bonded aggregate.

This is not a compromise wording. It is what b ~ A·V says, and it is why 4097 (velocity is not
intrinsic to a CP; it lives in the DP arcs) and the helicity bit fit together: the V-side of the
bit was never the CP's own to carry.

**PD-008 — the convenient branch, marked.** Reading the founder as "seed carried + handedness
emergent" is the reading that **keeps the amendment I have spent a session on.** The inconvenient
reading is that he means *nothing* is carried, which would withdraw the amendment. I reject that
reading on the three corpus results above and on his own claim 2, which names the 4D bits as
ingredients — not on its inconvenience. The next window should check that.

## 3. What this changes

**R-F3 is closed as a founder question.** Answered at 4109, re-confirmed today. Under PD-006/007
the working PCD extension is: **per-CP, per-Moment declaration to its own GP; summation of all
channels, A_i included, at the receiving GP over its PSR shell.** No further picture is owed by

**F3 becomes a calculation, not a ruling.** Under that extension the strong sector is P-even iff
the summed A·V a confined quark reads vanishes on the real cage. One structural observation,
**stated, not yet tested**: under point inversion through the cage centre a polar V goes to −V and
an axial A to +A, so any cage configuration that is inversion-symmetric — *spins included* — has
b(−r) = −b(r) and the shell sum cancels pairwise. That is 4101's antipodal-pairing result in the
amendment's language, and a uniform net spin is automatically inversion-even. **What is not
established** is whether the corpus's actual nucleon cage (SF-2/SF-3 type and polarity
assignments, and whatever spin assignment the amendment implies — none was ever made, per the
4107 ruling) *is* inversion-symmetric. Per D-3 that must be located before anything is built on
it, and per the Session 233 method warning it must be run on the real 12- and 20-shells, not a
generic configuration. Filed **TODO-4133-F3CALC**.

**A caution the same observation raises, recorded so it is not lost:** the W bracelet's D6 ring
is *also* inversion-symmetric in position. So the argument cannot be "cages cancel, bracelets do
not" on geometry alone. The corpus's account (`founders_voice/4097_…`, worker note: *"bracelet reads a biased bit stream"*) is that the
bracelet reads the **incoming free particle's** bit — whose arc cohort has a persistent
direction — not a bit of its own. The founder's phrase "a structure, like a neutron or W⁰ … bonded
in a way that interacts to produce a handed effect" is compatible with that (the handed *effect*
is the response), but TODO-4133-F3CALC must keep the two cases on the same footing and show why
they differ, or it proves too much.
