# The Principle That Selects T₁g — Sources, Not Representations

**Patch:** 4172. **Lane:** EW/GR. **Session:** 234.
**Answers:** TODO-4171-PARITYPARTNERS.

## 1. The question 4171 left

Either a principle selects T₁g and excludes A_u and H_u, or A3′ is still incomplete by two channels.
4171 could not find the principle stated. **It is there, and it is in A3′'s own text — just not
where the amendment looked.**

## 2. The principle: a channel exists iff matter supplies a source for it

A3′ states the source for the tensor channel explicitly:

> *"…Q_ij sourced by **−(16πG/c⁴)T_ij^{TF}** (λ fixed by the scalar sector's G — zero new
> parameters)…"*

So the broadcast channels are not an abstract representation catalogue — **they are the pieces of the
matter source, decomposed.** And the matter source available is the stress–energy tensor, which
decomposes exactly and only as:

| piece of T_μν | rank/parity | irrep | channel |
|---|---|---|---|
| T_00, energy density | scalar, even | **A_g** | Φ |
| T_0i, momentum density | vector, **odd** | **T₁u** | V_i |
| T_ij^TF, stress | rank-2, even | **H_g** | Q_ij |

**1 + 3 + 5 = 9.** Not a coincidence and not a point-group fact — it is the decomposition of a
symmetric rank-2 source.

**There is no pseudoscalar in T_μν, and no parity-odd rank-2.** So **A_u and H_u have nothing to
source them.** They are absent on principle, not by oversight.

## 3. And the same principle is the amendment's real argument

Matter has a **second** conserved current that T_μν does not contain: **spin density, an axial
vector.** In the symmetric (Belinfante) stress tensor it is absorbed; it is not independently
represented. So:

> **A_i exists because spin is a source and had no channel — not because a representation catalogue
> was short of an entry.**

That is a **better argument than the one 4120 gave**, and it is the principle 4171 asked for. It
selects T₁g and excludes A_u and H_u in one stroke: three sources from T_μν, one from spin density,
nothing pseudoscalar, nothing odd-rank-2.

## 4. Consilience — and it is mine, not the corpus's

This is exactly the structure of **Einstein–Cartan** gravity: energy–momentum sources curvature,
**spin sources torsion**, and the two are independent because spin is not in T_μν. **A_i is CPP's
torsion analogue.**

The corpus does not say this. I searched: every occurrence of "torsion" in the corpus is *mechanical*
torsion of dark-matter rods, and Einstein–Cartan appears nowhere. **So this is a proposal, and it
comes with a bill attached** — Einstein–Cartan torsion effects are bounded experimentally, and if
A_i is the torsion channel those bounds apply to it directly. That would be the first real
constraint the channel has faced from outside the corpus. Filed as **TODO-4172-TORSION**.

## 5. The gap this exposes, and it explains the whole session

A3′ gives Q_ij a source **with a coefficient** — −(16πG/c⁴). **The amendment gives A_i no source
equation and no coupling constant at all.** Its text says what A_i *is* (the CP's ZBW spin) and
which irrep it fills, and stops.

**That is why the channel has no empirical signature.** 4155 §4, 4165 §6, 4170 §3 and 4171 §1 each
found the same emptiness from a different direction and none of them named the cause. **A channel
with no coupling constant cannot predict a magnitude — only a sign structure.** Everything this
session derived about A_i was sign structure: THEO-CHIR-1 is a selection rule; ⟨b⟩ = (v/c)cos θ got
its magnitude from kinematics, not from a coupling.

**So the amendment's real deficiency is not a missing channel. It is a missing source equation.**
Filed as **TODO-4172-SOURCEEQ** — write A_i's source clause in A3′'s own form: A_i sourced by
(coefficient)×(spin density), with the coefficient fixed by an existing constant or declared as a
new parameter. **If it requires a new parameter, that is a cost the programme's zero-parameter claim
has to absorb, and it should be stated as such.**

## 6. PD-008 — the convenient branch, marked

The convenient branch was §3 alone: find the principle, report that the amendment is fine, close
4171. §5 is the inconvenient half — the same principle that rescues the amendment's *structure*
exposes that its *dynamics* was never written, and that a zero-parameter programme may be about to
acquire a parameter. §4 is inconvenient in a second way: the consilience I am pleased with is also
an exposure to bounds the channel has never been tested against.
