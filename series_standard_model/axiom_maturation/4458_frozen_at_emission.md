as its speed approaches c (to 0.44 of the outside rate at 0.9c, 0.14 at 0.99c, 0.0014 at 0.999999c), and at c it stops. So nothing happens inside a photon
on the way: no internal clock, no self-initiated change. The firmer statements: a massless photon cannot decay (it is forbidden by energy and momentum),
