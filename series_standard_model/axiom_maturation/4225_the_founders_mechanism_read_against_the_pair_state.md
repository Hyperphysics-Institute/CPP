   framework's own switch ("activated when a charge is captured at the D₆ centroid," 4198/4208 §4 item 1),

   electron takes the quark's original orbital.
3. **The open core picks up a random Sea eDP passing tangentially; if it can separate, an antispin eDP is
   created with it — the antineutrino.** — 4208's emergent refill and 4219's counter-spinning partner, with
   one word that matters: **random**. A tangential pass in any plane gives the new orbital an axis in any
   direction, not just ±s. That is exactly the freedom 4223 §2 needed: the "3" in 1 + 3λ² is the number of
   directions the refill can carry the flipped unit in, and a mechanism whose refill axis were confined to
   the parent's axis could not produce it.
4. **The W⁰ breaks the antineutrino off the new orbital and frees the electron in the same act; the two
   leave together, mixing spins until they separate.** — This is the "one coherent two-GP event" of

   And "mixing spins until they separate" is the mutual induction 4224 §4 asked for. Fork answered yes.

## 2. Two phrases that must be read non-literally — one of them computed

**(a) "go off together in the same direction."** Literally, collinear emission is a = +1: the pure-Fermi
signature. Measured a ≈ −0.105: a slight preference for *opposite* directions (GT dominates). So "together"
must mean *from one site in one event*, after which the handed rule sends each lepton along its own spin
(electron against, antineutrino along) and the directions come out as 4223 computes them. If it were
meant literally, it would be a roadblock at the first number.

**(b) "at which time the spin is fixed."** Two readings. **(A)** The pair leaves in the coherent pair state
and each spin is fixed only when something acts on it — the ejection rule acting on the *pair*. **(B)** At
separation the pair collapses to one definite configuration (e up / ν̄ down, etc.) with 4224's
probabilities, and each lepton is then ejected with its fixed spin. Rows verbatim (D-11):

```
                                                   a       A       B
measured lambda, (A) coherent                 -0.107  -0.119  +0.987
measured lambda, (B) decohered at separation  -0.037  -0.121  +0.987
pure Fermi, (A) coherent                      +1.000  -0.000  +0.000
pure Fermi, (B) decohered at separation       +0.351  +0.013  -0.007
pure GT, (A) coherent                         -0.333  -0.667  +0.667
pure GT, (B) decohered at separation          -0.116  -0.670  +0.668

measured: a = -0.104 to -0.108 (recent measurements, uncertainty below 0.001); A = -0.119; B = +0.987
```

**A and B cannot tell the two apart — they depend only on the populations. a can, and does: reading (B)
gives a = −0.037 against a measured −0.105 with sub-percent error.** The electron–antineutrino angular
correlation is the direct witness that *the pair leaves entangled* — the relative phase between "electron
up, antineutrino down" and "electron down, antineutrino up" survives separation and is what the two
ejection directions are correlated through. In the founder's words this means: the spins are not "fixed"
at separation; what is fixed at separation is the *exchange symmetry* (4224 §3), and each lepton's own
sense is fixed only when its ejection direction is chosen. **"After the time of uncertainty" must be the
Moment of ejection, not the Moment of separation.** This is the first item on the watch-list and it is
already resolved in favour of the mechanism *provided* the mixing is coherent — which it must be in c03
anyway, since c03's selection is an amplitude sum, not a population sum.

## 3. What the mechanism still owes — the roadblock watch-list, in order of computability

1. **The symmetry rule.** Why does a same-sense refill leave the pair *antisymmetric* under exchange and an
   opposite-sense refill *symmetric*? In the founder's picture the refill and the antineutrino are born as a
   pair (step 3) and the electron's orbital is the third DP; the mixing is among three DPs at two GPs. The
   requirement is one two-particle rule: the exchange symmetry of the two departing DPs equals the sign of
   (refill sense · original sense). Not yet a CPP statement. **This is the next computation.**
2. **The breaking asymmetry (step 4).** "When the antineutrino portion collides with the W⁰" — the W⁰ is
   achiral (THEO-CHIR-1); the collision supplies the *event*, and the handedness is still imported by the
   released −eCP's own A (4208 §2). Consistent, but the collision's role in fixing *which* Sea DP becomes
   the antineutrino is the DP-arc-reach object (CONJ-FP-1) again — λ's magnitude.
3. **Rate.** "Many tangential passes will not create an orbital" — a rate statement, the neutron lifetime's.
   Not this arc; filed only.
