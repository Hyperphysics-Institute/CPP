## 1. What the statement settles

- **The binding is the core's charge, acting through the ZBW cycle**: the eDP's −eCP pole falls to the +qCP,
  superimposes on it (zero SSV gradient for a Moment — a reset), and at that Moment is free to be pulled by
  whatever else is present; the cycle repeats. The orbital is not a closed Kepler orbit but a
  fall-reset-return oscillation, and *the reset Moment is where the binding is decided each cycle*.
- **The release is the same reset at a different place**: the W⁰ centroid is a second zero-gradient pocket;
  when the −eCP pole transits it, the pole is reset there instead of at the core, and the strongest gradient
  present in the pocket — the stabilised linear −eCP of the founder's 4225 step 1, repulsive — sends it
  outward; the +eCP pole follows. **This is 4230's "charge" branch with its consequence taken: the release
  is the W⁰'s act, and it needs no screening.** 4230 §2's negative result is therefore not a roadblock; it
  said only that the *pair creation* does not release the orbital, and this mechanism does not ask it to.
- 4204's fork, 4228's allocation and 4229's ledger are all consistent with it: the orbital leaves as the
  antineutrino by being reset at the pocket, not detached from the core by force.

**(a) The mechanism gives B = +1 by geometry.** Any kick delivered while the pole is inside a pocket 250×
smaller than the orbit has a lever arm about the pole of at most the pocket radius, so it changes the eDP's
angular momentum about the core by at most 0.4%. The released orbital keeps its ħ/2 to better than one part
in a hundred — *which is exactly what G-EW-SWAP-4228's vertex (B = +1) asserted and 4229's ledger required*.
A pocket kick can change the radius the pole goes to next; it cannot change L. The mechanism and the ruling
agree on a number neither was built to fit.

**(b) The lifetime budget.** 6.7×10²⁵ ZBW cycles per neutron lifetime; the pole is in an in-plane, on-circle
pocket 1.2×10⁻³ of the time; so the product (a W⁰ present at the orbit and aligned) × (the reset sends the
pole outward rather than back) must be ~10⁻²³ per cycle. A virtual W⁰ lives 6×10⁻⁴ cycles. This is the
structure of the neutron lifetime in the picture: **rate = (W⁰ formation attempts per cycle) × (6×10⁻⁴) ×
(alignment) × (1.2×10⁻³) × (outward-branch fraction).** Not derived; budgeted, with each factor named. Filed
as the rate item's new shape (4225 watch-list item 3).

## 3. What it owes — the energy

4229's ledger stands and is now sharper: the reset sends the pole outward at ~c with L fixed, but the eDP's
circulation energy (~313 MeV) must not leave with it. With L = ħ/2 fixed and the radius growing 10⁹–10¹⁰-fold,
the circulation speed must fall by the same factor; **where the energy goes on the way out is the open step.**
The refill half re-establishes the constituent mass at the core; the natural reading is that the outgoing pole's
energy is what the Sea converts into the created counter-pair — the release *pays for* the refill — but no
calculation says so. This is the one item on which the picture could still fail: if the outgoing eDP cannot
shed its energy to the pair creation, it leaves as a 313-MeV object, which is not a neutrino.
