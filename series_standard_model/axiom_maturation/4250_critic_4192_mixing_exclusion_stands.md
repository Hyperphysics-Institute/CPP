one reading that would void the test — the environment's axial content dressing the CP each Moment without
accumulating — is not the founder's: 4182 has the CP *"hop carrying that computed spin"* and mix again at the next GP,
which is the accumulating loop 4192 computed. And under that alternative reading the channel is inert (a 10⁻⁴³-rad
tilt), so **the physics conclusion — mixing at ε = a/r cannot organise an electron, and mixing strong enough to do so
would already have been seen in every magnet — holds on either reading.** What the reading decides is only whether

void the exclusion is a 1/r² count-dilution falloff; 4186 grounds 1/r for a relayed static-snapshot payload, so it is
recorded, not adopted. Consequence unchanged from 4192: p4187 §3's FOR argument loses *"costs no parameter"*.
