## 3. The rule, quantitatively (script part 2)

Write SSV_abs as the census u = Σ GM/(rc²). Newton's acceleration, per Moment and in units of light speed, is

    change of drift per Moment = g t_P / c = (g/c²) l_P = half the inner-minus-outer census difference,

the acceleration. Because the census is harmonic, the inverse square and the shell theorem follow. (The PSR's directional
corrections are of order u and do not change this.)
