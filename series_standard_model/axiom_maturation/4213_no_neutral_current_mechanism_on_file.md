## 1. What SF-2 says the Z can and cannot do

- **Theorem Zicos / Cor. Zinert:** the Z cage is a closed icosahedral shell with *"no open interior
  accessible to external CPs — there is no Z-centroid analog of the bracelet centroid where an
  external CP could bind. Therefore there is no 'activated Z' state."* SF-2 uses this to explain
  the absence of tree-level FCNC.
- So **the Z, as shipped, cannot host the event 4196 needs**: an electron transiting weak-charged
  matter and being displaced along its own spin in proportion to the local weak charge. That is a
  neutral-current interaction between two *different* fermions, and SF-2 gives the Z no way to
  couple to anything that is not already part of it.

## 2. The measured effect is real and is not optional

Atomic parity violation (caesium, 0.35%) and the Qweak proton measurement (4197: +0.0719 ± 0.0045)
are the neutral current acting between an electron and quarks, at contact range, with the weak
charges 4197–4198 sorted onto CPP constituents (+1/φ² per +qCP core, −1.073 per linear oscillator).
**No CPP mechanism produces them.** This is a gap of the same kind 4103 found for CKM: a measured
number with no derivation chain. **Registered as such below.**

## 3. Two routes, both worker proposals

**(a) The elastic W⁰ event.** The electron's bare −eCP is a non-zero-gradient host CP (Prop 5.2)
and is captured by a transient bracelet near a quark core; the activated W⁻ disintegrates without
species change and the −eCP is re-ejected — by the ejection rule (4201), along −A_e. Range: the
bracelet's. Weight: the rate of bracelet formation at that point, which would have to reproduce the
+1/φ² : −1.073 pattern of cores versus oscillators. Costs nothing new; asks the catalyst framework to
allow a capture that changes nothing. **First to try**, because everything it uses is on file.

**(b) An interacting Z.** Amend Cor. Zinert so the closed shell couples to co-located content
without opening — e.g. the DI-bit content of the Sea inside the icosahedron's volume. Costs a
theorem. **Second**, and only if (a) fails the core/oscillator pattern.

Neither is attempted here. Both are conditional on the ejection rule (4201), which is itself
imposed, not derived.
