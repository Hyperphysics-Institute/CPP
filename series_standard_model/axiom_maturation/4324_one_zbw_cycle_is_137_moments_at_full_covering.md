## 1. The proposal

(a) Only the DI-bits on the GP where the CP lands matter, since their sum directs its next displacement; *"every GP had
68 or 69 DI-bits, which, over many oscillations, would produce an average total DI-bit influence of 137 from apogee to
apogee."* (b) A two-Moment cycle leaves no time for DP-arcs to store potential energy and return it as kinetic, so
*"the ZBW cycle for a DP at ħ/2 units of action is much larger than two moments."*

## 2. (a) read per Moment is inverted; read per half-cycle it is right (rows)

```

    i.e. 68.5 is GPs per DI-bit, not DI-bits per GP.  Read PER HALF-CYCLE instead, it is (c) below.
```

But his count is right if it is accumulated **over the Moments of a half-cycle**, which is exactly what (b) makes
possible.

## 3. (b) The general relation, and 137 Moments (rows)

Let the half-cycle take K Moments along a path L. Its action is the sum of its per-Moment actions against the least
force, ħ/2 = f₁·L·t_M (4322 was K = 1, L = PSR). With 4323's Coulomb counting at occupancy c:

  **α = c · PSR / (2L).**

```
(b)/(c) a cycle of M Moments, CP stepping one PSR every Moment (constant speed c, a triangle wave):
    half-cycle K = M/2 Moments, L = (M/2) PSR  ->  alpha = c / M
    occupancy c = 1.0: M = c/alpha = 137.036 Moments per full ZBW cycle
    With full covering (c = 1, the founder's 4303 picture): ONE FULL ZBW CYCLE = 1/alpha = 137.04 MOMENTS.
    The CP lands on one GP per Moment holding one DI-bit: 68.5 landings per half-cycle, 137 per cycle --
    the founder's '68 or 69 per ... 137 from apogee to apogee', counted over Moments rather than per GP.

    the founder's profile (rest at apogee, fastest at the centre): sampled sine, largest step one PSR
    c = 1: alpha = sin(pi/M)/2  ->  M = 215.25 Moments per cycle (= 137.04 x pi/2 = 215.26 in the limit)
```

**His two ideas fit together.** Keep his original full covering (every GP of the shell receives a DI-bit each Moment,
4303), let the CP move one PSR per Moment (light speed, 4322), and let the ZBW cycle be long, as DP-arc storage needs:
then **α = 1/M, and one ZBW cycle is 137 Moments** at constant speed. The CP lands on 68.5 GPs in a half-cycle, one DI-bit
each: his "68 or 69" and "137 from apogee to apogee" exactly. With his slowing-at-apogee profile the cycle is 215
Moments (137 × π/2) instead. The 1/68.5 thinning that 4323 needed is not needed: the 137 moves out of the landing
occupancy and into the length of the cycle.

## 4. What the per-Moment action becomes (rows)

```
    a = f1 * PSR * t_M;  e^2/(4 pi eps0) = f1 PSR^2  ->  a = e^2/(4 pi eps0 c)  (the charge's own action unit)
    hbar = M a  ->  a/hbar = 1/M = alpha = 0.007297:  hbar is 137 per-Moment actions of one charge (constant speed)
```

With full covering, the action of one Moment (one PSR against the least force) is **e²/4πε₀c**, the charge's own action
unit. **ħ is then M of those: 137 Moments of one charge's elemental action per ZBW cycle.** This reverses the usual
order: the charge's quantum is the elemental one, and ħ is a count of it over one ZBW cycle.
