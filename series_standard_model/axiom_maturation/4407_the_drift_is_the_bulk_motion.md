## 2. What "the drift" means, and what the corpus has

By "drift" Claude meant the body's overall motion, its velocity as a whole, as opposed to the internal swinging and
orbiting of its CPs. The corpus calls this the **bulk** motion.

**What the corpus has:** it reproduces the slowing of a moving body's internal processes (time dilation, by the Lorentz
factor) at SR-1 strength, with γ imported or grounded at the energy level through the SF-6 pin plus Laue (OPEN-SR-EPSILON
resolved at W2 strength, 2502). Time dilation is measured: muons in the CERN storage ring at γ = 29.3, to about 0.1%
(Bailey et al. 1977).

**What it does not have:** a substrate rule for how each CP shares its one-PSR step between the bulk motion and its
internal swing. VTD-1 (2037) found the "quadrature" split equivalent to SR-1's effective internal rate, but its
confirmation (2038) declined to approve a literal per-CP allocation, and later work went further: the H.1 class theorem
was withdrawn (2475), SR-1's "bulk velocity consumes part of the displacement budget" was withdrawn as a premise
(2471–2475), and the geometric route closed negative (OPEN-SR-H1-CLASS, 2493; Round 2 Q1, 2500). The quadrature survives
only as an effective identity (PROP-SR-H1-1).

**Why a literal split would fail (script part 4):** for a CP swinging along the direction of travel at v = 0.5c, a bulk
step of 0.5 l_P plus an internal step of 0.87 l_P in the same direction is 1.37 l_P, beyond the cap. In special
relativity the honest picture is re-timing: both legs of the swing stay at or below the cap in the lab, the forward leg
lasts longer than the backward one, and the cycle lengthens (with length contraction, by exactly γ). That is 4406's
picture, and it is still the open item: **4406 item 2 (the drift as a whole-pattern boost under the cap) stays open,
sharpened.** It is re-put to the founder in §8 with "drift" defined.

**Equal fall, stated conditionally:** a falling body's internal rate changes by about 5×10⁻¹⁶ after a second (script
part 2), a function of its speed alone. But equal fall itself rests on the bulk gain being a whole-pattern boost and on
the sea depositing inertia κᵢ = Eᵢ/c² for each constituent (4406 items 2 and 3, and the lunar laser ranging
requirement). It holds **if** those hold.
