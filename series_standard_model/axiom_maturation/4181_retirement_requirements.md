## 1. The steelman, attempted properly — four candidate reasons to keep it

**(a) "The GP must hold A to imprint it on DI-bits."** The founder's own point at 4166, and it is
right — but it establishes **AP-4** (transport), not **A3′** (an LSP′ irrep component with a source
and dynamics). **Those are different amendments** and the argument reaches only the weaker one.

**(b) "Something at a distance needs to know a remote CP's spin."** Nothing identified does. b uses
the CP's **own** A, locally (4165). Magnetism comes from ZBW charge circulation (SM-2). The
quantum-phase identification uses the **V_i** register (QM-1, FI-QMRG-1). **No consumer found.**

**(c) "Angular-momentum conservation needs a transport channel for spin."** It would, if spin were
intrinsic. **It is not** — r_ZBW = 0.631 fm is resolved circulation (4178), so its angular momentum
is orbital and **V_i carries it.**

**(d) "b must be *derived*, not postulated"** — 4120's own stated reason for bundling all three.
Checked: **b = A_CP · V_i needs A1′ (the CP's attribute) and V_i, which A3′ already carried *before*
the amendment.** The derivation does not use the new channel.

## 2. What retirement requires — and the answer to "do we need something else instead?"

**Nothing else is needed physically.** Everything the channel was introduced to support survives on
A1′ plus the pre-existing V_i. But **six things must be done**, and skipping them is how a
retirement becomes a corpus inconsistency.

1. **A1′ must be made to stand alone.** 4120 says *"all three required together."* If two go, A1′'s
   clause must not reference them. **This is the one that matters most** — A1′ carries χ₄'s physics.

2. **b's definition must be restated at source.** It should read: **A1′'s carried attribute
   contracted with the pre-existing V_i register** — not "derived from the amendment bundle."

3. **Decide AP-4's A-clause separately.** It is not automatically retired with A3′. **Keeping it
   costs nothing and preserves the founder's spin-mixing proposal (4166); retiring it forecloses
   that proposal**, which is bounded at ε ≤ 5×10⁻⁴⁷ and so no observable loss — but it is his
   proposal and the choice should be explicit, not a side effect.

4. **Audit the falsifier suite.** A3G-2 and A3G-3 test the CP attribute and b, and **survive**.
   **A3G-1 (vacuum magnetisation) tested the summed A_i register's field and may become moot** — if
   there is no register, there is nothing to sum. Its recorded "does not fire" would then be a
   result about a channel that no longer exists.

5. **Verify — do not assume — that THEO-CHIR-1 and ⟨b⟩ = (v/c)cos θ use A1′ only.** I believe they
   do. **D-4 says believing is not checking**, and this session has twice punished that.

6. **Record why 4113's observation is true but unused.** T₁u ≠ T₁g under I_h is correct and stays
   correct. The corpus should say that the axial content is nonetheless **∇ × V_i — derived, not
   independent (4179)** — so a future window does not rediscover 4113 and re-propose the channel.

## 3. What retirement does NOT cost

- **χ₄'s physics: nothing.** V−A, F3, R-F3-ISO, THEO-CHIR-1, ⟨b⟩ = (v/c)cos θ all rest on A1′.
- **The zero-parameter claim: nothing** — it *removes* the coefficient question (TODO-4172-SOURCEEQ)
  entirely rather than answering it.
- **The falsifier record: nothing withdrawn** — A3G-2 and A3G-3 keep their results; A3G-1's becomes
  moot rather than wrong.
