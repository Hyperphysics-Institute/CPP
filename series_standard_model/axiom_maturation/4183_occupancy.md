## 1. The causal argument — and it outranks everything in my steelman

> *"If there is no mechanism for one CP's spin to affect another CP's spin, then the CP has no
> causal mechanism to alter its spin, which, to me, doesn't sound physical."*

**This is right, and it is the strongest reason to keep A3′ that has been offered.** A spin that
cannot be changed by anything is not conserved in the physical sense — it is **inert**, a label
rather than a dynamical variable. Every conserved quantity in physics is conserved *against*
interactions that could in principle change it. A quantity with no such interaction is a different
kind of object.

**None of the four reasons I constructed at 4181 §1 was this one.** I looked for a *consumer* of the
channel's information and did not ask whether spin needs a *cause*. Recorded as a failure of my
steelman, not of his position.

## 2. The counting objection — he is right, and the error is larger than he says

I wrote at 4182 that the environment dominates the register by ~10⁴⁵, from N ≈ 1.3×10⁹⁰ GPs in the
PSR band. **I counted GPs. Axial content comes only from GPs that carry a CP.**

| GP spacing (c01) | a = l_P/10³⁰ = 1.62×10⁻⁶⁵ m |

> **In ordinary matter the PSR shell contains no other particle's CP at all — by seventy-six orders.
> Not "few". None.**

So the 10⁴⁵ domination figure is **withdrawn**: it treated every GP as a spin source. **The founder
is right that the proportion is wrong, and it is wrong far more strongly than he suggested.**

## 3. Which relocates the whole question to the DP Sea

If matter supplies no neighbours at PSR range, **the only possible environmental axial source is the
DP Sea** — and the ratio he asks about is then set entirely by the Sea's spin structure:

- **If Sea DPs are spin-paired with anti-aligned partners**, each pair contributes zero and the
  residual is not √N but whatever the pairing fails to cancel. **The local CP could then dominate
  outright**, exactly as he suspects.
- **If Sea spins are independent and random**, the residual is √N over the *occupied* count, and the
  balance depends on the Sea's occupancy fraction, which I have not found stated.

**I could not find the Sea's spin-pairing structure in the corpus.** The DP is defined by *charge*
polarity (+/−); whether its two CPs' axial attributes are correlated is not stated anywhere I
searched. **That is the quantity the ratio depends on**, and it is prior to any calibration. Filed
as **TODO-4183-SEASPIN**.

## 4. On calibration

> *"the CP vs. GP mixing ratio would probably have to be calibrated."*

**If it is calibrated, it is a free parameter** — the thing 4172 §5 and 4175 §4 both flagged as the
cost the programme would have to absorb. **But it may not need calibrating:** if the Sea's pairing
structure fixes the residual, the ratio is *derived* from the Sea, not fitted. **That is the outcome
worth aiming at, and TODO-4183-SEASPIN is the prerequisite for either answer.**

So the order of work is: **Sea spin structure first, ratio second, calibration only if the first two
fail.**
