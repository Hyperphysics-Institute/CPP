## 1. Identification

The steady-percentage (one-PSR, λ = 0) exterior of 4365–4374,

    ds² = −e^{−2m/r} dt² + e^{2m/r}(dr² + r²dΩ²),

is the **exponential metric** of Papapetrou and Yilmaz, studied in the literature for decades as an alternative to
Schwarzschild. Boonserm, Ngampitipan, Simpson and Visser (arXiv:1805.03781, *"The exponential metric represents a
traversable wormhole"*) showed that it has no horizon and is a traversable wormhole with its throat at isotropic r = m.
That is exactly the throat the 4369 critic found independently (areal radius e·m = 2.718 m).

**Cross-checks with published numbers:**
- Mazharimousavi (arXiv:2309.02874) gives the ISCO of the spherically symmetric exponential metric at isotropic
  r = (3 + √5)m = 5.236 m. That converts to areal 5.236·e^{1/5.236} = **6.338 m**, the value the 4369 critic computed
  independently (6.34 m).
- The photon sphere at isotropic r = 2m and the critical impact parameter 2e·m (4365) agree with the standard treatment
  of this metric.

## 2. What this means for CPP

- **CPP has no wormhole.** In CPP the exterior stops at the cap, where the PSR sphere is full. Under the steady reading
  that cap is at ε = ln 2, i.e. isotropic r = 1/ln 2 = **1.443 m**, outside the throat at r = 1 m. The wormhole's throat
  and its other side are never reached; the region inside the cap is the founder's packed lattice (4370), not a tunnel.
- **The external observables are the exponential metric's:** the shadow (+4.6%), the light ring, the ISCO, and the
  ringdown of a horizonless object (4374: −4.1% for ℓ = 2). Published work on this metric applies directly to CPP at
  λ = 0. That is useful leverage: CPP's one-PSR prediction is a named, studied geometry, not an ad hoc one.
- **Spin: nothing to borrow.** The search found no published rotating version of the exponential metric with
  quasinormal modes. The "axially symmetric exponential metric" (arXiv:2309.02874) is a static deformation, not
  rotation. Newman–Janis-type rotating versions exist for related metrics but are not derivations. The spinning
  ringdown remains owed and is now known to be original work.
