## 3. What is established

**(i) 4240's B2 moment is now identified, not estimated.** For a spin-½ core moving with momentum k, averaged over
directions, the axial projection is R = 1/3 + (2/3)m/E and the spin magnetisation is S_M = 2/3 + (1/3)m/E — which is
**identically (1 + R)/2**. So the B2 formula of 4240 is exactly the spin magnetisation of a moving core with no orbital
current from its motion.

**(ii) 4240's B1/B2 split was mislabelled, and is corrected here.** The full one-quark Dirac current for the same
state (3D spread r_ZBW) gives μ_p = 1.63 (−42%), not 2.61: the p-wave convection term costs ~1 μ_N. That term is
present in *every* κ = −1 spinor, cavity or not. So what 4240 excluded was not "the cavity" but **"a quark whose
magnetisation includes the convection current of the motion that reduces its spin projection."** 4240 §3(B)'s
exclusion stands with that wording; its §3(C) "survives" holds only in the no-convection reading.

**(iii) The founder's motion is of the kind the data need.** A core breathing straight in and out against its vertex
has no circulation about it; the quark's spin is a carried circulation (the orbital eDP, 4228/4231), not the
convection current of a point Dirac particle. Under that reading the only magnetisation is the carried dipole's, and
**g_A and μ are tied with no free parameter:** μ_p/μ_N = (M_N/m_q)·(1 + 3g_A/5)/2. With measured g_A and m_q = m_p/3:
μ_p = 2.648 (−5.2%), μ_n = −1.765 (−7.7%), ratio −3/2 — the same residual as 4126's scale question (m_q), and now
with the spin structure fully accounted for. Conditional on: the carried dipole's lab moment reducing by the free-spinor
Gordon spin factor (the core + circulation treated as one moving spin-½), and no orbital current from the breath.

**(iv) R's value is set by the breath's momentum spread, which is not yet fixed.** Readings of SS-2's size give
g_A = 1.24 (3D spread, −3.0%) to 1.57 (1D breath along a line, +23%). The measured value needs a 1D breath with
σ_x = 0.18 fm (σ_p = 1.76 m_const), or a 3D spread with rms 0.70 fm (1.11 r_ZBW).
