## 3. Triangulation leg T1: the running of α (rows)

```
   1/alpha: 137.036 (Q -> 0)  ->  127.95 (Q = M_Z)
   L(M_Z)/L0 = alpha0/alpha(M_Z) = 0.9337: the swing must be 6.6% shorter at the Z scale
   Sign: charge stress shortens the swing (founder 4301 rule) -> alpha grows -> SAME SIGN as the measured running.
   Shape: QED's running is logarithmic in Q (leading log: 1/alpha(Q) = 1/alpha0 - (2/(3 pi)) sum_f N_c Q_f^2 ln(Q/m_f));
   the stress law for L must reproduce that logarithm to count as a triangulation, not a sign.
```

Measured α is not constant: it grows at short distances (high momentum transfer), from 1/137.036 to about 1/127.95 at the
Z mass. In the swing picture, the founder's own 4301 rule (a more stressed environment carries more energy per
displacement) shortens the swing where the sea is more stressed. Closer to a charge the SSV_net stress is higher, so α
should grow at short distance. **That is the measured sign.** It is a sign, not yet a triangulation: the stress law for L
must reproduce QED's logarithmic running (a 6.6% shorter swing at the Z scale) before it counts.

## 4. Triangulation leg T2: local position invariance (rows)

```
   Sun, annual (eccentricity), Earth  d(U/c^2) = 3.3e-10: |d alpha/alpha| < 5.2e-16  -> |dL/L| (relative to PSR) < 5.2e-16
   Earth surface vs infinity          d(U/c^2) = 7.0e-10: |d alpha/alpha| < 1.1e-15  -> |dL/L| (relative to PSR) < 1.1e-15
   Sun at 1 AU vs infinity            d(U/c^2) = 9.9e-09: |d alpha/alpha| < 1.6e-14  -> |dL/L| (relative to PSR) < 1.6e-14
```

Atomic dysprosium over the Earth's annual change in solar potential bounds the coupling of α to the gravitational
potential: **k_α = (−5.5 ± 5.2) × 10⁻⁷** (Leefer et al., PRL 111, 060801, 2013; arXiv:1304.6940). The rows use a 2σ
bound. Gravity is stress too (SSV_abs), so on the same 4301 rule a gravitational well would shorten the swing and raise
α. **The bound forbids it at better than 10⁻¹⁵.** So **gravitational stress (SSV_abs) must rescale the swing and the PSR
together (L/PSR invariant), while charge stress (SSV_net) must shorten the swing relative to the PSR (T1).**

That is the founder's own split (4318: gravity is the SSV_abs gradient, charge is SSV_net), and it is Patch 0739's
finding restated for the swing (the Δc / LPI filter: the gravity channel is symmetric, A_grav ≲ 10⁻⁶). **The swing
postulate is compatible with LPI only if the DP-arc stress that sets the swing's turning point is SSV_net-type, not
SSV_abs-type.** That is a sharp, checkable requirement on the DP-arc picture.

## 5. What I think

Postulating the swing is the right move at this point, and it is honest: α is a measured input, and the postulate
converts it into a lattice statement (68.5 PSR) whose worth is tested elsewhere. The two legs above are what make it
more than a relabelling:

- **T1 already passes at the level of sign**: the founder's stress rule gives α that grows at short distance, as
  measured. It needs the logarithm to pass quantitatively.
- **T2 is a constraint with teeth**: the same stress rule, applied to gravity, would violate local position invariance
  by nine orders of magnitude. It survives only if the swing responds to charge stress (SSV_net) and merely rescales with
  gravitational stress (SSV_abs). That is a physical statement about DP-arcs that the founder can judge.

The third leg, gravity in counts (O2), stays queued: with α's content now in the swing, the mass side needs the matching
statement.

## 6. Founder question (a physical picture)

**Do DP-arcs respond to the charge-type stress (SSV_net) and not to the gravitational stress (SSV_abs)?** For the swing
postulate to agree with atomic clocks, a gravitational well must stretch or shrink the Planck ZBW's swing exactly as it
stretches or shrinks the PSR (so their ratio, and α, stay fixed to better than one part in 10¹⁵ near the Sun). But
charge-type stress close to an electron must shorten the swing relative to the PSR, which is how α grows at short
distance. In your picture of DP-arcs, is that how the two kinds of stress act on them?
