## What is Excellent (Ready for Archival)

"The first time the CPP model has a mechanistic explanation for the pre-gap quark masses." The Shell 3 relay is "the strongest conceptual contribution in SM-10" — "elegant, falsifiable, and physically meaningful."

### 2. CPU proof-of-concept — "honest and rigorous"
Clearly states what failed, what succeeded, why, parameter counts, degrees of freedom, and circular validation framing. "Exactly what a referee wants to see."

### 3. GPU success criteria — "well-defined"
"A real scientific test, not a curve-fit." Exact f₀ values, geometric law, PDG ratios, convergence tests, validation sequence.

### 4. Numerology audit — "superb"
"The kind of epistemic hygiene that makes a speculative model credible."

### 5. Epistemic status — "honest and precise"
"Rare in theoretical physics papers and reflects scientific maturity."

## What Needs Tightening (for v2)

1. **Chain-formation algorithm**: needs explicit pseudocode, bonding/growth/termination/conflict rules for reproducibility
2. **DP-Sea energy functional**: needs formal expression (E = Σ f(length, curvature, bond type))
3. **Shell 3 relay diagram**: mechanism clear in text but needs a figure

## What is Missing (for v2)

1. **Formal definition of "organised DP"**: binary, graded, chain-membership, or energy-based criterion
2. **Scaling-limit argument**: pair count → V², chain density → V^(1/3), organised DPs → V^(7/3)
3. **Spectral dimension integration**: d_s ≈ 3.57 from GPU walk — why cage is super-3D, how it affects chain density, whether d_s predicts cascade rate
