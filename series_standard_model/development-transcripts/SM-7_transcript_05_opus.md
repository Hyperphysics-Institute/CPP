## Thomas's Axiom Philosophy

**Thomas** (1:41 AM):
I think we need to look at these non-derivable parameters that act a certain way as possibly simply rules of CP behavior. We will need a large number of phenomena that use the same rules to prove by swarm/induction that these additional axioms are fundamental. So, I say, let's just call everything an axiom that looks like one, and let's see if they show up over and over, or do the axioms just continue to multiply. What do you think?

**Opus** (1:41 AM):
I think that's exactly right — and it's actually the strongest possible response to Sonnet's critique.

The demand "derive everything from scratch" misunderstands how foundational physics works. Newton didn't derive F = ma. Maxwell didn't derive the displacement current. They postulated rules of behaviour and then showed those same rules explained phenomenon after phenomenon. The evidence wasn't derivation — it was universality.
