## Discovery Timeline

### 9 April 2026 — FEM Closure and the Grounding Question
After completing the SM-10 FEM cascade model and demonstrating that first-principles quark mass derivation is blocked by unknown DP-DP interaction law (SS-series), Thomas identified the foundational problem: "CPP is destined to marginalization until we make it real at the level of a conversion constant that translates the meter's magnitude to the dimensions of the lattice."

### 10 April 2026 — Five Routes to l_unit
Five independent approaches explored: QCD string tension (outlier), confinement radius from f_π (0.589 fm), proton magnetic moment (not computed), α_s running from α_geom (0.589 fm), nuclear matter density (0.653 fm). Routes 2 and 4 converged independently at 0.589 fm.

### 10 April 2026 — α_geom = 1/√5 as Bare QCD Coupling
Running α_geom from the lattice scale to m_Z reproduced α_s(m_Z) = 0.1179 exactly. Prediction at m_H: α_s = 0.1132 vs measured 0.1130 (+0.2%). This connected a geometric constant to a precision electroweak measurement.

### 10 April 2026 — Tetrahedral Proton (Thomas's Model)
Thomas proposed the proton as a hybrid tetrahedron: two up quarks at − vertices, one down quark at + vertex, one open + vertex for nuclear binding. Force balance between EM repulsion and colour confinement gave distortion ε = 1.94.

### 10 April 2026 — ZBW Orbit = Lattice Spacing
r_ZBW = ℏc/m_const = 0.631 fm ≈ l_unit = 0.589 fm. Ratio 1.07. Deep self-consistency.

### 10 April 2026 — Proton Charge Radius at 5%
r_proton = 0.883 fm (measured 0.841 fm) from zero parameters. String tension derived as σ = M₀zπ/(φ l_edge) = 243 MeV/fm.

### 10 April 2026 — Neutron Linear Oscillator (Thomas's Insight)
Thomas proposed: down quarks have captured −eCP linear oscillators. In the neutron, two −eCPs repel, oscillate 180° out of phase. The distorted tetrahedron alone produces r²_n < 0 (correct sign). Small inward eCP displacement δ = −0.067 matches r²_n = −0.1161 fm² exactly.

## Key Decisions

1. **l_unit = 0.589 fm adopted** based on Route 2/4 convergence (not fitted to any nucleon observable)
2. **σ = M₀zπ/(φ l_edge)** chosen as the string tension ansatz — physically motivated, labelled as conjecture
3. **Proton radius computed at δ=0** (zero parameters) rather than fitting δ to improve agreement
4. **Neutron δ fitted** (1 parameter) — honest about the parameter count
