# Glossary — SM-8: Quark Generation Structure from 600-Cell Distance Shells

**Paper:** SM-8 v4.1 (9 April 2026)
**Series:** Standard Model

---

## Shell and Cage Terms

**Distance shell** — The set of all vertices at a fixed graph distance from a chosen reference vertex in the 600-cell. There are 8 shells (plus the reference vertex itself).

**Bonded shell** — A distance shell whose vertices are connected by lattice edges (nonzero intra-shell adjacency). Only Shells 1, 2, and 4 are bonded; Shell 3 is edgeless.

**Shell 3 gap** — The structural absence of lattice edges at Shell 3 (d ≈ 1.176, V = 12, E = 0). This is a forced combinatorial property of the 600-cell, not a model choice.

**Cage** — A bonded-shell polyhedron interpreted as a quark confinement structure. The four cage types are: tetrahedron (V=4), icosahedron (V=12), dodecahedron (V=20), icosidodecahedron (V=30).

**Cage-quark correspondence** — The unique order-preserving bijection mapping larger cages to heavier quarks: tet → s, ico → c, dod → b, icosidod → t.

## Formula Terms

**M₀ = m_e z/φ** — The derived energy-scale prefactor (3.790 MeV). Product of the electron mass, the 600-cell coordination number, and the reciprocal golden ratio.

**V^(7/3)** — The scaling exponent for quark mass. Decomposes as V² × V^(1/3): pair-counting energy budget times linear cage dimension. Derived in SM-9.

**z × C_F = 16** — The post-gap multiplier for the top quark. z = 12 (coordination bonds activated) × C_F = 4/3 (SU(3) fundamental Casimir per bond).

**Zero-parameter formula** — M_q = m_e(z/φ)V^(7/3) × [1 or 16]. Contains no fitted parameters; all constants derived or measured.

## Structural Terms

**Palindrome symmetry** — The mirror relationship between inner and outer distance shells: Shell k ↔ Shell (8−k) in vertex count, edge count, and coordination number.

**Antipodal identification** — In the tessellated lattice, Shell 7 of cell A is Shell 1 of cell B (the neighbouring cell near the antipode). This identification limits the SM to three generations.

**Three-generation theorem** — The proof (Theorem 8.1) that exactly four independent cage types exist in the tessellated 600-cell, corresponding to exactly three quark generations (s/d, c/u via Koide, b, t).

## Charge and Bond Terms

**Attractive fraction** — The fraction of cage edges connecting opposite-polarity CPs. At the 2/3 assignment: E_attr = (2/3)E for all cages.

**Charge census** — The classification of cage edges into four DP types: eDP, qDP, hDP-A, hDP-B, plus repulsive (same-polarity) edges. Ratio 1:1:2:2 is universal.

**K₄ subgraph** — A complete graph on 4 vertices. Present only in the tetrahedron; absent from larger cages.

## Physical Mechanism Terms

**Near-field / far-field transition** — The ZBW back-EMF picture: pre-gap chains operate in the near-field (ZBW wavelength > cage radius); post-gap chains operate in the far-field (ZBW wavelength < Shell 4 radius), enabling radiation-mode coupling.

**Impedance boundary** — The cage surface acts as an acoustic impedance mismatch: organised chains inside, disordered Sea outside. Partial transmission allows energy exchange.

**Coordination tunneling** — The mechanism by which post-gap chains traverse Shell 3 via the z = 12 coordination bonds of the ambient lattice, each carrying C_F = 4/3.
