## Elevator Pitch

SM-7 derives the strong coupling constant α_s = 5/(8φ) from the face
geometry of the 600-cell lattice — the same polytope whose edge geometry
gives sin²θ_W in SM-6. The two couplings satisfy sin²θ_W + α_s = 1/φ
(no SM analogue) and α_s/sin²θ_W = 5/3 (exact at all scales). The quark
Koide phase is derived from the combined EW + colour ε-shift on the K₃
cage, with the bond-count asymmetry (2 EW vs 12 colour) established by
a projector lemma. Calibrating to the charm mass alone, the formula
predicts m_b and m_t to better than 2%. Combined with SM-6, two
calibration constants replace eight SM parameters.

## One-Line Summary

The strong coupling and heavy quark masses emerge from counting
face-circulation modes on the 600-cell lattice.
