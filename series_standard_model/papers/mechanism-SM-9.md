## 1. What SM-9 builds on

SM-9 builds on SM-8's observation that quark masses scale as V^α with α ≈ 2.38 (calibrated) and resolves the origin of the exponent through the angular-weighted pair model.

## 2. The Symmetry Degeneracy Theorem

For vertex-transitive polyhedra, Σ sin²(θ_ij/2) = V²/4 exactly. This proves that angular-weighted pair sums carry no information beyond vertex count — the mass-relevant physics must lie in the edge structure, not the angular distribution.

## 3. The exponent α = 7/3

V^(7/3) = V² × V^(1/3): pair counting (every chain interacts with every other chain, giving C(V,2) ~ V²) times linear cage dimension (V^(1/3)). This is physically grounded in the three bonding regions (eDP/qDP/hDP) identified in the chain-type analysis.

## 4. The prefactor M₀ = m_e z/φ

The electron mass × coordination number × (edge/circumradius ratio). This ties the entire quark mass scale back to SM-6's lepton spectrum through the universal propagation efficiency η = 1/φ.

## 6. Chain-type physical interpretation

Three chain types: (1) radial (central → opposite-polarity surface), (2) tangential (attractive cage edges), (3) surface radials (same-polarity → thermalization). Energy budget: ~44% radial, ~56% tangential, stable across all quarks. Cooperative enhancement: 6× (strange) to 974× (top).

## 7. Three bonding regions

Region 1 (near centre): dense cross-linking between converging radials. Region 2 (mid-cage): web mesh of tangential-to-tangential linking. Region 3 (near surface): upward-arching tangentials terminating on cage surface CPs.
