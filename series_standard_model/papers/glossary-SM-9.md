# Glossary — SM-9: The Quark Mass Scaling Exponent

**Paper:** SM-9 v2.2 (9 April 2026)
**Series:** Standard Model

---

**Angular-weighted pair model** — A model where the energy of the cage chain network is computed as the sum over all chain-chain pairs, weighted by geometric factors (angular separation, bond type).

**Symmetry Degeneracy Theorem** — For vertex-transitive polyhedra on S², the angular pair sum Σ sin²(θ/2) = V²/4 exactly, independent of the polyhedron's edge structure.

**Edge structure** — The pattern of bonded vs non-bonded pairs in a cage. The bonded fraction E/C(V,2) varies non-monotonically: tet 100%, ico 45%, dod 16%, icosidod 14%.

**Pair counting** — The V² contribution to the exponent: every chain interacts with every other chain through the shared SSV field, giving C(V,2) ~ V² pairs.

**Cage linear dimension** — The V^(1/3) contribution to the exponent: the effective radius of the cage volume scales as the cube root of vertex count.

**Cooperative enhancement** — The factor by which each chain link's energy exceeds the bare DP energy M₀, due to mutual SSV reinforcement from all other chains. Ranges from 6× (strange) to 974× (top).

**Chain-type decomposition** — The partition of quark mass into contributions from radial chains (Type 1), tangential chains (Type 2), and surface radials (Type 3).

**Pine tree model** — Thomas's physical picture: each radial chain is a tree trunk; tangential branches arch outward from each CP along the trunk, creating a fractal volume-filling network.

**Three bonding regions** — Region 1 (near centre): cross-linked radials. Region 2 (mid-cage): web mesh. Region 3 (near surface): surface-converging tangentials.

**Surface blanket** — The tangential chain network launched by same-polarity surface vertices, structurally identical to the up quark's radial-plus-tangential structure.

**EW feedback** — Conjectured correction ε ≈ α_geom/z² ≈ 0.003 to the scaling exponent, representing electroweak-sector influence on the strong-sector mass formula.

**Exponent drift** — The variation of the pairwise scaling exponent across quark pairs: α(s→c) = 2.376, α(c→b) = 2.332, converging toward 7/3 = 2.333.
