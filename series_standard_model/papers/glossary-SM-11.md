# Glossary — SM-11: Lattice-Scale Grounding and Nucleon Structure

**Paper:** SM-11 v1.0 (10 April 2026)
**Series:** Standard Model

---

**Lattice unit (l_unit)** — The 600-cell circumradius in physical units. l_unit = ℏc/Λ_QCD ≈ 0.589 fm. The fundamental conversion constant between CPP geometry and SI units.

**Lattice edge (l_edge)** — The 600-cell edge length in physical units. l_edge = l_unit/φ ≈ 0.364 fm.

**α_geom** — The geometric coupling constant 1/√5 = 0.4472, arising from the 600-cell's icosahedral symmetry. Identified as the bare QCD coupling at the lattice scale.

**Bare coupling** — The value of the strong coupling at the lattice energy scale Λ, before renormalisation group running.

**Confinement radius** — r_conf = ℏc/Λ_QCD, the characteristic length scale of quark confinement. In CPP, this equals one lattice unit.

**Hybrid tetrahedron** — The proton/neutron model: a tetrahedral cell of the 600-cell with three quark vertices and one open binding vertex. Net charge +1 (proton) or 0 (neutron).

**Open vertex** — The fourth tetrahedral vertex, unoccupied by a quark. Provides the nuclear binding site: proton's open (+) attracts neutron's open (−).

**Distortion parameter (ε)** — The fractional stretch of the like-charge quark edge beyond the regular tetrahedral edge. ε = r_eq/l_edge − 1. For the proton (u-u), ε = 1.94.

**Force balance** — The equilibrium between EM repulsion, colour Coulomb attraction, linear confinement, and relativistic kinetic energy that determines ε.

**String tension (σ)** — Energy per unit length of the confining flux tube. CPP derives σ = M₀zπ/(φ l_edge) = 243 MeV/fm. [Status: CONJ-SM-11-1]

**ZBW orbit** — The Zitterbewegung circular orbit of the central CP around its lattice vertex. Radius r_ZBW = ℏc/m_const.

**ZBW smearing** — The contribution of the ZBW orbit to the charge radius: each quark's charge is distributed over a sphere of radius r_ZBW rather than concentrated at the vertex.

**Linear oscillator** — The down quark's captured −eCP, which oscillates linearly through the central +qCP. In the neutron, two linear oscillators interact, producing the negative charge radius.

**eCP displacement (δ)** — The time-averaged displacement of the −eCP from its home vertex, in units of l_edge. Positive = outward, negative = inward. For the neutron, δ = −0.067.

**Constituent quark mass** — The effective quark mass inside a hadron, m_const ≈ m_p/3 = 313 MeV, including the DP cloud dressing.
