## Relationship to the Standard Model

CPP does not derive from the Standard Model Lagrangian. It derives SM observables from lattice geometry. The relationship is analogous to statistical mechanics and thermodynamics: CPP is the microscopic theory; the SM (with ~25 free parameters) is the emergent macroscopic description.

This means criticisms of the form "but where is the Higgs mechanism?" or "how does this connect to Yukawa couplings?" are category errors. CPP proposes to replace these mechanisms, not to derive from them.

The paper is transparent about what it does NOT achieve:

- The exponent 7/3 is partially derived, not proven (target: SM-10 FEM)
- The post-gap mechanism is qualitative, not quantitative (target: SM-10)
- The strange quark residual (+3.1%) is unexplained
