## Key Decisions

1. **z=12 → z×C_F=16**: The most consequential correction in the paper's history. Reduced top quark precision from 0.02% to 1.8% but eliminated both free parameters.

2. **Retaining the calibrated formula**: Both v3.x (2 params, 0.02%) and v4.0 (0 params, 2.1%) are reported. This transparency was praised by all reviewers.

3. **Physical axioms A/B**: Added at Copilot's suggestion to make the gauge structure explicit. These are postulates, clearly labeled.
