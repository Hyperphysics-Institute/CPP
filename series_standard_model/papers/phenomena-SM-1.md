## Section 3: Consilience Cases

Consilience cases are phenomena where CPP and the Standard Model
agree numerically but reach the same number through completely
different derivation routes. These provide inductive evidence that
CPP is not just fitting the same data — it is independently
recovering the same results from a different geometric foundation.

### C1. δ = 1/3: Charge Quantisation from Two Independent Routes

**The number:** All observable charges are integer multiples of e/3.
Quark charges are +2/3 and −1/3 of the electron charge.

**Standard Model route:** Follows from anomaly cancellation — the
requirement that triangle diagrams in the SM be anomaly-free
constrains the sum of charges in each generation. The value 1/3
is not derived independently; it is required by self-consistency
of the QFT with its fermion content.

**CPP route:** δ = 1/3 exactly from SM-1 Theorem 1 (C₃ cage
symmetry + cage completeness). The three base vertices of the
tetrahedral cage are geometrically equivalent (C₃ rotation is
an exact isometry); they collectively account for all charge
screening (completeness); therefore each accounts for 1/3. The
derivation requires no knowledge of the other quark and lepton
charges — it follows from the geometry of the K₃ base triangle alone.

**Consilience significance:** Two frameworks — one algebraic
(anomaly cancellation), one geometric (C₃ cage symmetry) —
independently require δ = 1/3. The CPP route is more parsimonious
(no QFT required) and more explanatory (gives a specific mechanism
for why 1/3 rather than some other fraction).

**The number:** Exactly three generations of quarks and leptons.

**Standard Model route:** Three generations are required by
electroweak self-consistency (anomaly cancellation again requires

**Consilience significance:** CPP derives three generations from

structure.

**CPP route:** ~99% of the proton mass is qDP chain energy — the
energy stored in the network of Dipole Sea pair chains connecting

**Consilience significance:** CPP and QCD agree on the 99:1 ratio
but through different conceptual frameworks. In CPP, the ~99%
has a specific mechanical identity (qDP chain network energy);
in QCD it is gluon field energy. These should be the same physics
described at different levels.
