# Reasoning — Patch 4027 (my own headline, one patch old, retracted)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## How I came to check it

I was chasing the loose end 4026 left — the n̂-versus-transverse asymmetry I had recorded "without a
story". To resolve it I vectorised η so I could run many more realisations cheaply. Then, having the
machinery, I re-ran 4026's own headline number at ten times the sampling.

It fell apart.

## The number

| realisations | along n̂, 0.5–1.0 | transverse, 0.5–1.0 |
|---|---|---|
| **400** (4026) | **−0.012344 ± 0.003580 (3.4 sem)** | −0.002448 ± 0.000694 (3.5 sem) |
| **4000** | **−0.001708 ± 0.001338 (1.3 sem)** | −0.000185 ± 0.000217 (0.9 sem) |

**The value drops sevenfold.** A real correlation does not shrink with more sampling; a fluctuation does.

So 4026's "real short-range anticorrelation" is retracted — and with it the story I hung on it, that
4025's null had been "partly a power problem after all". **4025's null was correct.** I corrected a right
result into a wrong one and was pleased with myself for the correction.

## Root cause, and it is mine twice over

Both 4025 and 4026 quoted the **sem over pairs** in a distance band. **Pairs within a band share sites.**
They are not independent, and the naive sem treats them as if they were. Bootstrap over realisations gives
a bar **1.57× larger**.

So 4026's 3.4 sem was really about 2.2 sem *before* the value collapsed. Two compounding errors: an
understated bar and too few realisations.

And the second is downstream of a performance limit. 4026 used 400 realisations **because the per-site
determinant loop was slow**. The whole retraction traces back to that. The vectorised η shipped in this
file makes 4000 cheap, which is why the error was findable at all today rather than in six months.

## The rule

Bootstrap over **realisations**, never sem over pair-bins. And check **value-stability against reps**
before quoting any significance — the 400-vs-4000 comparison in T1 took two minutes and would have caught
this before it shipped.

I am not adding a fourth gate for this. The gates so far catch classes of error that recur across lanes;
this one is a discipline inside a single kind of computation, and the practical fix is the fast η plus the
stability check, both of which are in the file.

## What survives

4026's **geometry** improvement is real — reach 12.7 edge lengths against 4025's ~3.

And **the conclusion is better than it was**. With corrected bars, nothing is resolvable at any separation:
0.6, 0.5, 0.2, 0.3, 0.7 sem across the bands from 1.0 out to 9. No long-range order along n̂, and no
short-range structure either. The estimator simply resolves nothing at this precision, which is the honest
state of the measurement and a cleaner statement than 4026's.

The anisotropy is also not established: rotating η's own selection axis and comparing gives
diagonal − off-diagonal = −0.0009 ± 0.0012, **0.7 sem**. The thing I recorded without a story does not need
one.

## Worth noting about the order parameter

This is the **second** defect found in η today. 4023 found it ill-defined on the unperturbed lattice
through projection ties; this finds the statistics built on it fragile enough that a 3.4-sem result
evaporates at 10× sampling. Neither invalidates a physics conclusion. Both are worth the CHIR lane's
attention, since η is the order parameter the whole susceptibility line runs on.
