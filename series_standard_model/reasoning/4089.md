# Reasoning — Patch 4089 (the founder's fourth-axis oscillation: the driver fails, the oscillation supplies the missing double rotation, and a sharp constraint falls out)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.** Lane EW (40xx), 17 Sep 2026. Founder: *"the 4th dimension as an axis of
oscillation… what is causing the motion along that axis? Maybe the plus/minus polarity of the CP would produce
the movement along that axis… Is this avenue worth pursuing?"* Scope: computation + an assessment ⇒ **not exempt.**

## Short answer: yes — but not for the reason offered

**The driver fails.** A displacement along w is P-even (physical parity inverts 3-space and leaves w alone), and
polarity is P-even. "Polarity drives w-motion" is therefore a P-even rule and cannot produce handedness. This is
the same wall the spin bit hit at 4076 B1.

**The oscillation succeeds, and it supplies exactly what the arc has been missing.** 4072 established that only a
**double rotation** carries a pseudoscalar; 4074 that it must mix w with 3-space. A w-oscillation phase-locked to
3D motion **is** that second plane — and it needs no new variable: w already exists and the CP already circulates.

## What the computation says

**First model was wrong and the failure was instructive.** I put the w-oscillation and the 3D circulation in only
three dimensions (w, x, y) and got identically zero — correctly, since a pseudoscalar needs all four. The motion
must be:

- a **(w, x) rotation** — the w-oscillation phase-locked to motion along the **direction of travel**, and
- a **(y, z) circulation** — 3D spin **transverse** to that direction.

Together these are **helicity**: spin about the direction of motion, with w supplying the fourth leg.

With that corrected: the pseudoscalar goes as **cos(phase)** — maximal when the w-oscillation is in phase or
antiphase with the motion along the direction of travel, zero in quadrature — and **flips with the transverse
spin sense**. So the hand is the product (phase sense) × (spin sense). Controls: phase 0 and 180 give ±2.8258,
quadrature gives −0.0000, spin reversal flips the sign.

## The sharp constraint, and it is the patch's real content

**At a 1:1 frequency ratio the pseudoscalar is exactly zero.** At resonance the two circulations lock into a
*simple* rotation, which carries no handedness (4072). Measured: 0.0000 at Ω/ω = 1, then −2.1000, −2.8258,
−4.0000 at ratios 1.05, √2, 2.

**So the natural first guess — that the w-oscillation just *is* the ZBW — would give exactly no chirality.** The
w-oscillation must be **detuned** from the 3D circulation it pairs with. That is a concrete, checkable
requirement the founder's picture must meet, and it is not obvious from the picture itself.

## What the avenue buys, and what it does not

- **Maximality becomes natural.** The maximum sits at in-phase or antiphase lock, and a driven oscillator locks
  there rather than at some tuned intermediate angle. V−A's all-or-nothing character would follow from the lock.
- **The sign becomes mechanical.** "Which hand" becomes "is the w-oscillation in phase or antiphase with the
  motion along the direction of travel" — a physical phase, not an abstract label.
- **It could discharge a stipulation.** If polarity fixes that phase, the polarity clause **CPT requires** (4085)
  stops being stipulated and becomes a statement about oscillator phase.

**What it does not buy:** what fixes the phase for a given polarity is still not derived. **The irreducible sign
is relocated into a phase convention, not eliminated.** That is progress in physical content, not in logical
economy — and the founder asked for a clean win, which this is not.
