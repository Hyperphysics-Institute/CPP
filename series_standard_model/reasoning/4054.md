# Reasoning — Patch 4054 (three in a row, and a stop)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The attempt

Lift 4041's verified 3D chiral orbit into 4D by **conjugation**, x → q x q̄. It fixes the real part and
acts as SO(3) on the imaginary part, so it should carry the 3D construction across unchanged.

## It fails twice

**The supposed 5-fold-axis seed gives a 60-point orbit**, where in 3D such a seed gives 12 and the cluster
stays achiral. **My special positions are not where I think they are in the conjugation frame.** I have a
map, not a lift.

**And the resulting shell has only ~120 4-hop paths.** Far too sparse to measure anything. The helicity it
returns is **4041's failure repeated exactly — a zero that means "I cannot see", not "there is nothing
there".**

I recognised that trap at 4041, wrote it up as a lesson, and built it again twenty patches later. Writing
the lesson down is evidently not the same as holding it.

## Three in a row

4051: both functionals wrong, retracted at 4053. 4052: diagnosis true in part, wrong reason, superseded.
4053: retraction — the object was never chiral. 4054: this.

**Four patches, one retraction, no result.**

**So the lane stops here.** Continuing past this point is how a second retraction gets made, and I would
rather hand over a clean position than another attempt. That judgement is itself the deliverable: PD-008
says press to the end, and the end of a line is a real place that has to be recognised rather than
overrun.

## What is actually established

1. SF-6's `OPEN-SD-CHIR-PRIMITIVE` names **n̂ on a host vertex** as its leading candidate, and **it cannot
   work** — Θ fixes n̂ and preserves the 600-cell (4046).
2. A chiral cluster with icosahedral **rotation** symmetry and an achiral z = 12 first shell **exists, is
   proved chiral by exact group count, and is measured — in three dimensions** (4041, 4042, 4047).
3. It **preserves the icosahedral and dodecahedral shells** CPP's derivations use (4043).
4. Its parity-odd dispersion enters at **O(k¹⁵)** — not excluded by the absence of vacuum optical activity,
   and not calibratable by it, with the n̂-tilt escape closed (4044, 4045).
5. A structural helicity bias **caps at ~2%**, so it cannot supply V−A's maximality (4048).
6. The **W bracelet is achiral**, by the corpus's own theorem and independently (4049).
7. **Ring bivector ∧ n̂ is the P-odd binary variable**, its two states **exactly degenerate** (4050).
8. **No chiral 4D structure has been built.** Everything chiral here is 3D (4053, 4054).

## The gap

**A chiral 4D point set with icosahedral rotation symmetry, tested with the 4D graph helicity on a graph
dense enough to resolve it.**

Both halves are requirements. This patch met neither, and saying which half failed is more use to the next
attempt than another attempt would have been.
