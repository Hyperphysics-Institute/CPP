## Three things follow, and one of them is free

**The lattice is necessarily aperiodic.** A polytope tiles space face-to-face only if its dihedral angle
divides 360° exactly. The 600-cell's is 164.4775°; 360/164.4775 = 2.1887. Not an integer, so no periodic
tiling of E⁴ — which is Coxeter's result, and which `frontier_sectors/SR.md` R4 already carries
panel-closed. I computed it rather than cited it, having twice this week asserted something on a single
sentence.

What makes this more than bookkeeping: **the founder's ruling lands on the same object the SR lane already
built its Lorentz world-call on.** R4/R5 concluded W2 — IR-exact Lorentz with an l = 6 anisotropy floor —
*because* the substrate is an icosahedral quasicrystal rather than a crystal. So "innumerable 600-cells,
every GP the centre of its own" is not a new commitment; it is the commitment a panel-closed result in
another sector already depends on. That is the kind of cross-sector agreement CPP's whole validation
argument rests on, and it cost nothing to notice.

**4006's measurement is truncated.** This is the part I would rather not have found. Graph-distance shells
on one closed 600-cell are 1, 12, 32, 42, 32, 1 — **d ≤ 2 covers 37.5% of the entire graph**, and shell 5
is one vertex. There is no far region. So "d ≥ 2 stays at noise" was never a measurement of decay; it was
a measurement on a graph with no long distances in it. The d = 1 result survives untouched — one hop is one
hop either way — but the thing 4006 was actually asked to determine is undetermined.

## The blocker, found by not reaching for the obvious object

The corpus does not construct the lateral lattice anywhere I can find. And the object that looks like the
construction is the wrong one: SR-1's φ-self-similar nested-600-cell hierarchy, R/a = φ at every level. It
would have been easy, and wrong, to build the scaling study on it — 0736 states that nesting runs
**inward**, *down to true GP spacing ~ l_P/10³⁰*. Sub-Planck structure, not lateral extent. I checked
because the phrase "nested 600-cells" is exactly the kind of near-match D-7 is about.

The one real pointer is the DM lane's **icosian registration**. That one I could verify rather than
forward, and did: the 120 vertices are unit quaternions and are **closed under quaternion multiplication**,
so they are the binary icosahedral group 2I — the unit icosians. The icosian ring is therefore the natural
generator of an extended structure with z = 12 preserved, which is what SF-4 needs.

## What I did not do, and why it matters more than usual here

**I did not build the lattice.** Two patches ago I asserted the route had run on the wrong object, on one
misstated clause. One patch ago I withdrew it. Inventing a lateral lattice now and measuring a correlation
length on it would be the same failure a third time, and larger, because a number would come out of it and
numbers get quoted.

The construction is the blocker. It is named, its natural generator is identified and verified, and it is
filed as the next piece of work rather than assumed into existence.
