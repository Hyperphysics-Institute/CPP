# Reasoning — Patch 4017 (asking the question outside the box I had drawn around it)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## Why I widened the question

4013 closed the strict reading *for bounded-window cut-and-project sets* and I wrote that the construction
class was "forced." That sentence was doing work I had not done. The class is forced **in flat space** —
because the icosian ring is dense in ℝ⁴ and a window is needed — and I never asked whether flat was
required.

So I asked. The tool is the Coxeter Gram signature: for a linear diagram, positive definite means a finite
polytope, positive semidefinite means a Euclidean honeycomb, signature (n,1) means hyperbolic. I made the
method reproduce five known cases first — 600-cell and 120-cell spherical, icosahedron spherical, cubic
honeycomb and 24-cell honeycomb Euclidean — before trusting it on anything new.

## What came back

**The Euclidean regular honeycombs of 4-space are exactly three: {3,3,4,3}, {4,3,3,4}, {3,4,3,3}.** None
is a 600-cell honeycomb. That reproduces 4009's dihedral-angle result by a completely unrelated route,
which is worth more than the result itself.

**And {3,3,5,3} — the honeycomb of 600-cells — exists, and is hyperbolic.**

So the strict reading is not impossible. *Innumerable 600-cells, every GP the centre of its own, every
environment identical* is exactly {3,3,5,3}: regular, vertex-transitive, and living in H⁴. If I had stopped
at 4013 and the founder had ruled the weak reading on my recommendation, the corpus would have adopted a
weakened axiom-level statement when the strong one was available — at a price nobody had priced.

## The price

The vertex figure of {3,3,5,3} is **{3,5,3}**, and {3,5,3} is **itself hyperbolic** — an infinite
honeycomb. So in the 600-cell honeycomb, **every vertex has infinitely many neighbours. z = ∞.**

The contrast is exact and it is the whole story: the *single* 600-cell has vertex figure **{3,5}**, the
icosahedron — spherical, finite, **z = 12**. Put 600-cells together in the only way that keeps every vertex
identical, and the icosahedron inflates into an infinite hyperbolic honeycomb.

z = 12 is load-bearing in SS-1, SM-1, SM-7, SM-8, SM-9 and SF-4. One patch ago I measured what SF-4 alone
has riding on it: Σm_ν ∝ z⁻⁹, with one integer step halving a cosmologically constrained prediction. **z =
∞ is not a value the mass ladder can take.**

So the strict reading closes — but now on a complete argument. It is impossible in flat space, and in
curved space it costs the coordination number the programme is built on. That is a different and better
statement than "no cut-and-project window works."

## The bound, stated here rather than left to be found

The Coxeter classification covers **regular** honeycombs. A **uniform (vertex-transitive) but non-regular**
structure is not covered and is **not** ruled out. **Searched unscoped before saying so** (`vertex-transitive`, `uniform polytope`, `uniform honeycomb`, `quasiregular`, `honeycomb`): the corpus does use *vertex-transitive*, but always of the **single** 600-cell under H₄ — SF-2 §§463, 580, 701, and FP.md's L4-B vertex-uniformity. **Nothing addresses an extended uniform-but-non-regular structure.** That is the gap, narrowed to what the search actually supports.

What is exhausted is three construction classes, by three arguments with nothing in common:

- no periodic Euclidean tiling — 4009, from the dihedral angle
- no bounded-window cut-and-project set — 4013, from the window boundary
- no regular honeycomb at any curvature — here, from the Gram signature and the vertex figure

Three unrelated routes agreeing is the kind of thing worth having. It is still not a proof that nothing
works.

## What this changes about the referral

At 4010 and again at 4013 I sent the founder a question. This time the question is the same but the
material under it is different: the option space has been **exhausted** rather than **sampled**. The weak
reading is the only survivor in play, and he can rule knowing what the strong one would have cost rather
than knowing only that I could not build it.

That distinction is the point of the patch. I do not adopt the weak reading here either.

No panel. No verdict moved. SF-4 unrevised.
