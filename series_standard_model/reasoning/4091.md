## A dimensional fact first

In ℝ⁴ two orthogonal 2-planes already span the space (2 + 2 = 4), so **three mutually orthogonal 2-planes cannot
exist**. SPIN-1's conjectured three colour planes therefore cannot be mutually orthogonal, whatever else they
are. Only a **pair** can be — and a pair is all a double rotation needs.

## My first test was too narrow, and it gave the wrong answer

I first tested each neighbour-spanned plane's orthogonal complement against only the **12 nearest-neighbour**
directions and got **zero** hits — an apparently clean negative. That test was wrong: a complement may be spanned
by directions to **non-neighbour** vertices. Widened to all 119 vertex-difference directions, the count is
**60 of 66**, at two different vertices independently. **The verdict reverses.**

I am recording the narrow version because it would have been reported as a negative result had I stopped there,
and the difference between "0 of 66" and "60 of 66" is the whole conclusion.

## What this establishes

**The 600-cell does supply orthogonal plane pairs.** So the double rotation that handedness requires (4072) can
be built from **existing lattice geometry** — two orbits in orthogonal lattice planes — with no new oscillation
postulated.

## And what it does not let us escape

**Any such pair necessarily leaves physical 3-space.** Measured: across all 60 pairs, the smallest out-of-3-space
component of either plane is **0.8507** — never zero. This is dimensionally forced: two orthogonal 2-planes span
ℝ⁴ while physical 3-space is only 3-dimensional, so at least one plane must have a component along the fourth
axis.

**So the founder's oscillation is grounded rather than eliminated.** Orbiting in one of these lattice planes
*is* motion with a fourth-axis component; it is not an extra postulate laid on top of the geometry, it is what
the geometry already contains. That is a genuine improvement in standing: at 4089 the w-leg was an unexplained
addition; here it is a consequence of using the lattice's own orthogonal planes.

**But the sign is still absent.** Both hands are available among the 60 pairs, exactly as at every earlier step
(4063, 4068, 4071, 4074, 4078). Geometry supplies carriers, never a choice.
