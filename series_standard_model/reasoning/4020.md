# Reasoning — Patch 4020 (the ruling could have given the arc its mechanism; it did not)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## Why I tested this first

The founder ruled the distorted cage. The first thing to check was not z = 12 or the construction target —
it was whether the ruling **breaks the chirality cancellation**, because it had every reason to.

4011, 4012 and 4015 all rest on one thing: `R = diag(1,1,1,−1)` is an **exact** symmetry of the 600-cell,
so every path has an exact mirror partner. **A distorted cage does not have that symmetry exactly.** So the
protection that has carried FI-C-9 = V3 through three tests was, as of this ruling, resting on an object
the founder had just said does not exist in that form.

If the break were systematic, the arc would have had its mechanism — after months of looking.

## It breaks, and the break is not systematic

Twelve independent strain realisations at each of ε = 0.01, 0.03, 0.08. In **every single realisation** the
3-hop sum is nonzero — the cancellation really does break. But the **sign wanders with the seed**:

    eps=0.010   mean  -8.00   sd 49.51   |mean|/sd 0.16   signs --+--++-++-+
    eps=0.030   mean  -0.67   sd 51.65   |mean|/sd 0.01   signs --+--++-++-+
    eps=0.080   mean +11.17   sd 76.08   |mean|/sd 0.15   signs -++--++++--+

Each strained cage is chiral. The ensemble is not. **That is the difference between a chiral object and a
chiral law**, and it is the whole answer: a substrate whose handedness is set by which way the strain
happened to fall is not a substrate that predicts a definite δ_CP.

FI-C-9 = V3 survives a fourth independent test — single cell, fan-out rule, extended lattice, and now the
distorted cage the founder has just ruled in.

I want to note that I ran this before working out any of the ruling's happier consequences. The temptation
after a ruling that unblocks a line is to go and use it; the honest first move is to point it at the result
you are most attached to.

## What the ruling does give, which is not nothing

**Random strain is not the physical strain.** The physical distortion is the **relaxation** of a
7.356°-per-edge frustration — structured, forced, and the same everywhere. In 3D icosahedral matter that
relaxation organises into **disclination networks**, and whether such a network carries a handedness is a
real open question in condensed matter rather than a CPP-specific one.

So the arc now has a **named place** where a mechanism could live, which it did not have this morning. It
is registered as an open problem and **not investigated here**: random perturbation cannot settle it in
either direction, and a patch that implied otherwise would be the D-4 failure with a result attached.
