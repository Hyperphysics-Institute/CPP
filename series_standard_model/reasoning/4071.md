## Part 1 — the free candidate, closed

**The counting theorem.** In R⁴ the *only* parity-odd scalar constructible from vectors is the Levi-Civita
contraction χ = det[a,b,c,d]. Therefore a CP carrying **k** internal polar directions can form a P-odd
quantity **iff k ≥ 4** and they are linearly independent. Verified over 20,000 random draws at each k:

| k | max &#124;pseudoscalar&#124; | |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (n̂ alone — SF-6's candidate) | 0.000e+00 | no P-odd quantity exists |
| 2 | 0.000e+00 | none |
| 3 | 0.000e+00 | none |
| 3 + ± polarities | 0.000e+00 | scalars are P-even; they cannot rescue a deficient set |
| **4** | **6.95e+01** | **exists**, and flips exactly under reflection (sum = 0.0e+00) |

This **rederives 4046 from the general argument** rather than from the specific Θ: n̂ alone cannot carry
handedness not because of a lucky symmetry but because one vector is three short of a pseudoscalar. Charge and
polarity contribute nothing — they are P-even.

**And the 600-cell closes the escape.** If the three extra directions are drawn from the lattice (the natural
move — a CP's neighbours are lattice directions), the configuration is H4-symmetric: over all 160 nonzero
quadruples at a host vertex, **80 are positive and 80 negative**, ensemble sum −2.8×10⁻¹⁷. The lattice supplies
the *magnitude* of a pseudoscalar but **no net sign**, which is the same failure mode as 4063's mirror-point
orbits and 4068's Θ-even dynamics, now met a third time.

**Conclusion: the candidate is closed, and it closes informatively.** Internal CP structure can host a P-odd
quantity, but only if the CP carries ≥ 4 independent directions *and* the fourth's sign is fixed by something
outside the lattice. **That is not a mechanism that avoids an axiom. It is an axiom.** The free option was
never free.

## Part 3 — the founder's axiom question: is it single-use?

**No. This is the part of the answer I would most want checked, and it is the strongest argument for the
axiom.** SF-6's own `OPEN-SD-CHIR-PRIMITIVE` was written to name **five** observable manifestations that a
single substrate chirality primitive must simultaneously account for:

1. **K3-doublet chirality** — Capotauro Δp_LR, the mass-mixing sector.
2. **W-bracelet V−A coupling** — electroweak, massless helicity limit.
3. **Electromagnetic handedness** — E×B / Poynting / Maxwell right-hand rule.
4. **Thermodynamic causal arrow** — DI-bit propagation direction, PCD ordering, Conscious Moment sequence.
5. **Cosmological vacuum asymmetry** — matter–antimatter, baryogenesis, Capotauro nucleation sign-selection.

**Two of those five must now be struck, and the corpus should record it:**

- **(3) is not a manifestation of chirality at all.** 4069 and 4070 established that EM handedness is a
  convention with no P-odd content: v·B and E·B vanish identically, and the physics is expressible in a
  bivector form with no hand to choose. **SF-6's item 3 is spurious** and should be removed from the list.
- **(4) is T-odd, not P-odd.** The causal arrow is the time-reversal asymmetry the corpus already carries as
  sign(δ) at W3 (TARROW-1/2), and 4068 showed δ breaks T *without* breaking P. It belongs to the arrow
  question, not the parity question. **Item 4 is misfiled** under a chirality primitive.

**That leaves three genuine P-odd manifestations — and they are not all W⁰:**

- **K3-doublet chirality (Δp_LR)** — mass-mixing sector, not electroweak catalysis.
- **W-bracelet V−A** — the weak sector, the founder's W⁰.
- **Baryogenesis / matter–antimatter asymmetry** — cosmological, and independent of both.

**So the answer to the founder's question is: no, the effects are not all W⁰-related, and a P-odd axiom would
not be single-use.** It would be load-bearing in at least three disjoint sectors — mass mixing, electroweak
coupling, and cosmology. That is precisely the criterion PD-007 sets for when an effective constant or
structure is legitimate: *"legitimate only as labeled derivation targets carried across many phenomena."* A
P-odd primitive carried across three sectors meets that test; one introduced to fix V−A alone would not.

**What the axiom would have to say, minimally.** From Part 1, the cheapest form is: *the CP carries an
orientation — a pseudoscalar sign — that is not derived from the lattice.* Concretely, either a fourth
internal direction whose sign is primitive, or equivalently a primitive ± label that enters the PCD cycle
with opposite sense for the two values. It does **not** require a new particle, a new field, or a change to
the 600-cell. It requires the axiom set to contain **one** genuinely P-odd element, which it currently does
not. That is a small axiomatic addition with a large footprint — the founder's "deep modification" is deep in
significance, not in machinery.

**What I am not claiming.** I have not derived that such an axiom *would* reproduce V−A's maximality, Δp_LR's
magnitude, or the baryon asymmetry's size. Those are three separate derivations and each could fail. I have
established only that the three targets exist, are genuinely P-odd, are disjoint, and would all draw on the
same primitive — i.e. that the axiom is not single-use *in principle*. Whether it pays off in practice is the
next arc's work, and it should be attempted on **K3/Δp_LR first**, which is the most quantitative of the three
and the least entangled with unresolved structure.
