# Reasoning — Patch 4026 (I built the fix I named, and it found something)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## Why here

4025 produced a null along n̂ and then flagged the problem with it: a round patch makes "along n̂ with a
small transverse offset" a thin tube, so the direction where the theory has no control was also where the
measurement had least power — 1,518 pairs, reach about 3 edge lengths. I named the fix in the same breath:
an anisotropic patch elongated along n̂.

Under PD-008 naming a fix and handing it on is the move to avoid. So I built it: R∥ = 8.0, R⊥ = 1.3,
giving **3,006 along-n̂ pairs and reach 12.7 edge lengths**, over 400 realisations.

## It is not the flat zero 4025 saw

The nearest band carries a **real short-range anticorrelation**: **−0.01234 ± 0.00358** at 0.5–1.0 edge
lengths — **3.4 sem**, 1.24% of on-site. Then it dies:

| separation | ⟨ηη⟩_c | sem |
|---|---|---|
| **0.5–1.0** | **−0.01234** | **3.4** |
| 1.0–1.8 | −0.00085 | 0.5 |
| 1.8–3.0 | −0.00017 | 0.1 |
| 3.0–4.5 | −0.00286 | 1.7 |
| 4.5–6.0 | −0.00003 | 0.0 |
| 6.0–9.0 | +0.00174 | 1.1 |

**ξ ≲ 1 edge length. Finite correlation length, no long-range order.** And 4025's specific worry — that a
ξ of 5 or more would be invisible — is discharged rather than restated: this reaches past 6 and sees
nothing.

## The draft check was wrong, and that was the useful part

I wrote the check as *"no band exceeds 1% of on-site"* and it **failed on the nearest band**.

The check was mis-specified, not the measurement. "No band exceeds 1%" is a test for long-range order, and
a **finite** correlation length *should* show at one edge length. **A flat zero everywhere would have been
the suspicious result** — it would have meant the estimator was measuring nothing rather than measuring a
decaying correlation.

So the better geometry did two things at once: it discharged 4025's worry, and it revealed that 4025's
null was partly a power problem after all — the short-range signal was there and the round patch could not
see it.

Fourth time this session that writing the assertion at the strength of the claim has caught the claim.

## Measured, no story

On the same patch, the short-range structure is **stronger along n̂ (−0.01234) than transverse
(−0.00245 ± 0.00069)**. That is a real asymmetry and it is the sort of thing that would be interesting if
it held up. I am recording it and building nothing on it.

## Still not closed

The patch remains a **proxy** — the icosian cut-and-project is not the substrate, and a correlation length
is exactly the sort of quantity that could differ between proxy and lattice.

**VW-1's argument still cannot reach along n̂.** 4024 stands. This is evidence for the conclusion by an
independent route, not a repair of the proof, and I do not want the improved number to blur that.

The transverse bound is still ~10× tighter by geometry — unavoidable in a tube, and noted so the asymmetry
is not forgotten now that the along-n̂ number has improved.
