# Reasoning — Patch 4025 (measuring what the argument could not reach)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The move

4024 withdrew "outside VW-1's scope" and left a real gap: on the extended lattice the reflections where RP
holds generate only transverse motion, so VW-1's chain has **no traction along n̂**.

Under PD-008 the response is not to hand that on. The argument cannot reach along n̂ — but the *quantity*
the argument would have controlled is measurable, so measure it. If the η correlator decays along n̂, the
conclusion holds by a route that needs no RP, even though the proof does not.

## What came back

Extended 4D icosian patch: 4321 points, 960 usable interior sites, 260 realisations, η from
occupation-driven perturbations.

**Along n̂**, at separations 0.5–0.9, 0.9–1.4, 1.4–2.0 and 2.0–3.0 edge lengths:
−0.0048 ± 0.0030, +0.0047 ± 0.0032, −0.0048 ± 0.0031, −0.0013 ± 0.0035. **0.13% to 0.49% of the on-site
variance, within ~2 sem of zero at every band.** Transverse, for contrast: 0.02–0.06%. On-site ⟨η²⟩ =
0.9963, which is what η = ±1 requires and confirms the estimator is not quietly broken.

So VW-1's **conclusion** survives by measurement. Its **argument** still cannot reach there.

## The thing I want someone to attack

The along-n̂ bound rests on **1,518 pairs**. The transverse one rests on **183,146**.

The patch is roughly isotropic, so "along n̂ with a small transverse offset" is a thin tube through it. The
consequence is that **the direction where the theory has no control is also the direction where this
measurement has the least power**, and the along-n̂ bound is about ten times looser.

That is precisely the shape a null result takes when something is hiding in it. I am not claiming it is
hiding; I am saying the coincidence is unfortunate, it runs in the direction convenient to me, and it is
the first place to push. A better test would use an anisotropic patch elongated along n̂ — the geometry this
one lacks.

## Two more limits, said plainly

The patch reaches about 3 edge lengths, so a correlation length of 5 or more would not show **at all**.
This bounds long-range order at this size; it does not exclude it.

And the patch is a **proxy**. 4013 and 4015 established that the icosian cut-and-project is not the
substrate — window boundary, and z running over {12,…,26}. It is extended, 4D and near-icosahedral, which
is what a correlation question needs, but it is not the lattice, and a correlation length is exactly the
kind of quantity that could differ between a proxy and the real thing.

## Where this leaves the chain

FI-C-9 = V3 now rests on: four direct chirality measurements (4011, 4012, 4015, 4020), an exact-symmetry
argument that needs no RP on the finite substrate (4024), VW-1 with restricted RP transversally (4023, as
corrected), and this bounded measurement along n̂. The weakest link is the one in this patch, and it is
weakest exactly where I have least statistical power.
