**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## Why I did not start deriving

The instruction was to proceed to OPEN-SM-4 (a)/(b). D-2 says: *before working any item carried over from
more than two sessions back, re-read the patch that registered it and check its premise against what has
landed since.* The SM.md entry's own footer reads **16 May 2026**. That is not two sessions back; it is
four months and, as it turned out, ten patches.

D-2's worked example is VSL — named as the e-fold budget's *"most promising escape"* seven patches after
its premise was withdrawn, then carried in five successive handovers, and one whole session was spent
discovering it had never been alive. The shape here was identical enough that running the audit first
was not optional.

## What the audit found

**The SM-side entry and the CHIR-side entry are the same problem, four months apart.** `OPEN-CHIR-3` in
`frontier_sectors/CHIR.md` carries a full bridge decomposition — B-i through B-iv — in which **B-iii is
OPEN-SM-4 (a)/(b) by explicit identification** (Patch 0662). Every patch of that structure — 0662, 0663,
0668, 0669, 0670, 0679, 0680, 0682, 0694, 1100 — postdates 16 May. **None of it is cited in SM.md.** A
worker starting from the SM side, as I was told to, would have redone all of it.

Three specific things the entry still asks for:

**1. `derive χ = φ⁻³`.** Already done. Capotauro v2.0 derives it from the perturbative-distance-ratio
constraint on n̂-induced edge perturbations, *replacing v1.0's free magnitude postulate* — the paper says
so in its own opening section. CHI-1 builds it as the first-shell distance ratio (1−φ⁻¹)/(1+φ⁻¹), which
is φ⁻³ **exactly**, not to within a fit; I checked the identity rather than trusting the prose.

**2. `[600-cell] × ℤ₂ → [600-cell]`.** This is the part that matters, and it is not staleness — it is
tension. **THEO-CHIR-VW-1** (0680, review-closed 3/3 at 0682) argues that conditional on H1 the det-coset
ℤ₂ **cannot break spontaneously within the substrate axioms at all**. The entry is asking a worker to go
derive the event a review-closed theorem in the same corpus says does not happen. Whether VW-1 is right is
a separate question — H1 is unproved and openly labelled so — but the entry should not be silent about it.
FI-C-9 had already been moved off the SSB framing at 0413, from *broken-symmetry order parameter* to
*primitive substrate feature*, for the same underlying reason.

**3. The real residuals.** B-iii has been reduced twice — capacity ⟺ sign(μ²) (0668), then
sign(μ²) = sign(m²) (1100) — and what remains is two named gaps, **(H1)** and **(H-NESS)**. That is a far
smaller and far more tractable thing than "formalise the Capotauro mechanism", and it is what should have
been on the SM entry for three months.

## The sharpest finding, and it is not a bookkeeping one

Patch 0692 states: *"the μ²-sign computation is now cleared on the reviewed foundation."* CAPACITY-1,
reserved at the time, was **enacted at 0960**. Its two gating arms — Mechanism A and pointwise
non-degeneracy — were discharged at 0960 and 0968. So the computation that decides the corpus's own
**sole verdict-moving engine** has been cleared to run since 8 June, its remaining conditionality has
since been discharged, and **nobody has run it**.

The mechanism of the drop is visible in the grep: `H-NESS` occurs in **exactly one live file** — CHIR.md's
8 June header line. It was never in `todolist.md`. That is D-9 in its pure form: a record is not a queue,
and a status line is read by nobody who has to act. It is now filed.

floor p(v) ≥ 4 for the *dynamical* η — a statement about the η-field rather than about the single walker,
which is exactly the half of (H-NESS) that 1100 described as *"lift π to the η-field measure."* It is
suggestive. It is also unchecked, and D-4 is explicit that a flagged check is not a performed check, so it
goes in as a lead labelled NOT PERFORMED rather than as progress. The temptation to write it up as though
the connection were made is the specific thing D-4 exists to stop.

**No panel.** Nothing here moves a verdict; the economy dispatches on a win or when stuck, and this is
neither.

**No derivation.** Starting one on a four-month-old premise statement was the error available today, and
it is the one the audit was run to avoid.
