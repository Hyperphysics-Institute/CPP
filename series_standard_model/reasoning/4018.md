## Bravais lattices, in any dimension

The 600-cell's twelve nearest neighbours all share the **same** inner product with the vertex, −0.190983.
So they lie in a single 3-flat — and, more to the point, **strictly on one side of the vertex.** Their
offsets sum to 2.291796, not zero, and {2v₀ − wᵢ} ≠ {wᵢ}.

A Bravais lattice has inversion symmetry at every point. Its nearest-neighbour set is therefore **always**
centrally symmetric about that point. **The 600-cell's vertex figure is not.** So no Bravais lattice, in any
dimension, can carry it.

That is a one-line argument on a quantity that has been sitting in plain view in every script I have run
this week. It did not need the icosian ring, the Coxeter classification, or a window.

## H₄ is not crystallographic

The isoclinic rotations L_q for q ∈ 2I have traces in {0, ±2, ±4} — and **±2/φ and ±2φ**, which are
irrational. Trace is **basis-independent**, so no conjugate of H₄ lies in GL(4,ℤ). H₄ has no faithful
integral representation and is therefore not crystallographic in 4D, and no space group of E⁴ can have it
as a site group.

## The number that turned out to be one number

|Σ(wᵢ − v₀)| = **2.291796068**.

That is **exactly Patch 1100's graph-Laplacian spectral gap λ₁** — the number the μ²-sign computation runs
on — and both equal **12(1 − φ/2)**.

So the vertex figure's central *asymmetry* and 1100's *spectral gap* are one quantity seen twice. It may be
structural; it may be that both are built from the same 12 and the same φ and there is nothing more to it.
**I am recording it and attaching no story.** This session has cost several retractions to the habit of
deciding what a suggestive number means before checking, and I would rather leave a clean identity for
someone than a claim I have to withdraw.

## What is closed and what is not

Five classes, five unrelated arguments:

1. periodic Euclidean tiling — 4009, dihedral angle
2. bounded-window cut-and-project — 4013, window boundary
3. regular honeycomb at any curvature — 4017, Gram signature and vertex figure
4. Bravais lattice, any dimension — here, central asymmetry
5. periodic with full H₄ site symmetry — here, irrational trace

**Not closed**, and I want this in the same breath rather than in a footnote: periodic vertex-transitive
structures whose site symmetry is a **proper subgroup** of H₄ but whose twelve nearest neighbours still form
a regular icosahedron; and **aperiodic** uniform structures outside the cut-and-project class.

Five closed classes is a strong cumulative case and it is **not** a proof that nothing works. Saying
otherwise would be the same over-reach as 4013's "the class is forced", which is three patches old and was
corrected two patches ago.

Nothing here adopts the weak reading. The question is still the founder's.
