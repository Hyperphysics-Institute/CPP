## The correction comes first, because it is mine and it matters

Physical parity inverts 3-space (4074 ruling). Under that definition:

1. **4071's "four independent directions" was the threshold for the 4D invariant, not for physical parity.**
   det₃[a,b,c] of three *spatial* vectors flips under P in 20,000/20,000 draws — (−1)³ = −1. **Three spatial
   directions suffice.**
2. **4073/4074's "no composite confined to 3-space can be handed" conflated two objects.** For **rotations** it
   is true and stands (B∧B ≡ 0 over 5,000 superposed 3-space rotations). For **arrangements** it is false: a
   fixed cyclic triad is det₃ = +1 and mirrors to −1. That is ordinary 3D chirality — a left hand is not a right
   hand — and my phrasing excluded it.

I wrote those summaries; the founder has been reading them. The practical consequence is that **a chiral
arrangement in ordinary space is P-odd with no fourth-axis machinery at all**, which is ground I had ruled out.

## Four routes, in the order I would pursue them

**K3 — procedural chirality (cheapest, and my pick).** When a CP's move is degenerate, *something* must resolve
it. The corpus has never specified what. A tie-break using sign(det₃[previous step, option, reference]) is P-odd
and adds **no variable** — it specifies an existing unspecified rule. Simulated on a walk: chiral tie-break gives
a net P-odd invariant of 1.000 per step; a P-even tie-break gives exactly 0.000. Degeneracy is also where a
binary rule has maximal leverage, which is what V−A's all-or-nothing character needs (4050).

**K2 — chiral packing (Boerdijk–Coxeter).** Regular tetrahedra stacked face-to-face form a helix with a definite
hand: torsion +9.19, mirror image −9.19, sum 0. No new variable; the hand is in the **packing**. This connects to
an open wound in the corpus: 4019 showed the 600-cell cannot tile flat ℝ⁴, and helical packing is precisely what
tetrahedra do instead. The unfinished global-lattice question and the chirality question may be **the same
question**.

**K4 — the CPT route.** P-odd = (C-odd) × (T-odd). CPP already has a T-odd element (sign δ, TARROW-1/2 at W3);
nature has a C-odd fact (the matter excess). Their product is C-odd and T-odd, hence P-odd if CPT is exact. **No
new primitive is required — what is owed is the link.** Most speculative of the four, and the most valuable if it
holds, because it would make parity violation a *consequence* of the arrow of time rather than a separate
postulate.

**K5 — a P-odd initial condition.** Listed for completeness and I think it is weak: V−A is maximal and exact
everywhere, whereas a frozen initial condition would be expected to vary across regions or dilute.
