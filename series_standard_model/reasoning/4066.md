## The error

In chat at the close of 4064 I wrote that the corpus "already carries handedness through the individual
Conscious Points' own orientation." **That is wrong, and 4046 refutes it in the corpus's own words:** a vector
is not a chirality. Θ = diag(1,1,1,−1) preserves the 600-cell, has det = −1, and **fixes n̂ exactly**. So
(600-cell, n̂) is achiral. n̂ alone carries no handedness.

What the corpus actually says is narrower and weaker: **FI-C-9 = sign(n̂) is the one currently-identified
irreducible chirality primitive, at verdict V3 — meaning NOT YET DERIVED** (chirality_determination_closure.md).
V3 is a statement about where the derivation stopped, not a mechanism that works. I upgraded "the open primitive
we have not derived" into "the mechanism that already does the work." That is the same class of error as 4049's
and 4054's: taking a registered gap for a registered result.

The founder caught it by asking the right question: *if the lattice is not where chirality comes from, is the
EW/W⁰ examination what settles it?*

## Answer to the founder's three questions, from the record

**(1) Have we worked out W⁰ handedness? No.** 4050 established, on objects already in the corpus:
- the ring bivector ∧ centroid ∧ n̂, contracted with the 4D Levi-Civita, is **P-odd by construction** — a real
  two-state variable on the bracelet, all 63 induced 6-cycles through a vertex carrying helicity **1/(2φ) =
  0.309017**;
- under Θ the helicity **flips for all 63 rings and is unchanged for none** ⇒ **the two states are exactly
  degenerate**, and nothing in the present setup prefers one.

4050 then says in its own words: *"The splitting is not computed... It does not show that a chiral substrate
splits them, nor by how much, nor with which sign."* **That is the open step, and it is the whole remaining
step.** It has stood untouched since 15 Sep.

**(2) Can we produce the handed catalytic effect? Not demonstrated.** 4050 also flags that SF-2 describes the
W⁰ as a catalyst activated at a D₆-symmetric centroid, **not** as a helicity state, and that whether the W state
carries this variable is SF-2's to say. So there are two unclosed links, not one: (a) does a chiral environment
split the degenerate pair, with what sign and magnitude; (b) is the split variable the one SF-2's catalysis
actually uses.

**(3) Are the 7,200 GPs necessary for a handed W⁰? No — and that is now a sharper claim than 4064 made.** The
bracelet's achirality (4049, SF-2 Thm 4.2, D₆ stabiliser) is not an obstacle but the **precondition**: an achiral
bracelet is what guarantees exactly two degenerate states for something else to split. What the splitting needs
is a **parity-odd asymmetry in the environment**, which Mechanism A supplies **dynamically** (Q = L + δC, L
H4-invariant, C parity-odd) without any change to the lattice. U's 7,200 GPs are one way to source a P-odd
environment; the δ-term is another, already in the corpus, and costs no sites. So the honest statement is: the
7,200 GPs are **not necessary**, because a cheaper P-odd source exists — **not** because handedness is already
solved elsewhere. 4064's recommendation stands; 4064's reason #1 ("it is not needed — the corpus already has a
reviewed route") is **corrected here to**: the corpus has a cheaper *candidate* route, whose decisive step is
unexecuted.
