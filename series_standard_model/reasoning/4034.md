# Reasoning — Patch 4034 (I narrowed the question instead of asking it)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## Why not just ask

4033 ended by asking the founder to specify SR-1's construction. That is a legitimate hand-up — it is a
physical picture, and PD-006(a) reserves those. But PD-008 says narrow it first, and "write out your
construction" is an expensive question: it invites a long answer and gives him nothing to react to.

So before asking, I tested whether the specification is even free.

## It is not

**Four more candidates fail** — φ-inflation shells, and motif-at-each-vertex at t = 1, φ and 2, all at a
z = 12 fraction of **0.000**.

And the reason is a trichotomy in the translation scale:

| t | nearest distance | z = 12 fraction | max hole |
|---|---|---|---|
| φ (overlapping) | 0.381966 | 0.000 | 0.363 |
| φ² (just touching) | 0.618034 | 0.000 | 0.588 |
| 2φ (separated) | 0.381966 | 0.000 | 0.853 |
| **4.0 (well separated)** | 0.618034 | **0.741** | **1.280** |

Motif edge is 0.618034. **Dense enough to tile and vertices from different copies land closer than the
motif's own edge — so the nearest-neighbour shell stops being the motif's twelve. Sparse enough to preserve
z = 12 and the holes are twice the edge length — so it does not tile.**

**For translation sets drawn from the motif's own vertex directions, no scale does both.** SR-1's
*"overlapping … tile flat ℝ⁴"* and SM's *exactly twelve at the edge distance* pull against each other, and
the pull is **geometric** — not a matter of choosing well.

## The scoping, which matters here more than usual

I tested translation sets **drawn from the motif's own vertex directions**. That is the natural reading of
*modular repetition of 600-cell motifs*, and it is not the only possible one. **A general translation set
could differ, and nothing here rules one out.**

I want that stated at full strength because the result is tempting to state as "SR-1's construction is
impossible", which is three steps further than I have gone.

And it is **not** a claim that SR-1 is wrong. SR-1 says **approximation**. What this does is quantify what
the approximation costs in the one currency SM cannot spend.

## φ², recorded

At t = φ² a single translated pair touches **exactly** at the edge: 0.618033989 against 0.618033989. The
only tested scale where one copy-pair neither interpenetrates nor separates.

The **full union still coincides**, because pairs *between different translates* do not respect the
single-pair condition — so it does not rescue the construction. But the value is exact and this corpus
lives on φ², so it goes in the record rather than the bin. No story attached.

## The question, sharper

4033 asked *specify the construction*. The better question is narrower, because the specification is not
free:

**Which does the substrate give up — overlap/tiling, or exactly-twelve?**

Both are load-bearing. *Overlapping … tile flat ℝ⁴* is SR-1's own wording and its figure's caption.
*z = 12* is SS-1, SM-1, SM-7, SM-8, SM-9 and SF-4, with SF-4 carrying Σm_ν ∝ z⁻⁹.

That is a question he can answer in a sentence, which is the point of having narrowed it.

No panel. No verdict moved. SR-1 and SF-4 both unrevised.
