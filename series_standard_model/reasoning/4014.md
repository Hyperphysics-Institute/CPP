# Reasoning — Patch 4014 (a gate crashed, and that was the only signal)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## How this was found

Not by review. `code/deferral_gate.py` died with `UnicodeDecodeError` while reading the Patch 4013 diff.

The cause: a heredoc mangled `E₈` into the bytes `E 0x82 0x88` — the ASCII letter `E` followed by the tail
of U+2088 with its lead byte lost. **It renders as nothing unusual. `git show` shows nothing unusual.** It
had just been committed into `series_standard_model/reasoning/4013.md`, a Tier-4 verbatim file, and it
would have stayed there.

The easy response was to add `errors="replace"` to the gate and move on. That is what makes this worth a
patch: the crash *was the detection*. Silencing it without asking what it was reading would have converted
a caught error into a permanent one.

## The sweep, and the one that is not mine

Repo-wide: 7908 tracked files, 135 flagged, 133 of them binaries (PDFs, PNGs, .gz) which is expected. Two
genuine text corruptions:

1. `reasoning/4013.md` — mine, minutes old.
2. `series_phenomena/cosmology/dark_matter/relic1_qm2_gate_smA_fails.md` — **`Eötvös` stored as
   `E\xb6tv\xc3\xb6s`.** Written broken, by another lane, some time ago, never caught.

Both repaired. Byte-level transcription repair, not a content edit — which matters for 4013.md, since
Tier-4 files are preserved unedited and I want the distinction on record.

**Both are the same failure shape:** one lead byte lost from a multi-byte character, leaving something that
still looks almost right. That the DM instance survived independently of me is the part that says this is a
corpus-level problem rather than a mistake I made once.

## Two mechanizations

**`deferral_gate.py` hardened** — decodes with `errors="replace"`. A gate that crashes on bad input is a
gate that is not run: the next worker to hit this would have commented it out or skipped it, not
investigated it.

**`code/encoding_gate.py` added** — refuses any patch that introduces invalid UTF-8 into a text file,
skipping binaries by extension, with `--all` to sweep the tree. Currently 0 of 7908.

Silent mojibake is the worst class of corpus error. It does not fail. It does not render wrong enough to
notice. It survives review. So it accumulates, and the only reason this one surfaced is that it happened to
sit in the input of a script that was strict about decoding.

## What the three gates now say about this lane

The corpus carries three gates on classes of error this lane has actually committed:

- **deferrals** — D-9, owed items lost between windows
- **absences** — 4012, "not in the corpus" claimed on a scoped grep
- **encoding** — here

All three exist for the same reason: the mistake was made more than once, the rule was written down, and
writing it down did not stop it. I would rather that pattern be visible in the tooling than restated in
another reasoning fragment that nobody reads at the moment it matters.
