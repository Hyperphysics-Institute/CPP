## What the corpus actually says

**AP-4, verbatim:** the DI-bit imprint is *"a STATIC SNAPSHOT of the origin GP's computed registers"*; the
receiver extracts **V_i = E + S**, where E is the **vector sum** of contributions; and *"every GP emits the
same fixed number of DI-bits every Moment"* — fixed, with **no value specified anywhere**.

**So 4079 measured the wrong quantity.** I modelled degeneracy as equal *arrival counts* and got a 14.3% generic
tie rate, which looked like a fatal leak into EM. But a decision is made on equal **V_i vector sums**, and
those are register values, not tallies. The controlling parameter is **register depth**, not how many bits arrive.

## The result

Generic (asymmetric) tie rate falls as roughly 2⁻ᵇ: 0.177 at 4 bits, 0.0114 at 8, 0.0033 at 10, 0.00056 at 12,
0.00000 at 16. **The symmetric rate is 1.0000 at every depth.**

The asymmetry has a clean reason. The 600-cell's vertex stabiliser is icosahedral and **transitive on the 12
neighbours**, so an isotropic source produces twelve *identical* contributions — equal by symmetry, exactly, at
any precision. A generic source produces twelve different ones that coincide only by accident, at a rate set by
quantisation.

**So the separation between "maximal violation in symmetric structures" and "none in generic matter" is
exponential in register depth, not the marginal 7× that 4079 reported.** K3's requirement softens from the
demanding "DI-bit counts ≳10³" to the mild **"registers deeper than a few bits."** At 10 bits the EM leak is 0.3%;
at 16 it is below anything measurable.

## The error I caught, and what caught it

My first symmetric model gave every neighbour the **same register vector**. That is not a symmetric configuration:
the geometric direction differs per neighbour, so the contributions differ and it tied only 24% of the time — and
the symmetric rate then fell with depth, which is physically absurd (symmetry is exact, not approximate). **The
control — "the symmetric rate must not fall with b" — is what exposed it.** Corrected to an isotropic source.
Seventh instance in this arc of a model artifact caught by a control rather than by inspection.

**Convenient.** This patch rescues the route I had ranked first, by correcting a model I wrote one patch earlier.
