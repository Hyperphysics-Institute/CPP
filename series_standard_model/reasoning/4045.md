# Reasoning — Patch 4045 (the escape route, tested)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## Why look

4044 ended with *a different observable would be needed and I do not have one to name*. Under PD-008 that
gets looked for rather than left, and one route suggested itself immediately.

## The hypothesis, and it was a good one

4044's O(k¹⁵) assumed **full** icosahedral symmetry. But CPP's substrate carries a **preferred direction
n̂** — Mechanism A's drive — which reduces I to the stabiliser of n̂. **Lower symmetry permits lower-degree
invariants.** If so, the parity-odd term is lifted straight out of its k¹⁵ grave.

## And it is lifted, by fourteen orders

With δ ≠ 0 the parity-odd term is **O(k¹)**, measured slope 1.00 across k = 0.05 to 0.40.

For a moment that is the result the whole arc has been looking for.

## But it is not the chirality

Per-point parity-odd amplitude at k = 0.05, δ = 0.10:

| shell | total | per point |
|---|---|---|
| chiral 60 | 5.3084e-02 | **8.847293e-04** |
| icosahedron 12 | 1.0617e-02 | **8.847293e-04** |
| dodecahedron 20 | 1.7695e-02 | **8.847293e-04** |

**Identical to seven significant figures.** The chiral shell's larger total is entirely its larger point
count.

**The term is the drive's asymmetry, not the shell's handedness.** The weight (1 + δ·v̂·n̂) is itself not
centrally symmetric, and that — and only that — is what the measurement sees. At δ = 0 the chiral shell
returns to machine noise, exactly as 4044 found.

I would not have caught this from the totals alone. 5.3e-2 against 1.1e-2 is a factor of five and reads
like a signal; dividing by the point count is what turns it into a null.

## So the route is closed

Any observable built on the tilted dispersion measures **δ**, not chirality. Isolating the chirality means
subtracting the achiral contribution exactly — and once subtracted, what remains is back at O(k¹⁵).

**The k¹⁵ suppression is robust against the substrate's own preferred direction**, which was the most
plausible escape available and the one I would have reached for next.

Testing it was worth more than leaving it as a hope. An untested escape route is an open question that
reads like an opportunity; a tested one is a closed door that stays closed.

## Not claimed

That no observable exists. One route tested and eliminated. The space of observables is not enumerated and
this patch does not pretend otherwise.

Chirality remains a structural parameter rather than a prediction — with one candidate now eliminated
rather than merely unexplored, which is a slightly better state than 4044 left.

No panel. No verdict moved. FI-C-9 = V3 stands.
