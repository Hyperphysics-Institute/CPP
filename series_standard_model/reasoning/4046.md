# Reasoning — Patch 4046 (the arc walked into a named problem)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## What I went looking for

Whether geometric chirality is even the right *kind* of thing to answer CPP's chirality question. A P-odd
geometric handedness is no use if the physical target is P-even, and I had not checked.

It is the right kind. SF-6's `OPEN-FP-6-EMHAND` is *the entry of substrate chirality into electromagnetic
phenomenology*; SF-2's `OPEN-FP-SF-2-CHIR` is *chirality emergence in W bracelet structure (V−A coupling)*.
**V−A is parity violation, which is P-odd.** The kinds match.

## And then the larger find

SF-6 — **shipped** — carries `OPEN-SD-CHIR-PRIMITIVE`: *"Derive the universe's primitive chirality bias
from a single substrate-level mechanism"*, and names its **current leading candidate: a primitive 4D
direction n̂ aligned with a 600-cell host vertex.**

**That candidate cannot work, and this session demonstrated it five times without knowing what it was
demonstrating.**

**A vector is not a chirality.** n̂ is P-odd, but reflection in any plane *containing* n̂ leaves n̂
invariant. Verified with n̂ aligned to a host vertex exactly as SF-6 specifies: **Θ = diag(1,1,1,−1) maps
the 600-cell to itself with det = −1 and fixes n̂ exactly, |Θn − n| = 0.0.** The pair (600-cell, n̂) is
invariant under an improper isometry. The configuration is achiral, and no amount of n̂ changes that.

**And that Θ is precisely the map 4011, 4012, 4015, 4020 and 4024 all used.**

Those five look like five independent confirmations of V3, and I have described them that way all day.
They are five demonstrations that **SF-6's leading candidate supplies no chirality** — the same fact, read
as a property of the substrate rather than as a refutation of a named proposal.

Nothing new was computed to see this. What was needed was to notice what the five had in common — 4038 did
that — and then find the proposal they bear on, which took one search of a directory I now remember to
search.

## The replacement

4041's cluster has **no improper symmetry at all**. There is no Θ to fix n̂ because there is no Θ. **The
construction removes precisely the thing the leading candidate leaves intact.**

So 4038–4045 is a candidate answer to a named, shipped open problem. That is a better statement of what
this arc is than anything it has managed about itself: **it was not looking for OPEN-SD-CHIR-PRIMITIVE; it
walked into it.**

## What is still owed, and the renaming changes none of it

A **lattice** rather than a 72-point cluster — 4034's trichotomy untouched. A **magnitude** — 4042 leaves
it free over 0.012–0.187. An **observable** that fixes it — k¹⁵ closed at 4044, n̂-tilt closed at 4045. And
a **derivation of V−A from the handedness**, which is SF-2's own open problem and is not touched here.

**Not claimed: that the chiral lattice solves OPEN-SD-CHIR-PRIMITIVE.** It is a candidate that survives the
tests the previous candidate fails. That is a real change of status and it is not a closure — and the
distance between those two is most of what this session has been about.

No panel. No verdict moved. No paper revised.
