**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.** Lane EW (40xx), 17 Sep 2026. Founder: *"please proceed."* The bounded question
from 4089: does the corpus contain a **detuned** oscillation, given that a 1:1 frequency ratio kills the

## It does, and it is derived

SPIN-1, verbatim: the captured DP's positive CP falls to r_in while its negative CP is pushed to 2 r_in; both
orbit the central −eCP; *"the exact ratio of their angular frequencies … is 2√2 ≈ 2.83. This ratio is not
assumed; it falls out of the 1/r² Coulomb force law and the geometric fact that the outer radius is exactly
twice the inner radius."*

**Reproduced independently:** circular orbits under a 1/r² central force give ω ∝ r^(−3/2), so with r_out = 2 r_in
the ratio is 2^(3/2) = 2.828427 — exact.

**So the detuning 4089 requires exists in CPP already, derived rather than fitted.** At that ratio the
pseudoscalar is −5.6564 rather than the 0.0000 that 1:1 resonance gives. **The obstruction 4089 raised is
removed by a structure the theory already owns, with nothing added.**

## And now the part that matters more

**The magnitude is exactly linear in the frequency ratio: |pseudoscalar| = 2 × (Ω/ω), to four figures across
1.05, √2, 2, 2√2 and 4.** So 2√2 produces 5.657 and nothing distinguished. **This yields no zero-parameter
prediction.** It removes an obstruction; it predicts nothing.

I checked this precisely because the alternative was tempting: a derived ratio of 2√2 appearing exactly where a
frequency ratio was needed looks like a result, and reporting "the corpus supplies the detuning it needs" without
the scaling check would have let it read as one. It is not one.

## What it does not settle, and this is the live gap

**SPIN-1's two orbits are coplanar** — inner and outer CPs circle the same central −eCP about a common axis. Two
coplanar circulations do **not** make a double rotation: a pseudoscalar needs two **orthogonal** planes spanning
all four dimensions (4072). **So the detuned pair supplies the frequency ratio the constraint needs but not the
second plane.** The w-leg is still required, and still unexplained. The founder's oscillation remains the only
candidate for it.
