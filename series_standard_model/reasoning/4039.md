# Reasoning — Patch 4039 (a failure with a diagnosis)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The attempt

4038 named the target and recorded three failures. This is the fourth: decorate each of the 600-cell's 600
tetrahedral cells with a signed radial offset, the sign from the cell's vertex determinant. Keep the
rotations, break the mirrors.

## Half of it worked, which is the trap

**Improper symmetries went 96 → 0** at every ε. If I had stopped there I would have shipped "chiral
decoration achieved."

**Proper symmetries went 96 → 1.** The decoration is not rotation-equivariant, so it realises neither I_h
nor I — it realises nothing. The mirrors did not break; the whole symmetry did.

## And the chirality measure caught it independently

Sums at ε = −0.10, −0.05, +0.05, +0.10: **+8, +35, +5, +8.**

**The sign does not flip with ε.** A genuine chiral decoration is its own mirror image under ε → −ε, so the
sum must flip. It does not. What is being measured is the arbitrariness of the decoration, not a handedness.

Two independent checks agreeing on a negative is worth more than either, and the ε → −ε test is the one I
would keep: it is cheap, it is decisive, and it does not care how the decoration was built.

## Why it cannot be patched

The per-cell sign used `det[V_i, V_a, V_b, V_c]` with vertices in **index order**. Index order is not a
geometric property of the cell — a rotation permutes cells without carrying my labelling, so the sign is
assigned inconsistently.

And no better ordering exists, because **a regular tetrahedron is achiral** — twelve improper symmetries of
its own, verified. **There is no per-cell handedness to decorate with.**

That is the third instance today of the same shape: **a quantity defined by an arbitrary choice among
equals is not a quantity.** 4023 found it in η's tied top-four selection; 4030 found it in the
over-populated coordination shell; here it is in a per-cell determinant sign. Worth naming as a pattern
rather than fixing three times.

## What it sharpens

The decoration must sit on a motif that is **itself handed**. In the 600-cell the natural candidate is the
**ring of five tetrahedra around an edge**: five is odd, a cyclic ring of five admits two traversal senses,
and a ring has an orientation where a simplex does not.

And it is the same five whose 5 × 70.5288° = 352.644° leaves the **7.356° deficit** that 4019 identified as
the single cause beneath every closure in this arc.

**That the handedness candidate and the frustration are the same five tetrahedra is worth noticing.** I am
noticing it and building nothing on it — the day's record on suggestive coincidences is poor, and this one
is untested.

No panel. No verdict moved. FI-C-9 = V3 stands; no chirality has been generated.
