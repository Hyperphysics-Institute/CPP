## The real reason is a fact about four dimensions

The quaternion **−1 lies in 2I**, so left-multiplication by it is **L₍₋₁₎ = −I** — precisely the inversion
x → −x. **The shell is centrally symmetric, and central symmetry kills every odd moment identically, at

These are compatible because **in four dimensions inversion is proper: det(−I₄) = +1, against det(−I₃) =
−1.**

**Central symmetry and chirality coexist in 4D. They cannot in 3D.**
