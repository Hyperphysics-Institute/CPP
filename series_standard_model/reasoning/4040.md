# Reasoning — Patch 4040 (a failed attempt that closes a method)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## 4039's prediction was right about the geometry

The ring of five tetrahedra around an edge is real: five cells, centroids forming a **regular pentagon**,
all radii equal to 1e-9. A ring does have an orientation where a simplex does not.

## And wrong about what that buys

Computed by a nearest-neighbour walk, the traversal sense splits **358 / 362** across the 720 edges.

An almost even split is not a result; it is a **tell**. From any vertex of a pentagon, **two** neighbours
are equidistant, and my walk broke the tie by index. The 358/362 is my tie-break, sampled 720 times.

**Fifth instance today of one shape: a quantity defined by an arbitrary choice among equals is not a
quantity.** 4023's η ties, 4030's over-populated shell, 4039's per-cell determinant, and now this. I have
stopped being surprised by it and started expecting it, which is probably the useful adaptation.

And doing it properly gives nothing either. Orient the ring's 2-plane from (d, m) and the ambient ℝ⁴
orientation, then read the cyclic order **off that orientation** — now the sense is fixed by construction,
the same on every edge, and carries no per-edge information at all.

## The reason generalises, and that is the patch

The 600-cell is a **regular** polytope. Its symmetry group is transitive on flags, hence on vertices,
edges, faces and cells separately — verified directly: all 120 vertices at one radius, all 720 edge
midpoints at one radius.

A decoration assigns something to each vertex, edge or cell. **Transitivity means any rotation-equivariant
assignment is constant across its orbit — and a constant carries no handedness.**

**So no local decoration of the 600-cell can be chiral.** 4039 failed on cells, this fails on edges, and
vertex-transitivity closes the third case without a sixth attempt.

**These are not unlucky attempts. It is regularity — and regularity is the property the 600-cell was chosen
for.** The thing that makes it the right object for everything else is exactly the thing that forbids this.

## What survives

4038's target is unchanged: icosahedral **rotation** symmetry, no improper operations, nearest shell of
exactly twelve. What 4040 adds is that **it cannot be reached by decorating the 600-cell.** The structure
must have symmetry **I from the start**, not H₄ with something added.

In 3D that is a chiral icosahedral quasicrystal, which exists in nature. The 4D analogue is what would have
to be built, and that is now the whole of the remaining work on this line.

## Five attempts, on record

Close-packings (achiral), golden screw (loses z = 12), snub-24 (z = 9), cell decoration (kills the
rotations), edge-ring (no invariant). **The last two fail for a single reason, now named rather than
rediscovered** — which is the only thing that makes a fifth failure worth a patch.

No panel. No verdict moved. FI-C-9 = V3 stands; no chirality has been generated.
