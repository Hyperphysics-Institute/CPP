## The question K3 stood or fell on

K3 puts the hand in whatever resolves a degenerate CP move. Its force depends on how often CPP is actually
degenerate. If SSV_net were real-valued, exact ties would be measure-zero and K3 could never produce V−A's
**maximal** violation. But the Perceive stage **counts DI-bit arrivals** — integer arithmetic, where majorities
tie exactly and often. A3′ C5's "unique assembled metric" governs the layer-1 displacement; it does not make the
arrival counts non-degenerate.

## What the measurement says

On the real 600-cell with z = 12 and integer arrivals: **symmetric configurations tie 100% of the time**; a ring
source with ambient noise, 86.6%; a generic asymmetric source, **14.3%**; and the continuum control, 0.0%.

**The half that works, and it is the first time in this arc anything has.** The W bracelet is D₆-symmetric.
In a symmetric structure every decision is a tie, so a chiral tie-break governs **~100%** of them — **maximal**
violation, with no new variable. Every earlier route supplied at most a small bias (4048's ~2% cap); this supplies
all-or-nothing, because a symmetric structure presents nothing but ties.

**The half that fails.** 14.3% is not ~0. The same rule would bias 14% of decisions in *generic* configurations —
electromagnetic ones included — giving parity-violating EM at a level 4069/4070 exclude. **K3 does not separate
the sectors on its own.** I ranked it first at 4078 on the expectation that ties would be confined to symmetric
structures; that expectation was wrong.

## What the failure converts into — the useful part

The generic tie fraction is set by the DI-bit **dynamic range**, which the corpus nowhere specifies: 0.878 at
counts 0–4, 0.140 at 0–40, 0.0054 at 0–1000, 0.0007 at 0–10 000. The symmetric case ties at 1.0000 for **every**
range.

So K3 survives as a **quantitative requirement**: parity violation is confined to symmetric structures only if
typical DI-bit counts in ordinary matter are large. **This is falsifiable against a number the corpus has never
specified** — and it is the sharpest statement this arc has produced. It also makes a prediction of the right
shape: maximal violation where structures are symmetric, vanishing violation elsewhere, from **one** rule.

## Convenient branch — marked

**Inconvenient, against my own ranking.** I put K3 first last patch and the test partly refuted it. The convenient
report would have been the symmetric result alone — 100% ties in the bracelet, maximal V−A, one rule, no new
variable — which is a genuinely exciting number and would have read as a breakthrough. The 14.3% leak is the part
that matters and it is the part I would have been tempted to defer. **What would rescue K3 fully:** a
demonstration that DI-bit counts in ordinary matter are ≳10³, making the leak ≲0.5%. **What would kill it:** counts
of order 10, where EM would be visibly handed.
