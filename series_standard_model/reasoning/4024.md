# Reasoning — Patch 4024 (I attacked my own convenient step, and it broke)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## Why this patch exists

4023 ended by flagging two attack points and handing them to the next window. That is exactly the move
PD-008 was enacted to stop, and I made it in the same patch that enacted PD-008 — marking the convenient
branch is not the same as testing it. So I tested it.

## Attack (b) — repelled

Θ = diag(1,1,1,−1) **commutes with the NESS generator exactly**: |ΘQΘ⁻¹ − Q| = 1.8 × 10⁻¹⁵. Not a
symmetry of the measure alone, a symmetry of the **dynamics**. And it is not time reversal:
|ΘQΘ⁻¹ − Qᵀ| = 0.2 at δ = 0.10 and 0.7 at δ = 0.35, growing with the drive.

So P and T are genuinely distinct objects here, 0973's split holds, and 4023's Step 2 stands.

## Attack (a) — lands, on me

The n̂-fixing reflections generate a group of **8 elements, none of which moves n̂**. Displacement exactly
zero over the whole group.

A chessboard/OS estimate is built by **tiling the lattice with reflections**. If the reflections where RP
holds generate only transverse motion, the estimate controls transverse correlations and has **no traction
along n̂ at all**.

**So "the failure is outside VW-1's scope" is withdrawn.** No-SSB is a statement about the whole system. A
direction the argument cannot reach is a gap **inside** the scope, not a region outside it. I marked that
sentence as the convenient reading one patch ago and it was convenient because it was wrong.

## The net, which is not what I expected

**Finite substrate: stronger than 4023 said.** Attack (b)'s result does work here — because Θ commutes with
Q, π is exactly Θ-invariant, and the perturbation ensemble is Θ-symmetric, so **E[⟨η⟩] = 0 by symmetry
alone, with no RP anywhere.** 4023 presented the finite case as resting on RP. It does not.

That correction came out of the same computation as the withdrawal, and it runs the other way.

**Extended lattice: weaker than 4023 said.** VW-1 is genuinely weakened there, not merely incomplete. That
is the live gap and it now reads as one rather than as a footnote to a survival claim.

## And the check caught me a second time inside the attack

My draft of the finite-substrate check asserted ⟨η⟩ = 0 for a **single** configuration. It failed, at
−8.6e-5 through +4.4e-3.

The draft was wrong for a reason worth keeping: π is Θ-invariant and η is Θ-odd **as a functional**, but a
single random X is not itself Θ-symmetric, so η_{Θv}(X) ≠ −η_v(X) for that X. The symmetry statement is an
**ensemble** statement — and it reproduces 4020 exactly: single realisations span ±0.18, the ensemble mean
sits inside its own standard error. Each cage chiral, the ensemble not, arrived at from the symmetry side
instead of the measurement side.

I had again written an assertion stronger than what holds, and again the only thing that caught it was
writing the check at the strength of the claim.

## What this says about PD-008

Had 4023 gone to the next window as it stood, the withdrawal might well have been found — the convenient
sentence was flagged and a fresh reader attacks flagged sentences. **The finite-case strengthening probably
would not have been**, because it came out of attack (b), which was flagged as a *worry* rather than as a
computation, and a fresh window with no context would have had no reason to run the generator-commutation
check.

That is the case for the rule, and it is worth recording while it is concrete rather than as a principle.

Still submitted for critique, now as a completed argument with one attack landed and one repelled.
