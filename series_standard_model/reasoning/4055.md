## What was owed

4022 established RP holds for n̂-**fixing** reflections and fails for n̂-**flipping** ones — on **single-site
occupations only**. It said plainly that passing was a **necessary condition only**, since RP demands
positivity for all functions on one side. Extending it was owed.

## Why the extension is closed-form rather than sampled

For multinomial(K, p) with all indices **distinct**, E[n_{a₁}…n_{aₘ} n_{c₁}…n_{cₘ}] =
K(K−1)…(K−2m+1)·∏p **exactly**.

And **Θ maps the + side to the − side**, so {Θa} and {c} never overlap. Every index is distinct and **the
multinomial's coincidence terms never enter.**

So the Gram matrix is exactly **G = K^(2m)·u⊗v**, with u_A = ∏p_{Θa} and v_C = ∏p_c — **rank one at every
m**, and symmetric precisely when π is Θ-invariant.

That is a structural fact, not a numerical one, and it is why the answer cannot depend on m.

## Measured

At m = 2 and m = 3: n̂-fixing gives symmetric to ~1e-13 and PSD with minimum eigenvalue ~−5e-13, at
δ = 0, 0.10 and 0.35 alike. n̂-flipping gives −8.21 and −61.3 at m = 2, −5.63 and −29.7 at m = 3.

**Identical verdict to single sites, and m-independent by construction.**

**So 4022's "necessary condition only" becomes "necessary and sufficient within all product
observables."** That is a real strengthening of VW-1's sole residual.

## What is still not covered

**Non-product observables**: a repeated index (n_a²), or any function of a sum. Those bring in the
coincidence terms — the +K·p_a on the diagonal that 4022's single-site Gram already carried — and the clean
rank-one form breaks.

**That is where a counterexample to RP would have to live if one exists.** Not tested here, and named so
that "the product hierarchy is closed" is not read as "H1 is closed."
