# Reasoning — Patch 4057 (the fix works, and the founder has already forbidden it)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The cause

4056's indefiniteness was **p-independent**, so it lives in the **multinomial**, not the physics. A
multinomial carries a **fixed total K** — a **non-local constraint**, and non-local constraints are the
classic way to break reflection positivity.

So I tested the grand-canonical version: independent Poisson occupations, where E[n_i²n_j²] factorises and
the Gram is rank one.

**In the n̂-fixing sector Poisson is PSD at every δ** — minimum eigenvalues −2.2 × 10⁻¹³ and −5.8 × 10⁻¹³,
machine zero — against multinomial's −5.44 × 10⁻³ and −4.74 × 10⁻².

**And the n̂-flipping failure survives the change**: Poisson gives −9.32 and −85.2. That one is a genuine
Θ-asymmetry of the measure, not a constraint artifact.

Two failures, two different causes, cleanly separated.

## And the fix is forbidden

GR-FE-1, 19 August 2026: **"The GPs, the CPs, and DI-bits are all conserved"**, recorded in
`T2_T3_uniqueness_and_source.md` as the registered picture — **"CPs are conserved."**

**So the fixed total is physical.** The Poisson measure is not a better model of the same physics; it is a
model of **different** physics, and adopting it would relax an axiom-level conservation law to buy a
positivity condition.

## The shape of this, which I want on record

The convenient answer was **available and correct-looking.** Poisson restores RP exactly where it was
needed. I could have written *the failure is a modelling artifact, [PCD-EXT] should be replaced* — and the
computation would have backed me.

**The check that killed it was a corpus search, not a computation.** The computation supported the
convenient reading. Only a three-week-old founder ruling, in a lane I was not working, refutes it.

That is the second time today the corpus has held a fact that changed a conclusion I had already reached,
and both times it took a deliberate search to find it.

## What it leaves

RP on the physical measure fails in **both** sectors for δ > 0. The n̂-flipping failure is intrinsic; the
n̂-fixing failure is **caused by CP conservation**, which is axiom-level and cannot be traded away.

**H1 is not merely unproven. On the physical measure it is false, and no occupation model respecting CP
conservation will do better.** That is worse news than 4056's, and it is the honest version.

4056 stands. 4023 stays undermined. 4024 stays standing.

**Not claimed: that no positivity condition survives.** Only that **reflection** positivity, on the
squared-occupation class, does not. Whether a weaker condition suffices for a VW-1-style argument is that
theorem's question.
