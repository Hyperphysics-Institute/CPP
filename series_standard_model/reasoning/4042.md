# Reasoning — Patch 4042 (the measurement, and where it lands)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The fix was one line of reasoning

4041's measure sampled 4000 random rotations and floored at ~0.03 for everything, including sets that are
exactly achiral. A measure that cannot return zero is not measuring.

But an achiral set's improper symmetry **is** −R for some R ∈ I. So seed the search from the 60 group
elements and it lands on that symmetry exactly rather than near it. Then refine locally.

**All three achiral controls now score exactly 0.00000000. The chiral cluster scores 0.01173.**

## Validated, because a plausible number is not a validated one

As the second-shell seed approaches a 5-fold axis, the CCM goes to zero **linearly** — CCM/offset
converging to **0.5012** — and is **exactly zero at the axis**, where the orbit collapses from 60 points to
12 and the cluster must be achiral.

A draft of that check tested consecutive ratios for a factor of two and **failed**, because the offsets do
not all halve. The data was linear; the **check** was mis-specified. Fourth time today a check written at
the strength of the claim has caught the claim-writer rather than the claim, which is a different and more
useful thing than catching the claim.

## And the answer is a range

Across generic seeds the chirality is **tunable over 0.012 to 0.187**. The second shell's position is a
continuous parameter, so the structure admits a **continuum** of chirality strengths with nothing to choose
among them.

**Symmetry permits chirality. It does not determine the magnitude.** And a magnitude is what any physical
prediction would need.

## Where that lands

**Patch 4021** showed that a strain functional built from pairwise distances is invariant under every
isometry and so cannot prefer a handedness, and named what a mechanism would require: an
achirality-breaking term in the dynamics — an energy functional not a function of distances alone, which
nothing in the axioms supplies — or an external bias, which *is* a primitive handedness.

**4042 shows, from the geometry side, that the structure supplies a continuum of handedness strengths with
no preference among them.** The thing that would select one is exactly what 4021 said was missing.

Twenty-one patches apart, from opposite ends: 4021 from the energetics, 4042 from the geometry. **This is
not a new result. It is a convergence** — and what it says is that the remaining gap is an **axiom
question**, not a construction one. The construction exists now. What it lacks is a term in the dynamics
that distinguishes left from right, and that is the founder's to supply or decline.

## The verdict, unchanged and for the same reason as before

**FI-C-9 = V3 stands.** A substrate that permits any handedness equally does not predict a definite δ_CP —
which is the objection 4020 raised against random strain, reappearing on a structure built specifically to
admit handedness. The object changed; the objection did not.

No panel. No verdict moved.
