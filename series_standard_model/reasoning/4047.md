# Reasoning — Patch 4047 (a number that is not zero)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The measure

Graph helicity: Σ sign det[u₁, u₂, u₃] over 3-hop nearest-neighbour paths. On an achiral set every path
has an exact mirror partner of opposite sign, so it is **identically zero**. It is the lattice precursor
of spin-momentum locking — the question of whether a walker's propagation direction and its rotational
sense are correlated.

## The result

**Achiral S1+S2: exactly zero at every cutoff. Chiral S1+S2+S3: −60 at cut 1.30 over 19,740 paths, −120 at
cut 1.50 over 136,680.**

This is the **first nonzero path-chirality in the arc**. 4011, 4012, 4015 and 4041 all returned exact
zeros — and 4041's was zero *for lack of paths*, which this three-shell cluster finally supplies.

The magnitudes are **multiples of 60 = |I|**: one group orbit's worth of net handedness, which is what a
symmetric structure carrying a handed decoration should give.

**Validated exactly:** the mirror returns +60 and +120, sums exactly zero at both cutoffs.

## Why this is different from 4020

4020 strained the 600-cell and found **every realisation chiral and the ensemble not** — a chiral *object*,
not a chiral *law*. That is precisely why it supplied no mechanism and why V3 survived it.

**Here the handedness is a fixed property of the structure.** The same value every time it is computed,
flipping only under reflection. **This is the first thing in the arc to clear the bar 4020 set and 4021
restated.**

I have been careful all day about the difference between an object being chiral and a law being chiral,
and this is the first time the distinction has come out on the useful side.

## What it is not, and both limits are real

**The sign as well as the magnitude depends on the seed** — +600, −360, −120 across three choices. So the
handedness remains a **free structural parameter**, consistent with 4042. Nature picks one; the theory does
not yet say which, and it does not say why it is small either.

**And it is not a derivation of V−A.** What it shows is that the substrate has a definite handedness *that
propagating paths can see* — the structural ingredient spin-momentum locking requires, and without which no
V−A derivation could begin. Connecting it to the weak coupling is SF-2's `OPEN-FP-SF-2-CHIR`, and this
patch does not touch it.
