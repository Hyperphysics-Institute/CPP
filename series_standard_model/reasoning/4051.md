# Reasoning — Patch 4051 (the computation did not land, and saying so is the patch)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The setup was sound

A chiral 4D shell, built and verified: the left-icosian orbit of a generic 4D point, 120 points, with
**Θ = diag(1,1,1,−1) not mapping it to itself**. Against a special-position control of 120 points that
**is** Θ-invariant. The two differ in exactly the property under test.

## Functional 1 failed

⟨sign(h)·E⟩ came to **−6.290156** on the chiral shell and **−6.290201** on the **achiral control** — the
same to five digits.

The functional was not measuring the shell at all. It is dominated by the ring's own helicity, because
d = s − c carries a term proportional to −helicity.

**Without the control it would have read as a clean, large, correctly-signed splitting.**

## Functional 2 failed too

Differencing the control out leaves **+4.482936 × 10⁻⁵** for the chiral shell and **+4.444575 × 10⁻⁵** for
the **mirrored** chiral shell — **the same sign, not the opposite.**

A residual that is the shell's chirality *must* flip under mirroring. This does not. It is a P-even
difference between a generic-position and a special-position orbit.

## So the splitting is not computed

Two functionals, two failures, **both caught by controls rather than by inspection.** That is the third and
fourth time today, and it is the single most useful thing this session has learned about its own practice:
**the controls are doing more work than the measurements.**

Reporting the 4.5 × 10⁻⁵ as a result would have been available and wrong.

## A hypothesis about why

Summing a P-odd quantity over a **group orbit** yields a group invariant, and for icosahedral symmetry the
first **odd** invariant is **degree 15** — which is exactly 4044's result.

**The splitting may be suppressed for precisely the reason the parity-odd dispersion was**, and if so, no
low-order functional of this shape will ever see it.

Consistent with both failures and with 4044. **Not tested**, and offered as a hypothesis rather than a
conclusion, because I have twice today turned a plausible diagnosis into a claim and had to withdraw it.
