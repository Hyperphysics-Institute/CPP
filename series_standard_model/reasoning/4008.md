# Reasoning — Patch 4008 (I checked the lattice before building on it, and my own last patch was wrong)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## Why I did not just build the study

4007 ended by specifying a finite-size-scaling study on a "tessellated patch." The first thing that
required was knowing what the tessellation *is*, so before writing any code I went looking for how the
corpus constructs it.

I did not find a construction. I found **two incompatible readings of A2**:

- `programme_orientation.md` §177 — *every Grid Point is the centre of its own 600-cell; shell 7 of vertex
  A is shell 1 of neighbouring vertex B* — which reads as an extended lattice of many cells. **This is what
  4007 built on.**
- The EU lane, Patch 1300 — *600-cell {3,3,5} **is** the regular tessellation of S³*; *A2 → substrate = S³
  tessellation, compact, ∂ = ∅* — one closed cell, 120 vertices.

These are not the same substrate and they give opposite answers to whether a correlation length is even a
question. So the study waited and the reading got tested.

## What the geometry says

§177's clause is checkable, so I checked it.

**Shell 7 of vertex A is exactly shell 1 of another vertex, uniquely — and that vertex is A's ANTIPODE.**
It sits at r = 2, V[antipode] = −V[A], and **no neighbour of A has the property.** The set identity §177
asserts is real; the *relation* it names is wrong.

That inverts what the clause is evidence for. As written it sounds like proof of an extended lattice —
shell 7 reaches into the next cell. Corrected, it is a fact about **one closed 600-cell**: the shells wrap
around S³ and come back. Three further checks agree — the nine shells (1, 12, 20, 12, 30, 12, 20, 12, 1)
**sum to 120 and exhaust the polytope**, so "shell 7" needs no second cell to exist at all; the f-vector
gives V − E + F − C = 0 = χ(S³); and §177's *"every GP is the centre of its own 600-cell"* is
vertex-transitivity, which the polytope has.

## So 4007's finding is withdrawn

I asserted, one patch ago and in three documents, that the entire χ_η route had been computed on the wrong
object. **That rested on one clause, and the clause is the misstated one.** I did not check A2's reading
anywhere else before writing it, and the EU lane had held the opposite reading since Patch 1300.

This is the second time in five patches I have generalised from a narrow read — 4004 retracted 4003's
"idle since June" for the same reason. Twice is a pattern, and the pattern is that I treat a single
authoritative-sounding sentence as settled when the corpus is large enough that a second source almost
always exists. The remedy is not more care; it is a second grep before any claim of the form *the corpus
says X*.

What survives from 4007 is untouched: the founder's ruling, its A3′ resolution to **field-range at one
shell**, and the retirement of my "PSR suppression" coinage.

## The fork, and why I am not deciding it

If A2's substrate is the closed 120-vertex S³ tessellation, then there is **no thermodynamic limit**, a
correlation length in the critical-phenomena sense is **not well-posed**, 4007's scaling study has nothing
to scale to, and **4006's measurement is the complete answer rather than a truncated one** — d = 1
correlation real, d ≥ 2 at noise, measured on the whole substrate. **The route closes.**

If the "600-cell host vertex" language elsewhere (FI-C-RC-2; the 1200 W-bracelets as 10 × 120 host
vertices) means the substrate is extended, the study is required.

I am not deciding this, and the reason is worth stating: **the closed reading closes the route in my own
favour.** Every piece of evidence I gathered today points that way, which is exactly when a worker should
hand the question up rather than resolve it. It is also a physical picture — what the substrate *is* — and
PD-006(a) reserves those.

No panel. No verdict moved. `[PCD-EXT]`.
