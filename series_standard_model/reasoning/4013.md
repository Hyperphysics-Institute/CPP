## The argument, which is window-independent

In any cut-and-project set, a point with perpendicular coordinate y is joined to its full 600-cell shell
**iff y + g\* ∈ W for all 120 generators**, where g\* is the Galois conjugate of g. Two facts settle it:

1. **Every conjugate g\* is a unit vector.** Checked — the conjugate norms are exactly {1.0}.
2. **W must be bounded.** 4010 established the unwindowed icosian ring is dense; an unbounded window gives
   no discrete set at all.

Now take the y ∈ W that maximises the projection onto **any** direction u. Every g\* with g\*·u > 0 carries
it **out** of W. That point is coordination-deficient.

**The window's boundary always produces deficient points, for a bounded window of any shape.** No
E��-Voronoi window rescues it. The caveat is discharged rather than left standing.

Measured across five window sizes: centre point 120/120 every time; extremal point **33/120** at W = 1.0
and 1.5, **45/120** at W = 2.0, 3.0 and 5.0. The falloff against |y\*| at W = 2.0 runs 120, 120, 120, 75,
45, 45.

And the scan I had left at three points, finished at nine: **46 → {58, 88} → {58, 100} → {109, 128, 140} →
{141, 165, 173} → six distinct values.** Never uniform, and the number of distinct local environments
*grows* with the window. That growth is the quasicrystal signature — the same fact Coxeter's obstruction
gives from the other side — not a tuning failure I could have fixed with a better W.

## What this changes for the question I put upstairs

At 4010 I refused to adopt the weak reading, on the grounds that quietly weakening an axiom-level statement
to make a computation possible is the one move a delegated worker must never make. That reason still holds
and I am still not adopting it.

What has changed is that it is no longer a **choice between two readings**. The strict reading is
unavailable in this construction class, and the class is forced by the ring's density. So the founder is
now deciding whether the weak reading is what he meant, or whether the substrate is built some other way
entirely — with the mathematical fact in hand rather than with my preference. That is a better question to
hand someone than the one I handed him three patches ago.

## The thing I found and am deliberately not making a story out of

**2I is not closed under Galois conjugation.** The conjugate set maps into 2I by an **improper**
transformation, det = −1. So in the icosian cut-and-project, **perpendicular space carries the mirror of
physical space's 600-cell.**

A left/right distinction sits inside the construction. In a chirality programme that is exactly the kind of
thing one wants to seize, and I am recording it without a story attached, because:

- physical space's own copy is **achiral** — verified at 4011 over 2.1 million paths, and re-verified at
  4012 on the founder's actual propagation rule;
- a mirror relation **between two spaces** is not a handedness **in** either of them.

It may be worth something to someone who knows the E₈ side better than I do. It is not evidence for
anything yet, and this session has already cost three retractions to the habit of deciding what a
suggestive fact means before checking.
