# Reasoning — Patch 4006 (the SSV coupling, and why the answer is a picture)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## Resolving the mechanism before modelling it

4005 left one question: does the SSV coupling between CPs generate a correlation length? D-7 says resolve
every symbol against the lane that wrote it, so before writing any dynamics I went to `master_glossary.md`:

- A1′: *every CP executes one Displace step per its GP's computed V_i.*
- PSR is *the effective displacement a CP can achieve per Absolute Moment*, and it **shrinks as SSV_abs
  rises**; at lattice saturation PSR → 0.
- V_i is *the vector sum of **all** SSV contributions at a point.*

So the coupling is not something I had to invent: occupancy raises local SSV, SSV shrinks PSR, and a
shrunken PSR means a suppressed hop. That is a rate suppressed by local density.

## The thing I did not expect

Reading those three entries together, **the corpus answers the range question twice, differently**. The
rate is set at the CP's *own* GP — that is zero-range. The V_i *at* that GP is sourced by every CP
in the field — that is not. Both are in the glossary, neither is wrong, and they are not the same model.

That is why this patch tests two, and it is why the patch does not end with an answer.

## Zero-range: closed, and by a theorem rather than a sweep

A hop rate depending only on the departure site's own occupation makes this a **zero-range process**, and
a ZRP's stationary measure is **exactly product** — for any rate function, at any coupling strength. Not
off-critical-as-it-happens; product, exactly, as a structural fact.

I ran it anyway at k = 0, 0.3, 0.6, 0.9 rather than cite it, because a theorem I have invoked and not
checked against my own implementation is a place for a bug to live. All d ≥ 1 correlations sit at the
on-site floor. **In this regime χ_η is finite at any SSV coupling: no tuning, no critical point, nothing
left to compute.** That is a real closure, and it is the strongest thing in the patch.

## What the sweep showed that the correlation test would have hidden

The on-site variance rises **2.90 → 10.54** across that sweep. The correlation checks all pass, and if I
had reported only those, this would have gone unmentioned.

It is **ZRP condensation** — decreasing rates concentrate occupancy — and it is a change in the
single-site *marginal*, structurally the same shape as 4005's O(δ) skew. It is not a correlation length
and it does not disturb the closure. But a 3.6× change in a distribution is not nothing, and "my test
passed" is not a reason to omit what the test does not look at. Reported, and filed as uncosted.

## Field-range: not closed, and the protection is genuinely gone

Once the suppression is sourced by *neighbour* occupancy the ZRP theorem does not apply, and correlation
appears: **d = 1 goes from −0.034 ± 0.002 at k = 0 to +0.133 ± 0.003 at k = 0.30.** Many sem, sign-flipped,
unambiguous. **So 4005's non-interacting condition cannot be waved away** — in this regime the interaction
does something.

What I will not claim is the *shape*. |d1| runs 0.034, 0.133, 0.589, 0.355 across the sweep — non-monotone,
with the k = 0.6 point carrying a large spread. Three seeds is enough to establish that correlation exists
and not enough to characterise how it grows. Single-seed numbers from my first run told a tidier story and
I discarded them; the tidier story was the one that would have been quoted.

## The limit this lattice cannot pass, said plainly

**120 sites, diameter 5.** A correlation length beyond about 2 does not fit. So "d ≥ 2 stays at noise" means
"no correlation length that fits in a diameter-5 graph" — it is not evidence against a critical point, and
it cannot be cited as such later. Establishing or excluding one needs finite-size scaling on nested
lattices, which this is not and which I did not do.

## Why this ends with a question rather than a result

The deciding variable is the coupling's range, and range is not something I can compute my way into — it is
a statement about what a CP perceives when its GP computes V_i. That is a physical picture, and
PD-006(a) reserves exactly those for the founder. PD-007 gave me the *working extension*; it did not give
me the substrate's own answer, and manufacturing one here by picking whichever branch closes the route
would be the failure mode the whole delegation is built to prevent.

Zero-range closes the route outright. Field-range requires the scaling study. Both are filed. The question
is asked, once, plainly.

No panel. No verdict moved. `[PCD-EXT]`.
