## The attack on 4057, and where it lands

4057's argument has three steps. (1) 4056's fixing-sector violation is p-independent, so it lives in the
multinomial's fixed total. (2) Independent Poisson removes it. (3) Poisson is inadmissible because GR-FE-1
rules CPs conserved, so the fixed total is physical.

Steps (1) and (2) are right and are sharpened here. Step (3) is a D-7 failure: it resolved *conserved* as
*fixed on this window*. The lane that wrote the conservation law states it as a continuity equation with a
flux, and scopes constancy of the census to an isolated system with J = 0 on the boundary. A 600-cell is not
an isolated system: A2's substrate is tessellated, and 4007 already established that one 600-cell is a first
shell, not the substrate.

## What the computation says

- **4056's "δ = 0 is untouched" is wrong.** Its class had no constant term. With the unital algebra, the
  single observable F = n_a/(K p_a) − 1 gives ⟨Θ(F)F⟩ = −1/K exactly, at every p and every δ, including 0.
  So the violation is not a NESS effect; it is nothing but the fixed total.
- **The entire violation is one K-only matrix.** In the rescaled basis every multinomial entry is
  K^(r+s falling)/K^(r+s) and p drops out. λ_min scales as −2/K and vanishes; at K = ∞ the matrix is all-ones,
  rank one, PSD, and is exactly the independent-Poisson Gram. **4057's grand-canonical measure is not a rival
  physics; it is the limit of the canonical one.**
- **Embedding.** A window of mean occupancy 200 inside a closed system of total K_tot carries the same matrix
  with K = K_tot. An isolated 600-cell is the special case K_tot = window count.
- **The flipping-sector failure is untouched** and intrinsic (Poisson −5.26), as 4022 and 4057 found.

## The quantifier, stated because this is the convenient branch

For any finite closed total, RP fails by exactly −1/K_tot on the explicit F. **H1 is not true on any finite
window and that is not claimed.** What is restored is H1 as a property of the limit state — which is where
VW-1 itself places reflection positivity: CONT-1 is "the natural home" for RP, "which a finite polytope does
not literally possess". The refutation proved something VW-1 had already disclaimed.
