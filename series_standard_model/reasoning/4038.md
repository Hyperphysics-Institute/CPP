# Reasoning — Patch 4038 (the alternative is a subgroup, not a lattice)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## A cost 4036 missed

Before answering, one correction to my own pricing. **FCC/HCP cannot replace the 600-cell for SR.**
Symmetrising a generic rank-4 tensor over each point group: **cubic gives 3 independent elastic constants,
icosahedral gives 2.** Isotropy has two. So a cubic substrate is **anisotropic at rank 4**, and SR's R5 —
whose entire result is isotropy through rank 4 with an l = 6 floor — loses its W2 world-call.

Horn (C) costs the cages *and* relativity. 4036 priced only the cages.

## The answer

Not a different lattice type. **The same icosahedral symmetry with the improper operations dropped:**
point group **I (532, order 60)** instead of **I_h (order 120)**.

**I and I_h give the same elastic count — 2 and 2.** Elasticity is a rank-4 **even** tensor property, so
improper operations contribute nothing to it; the same is true of the l = 6 anisotropy floor. **SR loses
exactly nothing.**

**And I has no improper operations — which is precisely what the chirality cancellation needs.**

That last point is the one I want kept. Patches 4011, 4012, 4015, 4020 and 4024 look like five independent
confirmations of V3, and I have been describing them that way. **They share a single load-bearing
assumption:** an improper symmetry that fixes n̂, so every path has an exact mirror partner of equal weight
and opposite sign. **Remove that operation and all five stop applying at once.** Five confirmations resting
on one assumption is a different thing from five independent confirmations, and it took the founder's
question to make me look at what they had in common.

And SM keeps everything it uses: 4037 established its load-bearing structures are local — a three-vertex
triangle, a count of twelve, a first-shell distance ratio — and **all are rotation-invariant.**

## What I could not do

Build one. Three attempts, recorded because the failures say where the difficulty is:

- **Close-packed polytypes** — ABC, AB, ABAC, ABCACB, ABCB, ABCBAC, ABCACBACB. Every one gives **z = 12
  exactly** and a 3D chirality sum of **exactly 0**. Swapping inside the z = 12 close-packing family does
  not buy chirality.
- **A golden-angle screw** (θ = 2π/φ²) — chiral, but **z falls to 6–10** at every spacing tried. Chirality
  bought with the coordination.
- **The snub 24-cell** — I took the 96 non-24-cell vertices of the 600-cell expecting the chiral object,
  and got **z = 9 with 96 improper signed-permutation symmetries** still present. Either that is not the
  snub 24-cell or my identification of the inscribed 24-cell was wrong. Reported as an attempt.

## So the deliverable is a target

A point set in flat space with icosahedral **rotation** symmetry, **no** improper operations, and a
nearest-neighbour shell of **exactly twelve**.

SR keeps isotropy and its floor. SM keeps every local structure. The cancellation stops being forced.

**And such things are physically real:** chiral icosahedral quasicrystals occur in nature. That is a known
structure class with a literature, which is a much better place to be than the unspecified family 4033
found.

## Stated plainly

**FI-C-9 = V3 still stands.** Nothing here generates chirality. It removes the obstruction that forbade it,
which is a different and smaller claim, and the difference matters after a day in which the tempting
version has been wrong six times.
