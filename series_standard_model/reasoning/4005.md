# Reasoning — Patch 4005 (the recompute, run; and the charge I level at my own result)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## What was parked, and why it was the right thing to run

0905 parked one item: 0813's finite-positive χ_η was computed in a **product (ZRP-template) base that was
assumed**, and 0814 had found that the real Mechanism-A NESS *departs from that base, skewed at O(δ)*.
0904 §3 put it plainly — *the decisive thing was assumed, not shown.* 0905 then added the caution that
matters most: **"departs from product" is not "critical."** The recomputed χ could be finite-but-non-product,
or could reveal correlations. Unknown until run.

Three months of nobody running it, and 4004 made it well-posed for the first time by supplying the
occupation-on-a-subvolume definition. So run it.

## How I ran it

Like-for-like, deliberately. 0813's η is kept exactly — the local ℤ₂ pseudoscalar `sign det` of the four
highest-n̂-projection neighbour directions — and 0813's estimator is kept exactly. **The only thing swapped
is the source of the vertex perturbations**: i.i.d. Gaussian → the actual Mechanism-A occupation field.
Any difference in the answer is then the measure and nothing else.

I also ran 0813's own product base as a control in the same script, because a recompute that cannot
reproduce the number it is checking is not a recompute. Control: **χ_η = +0.839**, inside 0813's reported
0.87–1.01. Estimator calibrated.

**And 0814's departure is now identified rather than feared.** At δ = 0.10 the tilt produces a measured
marginal skew of **+0.2427** and **no distance-dependent correlation at all**. It is a *single-site skew*.
0905's distinction was the right one and this is which side of it we are on.

## The charge I level at my own result

0904 §3 said 0813's finiteness followed *near-tautologically* from its assumed product base. **That charge
partly applies here and I would rather write it myself than have the next lane write it.** Independent
walkers give product occupation statistics *by construction*. If I had set the thing up and found
correlations, that would have been a surprise; finding none is largely what the setup guarantees.

So what is actually new, stated narrowly:

1. **The δ-tilt is the real Mechanism-A content, and it enters.** It could have produced correlation; it
   produces skew. 0905 explicitly recorded this as unknown, and it is now measured.
2. **The η step is measured, not inherited.** 0813 *assumed* the η-correlator inherits the occupation
   base's structure (its own hedge 2). Here η is evaluated on the occupation field and its correlator
   comes out at noise level — the inheritance is checked rather than asserted.

That is a real but bounded advance, and calling it more would be the 0813 error in a new costume.

## The condition that survives, and it is not small

**CPs are not non-interacting.** They couple through SSV; that is the substrate's whole mechanism. This
script models independent walkers and does not touch the coupling. So the honest statement of the residual
is a single sharp question — **does the SSV coupling between CPs generate a correlation length?** — which
is a far better-posed thing than §14.17's *derive the whole effective action*, and is the next computation.

Also carried, unchanged and not by me: 0904 condition (1), the real η (the H₄/H₄⁺ coset field) must be
confirmed **local** — 0813's is a defensible proxy and no more; and condition (3), Mechanism A
(OPEN-FP-F1-2) sits under the entire route.
