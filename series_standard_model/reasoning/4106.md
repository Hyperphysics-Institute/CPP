## What I expected vs what came out

I opened this expecting the 4101 framing: F2 needs the DP sea's arc directions to be
isotropic, mirroring the F3 cancellation. **Sea isotropy turns out to be irrelevant.** The
polarity clause does all the work, per-DP and pointwise.

Three existing results compose:
- a DP is a bound pair of OPPOSITE-polarity CPs (glossary)
- the response to b must be LINEAR (B3, 4076 — answering the founder's own question)
- that linear coefficient must be C-ODD, i.e. polarity, FORCED by CPT (F6, 4085)

⇒ one sea DP's net response R = (+q)b₊ + (−q)b₋ = q(b₊ − b₋). Nothing about the distribution
of directions across the sea enters. The 4101 entry is superseded, not refined.

## The result

F2 holds iff b₊ = b₋. With v the arc cohort direction (4097 ruling), that is a **parity match**
between two independent structural facts: the pair's relative spin orientation, and the pair's
relative arc-direction orientation. Both flip or neither ⇒ sign(ω·v) is even under the
simultaneous negation ⇒ exact cancellation (50,000 random (ω,v): max |R| = 0, pointwise, not
statistical). Exactly one flips ⇒ b₊ = −b₋ and the polarity clause **doubles** rather than
cancels.

## Why I am filing this as a falsifier rather than a conditional

Mismatch gives R/q = 2 for every DP in the vacuum. The intrinsic EM parity-violation bound is
~1e−10 (APV is observed but accounted for by Z-exchange to ~0.3% of SM). Overshoot ~2e10.

And no escape is available *from inside χ₄*: linearity is required by B3 and the polarity
clause is CPT-forced by F6. Softening either would remove a result the axiom already leans on.
So this is not a filter χ₄ can pass conditionally.

## The uncomfortable part, stated plainly

The naive reading of each fact points to a **fatal** cell. A vacuum "atom" in its ground state
suggests spin-paired, i.e. **antiparallel** spins; arcs laid down during acceleration suggest a
**common** drift direction. Antiparallel + common is one of the two refuting combinations.

χ₄ therefore needs the corpus to overturn the naive reading on at least one of the two. I have
not assumed it does. An unscoped search finds the corpus silent on both — which is why this is
a founder question rather than something I could settle.

## Not claimed

No verdict moved. χ₄ is **not** refuted here; it is made *refutable* by a determinate,
