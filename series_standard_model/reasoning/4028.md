# Reasoning — Patch 4028 (asking what else the defect touched)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The question 4027 left

4027 found the error-bar defect and fixed the two patches that had it. The obvious next question — how far
back does it reach — is the kind that gets left for a successor who would then have to reconstruct today's
context in order to ask it. So I asked it here.

The exposed candidate was **4006**. Everything from 4007 onward sits downstream of it: the founder's range
question, the extended-lattice ruling, the whole 4009–4027 arc. And it quoted a sem over **three seeds**.

## 4006 survives

Sixteen seeds instead of three. 4006 reported −0.034 → +0.133; the audit gives **−0.0322 ± 0.0011 →
+0.1323 ± 0.0037**. Free case 29.9 sem, k = 0.30 field case 35.4 sem, and the sign flip between them a
**42-sem** effect.

The number it reported is the number that is there.

## The part worth more than the re-run

**The diagnosis is confirmed by a case where it correctly predicts survival.**

4006 averaged over **independent simulation runs** and took the sem over those. 4026 averaged over **pairs
within a distance band** and took the sem over those. The first is the right structure with too few samples
— fixable by running more, which is what happened. The second is the **wrong structure**, and no amount of
sampling fixes it.

A rule that only ever condemns is not a rule. This one distinguishes, and it distinguishes correctly on a
case it was not derived from.

## Scope, checked rather than assumed

Every 40xx script, scanned: only **4025 and 4026** used sem-over-pairs. 4024 and 4006 use the correct
structure. Nothing else in the arc needs revisiting on this account.

I checked rather than asserted because the assertion "only those two" is exactly the shape of claim that
has cost this session five retractions.

## 4006's own caution, vindicated

4006 refused to assert the *shape* of the correlation's growth on three seeds, reporting |d1| = 0.034,
0.133, 0.589, 0.355 and saying the trend was real but its form unresolved. At sixteen seeds k = 0.60 still
gives **+0.612 ± 0.092** — a large spread. **That refusal was correct**, and it is worth noting that the
patch which under-sampled its error bars still knew not to over-read its own curve.

## An operational finding that is not physics

`origin/main` was at **4026** at the start of this window, although 4027 had been reported applied. The
commit was not on origin. I recovered it from the local reflog — a `git reset --hard origin/main` at bootup
had discarded it, which is the standard first move and would have destroyed it outright had the reflog been
pruned.

**4028 will not apply to an origin that lacks 4027.** Flagged in the frontier header as well as here,
because a patch that silently fails to apply is the one failure mode this workflow has no gate for.

No panel. No verdict moved.
