## F6 — CPT, and it closes a question I had left open

The helicity bit b = sign(ω·v) is **P-odd** (ω axial, v polar), **C-even** (charge conjugation does not act on
space), **T-even** (ω and v both flip). Therefore b is **CPT-odd**, and a bare term linear in b with a constant
coefficient is **forbidden**. It is allowed only if the coefficient is itself C-odd — the sign must **flip with
polarity**.

**That is the clause I drafted at 4072 and explicitly flagged as "open whether necessary." CPT makes it
mandatory.** I had presented it as an attractive option because it reproduced the SM's C/CP pattern; it is in fact
forced, and the pattern it reproduces is a consequence rather than a coincidence. This is the cleanest step the
maturation has produced, and it is the one place where the arc *derived* something instead of encoding it.

## F5 — fails, and the number is not close

χ₄ conserves CP exactly, so the small observed CP violation needs a separate source. The corpus's T-odd sign(δ)
is a legitimate candidate and — unlike K4 (4082) — **not circular**, since δ comes from TARROW-1/2 and does not
presuppose baryogenesis. But: δ³ = 1.32×10⁻² is ~400× larger than the Jarlskog invariant J = 3.08×10⁻⁵, and
δ⁹ = 2.28×10⁻⁶ is ~13× smaller. **No power of δ reproduces J.**

I could have reported "a source exists in the corpus" and moved on — that sentence is true and it is what the
filter nominally asks. It would have been misleading. **F5 is an open quantitative problem.**

## F3 — still a clause, with one candidate way out

The strong sector must stay P-even, which requires its response to be **even** in b. Nothing explains why **only**
the weak response is linear; as stated, F2 and F3 are both stipulations, and stipulating two of nine filters is a
weak position.

**The candidate that would fix both, untested:** the W bracelet **captures** a CP at its centroid (SF-2 §5) and so
reads that CP's own register, whereas EM and the strong force act through DP-sea **polarisation and exchange**,
reading displacement magnitudes rather than the captured particle's bit. If that distinction holds, **linearity in
b follows from capture**, and F2 and F3 become consequences instead of clauses. This is now the most valuable open
item in the maturation document.
