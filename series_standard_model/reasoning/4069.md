## The finding

**The right-hand rule is not a handedness of the world. It is the bookkeeping of a curl.**

B is an **axial** vector *precisely because* it is the curl of a **polar** displacement. Under a mirror, a polar
field flips and an axial field does not, and the pair transforms consistently: verified numerically, the B
computed from mirrored sources equals the axial transport of the original B, to 10⁻¹². **The mirror image of a
moving charge with its E and B is another perfectly valid moving charge with its E and B.** Classical EM — and
SF-6's substrate mechanism for it — is exactly parity-symmetric.

**The decisive test is the pseudoscalar one.** A P-odd source requires a nonzero P-odd invariant. From
(v, E, B) the P-odd scalars are v·B and E·B. For a moving charge, SF-6's own mechanism gives B = v × E, which is
orthogonal to **both** v and E by construction, so **both pseudoscalars vanish identically** — confirmed over
20,000 random (v, x) at float noise (3×10⁻¹²). The P-even scalar v·(E×B) is nonzero (9×10⁻²), so the
configuration is not trivial; it simply carries no parity violation.

**Control (the instrument is not blind):** a hand-built configuration with E ∥ B gives E·B = 1.000. The test
detects P-oddness when present. The null is a result.

## Why this answers the founder's framing, and corrects it

The universe *is* strongly chiral — but **not in electromagnetism**. The right-handed rule is a sign convention
consistently applied: flip every convention and physics is unchanged. Parity violation is confined, in the
Standard Model, to the **weak** sector, and it is maximal there. That is exactly why the corpus's chirality arc
kept landing on the W bracelet (SF-2) rather than on EM: **V−A is where parity is actually broken.**

So the DP/magnetism arc cannot supply the missing P-odd source — not because the mechanism is incomplete, but
because the phenomenon it describes is parity-symmetric and would be wrong if it were not. Any proposed
substrate mechanism that made EM P-odd would **contradict experiment**, and would have to be rejected on that
ground.
