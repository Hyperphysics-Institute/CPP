# Reasoning — Patch 4058 (what falls with H1, and what does not)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The worry, checked rather than assumed

After refuting a theorem's sole residual, the reflex is to assume everything downstream falls. The verdict
most exposed is **V1 EXCLUDED / V3 CONFIRMED**.

**CAPACITY-1's conditionality, narrowed at 0960, names exactly three things:** MA.1's reversal-odd first
harmonic (derived and unique up to scale at 0949); per-edge independence; and pointwise non-degeneracy of
the dynamical η. **None of them is reflection positivity, and VW-1 and H1 appear nowhere in it.**

**So 4057 does not reopen V1.** H1 was **VW-1's** residual, and VW-1 and CAPACITY-1 are different theorems
reaching a compatible conclusion by different routes. Refuting H1 removes VW-1's support and leaves
CAPACITY-1 untouched.

And 4005's direct measurement is a **third leg** — χ_η finite with d ≥ 1 correlations at ~10⁻³, independent
of both theorems.

**V1 stays excluded. V3 stays confirmed.** Worth establishing explicitly, because the alternative was to
assume it and be wrong in either direction.

## And then the thing I did not expect

CAPACITY-1's third condition is **pointwise non-degeneracy of the dynamical η** — and that is precisely
what 4023 found defective.

η is *sign det of the four highest-n̂-projection neighbours*, and its 4th and 5th projections **tie at
120/120 vertices on the unperturbed lattice**. The selection is arbitrary; η is pointwise degenerate there.

**The condition holds where CAPACITY-1 uses it.** Perturbation breaks the ties at every amplitude tested,
and 0813 and 4005 both evaluate η on perturbed configurations. **No result is invalidated** — which is what
4023 said.

**But the statement of piece 1 carries no "on perturbed configurations" qualifier.**

## Why that matters more than 4023 knew

4023 reported the defect as a tidy-up for the CHIR lane's text. It is not only that: **the assumption is
now known to be false in the unperturbed limit, and a 3/3 review-closed theorem names it as a standing
condition without the qualifier that makes it true.**

Nothing is wrong. But a reader of CAPACITY-1 today would take piece 1 at face value and be taking a false
statement as an assumption — and would have no way to know from the theorem that the limit matters.

**Filed for the CHIR lane as a text fix, not a physics one.** Not this lane's to edit; CAPACITY-1 is
review-closed.

No panel. No verdict moved. V1 excluded and V3 confirmed both stand.
