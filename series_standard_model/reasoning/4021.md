## The argument, which is one line

A strain functional built from **pairwise distances** is invariant under every isometry — proper **or
improper**. E(X) = E(RX), checked at four random configurations to 1e-12.

So the relaxation landscape is **mirror-symmetric by construction**, and every minimum has a mirror partner
of exactly equal energy. **No gradient flow on an achiral functional can prefer one.**

I tested it rather than resting on the argument: mirror-image starting conditions, relaxed under the same
flow, give end states of identical energy and **exactly opposite handedness** — ±8.0, ±17.0, ±3.0 across
three seeds. Not approximately opposite. Exactly.

## And that is VW-1's question

If the relaxation cannot bias the sign, then any handedness it produces is **spontaneous**: two exactly
degenerate minima, one picked per domain. That is a ℤ₂ spontaneous symmetry breaking on the substrate — and
**THEO-CHIR-VW-1 is about exactly that**, with its sole residual H1 already sitting on this queue.

So the "new open problem" I registered one patch ago is not new. **Registering it as new would have split
one problem into two**, which is the failure D-2 and D-9 are both about arriving from the other side — not
losing an item but duplicating one, so that two windows work the same question under two names and neither
finds the other's progress.

It also explains 4020's measurement rather than merely coexisting with it: degenerate minima picked at
random per domain give precisely *each cage chiral, the ensemble not*.

## What a mechanism would actually need

Two options, and both lead back:

**(i) An achirality-breaking term in the substrate dynamics** — an energy functional that is not a function
of distances alone. **Nothing in the axioms supplies one**; A1′ and A3′ are distance-and-census based. This
is an axiom change, and the founder's.

**(ii) An external bias** selecting between the degenerate minima — which *is* a primitive handedness.
FI-C-9 = V3 restated, not derived.

That is the fifth independent route to the same place today, and the fifth is worth less than the first —
the value now is in having closed off a wrong turn cheaply, not in another confirmation.

## What I am not claiming

Not that no relaxation mechanism exists. Only that a **distance-based** strain functional cannot supply
one, and that anything which could is either an axiom change or VW-1's H1.

**The disclination-network question 4020 raised is untouched at the network level.** This patch treats the
energetics; the topology of a defect network is a different object and I have not looked at it. Whoever
does should know that the energetic route is closed before spending time on it, which is the point of
saying so here.
