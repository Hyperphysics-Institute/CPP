# Reasoning — Patch 4048 (the negative, and what it points at)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## What V−A actually demands

Maximal parity violation: the right-handed coupling is **exactly zero**, not merely smaller. A structural
helicity bias gives a **fraction** of paths curling one way. So the question is how large that fraction can
get.

## 2.069%

Six seeds × two outer-shell radii × two cutoffs — twenty-four configurations. **Largest fractional helicity
2.069%.** V−A requires 100%. A factor of about fifty short, and it does not trend toward 1 in any direction
tried.

## And the reason is structural

The inner two shells contribute **exactly zero** — +0 over 10,320 paths — because **4041 established that
the icosahedron is achiral and that z = 12 icosahedral forces it to be.** Every short path cancels exactly.

So the helicity can live only in the longer paths reaching the outer shell, and those are a small minority.
**The 2% is not a failure of search. It is the ratio of chiral paths to total paths in a structure whose
core is forced achiral** — and the core is forced achiral by the same requirement (z = 12 icosahedral) that
the mass sector needs.

**A structural helicity bias of this kind cannot deliver V−A.** That closes the most direct route from the
chiral lattice to the weak coupling.

## But it may be the right division of labour

In the Standard Model, **V−A's maximality does not come from a statistical bias either.** It comes from the
gauge structure: SU(2)_L acts on left doublets and not on right singlets, **by construction**. Nothing
counts paths.

So the substrate would supply the **sign** — which handedness is preferred — and the W bracelet would
supply the **maximality**.

That matches SF-2's own framing. Its open problem is *chirality emergence in the W bracelet structure*, not
*chirality from the substrate alone*. I had been reading it as the latter.

And 4047 showed the chiral lattice is well-suited to supplying a sign: a fixed property of the structure,
the same value every time, flipping only under reflection. **A sign is exactly what that is** — and a sign
is all a bias of 2% can honestly be asked to provide.

## What is and is not established

**Not claimed: that the division works.** It is a hypothesis about where the labour divides. SF-2 owns the
bracelet side and nothing here touches it.

**Established, and it is the harder half:** the substrate side **cannot** carry the maximality. So if the
division fails — if the bracelet cannot supply maximality from a substrate sign — the whole route fails,
and that is now a definite dependency rather than an open hope.
