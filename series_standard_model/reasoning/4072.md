## Two corrections

**1. "n̂ versus −n̂" is not a handedness in four dimensions.** det(−I₄) = +1; the 600-cell is centrally
symmetric, so −I₄ ∈ H4⁺ and maps n̂ to −n̂ by a *proper* rotation. The two configurations are congruent, not
mirror images. I imported the 3D intuition (where inversion *is* parity) and so, I think, did the CAP-1 scope's
R1 reading. As worded — "which of {n̂, −n̂} is physical" — FI-C-9 names no chirality. **No verdict moved:**
CAPACITY-1's V3 is a claim about *whether the substrate spontaneously breaks*, and it is unaffected; what changes
is that the primitive V3 points to needs a different reading to be P-odd at all.

**2. 4071's four-directions threshold was scoped too narrowly in its conclusion.** The counting theorem is right
for *vector* attributes. But a single 4D **rotation** is a bivector, and B∧B is a pseudoscalar built from one
object. 4071 concluded the minimal axiom was "a fourth direction with a primitive sign"; that missed the more
natural route. Recorded here rather than by editing 4071's fragment, which stays verbatim.

## The finding that makes the axiom look native

A **simple** rotation (one plane) has B∧B ≡ 0 — no handedness, verified at 1.8×10⁻¹⁵. A **double** rotation
(two orthogonal planes) has B∧B ≠ 0, with isoclinic self-dual and anti-self-dual giving ±2 exactly. It is
invariant under proper rotations and flips under reflections.

The corpus describes the ZBW in a single "distinguished plane" (FI-QMRG-1). **A single-plane ZBW is exactly the
case with no handedness.** So the axiom's natural content is a specification of the ZBW that already exists:
*a second plane of circulation, with its relative sense tied to polarity.* No new attribute is added; handedness
is read off a rotation CPP already has. That is the difference between an axiom and a patch.

## The C/CP pattern — stated with its limits

Tying duality to polarity gives, as a truth table: P violated, C violated, CP conserved. That is the weak
sector's observed pattern. **This is a consistency property of the rule's form, not a derivation.** It shows the
rule is the *right shape*; it does not show V−A's maximality, and it predicts CP *exactly* conserved, whereas
nature has a small CP violation (the CKM phase) — so a second, small source is needed. The T-arrow sign(δ) is a
natural candidate via CPT, and I am registering that as untested, not claimed.
