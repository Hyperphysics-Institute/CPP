# Reasoning — Patch 4012 (the third time, and why writing the rule down did not work)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The retraction

At 4011 I wrote that the *"10% of the radius of the PSR"* shell *appears nowhere in the corpus*. He said it
was a topic we had confirmed several times and pointed me at the GR derivation. He is right, and it is not
close.

The quantity is the **PSR shell radial thickness**, **σ_r/⟨r⟩ ≈ 0.096 — finding F-E2-3**. It was recomputed
at **D-SUBPSR-FIELD pass 3** under **R-OUTWARD-FANOUT** (Patch 3135, 14 August) as **0.093–0.076 over
N = 6–22 hops**, with **D-ARC-GAMMA** minted as the retention geometry on top of it, and several
`founders_voice/` files carrying it. **Derived, not an estimate. Registered, not missing.**

My grep covered four paths and searched one string — `10% of`. It missed `F-E2-3`, `D-ARC-GAMMA`,
`R-OUTWARD-FANOUT`, "shell thickness", and the entire `founders_voice/` directory.

## Why this one stings more than the other two

Because at 4008, having already made this error twice, I wrote the fix in my own reasoning fragment:

> *The remedy is not more care; it is a second grep before any claim of the form "the corpus says X".*

Three patches later I made the error again. So the interesting finding here is not the retraction — it is
that **writing the rule down did nothing.** I had diagnosed it correctly and prescribed correctly and it
did not change the behaviour, because a reasoning fragment is read by nobody at the moment of the mistake,
including me.

The corpus already knew this. D-9 and `code/deferral_gate.py` exist because the same thing happened with
deferrals: the rule was stated repeatedly, lost repeatedly, and only held once it became a script that
fails. So: `code/absence_gate.py`. It scans a patch's message and added lines for absence vocabulary and
fails unless there is evidence of an unscoped search. **It fails on Patch 4011.**

The gate also says something a gate can say and a note cannot: an unscoped grep for the *wrong string* is
still a scoped grep. 4011 would have passed a naive "did you run it repo-wide" check.

## The second correction, which I did not expect

Chasing the 10% shell turned up `founder_clarification_outward_fanout_2026-08-14.md`, in which he writes:

> *I think this will produce a PSR shell thickness (10% before), as you calculated earlier, **because the
> total DI-bit hop count varies when it's specified as the path from GP_origin to GP_PSR**.*

That is 4011's claim (A), in his words, a month earlier — and already computed. So when I reported "the
path-length disparity is real and begins at hop 2" as a finding, it was a re-measurement of something
registered in August. **The measurement is fine; the framing overstated it.**

## And the part that got stronger

4011 summed over all non-backtracking paths. That was a set of my own choosing, and the corpus registers a
different one: **R-OUTWARD-FANOUT** — at every hop the DI-bits split among neighbours with strictly
positive outward radial component, anti-radial excluded. A smaller, directed set. The cancellation could
have been an artifact of summing over a symmetric superset.

It is not. On the fan-out set: **zero to machine precision at K = 3, 4, 5, at δ = 0 and 0.35.** And I
checked the step that matters rather than asserting it — `R = diag(1,1,1,−1)` **preserves the
outward-radial test `x·d` itself**, so the restriction is mirror-invariant and the pairing survives it.

So the answer to his question now holds on the propagation rule the corpus actually registers, not on one I
invented. That is the version worth having.

## A flag I am raising but not acting on

The corpus's own 10%-band derivation, `3133_subpsr_cascade.py`, builds an **FCC lattice** — integer
triples, even coordinate sum, 12 neighbours. z = 12 matches the icosahedral coordination, but FCC is **3D
and periodic** where the substrate is 4D and aperiodic.

I am flagging, not condemning. For a shell-*thickness* number a z = 12 proxy may be entirely adequate, and
whether it is belongs to the lane that built it. It does **not** unblock 4010 — FCC is not the lateral
600-cell lattice. (It is also achiral, so it could not have produced chirality either.)

## Free consistency check

The single 600-cell's outward-fanout band is σ_r/⟨r⟩ = 0.031 at K = 3, 0.037 at K = 4, **0.000 at K = 5**,
and there are **no 6-hop fanout paths at all** — diameter 5. So the corpus's N = 6–22 hop regime cannot
live on one cell. That is independent agreement with his 4009 extended-lattice ruling, arrived at from a
direction that had nothing to do with it.

No panel. No verdict moved.
