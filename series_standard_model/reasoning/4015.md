# Reasoning — Patch 4015 (checking whether my own best result was truncated)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## Why I went back to a result that was passing

4009 retired 4006's *"d ≥ 2 stays at noise"* as a measurement on a graph with no long distances in it. The
lattice was too small for the question, and I had said so myself at 4006 and then said it more sharply at
4009.

4011 was measured on **the same single 600-cell**. And 4011 is now the result this lane leans on hardest —
the independent confirmation of V3, the thing I told the founder twice was worth having. If it had the same
defect, I would be leaning on something already known to be untrustworthy, and I would have been the one to
know it.

So before doing anything else, check it. Not because anything flagged it — because the failure mode was
demonstrated two patches earlier on a neighbouring result.

## It survives, and the reason is worth more than the result

On a 4321-point extended cut-and-project patch: **96 improper signed-permutation symmetries at every window
tested** — W = 1.2, 1.4, 1.5 — including `R = diag(1,1,1,−1)`, the exact map used at 4011. And directly:
the 3-hop sum over **153,576 paths** is **0.0000** at δ = 0, and 5.8 × 10⁻¹⁴ at δ = 0.35.

**Why one result extended and the other did not:**

- A **correlation length** is a property of the whole structure. A diameter-5 graph cannot express one, so
  4006 was measuring something other than what it claimed. Truncated, correctly retired.
- A **mirror pairing** is a property of the **generators**. Every path — however long, on whatever lattice
  — is built from steps drawn from the same 120 vectors, and those 120 admit an improper symmetry fixing
  n̂. Extending the lattice adds more paths. It does not add a step vector with no mirror partner.

That is why the achirality holds at *every* window rather than at a tuned one: it is inherited, not
accidental. And it is a usable rule for the next window — **ask whether a result depends on the whole
structure or on the generators before trusting it past the lattice it was measured on.**

## What this changes about the programme's shape

The susceptibility route — 4005 through 4009 — is blocked on the founder's outstanding reading and on a
lateral construction nobody has. It may stay blocked for a while.

**FI-C-9 = V3 no longer depends on it.** The path route reaches the same conclusion and depends on neither
the reading nor the construction. Two independent routes to chirality-is-primitive, and one of them is not
waiting on anything.

I want to be careful not to oversell that. It does not unblock the susceptibility work, it does not close
OPEN-SM-4, and it does not make the lateral lattice less needed. What it does is mean the programme's
verdict on FI-C-9 is not hostage to a question the founder has not answered yet.

## Found in passing, not sought

Edge coordination on the extended patch runs **{12, 13, 14, 18, 19, 26}** — only **120 of 4321** points
have z = 12. **SF-4 requires z = 12.**

So this construction class fails on coordination as well as on shell count, by an argument with no overlap
with 4013's window-boundary one. Two independent failures pointing the same way is worth more than either
alone, and it is worth stating plainly that I did not go looking for it — it fell out of the neighbour
lists I had built for the path sum.

It is still **not** a claim that no construction works. 4013 closed the strict reading for this class; this
adds a second reason the class itself looks wrong. The founder's outstanding question is what decides it,
and it is not answered here.

No panel. No verdict moved — V3 reinforced, now on the extended lattice rather than on one cell.
