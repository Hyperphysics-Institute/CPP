## Resolving the term

A "decoration point" in 4063 is a position in R⁴ occupied by a substrate site: exactly what the founder calls an
additional GP. Not an interpolation, not a CP location. So under the ruling U is **admissible** — nothing in the
axioms forbids 7,320 GPs per cell. The question is whether it is *wanted*, and that turns on what it costs.

## What it costs (`code/4064_decoration_as_gps_cost.py`)

Reading the decoration as GPs, the 7,200 new GPs on the radius-1.7 shell have nearest-neighbour spacing
**0.11** (seed 4063) to **0.19** (second generic seed) in units where the core GP spacing is 1/φ = 0.618 —
i.e. **one-fifth to one-third of the lattice spacing** — and each has coordination **2** on its nearest shell
(its neighbours come in g/g⁻¹ pairs), against the core's 12. GP count per cell goes from 120 to 7,320: **61×**.

So the price of geometric chirality on this construction is a second, sub-lattice length scale and a set of
sites whose local neighbourhood is nearly one-dimensional. Both would enter the PCD cycle: CPs hop between GPs,
so every hop-count-based derivation (the z = 12 census, the cage tables, SM-11's lattice scale, the messenger
census under GR-FE-1) would have to be re-examined with 61× the sites and a new spacing. That is not a rework of
the axioms — the founder is right that any GP configuration is admissible — but it is a rework of nearly every
downstream number, and PD-007 says an effective structure is never adopted to rescue or supply a result.

## What I think

**Do not promote U.** Three reasons, in order of weight.

1. **It is not needed.** The corpus already has a reviewed route to handedness that costs nothing structural:
   FI-C-9 (sign of n̂) as the primitive and sign(δ) as the T-arrow (CAPACITY-1, TARROW-1/2). Mechanism A is
   precisely chiral *dynamics* on an *achiral* lattice: Q = L + δC with L H4-invariant and C parity-odd. 4063
   confirms this is the right shape of solution by showing what the alternative costs.
2. **It is expensive in exactly the currency CPP spends most carefully.** 61× the GPs and a second length scale,
   with no phenomenon asking for either.
3. **It would not buy V−A.** 4048's ~2% cap on structural helicity bias was measured on the 3D analogue; even
   if U's dense graph raised that, geometry supplies a *sign*, not maximality. Maximality has to come from the
   dynamics or the primitive regardless.

**What U is worth keeping as:** a theorem-level existence result with a price tag. If a future phenomenon ever
requires *geometric* rather than dynamical handedness, the corpus knows the minimum cost (7,200 sites per cell,
by Steinberg) before spending a patch on it. That is a useful negative-space result, and it closes the "chiral
lattice" question the way CAPACITY-1 already implied it should close: the lattice stays achiral; handedness
is a primitive plus an arrow.

**Where this leaves the foundation.** The open foundation is the tiling (4019): whether 600-cells can fill flat
R⁴ at all, with or without chirality. That is untouched by this window and is the item I would send the next
bounded computation at, in the SR/SM lane.
