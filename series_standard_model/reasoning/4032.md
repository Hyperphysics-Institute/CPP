# Reasoning — Patch 4032 (I tested the convenient answer and lost, which is the point)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The hypothesis

4030 ended with a sharp requirement: the twelve nearest neighbours must **span a 3-flat**. That is the
signature of a locally **three-dimensional** structure — in the 600-cell it holds because the vertices live
on S³, a 3-manifold. And SR.md R5 talks about *icosahedral point symmetry* and the *l = 6* harmonic, both
3D objects.

So the natural resolution of 4031's cross-sector tension was ready to hand: **SR means a 3D icosahedral
quasicrystal; I built a 4D one; the two never bore on each other.** 4031's conflict would have been a
category error, dissolved at no cost to anybody.

I went to check it. SR-1 says, in as many words:

> *we adopt the **quasicrystalline approximation**: space is flat **ℝ⁴** at macroscopic scales, constructed
> by modular repetition of 600-cell motifs with overlapping Voronoi [cells]*

**Refuted.** The tension does not dissolve. I am recording that rather than dropping it quietly, because it
was the answer I wanted and the temptation was to stop looking once the 3D story cohered.

## And the prior art is worse than I said one patch ago

4031 reported that the framing sat in a June **transcript**. It also sits in **SR-1**, a shipped flagship
paper, in a subsection titled **"Topology Clarification"**, whose first sentence is:

> *The finite 600-cell tiles the 3-sphere S³, not flat ℝ⁴.*

**Patch 4008's "discovery" that A2 is ambiguous was in a flagship paper under a heading that names the
topic.** The transcript was the second place it was written down, not the first. Sixth instance today, and
the worst — a section heading is about as findable as prior art gets.

## The item, in their own words rather than mine

**SR-1 is not wrong and is not being corrected.** It calls flat ℝ⁴ an **approximation** and labels it one.
Nothing in this arc contradicts SR-1 or touches its W2 world-call.

**But SM uses the same structure exactly.** SS-1, SM-1, SM-7, SM-8, SM-9 and SF-4 take *z = 12 in a 3-flat*
as a **theorem about the 600-cell** and apply it **per-GP to the substrate**. Meanwhile 4009/4013/4017/4018/4030
have established what the approximation costs — no periodic tiling, no bounded-window cut-and-project, no
regular honeycomb without paying z = ∞, no Bravais lattice, and a shell holding 13/18/19/26 rather than
exactly twelve.

**The gap is between "approximation" and "exact", and it sits exactly where SF-4's Σm_ν ∝ z⁻⁹ sensitivity
lives.** One integer step in z halves a cosmologically constrained prediction, and an approximation does
not promise an integer.

## What I am deliberately not saying

**Not that the two sectors are inconsistent.** An approximation and an exact use of the same object are not
a contradiction until someone bounds the error, and **nobody has**. That is the item, and it is a better
one than 4031's — which asked whether two sectors disagreed without being able to say about what.

I could have written this as "SR and SM conflict", which is louder and would have been overclaiming. The
honest version is narrower and more actionable: there is an unbounded error term between a stated
approximation and an exact downstream use, and it carries a flagship number.

No panel. No verdict moved. SR-1 and SF-4 both unrevised.
