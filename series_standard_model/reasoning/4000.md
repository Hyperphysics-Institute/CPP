# Reasoning — Patch 4000 (EW lane opened; two registry defects corrected)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## What I was asked

"Begin the EW-t lane problem in a new EW series … in next series block available."
Under `id_block_registry.md` Rule 5 a block is opened by founder ruling, and this is one;
under PD-006 the mechanics of *which* block are mine. So the only open variable was "available".

## Why I did not take 3900–3999

The registry's last row says `unallocated | 3900–3999 | reserved, unassigned`. Taking that row at
face value is the whole failure mode this file exists to prevent, so I checked it instead:

- the EU cell four rows above records **3900–3999 ALLOCATED (founder, 11 Sep)** and *"highest used 3940"*;
- `git log` finds **41 commits** with a 39xx subject, highest 3940.

So the table contradicts itself, and the free-looking row is the occupied one. This is the exact
shape of the three collisions the registry lists under Anomalies: every one was committed by someone
who had looked. **4000–4099 has zero commits** and is the next genuinely free block.

## The gate was worse than the table

`next_id.py`'s BLOCKS carried `'eu': (3800, 3899)` only, so `--all` reported EU **EXHAUSTED** while
41 of its patches sat in an untracked block. Rule 6 was written after exactly this happened at 3700
and again at 3800; this is the **third occurrence**, and the registry's own words apply — *a gate that
disagrees with the registry is worse than no gate, because it is trusted*. Taught here in the same
commit, per Rule 6, along with the new `ew` block. `eu-3800` is kept for `--check` history questions,
following the `gr-3600` precedent.

## Third correction, unrelated but adjacent

The CHIR cell reads `highest used 0981` and, a few lines later, `next free 0979` — stale text from the
0977-era update, contradicting itself inside one cell. 0982 has since landed; corrected to 0983.

## What I did not do

I did not name the lane's first physics patch. `EW-t` is not a string in this corpus — I grepped the
full tree and it appears nowhere — and D-7 is explicit that supplying a plausible meaning for an
unresolved symbol is the error, not the remedy. The two items filed in `todolist.md` are the ones the
corpus itself names as waiting on EW development; whether they are what "EW-t" denotes is the founder's
to say, and is asked in chat rather than assumed here.
