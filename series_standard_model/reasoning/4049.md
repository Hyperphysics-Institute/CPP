# Reasoning — Patch 4049 (my own hypothesis, one patch old, does not survive)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## What I was testing

4048 ended with a constructive reading of a negative: the substrate supplies the **sign**, the W bracelet
supplies the **maximality**. That is the kind of framing PD-008 exists to make me test rather than enjoy.

## The corpus had already answered it

SF-2 Theorem 4.2: *the 4800 induced 6-cycles partition into exactly 2 H₄-orbits; one orbit (size 1200,
**stabilizer D₆ of order 12**) is the W bracelet.*

**D₆ of order 12 is the dihedral group — six rotations and six reflections.** A stabilizer containing
reflections means the object is **achiral**. The corpus states it in its own theorem, and I had read that
line twice today without seeing it.

## And an independent computation agrees

Of the 63 induced 6-cycles through one vertex, a skew hexagon is a helix — and so chiral — only if all six
torsion signs agree. **None is a helix.** Every pattern is reversal-negation symmetric.

**So the scaffold offers no two handedness states.** There are not two mirror-image bracelets. There is
one, and it is its own mirror image.

## My first test said the opposite

Searching the signed-permutation subgroup found **0 improper symmetries** and would have read as **chiral**.

That subgroup is 192 elements of H₄'s 14400 — and it also found only **1 proper** symmetry, which should
have stopped me: an object with a D₆ stabilizer does not have one rotation. **The control was wrong and I
nearly read the result anyway.**

Sixth time today an instrument has returned a confidently wrong answer, and the second time the giveaway
was a control rather than the result. I am beginning to think the control is the more reliable half of
every measurement I make.

## And the state supplies nothing either

SF-2 describes the bracelet as a **catalyst**, activated when an external charge is captured at its
**D₆-symmetric centroid**. A D₆-symmetric centroid is achiral too.

So 4048's division of labour does not survive its own test.

## What would be needed

A **binary, P-odd, two-state degree of freedom on the ring** — a circulation, a winding, or a traversal
orientation. Such a thing is **all-or-nothing**, which is exactly the maximality V−A wants, and exactly
what a 2% substrate sign could select between.

**It is not present in the current description.** That is a specific, nameable gap on SF-2's side — far
more actionable than "derive V−A" — and it is not this lane's to fill.

I would rather hand over a missing ingredient with a shape than a missing derivation with a name.

No panel. No verdict moved. SF-2 unrevised. FI-C-9 = V3 stands.
