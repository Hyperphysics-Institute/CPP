# Reasoning — Patch 4011 (the founder's mechanism, tested; half of it is right)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## What he actually proposed

Not a vague intuition — a mechanism with a named origin. DI-bits can only travel **along edges**, so the
distance a signal has covered after k re-radiations is **path dependent**, and that path dependence might
accumulate into a handedness. He located it precisely: *the second and third re-radiation*.

That is testable as stated, so I tested it as stated rather than translating it into something I already
had machinery for.

## He is right about the disparity, and right about where it starts

Hop count and physical distance are different functions on the 600-cell, and they come apart at exactly
hop 2:

| hops | vertices | Euclidean distances present |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 12 | 0.61803 |
| **2** | 32 | **1.00000 and 1.17557** |
| **3** | 42 | **1.41421 and 1.61803** |
| **4** | 32 | **1.73205 and 1.90211** |
| 5 | 1 | 2.00000 |

From the second re-radiation on, one hop count spans two physical distances. This is not an approximation
or a small effect — 1.00 versus 1.18 is an 18% spread at the same hop number.

## And wrong about what follows, for a reason worth having

**At two hops it cannot work in principle.** A parity-odd invariant in four dimensions requires four
independent vectors. Two step vectors plus the propagation direction gives three. There is no 4D
pseudoscalar to be had at two hops, whatever the path lengths do. So the earliest candidate is *three*
hops — which is a sharpening of his own guess rather than a rejection of it.

**At three, four and five hops it cancels exactly.** Summing `w · sign det[u₁, u₂, u₃, n̂]` over every
non-backtracking path, with `w` the Mechanism-A tilt weight, gives zero to machine precision at δ = 0,
0.10 and 0.35, over 2.1 million paths. Per vertex at three hops: **480 left-handed, 480 right-handed, 492
degenerate.** That balance is the tell — it is not a small residual, it is a pairing.

**And the pairing is a symmetry, which is why this settles every hop count and not just the three I ran.**
`R = diag(1,1,1,−1)` maps the 600-cell to itself, has determinant −1, **and fixes n̂**. That last clause is
the one that matters: a reflection fixing n̂ preserves `û·n̂`, so the Mechanism-A weight is mirror-invariant
while the pseudoscalar flips sign. I verified the pairing path by path rather than asserting it — equal
weight, opposite sign, 4356 paths checked, no exceptions.

So: H₄ has order 14400 and its rotation subgroup 7200. Index 2. **The polytope is achiral**, and every path
has an exact mirror partner living inside the same structure. Chaining re-radiations forever does not help,
because each additional hop is still built from mirror-paired steps.

## A small thing I got wrong on the way

My first improper map was a coordinate transposition, and it is **not** a symmetry of this vertex set — the
even-permutation family in the standard coordinates excludes it. The check failed, correctly. I found a
real improper symmetry by searching the signed permutations (96 of 192 are improper) instead of reaching
for the obvious one.

Worth recording because the failure was informative: had I assumed the transposition worked, I would have
"proved" achirality with a map that is not in the group.

## What this is worth

It confirms **FI-C-9 = V3** — chirality primitive, not emergent — by a route with no overlap with the
susceptibility line. That line reached it through η-field correlators and a Landau potential; this reaches
it through path geometry on the raw graph. Same conclusion, disjoint machinery.

It is also the same fact as 0973's *sign(δ) is P-even, T-odd*, arrived at from the other side: 0973 found
the tilt gives no cycle affinity in its even part; here the tilt weight survives the mirror unchanged. Two
descriptions of one thing, which is what a cross-check is supposed to look like.

## Not absorbed

The *"10% of the radius of the PSR"* shell in his picture is **nowhere in the corpus** — I grepped before
using it, having coined a term myself three patches ago and had it bounced. I did not adopt it and did not
guess what it means. **The result does not depend on it**: the cancellation is a symmetry argument and is
independent of the shell radius entirely. But a quantity being reasoned from should be registered or
labelled an estimate, and it is neither.

No panel. No verdict moved — V3 reinforced, not moved.
