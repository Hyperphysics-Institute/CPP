# Reasoning — Patch 4002 (OPEN-EW-7: the W mass breakdown)

**STATUS: verbatim, at patch.**

## The question, and why it had no purchase

*Does SM-2's W mass breakdown depend on the species of the W's 12 CPs, or only on their count?*

Registered at 0945 because the corrigendum changed the W's constituents (6 hDPs → 3 qDP + 3 eDP) while
leaving the member count at 12, so it looked like a clean species-vs-count discriminator.

D-3 says locate the object before building on it, so before reasoning about species I went and read the
row. The W row is:

    W & Linear 6-hDP chain & 40190 & 13397 & 0.0 & 4019 & 0.0 & 22774 & 80380

Divide through by 80380: **0.5000, 0.16667, 0, 0.0500, 0, 0.28333**. Those are 1/2, 1/6, 0, 1/20, 0,
17/60, and they sum to exactly 1. So the row is not a computation over the W's constituents that happens
to land on 80380. **It is 80380 — the PDG input — cut into fixed fractions, with the residual sized to
close the sum.** I checked all twelve rows; every one does this.

That answers the question as asked, and the answer is **neither branch**. Species cannot enter, because
nothing about the constituents enters. Count cannot enter either, for the same reason. The framing
assumed the row was a bottom-up sum; it is a top-down partition.

## What the question missed

A partition still has structure: *which* terms are switched on. So I looked at what turns them on.

`E_inter` is either **exactly 0.10 of the total or exactly zero**, in all twelve rows, with no third
value. Off: electron, up, down, W. On: muon, tau, strange, charm, bottom, top, Z, Higgs. Sorting by the
paper's own Cage-hypothesis column, that is exactly **cage vs no cage** — 1-vertex, bare and linear-extra
objects are off; every tetrahedral, icosahedral and dodecahedral object is on. 12/12, no counterexample
in either direction.

And that is where the relabel actually bites. SM-2's old W was a *linear chain* — no cage, `E_inter = 0`,
and that is why the W was the only boson with a zero there. The corrigendum's W is a **closed ring on the
Petrie hexagon of the first-shell icosahedron** (SF-2 v1.0 Thm 4.2; Capotauro §W-bracelet puts the same
6 vertices on the same 12-vertex icosahedral cage the Z uses). **The relabel carries the W across the
switch.** `E_inter` 0 → 8038, residual 22774 → 14736, **total unchanged at 80380**, and the W row comes
out term-for-term identical to Z and Higgs. Three bosons, one form — the relabel makes the table *more*
regular, not less.

## The formulation I shipped is not the one I started with

My first version of the finding was "the table uses exactly two fraction schemes, A and B." The verify
script **falsified it on the electron and the up**: `E_cloud`/Total is 0.10, 0.02, 0.06 and 0.05 in
different rows, with no rule given anywhere. So the two-scheme claim was wrong, and the parts that
survive — `E_eDP = Base/3` exactly in all twelve rows, `Base/Total ∈ {0.6, 0.5}` crossing the
lepton/quark line in both directions, and the `E_inter` switch — are what the script now tests. The
irregularity is recorded in `todolist.md` rather than swept; it is pre-existing and has nothing to do
with the W.

Worth writing down because it is the D-series pattern in miniature: the tidy version was available
first, and only running it broke it.

## Why I applied it in print rather than asking

Patch 3850's rule: *when a result touches a number in print, say so loudly and change it in print.* Two
printed cells move. PD-007 is explicit that asking the founder to adopt or reject a computed value is
exactly the yes/no it was written to avoid, so punting was not available either. I applied it, and put
the one soft point where it cannot be missed — in the paper, in the sector file, in the frontier header:

> the switch rule is read off this table's own 12/12 consistency; it is not stated in prose anywhere
> in SM-2.

That is an empirical regularity in a calibrated table, not a derivation from the mass formula. It is
strong — twelve for twelve, clean geometric reason, and the total does not move — but it is not a
theorem, and the cost of being wrong is bounded at two cells with no total affected. Filed as owed:
derive the rule or state it in SM-2's Universal Refinements section.
