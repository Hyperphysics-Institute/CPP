## Why H1 and why now

Everything funnelled here. 4003 named (H1) and (H-NESS) as the two residuals of the μ²-sign route; 4004
closed H-NESS as ill-posed; 4005 discharged the recompute it gated; 4021 showed the frustration-relaxation

It is also the kind of item that gets carried rather than worked — a hypothesis inside a review-closed
theorem, easy to cite and hard to open. So: open it.

## What it turned out to be

Not true. Not false. **Conditional on whether the reflection fixes n̂.**

With Θ = diag(1,1,1,−1), an exact improper symmetry of the 600-cell splitting it 45/45:

| | π Θ-invariant? | Gram symmetric? | min eigenvalue | RP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| n̂ fixed, δ = 0 / 0.10 / 0.35 | 1.0e-17 | 1.2e-14 | −3e-14 | **holds** |
| n̂ flipped, δ = 0.10 | 5.4e-3 | **1.2** | **−0.497** | **fails** |
| n̂ flipped, δ = 0.35 | 2.0e-2 | **3.5** | **−4.79** | **fails** |

At δ = 0 it holds for every reflection, as an untilted measure must. **The failure scales with δ: it is the
Mechanism-A tilt that breaks it**, which is the right kind of answer — the thing that breaks RP is the thing
that makes the measure a NESS rather than an equilibrium.

## The asymmetry between the two answers

This is the part I most want on the record. **The failure is a proof. The success is not.**

RP demands positivity for *all* functions supported on one side. A single negative eigenvalue on *any* test
class refutes it — so the n̂-flipping case is settled, by witness, and does not depend on my choice of test
functions being clever. The positive case is different: passing on single-site occupations is a **necessary
condition only**. Reporting "RP holds" without that sentence would be a stronger claim than the computation
supports, and extending it to a richer class is owed and filed.

## Why it matters, and it is a good outcome

**The chirality argument needs exactly the sector where RP holds.** `R = diag(1,1,1,−1)` with n̂ = e₁ is
precisely the map 4011, 4012, 4015 and 4020 all used, and 0973 puts sign(δ) as **P-even** — so the P-face
lives in the n̂-preserving sector. H1 holds where the argument needs it.

## Where I stop

**Whether VW-1's argument runs on restricted RP is not mine to decide.** Whether a transfer-matrix or
Osterwalder–Schrader construction can be built with reflection positivity holding only on the n̂-preserving
subgroup is a technical question about VW-1, and it belongs to whoever holds that theorem. Deciding it here
would be exactly the over-reach 4013 committed and 4017 had to correct — and I would be deciding it in the
direction convenient to me, which is when to stop.

## The echo, recorded with nothing attached

RP fails precisely for the reflections that **flip n̂**. 0973 located the **T-arrow** in exactly that
direction: sign(δ) is P-even and **T-odd**. The RP failure and the T-arrow occupy the same place.

That is either structural or an artifact of both quantities being about the same tilt. **Nothing is built on
it.** This session has cost several retractions to deciding what a suggestive coincidence means before
checking, and I would rather leave a clean observation than a claim someone has to withdraw.
