### 2. The Grid Point and the 600-Cell Lattice

*Source: Thomas's description, 4 April 2026:*

The 600-cell lattice is not a single grid at one scale. It is a hierarchy of 600-cells nested at progressively finer scales. Every Grid Point is simultaneously a vertex in many 600-cells of different sizes. The finer/tighter lattice with fewer GPs between vertices is realised along edges. **SSV_abs selects the subgraph:** The SSV_abs at a CP's current Grid Point determines which 600-cell subgraph governs its next advance. Higher SSV_abs means a finer subgraph, shorter edge length, shorter displacement per Absolute Moment, and slower effective time. The CP doesn't hop a shorter distance on the same lattice — it hops on a finer lattice within the nested hierarchy. This is the geometric mechanism behind the PSR formula from SR-1.

**Implication for bond-counting:** The z=12 coordination number in SM-6 and SM-7 is the full-occupancy limit. The effective coordination number is z_eff = (occupancy fraction) x 12. At STP, z_eff is approximately 12. The small residuals in SM-6/SM-7 predictions may partly reflect occupancy being slightly less than 1.

The fundamental cage structure in CPP is the tetrahedron — 4 vertices, 6 edges, 4 triangular faces.

### 16 April 2026 — The Swarm Validation Strategy: Celestial Navigation as Proof Architecture (Thomas's insight, Opus formulation)

**The celestial navigation analogy (Thomas):** Each CPP paper is like a celestial navigation star shot. Each individual shot has measurement error — imported formalism, approximations, modelling choices. But when you shoot dozens of independent stars, the triangulation box shrinks with the square root of N. The location point (the axiom set) becomes ever more precise, even if no individual measurement has improved. You don't need perfect star shots. You need many independent ones.

**Why independence matters (Opus analysis):** The key word is *independent*. If every paper imported the same Layer B assumption, the star shots would be correlated — fifteen atmospheric distortions of one star, not fifteen stars. - SM-6's Weinberg angle depends on mode-counting traces, not cage-volume scaling.
- SS-2's proton radius depends on Voronoi geometry and ZBW smearing, not operator algebras.

Those five are partially correlated. Closing SS-4 (the operator formalism derivation) would decorrelate them. But the papers outside that thread are already independent anchors.

**The Fisher argument (quantitative):** Suppose each individual CPP prediction has a 1-in-100 chance of being an accidental match. (This is conservative — sin²θ_W matching to 0.24% with zero parameters is far less likely than 1-in-100 by chance.) Fifteen independent results at that level give a combined probability of 10⁻³⁰. Twenty-five results give 10⁻⁵⁰. The probability that the axiom set is wrong but accidentally produces correct physics across every sector becomes less parsimonious than accepting the axioms.

**The metaphysical axiom argument (Thomas's deepest point):** Consciousness as a primitive is inherently outside empirical reach — no experiment can detect a conscious point *as* conscious. The alternative hypothesis — "the axioms are wrong but accidentally produce correct physics everywhere" — is less parsimonious than the axioms themselves. This is exactly how we validate any foundational axiom in physics. Nobody has ever observed a point particle, a field, or a wavefunction directly. We accept them because the frameworks built on them work. CPP asks for the same epistemic standard, applied to a different primitive.

**Operational consequence:** Extend territory aggressively. Every new sector covered — nuclear binding, boson masses, cosmological predictions — is a new star shot that further constrains the intersection. Layer A/B/C cleanup makes each existing shot more precise, but a new shot in a new sector is worth more than a precision improvement on an existing one. The swarm grows by breadth, not by polish.

**This is the programme's guiding star.**

### 19–21 April 2026 — SS-7 Alpha-Cluster Regime: The 3N−6 Edge Formula, Twelve Concurrent Predictions, and the First Retired Open Problem

**Context:** SS-5 v6 and SS-6 v0.1 closed out the light-nuclei sector ($A \leq 4$) and the deuteron observables scoping on 18 April 2026. The natural next target registered as OPEN-SS-18 was the alpha-cluster regime at $A \geq 6$. SS-7 executed that target. The paper's life-cycle from v0.1 through v1.2 spans roughly 72 hours and includes the programme's first retired open problem.

**The physical picture Thomas carried into SS-7:** The SS-5 nucleon-nucleon base-to-base K₃ collective-mode mechanism is scale-invariant. The same K₃ graph — three oscillator bonds across a triangular contact face — appears at two physical scales. At the nucleon scale (SS-5): three quark-quark bonds across the triangular quark face, producing one collective mode at $B_\text{pair} = M_0/\varphi = 2.342$ MeV per nucleon-nucleon contact. At the alpha scale (SS-7): three nucleon-nucleon bonds across the triangular face where two alpha particles meet base-to-base, producing one collective mode at *the same quantum* $B_\text{pair}$ per alpha-alpha contact. The recurrence is structurally allowed because the K₃ graph is a pure combinatorial object that does not care about the physical scale of its vertices.

**The mathematical recognition (SS-7 v0.1, ~19 April 2026):** If each alpha-alpha contact delivers one $B_\text{pair}$, and an $N_\alpha$-alpha nucleus arranges its alphas as vertices of a convex triangular-faced polytope, the binding scales with the *edge count* of that polytope. Euler's formula for a closed triangulated 2-sphere forces the edge count exactly: $V - E + F = 2$ plus $2E = 3F$ gives $E = 3V - 6$. For a simplicial polytope on $N_\alpha$ vertices, $E = 3N_\alpha - 6$, independent of which specific polytope is realised (the octahedron and triangular antiprism at $N_\alpha = 6$ both have 12 edges). This is Theorem 2.1 of SS-7 — a pure-combinatorics theorem invoked under a structural hypothesis (C4: alpha clusters realise simplicial connectivity).

**The central formula:**
$$B(N_\alpha) = N_\alpha \cdot B_\alpha + (3N_\alpha - 6) \cdot B_\text{pair}$$

with $B_\alpha = 28.296$ MeV (from SS-5) and $B_\text{pair} = M_0/\varphi = 2.342$ MeV (also from SS-5, now recurring at the alpha scale). Zero fitted parameters. Against the AME 2020 atomic mass evaluation, the formula reproduces the binding energies of twelve strict $N{=}Z$ alpha-chain nuclei from $^{12}$C through $^{56}$Ni — all within ±1.5%, RMS 0.80%, widest ${}^{20}$Ne at +1.19%.

**Thomas's structural insight on assumption C4:** The theorem-vs-hypothesis split was formalised explicitly in SS-7 v1.0 (20 April 2026). Thomas's standing principle — *"honesty is always appropriate; people know there is something missing, and acknowledgment engenders trust"* — led to labeling the four structural inputs (C1 alpha rigidity, C2 base-to-base contact, C3 K₃ collective mode, C4 simplicial polytope connectivity) as paper-level assumptions rather than programme-level axioms. The theorem is about *any* simplicial polytope; the hypothesis is that alpha clusters *realise* simplicial connectivity. Future derivation of C4 from CPP lattice primitives becomes OPEN-SS-24.

**The hostile-geometry stress test (ChatGPT re-review contribution, SS-7 v1.0):** The adversarial question — *does the 12-nucleus agreement genuinely select* $E = 3N_\alpha - 6$, *or would any reasonable lower-edge polytope fit equally well?* — was posed during v1.0 review. The response was an adversarial test: replace the simplicial edge count with physically arguable lower-edge alternatives (cube, square antiprism, monocapped antiprism, etc.) at fixed CPP constants $(B_\alpha, B_\text{pair})$. In all five tested cases (${}^{32}$S at two alternatives, ${}^{28}$Si wheel, ${}^{36}$Ar monocapped antiprism, ${}^{40}$Ca pentagonal-antiprism-type), the simplicial rule outperforms. The tightest case — ${}^{36}$Ar dropping a single edge from 21 to 20 — degrades the fit from −0.94% to −1.70%, exactly the one-$B_\text{pair}$ single-quantum difference expected. This establishes **edge-count dominance**: binding tracks edge count $E$ almost exclusively, and the data prefer $E = 3N_\alpha - 6$ at single-quantum sensitivity. The paper transitions from "plausible counting that matches data" to "constrained combinatorial model that survives systematic perturbation."
