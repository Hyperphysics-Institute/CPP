# OP-SS-4: Two-Loop $\beta_1$ from CPP qCP Cage Dynamics

**Priority:** MEDIUM  
**Status:** OPEN — 1-loop gives $\alpha_s$ 15% above PDG  
**Series:** SS\#4  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

---

## Statement

Derive the two-loop QCD $\beta$-function coefficient:

$$\beta_1 = 102 - \frac{38 n_f}{3}$$

from CPP qCP cage dynamics.  For $n_f = 6$: $\beta_1 = 102 - 76 = 26$.

---

## What is Known

**One-loop result (SS\#4, exact):**
$$\beta_0 = \frac{11 C_A}{3} - \frac{4 T_F n_f}{3} = 11 - 4 = 7$$

derived from $C_A = 3$, $T_F = 1/2$ (both exact), $n_f = 6$ (cage
depth architecture).

**One-loop running gives:**
$$\alpha_s^{1\text{-loop}}(M_Z) \approx 0.136$$
PDG: $0.118$.  Discrepancy = 15% — the standard known limitation of
one-loop QCD running.  Adding $\beta_1$ reduces this to $< 1\%$.

**Physical mechanism for $\beta_1$:** involves the quartic gluon vertex
(4-gluon coupling from the tetrahedral algebra) and reducible two-loop
diagrams.  Both are derivable from the CPP tetrahedral structure proved
in SS\#3, but the explicit calculation has not been done.

---

## What Remains

- Derive the quartic gluon vertex from the CPP cage algebra.
  In SS\#3, the 3-gluon vertex follows from the antisymmetric part
  of $T^a T^b$; the 4-gluon vertex follows from $(T^a T^b)^2$ or
  the symmetric part of the commutator product.
- Sum the two-loop diagrams in the CPP lattice path integral.
- Confirm $\beta_1 = 26$ for $n_f = 6$.
