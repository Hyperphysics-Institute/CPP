# CPP Conjectures Register — Strong Sector (SS)

**Last updated:** 29 March 2026
**Purpose:** Register of proposed physical claims that are observationally
consistent and physically motivated but not yet proved from CPP axioms.
A conjecture becomes a theorem when a proof is found; it becomes a
falsified conjecture when a counter-example or inconsistency is identified.
No entry is ever deleted — the record of what was proposed and why is

## CJ-SS-1: W₀ Bracelet Locally-Linear Coupling Face

**Status:** CONJECTURE — observationally consistent, mechanism identified
**Registered:** 29 March 2026
**Source:** Session discussion 29 March 2026; mechanism-SS-1.md Steps 24–27

### Statement

The W₀ bracelet (a closed ring of hDP pairs) presents a **locally
linear coupling face** to the central qCP at the point of interaction.
At the contact point, the relevant interaction geometry is a tangent
line segment of the ring rather than the full closed structure. This
directional asymmetry is the geometric origin of the W's
polarity-inverting coupling, distinguishing it from the Z's
symmetric icosahedral cage coupling.

### Physical Motivation

A closed ring is locally linear at every point — its tangent is
well-defined and its curvature is nonzero but small at the scale
of the qCP-W interaction. The relevant interaction at the contact
point therefore has the character of a linear hDP segment, not a
closed bracelet. This linear character:

1. Provides a preferred coupling axis (the tangent direction)
2. Makes the interaction directional rather than symmetric
3. Is the geometric reason the W coupling is polarity-inverting
   (asymmetric charge transfer) while the Z coupling is not

### What Would Prove It

A derivation from the bracelet geometry showing that the coupling
Hamiltonian between the W bracelet and the qCP is dominated by
the locally-linear (tangent) term at leading order in the
curvature expansion $\kappa = 1/R_{\rm bracelet}$.

### What Would Falsify It

A calculation showing that the full bracelet ring geometry produces
a coupling that is isotropic (not directional), or that the
polarity inversion arises from a different mechanism unrelated to
the ring topology.

### Related Open Problem

OP-SS-12 (W bracelet polarity inversion from CPP first principles)

## CJ-SS-2: Universal qCP Polarity Switching in Quark Flavor Transitions

**Status:** CONJECTURE — observed universally across all known decay pathways
**Registered:** 29 March 2026
**Source:** Session discussion 29 March 2026; systematic check with Grok (xAI)

### Statement

Every Standard Model quark flavor transition — whether within a
generation or across generations — involves the central qCP
switching polarity:
- Up-type ($+$qCP) → down-type ($-$qCP): polarity $+1 \to -1$
- Down-type ($-$qCP) → up-type ($+$qCP): polarity $-1 \to +1$

No quark flavor transition has been identified in which the qCP
polarity remains unchanged. This pattern is universal and exceptionless
among all known quark decays.

### Evidence

Checked systematically across all six quark flavors and all
generation-crossing transitions:

| Decay | qCP before | qCP after | Polarity switch |
|-------|-----------|-----------|----------------|
| $u \to d + W^+$ | $+$qCP | $-$qCP | ✓ |
| $c \to s + W^+$ | $+$qCP | $-$qCP | ✓ |
| $t \to b + W^+$ | $+$qCP | $-$qCP | ✓ |
| $d \to u + W^-$ | $-$qCP | $+$qCP | ✓ |
| $s \to u + W^-$ | $-$qCP | $+$qCP | ✓ |
| $b \to c + W^-$ | $-$qCP | $+$qCP | ✓ |
| $s \to c + W^-$ | $-$qCP | $+$qCP | ✓ |
| $b \to u + W^-$ | $-$qCP | $+$qCP | ✓ |

No exception found across all checked pathways.

### Physical Consequence

Universal polarity switching means that up-type and down-type are
not merely labels for charge but physically distinct configurations
of the central qCP — configurations that can only be interconverted
by a specific structural transformation (the W bracelet interaction).
The W is not simply an energy carrier but a structural catalyst that
changes the physical nature of the qCP it interacts with.

### What Would Prove It

A proof from CPP EW-strong coupling showing that W-mediated
interactions necessarily and exclusively produce qCP polarity
switches. This is the content of OP-SS-12.

### What Would Falsify It

Any Standard Model decay pathway in which a quark changes flavor
without a corresponding qCP polarity switch in the CPP description.
(Such a pathway would require a new type of weak interaction not
mediated by the W bracelet.)

### Related Open Problem

OP-SS-12 (W bracelet polarity inversion from CPP first principles)

## Candidate Postulate: 2:1 ZBW Orbital Frequency Ratio for Quarks

**Status:** PROPOSED POSTULATE — asserted in SS-1 Table 1 and
mechanism-SS-1.md, not yet formally stated as a CPP postulate or
derived from CPP axioms.

**Registered:** 29 March 2026

### Statement

All quarks carry an inner orbital ZBW dipole pair oscillating at
**twice the frequency** of the outer orbital (2:1 inner-to-outer
frequency ratio). This is the same ratio as the electron's
spin-generating orbital and is the CPP source of spin-½ for all
fermions.

### Current Status

This ratio is used implicitly throughout SS-1 (Table 1) and
explicitly in mechanism-SS-1.md (Step 28) but has not been:
(a) formally stated as a CPP postulate, or
(b) derived from the SSV field geometry of the cage

It should either be elevated to a named CPP postulate (P-SS-1)
with explicit justification for why 2:1 rather than some other
ratio, or derived from the cage geometry as a consequence of the
boundary conditions on the ZBW Schrödinger equation.

### Recommendation

Add as CPP Postulate P-5a (a special case of the general ZBW
postulate P-5): "The inner orbital ZBW DP of all fermions
oscillates at twice the frequency of the outer orbital." This
would be consistent with the spin-½ requirement and with the
existing treatment in SR-1.
