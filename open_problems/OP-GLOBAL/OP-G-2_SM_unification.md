# OP-G-2: Full Standard Model from Single 600-Cell

**Priority:** HIGHEST  
**Status:** OPEN — capstone problem  
**Series:** All series  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

## Statement

Show that the complete Standard Model — all gauge groups, all particle
masses, all coupling constants, and the CKM matrix — follows from the
single geometric object: the 600-cell regular polytope in 4D Euclidean
space, together with one free parameter (sea\_strength = 0.185).

## The Capstone

OP-G-2 is the goal of the entire CPP programme.  It closes when all
sector open problems are resolved and a single coherent derivation
connects the 600-cell geometry to all 19 (or 26, with neutrinos)
Standard Model parameters.
