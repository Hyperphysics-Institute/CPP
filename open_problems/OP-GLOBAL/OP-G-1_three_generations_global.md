# OP-G-1: Three SM Generations — Quarks and Leptons Unified

**Priority:** HIGHEST  
**Status:** OPEN  
**Series:** Cross-series (SS#1, lepton series)  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

## Statement

Prove that the 600-cell geometry forces exactly three generations of
both quarks and leptons, from a single geometric principle shared by both
sectors.

## What is Known

**Quark side (SS#1):** Three generation pairs arise from cage depths
0, (1–2), (3–4).  The 600-cell eigenvalue spectrum groups into three
conjugate pairs — this coincidence is noted but not proved.

**Lepton side:** The lepton series (developed separately by Thomas and Grok)
addresses this from the lepton mass side.  The convergence point between
quark and lepton generation structure is the CPP proof of exactly-three
generations.

## Why Exactly Three

The 600-cell has 120 vertices, 720 edges, 1200 triangular faces, and
600 tetrahedral cells.  The symmetry group is the 600-cell symmetry
$[3,3,5]$ of order 14400.  The three-generation structure should emerge
from the representation theory of this group — specifically, from the
number of irreducible representations of a fixed type.
