# OP-SD-5: Apparatus DP Sea Anisotropy $\delta_0$ from SSV

**Priority:** MEDIUM  
**Status:** OPEN  
**Series:** SD#5  
**Last updated:** 23 March 2026

## Statement

Derive the apparatus-induced DP Sea anisotropy parameter $\delta_0$
from the SSV field of the measurement apparatus.  This parameter
sets the Born rule probability in the CPP interpretation of quantum
measurement.  Showing that $\delta_0 \to 0$ as apparatus SSV $\to 0$
(no measurement) and $\delta_0 \to 1$ as SSV $\to \infty$ (classical
measurement) would complete the CPP measurement theory.

## Prerequisite
- OP-SD-1

## Feeds Into
- CPP measurement theory
- Experimental tests distinguishing CPP from Copenhagen
