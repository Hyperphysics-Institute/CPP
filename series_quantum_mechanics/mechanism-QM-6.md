## Part 1: The Four Primitives

The entire QM series is built from four primitives that are themselves either CPP axioms or direct consequences of them:

**Primitive 2 — DI-bits:** Relational quanta exchanged between CPs, carrying both density ρ and geometric phase φ. The complex amplitude ψ = √ρ × e^{iφ} encodes the full DI-bit state. Phase is deterministic — it accumulates at c = l_P/t_P along lattice edges.

**Primitive 3 — 600-cell lattice:** 120 Grid Points, 720 edges, coordination z = 12, adjacency matrix with exactly six distinct eigenvalues {12, 1+φ, φ−1, 1−φ, −φ, −(1+φ)} (AXIM-2).

**Primitive 4 — Nexus:** Atemporal global constraint enforcing DI-bit number conservation and total phase circulation at every Absolute Moment tick. Not a signal; not a hidden variable; not a field (AXIM-6).
