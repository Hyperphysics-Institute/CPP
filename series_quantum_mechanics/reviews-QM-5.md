### Q2. "Does finite renormalisation solve the hierarchy problem?"

It replaces infinite fine-tuning with large-but-finite fine-tuning. The hierarchy problem becomes: why is m_H/m_P ≈ 10⁻¹⁷? This is the same as OPEN-P-EW-1 (the η derivation). Infinite → finite is progress; 10⁻¹⁷ remains unexplained.
