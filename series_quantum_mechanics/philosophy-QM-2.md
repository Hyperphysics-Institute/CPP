## Superposition Without Collapse

In QM-2, superposition is not a special quantum property requiring explanation — it is the natural consequence of DI bits propagating simultaneously along all lattice geodesics from a source. When two paths meet at the same Grid Point, the DI-bit amplitudes add (constructive interference) or cancel (destructive interference) based on their relative phases. This is deterministic wave mechanics, not the mysterious co-existence of classical alternatives.

The conceptual gain is that wave-particle duality becomes mechanically transparent: the DI-bit propagates as a wave (distributed amplitude along all paths) and registers as a particle (single Grid Point, determined by DP Sea decoherence). There is no paradox because the "wave" and the "particle" are two aspects of the same DI-bit field at different scales of observation.

## The Born Rule as DI-Bit Density

QM-2 derives the Born rule from the identification |ψ|² = ρ (DI-bit number density). This is THEO-QM-2. The philosophical significance is that the Born rule is not a separate postulate about the relationship between the wavefunction and probabilities — it is the definition of what the wavefunction represents. The probability of finding a particle at position x is the DI-bit density at x, which is the fraction of the total DI-bit count located in the neighbourhood of x. Probability emerges from counting, not from axiom.
