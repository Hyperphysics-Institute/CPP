## The Nexus Is Not a Hidden Variable

The most philosophically important result in QM-3 is the no-signaling proof (THEO-QM-5) alongside the Bell violation derivation (THEO-QM-4). But because the Nexus operates atemporally — outside the spacetime it coordinates — it transmits no causal signal between the two measurement sites. The correlations are real and non-local in the standard sense, but no information travels.

This resolves the tension with relativity that makes the EPR/Bell situation philosophically puzzling in standard QM. In CPP, the Nexus is neither a local hidden variable (Bell's theorem forbids those) nor a superluminal signal (no-signaling is proved). It is a third category: an atemporal global constraint that maintains correlations without sending information.
