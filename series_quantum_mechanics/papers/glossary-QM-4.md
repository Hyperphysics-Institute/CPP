## Decoherence Rate (γ)

The rate at which off-diagonal elements of a qubit's reduced density matrix decay: γ = (sea_strength)² × E_P / ħ ≈ 5.9 × 10⁴² s⁻¹. Derived from the DP Sea bath coupling in THEO-QM-6.

---

## Lindblad Master Equation

The equation governing the evolution of the reduced density matrix of a system coupled to the DP Sea bath: dρ/dt = −(i/ħ)[H,ρ] + γ(LρL† − ½{L†L,ρ}). Derived in QM-4 from the DP Sea thermal statistics.

---

## Pointer Basis

The preferred measurement basis selected by the system-apparatus SSV coupling. In CPP, the pointer states are the eigenstates of the SSV observable — the states that are robust against DP Sea decoherence. This resolves the preferred basis problem.

---

## DP Sea Bath

The Dipole Sea viewed as a thermal reservoir. Every measurement involves coupling the quantum system to the DP Sea through SSV gradients. The bath has ~10¹²⁰ degrees of freedom per Planck volume, making decoherence effectively instantaneous for macroscopic systems.

---

## Global Unitarity

The total system (qubit + DP Sea + Nexus) evolves unitarily even though the reduced system decoheres. No information is lost — it is transferred to the DP Sea bath. The Nexus ensures that the global state remains consistent at every Absolute Moment.
