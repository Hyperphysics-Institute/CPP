## Four Primitives

The minimal set from which all of quantum mechanics emerges in CPP: (1) Conscious Points with polarity and position, (2) the 600-cell lattice topology, (3) DI-bit complex amplitudes propagating at c, (4) the Nexus global constraint. Everything else — the Schrödinger equation, Born rule, Bell violations, decoherence, QFT — is derived.

---

## Emergence Chain

The logical sequence: lattice → hopping → Schrödinger equation → path summation → interference → Born rule → entanglement → Bell violations → decoherence → measurement → QFT → generations. Each step follows from the previous without additional postulates.

---

## Nexus Roles

The Nexus serves different functions at different levels: (1) enforces lattice consistency (QM-1), (2) selects allowed paths (QM-2), (3) enforces singlet correlations (QM-3), (4) maintains global unitarity during decoherence (QM-4), (5) enforces occupation statistics (QM-5). All are the same constraint seen at different scales.

---

## Derived vs Postulated (QM Status)

Derived: Schrödinger equation, Born rule, interference, Bell S = 2√2, no-signaling, Lindblad equation, commutation relations, fermion statistics. Postulated (axioms): CP existence, 600-cell lattice, DI-bit propagation, Nexus. Open: ħ derivation from four primitives, pointer basis formal proof.
