## QM-5 Specific Content

**Central results:** Field operators from 600-cell eigenmode expansion; THEO-QM-7 (commutation from eigenmode orthonormality); THEO-QM-8 (fermion statistics from CP non-co-occupation); finite renormalisation from Planck UV cutoff; eigenvalue bridge to EW series.

**The eigenvalue bridge:** The six 600-cell adjacency matrix eigenvalues {12, 1+φ, φ-1, 1-φ, -φ, -(1+φ)} appear in this paper as the dispersion modes of free DI-bit fields. These are the same six eigenvalues that the EW series uses to select electroweak boson topologies. QM-5 §8 (Six Eigenvalues and Three SM Generations) makes this connection explicit and is the founding document of the EW series.

**Finite renormalisation:** Loop integrals are finite because the 600-cell lattice provides a Planck-scale UV cutoff. The one-loop self-energy Σ|_CPP ~ (e²/4π²)E_P² is large but finite, converting QFT renormalization from infinite subtraction to large finite subtraction.
