# Glossary — QM-3: Bell Inequality and Entanglement from the Nexus

**Paper:** QM-3
**Last updated:** 2 April 2026

## Nexus

The global consistency constraint in CPP — the requirement that all CP states at every Absolute Moment satisfy the 600-cell lattice topology simultaneously. The Nexus is not a force, not a signal, and not a hidden variable. It is a boundary condition on the joint state of the entire lattice. In QM-3, the Nexus enforces the singlet correlation cos(θ) without transmitting information.

---

## ZBW Helix

The Zitterbewegung helical trajectory of a DI-bit cage, which defines the spin-½ qubit. The helix handedness (left/right) maps to spin-up/down. The angle θ between two ZBW helices determines the measurement correlation.

---

## Singlet State

A non-separable joint state of two entangled DI-bit cages: |ψ⟩ = (|↑↓⟩ − |↓↑⟩)/√2. In CPP, the singlet is not a superposition of product states but a single joint configuration of the two ZBW helices, enforced by the Nexus at creation.

---

## CHSH Parameter (S)

The Bell-CHSH combination S = E(a,b) − E(a,b') + E(a',b) + E(a',b'). Classical (LHV) bound: |S| ≤ 2. Quantum bound (Tsirelson): |S| ≤ 2√2. CPP derives S = 2√2 from the singlet correlation function and ZBW geometry (THEO-QM-4).

---

## Tsirelson Bound

The maximum quantum value of the CHSH parameter: |S| = 2√2 ≈ 2.828. In CPP, this follows from the sinusoidal correlation function E(a,b) = −cos(θ) of the ZBW singlet, combined with the optimal measurement angles. Derived in THEO-QM-5.

---

## No-Signaling Theorem (THEO-QM-5)

The proof that the Nexus cannot be used to transmit information faster than light. Alice's marginal statistics are independent of Bob's measurement choice, despite the non-local correlation. The Nexus is a constraint, not a channel.
