## QM-3 Specific Content

**Central results:** Non-separability of singlet state from Nexus constraint; THEO-QM-3 (non-separability); THEO-QM-4 (Tsirelson bound |S| = 2√2); THEO-QM-5 (no-signaling in CPP).

**Key derivation:** The singlet |Ψ⁻⟩ = (|↑↓⟩ − |↓↑⟩)/√2 is forced to be non-separable because the Nexus conservation law does not permit the joint DI-bit state to factorise when the total spin must be zero. This is a structural consequence of the Nexus, not a choice.

**The Tsirelson bound:** |S|_max = 2√2 follows from the geometry of the singlet correlations E(â,b̂) = −cos θ. This is the same result as standard QM — CPP does not modify the Bell correlations, it explains their origin.
