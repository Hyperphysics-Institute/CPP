The decision that shaped the whole sweep was made before reading a single
paper: hold the 2988 method fixed rather than improvising a lighter check.
The temptation with five downstream papers is to grep for "phase-carrying"
and call each hit a conflict — but the QM-1 audit's value came precisely
from the separation of the inventory from the separability analysis, and
the separability half is where every verdict actually gets decided. So each
paper got a full read of its derivation spine, not just its grep hits.

The substantive judgment calls, in the order they arose:

**QM-2's Eq. (phaseedge) is not QM-1's Eq. (phasehop), even though it
looks like its twin.** QM-1's per-hop phase equation was decorative — never
consumed by the theorem (2988 Finding S-1). QM-2's per-edge phase IS
consumed: the interference fringes, the which-path prediction, the whole
point of the paper runs through φ_k's value. My first instinct was to class
this as math-layer contamination. What resolved it: asking what the
mathematics actually indexes. The sum is over PATHS; φ_k = S_k/ℏ is a
functional of path geometry + potential; no step reads or writes a register
on an individual bit. The equation is load-bearing as a path functional and
merely mis-labeled as bit content. That distinction — load-bearing vs.
mis-attributed bearer — is what keeps QM-2 at prose-layer with a re-label
obligation instead of a withdrawal. It is also the sweep's only genuinely
close call.

**QM-3 and QM-4 nearly classify themselves.** QM-3 line 166–167 already
says the joint state is "a property of the global Nexus ledger, not
carried locally by either particle" — the shipped paper disclaims local
carriage in its own words; the conflict is a naming convention ("DI-bit
state") five times over. QM-4 is the same class: every theorem is operator
algebra on a density matrix whose substrate identity is inherited, not
constructed. I checked whether the "random phase kicks" language smuggles
per-bit phase back in — it doesn't; those are bath-interaction phases
acting on the system state.

**QM-5 contains its own separability remark.** Lines 195–198: commutation
relations "follow purely from eigenmode orthonormality. No appeal to
'120°/240° phase geometry' is needed." A shipped paper pre-answering the
sweep's question about itself. And the ĉ_i operators act on site occupation
NUMBER — count-like, P-2-compliant — so the second-quantization structure
is actually better grounded under the new ontology than under the old one.

**QM-6 flips the severity ordering.** Going in, I expected QM-2 (the only
load-bearing equation) to be the top revision target. But QM-6's §1 states
the retired ontology as the series' four PRIMITIVES — and misclassifies
DI-bits as "relational quanta exchanged between CPs" against A1′'s
three-types ruling, a second, independent conflict I wasn't looking for.
A capstone with zero independent proofs turns out to be the most urgent
fix, because it's the normative entry point: anyone reading the series
through QM-6 receives the denied ontology as axioms. Severity is set by
normative position, not by mathematical load.

conflicts, and more — SF-6's shipped photon ontology (phase in the

blank-page derivation. This materially de-risks the P1 item and is the
