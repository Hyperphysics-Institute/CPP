# Tier-4 reasoning fragment — Patch 3009 (OPEN-QMRG-ETA: mode-independence derived)

**Captured at-patch, producing window. First post-arc patch: ordinary
bookkeeping, no conditionality machinery, panel touchpoint batchable.**

The pleasant surprise of this patch is that the hard-looking half of
OPEN-QMRG-ETA was the easy half, and for a principled reason. The
quarantine at 3006 split the unknown into a universality CLAIM
(mode-independence) and a numerical VALUE, and I had assumed both
would need the microscopic relay computation. They do not: the
universality claim falls to a cancellation between the same two
ratified clauses this entire arc has run on. A3' makes the turnover
rate the Moment cadence — a property of TIME ITSELF in this
substrate, not of any mode — so relayed content per Moment is N hbar
omega. I-3 makes the effective content per transit hbar omega. Divide,
and omega is gone. Mode-independence is not an extra assumption
propping up the quarantine; it is what the ratified structure forces.
The one number left, c_geo, is relay-multiplicity geometry: finite,
well-posed, and physically inert (it disappears into the convention
table the 3006 labeling obligation already governs).

The verify design faced a tautology risk squarely: a simulation that
IMPLEMENTS the two ingredients will of course show the cancellation.
The script's evidential content is therefore in the CONTROLS, which
break one ratified ingredient at a time: fixed-energy packets
(breaking I-3) send eta to slope +1.00 in omega; an
omega-proportional relay cadence (breaking A3' universality) sends
it to +1.02. The ratified pair alone sits at +0.00 across a 6.5x
frequency span with quantization noise live. That is the right shape
for a bookkeeping-grade result: the derivation carries the claim,
and the sim proves each premise is individually load-bearing rather
than decorative — the same rhetorical structure the 3005 moat
taught.

Key design applied the freshly amended rule's clause (c) for the
first time: the sentinel is a packet-count INTEGER at a random
unprinted configuration — a number with no theoretically anticipated
value (unlike the KEY-J virial coefficient, whose ~1.00 the record
itself predicted). Three key generations, three designs, each
correcting the last; the current one has no known reconstruction or
prediction route, and the next dispatch that wants a key has it
