# Tier-4 reasoning fragment — Patch 3006 (B1-CONST: derive-and-quarantine)

**Captured at-patch, producing window.**

The trigger said "derive or formally quarantine," and the work's
central insight was that the disjunction is answered by a PARTITION,
not a choice. GPT's CONV-015 dependency list (peak vs RMS vs complex;
per-oscillator vs per-mode; quadrature counting; participation
fraction; the definition of mu) reads at first like five reasons the
coefficient is out of reach. Sorted by KIND, it is four convention
questions and one physics question. Convention questions are not
unknowns — they are un-DECLARED choices, and once the convention is
declared (cycle-averaged mean-square of the in-plane register, which
is what the per-Moment sampling of a rotating pattern operationally
produces), each row of the table is a one-line derivation. The virial
theorem gives the mean-square coefficient as exactly 1. The peak
convention's 2 is trigonometry. And the canonical 1/2 — the number
the whole "1/(2 omega)" mystique hangs on — turned out to be the most
physical statement in the patch: a ROTATING planar vector has
constant |S|^2 while each Cartesian quadrature time-averages to half
of it. In CPP's picture the phase vector literally rotates (that is
what phase IS under FI-QMRG-1), so the canonical per-quadrature half
is not a normalization choice inherited from field theory — it is
what rotation does to components. I find this genuinely satisfying:
the re-grounding arc began by relocating phase onto a rotating
register, and the relocation now EXPLAINS a factor that canonical
quantization postulates.

The one genuine physics unknown is eta = rho/N — how many registered
DI-bit counts maintain one mode quantum. That is lattice physics
(the microscopic turnover law and participation structure), and
pretending to derive it from the toy would have been exactly the
overclaim class this arc exists to correct. The quarantine is
therefore formal in the strong sense: eta is a NAMED registered
parameter with a NAMED checkable claim (mode-independence in the
linear regime — the claim that makes the constant a constant), and
the record states what remains barred until its lattice derivation
(exact rho-calibrated amplitudes).

The verify design's load-bearing element is check (d), and it took
some thought to see WHY it is the right check. "Formally quarantine"
is only meaningful if the quarantine boundary is real — if the
derived layer is provably indifferent to the quarantined one.
Turnover details (gamma) control how much energy the mode holds for
given input; the derived layer is the displacement-per-stored-energy
relation. The gamma-scan varies the stored energy by a factor of 17
and the coefficient moves from 1.000 to 0.987 — and even that drift
is the PREDICTED O((gamma/omega)^2) virial correction, quantifying
the regime edge rather than contaminating the layer. A clean
separation, measured rather than asserted, is what lets the corpus
say "the proportionality and its coefficient are proved; one named
number is not" without the two claims bleeding into each other.

Panel-attack surface I disclosed deliberately in the record: (i) the
convention declaration itself (is cycle-averaged mean-square the
right operational reading of the register statistic? — I believe the
P-3/3002 cycle-sampling argument makes it so, but the declaration is
a choice and the panel should test it); (ii) E = N hbar omega's
standing HERE — it was I-3 of the adjudicated 3002 derivation, but
the exact coefficient now leans on it harder than the proportionality
did, and a reviewer may reasonably ask whether quantized mode energy
is available at this point in the derivation order without
circularity; (iii) whether eta's mode-independence is the right
quarantine boundary. These go in the CONV-016 dispatch as the
sharpest W-entries.
