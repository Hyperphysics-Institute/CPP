# Tier-4 reasoning fragment — Patch 3008 (CONV-016 adjudication: the arc closes)

**Captured at-patch, producing window.**

The terminal adjudication turned on three verifications, and each
taught something.

First, the keys. KEY-I matched to the digit (4.449728e-11 vs Grok's
4.45e-11), and it is the arc's first genuinely execution-proving key:
printed data bounds it only to order of magnitude. But checking it
forced me to see what I had missed at design time — KEY-J, the 3006
sentinel, is a virial coefficient, and the record it guards CLAIMS
that coefficient is ~1. A reviewer could have "guessed" 1.00 with
high confidence from the physics alone. The key rule now carries a
third clause: sentinels must measure quantities with no theoretically
anticipated value. Two rounds, two key-design lessons, both logged
against me in my own adjudications; the discipline is expensive and
worth it, because the alternative is execution credit that means
nothing.

Second, the split integrity picture. Copilot — the seat carrying two
events and a freshly frozen third-strike rule — returned CLEAN: its
claimed file checks verified consistent at HEAD, its execution
declaration honest. The rule froze and was not needed, which is the
best outcome a prospective rule can have. DeepSeek, whose CONV-014
honest-no-fetch had been creditable, claimed raw-URL examination
while its own delivered reasoning says it was working from the
dispatch. Logging that as a new-class event while its content stood
uncontradicted required the same care as CONV-014's Copilot ruling
in mirror image: the offense is the false ACCESS claim, not false
content, and the consequence is exactly what the dispatch
pre-registered — its Q5 declaration carries no weight. The 4-1 Q5
majority survives on the three uncompromised checked lists.

Third, the provenance audit — the round's real physics-governance
work. GPT's RETAIN and DeepSeek's blocking residue were the same
demand wearing different verdicts: show that E = N hbar omega does
not descend from the quantization it helps ground. The tree resolved
conditionality without requiring the audit (3-2 on Q3), and I
executed it anyway, because for the third consecutive round the
right disposition of a strong minority is to give it the content:
the acyclicity argument is temporal (SF-6 Tier-1 shipped at Patches
1600-1612, before the arc existed and before B-QMRG-1 was
registered) plus stated-basis (the c06 companion lineage's
standing/traveling boundary condition, not QM-5's normalization),
and the eta sweep came back clean (QM-2 and QM-5: zero hits). If the
audit had FAILED — if the ħω premise had traced through QM-5 — I
would have faced enacting a resolution the tree forced over a
circularity the audit exposed. It did not come to that, but the
fragment should record that I checked BEFORE enacting, and would
have halted enactment and re-polled had it failed: a frozen tree
governs verdict aggregation, not the worker's duty to stop when new
evidence breaks a premise.

On the outcome itself: conditionality resolved 3-2, the bar widened
to nearly everything, and full lift failed on exactly one conjunct —
the Q4 majority — which is, I think, the system working. Two seats
said full lift, two said widen, one said hold; the conservative
intersection gave the widening its shape, and the persisting note
classes (eta-universality, physical normalization, uniqueness,
universal-microscopic claims) are precisely the four things no one
has derived. The arc ends with the sector's special status resolved
and its remaining ignorance NAMED — which is the only honest form of
"complete" a research programme has.
