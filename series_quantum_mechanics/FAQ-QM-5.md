### Q1. "Does CPP add anything beyond standard QFT?"

Three things: (a) UV completion — loop integrals finite from Planck cutoff; (b) fermion statistics derived from CP non-co-occupation rather than postulated; (c) eigenvalue bridge — the same six 600-cell eigenvalues that are QFT dispersion modes (QM-5) select electroweak boson topologies (EW-1 through EW-5). This unification is not available within QFT alone.

### Q2. "Does finite renormalisation solve the hierarchy problem?"

It replaces infinite fine-tuning with large-but-finite fine-tuning. The hierarchy problem becomes: why is m_H/m_P ≈ 10⁻¹⁷? This is the same as OPEN-P-EW-1 (the η derivation). Infinite → finite is progress; 10⁻¹⁷ remains unexplained.
