## Running the committed instrument before writing a word (3234)

The founder's ask was "draft Spin III from the materials," and the materials
included a committed computation — so the computation was executed before
the paper was outlined, on the principle that a paper drafted around an
unexecuted script inherits whatever the script would have told us, unseen.
It told us something:

- **Parts 1–2 reproduce bit-identically.** `diff` of the regenerated CSV
  against `data/spin3_eigenvalues.csv`: no differences. The continuum
  spectrum and its FD verification are solid.
- **Part 3 fails its own diagnostic at BOTH 8k and the default 20k
  points** — "(Increase --n-points for cleaner mode identification)" — and
  the failure is structural, not statistical. The low spectrum at both
  sizes is a 4-fold near-degenerate group followed by a gap (0.0115×4 at
  20k; 0.018×4 at 8k). Four near-degenerate lowest modes in a 4D domain is
  the dipole signature: the kNN graph Laplacian on uniform interior samples
  approximates the CLOSED (free-boundary) problem, and nothing in the
  construction ever encodes the CP-Exclusion Dirichlet node at r = 0. The
  open–closed Mode 2 cannot appear in an instrument that solves the closed
  problem. Consistently, the committed figure has panels for Parts 1, 2 and
  4 only — the March session never recorded a Part-3 identification either.

This became the V0's central honest finding rather than a footnote, because
the alternative — a draft that cites Part 3 as "24-cell verification" on the
strength of its docstring — is precisely the overclaim the arc's own
development notes forbade ("I don't want to write a paper that asserts the
600-cell connection without being able to prove it").

observation with a checkmark. Given a0 = r_C/α and r_th = r_C/2, the ratio

addition a V0 assembly may make: it strengthens claims already present in
the materials without introducing any new mechanism.

## The verify script's own bug, kept for the record

First version of check 1 imposed the centered-ghost Neumann condition by
editing one off-diagonal entry — breaking matrix symmetry, which `eigsh`
silently assumes; the eigenvalues were garbage (17× errors) and all five
FD checks failed. Fix: the standard symmetric lumped-mass formulation
(generalized problem M u = k² B u, half mass at the free node). Lesson in
the same family as 3228's two traps: symmetric solvers do not warn on
asymmetric input. Final: 10/10.
