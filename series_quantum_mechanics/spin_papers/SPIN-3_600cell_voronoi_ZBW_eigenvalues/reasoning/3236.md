## Instrument design choices, and their declared costs

- Angular operator: cotangent Laplacian + Voronoi-area lumped mass on an
  icosphere (levels 2 and 3 as the two densities). Radial: per-ray FEM in
  s = r/R(ω), Dirichlet at s=0, natural at s=1 — on the sphere this
  assembly is EXACT separable FEM, which is why the control comes out at
  π/2 to five decimals.
- The one honest approximation: the angular coupling weight is
  shell-averaged (w_j ∝ 1/(⟨R⟩ s_j²)), and ray-coordinate cross-terms are
  dropped — both exact in the sphere limit, both entering at anisotropy²
  (~1% positions for the dodecahedron's ±11% radial variation). Declared in
  the script header, the record, and the paper.
- Isotropy score: mass-weighted fraction of a mode's energy carried by its
  angular-mean profile. On both domains it cleanly separates the radial
  family (scores 0.999–1.000) from everything else — the diagnostic the
  March sign-counter lacked.
- 24-cell leg deferred WITH REASON, not dropped silently: the published
  radial problem is 3D (u = r·psi); a 4D-cell run answers a
  differently-dimensioned question. Founder's "as you see best" covers
  this; the historical instrument stays in the record.
