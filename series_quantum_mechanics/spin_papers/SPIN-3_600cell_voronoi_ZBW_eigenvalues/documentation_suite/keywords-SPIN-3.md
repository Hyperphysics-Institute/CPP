# Keywords — spin-III

**Primary:** Zitterbewegung; electron spin; spin-1/2 derivation; 600-cell
lattice; Voronoi cell; regular dodecahedron; icosahedral symmetry; mode
selection; Conscious Point Physics.

**Secondary:** boundary perturbation theory; character theory; invariant
harmonics; open–closed resonator; graph Laplacian; icosphere
discretization; frozen-verdict measurement; crystal-field selection rules.

**PACS/MSC (nearest):** 03.65.-w (quantum mechanics), 02.20.-a (group
theory), 61.50.Ah (crystal symmetry theory); MSC 20C35, 35P15, 81Q35.

**Elevator pitch:** The electron's spin-½ standing wave lives inside one
cell of a Planck-scale icosahedral lattice. We prove the cell's symmetry
forbids every competing wave below a high threshold — so the lattice must
select exactly the mode that yields ħ/2 — and confirm it with a
sphere-validated measurement whose residuals the theorem predicted.

**SEO:** derivation of electron spin from lattice geometry; icosahedral
selection rules; dodecahedral resonator eigenmodes; Zitterbewegung
standing wave; discrete space spin one-half.
