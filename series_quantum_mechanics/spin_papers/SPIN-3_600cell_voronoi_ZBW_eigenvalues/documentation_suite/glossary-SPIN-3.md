# Glossary — spin-III

- **ZBW (Zitterbewegung) cloud** — the polarization pattern of the Dipole
  Sea around a bare charged CP; its thermal boundary sits at
  r_th = ħ/(2m_e c).
- **u-field** — u = r·ψ; the variable in which Spin II's resonator is an
  open–closed 1D string (node at center, free end at r_th).
- **True Voronoi cell** — the regular dodecahedron: the territory of one
  Grid Point in the 600-cell lattice (600-cell dual = 120-cell).
- **Support function** — R(ω) = ρ_in / max_f(ω·n̂_f): the cell's boundary
  radius in direction ω, over the 12 icosahedral face normals.
- **Trivial irrep (A-sector)** — the icosahedrally-invariant subspace of
  modes; the home of the ZBW cloud's scalar breathing states.
- **Forbidden channels** — spherical-harmonic orders l = 1..5 (and all odd
  l under I_h parity): no icosahedrally-invariant harmonic exists there —
  exact character arithmetic, not smallness.
- **ε₆** — the relative l=6 harmonic amplitude of the cell boundary
  (0.0510 for the dodecahedron); the leading anisotropy channel.
- **Selection Theorem** — within the trivial irrep, the first
  anisotropy-carrying mode sits at kR = 8.211 > 3π/2, so the second
  invariant mode IS Spin II's anchoring Mode 2.
- **Protection Theorem** — fractional node/antinode positions shift only
  at O(ε₆²) under the cell's anisotropy (first order killed by symmetry).
- **Interlopers** — the l=1⊕l=2 multiplets (8 states) lying between the
  radial modes globally but excluded from the invariant sector.
- **MODE2-RECOVERED** — the frozen measurement verdict: one interior zero
  in [0.60,0.73], antinode in [0.28,0.40], stable across two densities.
- **Scale bridge** — the exact identity r_in(I)/r_in(II) =
  6/[4α(1+√2)²] = 35.2675 linking Spin I's orbital radius to Spin II's
  resonator geometry.
