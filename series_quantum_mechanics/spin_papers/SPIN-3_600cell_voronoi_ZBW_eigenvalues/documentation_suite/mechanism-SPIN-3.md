   field obeys a radial wave equation; CP Exclusion pins u(0) = 0; the

3. **Symmetry forbids the competitors**: the icosahedral group admits no
   invariant harmonic for l = 1..5 (five-class character sum, exact). The

   within the invariant sector, the lowest l=6-carrying mode sits at

5. **The instrument confirms**: solving the u-equation directly on the
   true cell (sphere-validated) returns MODE2-RECOVERED — node 0.6670 vs

not delete the eight interloper modes from the spectrum; it expels them
