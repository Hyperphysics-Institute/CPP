## Part II — Candidate filler for the middle: qDP chaining + eDP buffering = saturation

**Intuition (founder).** qDPs chain head-to-tail (± dipole polarization + color/strong longitudinal bonding → *strong* chains). eDPs (also ± dipoles) coat the chains. The coating prevents qDPs from approaching *longitudinally side-by-side in overwhelming numbers* — chains bond only where they cross more *orthogonally*, forming **nodes** in a web of qDP substructure across fractal generations → the **nodes, filaments, voids** of intergalactic space. Side-to-side color attraction between chains drives alignment → filaments → spherical concentrations, *depleting the voids* (qDP density → 0 between concentrations). The restraining force against collapse into a *galactic glueball* is the **eDP chaperoning** of chain-to-chain bonding. Collider evidence: glueballs form only in high-energy environments — i.e. the eDP buffer is normally sufficient, overcome only at high energy.

**Physical contrast — what it plausibly fills (Era 2):** the eDP buffer is, structurally, a **saturation** mechanism — color attraction + eDP excluded-volume/screening repulsion → a stable *finite* equilibrium density rather than collapse. This is the physics of **nuclear saturation** (attraction + short-range hard core → saturation density, nuclei don't implode) and of any Lennard-Jones condensed phase. It is **mathematically formulable**: an effective potential / equation of state `V(ρ)` for the qDP+eDP medium with attraction + eDP repulsion → a minimum at `ρ_sat`; glueball-avoidance is `ρ_sat < ρ_confinement`. The collider fact (glueballs only at high energy) is exactly the signature of a saturation barrier overcome only above a threshold. **This is the strongest, most tractable part of the picture, and it connects to existing pieces** (qDP/eDP/color-force already in the framework; Step-1 σ/m fixes the interaction strength; excess-sourcing supplies gravity).

**A bonus the saturation buys (Era 4, discriminating):** a density that *saturates* rather than cusps gives a **central core, not a cusp** — the eDP buffer prevents the halo center from collapsing toward a glueball, flattening the inner profile. This is a natural candidate resolution of the **core–cusp problem** (observed dwarf cores vs ΛCDM cusps), analogous to SIDM / degeneracy-pressure cores — a *testable, discriminating* prediction, exactly the Tully-Fisher-class handle the arc wants.

**Physical contrast — three tensions in the *strong-force-drives-the-web* reading (do not wave away):**
1. **Scale range.** Color/strong forces are short-range (confined/screened). A side-to-side color attraction acts at sub-quantum scales; the cosmic web is at Mpc — ~40 orders of magnitude apart. It cannot drive Mpc structure unless the chains percolate as a space-filling fractal, and even then see (2). There is also an *internal* tension: the color force must be *unshielded* to attract at range but *shielded* (eDP-buffered) to avoid the glueball — it cannot be both at once.
2. **Statistics matching.** The observed web (filaments/nodes/voids) is, in ΛCDM, the *gravitational* growth of the adiabatic seeds (Zel'dovich), and it matches the matter power spectrum precisely. A strong-force-driven (percolation/aggregation) web has *different* statistics set by the bonding dynamics; reproducing the gravitational web's statistics would require coincidence or fine-tuning.
3. **Rigidity vs CDM.** A strong-force-bonded chain/filament network is an *elastic, connected* structure (gel/solid) — it carries stress, i.e. a pressure. But the DM phenomenology (collisionless bounds, velocity-dispersion-supported halos, pressureless transfer) is built on CDM behaving as a gas of effectively-free point masses. A bonded space-spanning web is not pressureless CDM.

**The resolution — the hybrid (gravity for the web, qDP/eDP for the micro & core):**
- **Large scale:** *gravity* (the only long-range force) drives the concentration — the qDPs gravitationally cluster, tracing EU-1's adiabatic seeds, CDM-like, matching the web and power spectrum (Eras 0,1,3,4 inherit standard CDM).
- **Local / micro:** the strong-force chaining + eDP buffering set the internal structure — chain formation, the saturation density, glueball-avoidance (Era 2), and the central **core** (Era 4).
- **The constraint that makes the hybrid work:** the eDP buffer must prevent not only longitudinal glue-balling but also the *macro-aggregation* of chains into a rigid space-spanning web — it must keep the qDPs a gravitationally-clustering **"dust" of buffered chain-segments**, not a strong-force-bonded solid. That is the precise statement the buffering has to deliver.
