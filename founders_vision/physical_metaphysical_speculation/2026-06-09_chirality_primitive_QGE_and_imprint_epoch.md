## Thread 1 — entropy-maximization as the qualitative shadow of the C-1 closure

These were later replaced by quantitative requirements — the 600-cell geometry, CP properties, and rules of relationship. **Physical contrast.** The intuition is correct, and precisely so. Strip the chirality-specific language off the 0828 closure and what remains is a statement about a *phase*: `ρ(M) < 1` (the spectral radius of the det-coset sign-correlation matrix below 1) is the **disordered, high-entropy, symmetry-unbroken phase** of the order parameter η; `ρ(M) ≥ 1` would be the ordered, condensed, low-entropy phase. The content of CAPACITY-1 is therefore "the substrate, left to its own dynamics, stays in the maximum-entropy phase over η — it does not pay the entropic price to condense." The old two-quantity story was not discarded when the programme went quantitative; it was *instantiated*. Energy conservation ↔ the coupling structure (the 600-cell geometry + CP rules setting how strongly neighbouring η correlate). Entropy maximization ↔ the fact that that coupling sits **off-critical**, so fluctuations win and nothing orders. The Landau picture used in the closure — the sign of μ² — is exactly the free-energy balance between those two, and "the search for the calibration that selected chirality" the founder remembers is, in hindsight, the search for `sign(μ²)`. The result: μ² > 0, entropy wins.

**Where it could become rigorous (a real next move).** State the substrate dynamics as a genuine entropy / free-energy variational principle and check whether (a) it reproduces μ² > 0 from a *maximization* rather than from the spectral bound, and (b) the PCD "Perceive" step (inherently a 4-D per-vertex orientation read) falls out as the guarantee of **pointwise non-degeneracy of the dynamical η** — which is the one *located residual* the enacted verdict still rests on (whether the substrate guarantees `p(v) ≥ 4` at every vertex). That would turn the nap intuition into the closure of the residual. Conjecture, not done.

## Thread 2 — does chirality need a *discriminatory primitive*?

**Intuition (founder).** Now that chiral behaviour is confirmed primitive, is a "discriminatory primitive" helpful or necessary? And — refined — the single CP does not exhibit choice-based chirality (C-1 proved the PCD rule layer is symmetric), so the question is whether a *group* of CPs together may carry a primitive discriminator.

**Physical contrast.**
- The thing that makes entropy-maximization deliver V3 is that it is **symmetric under η → −η** — non-discriminatory. A discriminatory primitive is the *opposite* of what closed CAPACITY-1; its **absence** is the verdict's content. A discriminator built into the dynamics would give η a preferred sign, break the symmetry, and land in V1/V2 (emergence), which was excluded. Introducing a new substrate-level discriminator now would be a chirality-*generating* mechanism and would *undo* the just-confirmed V3.
- The discriminator is **already collective, and already present**: FI-C-9 is the frozen orientation of the *whole 600-cell lattice* (the global enantiomorph n̂), not a property of any single CP. So "the group, not the individual CP rule" is not a new proposal — it is an accurate description of where the framework *already* puts the handedness. C-1's addition is the proof that the *local dynamics* of the collective do not generate a *second* one by condensation.
- The framework has established, 3/3-reviewed (STATUS-2, TARROW-1), that there is **no axiom-level discriminator** — every static-geometric invariant is parity-even, and the only primitive pseudoscalar is FI-C-9 itself (so a substrate sign-fixer would be circular). The single admissible discriminator door is *cross-sector*: the SM CP/T phase, parked at **OPEN-SM-4**, CPT-linked to the temporal arrow.

**Net.** No *new* discriminating primitive is helpful or necessary for what is enacted. FI-C-9 is the discriminator already in hand (collective, frozen); the entropy-maximizing dynamics around it are non-discriminatory; and the only place a discriminator could legitimately enter is OPEN-SM-4.

## Thread 3 — the QGE, panpsychism, and the combination problem

**Intuition (founder).** The QGE (Quantum Group Entity): a postulate from the qualitative-derivation era that component consciousnesses meld into a larger unity at many levels (lepton–quark, atomic, molecular, crystalline/amorphous, …), such that aggregation reliably produces conformations summing to a new entity. The founder's landed position (this session): **panpsychist on the substrate, but the *weak/relational* reading of group entities** — not "the spirit of a chair" as a standalone strong entity, but an *imprint that requires relationship and function*; the residue of violence, the personality of a plant, team spirit, the sacred mountain — spirit-in-relationship, a conformation of layers, rather than a new free-standing being.

**Physical contrast.**
- Strong vs. weak emergence is a genuinely open question in philosophy of science; "weak" (higher patterns reducible in principle, novel only epistemically) is the safe default; "strong" (genuinely new causal powers) is a real minority position not decisively killed. The founder explicitly rejects the strong form — consistent with the relational/imprint reading.
- A melding of micro-experiences into a unified macro-experience is exactly the **combination problem** in panpsychism — a *named, recognized, unsolved* problem, not a fuzzy intuition. If CPP attributes proto-experience to CPs (the PCD "Perceive" step flirts with this), then the QGE *is* a combination-problem proposal, and that is the precise frame in which to develop it.
- "Can a group carry a chirality discriminator the parts don't" has a hard-science twin: **biological homochirality** (all-L amino acids, all-D sugars) — chirality robust at the molecular/biological aggregate level, with origin-of-life research split between pure dynamical amplification of a seed bias (cf. the Soai autocatalytic reaction) and a deeper source (e.g. electroweak parity violation). CPP's current stance is a *definite, falsifiable* position in that debate: there is **one** frozen substrate handedness (FI-C-9), and all higher-level chirality is *propagated* from it (the SD-CHIR / Chirality Continuum arc), **not** independently generated at each tier. The strong QGE picture (aggregation *adds* chirality content per level) is the opposite claim; it would require a *different* order parameter, non-local or hierarchical in a way that escapes C-1's pointwise-non-degenerate class. That is the tractable version: "is there a hierarchical chirality order parameter outside C-1's class?" — answerable — rather than "is there a group spirit?" — not decided by the physics.

## Thread 4 — the imprint epoch: where/when was the substrate handedness set?

**Intuition (founder).** Candidate epochs for the chirality imprint: (i) first-moment Big-Bang fiat over all creation; (ii) inflationary-era imprint; (iii) a late universe-wide precipitation (Grok's "Capotauro at ~120 Myr" — *feels too late*); (iv) **R-sectors and L-sectors** — volumes of space each with its own chirality, set by local early-universe imprints, with collisions/swirls in the primordial maelstrom producing two-sided (R/L) structures and the L-side as the angular-momentum complement of the R-side. The founder likes (iv) for **conservation** (net chirality zero globally), is uncomfortable that a single universe-wide choice "feels like divine fiat," and notes (iv) is awkward for a relativity principle but believes it is empirically inaccessible anyway.

**Physical contrast.**
- **The premise "we could never do the experiment" is no longer quite true.** There is an active observational programme. The lowest-order statistic that can detect cosmic-scale parity violation is the galaxy **four-point correlation function**, which counts quadruples of galaxies forming a **tetrahedron** — the minimal 3-D shape not superimposable on its mirror by rotation (a pleasing resonance with "Tetrahedrons All the Way Down"). The BOSS survey yielded a parity-violation signal at up to ~7σ in some analyses and ~2.9σ in a blind test (Philcox 2022; Hou, Cahn et al. 2023), but the significance is **contested** (covariance-estimation systematics; Cahn et al. 2023). CMB cosmic birefringence shows a hint that rose 2.4σ → 3.6σ (Minami & Komatsu 2020; Eskilt 2022), possibly contaminated by Galactic dust; the CMB 4PCF is **consistent with parity conservation** (Philcox 2023), in tension with the galaxy signal. Contested and unconfirmed — but a real handle, sharpening with DESI/Euclid/Rubin.
- **Decisive constraint against literal R/L domains: the domain-wall problem.** Independent local breaking of a discrete (ℤ₂) chirality symmetry across causally-disconnected regions is the Kibble mechanism, which generically produces domains separated by **domain walls** (Zel'dovich–Kobzarev–Okun 1974). Wall energy density dilutes too slowly; a wall network would dominate the budget and wreck CMB smoothness. A literal R/L patchwork *inside the observable universe* is essentially excluded — unless the symmetry is broken *explicitly* (one handedness preferred; kills the conservation), or inflation intervenes.
- **The conservation intuition has a famous twin, and nature's verdict is instructive.** "Globally chirality-symmetric, split into balancing R/L sectors" is structurally identical to **matter–antimatter symmetric domain cosmology**. That idea was excluded (Cohen–De Rújula–Glashow 1998): no annihilation gamma-rays at domain boundaries ⇒ for any domain smaller than ~the observable universe, ruled out. Nature appears to have made **one global choice** (matter over antimatter), enabled by a parity/CP-violating process (Sakharov 1967: B-violation + C and CP violation + departure from equilibrium). The "fiat" the founder dislikes is, for matter/antimatter, replaced by *a CP-violating mechanism* — not fiat, but also not a conserved patchwork.
- **The reconciliation that dissolves the fiat-vs-natural dilemma: inflation + the EU-1 contact era.** The founder's own EU-1 picture — the era of full cross-universe light-speed contact — is the mechanism that makes a universe-wide *single* imprint **natural** rather than fiat: if the chirality symmetry breaks while the whole (then causally-connected) volume is in contact, the choice **correlates across the whole patch by causality** — one handedness, homogeneous, no fiat. Inflation then stretches that single domain far beyond today's horizon. This satisfies *both* intuitions at once: **locally** (inside our horizon) one homogeneous handedness — the "universe-wide imprint" appearance, now causal not fiat; **globally** (super-horizon) the conserved R/L patchwork survives — each domain inflated beyond the horizon, so we see only our own; and the **domain walls are inflated away** (the standard inflationary cure for the Kibble defect problem). So the founder's "120 Myr seems too late" instinct is *correct and principled*: a late synchronized imprint across already-disconnected regions is the one that genuinely requires acausal coordination (fiat) or re-creates the walls. The imprint wants to be set **while the volume is still in contact** — very early / contact-era / inflationary.
- **A genuine counter-tension (do not wave away).** The cosmology literature notes a *primordial* parity violation "would be at odds with most models of inflation" — standard single-field inflation tends to *erase* such asymmetries (part of why the BOSS hint is provocative). So "early + inflationary" is the natural CPP scenario, but making inflation *preserve* rather than dilute the chirality imprint is a real model-building constraint, not a free lunch.
- **The relativity-principle worry is smaller than feared.** Different domains with different handedness do not violate Lorentz invariance — the *laws* are identical in each, only the frozen vacuum orientation differs, exactly as ferromagnetic domains do not violate the rotational invariance of electromagnetism. Spontaneous vacuum selection, not a law that changes between sectors. It is the domain *walls*, not the relativity principle, that do the damage.

**CPP placement.** This is exactly the open content of **OPEN-SM-4 sub-claim (a)** — "the Capotauro nucleation event, the universe-wide sign-selection event downstream of the magnitude mechanism, remains open." The temporal verdict already names the arrow's origin as *cosmological inheritance — a low-entropy boundary state*, and by CPT the spatial handedness shares that origin; the framework is therefore *already committed* to "inherited from an early boundary condition" (the V3/W3 content), and the open question is *which* boundary / *which* epoch. The framework's own logic, plus the domain-wall and matter-antimatter precedents, points to the **early causal-contact/inflationary imprint** — homogeneous within our horizon by causality, conserved globally as super-horizon R/L structure — and away from both the late-precipitation and the bare-fiat readings.
