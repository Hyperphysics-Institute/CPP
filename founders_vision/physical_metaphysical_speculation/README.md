# founders_vision/physical_metaphysical_speculation/

**The segregated speculative register: metaphysical ideas held in contact with physical constraint.**

This folder is the home for the programme's *disciplined speculation* — the place where the founder's metaphysical intuitions (QGE / group entities, consciousness aggregation, the qualitative origin stories, the "spirit"/imprint readings, cosmological just-so pictures) are written down **next to the physical constraints, data, and open problems they must answer to.** It is deliberately walled off from the reviewed corpus.

## The admission standard (the reason this folder exists)

Per the founder's stated principle:

> *The physical and the metaphysical should always be considered in the same space, since the metaphysical is always physical to some extent. If the metaphysical isn't contrasted with the physical, it's just imagination.*

So the entry criterion is not "is this idea correct" (it usually isn't, yet) but **"is each metaphysical claim placed in contact with a physical constraint, datum, or open problem?"** An entry that asserts a metaphysical picture *without* contrasting it against the physics does not belong here — it is imagination, and belongs (if anywhere) in the broader `founders_vision/` archive as pure vision. An entry that takes a metaphysical intuition and asks "what would the physics have to look like for this to be true, and what rules it in or out?" belongs here. Every entry should read as two interleaved columns: the intuition, and its physical contrast.

## What this folder is NOT (the guardrails)

- **Nothing here is a claim or a result.** No entry registers a finding, a theorem, a prediction, or a conjecture-ID in the reviewed registries.
- **Nothing here moves a verdict.** The chirality verdicts (V3/W3, CAPACITY-1, TARROW-2), the predictions swarm, and the theorem registry are untouched by anything in this folder.
- **Nothing here has been swarm-reviewed.** These are exploratory conversations, not adjudicated artifacts.
- **A future window must not lift anything from here into the frontier as established.** Material may *graduate* — but only by the normal road: an independent derivation, registered as a candidate, then swarm-reviewed. A pointer from an open problem (e.g. OPEN-SM-4) to an entry here is **advisory only** — it tells a future worker "the constraint landscape for this question has been mapped speculatively," not "this is the answer."

## Relationship to the rest of the corpus

- `founders_vision/` (parent) — pure vision and origin narrative.
- `founders_vision/physical_metaphysical_speculation/` (here) — vision **in contact with** physics; the speculative bridge layer.
- `frontier_sectors/`, `theorem-registry.md`, the predictions registry — the reviewed, hard-physics layer. Verdicts and findings live only here.

The value of keeping this register *on the record and clearly labeled* (rather than hiding it, as most programmes do) is intellectual honesty about where the framework's intuitions come from, and a documented map of which speculative roads have already been walked and what constraints they hit.

## Naming convention

`YYYY-MM-DD_<scope>.md` — date-prefixed for chronological sort, scope-slug for findability.
