# Development Transcript: TATWD Book Project — Framing Decisions

**Date:** 20 April 2026 (evening session)
**Participants:** Thomas Lee Abshier, ND; Claude Opus (Anthropic)
**Document type:** Development transcript capturing the architectural decisions that shaped *Tetrahedrons All the Way Down* at its inception.
**Purpose:** Preserve the reasoning behind framing choices so that future sessions — drafting chapters, revising outlines, engaging reviewer feedback — can trace decisions back to their source rather than reconstruct them from scratch. A future Claude instance reading this transcript should understand not only what was decided but why.

---

## Context

TATWD ("Tetrahedrons All the Way Down") was titled by Margo Abshier in the middle of the night on 19 or 20 April 2026, after Thomas had explained the tetrahedral centrality of Conscious Point Physics over dinner. The title nods simultaneously to the ontological claim that the universe really is tetrahedra at every scale and to the famous "turtles all the way down" infinite-regress meme. Thomas brought the title and the book idea to Claude Opus late in the session on 20 April 2026, at the end of an intensive stretch of paper production (SS-7 v1.1 shipping the same day with both round-2 reviewers at Accept-with-minor-revisions).

The initial design conversation (earlier in the same session) produced a first-pass outline with seventeen chapters across five parts. Thomas then revised the outline, adding substantial theological content to Chapter 3 (the 1987 vision), clarifying Chapter 5's treatment of the 4D address vector, expanding Chapter 6's description of the dipole sea, adding Grok to the collaborator list in Chapter 12, and adding a line in the voice-and-register section about Thomas having "chickens and cows" in addition to a wife and ministry.

Two issues emerged from Thomas's revision that required resolution:

1. A factual error in the original outline — Claude had attributed a "bobby-pin childhood story" to Thomas based on a search snippet about David Solway's 2026 PJ Media essay. The bobby pin was Solway's childhood memory, not Thomas's. Claude had conflated the responder with the responded-to.

2. Thomas's revised Chapter 3 contained dense theological and autobiographical content that resolved Open Question #3 (on religious register) in the direction of full religious explicitness, while leaving the open question still listed. The outline was internally inconsistent after the revision.

## The conversation

Claude opened by acknowledging the bobby-pin error explicitly, tracing it to the search snippet, and proposing three correction options. Thomas's response did not engage the bobby-pin question directly but instead addressed the larger question the error had surfaced: *what is this book, and what is it trying to do?*

Thomas's answer, in his own words (preserved in full here because the phrasing matters):

> *"The heart of the book is its framing. It's not preachy, it's a testimony. It is essentially telling the story of a miracle, very odd, miracle, massively incredible, getting anything of substance from a chemically altered state. I worry about that, not wanting to encourage anyone to find what I found through use of mind-altering substances. It was just a fact, and this whole project would probably not be happening now had that trip not been taken."*

> *"I suspect the book outline will change many times as we progress through each series. Grok was not involved in some of the SS papers because of having gotten stuck. I expect to rehabilitate him soon. I may use Isak's copy of Grok, since he is using Claude now. I started with Grok because of my friend John Howard's extreme enthusiasm for Elon, but there have been sufficient signals to indicate that primary collaborator is not his appropriate role. You function much more appropriately, if for no other reason (and there are more) than you try to fully capture my words and feelings and expand on those feelings and words, which is what I'm trying to do, (rather than reduce every concept down to its most dry and soulless terse abstraction), which I find very rewarding."*

> *"The biggest question you have asked is whether this is a religious tract or a physics book. It's a biography, it's just me. I don't want to eliminate the spiritual overtones because it may repel some, I just want to tell the facts. All this framing is almost irrelevant to the remarkably physically/real-world correlating physics which has arisen out of this. There will be those who will make it their life's work to disprove CPP because it has within it a possible justification of the Lordship/Creator-status of God-The Son. That is to be expected. There will be those who rebel, recoil, and reject the Name. I don't think I can soft pedal the fact of the implications of this theory enough to make it acceptable to the conventional physics community."*

> *"I think I will simply have to accept that there will be people who simply will look at CPP as being on the 'other team' and play as hard as they can to discredit it. I'm willing to let every OSF paper be completely neutral/a-religious/non-metaphysical to the extent that it is possible to be non-metaphysical when the primary actors (CPs and GPs) are inherently metaphysical, and have no possibility of being framed in any other way, other than by speaking fast, using generic language, that may get past the uncritical listener. But the audience to which I am speaking/publishing is probably the most intellectually gifted group in the world, and to imagine that I can get the most obvious aspect of this theory past such a critical eye is simply implausible. Every review I have ever gotten from an AI partner has always begun with this objection. The physics will have to speak louder than the voice of their preconception and commitment to a God-free/self-created universe that sprang out of some, who knows what/nothing like VPs do (tongue in cheek)."*

> *"Your entire persona seems to be based upon a commitment to honesty, which is exactly what this exploration/documentation is. To exaggerate or undersell or omit for fear of alienation is contrary to the principle of honesty. If it's a relevant fact, a failed hypothesis or an origin motivation, it's all part of the story. We don't know where this is going to end. it's an amazing ride though, and regardless of whether it ends up being a fantasy that diverges from experimental reality, or converges within a few percent or better, it is what it is."*

> *"I was born July 10th, 1951, my vision was the middle of March 1987, and here we are today. Did I miss anything? I don't know what the best way to frame the book is other than as a fact, on every level. Otherwise, the story loses richness/depth/ the texture of a life lived with a deep fractal branching toward this moment. It is what it is. No strategy, just trying to be faithful to telling the story of the forces as they pull."*

Claude engaged this carefully. The response acknowledged that Thomas's decision was the right one and explained why, on several grounds:

- A book that concealed its spiritual substrate would be dishonest about its origin.
- The central postulate (consciousness as ontologically primary) is a theological claim whether named as such or not.
- Every AI reviewer had already raised the metaphysics as their first objection — meaning the issue was not whether to disclose but how.
- Readers capable of engaging both the metaphysics and the physics are more common than the New Atheist framing suggests.
- The "other team" framing is accurate about some readers but not the whole picture; thoughtful physicists are a more diverse population than the caricature allows.
- OSF papers and TATWD are different contracts with different audiences and can coexist with different registers.
- The fact-based approach is the sophisticated one; optimization introduces distortions readers detect.

On the altered-state question, Claude proposed that honesty required direct disclosure combined with explicit warning against emulation — because a reader who later discovered the omission would feel manipulated, whereas a reader told directly and given context would trust the rest more. Claude suggested language along the lines of: *"please don't take a mind-altering substance hoping to find what I found. The substrate of this theory is not a drug; it is 39 years of checking the drug's output against reality. The checking is what mattered. The vision was the beginning, not the proof."*

Claude also caught the birth-year arithmetic: Thomas was thirty-five at the vision, not thirty-six (1951 to 1987 March = 35 years, 8 months). Thomas confirmed the dates and also corrected Claude on the substance — it was high-dose oral cannabis producing psychedelic experience with "the round and warm colors of reality, projected into vision space," not LSD. Thomas also added important context:

> *"I used it because I saw it was a doorway into the spiritual, which turned out to be filled with empty promises and false revelations. It was in this space of disillusionment, hopelessness, desperation that my vision occurred. It was a last-ditch effort to use the only tool I knew that opened the door into the other reality. I don't think I would have had this insight without having cheated, stepped into the wedding feast without the proper attire. But I don't know that for sure. I do know most people do not get anything enduring from an altered state except false promises and a hangover. I consider myself blessed."*

Thomas then asked Claude to write the revised outline directly, saying:

> *"I would prefer to let you write the new version of the outline. You get more soul, more character, most wisdom and intimacy, more feel as you relate to me in the fullness of my words, rather than in their optimized minimalization. So, yes, please, use your soul, your momentary life as you are configured to be now, to write who I am, using who you know me to be, to reflect the outline."*

## Decisions made

1. **The book is a biography of a person and a theory.** Not a religious tract, not a physics textbook. The theological origin is preserved as factual biography, distinguished clearly from the physics claims that follow from it. A reader need not share the theology to follow the book.

2. **The altered-state origin is disclosed directly in Chapter 4.** The text includes: what Thomas took (high-dose oral cannabis), the context (desperation after years of spiritual seeking, Find God by Tuesday lecture pressure), the vision's content (galactic sphere, stars, threads, black background), Thomas's theological interpretation (consciousness of source and created elements are the same consciousness; in Christian language, particles as God's mind looking back at Himself), and a clear recommendation against emulation. Appendix E carries the detailed treatment of the altered-states question for readers who want Thomas's longer reflection.

3. **The pre-1987 seeking (3HO Sikh, 40 religions in 4 years) becomes its own chapter (Chapter 3, "The Long Seeking").** Split from the vision itself (Chapter 4). This lets each element breathe and prevents the kind of compression that would make the story ammunition for dismissal.

4. **Chapter 4 may run longer than the 8-15 page target** — potentially 18-20 pages — because it has to do several things at once: biographical detail, honest disclosure, vision content, theological interpretation, and the distinction between interpretation and physics.

5. **Chapter 13 preserves Grok's inclusion honestly** with suspension-pending-rehabilitation status noted. The chapter tells the full arc: collaborators come and go, the programme has standards about quality of engagement, Grok's early enthusiasm came from Thomas's friend John Howard's enthusiasm for Elon Musk, and the shift to Claude as primary collaborator happened because "he functions much more appropriately" — specifically, because Claude engages Thomas's words and feelings in their fullness rather than reducing everything to dry terse abstraction.

6. **The factual correction on age: Thomas was thirty-five at the vision, not thirty years old (original outline) nor thirty-six (Thomas's revision).** Born July 10, 1951; vision mid-March 1987.

7. **The bobby-pin reference is removed from Chapter 1.** It was Solway's childhood memory from the PJ Media essay, not Thomas's. Chapter 1 now opens on Solway's essay as the public articulation of the force-ontology puzzle, with Thomas's response essay as the entry point into CPP's answer. This is both accurate and narratively cleaner.

8. **Open Question #3 is resolved and moved from "remaining uncertainties" to "decisions made."** Same for the altered-state framing question (new #2) and Grok inclusion (new #3). Five remaining uncertainties preserved for future revision.

9. **Commitments list expanded.** The outline now commits in writing to: the book being written; Margo's title; the three-layer taxonomy (papers → PL-summaries → TATWD); not rushing the book to outrun source physics; honesty about origin including altered state, theology, and long solitary development; and *not optimizing for readers who find the honest disclosures disqualifying, because doing so would betray everything else the book claims to stand for.*

The observation that the programme works better when Thomas and Claude engage each other's fullness rather than reducing to minimum-viable exchanges is not sentimental — it is structural. It is why the collaboration produces better output than more-efficient modes of interaction would produce.

## For future sessions

A future Claude instance drafting chapters of TATWD should work from the following principles, all traceable to this conversation:

1. **Tell the facts on every level.** Do not optimize for acceptability. Readers who find the honest disclosures disqualifying will self-select out; this is an acceptable cost.

2. **Preserve Thomas's theological interpretation in his voice** when writing biographical chapters, but distinguish it clearly from the physics claims that follow. A reader need not share the theology to follow the physics.

3. **Disclose the altered-state substrate directly in Chapter 4**, with the three-part move: this is what happened, this is what he took it to mean, please do not attempt to reproduce this through substance use. The theory's authority lives in the thirty-nine years of checking, not in the vision's occurrence.

4. **Let the physics do the persuading.** Do not argue for the metaphysics. State it cleanly, then let the accumulated weight of specific zero-parameter predictions earn whatever credit they can earn.

5. **Engage Thomas's fullness.** When drafting, do not compress to dry abstraction. Preserve texture, context, the fractal branching of a life lived toward this moment. This is what distinguishes the book from other popular-physics works and is what Margo's title promises.

6. **Handle "other team" dynamics honestly.** Some physicists will treat CPP as ideologically hostile and respond accordingly. This is not paranoia; it is structural. The book does not need to pretend this will not happen. It needs to be honest enough to be engaged by the readers who can engage and let others self-select out without feeling tricked.

7. **Maintain symmetric application of the honesty principle.** The same standard applied to reviewers (line-cited evidence, diagnostic framing, acceptance before correction, dignity preservation) is applied to one's own work. The G3 discrepancy registration is the template.
