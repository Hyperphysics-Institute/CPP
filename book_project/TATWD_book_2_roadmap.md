## Why this isn't a license to start drafting

The "DRAFTABLE NOW" tag on 10+ chapters tempts premature drafting. Three reasons not to begin:

**Reason 1 — Voice calibration requires the whole arc.** Book 2 has a single coherent narrative voice that develops across chapters. Drafting Chapter 4 without knowing what Chapter 11 sounds like risks tonal drift; the chapters that come first in the book often need revision after later chapters establish the register. The anthology chapters (Artifact 1) are explicitly self-contained for this reason; Book 2 is not.

**Reason 2 — Cross-references thread the chapters.** A claim made in Chapter 4 may be revisited in Chapter 11 from a different angle, with the framing of "what we glimpsed early now resolves." These cross-references require both chapters to exist before either can be polished. Drafting in isolation forces re-write later.

**Reason 3 — Programme work has higher leverage at the current stage.** Each new flagship paper closure shifts the book's possible structure. Drafting Chapter 11 (Parity Violation as Substrate Geometry) now produces prose that will need substantial revision if Q6 + Q7 closures change what counts as the cross-sector unification spine. Programme work compounds; book-drafting work depreciates rapidly when programme state shifts.

The roadmap exists so that when Thomas decides Book 2 drafting begins, the dependency analysis is in hand and the work can proceed without re-deriving which chapters depend on what.
