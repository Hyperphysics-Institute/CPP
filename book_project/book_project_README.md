## The three-layer taxonomy (orientation for future sessions)

The CPP corpus has three distinct audiences and therefore three distinct works:

**Layer 1 — Technical papers** (e.g., `series_strong/papers/SS-7_alpha_cluster_edge_formula.tex`).
Audience: physicists, reviewers, the ViXra/OSF record. Voice: precise, formal, derivation-forward. Governed by `operating_system.md`. Status: actively shipping.

**Layer 2 — Plain-Language summaries** (e.g., proposed `lay-summary-SS-7.md`, one per paper).
Audience: the technical layperson — someone with a B.S. in a quantitative field, a curious reader of *Scientific American*, a retired engineer, a philosophy-of-science student. Voice: accessible but not condescending; assumes enthusiasm for physics but not specialized training. Governed by the documentation_suite pattern. Status: proposed (20 April 2026); first exemplar being produced concurrently with this directory.

The PL-summaries serve two purposes:
- Standalone accessibility for each paper's results to a broader audience than the technical paper reaches
- **Predigested source material** for the TATWD book, making it easier for a future writing session to assemble a narrative without re-translating from the technical papers each time

The PL-summaries themselves, compiled, may form a *second* book — a niche work for the technical layperson — distinct from TATWD.

**Layer 3 — *Tetrahedrons All the Way Down*** (this directory).
Audience: the general educated reader. Voice: narrative, personal where appropriate, builds the ontology before exposing specific results. Governed by its own standards, not the paper production workflow. Status: outlining.

The distinction that matters: TATWD is not a union of PL-summaries. It is a freshly-written narrative that *draws from* PL-summaries and papers but is organized by its own storytelling logic. A reader opens TATWD expecting to be taken on a journey from curiosity to comprehension; the journey has its own arc, not the arc of the paper series.

## Why Margo's title is right

"Tetrahedrons All the Way Down" accomplishes three things at once:

1. **States the ontological claim.** The universe is tetrahedra all the way down. The 600-cell — the fundamental space-discretization unit CPP posits — is built from 600 tetrahedral cells. Every proton, neutron, and nucleus in the theory is assembled from tetrahedral structures of tetrahedral structures of tetrahedral structures. The title is accurate.

2. **Signals self-awareness via the "turtles" reference.** The turtles-all-the-way-down story is a famous ontological meme: a response to infinite regress, often used to make fun of bad metaphysics. By invoking it, the title tells readers the authors know this is an unusual claim, are owning it with a wink, and are confident enough to joke about it. This is the right posture for a book asking readers to take seriously a non-standard theory of physics.

3. **Is memorable and shelves well.** Five words, concrete image, faintly mysterious. Unlike "Conscious Point Physics: A Theory of Everything" (which reads as grandiose) or "The 600-Cell Universe" (which sounds like sci-fi), TATWD is grounded and inviting.

## Draft timeline (aspirational, subject to programme priorities)

The programme's active priorities (20 April 2026) are SS-8 (OPEN-SS-22, icosahedral closure) and SS-9 candidate (OPEN-SS-24, C4 derivation). The book should not displace the physics work.

Realistic sequencing:
- **Now (20 April 2026):** This README, the outline document, and the first exemplar lay-summary (SS-7) are produced. The pattern is established.
- **Next several months:** Additional lay-summaries are produced incrementally as each existing paper is revisited and as new papers ship. Book outline is refined based on what material actually reads well in PL form.
- **When critical mass exists:** Begin TATWD chapter drafting. "Critical mass" is probably 4-6 lay-summaries plus completion of SS-8 (so that the narrative has a satisfying medium-mass-nuclei arc through iron peak).
- **Review cycle:** TATWD goes to 3-5 test readers (educated non-specialists, not physicists), not AI reviewers — the review pattern developed for papers does not transfer cleanly to trade writing.
- **Publication decision:** Self-publish vs. trade press. Trade press is high-effort and slow; self-publish is faster and preserves programme control. Decision deferred until a full draft exists.

Thomas's stated programme principle is territory-first: ship papers at quality threshold, defer polish. The same principle suggests the book should not be rushed — it will read as forced if it outruns the physics results it depends on.

## Relationship to the rest of the CPP programme

The book is an *outreach* work, not a *research* work. Its success metric is not peer acceptance but reader comprehension. A reader who finishes TATWD should be able to:

1. Explain to a friend what CPP claims, at a hand-wavy but accurate level
2. Understand why the 600-cell specifically, and not some other lattice
3. Grasp at least three specific predictions the theory has made and how they compare with experiment
4. Understand the difference between a zero-parameter prediction and a calibrated fit
5. Know what's still open and what would falsify the theory
6. Come away with a clear sense of who Thomas Abshier is, how this work started, and why a naturopathic physician in Montana developed a novel theory of physics over 39 years

That last item is important. The book needs a human center. The 1987 vision, the long solitary development, the recent AI collaboration — these are part of what makes the book work as narrative. Without the personal arc, it becomes a physics textbook for lay readers, which is not what "Tetrahedrons All the Way Down" promises.
