## The conjecture (precisely, as c08 states it)
c08 (`series_relativity/SR_companion_papers/c08_strong-field_GR.tex`) defines the **CPP field
equation** as a self-consistency condition between the LSP density and the metric it reconstructs:

> `M[ρ_LSP] = G[ M[ρ_LSP] ]`  — the metric reconstructed from the LSP density equals the LSP
> propagation operator evaluated on that metric (the CPP analog of Einstein's equations).

Its explicit form is a nonlinear operator `F` on the metric, `F(g[PSR_eff], …) = (8πG/c⁴) T`, with a
weak-field/excess reduction written as

> `∇²(Δ|SSV|) = (8πG/c⁴) T`   — the Laplacian of the **SSV excess** `Δ|SSV|` (the SSV *above* the
> local Sea ground state) is sourced by `T`.

**c08's central open problem (`op:einstein`):** whether the nonlinear `F` reduces, in the continuum
limit, **exactly** to `G_μν = R_μν − ½g_μν R = 8πG T_μν/c⁴` — i.e. whether CPP recovers Einstein
gravity — and, for our purposes critically, whether the source is the **excess** `Δ|SSV|`
(ground state *excluded*) rather than the **absolute** `|SSV|`. c08 declares this unsolved.

## Why it caps the whole dark sector (one conjecture, two ends)
Both CPP dark-sector results inherit `op:einstein` through the **excess-vs-absolute** question:

1. **CC suppression (SR-5 Step D2).** Ground-state exclusion — the reason the uniform Sea sources
   *zero* gravity and the vacuum catastrophe never appears — is exactly the claim that `T` couples to
   `Δ|SSV|`, not `|SSV|`. Step D2 is a **conditional** pass resting on this.
2. **DM R2 split (CC-U/5).** "Uniform Sea inert / swirls gravitate" is the *same* claim: uniform mode
   has `Δ|SSV| = 0` (inert), swirl inhomogeneities have `Δ|SSV| > 0` (gravitate as DM).

**The failure mode (the one wholesale kill).** If the rigorous reduction of `F` sources from absolute
`|SSV|` instead of the excess `Δ|SSV|`, then the ground state gravitates: the vacuum catastrophe
returns (Ω_Sea ~ 10⁴⁵–10¹²⁰) **and** R2's inert-Sea premise fails — the CC suppression and the
dark-matter identification break **together**. This is why CC-U/4 is the cap: everything else in the
arc is reconciliation of accounts that individually almost work; this is the single load-bearing
conjecture underneath all of them.

## What closing it would require
A derivation (not an assumed form) showing, in the continuum/weak-field limit of the nonlinear CPP
self-consistency operator `F`:
- **(a) GR recovery:** `F → G_μν = 8πG T_μν/c⁴` (the Einstein limit), and
- **(b) excess-sourcing:** the source is genuinely `∇²(Δ|SSV|) = (8πG/c⁴)T` with the ground state
  excluded — i.e. the excess form (c08 eq. near line 542) is *the* rigorous reduction, not a
  separately-posited ansatz.
Either result conditionalizes or unconditionalizes the entire dark sector at once.
