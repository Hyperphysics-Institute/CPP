# Is Capotauro Still Needed? — Assessment on the Founder's Question

**Patch:** 4116. **Lane:** EW (CHIR cross-lane). **Status:** assessment for a founder decision.
Nothing retired here; retiring a shipped flagship is the founder's call.

**Founder's question (18 Sep 2026):** *"It looks like we derived all the chiral, mirror, and
parity effects using the 4D helical bit. It seems we may no longer need the postulate of a
Capotauro event … The Capotauro was Grok's postulate, which you inherited, and I don't think
it's still needed as the mechanism it was proposed to explain. What do you think?"*

---

## Short answer

**The event: yes, retirable — you are right about it.
The paper: no — it carries a validated magnitude χ₄ cannot produce.
And retiring the event has a cost you should weigh first: it strands F5 completely.**

## 1. Capotauro and χ₄ are not competitors — this was settled at Patch 4084

> *"Every closed umbrella result is a MAGNITUDE (|M^K3| = |M^W| = χ/6 = 0.039345) … so the
> sign is exactly what they lack and what χ₄ supplies — **χ₄ is the missing factor in
> Capotauro, not a competitor.**"*

The division of labour is clean and it has held all session:

| | supplies | supplied by |
|---|---|---|
| **magnitude** | \|M\| = χ/6 = φ⁻³/6 ≈ 0.0394 | Capotauro, from the perturbative-distance-ratio constraint on the 600-cell |
| **sign** | which hand | χ₄'s b = sign(ω·v) |

**χ₄ produces no magnitude.** b is binary, ±1. An unscoped search finds no place where χ₄
yields 0.0394 or any other number. Retiring Capotauro would leave the programme with a sign
and nothing to multiply it by.

## 2. What would be lost with the paper

- **THEO-CAP-1** (#62), **THEO-SD-CHIR-1** (#63), **THEO-SD-CHIR-2** (#64),
  **THEO-CHIR-CONT-1** (#65) — four registered theorems
- **PRED-O-25** (Δp_LR = χ/6 ≈ 0.0394 on the K3-doublet — the primary empirical prediction,
  **validated within 2%**), plus **PRED-O-26**, **PRED-O-27**, **PRED-O-31**

A 2%-validated zero-parameter prediction is not something to give up to tidy an origin story.

## 3. Where you are right — the nucleation event specifically

The **event** and the **magnitude machinery** are separable, and only the event was ever about
handedness selection:

- The magnitude comes from **lattice geometry** — the perturbative-distance-ratio constraint on
  n̂-induced edge perturbations. It does not reference the event.
- The **nucleation event (sub-claim (a))** existed to answer *which enantiomorph got selected,
  and when* — the cosmological freezing story.

Two things have happened to that job:
1. **Patch 4104** withdrew Capotauro v2.0's sign-from-n̂ clause outright: −I₄ is proper in ℝ⁴ and
   30 improper symmetries fix n̂, so n̂ selects no enantiomorph. The paper's own sign mechanism
   is already gone.
2. **χ₄ supplies handedness structurally, not historically** — b is written per CP per Moment,
   with no appeal to a past event.

So the event is doing no work that χ₄ does not now do better, and its stated mechanism has
already been withdrawn. **Retiring sub-claim (a) is defensible on the merits.**

## 4. The cost you should weigh before deciding

**Patch 4103 established that F5's only named home is Capotauro sub-claim (a)** — the
nucleation event. F5 needs an O(1) CP phase near 65.5°; the arc produces magnitudes and at most
a sign; and 4103 traced the phase's sole forward-pointer to *"derived (in future work) from the
Capotauro nucleation event (sub-claim (a))."*

> **Retire sub-claim (a) and F5 has no candidate mechanism anywhere in the corpus.**

That does not make F5 worse in substance — it was already a missing mechanism (4103), and an
empty referral is arguably more honest than a circular one. But it removes the last place
anyone had written down where the phase might come from. **F5 is the sole reason χ₄ is not
panel-ready**, so this is worth deciding deliberately rather than as a side effect.

## 5. Recommendation

**Three-way split, not a retirement:**

1. **KEEP** THEO-CAP-1 and the χ/6 magnitude machinery, with PRED-O-25/26/27/31 intact. It is
   geometry, not cosmology, and it is empirically validated.
2. **RETIRE** sub-claim (a), the nucleation event, as the handedness-selection mechanism —
   superseded by χ₄, and its own sign clause already withdrawn at 4104. Record it as
   superseded rather than deleted, with the reason.
3. **RE-HOME F5 explicitly** as part of the same decision, so the CP phase does not become
   silently homeless. Either name a new target or record that it has none — the second is
   acceptable and honest, but it should be *recorded*, not left to be discovered.

**Provenance note, since you raised it:** that Capotauro originated as Grok's postulate is not
by itself a reason to retire it — THEO-CAP-1's magnitude has since been re-derived from lattice
geometry and validated at 2%, which stands on its own regardless of who first proposed it. The
reason to retire the *event* is that χ₄ now does its job and its own mechanism failed at 4104,
not its authorship.
