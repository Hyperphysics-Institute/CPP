## Relationship to Standard Model

**Categorized relationships:**

- **Extends SM** in the following respects:
  - Provides substrate-level mechanism for the existence of parity violation in weak interactions (PHEN-E-CAP-1). The SM accommodates parity violation phenomenologically via the V-A structure of W± couplings; CPP/Capotauro derives the underlying chirality magnitude $\|\chi\| = \phi^{-3}$ from the 600-cell substrate geometry.
  - Provides substrate-level mechanism for the right-hand-rule structure of the ExB law at all scales (PHEN-E-CAP-2). The SM treats the right-hand rule as a convention; CPP/Capotauro derives it from substrate-vacuum primitive chirality magnitude.
  - Predicts cross-sector consistency: the K3-doublet's $D_6$ stabilizer + cage-shell averaging matches the SF-2 W bracelet's $D_6$ stabilizer with structural identity $V_\text{cage} = \|D_6\| = 12$ (PHEN-X-CAP-4). This is a CPP-specific cross-sector structural prediction not present in SM.

- **Reproduces SM** in the following respects:
  - PMNS TBM mixing structure (inherited from SM-5; the K3-doublet basis is TBM-aligned by construction).
  - W± V-A coupling structure (cross-sector closure pair with OPEN-FP-SF-2-CHIR; the Capotauro $D_6$ structure and W bracelet $D_6$ structure share substrate-symmetry origin).
  - Charged-lepton mass hierarchy (inherited from SF-4 v4.0 via FI-C-6 precedent; FI-C-10 extends this to chirality observable class).

- **Disagrees with SM** in no current respects — the Capotauro v1.0 prediction $\Delta p_{LR} \approx 0.0394$ has no SM counterpart at this level of structure-vs-fitting distinction. If a future SM analysis derives a different value for the same observable, this would constitute a disagreement; at v1.0 SHIP the prediction is in the "new prediction at zero parameters" category, not the "disagreement" category.

- **SM accommodations** (where SM is preserved but Capotauro provides additional structure):
  - $\eta_B$ matter-antimatter asymmetry — SM cannot derive $\eta_B$ from first principles (this is one of the open problems of SM cosmology); Capotauro provides the primary parity-violation asymmetry $\Delta p_{LR}$ that feeds the leptogenesis chain. ## v2.0 philosophical additions (Session 135 v2.0 v1.0 SHIPPED)

The v2.0 paper introduces two philosophical commitments not articulated in v1.0: the explicit Layer 1 dynamical engine framing at §13.6 and the constrained-closure-and-inheritance research-programme self-characterization.

### Layer 1 dynamical engine epistemology (paper §13.6)

The v2.0 paper's §13.6 subsection makes explicit a structural-vs-dynamical physics distinction that the v1.0 paper had implicit but did not articulate. The Substrate-Locality Theorem, the cage-shell averaging structure, the pairing-convention identifications — all of the closure machinery the v2.0 paper deploys — are **structural physics**: physics of the static geometric substrate organized by the primitive direction $\hat{n}$. What none of this derives is the **dynamical** content beneath: the conscious-point-level rules (perceive-compute-displace cycles, DI-bit propagation, Nexus connectivity) that produce the static geometric structure in the first place.

§13.6 registers the Layer 1 dynamical engine work as explicitly open. This is a posture of epistemic discipline, not a concession of weakness. The position the subsection articulates:

(1) **The Layer 1 chirality investigation is genuine open inquiry** with an outcome to be determined by which lines of investigation prove tractable against experimental reality and which dead-end. There is no pedagogical or scientific obligation to name an endpoint that is not yet determined.

(2) **The investigation is conducted under a specific methodology**: experimental reality as the determinant of truth + convergence of derived mathematical structure with experimental reality as marker of correct identification of substrate primitives + coherence with multi-layer programme architecture as consistency check + the primitive structure approached from above by working inward from layers of macroscopic axiomatic content whose convergence with experimental reality has already been demonstrated.

(3) **The methodology is grounded in the corpus track record**: SS-7 twelve zero-parameter predictions through ⁵⁶Ni within 1.5%; SS-2 proton charge radius 0.851 fm at zero parameters; SS-5 deuteron binding −0.09% at zero parameters; SS-4 QCD string tension 926.5 MeV/fm at zero parameters; SM-8 cage-mass scale $M_0 = m_e \cdot z/\phi$ at zero parameters; SF-4 v1.0 7/8 zero-parameter neutrino-sector predictions; Capotauro v2.0 three-way cross-sector unification at 2% empirical anchor at zero parameters. The methodology is not abstract; the methodology is in the corpus track record.

(4) **The methodology remains a falsifiable claim on the programme as a whole**: whether the methodology continues to produce additional convergences as new sub-problems are addressed is a falsifiable claim on the programme. Each new closure is itself a test of the methodology that produced it. The corpus track record carries the methodology forward to the next test; it does not exempt the methodology from the next test.

**Generalizable pattern**: the structural-vs-dynamical physics distinction is not unique to Capotauro. Any flagship paper that closes structural theorems at Layer 3 while the corresponding Layer 1 dynamical foundation remains under investigation can adopt the same epistemic posture: register the Layer 1 work as explicitly open, ground the open-work claim in the corpus methodology track record, leave the methodology's continuation as a falsifiable claim on the programme. The pattern templates future flagship papers' approach to the structural-dynamical distinction.

### Constrained closure-and-inheritance research programme

ChatGPT's v0.9 round-2 revised review (Patch 0474 Session 135) produced a manuscript-identity reframing that the framework now adopts as its standing identity. The reframing: Capotauro v2.0 is not best read as a speculative substrate theory attempting to become physics. It is better read as a **constrained closure-and-inheritance research programme** whose substrate dynamical completion remains explicitly unresolved. The structural closures the paper does — three sector closures at full Layer 3 rigor — do substantial work; the dynamical completion the paper does not yet do is open work; neither the structural work nor the dynamical work attempts to be self-contained within the paper.

What the framing names is a posture different from the conventional posture of mid-twentieth-century theoretical physics. The conventional posture frames a derivation as **self-contained**: within a paper's scope, all foundational inputs are derived from first principles in the same paper. The Standard Model's electroweak unification papers from the 1960s–1970s have this character: the V−A coupling is part of the same Lagrangian that produces the gauge symmetry and the Higgs mechanism. The conventional reader expects a derivational framework to have this self-contained character; departures from it look like incompleteness.

The Capotauro v2.0 paper announces — through its master architecture figure (§3.5), its §13.6 subsection, its 11-route falsifier set, and its honest scope-limitation framing in §12 — that it is not self-contained in this sense and is not trying to be. It is **one node in a research programme** — the CPP programme — that contains many such papers, each closing specific theorems under specific inherited inputs that other papers in the programme contribute. The completion of the programme is the open horizon. No individual paper claims to close it.

The closest historical analog: the **lattice-gauge-theory community's relationship to continuum quantum chromodynamics through the 1980s and 1990s**. Lattice gauge theory produced rigorous theorems and numerical agreements at zero parameters in specific calculational sub-problems (string tension, hadron mass spectrum, deconfinement phase transition) while the full continuum limit and the connection to perturbative QCD remained open work. The lattice community did not claim to be doing speculative physics; it claimed to be doing constrained-closure-and-inheritance physics, with the continuum limit as registered open work. The community's reviewers eventually recognized this as a legitimate research-programme identity.

**Generalizable pattern**: the constrained-closure-and-inheritance framing applies to any CPP flagship paper that closes specific theorems at full Layer 3 rigor while inheriting foundational inputs from elsewhere in the programme (prior corpus closures or explicitly-registered open work). The framing is the framework's standing identity going forward.

### Three-way cross-sector unification as methodological signal

The v2.0 paper's three-way cross-sector unification produces the same numerical magnitude $\chi/6 \approx 0.0394$ through three structurally distinct mechanisms (K3-doublet under 3D inversion, W-bracelet under 4D icosahedral-center inversion, qDP/eDP under combined $C \times P$). This is methodologically distinct from v1.0's single-sector closure: the robustness of the substrate-handle claim increases by a factor that is not easy to put a number on but which is structurally large. **Same primitive magnitude produced through three different cage-shell averaging operations on three different stabilizer subgroups with three different pairing-convention generators is a different kind of result than the same primitive magnitude produced through one chain.**

The methodological signal: when a foundational input (here $|\chi| = \phi^{-3}$) propagates through multiple structurally distinct sector mechanisms producing the same numerical magnitude, the convergence is evidence that the foundational input is correctly identified rather than fitted. Three-way convergence is harder to engineer by parameter choice than single-sector convergence; the parameter-choice hypothesis becomes structurally implausible as the number of convergent sectors increases. The v2.0 paper's three-way unification is therefore stronger empirical evidence for the substrate-handle claim than the v1.0 paper's single-sector closure, even though the numerical prediction is unchanged at the leptogenesis-anchor level.

**Generalizable pattern**: cross-sector unification at substrate level is a methodological signal that the substrate handle is correctly identified. Future cross-sector unification results in the CPP corpus (and elsewhere) should be assessed not only by the numerical match within each sector but by the structural distinctness of the mechanisms producing the convergent numerical values.
