## Part 2 — FAQ

### Methodology

**Q1: Why is the Capotauro paper a "conditional theorem closure paper" rather than a full closure paper?**

The Capotauro mechanism decomposes OPEN-SM-4 into three sub-claims: (a) Capotauro nucleation event derivation, (b) substrate chirality mechanism candidate derivation, (c) substrate-to-K3-doublet matrix element derivation. The v1.0 SHIP closes sub-claim (c) at conditional theorem level via THEO-CAP-1, with sub-claims (a) and (b) deferred to v2.0+ work. The "conditional" qualifier on THEO-CAP-1 reflects its dependence on foundational inputs FI-C-1 through FI-C-10 + CPP axioms A1, A3, A4, A7 — particularly FI-C-9 substrate primitive chirality magnitude (whose first-principles derivation is sub-claim (b)) and FI-C-10 cage-shell observable-class extension (whose first-principles derivation is separate open work). See `philosophy-capotauro.md` §1 + Layer classification.

**Q2: Why is the substrate's primitive chirality treated as a "primitive feature" rather than via spontaneous symmetry breaking?**

Per CPP's core methodological commitment that mathematical descriptions are not physical mechanisms (the principle distinguishing CPP from continuum-EFT traditions), SSB is a mathematical description of symmetry-breaking patterns in continuum field theories rather than a physical mechanism in CPP's discrete-substrate framework. Adopting SSB as the primary §2 framing at v1.0 SHIP would import continuum-EFT ontological commitments inconsistent with CPP. The primitive-feature framing treats the substrate's chirality as a foundational feature coeval with CPs/GPs and the rules of their interaction — like the existence of CPs themselves, not the outcome of a more-primitive substrate undergoing symmetry-breaking dynamics. SSB framing is preserved in Remark 2.2 as mathematically-equivalent alternative for readers familiar with EFT traditions. See `reasoning-capotauro.md` §4 for verbatim Tier 4 capture of the Session 120 reframe decision.

**Q3: How was THEO-CAP-1 registered in `theorem-registry.md` before the paper was published?**

Session 103 Patch 0397 registered THEO-CAP-1 in the SF-Line section of `theorem-registry.md` under a four-condition methodological pattern: (i) rigorous proof at Wigner-Eckart factorization level; (ii) end-to-end numerical verification at machine precision ($10^{-17}$); (iii) primary empirical prediction $\Delta p_{LR} \approx 0.0394$ validated within 2% of empirical anchor; (iv) honest scope-limitation framing for re-scoped sub-problems (Q11 $\sin^2\theta_{13}$ deferred to SF-2 v2.0+). This was the **first programme-level theorem registered ahead of its own flagship paper publication in the CPP corpus** and establishes a methodological pattern applicable to future flagship-paper-pending theorems. Paper-level confirmation occurred at Patch 0415 v1.0 SHIP.
