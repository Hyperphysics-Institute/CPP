## Challenge 2 — *"On what basis keep the number if the mechanism that produced it has been eliminated?"*

**Because the event is not the mechanism that produced it.** The paper's own architecture
separates them, and has from the start:

| sub-claim | what it is | status |
|---|---|---|
| **(a)** | the Capotauro **nucleation event** — *"the substrate-dynamical event by which the universe localizes into one specific enantiomorph"* | **OPEN**, never closed |
| (b) | substrate chirality mechanism | OPEN |
| **(c)** | **the magnitude** — THEO-CAP-1, \|M\| = χ/6 | **SHIPPED** v1.0 |

Sub-claim (a) is a **hand-selection** claim — which enantiomorph, and when. Sub-claim (c) is a
**scale** claim. The shipped, validated result is (c). Retiring (a) removes an open sub-claim
that was never load-bearing for (c), and whose own stated mechanism was withdrawn at 4104.

So it is not "keeping a number whose mechanism is gone." It is **dropping an open sub-claim and
keeping a closed one that never depended on it.**
