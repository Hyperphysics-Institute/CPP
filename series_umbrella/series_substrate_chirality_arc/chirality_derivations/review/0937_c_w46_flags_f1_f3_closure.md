## 1. F1 — the inversion centre (T1, T2a)

The local point group at the host under vertex-aligned Reading C is the H₄-stabiliser of the host vertex, H₃ ≅ I_h, acting on the 3-space **perpendicular to n̂** and fixing the host and n̂. Built from the lattice (T2a: order 120), its inversion element is w ↦ −w on the ⟂ component with the n̂ component fixed, i.e. on the first shell **p ↦ φ n̂ − p** — the icosahedral-centre inversion, identical to ζ^W of C-W43 §17.3. It maps every first-shell vertex to a first-shell vertex at the same projection û·n̂ = −1/(2φ) (T1). The host-centred 4D inversion 2 v_host − p is not an element of the local group and lands off-lattice; the ambient direction −û from the host is not a first-shell configuration either (T1, agreeing with 0936 T3).

So R1 is not a reading to be chosen among; it is the geometric content of "inversion" at the host. Definition 20.2's first flip, "v ↦ −v", is this element in host-centred coordinates of the ⟂ space (there v ↦ −v *is* p ↦ φn̂ − p). Its third flip, "n̂ ↦ −n̂", is **not** an element of the local group — nothing in H₃ moves n̂ — and should be read as what it was reaching for: the 3D inversion reverses the orientation of the 4D frame (n̂, ⟂-triad), which is the statement that χ, the pseudoscalar of the mirror substrate, changes sign under the substrate mirror. As a c-number parameter of *this* substrate χ does not change sign under any operator on the four-state space; its sign is FI-C-9's (MERGE-2), fixed by convention.

## 2. F2 — the irrep of Ĉ^qDP (T2b–T2d, T3, T4, T5)

**The pair-stabiliser is D₅d** (T2b: order 20, class sizes 1,2,2,5,1,2,2,5, five perpendicular C₂). Restricting the pseudoscalar A_u(I_h) — character det g — to it gives **A₁u**, not A₂u (T2c). The character C-W46 §20.5 prints, (1,1,1,−1,−1,−1,−1,1), is A₂u: +1 on the σ_d mirrors and −1 on the *proper* C₂ rotations (T2d). A pseudoscalar is even under every proper rotation; the printed character is that of a polar coordinate along the pair axis. This is the slip 0936 suspected, now on the record.

**The four-state space under the extended group D₅d × Z₂^C** (geometric group times charge conjugation, order 40) decomposes as A₁g⁺ ⊕ A₁g⁻ ⊕ A₂u⁺ ⊕ A₂u⁻, each once (T3a; ± = C-parity). C-W46's doublet is Ψ⁽¹⁾ = (A₁g⁺ + A₂u⁻)/√2, Ψ⁽²⁾ = (A₁g⁻ + A₂u⁺)/√2 (T3b) — each a *mixture* across irreps, so a one-Z₂ parity check (ζ-parity) is necessary but not sufficient; the full selection rule is stricter.

**Operator support (T4).** The 16-dimensional operator space on the four states carries only A₁g and A₂u of the geometric D₅d (four copies each, split evenly by C-parity). **No A₁u or A₂g operator exists on the space.** Consequently a pure pseudoscalar of the local point group has *no* nonzero matrix element in the antipodal-pair space — the refinement C₅v → D₅d of §20.1 does not by itself resolve the vanishing it was introduced to resolve. What resolves it is the charge-sign content C-W46 §20.2 describes in words ("the qCP-sign × n̂ coupling"); §20.5's group label did not carry that content.

**Selection rule (T5a).** ⟨Ψ⁽¹⁾|Ô|Ψ⁽²⁾⟩ ≠ 0 iff Ô ∈ **A₁g⁻** or **A₂u⁺**. A₁g⁻ is spanned by the charge-sign operator ŝ ⊗ 1 (T5b: element +1) and ŝ ⊗ swap; A₂u⁺ by the position-parity operator |v⟩⟨v| − |v′⟩⟨v′| (T5c) and its antisymmetric partner. C-W46's literal assignment "A₂u, ζ-odd" is A₂u⁻, e.g. ŝ ⊗ (position parity), whose doublet element is **zero** (T5d) — the ζ-parity bookkeeping of §20.6 passes it (EVEN × ODD × ODD), the full group forbids it.

**Which of the two survives at first order in χ.** Under the O(ε) perturbation of Reading C the local I_h is preserved (Theorem `thm:local_ih_preservation`, Finding C-W39): every first-shell direction is equivalent, and in particular v and v′ sit at the same projection −1/(2φ) with the same stabiliser orbit. An A₂u⁺ operator is one that distinguishes v from v′ — a *polar* preference for one first-shell placement — and the preserved symmetry supplies none at this order (T7: the placement energy f(û·n̂) is A₁g⁺ with doublet element 0). The A₁g⁻ operator ŝ is available: it is C-odd and geometrically trivial, and the pseudoscalar enters as its **coefficient**, the c-number χ. Hence

  Ĉ^qDP = χ · (1/6) · ŝ ⊗ 1,  ⟨Ψ⁽¹⁾|Ĉ^qDP|Ψ⁽²⁾⟩ = χ/6,

with the 1/6 the cage-shell factor of §20.6 unchanged (d_Γ = 2 for the 2D doublet, V_cage = 12). Correct irrep statement: **Ĉ^qDP ∈ A₁g(D₅d) ⊗ (C-odd)**, ζ^qDP-ODD as C-W46 says, with the ungerade character living in the substrate-mirror Z₂ (the sign of χ) rather than in the geometric D₅d. The Wigner–Eckart line of §20.6 becomes ⟨A₁g⁺|A₁g⁻|A₁g⁻⟩ + ⟨A₂u⁻|A₁g⁻|A₂u⁺⟩ on D₅d × Z₂^C, both ∋ A₁g⁺.

## 3. F3 — configuration-basis form (T6)
