\begin{lemma}[The OPEN-SM-4 activation $\Ztwo$ is the det-coset $\Ztwo$]
\label{lem:sm4z2}
The $\Ztwo$ factor in the OPEN-SM-4 activation $[\text{600-cell}]\times\Ztwo \to [\text{600-cell}]$ is
the orientation/enantiomorph $\Ztwo$: its breaking selects $\sgn(\chi)$, and $\sgn(\chi)$ is the
substrate enantiomorph $=\sgn(\nhat)$ (the realized handedness; F1/F4). Hence its nontrivial element
is an orientation-reversing operation and its order parameter is the pseudoscalar $\sgn(\nhat)=$ FI-C-9.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
OPEN-SM-4's activation breaks the $\Ztwo$ to fix $\sgn(\chi)$ (it ``establishes $\chi$''). By F4,
$\chi$ is the FI-C-9 magnitude and $\sgn(\chi)$ is the substrate enantiomorph; by F1/F2 the
enantiomorph is exactly the det-coset value $\sgn(\nhat)$. An operation that exchanges the two
enantiomorphs reverses orientation (it is improper, $\det=-1$); the quantity it flips --- the order
parameter --- is the pseudoscalar $\sgn(\nhat)$. \emph{This reading of OPEN-SM-4's $\Ztwo$ as the
enantiomorph $\Ztwo$ is an interpretation of the OPEN-SM-4 registration, not a re-derivation of
Capotauro} (the flagged premise P2; falsifier B1).
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[The $\Ztwo$-match]
\label{thm:match}
The OPEN-SM-4 chirality-activation $\Ztwo$ and the STATUS-2 chiral-vacuum quotient $\Ztwo=\Hf/\Hfp$ are
the \emph{same} $\Ztwo$ object: both are the 600-cell det-coset $\Ztwo$, with
\[
\text{generator } = \text{ an orientation-reversing reflection } (\det=-1),\qquad
\text{order parameter } = \sgn(\nhat)=\sgn(\chi)=\text{FI-C-9},
\]
and identical action (enantiomorph exchange). They coincide as the tuple
$(\text{group},\,\text{generator-class},\,\text{order-parameter},\,\text{action})$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
By F2 the STATUS-2 $\Ztwo$ is the det-coset $\Hf/\Hfp$ (generator: any reflection $r$ with $\det r =
-1$; order parameter $\sgn(\nhat)$; action: exchange the two det-cosets $=$ enantiomorphs). By
Lemma~\ref{lem:sm4z2} the OPEN-SM-4 $\Ztwo$ has the \emph{same} generator-class (orientation reversal),
the \emph{same} order parameter ($\sgn(\chi)=\sgn(\nhat)=$ FI-C-9), and the \emph{same} action
(enantiomorph exchange). Two $\Ztwo$'s agreeing on generator-class, order parameter, and action are the
same $\Ztwo$ object. (Machine-checked, \texttt{verify\_bridge\_1\_z2\_match.py} CHECK 1--2.)
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The match is \emph{kinematic} --- the load-bearing honest cap]
\label{rem:kinematic}
Theorem~\ref{thm:match} identifies a \emph{group object and its order parameter}; it is silent on
\emph{dynamics}. It does \emph{not} establish (i) that the $\Ztwo$ \emph{breaks} (whether a chiral
vacuum forms --- the capacity question, B-iii / 1d-$\beta$-ii), nor (ii) that the breaking, if it
occurs, \emph{is} electroweak symmetry breaking (the dynamical identification, CONJ-CHIR-1). It rests

of the OPEN-SM-4 registration). STATUS-2 itself flagged even the ``$\Ztwo = $ FI-C-9'' identification
as a framework step; the same honesty applies here.
\end{remark}
