## §3 Decomposition: the five sub-targets 1d-β-i..v

### §3.1 1d-β-i — The breaking-chain structure (group theory; what must break)

**One-line:** Identify precisely the symmetry-breaking chain a chiral substrate vacuum requires, and the order parameter (the enantiomorph) that labels the broken phase.
**What a solution produces:** the achiral isometry group of the 600-cell is the Coxeter group **H₄** (order 14400, generated by reflections — contains improper operations; MERGE-2 Lemma achirality). Its maximal **chiral** (orientation-preserving / rotation) subgroup is the proper subgroup **H₄⁺** (order 7200). A chiral vacuum is one whose stabilizer lies in H₄⁺ (no reflection survives). 1d-β-i catalogues: the achiral group H₄; the chiral subgroup H₄⁺ (and the relevant further reductions — the host-axis reduction `n̂` already breaks H₄→H₃=I_h, *still achiral*; the chiral reduction to I = A₅ rotation sense is OPEN-CHIR-1b / audit E13); and the **order parameter** distinguishing the two enantiomorphs (a pseudoscalar, = `sign(n̂)` = FI-C-9). Output: a precise statement of *what symmetry must break* (the reflection cosets of H₄ must be lifted) and the order parameter, with no dynamics.
**Reachability:** plausibly **Layer 2.5 reachable now** — pure group theory / polytope symmetry, building directly on MERGE-2's achirality result and OPEN-CHIR-1b's chiral-subgroup work. No SM/SS machinery. Estimated 1–3 sessions.
**Honest cap:** identifying *what must break* and the order parameter does NOT show that it *does* break (that is 1d-β-ii) nor *which way* (1d-β-iii).

### §3.2 1d-β-ii — Chiral-vacuum existence + mechanism (the dynamics; does it break)

**One-line:** Is there a substrate dynamical mechanism (DP-Sea condensation / PCD dynamics / the A-axioms) that drives the vacuum to the chiral (H₄⁺) phase rather than the symmetric (achiral H₄) one?
**What a solution produces:** an energetics/dynamics argument that the symmetric (achiral) substrate configuration is unstable (or higher-energy) relative to a chiral one, so the vacuum spontaneously condenses into a handedness-bearing state — the **emergence of the capacity** for chirality. This is the genuine "FI-C-9 elimination" engine: if a mechanism generates a chiral vacuum, chirality-*as-a-phenomenon* is emergent (even if the specific sign is contingent — see iii).
**Reachability:** **deep; cross-sector; NOT Layer-2.5-reachable now.** Requires the substrate vacuum-structure machinery (condensation energetics, the DP-Sea ground state) that lives in the SS-corpus / OPEN-SM-4, behind the F.1 §14.17 viability ceiling. This is the heart of the deferral. Estimated: a multi-session cross-sector arc, gated on SS/SM vacuum-structure development.
**Honest cap:** this is where the "deep, deferred" lives. Until 1d-β-ii is built, FI-C-9 remains an irreducible primitive at current rigor (the verdict V3 of §3.4) — no mechanism ⇒ no emergence.

### §3.3 1d-β-iii — Sign-selection (which enantiomorph)

**One-line:** Given a chiral vacuum forms (1d-β-ii), is the specific enantiomorph (`sign(n̂)` = +1 vs −1) (a) spontaneously / contingently selected, (b) explicitly fixed by a deeper substrate asymmetry, or (c) irreducibly free?
**What a solution produces:** a determination of the **emergence of the value**. Three outcomes: **(a) spontaneous** — like a ferromagnet's domain or a spontaneously-broken vacuum, the mechanism generates chirality but the sign is a contingent fact of the realized vacuum (emergent-mechanism, contingent-value); **(b) explicit** — some deeper registered asymmetry (a T-arrow coupling? a boundary condition? the SM CP-violation?) fixes the sign (fully derived); **(c) free** — no mechanism or asymmetry fixes it; the sign is an irreducible primitive boundary condition.
**Reachability:** **partially Layer-2.5 reachable now** — one can ask *now* whether the registered axiom set A1–A11 contains ANY pseudoscalar asymmetry capable of fixing the sign (if not, outcome (b) is excluded at current rigor, leaving (a) or (c)). The full determination (which of a/c) depends on 1d-β-ii. Estimated: a partial structural result now (1–2 sessions: "no axiom-level pseudoscalar exists to fix the sign ⇒ not (b) ⇒ spontaneous-or-free"), full closure gated on ii.
**Honest cap:** the sign-selection is logically downstream of vacuum-existence (ii); a partial "not explicitly fixed" result is reachable, the full a-vs-c verdict is not.

### §3.4 1d-β-iv — The primitive/emergent verdict structure (the synthesis; the goal's answer-form)

**One-line:** Define precisely what counts as "FI-C-9 emergent" vs "primitive," so the programme's headline goal has a definite answer-form.
**What a solution produces:** the verdict space, distinguishing **emergence of the capacity** (ii: a mechanism generates a chiral vacuum) from **emergence of the value** (iii: the sign is derived):
- **V1 — emergent mechanism, contingent sign:** 1d-β-ii succeeds, 1d-β-iii is (a) spontaneous. Chirality-as-a-phenomenon is *derived*; the specific handedness is a contingent fact of our vacuum (like the direction a ferromagnet froze). *This is the "chirality is emergent" outcome in the physically standard sense (spontaneous symmetry breaking).*
- **V2 — fully emergent:** ii succeeds and iii is (b) explicit. Both the mechanism and the specific sign are derived. Strongest, least likely (would need a deeper asymmetry).
- **V3 — irreducible primitive:** ii fails / no mechanism is found. FI-C-9 is a frozen boundary condition — the one genuine chirality primitive. *This is the current-rigor status.*

This sub-target gives the goal ("primitive/emergent status of chirality") a precise, defensible answer-form: **at present rigor V3 (FI-C-9 is the one irreducible chirality primitive); the achievable upgrade is V1 (emergent mechanism, contingent sign) via 1d-β-ii; V2 is the stretch.**
**Reachability:** **Layer 2.5 reachable now** — it is a definitional/structural synthesis (define the verdict space, place the current rigor at V3, state what each upgrade requires). Estimated 1 session; pairs naturally with 1d-β-i. **Highest immediate value for the goal** — it lets the programme *state* chirality's status honestly today (V3, with a mapped path to V1).
**Honest cap:** defining the verdict space and placing current rigor at V3 is not the same as *achieving* V1/V2 (that needs ii/iii).

### §3.5 1d-β-v — The SM-chirality cross-link (the deep payoff question)

**One-line:** Is the substrate enantiomorph (FI-C-9) the *same* chirality that manifests as electroweak parity violation (the weak interaction is chiral), or are they independent?
**What a solution produces:** a determination of whether deriving FI-C-9's emergence (1d-β-ii) is the *same problem* as deriving why the weak force is chiral (SM parity violation), via the OPEN-SM-4 (δ_CP / Capotauro) ↔ SS-corpus link. If they are the same, 1d-β-ii and the SM-chirality derivation share a mechanism — a major unification of the chirality-status question with the electroweak sector. If independent, FI-C-9 is a substrate-only enantiomorph distinct from SM chirality.
**Reachability:** **deep, cross-sector** — requires the SM-sector chirality machinery (OPEN-SM-4) and the SS-corpus. Not Layer-2.5-reachable now; it is the highest-stakes dependency. A *scoping* of the link (what would establish sameness vs independence) is reachable; the determination is not.
**Honest cap:** this is the biggest prize and the deepest dependency; the scope flags it as the cross-sector home of 1d-β-ii, not a near-term target.
