## 1. The headline: chirality is NOT one of the 9 axioms

The axiom set A1–A11 contains **no handedness**. A1 gives Conscious Points a polarity (±), a type, and a position; A2 places them on the 600-cell. The 600-cell's isometry group is **H₄, order 14400, which *contains reflections*** — i.e. the bare axiomatic substrate is **achiral**. Nothing in A1–A11 distinguishes a left- from a right-handed world.

Chirality therefore enters **one layer above the axioms**, as **Foundational Inputs (FIs)** in the substrate-orientation (Capotauro/Reading-C) framework — not as a postulate in the 9-axiom core. This is the precise sense in which "the world is handed": it is handed by an *input*, on top of an axiom base that is itself handedness-neutral.

## 2. The carrier: `n̂`, `sign(n̂)`, and FI-C-9

- **`n̂` — the primitive 4D direction (FI-C-RC-1).** A single direction in the substrate's ambient four-space. It is a Foundational Input (registered Capotauro v2.0), with FI-C-RC-2 giving the vertex-aligned reading `n̂ = v_host` at Layer 2. `n̂` is what selects an orientation in the otherwise achiral substrate.
- **`sign(n̂)` — the orientation pseudoscalar (the det-pseudoscalar).** Choosing `n̂` breaks the achiral group to its rotation subgroup, **H₄ → H₄⁺** (order 14400 → 7200, index 2, quotient ℤ₂). `sign(n̂)` is the order parameter of that breaking: the ℤ₂ bit that says **which of the two enantiomorphs** (the two degenerate, reflection-related vacua) the world is in. It is odd under reflection (`n̂ → −n̂`). This is the handedness bit itself.
- **FI-C-9 — the substrate chirality primitive.** It carries both faces of the chirality: the **magnitude** χ = φ⁻³ ≈ 0.236 (the size of the handed bias) and the **enantiomorph sign** `sign(n̂)` (which way). The determination arc (THEO-CHIR-MERGE-2) reduced *all* chirality to this one primitive, plus the temporal arrow `sign(δ)`.

## 3. How it is actualized on the 600-cell (the "hinge point")

The substrate-level mechanism by which `n̂` becomes physical handedness is a **direction-correlated edge-length perturbation**:

> `ε(ê · n̂)` — each 600-cell edge `ê` is perturbed by an amount set by its projection onto `n̂`.

This pattern (verified over all **720 edges**) is **odd under `n̂ → −n̂`**, with maximum magnitude **max|δ| = φ⁻³**. Reading off its two invariants:

- its **sign** = `sign(n̂)` = which enantiomorph (the FI-C-9 frozen handedness);
- its **magnitude** = φ⁻³ = FI-C-9's value, which the locality criterion of THEO-CHIR-CHI-1 (1d-α) selects as the symmetric bias of the host vertex's two nearest distance shells (`(1−φ⁻¹)/(1+φ⁻¹) = φ⁻³`).

So "where chirality is actualized" is concretely: **`n̂` imprints `ε(ê·n̂)` on the edges of the 600-cell**, and that imprint *is* the handedness — sign and size together. (The DM/F.1 lane's Patch-0813 construction builds a *local* version of this: a signed-determinant pseudoscalar `η` over canonical-frame neighbours that flips under reflection. ## 4. Where it sits relative to the axioms (precise attribution)

The FI chirality layer **rests on**, but is not contained in, the axiom base:

- **A2** — the 600-cell geometry whose 720 edges carry `ε(ê·n̂)`, and whose achiral H₄ is the group that `n̂` breaks;
- **A1** — the polar CPs that populate the perturbed lattice;
- the Capotauro substrate-orientation framework additionally engages **A3** (the complex DI-bit amplitude ψ = √ρ·e^{iφ}) and the face-circulation content (**A7**, now consolidated into A6'); the ledger attributes the substrate-chirality theorems to **A1 + A2 + A4 + A7 + the FI stack**.

In every case the axioms supply the **arena**; the **handedness** is supplied by the FI layer (`n̂` = FI-C-RC-1; χ = φ⁻³ = FI-C-9). No axiom fixes the sign — which is also why STATUS-2 found V2 excluded "by the presently registered axiom inventory."

The framework does **not** hold chirality to be an irreducible axiom. FI-C-9 is a **Foundational Input** with a standing open programme to *derive* it — **OPEN-FI-C-9-FP-MECHANISM** (the Capotauro Reading-C trajectory) and **OPEN-CHIR-1d-β / 1d-β-ii** (the chiral-vacuum symmetry-breaking dynamics, F.1 §14.17 / OPEN-SM-4).
