% ============================================================

Conscious Points form Dipole Pairs --- and when a CP is captured within a structure --- does
the partnering/capture step \emph{prefer one handedness}? The audit classified it

(an axiom A1--A11, a registered foundational input, or a registered theorem output); a step
that cannot is reported as a gap, not a closure. The admissible verdicts are
\emph{emergent} (E19 reduces to registered objects) \emph{or} \emph{register-as-primitive}
(E19 is irreducible); both are honest.

The framework already registers the pairing/capture dynamics:

\begin{description}
\item[Polarity pairing (PROP-SS-5-1).] A ZBW attractive edge requires \emph{opposite}

left/right alternation is spatial but not, on its face, handed.

None of these registered rules states a handedness preference. The handedness --- \emph{which}
chiral orientation the pairing/capture selects --- is the increment E19 names. The rest of the
theorem shows that increment is not a new primitive.

deriving FI-C-9 itself.

sign genuinely independent of the enantiomorph) --- would close the cross-link in the
\emph{non}-merge direction, leaving two sign data in the programme rather than one.
