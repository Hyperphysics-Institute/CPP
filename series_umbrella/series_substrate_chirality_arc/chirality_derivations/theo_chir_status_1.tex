\begin{abstract}
\noindent
The Substrate Chirality Arc's headline goal is to determine whether chirality in CPP is
\emph{primitive} or \emph{emergent}. THEO-CHIR-MERGE-2 (cycle closed 3/3, Patches 0647--0651,
conditional on MERGE-$\alpha$) reduced \emph{all currently-identified} chirality (pseudoscalar)
content to \textbf{one} primitive: $\text{FI-C-9} = \sgn(\nhat)$, the frozen substrate-vacuum
enantiomorph. The temporal-arrow $\sgn(\delta)$ is \emph{not} a chirality (it is the $\Te$-arrow,
OPEN-CHIR-2a) and is tracked separately. Hence the chirality-status question reduces to:
\emph{is FI-C-9 primitive or emergent?} --- which is OPEN-CHIR-1d-$\beta$. This theorem is its
sub-target 1d-$\beta$-iv: it does \emph{not} derive FI-C-9, but it \textbf{formalizes the verdict
structure} and \textbf{places current rigor}. Decomposing emergence into a \emph{capacity} axis
(is there a derivable mechanism producing a chiral vacuum?) and a \emph{value} axis (is the
enantiomorph sign derived, spontaneous, or free), we define three verdicts --- \textbf{V3}
(irreducible primitive: no mechanism), \textbf{V1} (emergent mechanism, contingent sign), \textbf{V2}
(fully emergent: mechanism $+$ sign derived) --- and prove they exhaustively partition the outcome
space (Theorem~\ref{thm:partition}). We then show (Theorem~\ref{thm:placement}) that under the present
registered framework the status is \textbf{V3}: the 600-cell is achiral (so geometry supplies no
handedness), every registered chirality theorem (CHI-1, CAP-1, MERGE-2) \emph{consumes} FI-C-9, and
no registered substrate mechanism derives a chiral vacuum (1d-$\beta$-ii open). Crucially, \textbf{V3
means ``not yet derived,'' not ``underivable''}: it is the current-rigor placement, explicitly
upgradeable. The achievable upgrade is \textbf{V1} (Theorem~\ref{thm:upgrade}), reached iff
1d-$\beta$-ii derives a chiral-vacuum mechanism; V2 additionally requires a sign-fixing asymmetry
(1d-$\beta$-iii-explicit), currently not in evidence. \textbf{Honest one-line status of the goal:}
\emph{chirality in CPP is emergent down to one currently-identified irreducible primitive (FI-C-9,
verdict V3, conditional on MERGE-$\alpha$) plus the separately-tracked $\Te$-arrow (2a), with a
mapped (deep, cross-sector) path to upgrading FI-C-9 to V1.} Layer 2.5, provisional; a
classification result, not a derivation. Verified by
\texttt{code/verify\_status\_1\_verdict\_partition.py}.
\end{abstract}

\section{Setup: the goal, and what reduces to what}
\label{sec:setup}
% ============================================================

The CHIR programme asks: is chirality in CPP primitive or emergent? Two registered results frame the
question.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{THEO-CHIR-MERGE-2 (cycle closed 3/3; conditional on MERGE-$\alpha$).} All
\emph{currently-identified} chirality (pseudoscalar, $\Po$-odd) content reduces to the single
primitive $\text{FI-C-9} = \sgn(\nhat)$: the spatial capture handedness is $\zeta\times\text{FI-C-9}$
(CAP-1, $\zeta$ a $\Po$-even involution) and the temporal cycle handedness is
$\sgn(\delta)\times\text{FI-C-9}$ (MERGE-2), each FI-C-9 modulated by a $\Po$-even factor. The
modulating $\sgn(\delta)$ is the $\Te$-arrow, \emph{not} a chirality (OPEN-CHIR-2a).
\item \textbf{The 600-cell is achiral} (MERGE-2 achirality lemma: closed under
$\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1,1)$): the substrate geometry alone supplies \emph{no} handedness.
\end{itemize}

\noindent
Therefore the chirality-status question reduces, at the current rigor, to a single question about a
single object: \emph{is $\text{FI-C-9}$ primitive or emergent?} This is OPEN-CHIR-1d-$\beta$. This
theorem is its sub-target \textbf{1d-$\beta$-iv}: the verdict structure and current-rigor placement.

\begin{remark}[Scope: spatial chirality only]
\label{rem:scope}
This theorem concerns the \emph{spatial} chirality (pseudoscalar) primitive FI-C-9. The status of the
$\Te$-arrow $\sgn(\delta)$ --- whether it is primitive or emergent --- is the \emph{parallel,
separately-tracked} question OPEN-CHIR-2a (gated on F.2). ``The status of chirality'' in full is the
conjunction of this result (the pseudoscalar primitive) and 2a (the arrow).
\end{remark}

\section{The verdict structure}
\label{sec:verdicts}
% ============================================================

We decompose ``$\text{FI-C-9}$ is emergent'' along two independent axes.

\begin{definition}[The two emergence axes]
\label{def:axes}
\textbf{Capacity} $\mathcal{C} \in \{\textsf{No}, \textsf{Yes}\}$: is there a \emph{derivable
substrate mechanism} that drives the vacuum to break the achiral $\Hf$ symmetry to a chiral phase
(i.e.\ produces a handedness-bearing vacuum at all)? \textbf{Value} $\mathcal{V} \in
\{\textsf{derived}, \textsf{spontaneous}, \textsf{free}\}$: \emph{given} a chiral vacuum, is the
specific enantiomorph sign $\sgn(\nhat)$ fixed by a derivable asymmetry (\textsf{derived}),
selected contingently by the realized vacuum (\textsf{spontaneous}, as in spontaneous symmetry
breaking), or an unconstrained boundary condition (\textsf{free})? The $\textsf{free}$ case
\emph{includes} a sign \emph{inherited} from a pre-substrate / cosmological boundary state --- fixed
externally rather than by the substrate dynamics (a review-calibration subtype, Patch 0656; from
CPP's standpoint such an inheritance carries no internal mechanism, so it places the capacity axis at
$\mathcal{C}=\textsf{No}$, verdict V3, exactly as a free boundary datum).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[The three verdicts]
\label{def:verdicts}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{V3 (irreducible primitive):} $\mathcal{C} = \textsf{No}$. No mechanism produces a
chiral vacuum; $\text{FI-C-9}$ is a frozen boundary condition --- a genuine chirality primitive.
\item \textbf{V1 (emergent mechanism, contingent sign):} $\mathcal{C} = \textsf{Yes}$ and
$\mathcal{V} \in \{\textsf{spontaneous}, \textsf{free}\}$. Chirality-\emph{as-a-phenomenon} is
derived (the mechanism is); the specific handedness is a contingent/unconstrained fact of the
realized vacuum. This is the physically standard sense of ``chirality is emergent'' (spontaneous
symmetry breaking).
\item \textbf{V2 (fully emergent):} $\mathcal{C} = \textsf{Yes}$ and $\mathcal{V} =
\textsf{derived}$. Both the mechanism and the specific sign are derived.
\end{itemize}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Exhaustive partition]
\label{thm:partition}
The verdicts $\{$V1, V2, V3$\}$ partition the outcome space $\mathcal{C}\times\mathcal{V}$
exhaustively and mutually exclusively:
\[
\text{V3} \;\Leftrightarrow\; \mathcal{C}=\textsf{No}; \qquad
\text{V2} \;\Leftrightarrow\; \mathcal{C}=\textsf{Yes}\wedge\mathcal{V}=\textsf{derived}; \qquad
\text{V1} \;\Leftrightarrow\; \mathcal{C}=\textsf{Yes}\wedge\mathcal{V}\in\{\textsf{spontaneous},\textsf{free}\}.
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The outcome space is $\mathcal{C}\times\mathcal{V}$ with $|\mathcal{C}|=2$, $|\mathcal{V}|=3$, six
cells. When $\mathcal{C}=\textsf{No}$ the value axis is moot (no vacuum to assign a sign to), so the
three $\mathcal{C}=\textsf{No}$ cells collapse to one verdict, V3. The three $\mathcal{C}=\textsf{Yes}$
cells split by $\mathcal{V}$: \textsf{derived}$\to$V2; \textsf{spontaneous},\textsf{free}$\to$V1
(both: mechanism derived, sign not derived). Every cell maps to exactly one verdict and every verdict
is realized by at least one cell; the three are mutually exclusive (distinct $\mathcal{C}$ for
V3 vs $\{$V1,V2$\}$; distinct $\mathcal{V}$ for V1 vs V2). (Machine-checked,
\texttt{verify\_status\_1\_verdict\_partition.py} CHECK 1.)
\end{proof}

\section{Current-rigor placement: V3}
\label{sec:placement}
% ============================================================

\begin{theorem}[Current-rigor status is V3]
\label{thm:placement}
Under the present registered framework, the capacity axis is $\mathcal{C}=\textsf{No}$ (not
established), so the current-rigor verdict is \textbf{V3}: $\text{FI-C-9}$ is the one
currently-identified irreducible chirality primitive.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The capacity axis is $\textsf{Yes}$ only if a \emph{derivable substrate mechanism} producing a chiral
vacuum is registered. The registered record establishes the contrary at present:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(P\arabic*),leftmargin=2.4em]
\item (MERGE-2, 3/3, conditional on MERGE-$\alpha$) all currently-identified chirality reduces to
$\text{FI-C-9}$ --- so the question is well-posed about this single object.
\item (MERGE-2 achirality lemma) the 600-cell is achiral; the substrate geometry supplies no
handedness, so any chirality requires a symmetry-breaking \emph{event}, not mere geometry.
\item (CHI-1, CAP-1, MERGE-2) every registered chirality theorem \emph{consumes} $\text{FI-C-9}$
(it enters as an input; none derives it).
\item (1d-$\beta$-ii open) no registered substrate dynamical mechanism drives the vacuum to a chiral
phase --- the chiral-vacuum mechanism is the deep, cross-sector, deferred target (F.1 §14.17;
OPEN-SM-4 $\leftrightarrow$ SS-corpus).
\end{enumerate}
By (P2)+(P4) no mechanism is established, so $\mathcal{C}=\textsf{No}$ at current rigor; by
Theorem~\ref{thm:partition} the verdict is V3.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[V3 means ``not yet derived,'' not ``underivable'']
\label{rem:not-ontological}
Theorem~\ref{thm:placement} is a statement about the \emph{present registered framework}, not an
ontological claim. $\mathcal{C}=\textsf{No}$ reads ``no mechanism is \emph{yet} derived,'' not ``no
mechanism \emph{can} exist.'' V3 is therefore explicitly upgradeable (§\ref{sec:upgrade}); it is the
honest floor, not a closure. (Cf.\ the THEO-CHIR-MERGE-2 v1.2 residual: ``one
\emph{currently-identified} chirality primitive,'' not an unconditional ontological count.)
\end{remark}

\section{The upgrade condition: V1 (and the stretch, V2)}
\label{sec:upgrade}
% ============================================================

\begin{theorem}[V1 upgrade condition]
\label{thm:upgrade}
The current verdict upgrades V3 $\to$ V1 \emph{if and only if} 1d-$\beta$-ii derives a substrate
mechanism producing a chiral vacuum ($\mathcal{C}:\textsf{No}\to\textsf{Yes}$) and the value axis is
$\mathcal{V}\in\{\textsf{spontaneous},\textsf{free}\}$. The further upgrade V1 $\to$ V2 requires, in
addition, a derivable sign-fixing asymmetry ($\mathcal{V}=\textsf{derived}$, 1d-$\beta$-iii-explicit).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Immediate from Theorem~\ref{thm:partition}: flipping $\mathcal{C}$ to \textsf{Yes} leaves the verdict
in $\{$V1,V2$\}$, selected by $\mathcal{V}$; \textsf{spontaneous}/\textsf{free}$\to$V1,
\textsf{derived}$\to$V2.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The achievable target is V1; V2 is the stretch]
\label{rem:v1-target}
The standard outcome of a symmetry-breaking mechanism acting on an achiral substrate is V1: the
mechanism is derived, the sign is spontaneously selected (no preferred direction survives the
breaking). V2 would require a \emph{deeper} pseudoscalar asymmetry to fix the sign. A partial
1d-$\beta$-iii result --- if the axiom set A1--A11 contains no pseudoscalar capable of explicitly
fixing $\sgn(\nhat)$ --- would exclude $\mathcal{V}=\textsf{derived}$ at axiom level, pinning the
$\mathcal{C}=\textsf{Yes}$ outcome to V1. (That partial result is a separate sub-target, not claimed
here.) So the realistic emergence-upgrade for chirality is \textbf{V1: emergent mechanism, contingent
sign} --- the physically normal sense in which a handedness ``emerges.''
\end{remark}

\section{What is and is not established}
\label{sec:caps}
% ============================================================

\textbf{Established (Layer 2.5, provisional).} The verdict space $\{$V1,V2,V3$\}$ is well-defined and
exhaustive (Thm~\ref{thm:partition}); the current-rigor status of the one currently-identified
chirality primitive FI-C-9 is \textbf{V3} (Thm~\ref{thm:placement}); the achievable upgrade is
\textbf{V1} via 1d-$\beta$-ii (Thm~\ref{thm:upgrade}). This gives the programme goal a precise,
defensible answer-form.

\textbf{Not established (honest caps).}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{This is a classification/status result, not a derivation.} It changes no substrate
physics; it formalizes where the programme stands and what would move it. FI-C-9 is not derived.
\item \textbf{V3 is current-rigor, not ontological} (Rem~\ref{rem:not-ontological}): ``not yet
derived,'' upgradeable.
\item \textbf{Inherits MERGE-2's conditionality.} ``One primitive'' is conditional on MERGE-$\alpha$
(the $\jnet$-arrow identification); and ``currently-identified'' per MERGE-2 G6 (an independent
$(\Po\text{-odd},\Te\text{-odd})$ primitive would add to the count).
\item \textbf{Spatial chirality only} (Rem~\ref{rem:scope}). The $\Te$-arrow $\sgn(\delta)$ status is
the parallel OPEN-CHIR-2a; the full chirality status is this $\wedge$ 2a.
\item \textbf{The upgrade engine is deep and cross-sector.} 1d-$\beta$-ii (the chiral-vacuum
mechanism) is deferred behind F.1 §14.17 (OPEN-SM-4 $\leftrightarrow$ SS-corpus); this theorem does
not attempt it.
\end{itemize}

\section{Falsifiers}
\label{sec:falsifiers}
% ============================================================

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item \textbf{(H1)} THEO-CHIR-MERGE-2 fails (chirality does not reduce to FI-C-9 alone) --- the
single-object framing of §\ref{sec:setup} and the V3 count change.
\item \textbf{(H2)} a registered substrate mechanism producing a chiral vacuum appears (1d-$\beta$-ii)
--- $\mathcal{C}\to\textsf{Yes}$; upgrade V3 $\to$ V1.
\item \textbf{(H3)} a derivable sign-fixing pseudoscalar asymmetry appears (1d-$\beta$-iii-explicit)
--- $\mathcal{V}=\textsf{derived}$; upgrade $\to$ V2.
\item \textbf{(H4)} the verdict space is shown non-exhaustive (a fourth outcome not in
$\mathcal{C}\times\mathcal{V}$) --- restate Def~\ref{def:verdicts} / Thm~\ref{thm:partition}.
\item \textbf{(H5)} an independent $(\Po\text{-odd},\Te\text{-odd})$ primitive is registered (MERGE-2
G6) --- the ``one primitive'' count changes; the verdict structure still applies per-primitive.
\end{itemize}
