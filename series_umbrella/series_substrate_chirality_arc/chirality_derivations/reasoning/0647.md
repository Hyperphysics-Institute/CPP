same kind of object. They are not.** Track P (parity, n̂→−n̂) and T (time reversal):

- **δ**: P-EVEN — r is a P-even rate, ê·n̂ is P-even, so δ must be P-even. And T-ODD — under T,

  bias is arrow-like, like a drift/current bias.) Consistency: j=(6δ/φ²)n̂ is a current (T-odd);
  δ T-odd × n̂ T-even = T-odd ✓.

- **σ_cycle** (ω_PCD=σ_cycle·n̂): P-odd (axial/polar), T-odd (T-odd/T-even). A pseudoscalar that is
  also a T-arrow.

Matching parities in σ_cycle = sign(δ)·sign(n̂): (P,T) = (even·odd, odd·even) = (odd,odd) ✓. So the
relation is P,T-covariant, and it **decomposes σ_cycle by symmetry character**:
