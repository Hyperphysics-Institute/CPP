## The adjudication, and I do not get to change it

The 0988 rule said ZERO if |λ_min| < 10⁻²⁴, NEGATIVE if λ_min < −10⁻¹² and stable, else report and
claim nothing. Neither fires. **Run 1 is "claim nothing," and that is its verdict.** The reason
neither fires is that I sized the thresholds before knowing the spectrum's tail: the smallest genuine
eigenvalue of Π e^{2tQ} is about p_min·e^{−2t·λ_max(Q)} ≈ 0.008 × e^{−31.4} ≈ 1.9×10⁻¹⁶ at t = 1 — which
is exactly the δ = 0 return. A ZERO bar of 10⁻²⁴ and a NEGATIVE bar of 10⁻¹² were both unreachable.
That is an instrument-design error of mine; the remedy is a corrected rule fixed **before** the next
run, not a reinterpretation of this one.

## An external reading, and why its central claim is wrong

The founder accidentally posted the console to ChatGPT and passed its analysis on. Two of its points are
right: the run does not meet the frozen rule, and passing δ as a Python float (`mp.mpf(0.35)`) is
sloppy. Its central claim — that the 2.2×10⁻¹⁷ binary error in δ is the cause of the −3.5×10⁻¹⁵ — is
wrong, and quantifiably. λ_min is a smooth function of δ with |∂λ_min/∂δ| ≤ ‖Π‖·2t·‖∂Q/∂δ‖ ≈ 0.01×2×12
≈ 0.25 (e^{2tQ} is a contraction). So the input error moves λ_min by at most ~5×10⁻¹⁸, three orders
short of the thing it was offered to explain. Its earlier hypothesis, that the eigensolver was
float64-limited, it withdrew itself: the solver is `mp.eigsy`. The dps-30/45 invariance therefore means
the opposite of contamination — the value is **exactly resolved**, seventeen orders above the arithmetic
floor (≈ max·10^{2−dps} ≈ 10⁻³² at dps 30). I adopt the string-input fix as hygiene and add an assert that
δ = 7/20 exactly, so nobody has to have this argument twice.

## What the number is, if the corrected rule confirms it

The single-time OS pairing ⟨A, Π e^{2tQ} A⟩ of the single-walker [PCD-EXT] toy measure has a negative
direction at δ = 0.35 for t ≥ 1 and none at t = 0.5. That is what a non-reversible chain is allowed to
do — VW-2's Theorem A proves positivity from detailed balance at δ = 0 and claims nothing beyond it — so
a confirmed NEGATIVE is consistent with the corpus, is a toy result, and moves no verdict. It would be
the first computed instance in the corpus of the necessary condition for VW-a-4 failing on any measure,
which is worth having exactly once and at exactly its size.
