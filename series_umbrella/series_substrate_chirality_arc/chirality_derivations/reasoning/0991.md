## What was found, at its size

On the single-walker [PCD-EXT] toy measure on the 600-cell — 4022's measure, the one every H1 patch
this month used — the single-time Osterwalder–Schrader pairing ⟨A, Π e^{2tQ} A⟩ has negative directions
at tilt δ = 0.35 for separations t ≥ 1 and none for t ≤ 0.75. The eigenvalue crosses zero between t =
0.75 and t = 1 at that tilt. Since the pairing is a necessary condition for Θ_OS positivity, **VW-a-4
fails on this toy at δ = 0.35** — the first computed instance in the corpus of the H1-side condition
failing on any measure.

Its size: a non-reversible Markov chain is under no obligation to have a positive OS pairing; VW-2's
Theorem A proves positivity from detailed balance at δ = 0 and stops there; TARROW-2's O(δ³) current is
the non-reversibility. So the finding is consistent with everything in the corpus and surprises nothing
in it. It is not H1 on the DSL measure (many walkers, interactions), and it moves no verdict. I state it
once and do not enlarge it.
