The founder ruled on the PD-006(a) question left by 0972/0973: *"Relabel accepted."* Recorded it verbatim

**The chirality_continuum instruction is wrong as written.** The corrigendum says *"`chirality_continuum.tex`
cites the A₂u label at four places (grep `A_{2u}`) — same correction, same recompile."* Grepping gives five
occurrences across four lines, and **they are not the same object.** Three are the matter-doublet **state
space** — the 2D subspace of `A₁g ⊕ A₂u` in `D₅d` — which 0937 never touched and which are correct as
written. 0937 changed the **operator** assignment: `Ĉ^qDP` moves from `A₂u(D₅d)` to `A₁g(D₅d) ⊗ C-odd`,
with the pseudoscalar content in the coefficient `χ` rather than in a `D₅d` irrep. A blind grep-and-substitute
would have corrupted three correct statements while fixing two. This is D-7 in its exact form — the symbol
`A₂u` means two different things in the same file, and the instruction resolved neither.

Recorded in the patch because the instruction will be read again at the recompile, by someone who may not
re-derive the distinction.

**Method, adopted after the first failure.** I wrote each edit as a unique-match replacement with an assert
on the match count. The SM-2 edits failed that assert on first attempt — my `old_str` was built from the
corrigendum's quoted text, and the file wraps its lines differently. **The assert is what caught it.** The
alternative I did not take was to loosen the match to make it apply; I re-read the file's actual wrapping and
rebuilt the strings against that. Brace balance checked on all five files after editing.

**Held deliberately: SM-2 edit (g).** The mass-breakdown W row still reads `Linear 6-hDP chain` on purpose.
It is gated on `OPEN-EW-5` — does the W mass breakdown depend on the species of the 12 CPs or only on their
count — and it is the only place a published SM-2 number can still move. Applying it now would assert a pure
relabel that may be false. Edits (a)–(f) are independent of it, so the rest went in. The corrigendum itself
says "do not apply until OPEN-EW-5 returns," and the temptation to finish the set is exactly why that
sentence is there.

**Capotauro relabel placed as a footnote, not a rewrite.** The ruling is that the equation stands and only
its description was wrong, so touching the derivation would overstate it. I added the scope note that
§`sec:substrate_locality` evaluates `ê_ij` consistently outward from the host vertex and is therefore
