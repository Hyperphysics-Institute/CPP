**Step D, and the part I got wrong.** I checked patch by patch before writing anything: 5 of 39 have a
patch-time fragment, 34 do not. I then declined to write the 34, and that refusal was right — Tier 4 is
*defined* as reasoning preserved verbatim, every other tier is defined as derived from it, and composing
fresh prose today under a `reasoning-<patch>.md` name would put a narration where the canonical record
belongs, undetectably, a year out. The protocol's `STATUS: reconstructed` header is for reconstruction from
surviving raw material, and I had none, so the header would have been a courtesy stretched over an
invention.

**But I wrote that the record was "a permanent loss," and that was a claim about the world made from the
state of the repo alone.** I had searched the repository. I had not asked whether the originating window was
still reachable. It was, and 0975b then captured 20 of the 34 from it as primary source. The honest status
was *not recovered*, not *unrecoverable*, and the difference is the difference between a gap and an
epitaph. **The failure is not the refusal to fabricate; it is closing an inquiry at the boundary of the
tool I happened to be holding.**

**What I wrote instead of the fragments**, and I still think this was right: a capture-gap section in the
Tier-2 index listing the five patches with verbatim, and for the other 34 a pointer-map to where the
substance sits. Every pointer path checked to resolve before committing — a broken pointer in a
substitute-for-the-record is worse than no substitute. I also stated what the substitute is *not*: those
artifacts carry conclusions and generally not discarded alternatives, so they answer what the lane concluded
and largely not what it considered and rejected.
