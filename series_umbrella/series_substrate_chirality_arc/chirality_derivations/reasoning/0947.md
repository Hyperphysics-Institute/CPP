"The handedness should reflect empirics" is the right criterion and I could not apply it directly, which is the finding.

**The W⁰ is not an observable.** It is the neutral member of the SU(2)_L triplet — a gauge eigenstate, mixing with B⁰ to give the physical Z and photon. Nobody measures a W⁰. So there is no "reality of the W⁰ handedness" to read off, and I had to say that before anything else rather than quietly substituting a proxy.

What *is* measured is the coupling: maximal parity violation, V−A. And CPP already cashes that — |M^W| = χ/6 = 0.0393 against Δp_LR ≈ 0.04, agreement to 1.6%. So if the ring order were *also* assigned a handedness to account for parity violation, we would be spending one observable twice. That is the second reason the direct route fails.

**The route that works runs through χ.** χ is a pseudoscalar with sign fixed by FI-C-9, and it is odd under all 60 orientation-reversing elements. So once χ ≠ 0 the lattice reflections are not symmetries of the *physical* substrate — only of the bare geometry. Under the surviving proper subgroup, the chiral arrangement splits into **two inequivalent states**; the achiral ones do not. The SM has exactly one W³, and the second cannot be absorbed as a charge partner because the W⁰ is neutral and self-conjugate.

So empirics selects achiral, by consequence rather than by inspection.

**Where I stopped.** Between alternating and blocked, I have a preference and not an argument: alternating keeps stabiliser order 6 including C₃, blocked keeps only order 2. C₃ is the symmetry the K₃-base arguments run on. That is symmetry economy, not an observable, and I put it to the founder as a lead rather than writing it in.
