## The move

B-iii (0668) reduced the chirality capacity question to one bit — `sign(μ²)` of the ℤ₂-even Landau potential `V(η)` — and pinned that bit behind §14.17 ("fix the DSL effective action, read off μ²"). The insight here: **that is the brute-force route to the bit; the verdict needs only the sign, and a sign can be forced by a principle or read from one correlator, neither of which needs the whole action.** Two such routes are native to existing programme assets:

1. **Vafa–Witten reflection-positivity (primary).** VW: in a reflection-positive, vector-like theory with no θ-term, parity is not spontaneously broken — i.e. `μ² > 0` is *forced*. The det-coset ℤ₂ *is* a parity (det = −1, enantiomorph exchange), `η` is its P-odd order parameter, capacity = "is this parity spontaneously broken?". So **if** VW's three hypotheses (H1 measure positivity, H2 vectorial-not-axial, H3 no θ-term) hold for the substrate (sharpest on the Φ-continuum EFT, CONT-1), VW delivers `sign(μ²) > 0` as a theorem with no §14.17. The contrapositive is equally informative: VW fails only via {chiral content, θ-term, complex measure}, and (audit VW-c) each appears to enter only through the SM bridge, not the bare substrate — which is *exactly* STATUS-2's axiom-level V2-exclusion restated in VW language (the symmetric substrate has no internal handle to break/select parity; an external cross-sector pseudoscalar is required). Recognizing V2-exclusion (value side) and the VW no-go (capacity side) as two faces of one reflection-positivity structure is the proposed first artifact.

2. **Chiral susceptibility (backstop).** `μ² ∝ χ_η⁻¹`; `sign(μ²) = sign(χ_η⁻¹)` from the connected `η`–`η` two-point function. The THEO-DSL-4..12 path-enumeration + PSLQ pipeline already computes exactly this class of 600-cell correlator in closed `ℚ[φ]` form — so the sign/divergence of one correlator is reachable in the programme's native zero-parameter style. Opens only if VW-a (measure positivity) stalls; carries a verify script when it lands; finite-lattice sign is suggestive, the block-spin trend is the honest continuum statement.
