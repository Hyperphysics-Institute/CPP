## The attack

0984's argument has one place it could be wrong: if the continuum CONT-1 builds mixes the Moment index
with the substrate's R⁴ into a Euclidean rotation group, η's image could be a full pseudoscalar of that
group, carry a "time index," and pick up VW's i in the continuum even though it has none on the lattice.
So the question is what the corpus takes the four dimensions and the Moment to *be*.

It is not ambiguous. The glossary defines the Moment as the unit of time (one PCD cycle). It defines ζ^W
as an inversion "in 4D ambient space" and ζ^qDP as "spatial inversion (v → −v) combined with n̂-flip":
R⁴ is space, n̂ is a spatial direction. VW-2's Theorem A builds Θ_OS from the transition kernel over
Moments. CONT-1's Φ is a Wilson–Fisher block-spin map on the substrate Hilbert space, equivariant under
the discrete symmetry groups; it inherits the substrate's split of space from time, it does not manufacture
a rotation between them. Under the corpus's own reading, η stays an equal-time pseudoscalar through Φ and
0984 stands.

The alternative — take n̂ as Euclidean time, so that η, odd under the whole det coset of O(4), is a genuine
4D Euclidean pseudoscalar and gets the i — is a coherent reading and it is *not* the corpus's. It would
also make the n̂-**flipping** reflection the OS reflection, which is exactly the one 4022 found the tilted
measure is not invariant under. I note it as a physics reading a founder could take; I am not taking it.
