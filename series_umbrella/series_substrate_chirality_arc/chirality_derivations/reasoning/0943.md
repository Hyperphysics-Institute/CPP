# reasoning-0943 (verbatim, captured late — see 0942 header note; same provenance)

The founder said "fix the other SM particles with the wrong charge." The literal reading was: patch strange and bottom, which I had flagged at 0942. I did not do that, and the reason is worth recording.

"The other particles with the wrong charge" is not a list I had — it is a question I had not asked. I had found three defects by reading; I had not *audited*. So I wrote the audit as a script over every cage entry against SM-2's own two rules (δ = 1/3 screening on a confined central qCP; DP species carry charge 0), rather than spot-fixing the two I already knew about.

That found the third: **the W**. Assigned "Linear hDP chain" — a chain of bound neutral pairs, charge 0 — while W^± carries ±1. Z and Higgs are genuinely neutral and unaffected, so the defect is specific to the charged member of the weak triplet. I would not have found it by patching s and b.

Strange and bottom confirmed as the identical defect, and the gap is −1 for all three despite N_k of 2.5, 30 and 3000 — cage-independent, so no cage-size argument distinguishes them and the repair generalises exactly. That mattered: it is why extending the founder's down-only ruling to s and b is a consequence rather than an assumption.

**Deliberately not done:** I did not extend the ruling to the W. The founder's 3531 statement covers a down-type centre; nothing in it determines a W composition, and inventing one would have been exactly the overreach the s/b extension is *not*. Escalated instead.
