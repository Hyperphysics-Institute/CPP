3. **V is ℤ₂-even ⇒ only even powers:** V = V₀ + μ²η² + λη⁴ + 𝒪(η⁶). The absence of odd terms is

4. **The reduction (λ>0):** vacuum structure is fixed by sign(μ²): μ²>0 ⇒ unique min at η=0 (symmetric,
   capacity NO); μ²<0 ⇒ minima at ±√(−μ²/2λ) (double-well, capacity YES); μ²=0 = bifurcation.
