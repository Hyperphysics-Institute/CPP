# reasoning-0944 (verbatim, captured late — see 0942 header note; same provenance)

The founder ruled the W⁰ a "12-member ring (three qDPs and three eCPs)" and told me to harmonise with the Weak Sector lane. Two jobs: find the lane's object, and check the composition against it.

The lane's object is the **W-bracelet** — SF-2 v1.0 Thm 4.2, carried into `capotauro.tex`. A Petrie hexagon of the first-shell icosahedron: **six** vertices, stabiliser D₆ of **order** 12. The "12" in the lane's material is a group order, not a membership count. That mattered immediately.

Then the arithmetic, which is where it broke. Three qDPs (2 CPs each) plus three eCPs (1 CP each) is 9 CPs, or 6 objects. Neither is 12. And worse: three eCPs at ±1 each total ±3 or ±1 — **never 0**. An odd number of unit charges cannot be neutral.

I checked whether that was an artefact of the grouping before reporting it, because "your composition can't be neutral" is a strong thing to say to the person who wrote it. Exhaustively: every 12-CP composition with an odd eCP count fails neutrality regardless of how many DPs accompany it. It is a parity obstruction, not a counting accident.

**Three qDPs + three eDPs** closes all four constraints at once — 12 CPs, six objects for six Petrie sites, neutral because every constituent is a bound pair, and it preserves SM-2's existing 12-CP count since "Linear 6-hDP chain" is also 12 CPs. So the correction changes topology and species and leaves the count alone.

I did **not** assume the substitution. Confirming a composition is the founder's call and the eCP/eDP difference is one character; guessing it right would still have been guessing. Asked, and he confirmed the typo.

The W^± half needed nothing — W⁰ plus one carried eCP is exactly ±1, with charge on the unpaired CP, which is the 3513 partnerless-third structure the DM lane already uses. Said so, rather than treating the whole ruling as suspect because part of it had a slip.
