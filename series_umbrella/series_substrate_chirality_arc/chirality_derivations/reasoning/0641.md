## Why now, and the decision about what to build

This consolidation fires at the agreed milestone: the three audit-downstream derivations
(E20 / E21 / E19) are all resolved. Per the four-tier discipline, Trigger 2 (the synthesized
companion suite) fires when a body of work is unambiguously done; the three-derivation set is
that body. The per-patch Tier-4 verbatim capture (reasoning fragments + scripts) was complete
throughout — verified on disk before building — so this is purely the synthesis layer over an
intact canonical record, not a recovery operation.

## The fit-for-purpose decision (not mechanical seven-file production)

The standard flagship companion set is mechanism/glossary/phenomena/philosophy/keywords/reviews/
FAQ. I adapted rather than copied, because chirality_derivations/ is a derivation sub-corpus, not
a single flagship paper:
- INCLUDED: README (the missing entry point), mechanism, development, reasoning-index, keywords,
  glossary, phenomena, changelog.
- OMITTED: philosophy (no distinct philosophical thesis beyond the programme's), FAQ (premature —
  no external-question corpus yet), reviews (the audit's review cycle lives in
  chirality_audit/review/; the derivations have not been through their own multi-AI review).
Producing empty philosophy/FAQ/reviews files to hit a count would be box-checking; omitting them
with this note is the honest choice. They can be added if/when a CHIR-derivation paper SHIPs.

## The canonical-record discipline in the consolidation

The single most important structural decision: the `reasoning-index` file is a POINTER-MAP, not a
copy. The per-patch `reasoning/<patch>.md` fragments remain the Tier-4 canonical record; the
documentation_suite synthesizes from them but does not supersede or duplicate them. I stated this
explicitly in the README ("canonical record note") and the reasoning-index header, so a future
reader knows the fragments are authoritative and the suite is derived. This preserves the
anti-erasure property: if the suite and a fragment ever disagree, the fragment wins.

## Honesty carried into the synthesis

Every doc-suite file carries the same caps the theorems do: emergent (provisional) not
established; FI-C-9 consumed not eliminated; the E19/E20 merge a hypothesis not a result;
the signposts (δ_CP, Δp_LR) flagged as signposts not derivations and explicitly not used in any
proof. The mechanism/phenomena files name the open frontier (1d-β, the form assumption, the
merge) rather than presenting the three resolutions as a finished story. The synthesis must not
launder provisional results into apparent closure — that would be a worse erasure than losing the
fragments, because it would misrepresent them.
