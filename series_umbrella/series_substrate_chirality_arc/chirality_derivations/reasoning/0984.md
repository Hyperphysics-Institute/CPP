## The question I set myself at 0983

Does VW-1's Theorem 6.1 consume Θ_OS positivity alone, or spatial-parity positivity too? I read the
theorem's *argument*, not VW-2's remark about it. The answer is neither, and the reason is not subtle.

## What Vafa–Witten actually uses

The parity theorem (VW 1984) is one inequality: the free energy in the presence of a parity-odd source
satisfies F(λ) ≥ F(0). It comes from |∫dμ e^{iλO}| ≤ ∫dμ, which needs **two** things: the Euclidean
weight dμ is real and non-negative, and **the source is a phase**. The i is not decoration. It is there
because a Lorentz-invariant pseudoscalar contains ε_{μνρσ}, one index is time, and Wick rotation supplies
a factor i. VW-1's own gloss says as much — *"the mechanism is a positivity bound: with a positive
measure, an explicit parity-breaking source can only raise the free energy"* — and the second half of
that sentence is silently the phase.

## What the substrate has

The stationary law of the PCD process is a probability. So "no sign problem" — the operational clause in
VW-1's Definition 2.1 — is trivially true of any classical stochastic substrate and carries no information
about sign(μ²). That is the first half.

The second half fails. Euclidean time is the Moment index (VW-2 builds Θ_OS from the transition kernel
over Moments). η, the det-coset order parameter, is odd under a reflection of R⁴ at *fixed* Moment: an
equal-time O(4)-pseudoscalar, a real observable. Its source is e^{λη}, real. And for a real source Jensen
gives Z(λ) ≥ e^{λ⟨η⟩} = Z(0) — **the opposite inequality**. The symmetric point *maximises* Z; the free
energy is concave in the source; nothing forces μ² > 0. On 4022's toy measure this is exact: Z(1) = 1.45,
|Z_phase(1)| = 0.65, at δ = 0 and at δ = 0.35 alike.

So the hypothesis Theorem 6.1 (ii) really consumes is not H1. It is an unstated H1′: *η's Euclidean source
enters as a phase*. And H1′ is false for any real equal-time observable of a classical Markov measure.

VW-1 v1.1's review calibration folded *"the vector-like-gauge-theory measure-class transposition"* into
H1's scope rather than treating it as satisfied. That was the right caution and it was never cashed. This
patch cashes it: the transposition is exactly the step where the i is needed, and as posed it does not go
through. I want to be precise about what is and is not claimed. VW-2's Theorems A and B are untouched as
statements about reflection positivity; 0983 stands. What falls is the *inference* from H1 to μ² > 0 —
the VW route delivers no bound on sign(μ²) for the substrate as modelled, whatever H1's truth value.
