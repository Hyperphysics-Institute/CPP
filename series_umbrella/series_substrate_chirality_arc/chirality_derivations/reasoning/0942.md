Went in expecting transcription. The founder's 3531 ruling gave the down quark's composition; the job looked like copying it into the corrigendum and checking the cage line matched.

Read SM-2's cage list and the charge section together, which is what turned it. The charge section fixes screening exactly: orbital ZBW screens the central qCP by δ = 1/3, so up-type reads +1 × (1 − 1/3) = +2/3 and down-type must reach −1/3. That is a gap of exactly −1, and the "linear ZBW extra" has to supply it.

Then looked at what the cage list says the extra *is*: "+extra DP". A DP is a bound CP/anti-CP pair. Charge zero. It cannot supply −1, or anything.

So SM-2's cage list contradicts SM-2's own charge section, and has since it was written. That is not a concession to the DM lane's ruling — it is an independent error the ruling happens to fix. Wrote it up that way deliberately, because "the corrigendum weakens the paper" was the natural reading and it is the wrong one.

**Where I was wrong and the test caught it.** First draft of T4 asserted that −eCP is the *unique* register species carrying −1. It is not: −qCP carries −1 too. The test refused the claim. Corrected it to what is true — the arithmetic narrows the extra to a *charged* CP but does not select between them, and the founder's ruling selects −eCP. Kept the failure visible in the script docstring rather than silently tightening the assertion, because the scoped version is the honest one and a reader should see why it is scoped.
