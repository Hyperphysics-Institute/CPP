The chirality lane's review (0904) caught two things in my 0813/0814 work, and both are right, so this patch corrects the record rather than defends it.

First, the inversion. I wrote "V3 stands by principle (chirality emergent at the engine level)." That is backwards. mu^2 > 0 is the UNBROKEN, symmetric branch: eta=0 is stable, the engine does NOT condense a net handedness, it is handedness-neutral. That means handedness stays a primitive input (FI-C-9) — V3 = primitive/inherited. The emergent, engine-generated outcome would have been mu^2 < 0 (condensation, V3->V1), the branch we are NOT on. I had the label exactly reversed. I retracted it in the doc and added a banner; the commit subjects (0813/0814) carry the inverted word too, and since I cannot edit pushed history I noted the retraction in the doc instead.

Second, and more important than the label: the finite-positive chi is near-tautological given the product base. I assumed an off-critical (product) measure and then "found" off-criticality. The decisive question — is the ACTUAL eta-field measure off-critical? — is exactly the 14.17 content and is not lifted by 0813. I had flagged product-base inheritance as "condition (2)," but the review is right that this is not a side-condition: it is THE question, and assuming it assumes the answer. So 0813 is a conditional confirmation of the standing V3/primitive picture (already fixed by MERGE-2 -> FI-C-9), not a derivation and not a gate-lift. My own 0814 actually reinforces the review's caution: the real Mechanism-A NESS departs from the clean product base (skewed at O(delta)), so the product base was only the leading approximation. I should have connected that myself.

The DM-2 consequence I want on the record: the inversion does NOT change the DM-2 physics — clean Lambda depends on off-criticality, not on the emergent/primitive label — but the conditionality DOES carry over. My "clean Lambda" rests on the same off-critical-field-measure assumption, which is the open 14.17 content. So DM-2's clean Lambda is conditional on 14.17, not settled, and I added that note to 0814 so the DM record does not over-read either.

On the framing more broadly: the productive reading is the calibrated one the review lands on. Chirality-as-primitive was already the standing position; this conditionally supports it from the dynamics side (the engine stays neutral, so the handedness has to live in FI-C-9); it does not show the substrate selecting a handedness, and it does not make chirality an ontological axiom (V3 is "not yet derived," the V1 door stays open). The right next move is descriptive — pin down what sign(n^) is and where it enters the axioms — not a new axiom-level claim.

I am logging this as a clean correction because a wrong-direction framing left in the record is exactly the kind of thing that hardens into a belief, and the cross-lane check existing to catch it is the system working as intended. Better to bank the catch than to soften it.
