Three things, two computed and one by symmetry. The current scales as delta^3.09 (re-confirms 0814) and at the physical bias delta=phi^-3 it is 3e-5 -- even smaller than the delta^3 ~ 0.013 bound, because the prefactor is small. The divergence is zero to machine precision: stationarity makes the current divergence-free, so it has no net source to pump an ordering mode -- it circulates. And the symmetry argument I lean on: the current is T-odd, the order parameter T-even, so the current can only couple to ordering at even powers, O(J^2)=O(delta^6) ~ 0.0002. The margin to close is 36% of K_c. O(delta^3) ~ 0.013 and O(delta^6) ~ 0.0002 are both far below it. So no K_c collapse, no current-induced ordering, at the physical bias.

I was careful to state the caveat honestly: this is a parametric / physical-scale argument, not an all-orders proof. The all-orders version is the full non-equilibrium field theory. But the suppression at the actual delta is overwhelming (3e-5), so I am comfortable calling residual 2 closed at the physical bias while naming what the rigorous version would be.
