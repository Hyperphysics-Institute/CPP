## §1 Problem statement

Extend THEO-DSL-9 (face $\mathcal{O}(\delta^2)$ closure) to $\mathcal{O}(\delta^3)$ by computing the closed-form coefficients of the third-order substrate-current vector $\vec j_3^{\,\text{face}}(v_{\text{host}})$ at face-aligned Reading C in the 600-cell, under the $V_4$-invariant 2-D subspace $\mathrm{span}\{\hat n_\rho, \hat n_{\text{ax}}\}$ established at THEO-DSL-9 (multi-AI confirmed at Patch 0617).

Assembly:
$$\vec j_3(v_{\text{host}}) = \sum_{P \in \mathcal{P}_3(v_{\text{host}})} (\hat e_1 \cdot \hat n_{F\perp})(\hat e_2 \cdot \hat n_{F\perp})(\hat e_3 \cdot \hat n_{F\perp})\,\hat e_1$$
summed over the $|S_1|^3 = 1728$ directed 3-edge paths, with $\hat n_{F\perp} = (v_h + u_i + u_j)/\|v_h + u_i + u_j\|$.

## §2 The $\sqrt 3$ surprise

### §2.1 Initial PSLQ search failure

At $k=2$ face (THEO-DSL-9, Patch 0615) the coefficients came out clean in $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$: $\alpha_2^{(\rho)} = -7/\phi^2 = -14 + 7\phi$ and $\alpha_2^{(\text{ax})} = -6 + 2\phi$. The natural expectation at $k=3$ was the same algebraic structure.

Numerical assembly at $k=3$ gave $\alpha_3^{(\rho)} \approx +0.71834$ and $\alpha_3^{(\text{ax})} \approx -2.48547$, with the two non-invariant components vanishing at machine precision (V_4 structural inheritance working as expected). But PSLQ at 80-digit precision in basis $\{1, \phi\}$ failed to find any rational relation up to `maxcoeff=1e20`. Same machinery that produced clean closed forms at $k=2$ — failing at $k=3$.

### §2.2 Diagnosis: $\|v_h + u_i + u_j\| = \phi\sqrt 3$

The diagnosis came from inspecting the norm:
$$\|v_h + u_i + u_j\|^2 = 3 + 2(v_h \cdot u_i) + 2(v_h \cdot u_j) + 2(u_i \cdot u_j) = 3 + 3\phi = 3\phi^2$$
so $\|v_h + u_i + u_j\| = \phi\sqrt 3$. This means $\hat n_{F\perp}$ carries an unavoidable $1/\sqrt 3$ in its normalization — it lives in $\mathbb{Q}[\phi, \sqrt 3]^4$, not $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]^4$.

By contrast:
- Vertex variant: $\hat n_\rho = v_h \in \mathbb{Q}[\phi]^4$ (no $\sqrt 3$).
- Edge variant: $\hat n_{\text{edge}} = \phi(u_1 - v_h) \in \mathbb{Q}[\phi]^4$ (no $\sqrt 3$; edge length is $1/\phi$).
- Face variant: $\hat n_{F\perp}$ carries $1/\sqrt 3$ from the centroid norm.
- Face axial: $\hat n_{\text{ax}} = u_i + u_j - \phi v_h$ has EXACT unit norm (Lemma 1 of artifact), in $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]^4$ — no $\sqrt 3$.

The $\sqrt 3$ is specific to the centroid direction $\hat n_{F\perp}$.

### §2.3 Parity case analysis

Each factor $\hat e_j \cdot \hat n_{F\perp}$ has the form $c_j/\sqrt 3$ with $c_j \in \mathbb{Q}[\phi]$. The $k$-fold product:
$$\prod_{j=1}^k (\hat e_j \cdot \hat n_{F\perp}) = \frac{\prod_j c_j}{(\sqrt 3)^k} = \frac{\prod_j c_j}{3^{k/2}}$$

- $k$ EVEN ($k=2, 4, ...$): $3^{k/2}$ is rational; product in $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]/3^{k/2}$. Clean $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$ after summation.
- $k$ ODD ($k=3, 5, ...$): $3^{k/2} = 3^{(k-1)/2}\sqrt 3$ — irrational; product in $\sqrt 3 \cdot \mathbb{Q}[\phi]/3^{(k+1)/2}$.

At $k=2$ (even): $\alpha_2^{(\rho)}, \alpha_2^{(\text{ax})} \in \mathbb{Q}[\phi]/3$ — but the $1/3$ absorbs into the rationals giving clean $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$. ✓ (matches THEO-DSL-9: $-14+7\phi, -6+2\phi$).

At $k=3$ (odd): $\alpha_3^{(\rho)}, \alpha_3^{(\text{ax})} \in \sqrt 3 \cdot \mathbb{Q}[\phi]/9 \subseteq \sqrt 3 \cdot \mathbb{Q}[\phi]/3$.

This is Theorem 1 of the artifact: the **parity-dependent algebraic structure** of face-aligned coefficients.

### §2.4 PSLQ in the right basis

Re-running PSLQ in basis $\{1, \phi, \sqrt 3, \sqrt 3\phi\}$ at 80-digit precision immediately found:
$$\alpha_3^{(\rho)} = 29\sqrt 3 - \frac{53\sqrt 3}{3}\phi = \frac{(87 - 53\phi)\sqrt 3}{3}$$
$$\alpha_3^{(\text{ax})} = \frac{41\sqrt 3}{3} - \frac{28\sqrt 3}{3}\phi = \frac{(41 - 28\phi)\sqrt 3}{3}$$

The rational and $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$-only components (the $1$ and $\phi$ coefficients) are EXACTLY ZERO in PSLQ output, confirming the pure-$\sqrt 3$ structure. The $\sqrt 3$ and $\sqrt 3\phi$ components are $(29, -53/3)$ and $(41/3, -28/3)$ respectively.

### §2.5 Verification by dividing out $\sqrt 3$

The verify script confirms the structure by computing $\alpha_3^{(\rho)}/\sqrt 3 \approx +0.41473$ and $\alpha_3^{(\text{ax})}/\sqrt 3 \approx -1.43498$, then identifying these with the $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$ targets $29 - 53\phi/3$ and $41/3 - 28\phi/3$ to machine precision. Both targets pass.

## §3 Structural inheritance preserved at $k=3$

The $V_4$-forced vanishing of the two non-invariant components (THEO-DSL-9 Lemmas 1–2, multi-AI confirmed at Patch 0617) holds **order-by-order** because:
- $\sigma_E$ kills the $\hat n_{\text{diff}}$ component at any $k$;
- $\sigma_F$ kills the $\hat n_{\perp 3}$ component at any $k$;
both are 600-cell automorphisms fixing $v_h$ and $\hat n_{F\perp}$, so the $V_4$-equivariance of the assembly forces the result into the 2-D invariant subspace $\mathrm{span}\{\hat n_\rho, \hat n_{\text{ax}}\}$.

The verify script confirms this cell-by-cell: ALL 13 non-empty $(s_{v'}, s_{v''})$ cells have $\hat n_{\perp 3}$- and $\hat n_{\text{diff}}$-components at machine zero ($\sim 10^{-16}$), not just the global total. This is stronger than the global-vanishing claim, and verifies the inheritance from THEO-DSL-9 publication-grade structural lemmas.

## §5 Dead ends and alternative searches

- **Naive PSLQ in $\{1, \phi\}$**: failed up to `maxcoeff=1e20` at 100-digit precision. Initial confusion: thought the numerics might be off. Verified by independent numerical reconstruction at 80 digits with `mpmath` matching to all 80 digits. Resolution: the closed form is in $\sqrt 3 \cdot \mathbb{Q}[\phi]/3$, not $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$ — basis was wrong, not the numerics.

- **Denominator scan**: tried $\alpha_3^{(\rho)} = (a + b\phi)/d$ for $d \in \{1, ..., 1000\}$ — no clean match. (Same reason: no clean $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]/d$ form exists.)

- **Other algebraic extensions**: considered whether the coefficient could involve $\sqrt 5$ (since $\phi = (1+\sqrt 5)/2$). But $\sqrt 5 \in \mathbb{Q}[\phi]$ trivially ($\sqrt 5 = 2\phi - 1$), so any $\sqrt 5$ form would have been caught by the $\{1, \phi\}$ PSLQ. The $\sqrt 3$ is the genuinely new algebraic feature.

- **Alternative basis for $\mathrm{span}\{\hat n_\rho, \hat n_{\text{ax}}\}$**: I attempted to find a $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]^4$ basis that would absorb the $\sqrt 3$ via re-scaling, but no such basis exists. The frame $\{\hat n_\rho, \hat n_{\text{ax}}\}$ I'm using is already in $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]^4$ (both unit norm exactly in $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$, per Lemmas in artifact §2). The $\sqrt 3$ enters strictly through the source direction $\hat n_{F\perp}$ via $\|v_h + u_i + u_j\| = \phi\sqrt 3$. Re-scaling $\hat n_{F\perp}$ doesn't help — it changes the size of $\delta$, not the algebraic structure of the coefficients.

- **Alternative substrate primitive direction**: one could use the un-normalized $\vec n_{F\perp}^{\text{un-norm}} = v_h + u_i + u_j$ instead of the unit $\hat n_{F\perp}$. With this convention, the rate function becomes $r(\hat e) = r_0(1 + \delta \hat e \cdot (\phi\sqrt 3) \hat n_{F\perp})$ — the $\phi\sqrt 3$ gets absorbed into $\delta$. With the un-normalized convention, the coefficient at order $k$ would be $\alpha_k^{(\text{un-norm})} = \alpha_k \cdot (\phi\sqrt 3)^k$. At $k=3$: $(\phi\sqrt 3)^3 = \phi^3 \cdot 3\sqrt 3 = (2\phi+1) \cdot 3\sqrt 3 = (6\phi + 3)\sqrt 3$, so multiplying $\alpha_3^{(\rho)}$ by this absorbs the $\sqrt 3$ if and only if the original $\sqrt 3$ matches — which it does, giving an un-normalized coefficient in $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$. But this convention is not the one used by THEO-DSL-9; the unit-$\hat n$ convention is standard.

## §6 Falsifier targets for future multi-AI review

The Patch 0620 anti-erasure remark invites multi-AI verification on the $\sqrt 3$ finding. Specific falsifiers a future review could surface:

1. **Counter-derivation in clean $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$**: would contradict Theorem 1 (the parity-dependent algebraic structure) unless the $V_4$-invariant subspace admits a basis other than $\{\hat n_\rho, \hat n_{\text{ax}}\}$ that hides the $\sqrt 3$. I have not been able to construct such a basis, but a reviewer might.

2. **Different numerical value of the closed-form coefficients**: would suggest a computational error in the 1728-path enumeration or the dot-product evaluation. Falsifiable by independent reproduction.

3. **Non-vanishing of $\alpha_3^{(\perp 3)}$ or $\alpha_3^{(\text{diff})}$**: would refute the $V_4$-equivariance carry-over to $k=3$, which would be a substantive issue since THEO-DSL-9's $V_4$ lemmas are multi-AI confirmed at $k$-independent rigor.

4. **Path-count $\neq 1728$**: would suggest the 9-shell classification is over-counting or under-counting. The verify script reports $1728$ exactly.

5. **Vertex cross-check $\neq -126 + 81\phi$**: would refute the assembly convention (which is the same as THEO-DSL-5/-7/-9/-10).
