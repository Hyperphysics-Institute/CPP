## What this patch does
Closes the open computation of OPEN-FP-F1-5 Sequence-2A — the O(δ²) edge-aligned
substrate-current coefficients α₂^(ρ), α₂^(edge) in the 2D D₅-invariant subspace
span{n̂_ρ, n̂_edge} (THEO-DSL-6). This was the single open item carried out of

## The method, and why it is the same as vertex
Mechanism A rate r(ê)=r₀(1+δ ê·n̂). The O(δ²) current under (H5_E) W(P)=1 is the
path-class sum over the 144 directed 2-edge paths v_host→u_i→v':

    j₂ = Σ_P (ê₁·n̂)(ê₂·n̂) ê₁        [direction = first edge ê₁ = û_i]

This is *exactly* the vertex assembly of Patch 0591 (THEO-DSL-5); only n̂ changes
from n̂_ρ=v_host to n̂_edge=(u₁−v_host)/|·|=û₁. Keeping the assembly identical is
what makes the vertex result a valid cross-check.

## The key conceptual point (why Session 146 deferred it)
Vertex case: Ω(u_i) = (û_i·n̂_ρ)·Σ_{v'~u_i}(ê₂·n̂_ρ) = (−1/2φ)(−3) = 3/2φ is UNIFORM
across all 12 first-shell vertices (I_h). So j₂ = (3/2φ)Σ_i û_i = (3/2φ)(−6/φ)n̂_ρ
= −9/φ² n̂_ρ. Collapses to 1D (the k=2 case of THEO-DSL-4).

Edge case: under D₅ (order 10, not 120) the 12 first-shell vertices split into
orbits {1,5,5,1} (G1_E), and BOTH factors of Ω vary by orbit. So the vector sum
Σ Ω'(u_i) û_i does NOT collapse to a multiple of one symmetric vector — it keeps a
real n̂_ρ AND n̂_edge component. That is the "2D vector path-class assembly the
vertex machinery does not give directly" the handover warned about. The honest
resolution: don't try to massage the vertex 1D scalar template into 2D — just carry
the full 4D vector sum explicitly and decompose at the end. That is what the verify
script does (explicit 600-cell, sum 144 vectors, solve 2×2 Gram system).

## The sign-suspicion, resolved
The handover flagged 9/(2φ), 9φ−12 (positive) as "sign-suspicious" vs vertex −9/φ²
(negative), and asked: reproduce vertex with the same machinery FIRST. Done — same
code gives vertex −9/φ² (perp ~1e-16) and edge +9/(2φ), +9φ−12. So the positive sign
is REAL, not a bug. Structural cause: the B.2 first-shell-trans class vanishes
identically at vertex (every icosahedron edge ⟂ n̂_ρ, G1.2) but is non-zero at edge
(G1_E.2 projections {0,±1/2,±φ/2}); and the cross-shell B.3 dominance that makes the
vertex coefficient negative no longer dominates. So the reverted Patch-0605 values
were CORRECT all along — Patch 0605 was reverted for *grounding/process* reasons
(wrong dir, unverified, no cross-check), not because the numbers were wrong. This
patch is the grounded re-derivation that vindicates them.
