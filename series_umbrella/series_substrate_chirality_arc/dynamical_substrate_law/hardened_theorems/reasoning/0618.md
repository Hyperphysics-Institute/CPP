## §1 Problem statement

Extend THEO-DSL-7 (edge $\mathcal{O}(\delta^2)$ closure) to $\mathcal{O}(\delta^3)$ by computing the closed-form $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$ coefficients of the third-order substrate-current vector $\vec j_3^{\,\text{edge}}(v_{\text{host}})$ at edge-aligned Reading C in the 600-cell, under the $D_5$-invariant 2-D subspace $\mathrm{span}\{\hat n_\rho, \hat n_{\text{edge}}\}$ established at THEO-DSL-6.

The assembly is the natural $k=3$ extension of THEO-DSL-7's $k=2$ assembly:
$$\vec j_3(v_{\text{host}}) = \sum_{P \in \mathcal{P}_3(v_{\text{host}})} (\hat e_1 \cdot \hat n_{\text{edge}})(\hat e_2 \cdot \hat n_{\text{edge}})(\hat e_3 \cdot \hat n_{\text{edge}})\,\hat e_1,$$
summed over the $|S_1|^3 = 1728$ directed 3-edge paths $v_{\text{host}} \to u_1 \to u_2 \to u_3$.

## §2 Structural inheritance (publication-grade)

The 2-D $D_5$-invariant subspace $\mathrm{span}\{\hat n_\rho, \hat n_{\text{edge}}\}$ from THEO-DSL-6 (Patch 0596) carries over **unconditionally** to $k=3$: the proof depends only on $D_5$-equivariance of the assembly and $D_5$-fixedness of $\hat n_{\text{edge}}$, both of which hold order-by-order. Hence $\vec j_3^{\,\text{edge}} \in \mathrm{span}\{\hat n_\rho, \hat n_{\text{edge}}\}$ is Layer-3 publication-grade. The $(H5_E^{(3)})$ ansatz is needed only for the specific coefficient values, not for the subspace containment.

## §3 Computation chain (sketch-document Layer 3 under $(H5_E^{(3)})$)

### §3.1 Numerical assembly

Step 1: build 600-cell from first principles (16-cell + tesseract + snub 24-cell with even-permutation parity); 120 vertices, 12-regular adjacency at $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle = \phi/2$, edge length $1/\phi$.

Step 2: enumerate the 1728 directed 3-edge paths from $v_{\text{host}}$ (12 first-shell × 12 neighbours × 12 neighbours).

Step 3: classify by $(s_{v'}, s_{v''})$ in the full 9-shell vertex classification (dots $\{\pm 1, \pm\phi/2, \pm 1/2, \pm 1/(2\phi), 0\}$ around $v_{\text{host}}$). At $k=3$, paths reach as far as shell $B_5$ (dot $= -1/(2\phi)$); the simpler $B_0, B_1, B_2, B_3$ coarsening used at $k\leq 2$ misses reachable shells. This was the **bug** caught during initial verify-script construction: the original `shell()` function returned `None` for $B_4..B_8$ and the corresponding `if s is None: continue` silently filtered 252 of the 1728 paths, producing a wrong numerical answer that disagreed with the PSLQ-extracted closed form. Fixed by extending `shell()` to the full 9-shell classification; total path count then matches $1728 = 12^3$.

Step 4: project the summed vector onto the non-orthogonal basis $\{\hat n_\rho, \hat n_{\text{edge}}\}$ via Gram solve with $\hat n_\rho \cdot \hat n_{\text{edge}} = -1/(2\phi)$ (the universal host-to-$S_1$ projection THEO-DSL-2).

Step 5: extract closed forms in $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$ at 60-digit mpmath precision via PSLQ. Output: $\alpha_3^{(\rho)} = -84 + 54\phi$, $\alpha_3^{(\text{edge})} = -63/2 + (21/2)\phi$.

Step 6: verify the closed forms to machine-double precision; verify robustness over all 12 anchor choices; verify the residual $\|\vec j_3 - \alpha_3^{(\rho)} \hat n_\rho - \alpha_3^{(\text{edge})} \hat n_{\text{edge}}\| < 10^{-12}$.

### §3.2 Vertex cross-check at $k=3$

The same 1728-path assembly with $\hat n = v_{\text{host}}$ produces $\alpha_3^{(\text{vertex})} = -126 + 81\phi = 9(9\phi - 14) = 9(4-\phi)/\phi^3 \approx +5.061$. The perpendicular residual is $\sim 10^{-15}$, confirming THEO-DSL-4's 1-D $I_h$-invariance at $k=3$.

The $k=1, k=2$ cross-checks $\alpha_1 = 3/\phi^2 = 6-3\phi$ and $\alpha_2 = -9/\phi^2 = -18+9\phi$ pass to machine precision, locking the assembly convention to the one used in THEO-DSL-5 and THEO-DSL-7. The programme-normalised $\alpha_1 = 6/\phi^2$ (THEO-DSL-3) corresponds to the **net-flux** assembly (both directions of each edge contributing $r_+ - r_- = 2\delta r_0 (\hat e \cdot \hat n)$); the one-directional assembly used here yields the half value $3/\phi^2$, consistent with the verify-script convention since Patch 0591.

## §4 Sign-pattern observation

The mixed-sign pattern at $k=3$ edge ($\alpha_3^{(\rho)} > 0$, $\alpha_3^{(\text{edge})} < 0$) is distinct from the patterns at $k=2$:

| $k$ | Vertex $\alpha_k$ | Edge $\alpha_k^{(\rho)}$ | Edge $\alpha_k^{(\text{edge})}$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | $+3/\phi^2 > 0$ | $-1/2 + (1/2)\phi > 0$ | $-3/(2\phi) < 0$ (THEO-DSL-1 dichotomy) |
| 2 | $-9/\phi^2 < 0$ | $9/(2\phi) > 0$ | $9\phi - 12 > 0$ |
| 3 | $-126 + 81\phi > 0$ | $-84 + 54\phi > 0$ | $(21/2)(\phi - 3) < 0$ |

The vertex coefficient alternates with $k$ (positive at odd $k$, negative at $k=2$); the edge $\alpha_k^{(\rho)}$ stays positive at $k=2, 3$ but the edge $\alpha_k^{(\text{edge})}$ changes sign at $k=3$. This is registered as an empirical sign-pattern observation, not a theorem.

## §5 Q[$\phi$] structure preserved at edge variant

Unlike the face variant (Patch 0620), the edge variant maintains a clean $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$ closed form at $k=3$. This is because $\hat n_{\text{edge}} = \phi(u_1 - v_{\text{host}}) \in \mathbb{Q}[\phi]^4$ — there is no $\sqrt 3$ in its normalisation (edge length is $1/\phi \in \mathbb{Q}[\phi]$). The vertex variant similarly stays in $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$ ($\hat n_\rho = v_{\text{host}} \in \mathbb{Q}[\phi]^4$). Only the face variant picks up the $\sqrt 3$ factor from $\|v_{\text{host}} + u_i + u_j\| = \phi\sqrt 3$.

## §6 Dead ends and alternative searches considered

- **Initial PSLQ at 50-digit precision with maxcoeff $10^6$**: failed to find a relation for face $k=3$ in $\{1, \phi\}$ basis. Misled me momentarily into thinking the numerics were off. Resolution: see §5 — the face coefficients are in $\sqrt 3 \cdot \mathbb{Q}[\phi]$, not $\mathbb{Q}[\phi]$. For the edge variant, $\{1, \phi\}$ basis with 50 dps + maxcoeff $10^6$ was sufficient.
- **Naive shell-bug**: the initial verify-script `shell()` function only handled $B_0..B_3$. At $k=3$, 252 of the 1728 paths (those reaching $B_4..B_5$) were filtered, producing a wrong numerical sum ($\alpha_3^{(\rho)} = 3.959$ instead of $3.374$). Caught immediately by the vertex cross-check FAIL (expected $-126 + 81\phi$, got $-5.137$). Resolution: extended `shell()` to all 9 shells; total path count then matches $1728 = 12^3$ exactly and all closed-form checks pass.
- **Sign-alternation conjecture**: explored whether $\alpha_k^{(\rho)}/\alpha_{k-1}^{(\rho)}$ has a closed form (e.g. constant ratio, or $\propto -3/\phi^{?}$). Numerics at $k=3$: $\alpha_3^{(\rho, \text{edge})}/\alpha_2^{(\rho, \text{edge})} = (-84+54\phi)/(9/(2\phi)) = (2\phi/9)(-84+54\phi) = (1/9)(2\phi)(54\phi - 84) = (12\phi^2 - (56/3)\phi)$... not a clean form. Registered as future exploration target.
- **Alternative basis for closed form**: tested whether $\alpha_3^{(\rho)}$ has a cleaner expression as $c/\phi^n$ for various $n$. Cleanest forms: $-84 + 54\phi = 6(9\phi - 14) = 6(4-\phi)/\phi^3$. The factor of 6 (vs the vertex factor of 9 at $k=3$) is a curious empirical observation worth flagging.

## §7 Falsifier targets

A future multi-AI review could refute this artifact by exhibiting:
1. A different value of $\alpha_3^{(\rho)}$ or $\alpha_3^{(\text{edge})}$ from independent computation;
2. A vertex cross-check disagreeing with $-126 + 81\phi$;
3. A non-empty $\hat n_{\text{diff}}$- or $\hat n_{\perp 3}$- component of $\vec j_3$ (would refute Lemma 1 inherited from THEO-DSL-6 at $k=3$);
4. A path-count $\neq 1728$ from the assembly enumeration;
5. A coefficient sum across the 13 per-class cells that doesn't reproduce the closed-form totals.

Targets 1–2 (numerical) are testable by any reviewer with the verify script. Targets 3–5 (structural) require independent path enumeration.
