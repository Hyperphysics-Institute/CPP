## Reviewer's strongest features identified (verbatim, condensed)

### 1. Chirality treated as emergent constraint rather than primitive ontology

The manuscript's biggest conceptual improvement. Earlier drafts implied "chirality exists because the substrate is fundamentally chiral." v5 increasingly argues "chirality emerges as a stability-preserving consequence of orientation continuity and closure inheritance." Changes chirality from metaphysical declaration into derivational consequence.

### 2. Orientation-closure hierarchy much cleaner

The manuscript better distinguishes:

| Level                       | Role                           |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| substrate orientation       | primitive continuity condition |
| local closure asymmetry     | emergent geometric imbalance   |
| inheritance propagation     | stability transmission         |
| effective chirality         | EFT manifestation              |
| observable parity asymmetry | downstream projection          |

Especially important: the framework increasingly avoids conflating geometric handedness with measurable weak-interaction chirality.

### 3. Weak-sector routing far more plausible

The chain from orientation continuity → closure imbalance → ΔpLR → V–A phenomenology is now substantially more operational. The manuscript increasingly acknowledges finite-mass complications, helicity limitations, EFT mediation, and projection-layer ambiguity. That honesty increases credibility.

### 4. Framework controls ontological inflation

Earlier versions risked proliferating orientation categories, introducing too many manifestation layers, and allowing chirality language to spread everywhere. v5 increasingly insists that not all asymmetry is chiral, not all chirality is fundamental, and not all substrate structure projects observably.

### 5. Closure logic is becoming the real core

At this stage, the strongest part of the framework is no longer "chirality." It is closure propagation under orientation continuity. Chirality increasingly appears as a downstream stability phenomenon, rather than the foundational primitive itself.

### 6. Manuscript much better at identifying open problems

v5 increasingly labels conjectural sectors, incomplete derivations, heuristic mappings, and phenomenological approximations. Speculative frameworks become much stronger when they explicitly expose their uncertainty structure.

## Reviewer's central remaining problems (verbatim, condensed)

### 1. Framework still lacks a genuine dynamical law

**Dominant issue.** The manuscript has orientation topology, continuity logic, inheritance structure, closure propagation, asymmetry emergence, but still lacks a true substrate evolution equation, action principle, or dynamical field structure. Right now the framework explains what stable chirality-like structure should look like, but not fully why the substrate must dynamically evolve into it.

### 2. Chirality emergence suggestive but not yet inevitable

The framework shows plausibility, structural consistency, and recurrence, but not yet uniqueness. Critics will still ask: "Why this chirality structure instead of another closure arrangement?"

### 3. EFT mapping still needs more precision

The manuscript has improved considerably here. But the transition from substrate geometry to Standard Model observables still contains interpretive gaps. Especially:
- mass-sector projection,
- gauge emergence,
- renormalization correspondence,
- and chirality protection mechanisms.

At present, the EFT layer behaves more like a guided correspondence than a rigorous derivation.

### 4. Theory risks becoming too architecturally complete

The more internally elegant the framework becomes, the more reviewers may worry that every result is explainable after the fact. This creates the danger of **retrospective closure**. The manuscript must continue emphasizing possible failure modes, experimentally vulnerable sectors, and nontrivial predictive constraints.

### 5. Framework needs one canonical "mechanism figure"

The framework is now large enough that readers need a cognitive map. You need one master diagram showing:

$$\text{orientation continuity} \rightarrow \text{closure imbalance} \rightarrow \Delta p_{LR} \rightarrow \text{effective chirality} \rightarrow \text{observable asymmetry}$$

including theorem dependencies, conjectural sectors, EFT bridges, falsifiers, and unresolved dynamical gaps.
