## HONEST SCOPE LIMIT (important for future hardening)

I did NOT re-derive 6/phi^2 from a per-orbit (B.1, B.3) path-class sum, because
the vertex-aligned first-order B.1 and B.3 sub-class values were NOT in my
context at patch-time. I tried to reverse-engineer them:
  - At vertex-aligned: B.2 == 0, B.4 = -phi/2 uniform (both from Patch 0591).
  - B.4 contribution to alpha_1 = -(phi/2) * sum(p_i). And sum(p_i) =
    1 + 1/2 - 1/(2phi) - phi/2 = 2 - phi = 1/phi^2, so B.4 contributes
    -(phi/2)(1/phi^2) = -1/(2phi).
  - To hit alpha_1 = 6/phi^2 the (B.1+B.3) projected sum must supply
    6/phi^2 + 1/(2phi); but with two unknowns per orbit and no closed forms,
    this is underdetermined. I could not uniquely recover B.1, B.3.
So I CITED 6/phi^2 from Sequence-2A and registered the re-derivation as
OPEN-A1V-1 (Layer 4). The sign (+) is by the Reading-C convention, registered
as OPEN-A1V-2. The patch is therefore STRUCTURAL + cited-magnitude, not a
from-scratch closure. Stated this plainly in the .tex proof body and CHANGELOG
so nobody mistakes it for a full derivation.
