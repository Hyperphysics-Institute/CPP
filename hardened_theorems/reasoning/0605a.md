## Nature of the patch

Umbrella registration combining Patch 0600 (first-order edge-aligned
structural) + Patch 0605 (second-order edge-aligned closure) into one citable
theorem THEO-DSL-7. No new physics; the reasoning worth keeping is the
finding-selection rationale and a couple of framing decisions.

## Why these four umbrella findings (not others)

The selection criterion I used: a finding earns umbrella status only if it is a
CROSS-PATCH observation invisible at the level of either constituent alone.
That filter produced exactly four:

- F-DSL-7-1 (order-by-order 2D confinement). The non-trivial content: the
  symmetric tensor square of a 2D subspace need not stay 2D (could be 3D), so
  j_2 staying in the j_1 subspace is a real constraint, not automatic. I almost
  wrote this as "obvious"; corrected myself — it is a D_5 representation-content
  fact and deserves the finding.
- F-DSL-7-2 (path-class weights orientation-independent). This is the load-
  bearing observation for the whole edge<->vertex program: it means only the
  projection functional S_*(O_i) changes between orientations, never the
  combinatorial weights. I promoted it to a named finding because Patches 0606
  and 0606a both lean on it.
- F-DSL-7-3 (B.2 as orientation discriminant). Pairs with F-DSL-7-2: weights
  fixed, the discriminating structure is B.2 (0 at vertex, nonzero at edge).
- F-DSL-7-4 (program complete at O(delta^2) edge-aligned). Bookkeeping closure.

Rejected candidates: I considered a finding about the specific numeric ratio
alpha_2_edge/alpha_2_rho, but it is not cross-patch (lives entirely in 0605),
so it stayed a within-patch observation.

## Framing decision

Wrote the umbrella as "no new proof obligations introduced — registration
provides a single citation handle." This keeps the rigor honest: the umbrella
does not upgrade the Layer-3 status of its constituents, it just packages them.
