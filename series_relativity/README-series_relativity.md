## Overview

SR-1 derives special relativistic effects — time dilation, length contraction, and the twin paradox — from geometric constraints in the 600-cell lattice. Kinetic energy stored as excess Space Stress Vector (ΔSSV) reduces effective Voronoi cell volumes, shrinking the Planck Sphere Radius (PSR) and limiting displacement per Absolute Moment. The coupling constant k ≈ 2.16 × 10⁻¹¹⁴ m³/J is derived from first principles and confirmed numerically. Existing muon storage-ring data at 10¹⁸g accelerations are fully consistent with CPP.

## Appendices (in SR-1)

SR-1 is the most extensively appendixed paper in the CPP programme (7 appendices, 50 pages total):
- **A:** PSR derivation from 600-cell lattice geometry (Voronoi cells, SSV distortion, k derivation)
- **B:** Time dilation mechanism clarification
- **C:** Topology and quasicrystalline nature of the lattice
- **D:** Rigorous analytic PSR derivation from packing geometry
- **E:** Nonlinear extensions and full polytopal volume integrals
- **F:** Mode-counting derivation of Unruh temperature modification
- **G:** Uniqueness of the 600-cell: lattice selection theorem
