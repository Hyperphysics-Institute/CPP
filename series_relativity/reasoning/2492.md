## The opening frame

The handover predicted the fitting pressure would peak here, and it did. M2 is
where "the drift pre-empts a floor of the in-plane budget" must be DERIVED. The
first structural decision, before any physics: decompose the target rule into
its independent features — (i) floor, (ii) Π-projection magnitude, (iii)
Π-isotropy — because a derivation must source each, and a launder would source
none while sounding like it sourced all three. The second decision: enumerate
the composition rules a target-ignorant physicist would write down for "drift
meets per-Moment budget," and only then ask what the postulates say.

## The attempted routes, in order, and where each died

**Route 1 — translation.** The obvious first move: the per-Moment displacement
is ONE computed act (PCD); the drift is a component of its output; the
admissible set is the arena, unchanged. Consequence: no exclusion, ε = 0. Not
rejected — recorded as the default reading of the current postulates. If the
forward answer is R1, the mechanism claim is dead and K1 fires; but certifying
R1 requires certifying that the motion state adds NO per-Moment constraint,
which is the same unpinned question as certifying that it adds one.

**Route 2 — the dressed-state narrative (ABANDONED, K3′).** Drafted: a
uniformly moving CP is dressed by co-moving Sea polarization; per-Moment the
dressed configuration must be reproduced translated by the drift; the
reproduction consumes cycle capacity; the consumed capacity is in-plane and
direction-indifferent of magnitude d — hence the tube. Applied the K3′ test at
the last clause and it failed loudly: WHY in-plane-isotropic, WHY a magnitude
floor? The only answer available was "because that is the tube's shape." The
narrative's earlier clauses are plausible physics, but their per-Moment
quantitative content — what reproduction of the state costs the cycle — is
precisely the SF-6 storage mechanism, which the corpus does not have. The
polaron reframe (Gate-1/B1 arc) names the object but does not quantify its
per-Moment budget interaction. Abandoned at the fork, before writing it into
the session log as derivation.

**Route 3 — the c01 template transfer.** The corpus HAS one established
per-Moment obligation: the τ-advance — exact, mandatory, stress-invariant. If
the drift inherited that structure, the rule is a slice x₁ = d. Computed the
consequence: measure-zero free set, ε = ∞ for any v > 0, and the rest-limit is
violently discontinuous. The corpus's own obligation template KILLS itself
here — a genuinely useful negative: whatever the drift obligation is, it is
NOT c01-shaped. This also blocks the tempting shortcut "the drift is obligatory
in the same sense the τ-advance is" — it demonstrably cannot be.

**Route 4 — the rest-limit filter (KEPT — the session's one forward
elimination).** M1 step (iv) established the distinguished structure dies at
v = 0 with d → 0. Continuity of the capacity fraction as v → 0⁺ follows from
c01's displacement-response being continuous in net SSV (an infinitesimally
moving aggregate = an infinitesimally perturbed Sea state; nothing in the
corpus quantizes the motion state discontinuously at v = 0). Applied to the
enumeration: kills R3h (half-space; fraction → 1/2) and R3s (slice;
fraction → 0). Checked the filter's own K3′ status: it kills across exponent
classes (a would-be n = 1 member and the no-identity slice), so it is not
sculpting toward the tube. Kept.

**Route 5 — the budget-metric anchor (RETAINED, DEMOTED).** SR-1's strain
variable is the round 4D radius: at the budget level the corpus treats τ̂ and
ê_motion symmetrically. First draft leaned this into support for the tube;
the honest weight: it grounds feature (iii) ONLY — given a magnitude-floor
rule at budget level, roundness makes it Π-isotropic — and says nothing about
why a floor (i) or why the Π-projection (ii). Demoted to a conditional anchor
and logged as such.

**Route 6 — the burden-text trap (CAUGHT).** Momentary impulse to argue "M2
itself says full symmetric neighborhood, so symmetric it is." Caught
immediately: the burden text was panel-designed after the target was known
(§1c is explicit about this); it specifies the obligation, it cannot ground
it. Using the burden as premise is laundering the target through the panel's
own instrument. Recorded because the trap is subtle and a future instance will
meet it.

## The disposition reasoning

The strongest pull of the whole session was here, and it was NOT toward
claiming the tube — it was toward self-adjudicating the kill in the merciful
direction ("clearly this should suspend pending SF-6"). The 2489 discipline
applies symmetrically: a session that lawyers its kill conditions toward
survival is corrupt, and a session that unilaterally suspends its own clock is
the same failure in slower motion. So: both options (α: K1 fires; β: explicit
founder extension gated on SF-6, with the pre-commitment that an SF-6 landing
on R1/R2/R3y kills with no further extension) are written out, each with its
against-interest framing, and the choice ships upward. One addition made
during drafting: if (β) is taken, the fork must NOT be shown to the SF-6
working instance — the inertia investigation was opened blind to this fork,
and that blindness is exactly the independence that would make its verdict
mean something. The fork goes to the panel; the SF-6 window gets only its
original charter.
