# Tier-4 reasoning fragment — Patch 3030 (F-SW-7 R-B/R-C/R-E fix pass + O-7 c01 sub-item)

**Captured at-patch, producing window.**

The pass's central judgment was how hard to lean on AP-4 before
ratification. The 3016 note held R-B/R-C/R-E precisely because the
target ontology was moving; CONV-013's endorsement fixed it ("AP-4
v1.1 as the eventual ratified text") without enacting it. The
resolution adopted: every fix is grounded in the RATIFIED clauses
(AP-2 minimal content, FI-QMRG-1 phase-as-register) — which already
suffice to retire phase-on-messenger language on their own — with
AP-4a/AP-4d cited as the endorsed successor reading and marked
"ratification pending" at every occurrence. If ratification were
somehow refused, the edits would remain correct under the ratified
ontology; only the forward-pointing citations would need trimming.
That asymmetry is what made it safe to execute now rather than wait.

**"Phase-coherent SSV distribution" was kept, not purged.** Three of
the five R-B sites contained that phrase, and a mechanical sweep
would have rewritten it everywhere. But under FI-QMRG-1 the phrase is
TRUE — phase is exactly a property of the SSV_net register field, and
the distribution of registers across GPs is precisely where coherence
lives. The error in the old text was only the transport story
(strings "propagating in phase"). So the fix relocates the bearer and
keeps the register-level claim, adding "not on the messengers" where
the ambiguity was live. Purging correct register-level language to
kill incorrect messenger-level language would have been the 2998
QM-6 lesson in reverse — organizing prose around fear of a phrase
rather than around the ontology.

**The Compute-item fix required the relay picture, not just a
re-label.** "Propagated in phase since then" was wrong twice: phase
on the messenger, AND a single messenger persisting from the emission
event. Under Version B relay + AP-4c reset, no messenger survives
past one Moment; what persists is the register lineage. The corrected
sentence had to supply that mechanism ("descend, Moment by Moment
through the relay") or the coherence claim would have become
unsupported — a reader could fairly ask what maintains coherence if
nothing carries it. This is the one place in the pass where the
prose-layer fix carried real explanatory load.

**SR-1's glossary DI-bit entry was deliberately NOT edited.** The
residual grep surfaced it (per-CP "binary charge state" content
reading — pre-AP-4, no phase). Editing it now would have been
tempting completeness, but it fails two tests: it is content
reconciliation (O-7's job, chartered "on enactment"), not phase
relocation (this pass's job); and rewriting a shipped flagship's
definitional entry against a not-yet-ratified amendment inverts the
enactment order. Registered in the sweep record §4 instead. Same
call for master_glossary.md.

**The hygiene fix earned its place the same way QM-4's did.** c06's
`\newcommand{\hbar}{\hbar}` is illegal LaTeX that failed at baseline
(stash-verified before touching it — the verification mattered; had
the failure been introduced by the edit, the right move would have
been fixing the edit, not the preamble). File open, one line, flagged
in CHANGELOG and record. c07's SVG failure got the opposite
treatment — also pre-existing, but environmental (container lacks the
inkscape conversion), owned by the ClearPC workflow, so noted and
left alone.

**The residual grep caught its one site again** (c06's
no-double-counting summary, "DI-bit strings" — naming-only). Third
consecutive sweep where the line-cited inventory missed exactly the
summary/abstract restatement class. The pattern is now sharp enough
to state: inventories built by reading arguments miss the sites that
merely RESTATE arguments. Future sweeps should grep the
summary/conclusion/abstract sections as a distinct step.

Versioning: SR-2 v1.0 → v1.1 (shipped flagship, prose-layer,
CHANGELOG per its own header convention); companions get point bumps
(c01 3→3.1, c03 2→2.1, c06 2→2.1, c07 3→3.1) matching the 2998
attribution-only precedent (v1.1→v1.2 class), with CHANGELOG headers
added to the three companions that lacked them.
