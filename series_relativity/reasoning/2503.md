## The structural decision: what "rewrite" means under G7

The temptation in a rewrite after a favorable ruling is to walk back withdrawals — the
α ruling grounds ε = γ−1, so why not restore something? The handover forecloses this
(task 2: withdrawals STAY; gate: no re-parametrizing toward restored predictions), and
the physics agrees: the ruling makes the withdrawals *stronger*, not weaker. At v19 the
predictions were withdrawn because no acceleration law existed — an absence, in
principle repairable. At v20 the pinned mechanism supplies the acceleration law, and it
is exactly the wrong shape for the predictions: the store is ½κv² (velocity), the
acceleration response is F_self = −κa at Laue coefficient exactly 1 for closed patterns
— i.e. the acceleration response *is* relativistic inertia, exhausted, nothing left
over. So the withdrawal graduates from "not derived" to "settled negative within the
pinned mechanism." That is the mechanism-level diagnosis task 2 asked for, and it is
why the new subsection ends "The withdrawal is therefore not provisional."
