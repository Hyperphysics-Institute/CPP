That is precisely the α-freedom, exercised for V₀ ≈ 16.693 — a factor thirty times larger
than the α_geom the paper fought to delete. The paper *performs* the rescaling it denies

The 4D→3D projection prefactor R_4D,0/r_3D,0 = φ√2/√(2+√5) is *"absorbed into the Planck
