## The stake check, made first

Before comparing anything: does this window have an authorship stake? It authored
neither the SR-MECH arc nor the pin, so the charter's no-authorship-stake condition
is satisfied in the ordinary sense. But an honest stake audit found one anyway, of a
different kind: this window knows the Patch-2493 pre-committed semantics (exposed at
bootup via commit messages — disclosed then, on the record). Knowing which fork
landing "wins" is a stake in the adjudication even without authorship. Consequence
drawn immediately and held throughout: the reconciliation may compare, it may
register, it may draft the round package — it may not score the fork. Every time the
comparison brushed the fork (T1 especially), the move was capture-and-delay, not
resolution.

## The pull that had to be resisted, named

The strongest fitting pressure of this session: the pin's "uniform motion costs
nothing" reads, at first contact, as R1 — and R1 settles the mechanism question
negative, which would make the already-fired K1 look prescient and the whole SR
closure tidy. The tidiness is exactly the hazard. Two independent reasons the
R1 reading is not free: (i) the pin's model has no capacity set — "no cost" in a
continuum toy is not automatically "no constraint on the PSR admissible set"; a
mechanism can be budget-silent rather than budget-zero; (ii) the reader who knows
the semantics (this window) is the wrong reader to make that call. So T1 was framed
as the round's Q1 with both readings stated, and the record explicitly says the
reconciliation does not answer it. If the panel finds the mapping trivially R1, fine
— it will have found it, not inherited it.

## The near-miss (logged)

First draft of §6 item 2 wrote: "the pin's acceleration-dependent back-reaction is
the acceleration-dependent contribution OPEN-SR-1-PREDICTIONS asked for." Checked
and softened before it hardened: the item asks for a contribution yielding a
*genuine residual beyond γ_SR*; the pin's F ∝ a is the ordinary inertial response,
and for closed patterns the Laue result gives coefficient exactly 1 — i.e. possibly
*no* residual, which would resolve the item in the accept-interpretation direction
rather than the restore-predictions direction. Writing the first draft's sentence
would have quietly promised SR-1 new predictions the mechanism may in fact forbid.
The corrected text states the gate (T1/T2 + the O(β²) debt) instead.

## Why one open item rather than three

T1 and T2 are both consumed by the same pre-registered round, and T3 is a recorded
no-op; three IDs would be registry noise with one resolution event. One item
(OPEN-SR-SF6-RECON-1) tracking all three, closing when the round reports, keeps the
count honest. Grep run before registering: no collision.

## Convergence-weighting discipline

C1 was weighed hardest because it is the only convergence where the two windows
answered the *same* question (does uniform motion consume the cycle?) from opposite
sides of the firewall and agreed. C2–C4 are consistency observations, real but
lighter; the record ranks them by placement, not by adjectives. The temptation to
bill C1 as "confirming" the K1 closure was resisted in wording: the closure was a
ruling on the corpus-as-derived; C1 is mechanism-level support for the same
direction. "Same direction, opposite side of the firewall" is the honest phrase and
it is the one used.

## What was deliberately not done

No fork scoring (K4; the round's job). No coefficient promotion (anti-priority).
No FI-SR-REACH work (2495 ruling; the pin's charter never raised it). No dispatch
of the round package (founder timing). No edit to PROP-SR-H1-1 (its GEOMETRIC
EXISTENCE ONLY marking is untouched by a reconciliation that declines to test
mechanism against geometry). Dashboard touches limited to the owed mechanical syncs
(FP row for OPEN-FP-6-INERTIA-1's existence; SR row for the new item) — the first
unblinded dashboard session pays the debt the blind session recorded.
