## The three load-bearing reasoning steps

1. **Q2, not Q1, decides inflation.** From Brick #1 §3: PSR_eff ≤ l_P under either Q1 reading, so
   ceiling expansion is linear at c. De Sitter = unbounded super-luminal comoving recession ⇒ requires
   l_P itself to vary. Hence the inflation verdict is entirely a Q2 question. Brick #2 closing Q1 does
   not move it.

2. **The two roles of l_P must be split.** l_P is both (a) the timelike advance per Moment (c01,
   graph-operation, stress-invariant) and (b) the spatial reach ceiling (baseline PSR). "Variable
   metric" only makes sense, and only avoids contradicting c01's fixed Absolute Moment, if it is read
   as: the graph operation (one tick) is invariant; the *physical distance* that one tick's reach
   represents is medium-set (DP-Sea μ,ε) and hence epoch-dependent. This disambiguation is new in
   Brick #3 and is required for Brick #4 to be coherent.

3. **The anti-correlation is the crux.** Branch F dissolves the first-moment infinity (finite
   geometric ceiling of the bare 600-cell ⇒ H-axiom unnecessary) BUT forecloses inflation (linear at
   c). Branch V opens inflation (varying l_P ⇒ super-luminal comoving recession) BUT reinstates the
   infinity (no medium ⇒ l_P large/undefined ⇒ needs a regulator). The same knob does both, opposite
   ways. You cannot keep the finite first moment AND get native inflation from this mechanism. Brick
   #4 picks one and pays its cost.
