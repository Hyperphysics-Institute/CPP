## §1 — The claim to be proven

**The PSR-only coupling lemma.** SSV_abs enters CPP dynamics through
no channel other than the PSR — the per-Moment propagation step
length (SR-1). If that holds, then since t_P is the universal fixed
tick, a change in SSV_abs is a pure rescaling of the local
step-per-tick: every process built from propagation slows by the one
common factor PSR(SSV_abs)/PSR₀. All dimensionless gearings — α, μ =
m_p/m_e, g-factors, every ratio of rates — are then **exactly**
epoch-invariant: not to a part per million, but identically. The
ppm nulls of Patch 3440 §5.2 and the Ġ/G bounds of §5.3 are passed
with unbounded margin, and the "(1+z) as ancient-clocks-slower"
reading of §5.4 becomes observationally admissible.

This is CPP's Einstein equivalence principle for rates. GR passes the
α/μ/Ġ tests because gravity couples through the metric only; CPP
passes them iff SSV couples through the PSR only. OPEN-SR-8 (kinetic
vs gravitational SSV) and OPEN-SR-9/R2 (photon-speed LPI) are the
same principle's other two faces; this is the broadest one.

## §2 — What the founder's premise covers, and what remains

The premise settles the **link**: PSR is a definitional function of
SSV_abs (SR-1's registered formula; the kernel picture's "l_P
determined by SSV_abs"). Two further properties are needed, and
neither is definitional:

- **Exclusivity (violation channel V1).** No *second* SSV-coupled
  quantity scaling differently from the PSR. The known live candidate
  is the DP stiffness C: OPEN-SR-9's channel-identity sub-question (2)
  is precisely whether c varies through C, the bare coupling, or the
  kinematic PSR. If any physical rate inherits SSV through C with a
  different functional form than through PSR, gearings drift. UNIV-1
  and SR-9 share this fork; whichever resolves it first feeds the
  other.

- **Completeness (violation channel V2).** No rate-setting primitive
  that is *Moment-fixed* rather than propagation-paced. If a bond
  flip, a quark colour switch, an evaporation event, or any Compute-
  phase operation costs a fixed number of Moments independent of
  SSV_abs, then a process's duration is
  N_prop·(d/PSR) + N_fixed·k: the first term stretches with SSV, the
  second does not, and two processes with different prop/fixed mixes
  change their *ratio* — species-dependent drift, the thing α(z)
  kills.

## §3 — The quantitative confrontation

Over cosmic history the Sea's mass density fell substantially (3439:
ρ_m ∝ m̄, falling), so SSV_abs changed by a factor λ of order unity or
more. A V2 primitive holding budget share ε of an atomic rate yields
gearing drift ~ ε·(1 − 1/λ). The α null at ~10⁻⁶ then forces
**ε ≲ 10⁻⁶** for every process pair — untenable as a tuning, so the
outcome is binary: **either every rate-setting primitive is
propagation-paced by construction (lemma holds, exact universality),
or the §5.4 reading is dead.** There is no partial credit.
