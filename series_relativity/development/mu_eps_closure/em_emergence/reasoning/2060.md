2. **2I is compact.** 600-cell vertices = unit icosians = 2I ⊂ S³ ≅ SU(2). SL(2,ℂ) = SU(2)·(boosts) by
   polar decomposition; SU(2) is the maximal compact, boosts are the non-compact Hermitian-positive part
   (eigenvalues e^{±η/2}). Unit quaternions are unitary ⇒ 2I ∩ {boosts} = ∅. So the vertices give the
   rotation half *only*; the boost half must come from the dynamics. This already tells me the
   "quaternion bridge falls embarrassingly" hope (handover §3 World 1) cannot work *via the vertices
   alone* — the boosts aren't in there.

3. **So: can the PCD update supply the boost directions?** The only velocity-carrying object in the PCD
   rule is the budget split l_P² = (c·Δτ)² + |d_spatial|², |d_spatial| = l_P β. I wrote the rest→motion

   the **unit CIRCLE**. That's a Euclidean rotation by α = arcsin β, generator antisymmetric, M² = −I.
   Compact. Not a boost.

4. **The signature is the whole story.** A boost needs the symmetric generator N, N² = +I, hyperbolic,

   Euclidean length, not a Minkowski one.
