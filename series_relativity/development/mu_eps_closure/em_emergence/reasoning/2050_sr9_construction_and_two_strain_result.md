## 1. Founder adjudication (TLA, captured verbatim) — the §10 contribution

The window asked TLA the sharp mechanism question: in the c06 Perceive→Compute→Displace reconstruction, is the
photon's forward advance set by the scalar PSR alone, or can the magnetic curl-coupling K bias it so that the
budget speed c_b inherits a tensor structure K_ij under anisotropic strain? TLA's ruling:

> A foundational principle of CPP is that the sum of the SSV alone is the determinant of the PSR, which is the
> Planck length (l_p). To emphasize the irrelevance of the B field vector, we note that the origin of the B
> field is merely a configuration of motion of charges, which is purely the E field, which is the source of all
> SSV. And the determinant of the PSR is the scalar sum of all SSV contributions from all charges from the
> entire universe at each GP (600-cell vertex). Thus, all Space Stress Vectors arriving at a GP are stripped of
> their vector-direction component (plus and minus, point of origin to GP), and their absolute magnitude summed
> as a scalar to determine the PSR. In other words, the speed of light in a medium is determined only by the
> scalar magnitude of the SSV at each GP.
>
> Aside: The B field is generated by the motion of a charge. The B field is not a fundamental force; it is a
> complex aspect of the interaction between the E field and charges. In particular, the B field is an artifact
> of the movement of a charge through a Sea of Dipoles. Dipoles are forced into a rotation-like motion around a
> point between the dipole charges. The like charges attract, and opposite charges repel, with the rotational
> effect not being strictly circular because of the differential in repulsion and attraction due to the inverse
> square force and displacement. The point being that the rotational motion of the CPs of the DP has no effect
> on the SSV. The effect that produces the rotational motion of charges does not add or subtract SSV any more
> than that produced by the scalar effect of the charge on the SSV, which in turn produces the PSR.

**Adjudication, in one line:** the advance is **purely scalar-PSR kinematic**; the B/curl/K-channel contributes
zero SSV, hence zero to PSR, hence zero to c_b. Equivalently: B is **not an independent stiffness** — there is
one independent stiffness, the E/SSV scalar channel.
