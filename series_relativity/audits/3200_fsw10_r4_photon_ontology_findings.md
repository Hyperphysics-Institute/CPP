## §3 — Finding R-4.3: TERMINOLOGY DETERMINATION — `soliton` does not enter the SR corpus

The founder's 15 Aug wording described the photon as a "soliton," and on
examination withdrew the term as imprecise (*"I was using the soliton to
describe the second pattern... perhaps soliton-reminiscent"*), asking for a
tighter name.

**Determination (PD-006, terminology only — no physics changed):**

1. `soliton` is **not** imported into the SR corpus. Recon confirms zero
   occurrences at any photon-ontology assertion site; the risk was prospective,
   arising from new 3139-era language, not from existing text. Nothing to fix.
2. `soliton` retains its existing unrelated corpus usages (DM-1 and the
   development notes). The two vocabularies stay separated.
3. The canonical names remain the corpus's own: **traveling ZDC/ZBWC pattern**
   (structure) propagating by **constructive summation** (mechanism).
4. **"Correlation wave"** is registered as the plain-language synonym,
   attributed to **Steve Smith**, the founder's physics mentor, from whom the
   founder has the phrase. Prior to Patch 3139 the phrase had *zero* corpus
   occurrences; the attribution is recorded here so the term's lineage travels
   with it.

**Rationale — why not "soliton-reminiscent" either.** A soliton is a structure
*actively defended* against dispersion by a balancing nonlinearity. The CPP
photon is reconstructed from scratch each Absolute Moment by constructive
summation (SF-6 §232). There is no continuant to defend and no nonlinearity
debt incurred against exact linear superposition — consonant with the gapless
acoustic result of the CC arc. The two mechanisms are not neighbours; they are
opposites that present alike externally. "Soliton-reminiscent" would invite
precisely the conflation this determination exists to prevent. For the same
reason the founder's interim phrasing "self-maintaining structured
organization" is **not** adopted as canonical: no maintenance occurs.
