## What Kind of Explanation Is This?

Einstein's SR explains relativistic phenomena by changing the concept of spacetime — replacing Newtonian absolute space and time with a 4D spacetime manifold in which the speed of light is invariant. This is a formal, structural explanation: it reorganises the conceptual framework of physics so that the phenomena fall out of the new structure. It does not ask why the speed of light is invariant; it posits this and shows what follows.

CPP's SR-1 offers a different kind of explanation: a mechanical one. It asks what physical process underlies time dilation. The answer is that physical clocks require cyclic displacement processes, and those processes slow when the displacement budget per Absolute Moment is reduced by stored SSV energy. This is the same kind of account a clockmaker gives for why a clock slows when its spring is weak — not a reference to spacetime structure but a description of the mechanism.

The philosophical gain of a mechanical explanation is intelligibility. At v20 the relabeling risk is answered differently and better: the bridge is grounded by an *independent, firewalled* derivation — the SF-6 inertia mechanism, pinned blind, yields the relativistic energy relation for closed patterns with coefficient exactly 1 at W2 world-call strength. A mechanism derived by a session that could not see the target, landing on the target, is the opposite of relabeling. CPP does not claim to eliminate the need for physical input; it claims the input is now supplied by substrate dynamics rather than assumed.
